## Description
SLC16A14 (MCT14) encodes a 510 amino acid Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) transporter protein (UniProt Q7RTX9). InterPro analysis reveals canonical MFS architecture including IPR011701 (MFS family, aa 41-445), IPR020846 (MFS domain, aa 36-496), IPR036259 (MFS superfamily, aa 32-492), PF07690 (MFS, aa 41-445), and IPR050327 (Proton-linked Monocarboxylate Transporter, aa 29-490). The dual-domain MFS fold (G3DSA:1.20.1250.20 at aa 32-231 and 310-488) confirms classical 12-transmembrane helix topology. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q7RTX9-F1) shows high confidence (pLDDT 79.8) across full length, indicating stable folded transporter. HPA expression reveals salivary gland enrichment (17.5 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced specificity, ER subcellular localization, and immune cell enrichment. Critically, STRING interactions identify 10 partners including SLC5A12 (score 0.491) and SLC5A8 (0.487) - other solute carriers - plus BSG (0.473), the basigin chaperone essential for MCT family plasma membrane trafficking. This BSG interaction strongly supports functional classification as a bona fide MCT. ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (chromosome 2q35-37), though specific diseases remain unannotated. Literature search returned 22 papers, with key studies identifying abundant CNS and kidney expression in mouse, yet substrate specificity remains unknown - confirming orphan transporter status. The combination of MFS domains, BSG chaperone interaction, salivary gland/immune enrichment, and clinical variant burden suggests SLC16A14 functions as an ER-localized proton-linked monocarboxylate symporter regulating metabolite flux in secretory tissues, with copy number alterations potentially contributing to metabolic or developmental disorders.
