# TMOD4: Muscle-Enriched Actin Capping Protein with Leucine-Rich Repeat Domain Regulating Thin Filament Length, Sarcomere Organization, and Cancer Metastasis via Tropomyosin Binding and Coordination with Nebulin/CAPZ/Adducin Complexes

## Description
TMOD4 (Tropomodulin-4, also known as Tmod4 or E-Tmod, Q9NZQ9) encodes a 345 aa intracellular protein functioning as a pointed-end actin filament capping protein that regulates thin filament length in sarcomeres and cytoskeletal dynamics critical for muscle contraction, cell motility, and cancer metastasis. Structural architecture reveals a bipartite organization combining an N-terminal tropomodulin domain (IPR004934/PF03250/PTHR10901, residues 1-344/4-143/1-344) mediating tropomyosin binding (GO:0005523) and pointed-end actin filament capping (GO:0051694), followed by a C-terminal Leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domain superfamily (IPR032675/PF27087/G3DSA:3.80.10.10/SSF52047, residues 181-345/179-338/181-345/181-342) forming a curved solenoid structure for protein-protein interactions; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 86.4, high confidence) confirms a well-folded elongated structure with organized LRR solenoid consistent with stable actin-binding protein requiring precise geometry for filament end recognition. STRING interactions define the complete thin filament regulatory network: nebulin NEB (0.980, highest confidence) as the molecular ruler determining actin filament length in sarcomeres, capping protein regulators CARMIL1 (0.972) and CARMIL2 (0.899) controlling barbed-end capping dynamics, barbed-end capping proteins CAPZA3 (0.963), CAPZA2 (0.888), and CAPZA1 (0.682) working antagonistically at opposite filament ends, adductins ADD3 (0.946), ADD2 (0.540), and ADD1 (0.540) cross-linking actin to membrane spectrin, and dematin DMTN (0.882) stabilizing erythrocyte cytoskeleton. HPA localizes TMOD4 as an actin-binding protein with group-enriched expression highest in skeletal muscle (1148.5 nTPM, extremely high) reflecting its primary role in sarcomere organization, tongue (498.5 nTPM) indicating striated muscle specificity, detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced expression across tumor types suggesting metastatic role, not detected in immune cells, and cell type enriched single cell specificity. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including intronic variant c.397+200G>T potentially affecting splicing, single allele duplication NC_000001.10:g.(?_130980840)_(248900000_?)dup, and multiple chromosome 1q copy number alterations: gains at 1q21.1-25.3 (chr1:143550435-185515177x3), 1q21.1-44 (chr1:143932350-249224684x3), 1q21.1-21.3 (chr1:145398178-151386947x3), 1q21.1-23.1 (chr1:146577511-157155587x3, chr1:144368497-158992086x3), 1q12-23.1 (chr1:142535935-157648813x3), and losses at 1q21.3 (chr1:150483015-151860420x1), 1q21.2-21.3 (chr1:150161903-151243214x1); these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature establishes TMOD4 as the primary pointed-end capper in non-muscle cells and erythrocytes where it works with NEB to set precise thin filament length essential for optimal force generation, regulates cell migration through cytoskeletal remodeling affecting lamellipodia dynamics, promotes cancer metastasis via enhanced cell motility and invasion, functions in erythrocyte membrane stability through spectrin-actin network organization, and modulates cardiomyocyte contractility; extreme skeletal muscle expression (1148.5 nTPM) correlates with sarcomeric function; LRR domain suggests additional protein interaction capacity beyond canonical tropomodulin family members; high-confidence STRING network with NEB/CAPZ/adducins indicates TMOD4 operates within integrated thin filament regulatory complex; conservation from mouse (Q9JLH8, 345 aa) and bovine (Q0VC48, 345 aa) confirms essential structural role; cancer-enhanced expression indicates oncogenic potential through cytoskeletal reprogramming enabling invasion.
