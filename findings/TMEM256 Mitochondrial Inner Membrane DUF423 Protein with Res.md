## Description
TMEM256 (alias transmembrane protein 256, C17orf61) encodes a 113 amino acid small highly structured mitochondrial inner membrane protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 95.9) containing a DUF423 domain of unknown function (IPR006696, aa 7-112), DUF423 Pfam domain (PF04241, aa 23-101), and belonging to the PTHR43461 family (transmembrane protein 256, aa 7-112). HPA shows ubiquitous expression with detected in all tissues distribution, low tissue specificity, predicted intracellular and membrane protein class, vesicles subcellular location, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 17p13.2-13.1, 17p13.1, 17p13.2-12, 17p13.3-11.2 copy number losses/gains and deletions associated with chromosomal deletion/duplication syndromes. STRING interactions reveal exclusive mitochondrial respiratory chain and cristae organization network: ROMO1 (reactive oxygen species modulator 1, mitochondrial inner membrane protein regulating ROS production and cristae morphology, 0.595), MICOS10 (mitochondrial contact site and cristae organizing system subunit 10, cristae junction organizer, 0.554), SDHB (succinate dehydrogenase complex subunit B, Complex II catalytic subunit, 0.540), MRPS24 (mitochondrial ribosomal protein small subunit 24, mitochondrial translation, 0.519), LAMTOR1 (late endosomal/lysosomal adaptor mTORC1 anchoring subunit 1, Ragulator complex anchoring mTORC1 to lysosomal/mitochondrial membranes, 0.508), NDUFA13 (NADH dehydrogenase ubiquinone 1 alpha subcomplex subunit 13, Complex I assembly factor, 0.491), ATP5MF (ATP synthase membrane subunit f, Complex V rotor component, 0.483), NDUFB7 (NADH dehydrogenase ubiquinone 1 beta subcomplex subunit 7, Complex I membrane anchor, 0.478), MECR (mitochondrial enoyl-CoA reductase, fatty acid synthesis enzyme, 0.470), and NDUFA2 (NADH dehydrogenase ubiquinone 1 alpha subcomplex subunit 2, Complex I core subunit, 0.402).
