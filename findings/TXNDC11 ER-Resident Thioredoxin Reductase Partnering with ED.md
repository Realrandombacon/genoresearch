# TXNDC11: ER-Resident Thioredoxin Reductase Partnering with EDEM2 for Glycoprotein ERAD and Mannose Trimming

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
TXNDC11 (Q6PKC3) is a 985-aa thioredoxin domain-containing protein localized to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 70.9). InterPro analysis reveals two canonical thioredoxin domains (IPR013766/PF00085: positions 116-210 and 693-773) plus TXNDC11-specific thioredoxin-like domain (IPR058777/PF26234: positions 216-355), suggesting dual redox active sites. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, consistent with housekeeping ERAD function. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally strong binding to EDEM2 (score 0.966), with medium-confidence interactions to EDEM1 (0.443), EDEM3 (0.600), HSPA5/BiP (0.547), and DNAJC10/ERdj5 (0.477) - all core ERAD machinery components. Literature (27 papers) demonstrates TXNDC11 forms stable disulfide bonds with EDEM2 to catalyze the first mannose trimming step (Man9GlcNAc2→Man8GlcNAc2) in glycoprotein ERAD, marking misfolded proteins for degradation. ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense (p.Ala831Ser) and copy number gains, though specific disease associations remain undefined. The dual thioredoxin domain architecture suggests TXNDC11 may coordinate redox-dependent substrate recognition with EDEM2's mannosidase activity, positioning it as a critical checkpoint in ER quality control. Elevated TXNDC11 expression correlates with unfavorable glioma prognosis, implicating ERAD dysregulation in cancer progression.
