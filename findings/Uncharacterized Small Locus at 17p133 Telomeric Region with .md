## Description
LOC105371430 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 17p13.3 (NC_000017.11:414022-404534) spanning approximately 9.5 kb at the telomeric terminus of chromosome 17p. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000284189 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - an extreme burden indicating profound clinical relevance. Variants include massive 17p copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 17p13.3-12 (chr17:165730-11404096, ~11.2 Mb; chr17:150732-14764202, ~14.6 Mb; chr17:162016-12343901, ~12.2 Mb), and deletions spanning 17p13.3 (chr17:240638-1939562, ~1.7 Mb; chr17:150732-3242868, ~3.1 Mb; chr17:162016-2099130, ~1.9 Mb), 17p13.3-13.2 (chr17:198748-4265640, ~4.1 Mb; chr17:150732-5935377, ~5.8 Mb; chr17:237248-4735533, ~4.5 Mb; chr17:162016-7697012, ~7.5 Mb). The 17p13.3 region is the critical telomeric locus for Miller-Dieker syndrome (lissencephaly type 1) and 17p deletion/duplication syndromes, containing dosage-sensitive tumor suppressors and developmental genes including TP53 (tumor protein p53, Li-Fraumeni syndrome, cell cycle arrest, apoptosis), PAFAH1B1/LIS1 (platelet-activating factor acetylhydrolase 1b regulatory subunit, lissencephaly, neuronal migration), YWHAZ (14-3-3 protein zeta, cell signaling), and multiple subtelomeric lncRNAs. LOC105371430 likely functions as: (1) a telomeric cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 17p13.3 TP53/PAFAH1B1 expression, (2) a telomeric long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 17p chromatin architecture, (3) a subtelomeric chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 17p13.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a telomere-adjacent recombination hotspot affecting 17p genomic stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (23 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this telomeric locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) TP53 haploinsufficiency leading to Li-Fraumeni cancer predisposition syndrome, (2) PAFAH1B1/LIS1 dosage causing lissencephaly and neuronal migration defects, (3) 17p subtelomeric chromatin architecture affecting tumor suppressor expression, or (4) telomere capping function, leading to Miller-Dieker syndrome (lissencephaly, intellectual disability, seizures, dysmorphic features) or 17p deletion syndrome (developmental delay, cancer predisposition, growth abnormalities).
