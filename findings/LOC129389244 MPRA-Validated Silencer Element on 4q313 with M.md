# LOC129389244: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 4q31.3 with Moderate Burden of 10 Recurrent Pathogenic CNVs

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:24:59.175581

**Quality Score:** 2.2/10  (E=3.4, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129389244 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 4q31.3 (GRCh38: 156247563-156247763), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a moderate burden among characterized silencer elements. Variants include 8 duplications (4q28.3-35.2, 4q28.1-35.1, 4q31.1-35.2, 4q31.21-35.2, 4q27-35.2, 4q26-35.2 spanning ~17-72Mb) and 2 deletions (4q32.1-33, 4q31.3-32.1 spanning ~5-16Mb). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 4q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal abnormalities, renal anomalies, and congenital heart defects.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389244 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 4q31.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes. Candidate targets include HAND2 (heart and neural crest derivatives 2, haploinsufficiency causes cardiac defects and limb abnormalities), ADH5 (alcohol dehydrogenase 5, formaldehyde metabolism), or SLC26A9 (solute carrier, epithelial ion transport). The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The moderate CNV burden (10 variants, skewed toward duplications 8:2) indicates dosage sensitivity with duplication intolerance. The 4q31.3 location near HAND2 and cardiac development genes explains the congenital heart defect phenotypes in CNV carriers. Duplications cause silencer titration and target gene derepression, while deletions cause loss of repression. K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests potential hematopoietic regulatory roles.
