# SPATA7: Ciliary Transition Zone Scaffold Protein in Photoreceptors with High Retinal Disease Variant Burden

## Description
SPATA7 (spermatogenesis associated 7, aliases LCA3, RP94, HSD3, HEL-S-296) encodes a 599 amino acid protein essential for photoreceptor cilium integrity and retinal function. InterPro analysis identifies the SPATA7 family domain IPR029357 (residues 9-599) and PF15244 HSD3 domain (residues 10-422), defining this as a ciliary scaffold protein. STRING-DB reveals 10 high-confidence interaction partners forming a retinal disease interactome: RPGRIP1 (0.951), RDH12 (0.916), RD3 (0.851), RPGR (0.850), RPE65 (0.795), IMPDH1 (0.739), TULP1 (0.726), AIPL1 (0.622), LCA5 (0.572), LRAT (0.447). These interactions place SPATA7 at the photoreceptor connecting cilium transition zone, scaffolding protein complexes critical for outer segment disk morphogenesis and visual transduction. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (53.2 nTPM) with additional retinal expression, consistent with dual roles in spermatogenesis and photoreception. Subcellular localization includes vesicles, primary cilium transition zone, and sperm midpiece. ClinVar contains 109 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (p.Asp270fs, p.Leu193fs, p.Ser75fs), nonsense (p.Glu315Ter), and splice site mutations causing Leber congenital amaurosis type 3 (LCA3) and retinitis pigmentosa type 94 (RP94). AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 57.5) indicates medium confidence with partially disordered regions, consistent with scaffold protein flexibility. Mouse ortholog Q80VP2 (582 aa) shows strong conservation. Mechanistic hypothesis: SPATA7 acts as a transition zone diffusion barrier scaffold, recruiting RPGR-RPGRIP1 complex to maintain ciliary protein composition; loss disrupts outer segment disk renewal causing photoreceptor degeneration.
