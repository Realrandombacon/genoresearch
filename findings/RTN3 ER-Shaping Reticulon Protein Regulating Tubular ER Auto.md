## Description
RTN3 (Reticulon-3, O95197) encodes a 1032 aa endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane protein functioning as a critical ER-shaping factor that generates and maintains tubular ER architecture through membrane curvature induction. Structural architecture reveals a canonical reticulon organization with C-terminal reticulon domain (IPR003388/PF02453/PS50845, residues 844-1032/844-1007/844-1032) containing two hydrophobic hairpin loops that embed into the ER membrane outer leaflet, inducing positive curvature; the extensive N-terminal region (residues 1-843) is intrinsically disordered (AlphaFold pLDDT 42.9), enabling dynamic protein-protein interactions and conformational flexibility required for ER remodeling. STRING interactions define ER dynamics and autophagy networks: beta-secretases BACE1 (0.909)/BACE2 (0.962) implicated in amyloid precursor protein processing, ER-shaping partners REEP5 (0.906)/ATL3 (0.905)/RTN4 (0.826), autophagy receptors GABARAP (0.797)/GABARAPL2 (0.691) mediating selective ER-phagy, ER-phagy receptor CCPG1 (0.794), ER translocation component SEC62 (0.454), and ER-phagy regulator RETREG1 (0.400). HPA localizes RTN3 to endoplasmic reticulum with tissue-enhanced expression peaking in brain (620.3 nTPM), classified as membrane protein involved in apoptosis, ER-Golgi transport, stress response; immune cell-enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic variants including 11q12-13 CNVs and splice site mutations (c.3053+4_3053+7del). Literature (203 papers) demonstrates multifaceted roles: hypoxic tumor-derived exosomal miR-4488 induces macrophage M2 polarization promoting pancreatic neuroendocrine neoplasm liver metastasis through RTN3/FABP5-mediated fatty acid oxidation (44x citations, 2024), RTN3 regulates collagen biosynthesis and profibrotic macrophage differentiation promoting pulmonary fibrosis via CRTH2 interaction (2025), full-length RTN3 regulates tubular ER turnover via selective autophagy/ER-phagy (2x citations, 2025), RTN3 deficiency exacerbates cisplatin-induced acute kidney injury through mitochondrial stability disruption (5x citations, 2024), lipid overload-induced RTN3 activation causes cardiac dysfunction. Mechanistic hypothesis: RTN3 hairpin domains insert asymmetrically into ER outer leaflet, generating membrane curvature for tubule formation; during ER stress or metabolic overload, RTN3 recruits GABARAP/GABARAPL2 via LIR motifs to initiate ER-phagy, selectively degrading excess ER membranes; RTN3 interaction with BACE1/2 localizes these secretases to curved ER/ Golgi membranes, modulating amyloid-beta production in Alzheimer's disease; in cancer
