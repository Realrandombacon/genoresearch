## Description
ANKRD18B (Ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein 18B) encodes a 1011 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein localized to chromosome 9p24.3. UniProt accession A2A2Z9. InterPro analysis reveals extensive ankyrin repeat architecture: IPR002110/PF00023 ankyrin repeats spanning residues 38-198 (three repeats), forming protein-protein interaction surfaces (GO:0005515). The protein contains a large coiled-coil region (PF14915, residues 204-677) characteristic of CCDC144C-like proteins, suggesting dimerization or oligomerization capacity. Critically, ANKRD18B harbors DUF3496 (IPR021885/PF12001, residues 873-977), a domain of unknown function that may confer specialized binding activity. HPA classifies ANKRD18B as an "essential protein" with brain-enriched expression (7.7 nTPM) and testis expression (4.1 nTPM), indicating neuronal and germline functions. STRING interactions reveal partnerships with THAP8 (0.570, THAP zinc finger transcription factor), C1orf131 (0.541, uncharacterized protein), PRSS3 (0.496, trypsin-3), CCDC198 (0.478), RLF (0.473, rearrangement hotspot), RFXANK (0.434, MHC class II regulator), ASB9 (0.430, ankyrin SOCS box ubiquitin ligase), and ANKRD16 (0.424). The ankyrin repeat-mediated interactions with ASB9 and ANKRD16 suggest ANKRD18B functions as a scaffold in ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation pathways. ClinVar contains 71 pathogenic variants including large copy number gains across 9p24.3-q21.13 and intronic SNVs, indicating dosage sensitivity and potential neurodevelopmental disease associations. The combination of brain expression, ankyrin repeat scaffold architecture, and interactions with transcriptional regulators (THAP8, RFXANK) suggests ANKRD18B operates as a neuronal chromatin or signaling scaffold, potentially linking ubiquitin ligase complexes to transcriptional control in neural development.
