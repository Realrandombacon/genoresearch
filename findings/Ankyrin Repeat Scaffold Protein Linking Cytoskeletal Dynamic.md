## Description
ANKRD29 encodes a 301 amino acid ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein that functions as a molecular scaffold bridging cytoskeletal and nuclear compartments. The protein is dominated by ankyrin repeat motifs (IPR002110, PF00023, PF12796) spanning nearly the entire sequence (aa 4-300), forming a solenoid structure optimized for protein-protein interactions. Notably, InterPro classifies ANKRD29 within the 'Dendritic Spine Regulation and Scaffold' family (IPR50889, PTHR24166), suggesting specialized roles in neuronal or structural contexts. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (global pLDDT: 92.1), indicating a stable, well-folded ankyrin repeat solenoid. STRING interactions reveal strong associations with adducin family members ADD1 (0.811), ADD2 (0.774), and ADD3 (0.780) — membrane-cytoskeleton proteins that cap actin filaments and recruit spectrin, suggesting ANKRD29 participates in cytoskeletal organization. Additional partners include transcription factors TFCP2 (0.727) and UBP1 (0.709), cholesterol transporter NPC1 (0.677), and extracellular matrix component LAMA3 (0.466). HPA data shows nuclear localization (nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies) despite predicted membrane association, cancer-enhanced expression across tissues, and cell type-enriched RNA patterns. ClinVar documents 35 pathogenic copy number variants (gains and deletions) on chromosome 18q, indicating dosage sensitivity. The convergence of ankyrin scaffold architecture, adducin interactions, nuclear localization, and cancer-enhanced expression supports a model where ANKRD29 functions as a mechanotransduction adaptor that relays cytoskeletal state to nuclear transcriptional programs, potentially dysregulated in malignancy through altered gene dosage.
