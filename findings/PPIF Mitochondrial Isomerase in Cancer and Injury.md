# PPIF: Mitochondrial Isomerase in Cancer and Injury

## Description
PPIF (Peptidylprolyl isomerase F) is a well-studied mitochondrial protein (UniProt P30405) belonging to the Cyclophilin-type peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase family. It functions as a critical component of the Mitochondrial Permeability Transition Pore (MPTP), interacting with high-confidence partners VDAC1, SLC25A4, and TP53. While classified as 'WELL-STUDIED' with 224 literature papers, it exhibits significant clinical relevance through 11 pathogenic copy number variants and roles in Colorectal Cancer (neutrophil NETosis), Hepatic Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury, and Sepsis. Structurally, it is a well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 88.3).
