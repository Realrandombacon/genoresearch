## Description

The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal immunoglobulin domain (G3DSA:2.60.40.10, positions 41-160) providing protein-protein interaction surface, followed by two Link domains (IPR000538, positions 161-268 and 272-365) with hyaluronic acid binding activity (GO:0005540) and cell adhesion function (GO:0007155) that anchor HAPLN4 to extracellular matrix.
