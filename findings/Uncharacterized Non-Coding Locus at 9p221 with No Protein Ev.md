## Description

Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. LOC102724102 likely represents a non-coding RNA, pseudogene, or regulatory element within the 9p22.1 chromatin domain. The complete absence of protein evidence across all databases (UniProt, STRING, HPA protein) combined with zero ClinVar variants suggests this locus may be transcriptionally silent, functionally redundant, or evolving neutrally. Alternative hypotheses include: (1) tissue-specific or developmental stage-specific expression not captured by HPA survey, (2) non-coding RNA function without protein translation, (3) cis-regulatory element (enhancer/silencer) controlling neighboring gene expression, or (4) processed pseudogene with no functional constraint. The lack of purifying selection signal (no pathogenic variants) distinguishes this from dosage-sensitive dark genes like LOC497256, LOC400541, and LOC105373100 which show high CNV burden.
