## Description
LOC121725085 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 13650 located on chromosome 2p (genomic coordinates: 28550401-28550695 on NC_000002.12). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional enhancer using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all are copy number gains (triplifications) spanning the 2p region. The 100% gain predominance (3/3 variants) indicates strong dosage sensitivity where amplification disrupts cellular homeostasis. The 2p chromosomal region contains clinically important genes including ALK (anaplastic lymphoma kinase at 2p23, oncogene in neuroblastoma and lymphoma), MYCN (2p24.1, amplified in neuroblastoma), and IRF4 (2p25.1, transcription factor in lymphoid malignancies). As an enhancer, this element likely drives expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 2p domain. Amplification leads to over-activation of oncogenes or developmental regulators, consistent with the gain-only pathogenicity pattern. The lower variant count (3 vs 29 for LOC121852968 on 18q) suggests this enhancer may regulate less dosage-sensitive targets or have more restricted tissue specificity. This element may contribute to 2p deletion/duplication syndromes, cancer predisposition (particularly neuroblastoma given 2p24 MYCN amplification), or developmental disorders through dysregulation of critical 2p genes.
