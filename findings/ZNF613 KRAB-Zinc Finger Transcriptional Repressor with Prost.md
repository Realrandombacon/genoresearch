## Description
ZNF613 encodes a 617-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as a transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 7-78) that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor complexes to silence target genes through heterochromatin formation, plus three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 204-282) for sequence-specific DNA binding. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with prostate as top tissue (18.2 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, and nucleoplasmic/vesicular localization. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnership with ZNF799 (score 0.780) and TRIM28 (score 0.522), plus medium-confidence interactions with ZNF615, ZNF816, ZNF790, ZNF350, and ZNF17—forming a KRAB-ZFP repression network. Sequence composition shows 32.1% charged residues (198/617 aa) and 27.4% hydrophobic residues, consistent with intrinsically disordered transcriptional regulators. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19q13.33-13.43 copy number gains affecting the ZNF613 locus, suggesting dosage sensitivity in disease contexts. The 617 aa length and domain architecture place ZNF613 among medium-sized KRAB-ZFPs that likely silence retrotransposons or developmentally regulated genes in prostate tissue.
