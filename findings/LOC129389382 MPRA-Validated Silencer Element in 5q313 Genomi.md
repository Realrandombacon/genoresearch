# LOC129389382: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 5q31.3 Genomic Region with 5q Deletion Syndrome and Cytokine Cluster Association

**Quality Score:** 3.98/10  (E=4.75, D=3.2) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129389382 (Gene ID: 129389382) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 5q31.3 (143,965,040-143,965,240 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak5509 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic copy number variants all involving 5q31-33 region - 5q31.3-32 duplication (8.4 Mb), 5q23.3-33.2 duplication (23.5 Mb), 5q21.3-33.2 deletion (49.5 Mb), 5q31.2-32 deletion (6.9 Mb), and 5q23.3-33.2 duplication (24.5 Mb); (4) No HPA expression expected (non-coding); (5) No STRING interactions or InterPro domains (non-coding). The 5q31 region is clinically critical - 5q deletion syndrome (del(5q) MDS, OMIM #150620) causes myelodysplastic syndrome with refractory anemia, thrombocytosis, and low blast count; 5q31 microdeletion causes developmental delay, intellectual disability, growth retardation, cardiac defects, and immune dysregulation. Critical 5q31 region contains: cytokine gene cluster (IL5, IL4, IL13 - Th2 immune responses), NIPBL (cohesin loader, Cornelia de Lange syndrome), CDH1 (E-cadherin, cell adhesion, cancer metastasis), IRF1 (interferon regulatory factor, immunity), and multiple developmental transcription factors. 5q rearrangements are recurrent in myeloid malignancies (MDS, AML) and developmental disorders. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129389382 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or immune contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in erythroid/myeloid lineage. 5q31.3 is gene-dense with cytokine cluster (Th2 differentiation), NIPBL (chromatin loop extrusion), and developmental regulators. Silencer disruption via CNVs alters TAD architecture and long-range regulation of IL4/IL5/IL13 cytokine expression (immune skewing), NIPBL dosage (cohesin-mediated chromatin organization), and neighboring developmental genes. Haploinsufficiency of NIPBL causes Cornelia de Lange syndrome; cytokine cluster dysregulation causes immune phenotypes; 5q- syndrome involves RPS14 haploinsufficiency (ribosomal stress, p53 activation). This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes.
