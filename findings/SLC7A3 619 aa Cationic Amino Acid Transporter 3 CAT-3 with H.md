## Description

The 14-TM structure creates a central substrate-binding pocket with alternating access mechanism. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests tumor cells upregulate CAT-3 to support increased arginine demand for nitric oxide synthesis and polyamine production. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt transporter expression causing lysinuric protein intolerance-like phenotypes with impaired renal reabsorption of cationic amino acids. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 618 aa, rat 619 aa) confirms essential metabolic role. NOTE: This gene is well-characterized as CAT-3 with established transporter function.
