## Description
PHOSPHO2/Q8TCD6 (241 aa) is an exceptionally confident (AlphaFold pLDDT: 96.1) haloacid dehalogenase (HAD) superfamily phosphatase functioning as a critical regulator of pyridoxal phosphate (vitamin B6) metabolism and lipid phosphate homeostasis. InterPro analysis reveals canonical HAD hydrolase architecture containing the HAD hydrolase subfamily IA pyridoxal phosphate phosphatase-like domain (IPR006384/G3DSA:3.40.50.1000/IPR023214, residues 2-200/1-197/1-197) with phosphatase activity (GO:0016791), classified within the phosphatase PHOSPHO-type family (IPR016965, residues 2-240) and putative phosphatase Pfam family (PF06888, residues 3-239), distinguishing it from the related bone mineralization enzyme PHOSPHO1. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity but cell type-enhanced pattern, classification as an enzyme/metabolic protein/predicted intracellular protein with hydrolase molecular function, localized to nucleoplasm and vesicles consistent with intracellular phosphate metabolism. The convergence of exceptional structural confidence (pLDDT 96.1, among highest observed), exclusive vitamin B6 metabolism partnerships (PNPO-PDXK-PDXP axis), ubiquitous expression with vesicular localization, dual pyridoxal phosphate/lipid phosphate phosphatase activities, PHOSPHO2-KLHL23 read-through fusion driving gastric cancer proliferation, and chromosome 2q dosage sensitivity suggests PHOSPHO2 functions as a specialized HAD phosphatase coordinating pyridoxal phosphate dephosphorylation for vitamin B6 homeostasis, regulating lipid phosphate signaling through phospholipase C activity, and maintaining cellular phosphate balance in nucleoplasmic and vesicular compartments, with therapeutic potential in understanding vitamin B6-related neurodevelopmental disorders, targeting PHOSPHO2-KLHL23 fusion in gastric cancer, modulating lipid phosphate signaling in metabolic disease, and investigating chromosome 2q deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes.
