# CCDC89: Testis-Enriched Coiled-Coil Scaffold Protein with High Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
CCDC89 encodes a 374 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt Q8N998) localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein is defined by a single extensive coiled-coil domain (PF28422, IPR060354) spanning residues 52-372, comprising >85% of the mature sequence, suggesting a structural scaffold role rather than enzymatic activity. HPA expression profiling reveals testis-enhanced expression (24.3 nTPM) with secondary expression in fallopian tube (7.3 nTPM) and a "group enriched" single-cell pattern, indicating a specialized role in germ cell development or spermatogenesis. STRING interaction analysis identifies medium-confidence partners including EFCAB12 (0.600), CCDC182 (0.543), and notably HEY1 (0.517), a bHLH transcriptional repressor involved in cardiovascular and neural development; this link suggests CCDC89 may function as a co-regulatory scaffold modulating HEY1-mediated transcription in specific tissues. Additional partners include FRG2B, SPACA7 (sperm acrosome associated), and TSPY4, reinforcing a potential germline-specific complex. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses (deletions) and gains (duplications) spanning 11q14.1, highlighting extreme dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts cellular homeostasis, likely leading to developmental defects or infertility. The conserved coiled-coil architecture (mouse ortholog Q9DA73, 98% identity) supports a mechanism of oligomerization or filament formation, potentially serving as a nuclear scaffold that organizes transcriptional repressors like HEY1 during spermatogenesis.
