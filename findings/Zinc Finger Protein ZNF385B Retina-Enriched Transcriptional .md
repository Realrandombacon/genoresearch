## Description
ZNF385B (Zinc Finger Protein 385B) is a 471-amino acid nuclear protein with 21 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein contains three conserved C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, positions 34-58, 157-181, 285-309; PF12874) with Matrin/U1-C-like zinc finger architecture (IPR003604, positions 30-65, 154-188, 282-316) that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding, plus membership in the ZNF385 family (IPR051845, PTHR23067). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 60.5), consistent with intrinsically disordered transcriptional regulators containing ordered zinc finger DNA-binding modules connected by flexible linkers. HPA reveals extreme retina-enriched expression (82.1 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, and nucleoli fibrillar center localization with apoptosis-associated biological process annotation. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily chromosome 2q31.1-32.2/35 copy number losses and gains, plus splice variants c.442-133G>T and c.962-149G>A), though specific disease phenotypes beyond cancer remain uncharacterized. STRING interactions show ZNF385B functions within a zinc finger transcription factor network with high-confidence connections to ZNF674 (0.856), ZNF41 (0.836), ELMO1 (0.721), plus additional zinc finger proteins ZNF774 (0.572), ZNF548 (0.561), ZNF778 (0.541), ZNF517 (0.511), ZNF559 (0.506), ZNF81 (0.432), and VSTM2L (0.499). Recent studies (2020-2025) identified ZNF385B as a potential prognostic and immunotherapy biomarker in ovarian cancer via pan-cancer analysis, downregulated ZNF385B mRNA as an independent predictor of breast cancer outcomes, genetic variants associated with autism spectrum disorder in Bangladeshi children, and a role in TP53-dependent apoptosis pathways. We hypothesize ZNF385B functions as a retina-specific transcriptional regulator that binds DNA via its C2H2 zinc finger array to control expression of apoptosis-related genes during retinal development and homeostasis, with the nucleoli fibrillar center localization suggesting involvement in ribosomal RNA processing or stress response. The ZNF674/ZNF41 interactions (X-linked intellectual disability-associated zinc fingers) hint at neurodevelopmental functions consistent with autism associations, while ELMO1 connection (phagocytosis regulator) suggests potential roles in retinal pigment epithelium phagocytic clearance of photoreceptor outer segments. Cancer-enhanced expression and ovarian/breast cancer biomarker status indicate ZNF385B dysregulation contributes to tumor progression, potentially through TP53 pathway modulation.
