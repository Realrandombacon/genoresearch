## Description
NTNG1 (Netrin-G1, Q9Y2I2) encodes a 539 aa glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored membrane protein functioning as an axon guidance cue and synaptic organizer distinct from secreted netrin family members, mediating thalamocortical axon pathfinding, dendritic arborization, and excitatory synapse formation through interaction with NGL-1 (LRRC4) receptor. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded multi-domain organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 83.6): Laminin/Netrin Extracellular Matrix family domain (IPR050440, residues 31-503) defining the conserved netrin fold, Laminin N-terminal Domain VI (IPR008211/PF00055, residues 46-295/50-295) forming the N-terminal globular module for protein-protein interactions, multiple Laminin-type EGF-like domains (IPR002049, residues 296-354/364-420/420-469) establishing rigid rod-like spacers between functional modules, EGF_CA calcium-binding EGF domain (cd00054, residues 474-498) mediating calcium-dependent conformational stability, EGF_Lam laminin-type EGF domains (cd00055, residues 296-342/364-412/420-467) providing structural rigidity, Laminin G3DSA:2.10.25.10 homologous superfamily domains (residues 30-53/298-354/364-420/421-468) forming the characteristic beta-sheet rich fold, Galactose-binding domain-like G3DSA:2.60.120.260 (residues 54-297) creating the central interaction surface, PF00053 Laminin EGF domain (residues 420-458), and PF24973 Laminin/attractin EGF domains (residues 297-340/364-408) completing the extracellular module arrangement optimized for receptor binding.
