## Description
EPB41L4B (UniProt Q9H329) encodes a 900-amino acid band 4.1-like protein containing a canonical FERM domain (IPR000299, aa 85-369) with three subdomains (F1: aa 85-168, F2: aa 177-269, F3: aa 273-366), an ERM-like family signature (IPR000798), and a FERM-adjacent domain (IPR014847, aa 378-422). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT: 58.0), consistent with scaffold proteins containing both ordered FERM domains and flexible linker regions for membrane-cytoskeleton coupling.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal EPB41L4B's connection to polarity and signaling complexes: strongest link to RhoGEF ARHGEF18 (0.958), followed by atypical protein kinase C members PRKCZ (0.925) and PRKCI (0.914)—both critical for apical-basal polarity establishment. Additional partners include trace amine receptor TAAR1 (0.850) and calsequestrin CASQ2 (0.667). HPA data shows pancreas-enriched expression (140.8 nTPM) with significant liver expression (83.1 nTPM), localizing to plasma membrane and cytosol as expected for FERM proteins.\n\nWith only 25 papers, EPB41L4B remains underexplored despite clear functional importance. The key mechanistic study (2018, 3 citations) demonstrates that EPB41L4B cooperates with polarity protein CRB3 to regulate mammary epithelial cell proliferation through amphiregulin release—linking cell polarity to growth factor signaling. ClinVar contains 38 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 9q31.3 and missense mutations (p.Val328Ile). This positions EPB41L4B as a transitional dark gene—a FERM scaffold bridging polarity complexes to proliferative signaling, with untapped relevance in breast cancer and pancreatic biology.
