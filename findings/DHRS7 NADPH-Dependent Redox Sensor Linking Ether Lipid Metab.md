## Description
DHRS7 (Dehydrogenase/reductase SDR family member 7, UniProt Q9Y394) is a 339 aa oxidoreductase with a canonical NAD(P)-binding Rossmann fold (IPR036291, positions 45-312) and 11beta-HSD1-like SDR domain (cd05332, positions 48-308). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 94.4), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (634.3 nTPM), localized to nucleoplasm and mitochondria. STRING interactions identify partners in lipid/ether metabolism (TMEM126A score 0.552, TMEM38A 0.479), related SDR enzymes (DHRS1 0.474), and mitochondrial redox proteins (FDX1 0.474, FDX2 0.463, HAO2 0.470, OXSM 0.459). ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic copy number variants at 14q22.3-23.2. Recent literature (40 papers) indicates DHRS7 integrates NADP+/NADPH redox sensing with inflammatory lipid signaling via the oxoeicosanoid pathway, produces distinct plasmalogen species from DHRS7B, and serves as a biomarker in prostate cancer and necrotizing enterocolitis. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: DHRS7 acts as a metabolic checkpoint enzyme that couples cellular redox state (NADPH availability) to ether lipid biosynthesis and inflammatory mediator production. The mitochondrial/nuclear dual localization suggests it may regulate both membrane lipid composition and transcriptional responses to oxidative stress. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: DHRS7 reduces alkylglycerone phosphate intermediates using NADPH, competing with DHRS7B to determine plasmalogen species output. Under inflammatory conditions, altered NADP+/NADPH ratios modulate DHRS7 activity, shifting oxoeicosanoid production and affecting immune cell function in conditions like necrotizing enterocolitis.
