## Description
PIP5K1C encodes a 668 aa cytosolic and nucleoplasmic lipid kinase (UniProt O60331) that functions as the primary generator of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate [PI(4,5)P2] at the plasma membrane, a critical signaling lipid governing cytoskeletal dynamics, membrane trafficking, and ion channel regulation. This moderately studied gene (85 publications) has definitive clinical significance as the causative gene for Lethal Congenital Contractural Syndrome 3 (LCCS3), a severe autosomal recessive disorder characterized by fetal akinesia, multiple joint contractures, and pulmonary hypoplasia leading to perinatal death. The protein features a conserved phosphatidylinositol phosphate kinase (PIPK) architecture: N-terminal PIPK domain (IPR027484/G3DSA:3.30.800.10, residues 73-240) mediating membrane association and regulatory interactions; central core catalytic domain (IPR002498/IPR023610/PF01504/PS51455/cd17308, residues 75-449/49-445/159-441) containing the ATP-binding pocket and substrate recognition site for phosphorylating PI4P to PI(4,5)P2; C-terminal domain superfamily (IPR027483/G3DSA:3.30.810.10, residues 241-458) completing the catalytic fold; classified within PIP5K family with GO terms for phosphatidylinositol kinase activity, phosphatidylinositol metabolic process, cell adhesion, chemotaxis, endocytosis, exocytosis, and lipid metabolism. STRING interactions reveal PIP5K1C operates within extremely high-confidence cytoskeletal and membrane trafficking networks: TLN2 (0.969)/TLN1 (0.968), talin-2 and talin-1 linking integrins to actin cytoskeleton via direct PIP5K1C binding; SYNJ1 (0.951), synaptojanin-1 phosphoinositide phosphatase regulating endocytic recycling; PIP5K1A (0.934), PIP5K type 1 alpha paralog; VCL (0.907), vinculin stabilizing focal adhesions; PI4KA (0.792)/PI4KB (0.773)/PI4K2A (0.783)/PI4K2B (0.792), phosphatidylinositol 4-kinases providing the PI4P substrate; ARF6 (0.435), ADP-ribosylation factor 6 regulating membrane traffic. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with brain enrichment (116.0 nTPM), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, low cell type specificity, nucleoplasm and cytosol localization consistent with lipid signaling function, classified as enzyme/metabolic protein/kinase/transferase with disease variant involvement. ClinVar lists 54 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations within the catalytic core (p.Gly385Val, p.Ser318Gly, p.Gly337Asp, p.Arg132Ser, p.Pro347Leu, p.Phe131Cys, p.Gly86Asp, p.Arg234Cys, p.Ala297Thr) disrupting kinase activity or substrate binding, and large-scale copy number gains (19p13.3 3.1-5.5Mb x3), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity and direct causality for LCCS3. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 63.8), revealing a structured catalytic core consistent with known PIPK folds connected by flexible linker regions enabling conformational changes during membrane binding and catalysis. Mechanistically, PIP5K1C operates through integrated modes: (1) PI(4,5)P2 synthesis—catalyzes rate-limiting step converting PI4P to PI(4,5)P2 at plasma membrane; (2) Focal adhesion assembly—direct binding to talin (TLN1/2) recruits PIP5K1C to integrin sites generating local PI(4,5)P2 pools that stabilize talin-actin linkage and recruit vinculin; (3) Endocytosis regulation—SYNJ1 interaction coordinates PI(4,5)P2 synthesis and hydrolysis during clathrin-coated vesicle formation; (4) Viral entry restriction—2024 study (12 citations) identifies PIP5K1C as host factor required for SARS-CoV-2 entry, with dual PIP5K1C/PIKfyve inhibitors blocking infection; (5) LCCS3 pathogenesis—loss-of-function mutations cause defective neuromuscular junction formation and muscle contraction failure due to impaired PI(4,5)P2-dependent synaptic vesicle cycling; (6) Bone remodeling—2024 study (7 citations) shows osteocyte-specific PIP5K1C regulates bone mass; (7) Osteoarthritis prevention—2023 study (12 citations) demonstrates chondrocyte PIP5K1C loss causes spontaneous osteoarthritic lesions; (8) Neuronal function—brain enrichment and synaptic localization support role in neurotransmitter release. Loss-of-function causes LCCS3, defective focal adhesions, impaired endocytosis, increased viral susceptibility, and skeletal disorders.
