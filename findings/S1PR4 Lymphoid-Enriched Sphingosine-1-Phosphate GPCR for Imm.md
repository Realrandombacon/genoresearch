## Description
S1PR4 encodes a 384 aa sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor (UniProt O95977), also known as EDG6, functioning as a G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) for lysosphingolipid signaling in immune cell trafficking, macrophage differentiation, and tumor microenvironment modulation. The protein contains a canonical rhodopsin-like 7-transmembrane domain architecture (IPR000276/PF00001/G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, aa44-323) with S1P receptor family signatures (IPR004061, aa20-130) and EDG-6 specific motifs (IPR004064, aa10-165). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 78.4), indicating a well-folded 7TM structure typical of class A GPCRs. HPA shows lymphoid tissue-enhanced expression (58.6 nTPM) with bone marrow expression (18.5 nTPM) and mitochondrial localization, consistent with immune cell-specific sphingolipid sensing. STRING interactions reveal S1PR4 couples to multiple G protein alpha subunits: GNA12 (0.989), GNA13 (0.924), GNAI1/2/3 (0.909-0.912), GNA15 (0.892), GNA11 (0.593), plus S1P pathway enzymes SPHK1 (0.875)/SPHK2 (0.922) and paralog S1PR1 (0.948). ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants, predominantly 19p13.3 copy number gains/losses, though S1PR4-specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (134 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: tumor-educated hepatic stellate cell-derived S1P binding S1PR4 promotes tumor-associated macrophage (TAM) differentiation through fatty acid oxidation (FAO) modulation, S1P/S1PR4 axis drives CD8+ tissue-resident memory T cell differentiation aggravating bile duct injury in biliary atresia, S1PR4 promotes cell viability/invasion/glycolysis via mTOR pathway in endometriosis, and etrasimod (S1PR modulator) exerts S1PR4-dependent effects on myeloid immune cell activation. S1PR4 likely functions as a lymphoid-specific S1P gradient sensor coupling to Gα12/13 and Gαi proteins to regulate Rho/ROCK cytoskeletal remodeling for immune cell migration, while simultaneously modulating metabolic reprogramming (FAO, glycolysis) through mTOR signaling. The mitochondrial localization suggests potential roles in apoptosis regulation or metabolic sensing. 19p13.3 CNVs may contribute to immune dysregulation phenotypes through S1PR4 dosage effects on lymphocyte trafficking and macrophage polarization in cancer and autoimmune contexts.
