## Description
BICD1 (BICD Cargo Adaptor 1, also known as Bicaudal D Homolog 1, UniProt Q96G01) is a 975 amino acid vesicular cargo adaptor protein with moderate characterization (69 papers) that functions as a dynein-dynactin adaptor linking the minus-end directed microtubule motor to vesicular cargo for retrograde transport. The protein contains Bicaudal-D protein family domain (IPR018477/PTHR31233, residues 1-821/1-821) with cytoskeletal anchor activity (GO:0008093) and dynein complex binding function (GO:0070840), and microtubule-associated protein Bicaudal-D coiled-coil domain (PF09730, residues 74-799), placing it in the essential intracellular transport machinery. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with dynein-dynactin motor complex components and cargo adaptors: DCTN2 (0.878, dynactin subunit 2), DDX11 (0.855, DEAD/H-box helicase 11), DYNC1H1 (0.830, cytoplasmic dynein 1 heavy chain 1), DYNLL1 (0.814, dynein light chain 1), BICD2 (0.797, paralog bicaudal D homolog 2), DCTN1 (0.796, dynactin subunit 1/p150-glued), CELF4 (0.768, CUGBP Elav-like family member 4), DYNC1LI1 (0.670, dynein 1 intermediate chain 1), DYNC1LI2 (0.621, dynein 1 intermediate chain 2), and RAB6A (0.615, Ras-related protein Rab-6A), confirming membership in the complete dynein-mediated retrograde transport network. HPA data shows vesicles subcellular localization, low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, cancer enhanced RNA cancer specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, and classification as predicted intracellular protein involved in host-virus interaction. ClinVar contains 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 12p13.33-11.1/12p13.33-q13.12/12p13.33-11.1/12p13.2-11.1/12p13.33-11.1 (chr12:64621-34650483/82453-49847230/173787-34835837/10155706-34051988/173787-34835837), duplications, and splice site SNVs (c.1006-15C>A, c.426+20A>G), with recent literature linking biallelic loss-of-function variants to peripheral neuropathy and hearing loss. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 71.3) indicates high confidence with well-folded coiled-coil architecture typical of dynein adaptors forming elongated homodimeric structures. Functional hypothesis: BICD1 acts as a cargo adaptor that bridges dynein-dynactin motor complex to vesicular cargo including neurotrophin receptors and Rab6A-positive vesicles for retrograde microtubule transport toward the cell center, where research demonstrates: (1) Biallelic loss-of-function variants in BICD1 cause peripheral neuropathy and hearing loss (2023, 0 citations)—defective retrograde axonal transport leads to neuronal dysfunction, (2) BICD1::BRAF fusion in melanocytic tumors (2022, 2 citations)—oncogenic kinase fusion driving melanoma progression, (3) Baicalein promotes KDM4E to induce BICD1 inhibiting triple-negative breast cancer via PAR1 signaling blockade (2024, 5 citations)—BICD1 upregulation suppresses TNBC progression, (4) PTPN23 binds BICD1 required for endocytic sorting of neurotrophin receptors (2020, 15 citations)—BICD1 mediates neurotrophin receptor trafficking essential for nervous system development, (5) STRING partners (DCTN1/2, DYNC1H1, DYNLL1, DYNC1LI1/2, BICD2, RAB6A) confirm integration into complete dynein-dynactin transport machinery where BICD1 recruits motor complex to cargo vesicles, (6) Vesicles localization reflects role in endosomal/secretory vesicle transport, (7) Cancer enhanced expression suggests dysregulation in tumor cells potentially affecting growth factor receptor trafficking, (8) 37 ClinVar variants at 12p13.33-11.1 indicate disease causality with loss-of-function mechanism leading to defective retrograde transport in neurons causing peripheral neuropathy and hearing loss, (9) High confidence structure (pLDDT 71.3) indicates stable coiled-coil dimerization domain enabling dynein-dynactin recruitment, making BICD1 a potential therapeutic target for neuropathy through transport pathway restoration and cancer through trafficking modulation.
