## Description
SLC35F6 encodes a 371 amino acid solute carrier family 35 member F6 (UniProt Q8N357), a predicted nucleotide sugar transporter with atypical endosomal/lysosomal localization. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the SLC35F1/F2/F6 family (IPR009262, aa 95-224) and the broader nucleotide-sugar transporter superfamily (IPR012404, aa 1-369), sharing structural homology with the multidrug resistance efflux transporter EmrE (IPR037185, PF06027). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8N357-F1) predicts a highly stable, well-folded 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 84.3), consistent with a functional multi-pass membrane transporter despite its non-Golgi localization. HPA expression reveals ubiquitous tissue distribution with enhanced expression in specific cell types, localized to the nucleoplasm, vesicles, and cytosol, supporting recent findings of endosomal/lysosomal residence rather than canonical Golgi retention. STRING interactions identify 10 partners involved in ubiquitination and metabolic regulation including SLC25A5 (0.680, mitochondrial ADP/ATP carrier), OTUD4 (0.654, deubiquitinase), FBXO6 (0.625, E3 ligase), UBQLN4 (0.610, ubiquitin-like protein), and NUDT3 (0.536, diadenosine triphosphatase), suggesting a role in protein turnover and metabolic signaling distinct from classical glycosylation. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 2p copy number gains and losses (2p25.3-23.1, 2p24.1-22.2), though no specific monogenic disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature (6 papers) identifies SLC35F6 as a critical regulator of hepatocellular carcinoma proliferation via miR-542-3p targeting, bladder urothelial carcinoma progression through outer mitochondrial membrane permeability modulation, and apoptotic signaling activation. The combination of nucleotide transporter domains, high-confidence AlphaFold structure, endosomal/lysosomal localization, ubiquitination-related interaction network, 2p CNV burden, and emerging cancer/apoptosis functions suggests SLC35F6 functions as an atypical endosomal nucleotide sugar transporter coordinating lysosomal metabolism with mitochondrial apoptosis and tumor suppression, with copy number variations potentially contributing to cancer susceptibility and metabolic dysregulation.
