## Description
IFT56 (TTC26) is a 554 amino acid intraflagellar transport protein featuring multiple tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domains that serve as a structural scaffold within the IFT-B complex. InterPro analysis reveals extensive TPR architecture including G3DSA:1.25.40.10 superfamily domains at positions 29-152, 153-269, and 273-530, plus canonical TPR repeats (IPR019734, SM00028) at positions 57-90, 91-124, and 151-184, and a PF30475 IFT56-specific domain spanning residues 61-553. The AlphaFold structure (AF-A0AVF1-F1) shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.6) across all 554 residues, indicating a stable, well-folded helical bundle typical of TPR proteins. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with subcellular localization to primary cilium, nucleoplasm, mitochondria, and cytosol. STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to the IFT machinery with high-confidence partners including IFT52 (0.997), TTC30B/IFT30 (0.997), IFT57 (0.995), IFT88 (0.995), IFT27 (0.994), and unexpectedly strong connections to the VCP-UFD1-NPLOC4 ubiquitin-dependent protein degradation complex (VCP 0.996, UFD1 0.994, NPLOC4 0.994). ClinVar contains 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains and splice site mutations (e.g., c.1069+1G>A), linking IFT56 dysfunction to ciliopathy phenotypes. With only 8 publications, IFT56 remains undercharacterized despite clear disease relevance. The TPR domain architecture suggests IFT56 functions as a molecular scaffold that bridges IFT-B subcomplexes while potentially recruiting ubiquitin-mediated quality control machinery via VCP complex interactions to regulate ciliary protein turnover.
