## Description
ZNF20 (Zinc Finger Protein 20, alias KOX13, UniProt P17024) is a 532 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein with canonical transcriptional repressor architecture. InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal KRAB-A box domain (PF01352, residues 6-47; IPR001909, residues 6-78; IPR036051 superfamily, residues 2-60) that mediates transcriptional repression through recruitment of TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complexes, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096/PF13894/PF13912, IPR013087) spanning residues 103-132, 143-170, 171-198, 227-249, 256-277, 283-305, and 339-361 providing sequence-specific DNA binding capability. AlphaFold structure prediction shows confident folding (global pLDDT 72.2), consistent with well-ordered KRAB-ZFP architecture featuring structured zinc finger domains. HPA expression demonstrates low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues and cell type-enhanced pattern, with predicted nucleoplasmic, nuclear membrane, and nuclear bodies localization—consistent with transcriptional regulatory function. STRING interaction network identifies TRIM28 (score 0.587) as the primary partner—the essential cofactor for KRAB-mediated heterochromatin formation that bridges KRAB-ZFPs to SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 chromatin modifiers. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic copy number variants including both gains and losses spanning chromosome 19p13.2-13.1 regions, indicating dosage sensitivity where both overexpression and haploinsufficiency may be deleterious. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF20 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that binds target gene promoters/enhancers via its C2H2 zinc finger array and recruits the TRIM28-SETDB1-HP1 histone methyltransferase complex through its KRAB domain, establishing H3K9me3-marked constitutive heterochromatin. Ubiquitous expression suggests housekeeping regulatory roles across multiple cell types, potentially silencing retrotransposons, endogenous viral elements, or regulating developmental gene networks. The nuclear bodies localization may indicate involvement in promyelocytic leukemia (PML) body-associated processes such as stress response or antiviral defense. Dosage sensitivity indicated by ClinVar CNVs (both gains and losses) suggests precise ZNF20 expression levels are critical for maintaining proper chromatin states and transcriptional programs.
