## Description
LOC126860253 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 7q36.3 (genomic coordinates 157562474-157563673, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a P300/CBP STRONGLY-DEPENDENT GROUP 1 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors with STRONG DEPENDENCE on P300/CBP (histone acetyltransferases that acetylate histone H3K27 to open chromatin and recruit transcriptional machinery). This cofactor profile suggests it operates through chromatin remodeling and histone acetylation mechanisms. ClinVar contains 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants affecting this locus through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements — this is the HIGHEST variant burden of any enhancer analyzed in this study. The variant pattern shows MIXED pathogenicity: multiple copy number GAINS (chr7:138620939-159233475x3, chr7:137463392-159345973x3, chr7:131171478-159327017x3, chr7:132438072-159327017x3, chr7:131228764-159335866x3) AND multiple copy number LOSSES (chr7:154446117-159206757x1, chr7:147144002-159327017x1, chr7:155262313-159117047x1, chr7:150260297-159325876x1). This bidirectional pattern with extreme variant burden (43 total) indicates strict dosage requirements for developmental gene regulation. The 7q36 region contains critical developmental genes including LMX1B (LIM homeobox transcription factor 1-beta, causing nail-patella syndrome), ACTB (beta-actin), and genes involved in limb and kidney development. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with extreme clinical relevance — the P300/CBP-dependent enhancer activity combined with 43 pathogenic variants suggests LOC126860253 functions as a dosage-critical enhancer controlling 7q36 developmental gene expression, where both amplification and deletion cause developmental disorders.
