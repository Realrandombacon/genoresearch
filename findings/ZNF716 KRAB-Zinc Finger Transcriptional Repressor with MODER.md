## Description
ZNF716 (UniProt A6NP11) is a 495 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein located on chromosome 7p11.2 (GRCh38: 57450176-57473558). InterPro analysis reveals canonical domain architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 16-87) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger arrays (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 150-493) organized in tandem repeats. The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1 (STRING interaction score 0.522) to form heterochromatin-inducing repressor complexes via HP1 binding and histone H3 lysine 9 trimethylation (H3K9me3). ZNF716 may function in early embryogenesis where precise heterochromatin control is critical, with reactivation in cancer representing oncogenic co-option of developmental silencing machinery.
