## Description
TMEM265 encodes a 108 amino acid type II transmembrane protein (UniProt A0A087WTH1) belonging to the CD225/Dispanin family (IPR007593, PF04505, residues 33-92), structurally homologous to interferon-induced transmembrane proteins (IFITMs). The protein contains a conserved dispanin fold with two transmembrane helices flanking a short intraluminal/extracellular loop. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skin (11.9 nTPM) and esophagus (9.0 nTPM), and group-enriched single cell type specificity suggesting specialized function in barrier epithelia. STRING interactions reveal TMEM265 associates with immune and RNA processing machinery: U2AF1L5 (0.762, high confidence) is a splicing factor paralog of U2AF1 involved in 3 splice site recognition, GIMAP1-GIMAP5 (0.622) are GTPases critical for T-cell survival and lymphocyte homeostasis, MLF1 (0.506) is a myeloid leukemia factor regulating differentiation, CES4A (0.477) is a carboxylesterase, and GLB1L (0.414) is a beta-galactosidase-like protein. ClinVar documents 4 pathogenic copy number gains on chromosome 16p11.2, indicating dosage sensitivity. The dispanin/IFITM topology suggests TMEM265 may function in membrane fusion regulation or vesicular trafficking, potentially modulating endolysosomal pH or viral entry restriction. Interaction with GIMAP GTPases implies a role in lymphocyte survival signaling, while U2AF1L5 association suggests coupling of membrane trafficking to RNA splicing fidelity. TMEM265 may serve as an interferon-inducible membrane adaptor coordinating immune cell survival with post-transcriptional gene regulation.
