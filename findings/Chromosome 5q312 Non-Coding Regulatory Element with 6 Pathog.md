## Description
LOC100128966 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 5q31.2 (NC_000005.10, positions 138332104-138337145, ~5 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases (0 results). Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression across any tissue. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (variants 149858, 145330, 144268, 59672, 58361, 57483), including 4 copy number gains/duplications and 2 copy number losses/deletions, all with uncertain significance (?). Variant sizes range from regional CNVs (~5 Mb, chr5:137836682-140696361) to large chromosomal events (~50 Mb, chr5:106619588-156124387). The 5q31 chromosomal region contains AFF4 (ALF transcription elongation factor 4, RNA polymerase II elongation, WNT signaling, CHOPS syndrome causative gene, developmental transcription), IL4 (interleukin 4, Th2 cytokine, B-cell class switching to IgE, allergic inflammation, asthma, atopic dermatitis), NR3C1 (nuclear receptor subfamily 3 group C member 1, glucocorticoid receptor, stress response, immune suppression, anti-inflammatory signaling), as well as other 5q31 genes including IL5, IL13, CSF2, CDX1, and PDHA2. Functional hypothesis: LOC100128966 likely represents a critical Th2 cytokine locus control region (LCR) or super-enhancer coordinating IL4-NR3C1-AFF4 expression in T-helper 2 cells, mast cells, and eosinophils. The 6 pathogenic CNVs indicate strong dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts Th2 cytokine production and glucocorticoid responsiveness. Disease mechanism involves IL4 pathway dysregulation affecting allergic inflammation, IgE production, and eosinophil activation. IL4 overexpression drives atopic diseases (asthma, allergic rhinitis, eczema) through STAT6 signaling and GATA3 transcription factor activation. NR3C1 dysregulation affects glucocorticoid sensitivity in inflammatory diseases. AFF4 mutations cause CHOPS syndrome (craniofacial anomalies, hearing loss, obesity, pulmonary stenosis, short stature, intellectual disability). The 5q31 duplication/deletion syndromes may present with immunodeficiency, severe allergic disease, developmental delay, and endocrine abnormalities. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (6 CNVs) and IL4-AFF4-NR3C1 immune-transcriptional gene neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a Th2 lineage super-enhancer regulating cytokine production through chromatin looping, germline transcription, or STAT6-GATA3 binding site clustering critical for allergic inflammation and immune homeostasis.
