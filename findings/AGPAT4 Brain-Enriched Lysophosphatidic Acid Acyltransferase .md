## Description
AGPAT4 encodes a 378-amino acid 1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase delta (UniProt Q9NRZ5), also known as lysophosphatidic acid acyltransferase delta (LPAATδ), a very well-folded membrane-associated enzyme functioning in phospholipid biosynthesis through the Kennedy pathway. The protein contains a conserved phospholipid/glycerol acyltransferase catalytic domain (IPR002123, positions 87-218; PF01553, positions 87-218; SM00563, positions 90-212) with acyltransferase activity (GO:0016746) that transfers fatty acyl groups from acyl-CoA to lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) producing phosphatidic acid (PA), plus an acyltransferase C-terminal domain (IPR032098, positions 243-314; PF16076, positions 243-314) for structural stability, classified within the 1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase family (PTHR10983, positions 13-360; cd07990 LCLAT1-like, positions 62-256; SSF69593 glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase, positions 54-281). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 94.0, residues 1-378) indicating stable folding with well-defined catalytic core suitable for substrate binding and catalysis. STRING interaction analysis reveals AGPAT4 forms part of the phospholipid biosynthesis network with ultra-high confidence partners: AGPAT1 (0.974), AGPAT2 (0.965), MBOAT1 (0.944), CDS2 (0.928), AGPAT5 (0.921), AGPAT3 (0.921), PLPP4 (0.921), PLPP5 (0.921), MBOAT2 (0.904), CDS1 (0.903), confirming functional redundancy within the AGPAT family (5 paralogs) and coordination with CDS (CDP-diacylglycerol synthase) and PLPP (phospholipid phosphatase) enzymes for phosphatidylinositol/phosphatidylcholine synthesis. HPA data shows tissue-enriched expression with brain enrichment (53.1 nTPM, top expression), detected in many tissues, nucleoli/Golgi apparatus/vesicles subcellular localization consistent with ER/Golgi membrane association for lipid biosynthesis, annotated for lipid biosynthesis/metabolism and phospholipid biosynthesis/metabolism biological processes with acyltransferase/transferase molecular functions. ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (6q25.2-27, 6q24.2-27), losses (6q25.3-27, 6q25.2-27), deletions, and splice site variants (c.178+5G>C, c.664+1G>A), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined, suggesting AGPAT4 dosage sensitivity. With 70 publications, AGPAT4 has emerging roles in cancer and reproductive pathology: AGPAT4 targeted covalent inhibitor potentiates targeted therapy to overcome cancer cell plasticity in hepatocellular carcinoma (2025, 6 cit), AGPAT4 significance in endometriosis pathogenesis via multi-omics approach (2024, 1 cit), chemoproteomics-enabled discovery of covalent AGPAT4 inhibitors for drug resistance in HCC (2024), prognostic impact of AGPAT4 in thyroid cancer tumor immune microenvironment (2026). The functional hypothesis: AGPAT4 serves as a rate-limiting acyltransferase in the Kennedy pathway that converts lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) to phosphatidic acid (PA) by adding a fatty acyl chain at the sn-2 position, a critical step for de novo phospholipid biosynthesis required for membrane biogenesis and lipid signaling; brain enrichment suggests specialized roles in neuronal membrane synthesis, myelination, or synaptic vesicle formation; nucleoli/Golgi/vesicles localization indicates AGPAT4 operates at ER-Golgi interfaces where phospholipid synthesis occurs for membrane expansion; ultra-high confidence interactions with AGPAT1/2/3/5 suggest functional redundancy and potential compensation among paralogs, explaining lack of severe Mendelian phenotypes; CDS1/2 interactions indicate coupling of PA production to CDP-diacylglycerol synthesis for phosphatidylinositol pathway; PLPP4/5 interactions suggest coordination with lipid phosphatases that dephosphorylate PA to diacylglycerol (DAG) for triacylglycerol synthesis; MBOAT1/2 interactions link AGPAT4 to membrane-bound O-acyltransferases for cholesterol esterification; hepatocellular carcinoma vulnerability suggests AGPAT4 supports tumor membrane biogenesis and cancer cell plasticity through lipid metabolic reprogramming, making it a therapeutic target; endometriosis association indicates altered phospholipid metabolism in ectopic endometrial cells; thyroid cancer prognostic value implies AGPAT4 expression correlates with tumor immune infiltration; 6q CNVs may disrupt AGPAT4 dosage causing phospholipid biosynthesis defects affecting multiple organ systems; very high structural confidence (pLDDT 94.0) indicates stable catalytic domain suitable for small molecule inhibitor design, as demonstrated by recent covalent inhibitor development.
