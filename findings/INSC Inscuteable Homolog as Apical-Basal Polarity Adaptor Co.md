# INSC: Inscuteable Homolog as Apical-Basal Polarity Adaptor Coupling LGN/GPSM2-NuMA-Dynein Spindle Orientation Machinery to Asymmetric Cell Division in Neurogenesis and Parathyroid Development

**Date:** 2026-06-17T23:58:10.035346

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
INSC (Inscuteable Homolog, Q1MX18) encodes a 579 aa adaptor protein functioning as a central regulator of asymmetric cell division that bridges apical-basal polarity complexes with spindle orientation machinery during neurogenesis and epithelial tissue development. Structural architecture reveals N-terminal LGN-binding domain (LBD) (IPR031938/PF16748/cd21966/G3DSA:6.20.200.10/IPR038205, residues 70-116/65-116/71-379) for interaction with G-protein signaling modulators, central region with Armadillo repeats (IPR000225, residues 310-353/448-499/500-545) forming helical scaffold (G3DSA:1.25.10.10/IPR011989/IPR016024, residues 154-568/225-567), and C-terminal domain (IPR045789, residues 127-579); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 83.1) confirms high-confidence well-folded structure with distinct functional domains enabling multi-protein complex assembly. STRING interactions define polarity and spindle orientation networks: G-protein signaling modulator GPSM2/LGN (0.994) serving as primary binding partner for cortical anchoring, partitioning defective protein PARD3B (0.849)/PARD3 (0.488) establishing apical-basal polarity, GPSM1 (0.711) paralog, nuclear mitotic apparatus protein NUMA1 (0.545) linking to dynein motor complex, cell fate determinants NUMBL (0.499)/NUMB (0.497), SEC61G (0.608) for ER translocation, PI4KB (0.495) for membrane phosphoinositide signaling, and galectin LGALS4 (0.409). HPA localizes INSC as developmental protein involved in differentiation/neurogenesis with tissue-enriched expression in parathyroid gland (23.8 nTPM), detected in some tissues, cancer-enhanced specificity. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic variants including extensive 11p15.5-14.2 copy number gains (chr11:230615-26881146, chr11:13970757-27565888, chr11:11053978-34732891 duplications/triplications) and deletions. Literature demonstrates conserved roles from Drosophila to mammals: INSC ortholog essential for neuroblast asymmetric division in Drosophila, mammalian INSC regulates neural progenitor symmetric vs asymmetric division balance determining neuronal output, parathyroid enrichment suggests role in endocrine cell lineage specification. Mechanistic hypothesis: INSC functions as molecular bridge between apical polarity determinants and spindle orientation machinery; N-terminal LBD binds GPSM2/LGN tetratricopeptide repeat domains recruiting INSC to apical cortex; ARM repeats provide scaffold for additional protein interactions; during mitosis, INSC-GPSM2 complex anchors NuMA-dynein-dynactin to cell cortex generating pulling forces on astral microtubules aligning mitotic spindle with polarity axis; this ensures asymmetric segregation of cell fate determinants (NUMB/NUMBL) to daughter cells determining proliferative vs differentiative fates; in neural progenitors, INSC upregulation promotes asymmetric neurogenic divisions while downregulation favors symmetric proliferative divisions expanding progenitor pool; parathyroid gland enrichment (23.8 nTPM) suggests INSC regulates endocrine progenitor differentiation potentially affecting calcium homeostasis; 11p15 CNVs encompassing INSC cause Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome and other imprinting disorders through dosage effects on asymmetric division; cancer-enhanced expression indicates INSC dysregulation contributes to tumor heterogeneity through altered stem cell division patterns.
