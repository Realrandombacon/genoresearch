## Description
CHMP1A (Q9HD42) encodes a 196 amino acid charged multivesicular body protein 1A that functions as an essential ESCRT-III (Endosomal Sorting Complex Required for Transport III) component mediating endosomal membrane scission, nuclear envelope reformation during mitosis, and chromatin regulation through dual cytoplasmic and nuclear roles. InterPro analysis reveals canonical ESCRT-III Snf7 family architecture: a Snf7 family domain (IPR005024/PF03357, positions 7-196/7-172) forming the characteristic four-helix bundle structure required for membrane deformation and polymerization; G3DSA helical superfamily (G3DSA:6.10.140.1230, positions 1-167) indicating structural conservation across ESCRT-III proteins; and PTHR family classification (PTHR10476, positions 7-196) confirming membership in the charged multivesicular body protein family with vacuolar transport function (GO:0007034). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 77.9) consistent with stable four-helix bundle fold enabling membrane binding and polymerization into spiral filaments.
