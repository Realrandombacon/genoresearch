## Description
TP53I11 encodes a 189 aa intracellular membrane-associated protein (UniProt O14683), also known as PIG11 (p53-inducible gene 11), that functions as a critical effector of p53-mediated apoptosis and cellular stress response through regulation of endoplasmic reticulum calcium homeostasis and tumor microenvironment angiogenesis. This partially characterized gene (29 publications) has emerging clinical significance for breast cancer metastasis suppression, gastric cancer progression, and pan-cancer angiogenic regulation. The protein features conserved but uncharacterized domain architecture: TP53I11 family domain (IPR028266/PTHR31584, residues 1-188/1-189) spanning nearly the entire protein length; Pfam tumor protein p53-inducible protein 11 family (PF14936, residues 10-188) representing a unique scaffold without homology to known functional domains; classified within p53-inducible protein family with predicted membrane association.
