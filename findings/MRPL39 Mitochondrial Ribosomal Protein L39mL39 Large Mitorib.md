## Description
MRPL39 (Gene ID 54148, UniProt Q9NYK5, aliases C21orf92/COXPD59/L39mt/L5mt/MRP-L5/MRPL5/MSTP003/PRED22/PRED66/RPML5/mL39) encodes a 338-amino acid large ribosomal subunit protein mL39 on chromosome 21q21.3, representing a partially characterized gene with 27 total publications on Semantic Scholar that has recently emerged as a cause of pediatric mitochondrial disease. Recent literature (2023) identifies large mitoribosomal subunit instability caused by pathogenic MRPL39 variants as a cause of pediatric-onset mitochondrial disease (cited 18x); 2024 studies demonstrate lncRNA MRPL39 inhibits cell proliferation and migration by regulating miR-130/TSC1 axis in non-small cell lung cancer (cited 1x); 2023 research shows upregulated lnc-MRPL39-2:1 induces growth and invasion of nasopharyngeal carcinoma by binding HuR and stabilizing β-catenin mRNA (cited 11x); 2018 studies characterize long noncoding RNA MRPL39 inhibiting gastric cancer proliferation by directly targeting miR-130 (cited 13x).
