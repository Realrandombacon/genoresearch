## Description
GALNT13 (Polypeptide N-Acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 13, Q8IUC8) encodes a 556 aa Golgi-resident glycosyltransferase functioning as a critical initiator of mucin-type O-linked glycosylation by transferring N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) from UDP-GalNAc to serine/threonine residues of target proteins, with emerging roles in neural development, cancer progression, and athletic performance phenotypes. Structural architecture reveals a very high-confidence organization with AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 93.0): pp-GalNAc-T catalytic domain (cd02510/IPR045885/IPR001173/PF00535, residues 118-418/118-418/118-302/118-302) forming the catalytic core with GT2 fold (G3DSA:3.90.550.10/SpsA-like, residues 42-426) establishing the nucleotide-sugar binding pocket and catalytic mechanism with GO term glycosyltransferase activity; ricin B-type lectin domain (IPR000772/cd23467/PF00652/PS50231/G3DSA:2.80.10.50/IPR035992, residues 427-550/425-551/430-547/438-550/427-555/423-552) forming the C-terminal carbohydrate-binding module that recognizes glycosylated substrates for sequential modification, nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferases superfamily (IPR029044, residues 42-426) defining the broader enzyme family; the very high pLDDT score indicates an exceptionally rigid, well-defined bi-domain architecture optimized for precise substrate recognition and catalysis.
