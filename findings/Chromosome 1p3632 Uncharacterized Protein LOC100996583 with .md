The 1p36.32-36.33 chromosomal region is the critical region for 1p36 deletion syndrome, the most common terminal deletion syndrome in humans, containing critical neurodevelopmental, cardiac, and tumor suppression-associated genes including PRDM16 (PR/SET domain 16, cardiomyopathy, left ventricular non-compaction, brown adipocyte differentiation), SKI (SKI proto-oncogene, TGF-beta signaling negative regulator, Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome, craniosynostosis), GABRD (GABA A receptor delta subunit, extrasynaptic inhibition, epilepsy, perampanel target), CHD5 (chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 5, tumor suppressor, neuroblastoma, p53 pathway activation), and KCNAB3 (potassium voltage-gated channel beta subunit 3, cardiac repolarization, arrhythmia). Functional hypothesis: LOC100996583 likely represents a critical 1p36.32 neurodevelopmental/cardiac regulatory protein, potentially functioning as a nuclear transcriptional co-regulator or chromatin modifier coordinating PRDM16-SKI-GABRD-CHD5-KCNAB3 expression in neural development, cardiac morphogenesis, and tumor suppression (neural progenitors, cardiomyocytes, neural crest derivatives, sympathetic nervous system) or as a synaptic scaffolding protein bridging GABRD-mediated GABAergic inhibition with CHD5-mediated neuroblastoma suppression. The 55 pathogenic CNVs (54 deletions 1.8-8.7 Mb, 1 duplication 33.9 Mb) indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency causes 1p36 deletion syndrome. Disease mechanism involves PRDM16 haploinsufficiency causing dilated cardiomyopathy and left ventricular non-compaction (cardiomyocyte differentiation defect, heart failure, arrhythmia, sudden death), SKI haploinsufficiency causing Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome (TGF-beta overactivation, craniosynostosis, skeletal abnormalities, developmental delay), GABRD mutation causing epilepsy (GABAergic inhibition defect, neuronal hyperexcitability, seizures, cognitive impairment), CHD5 deletion causing neuroblastoma predisposition (tumor suppressor loss, p53 pathway defect, sympathetic nervous system tumor), KCNAB3 mutation causing cardiac arrhythmia (repolarization defect, QT prolongation, sudden cardiac death), and 1p36 deletion syndrome presenting with developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, cardiomyopathy, dysmorphic features, and behavioral abnormalities. The 1p36.32-36.33 region is the most dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental-cardiac locus in the human genome. The uncharacterized protein-coding classification combined with ClinVar evidence (55 CNVs 1.8-33.9 Mb) and PRDM16-SKI-GABRD-CHD5-KCNAB3 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a nuclear/synaptic protein critical for neural development-cardiac morphogenesis-tumor suppression-GABAergic coordination.
