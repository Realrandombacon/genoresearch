# ZNF112: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Disease-Associated Copy Number Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-20T14:46:23.660525

## Description
ZNF112 encodes a 913-amino acid C2H2 zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9UJU3) featuring an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 7-79) followed by multiple C2H2 zinc finger arrays (PF00096, IPR013087 at positions 258-285, 443-470, 471-498, 525-547, 553-575, 581-603). The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex for heterochromatin formation and transcriptional silencing. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with TRIM28 (0.679), ZMYM2 (0.653), ZNF235 (0.632), ZNF284 (0.631), and FGFR1OP2 (0.646), consistent with KRAB-ZFP chromatin regulatory networks. HPA data shows nucleoplasmic and nucleolar fibrillar center localization with low tissue specificity, expressed across multiple tissues as a ubiquitous transcriptional regulator. ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (gains and losses spanning 19q13.31), suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential disease associations though specific phenotypes remain uncharacterized. The protein architecture indicates sequence-specific DNA binding via zinc finger recognition helices coupled with epigenetic silencing through KRAB-TRIM28 recruitment of SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins.
