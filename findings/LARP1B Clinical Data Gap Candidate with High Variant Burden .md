## Description
LARP1B (La-related protein 1B) is a high-priority "Clinical Data Gap" candidate. Despite 19 papers focusing on its oncogenic role as circ-LARP1B and 30 documented pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar, there are ZERO cataloged diseases associated with LARP1B mutations. This paradox suggests that LARP1B variants cause heterogeneous, pleiotropic phenotypes (e.g., neurodevelopmental disorders, cancer predisposition) that are too variable to be grouped under a single Mendelian syndrome. The gene\'s ubiquitous tissue expression (detected in all tissues) and its role as a scaffold in the La ribonucleoprotein complex (interacting with LARP1, EIF4G1, PABPC1) likely disrupt RNA metabolism and stress granule dynamics
