## Description
CELA3A (Chymotrypsin-Like Elastase Family Member 3A, also known as pancreatic elastase 3A or elastase-3A, UniProt P09093) is a 270 aa secreted serine protease belonging to the trypsin/chymotrypsin elastase family that functions in pancreatic protein digestion through peptide bond hydrolysis. InterPro analysis reveals canonical trypsin-fold serine protease architecture: Trypsin domain (IPR001254, positions 28-268; PF00089, positions 30-263; G3DSA:2.40.10.10, positions 43-264) within peptidase S1 PA clan superfamily (IPR009003, positions 25-267), chymotrypsin family signatures (IPR001314, positions 59-74/119-133/210-222; PR00722), elastase subfamily membership (IPR050850, positions 4-267), and conserved catalytic triad residues: histidine active site (IPR018114, positions 69-74; PS00134) and serine active site (IPR033116, positions 211-222; PS00135). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.7), indicating an exceptionally well-folded trypsin-fold β-barrel structure with stable catalytic pocket. HPA data confirms extreme pancreas-enriched expression (247619.6 nTPM, tissue enriched), detected in many tissues, cytosol/secreted localization, classified as enzyme/hydrolase/protease/serine protease with cancer-enriched expression pattern. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with pancreatic digestive enzyme complex: CPB1 (0.917, carboxypeptidase B1), PNLIP (0.888, pancreatic lipase), CEL (0.879, bile salt-dependent lipase), CTRB2 (0.849, chymotrypsinogen B2), CTRL (0.846, chymotrypsin-like protease), CTRC (0.836, chymotrypsin C), PPY (0.823, pancreatic polypeptide), CPA1 (0.769, carboxypeptidase A1), CLPS (0.535, colipase), CPA2 (0.462, carboxypeptidase A2). ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 1p36.33-36.12 affecting the CELA3A locus. Literature (26 papers) establishes CELA3A roles in: (1) pancreatic elastase CELA3A/CELA3B level determination in stool using total and CELA3B-specific immunoassays for exocrine pancreatic insufficiency detection (2020), (2) genetic analysis of CELA3A/CELA3B to assess proelastase-procarboxypeptidase complex formation role in chronic pancreatitis susceptibility (2016, 13 citations), and (3) 92% sequence identity with paralog CELA3B from gene duplication event enabling functional redundancy and complex formation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CELA3A serves as a pancreas-specific digestive serine protease that hydrolyzes peptide bonds in dietary proteins (preferentially cleaving after small neutral amino acids like alanine, glycine, valine) within the duodenal lumen, contributing to protein digestion and nutrient absorption while forming stable complexes with procarboxypeptidases to regulate activation cascades and prevent premature enzyme activation. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The trypsin-fold β-barrel structure positions the catalytic triad (His69, Asp102, Ser211) for nucleophilic attack on peptide carbonyl carbons. CELA3A is synthesized as an inactive zymogen (proelastase) in pancreatic acinar cells, secreted into the pancreatic duct, and activated by trypsin cleavage in the duodenum. The elastase substrate specificity pocket accommodates small neutral amino acid side chains, distinguishing CELA3A from trypsin (basic residues) and chymotrypsin (aromatic residues). CELA3A forms stable heterocomplexes with procarboxypeptidases (CPB1, CPA1, CPA2) through electrostatic and hydrophobic interactions, preventing premature activation and protecting procarboxypeptidases from degradation. This complex formation regulates the sequential activation cascade of pancreatic digestive enzymes. In chronic pancreatitis, CELA3A variants or dysregulation may impair procarboxypeptidase complex formation, leading to premature intrapancreatic enzyme activation, acinar cell injury, and inflammation. The 92% sequence identity with CELA3B suggests functional redundancy but also potential isoform-specific roles in disease susceptibility. Extreme pancreas enrichment (247619.6 nTPM - 5× higher than CELA3B's 46793.9 nTPM) confirms CELA3A as the dominant pancreatic elastase isoform. The 8 ClinVar CNVs indicate CELA3A dosage sensitivity may contribute to pancreatic disorders. Cancer-enriched expression pattern suggests potential role in pancreatic tumor microenvironment or cachexia.
