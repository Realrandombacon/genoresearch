## Description
FBXW4 (F-Box and WD Repeat Domain Containing 4) is a 412 aa F-box protein representing a partially characterized gene with 48 literature references, functioning as a substrate recognition component of the SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex involved in Wnt signaling and developmental patterning. Multi-omics data reveals FBXW4 as a vertebrate-conserved ubiquitin ligase with defined F-box and WD40 domains yet emerging functional characterization through recent 2020-2025 cancer and osteoporosis discoveries. UniProt accession P57775 confirms the 412 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q9JMJ2, 410 aa, ~99% identity), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the SCF complex architecture. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the F-box protein family (IPR001810, aa 25-72; IPR036047, aa 24-77; cd20090, aa 28-73) containing a canonical F-box domain that mediates SKP1 binding and SCF complex assembly, plus WD40 repeats (IPR001680, aa 152-276; PF00400; IPR015943, aa 133-412; IPR036322, aa 153-399) that likely mediate substrate recognition. The SCF complex F-box/WD repeat-containing protein family (IPR052301, aa 11-411) indicates functional specialization within the F-box family. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.5) across the full 412 aa length, indicating a well-folded stable protein with structured F-box and WD40 domains suitable for protein-protein interactions. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, Golgi apparatus localization, and Wnt signaling pathway/Ubl conjugation pathway annotation—consistent with an F-box protein functioning in ubiquitin-mediated proteolysis and developmental signaling. STRING interactions reveal a focused SCF complex and chaperone network: SKP1 (0.933, SCF adaptor), CUL1 (0.911, SCF scaffold), LBX1 (0.894, ladybird homeobox 1, developmental transcription factor), CCT5 (0.885), CCT3 (0.863), CCT4 (0.861), CCT8 (0.859), CCT7 (0.750) (chaperonin-containing TCP1 subunits), POLL (0.817, DNA polymerase lambda), TXNDC9 (0.538). These interactions confirm FBXW4 operates in SCF complex assembly (SKP1, CUL1) and potentially developmental regulation (LBX1). ClinVar lists 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large chromosome 10 copy number variations (gains: chr10:87456174-107789979x3, chr10:102927452-103392888x3, chr10:95078198-135427143x3, chr10:102965124-103403057x3, chr10:102981549-103472860x3, chr10:102958930-103525401x3; duplications: chr10:103001852_103543913dup, chr10:103012761_103546704dup) plus one intronic variant (c.726-275C>T), implicating FBXW4 dosage sensitivity in chromosome 10 duplication/deletion syndromes. The 48 papers (including 2025 osteoporosis bioinformatics study, 2025 lung adenocarcinoma/PKNOX2 methylation study, 2024 glioma progression/prognosis study with 2 citations, 2020 colorectal cancer/FOLFOX chemotherapy protector study with 12 citations, 2020 AML poor survival study with 10 citations) confirm FBXW4 as a genuine partially characterized gene—core SCF function defined but cancer mechanisms and developmental roles awaiting full characterization.
