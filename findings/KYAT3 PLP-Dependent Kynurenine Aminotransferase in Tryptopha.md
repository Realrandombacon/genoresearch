## Description
KYAT3 (Q6YP21) is a 454-aa PLP-dependent aminotransferase with exceptional AlphaFold structural confidence (pLDDT 91.3), indicating a highly stable well-folded enzyme. InterPro analysis reveals canonical type I/II aminotransferase architecture with AAT_like domain (cd00609: positions 66-448), PF00155 aminotransferase class I/II domain (positions 64-441), and specific kynurenine--oxoglutarate transaminase family signatures (IPR051326/PTHR43807: positions 39-450), plus pyridoxal phosphate binding capability (GO:0030170). HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoli, cytokinetic bridge, and cytosol, consistent with housekeeping metabolic enzyme function. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling to kynurenine pathway enzymes with very high confidence: KYNU/kynureninase (0.982), KMO/kynurenine 3-monooxygenase (0.980), AFMID/arylformamidase (0.964), AADAT/aminoadipate aminotransferase (0.960), plus CTH/cystathionine gamma-lyase (0.955), BHMT2 (0.940), GOT1 (0.940), MTR/methionine synthase (0.932), CBS/cystathionine beta-synthase (0.931), and GOT2 (0.420). This interaction network positions KYAT3 within the tryptophan-kynurenine metabolic pathway converting kynurenine to kynurenic acid, a neuroactive metabolite implicated in neurological disorders. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 1p31.3-21.3 and 1p31.1-22.2, copy number gains at 1p34.1-22.2 and 1p36.33-q44, and splice site variant c.954+2T>G, establishing clinical significance though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature shows only 10 papers (genuine dark gene) with recent mentions in medulloblastoma siblings, skeletal muscle myokine expression in critical illness, and diabetic zebrafish neurometabolic protection studies. The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 91.3), PLP-binding aminotransferase domains, kynurenine pathway interaction network, and pathogenic variant burden suggest KYAT3 functions as essential kynurenine aminotransferase producing kynurenic acid from kynurenine and 2-oxoglutarate, coupling tryptophan catabolism to NAD+ biosynthesis and neuroactive metabolite production, with disruption potentially contributing to neurological phenotypes through altered kynurenic acid levels affecting NMDA receptor antagonism and neuroprotection.
