# LOC129390829: MPRA-Validated Chromatin Silencer Element on Chromosome 17p13.3 with Moderate CNV Burden

**Date:** 2026-03-23T11:26:12.389951

**Quality Score:** 4.25/10  (E=4.5, D=4.0) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129390829 is a non-coding cis-regulatory DNA element classified as a transcriptional silencer, validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This 200bp genomic region (chr17:6223325-6223525, GRCh38) at 17p13.3 demonstrates capacity to repress super core promoter (SCP1) activity, confirming silencer function. As a regulatory DNA element, it lacks protein-coding features: no UniProt accession, no InterPro domains, no STRING interactions, and no HPA expression data. Clinical significance is moderate: ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (CNVs) spanning this locus—including both duplications (x3, 5 variants) and deletions (x1, 4 variants) across 17p13.3-13.1 regions. This CNV burden is intermediate compared to other silencer elements (LOC129389713: 14 CNVs; LOC129390646: 6 CNVs; LOC129389488: 1 CNV). Hypothesis: LOC129390829 functions as a chromatin silencer recruiting repressive complexes (Polycomb PRC2, HDACs, or KRAB-ZNF co-repressors) to silence target genes on 17p13.3, a clinically critical region containing TP53 tumor suppressor and Miller-Dieker syndrome loci (LIS1/PAFAH1B1). The moderate CNV burden suggests this element regulates dosage-sensitive genes where disruption contributes to 17p13.3 microdeletion/duplication syndromes—potentially causing developmental delay, lissencephaly, or hematologic abnormalities. K562 erythroid validation indicates relevance to blood cell development, and 17p13.3 rearrangements are common in acute myeloid leukemia. The silencer may coordinate long-range chromatin interactions to repress multiple target genes, and CNVs disrupt this three-dimensional genome architecture.
