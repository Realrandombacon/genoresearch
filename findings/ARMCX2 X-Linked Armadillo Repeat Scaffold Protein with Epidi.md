## Description
ARMCX2 encodes a 632-amino acid armadillo repeat-containing X-linked protein 2 (UniProt Q7L311), a partially structured scaffold protein functioning in protein-protein interactions and potential cell cycle regulation. The protein contains multiple armadillo repeat domains (IPR000225, positions 416-457; IPR006911, positions 383-601; PF04826, positions 383-601; SM00185, positions 416-457) forming an armadillo-type fold (IPR016024, positions 387-581; IPR011989, positions 377-632) characteristic of scaffold proteins mediating protein-protein interactions through stacked alpha-helical repeats, classified within the armadillo repeat-containing X-linked regulator family (IPR051303, positions 1-630; PTHR15712, positions 1-630). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 62.6, residues 1-632) indicating partial folding with potentially disordered N-terminal regions and more structured C-terminal armadillo domain, consistent with scaffold proteins containing flexible linkers. STRING interaction analysis reveals ARMCX2 forms part of a protein interaction network with medium-confidence partners: SPTAN1 (0.653, spectrin alpha chain), ARMCX1 (0.584, armadillo repeat X-linked protein 1 paralog), RB1 (0.553, retinoblastoma tumor suppressor), HABP4 (0.516, hyaluronan binding protein 4), PSME3IP1 (0.515, proteasome activator), CCDC70 (0.513, coiled-coil domain protein), ATP6V1E2 (0.500, V-type ATPase subunit), CCDC69 (0.490, coiled-coil domain protein), DDX55 (0.478, DEAD-box helicase), and WDR49 (0.406, WD40 repeat protein), suggesting roles in cytoskeletal organization, cell cycle control, and protein degradation. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with striking epididymis specificity (146.2 nTPM, highest expression), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, nucleoplasm and mitochondria subcellular localization, consistent with roles in male reproductive biology and cellular metabolism. ClinVar contains 163 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large X-chromosome copy number gains (Xq13.1-22.2, Xq13.1-27.1, Xq21.1-22.3) and losses (Xq21.1-22.3, Xq21.33-24, Xq21.32-26.1, Xq21.31-23, Xq21.2-22.3), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With only 14 publications, ARMCX2 is genuinely a dark gene: German thesis characterizing ARMCX2 in kidney cells (2012, 0 cit), ARMCX family expression as prognostic biomarkers in gastric cancer (2020), tumor invasion-related genes in esophageal cancer (2025), angiogenesis subtypes in colon cancer (2023). The functional hypothesis: ARMCX2 serves as an armadillo repeat scaffold protein mediating protein-protein interactions in cell cycle regulation and cytoskeletal organization, potentially bridging RB1 tumor suppressor with spectrin cytoskeleton (SPTAN1) to coordinate cell division and chromatin organization; the epididymis-specific high expression (146.2 nTPM) suggests critical roles in male reproductive biology, possibly sperm maturation or epididymal epithelial function; X-chromosome location and armadillo repeat architecture parallel ARMCX family members involved in mitochondrial regulation and cancer; RB1 interaction suggests potential cell cycle checkpoint functions, with armadillo repeats providing binding surfaces for E2F transcription factors or cyclin-dependent kinases; cancer-enhanced expression pattern and Xq CNVs implicate ARMCX2 dosage sensitivity in tumorigenesis, potentially through dysregulated cell cycle control or cytoskeletal remodeling; mitochondrial localization suggests metabolic roles coordinating energy production with cell proliferation; paralog ARMCX1 interaction indicates functional redundancy or heterodimerization within ARMCX family scaffold network.
