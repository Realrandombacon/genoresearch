## Description
ELP5 (UniProt: Q8TE02, aliases C17orf81/DERP6/HSPC002/MST071/MSTP071) encodes a 300 amino acid elongator complex protein representing a partially characterized gene with 28 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 17p13.1, ELP5 is a core subunit of the evolutionarily conserved elongator holoenzyme complex required for multiple tRNA modifications. InterPro domain analysis reveals elongator-specific architecture: cd19496 (Elongator subcomplex subunit Elp5, residues 16-174), IPR019519 (Elongator complex protein 5, residues 9-300, GO:0002098 tRNA wobble uridine modification, GO:0033588 elongator holoenzyme complex), PF10483 (Elongator subunit Iki1, residues 9-177 and 233-281), and PTHR15641 (Elongator complex protein 5, residues 12-300). The dual PF10483 domains suggest a bilobed structure enabling multiple protein-protein interactions within the complex. AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 78.9), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined tertiary structure suitable for stable complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners forming the elongator machinery: ELP2 (elongator complex protein 2, score 0.998 - highest confidence), ELP3 (elongator complex protein 3, 0.926 - catalytic acetyltransferase subunit), PIPOX (pipecolate oxidase, 0.842), ELP4 (elongator complex protein 4, 0.755), CTU1 (cytosolic thiouridylase 1, 0.739 - collaborates in tRNA thiolation), KTI12 (KTI12 chromatin associated homolog, 0.730), ELP1 (elongator complex protein 1, 0.659 - scaffold subunit), DPH3 (diphthine biosynthesis protein 3, 0.647), ELP6 (elongator complex protein 6, 0.496), and MOCS3 (molybdenum cofactor synthesis 3, 0.414 - sulfurtransferase for tRNA modification). This interaction network defines ELP5's role within the six-subunit elongator complex (ELP1-6) that catalyzes formation of 5-methoxycarbonylmethyl uridine (mcm5U), 5-methoxycarbonylmethyl-2-thiouridine (mcm5s2U), and 5-carbamoylmethyl uridine (ncm5U) at the wobble position of tRNA(Lys), tRNA(Glu), and tRNA(Gln). Human Protein Atlas has no expression data for ELP5. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including single nucleotide variants (e.g., c.688G>C/p.Val230Leu) and copy number losses/gains spanning 17p13.3-11.2, though no specific disease names are assigned. Recent literature (2019, cited 86x) identifies ELP5 as a determinant of gemcitabine sensitivity in gallbladder cancer via genome-wide CRISPR screen - ELP5 loss enhances gemcitabine response, suggesting tRNA modification status affects chemotherapy efficacy. Additional studies (2021) describe lncRNA EGFR-AS1 regulating breast cancer cell function and docetaxel sensitivity via miR-149-5p/ELP5 axis, (2015) report common variants in CLDN7/ELP5 locus predicting adiponectin change with lifestyle intervention in obese diabetics, and (2012, cited 59x) characterize DERP6 (ELP5) and C3ORF75 (ELP6) as elongator subunits regulating melanoma cell tumorigenicity and migration. Based on high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 78.9), elongator-specific domain architecture (dual Iki1 domains), ELP1/2/3/4/6 interaction network, tRNA wobble uridine modification function, and chemotherapy sensitivity phenotype, ELP5 functions as a structural scaffold subunit within the elongator holoenzyme that positions tRNA substrates for catalytic modification by ELP3. The elongator complex acetylates uridine wobble bases, enabling efficient codon-anticodon pairing during translation of AA-ending codons. ELP5 likely stabilizes the ELP1-2-3 catalytic core while recruiting ELP4-5-6 subcomplex for tRNA binding. Loss of ELP5 disrupts mcm5U/mcm5s2U/ncm5U modifications, causing ribosome pausing at specific codons and altered translation of proteins enriched in Lys/Glu/Gln residues. This explains the gemcitabine sensitivity phenotype - defective tRNA modification may impair translation of DNA repair or drug efflux proteins. The CTU1 and MOCS3 interactions link elongator to parallel tRNA thiolation pathways. ELP5 exemplifies a tRNA modification enzyme subunit connecting translational fidelity to cancer drug response and metabolic regulation.
