## Description
ANKRD62 (UniProt A6NC57, 917 aa) is a testis-enriched ankyrin repeat protein localized to nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, and actin filaments. InterPro analysis reveals extensive ankyrin repeat architecture: IPR002110 (ankyrin repeats, aa 71-263 with 3 tandem repeats at aa 71-100, 104-133, 137-166), G3DSA:1.25.40.20 (ankyrin repeat superfamily, aa 20-315), PF12796 (3-copy ankyrin repeats, aa 88-263), and SSF48403 (ankyrin repeat fold, aa 32-262). C-terminal region contains PF14915/CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (aa 544-905) and IPR039497 (coiled-coil domain, aa 544-905) - suggesting oligomerization or scaffold function. STRING interactions show 10 medium-confidence partners (0.507-0.589 scores): C3orf22 (0.589), EEF2KMT (0.563, tRNA methyltransferase), RNASE12 (0.544, ribonuclease), C12orf56 (0.543), C1orf167 (0.540), WDR97 (0.534, WD40 repeat), SMIM17 (0.527, membrane protein), MTMR12 (0.524, phosphatase), SH2D7 (0.507, SH2 domain), CC2D2B (0.507, coiled-coil). HPA shows testis-enriched expression (1.3 nTPM), single tissue distribution, nucleoplasm/nuclear bodies/actin filament localization. ClinVar contains 90 pathogenic variants all involving 18p11.32-11.21 CNVs - 18p deletion syndrome (OMIM #601187) causes intellectual disability, growth delay, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features; 18p duplication causes variable neurodevelopmental phenotypes. Critical 18p11.21 region contains: TSHZ1 (teashirt zinc finger, cortical development), TGIF1 (TGF-beta corepressor, holoprosencephaly), MYOCLN1 (epilepsy), and multiple neuronal genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: ANKRD62 functions as a nuclear scaffold protein that organizes protein complexes via ankyrin repeat-mediated protein-protein interactions. Ankyrin repeats (33 aa motifs forming curved solenoid structure) bind partner proteins through concave surface grooves. Testis enrichment suggests role in spermatogenesis - possibly chromatin remodeling during meiosis or post-meiotic haploid differentiation. Nuclear body localization indicates participation in nuclear speckles, Cajal bodies, or PML bodies - sites of transcription, splicing, or DNA repair. Coiled-coil C-terminus mediates homodimerization or heterodimerization, creating multivalent scaffold platforms. Actin filament association suggests nucleoskeletal functions - linking chromatin to nuclear lamina or coordinating nuclear actin polymerization. 18p CNVs disrupt ANKRD62 dosage alongside TSHZ1, TGIF1, and neuronal genes - combined haploinsufficiency causes 18p syndrome neurodevelopmental phenotypes. ANKRD62 represents a "dark gene" with clear domain architecture and tissue specificity but incompletely characterized molecular function.
