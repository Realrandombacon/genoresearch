## Description
DPF1 (Double PHD Fingers 1, also known as Neuro-D4, UniProt Q92782) is a 387 amino acid nucleoplasmic/mitochondrial/cytosolic chromatin regulatory protein with moderate characterization (42 papers) that functions as a SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling complex subunit regulating neurogenesis, transcription, and cancer progression through histone modification recognition. The protein contains two PHD-type zinc fingers (IPR001965/IPR019787/PF00628, residues 273-326/327-373/274-325/328-372) for histone mark recognition, a DPF1-3 N-terminal domain (IPR025750/PF14051, residues 13-83) specific to the DPF family, and a C2H2-type zinc finger (IPR013087/IPR036236, residues 195-223/191-219) for DNA binding, placing it in the SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling machinery involved in transcription regulation and neurogenesis. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with core SWI/SNF complex components: DPF3 (0.998, double PHD fingers 3 paralog), ARID1A (0.995, AT-rich interaction domain 1A, SWI/SNF core), SMARCE1 (0.992, SWI/SNF-related matrix-associated actin-dependent regulator), ARID1B (0.991, AT-rich interaction domain 1B), SMARCD1 (0.988, SWI/SNF-related D1), SMARCC2 (0.988, SWI/SNF-related C2), SMARCC1 (0.987, SWI/SNF-related C1), SMARCB1 (0.987, SWI/SNF-related B1/INI1), ACTL6B (0.964, actin-like 6B), and DPF2 (0.946, double PHD fingers 2 paralog), confirming membership in the BAF/PBAF chromatin remodeling complexes. HPA data shows nucleoplasm, mitochondria, and cytosol subcellular localization, tissue-enriched specificity with top expression in brain (30.9 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, group-enriched cancer specificity, and involvement in neurogenesis and transcription regulation. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all consisting of copy number gains at 19q11-13.2/19p13.11-q13.2/19q11-13.33/19p13.3-q13.43 (chr19:28271146-41508851, chr19:19546923-41313229, chr19:28271106-49213832, chr19:260912-58956888, chr19:37582250-41630908, chr19:11227942-44626354, chr19:37319377-42738688), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential neurodevelopmental disorders or cancer susceptibility through SWI/SNF complex dysregulation. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 67.5) indicates medium confidence with partially structured architecture typical of chromatin regulatory proteins containing flexible linkers between zinc finger domains. Functional hypothesis: DPF1 acts as a brain-enriched SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling complex subunit that recognizes specific histone modifications through its tandem PHD fingers, where recent 2023-2025 research demonstrates: (1) DPF1 role in hepatocellular carcinoma prognosis and therapy (2025)—DPF1 expression correlates with tumor progression and patient outcomes through chromatin-mediated transcriptional programs, (2) SWI/SNF-associated DPF1 as unique transcriptional regulator of malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumors (2023)—DPF1-containing BAF complexes drive MPNST-specific gene expression, (3) DPF1 as mitotic cell cycle gene with diagnostic/prognostic value in hepatocellular carcinoma (2025)—cell cycle regulation through chromatin remodeling, (4) STRING partners (ARID1A/B, SMARCB1, SMARCC1/2, SMARCD1, SMARCE1, ACTL6B) confirm core BAF/PBAF complex membership, with brain-enriched expression (30.9 nTPM) reflecting critical role in neuronal development and 8 ClinVar CNV gains at 19q11-13.2 potentially contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders, intellectual disability, or cancer through SWI/SNF complex dosage effects, making DPF1 a potential therapeutic target for MPNST and hepatocellular carcinoma through chromatin remodeling modulation.
