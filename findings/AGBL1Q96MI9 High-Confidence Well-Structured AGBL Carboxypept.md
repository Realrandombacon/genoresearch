## Description
AGBL1 (AGBL carboxypeptidase 1, also known as CCP4, FECD8, Cytosolic carboxypeptidase 4) is a 1112 amino acid cytosolic metalloprotease (UniProt Q96MI9) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 78.2), characteristic of cytosolic carboxypeptidases that function through zinc-dependent deglutamylation of polyglutamylated tubulin and other cytoskeletal proteins to regulate microtubule dynamics and ciliary function. The protein belongs to the cytosolic carboxypeptidase family (IPR050821, residues 149-1073; PTHR45766), contains a peptidase M14 carboxypeptidase A domain (IPR000834, residues 732-1022; GO:0004181 metallocarboxypeptidase activity, GO:0008270 zinc ion binding, GO:0006508 proteolysis), a zinc carboxypeptidase domain (G3DSA:3.40.630.10, residues 742-1042), an armadillo-like helical fold (IPR011989, residues 1-266; IPR016024, residues 8-267; G3DSA:1.25.10.10, residues 1-266), a cytosolic aminopeptidase 1/4 domain (IPR033852, residues 754-1040), a cytosolic carboxypeptidase N-terminal domain (IPR040626, residues 597-671; PF18027), and a zinc carboxypeptidase catalytic domain (PF00246, residues 763-867; cd06906, residues 754-1040). AGBL1 is located on chromosome 15q25.3 and is predicted to enable metallocarboxypeptidase activity and zinc ion binding, involved in proteolysis and tubulin deglutamylation.
