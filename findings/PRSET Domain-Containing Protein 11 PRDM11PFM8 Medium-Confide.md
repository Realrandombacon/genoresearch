## Description
PRDM11 (UniProt Q9NQV5), also known as PFM8, encodes a 511 aa nuclear transcriptional regulator functioning as a putative histone methyltransferase that suppresses MYC-driven lymphomagenesis and modulates airway inflammatory responses through integrated SET domain catalytic activity and zinc finger-mediated DNA recognition mechanisms.
