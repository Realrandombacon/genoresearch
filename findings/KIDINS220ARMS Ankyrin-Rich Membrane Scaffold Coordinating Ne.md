## Description
KIDINS220/ARMS (Q9ULH0, 1771 aa) is a partially structured (AlphaFold pLDDT: 61.7) membrane-associated scaffold protein functioning as a critical signaling hub downstream of neurotrophin receptors and regulator of phosphate export through XPR1 activation. InterPro analysis reveals a complex multidomain architecture with N-terminal ankyrin repeat clusters (IPR002110/PF00023/PF12796, residues 13-102/103-135/136-201/269-298) mediating protein-protein interactions, a central KAP family P-loop domain (IPR011646/PF07693, residues 440-952) potentially involved in nucleotide binding or conformational regulation, and a C-terminal SAM/pointed domain (IPR013761/IPR057092, residues 1205-1288) enabling oligomerization, classifying it within the neurotrophin-activated signaling adaptor family (IPR052771). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression with nucleoplasmic, vesicular, and cytosolic localization, consistent with scaffold functions in signal transduction and membrane trafficking, with disease involvement in hereditary spastic paraplegia, intellectual disability, neurodegeneration, and obesity. STRING interactions define a high-confidence neurotrophin signaling network linking KIDINS220 to Trk receptors NTRK1 (0.987) and NTRK2 (0.675), adaptor proteins CRKL (0.955) and CRK (0.917), phosphate exporter XPR1 (0.814), scaffolding protein MAGI2 (0.808), endosomal regulator SNX27 (0.732), protein kinase D isoforms PRKD3 (0.715) and PRKD1 (0.685), and ligand NGF (0.683), positioning it at the intersection of neurotrophic signaling, phosphate homeostasis, and endosomal trafficking. Literature (170 papers) establishes diverse mechanistic roles: 2025 studies demonstrate KIDINS220 and inositol pyrophosphate InsP8 synergistically activate XPR1 phosphate exporter critical for cellular phosphate balance; KIDINS220 mutations cause SINO syndrome (spasticity, intellectual disability, neuropathy, obesity); novel variants identified in hereditary spastic paraplegia with severe peripheral neuropathy; Kidins220-deficient hydrocephalus mice show altered glial phenotypes and AQP4 dysregulation in retina/optic nerve; and roles in neuronal differentiation and synaptic plasticity. ClinVar documents 137 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (p.Ser1320fs, p.Asp1339fs, p.Gly1436fs, p.Ile1242fs, p.Met109fs), nonsense mutations (p.Ser1483Ter, p.Arg179Ter), splice site variants, and copy number gains on chromosome 2p regions, confirming haploinsufficiency and dominant-negative mechanisms in neurodevelopmental disorders. The convergence of ankyrin-mediated scaffolding, neurotrophin receptor coupling, XPR1 phosphate export regulation, and spastic paraplegia associations suggests KIDINS220 functions as a molecular bridge coordinating growth factor signaling with metabolic homeostasis and neuronal maintenance, with therapeutic implications for neurodegenerative diseases, phosphate metabolism disorders, and hereditary spastic paraplegias.
