## Description
DCUN1D3 (Defective in Cullin Neddylation 1 Domain-Containing 3, also known as SCCRO3 or DCNL3) is a partially characterized E3 ligase cofactor with 13 publications, emerging as a critical modulator of cullin-RING ubiquitin ligase (CRL) activity through neddylation regulation. This compact 304-amino acid protein (UniProt Q8IWE4) features a conserved DCN1-like architecture optimized for cullin interaction. InterPro analysis reveals a potentiating neddylation domain (IPR005176/PS51229, positions 86-278), DCN1-like PONY binding domain (IPR042460/G3DSA:1.10.238.200, positions 175-284), defective-in-cullin neddylation protein family signature (IPR014764/PTHR12281, positions 75-281), cullin-binding domain (PF03556, positions 166-276), and EF-hand superfamily fold (G3DSA:1.10.238.10, positions 85-174). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 79.1), indicating a well-folded globular protein with stable cullin-binding interface.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm DCUN1D3 operates within the core neddylation and SCF ubiquitin ligase machinery: UBE2F (0.990, NEDD8-conjugating enzyme), CUL1 (0.962, cullin-1 scaffold protein), CAND1 (0.888, cullin-associated NEDD8-dissociated protein 1), RBX1 (0.886, RING-box protein 1 E3 ligase), CUL2 (0.808), PUM2 (0.797, PUMILIO RNA-binding protein), UBA3 (0.767, NEDD8-activating enzyme catalytic subunit), NEDD8 (0.748, neural precursor cell expressed developmentally downregulated protein 8), CUL3 (0.614), and UBE2M (0.511, second NEDD8-conjugating enzyme). The exceptionally high confidence links to UBE2F and CUL1 position DCUN1D3 as a direct regulator of NEDD8 transfer to cullin scaffolds, controlling CRL assembly and substrate ubiquitination.\n\nHPA shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, consistent with essential housekeeping function in protein quality control and cell cycle regulation. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm and cytosol, enabling regulation of both nuclear and cytoplasmic CRL complexes. Biological processes involve protein neddylation and ubiquitination. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains spanning chromosome 16p13.3-q24.3, though specific monogenic diseases remain uncharacterized—suggesting triplosensitivity may underlie developmental disorders or cancer susceptibility through hyperactivation of neddylation pathways. Conservation across mammals indicates strong functional constraint.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: DCUN1D3 functions as a context-dependent modulator of cullin neddylation that fine-tunes SCF ubiquitin ligase activity. Unlike its paralog DCUN1D1/SCCRO which promotes neddylation and oncogenesis, DCUN1D3 acts as an antagonist—2014 studies demonstrate DCUN1D3 inhibits SCCRO-promoted nuclear translocation and neddylation of cullins, functioning as a tumor suppressor. Under UV damage, DCUN1D3 activates SCF^SKP2 ubiquitin E3 ligase activity to enable S-phase progression and cell survival, explaining its UVC-responsive gene classification (2008). The UBE2F interaction suggests DCUN1D3 may preferentially regulate the UBE2F-NEDD8 axis over UBE2M, providing specificity in CRL activation. In breast cancer lymph node metastasis and bladder cancer progression, DCUN1D3 dysregulation contributes to tumor aggressiveness through altered protein turnover of cell cycle regulators (cyclins, CDK inhibitors). Recent 2025 mouse studies show DCUN1D3 is dispensable for spermatogenesis despite family member involvement, indicating functional redundancy or divergence among DCNL paralogs. Large 16p CNVs likely cause neurodevelopmental disorders via dosage effects on neddylation-dependent proteostasis.
