## Description

InterPro analysis reveals cation efflux protein transmembrane domains (G3DSA:1.20.1510.10, aa90-278; IPR027469, aa86-278; SSF161111, aa86-276) within the TMEM163 family scaffold (IPR026765/PTHR31937, aa1-289), indicating zinc ion binding and export capability consistent with SLC30 family zinc transporters. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with brain showing highest expression (44.4 nTPM), followed by pancreas (26.3 nTPM), subcellular localization to plasma membrane and synaptic vesicle membrane, classification as predicted membrane protein and transporter, and involvement in myelination and zinc export. This network positions TMEM163 at the intersection of kynurenine pathway metabolism (ACMSD), lysosomal ion homeostasis (TMEM175), and mitochondrial metabolism (MCCC1). ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (R138H, E80D, Q82L, H146R, R138C, L76R, L76P) and copy number gains spanning 2q21.2-23.2 and 2p25.3-q37.3 regions, with hypomyelinating leukodystrophy 25 as the primary disease association. The 2q21.3 chromosomal location and brain-enriched expression suggests oligodendrocyte function role. Mechanistic hypothesis: TMEM163 functions as a zinc efflux transporter that maintains intracellular zinc homeostasis in oligodendrocytes during active myelination; its cation efflux transmembrane domain (aa86-278, pLDDT 76.5) forms a stable 12-transmembrane helix bundle for zinc export across plasma membrane and synaptic vesicle membranes; ACMSD interaction couples zinc transport to kynurenine pathway metabolism regulating neuroinflammation; TMEM175 interaction coordinates lysosomal zinc and potassium homeostasis for autophagic myelin turnover; missense mutations in transmembrane helices (R138H/C, L76R/P, E80D, Q82L, H146R) disrupt zinc coordination and transport efficiency, causing intracellular zinc accumulation that impairs oligodendrocyte differentiation and myelin protein expression, explaining hypomyelinating leukodystrophy 25 pathogenesis in TMEM163 variant carriers.
