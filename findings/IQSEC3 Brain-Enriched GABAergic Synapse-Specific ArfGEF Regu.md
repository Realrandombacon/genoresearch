# IQSEC3: Brain-Enriched GABAergic Synapse-Specific ArfGEF Regulating Anxiety, Fear Memory, and Seizure Susceptibility Through Gephyrin Scaffolding

## Description
IQSEC3 encodes a 1182 aa IQ motif and SEC7 domain-containing protein (UniProt Q9UPP2) located at 12p13.33, functioning as a GABAergic synapse-specific guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) for ADP-ribosylation factors (Arfs). InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture: (1) Sec7 domain (IPR000904/PF01369, aa 644-839) responsible for Arf-GEF activity; (2) Pleckstrin homology (PH) domain (IPR011993/PF16453, aa 858-997) for membrane targeting; (3) IQ motif (PS50096, aa 315-344) for calmodulin binding; (4) bZIP-like region (cd14686, aa 32-63). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 57.0), indicating a partially structured protein with flexible regions typical of synaptic scaffolding regulators. STRING interactions reveal a synaptic network: RBFOX3/NeuN (0.848, neuronal marker), ARF6 (0.748) and ARF1 (0.646, small GTPase substrates), INSYN1 (0.662, inhibitory synapse protein), ARHGEF9/Collybistin (0.593, gephyrin-binding GEF), GPHN/Gephyrin (0.444, master inhibitory synapse scaffold), NPAS4 (0.400, activity-dependent transcription factor), and EVL (0.429, actin regulator). HPA shows tissue-enriched expression in brain (60.6 nTPM), classified as predicted intracellular protein localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, with cancer-enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar reports 38 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning 12p13.33, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain undefined due to the size of these CNVs encompassing multiple genes. Literature (30 papers) details: (1) Loss of IQSEC3 disrupting GABAergic synapse maintenance and decreasing somatostatin expression in hippocampus, leading to seizures mitigated by reducing activated microglia (23 citations, 2020; 2 citations, 2024); (2) Npas4 regulating IQSEC3 expression in hippocampal somatostatin interneurons to mediate anxiety-like behavior (17 citations, 2021); (3) IQSEC3 deletion impairing fear memory through upregulation of ribosomal S6K1 signaling in hippocampus (11 citations, 2021); (4) Identification as a gephyrin-binding component at GABAergic synapses essential for activity-dependent plasticity. Mechanistically, IQSEC3 functions through: (1) Arf Activation—Sec7 domain catalyzing GDP-GTP exchange on ARF1/ARF6 to regulate vesicle trafficking and receptor recycling at inhibitory synapses; (2) Gephyrin Scaffolding—binding gephyrin to organize postsynaptic density and stabilize GABA_A receptors; (3) Activity-Dependent Plasticity—NPAS4-mediated transcriptional upregulation during neuronal activation to remodel inhibitory circuits; (4) Calmodulin Regulation—IQ motif potentially linking calcium signaling to GEF activity. In disease, IQSEC3 dysfunction contributes to: epilepsy/seizures via loss of GABAergic inhibition; anxiety disorders through disrupted somatostatin interneuron function; impaired fear memory consolidation via S6K1 pathway dysregulation; potential neurodevelopmental disorders given 12p CNV associations. The brain-enriched expression (60.6 nTPM) and specific role in somatostatin interneurons position IQSEC3 as a critical regulator of inhibitory tone, with therapeutic potential for epilepsy, anxiety, and PTSD.
