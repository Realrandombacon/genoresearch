## Description
GEMIN6 is likely involved in spliceosomal complex assembly and mRNA processing. It interacts with other GEMIN proteins and is localized to the nucleoplasm and nuclear bodies. The protein contains domains associated with gem-associated protein 6 and is expressed in all tissues with low tissue specificity. There are 19 reported pathogenic variants associated with GEMIN6.
