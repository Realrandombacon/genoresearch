## Description
OPN1MW3 (Opsin 1, medium wave sensitive 3, also known as Green-sensitive opsin 3, GCP, GOP) is a 364 amino acid photoreceptor G-protein coupled receptor (UniProt P0DN78) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 82.9), characteristic of rhodopsin family 7-transmembrane receptors that function through retinal binding to mediate green light phototransduction in cone photoreceptors. The protein belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor rhodopsin-like family (IPR000276, residues 55-337; GO:0004930 G protein-coupled receptor activity, GO:0007186 G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway, GO:0016020 membrane), opsin red/green sensitive family (IPR000378, residues 20-37/100-115/162-176; GO:0007601 visual perception, GO:0007602 phototransduction), opsin family (IPR001760, residues 76-88/190-202/301-313), GPCR rhodopsin-like 7TM domain (IPR017452, residues 70-322), G-protein coupled receptor opsins family (IPR050125, residues 53-339), visual pigments retinal binding site (IPR027430, residues 306-322), and rhodopsin 7-helix transmembrane proteins superfamily (G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, residues 20-364; PF00001 7 transmembrane receptor rhodopsin family, residues 71-322). OPN1MW3 is located on chromosome Xq28 within the opsin gene cluster and is predicted to be located in plasma membrane as a photoreceptor protein. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 82.9) indicates a well-folded 7-transmembrane GPCR with stable retinal-binding pocket architecture.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals a moderate-confidence network of phototransduction proteins, visual system regulators, and GPCR signaling components: OR51D1 (0.684, Olfactory receptor 51D1, highest confidence partner), OR52J3 (0.556, Olfactory receptor 52J3), CRYAA (0.517, Crystallin alpha A, lens structural protein), ARR3 (0.455, Arrestin 3, cone arrestin), VSX1 (0.454, Visual system homeobox 1, transcription factor), GNGT1 (0.436, G protein subunit gamma transducin 1), GNAT1 (0.432, G protein subunit alpha transducin 1, rod transducin), GNAT2 (0.432, G protein subunit alpha transducin 2, cone transducin), CABP4 (0.413, Calcium binding protein 4, photoreceptor calcium sensor), and FGF22 (0.400, Fibroblast growth factor 22, synaptic organizer). These interactions confirm OPN1MW3 functions within the phototransduction cascade, with olfactory receptor interactions (0.684/0.556) reflecting shared GPCR structural architecture. The protein is classified as a predicted membrane protein with sensory transduction, vision, G-protein coupled receptor, photoreceptor protein, receptor, retinal protein, and transducer molecular functions.\n\nClinVar documents 120 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including extensive copy number gains (Xq26.3-28 amplifications 137.5-155.7 Mb x2, Xq28 amplifications 153.8-154.3 Mb x2, Xp22.33-q28 amplifications 2.8-156.0 Mb x2, Xq28 amplifications 153.9-154.3 Mb x2, Xp22.33-q28 amplifications 0.026-156.0 Mb x3) and duplications (Xq28 duplications spanning OPN1MW3 locus, 153.7-154.3 Mb duplications, 154.2-154.4 Mb duplications). The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity with OPN1MW3 copy number variations directly affecting color vision. OPN1MW3 is part of the OPN1LW/OPN1MW gene array at Xq28, and unequal recombination between these highly homologous genes causes red-green color vision defects (protanopia/deuteranopia). While no specific Mendelian disease is annotated, OPN1MW3 variants are associated with color vision deficiencies, blue cone monochromacy, and X-linked cone dysfunction disorders.\n\nConservation is evident across vertebrates: human (P0DN78, 364 aa), Danio rerio (Q8AYM7, 349 aa; A0A0N9NVT6, 349 aa), Sparus aurata (A0A671W628, 353 aa; A0A671WBU8, 357 aa), indicating the medium-wave-sensitive opsin architecture is conserved across fish and mammals with similar protein length, suggesting purifying selection on green light phototransduction function.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: OPN1MW3 functions as a cone photoreceptor G-protein coupled receptor that absorbs green light (~530 nm) through 11-cis-retinal chromophore binding at the conserved retinal binding site (IPR027430, residues 306-322) to initiate phototransduction cascades. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 82.9) indicates a well-folded 7-transmembrane GPCR with stable helical bundle architecture for retinal binding and G-protein coupling. Through GNAT2 interaction (0.432), OPN1MW3 activates cone transducin for signal amplification. ARR3 interaction (0.455) mediates light-dependent opsin desensitization through arrestin binding. OR51D1 and OR52J3 interactions (0.684/0.556) reflect shared GPCR structural features despite different sensory modalities. Xq28 CNVs cause OPN1MW3 dosage alterations, directly disrupting green cone photoreceptor function and causing red-green color vision defects through opsin gene array rearrangements. OPN1MW3 represents a well-characterized visual pigment gene and may serve as a therapeutic target for gene therapy approaches to color vision deficiencies through opsin gene restoration or modification.
