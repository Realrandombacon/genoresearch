## Description
ZNF784 (Zinc Finger Protein 784, UniProt Q8NCA9) is a 323 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor encoded on chromosome 19q13.42 (GRCh38: 55620740-55624565). InterPro analysis identifies multiple C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) spanning residues 65-274, with 3-4 tandem zinc finger motifs characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulators. The classic zinc finger superfamily (G3DSA:3.30.160.60) and C2H2-type signatures (PS00028, PS50157) confirm canonical major groove DNA recognition via alpha-helix insertion.\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals 7 medium-confidence partners (scores 0.412-0.498): HAUS7 (0.498, augmin complex microtubule nucleation), RFPL4B (0.456, retina-specific E3 ligase), GPN3 (0.451, GTPase nuclear import), ZNF787 (0.422, paralogous zinc finger), FIZ1 (0.420, transcriptional coactivator), UTS2B (0.417, urotensin II receptor), KIF4B (0.412, kinesin chromosome condensation). This profile suggests roles in transcriptional regulation coupled with cell cycle/chromatin dynamics, particularly the HAUS7-KIF4B connection to mitotic spindle assembly and chromosome segregation.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (40.2 nTPM), detected in all tissues with nucleoplasmic localization. The skeletal muscle enrichment is notable for a zinc finger transcription factor, suggesting potential roles in myogenic differentiation or muscle-specific gene regulation. Protein class annotation confirms transcription factor activity with DNA-binding molecular function.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—an exceptionally high burden. Variants are predominantly copy number gains (duplications spanning 19q13.33-13.43 regions, ~5-10Mb) with rare deletions (~3:15 ratio). The strong duplication bias indicates triplosensitivity where ZNF784 overexpression causes pathogenic phenotypes. Associated conditions include 19q13.42-13.43 duplication syndrome with developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, and skeletal anomalies.\n\nAlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 63.4, medium confidence) shows partially structured architecture consistent with zinc finger domains embedded in flexible linker regions—typical of C2H2 transcription factors where structured DNA-binding motifs connect via disordered linkers enabling conformational flexibility for target site scanning.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ZNF784 functions as a skeletal muscle-enriched transcriptional repressor/activator regulating myogenic differentiation genes through C2H2 zinc finger DNA recognition. Candidate targets include myosin heavy chain genes (MYH family), myogenic regulatory factors (MYOD1, MYOG), or muscle-specific metabolic enzymes. The triplosensitivity burden (15 duplications:3 deletions) suggests dosage-sensitive repression where ZNF784 overexpression causes hyper-repression of muscle development genes, disrupting myofiber maturation. Interaction with HAUS7-KIF4B complex suggests potential coupling of transcriptional regulation to mitotic chromosome dynamics in proliferating myoblasts. Pathogenic duplications may cause ZNF784 overexpression leading to aberrant silencing of myogenic programs, contributing to 19q13.42-13.43 duplication syndrome with skeletal anomalies and developmental delay.
