## Description
OSBPL10 (Oxysterol-Binding Protein-Like 10, ORP10, Q9BXB5) encodes a 764 aa lipid transfer protein functioning as a phosphatidylserine (PS) transporter at endoplasmic reticulum-Golgi membrane contact sites that coordinates lipid homeostasis through VAP-mediated tethering and regulates cancer metabolism and neuronal survival pathways. Structural architecture reveals N-terminal pleckstrin homology domain (IPR001849/IPR041680/G3DSA:2.30.29.30/cd13291, residues 70-176/78-171/74-173/77-183) for membrane targeting via PI4P recognition, central linker region, and C-terminal oxysterol-binding protein-related domain (ORD) (IPR000648/IPR037239/PF01237/G3DSA:2.40.160.120/3.30.70.3490, residues 394-763/403-704/76-762) containing conserved ligand-binding site (IPR018494, residues 531-541) for sterol/phospholipid binding; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 73.3) confirms confident well-folded structure with distinct PH and ORD domains connected by flexible linker enabling membrane contact site localization. STRING interactions define lipid transport and membrane contact networks: ER membrane protein VAPB (0.805)/VAPA (0.780) forming membrane tethers at ER-organelle junctions, dipeptidyl peptidase DPP3 (0.657), mitochondrial protein NIPSNAP2 (0.635), mitochondrial phosphatase PGAM5 (0.625) linking to mitophagy, pleckstrin proteins PLEK2 (0.616)/PLEK (0.603) for membrane dynamics, zinc finger ZNF860 (0.543), apolipoprotein APOB (0.527) for lipoprotein metabolism, and paralog OSBPL11/ORP11 (0.430). HPA localizes OSBPL10 to nucleoplasm/Golgi apparatus/plasma membrane as intracellular protein involved in lipid biosynthesis/metabolism/transport with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues and group-enriched blood cell specificity. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic variants including extensive 3p26.3-22.3 copy number gains (chr3:2263690-55016039, chr3:60000-34461438, chr3:61891-33946644 duplications) and losses/deletions. Literature (75 papers) demonstrates emerging disease mechanisms: OSBPL10-CNBP axis mediates hypoxia-induced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma development (6x citations, 2024), OSBPL10 alleviates neuronal ferroptosis via lysosomal membrane repair in PS-dependent manner after spinal cord injury (2026), OSBPL10 drives lipophagy-mediated lipid mobilization promoting PDAC progression (2026), DNA alterations in OSBPL10 upstream region in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (2026), pan-cancer prognostic biomarker associated with immune infiltration (2025). Mechanistic hypothesis: OSBPL10 functions as lipid counter-transporter at ER-Golgi membrane contact sites; N-terminal PH domain binds PI4P on Golgi membranes recruiting OSBPL10 to contact sites where FFAT-like motif interacts with VAPA/VAPB ER anchors; C-terminal ORD domain extracts phosphatidylserine from ER membrane and transfers it to Golgi while simultaneously transporting PI4P in opposite direction (counter-transport); this maintains PS asymmetry essential for vesicle trafficking and Golgi function; in pancreatic cancer, hypoxia-induced OSBPL10 upregulation enhances lipid mobilization through lipophagy supporting tumor growth; in spinal cord injury, OSBPL10-mediated PS transfer repairs damaged lysosomal membranes preventing ferroptotic cell death; 3p CNVs alter OSBPL10 dosage disrupting lipid homeostasis contributing to lymphoma and other malignancies; paralog OSBPL11 shares overlapping functions with distinct substrate specificity.
