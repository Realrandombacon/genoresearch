## Description
KHDRBS2 encodes a 349 aa nuclear RNA-binding protein (UniProt Q5VWX1), also known as SLM-1 (Sam68-Like Mammalian protein 1), that functions as a critical post-transcriptional regulator controlling alternative splicing, mRNA stability, and translation efficiency through sequence-specific RNA recognition. The protein features a modular domain architecture characteristic of STAR (Signal Transduction and Activation of RNA) family proteins: an N-terminal Qua1 dimerization domain (IPR032571/PF16274, residues 5-57) mediating homo-oligomerization essential for cooperative RNA binding; a central type I KH RNA-binding domain (IPR004087/PF22675/PS50084, residues 58-156; cd22469) recognizing specific RNA sequences (typically UAAU motifs) in target transcripts; and a C-terminal tyrosine-rich domain (IPR032335/PF16568, residues 267-321) serving as a protein interaction hub for signaling kinases and splicing regulators. STRING interactions reveal KHDRBS2 operates within extensive RNA processing networks: PTK6 (0.914), protein tyrosine kinase 6 phosphorylating KHDRBS2 to regulate RNA-binding activity; RBMX (0.885), RNA-binding motif protein X involved in spliceosome assembly; KHDRBS3 (0.835), paralogous family member suggesting functional redundancy or cooperation; CRYL1 (0.819), crystallin lambda-1 potentially linking to stress responses; PRMT1 (0.779), protein arginine methyltransferase modifying KHDRBS2 or associated proteins; HNRNPK/HNRNPA0 (0.676-0.724), heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoproteins coordinating mRNA processing; RBM3 (0.705), cold-shock RNA-binding protein; KHDRBS1/Sam68 (0.660), founding family member; and YTHDC1 (0.635), m6A reader linking to epitranscriptomic regulation. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with brain enrichment (6.4 nTPM), thyroid (3.8 nTPM), and retina (3.7 nTPM), classified as cancer-enhanced and group-enriched in immune cells, consistent with roles in neuronal RNA metabolism and tumor progression. ClinVar lists 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all representing large copy number gains (6p21.1-q12 spanning 44-65Mb, 6p22.1-q14.1 spanning 29-81Mb, 6p25.3-q27 spanning 0.16-171Mb), demonstrating dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 69.9), revealing partially structured protein with well-folded KH domain and flexible tyrosine-rich regions characteristic of dynamic RNA-binding proteins requiring conformational adaptability. Mechanistically, KHDRBS2 operates through integrated modes: (1) Dimerization-dependent RNA binding—Qua1-mediated oligomerization increases affinity and specificity for target RNAs; (2) Phosphorylation regulation—PTK6 and other kinases phosphorylate tyrosine-rich domain modulating RNA-binding affinity and subcellular localization; (3) Alternative splicing control—binds intronic/exonic splicing enhancers recruiting spliceosome components (RBMX, HNRNPs); (4) mRNA stability regulation—protects target transcripts from degradation or promotes decay via recruitment of decay machinery; (5) Cancer progression—2025 studies link low KHDRBS2 expression to immune cell invasion and poor prognosis in glioblastoma; regulates AR-V7 splice variant in prostate cancer affecting androgen signaling; (6) Neurodegeneration—2021 study shows pseudogene ACTBP2 promotes KHDRBS2 transcription via KMT2D/WDR5 recruitment increasing blood-brain barrier permeability in Alzheimer's disease Aβ microenvironment. Loss-of-function causes aberrant splicing patterns
