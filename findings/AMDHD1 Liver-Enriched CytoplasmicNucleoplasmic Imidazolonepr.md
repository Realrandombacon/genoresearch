## Description
AMDHD1 (Amidohydrolase Domain Containing 1, also known as Probable Imidazolonepropionase, UniProt Q96NU7) is a 426 amino acid cytoplasmic/nucleoplasmic metabolic enzyme with partial characterization (25 papers) that functions as an imidazolonepropionase catalyzing the hydrolysis of imidazolone-5-propionate to N-formiminoglutamate in the histidine catabolic pathway, a critical step linking histidine degradation to glutamate production and one-carbon metabolism. The protein contains an imidazolonepropionase domain (IPR005920/PTHR42752/cd01296, residues 4-425/4-425/43-420) with hydrolase activity acting on carbon-nitrogen bonds in cyclic amides (GO:0016812), belongs to the amidohydrolase-related family (IPR006680/PF01979, residues 79-421/79-421), and is part of the metal-dependent hydrolase superfamily (IPR011059/IPR032466/G3DSA:3.20.20.140/SSF51338, residues 33-424/81-382/82-381/46-82) with L-histidine catabolic process function (GO:0019556), placing it in the essential amino acid metabolism machinery.
