The 'like' suffix in LOC112268265's annotation strongly suggests this locus represents either a processed pseudogene derived from ANKRD26 through retrotransposition, an unprocessed gene duplication with degraded coding potential, or a transcriptional artifact. Functional hypothesis: LOC112268265 likely represents a non-functional pseudogene rather than a bona fide protein-coding dark gene. If transcribed, it may produce non-coding RNA that could potentially regulate ANKRD26 expression through competitive endogenous RNA (ceRNA) mechanisms or antisense regulation. Alternatively, rare tissue-specific expression or rapid protein turnover could explain absence from proteomic databases. Priority for experimental validation includes: (1) RNA-seq analysis to confirm transcription, (2) ORF integrity assessment comparing to ANKRD26 consensus sequence, (3) conservation analysis across primates to assess selective pressure, and (4) CRISPR-based knockout to test phenotypic consequences. Until molecular characterization confirms protein-coding capacity, LOC112268265 should be classified as 'ANKRD26-related locus of uncertain coding potential' rather than confirmed dark gene.
