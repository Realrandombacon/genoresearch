## Description
NPEPPS/P55786 (919 aa) is an exceptionally well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 91.3) cytosolic metallopeptidase functioning as a critical regulator of peptide homeostasis, neuronal migration, and cancer chemoresistance. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical M1 aminopeptidase architecture (IPR001930/PF01433/PTHR23466, residues 185-250/61-499) containing the catalytic HEXXH zinc-binding motif and ERAP1-like C-terminal domain (IPR024571, 578-891) essential for substrate recognition and proteolytic activity, classifying it within the gluzincin aminopeptidase family. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, exclusive cytosolic localization consistent with its role in intracellular peptide turnover, and classification as an essential metabolic protein. The convergence of ultra-high confidence catalytic structure, SRGAP2/NBPF1 neurodevelopmental partnerships, platinum resistance mechanisms, TBC1D3-mediated cortical expansion, and immune-metabolic dysregulation suggests NPEPPS functions as a proteostatic checkpoint coordinating neuronal migration, tumor survival under genotoxic stress, and human brain evolution, with therapeutic potential in overcoming platinum resistance, modulating neurodevelopmental disorders, and targeting fusion-driven carcinomas.
