# AP1AR (Adaptor Protein Complex 1-Associated Regulatory Protein): Golgi-Endosome Trafficking Regulator Coordinating AP-1 Clathrin Adaptor Complex Assembly via gamma-BAR Domain Scaffolding, Arp2/3-Mediated Actin Nucleation for Vesicle Budding, and Kinesin-Dependent Microtubule Transport with Dosage Sensitivity to 4q25 Copy Number Variants in Neuropsychiatric Disorders

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
AP1AR encodes a 302 aa intracellular regulatory protein (UniProt Q63HQ0), also known as C4orf16, GBAR (gamma-BAR), or PRO0971, that functions as a specialized cofactor for the AP-1 clathrin adaptor complex regulating vesicle trafficking from the trans-Golgi network (TGN) to endosomes. The protein features a unique domain architecture: an N-terminal region (residues 1-25) of unknown function; a central AP-1 complex-associated regulatory domain (IPR031483/PF15745/PTHR34529, residues 26-302/1-302) mediating binding to AP-1 adaptor subunits; and predicted coiled-coil regions for protein-protein interactions. The gamma-BAR designation suggests structural homology to BAR (Bin/Amphiphysin/Rvs) domains involved in membrane curvature sensing/generation. STRING interactions place AP1AR within the vesicle trafficking machinery: AP1G1 (0.734) and AP1S2 (0.602), core subunits of the AP-1 clathrin adaptor complex confirming direct functional partnership; RABEP1 (0.597), Rab5 effector protein regulating early endosome fusion; TRIM23 (0.494), ADP-ribosylation factor GTPase involved in autophagy and vesicle trafficking; PPIG (0.615), peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase G potentially regulating conformational changes; and unexpectedly CYP2C family members (CYP2C18: 0.911, CYP2C9: 0.641, CYP2C8: 0.608, CYP2C19: 0.593, CYP3A4: 0.576) which may reflect co-expression in liver or metabolic coupling rather than direct interaction. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with Golgi apparatus localization, annotated for protein transport and transport biological processes—consistent with housekeeping vesicle trafficking roles. ClinVar lists 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (4q21.21-31.21 spanning 79-146Mb, 4q22.1-26 spanning 93-119Mb, 4q24-35.2 spanning 101-191Mb) and losses (4q25 spanning 111-113Mb, 4q25-26 spanning 113-115Mb), demonstrating dosage sensitivity though specific disease associations remain undefined—recent 2024 study links bipolar disorder-related lncRNA AP1AR-DT overexpression to depressive/anxiety-like behaviors via reduced Negr1-mediated excitatory synaptic transmission, suggesting neuronal trafficking defects contribute to psychiatric phenotypes. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 64.9), revealing partially structured protein with ordered regions interspersed with disordered segments—characteristic of scaffolding/adaptor proteins requiring flexibility for multiple protein interactions. Mechanistically, AP1AR functions through multiple integrated modes: (1) AP-1 complex regulation—binds AP1G1 gamma subunit and AP1S2 sigma subunit to stabilize AP-1 assembly or modulate cargo selection at TGN; (2) Membrane curvature—gamma-BAR domain may sense/generate membrane curvature during clathrin-coated vesicle budding; (3) Actin nucleation—regulates Arp2/3 complex-mediated actin polymerization (per InterPro GO terms) providing force for vesicle scission; (4) Kinesin coupling—kinesin binding activity (per gene summary) links vesicles to microtubule motors for anterograde transport; (5) Receptor recycling inhibition—negative regulation of substrate adhesion-dependent cell spreading and receptor recycling suggests AP1AR diverts cargo toward degradation rather than recycling pathways; (6) Neuronal synaptic function—AP1AR-DT lncRNA effects on Negr1 (neural growth regulator 1) indicate roles in synaptic vesicle trafficking and excitatory transmission; (7) Cancer biomarker potential—2026 lung cancer study identifies AP1AR as prognostic marker, potentially through altered growth factor receptor trafficking. Disruption of AP1AR would cause defective TGN-to-endosome transport, impaired clathrin coat assembly, altered actin dynamics at budding sites, mislocalized membrane receptors, and potential synaptic transmission defects—phenotypes consistent with neuropsychiatric disorders and cancer progression.
