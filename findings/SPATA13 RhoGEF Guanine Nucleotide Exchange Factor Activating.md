# SPATA13: RhoGEF Guanine Nucleotide Exchange Factor Activating CDC42/RAC GTPases for Cell Migration

**Date:** 2026-03-20T13:19:49.373318

**Quality Score:** 2.05/10  (E=4.1, D=0.0) [WEAK]

## Description
SPATA13 (also known as ARHGEF29 or ASEF2) encodes a 652 amino acid Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor (UniProt Q96N96) that activates Rho-family GTPases to drive cytoskeletal reorganization and cell migration. The protein contains a Dbl homology (DH) catalytic domain (IPR000219, positions 240-424) responsible for guanine nucleotide exchange activity, an N-terminal SH3 domain (IPR001452, positions 147-206) for protein-protein interactions, and a C-terminal pleckstrin homology (PH) domain (IPR001849, positions 455-563) for membrane recruitment via phosphoinositide binding. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (61.5 nTPM), ubiquitous detection across all tissues, and nucleoplasmic/cytosolic localization. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with small GTPases CDC42 (0.949), RAC3 (0.918), RAC2 (0.823), RAC1 (0.709), and RHOA (0.624), plus adenomatous polyposis coli proteins APC (0.864) and APC2 (0.903), indicating SPATA13 acts as a scaffold linking Wnt signaling to Rho GTPase activation. Additional partners include PPP1R9B/neurabin-2 (0.818) and ARHGEF4 (0.730). ClinVar documents 41 pathogenic variants including deletions and copy number gains at 13q12.12. Mouse ortholog (UniProt Q5DU57, 656 aa) shows strong conservation. The filopodium, lamellipodium, and ruffle membrane localization combined with GEF activity positions SPATA13 as a key regulator of actin dynamics during cell migration, immune cell trafficking, and potentially tumor invasion.
