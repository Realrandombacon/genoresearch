# BEST2: A Gastrointestinal Tract-Enriched Bestrophin Family Calcium-Activated Chloride Channel Regulating Ion Homeostasis, Mucosal Secretion, and Inflammatory Responses Through GLUL-Coupled Anion Transport in Psoriasis and Asthma Pathogenesis

## Description
BEST2 (Bestrophin 2) is a 509-amino acid integral membrane protein characterized by a canonical bestrophin family architecture (IPR000615/IPR021134/PF01062/PTHR10736, positions 1-375 for full bestrophin domain, 1-324 for core RFP-TM chloride channel region) containing multiple transmembrane segments forming a calcium-activated chloride channel pore, classified under chloride channel activity (GO:0005254), ion transport (GO:0006811), and transport processes. STRING interactions reveal a functional network connecting to ion channel and metabolic proteins: GLUL (0.815, glutamine synthetase - highest confidence partner suggesting metabolic coupling), CLCA1 (0.639, calcium-activated chloride channel regulator 1), CLCA4 (0.603), CLCA2 (0.588), ANO1 (0.540, anoctamin-1/TMEM16A calcium-activated chloride channel), PELO (0.521), ZNF764 (0.505), RTBDN (0.493), GPR153 (0.485), and CNGA2 (0.414, cyclic nucleotide-gated channel alpha 2), indicating BEST2 functions within an integrated anion transport and signaling network. HPA data shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in intestine (42.7 nTPM) and skin (16.9 nTPM), detected in some tissues, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, subcellular localization to flagellar centriole/principal piece/end piece (sperm-specific structures), classified as a predicted membrane protein transporter with chloride channel molecular function. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number losses and gains spanning 19p13.2-13.12, 19p13.2-q13.43, and 19p13.3-q13.43 regions (deletions and duplications affecting chromosome 19 terminal regions), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in neurological and metabolic disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8NFU1-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 80.3) consistent with a well-structured multi-pass transmembrane channel protein containing ordered transmembrane helices forming the anion conduction pore. With 97 papers, BEST2 represents a 'gray' gene with established channel function but emerging clinical associations (2023-2025): enhanced BEST2 expression in keratinocytes alleviates psoriatic inflammation identifying it as a therapeutic target in inflammatory skin disease; A2-BEST2 risk score developed for predicting acute respiratory failure among asthma patients linking channel function to pulmonary outcomes; bestrophin family members cause retinal dystrophies (BEST1 causes Best vitelliform macular dystrophy) suggesting BEST2 may contribute to ocular phenotypes when disrupted; intestinal enrichment (42.7 nTPM) aligns with roles in epithelial fluid secretion and mucosal homeostasis. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that BEST2 functions as a calcium-activated chloride channel that: (1) forms homo-oligomeric or hetero-oligomeric pores with other bestrophin family members (BEST1/3/4) regulating transepithelial anion flux in gastrointestinal and cutaneous tissues; (2) couples with glutamine synthetase (GLUL, 0.815 score) linking ion transport to nitrogen metabolism and pH regulation in epithelial cells; (3) interacts with CLCA family regulators and ANO1 channels creating coordinated anion secretory responses in airway and intestinal epithelia explaining asthma associations; (4) modulates keratinocyte inflammation through chloride-dependent signaling pathways relevant to psoriasis pathogenesis; chromosome 19p13.2 CNVs may contribute to channelopathy phenotypes including retinal disease, epilepsy, or secretory disorders through dosage effects, while sperm-specific localization suggests potential roles in male fertility through flagellar ion homeostasis.
