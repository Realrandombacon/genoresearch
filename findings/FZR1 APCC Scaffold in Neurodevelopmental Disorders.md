## Description

Structural analysis (AlphaFold pLDDT 82.4) reveals a well-folded propeller-like architecture composed of canonical WD40 repeats (IPR001680), essential for mediating protein-protein interactions with core APC/C subunits (ANAPC2, CDC16, CDC23, CDC27) at high confidence (STRING score 0.999). Despite having 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (SNVs, CNVs), it shows zero explicitly tagged diseases in ClinVar, suggesting a 'dark gene' profile where variants disrupt complex stability leading to cell cycle checkpoint failure in developing neurons. HPA expression confirms nuclear localization and tags it as a 'Disease variant' with specific enrichment in Epilepsy and Intellectual disability.
