# ELOVL4: Retina-Enriched Very Long-Chain Fatty Acid Elongase Critical for Photoreceptor Membrane Integrity in Stargardt Dystrophy and Spinocerebellar Ataxia

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

## Description
ELOVL4 (UniProt Q9GZR5) encodes a 314-amino acid endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein containing the canonical ELO family domain (IPR002076/PF01151, aa 41-277) with a conserved ELO signature motif (PS01188, aa 154-162) essential for fatty acid elongase activity. The protein belongs to the GNS1/SUR4 family of very long-chain fatty acid elongases (IPR033678), catalyzing the condensation step in VLCFA (>C24) biosynthesis. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded transmembrane structure (pLDDT: 83.4), consistent with stable ER membrane integration.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal ELOVL4's position within the fatty acid elongation complex: strongest connections to interphotoreceptor matrix proteoglycan IMPG1 (0.978), parallel elongase HSD17B12 (0.954), paralogue ELOVL1 (0.945), and 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratases HACD2 (0.943)/HACD4 (0.939). Critically, ELOVL4 links to ABCA4 (0.894), the Stargardt disease transporter, establishing a functional pathway for photoreceptor lipid homeostasis. HPA confirms retina-enriched expression (163.6 nTPM), with secondary expression in lymphoid tissue (98.1 nTPM).\n\nClinVar contains 59 pathogenic variants—including frameshifts (p.Phe145fs, p.Asn39fs), missense mutations (p.Ser155Phe, p.Ile171Asn/Leu), and 6q12-14.1 copy number losses—causing autosomal dominant Stargardt-like macular dystrophy (STGD3/ADMD), spinocerebellar ataxia type 34 (SCA34), and ichthyosis. This positions ELOVL4 as a non-redundant VLCFA synthase whose dysfunction disrupts photoreceptor disc membrane composition, triggering lipofuscin accumulation and neurodegeneration. The ELOVL4-ABCA4 axis represents an untapped therapeutic target for inherited retinal dystrophies.
