## Description
DNMBP (Dynamin-Binding Protein, also known as Tuba, UniProt Q6XZF7) is a 1577 amino acid multi-domain scaffold protein with moderate characterization (39 papers) that functions as a CDC42-specific guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) and membrane curvature sensor regulating actin cytoskeleton dynamics, cell-cell junction assembly, and endocytic trafficking through its modular domain architecture. The protein contains six SH3 domains (IPR001452/cd11794-11798/cd12141/G3DSA:2.30.30.40 residues 6-56/70-123/149-199/247-296/1289-1345/1517-1573) for protein-protein interactions with dynamin, WIP, and Ena/VASP proteins, a Dbl homology (DH) RhoGEF domain (IPR000219/cd00160/G3DSA:1.20.900.10 residues 763-982/785-965, GO:0005085 guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity) for CDC42 activation, and a BAR domain (IPR004148/cd07589/G3DSA:1.20.1270.60 residues 992-1212/1017-1208, GO:0005515 protein binding) for membrane curvature sensing and dimerization. STRING interactions reveal high-to-medium confidence partnerships with membrane remodeling and actin regulators: BIN1 (0.918, bridging integrator 1—BAR domain protein), BIN3 (0.891, bridging integrator 3), CDC42 (0.836, cell division cycle 42—Rho GTPase substrate), AMPH (0.778, amphiphysin—BAR domain endocytosis protein), WIPF2 (0.675, WAS/WIP family member 2), DNM1 (0.670, dynamin-1—GTPase for vesicle scission), BIN2 (0.669), VASP (0.651, vasodilator-stimulated phosphoprotein—actin polymerization), ENAH (0.591, Ena homolog), and WASL (0.583, WAS/WASL actin nucleation promoting factor), confirming integration into the actin-membrane coordination machinery.
