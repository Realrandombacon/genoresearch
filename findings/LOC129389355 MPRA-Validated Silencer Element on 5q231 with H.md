# LOC129389355: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 5q23.1 with Haploinsufficient Regulatory Function

## Description
LOC129389355 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 5q23.1 (GRCh38: 124998602-124998802), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its classification as a non-coding regulatory element rather than a protein-coding gene. ClinVar analysis reveals 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number losses disrupting this region, ranging from focal 5q23.1-23.2 deletions (~6Mb, chr5:120739630-126823984) to large 5q21.3-33.2 deletions (~50Mb, chr5:116677122-156124387). These recurrent deletions are associated with 5q deletion syndromes (5q- syndrome) featuring refractory anemia, thrombocytopenia, and predisposition to myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS) and acute myeloid leukemia (AML).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389355 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 5q23.1 topologically associating domain (TAD), likely regulating expression of neighboring hematopoietic tumor suppressor genes such as EGR1, APC, or RPS14 through long-range chromatin interactions. The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit repressive histone marks (H3K27me3) at target promoters, maintaining basal repression of proto-oncogenes in erythroid and megakaryocytic lineages. Haploinsufficiency via 5q deletions abolishes this repressive function, leading to ectopic overexpression of oncogenic targets and disrupted hematopoietic differentiation. The recurrent nature of pathogenic CNVs encompassing this locus, particularly in myeloid malignancies, suggests strong selective pressure against silencer loss, positioning LOC129389355 as a non-coding tumor suppressor element whose disruption contributes to 5q- syndrome pathogenesis and MDS/AML predisposition. The K562 erythroleukemia cell line validation context supports a hematopoietic-specific regulatory role.
