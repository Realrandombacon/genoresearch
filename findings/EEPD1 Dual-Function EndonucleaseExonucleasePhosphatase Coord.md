## Description
EEPD1 (Endonuclease/Exonuclease/Phosphatase Family Domain-Containing Protein 1, Q7L9B9) encodes a 569 aa multifunctional enzyme functioning as a DNA repair nuclease, transcription elongation cofactor, and metabolic regulator through myristoylation-dependent PKA activation. Structural architecture reveals N-terminal helix-hairpin-helix DNA-binding motifs (IPR003583/IPR004509/IPR010994, residues 48-67/78-97/145-164/39-99/36-104/134-195) for DNA substrate recognition, central AF1531-like domain (G3DSA:1.10.150.280, residues 134-199), and C-terminal endonuclease/exonuclease/phosphatase catalytic domain (IPR005135/IPR036691/G3DSA:3.60.10.10/cd10283, residues 264-505/261-559/261-538) forming DNase I-like fold for nucleic acid cleavage; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 74.2) confirms confident well-folded structure consistent with stable enzymatic activity and protein-protein interactions. STRING interactions define transcription elongation and DNA repair networks: transcription elongation factor IWS1 (0.858) linking to RNA polymerase II processivity, FACT complex subunits SUPT16H (0.782)/SSRP1 (0.643)/SUPT5H (0.620)/SUPT4H1 (0.653) for chromatin remodeling during elongation, exonuclease EXO1 (0.697) for DNA resection, tankyrase-binding protein TNKS1BP1 (0.643) for telomere maintenance, PAF complex subunit RTF1 (0.576), CDC73/Parafibromin (0.586) for transcriptional regulation, and C6orf15 (0.620). HPA localizes EEPD1 to nuclear speckles/vesicles as intracellular protein with tissue-enhanced expression in skeletal muscle (45.3 nTPM), detected in all tissues, immune cell-enhanced specificity. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic variants including 7p14.3-12.3 copy number losses (chr7:29296048-47809018, chr7:33478398-39686397 deletions), 7p15.2-14.1/7p22.3-q36.3 gains (chr7:27507832-39072473, chr7:10365-159119707, chr7:43361-159119707 duplications/triplications), silent variant p.Ser342=, and complex alleles. Literature (54 papers) demonstrates emerging disease mechanisms: myristoylated EEPD1 enhances lipolysis and thermogenesis through PKA activation combating obesity (4x citations, 2025), LXR-regulated EEPD1 controls macrophage-specific lipid metabolism and cholesterol efflux (5x citations, 2025), EEPD1 regulates inflammation and endothelial apoptosis in atherosclerosis via KLF4-EEPD1-ERK axis (2x citations, 2025), EEPD1 regulates actin polymerization linking DNA repair to cytoskeletal dynamics (2x citations, 2025). Mechanistic hypothesis: EEPD1 functions through three distinct mechanisms depending on cellular context and post-translational modifications; (1) DNA repair: catalytic domain generates 3-OH termini at DNA double-strand breaks facilitating homologous resection by EXO1 and recruitment of repair machinery, helix-hairpin-helix motifs position DNA substrate in active site; (2) transcription elongation: EEPD1 associates with FACT complex and IWS1 at actively transcribed genes resolving R-loops and preventing transcription-replication conflicts; (3) metabolic regulation: N-terminal myristoylation targets EEPD1 to lipid droplets where it activates PKA through direct protein-protein interaction enhancing lipolytic enzyme phosphorylation (HSL, ATGL) and thermogenic gene expression (UCP1); LXR binding to EEPD1 promoter links cholesterol sensing to efflux pathways (ABCA1, ABCG1); in atherosclerosis, KLF4 transcriptionally activates EEPD1 suppressing endothelial apoptosis through ERK pathway modulation; 7p CNVs alter EEPD1 dosage affecting genome stability and metabolic homeostasis; skeletal muscle enrichment (45.3 nTPM) suggests role in exercise-induced DNA repair and metabolic adaptation.
