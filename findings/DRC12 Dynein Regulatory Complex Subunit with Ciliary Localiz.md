# DRC12: Dynein Regulatory Complex Subunit with Ciliary Localization and Disease-Associated Variants

## Description
DRC12 (Dynein Regulatory Complex subunit 12 homolog, alias CCDC153) encodes a 210 amino acid coiled-coil protein (UniProt Q494R4) that localizes to the cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, and motile cilium. InterPro analysis reveals the DRC12-like domain (IPR033585, residues 1-199) and PTHR28656 coiled-coil family membership, with a CATH superfamily fold (G3DSA:1.10.287.1490, residues 11-189). AlphaFold prediction shows high structural confidence (pLDDT 88.3), indicating a well-folded stable protein. HPA expression data demonstrates tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in choroid plexus (23.3 nTPM) and fallopian tube (22.8 nTPM), with cytosolic subcellular localization. STRING interaction network identifies 9 medium-confidence partners including MLF1 (0.579), LYPD2 (0.577), DAPL1 (0.563), and LZTFL1 (0.523) — notably LZTFL1 is a known ciliary trafficking factor. ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and deletions on chromosome 11q23.3-q25, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation across mammals (mouse, rat, bovine orthologs 202-210 aa) indicates functional constraint. The DRC12 protein likely functions as a structural scaffold within the dynein regulatory complex (DRC) that modulates ciliary dynein motor activity, potentially regulating microtubule sliding in motile cilia. Given its ciliary localization and interaction with ciliary trafficking factors, DRC12 may coordinate dynein arm positioning or activity in respiratory epithelium, ependymal cells, and fallopian tube cilia. Pathogenic CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity could disrupt ciliary beating, potentially contributing to ciliopathy phenotypes.
