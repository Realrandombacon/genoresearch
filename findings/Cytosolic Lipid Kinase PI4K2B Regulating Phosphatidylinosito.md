## Description
PI4K2B (Phosphatidylinositol 4-Kinase Type 2 Beta) is a 481-amino acid cytosolic enzyme catalyzing the phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol to generate phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate (PI4P), a critical lipid second messenger for membrane trafficking and signal transduction. InterPro analysis confirms its classification within the Type II PI4K family with domain IPR000403 (Phosphatidylinositol 3-/4-kinase catalytic domain, positions 120-451), IPR039756 (Type II PI4K Lsb6/PI4K2, 88-481), PF00454, and PS50290, sharing structural homology with PI4K2A but distinct in regulatory mechanisms. STRING interactions reveal a tight functional cluster within the phosphoinositide metabolism network: PI4KB (0.983), PIP5K1C (0.952), PIP5K1A (0.943), PIP5K1B (0.936), PIK3C2A (0.929), PI4KA (0.916), PIK3R2 (0.913), CDIPT (0.907), PIK3C3 (0.669), and PIKFYVE (0.558), suggesting coordinated regulation of PI4P/PI(4,5)P2 pools for vesicle budding and Golgi-to-endosome transport. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression predominantly in liver (54.1 nTPM) with ubiquitous detection, cytosolic localization, and classification as a metabolic enzyme involved in lipid metabolism. ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses on chromosome 4p16.3-p15.1 and silent/missense SNVs, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization; historical linkage studies associate the 4p15 region with bipolar disorder susceptibility, suggesting potential neuropsychiatric relevance. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8TCG2-F1) predicts confident folding (pLDDT 75.8) consistent with a stable kinase domain. With only 33 papers, PI4K2B represents a 'gray' gene: while its enzymatic activity is biochemically defined, its specific physiological substrates, tissue-specific regulatory partners beyond the core lipid network, and precise contribution to bipolar disorder or liver metabolic diseases remain largely unexplored compared to its paralog PI4K2A.
