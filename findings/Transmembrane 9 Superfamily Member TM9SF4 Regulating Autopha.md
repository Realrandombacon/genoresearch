## Description
TM9SF4 (Transmembrane 9 Superfamily Member 4) is a 642 aa multi-pass transmembrane protein functioning as an endomembrane regulator of vesicle trafficking and autophagic flux, with emerging roles in cancer progression and drug resistance. InterPro analysis identifies a defining Nonaspanin/TM9SF family domain (IPR004240, aa 23-642) and an Endomembrane protein 70 domain (PF02990, aa 55-598), consistent with its predicted 9 transmembrane helices forming a channel or scaffold within endosomal/lysosomal membranes. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.6) indicative of a stable, well-folded integral membrane protein. STRING interactions place TM9SF4 in vesicle trafficking networks with partners: SEC24C (0.787), a COPII coat component mediating ER-to-Golgi transport; POFUT1 (0.687), an O-fucosyltransferase in the ER; GPR107 (0.646), a Golgi GPCR involved in toxin retrograde transport; LMAN2 (0.630), an ER-Golgi cargo receptor; F3/Tissue Factor (0.592), linking to coagulation; KIF3B (0.584), a kinesin motor for anterograde transport; TMED2 (0.542), a transmembrane emp24 domain protein; and COPG1 (0.533), a COPI coat subunit for Golgi-to-ER retrieval. HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low specificity, consistent with a housekeeping trafficking role. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.771+1G>A) and large copy number gains on 20q11.21-11.23, though no specific Mendelian disease is yet defined. Literature (46 papers) establishes TM9SF4 as a cancer biomarker upregulated in colorectal cancer, hepatocellular carcinoma, and lung adenocarcinoma, where it mediates autophagy-dependent progression, crizotinib resistance, and cholestasis via the miR-370-3p/TM9SF4/KIT axis.
