## Description

The convergence of confident TOG domain-containing structure (pLDDT 76.1), exclusive centriolar/ciliary partnerships (CCP110-CEP97 cap complex, CEP290 transition zone), ubiquitous expression with cell type specificity, MAK kinase phosphorylation regulation of axonemal assembly, ciliary tip stabilization via cap complex, and chromosome 1p36 dosage sensitivity suggests CEP104 functions as a microtubule polymerase cofactor at the centriole distal tip and ciliary apex coordinating tubulin addition during axonemal elongation, maintaining ciliary tip integrity through cap complex oligomerization, and integrating phosphorylation signals from MAK kinase to control ciliary growth dynamics, with therapeutic potential in understanding ciliopathy pathogenesis (Joubert syndrome, intellectual disability), targeting ciliary assembly defects in developmental disorders, and modulating ciliary signaling pathways in ciliopathy spectrum conditions.
