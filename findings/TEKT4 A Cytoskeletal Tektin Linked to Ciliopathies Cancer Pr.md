## Description
TEKT4 (UniProt Q8WW24) is a 435 aa Tektin protein located on chromosome 2q11.1. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a highly stable, well-folded domain (pLDDT 91.1), consistent with its role as a structural component of the axoneme. Functionally, it localizes to perinuclear theca, connecting pieces, and flagellar centrioles, with expression enriched in testis (nTPM 12.8), aligning with its role in sperm motility. Clinically, it is associated with 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number gains/losses on chr2), suggesting a link to ciliopathies or infertility. Literature (24 papers) confirms its role in promoting papillary thyroid cancer proliferation via the PI3K/Akt pathway and conferring resistance to paclitaxel in breast cancer. While not a 'dark gene', TEKT4 represents a partially characterized gene where structural stability and copy number sensitivity may drive its pathogenicity in both developmental (sperm motility) and oncogenic contexts.
