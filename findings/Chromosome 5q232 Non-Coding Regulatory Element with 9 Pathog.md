Functional hypothesis: LOC101927421 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 5q23.2 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a neuronal enhancer coordinating LMNB1 expression in oligodendrocytes (myelination, nuclear lamina assembly) or as a chromatin boundary separating metabolism genes (ALDH7A1) from neurodegeneration genes (SNCAIP, LMNB1). Disease mechanism involves LMNB1 haploinsufficiency causing adult-onset autosomal dominant leukodystrophy (ADLD) through nuclear lamina disruption and oligodendrocyte dysfunction, ALDH7A1 deficiency causing pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy through impaired lysine metabolism and GABA synthesis, and SNCAIP dysregulation promoting alpha-synuclein aggregation in Parkinson disease. The 5q23.2 deletion syndromes may present with leukodystrophy, early-onset seizures, movement disorders, cognitive impairment, and metabolic abnormalities.
