## Description
SUPT3H (SPT3 Homolog, SAGA and STAGA Complex Component, UniProt O75486) is a 317 amino acid nuclear protein with moderate characterization (53 papers) that functions as an essential core subunit of the SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5 Acetyltransferase) and STAGA coactivator complexes, facilitating RNA polymerase II transcription initiation through histone-fold-mediated protein-protein interactions and TBP (TATA-binding protein) recruitment to gene promoters. The protein contains a histone-fold domain (IPR009072/G3DSA:1.10.20.10/SSF47113 residues 26-69) enabling heterodimerization with other SAGA subunits, and belongs to the transcription initiation factor TFIID subunit 13 family (IPR003195/PF02269/PTHR11380 residues 11-241) with Gene Ontology annotations for transcription by RNA polymerase II (GO:0006366) and protein heterodimerization activity (GO:0046982). STRING interactions reveal extremely high-confidence partnerships with core SAGA/STAGA complex components: SUPT7L (0.999, SPT3-like protein—SAGA scaffold), TAF12 (0.997, TATA-box binding protein-associated factor 12), TAF10 (0.997, TAF10), TAF6L (0.996, TAF6-like), TAF5L (0.995, TAF5-like), TADA3 (0.994, transcriptional adaptor 3), SGF29 (0.993, SAGA complex component), TADA2B (0.992, transcriptional adaptor 2B), KAT2A (0.969, histone acetyltransferase GCN5), and KAT2B (0.961, histone acetyltransferase PCAF), confirming integration into the histone acetylation and transcription initiation machinery.
