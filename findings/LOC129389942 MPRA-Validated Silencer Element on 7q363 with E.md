# LOC129389942: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 7q36.3 with Extraordinary Burden of 43 Recurrent Pathogenic CNVs

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:23:40.508330

**Quality Score:** 2.5/10  (E=4.0, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129389942 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 7q36.3 (GRCh38: 156034380-156034580), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—by far the highest burden among all silencer elements characterized in this study (previous records: LOC129389350=12, LOC129389747=11). Variants include 23 deletions (7q36.2-36.3, 7q35-36.3, 7q36.1-36.3, 7q36.3 spanning ~2-25Mb) and 20 duplications (7q34-36.3, 7q33-36.3, 7q32.3-36.3 spanning ~20-28Mb). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 7q36 deletion/duplication syndromes featuring acrofacial dysostosis, tibial hemimelia, polydactyly, holoprosencephaly spectrum, craniofacial dysmorphism, developmental delay, intellectual disability, and congenital heart defects.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389942 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 7q36.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes in the Sonic Hedgehog (SHH) pathway. Candidate targets include SHH itself (critical for limb and neural patterning), GLI2 (transcriptional effector), or HOXD cluster genes (limb development). The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The extraordinary CNV burden (43 variants—4× higher than typical silencers) indicates extreme dosage sensitivity and architectural importance. The 7q36.3 telomeric location near SHH and limb development genes explains the acrofacial dysostosis and tibial hemimelia phenotypes. Deletions cause SHH derepression (ectopic expression, patterning defects), while duplications cause silencer titration or TAD disruption. K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests additional hematopoietic regulatory roles.
