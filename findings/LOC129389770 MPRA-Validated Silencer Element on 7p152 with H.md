## Description
LOC129389770 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 7p15.2 (GRCh38: 25602394-25602594), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a high burden with both deletion and duplication events. Variants span large 7p regions including 7p22.3-15.2 duplications (~27Mb), 7p21.3-15.2 deletions (~15Mb), 7p15.3-15.2 deletions (~2.4Mb), and whole-arm 7p22.3-q36.3 deletions (~159Mb). This mixed CNV pattern suggests both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity where both silencer loss and gain cause pathogenic phenotypes. These recurrent CNVs are associated with 7p deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and growth abnormalities. The 7p15 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including HOXA cluster (HOXA1-HOA13, anterior-posterior patterning), EGFR (Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor, cell proliferation), BRAF (serine/threonine kinase, RAS-MAPK signaling), and GLI3 (Hedgehog pathway transcription factor, Greig cephalopolysyndactyly).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389770 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 7p15 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes. Candidate targets include HOXA cluster genes (embryonic patterning) or GLI3 (Hedgehog signaling, limb development). The silencer likely recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The high CNV burden (12 variants) with mixed deletion/duplication pattern indicates dual intolerance where both silencer loss causes target gene derepression (HOXA/GLI3 overexpression causing patterning defects) and silencer gain causes hyper-repression (HOXA/GLI3 underexpression causing limb/craniofacial anomalies). The K562 validation suggests potential roles in hematopoietic lineage specification. 7p15.2 synteny with HOXA cluster suggests potential cis-regulation of these critical developmental transcription factors, contributing to 7p deletion/duplication syndrome with craniofacial and limb abnormalities.
