## Description
ZBTB21 (Zinc finger and BTB domain-containing protein 21) is a 1066 aa transcription factor (UniProt Q9ULJ3) with an N-terminal BTB/POZ domain (aa 20-126, IPR000210/PF00651) for protein-protein interactions and multiple C2H2 zinc finger clusters (aa 546-598, 670-697, 775-803) for DNA binding. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 53.2) indicates partial disorder typical of transcriptional regulators. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with bone marrow highest (38.9 nTPM), nuclear localization. STRING interactions include CREB1 (0.457) and DYRK1A (0.450) - the latter is chr21-localized and overexpressed in Down syndrome. Recent literature (2024-2026) reveals: (1) ZBTB21 suppresses CRE-mediated transcription impairing synaptic function in Down syndrome, (2) SUMOylation regulates its transcriptional activity (zebrafish studies), (3) ZBTB21 acts as dual suppressor of pyroptosis and MHC-I antigen presentation promoting tumor immune evasion. ClinVar has 36 pathogenic variants (chr21 CNVs). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ZBTB21 functions as a SUMO-regulated transcriptional repressor that binds CRE elements via zinc fingers, recruits co-repressors via BTB domain, and modulates neuronal gene expression in DS and immune gene expression in cancer. The DYRK1A interaction suggests chr21 dosage-sensitive regulatory network. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: SUMOylation at conserved lysines alters ZBTB21 nuclear dynamics and DNA binding affinity, enabling context-specific repression of CRE-driven genes in neurons (synaptic function) vs. immune cells (pyroptosis/MHC-I).
