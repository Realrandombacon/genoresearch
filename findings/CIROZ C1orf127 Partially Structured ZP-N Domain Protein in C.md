## Description
CIROZ (C1orf127, Ciliated left-right organizer ZP-N domains-containing protein, UniProt Q8N9H9) is an 823-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 1p36.32. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the CIROZ family (IPR027956/PTHR38653, aa 20-810) characterized by a CIROZ beta domain (IPR049521, aa 261-365) containing a domain of unknown function DUF4556 with Ig-like fold (PF15094, aa 261-365). The protein name indicates involvement in ciliated left-right organizer function, critical for establishing embryonic left-right asymmetry through nodal cilia. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves medium confidence (pLDDT 52.7), indicating a partially structured protein with both ordered domains and intrinsically disordered regions — consistent with scaffold or regulatory proteins that require conformational flexibility for multiple interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas reveals tissue-enhanced expression with skeletal muscle enrichment (15.7 nTPM) and pancreas expression (4.2 nTPM), cancer-enriched RNA specificity, immune cell enhancement, and dual subcellular localization in nuclear speckles and cytosol, suggesting roles in both transcriptional regulation and cytoplasmic functions. STRING interaction network reveals 4 medium-confidence partners spanning diverse functional categories: OR5A1 (0.465, olfactory receptor), CCT6B/CCT3 (0.420-0.449, chaperonin-containing TCP1 subunits involved in protein folding), and UBIAD1 (0.401, UbiA prenyltransferase domain-containing protein). This interactome suggests CIROZ may function as a client or regulator of the TRiC/CCT chaperonin complex. ClinVar contains 44 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Tyr128Ter, p.Arg193Ter), frameshift deletions (p.Leu798fs, p.Gln404fs), missense variants (p.Ser56Leu, p.Thr19Met), and multiple copy-number losses/gains across 1p36, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity and haploinsufficiency where truncating variants cause disease phenotypes likely related to left-right patterning defects or ciliopathy. Conservation across primates (chimpanzee H2R9I4/A0A2I3RLZ4, 674-823 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin. CIROZ likely functions as a scaffold protein in ciliated cells of the left-right organizer, potentially regulating ciliary assembly, motility, or signaling through interactions with chaperonin complexes and nuclear speckle components.
