## Description
ZSCAN32 (Zinc finger and SCAN domain-containing protein 32, UniProt Q9NX65) is a 697-amino acid nuclear transcription factor located on chromosome 16p13.3. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture characteristic of KRAB-ZFP transcriptional repressors: an N-terminal SCAN oligomerization domain (IPR003309/PF02023, aa 33-142) mediating homo/heterodimerization with other SCAN-containing proteins, a central KRAB domain superfamily (IPR036051, aa 206-248) recruiting KAP1/TRIM28 co-repressor complexes for heterochromatin formation via H3K9me3 methylation, and three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, aa 521-548, 549-576, 577-604) recognizing specific DNA sequences. AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 54.1), indicating partial disorder consistent with transcriptional regulators requiring conformational flexibility for chromatin engagement. Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol, suggesting broad regulatory roles. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners exclusively comprising KRAB/SCAN zinc finger proteins: ZNF24 (0.855), ZNF446 (0.845), ZSCAN30 (0.844), ZBED9 (0.842), ZKSCAN3 (0.794), ZSCAN22/21 (0.537-0.538), ZKSCAN1 (0.531), ZNF174 (0.480), and ZKSCAN4 (0.451), placing ZSCAN32 within a cooperative transcriptional repression network. ClinVar contains 37 pathogenic variants including copy-number gains and losses across 16p13.3 and promoter mutations (c.-116G>A, c.-30C>G, c.271G>T p.Glu91Ter), demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and overexpression disrupt transcriptional programs. Conservation across vertebrates indicates ancient evolutionary origin. ZSCAN32 likely functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional silencer regulating developmentally important genes through KRAB-mediated recruitment of epigenetic modifiers.
