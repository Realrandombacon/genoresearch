## Description
LOC100505942 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 16q22.1 (NC_000016.10:67528744-67517705) spanning approximately 11.0 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000259804 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - a high burden indicating significant clinical relevance. Variants include both copy number gains and losses affecting 16q: large duplications spanning 16q21-24.3 (chr16:65511483-90096995, ~24.6 Mb; chr16:64389378-90081985, ~25.7 Mb; chr16:65313395-90081985, ~24.8 Mb; chr16:62925929-84585795, ~21.7 Mb), 16q21-23.3 (chr16:65957829-83611443, ~17.7 Mb), 16q12.2-24.3 (chr16:52899183-90088654, ~37.2 Mb), 16q12.1-22.1 (chr16:49685521-68401712, ~18.7 Mb), and deletions spanning 16q22.1 (chr16:66694180-67865445, ~1.2 Mb), 16q21-22.1 (chr16:64311275-68062011, ~3.8 Mb; chr16:62179331-67770414, ~5.6 Mb). The 16q22.1 region is the critical locus for 16q deletion/duplication syndromes, containing the cadherin gene cluster including CDH1 (E-cadherin, hereditary diffuse gastric cancer, lobular breast cancer), CDH3 (P-cadherin, hypotrichosis-lymphedema-telangiectasia syndrome), WFDC1 (WAP four-disulfide core domain protein 1), and multiple other dosage-sensitive genes. LOC100505942 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling cadherin cluster expression, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 16q22.1 epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) gene transcription, (3) a chromatin boundary element within the 16q22.1 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 16q genomic stability. The high pathogenic CNV burden (12 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) CDH1 haploinsufficiency leading to E-cadherin loss and cancer predisposition, (2) 16q22.1 chromatin architecture affecting cadherin cluster expression, (3) epithelial cell adhesion and EMT programs, or (4) developmental processes, leading to 16q deletion syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, growth retardation, cardiac defects, gastric cancer risk) or 16q duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, neurological abnormalities).
