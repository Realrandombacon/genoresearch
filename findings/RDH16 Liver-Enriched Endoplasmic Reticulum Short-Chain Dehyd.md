## Description
RDH16 (Retinol Dehydrogenase 16, UniProt O75452) is a 317 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum metabolic enzyme with moderate characterization (41 papers) that functions as a short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase (SDR) catalyzing the NAD(P)-dependent interconversion of retinol and retinal in the visual cycle and hepatic retinoid metabolism. The protein contains a short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase SDR domain (IPR002347/PF00106/PR00080/PR00081/PS00061, residues 30-217/30-217/104-115/157-165/176-195/31-48/104-115/151-167/163-191) with conserved catalytic site (IPR020904, residues 163-191), belongs to the short-chain dehydrogenases/reductases family (IPR036291/PTHR43313/SSF51735/G3DSA:3.40.50.720, residues 29-307/4-312/29-307/29-295/29-307) with NAD(P)-binding Rossmann-fold domain, placing it in the essential retinoid metabolism machinery. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with retinoid and lipid metabolism enzymes: AOX1 (0.921, aldehyde oxidase 1), DGAT1 (0.914, diacylglycerol O-acyltransferase 1), CYP27C1 (0.907, cytochrome P450 27C1, retinol 3,4-desaturase), LRAT (0.640, lecithin retinol acyltransferase), CYP26A1 (0.560, cytochrome P450 26A1, retinoic acid hydroxylase), BCO1 (0.550, beta-carotene oxygenase 1), ALDH1A3 (0.505, aldehyde dehydrogenase 1A3), ALDH1A1 (0.494, aldehyde dehydrogenase 1A1), ALDH1A2 (0.466, aldehyde dehydrogenase 1A2), and RETSAT (0.422, retinol saturase), confirming membership in the complete retinoid metabolic pathway. HPA data shows tissue-enriched specificity with exceptional liver expression (812.5 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, cancer-enriched specificity, and classification as enzyme/metabolic protein/oxidoreductase involved in lipid metabolism and steroid metabolism. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including silent mutation (p.Thr119=), copy number losses at 12q13.3-14.1 (chr12:57064059-59314016), and copy number gains at 12q13.2-14.1/12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:55552371-62126304/173787-133777902/1-133851895/121271-133196807), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—however recent literature links RDH16 to hepatocellular carcinoma. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 96.4) indicates very high confidence with well-folded architecture typical of SDR enzymes containing canonical Rossmann-fold nucleotide binding domain.
