## Description
QRICH1 (Q2TAL8, 776 aa) is a nuclear glutamine-rich transcriptional regulator with emerging roles at the intersection of endoplasmic reticulum stress response and innate immunity. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT: 57.5) indicates a partially disordered structure typical of transcriptional co-regulators. HPA data confirms nucleoplasmic localization with ubiquitous expression across tissues. The gene shows significant clinical relevance with 148 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants including multiple nonsense mutations (p.Gln193Ter, p.Gln57Ter, p.Leu671Ter, p.Gln442Ter) and frameshifts, associated with intellectual disability. STRING interactions reveal connections to purine metabolism enzymes (GMPS: 0.855, GART: 0.516, ATIC: 0.452, ADSL: 0.611), the translational repressor GIGYF2 (0.665), RNA polymerase II subunit POLR2B (0.490), and paralog QRICH2 (0.571). Recent literature (2025) demonstrates QRICH1 mediates CD8+ T cell activation via CARD11 signalosome assembly, regulates ATF6 transcription during ER stress in cardiac hypertrophy, and promotes arsenic-induced pyroptosis through PRMT1-dependent arginine methylation of cGAS. High-confidence interaction with EPHX2 (0.859) suggests potential coupling to lipid signaling.
