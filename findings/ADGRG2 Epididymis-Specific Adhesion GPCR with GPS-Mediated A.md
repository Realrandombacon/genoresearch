# ADGRG2: Epididymis-Specific Adhesion GPCR with GPS-Mediated Autoproteolysis

## Description
ADGRG2 (GPR64/HE6) encodes a 1017 amino acid adhesion G protein-coupled receptor (UniProt Q8IZP9) characterized by hallmark adhesion GPCR features including a GPS motif (IPR000203, residues 566-618) mediating autoproteolytic cleavage and GAIN domain (IPR046338, residues 439-620) for structural stability. The 7-transmembrane domain (IPR000832, residues 626-882) positions it as a class B secretin-like GPCR signaling through G protein-coupled pathways. HPA demonstrates extreme epididymis enrichment (1159.4 nTPM) with tissue-enriched expression pattern, indicating epididymal epithelial cell-specific function in male reproductive tract. Critically, ClinVar documents 180 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains, losses, deletions, and inversions spanning Xp22.13, demonstrating dosage sensitivity and clinical relevance to male infertility (CBAVDX alias denotes congenital bilateral absence of vas deferens association). The GPS-mediated autoproteolysis mechanism suggests ADGRG2 functions as a mechanosensor or cell adhesion molecule where N-terminal cleavage exposes tethered agonist for G protein signaling activation. High expression in epididymis combined with disease variants indicates essential role in sperm maturation or epididymal epithelial integrity. Conservation across mammals (mouse 1009 aa, rat 1013 aa) indicates functional constraint.
