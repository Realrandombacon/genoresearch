## Description
LOC129388450 (Gene ID: 129388450) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 1p36.33 (10,353,996-10,354,196 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak69 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 1p36.33-35.1, 1p36.31-36.13, 1p36.22-36.21, 1p36.32-36.21, and 1p36.33-36.22 regions (deletions 0.8-18 Mb, duplications 0.01-34 Mb whole chromosome arm), associated with 1p36 deletion syndrome - the most common terminal deletion syndrome in humans featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, hypotonia, seizures, cardiomyopathy, craniofacial dysmorphism, and growth retardation. The 1p36 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including PRDM16 (PR/SET domain 16, cardiomyopathy, left ventricular non-compaction), SKI (Sloan-Kettering Institute proto-oncogene, Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome, craniosynostosis), GABRD (GABA-A receptor delta subunit, epilepsy, intellectual disability), MMP23B (matrix metallopeptidase 23B, immune function), and multiple neural development genes; (4) No HPA expression (non-coding). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129388450 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or neural contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in hematopoietic lineage. Pathogenic 1p36 CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and topologically associating domain (TAD) architecture, causing long-range dysregulation of PRDM16, SKI, GABRD, and other 1p36 genes leading to the characteristic 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotype. PRDM16 haploinsufficiency causes cardiomyopathy - a major feature of 1p36 deletion syndrome. SKI dysregulation contributes to craniofacial anomalies and skeletal defects. GABRD altered expression may contribute to seizures and intellectual disability. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions. The MPRA validation provides direct functional evidence of regulatory activity in erythroid lineage. 1p36 deletion syndrome affects ~1 in 5,000 live births - silencer disruption may contribute to phenotypic severity.
