## Description
LOC100130027 is an uncharacterized protein-coding gene at chromosome 8q24.3 (genomic coordinates 144827039-144808809, ~18kb span on reverse strand). This locus demonstrates strong clinical relevance through copy number variation sensitivity. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all affecting gene dosage through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements. These include duplications/copy number gains spanning 8q21.2-24.3 (chr8:85765999-145070385x3), 8p23.3-q24.3 (chr8:226452-145068712x3), 8q22.3-24.3 (chr8:103306336-145068712x3), 8q21.13-24.3 (chr8:78614077-145054634x3), and multiple other amplifications. The exclusive presence of copy number gains (no deletions) among pathogenic variants suggests that overexpression or increased gene dosage drives pathology. No UniProt accession exists (no protein characterization), STRING found no interaction partners, and HPA could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID. Critically, recent PubMed literature (PMID:40684981, Kim et al. 2025, Environmental Research) identifies LOC100130027 as a COPD-related gene in integrative RNA-seq analysis of particulate matter exposure, suggesting a functional role in lung pathophysiology. This represents a dark gene with emerging disease relevance — dosage sensitivity combined with COPD association implies potential involvement in inflammatory or stress-response pathways in respiratory epithelium.
