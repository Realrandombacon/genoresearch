## Description
MFSD12 (Major facilitator superfamily domain 12) is a 480 amino acid protein encoded by a "dark gene" with 25 pathogenic variants but zero explicitly tagged diseases in ClinVar. Despite low tissue specificity in HPA (cancer enriched), its function is tightly linked to pigment metabolism via interactions with key pigmentation genes (SLC45A2, SLC24A5, OCA2) and localization to melanosomes. Structurally, it is a well-folded (pLDDT 84.8) canonical Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) transporter (PF13347, IPR036259) capable of symport activity. The accumulation of copy number variants (gains/losses) suggests dosage sensitivity is critical for its function. We hypothesize that MFSD12 acts as a general substrate transporter within the melanosome complex, regulating the availability of cysteine or other substrates required for melanin biosynthesis; dysregulation leads to defective pigment synthesis (albinism/HPS) and potentially contributes to cancer progression through aberrant cellular transport.
