## Description
C17orf75 (alias NJMU-R1/SRI2) encodes a 396 amino acid well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 76.7) containing a Njmu-R1 family domain (IPR028280/PF15053, aa 38-390) with GO annotations for vesicle tethering to Golgi (GO:0099041) and trans-Golgi network localization (GO:0005802). HPA confirms Golgi apparatus and cytosolic localization with ubiquitous tissue expression. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic variants consisting of 17q11.2 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal deletion/duplication syndromes. STRING interactions reveal strong network with ESCRT (Endosomal Sorting Complex Required for Transport) machinery: VPS25 (ESCRT-III, 0.896), TBC1D23 (endosome-Golgi trafficking, 0.832), VPS4A (ESCRT disassembly ATPase, 0.787), SNF8/CHMP2A (ESCRT-III subunits, 0.753/0.681), VPS36 (ESCRT-II, 0.744), and WDR11 (Golgi trafficking, 0.647). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The Njmu-R1 domain architecture combined with ESCRT complex interactions and Golgi localization strongly suggests C17orf75 functions as an adapter protein bridging ESCRT-III machinery to Golgi membrane tethering complexes, facilitating retrograde vesicle fusion and cargo sorting at the trans-Golgi network. Pathogenic 17q11.2 CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting Golgi homeostasis, affecting protein glycosylation, lysosomal enzyme sorting, and cytokine receptor trafficking. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure indicates stable folding suitable for specific protein-protein interactions with ESCRT components.
