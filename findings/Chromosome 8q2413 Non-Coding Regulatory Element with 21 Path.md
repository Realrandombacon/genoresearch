## Description
LOC101927588 (Gene ID: 101927588) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 8q24.13 (NC_000008.11, positions 124192690-124247397, ~55 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID (ENSG00000214803), consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all 21 are duplications/gains (variant 2579176: 8q23.3-24.23 ~20.0 Mb; variant 155592: 8q21.2-24.3 ~59.3 Mb; variant 154791: 8p23.3-q24.3 ~144.8 Mb; variant 154743: 8q22.3-24.3 ~41.8 Mb; variant 154530: 8q21.13-24.3 ~66.4 Mb; variant 153737: 8p21.3-q24.3 ~123.8 Mb; variant 153413: 8p23.3-q24.3 ~144.9 Mb; variant 153231: 8q22.3-24.3 ~44.2 Mb; variant 151754: 8q22.1-24.3 ~47.7 Mb; variant 150723: 8q11.1-24.3 ~93.3 Mb; plus 11 additional gains). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 8q24 chromosomal region is the most extensively studied cancer susceptibility locus in human genetics, containing critical oncogenes including MYC (MYC proto-oncogene bHLH transcription factor, cell cycle progression, apoptosis evasion, Burkitt lymph t(8;14) translocation), PVT1 (PVT1 oncogene long non-coding RNA, MYC stabilization, chemoresistance, amplification in breast/ovarian/gastric cancer), and CCAT2 (colon cancer associated transcript 2, long non-coding RNA, chromatin looping, MYC enhancer activity, microsatellite instability). Functional hypothesis: LOC101927588 likely represents a critical MYC distal enhancer element within the 8q24 gene desert, potentially functioning as a tissue-specific super-enhancer coordinating MYC-PVT1-CCAT2 expression in epithelial cells (chromatin looping via cohesin/CTCF, enhancer-promoter contact formation, transcriptional bursting) or as a topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary separating MYC from neighboring genes. The 21 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications, 20.0-144.9 Mb range) indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number drives MYC overexpression and oncogenic transformation. Disease mechanism involves MYC amplification causing Burkitt lymphoma (t(8;14) IGH-MYC translocation, constitutive MYC expression, B-cell proliferation), MYC overexpression promoting solid tumors (breast cancer, colorectal cancer, prostate cancer, neuroblastoma), PVT1 amplification stabilizing MYC protein (chemoresistance, tumor progression), CCAT2 overexpression enhancing chromatin looping (microsatellite instability, colorectal cancer progression), and 8q24 amplification syndrome presenting with multiple cancer predisposition. The 8q24 region contains >100 independent GWAS signals for prostate, breast, colorectal, bladder, thyroid, and basal cell carcinoma, making it the most pleiotropic cancer susceptibility locus in the human genome. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (21 duplications 20.0-144.9 Mb) and MYC-PVT1-CCAT2 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a MYC distal super-enhancer critical for cell cycle progression-apoptosis evasion-oncogenic transcription.
