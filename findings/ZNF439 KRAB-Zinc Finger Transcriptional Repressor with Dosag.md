## Description
ZNF439 encodes a 499 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 19p13.2. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical domain architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 18-101) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 242-376). The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex to silence target genes via histone H3 lysine 9 methylation. STRING interactions confirm functional partnerships: TRIM28 (score 0.522) as the canonical KRAB cofactor, GMNN (0.619) for cell cycle regulation, and ZNF559 (0.458) suggesting heterodimerization with related zinc fingers. HPA data shows nucleoplasmic localization with broad expression across tissues (low tissue specificity, detected in many) and immune cell enhancement, consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic copy number variants including deletions (19p13.2-13.13) and duplications (19p13.2-q13.31), indicating dosage sensitivity where gene copy number alterations cause disease phenotypes. The combination of KRAB repression domain, multiple zinc fingers for DNA recognition, TRIM28 interaction, and clinical CNVs supports a model where ZNF439 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor regulating developmentally important genes, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupting target gene silencing programs.
