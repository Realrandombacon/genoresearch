## Description
CLDN12 encodes a 244 aa claudin family member (UniProt P56749) with a confident 3D structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 81.1) containing the Claudin-12 domain (IPR013287) and conserved sites (IPR017974). Functionally, it acts as a core component of tight junction strands, evidenced by high-confidence interactions (0.988) with CLDN3 and (0.906) with TJP1, forming a physical barrier. It is localized to the membrane with low tissue specificity but is upregulated in epithelial cancers. Clinically, CLDN12 is a 'WELL-STUDIED' gene with 12 pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs) and is implicated in Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma (promoting EMT via LINC00857), Osteosarcoma (proliferation/migration), and Breast Cancer (chemotherapy response). It drives cancer progression by disrupting barrier function and facilitating cell migration.
