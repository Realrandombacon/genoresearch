## Description
MROH8 (Maestro heat-like repeat-containing protein family member 8, UniProt Q9H579) is a 483-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 20q11.21. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture dominated by HEAT repeats: an N-terminal MROH2B-like HEAT-repeat domain (IPR055408/PF23210, aa 76-230), a central Maestro-like HEAT-repeats domain (IPR048465/PF21047, aa 237-455), and an Armadillo-type fold (IPR016024/SSF48371, aa 256-438) characteristic of proteins mediating intracellular transport and protein-protein interactions. The MROH family signature (IPR045206, aa 245-471) defines this as a bona fide member of the maestro-related heat domain-containing protein family. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 73.9), indicating a well-folded stable structure with defined HEAT repeat helices forming a curved solenoid architecture typical of scaffold proteins that bind multiple partners. STRING interaction network reveals 10 diverse partners spanning multiple functional categories: OR9G1 (0.625, olfactory receptor), NUTM2G (0.507, chromatin regulator), KRTAP9-1 (0.505, keratin-associated protein), BBOF1 (0.505, male fertility factor), UBE3B (0.494, ubiquitin ligase), NFS1 (0.449, cysteine desulfurase for iron-sulfur cluster assembly), C3AR1 (0.448, complement receptor), MCRIP1 (0.418, MAPK signaling regulator), SHANK3 (0.417, postsynaptic scaffold), and POLR3F (0.411, RNA polymerase III subunit). This diverse interactome suggests MROH8 functions as a multifunctional scaffold coordinating processes from transcription to cytoskeletal organization. Human Protein Atlas has no expression data, suggesting tissue-restricted or low-abundance expression. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants including copy-number losses and gains across 20p-q, frameshift mutations (c.123_151dup p.His51fs), and missense variants (c.1364A>G p.Gln455Arg), demonstrating dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and overexpression disrupt cellular homeostasis. Conservation across mammals (mouse Mroh7, rat Mroh7, degus Mroh8) indicates ancient evolutionary origin. MROH8 likely functions as a HEAT repeat scaffold protein facilitating nucleocytoplasmic transport, chromatin remodeling, or signal transduction complex assembly through its curved solenoid structure providing multiple binding interfaces.
