## Description
ZNF207 (UniProt O43670) is a 478 amino acid BUB3-interacting and GLEBS motif-containing protein with dual roles in RNA splicing and mitotic checkpoint control. The protein contains two canonical C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087/PF00096) at positions 11-34 and 35-58 that mediate DNA binding, an N-terminal SUF4-like domain (cd20908, positions 10-90) resembling transcription factor domains from Arabidopsis SUPPRESSOR OF FRI 4, and belongs to the BUB3-Interacting GLEBS Motif-Containing family (PTHR23215, positions 1-385). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (global pLDDT 59.9), indicating a mix of ordered zinc finger domains and intrinsically disordered regions typical of proteins involved in dynamic protein-protein interactions. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to the nucleoplasm consistent with roles in both transcription/splicing and cell cycle regulation, classified functionally in cell cycle, cell division, chromosome partition, and mitosis pathways. STRING interaction network reveals exceptionally strong associations with splicing regulators: SRSF3 (score 0.741), a serine/arginine-rich splicing factor; HNRNPUL1 (0.717), heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein U-like protein 1; RNPS1 (0.684), RNA-binding protein with serine-rich domain 1 involved in exon junction complex formation; SRSF10 (0.681) and SRSF11 (0.618), additional SR splicing factors; plus FUBP1 (0.506), far upstream element-binding protein; critically BUB3 (0.467), the mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint protein that directly binds the GLEBS motif; and spliceosome components SF3A3 (0.437), SF3A2 (0.432), and HNRNPM (0.436). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.921+5G>A, c.828+5G>T, c.670+2T>C), a nonsense mutation (p.Gln56Ter within the zinc finger region), deletions, and large copy number gains/losses spanning 17q11.2 (chr17:28277040-31246638), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though no specific monogenic disease is yet attributed solely to ZNF207. The convergence of GLEBS motif architecture, BUB3 interaction, strong splicing factor associations, nucleoplasmic localization, and cell cycle annotations supports a mechanistic model where ZNF207 functions as a molecular bridge coordinating spliceosome assembly with mitotic progression, potentially ensuring proper mRNA processing of cell cycle regulators before checkpoint satisfaction.
