## Description
PCGF1 encodes polycomb group ring finger protein 1 (PCGF1/NSPC1/RNF68), a 259 aa nuclear protein (UniProt Q9BSM1) belonging to the polycomb group protein family that functions as the defining specificity subunit of non-canonical PRC1.1 (Polycomb Repressive Complex 1.1) critical for epigenetic transcriptional silencing through histone H2A lysine 119 monoubiquitination (H2AK119ub1) regulating anterior-posterior patterning, stem cell maintenance, and developmental gene expression programs.
