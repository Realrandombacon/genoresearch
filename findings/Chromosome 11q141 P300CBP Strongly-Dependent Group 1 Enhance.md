# Chromosome 11q14.1 P300/CBP Strongly-Dependent Group 1 Enhancer with 4 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants in GAB2-RSF1-DLG2-MIR708 Scaffold-Adaptor-Chromatin Locus

## Description
LOC126861299 (Gene ID: 126861299) is a validated P300/CBP strongly-dependent group 1 enhancer element on chromosome 11q14.1 (NC_000011.10, positions 94583889-94585088, ~1.2 kb region) with functional characterization as an active enhancer in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells despite absence of protein-coding potential. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction, consistent with non-coding regulatory element classification. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with cancer cell line-specific enhancer expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding DNA regulatory function. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all are copy number losses/deletions (variant 148976: 11q14.3-22.3 ~18.5 Mb monosomy; variant 144356: 11q13.5-22.1 ~22.4 Mb monosomy; variant 58919: 11q14.1-22.2 ~17.7 Mb monosomy; variant 58918: 11q14.1-22.3 ~28.5 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 11q14.1 chromosomal region contains critical scaffold protein, adaptor signaling, chromatin remodeling, and microRNA-associated genes including GAB2 (GRB2 associated binding protein 2, adaptor scaffolding protein, PI3K-AKT signaling, RAS-MAPK pathway, cytokine receptor signaling, hematologic malignancy, Alzheimer disease susceptibility), RSF1 (remodeling and spacing factor 1, SNF2H chromatin remodeler interaction, nucleosome spacing, transcriptional regulation, ovarian cancer overexpression, chemoresistance), DLG2 (discs large MAGUK scaffold protein 2, PSD-95 family, synaptic scaffolding, NMDA receptor clustering, neuronal signaling, intellectual disability), MIR708 (microRNA 708, tumor suppressor miRNA, EZH2 targeting, chromatin modification repression, cancer metastasis inhibition), and CLNS1A (chloride nucleotide-sensitive channel 1A, nucleotide-sensitive chloride channel, cell volume regulation, apoptosis modulation). Functional hypothesis: LOC126861299 likely represents a critical 11q14.1 colorectal carcinoma-specific enhancer element within the GAB2-RSF1-DLG2 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a P300/CBP-dependent enhancer coordinating GAB2-RSF1-DLG2-MIR708 expression in colorectal cancer cells (HCT116, DLD1, HT29) or as a chromatin boundary separating adaptor scaffolding genes (GAB2) from chromatin remodeling genes (RSF1) and neuronal scaffolding genes (DLG2). The STARR-seq MPRA validation confirms enhancer activity with BRD2-BRD4-P300/CBP-MED14-CDK7-dependent mechanism and strong P300/CBP dependence (group 1 classification). The 4 pathogenic CNVs (all deletions 17.7-28.5 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where reduced copy number disrupts GAB2-mediated PI3K-AKT signaling and RSF1-mediated chromatin remodeling. Disease mechanism involves GAB2 overexpression causing hematologic malignancy (PI3K-AKT hyperactivation, cell proliferation, survival, poor prognosis), GAB2 mutation causing Alzheimer disease susceptibility (amyloid-beta signaling dysregulation, tau pathology, neurodegeneration), RSF1 overexpression causing ovarian cancer (chromatin remodeling dysregulation, transcriptional activation, chemoresistance, platinum resistance), RSF1 dysfunction causing nucleosome spacing defect (transcriptional silencing, genome instability), DLG2 mutation causing intellectual disability (synaptic scaffolding defect, NMDA receptor mislocalization, cognitive impairment), DLG2 dysfunction causing neuronal signaling defect (synaptic transmission impairment, neurodevelopmental disorder), MIR708 downregulation causing cancer metastasis (EZH2 derepression, chromatin modification, EMT activation, invasion, poor prognosis), MIR708 overexpression causing tumor suppression (EZH2 repression, chromatin silencing, metastasis inhibition, chemosensitivity), CLNS1A dysfunction causing cell volume regulation defect (apoptosis dysregulation, osmotic stress sensitivity), and 11q14.1 deletion syndrome presenting with neurodevelopmental abnormalities and cancer predisposition. The 11q14.1 region is associated with scaffold-adaptor-chromatin genetic variation. The P300/CBP strongly-dependent group 1 enhancer classification combined with STARR-seq MPRA validation (HCT116 cells), ClinVar evidence (4 deletions 17.7-28.5 Mb), and GAB2-RSF1-DLG2-MIR708-CLNS1A neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a colorectal carcinoma-specific enhancer critical for adaptor scaffolding-chromatin remodeling-neuronal scaffolding coordination.
