## Description
LOC127459783 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 8q (genomic coordinates: 73984959-73985541 on NC_000008.11, spanning approximately 582 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in primed human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with two master pluripotency transcription factors: OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU domain class 5 homeobox 1) and NANOG (NK2 homeobox protein). The OCT4-NANOG enhancer signature indicates this element functions in the core pluripotency regulatory network maintaining primed pluripotency (post-implantation epiblast-like state). A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. The 8q chromosomal region contains critical developmental and oncogenes. As a primed hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4 and NANOG, LOC127459783 likely functions to maintain transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and pluripotency maintenance, potentially regulating genes involved in self-renewal and lineage priming.
