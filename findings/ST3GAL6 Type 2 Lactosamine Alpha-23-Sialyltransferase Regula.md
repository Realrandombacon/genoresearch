## Description
ST3GAL6 encodes a 331-amino acid type 2 lactosamine alpha-2,3-sialyltransferase (UniProt Q9Y274), a very well-folded glycosyltransferase functioning in protein sialylation and cancer progression. The protein contains a glycosyltransferase family 29 domain (IPR001675, positions 68-323; PF00777, positions 68-323) with sialyltransferase activity (GO:0008373) and glycoprotein biosynthetic process function (GO:0009101), classified within the sialyltransferase family (IPR012163, positions 35-330; PIRSF005557, positions 35-330; PTHR13713, positions 3-330) with GT29-like superfamily fold (G3DSA:3.90.1480.20, positions 87-329; IPR038578, positions 87-329; IPR051142, positions 3-330). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 91.6, residues 1-331) indicating stable folding consistent with glycosyltransferase catalytic architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals ST3GAL6 forms part of the glycosylation enzyme network with high-confidence partners: ST8SIA1 (0.952, alpha-N-acetylneuraminide alpha-2,8-sialyltransferase 1), B4GALT4 (0.948, beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase 4), B3GNT3 (0.945, beta-1,3-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase 3), GCNT2 (0.927, glucosaminyl (N-acetyl) transferase 2), FUT3 (0.919, fucosyltransferase 3), B4GALT1 (0.912, beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase 1), FUT9 (0.911, fucosyltransferase 9), FUT6 (0.910, fucosyltransferase 6), FUT1 (0.702, fucosyltransferase 1), and FUT7 (0.433, fucosyltransferase 7), confirming its role in coordinated glycan biosynthesis. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with liver enrichment (39.3 nTPM, highest), heart muscle expression (21.4 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, immune cell enhanced specificity, annotated for glycosyltransferase and transferase molecular functions. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including deletion (c.907_909+35del) and copy number losses (3q11.1-21.2, 3q11.2-12.3, 3q11.2-13.31, 3q11.1-13.31) and gains (3p13-q12.1, 3p26.3-q29, 3q11.1-13.11, 3q11.1-24), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 110 publications, ST3GAL6 has emerging cancer biology roles: ST3GAL6-AS1/ST3GAL6 axis regulates EGFR signaling in lung adenocarcinoma cell invasion (2022, 18 cit), circular RNA ST3GAL6 blocks gastric cancer malignant behaviors through autophagy regulated by FOXP2/MET/mTOR axis (2022, 31 cit), ST3GAL6 silencing reduces alpha-2,3-sialylated glycans regulating autophagy by decreasing HSPB8-BAG3 in hepatic encephalopathy (2024, 1 cit), ST3GAL6 roles in neural invasion (2023/2024). The functional hypothesis: ST3GAL6 catalyzes alpha-2,3-sialylation of type 2 lactosamine structures on glycoproteins and glycolipids, modifying cell surface receptors to regulate signaling pathways; liver enrichment suggests roles in hepatic glycoprotein synthesis and secretion; cancer-enhanced expression indicates oncogenic function through receptor sialylation modulating growth factor signaling; EGFR regulation in lung adenocarcinoma suggests ST3GAL6-mediated sialylation stabilizes EGFR or enhances ligand binding, promoting invasion and metastasis; circular RNA ST3GAL6 sponge mechanism in gastric cancer indicates post-transcriptional regulation of ST3GAL6 expression affecting MET/mTOR autophagy pathway; HSPB8-BAG3 autophagy regulation in hepatic encephalopathy suggests ST3GAL6 sialylation affects chaperone-assisted selective autophagy (CASA) complex function; coordinated glycosylation network with fucosyltransferases (FUT1/3/6/7/9), galactosyltransferases (B4GALT1/4), and other sialyltransferases (ST8SIA1) indicates ST3GAL6 functions in sequential glycan assembly requiring precise enzyme coordination; 3q CNVs may alter ST3GAL6 dosage causing glycosylation defects affecting cell adhesion, immune recognition, and receptor signaling; neural invasion roles suggest ST3GAL6-mediated sialylation of neural cell adhesion molecules (NCAM) or other neural receptors facilitating cancer cell invasion along nerve pathways.
