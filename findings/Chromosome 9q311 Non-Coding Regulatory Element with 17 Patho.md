## Description
LOC105376177 (Gene ID: 105376177, Ensembl: ENSG00000298775) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 9q31.1 (NC_000009.12, positions 100353049-100394197, ~41 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID (ENSG00000298775) but no quantitative expression data (nTPM unavailable). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 8 copy number gains/duplications (variant 160862: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 155409: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 153518: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 151006: 9q21.12-31.3 ~42 Mb; variant 150340: 9p11.2-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 150050: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 149828: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 147624: 9q21.11-31.2 ~38 Mb) and 3 copy number losses/deletions (variant 161058: 9q22.33-31.3 ~16 Mb; variant 149230: 9q22.33-31.3 ~11 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 9q31 chromosomal region contains critical pluripotency, DNA repair, and transcriptional regulation-associated genes including KLF4 (Krüppel-like factor 4, C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor, Yamanaka reprogramming factor, stem cell pluripotency, epithelial differentiation, tumor suppressor/oncogene context-dependent), RAD23B (RAD23 nucleotide excision repair protein B, ubiquitin-like domain, nucleotide excision repair, proteasome interaction, DNA damage recognition), VLDLR (very low density lipoprotein receptor, LDLR family, lipid metabolism, reelin signaling, neuronal migration), LMX1B (LIM homeodomain transcription factor 1B, dorsal limb patterning, nail-patella syndrome, podocyte differentiation), and TJP1 (tight junction protein 1/zo-1, cell-cell junction scaffolding, epithelial barrier function). Functional hypothesis: LOC105376177 likely represents a critical 9q31.1 enhancer element within the KLF4-RAD23B regulatory region, potentially functioning as a stem cell-specific enhancer coordinating KLF4-RAD23B-VLDLR expression in pluripotent tissues (embryonic stem cells, induced pluripotent stem cells, epithelial progenitors) or as a chromatin boundary separating pluripotency transcription factor genes (KLF4) from DNA repair genes (RAD23B) and lipid metabolism genes (VLDLR). The 17 pathogenic CNVs (mix of duplications and deletions, 11-138 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts KLF4-mediated transcriptional reprogramming and RAD23B-mediated nucleotide excision repair. Disease mechanism involves KLF4 dysregulation causing cancer (context-dependent tumor suppressor or oncogene, squamous cell carcinoma, colorectal cancer, stemness maintenance), KLF4 mutation causing developmental disorders (epithelial barrier defects, differentiation impairment), RAD23B deficiency causing DNA repair deficiency (nucleotide excision repair impairment, UV sensitivity, cancer predisposition, xeroderma pigmentosum-like phenotype), VLDLR mutation causing dysequilibrium syndrome (cerebellar hypoplasia, intellectual disability, reelin signaling defect), LMX1B mutation causing nail-patella syndrome (dorsal limb patterning defect, nephropathy, glaucoma), and 9q31 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with developmental abnormalities and cancer predisposition. The 9q31 region is associated with transcriptional regulation genetic variation (KLF4 polymorphisms) and DNA repair capacity. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (17 CNVs 11-138 Mb) and KLF4-RAD23B-VLDLR-LMX1B-TJP1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as an epithelial/stem cell-specific enhancer critical for pluripotency-DNA repair-lipid metabolism coordination.
