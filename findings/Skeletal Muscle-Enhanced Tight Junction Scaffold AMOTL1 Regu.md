## Description
AMOTL1 encodes angiomotin-like protein 1 (AMOTL1/CFCHS/JEAP), a 956 aa peripheral membrane protein (UniProt Q8IY63) belonging to the angiomotin family that functions as a critical component of tight junctions (TJs) and apical junctional complexes, regulating cell polarity, paracellular permeability, endothelial cell migration, and capillary formation through scaffolding interactions that sequester transcriptional co-activators and modulate Hippo-YAP and Wnt signaling pathways.
