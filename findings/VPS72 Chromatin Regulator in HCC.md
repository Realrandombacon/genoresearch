# VPS72: Chromatin Regulator in HCC

## Description
VPS72 (Vacuolar Protein Sorting 72) is a well-studied nuclear protein acting as a shared subunit of the TRRAP/TIP60 and SRCAP complexes, functioning as a chromatin regulator and DNA-binding protein localized to nuclear speckles. Structurally, it contains YL1 nuclear protein domains (N-terminal and C-terminal). While ubiquitously expressed with low tissue specificity, VPS72 plays a critical role in transcriptional regulation via H2AZ incorporation. Recent research identifies VPS72 as a key driver in Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC), where copy number gains (CNV) rather than overexpression lead to de novo lipogenesis and tumor progression via the ATF3/mTORC1/SREBP1 axis. Its role in regulating T-cell adaptation and hematopoietic stem cell development further underscores its importance in immune and developmental biology. Despite being well-characterized, the specific mechanism linking its chromatin remodeling activity to lipid metabolism in HCC remains an active area of investigation.
