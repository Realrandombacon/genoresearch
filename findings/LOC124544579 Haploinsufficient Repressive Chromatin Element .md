## Description
LOC124544579 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 15181 located on chromosome 11q (genomic coordinates: 92123582-92123909 on NC_000011.10). This 327 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element using Sharpr-MPRA (Systematic high-resolution activation and repression profiling with reporter tiling using massively parallel reporter assays) in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA expression). ClinVar analysis identified 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all are copy number losses spanning overlapping regions of 11q14.1-22.3 (78232836-106779420, 85242847-102920097, 75941754-98357960, and 91086659-109595582). Notably, all variants are losses with no gains observed, indicating haploinsufficiency rather than bidirectional dosage sensitivity—this regulatory element must be present for normal cellular function, and deletion causes disease. As a validated repressive element, LOC124544579 likely functions as a transcriptional silencer that regulates expression of target genes in cis on chromosome 11q. The 11q14 region contains genes involved in cell cycle regulation, DNA repair, and tumor suppression (including ATM nearby at 11q22.3). Loss of this repressive element may lead to aberrant activation of oncogenes or dysregulation of tumor suppressor pathways, potentially contributing to cancer predisposition or developmental disorders.
