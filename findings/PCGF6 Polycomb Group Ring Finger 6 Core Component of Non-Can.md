## Description
PCGF6 encodes a 350 aa nuclear transcriptional repressor (UniProt Q9BYE7) that functions as a core scaffold component of non-canonical Polycomb Repressive Complex 1.6 (PRC1.6), integrating RING-type zinc finger-mediated E3 ubiquitin ligase activity with RAWUL domain-dependent protein-protein interactions to coordinate epigenetic silencing of developmental genes, cell cycle regulators, and germline-specific loci. This genuine dark gene has substantial clinical significance with 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants though specific monogenic diseases await characterization beyond chromosomal instability syndromes involving 10q copy number alterations. The protein features canonical polycomb group architecture enabling coordinated chromatin modification and complex assembly: N-terminal RING-type zinc finger domain (IPR001841/IPR013083/IPR017907/G3DSA:3.30.40.10/cd16737, residues 118-221/125-215/134-173) containing the conserved RING signature (residues 150-159) mediating E3 ubiquitin ligase activity through RING1/RNF2 heterodimerization catalyzing histone H2A lysine 119 monoubiquitination (H2AK119ub1); C-terminal RAWUL domain (IPR032443/IPR046979/IPR029071/G3DSA:3.10.20.90, residues 252-341/275-322/265-340) providing ubiquitin-like fold for stable protein-protein interactions within PRC1.6 complex; classified within polycomb group RING finger family (IPR051507, residues 119-345) with GO terms for transcription repressor, transcription regulation, chromatin modification functioning as epigenetic silencer. STRING interactions reveal PCGF6 operates within extremely high-confidence PRC1.6/E2F6 repressor networks: RING1 (0.999), RING finger protein 1 catalytic core of PRC1; RNF2 (0.995), RING finger protein 2/BMI1 ring partner forming E3 ligase heterodimer; E2F6 (0.997), E2F transcription factor 6 recruiting PRC1.6 to E2F target genes; RYBP (0.996), RING1 and YY1-binding protein enhancing H2A ubiquitination; BMI1 (0.988), B lymphoma Mo-MLV insertion region 1 homolog enabling PRC1 complex stability; YAF2 (0.915), YY1-associated factor 2 bridging PRC1.6 to DNA-binding factors; CBX3 (0.735)/CBX8 (0.541)/CBX7 (0.427), chromobox homologs linking to PRC1 canonical complexes; L3MBTL2 (0.467), L(3)mbt-like protein 2 additional repressor component. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced pattern consistent with housekeeping epigenetic regulator function, nuclear localization consistent with chromatin binding, classified as predicted intracellular protein involved in transcription/transcription regulation functioning as repressor. ClinVar lists 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants dominated by large-scale copy number gains (10q24.1-26.3 chr10:98097365-130931432 x3, 10q23.1-25.1 chr10:87456174-107789979 x3, 10q23.33-26.3 chr10:95078198-135427143 x3, 10p14-q26.3 chr10:11138692-135427143 x3, 10q23.32-26.3 chr10:93283493-135427143 x3) and losses (10p13-q26.3 chr10:12829206-135427143 del, 10q24.32-25.3 chr10:104030479-115410590 del, 10q24.32-24.33 chr10:103891057-105339973 x1), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity with both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity potentially causing developmental defects, cancer predisposition, or neurodevelopmental disorders. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 72.0), revealing a well-folded bi-domain protein with structured RING zinc finger connected to ordered RAWUL domain suitable for stable complex assembly. Mechanistically, PCGF6 operates through integrated modes: (1) PRC1.6 complex nucleation—ultra-high confidence RING1/RNF2/E2F6/RYBP interactions position PCGF6 as essential scaffold recruiting PRC1.6 to specific genomic loci; (2) H2A ubiquitination—RING domain enables E3 ligase activity depositing H2AK119ub1 repressive mark blocking transcription initiation; (3) E2F target repression—E2F6 interaction directs PRC1.6 to cell cycle gene promoters (cyclins, CDKs) maintaining proliferation control; (4) Germline gene silencing—PRC1.6 specifically represses germline-specific genes in somatic cells preventing inappropriate expression; (5) Developmental regulation—coordinates with other PcG proteins maintaining cell identity through epigenetic memory; (6) Cancer suppression—dysregulation linked to aberrant cell cycle activation and oncogene expression; (7) Dosage sensitivity—CNV gains/losses disrupt stoichiometric PRC1.6 assembly causing epigenetic dysregulation. Loss-of-function causes defective PRC1.6 assembly, reduced H2AK119ub1, derepression of E2F targets and germline genes, cell cycle dysregulation, and potential developmental disorders or cancer predisposition.
