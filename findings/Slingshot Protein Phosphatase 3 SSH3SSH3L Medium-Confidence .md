## Description
SSH3 (UniProt Q8TE77), also known as SSH3L, encodes a 659 aa dual-specificity protein phosphatase functioning as a key regulator of actin cytoskeleton dynamics by reactivating ADF/cofilin proteins through dephosphorylation, thereby controlling cell migration, cancer metastasis, and developmental processes via integrated phosphatase catalytic activity and actin-binding mechanisms. InterPro analysis reveals multi-domain slingshot phosphatase architecture: N-terminal SSH domain (cd11652/IPR043587, residues 3-259/1-509) mediating actin binding (GO:0003779) and substrate recognition; central DEK C-terminal domain (IPR014876, residues 269-324) potentially involved in protein-protein interactions; C-terminal dual-specificity phosphatase catalytic core (IPR000340/IPR020422/cd14571/G3DSA:3.90.190.10, residues 326-469/328-469/336-464/321-478) containing tyrosine-specific phosphatase domain (IPR000387, residues 393-448) and conserved active site motif (IPR016130, residues 411-421) catalyzing dephosphorylation (GO:0016311) and protein dephosphorylation (GO:0006470); and full-length slingshot phosphatase family membership (IPR043587) defining phosphatase activity (GO:0016791) and negative regulation of biological processes.
