## Description
LOC100192426 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 18p11.23 (NC_000018.10:8367033-8360819) spanning approximately 6.2 kb. Comprehensive database analysis reveals no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. LOC100192426 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 18p11.23 gene expression in neural progenitor cells, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in neurodevelopment, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 18p11.23 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 18p11.23 microdeletion/duplication susceptibility. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (44 variants) with both gains and losses suggests: (1) this locus is highly dosage-sensitive for 18p region gene regulation, (2) copy number alterations disrupt 18p chromatin architecture leading to 18p deletion syndrome phenotypes (intellectual disability, growth delay, hypotonia, dysmorphic features, ptosis, ear anomalies), (3) gains/losses may alter neuronal development and TGF-beta signaling contributing to 18p syndrome phenotype, or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the 18p11.23 region with potent neurodevelopmental regulatory effects. 18p deletion syndrome is one of the most common chromosomal deletion disorders, occurring in ~1/50,000 births.
