## Description
EPPK1 (Epiplakin 1, also known as EPIPL, EPIPL1) is a giant 5088 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt P58107) belonging to the plakin family of cytoskeletal linker proteins. The protein contains multiple plakin repeat domains (IPR001101/PF00681/SM00250/G3DSA:3.90.1290.10/IPR035915/SSF75399, residues 34-233, 234-470, 547-801 and additional repeats throughout) that mediate protein-protein interactions with intermediate filament components, belongs to the enoplakin family (IPR043197/PTHR23169, residues 432-1136) involved in intermediate filament cytoskeleton organization (GO:0045104), and functions to maintain the integrity of keratin intermediate filament networks in epithelial cells. EPPK1 is located on chromosome 8q24.3 and is classified as a plasma protein and predicted intracellular protein with subcellular localization to intermediate filaments.
