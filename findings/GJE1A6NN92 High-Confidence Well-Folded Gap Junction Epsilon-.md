## Description
GJE1 (Gap junction epsilon 1, also known as CX23, Connexin-23) is a 205 amino acid plasma membrane protein (UniProt A6NN92) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 84.1), characteristic of connexin family gap junction channel proteins that form intercellular communication channels. The protein contains a connexin family domain (IPR000500/PF00029/PTHR11989, residues 1-104/5-104) that enables gap junction channel activity (GO:0005922) and cell communication (GO:0007154), an N-terminal connexin domain (IPR013092/PF00029, residues 5-104 and 113-195) involved in channel gating, a cysteine-rich connexin domain (IPR019570/SM01089/G3DSA:1.20.1440.80, residues 126-194/1-201) that mediates extracellular docking between hemichannels, and belongs to the connexin superfamily (IPR038359, residues 1-201).
