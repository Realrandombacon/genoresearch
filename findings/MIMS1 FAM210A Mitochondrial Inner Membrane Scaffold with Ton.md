## Description
MIMS1 (formerly FAM210A) encodes a 272 amino acid uncharacterized protein (UniProt Q96ND0) localized to mitochondria, nucleoplasm, and Golgi apparatus. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved FAM210A/B-like domain (IPR009688/PF06916, residues 124-210) and PANTHER family match (PTHR21377, residues 94-227), alongside a CATH-Gene3D homologous superfamily (G3DSA:6.10.140.1430, residues 227-271), indicating evolutionary constraint as a mitochondrial membrane component. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 64.6), characteristic of partially structured membrane proteins with flexible termini typical of inner membrane scaffolds. HPA data reveals tongue-enhanced expression (30.9 nTPM) with tissue distribution detected in all tissues, low cancer and immune cell specificity, and dual classification as predicted intracellular and membrane protein, suggesting roles in mitochondrial metabolism and structural integrity across epithelial tissues. STRING interactions identify 10 partners (0.404-0.594 scores): CD300C (immune receptor, 0.594), ZBTB40 (zinc finger transcription factor, 0.592), ATAD3A (mitochondrial inner membrane ATPase, 0.585), HIBADH (3-hydroxyisobutyrate dehydrogenase, 0.573), GFM1 (mitochondrial elongation factor, 0.546), DCDC1 (doublecortin domain protein, 0.527), SLC25A13 (mitochondrial aspartate/glutamate carrier, 0.521), CPED1 (caudal-type homeobox protein, 0.507), SPTBN1 (spectrin cytoskeletal protein, 0.493), and STARD3NL (steroidogenic acute regulatory protein-like, 0.404). This network strongly supports a mitochondrial role, linking MIMS1 to inner membrane organization (ATAD3A), metabolite transport (SLC25A13, HIBADH), and protein synthesis (GFM1). ClinVar contains 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses/gains spanning 18p11.32-11.21 region, indicating strong dosage sensitivity. The 18p11.21 chromosomal location overlaps with 18p deletion syndrome (Feierman syndrome), associated with intellectual disability and growth delay. Mechanistic hypothesis: MIMS1 acts as a mitochondrial inner membrane scaffold that stabilizes the cristae junction complex by bridging metabolic enzymes (HIBADH), transporters (SLC25A13), and the mitochondrial translation machinery (GFM1) via interaction with the AAA-ATPase ATAD3A, thereby coordinating oxidative phosphorylation efficiency with mitochondrial genome maintenance; haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts cristae architecture, leading to bioenergetic failure in neural and epithelial tissues, explaining the 18p11 deletion syndrome phenotype.
