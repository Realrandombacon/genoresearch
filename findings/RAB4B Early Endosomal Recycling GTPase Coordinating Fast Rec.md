## Description
RAB4B (Ras-Related Protein Rab-4B, P61018) encodes a 213 aa small GTPase functioning as a master regulator of early endosomal fast recycling pathway that returns internalized cargo proteins from early endosomes to plasma membrane while also controlling retrograde trafficking to trans-Golgi network and facilitating viral/bacterial pathogen entry. Structural architecture reveals canonical Ras-like GTPase fold (IPR001806/IPR005225/PF00071/PS51419/cd04113/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 1-207/10-170/4-213/9-169) with P-loop nucleotide binding motif (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/IPR027417, residues 1-187) for GTP/GDP cycling, switch I/II regions for effector binding, and C-terminal hypervariable region for membrane anchoring via geranylgeranylation; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 88.4) confirms high confidence well-folded structure consistent with stable GTPase activity and membrane association. STRING interactions define endosomal trafficking network: GDP dissociation inhibitor GDI1 (0.701) regulating cytosolic sequestration and membrane extraction, paralog RAB4A (0.630) for redundant fast recycling function, Rab geranylgeranyltransferase subunit RABGGTB (0.609)/CHM (0.514) for prenylation, receptor tyrosine kinase MET (0.572) as recycling cargo, ARF6 (0.562) coordinating parallel recycling pathway, intersectin ITSN2 (0.540) linking to clathrin-mediated endocytosis, Rab acceptor RABAC1 (0.488), late endosomal RAB7A (0.429) for sequential trafficking, and RIN3 (0.416) as RAB4-specific effector. HPA localizes RAB4B as hydrolase/enzyme involved in protein transport with tissue-enhanced expression in brain (108.8 nTPM), detected in all tissues, group-enriched cell type specificity reflecting neuronal trafficking demands. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic variants including extensive 19q11-13.2/19p13.3-q13.43 copy number gains (chr19:28271106-49213832, chr19:260912-58956888, chr19:11227942-44626354, chr19:37319377-42738688 triplications) altering gene dosage. Literature (57 papers) demonstrates emerging pathogen-host interactions: RAB4B essential for Japanese encephalitis virus E protein-mediated viral entry in early endosomes (3x citations, 2025), GARP complex-dependent endosome-to-TGN retrograde trafficking controlled by RAB4B (4x citations, 2024), host factor RAB4B mediates Glaesserella parasuis cytolethal distending toxin internalization and intoxication via EEA1 expression regulation in macrophages (5x citations, 2024). Mechanistic hypothesis: RAB4B functions through GTPase cycle regulated by GEFs (guanine exchange factors) and GAPs (GTPase activating proteins); GTP-bound active RAB4B recruits to early endosomal membrane via C-terminal geranylgeranyl anchor where it binds effectors including RIN3, rabenosyn-5, and EEA1 promoting formation of tubular recycling carriers; fast recycling pathway returns ~70% of internalized transferrin receptor within 5-10 minutes maintaining surface receptor homeostasis; in viral entry, Japanese encephalitis virus E protein hijacks RAB4B-positive early endosomes facilitating fusion pore formation and genome release; GARP complex interaction enables subset of cargo retrograde transport to TGN for lysosomal enzyme sorting; bacterial toxin CDT exploits RAB4B-dependent endocytosis entering cytosol where it arrests cell cycle; RAB4A/RAB4B redundancy ensures robustness but brain enrichment (108.8 nTPM) suggests specialized neuronal roles in synaptic vesicle recycling and receptor trafficking; 19q CNVs alter RAB4B dosage potentially disrupting endosomal homeostasis contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes; MET receptor recycling sustains oncogenic HGF signaling in cancer.
