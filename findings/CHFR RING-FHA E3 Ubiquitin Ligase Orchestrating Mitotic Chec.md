## Description
CHFR (Checkpoint with Forkhead and Ring Finger domains) is a 664 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase that functions as a critical mitotic checkpoint regulator, monitoring centrosome separation and spindle assembly before anaphase onset through ubiquitin-mediated degradation of key cell cycle proteins. InterPro analysis identifies canonical functional domains including forkhead-associated FHA domain (IPR000253, cd22672, residues 15-124) for phospho-threonine recognition and protein binding (GO:0005515), RING-type zinc finger (IPR001841, cd16503, residues 300-353) conferring E3 ubiquitin ligase activity, SMAD/FHA domain superfamily (IPR008984, G3DSA:2.60.200.20, residues 13-117), RING/FYVE/PHD-type zinc finger superfamily (IPR013083, G3DSA:3.30.40.10, residues 276-396), and CHFR-specific cysteine-rich multi-zinc binding domain (IPR040909). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q96EP1-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 72.0) across the 664 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded protein with structured FHA and RING domains connected by flexible linker regions suitable for substrate recognition and ubiquitin transfer. HPA data classifies CHFR as a cancer-related enzyme and metabolic protein involved in cell cycle, cell division, mitosis, and Ubl conjugation pathway, with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), cell type-enhanced expression, and subcellular localization to nuclear bodies and microtubules. STRING interactions reveal functional connections to DNA damage response machinery: APLF (Aprataxin and PNKP-like factor, 0.919), PARP1 (Poly ADP-ribose polymerase 1, 0.897), APTX (Aprataxin, 0.830), XRCC1 (X-ray repair cross-complementing protein 1, 0.770), LIG3 (DNA ligase 3, 0.758); ubiquitin conjugation enzymes UBE2D1/2/3 (0.755-0.825), UBE2N (0.567); and mitotic kinase PLK1 (Polo-like kinase 1, 0.561). ClinVar lists 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on chromosome 12q24, deletions, splice variants (c.584-10C>G, c.1843+3A>T), and nonsense mutations (p.Gln411Ter), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined. Literature (576 papers) establishes CHFR as a multifunctional regulator: mediates premitotic checkpoint arrest in response to microtubule stress through PLK1 and Aurora A ubiquitination; regulates taxane sensitivity in breast and oral cancers with promoter methylation silencing predicting chemoresistance; disrupts endothelial adherens junctions through VE-cadherin ubiquitination and degradation affecting vascular integrity; controls blood-testis barrier integrity via SMARCD1 ubiquitination under palmitic acid exposure; and coordinates with PARP1/APLF/APTX in DNA single-strand break repair. As a well-studied gene with established cancer relevance but incompletely characterized tissue-specific mechanisms, CHFR represents a critical node connecting mitotic surveillance, DNA damage response, and therapeutic vulnerability through ubiquitin-dependent protein turnover.
