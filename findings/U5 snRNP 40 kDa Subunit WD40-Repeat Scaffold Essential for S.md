## Description
SNRNP40 encodes a 357 amino acid WD40-repeat protein (UniProt Q96DI7) that serves as a critical structural scaffold within the U5 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex of the major spliceosome. The protein architecture is dominated by a seven-bladed WD40 beta-propeller structure (aa 54-357, IPR015943, PF00400) that provides multiple protein-protein interaction surfaces, preceded by an N-terminal nucleolar protein 10-like domain (aa 114-264, IPR056551) potentially involved in nucleolar localization. AlphaFold prediction shows high structural confidence (pLDDT 85.2), confirming a well-folded stable scaffold. STRING interaction network reveals SNRNP40 functions at the heart of the spliceosome with ultra-high confidence connections to core U5 snRNP components: PRPF8 (0.999), EFTUD2 (0.999), CDC40 (0.999), SNRNP200 (0.999), and assembly factors CDC5L (0.999), PRPF6 (0.999), BCAS2 (0.999). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nuclear speckle localization, consistent with constitutive splicing machinery. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1p35-36, and literature links hypomorphic mutations to syndromic immunodeficiency in mouse models. The WD40 beta-propeller likely serves as a molecular hub coordinating U5 snRNP assembly, bridging interactions between the catalytic PRPF8 protein and regulatory factors during spliceosome activation and catalytic step transitions.
