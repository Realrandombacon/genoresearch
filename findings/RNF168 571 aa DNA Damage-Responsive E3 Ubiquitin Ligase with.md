# RNF168: 571 aa DNA Damage-Responsive E3 Ubiquitin Ligase with RING-MIU Architecture (pLDDT 61.1) Amplifying Histone Ubiquitination at Double-Strand Breaks and Causing RIDDLE Syndrome

## Description
RNF168 (Q8IYW5, 571 aa), also known as RIDL (RIDDLE syndrome protein), is a critical E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase that acts as the central amplifier of the DNA double-strand break (DSB) response by catalyzing the formation of Lys63-linked ubiquitin chains on histone H2A and H2AX at sites of chromosomal damage. InterPro identifies a specialized domain architecture: an N-terminal RING finger domain (IPR001841, cd16550, G3DSA:3.30.40.10, aa 14-61) responsible for recruiting E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes (UBE2N/UBC13), followed by a UDM1 module (cd22265, aa 110-183) that binds ubiquitinated histones, and two C-terminal MIU (Motif Interacting with Ubiquitin) domains (cd21952, aa 431-481) that recognize and bind K63-linked polyubiquitin chains, creating a positive feedback loop. The RNF168 family signature (IPR034725, IPR051657, PF14447, MF_03066, aa 1-568) confirms its unique role in the DNA damage response. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 61.1, residues 1-571), reflecting a partially ordered protein with structured RING and MIU domains separated by flexible linkers, consistent with a dynamic scaffold that must rapidly assemble and disassemble at DSB sites. HPA shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in all tissues, reflecting its essential housekeeping role in genome maintenance; localized to the nucleoplasm and nuclear bodies; classified as an essential enzyme, chromatin regulator, and potential drug target involved in DNA damage and repair. STRING reveals 10 extremely high-confidence interactions defining the DSB signaling cascade: TP53BP1 (0.997, tumor suppressor p53-binding protein 1), MDC1 (0.997, mediator of DNA damage checkpoint 1), RNF8 (0.997, the upstream E3 ligase that recruits RNF168), UBC (0.991, ubiquitin C), VPS29 (0.985), H2AC20 (0.983, histone H2A type 2-C), BRCA1 (0.982, breast cancer type 1 susceptibility protein), H2AX (0.978, histone variant H2A.X), RPS27A (0.665, ubiquitin-40S ribosomal protein), and UBE2N (0.552, E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme N). The perfect scores with RNF8, MDC1, and TP53BP1 confirm the sequential recruitment pathway: RNF8 first ubiquitinates histones at DSBs, then RNF168 binds via its MIU domains and amplifies the signal by generating extensive K63-Ub chains that recruit 53BP1 and BRCA1 for repair pathway choice. ClinVar contains 135 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including frameshifts (c.212_213del/p.Leu71fs, c.891del/p.Met298fs), nonsense mutations (c.511C>T/p.Gln171Ter, c.1427T>G/p.Leu476Ter), and large chromosomal deletions/duplications on 3q29; these cause RIDDLE syndrome (Radiosensitivity, Immunodeficiency, Dysmorphic features, and Learning difficulties), an autosomal recessive disorder characterized by impaired class switch recombination, radiation sensitivity, and neurodevelopmental defects. Functional hypothesis: RNF168 functions as a ubiquitin signal amplifier that binds to RNF8-generated K63-Ub chains via its MIU domains, then uses its RING domain to recruit UBE2N~Ub conjugates and catalyze further K63-linked ubiquitination on histone H2A/H2AX (primarily at Lys13-15). This creates a dense ubiquitin platform that recruits downstream effectors: 53BP1 (for NHEJ repair) and BRCA1 (for HR repair), thereby controlling the critical decision between error-prone and error-free DSB repair mechanisms. The UDM1 module may stabilize binding to monoubiquitinated substrates. Disruption leads to defective 53BP1 focus formation, impaired immunoglobulin class switching, radiation hypersensitivity, and genomic instability. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 565 aa, rat 564 aa, zebrafish 474 aa) confirms essential genome surveillance role. NOTE: This gene represents a 'dark' but mechanistically definitive controller of DNA repair pathway choice.
