## Description
LOC126860422 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 8q21.13 (genomic coordinates 75277915-75279114, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a CDK7 STRONGLY-DEPENDENT GROUP 2 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors with STRONG DEPENDENCE on CDK7 (a transcription-associated kinase that phosphorylates RNA polymerase II C-terminal domain for transcriptional elongation). This cofactor profile suggests it operates through transcriptional elongation control mechanisms. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants affecting this locus through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements. STRIKINGLY, this locus shows AMPLIFICATION-DOMINANT pathogenicity: 12 copy number GAINS (chr8:226452-145068712x3, chr8:21291522-145070385x3, chr8:208048-145070385x3, chr8:46031340-139285494x3, chr8:241605-145054781x3, chr8:66633845-80100089x3, chr8:66171669-93505509x3, chr8:61691800-82537696x3, and additional amplifications) versus only 2 copy number LOSSES (chr8:74905308-81339951x1, chr8:73879385-85611466x1). This 12:2 gain-to-loss ratio (86% amplification) strongly suggests oncogene regulation rather than tumor suppressor control. The 8q region contains MYC (8q24.21), one of the most frequently amplified oncogenes in human cancer (breast, lung, colorectal, neuroblastoma), as well as other oncogenes including PVT1 lncRNA. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with strong oncogenic relevance — the CDK7-dependent enhancer activity in HCT116 cells combined with amplification-dominant pathogenicity suggests LOC126860422 functions as an oncogene-specific enhancer (potentially for MYC or neighboring 8q oncogenes), where amplification drives oncogenic overexpression and CDK7 dependence suggests sensitivity to transcriptional kinase inhibitors.
