## Description
PLXDC1 (Plexin Domain-Containing Protein 1), also known as TEM7 (Tumor Endothelial Marker 7), is a 500 amino acid transmembrane protein featuring plexin repeat architecture that functions as a critical regulator of tumor-stromal interactions, angiogenesis, and immune microenvironment remodeling in multiple cancer types. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic plexin family architecture including plexin repeat domain (IPR002165, PF01437, residues 303-347) for protein-protein interactions and signal transduction, NIDO-related domain (IPR063539, PF30706, residues 125-259) providing extracellular matrix binding capability, plexin domain-containing protein family signature (IPR031152, PTHR13055, residues 1-497) defining the TEM7-related protein family, and tumor endothelial marker 7 related classification indicating vascular and stromal function. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8IUK5-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 72.2) across the 500 residue sequence, indicating a stable extracellular protein with structured plexin and NIDO domains suitable for ligand binding and cell-cell communication. HPA data classifies PLXDC1 as a predicted membrane and intracellular protein with retina-enriched expression (44.9 nTPM, tissue enhanced), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type-enriched distribution, and implications in cancer progression and vascular biology. STRING interactions reveal moderate-confidence functional coupling to diverse signaling partners: ADGRA2 (Adhesion G Protein-Coupled Receptor A2, 0.670) for Wnt signaling and angiogenesis; CD248 (Endosialin/TEM1, 0.621) as a related tumor endothelial marker on pericytes and fibroblasts; HIGD1B (Hypoxia Inducible Domain Family Member 1B, 0.517) for hypoxia response; RASD2 (RAS Dexamethasone-Induced 2, 0.480) for GTPase signaling; ARHGEF17 (Rho Guanine Nucleotide Exchange Factor 17, 0.467) for cytoskeletal remodeling; NRF1 (Nuclear Respiratory Factor 1, 0.457) for mitochondrial biogenesis; FAM47E-STBD1 fusion (0.433) for glycogen metabolism; ANTXR1 (Anthrax Toxin Receptor 1, 0.430) for extracellular matrix sensing; FBXL20 (F-Box And Leucine Rich Repeat Protein 20, 0.426) for ubiquitination; and TNS3 (Tensin 3, 0.421) for focal adhesion signaling. ClinVar lists 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 17q12-21.2, 17p11.2-q21.2, 17p13.3-q25.3, 17q23.1-25.1, 17q21.33-24.2 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 17-81 Mb) and copy number loss on 17q12-22, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in cancer susceptibility or vascular disorder. Literature (42 papers) establishes PLXDC1 as a context-dependent tumor microenvironment regulator: PLXDC1-positive tumor-associated pancreatic stellate cells promote desmoplastic and immunosuppressive niche in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma through stromal remodeling; single-cell and spatial atlas reveals tumor-specific microenvironment convergence with prognosis-associated PLXDC1+ myofibroblast population in metastatic bladder cancer linking PLXDC1 to poor outcomes; PLXDC1 serves as potential prognostic marker involving malignant progression and macrophage polarization in colon cancer toward M2 immunosuppressive phenotype; PLXDC1 identified as biomarker for poor prognosis and immune evasion in gastric cancer; and PLXDC1 functions as a tumor endothelial marker regulating angiogenesis and stromal-tumor crosstalk. As a moderately characterized plexin family member with emerging roles in cancer biology, PLXDC1 represents a critical node connecting extracellular matrix sensing to tumor-stromal communication, myofibroblast activation, macrophage polarization, and therapeutic resistance through plexin-mediated signal transduction and NIDO-dependent matrix interactions.
