## Description
OR51Q1 is an olfactory receptor belonging to the G-protein coupled receptor family. It is involved in the process of olfaction and sensory transduction. The protein has a 7-helix transmembrane structure characteristic of rhodopsin-like receptors. High-confidence interactions with other olfactory receptors (OR51M1, OR51I2, OR51B6) and signaling proteins (GNAL, ARRB1, ARRB2) suggest a role in olfactory signal transduction pathways. The presence of 16 reported pathogenic variants indicates potential implications in olfactory disorders.
