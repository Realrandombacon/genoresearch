## Description
FCRLA/Q7L513 (359 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 75.2) type I transmembrane immunoglobulin superfamily receptor functioning as a B-cell co-receptor that modulates B-cell receptor (BCR) signaling thresholds and regulates lipid metabolism in cancer progression. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical immunoglobulin architecture with tandem Ig-like domains (IPR003599/IPR007110/IPR013783/IPR036179/G3DSA:2.60.40.10/SSF48726/SM00409, residues 70-267) containing two Ig V-set/C2-type domains (residues 77-167/168-267; 84-166/177-259), classifying it within the immunoglobulin Fc region receptor family (IPR050488/PTHR11481, residues 62-268) that regulates immune cell activation through ITAM/ITIM signaling motifs. HPA data confirms tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (92.1 nTPM), group-enriched blood cell expression, and cancer-enriched patterns consistent with roles in B-cell differentiation and lymphoid malignancies. STRING interactions define a high-confidence B-cell signaling network linking FCRLA to B-cell marker MS4A1/CD20 (0.800), BCR components CD79B (0.761) and CD79A (0.446), transcription factors MEIS1 (0.766) and HOXA9 (0.718), nucleoporin NUP98 (0.718), Src-family kinase BLK (0.705), pre-B cell receptor component VPREB3 (0.615), autophagy regulator NIBAN3 (0.570), and B-cell co-receptor CD19 (0.428), positioning it within the BCR signalosome complex. Literature (66 papers) establishes emerging oncological roles: 2025 studies demonstrate FCRLA targeting induces mitochondrial permeability transition-driven necrosis (MPTDN) in lung adenocarcinoma through novel therapeutic strategy; TNFα activation of PLEKHA5-FCRLA axis disturbs lipid metabolism driving cutaneous malignant melanoma progression; FCRLA identified as differentially expressed gene in lymph node metastatic breast cancer; and FCRLA serves as prognostic biomarker correlated with immune infiltration in ovarian cancer alongside CXCL13, PLA2G2D, and MS4A1. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1q regions (1q21.1-25.3, 1q23.1-23.3, 1q23.2-24.1, 1q23.1-24.2, 1q23.2-25.1, 1q23.3-25.3), implicating FCRLA dosage sensitivity in developmental and immunological disorders. The convergence of tandem Ig-like extracellular domains, B-cell receptor complex partnerships, lipid metabolism regulation in melanoma, MPT-driven necrosis induction in lung cancer, and lymphoid-enriched expression suggests FCRLA functions as an immunometabolic checkpoint coordinating BCR signaling with metabolic reprogramming in malignancy, with therapeutic potential in lung adenocarcinoma necrosis induction, melanoma lipid metabolism targeting, and B-cell lymphoma immunotherapy.
