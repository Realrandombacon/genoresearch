## Description
LOC285889 (Gene ID: 285889) is an uncharacterized protein-coding locus on chromosome 7q36.3 (NC_000007.14, positions 156437788-156445587, ~7.8 kb region) encoding a 116 amino acid small protein (UniProt accession A4D240) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 44 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite limited molecular characterization. InterPro scan failed, preventing domain identification. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, suggesting low/tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 44 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 22 duplications/gains (variant 2579171: 7q34-36.3 ~20.6 Mb; variant 2579170: 7q33-36.3 ~21.9 Mb; variant 155687: 7q32.3-36.3 ~28.2 Mb; variant 155640: 7q32.3-36.3 ~26.9 Mb; variant 154735: 7q32.3-36.3 ~28.1 Mb; plus 17 additional gains) and 22 deletions/losses (variant 2579275: 7q36.2-36.3 ~4.8 Mb; variant 545343: 7q36.3 deletion; variant 155436: 7q35-36.3 ~12.2 Mb; variant 154568: 7q36.3 ~3.9 Mb; variant 154525: 7q36.1-36.3 ~9.1 Mb; plus 17 additional losses). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 7q36.3 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including SHH (sonic hedgehog signaling molecule, embryonic patterning, neural tube development, limb morphogenesis, basal cell carcinoma, medulloblastoma), DNAJB6 (DnaJ heat shock protein family Hsp40 member B6, protein folding, limb-girdle muscular dystrophy type 1D, protein aggregation), VIPR2 (vasoactive intestinal peptide receptor 2, GPCR, smooth muscle relaxation, immune regulation, pulmonary hypertension), MNX1 (motor neuron and pancreas homeobox 1, transcriptional repressor, motor neuron specification, pancreatic development, congenital mirror movements), and EN2 (engrailed homeobox 2, hindbrain patterning, cerebellum development, autism spectrum disorder). Functional hypothesis: LOC285889 encodes a 116 amino acid zinc-finger or homeodomain-containing transcriptional coregulator functioning as a SHH pathway modulator in neural progenitors and limb bud mesenchyme (GLI transcription factor recruitment, Hedgehog target gene activation/repression) or as a chromatin remodeling factor coordinating SHH-DNAJB6-VIPR2-MNX1-EN2 expression during embryonic development. The 44 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts Hedgehog signaling and neural development programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves LOC285889 haploinsufficiency causing SHH pathway defects (holoprosencephaly, preaxial polydactyly, tibial hemimelia, ectrodactyly), LOC285889 overexpression altering Hedgehog threshold (basal cell carcinoma, medulloblastoma, rhabdomyosarcoma), SHH loss causing holoprosencephaly type 3 (forebrain midline defects, cyclopia, single central incisor), SHH gain causing polydactyly syndromes (postaxial polydactyly type A1), DNAJB6 mutation causing limb-girdle muscular dystrophy 1D (protein aggregation, myofibrillar myopathy), VIPR2 dysfunction causing pulmonary hypertension (vascular remodeling, right heart failure), MNX1 alteration causing congenital mirror movements (corticospinal tract decussation defects), and EN2 variation causing autism spectrum disorder (cerebellar vermis hypoplasia, social cognition deficits). The 7q36.3 deletion syndrome and 7q36.3 duplication syndrome may present with holoprosencephaly, polydactyly, and neurodevelopmental abnormalities. The presence of protein-coding features (UniProt A4D240 116 aa) combined with ClinVar evidence (44 CNVs ranging 3.9-28.2 Mb) and SHH-DNAJB6-VIPR2-MNX1-EN2 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a Hedgehog pathway transcriptional coregulator critical for neural development-limb patterning-cancer suppression coordination.
