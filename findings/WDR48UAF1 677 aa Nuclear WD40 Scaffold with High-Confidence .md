## Description
WDR48 (Q8TAF3, 677 aa), also known as UAF1 (USP1-associated factor 1), is a critical regulatory scaffold protein belonging to the WD repeat family (PTHR24103) that activates specific deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs). InterPro identifies a distinctive bipartite architecture: an N-terminal region containing multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680, PF00400, aa 14-280) forming a canonical seven-bladed beta-propeller for protein-protein interactions, and a unique C-terminal Ubiquitin-like (Ubl) domain (IPR021772, cd17041, aa 509-673) specific to the WDR48/Bun107 family. The conserved WD40 sites (IPR019775, aa 132-146) confirm structural integrity. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.9, residues 1-677) consistent with a rigid beta-propeller connected to a structured Ubl domain, ideal for stabilizing DUB complexes. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity; localized to vesicles and predicted intracellular; involved in DNA damage response, DNA repair, host-virus interaction, and Ubl conjugation pathway. STRING reveals 10 extremely high-confidence interactions defining its functional network: USP46 (0.999), WDR20 (0.999), USP1 (0.999), FANCI (0.998), UBC (0.985), ATAD5 (0.973), UBB (0.960), FANCD2 (0.434), USP12 (0.428), and RAD51AP1 (0.400). The perfect scores with USP1, USP46, and WDR20 confirm its role as an obligate co-factor. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, primarily large copy number gains (chr3:2263690-55016039, chr3:16923595-45249923, chr3:61892-197851986) and deletions (chr3:16710965-41275270), suggesting dosage sensitivity, though no specific monogenic disease is yet explicitly named (potential link to SPG60/spastic paraplegia via alias). Functional hypothesis: WDR48/UAF1 functions as an essential allosteric activator and substrate-recruitment platform for the USP1 and USP46 deubiquitinase complexes. The WD40 beta-propeller binds the catalytic USP domain of USP1/USP46, while the C-terminal Ubl domain recruits specific substrates or stabilizes the active conformation, dramatically enhancing catalytic efficiency (>100-fold). In the DNA damage response, the WDR48-USP1 complex removes monoubiquitin from FANCD2 and FANCI (scores 0.998, 0.434), turning off the Fanconi Anemia repair pathway after lesion resolution to prevent genomic instability. The interaction with WDR20 (0.999) suggests a parallel complex with USP12/USP46 regulating other targets like histone H2B. Ubiquitous expression reflects its housekeeping role in genome maintenance. Disruption via CNVs likely causes defective DUB activity, leading to persistent DNA damage signaling, replication stress, and potentially neurodegeneration (SPG60) or cancer predisposition. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 676 aa, chicken 678 aa) confirms essential function. NOTE: This gene is a well-defined but mechanistically rich regulator of ubiquitin signaling.
