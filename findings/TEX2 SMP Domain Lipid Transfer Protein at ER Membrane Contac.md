## Description
TEX2 encodes a 1127 aa testis-expressed protein containing a C-terminal synaptotagmin-like mitochondrial-lipid-binding (SMP) domain (IPR031468, residues 816-1101) that mediates lipid binding and transfer at membrane contact sites. The SMP domain places TEX2 in the TULIP superfamily of lipid transporters that shuttle lipids between organelle membranes. STRING interactions reveal associations with ER membrane proteins (BCAP31 score 0.496, PI4KA score 0.493), the TREX complex (THOC3 score 0.670), and RHOA (score 0.562) suggesting roles in ER-Golgi trafficking and cytoskeletal coordination. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with immune cell enrichment and nucleoplasm/ER localization. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants including 8 copy number gains spanning 17q23-24, indicating dosage sensitivity. Mouse ortholog Q6ZPJ0 (1128 aa, 99% length identity) confirms deep vertebrate conservation. TEX2 likely functions as a lipid transfer scaffold at ER membrane contact sites, coordinating sphingolipid metabolism and vesicular trafficking through SMP-mediated lipid exchange and protein interaction networks.
