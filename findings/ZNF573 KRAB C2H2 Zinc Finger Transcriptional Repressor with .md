## Description
ZNF573 encodes a 665-amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional regulator (UniProt Q86YE8) featuring an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 27-100) followed by multiple C2H2 zinc finger arrays (PF00096, IPR013087 at positions 134-156, 162-184, 190-212, 470-492). The KRAB domain mediates transcriptional silencing through recruitment of TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor (STRING score 0.678), which scaffolds SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins for H3K9me3 deposition and chromatin compaction. STRING interactions include TRIM28 plus diverse partners COL19A1 (0.475), ABCA13 (0.464), UNC80 (0.417), and SRPK2 (0.408), suggesting potential roles in chromatin organization and RNA processing. HPA data is unavailable, limiting tissue expression profiling. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number gains and losses spanning 19q11-13.33, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity; the CNV burden suggests ZNF573 haploinsufficiency or triplosufficiency may contribute to developmental phenotypes though specific disease associations remain uncharacterized. The multiple zinc finger arrays likely recognize distinct DNA sequence motifs enabling locus-specific targeting, while the KRAB domain provides epigenetic silencing effector function. Primate orthologs in Pan troglodytes (578-607 aa isoforms) indicate evolutionary conservation of the zinc finger architecture.
