## Description
CGREF1 (Cell Growth Regulator with EF-Hand Domain 1, UniProt Q99674) is a 318 aa calcium-binding protein belonging to the multiple coagulation factor deficiency protein 2-like family that functions in cell cycle regulation, Wnt/β-catenin signaling, and hepatic lipid metabolism. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal EF-hand domain pair (IPR002048/IPR011992, positions 68-142) containing two canonical calcium-binding sites (PS00018, positions 82-94 and 127-139) within the EF-hand superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.238.10/SSF47473, positions 68-142) and ER-Golgi transport regulator domain (PTHR23104, positions 1-155). Literature (37 papers) establishes CGREF1 roles in: (1) CGREF1 facilitating hepatocellular carcinoma cell proliferation, migration and invasion via EIF3H/Wnt/β-catenin signaling axis (2025, 5 citations), (2) CGREF1 as CREB-H-regulated hepatokine promoting hepatic de novo lipogenesis by mediating epididymal fat insulin resistance in metabolic disease (2025, 3 citations), (3) CGREF1 promoting cancer cell migration and predicting poor prognosis in colorectal cancer (2025), (4) CGREF1 modulating osteosarcoma proliferation by regulating cell cycle through Wnt/β-catenin pathway (2024, 4 citations). HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in brain (44.6 nTPM) and salivary gland (24.5 nTPM), Golgi apparatus localization, and involvement in cell adhesion, cell cycle, and growth arrest biological processes. STRING interactions reveal cell cycle and ER-Golgi network: PREB (0.573) - prolactin regulatory element-binding protein involved in ER-Golgi transport; TP53 (0.478) - tumor protein p53 cell cycle regulator; REEP1 (0.447) - receptor expression-enhancing protein 1 for ER shaping; CGRRF1 (0.446) - cell growth regulator with RING finger domain 1, paralog; BSCL2 (0.431) - berardinelli-seip congenital lipodystrophy 2 protein at ER membrane; PCNP (0.412) - PEST-containing nuclear protein. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants including large copy number gains at 2p25.3-23.1, 2p24.1-22.2, 2p25.3-22.3 and losses at 2p25.1-q13, plus splice variant c.218-35G>A and 3'UTR variant c.*106C>T, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 62.1) indicating partially folded protein with structured EF-hand domain and intrinsically disordered C-terminal region, consistent with calcium-dependent conformational switching and protein interaction flexibility. Conservation: mouse Cgref1 (Q8R1U2) is 281 aa, rat (P97586) is 281 aa - moderately conserved across mammals with human-specific C-terminal extension. Functional hypothesis: CGREF1 functions as a calcium-sensitive regulatory protein that couples intracellular calcium fluctuations to cell cycle progression and Wnt/β-catenin signaling through EF-hand-mediated conformational changes. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) Calcium binding to the paired EF-hand domains (Kd ~1-10 μM) induces conformational change exposing interaction surfaces for EIF3H translation initiation factor and β-catenin, (2) CGREF1-EIF3H complex enhances translation of Wnt pathway components and cell cycle regulators (cyclin D1, c-Myc), (3) Golgi-localized CGREF1 regulates vesicular trafficking of Wnt ligands or receptors, amplifying canonical Wnt signaling, (4) In liver, CREB-H transcription factor induces CGREF1 expression during fasting/refeeding cycles, and secreted CGREF1 acts as hepatokine targeting adipose tissue to promote insulin resistance and de novo lipogenesis. In HCC and colorectal cancer, CGREF1 upregulation drives proliferation through Wnt/β-catenin hyperactivation. In osteosarcoma, CGREF1 accelerates G1/S transition. Pathogenic CNVs at 2p25 disrupt CGREF1 dosage, potentially contributing to developmental disorders and metabolic diseases. CGREF1 represents an understudied calcium-dependent regulator linking metabolic signaling to cancer progression.
