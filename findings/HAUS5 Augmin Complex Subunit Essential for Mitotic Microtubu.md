## Description
HAUS5 (O94927) encodes a 633 amino acid subunit of the human augmin complex (HAUS complex), a 390-kD octameric protein complex critical for mitotic spindle assembly. The augmin complex, named from Latin 'augmentare' (to increase), generates new microtubules from existing ones within the spindle, amplifying microtubule density during cell division.\n\nSTRUCTURAL FEATURES: InterPro analysis reveals conserved HAUS5-specific domains (IPR026215, IPR029131, PF14817) spanning residues 7-618, with GO annotations for centrosome cycle (GO:0007098) and spindle assembly (GO:0051225). Critical non-HAUS partners include NEDD1 (0.869), a γ-tubulin recruitment factor, and TUBG1 (0.816), gamma-tubulin itself. NUMA1 (0.686) connection links augmin to spindle pole organization.\n\nEXPRESSION & DISEASE: HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping function in cell division. Classed as 'essential protein'. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and splice site mutations, though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized—suggesting potential embryonic lethality or rare mitotic disorders.\n\nLITERATURE GAP: Only 7 publications exist, predominantly cancer prognostic studies (hepatocellular carcinoma, breast cancer) rather than mechanistic work.
