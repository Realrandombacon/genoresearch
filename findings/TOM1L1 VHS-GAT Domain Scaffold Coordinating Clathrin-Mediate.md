## Description
TOM1L1 (Target of Myb1-like protein 1, UniProt O75674, alias SRCASM) is a 476 aa cytosolic/endosomal scaffold protein featuring an N-terminal VHS domain (IPR002014, positions 12-154) for ubiquitin/phosphatidylinositol binding and a central GAT domain (IPR004152, positions 200-288) for membrane trafficking. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 67.6). HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with cytosolic localization. STRING reveals 10 interaction partners including clathrin heavy chain CLTC (0.920), endosomal adaptor ZFYVE16 (0.876), clathrin light chain CLTCL1 (0.792), and Src-family kinase FYN (0.656), placing it at the intersection of endocytosis and kinase signaling. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants including copy number alterations at 17q22. Literature (53 papers) reveals multifunctional roles: (1) recruits FYN to clathrin-coated pits to regulate Akt signaling downstream of EGFR, (2) drives MT1-MMP membrane delivery promoting ERBB2-induced breast cancer invasion (co-amplified with ERBB2 in HER2+ tumors), (3) mediates exosomal sorting of tumor-suppressive miR-378a-3p in esophageal cancer. Mechanistically, TOM1L1 serves as a modular scaffold coupling ubiquitinated cargo recognition (via VHS) to clathrin coat assembly (via CLTC binding) while simultaneously recruiting Src-family kinases to phosphorylate signaling substrates at endocytic sites.
