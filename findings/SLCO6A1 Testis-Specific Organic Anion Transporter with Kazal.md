## Description
SLCO6A1 encodes a 719 aa solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 6A1 (UniProt Q86UG4), functioning as a gonad-specific organic anion transporter with unique Kazal domain architecture. The protein contains an organic anion transporter polypeptide domain (IPR004156, PTHR11388, aa83-706) characteristic of OATP/SLCO family transporters, an OATP family domain (PF03137, aa107-503, aa550-665) forming the transmembrane transport core, a Kazal-type serine protease inhibitor domain (IPR002350, PF07648, aa496-551) unusual for transporters suggesting regulatory or protein-binding functions, and belongs to MFS transporter superfamily (IPR036259, aa90-683). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 76.7), indicating a well-folded multi-domain transporter with stable transmembrane architecture. HPA shows tissue-enriched expression exclusively in testis (28.3 nTPM), classified as predicted membrane protein and transporter—confirming gonad-specific function. STRING interactions reveal SLCO6A1 connects to diverse signaling pathways: CDC42 (0.961, Rho GTPase regulating cytoskeleton and cell polarity—highest confidence), AKT1 (0.894, PI3K-AKT signaling kinase), GSTM1 (0.808), GSTM2 (0.748), GSTA1 (0.746) (glutathione S-transferases for detoxification), RTKN (0.589, rhotekin involved in Rho signaling), HPGDS (0.564, hematopoietic prostaglandin D synthase), TP53 (0.508, tumor suppressor), CDH1 (0.477, E-cadherin cell adhesion), GSTP1 (0.401). ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—all copy number variations on 5q14.3-23.2 including deletions (5q15-23.2, 5q21.1-23.2, 5q14.3-21.3) and duplications (5q14.3-21.3, 5q14.3-23.3), though no specific disease is yet assigned. Literature (13 papers) documents SLCO6A1 roles in: (1) cancer-testis (CT) antigen expression in human lung cancer—gonad-specific gene aberrantly expressed in tumors; (2) schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and major depressive disorder associations in Han Chinese population; (3) medulloblastoma CT antigen expression; (4) breast carcinoma genetic mutations and prognostic indicators in TCGA data; (5) Crohn's disease genetics via SNP-SNP interactions. SLCO6A1 functions as a testis-specific organic anion transporter—the OATP domain (aa83-706) forms 12 transmembrane helices typical of MFS transporters, mediating sodium-independent transport of organic anions including bile acids, steroid conjugates, thyroid hormones, and drugs across the plasma membrane. The Kazal domain (aa496-551) is highly unusual for transporters—Kazal domains typically function as serine protease inhibitors, suggesting SLCO6A1 may have dual functions: (1) anion transport via the OATP core, and (2) protease inhibition or protein-protein interaction via the Kazal domain, potentially regulating transporter stability, trafficking, or substrate specificity. Testis-specific expression (28.3 nTPM) aligns with gonad-specific anion transporter function—potentially transporting steroid conjugates, metabolites, or signaling molecules critical for spermatogenesis. CDC42 (0.961) and AKT1 (0.894) interactions suggest SLCO6A1 participates in cytoskeletal organization and cell survival signaling—transporter activity may be regulated by or regulate these pathways. GST family interactions (GSTM1/2, GSTA1, GSTP1; 0.401-0.808) indicate connections to detoxification pathways—SLCO6A1 may transport glutathione conjugates or coordinate with GSTs for xenobiotic metabolism. Cancer-testis antigen status explains lung cancer and medulloblastoma expression—germline-restricted genes like SLCO6A1 are epigenetically silenced in somatic tissues but reactivated in tumors, making them immunotherapy targets. Neuropsychiatric disorder associations (schizophrenia, bipolar, depression) suggest SLCO6A1 variants may affect brain organic anion transport, neurotransmitter metabolism, or blood-brain barrier function despite testis-enriched expression—potential extra-gonadal roles in CNS. The 12 ClinVar CNVs on 5q may cause SLCO6A1 dosage effects contributing to reproductive disorders, cancer susceptibility, or neuropsychiatric disease. The OATP-Kazal domain architecture represents a unique transporter variant—Kazal domain insertion may confer regulatory advantages for testis-specific transport functions or tumor antigenicity.
