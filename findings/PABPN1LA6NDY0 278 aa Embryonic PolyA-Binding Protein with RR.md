## Description

Functional hypothesis: PABPN1L acts as embryonic-specific poly(A)-binding protein that binds maternal mRNA poly(A) tails during oocyte maturation and early embryogenesis, recruiting CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex (CNOT6/6L/7) through protein-protein interactions to trigger controlled mRNA deadenylation and decay during MZT. The RRM domain recognizes poly(A) sequences while N-terminal/C-terminal regions mediate interactions with deadenylase machinery and potentially PABPN1 paralog. Brain-enriched expression suggests potential secondary role in neuronal mRNA regulation. The 68 ClinVar variants suggest 16q24 syndrome contributions where PABPN1L dysregulation affects embryonic development.
