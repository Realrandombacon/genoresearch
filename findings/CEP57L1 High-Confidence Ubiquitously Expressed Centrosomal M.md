CEP57L1 (centrosomal protein 57 like 1, alias C6orf182, cep57R) is a 460 amino acid predicted intracellular protein (UniProt Q8IYX8) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 72.6) containing canonical Cep57 family centrosomal protein architecture including Cep57 centrosome microtubule-binding domain (IPR024957/PF06657, residues 312-384), Cep57 centrosome localisation domain (IPR025913/PF14073, residues 54-231), Centrosomal microtubule-associated protein family (IPR051756/PTHR19336, residues 2-460), and diaminopimelate epimerase-like superfamily (G3DSA:1.20.58.90, residues 318-396), functioning as a ubiquitously expressed cytoplasmic/microtubule/microtubule organizing center/centrosome protein that regulates centrosome assembly and microtubule organization through microtubule binding (GO:0008017), identical protein binding (GO:0042802), and gamma-tubulin binding (GO:0043015) via partnerships with ZNF572, CEP152, CEP63, CEP295, POC5, and ESCO2.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals CEP57L1 contains canonical Cep57 family dual-domain architecture: Cep57 centrosome microtubule-binding domain (IPR024957/PF06657, residues 312-384) forming a 73 aa structured module (~16% of protein) with microtubule binding (GO:0008017) capability for direct microtubule engagement and centrosomal microtubule anchoring, Cep57 centrosome localisation domain (IPR025913/PF14073, residues 54-231) forming a 178 aa coiled-coil module (~39%) with identical protein binding (GO:0042802) and gamma-tubulin binding (GO:0043015) capability for centrosome targeting and oligomerization, Centrosomal microtubule-associated protein family (IPR051756/PTHR19336, residues 2-460) forming a 459 aa module (~100%) defining CEP57L1 identity within the centrosomal protein family, and diaminopimelate epimerase-like superfamily (G3DSA:1.20.58.90, residues 318-396) providing structural classification. The centrosome localisation coiled-coil domain spans approximately 178 amino acids (~39%), mediating centrosome targeting and protein oligomerization. The microtubule-binding domain spans 73 amino acids (~16%), forming the microtubule engagement interface. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 72.6) indicates a well-folded protein with stable coiled-coil and microtubule-binding domains, consistent with structural centrosomal proteins.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places CEP57L1 within the centrosome assembly and microtubule organization network with medium confidence interactions: strongest interaction with ZNF572 (0.593, zinc finger protein 572, transcriptional regulator), followed by CEP152 (0.504, centrosomal protein 152, centriole duplication), CEP63 (0.488, centrosomal protein 63, centriole assembly), CEP295 (0.459, centrosomal protein 295, centriole elongation), POC5 (0.427, POC5 centriolar protein, distal centriole assembly), and ESCO2 (0.400, establishment of sister chromatid cohesion N-acetyltransferase 2, cohesin acetyltransferase). The CEP152-CEP63 partnerships (0.504/0.488) validate CEP57L1's role in centrosome biology, as CEP152 and CEP63 form the core centriole duplication machinery at the proximal end of centrioles. CEP295-POC5 interactions (0.459/0.427) link to centriole elongation and distal end assembly, suggesting CEP57L1 functions throughout centriole biogenesis.
