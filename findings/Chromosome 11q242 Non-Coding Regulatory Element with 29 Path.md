## Description
LOC403312 (Gene ID: 403312, Ensembl: ENSG00000255537) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 11q24.2 (NC_000011.10, positions 125495213-125499525, ~4.3 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID (ENSG00000255537) but no quantitative expression data available. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 duplication (variant 545140: single allele), 2 copy number gains (variant 161071: 11q23.3-25 ~18.1 Mb; variant 150522: 11q23.3-24.2 ~10.4 Mb), and 24 copy number losses/deletions (variant 4755346: 11q24.1-25 ~11.7 Mb; variant 154045: 11q24.1-25 ~13.3 Mb; variant 154016: 11q24.1-25 ~13.5 Mb; variant 151354: 11q23.3-24.2 ~5.7 Mb; variant 151164: 11q24.2-25 ~10.5 Mb; variant 150820: 11q24.1-25 ~13.4 Mb; variant 150032: 11q23.3-25 ~15.7 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 11q24 chromosomal region contains critical oncogene and DNA damage response-associated genes including ETS1 (ETS proto-oncogene 1, ETS domain transcription factor, hematopoiesis, angiogenesis, cancer metastasis, immune cell development), CHEK1 (checkpoint kinase 1, serine/threonine protein kinase, DNA damage checkpoint, S/G2-M arrest, replication fork stabilization, cancer drug target), FLI1 (Friend leukemia integration 1, ETS family transcription factor, megakaryocyte differentiation, Ewing sarcoma t(11;22) translocation), ERG (ETS-related gene, ETS family transcription factor, prostate cancer TMPRSS2-ERG fusion, angiogenesis), and MYC (MYC proto-oncogene, bHLH transcription factor, cell proliferation, metabolism, ribosome biogenesis, cancer driver). Functional hypothesis: LOC403312 likely represents a critical 11q24.2 enhancer element within the ETS1-CHEK1 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a DNA damage response enhancer coordinating ETS1-CHEK1-FLI1 expression in hematopoietic tissues (megakaryocytes, lymphocytes, myeloid progenitors) or as a chromatin boundary separating ETS transcription factor genes (ETS1, FLI1, ERG) from cell cycle checkpoint genes (CHEK1). The 29 pathogenic CNVs (predominantly deletions, 5.7-18.1 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts ETS1-mediated transcriptional activation and CHEK1-mediated DNA damage checkpoint control. Disease mechanism involves ETS1 overexpression causing cancer (angiogenesis promotion, invasion, metastasis, hematologic malignancies), ETS1 haploinsufficiency causing Jacobsen syndrome (11q terminal deletion syndrome, thrombocytopenia, cardiac defects, developmental delay, Paris-Trousseau syndrome), CHEK1 inhibition causing cancer therapy sensitivity (replication catastrophe, synthetic lethality with ATR/WEE1 inhibitors, checkpoint abrogation), FLI1 translocation causing Ewing sarcoma (EWSR1-FLI1 fusion oncoprotein, primitive neuroectodermal tumor, bone/soft tissue sarcoma), ERG overexpression causing prostate cancer (TMPRSS2-ERG fusion, androgen-driven oncogenesis, invasion), MYC amplification causing cancer (cell cycle hyperactivation, metabolic reprogramming, ribosome biogenesis, tumor aggressiveness), and 11q24 deletion syndrome presenting with thrombocytopenia, cardiac abnormalities, and developmental delay. The 11q24 region is a well-established Jacobsen syndrome locus with 11q terminal deletions causing characteristic clinical features. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (29 CNVs 5.7-18.1 Mb) and ETS1-CHEK1-FLI1-ERG-MYC neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a hematopoietic-specific enhancer critical for ETS transcription factor-DNA damage checkpoint coordination.
