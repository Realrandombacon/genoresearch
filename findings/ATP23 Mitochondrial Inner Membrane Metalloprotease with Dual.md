## Description
ATP23 encodes a 246 aa mitochondrial inner membrane metalloprotease (UniProt Q9Y6H3) located at 12q13.3, functioning as a dual-function enzyme for ATP synthase biogenesis. InterPro analysis confirms peptidase M76 family membership (IPR019165/PF09768/PTHR21711, aa 22-223) with metalloendopeptidase activity (GO:0004222). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.2), indicating a well-folded mitochondrial enzyme. STRING interactions reveal an unexpected network of DNA repair proteins: XRCC6/Ku70 (0.941), DCLRE1C/Artemis (0.922), ERCC8/XPA (0.916), XRCC4 (0.914), XRCC5/Ku80 (0.913), ERCC1 (0.912), ERCC5/XPG (0.912), MSH2 (0.910), PRKDC/DNA-PKcs (0.910), ERCC4/XPF (0.909) - all critical for non-homologous end joining (NHEJ), nucleotide excision repair, and mismatch repair. ClinVar reports 6 pathogenic variants including a splice site mutation (c.454-2A>C) and multiple 12q13.3-14.1 copy number losses/gains. Literature (23 papers) details: (1) Yeast studies (109-141 citations) establishing dual function - processing Atp6 precursor and assisting F1Fo-ATP synthase assembly; (2) Mia40 chaperone-like folding activity in mitochondrial intermembrane space (93 citations); (3) T1121G COX1 mutation suppressing ATP23 null in yeast (3 citations); (4) Emerging human cancer role - anti-oncogenic and immunological functions in CMS4 colon adenocarcinoma with BRAF mutations (0 citations, 2026). Mechanistically, ATP23 functions through: (1) Proteolytic Processing - cleaving mitochondrial Atp6 precursor to mature form; (2) Chaperone Activity - assisting ATP synthase subunit assembly analogous to Mia40; (3) Metalloprotease Catalysis - M76 family zinc-dependent proteolysis in inner membrane; (4) Potential DNA Repair Crosstalk - STRING network suggests unexplored mitochondrial-nuclear signaling or moonlighting function in DNA damage response. The strong DNA repair protein interactions (all >0.90 confidence) are notable given ATP23's mitochondrial localization and raise hypotheses about: mitochondrial retrograde signaling to nucleus, ATP23 moonlighting in nuclear DNA repair, or coordinated response to mitochondrial DNA damage. Disease implications include: metabolic disorders from ATP synthase deficiency, cancer susceptibility (colon adenocarcinoma CMS4 subtype), potential neurodegeneration from mitochondrial dysfunction. HPA data unavailable, but mitochondrial localization and conservation suggest ubiquitous expression. This represents a partially characterized gene where yeast mechanism is defined but human-specific functions, disease phenotypes, and DNA repair network connections remain unexplored.
