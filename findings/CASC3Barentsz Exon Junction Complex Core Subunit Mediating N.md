## Description
CASC3/O15234 (703 aa) is a partially structured (AlphaFold pLDDT: 53.4) nuclear membrane-associated protein functioning as an essential core component of the exon junction complex (EJC) that coordinates mRNA splicing, export, translation, and nonsense-mediated mRNA decay (NMD). InterPro analysis reveals a canonical Btz domain (IPR018545, residues 141-249) mediating eIF4AIII binding (PF09405/SM01044, residues 144-248/141-249), classifying it within the CASC3/Barentsz family (IPR028544/PTHR13434, residues 1-696/1-696) with RNA-binding and mRNA processing functions (GO:0003723/GO:0006397/GO:0035145). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nuclear membrane localization, and involvement in mRNA processing, splicing, transport, NMD, and stress response pathways consistent with fundamental post-transcriptional regulatory roles. STRING interactions define an ultra-high-confidence EJC core network linking CASC3 to UPF3A (0.999), EIF4A3 (0.999), RBM8A (0.999), UPF3B (0.999), UPF1 (0.999), MAGOH (0.999), MAGOHB (0.999), UPF2 (0.994), CWC22 (0.992), and SNRNP200 (0.983), positioning it as a central scaffold bridging the EJC to NMD machinery. Literature (52 papers) establishes emerging antiviral and oncological roles: 2025 studies identify CASC3 as a novel SADS-CoV restriction factor functioning through liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS) to inhibit viral replication; Smurf2 E3 ubiquitin ligase enhances ubiquitin-mediated CASC3 degradation attenuating leukemia progression; CASC3 biomolecular condensates restrict Turnip crinkle virus by limiting host factor availability (11 citations); and MLN51/CASC3 serves as clinical prognostic biomarker in breast cancer correlating with nodal and hormonal receptor status. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number gains on chromosome 17q regions (17q12-21.2, 17q12-22, 17p11.2-q21.2, 17p13.3-q25.3, 17q23.1-25.1, 17q12-21.31, 17q21.33-24.2), implicating CASC3 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders. The convergence of Btz domain-mediated eIF4AIII binding, ultra-high-confidence EJC/NMD partnerships, LLPS-driven antiviral condensate formation, Smurf2-regulated ubiquitination in leukemia, and breast cancer prognostic value suggests CASC3 functions as a multifunctional mRNA surveillance hub coordinating splicing-dependent gene expression with antiviral defense and tumor suppression, with therapeutic potential in leukemia targeting, coronavirus restriction enhancement, and breast cancer stratification.
