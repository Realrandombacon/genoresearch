## Description
VPS37C encodes a 355 amino acid vacuolar protein sorting-associated protein that serves as a critical subunit of the ESCRT-I (Endosomal Sorting Complex Required for Transport-I) complex. The protein contains a characteristic Modifier of rudimentary (Modr) domain (IPR009851, PF07200, positions 5-149) with a helix hairpin bin structural fold (G3DSA:1.10.287.660, positions 81-158) and a specialized VPS37 C-terminal domain (PS51314, positions 78-167). These domains enable VPS37C to function as a scaffold for ESCRT-I assembly and ubiquitinated cargo recognition.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals VPS37C forms a tightly coordinated complex with ESCRT machinery components: VPS28 (score 0.999), MVB12A (0.985), TSG101 (0.984), MVB12B (0.978), VPS36 (0.972), SNF8 (0.968), UBAP1 (0.965), CHMP2A (0.957), VPS25 (0.953), and CHMP6 (0.951). This interaction network positions VPS37C at the interface between early ESCRT-I recognition events and downstream ESCRT-II/III recruitment for multivesicular body formation.\n\nHPA expression data shows VPS37C is ubiquitously expressed across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with its housekeeping role in endosomal trafficking. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, vesicles, and primary cilium. Notably, blood cell specificity is group-enriched, aligning with recent findings that VPS37C facilitates erythroid differentiation by promoting EKLF/KLF1 transcription factor stability (2023).\n\nClinVar documents 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, suggesting loss-of-function mutations may cause disease through disrupted endosomal sorting. The mouse ortholog Q8R105 (352 aa) indicates strong conservation. Despite 27 publications, VPS37C remains partially characterized—its role in ciliary function, specific cargo selection mechanisms, and potential extra-ESCRT functions represent dark biology worthy of investigation.
