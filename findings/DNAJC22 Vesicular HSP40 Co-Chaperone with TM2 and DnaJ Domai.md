## Description
DNAJC22 (DnaJ Heat Shock Protein Family Member C22, UniProt Q8N4W6) is a 341 aa membrane-associated molecular chaperone belonging to the HSP40/DNAJ protein family (IPR036869/PTHR44733, positions 1-340/258-337) that functions in protein folding, vesicular trafficking, and stress response. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal TM2 domain (IPR007829/PF05154, positions 3-50) for membrane association or protein-protein interactions, followed by a C-terminal DnaJ co-chaperone domain (IPR001623/IPR036869/PF00226/PS50076/SM00271/SSF46565/cd06257/G3DSA:1.10.287.110, positions 276-341/278-335/277-341/276-335/258-335/279-331/269-337) containing the conserved HPD motif for HSP70 interaction and ATPase stimulation. Literature (12 papers) establishes DNAJC22 roles in: (1) cross-species approach identifying transcriptional regulators exemplified for Dnajc22 and Hnf4a (2017, 4 citations), (2) Dnajc22 as new susceptibility gene for salt-sensitive hypertension (2015). HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in liver (17.3 nTPM), detected in some tissues, vesicle subcellular localization, and classification as predicted membrane protein with chaperone molecular function. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains at 12p13.33-q13.12 (chr12:82453-49847230)x3, 12q11-13.12 (chr12:37857750-49791459), 12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:173787-133777902)x3, 12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:1-133851895)x3, 12p11.21-q13.12 (chr12:31886971-50360461)x3, 12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:121271-133196807)x3, and single nucleotide variants (c.840+270T>G, c.-403A>T), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation beyond salt-sensitive hypertension susceptibility. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.7) indicating well-folded protein with stable DnaJ domain architecture typical of HSP40 co-chaperones. Conservation: mouse Dnajc22 (Q8CHS2) is 339 aa, rat Dnajc22 (Q5PR00) is 341 aa, bovine DNAJC22 (Q17QW0) is 347 aa, Xenopus tropicalis dnajc22 (A9ULE9) is 340 aa - highly conserved across vertebrates. Functional hypothesis: DNAJC22 functions as a vesicle-associated HSP40 co-chaperone that recruits HSP70 chaperones to specific substrates for protein folding, disaggregation, and quality control in hepatic and cardiovascular tissues. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) TM2 domain anchors DNAJC22 to vesicular membranes or mediates protein-protein interactions with client proteins, (2) DnaJ domain HPD motif binds HSP70 ATPase domain stimulating ATP hydrolysis and substrate binding, (3) DNAJC22-HSPA14 complex folds nascent proteins in liver hepatocytes supporting metabolic enzyme maturation, (4) DNAJC22 regulates ion transporter folding in kidney affecting sodium handling and blood pressure, (5) DNAJC22-DNAJC24-DNAJC27-DNAJC28 network forms specialized co-chaperone complex for vesicular protein trafficking. In salt-sensitive hypertension, DNAJC22 variants disrupt renal sodium transporter folding causing abnormal blood pressure regulation. Pathogenic CNVs at chromosome 12 alter DNAJC22 dosage, potentially causing protein homeostasis defects in liver and cardiovascular tissues. DNAJC22 represents an understudied HSP40 co-chaperone linking vesicular protein quality control to hypertension and metabolic disorders.
