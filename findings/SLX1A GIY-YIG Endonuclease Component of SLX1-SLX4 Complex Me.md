## Description
SLX1A encodes a 275-amino acid structure-specific endonuclease subunit SLX1 homolog A (UniProt Q9BQ83), a catalytic component of the SLX1-SLX4 heterodimeric complex essential for DNA interstrand crosslink repair and Holliday junction resolution. The protein contains a GIY-YIG catalytic endonuclease domain (IPR000305, PF01541, positions 12-95/14-91; cd10455, positions 13-89) characteristic of structure-specific nucleases that cleave DNA at branched structures, plus a C-terminal zinc finger/RING-type domain (G3DSA:3.30.40.10, IPR013083, positions 183-252) mediating protein-protein interactions with SLX4 scaffold, classified within the SLX1 structure-specific endonuclease family (IPR027520, positions 9-260; IPR050381, positions 9-265) with 5'-flap endonuclease activity (GO:0017108). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.2, residues 1-275) indicating stable folding consistent with GIY-YIG endonuclease architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals SLX1A forms part of the DNA repair nuclease network with ultra-high confidence interactions: MUS81 (0.999, structure-specific endonuclease), SLX4 (0.999, DNA repair scaffold protein), ERCC4 (0.998, XPF endonuclease), EME1 (0.997, MUS81 partner), SLX1B (0.935, SLX1 paralog), FANCD2 (0.916, Fanconi anemia protein), SLX4IP (0.910, SLX4-interacting protein), TOP3A (0.907, DNA topoisomerase), WRN (0.905, Werner syndrome helicase), and ERCC1 (0.655, nucleotide excision repair protein), confirming its central role in DNA damage response and Fanconi anemia pathway. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasm localization consistent with DNA repair machinery, annotated for endonuclease/hydrolase/nuclease molecular functions and DNA damage/recombination/repair biological processes. ClinVar contains 94 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (16p11.2 deletions) and gains (16p11.2 duplications), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With only 6 publications, SLX1A is genuinely a dark gene: recent work shows SLX1 silencing overcomes Olaparib resistance in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer by disrupting SLX4-mediated DNA repair complexes (2025, 1 cit), colorectal neuroendocrine tumor genomics (2020, 4 cit), and telomeric sister chromatid exchange in chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (2016, 21 cit). The functional hypothesis: SLX1A functions as the catalytic nuclease subunit within the SLX1A-SLX4 complex, providing structure-specific endonuclease activity that resolves DNA Holliday junctions, processes DNA interstrand crosslinks, and cleaves 5'-flap structures during homologous recombination repair; the GIY-YIG domain catalyzes DNA cleavage at branched intermediates while the C-terminal zinc finger anchors SLX1A to the SLX4 scaffold that coordinates multiple repair nucleases (MUS81-EME1, XPF-ERCC1); SLX1A may function redundantly or distinctly from its paralog SLX1B, with tissue-specific or cell cycle-dependent expression patterns; disease-associated CNVs at 16p11.2 may disrupt SLX1A dosage, impairing DNA crosslink repair and sensitizing cells to PARP inhibitors (Olaparib) through synthetic lethality; nucleoplasmic localization and interactions with Fanconi anemia proteins (FANCD2) suggest roles in replication fork stability and genome maintenance during S-phase.
