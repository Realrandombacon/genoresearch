## Description
PTBP3 (Polypyrimidine Tract-Binding Protein 3; UniProt O95758) is a 552 amino acid nuclear RNA-binding protein with 87 publications, representing a moderately-characterized splicing factor functioning as a master regulator of alternative splicing that controls cancer metastasis, immune escape, and erythrocyte maturation through sequence-specific binding to polypyrimidine tracts in pre-mRNA introns.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals PTBP3 contains four RNA recognition motif (RRM) domains characteristic of the hnRNP-L/PTB family (IPR006536, residues 57-552). RRM1 spans residues 54-143 (cd12779), RRM2 spans 177-284 (cd12784/IPR021790), RRM3 spans 358-432 (IPR000504), and RRM4 spans 462-552 (cd12703/IPR033110). These RRMs belong to the nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily (IPR012677/G3DSA:3.30.70.330) with three distinct structural regions (42-163, 178-283, 351-452). GO annotations include RNA binding (GO:0003723), mRNA processing (GO:0006397), nucleus (GO:0005634), and regulation of cell differentiation (GO:0045595).
