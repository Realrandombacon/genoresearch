## Description
FAM199X (Family with Sequence Similarity 199, X-linked) encodes a 388 amino acid protein (UniProt Q6PEV8) localized to the nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and cytosol. The protein contains a conserved FAM199X domain (IPR029672, PF15814, PTHR32003) spanning residues 66-388, suggesting a structured functional core.

Expression profiling (HPA) shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, indicating a housekeeping or fundamental cellular role. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein.

BLAST analysis reveals exceptional evolutionary conservation: 97.7% identity with mouse (Mus musculus), 88.4% with Xenopus tropicalis, and 81.4% with zebrafish (Danio rerio). This high conservation across 400+ million years of evolution suggests critical functional constraint.

ClinVar documents 166 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome X (Xq21.1-28). These structural variants are associated with developmental disorders, though specific disease mechanisms remain uncharacterized.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: FAM199X likely functions as a nuclear scaffold protein that organizes chromatin architecture or facilitates transcriptional complex assembly. The X-linked location and high pathogenic variant burden suggest dosage sensitivity—haploinsufficiency may disrupt nuclear organization in developing tissues. The conserved FAM199X domain may mediate protein-protein interactions within nuclear complexes, potentially linking chromatin remodeling to cell cycle regulation.
