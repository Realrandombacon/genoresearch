## Description
BLCAP (Bladder Cancer-Associated Protein, UniProt P62952) is a small 87-amino acid nucleolar protein functioning as a tumor suppressor that regulates cell proliferation through ADAR-mediated RNA editing, Rb1 pathway inhibition, and STAT3 signaling modulation. Functional hypothesis: BLCAP functions as a small nucleolar tumor suppressor where its conserved N-terminal domain (aa 1-68) interacts with cell cycle regulators to inhibit proliferation; ADAR-mediated A-to-I RNA editing of BLCAP transcripts creates edited isoforms with altered function, where unedited BLCAP inhibits Rb1 phosphorylation and STAT3 activation to suppress tumor growth, while edited BLCAP loses this inhibitory capacity, promoting cell proliferation in cancers; the nucleolar localization suggests involvement in ribosome biogenesis or RNA processing. Mechanistic proposal: The BC10 domain (aa 1-67) forms the structured core that mediates protein-protein interactions with Rb1 and STAT3 pathway components; BLCAP normally inhibits Rb1 phosphorylation, maintaining Rb1 in its active growth-suppressive state, and inhibits STAT3 activation, blocking pro-proliferative signaling; ADAR (STRING 0.422) edits BLCAP pre-mRNA at specific adenosine residues, converting them to inosine (read as guanosine), creating protein variants with altered function; in colorectal and cervical cancers, increased BLCAP editing abolishes tumor suppressor activity, allowing Rb1 phosphorylation and STAT3 activation to drive proliferation (2017-2022, 12-45 cit); MALAT1 lncRNA sponges miR-339-5p to increase BLCAP expression, but the functional outcome depends on editing status (2019, 59 cit); NNAT interaction (STRING 0.968) suggests coordinated regulation of imprinted genes in differentiation; nucleolar localization (HPA) positions BLCAP near RNA processing machinery for efficient ADAR editing; medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 62.7) suggests conformational flexibility compatible with regulatory protein function and post-translational modification; skeletal muscle enrichment (452.1 nTPM) may reflect high metabolic/proliferative demands; ClinVar CNVs suggest BLCAP dosage affects cell cycle control, though no specific syndrome is defined; BLCAP represents a small tumor suppressor whose activity is dynamically regulated by RNA editing, with therapeutic implications for cancers with dysregulated ADAR activity.
