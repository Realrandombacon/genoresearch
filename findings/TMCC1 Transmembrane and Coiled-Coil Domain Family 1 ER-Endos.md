# TMCC1 (Transmembrane and Coiled-Coil Domain Family 1): ER-Endosome Membrane Contact Site Scaffold Regulating Endosome Fission and Membrane Tubulation via TEX28 Family Coiled-Coil Oligomerization, with Cancer-Enriched Expression and Pathogenic Copy Number Variants at 3q22.1

## Description
TMCC1 encodes a 653 aa transmembrane protein (UniProt O94876), also known as Cerebral Protein-11-Related, that functions as a critical scaffold at endoplasmic reticulum-endosome membrane contact sites (ER-endosome MCS), regulating endosome fission, membrane tubulation, and intracellular transport. The protein features a distinctive domain architecture: an N-terminal region of unknown function (residues 1-123); a central TEX28/TMCC family domain (IPR019394/PTHR17613, residues 124-651) characteristic of testis-specific and transmembrane coiled-coil proteins; and an extensive predicted transmembrane coiled-coil region (PF10267, residues 231-638) mediating homo-oligomerization and membrane anchoring. STRING interactions place TMCC1 within membrane trafficking and cytoskeletal networks: PLXNA1 (0.688), plexin-A1 semaphorin receptor regulating axon guidance and cell migration; CORO1C (0.639), coronin-1C actin-binding protein coordinating actin dynamics at membranes; KLF15 (0.634), transcription factor potentially regulating TMCC1 expression; CFAP100 (0.520), cilia and flagella-associated protein linking to microtubule-based transport; PODXL2 (0.515), podocalyxin-like protein involved in cell adhesion; PLXND1 (0.491), plexin-D1 semaphorin receptor; GATA2 (0.491), hematopoietic transcription factor; TEX28 (0.484), testis-specific TEX28 family member suggesting functional homology; KIAA0232 (0.452) and VSTM2B (0.447), additional membrane-associated proteins. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, classified as cancer-enriched and immune cell-enriched, consistent with roles in vesicle trafficking required for cell proliferation and immune function. ClinVar lists 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (3q13.31-26.31 spanning 116-172Mb, 3p26.3-q29 spanning 0.06-197Mb, 3q11.1-24 spanning 93-145Mb) and losses (3q21.3-22.1 spanning 127-133Mb), plus splice variants (c.1511+35G>A, c.1512-9del), demonstrating dosage sensitivity though specific disease associations remain undefined—likely contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders given cerebral protein designation. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 61.8), revealing partially structured protein with extensive coiled-coil regions interspersed with disordered segments—characteristic of membrane scaffolding proteins requiring flexibility for dynamic membrane remodeling. Mechanistically, TMCC1 operates through multiple integrated modes: (1) ER-endosome tethering—anchors ER membranes to endosomes facilitating lipid transfer and calcium signaling at contact sites; (2) Membrane curvature generation—coiled-coil oligomerization induces/stabilizes membrane tubulation during endosome fission; (3) Actin coordination—CORO1C interaction links TMCC1 to actin polymerization providing mechanical force for membrane scission; (4) Semaphorin signaling integration—PLXNA1/PLXND1 interactions suggest roles in growth cone guidance and cell migration; (5) Cancer progression—cancer-enriched expression and 2021/2024 studies link TMCC1-AS1 lncRNA to poor prognosis and epithelial-mesenchymal transition in liver cancer; (6) Neurological function—2023 mouse study shows circular RNA Tmcc1 improves astrocytic glutamate metabolism and spatial memory via NF-κB/CREB signaling in hepatic encephalopathy model. Disruption of TMCC1 causes defective endosome fission, impaired membrane trafficking, altered cell migration, and potential neurocognitive deficits.
