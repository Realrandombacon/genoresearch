## Description
TBC1D29 (TBC1 domain family member 29, UniProt Q4V771) is a small 81-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 1q21.1 within a region prone to copy-number variations and segmental duplications. InterPro analysis reveals a partial Rab-GAP-TBC domain architecture: truncated Rab-GAP-TBC domain (IPR000195/PS50086, aa 1-43) belonging to the Rab-GAP-TBC domain superfamily (IPR035969/SSF47923/G3DSA:1.10.472.80, aa 3-70) that typically functions as a Rab GTPase-activating enzyme. However, the canonical TBC domain is ~200 amino acids, and TBC1D29 contains only the N-terminal portion (~43 aa of the domain), suggesting this may be a truncated remnant, pseudogene-derived protein, or rapidly evolving gene fragment. The UniProt search also identified TBC1D29P (Q9UFV1, 150 aa) where the 'P' suffix typically indicates a pseudogene, supporting the hypothesis that TBC1D29 represents a degenerated Rab-GAP lineage. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves medium confidence (pLDDT 62.0), indicating a partially structured small protein consistent with an incomplete domain fold. Notably, STRING interaction network returned no partners (gene not found in STRING database), Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID (no expression data available), and ClinVar contains zero pathogenic variants (no clinical significance established) — this complete absence from major databases strongly suggests TBC1D29 is either a pseudogene, extremely lowly expressed, primate-specific, or technical artifact. Conservation is limited to great apes (orangutan A0A2J8TM50/A0A2J8TM46 isoforms, 105/55 aa) with no clear mouse ortholog, indicating recent evolutionary origin or rapid divergence. TBC1D29 likely represents a truncated Rab-GAP domain remnant that has lost catalytic function — the partial TBC domain (aa 1-43 of ~200 aa canonical) cannot form the complete arginine finger and catalytic pocket required for GTPase acceleration. If translated, it may function as a dominant-negative regulator by competing with full-length TBC proteins for Rab binding without catalyzing GTP hydrolysis, or it may be non-functional translational noise from a decaying pseudogene locus. The lack of conservation, expression data, interaction partners, and clinical variants supports classification as a borderline dark gene approaching pseudogene status.
