# ZNF641: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with TRIM28 Interaction and Dosage Sensitivity

**Date:** 2026-03-23T05:34:24.569278

**Quality Score:** 1.9/10  (E=3.8, D=0.0) [POOR]

## Description
ZNF641 (Zinc Finger Protein 641) is a 438-amino acid KRAB domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor encoded on chromosome 12q13.11 (UniProt Q96N77). InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB repressor domain (residues 108-149, PF01352; IPR001909) that mediates transcriptional silencing through recruitment of corepressor complexes, and three C2H2-type zinc finger motifs (residues 264-342, PF00096; IPR013087) for sequence-specific DNA binding. STRING database identifies 10 protein interaction partners with medium confidence scores (0.401-0.563), notably including TRIM28/KAP1 (score 0.518), the essential coreceptor for KRAB-ZNF proteins that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase for H3K9me3 deposition and heterochromatin formation. Additional partners include TADA2B (transcriptional coactivator), OXNAD1, and multiple coiled-coil domain proteins (CCDC74B, CCDC169, CCDC190, CCDC125). Human Protein Atlas confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, dual nucleoplasmic/nucleolar localization plus cytosolic presence, and classification as a DNA-binding transcription factor. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants encompassing ZNF641, including large-scale deletions and duplications on chromosome 12 (12p13.33-q13.12 and 12q11-q13.12 regions), indicating dosage sensitivity. The KRAB-TRIM28 interaction architecture strongly suggests ZNF641 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that silences target genes through epigenetic chromatin modification. Given ubiquitous expression and clinical CNV burden, ZNF641 likely regulates fundamental cellular processes such as cell cycle control or differentiation, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression contributing to developmental phenotypes through dysregulation of downstream target gene networks.
