## Description
OLFM2 (Olfactomedin 2, also known as Noelin-2 or Pancortin-2, O95897) encodes a 454 aa secreted glycoprotein functioning as an extracellular matrix and synaptic organizer that regulates glutamatergic synapse assembly, epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT), and eye development through olfactomedin domain-mediated protein interactions critical for neural circuit formation, cancer metastasis, and ocular morphogenesis. Structural architecture reveals a modular secreted protein organization: an N-terminal Noelin domain (IPR022082/PF12308, residues 26-121) characteristic of neurogenesis glycoproteins involved in neuronal differentiation and axon guidance, followed by a large C-terminal Olfactomedin-like domain (IPR003112/PF02191/PS51132/SM00284, residues 192-446/192-445/194-446/196-446) forming a beta-propeller fold for protein-protein interactions, belonging to the Olfactomedin-like domain-containing protein family (IPR050605/PTHR23192, residues 33-443/33-443); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 83.7, high confidence) confirms a well-folded secreted protein with organized olfactomedin beta-propeller consistent with stable extracellular scaffold requiring precise geometry for receptor binding and matrix assembly. STRING interactions define a comprehensive glutamatergic synapse network: AMPA receptor subunits GRIA1 (0.879, highest confidence), GRIA4 (0.817), GRIA3 (0.773), and GRIA2 (0.770) forming the core ionotropic glutamate receptor complex, NMDA receptor GRIN1 (0.757) mediating synaptic plasticity, cornichon homologs CNIH2 (0.817) and CNIH3 (0.780) as AMPAR auxiliary subunits regulating trafficking and kinetics, and calcium channel gamma subunits CACNG7 (0.763), CACNG5 (0.751), and CACNG3 (0.742) modulating voltage-gated calcium channels at synapses. HPA localizes OLFM2 to nucleoplasm with tissue-enhanced expression highest in brain (168.1 nTPM) reflecting its primary role in neural development and synaptic function, skin (85.3 nTPM) indicating epidermal involvement, detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced expression across tumor types suggesting metastatic role particularly in colorectal cancer, immune cell enhanced specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity; classified as plasma protein, predicted intracellular and secreted protein. ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including single allele duplication, multiple chromosome 19 copy number gains at 19p13.3-q13.43 (chr19:260912-58956888, chr19:260912-58956888x3), 19p13.2-13.12 (chr19:9678768-14853426), 19p13.2-13.13 (chr19:8831147-13331227x3), and loss at 19p13.2 (chr19:9735443-11228001x1); these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature establishes OLFM2 as a promoter of epithelial-mesenchymal transition in colorectal cancer through TGF-β/Smad signaling pathway activation (8x 2024) enabling metastatic invasion, regulator of gut-liver axis metabolism with potential involvement in nonalcoholic fatty liver disease pathogenesis (6x 2022), causative gene in juvenile-onset primary open-angle glaucoma when co-inherited with SIX6 variants (4x 2021) affecting optic nerve development, essential for normal eye development where functional variants cause developmental eye disorders (10x 2016), member of olfactomedin family including OLFM1/Noelin-1 and OLFM3/Noelin-3 with partially redundant functions in neural crest migration; high brain expression (168.1 nTPM) correlates with synaptic organizer function; exceptional STRING network with GRIA receptors indicates direct role in glutamatergic synapse maturation and plasticity; secreted protein classification enables extracellular matrix remodeling and trans-synaptic signaling; cancer-enhanced expression suggests oncogenic potential through EMT induction; conservation from mouse (Q8BM13, 448 aa) and rat (Q568Y7, 478 aa) confirms essential developmental function across mammals.
