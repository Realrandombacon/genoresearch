## Description
PFDN2 (Prefoldin Subunit 2) is a partially characterized molecular chaperone with 28 publications, representing a critical component of the hexameric prefoldin complex that captures and delivers nascent actin and tubulin substrates to the cytosolic chaperonin CCT/TRiC for proper folding. This compact 154-amino acid protein (UniProt Q9UHV9) features a conserved prefoldin beta-barrel architecture optimized for substrate binding and complex assembly. InterPro analysis reveals a Prefoldin beta-like domain (IPR002777/PF01920, positions 25-128) characteristic of the prefoldin superfamily, membership in the Prefoldin homologous superfamily (IPR009053, positions 21-128), and the Prefoldin subunit 2 family (IPR027235/PTHR13303, positions 17-154/entire protein) with GO annotations for protein folding (GO:0006457) and prefoldin complex (GO:0016272).
