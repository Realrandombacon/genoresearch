## Description
RBMY1F (UniProt: Q15415, aliases YRRM2) encodes a 496 amino acid RNA-binding motif protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 1 publication in Semantic Scholar. Located in the AZFb (azoospermia factor b) interval of Yq11.223 in the nonrecombining portion of the Y chromosome, RBMY1F is one of multiple copies of this locus with six highly similar functional copies identified. The encoded protein contains an RNA-binding motif in the N-terminus and four SRGY (serine, arginine, glycine, tyrosine) boxes in the C-terminus, and is required for sperm development, possibly by participating in pre-mRNA splicing in the testis. InterPro domain analysis reveals canonical RNA recognition motif architecture: IPR000504/PF00076 (RNA recognition motif domain, residues 8-85/10-79, GO:0003723 RNA binding), cd12382 (RRM found in hnRNP G/Y chromosome RNA recognition motif 1/hRBMY/testis-specific hnRNP G-T, residues 7-85), IPR035979 (RNA-binding domain superfamily, residues 6-115, GO:0003676 nucleic acid binding), IPR012604/PF08081 (RBM1CTR/NUC064 family, residues 174-218), IPR012677 (Nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily, residues 2-123), IPR050441/PTHR48034 (RNA-binding motif family, residues 3-251), and G3DSA:3.30.70.330 (homologous superfamily, residues 2-123). The RRM domain forms a characteristic beta-alpha-beta-beta-alpha-beta fold for single-stranded RNA binding. AlphaFold structural prediction yields low confidence (global pLDDT: 49.8), indicating a mostly disordered protein with a structured RRM domain but extensive intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) in the C-terminal portion containing the SRGY boxes - characteristic of RNA-binding proteins that function through dynamic protein-RNA and protein-protein interactions. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners, all Y-chromosome genes involved in spermatogenesis: BPY2B (basic charge Y-linked protein 2B, score 0.850 - highest), BPY2C (0.850), BPY2 (0.816), PRYP3 (PTPRZ1-related Y-linked pseudogene 3, 0.789), USP9Y (ubiquitin specific peptidase 9 Y-linked, 0.781 - deubiquitinase), DDX3Y (DEAD-box helicase 3 Y-linked, 0.765 - RNA helicase), CDY1 (chromodomain protein Y-linked 1, 0.762 - chromatin modifier), UTY (ubiquitously transcribed tetratricopeptide repeat containing Y-linked, 0.738 - histone demethylase), PRY2 (0.697), and PRY (0.685). This interaction network places RBMY1F within the Y-chromosome spermatogenesis machinery alongside RNA helicases (DDX3Y), ubiquitin pathway enzymes (USP9Y), and chromatin modifiers (CDY1, UTY), suggesting coordinated regulation of RNA processing and chromatin state during germ cell development. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched expression with testis specificity (3.0 nTPM, detected in single tissue), no detection in cancer or immune cells, classification as predicted intracellular protein, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and nucleoli - consistent with pre-mRNA splicing function. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number losses (deletions) and gains (duplications) spanning Yq11.222-12, consistent with AZFb microdeletion syndromes causing male infertility and spermatogenic failure - variants include large deletions removing entire AZFb regions. The single literature report (2017, cited 19x) describes copy number variations in testicular maturation arrest. Based on its 496 aa length, RRM domain architecture (residues 8-85), low AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 49.8), testis-specific expression (3.0 nTPM), nucleoplasmic/nucleolar localization, high-confidence STRING interactions with DDX3Y/USP9Y/CDY1 proteins, and AZFb location, RBMY1F functions as an RNA-binding protein facilitating pre-mRNA splicing during spermatogenesis. The structured RRM domain binds specific RNA sequences or structures in germ cell transcripts, while the disordered C-terminal region with SRGY boxes enables dynamic protein-protein interactions with splicing factors and chromatin modifiers. The SRGY boxes (serine-arginine-glycine-tyrosine rich regions) are characteristic of germ cell-specific RNA-binding proteins and likely mediate assembly of large ribonucleoprotein complexes. RBMY1F likely operates in concert with DDX3Y (RNA helicase unwinding RNA secondary structures) and USP9Y (deubiquitinase stabilizing splicing factors) to process pre-mRNAs required for germ cell differentiation. The nucleolar localization suggests involvement in ribosome biogenesis or rRNA processing in addition to mRNA splicing. AZFb deletions removing RBMY1F cause spermatogenic arrest, indicating essential function in meiosis or post-meiotic germ cell development. RBMY1F exemplifies the emerging class of Y-chromosome RNA-binding proteins that coordinate male germ cell gene expression through RNA processing rather than transcriptional regulation, with clinical significance in male infertility diagnosis.
