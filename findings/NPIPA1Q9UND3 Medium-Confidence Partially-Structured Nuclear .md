## Description
NPIPA1 (Nuclear pore complex interacting protein family member A1, also known as NPIP, NPIPA, morpheus) is a 350 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9UND3) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 51.2), characteristic of nuclear pore-associated proteins that function through protein-protein interactions with flexible domains enabling nucleocytoplasmic transport. The protein belongs to the nuclear pore complex interacting protein family (IPR009443, residues 9-345; PTHR15438, residues 9-345) and contains a nuclear pore complex interacting protein N-terminal domain (IPR054697, residues 22-243; PF06409, residues 22-243). NPIPA1 is located on chromosome 16p13.11 and is predicted to be involved in mRNA transport (GO:0051028) and protein transport (GO:0015031), located in nuclear membrane (GO:0031965), and part of nuclear pore (GO:0005648).
