## Description
TSPAN32 (Tetraspanin-32, Q96QS1) encodes a 320 aa four-transmembrane protein functioning as a tumor suppressor and immune regulator within the imprinted 11p15.5 domain. Structural analysis reveals canonical tetraspanin architecture with large extracellular loop EC2 (IPR008952/IPR042782/PF00335, residues 113-195) containing conserved cysteine residues for disulfide bonding and tetraspanin/peripherin family signature (IPR018499, residues 11-221). AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 72.3) confirms confident tetraspanin fold with four transmembrane helices and structured EC2 domain. STRING interactions reveal TSPAN32 operates within tetraspanin-enriched microdomains (TEMs) alongside C11orf21 (0.936), TSSC4 (0.929, fellow 11p15.5 imprinted gene), CD151 (0.805), CD63 (0.799), CD9 (0.790), CD81 (0.768), PHLDA2 (0.739, another 11p15.5 imprinted gene), and CD37 (0.694), suggesting co-clustering in membrane complexes regulating cell adhesion and signaling. HPA shows group-enriched expression in bone marrow (18.8 nTPM), heart muscle (10.7 nTPM), and lymphoid tissue (5.2 nTPM) with nucleoplasm localization. ClinVar documents 30 pathogenic CNV gains/duplications at 11p15.5-15.4 disrupting the imprinted domain. Literature (33 papers) demonstrates tumor-suppressive function: downregulated in chronic myeloid leukemia, rheumatoid arthritis (2024), Burkitt lymphoma (TCF3/ID3-regulated, 2025), and T-ALL (TAL1/NOTCH1-controlled, 2025). Mechanistic hypothesis: TSPAN32 organizes TEMs that sequester growth factor receptors or integrins, limiting proliferative signaling; loss via 11p15.5 amplification disrupts imprinting control, releasing oncogenic restraint. Co-expression with TSSC4/PHLDA2 suggests coordinated imprinted tumor suppressor network.
