# CCDC62: Nuclear Receptor Coactivator with Testis-Enriched Expression and Spermatogenic Failure Association

**Date:** 2026-03-23T01:37:41.614881

**Quality Score:** 2.48/10  (E=3.65, D=1.3) [WEAK]

## Description
CCDC62 (Q6P9F0, 684 aa) encodes a coiled-coil domain protein functioning as a nuclear receptor coactivator that preferentially enhances estrogen receptor (ESR1/ESR2) transactivation, also modulating progesterone (PGR), glucocorticoid (NR3C1), and androgen (AR) receptors. HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (65.5 nTPM) with nucleoplasmic and plasma membrane localization. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partners: HIP1R (0.873, endocytic trafficking), ACMSD (0.776, tryptophan metabolism), MCCC1 (0.773, leucine catabolism), SYT12/SYT11 (0.720, synaptic vesicle trafficking), and ESR2 (0.706, direct receptor binding). ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 12q24 and truncating mutations (p.Gln148Ter), with SPGF67 designation for spermatogenic failure 67. Conservation across mammals: mouse Q92731 ortholog (701 aa), rat ortholog. The coiled-coil architecture (residues ~100-600 predicted) mediates protein-protein interactions with nuclear receptors and transcriptional machinery. Mechanistically, CCDC62 recruits coactivator complexes to estrogen-responsive promoters, bridging ligand-bound receptors to basal transcription apparatus. Testis-specific expression suggests critical role in spermatogenesis; loss-of-function variants disrupt androgen/estrogen signaling in Sertoli/germ cells, causing oligospermia/azoospermia. This gene is NOT truly "dark" — it has characterized coactivator function but remains understudied in reproductive disease contexts.
