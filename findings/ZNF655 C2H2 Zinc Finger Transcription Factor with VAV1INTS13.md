## Description
ZNF655 (Gene ID: 79027, UniProt Q8N720) encodes a 491 aa Krüppel-type C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor mapping to chromosome 7q22.1 (99,558,641-99,576,452 bp). Multiple evidence lines support classification as a DNA-binding transcriptional regulator: (1) InterPro domains confirm canonical C2H2 zinc finger architecture: three C2H2-type zinc fingers (PF00096, aa 212-234, 240-262, 303-325; IPR013087, aa 212-239, 240-267, 303-325) within beta-beta-alpha zinc finger folds (G3DSA:3.30.160.60)—C2H2 domains mediate sequence-specific DNA binding; (2) STRING interactions reveal connections to transcriptional/immune regulators: VAV1 (0.602, RhoGEF proto-oncogene), INTS13 (0.591, Integrator complex subunit 13), TRIM28 (0.573, transcriptional corepressor/KAP1), ANKHD1 (0.485, ankyrin repeat protein)—suggesting roles in transcriptional regulation and chromatin remodeling; (3) HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic and plasma membrane localization; (4) ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic variants (7q22.1 deletions/duplications spanning 2-10 Mb and intronic SNVs), linking ZNF655 dosage to 7q22.1 chromosomal instability; (5) AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 53.8) indicates medium confidence with partially disordered regions—typical of transcription factors with flexible linkers between structured zinc finger domains. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF655 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor that recruits chromatin-modifying complexes (TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor, INTS13/Integrator complex) to regulate target gene expression. The C2H2 zinc fingers provide modular DNA recognition, while interaction with VAV1 suggests potential crosstalk with Rho GTPase signaling pathways. TRIM28 interaction indicates ZNF655 may recruit heterochromatin machinery (SETDB1 histone methyltransferase, NuRD complex) for transcriptional repression. Ubiquitous expression indicates housekeeping transcriptional role, while 7q22.1 location in a fragile region raises possibility of co-regulation with neighboring genes. Dosage sensitivity (21 variants) indicates stoichiometric balance critical for transcriptional networks. This represents a "dark" transcription factor awaiting target gene identification and functional validation.
