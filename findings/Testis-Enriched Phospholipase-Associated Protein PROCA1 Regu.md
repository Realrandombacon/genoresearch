## Description
PROCA1 (Protein interacting with Cyclin A1, also known as Group III Phospholipase A2-like protein) is a 364 aa intracellular protein functioning as a regulator of lipid metabolism, cell cycle progression, and chemoresistance. Despite 18 literature references, its precise enzymatic activity and physiological substrates remain partially obscure, classifying it as a 'partially characterized' dark gene. InterPro analysis reveals a central Phospholipase A2-like domain (IPR016090/PF05826, aa 54-132) with homology to Group III PLA2 enzymes (cd04705, aa 32-132), suggesting potential glycerophospholipase activity (GO:0004623) for releasing arachidonic acid from membrane phospholipids. However, AlphaFold predicts a globally disordered structure (pLDDT 45.9), indicating that PROCA1 functions primarily as an intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) or scaffold rather than a canonical folded enzyme—the PLA2 domain may represent a degenerate or regulatory module. HPA shows striking testis-specific enrichment (122.2 nTPM) with additional expression in cancer tissues, localizing to the nuclear membrane and Golgi apparatus, consistent with roles in spermatogenesis and membrane trafficking. STRING interactions place PROCA1 in a network linking cell cycle, ubiquitination, and lipid metabolism: FAU (0.796, ubiquitin-like ribosomal protein), TBC1D22B (0.707, Rab GTPase regulator), CCNA1 (0.602, Cyclin A1—its namesake partner), GRPR (0.614, Gastrin-Releasing Peptide Receptor), and CUL7 (0.551, E3 ubiquitin ligase). ClinVar lists 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including a critical splice site mutation (c.440+2T>G) and large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 17, implicating PROCA1 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders and potentially male infertility. Recent literature (2024) directly links PROCA1 mutations to teratozoospermia (abnormal sperm morphology) in humans and Proca1 knockout mice show fertility defects, while 2023 studies identify PROCA1 as part of the miR-1275/PROCA1/ZFP36L2 axis that stabilizes BCL2 mRNA to confer cisplatin resistance in lung adenocarcinoma.
