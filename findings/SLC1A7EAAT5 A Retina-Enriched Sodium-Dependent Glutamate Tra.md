# SLC1A7/EAAT5: A Retina-Enriched Sodium-Dependent Glutamate Transporter Regulating Visual Signal Termination and Neuropsychiatric Susceptibility

## Description
SLC1A7 (UniProt O00341, also known as EAAT5) encodes a 560 amino acid sodium-dependent excitatory amino acid transporter belonging to the solute carrier family 1. InterPro analysis confirms canonical sodium:dicarboxylate symporter architecture including Sodium:dicarboxylate symporter family domains (IPR001991, PF00375, aa 22-475), conserved sites (IPR018107, aa 53-67/371-394), and Dicarboxylate/Amino Acid:Cation Symporter superfamily membership (IPR036458, IPR050746, PTHR11958). AlphaFold structure (AF-O00341-F1) predicts a well-folded transporter with high confidence (global pLDDT 77.4), consistent with active glutamate/aspartate symport across neuronal membranes. HPA expression reveals tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in retina (37.4 nTPM), detected in many tissues, classified as metabolic proteins and transporters with cell type-enhanced expression, reflecting its specialized role in visual processing. Literature (21 papers) confirms SLC1A7 as a retina-specific glutamate transporter mediating presynaptic inhibition in photoreceptors, with downregulation associated with depressive mood in low socioeconomic status populations and involvement in glutamate homeostasis at photoreceptor synapses. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number gains/losses on chromosome 1p34.1-31.1), indicating clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to neuropsychiatric disorders or retinal degeneration. STRING interactions identify medium-confidence partners including MCHR1 (0.585), SLC3A2 (0.559), SLC13A2 (0.488), GLUL (0.429), and other amino acid transporters (SLC17A8, SLC7A2, SLC7A5, SLC38A1), placing SLC1A7 within neuronal amino acid transport and glutamate metabolism networks. Functional hypothesis: SLC1A7 functions as a retina-enriched sodium-dependent glutamate transporter that terminates visual signals by clearing synaptic glutamate at photoreceptor terminals while providing presynaptic inhibition; its downregulation in depressive states suggests involvement in mood regulation via extra-retinal glutamate homeostasis, while copy number variations may disrupt neuronal excitability contributing to neuropsychiatric susceptibility. Mechanistic proposal: SLC1A7 mediates sodium-coupled glutamate uptake at photoreceptor synapses, preventing excitotoxicity and enabling rapid signal termination; reduced expression impairs glutamate clearance leading to altered synaptic transmission in both visual pathways and mood-regulating circuits, potentially explaining associations with depression and neuropsychiatric phenotypes through disrupted excitatory/inhibitory balance.
