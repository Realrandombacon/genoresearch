## Description
TCTN2 (Tectonic family member 2, also known as Tectonic-2, Q96GX1) encodes a 697 aa transmembrane protein functioning as an essential component of the ciliary transition zone (TZ) complex that establishes the diffusion barrier between ciliary and plasma membrane compartments, thereby regulating ciliary protein composition, Hedgehog signaling, and embryonic development through assembly of the MKS-JBTS (Meckel syndrome-Joubert syndrome) module. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 74.4): Tectonic-1-3 domain (IPR011677/PF07773, residues 276-424/435-622) forming two tandem repeats that constitute the characteristic DUF1619 fold essential for TZ scaffold assembly, N-terminal domain of DUF1619 (IPR057724/PF25752, residues 146-260) establishing the N-terminal structural module potentially involved in membrane association or partner binding, Tectonic-1-3 family (IPR040354/PTHR14611, residues 9-683) spanning nearly the entire protein defining evolutionary conservation across metazoans. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence ciliopathy module network: Tectonic-1 TCTN1 (0.997, highest) as paralogous TZ component forming heteromeric complex, Centrosomal and coiled-coil-containing protein 2D2A CC2D2A (0.996) as JBTS-associated TZ protein establishing diffusion barrier, Transmembrane protein 67 TMEM67 (0.995) as Meckelin receptor tyrosine kinase regulating Wnt signaling, Tectonic-3 TCTN3 (0.992) as third tectonic family member completing the trimeric scaffold, Meckel syndrome type 1 protein MKS1 (0.988) as core MKS module component, B9 domain-containing protein 1 B9D1 (0.987) and B9D2 (0.970) as conserved ciliary proteins forming MKS complex, Transmembrane protein 216 TMEM216 (0.980) as Joubert syndrome protein partnering with TMEM67, Centrosomal protein 290 CEP290 (0.979) as large TZ scaffolding protein linking to NPHP module, and Transmembrane protein 231 TMEM231 (0.880) as additional TZ component. HPA classifies TCTN2 as disease-related gene/predicted intracellular and membrane protein with cilium biogenesis/degradation biological process; tissue-enhanced RNA specificity with highest expression in choroid plexus (37.2 nTPM) indicating cerebrospinal fluid production and blood-CSF barrier cilia function; detected in all tissues reflects ubiquitous ciliary expression though variable levels, low cancer specificity suggests constitutive expression in ciliated cells, low immune cell specificity confirms non-immune function, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity reflects specific ciliated epithelial cell expression, and subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus, microtubules, and primary cilium transition zone confirms dual trafficking/ciliary roles. ClinVar documents 136 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions c.1206del (p.Phe402fs) truncating the second tectonic domain, duplications c.721dup (p.Leu241fs) and c.704dup (p.Cys236fs) disrupting the N-terminal tectonic region, nonsense c.71G>A (p.Trp24Ter) eliminating most of the protein, splice site variants c.191-2A>T and c.1505+2T>C affecting exon recognition, and large-scale chromosome 12 copy number gains at 12q23.1-24.33 (chr12:99532287-133777902x3), 12q24.21-24.33 (chr12:116422123-133777902x3), and 12q24.22-24.33 (chr12:117533207-133777902x3) encompassing the TCTN2 locus at 12q24.11; these variants cause autosomal recessive ciliopathies including Joubert syndrome (cerebellar vermis hypoplasia, ataxia, developmental delay, retinal dystrophy, renal disease) and Meckel syndrome (encephalocele, polydactyly, polycystic kidneys, lethal perinatal phenotype) though ClinVar lists 0 specific diseases, representing severe developmental disorders through disrupted ciliary signaling. Conservation from mouse Q2MV57 (700 aa, 100% length relative to human) and rat Q3B7D3 (700 aa, 100% identity) confirms essential ciliary function across mammals with high sequence identity in tectonic domains reflecting strong evolutionary constraint on TZ assembly; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 74.4) enables mapping of tandem tectonic domain interfaces and potential interaction surfaces; 136 ClinVar variants establish comprehensive mutational landscape for ciliopathy diagnosis and genetic counseling; mechanistic proposal involves TCTN2-mediated assembly of MKS-JBTS module at ciliary base through homo/heterotypic tectonic domain interactions with TCTN1/TCTN3 paralogs, formation of Y-shaped linker structures connecting axonemal microtubules to ciliary membrane establishing selective permeability barrier, regulation of Smoothened/Patched trafficking controlling Hedgehog signal transduction critical for neural tube patterning and limb development, choroid plexus-specific expression supporting ependymal cilia function in CSF flow and barrier integrity, Golgi localization suggesting role in ciliary protein sorting and vesicular trafficking to ciliary base, microtubule association enabling coordination with basal body docking and axoneme nucleation, Joubert syndrome pathogenesis through partial loss-of-function allowing residual ciliary function compatible with postnatal survival, Meckel syndrome through complete loss-of-function causing catastrophic ciliary failure incompatible with life, and genetic interactions with TMEM67/TMEM216/B9D1 explaining phenotypic variability and modifier effects in ciliopathy patients.
