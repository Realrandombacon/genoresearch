HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasm/nuclear membrane localization, classified as serine/threonine/tyrosine kinase. ClinVar lists 33 pathogenic variants including large copy number losses/gains at 2q32-33. Functional mechanism: CLK1 phosphorylates SR proteins on serine residues within RS domains, controlling their subcellular distribution (nuclear speckles vs nucleoplasm) and spliceosome recruitment. This regulates alternative splicing decisions for thousands of transcripts. Dysregulation implicated in cancer (aberrant splicing isoforms), neurodegeneration (tau splicing), and developmental disorders. The kinase acts as a splicing rheostat - concentration-dependent effects on splice site selection make it a potential therapeutic target for splicing-modulating drugs.
