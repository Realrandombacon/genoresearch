## Description
MYDGF (alias C19orf10, myeloid-derived growth factor) encodes a 173 amino acid well-folded secreted protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 87.8) containing a UPF0556 domain (PF10572, aa 40-170) and MYDGF family signature (IPR018887, aa 1-173). HPA classifies the protein as plasma protein with predicted secreted topology, ubiquitous tissue distribution, and Gene Ontology annotations for angiogenesis and apoptosis biological processes. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 19p13.3 copy number gains and losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with ER stress and secretory pathway components: IL25 (interleukin-25 cytokine, 0.840), SSR4 (signal sequence receptor subunit 4, ER translocation, 0.695), COPE (coatomer subunit epsilon, vesicle trafficking, 0.644), TMED9 (transmembrane p24 family protein 9, ER-Golgi transport, 0.607), HPCAL1 (hippocalcin-like protein 1, calcium sensing, 0.591), SDF2L1 (stromal cell-derived factor 2-like 1, ER stress response, 0.567), TXNDC11 (thioredoxin domain-containing protein 11, ER oxidoreductase, 0.531), CRELD2 (cysteine-rich ER-localized protein 2, unfolded protein response, 0.511), XBP1 (X-box binding protein 1, UPR transcription factor, 0.505), and P4HB (protein disulfide isomerase, ER chaperone, 0.491). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q9CPT4 166 aa, bovine P62248 174 aa) indicates functional constraint. The UPF0556 domain architecture combined with secreted topology and ER stress response protein interactions strongly suggests MYDGF functions as a secreted cytokine-like factor involved in unfolded protein response signaling and intercellular communication during ER stress. The protein likely undergoes canonical secretory pathway processing through ER-Golgi vesicular transport (COPE/TMED9 interactions) and may modulate angiogenic responses through paracrine signaling. Interaction with XBP1/CRELD2/SDF2L1 places MYDGF within the IRE1-XBP1 arm of the unfolded protein response, suggesting potential role in coordinating tissue repair or immune modulation during proteotoxic stress. The angiogenesis annotation indicates potential involvement in vascular remodeling or wound healing. Pathogenic 19p13.3 CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting secreted cytokine production, affecting tissue repair capacity and ER stress adaptation, potentially contributing to vascular defects and impaired stress response in metabolically active tissues.
