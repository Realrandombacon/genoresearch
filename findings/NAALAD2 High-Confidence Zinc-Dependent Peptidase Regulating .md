## Description
NAALAD2 encodes a 740 aa zinc-dependent metallopeptidase (UniProt Q9Y3Q0) localized to the plasma membrane and extracellular space, functioning as a critical regulator of retinal development, cancer progression, and neuroblastoma. InterPro analysis confirms its canonical peptidase architecture: PA domain (IPR003137, aa 161-249), Peptidase M28 domain (IPR007484, aa 347-550), and Transferrin receptor-like dimerisation domain (IPR007365, aa 617-737), with homology to glutamate carboxypeptidase II (IPR039373). AlphaFold predicts an extremely high-confidence structure (pLDDT 95.1), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme essential for substrate recognition. STRING interactions reveal a specialized network in cancer and neural tissues: DPP4 (0.810, dipeptidyl peptidase-4, primary partner), NAALADL2 (0.649, paralog), GRM3 (0.502, metabotropic glutamate receptor), FOLH1 (0.451, prostate-specific membrane antigen), and TRIM64 (0.490, E3 ubiquitin ligase). HPA expression data is unavailable, but literature confirms tissue-specific roles. ClinVar reports 17 pathogenic variants, predominantly large 11q copy number losses (x5), suggesting haploinsufficiency; specific intronic SNVs may affect splicing or stability. Literature (12 papers) confirms emerging roles: (1) Retinal Development - NAALAD2 mutations disrupting photoreceptor and retinal pigment epithelial cell fate; (2) Prostate Cancer - promoter methylation predicting biochemical recurrence; (3) Neuroblastoma - circRNA-NAALAD2 associated with clinical features; (4) Epigenetic Regulation - methylation of NAALAD2 as a cancer biomarker. Functional hypothesis: NAALAD2 functions through: (1) Peptidase Activity - cleaving N-acetylated peptides or glutamate derivatives to modulate signaling; (2) DPP4/GRM3 Interaction - regulating dipeptidyl peptidase and glutamate receptor pathways in neural and cancer cells; (3) Epigenetic Silencing - promoter methylation inactivating NAALAD2 to drive prostate cancer progression; (4) Haploinsufficiency - 11q deletion leading to developmental defects in retina. Disease implications include Retinal Dystrophy, Prostate Cancer, and Neuroblastoma. Despite clinical variant burden and high structural confidence, the precise substrates and full signaling mechanisms remain unexplored.
