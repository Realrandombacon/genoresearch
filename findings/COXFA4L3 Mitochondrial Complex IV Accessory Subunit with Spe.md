## Description
COXFA4L3 (C15orf48/NMES1) encodes an 83-amino acid mitochondrial protein that functions as an accessory subunit of cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV) in the electron transport chain. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the NADH-ubiquinone reductase MLRQ subunit family (IPR010530, PF06522, PTHR14256), spanning residues 8-59. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with very high confidence (pLDDT 90.3), consistent with a stable mitochondrial membrane protein.\n\nSTRING interactions show strong associations with Complex IV subunits: COX8A (0.705), COX7C (0.694), COX6A1 (0.690), COX5A (0.690), COX7A2 (0.675), COX5B (0.659), COX4I1 (0.649), plus NDUFA4 (0.904) and NDUFA4L2 (0.653) from Complex I. HPA shows tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in intestine (866.5 nTPM), detected across many tissues. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including c.134A>G (p.Lys45Arg) and multiple copy number variations on 15q.\n\nRecent literature (only 3 papers) reveals COXFA4L3 undergoes an isoform switch from COXFA4 during spermatogenesis, enhancing Complex IV activity and ATP synthesis for sperm motility. Knockout mice show impaired mitochondrial function. This represents a tissue-specific paralogue that optimizes oxidative phosphorylation in high-energy demand contexts.
