## Description
SLC7A14 (Q8TBB6, 771 aa), also known as Solute Carrier Family 7 Member 14 or PPP1R142, is a glycosylated cationic amino acid transporter belonging to the amino acid/polyamine transporter I family (PTHR43243). InterPro identifies comprehensive transporter architecture: amino acid/polyamine transporter I family (IPR002293, PF13520, G3DSA:1.20.1740.10, aa 50-444/53-434), cationic amino acid transporter C-terminal domain (IPR029485, PF13906, aa 627-677), with 14 predicted transmembrane domains typical of SLC7 family lysosomal transporters. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 72.7, residues 1-771) consistent with well-folded multi-pass transmembrane transporter containing ordered transmembrane helices forming substrate-binding cavity. HPA shows brain-enriched expression (brain 15.0 nTPM, pituitary gland 6.7 nTPM, retina 5.7 nTPM, group enriched); localized to nucleoplasm, vesicles, plasma membrane, and cytosol; classified as disease-related gene (retinitis pigmentosa), potential drug target, and predicted membrane protein involved in amino acid transport. Functional hypothesis: SLC7A14 functions as a lysosomal membrane cationic amino acid transporter that mediates efflux of arginine, lysine, and ornithine from lysosomal lumen to cytosol via proton-coupled antiport mechanism. The 14-TM structure creates a central substrate-binding cavity with specificity for basic amino acids.
