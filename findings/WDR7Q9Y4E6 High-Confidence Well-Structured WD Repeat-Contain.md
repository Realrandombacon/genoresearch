## Description
WDR7 (WD repeat domain 7, also known as TRAG) is a 1490 amino acid nucleoplasmic/cytosolic scaffold protein (UniProt Q9Y4E6) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 73.8), characteristic of WD40 repeat proteins that function through beta-propeller domains to mediate protein-protein interactions, vesicular trafficking, and transcriptional regulation. The protein belongs to the WD40 repeat family (IPR001680, residues 5-49, 53-95, 208-242; PF00400 WD domain G-beta repeat, residues 20-47, 59-95, 456-498; IPR019775 WD40 repeat conserved site, residues 34-48, 485-499; IPR036322 WD40-repeat-containing domain superfamily, residues 1359-1481), YVTN repeat-like/quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase superfamily (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 7-237, 402-734, 1356-1451; IPR011047 Quinoprotein alcohol dehydrogenase-like superfamily, residues 57-594; IPR015943 WD40/YVTN repeat-like-containing domain superfamily, residues 7-237, 402-734, 1356-1451), WDR72-like family (IPR049916 WDR72-like, residues 5-1490), and alpha-solenoid domain family (IPR057848 WDR72-like alpha-solenoid domain, residues 1014-1049; PF23123 WDR72 alpha-solenoid domain, residues 1-1490). WDR7 is located on chromosome 18q21.31 and is predicted to be located in nucleoplasm and cytosol as a scaffold protein.
