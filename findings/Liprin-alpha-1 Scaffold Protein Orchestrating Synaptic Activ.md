## Description
PPFIA1 encodes Liprin-alpha-1 (UniProt Q13136), a 1202 amino acid scaffold protein essential for organizing presynaptic active zones, regulating cell migration, and anchoring receptor tyrosine phosphatases. The protein functions as a multivalent hub, binding the intracellular domains of LAR-family phosphatases (PTPRF, PTPRS, PTPRD) and recruiting effectors like ERC1/CAST, GIT1, and UNC13 to assemble synaptic machinery. InterPro analysis reveals three C-terminal Sterile Alpha Motif (SAM) domains (IPR001660, residues 875-1120) mediating oligomerization, and a large N-terminal coiled-coil region (IPR057892, residues 250-497) for partner recruitment. STRING interactions confirm high-confidence partnerships with PTPRF (0.997), PPP2CA/PPP2R1A (protein phosphatase 2A subunits, 0.994), ERC1 (0.987), and GIT1 (0.931), defining its role in the LAR-Liprin-ERC complex that tethers synaptic vesicles. Although HPA RNA-seq data is unavailable for this specific entry, literature indicates ubiquitous expression with enrichment in brain and developing tissues. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, primarily large copy number gains/losses at 11q13.3, suggesting haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity may underlie neurodevelopmental phenotypes, though no specific monogenic syndrome is yet named. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 68.4) predicts medium confidence, consistent with a flexible scaffold containing ordered SAM domains and extended disordered linkers. Recent literature (76 papers) highlights roles in: (1) Overcoming imatinib resistance in CML via miR-181a regulation; (2) Promoting esophageal squamous cell carcinoma metastasis; (3) Forming novel ALK fusions in lung adenocarcinoma responsive to alectinib. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: PPFIA1 acts as a dynamic spatial organizer that clusters LAR-phosphatases to dephosphorylate specific substrates at leading edges (migration) and active zones (synapse), while simultaneously scaffolding vesicle tethering factors (UNC13, ERC1) to prime neurotransmitter release. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: Upon membrane recruitment, PPFIA1 oligomerizes via SAM domains to form a lattice that concentrates PTPRF/PTPRS, locally suppressing phosphorylation of cytoskeletal regulators (e.g., focal adhesion kinases) to promote turnover, while its N-terminus binds ERC1/GIT1 to dock synaptic vesicles, coupling adhesion signaling to exocytosis.
