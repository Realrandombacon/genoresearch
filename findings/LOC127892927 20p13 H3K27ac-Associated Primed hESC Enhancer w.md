# LOC127892927: 20p13 H3K27ac-Associated Primed hESC Enhancer with No Clinical Variants — Putative Pluripotency Regulatory Element

**Date:** 2026-03-19T03:12:22.258643

**Quality Score:** 2.88/10  (E=2.35, D=3.4) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC127892927 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 20p13 (genomic coordinates: 31988016-31988920 on NC_000020.11, spanning approximately 904 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in primed human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it is marked by the H3K27ac histone modification (acetylation of histone H3 lysine 27 — a canonical active enhancer chromatin mark distinguishing active from poised enhancers). An overlapping subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. The H3K27ac mark indicates active transcriptional regulatory function in primed pluripotent stem cells (post-implantation epiblast-like state). ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. This absence of ClinVar entries is consistent with other hESC enhancers in this series (LOC127894026: 0 variants) and contrasts with silencers showing strong triplosensitivity or haploinsufficiency patterns. The 20p13 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including MACROD2 (mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase diphtheria toxin-like 2 - chromatin regulation, DNA damage response), ADRB1 (adrenergic receptor beta-1 - cardiac function, lipolysis), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a primed hESC-specific enhancer marked by H3K27ac, LOC127892927 likely functions to activate transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and lineage priming. The dual enhancer/silencer activity suggests context-dependent regulation, potentially activating pluripotency factors in stem cells while repressing differentiation genes in somatic lineages. The absence of ClinVar variants suggests either functional redundancy, essentiality (deleterious variants are embryonic lethal), or that this element regulates genes with buffered dosage sensitivity in post-embryonic development.
