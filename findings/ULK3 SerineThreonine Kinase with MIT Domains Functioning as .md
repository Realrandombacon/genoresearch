# ULK3: Serine/Threonine Kinase with MIT Domains Functioning as Dual Regulator of Hedgehog Signaling and Autophagy Through SUFU Phosphorylation and ESCRT Complex Interactions

## Description
ULK3 (Q6PHR2) encodes a 472 amino acid serine/threonine protein kinase belonging to the Unc-51-like kinase family that functions as a critical regulator of both Hedgehog signaling pathway and autophagy through phosphorylation of key pathway components. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture: an N-terminal catalytic kinase domain (IPR000719/PF00069, positions 14-270/17-270) containing protein kinase active site residues (IPR008271, positions 133-145) and ATP binding region (IPR017441/PS00107, positions 20-49) classified within protein kinase-like domain superfamily (IPR011009, positions 11-271) and specifically as Atg1-like serine/threonine-protein kinase (IPR045269, positions 9-463); followed by two C-terminal MIT domains (IPR007330/PF04212, positions 277-354/372-450/281-345/376-442) classified within MIT domain superfamily (IPR036181, positions 274-362/366-445) mediating interactions with ESCRT-III machinery and membrane trafficking components. CATH-Gene3D homologous superfamily matches confirm transferase/phosphotransferase architecture (G3DSA:1.10.510.10, positions 94-276; G3DSA:1.20.58.80, positions 278-352/372-449; G3DSA:3.30.200.20, positions 2-93). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 88.6, high confidence) consistent with stable kinase-MIT modular organization required for coordinated signaling and membrane trafficking functions. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with Hedgehog pathway regulators and autophagy machinery: SUFU (0.977), suppressor of fused negative regulator of Hedgehog signaling that ULK3 phosphorylates to release GLI transcription factors; IST1 (0.933), ESCRT-III associated protein interacting via MIT domains; GLI1/GLI2/GLI3 (0.808-0.840), Hedgehog pathway transcription factors activated downstream of SUFU phosphorylation; ATG5/ATG3/ATG12/ATG13 (0.792-0.813), core autophagy proteins indicating ULK3's role in autophagosome formation; PIK3C3 (0.786), class III phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase essential for autophagy initiation. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, acrosome subcellular localization suggesting potential role in sperm function and fertilization, classified under enzymes/kinases with autophagy biological process and serine/threonine-protein kinase molecular function. ClinVar contains 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (c.625G>A p.Gly209Arg in kinase domain), splice variants (c.1246+2T>G), and large copy number losses/gains spanning chromosome 15q24.1-26.3 regions (e.g., chr15:74979036-81960184 deletions, chr15:73506509-102429112 duplications, chr15:67358491-91644328 amplifications), highlighting dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (118 papers) demonstrates ULK3 phosphorylates SUFU releasing GLI transcription factors to activate Hedgehog target genes; ULK3 essential for autophagy flux in multiple myeloma cell survival; BIN1 deficiency enhances ULK3-dependent autophagic flux reducing dendritic size in hippocampal neurons linking to Alzheimer's disease risk; genetic associations between ULK3 and epilepsy identified through Mendelian randomization studies; ULK3 identified as prognostic immune-related biomarker in esophageal cancer. The mechanistic hypothesis: ULK3 functions dually as (1) a positive regulator of Hedgehog signaling through direct phosphorylation of SUFU at specific serine/threonine residues within its kinase domain (positions 133-145 active site), causing SUFU conformational change that releases sequestered GLI transcription factors enabling their nuclear translocation and target gene activation; and (2) an autophagy initiator phosphorylating ATG proteins or regulating autophagosome formation through ATG5-ATG12 conjugation system; the two MIT domains (positions 277-354, 372-450) enable ULK3 recruitment to ESCRT-III complexes via IST1 interaction positioning ULK3 at endosomal membranes where it may coordinate membrane remodeling events required for both Hedgehog signal transduction and autophagosome biogenesis; acrosome localization suggests specialized function in sperm capacitation potentially through regulated exocytosis or membrane fusion events; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 88.6) indicates rigid kinase-MIT domain interfaces enabling precise substrate positioning; copy number variations on 15q may alter ULK3 dosage disrupting Hedgehog-autophagy crosstalk contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes, cancer progression, or reproductive defects.
