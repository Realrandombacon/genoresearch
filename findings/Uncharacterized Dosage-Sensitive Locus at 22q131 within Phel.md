# Uncharacterized Dosage-Sensitive Locus at 22q13.1 within Phelan-McDermid Syndrome Region with Pathogenic CNV Burden

## Description
LOC100506271 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 22q13.1 (NC_000022.11:37354838-37352189) spanning approximately 2.6 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000237862 but reported no detectable protein expression data, indicating this locus is not represented in the HPA protein atlas. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all large copy number alterations: duplications (trisomy) of 22q11.1-13.33 (chr22:16916743-50739785, ~33.8 Mb; chr22:16916608-50739836, ~33.8 Mb; chr22:37061769-50738932, ~13.7 Mb) and deletions (monosomy) of 22q12.3-13.1 (chr22:36552376-37669915, ~1.1 Mb; chr22:35333993-38900177, ~3.6 Mb). These structural variants are associated with 22q13 deletion syndrome (Phelan-McDermid syndrome), characterized by global developmental delay, intellectual disability, absent or severely delayed speech, hypotonia, and autistic behaviors, as well as 22q13 duplication syndrome featuring dysmorphic features and developmental anomalies. The 22q13.1 region is immediately telomeric to SHANK3 (SH3 and multiple ankyrin repeat domains 3, postsynaptic density scaffold protein critical for synapse formation and function, haploinsufficiency causes Phelan-McDermid syndrome) and contains other dosage-sensitive genes including BRD1 (bromodomain containing 1, chromatin regulator) and MAPK8IP2 (MAPK8 interacting protein 2, JNK signaling scaffold). LOC100506271 likely functions as part of the 22q13.1 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to Phelan-McDermid syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology, topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries, or long-range dysregulation of SHANK3 expression. The presence of pathogenic CNV burden (5 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where both hemizygosity and trisomy disrupt neurodevelopmental processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization.
