Functional hypothesis: LOC100506472 likely represents a critical 22q13.1 postsynaptic/neurodevelopmental regulatory protein, potentially functioning as a synaptic scaffolding protein or neuronal signaling adaptor coordinating SHANK3-MAPK12-SLC16A5-PARVB expression in excitatory synapse organization, neuronal energy metabolism, and actin cytoskeleton remodeling (glutamatergic synapses, cortical pyramidal neurons, striatal medium spiny neurons, cerebellar Purkinje cells) or as a postsynaptic density protein bridging SHANK3-mediated PSD-95 scaffolding with MAPK12-mediated stress signaling.
