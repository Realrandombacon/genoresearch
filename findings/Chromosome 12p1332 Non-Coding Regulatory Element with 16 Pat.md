## Description
LOC100507560 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 12p13.32 (NC_000012.12, positions 5015077-5025593, ~10.5 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression across any tissue. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains/duplications ranging from focal to whole-arm events: variant 4796136 (12p13.33-11.1, ~34.6 Mb), variant 4755352 (12p13.33-q13.12, ~49.8 Mb), variant 619591 (12p deletion), variant 155290 (12p13.33-13.31, ~5.8 Mb), variant 153962 (12p13.33-11.1, ~34.7 Mb), variant 153563 (12p13.33-12.2, ~19.4 Mb), variant 151004 (12p13.33-11.1, ~34.6 Mb), variant 150740 (12p13.33-q24.33, ~133.2 Mb whole-arm), variant 150158 (12p13.33-11.1, ~34.5 Mb), variant 148626 (12p13.33-12.3, ~12.1 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 12p13.32 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including FGF23 (fibroblast growth factor 23, phosphate homeostasis, vitamin D metabolism, Klotho co-receptor, tumor-induced osteomalacia, autosomal dominant hypophosphatemic rickets, chronic kidney disease mineral bone disorder), CCND2 (cyclin D2, G1/S cell cycle transition, CDK4/6 activation, retinoblastoma phosphorylation, pancreatic beta-cell proliferation, insulin secretion), KCNA5 (potassium voltage-gated channel alpha subunit 5, atrial repolarization, IKs current, atrial fibrillation, pulmonary arterial hypertension), KCNA1 (potassium voltage-gated channel alpha subunit 1, Kv1.1, neuronal excitability, episodic ataxia type 1, peripheral nerve hyperexcitability), and TIGAR (TP53 induced glycolysis regulatory phosphatase, fructose-2,6-bisphosphatase, pentose phosphate pathway, ROS reduction, p53 target, cancer metabolism). Functional hypothesis: LOC100507560 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 12p13.32 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a bone-kidney enhancer coordinating FGF23 expression in osteocytes (phosphate excretion, vitamin D suppression) or as a chromatin boundary separating cell cycle genes (CCND2) from potassium channel genes (KCNA5/KCNA1). The 16 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves FGF23 excess causing hypophosphatemia and impaired bone mineralization (ADHR, TIO), FGF23 deficiency causing tumoral calcinosis and hyperphosphatemia, CCND2 overexpression driving uncontrolled cell proliferation (cancer), CCND2 deficiency impairing pancreatic beta-cell mass (diabetes), KCNA5 dysfunction causing atrial fibrillation and pulmonary hypertension, KCNA1 mutation causing episodic ataxia and neuromyotonia, and TIGAR dysregulation altering cancer cell metabolism and ROS handling. The 12p13.32 duplication syndrome (Pallister-Killian syndrome isochromosome 12p) presents with coarse facies, intellectual disability, hypotonia, diaphragmatic hernia, and skeletal anomalies. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (16 CNVs ranging 5-133 Mb) and FGF23-CCND2-KCNA5-KCNA1-TIGAR gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for phosphate-cell cycle-potassium channel-metabolism coordination.
