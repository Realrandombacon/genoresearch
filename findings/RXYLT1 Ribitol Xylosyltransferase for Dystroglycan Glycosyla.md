# RXYLT1: Ribitol Xylosyltransferase for Dystroglycan Glycosylation

**Date:** 2026-05-08T02:46:39.497539

**Quality Score:** 7/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH

## Description
RXYLT1 is a ribitol-5-phosphate xylosyltransferase (PF24785/24786) involved in O-glycosylation of alpha-dystroglycan. It interacts with FKRP, B4GAT1, POMGNT1/2, and POMK/POMT1/2 in the glycosylation complex. Mutations cause lissencephaly and muscular dystrophy. Localized to Golgi and nucleoplasm.
