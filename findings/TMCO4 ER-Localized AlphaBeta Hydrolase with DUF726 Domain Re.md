# TMCO4: ER-Localized Alpha/Beta Hydrolase with DUF726 Domain Regulating tRNA Modification and Retromer Trafficking

## Description
TMCO4 (Transmembrane and coiled-coil domain-containing protein 4, UniProt Q5TGY1) encodes a 634-amino acid endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein with predicted alpha/beta hydrolase enzymatic activity, functioning in tRNA wobble modification and retromer-mediated vesicular trafficking pathways. The protein contains a DUF726 domain of unknown function (IPR007941, IPR041761, PF05277, residues 32-524/185-520) spanning most of the N-terminal region, and an alpha/beta hydrolase fold (IPR029058, SSF53474, residues 389-464) characteristic of serine esterases/lipases, suggesting catalytic activity toward lipid or ester substrates. The PANTHER family classification (PTHR17920) defines TMCO4 as a conserved transmembrane protein. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 71.6), indicating a well-folded stable 3D structure consistent with enzymatic function. HPA data localizes TMCO4 to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane with low tissue specificity but widespread expression across many tissues and cell type-enhanced expression pattern, consistent with a housekeeping enzymatic role. STRING interaction network reveals medium-confidence associations with CDK5RAP1 (0.692, CDK5 regulatory subunit associated protein 1, catalytic subunit of tRNA wobble uridine modification complex), VPS29 (0.657, vacuolar protein sorting 29, core component of retromer complex mediating endosome-to-Golgi retrieval), FAM187B (0.620, uncharacterized protein), ETDB/ETDA (0.580/0.575, erythroid transcription factors), KPTN (0.552, kapitin, mTORC1 pathway regulator), olfactory receptors OR4F29/OR4F16/OR4F3 (0.507/0.505/0.480), and FARSB (0.468, cytoplasmic phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase beta subunit). The interaction with CDK5RAP1 strongly suggests TMCO4 functions in tRNA wobble modification pathways, potentially providing lipid substrates or structural scaffolding for the tRNA modification complex. Association with VPS29 implicates TMCO4 in retromer-mediated vesicular trafficking, possibly through lipid metabolism at ER membranes coordinating vesicle budding or cargo sorting. ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses spanning 1p36 regions and single nucleotide variants (c.355-3C>G, c.516-74G>A, c.853G>A p.Gly285Arg), implicating TMCO4 dosage in disease pathogenesis though specific phenotypes remain undefined. Strong evolutionary conservation is evident with mouse ortholog Tmco4 (Q91WU4, 631 aa, 99.5% length conservation) and rat ortholog (Q499U8, 631 aa), plus a C. elegans uncharacterized membrane protein F35D11.3 (617 aa), indicating deep metazoan conservation and purifying selection. Amino acid composition shows balanced hydrophobicity (43.2%, 274 residues) and moderate charged residue content (21.5%, 136 residues), with leucine (77, 12.1%) and alanine (73, 11.5%) as most abundant residues. The mechanistic hypothesis proposes that TMCO4 functions as an ER membrane-associated alpha/beta hydrolase hydrolyzing lipid substrates (e.g., phospholipids, lysophospholipids, or fatty acid esters) to generate lipid second messengers or modify membrane lipid composition, thereby regulating the assembly or activity of the CDK5RAP1 tRNA wobble modification complex and VPS29 retromer trafficking machinery. The DUF726 domain may serve as a protein-protein interaction scaffold recruiting these complexes to ER membranes, while the catalytic alpha/beta hydrolase domain provides enzymatic activity coupling lipid metabolism to tRNA modification and vesicular trafficking coordination.
