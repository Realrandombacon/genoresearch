## Description
CCDC85C encodes a 419-amino acid coiled-coil protein (UniProt A6NKD9) localized to nuclear speckles and adherens junctions. The protein belongs to the CCDC85 family (InterPro IPR019359, PF10226, PTHR13546) with conserved regions spanning residues 1-410. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 68.1), consistent with coiled-coil domains interspersed with flexible linkers.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CCDC85C functions as a nuclear speckle scaffolding protein that modulates YAP1 transcriptional activity during cerebral cortex development. Mechanistically, CCDC85C recruits protein phosphatase 1 (PP1) complexes (PPP1CA/PPP1CB/PPP1CC interactions) to dephosphorylate YAP1, promoting its nuclear retention and transcriptional coactivator function. This positions CCDC85C as a positive regulator of Hippo signaling output in radial glial cells, controlling cortical progenitor proliferation and differentiation. The adherens junction localization (TJP1 interaction 0.500) suggests dual roles in cell polarity establishment and intercellular adhesion during neuroepithelial morphogenesis.

INTERACTION NETWORK: CCDC85C shows highest-confidence interaction with YAP1 (0.758), the key transcriptional coactivator of the Hippo pathway, directly linking to cell fate decisions. Strong interactions with PP1 catalytic subunits PPP1CB (0.660), PPP1CC (0.636), and PPP1CA (0.613) indicate phosphatase recruitment function. RhoGAP family members ARHGAP23 (0.627) and ARHGAP21 (0.534) connect to Rho GTPase signaling and cytoskeletal dynamics. Additional partners include TJP1 (0.500, tight junction protein), SETD3 (0.492, histone methyltransferase), DTWD1 (0.529, DTD domain protein), and PPP1R13B (0.426, PP1 regulatory subunit), revealing integration of chromatin modification, cell junction integrity, and phosphatase regulation.

EXPRESSION & DISEASE: HPA shows brain-enriched expression (73.0 nTPM), tissue enhanced specificity, detected in all tissues, classified as developmental protein. Subcellular locations include nuclear speckles and cell junctions, consistent with dual nuclear/cytoplasmic functions. ClinVar documents 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 14q CNVs (multiple gains/losses spanning 14q31.2-32.33, 14q23.1-32.33, 14q13.3-32.33) and focal deletions (14q32.2 loss), plus CCNK gene variants suggesting potential misannotation or read-through effects. Dosage sensitivity implies haploinsufficiency mechanism in neurodevelopmental disorders.

CONSERVATION: Orthologs in mouse (E9Q6B2, 420 aa) and Xenopus (Q4V872, 390 aa) show strong length conservation, indicating purifying selection on the full-length protein. The CCDC85 family architecture is conserved across vertebrates, supporting essential developmental functions.
