## Description
GALNT4 encodes Polypeptide N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 4 (578 aa, Q8N4A0), a Golgi-resident glycosyltransferase initiating mucin-type O-linked glycosylation through GalNAc transfer to serine/threonine residues. The POC1B-GALNT4 designation represents a readthrough/fusion transcript, but GALNT4 is the functional enzymatic component. InterPro analysis reveals canonical GALNT architecture: pp-GalNAc-T catalytic domain (cd02510, positions 135-429; IPR045885, positions 135-429; PF00535, positions 135-318) transferring GalNAc from UDP-GalNAc to acceptor substrates; ricin B-type lectin domain (cd23469, positions 439-574; IPR000772, positions 441-574; PF00652, positions 442-571; PS50231, positions 441-574) mediating substrate recognition and binding; glycosyltransferase 2-like domain (IPR001173, positions 135-318); nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily (IPR029044/G3DSA:3.90.550.10, positions 52-436) providing catalytic scaffold; ricin B-like lectin superfamily (IPR035992/G3DSA:2.80.10.50, positions 426-573/441-575) for carbohydrate binding. STRING interactions reveal O-glycosylation and mucin biosynthesis network: MUC1 (0.923, mucin-1 - highest confidence substrate), B3GNT6 (0.907, beta-1,3-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase 6 - glycosylation enzyme), MUC2 (0.811, mucin-2), C1GALT1C1L (0.761, COSMC homolog), C1GALT1 (0.654, core 1 synthase), GCNT1 (0.565, core 2 synthase), ST6GALNAC1 (0.546, sialyltransferase), C1GALT1C1 (0.545, COSMC chaperone), GALNT7 (0.454, paralog), B4GALNT1 (0.400) - positioning GALNT4 in mucin-type O-glycan initiation and elongation cascade. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with stomach enrichment (29.9 nTPM - top tissue); Golgi apparatus localization consistent with glycosylation function; molecular functions include glycosyltransferase and transferase; biological process annotated as metabolic protein. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 12q21.1-21.33/12q21.2-22/12q21.32-21.33 copy number losses and 12p13.33-q24.33 copy number gains plus single nucleotide variant (c.1194C>T p.Asn398=) indicating dosage sensitivity at 12q locus though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely characterized. AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.4) indicating exceptionally well-folded glycosyltransferase with stable catalytic and lectin domains. Literature (92 papers) reveals diverse disease roles: 2025 studies establish GALNT4 promotes non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) stemness through O-glycosylation of MUC5AC via ITGB4/PI3K/AKT signaling pathway (3 citations); 2025 research demonstrates GALNT4 controls aortic dissection by regulating vascular smooth muscle cell phenotype switch and dysfunction through TGF-β/Smad signaling (1 citation); 2016-2017 GWAS studies implicate GALNT4 locus in NT-proBNP regulation (46 citations) and atrial natriuretic peptide-mediated blood pressure control (26 citations); severe Joubert syndrome reported with POC1B mutations indicating ciliopathy involvement. Functional hypothesis: GALNT4 functions as initiating enzyme in mucin-type O-glycosylation pathway transferring GalNAc to serine/threonine residues of acceptor proteins (MUC1, MUC2, MUC5AC) in Golgi apparatus; catalytic domain (positions 135-429) performs glycosyl transfer from UDP-GalNAc donor; ricin B lectin domain (positions 439-574) recognizes and binds acceptor substrates ensuring specificity; tissue-enhanced expression suggests specialized glycosylation roles in stomach and cardiovascular tissues. Mechanistic proposal: GALNT4 initiates Tn antigen (GalNAcα1-Ser/Thr) formation on mucin tandem repeats enabling subsequent glycosylation by B3GNT6 (0.907), C1GALT1/COSMC (0.654/0.545), and GCNT1 (0.565) extending O-glycans to core structures; MUC1 interaction (0.923) positions GALNT4 in epithelial mucin glycosylation critical for barrier function and signaling; 2025 NSCLC study reveals GALNT4 O-glycosylates MUC5AC activating ITGB4 integrin signaling through PI3K/AKT pathway promoting cancer stem cell maintenance and tumor initiation; 2025 aortic dissection study demonstrates GALNT4 regulates vascular smooth muscle cell phenotypic switching through TGF-β/Smad pathway - altered glycosylation affects receptor signaling causing vessel wall weakness; GWAS associations with NT-proBNP and blood pressure suggest GALNT4 glycosylation affects natriuretic peptide processing or receptor function influencing cardiovascular homeostasis; 12q CNVs (10 ClinVar variants) suggest GALNT4 dosage affects glycosylation capacity - haploinsufficiency may cause hypoglycosylation phenotypes while gains cause hyperglycosylation; very high AlphaFold confidence (91.4) indicates stable folded structure suitable for substrate binding studies and inhibitor design; Golgi localization ensures GALNT4 acts during protein secretory pathway before surface expression; GALNT7 paralog (0.454) suggests functional redundancy potentially buffering loss-of-function variants; POC1B-GALNT4 readthrough may produce bifunctional protein combining centriolar (POC1B) and glycosylation (GALNT4) functions though this requires experimental validation; Joubert syndrome link through POC1B suggests ciliary glycosylation may affect ciliopathy phenotypes; mucin glycosylation defects link GALNT4 to inflammatory bowel disease, cancer, and cardiovascular disorders through altered cell surface glycan landscapes. This gene represents a characterized glycosyltransferase (92 papers) with emerging roles in NSCLC stemness, aortic dissection, cardiovascular regulation, and ciliopathy through substrate-specific O-glycosylation.
