# LOC123256950: Dosage-Sensitive Repressive Chromatin Element on 3q

## Description
LOC123256950 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 5391 located on chromosome 3q (genomic coordinates: 177652846-177653254 on NC_000003.12). This 408 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element using Sharpr-MPRA in K562 erythroleukemia cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA expression). ClinVar analysis identified 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all are copy number gains (triplifications) spanning overlapping regions of 3q25.31-29 (ranging from 103426882-198110178 to 176439911-198118383). Notably, all variants are gains with no losses observed, indicating that increased copy number (over-dosage) of this region is pathogenic. This contrasts with haploinsufficient elements where deletions cause disease; here, the repressive activity of LOC123256950 must be tightly regulated, and extra copies likely lead to excessive silencing of target tumor suppressor genes or cell cycle regulators in cis on 3q. The 3q26-29 region is frequently amplified in squamous cell carcinomas and contains oncogenes (e.g., PIK3CA at 3q26.32, TERC at 3q26.33). Paradoxically, amplification of a repressive element within this amplicon may represent a co-amplified "passenger" that exerts selective pressure by modulating the expression of neighboring drivers, or it may silence a specific tumor suppressor locus (e.g., FOXP1 at 3p14.1 if looping occurs, though less likely in cis). The consistent gain pattern suggests that cells tolerate or require extra copies of this repressor, possibly to buffer against the oncogenic stress of 3q amplification.
