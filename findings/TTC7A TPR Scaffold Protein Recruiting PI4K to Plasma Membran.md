## Description
TTC7A is an 858 aa tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) scaffold protein containing N-terminal domain (IPR045819/PF19440, aa9-403) and multiple C-terminal TPR repeats (IPR019734, aa414-447/487-531/566-599/746-777/814-844) forming helical interaction surfaces. AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 84.1) consistent with stable TPR superhelix architecture. TTC7A functions as component of plasma membrane targeting complex that recruits phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase alpha (PI4KA) for phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate (PI4P) production. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (38.0 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced distribution. STRING reveals high-confidence interactions with complex members: FAM126A/hyccin (0.880), EFR3B (0.867), TMEM150A (0.724), FAM126B (0.673), plus paralog TTC37 (0.666) and lipid kinases PI4KA (0.492)/PI4KB (0.488). The mechanistic model: TTC7A's TPR domains provide scaffolding surface binding both FAM126A and EFR3 proteins, anchoring the complex to plasma membrane where PI4KA generates PI4P pools required for vesicle trafficking, tight junction assembly, and epithelial polarity. ClinVar contains 139 pathogenic variants including nonsense (R546*, Y160*, Q611*, Q118*, Y396*), frameshift (F721fs, R202fs, K339fs), splice (c.764+1G>A), and in-frame deletions (L819_G827del), causing Gastrointestinal Defects and Immunodeficiency Syndrome 1 (GIDID-1)—an autosomal recessive disorder with intestinal atresia, multiple gastrointestinal malformations, and combined immunodeficiency. Recent 2025 studies reveal: (1) distinct heterozygous missense variants produce different intestinal epithelial phenotypes in pediatric IBD, (2) TTC7A-ALK fusion variant in metastatic lung adenocarcinoma responsive to crizotinib, (3) genotype-phenotype correlations in GIDID1 spectrum. With only 114 publications, TTC7A represents an incompletely characterized TPR scaffold bridging phosphoinositide signaling to epithelial barrier function and immune homeostasis.
