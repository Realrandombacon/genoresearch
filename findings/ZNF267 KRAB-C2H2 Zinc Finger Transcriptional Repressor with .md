# ZNF267: KRAB-C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Ubiquitous Expression and Chromosome 16 Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
ZNF267 encodes a 743 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein architecture features a canonical N-terminal KRAB domain (residues 4-75, IPR001909, PF01352, SM00349) which recruits the corepressor KAP1 (TRIM28) complex to silence target genes via heterochromatin formation through H3K9me3 histone methylation. The C-terminal region contains multiple C2H2-type zinc finger arrays (IPR013087, PF00096, SM00355) at positions 267-289, 322-342, 380-402, 408-430, and 436-458, providing modular DNA recognition specificity for GC-rich promoter/enhancer sequences.

**Expression Profile**: Human Protein Atlas reveals ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as "cell type enhanced" in single-cell analysis. Subcellular localization is nucleoplasmic, consistent with transcriptional regulatory function. The broad expression pattern suggests ZNF267 may regulate housekeeping genes or maintain basal transcriptional repression across cell types.

**Structural Analysis**: AlphaFold prediction (Q14586) shows medium confidence (pLDDT 61.6) across the 743 residue sequence, indicating partially structured domains interspersed with intrinsically disordered regions. The structured zinc finger domains likely mediate DNA binding, while disordered linkers may enable conformational flexibility for scanning chromatin.

**Clinical Significance**: ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number variations spanning chromosome 16 regions (16p13.3-q24.3, 16p11.2-11.1). These CNVs include both gains and losses, suggesting ZNF267 dosage sensitivity where both amplification and deletion disrupt normal transcriptional regulation. The 16p11.2 region is particularly associated with neurodevelopmental disorders.

**HYPOTHESIS**: ZNF267 functions as a broad-spectrum transcriptional silencer targeting developmentally regulated genes and/or endogenous retroviral elements (ERVs). The KRAB domain mediates heterochromatin recruitment via KAP1/HP1α interactions, while the tandem C2H2 zinc fingers confer DNA sequence specificity. Ubiquitous expression suggests ZNF267 maintains basal repression of lineage-inappropriate genes across tissues. Chromosome 16p11.2 CNVs may cause ZNF267 dosage alterations, leading to transcriptional dysregulation of neurodevelopmental target genes, potentially contributing to cognitive/behavioral phenotypes observed in 16p11.2 deletion/duplication syndromes.
