POLR3D contains a defining RPC4-specific domain (IPR007811/PF05132, positions 263-389) and belongs to the DNA-directed RNA polymerase III family (PTHR13408, positions 2-396), characteristic of proteins that form the catalytic core and regulatory interface of the Pol III holoenzyme.
