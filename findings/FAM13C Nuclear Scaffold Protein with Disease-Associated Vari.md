## Description
FAM13C encodes a 585 amino acid nuclear scaffold protein (UniProt Q8NE31) localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals a cons FAM13 family domain (IPR039102, residues 39-585) and a C-terminal FAM13A-like domain (IPR059029/PF26116, residues 511-579), suggesting structural homology with FAM13A/B paralogs. HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced RNA pattern with highest expression in brain (38.2 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression profile, and absence in immune cells. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-confidence partners including AHCYL1 (0.622), SRD5A2 (0.613), AHCYL2 (0.550), SFRP4 (0.520), and GSTM2 (0.507), implicating FAM13C in S-adenosylhomocysteine metabolism (AHCYL1/2), steroid hormone processing (SRD5A2), Wnt signaling modulation (SFRP4), and detoxification (GSTM2). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 10 copy number variations affecting FAM13C locus, indicating dosage sensitivity and disease association. Mouse ortholog Fam13c (Q9DBR2, 601 aa) shows ~80% sequence conservation. AlphaFold structure prediction unavailable (API error). Functional hypothesis: FAM13C acts as a nuclear scaffold protein that recruits metabolic enzymes and signaling regulators to chromatin, coordinating epigenetic state with cellular metabolism. The FAM13A-like C-terminal domain may mediate protein-protein interactions with AHCYL enzymes, linking NAD+/NADH redox state to transcriptional regulation. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt this scaffolding function, potentially causing metabolic-transcriptional uncoupling in disease.
