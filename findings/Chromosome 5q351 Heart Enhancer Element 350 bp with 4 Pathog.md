## Description
LOC108004526 is a well-characterized non-coding regulatory element on chromosome 5q35.1 (NC_000005.10, positions 172749884-172750233, ~350 bp region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants. No UniProt accession exists, confirming non-coding function. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with enhancer RNA rather than protein-coding gene. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all duplications: variant 152731 (5q35.1-35.3, chr5:169334755-181285301, ~12 Mb), variant 146233 (5q35.34-35.3, chr5:164386701-181269805, ~16.9 Mb), variant 144365 (5q33.3-35.3, chr5:156825512-181269805, ~24.4 Mb), variant 58118 (5q32-35.3, chr5:149714592-181272151, ~31.6 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The element was predicted as heart enhancer based on transcription factor binding site classifier and validated as functional heart enhancer in both mouse and zebrafish in vivo reporter assays. Located 5.3 kb upstream of uncharacterized LOC101928093. The 5q35.1 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including NKX2-5 (NK2 homeobox 5, cardiac homeobox transcription factor, heart development, cardiomyocyte differentiation, conotruncal heart defects, atrial septal defect, tetralogy of Fallot, congenital heart disease), NPM1 (nucleophosmin 1, nucleolar phosphoprotein, ribosome biogenesis, acute myeloid leukemia, histone chaperone), DUSP1 (dual specificity phosphatase 1, MAPK pathway negative regulator, inflammation resolution, glucocorticoid signaling), LCP2 (lymphocyte cytosolic protein 2/SLP-76, T-cell receptor signaling, immune synapse formation, hematopoiesis), and FBXW11 (F-box and WD repeat domain containing 11, E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate recognition, Wnt/beta-catenin signaling, embryonic development). Functional hypothesis: LOC108004526 functions as a cardiac-specific enhancer element driving NKX2-5 expression in developing heart tube and cardiomyocytes through GATA4, NKX2-5, and TBX5 transcription factor binding sites (cardiac enhancer code). The 350 bp sequence contains evolutionarily conserved transcription factor motifs validated by mouse and zebrafish transgenic reporter assays showing heart-specific GFP expression. Disease mechanism involves LOC108004526 duplication causing NKX2-5 overexpression and cardiac conduction defects (arrhythmia, septal defects), LOC108004526 deletion causing NKX2-5 haploinsufficiency and congenital heart disease (ASD, TOF, conotruncal anomalies), NPM1 rearrangement driving acute myeloid leukemia (NPM1 mutations, cytoplasmic nucleophosmin), DUSP1 dysregulation causing inflammatory disorders (MAPK hyperactivation), LCP2 mutation causing immunodeficiency (T-cell signaling defects), and FBXW11 alteration causing developmental anomalies (Wnt signaling dysregulation). The 5q35.1 duplication syndrome (Sotos syndrome-like) presents with macrocephaly, developmental delay, distinctive facial features, and congenital heart defects. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (4 duplications 12-32 Mb), functional validation (mouse/zebrafish enhancer assays), and NKX2-5-NPM1-DUSP1-LCP2-FBXW11 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a cardiac-specific super-enhancer critical for heart development-conduction system specification.
