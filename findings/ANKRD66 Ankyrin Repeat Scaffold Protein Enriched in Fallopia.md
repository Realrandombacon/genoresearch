# ANKRD66: Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein Enriched in Fallopian Tube with Cancer-Associated CNVs

## Description
ANKRD66 encodes a 196-amino acid intracellular scaffold protein containing multiple ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, PF12796, positions 2-178) that mediate protein-protein interactions. The protein shows high structural confidence (AlphaFold pLDDT 85.1) with a well-folded ankyrin repeat superfamily architecture (G3DSA:1.25.40.20, IPR036770). Expression is tissue-enriched in fallopian tube (38.3 nTPM, HPA) and cancer-enhanced, suggesting roles in reproductive tissue homeostasis and potential oncogenic functions. STRING interactions reveal associations with ANKUB1 (0.545), C11orf88 (0.519), FAM216B (0.491), MAP3K19 (0.489), and WDR31 (0.471) — partners implicated in ubiquitination, kinase signaling, and ciliary functions. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (gains and losses across 6p21.1-q12), indicating dosage sensitivity and disease relevance. Conservation is evident across mammals (mouse: J3QNN4 192aa, rat: A0A8I6B646 251aa, gorilla: A0A2I2YHT3 251aa). The ankyrin repeat scaffold likely functions as a molecular adaptor assembling signaling complexes in fallopian epithelium, with CNVs disrupting stoichiometry contributing to reproductive tract pathologies and cancer susceptibility.
