## Description
ZNF521 encodes a 1311-amino acid zinc finger protein (UniProt Q96K83) localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein contains multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (InterPro IPR013087, PF00096, PF13912) distributed across residues 46-1158, characteristic of DNA-binding transcription factors. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 60.8), consistent with intrinsically disordered regions common in transcriptional coregulators.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ZNF521 functions as a transcriptional coregulator that modulates chromatin remodeling complexes to control neural development and cell differentiation. Mechanistically, it recruits histone deacetylase complexes (HDAC1/HDAC3) and nucleosome remodeling factors (MTA1, RBBP7) to target gene promoters, acting as both transcriptional activator and repressor depending on cellular context. The strong interaction with RUNX2 (STRING score 0.761) suggests roles in osteoblast differentiation, while MBD3L1 (0.838) and SMAD4 (0.650) connections implicate functions in epigenetic regulation and TGF-β signaling.

INTERACTION NETWORK: ZNF521 shows high-confidence interactions with chromatin modifiers MBD3L1 (0.838), MTA1 (0.803, NuRD complex component), RBBP7 (0.803, nucleosome binding), HDAC1 (0.736), and HDAC3 (0.517), positioning it within histone deacetylation machinery. Additional partners include RUNX2 (0.761, osteoblast transcription factor), ZNF423 (0.659, zinc finger transcription factor), ZNF219 (0.652), SMAD4 (0.650, TGF-β pathway), and EBF1 (0.474, B-cell development). This network suggests ZNF521 coordinates lineage-specific transcription programs.

EXPRESSION & DISEASE: HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in brain (59.3 nTPM), detected in all tissues, classified as transcription factor and cancer-related gene. Subcellular location is nucleoplasm. ClinVar documents 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 18q11.2 copy number gains and losses, indicating dosage sensitivity. The cancer-related classification and proto-oncogene designation suggest roles in cell proliferation control.

CONSERVATION: Mouse ortholog (Q6KAS7, 1311 aa) shows complete length conservation, indicating strong purifying selection on this large zinc finger protein. The C2H2 zinc finger architecture is ancient and conserved across vertebrates.
