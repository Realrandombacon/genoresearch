## Description
SLC66A1 encodes a 291 amino acid lysosomal amino acid transporter 1 (UniProt Q6ZP29), a member of the SLC66 family of lysosomal/vacuolar amino acid transporters. InterPro analysis confirms canonical transporter architecture including IPR051415 (Lysosomal/Vacuolar Amino Acid Transporter 1, aa 12-279), PTHR16201 (Lysosomal/Vacuolar Amino Acid Transporter 1, aa 12-279), PF04193 (PQ loop repeat, aa 38-97), and IPR006603 (PQ-loop repeat, aa 38-97), indicating a PQ-loop mediated transport mechanism. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q6ZP29-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 83.1), consistent with a functional membrane protein. HPA expression reveals low tissue specificity but ubiquitous detection across all human tissues, with cell-type enhanced specificity in vesicles, suggesting a housekeeping role in lysosomal amino acid homeostasis. STRING interactions identify 10 partners including CTNS (0.837, high confidence), SLC38A7 (0.594), SLC38A9 (0.538), and SLC36A1 (0.408), linking it to the cystinosis pathway (CTNS) and other solute carrier families involved in lysosomal transport. ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number deletions of 1p36.13-1p36.12 regions, though no specific disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature search returned only 7 papers, confirming its status as a 'dark gene' with minimal characterization beyond family classification. The combination of high-confidence structural prediction, ubiquitous expression, strong interaction with CTNS (cystinosis gene), and a massive burden of 1p36 deletion variants suggests SLC66A1 functions as a critical lysosomal amino acid transporter regulating intracellular amino acid pools, with large deletions likely contributing to the severe neurodevelopmental and skeletal phenotypes observed in 1p36 deletion syndrome, potentially via disruption of lysosomal integrity or amino acid sensing pathways.
