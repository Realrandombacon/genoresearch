## Description
DDX1 (DEAD-Box Helicase 1) is a 740 amino acid RNA helicase that functions as a critical regulator of non-spliceosomal RNA splicing, R-loop processing, and chromatin reprogramming through DEAD/DEAH-box helicase-dependent ATP hydrolysis and RNA unwinding activity, mediating tRNA splicing via RTCB ligase complex assembly, TFIIH cooperation in DNA repair, and alternative splicing control of peroxisome-related genes through HNRNPK interaction. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic DEAD-box helicase architecture including N-terminal SPRY/B30.2 domain (IPR001870, IPR003877, cd12873, G3DSA:2.60.120.920, residues 70-247/93-245/130-246) for protein-protein interactions and substrate recognition with protein binding function (GO:0005515), DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545, cd17938, residues 26-70/283-413/288-430) performing ATP binding (GO:0005524) and nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001, residues 21-444) for catalytic core, RNA helicase DEAD-box type Q motif (IPR014014) for ATP-dependent RNA unwinding regulation, C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650, cd18787, residues 493-681/498-619) for substrate processing, P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 1-88/264-431/483-684) providing catalytic scaffold, and Concanavalin A-like lectin/glucanase domain superfamily (IPR013320, residues 58-250) for carbohydrate recognition. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q92499-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 86.6) across the 740 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded multi-domain helicase with stable SPRY and catalytic core suitable for RNA substrate processing and protein complex assembly. HPA data classifies DDX1 as an enzyme and plasma protein involved in antiviral defense, host-virus interaction, immunity, innate immunity, and mRNA processing with nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization reflecting nuclear RNA metabolism and cytoplasmic functions, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated RNA helicase function. ClinVar lists 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 2p24.3-24.2, 2p25.3-23.1, 2p25.3-22.3, 2p25.2-24.1 (spanning 6-35 Mb regions), copy number losses on 2p24.3, 2p25.1-q13, and intronic/splice variants (c.1686+34T>G, c.957-27A>T, c.260-190G>T), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (276 papers) establishes DDX1 as a well-studied RNA helicase: DDX1 methylation mediated MATR3 splicing regulates intervertebral disc degeneration by initiating chromatin reprogramming revealing epigenetic control in degenerative disease; DDX1 required for non-spliceosomal splicing of tRNAs but not XBP1 mRNA establishing specific substrate preference; trichothiodystrophy-causative pathogenic variants impair cooperative action of TFIIH and DDX1 in R-loop processing linking to DNA repair disorder; DDX1 crotonylation mediates ACOX1 alternative splicing through HNRNPK to increase peroxisomal oxidative damage demonstrating post-translational regulation; and DDX1 roles in antiviral defense and innate immunity confirming host-virus interaction function. As a well-characterized RNA helicase with emerging disease mechanisms, DDX1 represents a critical node connecting non-spliceosomal tRNA splicing to R-loop processing, chromatin reprogramming, peroxisomal function, and DNA repair through DEAD-box helicase-dependent RNA unwinding, RTCB ligase complex assembly for tRNA maturation, TFIIH cooperation in nucleotide excision repair, and HNRNPK-mediated alternative splicing disrupted in intervertebral disc degeneration, trichothiodystrophy, peroxisomal oxidative damage, and viral infection.
