## Description
TNFAIP1 (UniProt Q13829), also known as BACURD2 or EDP1, encodes a 316 aa BTB/POZ domain-containing protein functioning as a substrate-specific adapter for CUL3-RBX1 E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes (CRL3), targeting RhoA GTPase and other substrates for K48-linked polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. InterPro analysis reveals conserved bipartite architecture: N-terminal BTB/POZ domain (cd18401/IPR000210/IPR003131/IPR011333/PF02214/PS50097/G3DSA:3.30.710.10, residues 26-129/28-128/30-120/26-131) mediating CUL3 binding (GO:0005515 protein binding) and homooligomerization (GO:0051260); C-terminal KCTD10/TNFAIP1/KCTD13 domain (IPR063516/PF31104, residues 134-283/134-283) forming substrate recognition interface; and BTB/POZ domain-containing adapter family membership (IPR045068/PTHR11145, residues 18-280) defining the BACURD protein subfamily. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 80.9) indicating a well-folded two-domain adapter suitable for simultaneous CUL3 and substrate binding. The perfect FAF1/UBXN7/UBXN1 scores and strong CUL3/KCTD links confirm TNFAIP1 as integral CRL3 component regulating ubiquitin-proteasome system. HPA classifies TNFAIP1 as predicted intracellular protein with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity), localized to nucleoli consistent with ribosome biogenesis or stress response roles, functioning in Ubl conjugation pathway (ubiquitin-like modification), cell type enhanced specificity. ClinVar lists 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number gains (17p11.2-q11.2x3, 17q11.1-11.2x3 multiple coordinates, 17p11.2-q21.2x3, 17p13.3-q25.3x3) and intronic/regulatory SNVs (c.714+177G>T, c.-114-58G>T); while no single-gene disorder name is assigned, 17q11.2 duplications cause recognizable syndromes where TNFAIP1 dosage may contribute to neurodevelopmental and cardiovascular phenotypes.
