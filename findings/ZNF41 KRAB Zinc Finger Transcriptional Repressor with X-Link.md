# ZNF41: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with X-Linked Cognitive Disability Association

**Date:** 2026-03-23T05:52:06.222585

**Quality Score:** 1.62/10  (E=2.95, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
ZNF41 encodes an 821 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional repressor protein (UniProt: P51814) localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein contains a KRAB-A domain (aa 69-107, IPR001909, PF01352) and multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 282-434, characteristic of the Krüppel family. These structural domains enable sequence-specific DNA binding and transcriptional repression through recruitment of corepressor complexes.\n\nExpression data from Human Protein Atlas shows ZNF41 is detected in many tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a transcription factor and disease-related gene with nucleoplasmic localization. STRING interaction network reveals high-confidence associations with FTSJ1 (0.858), ARAF (0.841), ZNF385B (0.836), ZNF510 (0.787), and medium-confidence interactions with PQBP1 (0.698), PHF8 (0.643), and TRIM28 (0.522) - suggesting involvement in chromatin regulation and transcriptional control complexes.\n\nClinVar contains 148 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and deletions on chromosome Xp11.3. The gene has been associated with X-linked cognitive disability (MRX89 alias), though later studies have questioned this association (PMID:23871722). The KRAB domain mediates heterochromatin formation via TRIM28/KAP1 recruitment, leading to H3K9me3 methylation and gene silencing.
