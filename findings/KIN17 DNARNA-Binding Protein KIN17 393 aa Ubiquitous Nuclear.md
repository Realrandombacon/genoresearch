## Description
KIN17 (Kin17 DNA and RNA Binding Protein, UniProt O60870) is a 393 amino acid nuclear protein with moderate human characterization representing a partially characterized gene that functions as a multifunctional DNA/RNA-binding factor coordinating spliceosome assembly, DNA damage response, and transcription-coupled repair through interactions with splicing factors and chromatin modifiers.
