## Description
LOC441052 (Gene ID: 441052) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 4q34.1 (NC_000004.12, positions 171812137-171813606, ~1.5 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 11 duplications/gains (variant 155492: 4q28.3-35.2 ~55.1 Mb; variant 155264: 4q32.3-35.2 ~22.0 Mb; variant 152441: 4q32.1-35.2 ~29.3 Mb; plus 8 additional gains) and 11 deletions/losses (variant 4796144: 4q32.1-35.2 ~32.2 Mb; variant 155165: 4q33-35.2 ~19.1 Mb; variant 154300: 4q32.3-35.2 ~18.0 Mb; variant 153653: 4q32.3-35.2 ~22.7 Mb; variant 153168: 4q32.3-34.3 ~13.2 Mb; variant 151716: 4q32.3-35.2 ~24.7 Mb; variant 151692: 4q33-35.2 ~20.1 Mb; plus 4 additional losses). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 4q34.1 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including HPGD (15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase, prostaglandin E2 catabolism, pulmonary hypertension, digital clubbing, gastric polyposis), HMGB2 (high mobility group box 2, chromatin architecture, DNA repair, inflammation, sepsis), HAND2 (heart and neural crest derivatives expressed 2, bHLH transcription factor, cardiac morphogenesis, right ventricle development, limb patterning), HAND2-AS1 (HAND2 antisense RNA 1, lncRNA regulator of HAND2, endometrial cancer, preeclampsia), and SAP30 (Sin3A associated protein 30, histone deacetylase complex corepressor, transcriptional silencing, cell cycle). Functional hypothesis: LOC441052 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 4q34.1 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a cardiac/neural crest-specific enhancer coordinating HAND2 expression during embryonic heart development (right ventricular outflow tract formation, sympathetic neuron differentiation) or as a chromatin boundary separating prostaglandin metabolism genes (HPGD) from chromatin remodeling genes (HMGB2/SAP30) and cardiac transcription factors (HAND2). The 22 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple developmental and inflammatory programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves HPGD haploinsufficiency causing primary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy (prostaglandin E2 accumulation, digital clubbing, periostosis, pulmonary hypertension), HPGD overexpression altering inflammatory tone (gastric polyposis, cancer suppression), HMGB2 loss impairing DNA repair (genomic instability, sepsis susceptibility, aging), HMGB2 overexpression promoting inflammation (cytokine storm, rheumatoid arthritis), HAND2 deficiency causing congenital heart defects (Tetralogy of Fallot, right ventricular hypoplasia, Holt-Oram syndrome overlap), HAND2 overexpression causing limb anomalies (preaxial polydactyly), and SAP30 dysfunction causing transcriptional dysregulation (cell cycle arrest, differentiation defects). The 4q34.1 deletion syndrome and 4q34.1 duplication syndrome may present with cardiac defects, skeletal abnormalities, and inflammatory dysregulation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (22 CNVs ranging 13.2-55.1 Mb) and HPGD-HMGB2-HAND2-HAND2-AS1-SAP30 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for cardiac development-prostaglandin metabolism-chromatin remodeling coordination.
