## Description
ZNF460 encodes a 562 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) C2H2 zinc finger protein (UniProt: Q14592) at chromosome 19q13.43 that functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The domain architecture features an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 12-99) and three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 196-279). The protein belongs to the PRDM4/PRDM1/PRDM14-like zinc finger family (IPR050331), suggesting functional homology with PRDM transcriptional regulators. The KRAB domain mediates heterochromatin formation through corepressor recruitment.\n\nHPA expression profiling shows low tissue specificity with detection across many tissues, cancer-enhanced expression, and nucleoplasmic subcellular localization. The ubiquitous expression pattern suggests housekeeping transcriptional repression functions. Classification as a transcription factor with DNA-binding molecular function aligns with chromatin regulatory roles.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 10 partners revealing transcriptional repression and nuclear transport connections: TRIM28 (0.419, tripartite motif protein 28/KAP1, KRAB coreceptor), ZNF264 (0.478, C2H2 zinc finger paralog), IPO8 (0.629, importin-8 nuclear import receptor), CNOT6L (0.468, CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex), and KLHL11/KLHL15 (0.413, Kelch-like ubiquitin ligase adaptors). This interactome positions ZNF460 within KRAB-mediated heterochromatin silencing complexes.\n\nClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large 19q copy number gains (13 variants spanning 19q13.41-13.43) and one 5 prime UTR variant c.31-316A>T. The 19q13.43 region is dosage-sensitive; ZNF460 triplosensitivity may contribute to developmental phenotypes through aberrant transcriptional repression of target genes.\n\nAlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (pLDDT 66.1), indicating structured zinc finger domains with potentially flexible KRAB and linker regions. This organization is characteristic of KRAB-ZNF proteins with ordered DNA-binding regions and disordered repression domains.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: ZNF460 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruiting TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor via the KRAB domain to target gene promoters. TRIM28 recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins to establish H3K9me3 repressive chromatin marks. The C2H2 zinc fingers recognize specific DNA sequences while the KRAB domain mediates epigenetic silencing. Dysregulation through 19q copy number gains may cause aberrant repression of developmental genes.
