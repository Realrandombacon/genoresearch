## Description
PSMD13 (26S proteasome non-ATPase regulatory subunit 13, also known as p31 or Rpn15, Q9UNM6) encodes a 376 aa structural scaffold protein functioning as an essential component of the 19S regulatory particle of the 26S proteasome, mediating complex assembly, substrate recognition, and deubiquitination coordination through its PCI domain architecture. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 70.8): Proteasome component (PCI) domain (IPR000717/PF01399/SM00088, residues 171-356/251-335/263-356) forming the characteristic winged helix fold essential for scaffolding the proteasome lid subcomplex, PSD13 N-terminal repeats (IPR054179/PF22037, residues 22-237) establishing an N-terminal helical bundle for additional protein interactions, Winged helix DNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR036390/SSF46785, residues 261-339) defining the structural core of the PCI module though functioning here in protein-protein rather than DNA interactions, and 26S Proteasome non-ATPase regulatory subunit 13 family (IPR035298/PTHR10539, residues 5-376) spanning the entire protein. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence proteasome machinery network: 26S proteasome non-ATPase regulatory subunit 7 PSMD7 (0.999, highest) as adjacent lid subunit, 26S proteasome ATPase subunit 1 PSMC1 (0.999) as base AAA+ ATPase linking lid to core, Ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase 14 USP14 (0.999) as deubiquitinating enzyme regulating substrate entry, 26S proteasome non-ATPase regulatory subunit 11 PSMD11 (0.999) as another PCI domain scaffold, 26S proteasome ATPase subunit 4 PSMC4 (0.998) as base ATPase, 26S proteasome subunit alpha 2 PSMA2 (0.997) as core particle component, 26S proteasome non-ATPase regulatory subunit 8 PSMD8 (0.988) as lid subunit, 26S proteasome subunit beta 7 PSMB7 (0.988) as core catalytic subunit, PSMD5 (0.623) and PSMD10 (0.449) completing the comprehensive interactome demonstrating PSMD13 integration into the intact 26S holoenzyme. HPA localizes PSMD13 to nuclear speckles and cytosol with low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues indicating ubiquitous housekeeping function in protein quality control, classified as essential protein/predicted intracellular protein; low cancer specificity suggests constitutive expression required for cell viability, low immune cell specificity indicates broad lineage expression, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity reflects variable proteasome demands. ClinVar documents 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all consisting of large-scale chromosome 11 copy number gains including 11p15.5-15.4 (chr11:198510-3400939x3, chr11:192764-3362853x3, chr11:230615-8821443x3, chr11:230616-8250724x3, chr11:230615-5525355x3, chr11:230615-4851537x3, chr11:210300-8664358x3), 11p15.5-14.2 (chr11:230615-26881146x3), 11p15.5-13 (chr11:235934-33826995x3), and 11p15.5-15.3 (chr11:193146-12643136) encompassing the PSMD13 locus at 11p15.4; these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer predisposition though no specific monogenic PSMD13 phenotype established, potentially affecting proteostasis and cell cycle regulation through dosage sensitivity. Conservation from mouse Q9WVJ2 (376 aa, 100% length identity) and rat B0BN93 (376 aa, 100% identity) confirms essential proteasome function across mammals with high sequence identity in PCI domain and N-terminal repeats; ubiquitous expression correlates with fundamental protein degradation role; exceptional STRING interactions (all >0.988 for core subunits) position PSMD13 as integral non-redundant scaffold bridging lid and base subcomplexes; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 70.8) enables mapping of PCI winged helix fold critical for lid assembly; 24 ClinVar CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity for proper proteasome function; literature demonstrates PSMD13 stabilizes the proteasome lid structure, facilitates USP14 recruitment for substrate deubiquitination, and is essential for cell viability; mechanistic proposal involves PSMD13-mediated stabilization of the proteasome lid through PCI domain heterodimerization with other PCI subunits (PSMD7, PSMD11), coordination of ATPase ring assembly via PSMC1/PSMC4 interactions, regulation of substrate entry gate opening, and facilitation of USP14-dependent deubiquitination prior to substrate translocation into the 20S core particle.
