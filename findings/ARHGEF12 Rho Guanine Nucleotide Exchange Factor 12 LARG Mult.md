## Description
ARHGEF12 (Rho Guanine Nucleotide Exchange Factor 12, also known as Leukemia-Associated RhoGEF or LARG, UniProt Q9NZN5) is a 1544 amino acid cytoplasmic and membrane-associated protein with moderate characterization (123 papers) that functions as a RHOA-specific guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) linking G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) signaling to Rho GTPase activation and cytoskeletal reorganization through its multi-domain scaffold architecture. The protein contains a PDZ domain (IPR001478/cd23069/G3DSA:2.30.42.10 residues 64-151/70-145) for protein-protein interactions with membrane receptors, an RGS domain (IPR000340/cd08754/G3DSA:1.10.167.10 residues 349-570/349-563, GO:0005086 GTPase activator activity) for regulating Gα12/13 signaling, a Dbl homology (DH) RhoGEF domain (IPR000219/IPR001331/cd00160/G3DSA:1.20.900.10 residues 764-996/787-977/788-975/926-951, GO:0005085 guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity) for RHOA activation, and a Pleckstrin homology (PH) domain (IPR001849/cd13390/G3DSA:2.30.29.30 residues 995-1142/997-1142/995-1132, GO:0005515 protein binding) for membrane recruitment and phosphoinositide binding.
