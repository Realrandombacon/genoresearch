## Description
ATP2B1 (UniProt P20020), also known as PMCA1 or PMCA1kb, encodes a 1220 aa P-type cation-transporting ATPase functioning as the primary ubiquitously expressed plasma membrane calcium efflux pump maintaining intracellular calcium homeostasis against steep concentration gradients. InterPro analysis reveals conserved multi-domain P-type ATPase architecture characteristic of PMCA family: animal plasma membrane Ca2+-ATPase domain (cd02081, residues 78-928) defining PMCA1 membership within ATP2B1-4/PMCA1-4 subfamily; P-type ATPase catalytic core (IPR001757, residues 159-284/408-498/758-876) mediating ATP binding (GO:0005524), ATP hydrolysis (GO:0016887), and transporter activity (GO:0005215) through aspartyl phosphate intermediate formation; cation-transporting P-type ATPase N-terminal domain (IPR004014, residues 50-126) for regulatory interactions; cation-transporting P-type ATPase C-terminal domain (IPR006068, residues 880-1058) containing calmodulin-binding autoinhibitory region; P-type ATPase subfamily IIB membership (IPR006408) placing it within calcium/sodium/potassium transporter group; and multiple transmembrane/cytoplasmic domains (G3DSA:1.20.1110.10 transmembrane 104-177/293-296/339-498/812-1063, G3DSA:2.70.150.10 cytoplasmic transduction A 75-103/178-292, G3DSA:3.40.1110.10 cytoplasmic N 534-684, G3DSA:3.40.50.1000 HAD-like 685-811) forming the ion translocation pathway. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 74.6) indicating well-folded multi-pass transmembrane protein with stable catalytic core suitable for ATP-driven calcium transport. The TRPV6-ATP2B1 coupling confirms coordinated calcium influx-efflux cycling. HPA classifies ATP2B1 as disease-related enzyme/metabolic protein/FDA approved drug target with tissue enhanced expression highest in bone marrow (89.1 nTPM), detected in all tissues, low cancer/immune specificity, localized to plasma membrane consistent with calcium efflux function, involved in calcium/ion transport and calmodulin-binding/translocase activity, associated with intellectual disability. ClinVar lists 123 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.2517del p.Ala841fs, c.2750del p.Tyr917fs), nonsense mutations (c.3101C>G p.Ser1034Ter, c.559C>T p.Arg187Ter), and missense variants (c.3062T>C p.Ile1021Thr, c.1589C>T p.Pro530Leu, c.518A>G p.Asn173Ser, c.23C>T p.Ser8Leu, c.2887C>T p.Pro963Ser, c.1398G>T p.Met466Ile) though no single-gene disorder name is consistently assigned; MRD66 alias suggests mental retardation autosomal dominant 66 association. Functional hypothesis: ATP2B1 operates through integrated calcium homeostasis mechanisms: (1) Transport catalysis - P-type ATPase cycle uses ATP hydrolysis energy to pump 2 Ca2+ ions per ATP from cytosol to extracellular space against 10,000-fold gradient via E1-E2 conformational changes with aspartyl phosphate intermediate; (2) Calmodulin regulation - Apo-PMCA1 autoinhibited by C-terminal domain binding catalytic site; calcium-bound calmodulin binds C-terminus relieving inhibition enabling high-activity state; (3) Phosphorylation modulation - PKA/PKG phosphorylate specific serine/threonine residues enhancing pump activity and calcium affinity; (4) TRPV6 coupling - Functional pairing with apical calcium channel creates transcellular calcium transport pathway critical in intestine/kidney; (5) BSG trafficking - Basigin chaperone essential for proper PMCA1 plasma membrane localization; (6) Ubiquitous housekeeping - Unlike tissue-specific PMCA4 (smooth muscle), PMCA1 provides basal calcium clearance in all cell types; (7) Disease mechanism - Haploinsufficiency or loss-of-function causes intracellular calcium overload impairing cellular function; frameshift/nonsense mutations cause truncation while missense mutations in catalytic domains disrupt transport or regulatory domain mutations alter calmodulin/phosphorylation sensitivity; potential phenotypes include intellectual disability (MRD66), cardiovascular dysfunction, bone disorders (high bone marrow expression), and developmental defects; (8) Conservation - Mouse Atp2b1 and Rat Atp2b1 indicate essential conserved function across mammals.
