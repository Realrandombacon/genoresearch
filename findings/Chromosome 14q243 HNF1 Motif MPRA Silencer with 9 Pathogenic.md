## Description
LOC111591503 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 14q24.3 (NC_000014.9, positions 77390281-77390481, ~200 bp region) validated as an HNF1 motif-containing silencer through massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs/STARR-seq) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This genomic region lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrated reproducible silencer activity repressing super core promoter (SCP1) activity. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding regulatory element), preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID (no protein product). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants: 5 duplications (variant 155306: 14q11.2-32.33, chr14:20043514-106877229, ~87 Mb; variant 149176: 14q24.3-32.33, chr14:77222795-106879298, ~30 Mb; variant 146230: 14q11.2-32.33, chr14:20151149-106855263, ~87 Mb; variant 144518: 14q24.3-32.33, chr14:73655772-106879298, ~33 Mb) and 4 deletions (variant 155198: 14q24.3-31.1, chr14:73343213-78835059, ~5.5 Mb; variant 148347: 14q24.3-31.1, chr14:77193005-80476132, ~3.3 Mb; variant 144734: 14q24.1-31.1, chr14:69562099-81975384, ~12 Mb; variant 57809: 14q24.3-31.1, chr14:75489052-79610332, ~4.1 Mb; variant 57808: 14q24.3, chr14:73877072-78042422, ~4.2 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 14q24.3 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including PGF (placental growth factor, VEGF family, angiogenesis, placental development, pre-eclampsia, cancer angiogenesis), FOS (Fos proto-oncogene, AP-1 transcription factor component, cell proliferation, transformation, stress response, serum induction), TGFB3 (transforming growth factor beta 3, TGF-beta superfamily, embryonic development, palate fusion, lung development, fibrosis, cancer metastasis), NGB (neuroglobin, oxygen binding, neuronal hypoxia protection, Alzheimer disease, stroke), and NPC2 (Niemann-Pick C2 cholesterol transporter, lysosomal cholesterol egress, Niemann-Pick disease type C, lipid metabolism). Functional hypothesis: LOC111591503 functions as a cell type-specific transcriptional silencer containing HNF1 (hepatocyte nuclear factor 1) binding motifs, likely repressing target gene expression in erythroid/myeloid lineages (K562 cells) while potentially acting as enhancer in other contexts. The HNF1 motif suggests liver/pancreas/kidney regulatory activity. The 9 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts silencer activity and multiple gene expression programs. Disease mechanism involves PGF dysregulation causing abnormal angiogenesis (pre-eclampsia, cancer vascularization), FOS overexpression driving oncogenic transformation (AP-1 target gene activation), TGFB3 haploinsufficiency causing palatal defects and pulmonary fibrosis, NGB deficiency impairing neuronal hypoxia tolerance, and NPC2 dysfunction causing lysosomal cholesterol accumulation (Niemann-Pick type C). The 14q24.3 duplication/deletion syndromes may present with angiogenic abnormalities, cancer predisposition, craniofacial defects, neurodegeneration, and lipid metabolism disorders. The MPRA validation combined with ClinVar evidence (9 CNVs) and PGF-FOS-TGFB3 gene cluster confirms this locus functions as a critical HNF1-dependent silencer coordinating angiogenesis-oncogenesis-TGF-beta signaling in hematopoietic and epithelial tissues.
