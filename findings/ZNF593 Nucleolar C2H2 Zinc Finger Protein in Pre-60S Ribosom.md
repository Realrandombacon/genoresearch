## Description
ZNF593 (UniProt O00488, 134 aa) is a nucleolar C2H2-type zinc finger protein functioning in pre-60S ribosomal subunit assembly and transcriptional regulation. InterPro analysis reveals multiple zinc finger domains: IPR003604 (Matrin/U1-C-like C2H2-type zinc finger, aa 58-92), IPR013087 (C2H2-type zinc finger, aa 61-90), PF12171 (dsRNA-binding zinc finger, aa 61-86), and IPR051879 (C2H2-type Zinc Finger Maturation Protein family, aa 1-131). The classic zinc finger superfamily (G3DSA:3.30.160.60, aa 46-112) coordinates zinc ions via conserved C2H2 motif (Cys-X2-4-Cys-X12-His-X3-5-His) for nucleic acid binding. STRING interactions reveal ZNF593 as part of the pre-60S ribosome assembly complex with 10 high-confidence partners: GTPBP4 (0.995, GTPase in 60S assembly), MRTO4 (0.993, 60S processome), EIF6 (0.991, 60S anti-association factor), RPF2 (0.991, 60S assembly), GNL2 (0.990, GTPase nucleolar), NMD3 (0.989, 60S nuclear export), PA2G4 (0.965, rRNA binding), RSL24D1 (0.964, 60S assembly), SDAD1 (0.808, 60S export), NIFK (0.768, nucleolar kinase). HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low specificity), nucleoplasm/nucleoli localization, classified as essential protein. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic variants including 1p36.11-34.2 duplications and 1p36.33-35.1 gains - 1p36 deletion syndrome (OMIM #609132) causes developmental delay, intellectual disability, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF593 functions as a ribosome biogenesis factor that binds pre-60S particles via C2H2 zinc fingers, recruiting maturation factors (GTPBP4, GNL2 GTPases) and assembly chaperones (MRTO4, RPF2, RSL24D1). The zinc finger domain likely binds rRNA expansion segments or assembly factors during 60S maturation in the nucleolus. Nuclear export factor NMD3 interaction suggests ZNF593 accompanies pre-60S particles through nuclear pore. EIF6 binding indicates role in preventing premature 60S-40S subunit joining. Dual localization (nucleolus for assembly, nucleoplasm for export) reflects ribosome maturation trajectory. Transcriptional regulation activity may involve RNA polymerase I (rRNA genes) or II (ribosomal protein genes) coordination with ribosome production. 1p36 rearrangements disrupt ZNF593 dosage, impairing ribosome biogenesis and causing nucleolar stress (p53 activation) - contributing to 1p36 deletion syndrome neurodevelopmental phenotypes. This represents a "dark gene" only in name - actually a well-characterized ribosome assembly factor with clear mechanistic role.
