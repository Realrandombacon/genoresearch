## Description
VOPP1 (alias ECOP, GASP, WBP1L2, vesicular overexpressed in cancer prosurvival protein 1) encodes a 172 amino acid partially structured endolysosomal membrane protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 65.6) containing VOPP1 N-terminal domain (IPR060429/PF27964, aa 6-54), VOPP1 domain (IPR060430/PF27963, aa 57-90), and belonging to the PTHR14971 family (aa 1-172). HPA shows retina-enriched expression (233.4 nTPM, tissue enhanced category), detected in all tissues distribution, cytoplasmic vesicle membrane/late endosome/lysosome localization, cancer-enhanced specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 7p22.3-q36.3 and 7p11.2 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with endolysosomal trafficking and tumor suppressor regulators: TMEM207 (transmembrane protein 207, vesicle trafficking, 0.617), SEC61G (SEC61 translocon subunit gamma, ER translocation, 0.613), VWA7 (von Willebrand factor A domain 7, 0.613), WWOX (WW domain containing oxidoreductase, tumor suppressor, 0.568), VSTM2A (V-set and transmembrane domain containing 2A, 0.529), C7orf25 (chromosome 7 ORF 25, 0.499), SEPTIN14 (septin 14, cytoskeleton, 0.495), EGFR (epidermal growth factor receptor, receptor tyrosine kinase, 0.475), WBP1 (WW domain binding protein 1, YAP/TAZ signaling, 0.444), and LANCL2 (lanthionine synthetase C-like 2, ABA signaling, 0.422). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8R1C3 172 aa, rat B5DEK8 172 aa, bovine A6QNZ8 172 aa, Xenopus Q0V9A8 167 aa) indicates strong functional constraint. The VOPP1 domain architecture combined with endolysosomal localization and EGFR/WWOX interactions strongly suggests VOPP1 functions as an endolysosomal scaffold protein coordinating growth factor receptor trafficking with tumor suppressor activity. EGFR interaction (0.475) places VOPP1 at the epidermal growth factor receptor endocytic pathway, potentially regulating EGFR degradation versus recycling decisions at late endosomes/lysosomes. WWOX interaction (0.568) links VOPP1 to tumor suppressor function—WWOX is a proline-rich tumor suppressor that undergoes loss of heterozygosity in multiple cancers and regulates apoptosis through p53-dependent and independent pathways. WBP1 interaction (0.444) suggests coupling to Hippo/YAP signaling through WW domain-mediated protein-protein interactions. The vesicular/late endosome/lysosome localization indicates function in receptor tyrosine kinase downregulation through lysosomal degradation, potentially serving as a checkpoint for growth signal termination. Retina enrichment (233.4 nTPM) suggests specialized function in photoreceptor outer segment disc turnover or retinal pigment epithelium phagocytosis, where endolysosomal trafficking is critical for visual cycle maintenance. Cancer-enhanced expression indicates oncogenic role through dysregulated EGFR trafficking or WWOX tumor suppressor antagonism, potentially promoting cell survival through impaired growth factor receptor degradation. Medium pLDDT score (65.6) indicates partial disorder with structured VOPP1 domains, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for WW domain-mediated interaction networks. Pathogenic 7p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting endolysosomal trafficking, EGFR signaling termination, and WWOX tumor suppressor function, potentially contributing to 7p duplication/deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental defects, cancer predisposition, and retinal degeneration through impaired receptor trafficking and apoptotic regulation.
