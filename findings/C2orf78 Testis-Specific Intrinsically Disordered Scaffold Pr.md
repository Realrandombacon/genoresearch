## Description
C2orf78 (alias chromosome 2 open reading frame 78, DUF4629-containing protein) encodes a 922 amino acid large intrinsically disordered nuclear protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 42.5) containing a DUF4629 domain of unknown function (IPR027898, aa 495-643), DUF4629 Pfam domain (PF15442, aa 495-643), and belonging to the PTHR31466 family (GENE 5591-RELATED, aa 31-922). HPA shows tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (11.9 nTPM), detected in single tissues distribution, predicted nucleoplasm protein class, not detected in cancer, not detected in immune cells, and group enriched single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 2p13.3-12, 2p25.1-q13, 2p25.3-q37.3, and 2p16.1-11.2 copy number losses/gains associated with chromosomal deletion/duplication syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with GTPase, kinase, and neurodevelopmental proteins: GIMAP2 (GTPase of immune-associated protein 2, small GTPase regulating lymphocyte survival, 0.573), NBPF14 (neuroblastoma breakpoint family member 14, 1p36 deletion candidate, 0.447), CDKL4 (cyclin-dependent kinase-like 4, serine/threonine kinase, 0.445), and MELTF (melanotransferrin, iron transport glycoprotein, 0.415). Conservation across vertebrates (human, mouse, chimp, hedgehog, naked mole rat) indicates functional constraint. The DUF4629 domain architecture combined with large protein size (922 aa) and intrinsically disordered structure (pLDDT 42.5) strongly suggests C2orf78 functions as a large scaffold protein involved in multi-protein complex assembly and signal transduction. GIMAP2 interaction (0.573) is mechanistically central—GIMAP proteins are small GTPases critical for T-cell survival and lymphocyte homeostasis, but GIMAP2 is also expressed in testis where it regulates germ cell apoptosis, suggesting C2orf78 functions as a GIMAP2 scaffolding protein in spermatogenesis and germ cell survival. NBPF14 association (0.447) links to neurodevelopmental regulation—NBPF14 is a member of the neuroblastoma breakpoint family implicated in 1p36 deletion syndrome, suggesting C2orf78 may function as a NBPF cofactor in chromatin regulation or cell cycle control during germ cell development. CDKL4 interaction (0.445) connects to kinase signaling—CDKL4 is a serine/threonine kinase related to cell cycle regulation, suggesting C2orf78 may recruit or regulate CDKL4 activity in testis-specific phosphorylation events controlling meiotic progression or spermatid differentiation. MELTF association (0.415) places C2orf78 in iron metabolism—melanotransferrin is an iron-binding transferrin homolog highly expressed in testis and placenta, suggesting C2orf78 may coordinate iron homeostasis with germ cell development, as iron is essential for spermatogenesis and steroidogenesis. Testis enrichment (11.9 nTPM) is striking—this represents the highest expression level among all tissues, indicating specialized function in spermatogenesis and germ cell development, consistent with GIMAP2 role in germ cell apoptosis regulation and MELTF iron transport function. Detected in single tissues indicates highly restricted expression pattern, consistent with germ cell-specific function. Not detected in cancer indicates tumor suppressor rather than oncogene function. Not detected in immune cells despite GIMAP2 interaction suggests testis-specific paralogue function rather than canonical immune role. Group enriched single cell pattern indicates specific upregulation in spermatogenic cell lineages. Low pLDDT score (42.5) indicates intrinsically disordered protein with extensive flexible regions, consistent with large scaffold proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly and post-translational modification recognition. IDP architecture enables binding to multiple partners (GIMAP2, NBPF14, CDKL4, MELTF) through short linear motifs and facilitates rapid signaling complex assembly and disassembly. Nucleoplasm localization suggests nuclear function potentially involving chromatin regulation or transcriptional control during meiosis. Pathogenic 2p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting GIMAP2 scaffolding, NBPF complex formation, CDKL4 kinase regulation, and iron homeostasis, potentially contributing to 2p13 deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, and hypogonadism through impaired neurodevelopmental signaling and germ cell maturation.
