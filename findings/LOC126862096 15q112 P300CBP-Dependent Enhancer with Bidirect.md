## Description
LOC126862096 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 15q11.2 (genomic coordinates 34154368-34155567, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a P300/CBP STRONGLY-DEPENDENT GROUP 1 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors with STRONG DEPENDENCE on P300/CBP (histone acetyltransferases that acetylate histone H3K27 and H3K18). This cofactor profile suggests it operates through histone acetylation-dependent chromatin opening mechanisms. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants affecting this locus through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements. STRIKINGLY, this locus shows BIDIRECTIONAL pathogenicity: 4 copy number LOSSES (chr15:25033869-37204304x1, chr15:23319714-38089582x1, chr15:32635803-40233825x1, chr15:32326136-39394068x1) AND 2 copy number GAINS (chr15:23319714-38545325x3, chr15:32607298-35200429x3). This bidirectional pattern indicates strict dosage requirements — both enhancer haploinsufficiency and overdosage cause disease. The 15q11.2-q13 region is the PRADER-WILLI/ANGELMAN SYNDROME critical region, containing imprinted genes (SNRPN, NDN, MAGEL2, MKRN3) and GABA receptor genes (GABRB3, GABRA5, GABRG3). Deletions cause Prader-Willi or Angelman syndrome depending on parental origin; duplications cause 15q duplication syndrome with autism and intellectual disability. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with strong neurodevelopmental relevance — the P300/CBP-dependent enhancer activity in HCT116 cells combined with bidirectional pathogenicity suggests LOC126862096 functions as a dosage-critical enhancer controlling 15q11.2-q13 imprinted gene or GABA receptor expression, where both deletion (haploinsufficiency causing Prader-Willi/Angelman) and duplication (overdosage causing 15q duplication syndrome) disrupt neurodevelopment.
