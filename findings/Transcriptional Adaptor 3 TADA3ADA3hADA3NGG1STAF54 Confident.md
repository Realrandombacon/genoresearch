## Description
TADA3 (UniProt O75528), also known as ADA3, NGG1, STAF54, hADA3, or TADA3L, encodes a 432 aa nuclear transcriptional co-activator functioning as an essential adaptor subunit of the SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5-Acetyltransferase) and STAGA (SPT3-TAF9-GCN5-Acetyltransferase) histone acetyltransferase complexes that bridges sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional activators to the basal transcriptional machinery through integrated protein-protein interaction scaffolding and nucleosome acetylation facilitation mechanisms.
