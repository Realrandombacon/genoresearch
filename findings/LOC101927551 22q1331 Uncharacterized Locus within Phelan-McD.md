## Description
LOC101927551 (Gene ID: 101927551) is an uncharacterized genetic locus located on chromosome 22q13.31 (NC_000022.11, positions 45000564-45003618, ~3kb region). Despite having an Entrez Gene ID, this locus lacks protein-level characterization across major databases. No UniProt entry exists, preventing domain analysis via InterPro and structure prediction via AlphaFold. Human Protein Atlas has no expression data for this gene (ENSG00000230922), indicating the gene is either not expressed at detectable levels in surveyed tissues, lacks validated antibodies, or may not encode a stable protein product. STRING database returns no protein-protein interactions, consistent with absence of a characterized protein. However, ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number variations spanning chromosome 22q13 regions including deletions (chr22:44549957-50789329, chr22:44702479_50806138del) and duplications (chr22:42080077-50739836)x3. These CNVs range from localized (~6Mb) to extensive spans covering most of 22q13, suggesting dosage sensitivity of this genomic region. The 22q13.3 chromosomal location places LOC101927551 within the Phelan-McDermid syndrome critical region, which is caused by deletions or mutations affecting SHANK3 and neighboring genes. Functional hypothesis: LOC101927551 likely represents either (1) a very lowly expressed or tissue-restricted protein-coding gene that evades detection by standard proteomic surveys, (2) a long non-coding RNA with regulatory function where CNVs disrupt enhancer/promoter architecture affecting neighboring genes like SHANK3, or (3) a component of the larger 22q13 deletion syndrome critical region where dosage affects multiple genes simultaneously. The 22q13 deletion syndrome phenotype includes global developmental delay, intellectual disability, absent or severely delayed speech, hypotonia, and autism spectrum behaviors. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency where deletion of LOC101927551 contributes additively to the Phelan-McDermid syndrome phenotype alongside SHANK3 loss. The large CNV spans make it difficult to attribute phenotypic effects specifically to this locus versus adjacent genes including SHANK3, BRD1, and MAPK8IP2. Priority experiments include transcript identification via RNA-seq across diverse tissues (particularly brain given 22q13 syndrome associations), ORF prediction and conservation analysis across primates, CRISPR-based functional assays in neuronal models, and careful genotype-phenotype correlation in patients with smaller, more localized CNVs to determine if LOC101927551 independently contributes to neurodevelopmental phenotypes.
