## Description
ZNF581 encodes a 197-amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q9P0T4) localized to chromosome 19q13.42. InterPro analysis reveals three canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 87-109, 115-137, and 145-167, plus a KRÜPPEL-LIKE TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR family signature (PTHR23235, residues 38-167), indicating sequence-specific DNA binding capability. HPA confirms nuclear localization (nucleoplasm, vesicles, cytosol) with low tissue specificity but ubiquitous expression across all tissues examined. STRING interactions identify 4 medium-confidence partners: CCDC97 (0.607), HIKESHI (0.547), DIRAS2 (0.450), and DYRK1B (0.409), suggesting roles in nuclear organization, nuclear import, GTPase signaling, and cell cycle regulation. Clinical significance is high: ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (12 variants) and losses (6 variants) spanning 19q13.42-13.43, plus one splice region variant (c.-19-2A>G). This CNV burden exceeds typical transcription factors and suggests ZNF581 regulates dosage-sensitive target genes. Hypothesis: ZNF581 functions as a context-dependent transcriptional repressor or activator binding GC-rich promoter/enhancer elements via its three C2H2 fingers, recruiting co-repressor complexes (HDACs, NCoR/SMRT) or co-activators (p300/CBP) depending on post-translational modifications. Interaction with HIKESHI suggests regulated nuclear import; CCDC97 partnership implies chromatin remodeling involvement; DIRAS2 link indicates Rho-GTPase pathway crosstalk; DYRK1B association suggests cell cycle checkpoint regulation. High CNV burden on 19q13.43 implicates ZNF581 disruption in 19q deletion syndromes (developmental delay, growth retardation) and myeloid malignancies where 19q rearrangements are recurrent. AlphaFold structure unavailable (timeout), but C2H2 fold is highly conserved across eukaryotes.
