## Description
ANAPC13 (Anaphase-promoting complex subunit 13, Q9BS18) encodes a remarkably small 74 aa protein functioning as an essential structural component of the anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C), a multi-subunit RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase that controls metaphase-anaphase transition and cell cycle exit through targeted ubiquitination of securin and cyclin B, with emerging critical roles in oocyte maturation, female fertility, and regulatory T cell (Treg) function linking cell cycle machinery to reproductive biology and immune regulation. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 73.6): Apc13 domain (IPR008401/PF05839/PTHR28672, residues 1-74/1-73/1-74) spanning the entire protein length forming a compact structural module that integrates into the APC/C core scaffold, GO term anaphase-promoting complex (GO:0005680) confirming its role as integral subunit of this massive ~1.5 MDa cell cycle regulator; despite its small size, the high pLDDT score indicates a stable folded structure essential for complex assembly rather than a disordered linker.
