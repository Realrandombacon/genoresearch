## Description
TEX46 encodes a 121-amino acid testis-specific protein (UniProt H3BTG2) with strong tissue enrichment in testis (58.5 nTPM, HPA). The protein contains a DUF5531 domain (PF17671, residues 55-120) and belongs to the Testis-expressed protein 46 family (IPR038788, PTHR39412). ClinVar documents 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (gains and losses on chromosome 1p36), suggesting dosage sensitivity. No STRING interactions detected, indicating this protein may function independently or with uncharacterized partners. HPA classifies it as a predicted intracellular protein associated with fertilization. Mouse ortholog (Q9CPU3) is 165 aa, suggesting moderate conservation with C-terminal extension. The DUF5531 domain of unknown function likely mediates protein-protein interactions during spermatogenesis. CNVs affecting TEX46 locus may disrupt sperm maturation or fertilization capacity, consistent with testis-specific expression pattern.
