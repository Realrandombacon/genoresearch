## Description
UGT2B11 encodes a 529 aa UDP-glucuronosyltransferase (UniProt O75310) critical for phase II detoxification and steroid hormone metabolism. The protein contains the canonical UDP-glucuronosyl/UDP-glucosyltransferase domain (IPR002213, aa24-525; PF00201) with conserved glycosyltransferase signature (PS00375, aa356-399) and UDP-glycosyltransferase family conserved site (IPR035595, aa356-399). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 93.3), indicating a well-folded GT-B fold glycosyltransferase structure typical of UGT enzymes. HPA shows breast-enriched expression (225.4 nTPM) with tissue-specific distribution, suggesting specialized role in breast tissue steroid/xenobiotic metabolism. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling with cytochrome P450 enzymes: CYP3A4 (0.974), CYP2D6 (0.961), CYP2E1 (0.950), CYP1A1 (0.936), CYP2A6 (0.911), plus UDP-glucuronic acid pathway enzymes GUSB (0.942) and UGDH (0.937), placing UGT2B11 within the coordinated phase I/II drug metabolism network. Literature (30 papers) demonstrates UGT2B11 glucuronidates C19 and C21 hydroxysteroids with distinct regio- and stereo-selectivity, enabling estrogen metabolic process regulation. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants (primarily copy number losses/gains on 4q13.2), though specific disease associations remain undefined. UGT2B11 likely functions as a breast-specific detoxification enzyme coordinating with CYP450s for sequential oxidation-glucuronidation of endogenous steroids (estrogens, androgens) and exogenous xenobiotics, with the well-folded structure enabling stable UDP-glucuronic acid binding and transfer to diverse acceptor substrates.
