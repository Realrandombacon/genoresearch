## Description
TMEM31 encodes a 168-amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt Q5JXX7) with strong testis-specific expression (105.6 nTPM, tissue-enriched) and nucleolar fibrillar center localization. STRING interaction analysis reveals 10 high-confidence protein partners (scores 0.449-0.482) that are predominantly proteasome subunits: PSMC1 (ATPase), PSMD14/PSMD13/PSMD4/PSMD10/PSMD3 (19S regulatory particle), ADRM1 (proteasome receptor), and CCDC92. This interaction profile strongly suggests TMEM31 functions as a proteasome adaptor or assembly factor, potentially linking the 26S proteasome to nucleolar protein quality control. The X-chromosomal location (Xq13.1-q22.2) and 177 ClinVar pathogenic variants (primarily copy number gains/losses) indicate extreme dosage sensitivity—X-chromosome aneuploidies (Klinefelter, Turner syndromes) likely disrupt TMEM31 copy number, contributing to testicular dysfunction and spermatogenesis defects. The testis-enriched expression pattern combined with proteasome interactions suggests a specialized role in managing ubiquitinated substrates during meiosis or spermatid maturation, where precise protein turnover is critical. Mechanistically, TMEM31 may serve as a nucleolar proteasome anchor that recognizes specific ubiquitinated testis-expressed substrates, coordinating their degradation during germ cell development.
