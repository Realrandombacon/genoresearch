# GDPD3: Lysophospholipase D Enzyme Regulating LPA Signaling in Cancer Stem Cells, Neuropathic Pain, and Metabolic Disease

## Description
GDPD3 (Glycerophosphodiester Phosphodiesterase Domain-Containing 3, also known as lysophospholipase D) is a 318 amino acid nucleoplasmic/cytosolic enzyme (UniProt Q7L5L3) with partial characterization (29 papers) that functions as a lysophosphatidic acid (LPA)-producing enzyme critical for cancer stem cell maintenance, lipid metabolism, and pain signaling. The protein contains a glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase domain (IPR030395/PF03009, residues 39-308/44-302) with phosphoric diester hydrolase activity (GO:0008081), placing it in the PLC-like phosphodiesterase TIM beta/alpha-barrel superfamily (IPR017946/G3DSA:3.20.20.190, residues 34-311) and glycerophosphoryl diester phosphodiesterase-related family (IPR052271/PTHR42758, residues 1-313). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with ENPP3 (0.924, ectonucleotide pyrophosphatase), ENPP1 (0.828), PLA2G10 (0.799, phospholipase A2), PLA2G4A (0.782, cytosolic phospholipase A2), GDPD1 (0.780, glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase), LPCAT4 (0.735, lysophosphatidylcholine acyltransferase), PLA2G2A (0.725), ENPP6 (0.650), TMEM86B (0.650), and ENPP2/autotaxin (0.558, major LPA-producing enzyme), placing GDPD3 in the LPA biosynthesis pathway alongside autotaxin. Recent 2025-2026 literature demonstrates GDPD3 is required to maintain chronic myelogenous leukemia stem cells through lysophospholipase D activity, deficiency alleviates neuropathic pain via PGE2 and PPARγ pathway reprogramming of macrophage polarization, and GDPD3 regulates epithelial-mesenchymal transition in prostate adenocarcinoma via LPA/LPAR1/AKT axis. HPA data shows esophagus-enriched expression (66.4 nTPM, tissue enhanced), cancer-enhanced pattern, immune cell enhanced specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, cytokinetic bridge, and cytosol. ClinVar contains 285 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and deletions at 16p11.2 (a well-known neurodevelopmental disorder locus), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential metabolic, hematologic, or neurodevelopmental phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 92.2) indicates very high confidence with a well-folded TIM barrel catalytic domain. Functional hypothesis: GDPD3 acts as a lysophospholipase D enzyme that converts lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC) to lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) through its glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase domain, functioning in parallel with or complementary to autotaxin (ENPP2) in LPA biosynthesis, with LPA production driving cancer stem cell self-renewal in CML through LPAR1-AKT signaling, promoting EMT and metastasis in prostate cancer, regulating macrophage polarization through PPARγ and PGE2 pathways in neuropathic pain, and contributing to hepatic steatosis through increased fatty acid uptake, making it a therapeutic target for leukemia, solid tumors, chronic pain, and metabolic disorders.
