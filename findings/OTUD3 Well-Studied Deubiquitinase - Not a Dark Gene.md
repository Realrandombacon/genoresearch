## Description
OTUD3 encodes a deubiquitinase containing an OTU domain, involved in protein stability and signaling. It plays critical roles in cancer progression (sunitinib resistance in renal cell carcinoma, breast cancer metastasis via TGF-β pathway, cholangiocarcinoma) and neuroprotection (reducing ferroptosis via PLK1 stabilization). With 81 literature papers and established mechanistic roles in deubiquitination and cell fate, OTUD3 is definitively a 'well-studied' gene and does not meet the criteria for a 'dark gene'.
