## Description
LLGL2 (LLGL scribble cell polarity complex component 2, also known as Lethal(2) giant larvae protein homolog 2, Q6P1M3) encodes a 1020 aa intracellular scaffolding protein functioning as a critical regulator of epithelial cell polarity, asymmetric cell division, and tumor suppression through integration into the evolutionarily conserved Scribble polarity complex that controls basolateral membrane identity and apical-basal polarity establishment. Structural architecture reveals a multi-domain organization characteristic of polarity scaffolding proteins: Lethal(2) giant larvae protein family domain (IPR000664/PR00962, residues 15-33/296-318/348-368) defining evolutionary relationship to Drosophila Lgl tumor suppressor, WD40 repeat domains (IPR001680/PF00400/PS50082, residues 24-60/62-101/181-217/432-451/433-451) forming beta-propeller structures for protein-protein interactions with GO term for protein binding (GO:0005515), LLGL2-specific N-terminal domain (IPR013577/PF08366, residues 273-372) providing functional specificity within the Lgl family, YVTN repeat-like quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase superfamily (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 19-174/175-267/322-491) suggesting structural similarity to beta-propeller folds, and WD40/YVTN repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR015943/IPR036322, residues 19-174/175-491/26-610) confirming extensive protein interaction capacity; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 84.9, high confidence) confirms a well-folded 1020 aa scaffolding protein with organized domain architecture consistent with stable multi-protein complex assembly requiring precise spatial arrangement of interaction surfaces for polarity complex formation.
