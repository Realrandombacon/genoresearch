## Description
CEP164 encodes centrosomal protein of 164 kDa (CEP164/NPHP15), a large 1460 aa scaffolding protein (UniProt Q9UPV0) belonging to the microtubule and Golgi organization protein family that functions as an essential component of distal appendages (DAPs) at the mother centriole, serving as the critical platform for ciliogenesis initiation through recruitment of Tau tubulin kinase 2 (TTBK2) and subsequent loading of intraflagellar transport (IFT) machinery, while also participating in DNA damage response and chromosome segregation. Functional mechanism: CEP164 operates through hierarchical distal appendage assembly cycle: (1) Centriolar recruitment - CEP83 (0.997) and SCLT1 (0.997) anchor CEP164 to distal ends of mature mother centrioles via N-terminal domain (1-110); (2) DAP scaffold formation - Coiled-coil domain (622-1034) mediates CEP164 homodimerization creating platform for additional DAP proteins (FBF1 0.990, CEP89 0.979); (3) TTBK2 recruitment - WW domain (56-89) binds TTBK2 (0.441) or associated adaptors recruiting kinase to centriole; (4) TTBK2 activation - Recruited TTBK2 phosphorylates centriolar substrates triggering removal of CP110 capping protein; (5) IFT loading - Phosphorylation cascade enables recruitment of IFT-A/B complexes via RAB3IP (0.894), RAB8A (0.871) for axoneme extension; (6) Ciliary membrane docking - FBF1 (0.990) regulates transition zone permeability establishing ciliary compartment; (7) Cell cycle coordination - PLK1 (0.878) phosphorylation links ciliogenesis to cell cycle progression (cilia disassemble before mitosis); (8) DNA damage response - Nuclear pool participates in DNA repair independent of ciliary function.
