## Description
ZNF81 encodes a 661-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as an X-linked transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 20-92) that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor complexes for heterochromatin-mediated gene silencing, plus three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 274-408) for sequence-specific DNA recognition. HPA classification shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues, consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation roles. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with ZNF385B (score 0.870) and ARAF (score 0.809), plus medium-confidence interactions with TRIM28 (0.622), PHF8 (0.615), TIMP1 (0.603), POGZ (0.595), TBC1D25 (0.564), CHST7 (0.483), NXF5 (0.466), and WDR13 (0.459)—forming a chromatin regulation network. ClinVar documents 145 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly X chromosome copy number losses and deletions (Xp22.33-11.23 region), directly linking ZNF81 dosage haploinsufficiency to X-linked intellectual disability (MRX45). The X-chromosomal location (Xp11.23) and disease association indicate ZNF81 regulates neurodevelopmental genes, with mutations causing cognitive impairment through disrupted transcriptional repression of neuronal targets. Gene aliases HFZ20 and MRX45 confirm historical disease linkage.
