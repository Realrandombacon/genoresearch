# LRRC34: Leucine-Rich Repeat Scaffold Protein Localizing to Mitotic Apparatus and Primary Cilium with Testis-Enriched Expression

## Description
LRRC34 encodes a 464 aa leucine-rich repeat (LRR) scaffold protein with a ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10). InterPro analysis reveals 3 canonical LRR motifs (IPR001611, PF13516) at positions 152-228, classifying it within the "Leucine-rich repeat-containing regulator of pluripotency" family (IPR052201, PTHR24111). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.9), indicating a well-folded solenoid architecture typical of LRR proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions.

HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (11.9 nTPM) with subcellular localization to microtubules, cytokinetic bridge, mitotic spindle, primary cilium, and basal body—structures critical for cell division and ciliary signaling. This localization pattern suggests LRRC34 functions as a scaffold organizing protein complexes at the mitotic apparatus and ciliary base. The LRR domains likely mediate interactions with centrosomal or spindle-associated proteins, analogous to other LRR-containing cell cycle regulators.

ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number gains on chromosome 3q), implicating LRRC34 dosage sensitivity in disease. The combination of testis expression, mitotic localization, and clinical variants suggests a role in germ cell division or ciliary function—defects could cause ciliopathies or germ cell tumors. The ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold (residues 81-409) may also indicate RNA-binding capacity, potentially linking LRRC34 to post-transcriptional regulation during cell division.
