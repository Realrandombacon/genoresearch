## Description
SPAG11A encodes a 123 aa sperm-associated antigen 11A (UniProt Q6PDA7) located at 8p23.1, functioning as an epididymis-specific secretory protein critical for sperm maturation, epididymal immune defense, and reproductive tract integrity. InterPro analysis reveals a HE2/sperm antigen domain architecture: Sperm-associated antigen 11 A/B family domain (IPR007988, aa 1-100), Sperm antigen HE2 Pfam domain (PF05324, aa 5-71) characteristic of epididymal secretory proteins, and PTHR14081 superfamily membership, with extracellular region localization (GO:0005576). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 57.8), indicating a partially structured protein with potential intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with secretory proteins that undergo conformational flexibility for partner binding. STRING interactions reveal a defensin-centered network: DEFB113 (0.550), DEFB126 (0.544), DEFB119 (0.507), DEFB118 (0.426), DEFB103B (0.425) - all beta-defensin family antimicrobial peptides, plus WFDC9 (0.466, epididymal secretory protein), C10orf71 (0.507), TRIM64/TRIM64B (0.430-0.449, tripartite motif proteins), and OR5H14 (0.445, olfactory receptor). ClinVar reports 53 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 8p23.1 copy number losses (x1) and gains (x3) with uncertain significance, suggesting clinical relevance in chromosomal disorder syndromes affecting male reproduction. Literature (15 papers) details emerging reproductive functions: (1) Subfertility phenotype - SPAG11A knockout mice displaying subfertility with perturbations in sperm proteome (1 citation, 2024); (2) Epididymal histopathology - SPAG11A gene knockout causing epididymal structural defects with histopathological and molecular alterations (2 citations, 2024); (3) Tumorigenesis susceptibility - SPAG11A knockout male reproductive tract tissues showing increased susceptibility to DEN-induced tumorigenesis (0 citations, 2025); (4) Varicocele association - SPAG11A expression altered in infertile men with grade 1-2 varicocele before/after treatment (0 citations, 2024); (5) siRNA ablation studies - SPAG11A knockdown reducing sperm motility and epididymal immune responses. Functional hypothesis: SPAG11A likely functions through: (1) Sperm Maturation Factor - epididymal secretion binding sperm surface during transit through caput epididymis, modifying sperm membrane proteins for motility acquisition and fertilization competence; (2) Antimicrobial Defense - HE2/defensin-like structure providing broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity in epididymal lumen, protecting sperm from bacterial/fungal pathogens via membrane disruption mechanisms parallel to beta-defensins; (3) Immune Modulation - interaction with TRIM64/TRIM64B suggesting ubiquitin-mediated regulation of epididymal immune responses, potentially limiting inflammation while maintaining antimicrobial surveillance; (4) Reproductive Tract Tumor Suppression - knockout susceptibility to DEN-induced tumorigenesis suggesting SPAG11A maintains epithelial integrity and prevents malignant transformation in reproductive tract tissues; (5) Olfactory-Reproductive Crosstalk - OR5H14 interaction hinting at chemosensory regulation of epididymal function or sperm guidance mechanisms. The HE2-defensin architecture places SPAG11A within the epididymal antimicrobial peptide family, parallel to DEFB126 and other beta-defensins. Disease implications include: male subfertility/infertility, varicocele-associated sperm defects, epididymal infections, reproductive tract tumorigenesis, 8p23 chromosomal disorder syndromes. Despite 15 papers, SPAG11A's antimicrobial mechanism, tumor suppression pathway, and therapeutic potential for male infertility remain underexplored.
