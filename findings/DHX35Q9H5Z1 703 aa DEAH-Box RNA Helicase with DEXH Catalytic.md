## Description
DHX35 (DEAH-Box Helicase 35, UniProt Q9H5Z1) is a 703 aa nucleoplasmic ATP-dependent RNA helicase representing a partially characterized dark gene with 27 literature references and emerging clinical relevance in spliceosome fidelity and innate immunity. Structural analysis reveals canonical DEAH-box helicase architecture: N-terminal DEXH-box helicase domain (aa 54-238, cd17980) containing conserved DEAH motif (aa 171-180, IPR002464), C-terminal helicase domain (aa 243-406, cd18791/PF00271/IPR001650) forming the ATPase core, helicase-associated domain 2 (HA2, aa 458-549, IPR007502/IPR048333 winged-helix domain aa 459-488) mediating RNA substrate recognition, and C-terminal OB fold (aa 607-683, IPR011709) for single-stranded RNA binding. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT=86.6) consistent with well-folded DEAH-box helicase superfamily containing characteristic RecA-like ATPase domains (G3DSA:3.40.50.300 P-loop NTPase, aa 21-241/242-417) and RNA-binding modules. STRING interactions place DHX35 within spliceosome assembly network with very high confidence: PRPF19 (0.917, pre-mRNA processing factor 19), YJU2 (0.885, splicing factor), EFTUD2 (0.853, U5 snRNP component), CDC40 (0.843, cell division cycle 40), PLRG1 (0.839, pleiotropic regulator 1), TSSC4 (0.816, tumor suppressing subtransferase 4), SLU7 (0.815, splicing factor), CDC5L (0.809, cell division cycle 5-like), SYF2 (0.780, pre-mRNA splicing factor), and EAPP (0.552, E2F-associated phosphoprotein) — indicating DHX35 functions in spliceosome activation, catalytic step coordination, and aberrant substrate rejection. HPA expression confirms ubiquitous pattern: low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, nucleoplasm/nuclear bodies/centrosome localization consistent with nuclear RNA processing function, low cancer specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell expression. ClinVar identifies 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (20q11.21-13.12, chr20:31010829-44560369x1; 20q11.22-13.12, chr20:35237946-47631818x1), deletions, and copy number gains (20p13-q13.33, chr20:61569-62915555x3; 20p12.2-q12, chr20:9811433-39316956x3) affecting DHX35 locus on chromosome 20q, though specific DHX35-associated disease phenotypes remain incompletely defined. Literature establishes DHX35 function in: (1) spliceosome fidelity — DHX35-GPATCH1 complex mediates rejection of aberrant splicing substrates ensuring splicing accuracy (12 citations, 2025), (2) innate immunity — DHX35 functions as co-sensor for RIG-I-mediated antiviral response in human airway epithelial cells (8 citations, 2024), (3) long-COVID — autoantibodies against SUMO1-modified DHX35 detected in long-COVID patients with cognitive symptoms and anosmia (9 citations, 2022), (4) molecular function — ATP-dependent RNA helicase involved in pre-mRNA splicing catalytic activation and spliceosome disassembly. Functional hypothesis: DHX35 acts as spliceosome fidelity checkpoint helicase using ATP hydrolysis to proofread splice site selection and reject aberrant substrates through GPATCH1 interaction, with HA2 winged-helix domain recognizing RNA secondary structures and OB fold stabilizing single-stranded intermediates. The ubiquitous expression suggests essential housekeeping role in constitutive splicing, while centrosome localization indicates potential mitotic functions. Disease mechanism: DHX35 exhibits dual roles in RNA processing and innate immunity — spliceosome dysfunction from 20q CNVs may cause splicing errors contributing to developmental disorders, while DHX35 autoantibodies in long-COVID suggest persistent immune dysregulation targeting SUMO1-modified helicase affecting both splicing and antiviral signaling. DHX35-RIG-I co-sensor function indicates role in cytoplasmic viral RNA detection complementing nuclear splicing function. Priority experiments: (a) characterize DHX35-GPATCH1 complex structure via cryo-EM, (b) identify DHX35 RNA targets via CLIP-seq in airway epithelial cells, (c) test DHX35 role in RIG-I signaling via knockout models, (d) determine DHX35-associated disease phenotypes in 20q CNV patients. DHX35 exemplifies 'dark spliceosome helicase' where catalytic mechanism and immune functions are emerging but disease causality, tissue-specific splicing programs, and long-COVID autoantibody pathogenesis remain unexplored despite 7 ClinVar variants.
