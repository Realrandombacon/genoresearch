## Description
NPIPA2 (Nuclear pore complex-interacting protein family member A2, UniProt E9PIF3) is a 369-amino acid nucleoplasmic protein located on chromosome 16p13.11 within a region prone to copy-number variations. InterPro analysis reveals canonical nuclear pore complex-interacting protein architecture: NPIP family domain (IPR009443/PTHR15438, aa 2-364) with dedicated N-terminal NPIP domain (IPR054697/PF06409, aa 41-262) characteristic of proteins that associate with nuclear pore complexes to regulate nucleocytoplasmic transport. GO annotations suggest roles in nuclear transport and protein-protein interactions.
