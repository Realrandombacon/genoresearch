## Description
TAF11 encodes a 211-amino acid transcription initiation factor TFIID subunit 11 (UniProt Q15544), also known as TAFII28, a well-folded histone-fold protein functioning as an essential component of the TFIID complex for RNA polymerase II transcription initiation. The protein contains a TAFII28-like protein domain (IPR006809, positions 108-198; PF04719, positions 108-193) with a characteristic histone-fold structural motif (IPR009072, positions 113-201; G3DSA:1.10.20.10, positions 113-201; SSF47113, positions 113-201) that mediates heterodimerization with TAF13, classified within the TAF11 family (IPR045127, positions 14-210; PTHR13218, positions 14-210) with histone-fold domain (cd08048, positions 113-198). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 72.1, residues 1-211) indicating stable folding consistent with histone-fold architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals TAF11 forms part of the core TFIID complex with ultra-high confidence interactions: TAF7 (0.999), TAF8 (0.999), TAF13 (0.999), TAF5 (0.999), TAF4 (0.998), TAF10 (0.998), TAF12 (0.997), TAF3 (0.997), TBP (0.994, TATA-box binding protein), and TAF9B (0.967), confirming its role as an integral TFIID subunit. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus subcellular localization consistent with transcription factor function, annotated for transcription and transcription regulation biological processes. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (6p21.31-21.2) and gains (6p22.1-q14.1, 6p21.31-21.2, 6p25.3-q27, 6p25.3-12.3) plus a synonymous variant (c.486C>A p.Val162=), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 47 publications, TAF11 has emerging roles in development and disease: TAF11 variant contributes to non-syndromic cleft lip only through modulating neural crest cell migration (2024, 1 cit), TAF11 is differentially expressed in brain metastatic human breast cancer (2021), piRNA DQ690018 regulates macrophage polarization by inhibiting Taf11 mRNA 2'-O-methylation modification (2025), genetic association with skin traits in pigs (2026). The functional hypothesis: TAF11 serves as a structural scaffold within the TFIID complex through histone-fold heterodimerization with TAF13, stabilizing the core TFIID architecture required for promoter recognition and preinitiation complex (PIC) assembly; the histone-fold domain (positions 113-201) mimics nucleosomal histone H2B-H2A dimer structure, enabling TAF11-TAF13 to anchor TFIID to promoter DNA and recruit RNA polymerase II; ultra-high confidence interactions with all core TAFs (TAF3/4/5/7/8/10/12) and TBP indicate TAF11 participates in TFIID holoenzyme assembly and conformational changes during transcription initiation; ubiquitous expression reflects constitutive requirement for Pol II transcription across all cell types; cleft lip association suggests TAF11 dosage or function affects neural crest cell migration during craniofacial development, potentially through altered expression of developmental transcription factors; brain metastasis differential expression indicates TAF11 may regulate metastasis-specific gene programs in breast cancer cells that colonize the brain; piRNA-mediated 2'-O-methylation regulation suggests post-transcriptional control of TAF11 expression affects macrophage polarization states; 6p CNVs may disrupt TAF11 dosage causing transcriptional dysregulation affecting multiple developmental pathways; Golgi apparatus localization hints at potential non-canonical roles in secretory pathway or TFIID assembly outside the nucleus.
