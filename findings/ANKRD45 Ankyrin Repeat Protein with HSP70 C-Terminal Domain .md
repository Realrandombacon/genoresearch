## Description

ANKRD45 contains multiple ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, PF12796, positions 63-141; IPR036770, positions 7-172) and belongs to the Ankyrin Repeat and Cyclin-Dependent Kinase Inhibitor family (IPR050776, PTHR24201, positions 35-170), plus a Heat Shock Protein 70kD C-terminal domain superfamily (IPR029048, SSF100934, positions 164-243). Mechanistically, ANKRD45 deficiency could impair: (a) Cell cycle progression via disrupted ankyrin-mediated interactions with CDK/cyclin complexes; (b) ER proteostasis via altered chaperone network function, leading to ER stress and unfolded protein response activation; (c) Cytokinesis fidelity via defective midbody protein recruitment, causing aneuploidy and genomic instability.
