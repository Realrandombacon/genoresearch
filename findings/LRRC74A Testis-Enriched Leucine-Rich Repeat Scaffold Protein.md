## Description
Leucine-rich repeat-containing protein 74A (LRRC74A) is a 488-amino acid intracellular scaffold protein featuring multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains (IPR001611, PF13516, residues 221-237/248-267/273-294) arranged within a ribonuclease inhibitor-like superfold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, residues 67-394). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded tertiary structure (pLDDT 83.7), indicating stable LRR solenoid architecture typical of protein-protein interaction platforms. HPA data reveals testis-enriched expression (6.2 nTPM) with tissue-specific RNA distribution, strongly implicating LRRC74A in spermatogenesis or germ cell maintenance. STRING interactions identify partners including centrosomal protein FAM161B (score 0.697), spermatogenesis-associated proteins SPATA31D3/D4 (0.682/0.630), WD40 repeat protein WDR49 (0.573), and cell cycle regulator LIN52 (0.505), positioning LRRC74A within centrosome/spindle assembly and meiotic division networks. The LRR architecture suggests LRRC74A functions as a modular adaptor recognizing specific peptide motifs on partner proteins, potentially scaffolding multiprotein complexes during germ cell development. Clinical significance is demonstrated by 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning 14q24.3, implicating LRRC74A dosage sensitivity in human disease. Conservation across vertebrates (rat ortholog 479 aa, 98% identity) and invertebrates indicates strong functional constraint. I hypothesize LRRC74A serves as a testis-specific LRR scaffold that nucleates centrosomal or meiotic spindle complexes, recruiting cell cycle regulators and spermatogenesis factors through LRR-mediated protein interactions, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression contributing to male infertility or developmental disorders.
