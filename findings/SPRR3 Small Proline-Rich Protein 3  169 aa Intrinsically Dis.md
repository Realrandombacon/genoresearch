## Description
SPRR3 (Small Proline-Rich Protein 3, UniProt Q9UBC9) is a 169 aa intrinsically disordered cornified envelope protein belonging to the small proline-rich protein (SPRR) family that functions in epithelial barrier formation through transglutaminase-mediated cross-linking with involucrin, loricrin, and other SPRR family members during terminal differentiation of stratified squamous epithelia. InterPro analysis reveals characteristic SPRR architecture: Cornifin/SPRR3 family membership (IPR060016/PF02389, positions 67-167) and three proline-rich repeats (PR00021, positions 17-29, 40-49, 59-65) containing conserved peptide acceptor sites for cross-linking. AlphaFold structure shows low-medium confidence folding (pLDDT 52.7), indicating an intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) with flexible conformation typical of proline-rich structural proteins that adopt extended conformations for cross-linking. HPA data confirms tissue-enhanced expression with extreme enrichment in esophagus (18307.4 nTPM), vagina (6111.3 nTPM), and cervix (5402.3 nTPM), classified as cancer-enhanced/cancer-related gene/predicted intracellular protein with keratinization biological process and cell type-enriched single cell specificity. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SPRR3 serves as a transglutaminase substrate that forms ε-(γ-glutamyl)lysine cross-links with involucrin, loricrin, and other SPRR proteins to create the insoluble cornified envelope beneath the plasma membrane of terminally differentiated keratinocytes, while also functioning in cancer progression through NF-κB signaling activation, AKT phosphorylation, and ribosome biogenesis regulation in a cell context-dependent manner. The intrinsically disordered nature (pLDDT 52.7) allows SPRR3 to adopt extended conformations that maximize cross-linking density and mechanical stability of the cornified envelope. Extreme esophageal expression (18307.4 nTPM) reflects SPRR3's role in protecting stratified squamous epithelia from mechanical and chemical stress. SPRR3 represents a moderately studied structural protein with emerging roles in cancer biology beyond canonical cornified envelope formation.
