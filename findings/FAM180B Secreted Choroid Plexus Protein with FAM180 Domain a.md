## Description
FAM180B encodes a 183-amino acid predicted secreted protein (UniProt Q6P0A1) containing the FAM180 family domain (IPR029170, PF15173, residues 17-159). HPA data shows tissue-enriched expression in choroid plexus (25.6 nTPM), suggesting a role in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) production or blood-brain barrier function. STRING interactions reveal associations with metabolic enzymes (MTHFD1L, SUCLG2), metal transporters (SLC39A13, SLC24A4), and complement-related protein C1QTNF4 (scores 0.42-0.55), indicating potential involvement in folate metabolism and ion homeostasis. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 11 copy number variations (deletions and duplications spanning 11p11.2-q25), though no specific monogenic disease is yet defined. Orthologs exist in diverse vertebrates (naked mole rat, alligator, tarsier), indicating evolutionary conservation. The combination of secreted signal peptide, choroid plexus enrichment, and metabolic interaction partners suggests FAM180B functions as a CSF-secreted modulator of choroid plexus epithelial transport or CSF composition regulation. CNVs disrupting FAM180B may contribute to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through altered CSF homeostasis.
