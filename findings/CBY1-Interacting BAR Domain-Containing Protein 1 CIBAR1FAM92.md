## Description
CIBAR1 (CBY1-interacting BAR domain-containing protein 1, also known as FAM92A) is a 289 aa mitochondrial/ciliary protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 10 literature references despite essential roles in ciliogenesis, spermatogenesis, and developmental patterning. Multi-omics data reveals CIBAR1 as a specialized BAR domain protein coupling membrane curvature sensing with septin-based cytoskeletal organization. InterPro analysis confirms a dominant BAR domain architecture (IPR035590/cd07598, aa 8-218) belonging to the FAM92 protein family (IPR009602/PF06730, aa 1-219), with the characteristic Arfaptin homology/BAR domain superfamily fold (G3DSA:1.20.1270.60/IPR027267, aa 9-213) enabling membrane binding and curvature induction. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.1), indicating a well-folded crescent-shaped BAR domain dimer typical of membrane-deforming proteins. HPA shows testis-enhanced expression (102.1 nTPM, highest of all tissues) with cancer-enhanced expression and mitochondrial subcellular localization, consistent with roles in spermatogenesis and ciliary function. STRING interactions reveal a functionally coherent ciliogenesis network: CIBAR1 associates with CBY1 (0.949, Chibby1, a conserved antagonist of Wnt/β-catenin signaling and ciliary protein), DZIP1L (0.621, zinc finger protein regulating ciliary gating), DZIP1 (0.588, ciliary transition zone protein), IQCE (0.591, ciliary protein mutated in polydactyly), KIAA0825 (0.593, ciliopathy-associated protein), and TTC23 (0.515, tetratricopeptide repeat protein involved in ciliary transport). ClinVar lists 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including 8q21.12-24.11 copy number alterations (gains: chr8:79409349-119040631x3, chr8:84712253-146295771x3; losses: chr8:84127576-98263585x1, chr8:89179899-97978274del) and protein-truncating mutations (c.394_397del p.Arg132fs frameshift, c.364C>T p.Arg122Ter nonsense), implicating CIBAR1 haploinsufficiency in ciliopathy syndromes. Recent literature (2024-2026) identifies CIBAR1 as: (1) A component of the Cby3/ciBAR1 complex positioning the annulus along the sperm flagellum during spermiogenesis, forming a septin-based midpiece/principal piece boundary; (2) A ciliogenesis regulator whose loss causes embryonic lethality, pancreatic degeneration (exocrine and endocrine), altered glucose tolerance, and laterality defects in mice; (3) A causative gene for non-syndromic postaxial polydactyly type A9 (PAPA9) via homozygous variants; (4) A fusion partner in lipomas (HMGA2::CIBAR1-DT) with chromosomal rearrangements. The scarcity of literature (10 papers, most from 2024) despite clear mechanistic roles makes CIBAR1 a paradigmatic dark gene awaiting comprehensive characterization.
