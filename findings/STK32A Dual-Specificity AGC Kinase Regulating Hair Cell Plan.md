## Description
STK32A encodes a 396 aa serine/threonine-protein kinase 32A (UniProt Q8WU08), functioning as a dual-specificity AGC kinase that regulates hair cell planar polarity in the inner ear, sleep homeostasis, and cancer metastasis through kinase-mediated phosphorylation of acidic substrates. The protein contains a canonical protein kinase domain (IPR000719/PF00069, aa23-281/23-280) with Ser/Thr kinase active site signature (IPR008271, aa142-154: HRDLKxxN), ATP binding site (IPR017441, aa29-52), Yank1 catalytic domain (cd005578, aa22-281), and belongs to G protein-coupled receptor kinase family (PTHR24355, aa15-377). STK32A functions as an atypical AGC family kinase—the kinase domain (aa23-281) contains canonical catalytic residues including the HRD motif (aa142-154) for phosphotransfer and glycine-rich loop (aa29-52) for ATP coordination. The centrosomal localization suggests roles in cell cycle regulation and mitotic spindle organization, consistent with DPYSL3 interaction (axon guidance, neuronal migration) and PPP2R2B interaction (protein phosphatase 2A regulatory subunit). GPR149 interaction (0.474) suggests potential GPCR signaling crosstalk, while STAC2 connection (0.461) links to calcium channel regulation. Copy number variants on 5q may cause STK32A dosage effects contributing to developmental abnormalities or cancer predisposition.
