## Description
SPRED3 (Sprouty-Related EVH1 Domain Containing 3, UniProt Q2MJR0) is a 410 aa intracellular signaling protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with 23 literature references establishing roles in thyroid function, cancer, and lung development. Structural analysis reveals modular domain architecture: N-terminal EVH1/WH1 domain (aa 1-113, IPR000697/PF00568) for protein-protein interactions and cytoskeletal regulation, central c-Kit-binding domain (aa 195-244, IPR023337) for receptor tyrosine kinase interaction, and C-terminal Sprouty domain (aa 295-407, IPR007875/PF05210) mediating Ras/MAPK pathway inhibition. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT=65.1) consistent with multi-domain architecture containing both ordered domains and flexible linker regions. STRING interactions identify SPRED3 within Ras signaling network: NF1 (score 0.684), NRAS (0.596), HRAS (0.584), KRAS (0.563), RASA1 (0.431), RASA2 (0.514), KIT (0.499), and SH3D19 (0.575) — positioning SPRED3 as negative regulator competing with or modulating Ras-GTPase activating proteins. HPA expression shows brain-enriched pattern (3.5 nTPM), tissue-enhanced specificity, cancer-enhanced expression, and dual nucleoplasmic/plasma membrane localization consistent with signaling scaffold function. ClinVar identifies 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning chromosome 19q11-13.2, though specific SPRED3-associated diseases remain undefined — variants occur in contiguous gene deletion/duplication syndromes. Literature establishes SPRED3 function in: (1) thyroid homeostasis — SPRED3 knockout mice develop primary hypothyroidism with altered autophagy regulator expression (LC3, p62, ATG5), (2) thyroid cancer — SPRED3 regulates NF-κB signaling and promotes proliferation, (3) lung development — miR-342-5p downregulation exacerbates bronchopulmonary dysplasia via SPRED3/Raf1 axis, (4) glioblastoma — mutation analysis on 19q. Functional hypothesis: SPRED3 acts as tissue-specific negative regulator of Ras/MAPK signaling through EVH1-mediated scaffold assembly, competing with NF1/RASA GAPs for Ras binding, with brain and thyroid as primary physiological contexts. The c-Kit-binding domain suggests additional regulation of KIT-mediated signaling in hematopoietic or melanocytic lineages. Disease mechanism: SPRED3 haploinsufficiency or overexpression within 19q copy number variants may dysregulate Ras/MAPK signaling thresholds, contributing to neurodevelopmental, thyroid, or oncogenic phenotypes. Priority experiments: (a) determine tissue-specific interactome via co-IP/MS in brain vs thyroid, (b) assess Ras-GTP levels in SPRED3 knockout models, (c) characterize 19q CNV patient phenotypes for SPRED3-specific contributions, (d) resolve crystal structure of EVH1-Sprouty domain interface. SPRED3 exemplifies 'partially characterized signaling scaffold' where domain architecture and interaction partners are defined but tissue-specific functions and disease mechanisms require elucidation.
