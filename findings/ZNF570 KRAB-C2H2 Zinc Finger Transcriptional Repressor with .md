## Description
ZNF570 encodes a 536 amino acid nuclear zinc finger protein (UniProt Q96NI8) functioning as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 13-85) that recruits the corepressor TRIM28/KAP1, and three C-terminal C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 218-301) that recognize GC-rich DNA consensus sequences in target gene promoters. STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners with ZNF829 (0.791, highest confidence) and ZNF568 (0.669) as top interactors, both paralogous KRAB-ZNF proteins suggesting homodimerization or heterodimerization within the zinc finger transcription factor network. TRIM28 (0.522) interaction confirms the canonical KRAB-mediated recruitment of the KAP1 corepressor complex for heterochromatin formation via HP1 binding and histone H3K9me3 methylation. HPA data shows nucleoplasmic localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity, detected in all), consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional regulator role. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19q11-13.2 copy number losses and gains, implicating ZNF570 dosage sensitivity in genomic disorders. The KRAB domain architecture combined with TRIM28 interaction and ubiquitous expression suggests ZNF570 functions as a constitutive transcriptional silencer maintaining epigenetic repression of repetitive elements or developmentally regulated genes through recruitment of the KAP1-SETDB1 histone methyltransferase complex.
