## Description

The protein belongs to the CD300 family immune receptors (IPR050671, residues 1-180; PTHR11860, residues 1-180), contains an immunoglobulin V-set domain (IPR013106, residues 23-123; PF07686, residues 23-123; SM00409, residues 24-125; IPR003599, residues 24-125), an immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR007110, residues 13-125; PS50835, residues 13-125), and belongs to the immunoglobulin-like fold superfamily (IPR013783, residues 17-139; IPR036179, residues 22-128; G3DSA:2.60.40.10, residues 17-139; SSF48726, residues 22-128; cd05716, residues 21-125). CD300LD is located on chromosome 17q25.1 and is predicted to enable transmembrane signaling receptor activity and virus receptor activity, involved in immune response-activating signaling pathway, regulation of interleukin-6 production, and regulation of tumor necrosis factor production, located in plasma membrane.
