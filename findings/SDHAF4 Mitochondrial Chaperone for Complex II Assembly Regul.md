## Description
SDHAF4 encodes a 108 aa succinate dehydrogenase assembly factor 4 (UniProt Q5VUM1), functioning as a mitochondrial chaperone essential for Complex II (succinate dehydrogenase) assembly and mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation. The protein contains a succinate dehydrogenase assembly factor 4 domain (IPR012875, aa62-108), a DUF1674 domain of unknown function (PF07896, aa62-108) characteristic of SDH assembly factors, and belongs to SDHAF4 family (PTHR28524, aa5-108). AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence structure (pLDDT 65.3), indicating a partially structured protein with possible intrinsically disordered regions—consistent with chaperone function requiring conformational flexibility for substrate binding and release. HPA shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), localized to nucleoplasm and mitochondria, classified as chaperone. STRING interactions reveal SDHAF4 forms a dedicated Complex II assembly network: SDHAF3 (0.980, highest confidence—another SDH assembly factor), SDHA (0.920, Complex II catalytic flavoprotein subunit), SDHAF2 (0.849, assembly factor for SDHA flavinylation), SDHAF1 (0.837, assembly factor), SDHD (0.642, Complex II membrane anchor subunit), SDHC (0.585, Complex II membrane subunit), SDHB (0.561, Complex II iron-sulfur subunit), LYRM7 (0.548, Complex II assembly factor), COX18 (0.539, Complex IV assembly factor). ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple 6q12-13/6q13/6q12-14.1/6q13-14.1 copy number losses and 6q12-16.1/6p22.1-q14.1/6p25.3-q27 copy number gains, though no specific disease is yet assigned. Literature (17 papers) documents SDHAF4 roles in: (1) gastric cancer progression via N7-methylguanosine (m7G) tRNA modification activating SDHAF4-dependent mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation; (2) dilated cardiomyopathy—cardiac disruption of SDHAF4-mediated mitochondrial Complex II assembly causes cardiomyopathy with impaired OXPHOS; (3) gestational diabetes with SDHAF4 polymorphisms and altered placental expression; (4) plant stress signaling and SDH1 assembly. SDHAF4 functions as a dedicated Complex II assembly chaperone—the DUF1674 domain (aa62-108) mediates interactions with SDH subunits during Complex II biogenesis. The ultra-high confidence SDHAF3 interaction (0.980) suggests cooperative or sequential assembly steps, while SDHA (0.920), SDHAF2 (0.849), and SDHAF1 (0.837) interactions place SDHAF4 in the core Complex II assembly machinery. The medium confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 65.3) reflects chaperone characteristics—partial disorder enables conformational flexibility for binding multiple assembly intermediates. Mitochondrial and nucleoplasmic localization suggests dual roles: mitochondrial Complex II assembly and potential nuclear functions in SDH gene regulation or retrograde signaling. Ubiquitous expression aligns with essential OXPHOS function in all tissues. Cardiac-specific vulnerability (dilated cardiomyopathy) reflects heart's high OXPHOS dependence—SDHAF4 loss impairs Complex II assembly, reducing succinate oxidation and electron transport, causing energetic failure and cardiomyocyte death. In gastric cancer, m7G tRNA modification enhances SDHAF4 translation, boosting Complex II assembly and OXPHOS to support tumor metabolism—the SDHAF4-dependent OXPHOS activation represents a metabolic vulnerability. Gestational diabetes association suggests SDHAF4 variants alter placental OXPHOS, affecting glucose metabolism. The 11 ClinVar CNVs on 6q may cause SDHAF4 dosage effects contributing to metabolic disorders, cardiomyopathy, or cancer predisposition. The SDHAF1/2/3/4 network represents a coordinated assembly line: SDHAF1/2/3/4 sequentially chaperone SDHA/B/C/D subunits, ensure proper flavinylation (SDHAF2), iron-sulfur cluster insertion, and membrane integration before holoenzyme formation.
