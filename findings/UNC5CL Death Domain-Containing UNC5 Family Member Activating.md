## Description
UNC5CL (UNC5C-like protein) encodes a 518 aa intracellular protein (Q8IV45) functioning as a death domain-containing signaling adaptor in the UNC5 netrin receptor family. With 13 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined signaling roles but incomplete disease mechanism understanding. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: UNC5CL functions as a MyD88-independent activator of pro-inflammatory IRAK (interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase) signaling cascade, potentially operating as a death domain-mediated signaling hub linking netrin receptor pathways to NF-κB activation and inflammatory responses. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: UNC5CL operates through: (1) IRAK signaling activation - the 2011 foundational study (30 citations) demonstrated UNC5CL activates IRAK signaling independently of MyD88, suggesting alternative adaptor function in TLR/IL-1R pathway divergence; (2) Death domain-mediated protein complex assembly - C-terminal death domain (positions 402-494) recruits downstream signaling effectors through homotypic death domain interactions, potentially engaging PIDD1 (STRING score 0.403) and other death domain proteins; (3) Adhesion GPCR crosstalk - extremely high-confidence STRING interactions with ADGRL1/2/3 (latrophilins, scores 0.969-0.970) suggest unexpected functional linkage between UNC5CL and adhesion GPCR signaling, potentially coordinating cell adhesion with inflammatory responses; (4) Cancer susceptibility - rs2294693 variant associated with decreased esophageal carcinoma risk in J&K population (3 citations, 2021), cancer-enhanced expression pattern in HPA suggests oncogenic or tumor suppressor role context-dependent.
