Functional hypothesis: TMEM140 functions as a membrane-associated regulator of endosomal trafficking and multivesicular body biogenesis, potentially serving as a cargo receptor or scaffold protein that recruits ESCRT machinery (via MVB12A interaction) for membrane protein sorting. The skeletal muscle enrichment suggests a specialized role in myofiber membrane maintenance or excitation-contraction coupling. The high AlphaFold confidence score indicates a stable transmembrane fold, likely with an extracellular N-terminus and cytoplasmic C-terminus based on topology predictions. Copy number variants in ClinVar may disrupt endosomal trafficking in muscle and hematopoietic cells, contributing to myopathic or immunological phenotypes.
