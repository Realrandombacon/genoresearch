## Description
NELFE (Negative Elongation Factor E) is a 380-amino acid nuclear protein with 54 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein contains a canonical RNA recognition motif (RRM) domain (IPR000504, positions 262-332; PF00076, positions 269-323; PS50102, positions 262-332; SM00360, positions 263-328) within the Negative Elongation Factor E family (IPR033102, positions 214-372; PTHR17250, positions 214-372), belonging to the nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily (IPR012677, positions 257-343) and RNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR035979, positions 203-330). ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (chromosome 6p21.33-21.31, 6p22.1-q14.1, and 6p25.3-q27 copy number gains with uncertain clinical significance), indicating dosage sensitivity. STRING interactions reveal NELFE functions within the NELF complex and transcriptional elongation machinery with very high confidence connections to NELFA (0.999), NELFCD (0.999), SUPT5H (0.998) - SPT5 transcription elongation factor, NELFB (0.956) - Negative Elongation Factor B, high confidence interactions with NCBP1 (0.819) - nuclear cap binding protein subunit 1, CCNT1 (0.752) - cyclin T1 component of P-TEFb complex, SUPT4H1 (0.703) - SPT4 homolog 1, and medium confidence connections to CDK9 (0.641) - cyclin-dependent kinase 9 catalytic subunit of P-TEFb, NCBP2 (0.604), and CCNT2 (0.534). Recent studies (2025) established NELFE phase separation modulating chromatin accessibility to promote dichotomous signaling pathways in hepatocellular carcinoma, CAF-derived exosomal LINC01711 promoting breast cancer progression by activating miR-4510/NELFE axis and enhancing glycolysis, NELFE as prognostic biomarker at invasive tumor front in oral squamous cell carcinoma, modulating NELFE splicing as therapeutic strategy for hepatocellular carcinoma, and targeting NELFE using antisense oligonucleotides reducing tumor burden and increasing overall survival in vivo. We hypothesize NELFE functions as the RRM-containing RNA-binding subunit of the NELF complex that mediates transcriptional pausing through phase separation mechanisms, bridging RNA polymerase II elongation control (via SUPT5H, P-TEFb/CDK9-CCNT1 interactions) with chromatin accessibility regulation. The RRM domain (positions 262-332) likely binds nascent RNA transcripts to stabilize paused Pol II complexes. The medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 64.0) suggests disordered regions enabling biomolecular condensate formation. The NELF complex interactions (NELFA/NELFB/NELFCD at 0.956-0.999) confirm core complex membership. P-TEFb connections (CDK9 0.641, CCNT1 0.752) indicate regulatory crosstalk between pausing and release mechanisms. The 5 ClinVar variants suggest 6p dosage sensitivity potentially affecting transcriptional programs. NELFE represents an uncharacterized transcriptional pausing regulator linking phase separation, chromatin accessibility, and oncogenic signaling through the miR-4510/NELFE glycolysis axis in breast cancer and dichotomous pathway activation in hepatocellular carcinoma.
