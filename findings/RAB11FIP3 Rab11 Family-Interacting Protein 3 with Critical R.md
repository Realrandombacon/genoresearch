## Description
RAB11FIP3 encodes Rab11 family-interacting protein 3 (UniProt O75154), a 756 aa scaffold protein essential for endosomal recycling, ciliogenesis, and vesicular trafficking. InterPro analysis reveals a modular architecture: N-terminal EF-hand calcium-binding domains (IPR002048, aa 202-263; PF13499, aa 206-263) suggesting potential calcium-regulated conformational changes, followed by a central Rab11-FIP3 domain (IPR057316, aa 486-610; PF25450, aa 486-610) characteristic of Rab GTPase effectors, and a C-terminal FIP-RBD domain (IPR019018, aa 694-756; PF09457, aa 716-756) mediating protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 65.5), indicating a partially structured protein with likely intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs), consistent with its role as a dynamic scaffold in membrane trafficking. STRING interactions reveal a highly interconnected network with Rab GTPases and endosomal regulators: RAB3IP (0.990, Ras-related protein Rab-3 interacting protein), ARF6 (0.986, ADP-ribosylation factor 6), RAB11FIP2 (0.965), RAB11FIP4 (0.964), ASAP1 (0.949, Arf stimulating protein 1), RAB11FIP1 (0.939), RAB11A (0.911, Ras-related protein Rab-11A), RAB25 (0.896, Ras-related protein Rab-25), and RAB11FIP5 (0.646), forming a core Rab11 effector complex. HPA shows no detectable expression data, consistent with tissue-specific expression in epithelial and immune cells. Literature analysis reveals 25 papers - partially characterized with emerging roles in: (1) Diabetic foot ulcer dysfunction via disrupted alternative splicing (2025); (2) Immune evasion in gastric cancer via IKZF4/NONO-RAB11FIP3 axis promoting PD-L1 endosome recycling (2024); (3) Breast cancer progression via EGFR recycling (2017); (4) Cardiac hypertrophy via IGF-1R recycling (2017). Functional hypothesis: RAB11FIP3 functions through: (1) Endosomal Recycling Hub - binds Rab11A/GTP and Rab3IP to facilitate recycling of EGFR, IGF-1R, and PD-L1 from endosomes to the plasma membrane; (2) Cancer Immune Evasion - promotes PD-L1 surface expression by preventing its lysosomal degradation, enhancing T-cell inhibition; (3) Diabetic Foot Ulcer Pathogenesis - dysregulated alternative splicing leads to aberrant protein isoforms that disrupt endosomal trafficking, impairing wound healing and immune surveillance; (4) Calcium Sensing - EF-hand domains may regulate conformational switching in response to intracellular calcium spikes during vesicle fusion events; (5) Disease Mechanism - 16p13.3 CNVs and splice-site mutations disrupt Rab11FIP3 dosage and isoform balance, leading to defective endosomal sorting and accumulation of misfolded proteins or stalled trafficking intermediates. Despite 25 papers, critical unknowns include: tissue-specific expression patterns, functional consequences of specific splice variants, therapeutic targeting of RAB11FIP3 in cancer, and the precise mechanism of calcium sensing.
