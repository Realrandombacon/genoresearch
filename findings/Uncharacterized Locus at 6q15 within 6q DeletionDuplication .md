STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, and Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all large copy number alterations including deletions (chr6:75926199-92710793, ~16.8 Mb; chr6:85370716-90669793, ~5.3 Mb; chr6:83838303-98822313, ~15.0 Mb; chr6:85804273-93189258, ~7.4 Mb; chr6:82569098-93753476, ~11.2 Mb) and one duplication (chr6:74382807-142040500, ~67.7 Mb). The 6q15 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including PARK2 (parkin RBR E3 ubiquitin ligase) and PACRG (parkin coregulated gene), whose haploinsufficiency contributes to neurodevelopmental and reproductive phenotypes. LOC101928911 likely functions as part of the 6q15 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to 6q syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology or long-range gene regulation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with pathogenic CNV burden (6 variants) indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where structural variation disrupts neurodevelopmental and growth processes.
