## Description
NECAP1 (NECAP Endocytosis Associated 1, also known as Adaptin Ear-Binding Coat-Associated Protein 1) is a 275-amino acid intracellular protein with 23 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status as an emerging member of the clathrin-mediated endocytosis machinery. The protein belongs to the NECAP family (PTHR12847, positions 4-257; GO:0006897 endocytosis, GO:0016020 membrane) containing a canonical N-terminal PHear domain (IPR012466, positions 7-163; IPR011993 PH-like domain superfamily, positions 11-133; G3DSA:2.30.29.30 PH domain-like, positions 11-133; cd13228 NECAP PHear domain, positions 9-128; PF07933 DUF1681, positions 7-163) that functions as an adaptin ear-binding module for clathrin coat recruitment. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 69.6, medium confidence), consistent with ordered PHear domain plus flexible C-terminal regions typical of endocytic adaptor proteins. HPA reveals brain-enriched expression at 64.7 nTPM (very high), tissue-enhanced specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, with annotated functions in endocytosis, protein transport, and transport, disease involvement in epilepsy, and subcellular localization to vesicles and cytosol. ClinVar documents 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly chromosome 12p13.33-11.1 copy number gains, 12p13.33-q13.12 CNVs, and single nucleotide variants including c.780-168C>G, c.96-15C>G, all with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong dosage sensitivity potentially affecting clathrin-mediated endocytosis and synaptic vesicle recycling programs. STRING interactions reveal NECAP1 functions within the clathrin-mediated endocytosis network with high confidence connections to UBE2J2 (0.874) - ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 J2 involved in ER-associated degradation, FCHO2 (0.851) - F-BAR domain only protein 2 initiating clathrin-coated pit formation, AP2B1 (0.849) - AP-2 complex subunit beta, the core clathrin adaptor, EPS15 (0.847) - epidermal growth factor receptor pathway substrate 15, AP1G1 (0.841) - AP-1 complex subunit gamma-1 for Golgi-endosome trafficking, AP2M1 (0.798) - AP-2 complex subunit mu, FCHO1 (0.794) - F-BAR domain only protein 1, AP2A1 (0.739) - AP-2 complex subunit alpha, NECAP2 (0.711) - NECAP endocytosis associated 2 paralog, and EPN2 (0.673) - epsin 2 clathrin adaptor. Recent studies (2018-2025) established NECAP1 mutations causing early infantile epileptic encephalopathy 21 (EIEE21) with clinical delineation of the disease phenotype (2022, 7 citations; 2019, 6 citations; 2018, 7 citations), NECAP1 as a novel candidate gene for canine progressive retinal atrophy and retinal degeneration in Giant Schnauzer dogs (2019, 11 citations), and PCR-based detection of hereditary NECAP1 mutations in native Korean dog breeds (2025, 1 citation). We hypothesize NECAP1 functions as a clathrin-coated vesicle adaptor protein that binds the AP-2 complex ear domain through the PHear domain (positions 7-163), recruiting clathrin and accessory proteins to plasma membrane sites for endocytic pit formation and synaptic vesicle recycling. The PHear domain structure (positions 7-163) resembles pleckstrin homology folds but functions as an ear-like binding module rather than phosphoinositide recognition, distinguishing NECAP1 from classical PH domain proteins. The AP2B1 (0.849), AP2M1 (0.798), and AP2A1 (0.739) interactions confirm direct engagement with the AP-2 heterotetrameric complex, the core machinery for clathrin-coated pit assembly. The EPS15 (0.847) and FCHO1/FCHO2 (0.794/0.851) interactions indicate coordination with early endocytic scaffolds that initiate clathrin coat assembly. The EPN2 interaction (0.673) suggests collaboration with epsin adaptors for membrane curvature generation. The NECAP2 paralog interaction (0.711) indicates potential functional redundancy or heterodimerization. The brain expression (64.7 nTPM) and vesicle/cytosol localization indicate NECAP1 operates at synaptic terminals where rapid clathrin-mediated endocytosis retrieves synaptic vesicle membrane after neurotransmitter release. The epilepsy phenotype (EIEE21) indicates NECAP1 loss disrupts synaptic vesicle recycling, causing neurotransmission defects and seizure susceptibility. The canine retinal degeneration phenotype indicates NECAP1 functions in photoreceptor outer segment disc renewal through endocytic membrane trafficking. The 51 ClinVar chromosome 12p CNVs reflect dosage sensitivity affecting this genomic region. The medium AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 69.6) suggests the PHear domain is well-folded while C-terminal regions may be intrinsically disordered for flexible protein interactions. NECAP1 represents a partially characterized clathrin adaptor linking AP-2 complex recruitment, synaptic vesicle recycling, epileptic encephalopathy pathogenesis, and photoreceptor membrane homeostasis through PHear domain-mediated protein-protein interaction mechanisms.
