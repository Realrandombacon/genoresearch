## Description
Chromosome 19 open reading frame 12 (C19orf12, aliases: MPAN, NBIA3, NBIA4, SPG43) encodes a 141-amino acid small transmembrane scaffold protein containing the C19orf12 family domain (IPR033369, PF20721, PTHR31493, aa 1-141), characteristic of conserved membrane-associated proteins that organize lipid metabolic complexes. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=60.1, medium confidence) indicates partially structured architecture with potential transmembrane regions and disordered termini, consistent with small membrane scaffolds that undergo conformational changes upon partner recruitment. STRING interaction network reveals specific associations with lipid metabolism and neurodegeneration genes: CoA synthase COASY (score 0.724), pantothenate kinase PANK2 (0.694), phospholipase DDHD1 (0.657), calcium-independent phospholipase PLA2G6 (0.633), glucosylceramidase GBA2 (0.604), DCAF17 E3 ligase adaptor (0.581), fatty acid 2-hydroxylase FA2H (0.511), WD repeat protein WDR45 (0.502), ATPase ATP13A2 (0.450), and ferritin light chain FTL (0.447), positioning C19orf12 at the heart of the neurodegeneration with brain iron accumulation (NBIA) pathway and fatty acid/CoA metabolism. HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced pattern with adipose tissue enrichment (110.6 nTPM, highest of all tissues), tongue expression (98.1 nTPM), cytosolic subcellular localization, detected in all tissues, low cancer and immune cell specificity suggesting housekeeping lipid metabolism function. ClinVar analysis identifies 72 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.224_234del p.Gln75fs, c.245del p.Pro82fs, c.246del p.Ala83fs), nonsense mutations (c.271G>T p.Glu91Ter, c.211A>T p.Lys71Ter, c.302G>A p.Trp101Ter), missense variants (c.262C>T p.Leu88Phe, c.242C>T p.Pro81Leu), splice site mutations (c.161-2A>T), and copy number losses (19q11-13.13), indicating severe loss-of-function mechanism causing mitochondrial membrane phospholipid metabolism defects and brain iron accumulation. The combination of C19orf12 family domain architecture (aa 1-141), partial structure (pLDDT 60.1), adipose-enriched expression (110.6 nTPM), cytosolic localization, COASY-PANK2-PLA2G6-WDR45 interaction network (scores 0.447-0.724), and high pathogenic variant burden (72 variants) strongly suggests C19orf12 functions as a small transmembrane scaffold protein that coordinates fatty acid synthesis, CoA metabolism, and mitochondrial membrane phospholipid homeostasis by organizing the COASY-PANK2 enzyme complex and interacting with PLA2G6 phospholipase and WDR45 autophagy machinery, with adipose-specific importance reflecting high demand for lipid handling in adipocytes and brain-specific vulnerability due to iron-dependent lipid peroxidation when C19orf12 function is lost.
