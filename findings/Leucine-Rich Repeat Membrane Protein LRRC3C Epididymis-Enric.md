## Description

The protein architecture features multiple tandem LRR motifs (IPR001611, PF13855, residues 82-150) forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds, with AlphaFold predicting a well-folded conformation (pLDDT 79.0). LRR domains mediate diverse molecular recognition events including pathogen sensing, signal transduction, and cell adhesion. HPA expression shows epididymis-enriched expression (1.0 nTPM), suggesting specific roles in male reproductive tract function, potentially in sperm maturation or epididymal epithelial barrier integrity. STRING interaction network reveals high-confidence associations with ZPBP2 (score 0.718, zona pellucida binding protein involved in sperm-egg recognition), gasdermin family members GSDMB (0.616) and GSDMA (0.505, pyroptosis executioners), ORMDL3 (0.605, ER membrane protein regulating sphingolipid synthesis), and adhesion GPCRs ADGRL1/2/3 (0.551-0.565, latrophilins). This interaction profile suggests LRRC3C functions as a membrane-anchored scaffold organizing protein complexes at the cell surface, potentially linking innate immune signaling (gasdermins) with cell adhesion pathways (latrophilins) in epididymal epithelium. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic copy number variants affecting 17q regions, indicating clinical relevance though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Orthologs exist in gorilla, rat, and zebrafish, demonstrating evolutionary conservation of this LRR membrane protein.
