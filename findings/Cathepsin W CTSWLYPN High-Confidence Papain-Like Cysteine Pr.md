## Description
CTSW (UniProt P56202), also known as LYPN (lymphopain), encodes a 376 aa lysosomal/endoplasmic reticulum cysteine protease functioning as a specialized cytotoxic cell effector molecule that contributes to T-cell and NK cell-mediated target cell killing through integrated papain-like catalytic activity and BCL2 family protein interaction mechanisms. InterPro analysis reveals canonical cathepsin architecture: N-terminal cathepsin propeptide inhibitor domain I29 (IPR013201/PF08246, residues 42-99) serving as intramolecular chaperone and activity regulator; central papain-like cysteine peptidase catalytic core (IPR000668/IPR013128/IPR038765/IPR039417/PF00112/cd02248/G3DSA:3.90.70.10, residues 128-362/40-355/36-361/129-358) containing cysteine-type peptidase activity (GO:0008234) and proteolysis function (GO:0006508); conserved catalytic triad residues including histidine active site (IPR025660, residues 289-299) and asparagine active site (IPR025661, residues 326-345); and full-length membership in papain family cysteine protease superfamily defining hydrolase/thiol protease molecular function. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 83.6) indicating a well-folded stable protease suitable for granule storage and rapid activation upon immune synapse formation. The BCL2/BID/BCL2L1 clustering suggests CTSW regulates apoptosis through mitochondrial pathway modulation; NKG7/GNLY/GZMH/PRF1/GZMA interactions confirm localization to cytotoxic granules alongside canonical killing machinery. HPA classifies CTSW as enzyme/metabolic protein/plasma protein with hydrolase/protease/thiol protease functions, tissue enhanced expression detected in many tissues with highest expression in bone marrow (109.6 nTPM) and lymphoid tissue (40.0 nTPM), group enriched blood cell specificity consistent with cytotoxic lymphocyte restriction, cell type enhanced single cell specificity.
