## Description
LOC129390786 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 16q11.2-q12.1 (GRCh38: 47052783-47052983), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This locus lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory sequence, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, STRING interaction partners, or Human Protein Atlas expression data (not applicable for non-coding locus).\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region, representing LOW CNV burden (compared to FAM53A: 146 variants, RNF115: 134 variants, LOC101928389: 82 variants, LOC129390867: 53 variants, ZNF500: 30 variants, LOC129389193: 19 variants, LOC129389757: 16 variants). The variant spectrum shows STRONG DELETION BIAS: 5 copy number losses (deletions spanning 16q11.2-12.1 through 16q11.2-12.1, ~5-6 Mb genomic spans - variants 148231, 59486, 59468, 59467) versus 1-2 copy number gains (1 duplication single allele, 1 duplication 16q11.2-12.1 ~6 Mb - variants 1184380, 148087). This ~5:1 to 4:2 ratio (~83% deletions, ~17% duplications) indicates strong loss-of-function dosage sensitivity where reduced silencing activity is substantially more pathogenic than increased silencing, mirroring LOC129390117 (~97% deletions) and LOC129388445 (~83% deletions), contrasting with duplication-biased elements like LOC129389193 (100% duplications) and LOC132205950 (~86% duplications).\n\nThe genomic context places LOC129390786 within the 16q11.2-q12.1 region, associated with 16q11.2-q12.1 deletion/duplication syndromes (characterized by intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, cardiac defects, renal anomalies, and skeletal abnormalities). Nearby 16q11.2-q12.1 genes include CBFA2T3 (core-binding factor subunit alpha-2 translocation to 3, transcriptional corepressor, hematopoiesis), NODAL (TGF-beta family ligand, embryonic patterning, left-right axis determination), and METTL9 (methyltransferase-like 9, protein methylation). The 16q11.2-q12.1 region is critical for neurodevelopment, embryonic patterning, and hematopoietic regulation.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129390786 functions as a distal silencer within the 16q11.2-q12.1 topologically associating domain (TAD), potentially regulating CBFA2T3, NODAL, or METTL9 expression through long-range chromatin interactions. The strong ~83% deletion bias suggests that LOSS-of-silencing (haploinsufficiency, 50% activity) is substantially more pathogenic than GAIN-of-silencing (triplication, 150% activity). This indicates the target gene(s) require MAXIMAL repression - reduced silencing causes ectopic overexpression leading to developmental defects. The mechanism likely involves: (1) silencer-mediated repression of NODAL maintaining appropriate TGF-beta signaling during embryonic left-right axis determination; (2) recruitment of co-repressor complexes (possibly SMAD co-repressors, Polycomb complexes, or HDACs) to maintain basal NODAL/CBFA2T3 expression; (3) chromatin boundary function preventing ectopic enhancer activation of developmental genes; or (4) tissue-specific silencing in non-expressing cell lineages. In K562 cells (erythroleukemia), this silencer may regulate hematopoietic expression of 16q11.2-q12.1 genes. The 16q11.2-q12.1 location near NODAL (embryonic patterning ligand) and CBFA2T3 (transcriptional corepressor) raises the possibility that LOC129390786 prevents ectopic NODAL activation causing laterality defects or CBFA2T3 overexpression disrupting hematopoietic differentiation. NODAL overexpression causes heterotaxy and cardiac defects. CBFA2T3 dysregulation is associated with acute myeloid leukemia (t(8;21) translocation). The LOW 6 variant burden indicates modest mutational target size with strong loss-of-function dosage sensitivity. 16q11.2-q12.1 deletions cause intellectual disability, autism, and cardiac/renal anomalies. LOC129390786 dosage alterations may contribute to 16q11.2-q12.1 phenotypes through: (1) NODAL dysregulation causing laterality and cardiac defects; (2) CBFA2T3 dysregulation affecting hematopoietic development; or (3) METTL9 dysregulation affecting protein methylation homeostasis. The strong deletion bias suggests LOC129390786 functions as a critical repressor maintaining tight silencing of developmental genes.
