## Description
LOC126861498 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 12p11.21 (genomic coordinates 32926728-32927927, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a CDK7 STRONGLY-DEPENDENT GROUP 2 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors with STRONG DEPENDENCE on CDK7 (a transcription-associated kinase that phosphorylates RNA polymerase II C-terminal domain for transcriptional elongation). This cofactor profile suggests it operates through transcriptional elongation control mechanisms. The presence of tetrasomy (4 copies) events indicates extreme amplification pressure. The 12p region contains KRAS (12p12.1), one of the most frequently mutated oncogenes in human cancer, as well as CCND2 (cyclin D2) and other cell cycle regulators. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus.
