## Description
SNRPC (Small Nuclear Ribonucleoprotein Polypeptide C, also known as U1 snRNP C, UniProt P09234) is a 159 amino acid nuclear protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 30 literature references despite essential function in pre-mRNA splicing and emerging roles as an oncogene in multiple cancers. SNRPC contains defining C2H2-type zinc finger domains including Matrin/U1-C C2H2-type zinc finger (IPR000690, positions 4-36), Matrin/U1-C-like C2H2-type zinc finger (IPR003604, positions 1-37), U1-C C2H2-type zinc finger (IPR013085, positions 1-38), U1 zinc finger (PF06220, positions 1-38), and belongs to the U1 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein C family (IPR017340, positions 1-157; PTHR31148, positions 1-156), characteristic of RNA-binding proteins that recognize 5' splice sites. The AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 70.4), indicating a well-folded protein with stable zinc finger core suitable for RNA binding and protein-protein interactions within the spliceosome. STRING interactions reveal SNRPC functions exclusively in spliceosome assembly with very high-confidence partners (all 0.999) including SNRPD3, SNRPA, SNRPD1, SNRPD2, SNRPA1, SNRPG, SNRPE, SNRPB, SNRNP70, and SNRPF - all core U1 snRNP and Sm proteins, confirming SNRPC's role in 5' splice site recognition and spliceosome assembly. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, classified as essential protein, ribonucleoprotein, and RNA-binding protein localized to nucleoplasm, consistent with housekeeping splicing function.
