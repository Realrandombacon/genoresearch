## Description
FCRLB (Fc Receptor-Like B, UniProt Q6BAA4) is a 426 aa type I transmembrane protein belonging to the immunoglobulin superfamily that functions as an immune regulatory receptor on B cells. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a confident fold (pLDDT=74.6) containing three extracellular immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR003599, IPR007110, PF13895, aa 20-290) characteristic of Fc receptor family members, with a cytoplasmic tail containing immunoreceptor tyrosine-based motifs for signal transduction. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal connections to FCER1G (0.532), the Fc epsilon receptor gamma chain essential for activating signaling, FCGRT (0.447) the neonatal Fc receptor for IgG transport, FCRL1 (0.418) a family paralog, and CLEC1A (0.408) a C-type lectin, positioning FCRLB within the broader Fc receptor signaling network. HPA shows lymphoid tissue enrichment (1.6 nTPM) with cytosolic localization and cancer-enhanced expression patterns. ClinVar identifies 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1q23, though no specific disease is annotated, suggesting haploinsufficiency may contribute to immune dysregulation. Literature (22 papers) reveals emerging roles as a prognostic biomarker in thyroid cancer, colorectal cancer, and breast cancer (trastuzumab resistance), with recent studies demonstrating FCRLB-mediated control of tumor metabolism and macrophage polarization in lung cancer, and involvement in MuSK antibody-positive myasthenia gravis pathogenesis. The gene represents a 'semi-dark' immune regulator where canonical Fc receptor structure is established, but specific signaling mechanisms, ligand identity, and therapeutic targeting opportunities in cancer and autoimmunity remain underexplored.
