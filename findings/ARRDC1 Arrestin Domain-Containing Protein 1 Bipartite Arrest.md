## Description
ARRDC1 (Arrestin Domain-Containing Protein 1; UniProt Q8N5I2) is a 433 amino acid cytosolic scaffold protein with 55 publications, representing a moderately-characterized member of the alpha-arrestin subfamily. Unlike classical visual/beta-arrestins that regulate GPCR signaling, ARRDC1 functions primarily as an adaptor recruiting HECT-type E3 ubiquitin ligases to membrane cargo and driving the biogenesis of ARRDC1-mediated microvesicles (ARMMs) — an emerging non-viral therapeutic delivery platform — through its bipartite arrestin domain architecture and PPxY motif interactions.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals ARRDC1 contains the canonical alpha-arrestin fold with distinctive features: (1) Arrestin N-terminal domain (IPR011021/PF00339/SM01017, residues 7-139, ~133 aa) — beta-sandwich fold typical of arrestin superfamily; (2) Arrestin C-terminal domain (IPR011022/PF02752/SM01017, residues 163-284, ~122 aa) — second beta-sandwich completing the bipartite arrangement; (3) Immunoglobulin E-set domain superfamily (IPR014756/SSF81296, residues 6-147 and 147-295) — structural classification confirming arrestin topology; (4) Full-length arrestin domain-containing protein (IPR050357/PTHR11188, residues 4-428) — defines the alpha-arrestin subfamily; (5) C-terminal region (residues 285-433, ~148 aa) — intrinsically disordered tail containing multiple PPxY motifs (PY motifs) critical for NEDD4 family binding. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 77.4), indicating a well-folded bipartite arrestin core (~280 aa) connected to flexible C-terminal tail (~150 aa) containing WW domain-binding motifs.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals high-confidence interactions exclusively with HECT E3 ubiquitin ligases and ESCRT machinery: NEDD4 (0.872—highest; Neural Precursor Cell Expressed Developmentally Down-Regulated 4, prototype HECT E3 ligase), ITCH (0.862; Itchy E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase, immune regulation), WWP1 (0.818; WW Domain Containing E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase 1, oncogene), WWP2 (0.723; paralog of WWP1), HECW2 (0.715; HECT E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase 2, neuronal function), YAP1 (0.713; Yes-Associated Protein 1, Hippo pathway transcriptional co-activator), ARRB1 (0.683; Beta-Arrestin-1, classical arrestin paralog), NEDD4L (0.645; NEDD4-Like, sodium channel regulator), TSG101 (0.572; Tumor Susceptibility Gene 101, ESCRT-I component), and VPS37A (0.560; Vacuolar Protein Sorting 37A, ESCRT-I subunit). This interaction profile definitively places ARRDC1 at the intersection of ubiquitination and vesicle trafficking: PPxY motifs bind WW domains of NEDD4/ITCH/WWP1 E3 ligases; TSG101/VPS37A recruit ESCRT machinery for multivesicular body (MVB) sorting and microvesicle budding.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA classifies ARRDC1 as a predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues, reflecting broad regulatory function. Low cancer specificity suggests ARRDC1 is not oncogene-addicted but functions as a tumor suppressor. Single cell type specificity shows cell type enhanced pattern, potentially indicating specialized functions in secretory cells or immune cells.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 152 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses (9q34.3 x1, 9q33.2-34.3 spanning 5-17 Mb regions), copy number gains (9q33.2-34.3 x3), deletions (single allele), and intronic variants (c.118+20G>T). No specific monogenic disease is definitively assigned, but 9q34 deletions are associated with neurodevelopmental disorders. Literature reveals emerging roles in viral restriction and therapeutic delivery: (1) 2025 studies demonstrate ARRDC1 inhibits Semliki Forest virus replication by regulating ubiquitination and degradation of viral nsP4 nonstructural protein (2 cites)—first demonstration of alphavirus restriction; (2) 2025 highly-cited work shows targeted intracellular delivery via precision programming of ARRDC1-mediated microvesicles (4 cites)—therapeutic application of ARMMs for mRNA/CRISPR delivery; (3) 2024 studies show fusion with ARRDC1 enhances p53 loading into extracellular vesicles for tumor suppression (8 cites), and scalable production of engineered ARMMs in HEK293 suspension cells (1 cite); (4) ARRDC1 functions as tumor suppressor by downregulating integrin β4, c-Myc, and GLUT1; suppressing metastasis in triple-negative breast cancer; (5) ARRDC1 expression correlates with improved prognosis in multiple cancers.\n\nCONSERVATION: ARRDC1 orthologs confirmed across vertebrates (mouse Q99KN1, 434 aa; rat B0BNL6, 434 aa) with >90% sequence identity and conserved bipartite arrestin architecture plus C-terminal PPxY motifs, indicating evolutionary constraint on ubiquitin ligase recruitment and vesicle biogenesis functions since early vertebrate evolution.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ARRDC1 functions as an alpha-arrestin adaptor through an integrated ubiquitination-vesicle trafficking mechanism: (1) Cargo recognition—bipartite arrestin core (residues 7-284, pLDDT 77.4) binds membrane protein cargo (integrins, receptors) via conformational sensing; (2) E3 ligase recruitment—C-terminal PPxY motifs bind WW domains of NEDD4 (0.872)/ITCH (0.862)/WWP1 (0.818) HECT E3 ligases; (3) Ubiquitination—E3 ligases polyubiquitinate cargo marking them for ESCRT recognition; (4) ESCRT engagement—TSG101 (0.572)/VPS37A (0.560) ESCRT-I complex binds ubiquitinated cargo via UEV domains; (5) Microvesicle biogenesis—ARRDC1 drives budding of ARMMs from plasma membrane or MVBs, packaging cargo (proteins, mRNA, CRISPR components) into extracellular vesicles; (10) Disease variants—9q CNVs→ARRDC1 haploinsufficiency→impaired vesicle trafficking + cargo stabilization→neurodevelopmental defects + cancer susceptibility; (11) Therapeutic angle—ARMMs offer advantages over viral vectors (no immunogenicity, scalable production);
