## Description
AVL9 (Q8NBF6) encodes a 648 amino acid late secretory pathway protein that functions as a DENN (differentially expressed in normal and neoplastic cells) domain-containing guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) regulating RAB GTPase activation for endosomal cargo recycling, ER-to-Golgi vesicular trafficking, and cell migration while also driving pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma chemoresistance through NF-κB pathway modulation. InterPro analysis reveals canonical DENN domain architecture characteristic of RAB GEFs: an AVL9/DENND6 domain (IPR018307/PF09794, positions 16-519/16-519) defining the core functional unit specific to this protein subfamily; a tripartite DENN domain (IPR037516/PS50211, positions 17-593/17-593) consisting of upstream DENN (uDENN), catalytic DENN (cDENN), and downstream DENN (dDENN) modules forming the complete GEF catalytic apparatus; DENND11/AVL9 GEF family classification (IPR051731/PTHR31017, positions 9-643/9-643) confirming membership in the RAB activator superfamily with guanine nucleotide exchange factor activity. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 72.8) consistent with stable multi-domain fold required for precise RAB GTPase recognition and nucleotide exchange catalysis. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with trafficking machinery and DENN domain proteins: DENND10 (0.993) and DENND11 (0.938), paralogous DENN domain GEFs suggesting functional redundancy or complex formation; DNM1L (0.721), dynamin-like protein 1 mediating membrane fission during vesicle scission; DENND5A (0.564), alternative DENN family GEF; RIC1 (0.514), RAB interactor linking to Golgi trafficking; RAB11A (0.476), RAB11 small GTPase regulating recycling endosome function representing likely substrate; RAB33A (0.441) and RAB30 (0.441), Golgi-associated RAB GTPases indicating secretory pathway targeting; ZNF688 (0.438), zinc finger protein suggesting transcriptional coupling; DENND6A (0.428), closest paralog sharing AVL9/DENND6 domain. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity detected in all tissues reflecting fundamental role in vesicular trafficking, endoplasmic reticulum localization consistent with early secretory pathway function, classified under predicted intracellular proteins/predicted membrane proteins indicating association with trafficking membranes. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple chromosome 7p copy number losses (chr7:29296048-47809018x1, chr7:27133786-34466477x1, chr7:25451740-33864069, chr7:23877135-33139446x1) and gains (chr7:27507832-39072473, chr7:10745750-35305167, chr7:10365-159119707x3, chr7:30463886-43470805x3) plus intronic variant (c.1351-84C>T) potentially affecting splicing though specific monogenic disease associations remain incompletely annotated beyond emerging links to neurodevelopmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (32 papers) defines AVL9 as multifunctional trafficking regulator: hypoxic and acidic tumor microenvironment-driven AVL9 promotes chemoresistance of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma via assembly of AVL9-IκBα-SKP1 complex stabilizing IκBα and suppressing NF-κB-mediated apoptosis enabling gemcitabine/albumin-paclitaxel resistance; Avl9 defines a family of GTPase-activating proteins that regulate diverse cell biological functions including vesicle trafficking and cell migration; yeast DENN domain protein Avl9 contributes to recycling and sorting of endosomal cargos demonstrating evolutionary conservation of trafficking function; AVL9 originally identified as cell migration-associated gene linking vesicular trafficking to cytoskeletal dynamics and metastatic potential. The mechanistic hypothesis: AVL9 functions as a RAB GTPase exchange factor where the tripartite DENN domain (positions 17-593, pLDDT 72.8) forms a composite catalytic surface with uDENN, cDENN, and dDENN modules creating a binding pocket for inactive GDP-bound RAB GTPases; confident AlphaFold structure indicates stable fold enabling precise positioning of catalytic residues promoting GDP release and GTP loading activating RAB switches; ER localization positions AVL9 at early secretory pathway stations where it activates RAB11A/RAB33A/RAB30 controlling vesicle budding from ER and Golgi compartments; DENND10/DENND11 partnerships (scores 0.940/0.938) suggest formation of heteromeric GEF complexes expanding substrate specificity or regulatory control; DNM1L interaction connects GEF activity to membrane fission machinery coordinating RAB activation with vesicle scission; pancreatic cancer chemoresistance operates through non-canonical mechanism where AVL9 binds IκBα recruiting SKP1 E3 ligase adapter stabilizing IκBα through reduced ubiquitination preventing NF-κB nuclear translocation and pro-apoptotic gene expression enabling tumor cell survival under chemotherapy stress; hypoxic/acidic microenvironment induces AVL9 expression creating adaptive resistance mechanism; cell migration function links vesicular trafficking to integrin recycling and focal adhesion turnover where RAB activation controls membrane delivery of adhesion receptors enabling leading edge protrusion; pathogenic CNVs cause dosage imbalance where 7p losses reduce AVL9 levels impairing secretory trafficking and cell migration while gains enhance chemoresistance potentially contributing to tumor aggressiveness; intronic variant may disrupt splicing reducing functional protein.
