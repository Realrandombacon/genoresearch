## Description
PRMT9 (Protein Arginine Methyltransferase 9; UniProt Q6P2P2) is an 845 amino acid nuclear/cytosolic enzyme with 52 publications, representing a moderately-characterized type II arginine methyltransferase that uniquely functions as a spliceosome-associated regulator of alternative splicing. Unlike other PRMTs primarily involved in transcriptional regulation or signal transduction, PRMT9 specifically methylates splicing factors (e.g., SAP145/SF3B2) to modulate splice site selection, linking epitranscriptomic control to cancer stem cell maintenance, metastasis, and neurodevelopment.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals PRMT9 contains a complex multi-domain architecture characteristic of type II PRMTs with unique regulatory features: (1) Tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain (IPR019734/IPR011990/G3DSA:1.25.40.10, residues 26-146, ~120 aa) — helical repeat module mediating protein-protein interactions, likely recruiting PRMT9 to the spliceosome or specific substrates; (2) AdoMet-dependent methyltransferase domain (cd02440/G3DSA:3.40.50.150, residues 147-284/181-253, ~100-140 aa) — S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) binding core for methyl donor recognition; (3) Protein arginine N-methyltransferase catalytic domain (IPR025799/IPR055135/G3DSA:2.70.160.11, residues 300-466/530-845/750-839, ~300 aa split into two lobes) — catalytic core containing the double-E loop motif characteristic of type II PRMTs (producing symmetric dimethylarginine, SDMA); (4) Ribosomal protein L11 methyltransferase-like fold (PF06325, residues 175-234) — structural homology suggesting conserved methyltransferase mechanism. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 86.6), indicating a well-folded multi-domain enzyme with rigid TPR-methyltransferase interface suitable for precise substrate positioning and processive methylation.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals high-to-medium confidence interactions exclusively with splicing machinery, methyltransferase complexes, and chromatin regulators: PRMT5 (0.759—highest; Protein Arginine Methyltransferase 5, type II partner forming heteromeric complexes or parallel pathways), TMEM184C (0.732; Transmembrane Protein 184C, uncharacterized but highly correlated, potentially a substrate or recruiter), SF3B2 (0.723; Splicing Factor 3B Subunit 2/SAP145, core U2 snRNP component and confirmed methylation substrate), FBXO10 (0.672; F-Box Protein 10, SCF ubiquitin ligase component regulating protein turnover), NOC2L (0.522; Nucleolar Complex Associated 2 Homolog, transcriptional repressor), TRMT112 (0.517; TRM112-like Methyltransferase Activator, stabilizes methyltransferases), RPF2 (0.490; Ribosome Production Factor 2), RPS2 (0.488; Ribosomal Protein S2), SF3B4 (0.473; Splicing Factor 3B Subunit 4), and HELQ (0.471; Helicase POLQ, DNA repair). This interaction profile definitively places PRMT9 in the spliceosome: SF3B2/SF3B4 (0.723/0.473) are core U2 snRNP subunits directly methylated by PRMT9 to regulate branch point recognition; PRMT5 (0.759) suggests functional coupling in type II methylation pathways; TMEM184C (0.732) may serve as a novel substrate or membrane anchor.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA classifies PRMT9 as an enzyme with nucleoplasm, microtubule, and cytosol subcellular locations, consistent with dynamic shuttling between nucleus (splicing) and cytoplasm (mRNA surveillance). Molecular functions include methyltransferase and transferase activities. RNA distribution shows low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues, reflecting ubiquitous requirement for splicing regulation. Low cancer specificity suggests PRMT9 is essential for cell viability but dysregulated in specific contexts (stemness, metastasis).\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 48 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (4q28.3-35.2 x3 spanning 54 Mb, 4q27-35.2 x3 spanning 67 Mb, 4q24-35.2 x3 spanning 89 Mb, 4p12-q35.2 whole arm gain), frameshift deletions (c.734_735del p.Ile245fs, c.545del p.Leu182fs, c.491del p.Thr164fs), nonsense mutations (c.415G>T p.Glu139Ter), and missense variants (c.554G>A p.Gly185Glu).
