## Description
DECR2 encodes Peroxisomal 2,4-dienoyl-CoA reductase [(3E)-enoyl-CoA-producing] (UniProt Q9NUI1), a 292 aa NADPH-dependent oxidoreductase essential for peroxisomal beta-oxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acids with emerging roles in cancer metabolism and immunotherapy response. InterPro analysis reveals canonical short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase (SDR) architecture: Trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase/2,4-dienoyl-CoA reductase atypical SDR domain (cd05369, aa 26-273), NAD(P)-binding Rossmann-like domain (G3DSA:3.40.50.720, aa 2-278) for cofactor binding, SDR family signature (IPR002347, aa 30-270) with conserved catalytic tetrad (Tyr-X-X-X-Lys), enoyl-(acyl carrier protein) reductase domain (PF13561, aa 38-270), and ketoreductase domain (SM00822/PKS_KR, aa 29-275) characteristic of polyketide synthases. Literature analysis reveals 30 papers - partially characterized with emerging cancer roles: (1) Prostate cancer treatment resistance - DECR2 regulates lipid metabolism and promotes resistance to androgen deprivation therapy via peroxisomal fatty acid oxidation; (2) Ferroptosis regulation - DECR2-deficient tumors show increased resistance to ferroptosis due to diminished polyunsaturated ether phospholipid (PUFA-ePL) induction; (3) Immunotherapy efficacy - tumor cell-intrinsic DECR2 shapes immunotherapy response by modulating ferroptotic cell death and lipid peroxidation; (4) Metabolic reprogramming - peroxisomal beta-oxidation enzyme supports cancer cell survival under metabolic stress. Functional hypothesis: DECR2 functions through: (1) Polyunsaturated Fatty Acid Metabolism - reduces 2,4-dienoyl-CoA intermediates generated during beta-oxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acids (linoleate, arachidonate) to 3E-enoyl-CoA, enabling complete oxidation; (2) Ferroptosis Modulation - regulates cellular PUFA-ePL pools that serve as substrates for lipid peroxidation during ferroptosis; DECR2 activity determines ferroptotic sensitivity; (3) Peroxisomal Metabolic Hub - coordinates with ACOX1, HSD17B4, ECI1/2 to form functional beta-oxidation complex for very-long-chain and unsaturated fatty acid catabolism; (4) Cancer Metabolism - supports prostate cancer survival via lipid metabolic reprogramming, potentially generating acetyl-CoA for energy or biosynthetic precursors; (5) Disease Mechanism - 16p13.3 CNVs disrupt DECR2 dosage, potentially causing peroxisomal disorders (similar to X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy, Zellweger spectrum) or metabolic syndromes; cancer-associated upregulation suggests oncogenic metabolic adaptation. Despite 30 papers, critical unknowns include: endogenous substrate specificity, tissue-specific expression patterns, clinical phenotypes of DECR2 deficiency, and therapeutic potential of DECR2 inhibition in combination with immunotherapy.
