# ZNF217: C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor in Chromatin Remodeling Complexes

## Description
ZNF217 encodes a 1048 amino acid Krüppel C2H2-type zinc finger protein (UniProt O75362) functioning as a transcriptional corepressor. InterPro analysis reveals multiple C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 65-88, 128-155, 156-183, 368-405, forming classical beta-beta-alpha zinc finger structures (G3DSA:3.30.160.60) that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding. STRING interaction network identifies ZNF217 as a hub protein in chromatin remodeling complexes with high-confidence partners: HDAC1 (0.987), HDAC2 (0.989), KDM1A (0.986), HMG20B (0.965), PHF21A (0.956), NCOA3 (0.917), BCAS1 (0.852), and CTBP1 (0.845). HPA data shows nuclear speckle and mitochondrial localization with broad tissue expression. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 20q13.2-13.33 amplicon regions, consistent with ZNF217 amplification in breast/ovarian cancers. The protein likely functions as a scaffold recruiting histone deacetylases (HDAC1/2) and lysine demethylase (KDM1A) to target promoters, establishing repressive chromatin states through coordinated histone modification. Mechanism involves CTBP corepressor recruitment via PXDLS motifs and NCOA3 coactivator competition, positioning ZNF217 as a molecular switch in epigenetic gene regulation.
