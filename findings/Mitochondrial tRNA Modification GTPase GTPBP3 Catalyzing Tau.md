## Description
GTPBP3 (GTP Binding Protein 3) is a 492 aa mitochondrial GTPase functioning as an essential enzyme in the taurinomethylation of mitochondrial tRNA wobble uridines (τm5U), a critical modification for accurate mitochondrial translation and oxidative phosphorylation. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical tRNA modification GTPase MnmE architecture (IPR004520, aa 34-492) with an N-terminal TrmE domain (IPR018948, aa 35-152), a helical domain (IPR025867, aa 155-489), and a P-loop NTPase GTP-binding domain (IPR006073, aa 251-344) containing the G1-G5 motifs for GTP hydrolysis. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 81.9) consistent with the characteristic dimeric GTPase fold of MnmE/TrmE family enzymes. STRING interactions place GTPBP3 at the core of mitochondrial tRNA modification machinery with ultra-high confidence partners: MTO1 (0.996), the methyltransferase that forms a heterodimeric complex with GTPBP3; TRMU (0.975), the 2-thiouridylase for parallel tRNA thiolation; GTPBP8 (0.947), a related mitochondrial GTPase; MTG1 (0.936), a mitochondrial ribosome assembly GTPase; and ERGIC1/2/3 (0.931), suggesting potential ER-Golgi crosstalk. HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with mitochondrial localization, and disease involvement in cardiomyopathy and primary mitochondrial disease. ClinVar contains 96 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Trp98Ter, p.Trp35Ter), missense mutations (p.Cys20Tyr, p.His251Tyr, p.Arg238Leu, p.Pro386Ala), and splice site mutations (c.974+1G>C), causing Combined Oxidative Phosphorylation Deficiency 23 (COXPD23). Literature (51 papers) establishes GTPBP3's role in developmental angiogenesis via mtROS/HIF1α/ATF4/mTORC1 signaling, retinal cell protection through taurine regulation, and hypotonia/developmental delay phenotypes.
