## Description
BLOC1S5 (Biogenesis of Lysosome-Related Organelles Complex 1 Subunit 5, also known as Muted-Like Protein, UniProt Q8TDH9) is a 187 aa intracellular vesicular protein belonging to the BLOC1S5 family (IPR017243/PTHR31784, positions 1-187) with Muted-like protein domain (PF14942/PIRSF037610, positions 36-181/12-187) that functions as an essential subunit of the BLOC-1 (Biogenesis of Lysosome-Related Organelles Complex 1) complex regulating melanosome and platelet dense granule biogenesis, with pathogenic variants causing Hermansky-Pudlak Syndrome Type 11 (HPS11) and serving as fusion partner with IRF4 in TEMPI syndrome. The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal region (positions 1-11) for protein stability, central Muted-like protein domain (positions 12-187, PIRSF037610) with conserved Muted-like fold (PF14942, positions 36-181) for BLOC-1 complex assembly and protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal region (positions 182-187) for complex stabilization.
