## Description
PLA2G5, located on chromosome 1p36.12, encodes a phospholipase A2 enzyme that plays a crucial role in lipid metabolism and inflammatory responses. The protein contains a phospholipase A2 domain (IPR001211) and is involved in the hydrolysis of membrane phospholipids to produce arachidonic acid and lysophospholipids. High expression levels are observed in tissues such as the lung, spleen, and lymph node (TPM values: 10.4, 8.5, and 7.3 respectively). Interactome analysis reveals strong interactions with PTGS2 (score: 0.713), PLA2G4B (score: 0.849), and PAFAH2 (score: 0.711), suggesting its involvement in eicosanoid synthesis and inflammation. The presence of 19 pathogenic variants in ClinVar indicates its association with human diseases. Based on these findings, PLA2G5 likely plays a significant role in modulating inflammatory responses and lipid metabolism.
