## Description
MTERF2 (Mitochondrial Transcription Termination Factor 2) is a 385 aa mitochondrial protein functioning as a regulator of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) transcription and potentially termination. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical mTERF domain architecture (IPR003690, PF02536, aa 58-359) containing three helical repeats (SM00733 at aa 130-162, 167-197, 210-240) characteristic of the mTERF family, which typically bind mtDNA via a superhelical structure. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.2) consistent with a DNA-binding protein. STRING interactions identify key partners in the mitochondrial transcription machinery: MTERF4 (0.784), POLRMT (mitochondrial RNA polymerase, 0.655), TEFM (transcription elongation factor, 0.616), MTERF3 (0.572), and ribosomal/assembly factors like MRPL12 (0.591) and NSUN4 (0.560), placing MTERF2 at the heart of mtDNA expression control. HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low specificity, consistent with a fundamental housekeeping role in energy metabolism. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (exclusively large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 12q), suggesting that dosage imbalance of the 12q region including MTERF2 causes disease, though specific monogenic phenotypes are not yet isolated. Literature (24 papers) documents MTERF2's role in MPP(+)-induced mitochondrial dysfunction (a Parkinson's disease model), its contribution to oxidative phosphorylation modulation, and recent associations linking MTERF2 expression to systemic lupus erythematosus and breast cancer susceptibility.
