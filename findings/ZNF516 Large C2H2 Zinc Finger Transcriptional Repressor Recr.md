## Description
ZNF516 (Zinc Finger Protein 516, UniProt Q92618) is a large 1163-amino acid Krüppel-associated C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor functioning as a transcriptional repressor through recruitment of multiple co-repressor complexes. The protein contains multiple tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 34-84, 248-270, and additional clusters throughout) organized to recognize specific genomic sequences, classified within the Krüppel C2H2-type zinc-finger protein family (IPR051967/PTHR45925, residues 1-1118). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured architecture (pLDDT=50.6) with ordered zinc finger folds embedded within extended disordered linker regions — a characteristic feature of large transcription factors requiring conformational flexibility for chromatin scanning and multi-protein complex assembly. HPA classifies ZNF516 as a transcription factor with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), dual nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization, and immune cell-enhanced expression in blood cells — suggesting ubiquitous gene regulatory functions with particular importance in hematopoietic lineages. STRING interaction network reveals exceptionally high-confidence associations with established transcriptional co-repressors: CTBP1 (0.967, C-terminal binding protein 1), PRDM16 (0.926, PR/SET domain histone methyltransferase), CTBP2 (0.828), KDM1A/LSD1 (0.706, lysine-specific demethylase removing activating H3K4me marks), HDAC2 (0.700) and HDAC1 (0.589, histone deacetylases removing activating acetylation), LCOR (0.645, ligand-dependent nuclear receptor co-repressor), ZNF217 (0.639, oncogenic zinc finger), KLF3 (0.503, Krüppel-like factor 3), and RCOR1 (0.490, REST co-repressor). This interaction profile defines ZNF516 as a scaffold recruiting a powerful multi-enzyme repression machinery: CTBP proteins bridge ZNF516 to HDACs for histone deacetylation, KDM1A removes activating H3K4 methylation, and PRDM16 may add repressive H3K9 methylation — collectively establishing heterochromatin at target loci. ClinVar documents 160 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including numerous copy number gains and losses across chromosome 18q11.1-q23, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity cause disease, potentially developmental disorders or hematologic malignancies. The combination of widespread expression, immune cell enhancement, massive co-repressor complex recruitment, and CNV-associated pathology supports a model where ZNF516 functions as a master transcriptional silencer regulating critical gene expression programs in development and immunity, with dosage disruption causing pleiotropic disease phenotypes through dysregulation of multiple downstream targets.
