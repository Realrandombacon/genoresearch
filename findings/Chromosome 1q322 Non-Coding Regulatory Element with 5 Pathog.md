## Description
LOC148696 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 1q32.2 (NC_000001.11, positions 207818378-207822595, ~4.2 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression across any tissue. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 deletion (variant 545154: 1q32.2, chr1:204033173-208209798, ~4.2 Mb), 1 focal loss (variant 150129: 1q32.2, chr1:207583527-210159181, ~2.6 Mb), and 3 duplications (variant 155660: 1q32.2-44, chr1:207346642-248930485, ~41.6 Mb; variant 153790: 1q32.1-42.12, chr1:204764914-225408698, ~20.6 Mb; variant 58112: 1q31.1-42.11, chr1:187143981-224299417, ~37.2 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 1q32.2 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including CD34 (cluster of differentiation 34, hematopoietic stem cell surface marker, endothelial progenitor cell marker, angiogenesis, acute myeloid leukemia immunophenotype), HSD11B1 (hydroxysteroid 11-beta dehydrogenase type 1, cortisol regeneration from cortisone, metabolic syndrome, obesity, type 2 diabetes, glucocorticoid signaling), CR1 (complement receptor 1/CD35, C3b/C4b binding, immune complex clearance, erythrocyte complement receptor, Alzheimer disease amyloid-beta clearance, systemic lupus erythematosus, Knops blood group), MIR205 (microRNA 205, tumor suppressor, epithelial-mesenchymal transition regulation, ZEB1/ZEB2 targeting, cancer metastasis, squamous cell carcinoma), and CD55 (cluster of differentiation 55/decay-accelerating factor, complement regulation, C3 convertase inhibition, paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria, Cromer blood group, autoimmune disease). Functional hypothesis: LOC148696 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 1q32.2 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a hematopoietic stem cell-specific enhancer coordinating CD34 expression in bone marrow progenitors (stem cell maintenance, angiogenesis) or as a chromatin boundary separating complement genes (CR1/CD55) from metabolic genes (HSD11B1). The 5 pathogenic CNVs indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves CD34 dysregulation altering hematopoietic stem cell function (leukemia, angiogenesis defects), HSD11B1 overexpression causing cortisol excess and metabolic syndrome (obesity, insulin resistance, hypertension), CR1 deficiency impairing immune complex clearance and amyloid-beta removal (SLE, Alzheimer disease), MIR205 loss promoting epithelial-mesenchymal transition and cancer metastasis, and CD55 deficiency causing complement-mediated hemolysis (PNH, autoimmune hemolytic anemia). The 1q32.2 deletion/duplication syndromes may present with hematologic abnormalities, metabolic disorders, complement dysregulation, cancer predisposition, and neurodegeneration. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (5 CNVs ranging 2.6-41.6 Mb) and CD34-HSD11B1-CR1-MIR205-CD55 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for hematopoiesis-metabolism-complement-tumor suppression coordination.
