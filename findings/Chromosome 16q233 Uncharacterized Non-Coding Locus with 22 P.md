## Description
LOC101928446 (Gene ID: 101928446, Ensembl: ENSG00000260862) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 16q23.3 (NC_000016.10, positions 82773318-82829637, ~56.3 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas reported no data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000260862, consistent with non-coding or highly tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 21 copy number gains/duplications (variant 155675: 16q21-23.3 ~17.7 Mb trisomy; variant 155358: 16q12.2-24.3 ~37.2 Mb trisomy; variant 154631: 16q23.2-24.3 ~10.0 Mb trisomy; variant 154511: 16q21-24.3 ~25.7 Mb trisomy; variant 153758: 16q22.1-23.3 ~14.2 Mb trisomy; variant 153239: 16q23.1-24.3 ~13.8 Mb trisomy; variant 152591: 16q23.2-24.3 ~9.4 Mb trisomy; variant 150599: 16q21-24.3 ~24.6 Mb trisomy; variant 150450: 16q23.1-24.3 ~14.7 Mb trisomy) and 1 copy number loss/deletion (variant 2665080: 16q23.2-23.3 ~4.0 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 16q23.3 chromosomal region contains critical cell adhesion, signal transduction, steroid metabolism, oxidative stress response, and fatty acid metabolism-associated genes including CDH13 (cadherin 13/T-cadherin, GPI-anchored atypical cadherin, cell adhesion inhibition, adiponectin receptor, cardiovascular protection, tumor suppressor, autism susceptibility), PLCG2 (phospholipase C gamma 2, PIP2 hydrolysis, IP3/DAG production, calcium mobilization, B-cell receptor signaling, autoinflammation, leukemia), HSD17B2 (hydroxysteroid 17-beta dehydrogenase 2, estradiol/estrone inactivation, testosterone/androstenedione inactivation, steroid hormone regulation, endometrial cancer), OSGIN1 (oxidative stress induced growth inhibitor 1, ZIP kinase interaction, apoptosis induction, oxidative stress response, tumor suppressor), and MLYCD (malonyl-CoA decarboxylase, malonyl-CoA degradation, fatty acid oxidation regulation, malonic aciduria, cardiomyopathy, developmental delay). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928446 likely represents a critical 16q23.3 adipocyte/immune cell enhancer element within the CDH13-PLCG2 regulatory region, potentially functioning as an adipose tissue/B-lymphocyte-specific enhancer coordinating CDH13-PLCG2-HSD17B2-OSGIN1-MLYCD expression in metabolic and immune niches (white adipose tissue, bone marrow B cells, liver hepatocytes) or as a chromatin boundary separating cell adhesion genes (CDH13) from signaling enzyme genes (PLCG2) and metabolic enzyme genes (HSD17B2/MLYCD). The 22 pathogenic CNVs (21 duplications 9.4-37.2 Mb, 1 deletion 4.0 Mb) indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts CDH13-mediated cell adhesion/adiponectin signaling and PLCG2-mediated calcium mobilization. Disease mechanism involves CDH13 hypomethylation/loss causing cardiovascular disease (adiponectin resistance, atherosclerosis, hypertension, heart failure), CDH13 loss causing cancer progression (loss of contact inhibition, metastasis, poor prognosis in breast/lung cancer), CDH13 variant causing autism spectrum disorder (synaptic adhesion defect, neurodevelopmental impairment), PLCG2 gain-of-function causing autoinflammation (PLAID syndrome, APLAID, urticaria, immunodeficiency, B-cell hyperactivation), PLCG2 overexpression causing leukemia (constitutive BCR signaling, proliferation, survival), HSD17B2 loss causing endometrial hyperplasia (estradiol accumulation, unopposed estrogen stimulation, cancer risk), HSD17B2 dysfunction causing androgen excess (testosterone accumulation, hirsutism, virilization), OSGIN1 loss causing oxidative stress resistance defect (apoptosis evasion, tumorigenesis, chemoresistance), MLYCD deficiency causing malonic aciduria (malonyl-CoA accumulation, metabolic acidosis, hypoglycemia, cardiomyopathy, developmental delay, seizures), and 16q23.3 duplication/deletion syndrome presenting with metabolic abnormalities, immune dysregulation, and developmental delay. The 16q23.3 region is associated with metabolic-immune-oncologic genetic variation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (22 CNVs 4.0-37.2 Mb) and CDH13-PLCG2-HSD17B2-OSGIN1-MLYCD neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a metabolic/immune-specific enhancer critical for adhesion-signaling-steroid metabolism-oxidative stress-fatty acid coordination.
