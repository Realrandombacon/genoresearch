## Description
ZNF189 encodes a 626-amino acid Kruppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt O75820). The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (Pfam PF01352, residues 14-54; InterPro IPR001909, residues 14-89) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (Pfam PF00096, three fingers at residues 148-170, 176-198, 204-226; InterPro IPR013087). This domain architecture is characteristic of transcriptional repressors that recruit heterochromatin machinery via KRAB-KRAB domain interactions.

HPA data shows epididymis-enriched expression (79.2 nTPM), classified as tissue-enhanced with detection across all tissues. Subcellular localization to intermediate filaments suggests potential roles in cytoskeletal organization or nucleocytoplasmic shuttling. As a transcription factor, ZNF189 likely functions in sequence-specific DNA recognition and transcriptional repression through KRAB-mediated recruitment of corepressors.

STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners including TRIM28 (score 0.418), the canonical KRAB domain cofactor that mediates heterochromatin formation via HP1 recruitment. Additional partners include ZNF8 (0.610), MRPL50 (0.677), NOL12 (0.596), and HMG20A (0.450), suggesting involvement in chromatin remodeling complexes and nuclear processes.

ClinVar documents 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including the missense variant c.62T>G (p.Val21Gly) within the KRAB domain and multiple large copy number losses/gains spanning 9q31 region. The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity and functional importance of the KRAB-mediated repression mechanism.

Orthologs in Bos taurus (A5PJY6, 612 aa) and Pan troglodytes (K7BGW2, 626 aa) demonstrate strong evolutionary conservation. The combination of conserved KRAB-C2H2 architecture, epididymis expression, TRIM28 interaction, and disease-associated variants supports ZNF189 function as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor involved in reproductive tract development and chromatin regulation.
