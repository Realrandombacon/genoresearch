## Description
ASF1A (Anti-Silencing Function 1A Histone Chaperone, UniProt Q9Y294) is a 204 amino acid nucleoplasmic protein with extensive characterization (152 papers) that functions as a conserved H3-H4 histone chaperone critical for chromatin assembly, transcription regulation, and DNA damage response. The protein contains an ASF1-like histone chaperone domain (IPR006818/PF04729, residues 1-154/1-197) within the ASF1-like superfamily (IPR036747/G3DSA:2.60.40.1490/SSF101546, residues 1-157), placing it in the anti-silencing protein 1 family (PTHR12040, residues 1-197) with roles in chromatin organization and nucleosomal assembly. STRING interactions reveal maximum confidence partnerships with HIRA complex components UBN1 (0.999) and HIRA (0.999), histone H3 variants H3-3B (0.999), H3C12 (0.999), H3-4 (0.997), H3C13 (0.997), histone H4C6 (0.996), chaperone regulator CABIN1 (0.998), kinase TLK2 (0.997), and histone chaperone NASP (0.995), placing ASF1A in the HIRA-mediated histone deposition pathway. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, nucleoplasmic localization, with biological processes including DNA damage response, transcription, transcription regulation, and chromatin organization. ClinVar contains 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 6q21-23.2, copy number gains at 6q21-23.1, and splice site mutations (c.225+1G>T), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential developmental, cancer, or cardiovascular phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 84.1) indicates high confidence with a well-folded immunoglobulin-like histone-binding fold. Functional hypothesis: ASF1A acts as a replication-coupled and replication-independent histone chaperone that binds H3-H4 dimers through its immunoglobulin-like domain, delivering them to the HIRA complex (HIRA-UBN1-CABIN1) for nucleosome assembly during transcription and DNA repair, where recent 2024 research (84 citations) demonstrates ASF1A enables P300-mediated histone H3 lysine 18 lactylation promoting endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT) in atherosclerosis, while 2025 studies show direct MYC-ASF1A interaction disrupted by MYC phosphorylation links chromatin regulation to oncogenic transcription, with 2024 hepatocellular carcinoma research identifying ASF1A-HJURP as a two-gene prognostic model, suggesting ASF1A dosage sensitivity in rapidly dividing cells (cancer, endothelium), 6q copy number variants potentially contributing to developmental disorders or cardiovascular disease through chromatin assembly defects, making ASF1A a therapeutic target for atherosclerosis (via H3K18 lactylation inhibition) and MYC-driven cancers.
