## Description
CLEC3B (Tetranectin) is a 202 aa secreted protein characterized by a C-type lectin-like domain (IPR001304, PF00059) and conserved kringle motifs (PR01504). Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence (pLDDT=87.9) stable fold. Functionally, it binds plasminogen (STRING score 0.996) and TNF, suggesting roles in extracellular matrix remodeling and inflammation. Expression is highly enriched in adipose tissue (nTPM=460.6) and cancer-specific, yet absent in immune cells. ClinVar identifies 8 pathogenic variants (CNVs and SNVs), linking it to disease despite lacking direct disease annotations. This suggests CLEC3B acts as a cancer-associated scaffold that modulates plasminogen activation and inflammatory signaling in adipose-rich tissues.
