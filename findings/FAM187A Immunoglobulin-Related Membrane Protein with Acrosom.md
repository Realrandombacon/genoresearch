ClinVar documents 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains on chromosome 17q, suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential disease mechanisms through gene amplification. The immunoglobulin domain architecture combined with acrosomal localization supports a hypothesis that FAM187A functions as a cell surface recognition molecule, potentially mediating sperm-egg interactions or immune privilege in reproductive tissues. The choroid plexus expression further suggests possible involvement in cerebrospinal fluid barrier integrity or neuroimmune surveillance.
