## Description
MASP2 (MBL associated serine protease 2, also known as MASP-2, MAP-2, MAP19) is a 686 amino acid secreted serine protease (UniProt O00187) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 89.4), characteristic of complement system proteases that function through pattern recognition and proteolytic activation of the lectin pathway of complement. The protein contains multiple CUB domains (IPR000859, residues 11-137/184-295; cd00041, residues 11-136/184-295; G3DSA:2.60.120.290, residues 17-139/183-289), an EGF-like domain (IPR000742, residues 141-181; cd00054, residues 138-175; G3DSA:2.10.25.10, residues 140-182), two complement control protein/CCP/Sushi domains (IPR000436, residues 298-363/364-432; cd00033, residues 300-361; G3DSA:2.10.70.10, residues 290-363/364-439), and a trypsin-like serine protease domain (IPR001314, residues 445-682; cd00190, residues 445-682; PF00031, residues 447-680; G3DSA:2.40.10.10, residues 447-680). MASP2 is located on chromosome 1p36.22 and is predicted to be a hydrolase/protease/serine protease involved in complement pathway and innate immunity, located in Golgi apparatus and secreted as plasma protein.
