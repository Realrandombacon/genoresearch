## Description
TRARG1 (trafficking regulator of GLUT4 1), also known as TUSC5, BEC-1, DSPB1, IFITMD3, or LOST1, is located at chromosome 17p13.3 and encodes a 177 amino acid transmembrane protein belonging to the CD225/dispanin family. InterPro analysis confirms dispanin architecture including IPR007593 (CD225/Dispanin family, residues 101-166), IPR051423 (CD225/Dispanin domain-containing protein, residues 21-173), and PF04505 (Interferon-induced transmembrane protein), consistent with its predicted membrane localization (GO:0016020). The AlphaFold predicted structure (Q8IXB3) shows medium confidence folding (pLDDT=54.6), indicating a partially structured protein with intrinsically disordered regions typical of regulatory membrane proteins engaged in dynamic vesicle trafficking. STRING identified 10 high-confidence interaction partners including BHLHA9 (score 0.920), YWHAE/14-3-3ε (0.794), PAFAH1B1/LIS1 (0.776), PITPNA (0.768), SCARF1 (0.714), and PLIN4/perilipin-4 (0.643), suggesting roles in lipid metabolism, cytoskeletal dynamics, and vesicle transport. Critically, ClinVar documents 118 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—predominantly copy number losses and gains affecting 17p13.3 (e.g., accession 4851232: chr17:525-5728870 deletion; accession 4755351: chr17:240638-1939562 deletion)—indicating extreme dosage sensitivity. Literature (9 publications) demonstrates TRARG1 is a GSK3 substrate that positively regulates insulin-stimulated GLUT4 trafficking and glucose import, with membrane topology studies confirming multi-pass transmembrane structure. Mouse (Trarg1, 173 aa) and rat (Trarg1, 173 aa) orthologs show strong conservation. Recent work links TRARG1 to bovine fat development via circRNA-miRNA networks. HPA has no expression data. The dispanin family connection and interaction with lipid droplet protein PLIN4 suggest TRARG1 functions at the intersection of glucose metabolism and adipocyte biology, warranting investigation into its role in insulin resistance and metabolic disease.
