## Description
ATP5ME (ATP Synthase F(0) Complex Subunit E, Mitochondrial, UniProt P56385) is a 69 amino acid mitochondrial membrane protein with partial characterization (15 papers) that functions as an essential F0 complex subunit of ATP synthase regulating oxidative phosphorylation, proton transmembrane transport, and cardiomyocyte survival. The protein contains an ATP synthase F0 complex subunit E mitochondrial domain (IPR008386/PF05680/PTHR12427, residues 1-65/3-65/1-65) with proton transmembrane transporter activity (GO:0015078) and proton motive force-driven ATP synthesis function (GO:0015986), placing it in the core oxidative phosphorylation machinery.
