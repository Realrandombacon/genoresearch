## Description
TMEM239 encodes a 195 amino acid transmembrane protein located on chromosome 20p13. The protein contains a conserved TMEM239 family domain (PF15841, IPR031694) spanning residues 44-195, representing the majority of the protein sequence. AlphaFold predicts a confident 3D structure (pLDDT 72.4) with well-ordered transmembrane topology suitable for membrane integration. HPA data reveals striking testis-specific expression (36.0 nTPM) with tissue-enriched classification, suggesting a specialized role in spermatogenesis or male germ cell function. The protein is classified as having predicted membrane localization consistent with its domain architecture. STRING interactions identify associations with BPIFC (0.590), C1orf109 (0.580), TERB1 (0.534), SLC36A3 (0.532), and MORN2 (0.506) - several partners involved in chromosomal organization and meiotic processes (TERB1 is a telomere-binding protein essential for meiosis). ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning chromosome 20, indicating dosage sensitivity. The combination of testis enrichment, membrane localization, and interactions with meiotic proteins suggests TMEM239 functions in germ cell development, potentially as a component of the synaptonemal complex or blood-testis barrier. Chromosomal instability involving this locus may contribute to male infertility or developmental disorders.
