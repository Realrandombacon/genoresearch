## Description
PLCL1 (Phospholipase C-Like Protein 1, also known as PRIP1 - Phospholipase C-Related but Catalytically Inactive Protein 1, UniProt Q15111) is a 1095 aa cytosolic scaffold protein belonging to the phosphoinositide phospholipase C family that lacks catalytic activity but functions in GABA-A receptor trafficking, calcium signaling, and lipid metabolism regulation. The molecular architecture consists of multiple regulatory domains: N-terminal pleckstrin homology (PH) domain (cd13364/CD08597, positions 115-223; G3DSA:2.30.29.30, positions 106-228) for membrane targeting via phosphoinositide binding, tandem EF-hand motifs (cd16222/G3DSA:1.10.238.10, positions 243-394) for calcium-dependent conformational changes, catalytically inactive PLC catalytic domain (cd08597/G3DSA:3.20.20.190, positions 398-688) lacking critical catalytic residues, and C-terminal C2 domain (IPR000008/cd00275/G3DSA:2.60.40.150, positions 701-876) for calcium-dependent membrane association. Literature (85 papers) establishes PLCL1 roles in: (1) LCOR-PLCL1 pathway restraining lipid accumulation and tumor progression in clear cell renal cell carcinoma (2025, 1 citation), (2) PLCL1 regulating hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) quiescence during aging and stress by modulating intracellular calcium dynamics (2025), (3) RBPMS-AS1 sponging miR-19a-3p to enhance PLCL1-mediated pyroptosis in cervical cancer (2024, 1 citation), (4) PLCL1 suppressing tumor progression via AMPK/mTOR-mediated autophagy in renal cell carcinoma (2023, 9 citations), (5) PLCL1 facilitating GABA-A receptor clustering and trafficking to inhibitory synapses. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in tongue (38.1 nTPM), cancer-enhanced pattern, and immune cell-enhanced expression, consistent with roles in neurotransmission and metabolic regulation. STRING interactions reveal GABAergic synapse network: GABRA1 (0.913), GABRA5 (0.749), GABRD (0.747), GABRB1 (0.720), GABRB2 (0.688), GABRA3 (0.610), GABRA4 (0.597), GABRE (0.592), GABRA6 (0.591), GABRG1 (0.585) - all GABA-A receptor subunits, indicating PLCL1 functions as GABA-A receptor scaffold at inhibitory synapses. ClinVar contains 39 pathogenic variants including large copy number losses at 2q31.3-33.1, 2q32.3-33.3, 2q32.1-36.1 and gains at 2q32.1-36.1, plus SNV c.1707G>A (p.Ser569=), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.5) indicating well-folded multi-domain architecture with stable PH, EF-hand, and C2 domains, typical of functional scaffold proteins. Conservation: mouse Plcl1 (Q3USB7) is 1096 aa, rat (Q62688) is 1096 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: PLCL1 functions as a multifunctional scaffold protein that modulates GABA-A receptor surface expression, calcium homeostasis, and lipid metabolism through domain-specific protein interactions. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) PH domain anchors PLCL1 to PIP2-rich plasma membrane regions near GABA-A receptor clusters, (2) EF-hand motifs sense intracellular calcium fluctuations during neuronal activity, triggering conformational changes that regulate GABA-A receptor binding and trafficking, (3) inactive catalytic domain serves as protein interaction platform recruiting regulatory factors (LCOR, RBPMS-AS1) without hydrolyzing PIP2, (4) C2 domain mediates calcium-dependent membrane association and interaction with autophagy machinery (AMPK/mTOR pathway). In HSCs, PLCL1 maintains quiescence by buffering calcium transients during stress. In ccRCC, PLCL1 suppresses lipid accumulation and tumor progression via LCOR interaction and autophagy induction. In cervical cancer, PLCL1 promotes pyroptosis through miR-19a-3p regulation. Pathogenic CNVs at 2q32-33 disrupt PLCL1 dosage, potentially contributing to neurological disorders (altered GABAergic transmission) and metabolic diseases. PLCL1 represents an understudied scaffold protein linking neurotransmission, calcium signaling, and cancer metabolism.
