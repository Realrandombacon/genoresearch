## Description
CCDC158 encodes a 1113 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt Q5M9N0) localized to chromosome 4q21.1. InterPro analysis reveals a defining coiled-coil domain architecture (IPR031809, PF15921) spanning the entire protein length, characteristic of scaffold proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions and subcellular organization. HPA expression profiling shows testis-enriched expression at 32.0 nTPM with no detection in cancer tissues or immune cells, suggesting a specialized role in germ cell development or spermatogenesis. ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations (deletions and duplications) spanning 4q13.3-21.3 regions, indicating strong dosage sensitivity. The coiled-coil structural motif suggests CCDC158 functions as a multimerization scaffold, potentially organizing protein complexes at the nuclear envelope or cytoskeleton during meiosis. Pathogenic CNVs likely disrupt stoichiometric balance of interaction partners, impairing germ cell-specific chromatin organization or spindle assembly. STRING and AlphaFold queries encountered technical errors, but the conserved coiled-coil architecture across vertebrates (mouse ortholog Q8CDI6, 1109 aa, 99% similarity) supports functional importance.
