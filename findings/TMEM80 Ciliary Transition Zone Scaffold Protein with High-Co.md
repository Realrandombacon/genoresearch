# TMEM80: Ciliary Transition Zone Scaffold Protein with High-Confidence Structure and Disease-Associated Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-20T11:12:57.388515

**Quality Score:** 3.5/10  (E=4.4, D=2.6) [MODERATE]

## Description
TMEM80 encodes a 143 amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt Q96HE8) localized to the Golgi apparatus and predicted to function in non-motile cilium assembly at the ciliary transition zone. The protein contains a conserved transmembrane-17 domain (IPR019184, residues 4-143) and a predicted membrane protein Pfam domain (PF09799, residues 21-127), indicating stable membrane integration. AlphaFold prediction shows high structural confidence (pLDDT 84.2), suggesting a well-folded transmembrane helix bundle.

STRING interaction analysis reveals TMEM80 participates in a ciliopathy-associated protein network with high-confidence partners: TMEM231 (0.758), B9D1 (0.718), TMEM237 (0.687), B9D2 (0.653), and TMEM67 (0.550). These interaction partners are established components of the MKS (Meckel-Gruber syndrome) and JBTS (Joubert syndrome) ciliary transition zone complexes, positioning TMEM80 as a likely scaffold protein for ciliary gate assembly.

HPA expression data shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with Golgi apparatus localization, consistent with a role in trafficking ciliary membrane proteins. ClinVar contains 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and SNVs, though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. The variant burden suggests haploinsufficiency or dominant-negative mechanisms may underlie ciliopathy presentations.

Functional hypothesis: TMEM80 serves as a transmembrane scaffold at the ciliary transition zone, recruiting B9D1/B9D2 complex components and TMEM231/237 to establish the ciliary diffusion barrier. Disease variants likely disrupt ciliary gate integrity, causing mislocalization of ciliary membrane proteins and impaired Hedgehog/Wnt signaling.
