## Description
ZNF16 encodes a 682 amino acid zinc finger protein (UniProt P17020) localized to the nucleoplasm and nucleoli. InterPro analysis reveals multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 209-343, plus a PRDM-like zinc finger family domain (IPR050331, PTHR16515) spanning residues 399-545, indicating DNA-binding capability and potential chromatin modification activity. STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners with high-confidence interaction to NOX4 (score 0.770), medium-confidence interactions to ZNF34 (0.597), INCA1 (0.588), ZNF250 (0.551), and chromatin-associated proteins H1-1/H1-10 (0.485-0.489). HPA expression data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a transcription factor involved in cell cycle, cell division, and transcription regulation. ClinVar documents 55 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains on chromosome 8q24.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation analysis from UniProt search shows orthologs in Pan troglodytes (682 aa, 100% identity), Gorilla gorilla (682 aa), and Pan paniscus (673 aa), indicating strong primate conservation. Functional hypothesis: ZNF16 acts as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulator that modulates erythroid and megakaryocytic differentiation through recruitment of chromatin remodeling complexes (via H1 histone interactions) and potential redox regulation (via NOX4 interaction). The PRDM-like domain suggests possible histone methyltransferase activity or recruitment of PRDM family cofactors. Copy number gains in ClinVar may cause dysregulation of hematopoietic differentiation programs.
