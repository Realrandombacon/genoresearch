## Description
HSPA14 encodes a 509 aa heat shock 70 kDa protein 14 (UniProt Q0VDF9), also known as Hsp70-14 or Apg-1, functioning as an atypical member of the Hsp70 chaperone family that regulates ribosome-associated protein folding, viral replication, and tumor progression. The protein contains canonical Hsp70 family architecture (IPR013126/PF00012, aa3-506) with a nucleotide-binding domain (NBD; IPR042049/cd10238, aa2-378) for ATP binding and hydrolysis (GO:0005524, GO:0016887), and a substrate-binding domain (SBD; IPR029047/G3DSA:2.60.34.10, aa381-508) for client protein recognition, belonging to the heat shock protein 70kD peptide-binding domain superfamily with conserved ATPase signature (IPR018181, aa332-346). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.4), indicating a well-folded stable structure typical of canonical Hsp70 chaperones. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a predicted intracellular chaperone with cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal HSPA14 forms chaperone complexes: DNAJC2 (0.998, DnaJ homolog subfamily C member 2/Hsp40 co-chaperone), HSPA12A (0.821, heat shock protein family A member 12A), HSPA4 (0.807, Hsp70 family member), DNAJA1 (0.770, DnaJ homolog subfamily A member 1), DNAJB1 (0.620, Hsp40 co-chaperone), HSPA12B (0.514), DNAJB4 (0.502), DNAJB6 (0.412), DNAJC5 (0.404), HSP90AB1 (0.402, heat shock protein 90). ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 10p15.3-12.31 copy number gains and losses, deletions, and promoter variant (c.-219A>T). Literature (44 papers) demonstrates HSPA14 promotes breast cancer tumorigenesis and immune response modulation; HSPA14 expression affects HIV-1 replication in acutely infected patients as a molecular chaperone; fluorescent polysome profiling reveals stress-mediated regulation of HSPA14-ribosome interactions; HSPA14 functions as TLR agonist in recombinant mycobacterial strains for innate immune cell training. HSPA14 functions as a ribosome-associated chaperone—its NBD hydrolyzes ATP driving conformational changes that enable the SBD to bind nascent polypeptide chains emerging from ribosomes, preventing aggregation and facilitating proper folding. The ultra-high confidence DNAJC2 interaction (0.998) indicates HSPA14 operates with Hsp40 co-chaperones that deliver substrate proteins and stimulate ATPase activity. Unlike canonical cytosolic Hsp70s, HSPA14 shows specialized roles in viral replication (HIV-1) suggesting it may chaperone viral proteins or regulate host factors required for viral life cycle. Breast cancer pathogenesis reflects HSPA14 overexpression stabilizing oncoproteins and modulating tumor immune microenvironment. Copy number variants on 10p may cause HSPA14 dosage effects contributing to developmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. The well-folded structure (pLDDT 91.4) enables HSPA14 to function as a stable chaperone platform coordinating protein folding cycles through ATP-driven conformational switching.
