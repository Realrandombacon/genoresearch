## Description
Werner syndrome protein (WRN) is a RecQ-like DNA helicase located on chromosome 8p12. It possesses a bifunctional 3'-5' exonuclease and ATP-dependent helicase domain, localized to nuclear speckles. WRN is critically involved in DNA repair, replication stress resolution, and telomere maintenance. Clinically, it is associated with Werner syndrome (progeroid disorder) and cancer predisposition, with 574 pathogenic variants recorded in ClinVar. AlphaFold modeling (pLDDT 68.6) suggests a mix of structured domains and disordered regions. With 2,344 literature papers and high-confidence interactions with DNA repair factors (DNA2, XRCC6, RPA2), WRN is definitively a 'well-studied' gene and not a 'dark gene', making it a candidate for skipping in a search for uncharacterized proteins.
