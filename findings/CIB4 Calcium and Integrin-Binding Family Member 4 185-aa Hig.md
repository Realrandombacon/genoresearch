## Description
CIB4 (Calcium and Integrin-Binding Family Member 4) is a partially characterized dark gene with ~16 publications, representing a critical calcium sensor exclusive to the male germline. UniProt accession A0PJX0 identifies it as a compact 185-amino acid intracellular protein. InterPro scan reveals a defining architecture dominated by EF-hand motifs: a pair of canonical EF-hand domains (IPR002048/PF13499, residues 1-183) containing specific calcium-binding sites (PS00018 at 110-122 and 152-164). This structure classifies CIB4 as a member of the EF-hand superfamily (SSF47473), specifically the Calcium and Integrin-Binding family (IPR051433), which undergoes conformational changes upon calcium binding to regulate downstream effectors.\n\nAlphaFold predicts an exceptionally high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 89.9), indicating a rigid, well-folded globular domain.
