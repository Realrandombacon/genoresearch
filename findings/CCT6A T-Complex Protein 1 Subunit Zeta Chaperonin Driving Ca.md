## Description
CCT6A (Chaperonin Containing TCP1 Subunit 6A, T-Complex Protein 1 Subunit Zeta, P40227) encodes a 531 aa essential molecular chaperone functioning as a critical subunit of the CCT/TRiC (TCP-1 Ring Complex) hetero-oligomeric chaperonin that mediates ATP-dependent folding of newly synthesized polypeptides including actin, tubulin, and cell cycle regulators. Structural architecture reveals canonical chaperonin fold with equatorial ATPase domain (G3DSA:1.10.560.10, residues 27-139/406-522) forming nucleotide-binding pocket, intermediate domain (G3DSA:3.30.260.10, residues 140-207/370-405) transmitting conformational changes, and apical substrate-binding domain (G3DSA:3.50.7.10/IPR027409, residues 203-378/208-369) recognizing unfolded client proteins; conserved TCP-1 catalytic sites (IPR002194, residues 35-47/56-72/84-92) enable ATP hydrolysis driving folding cycle; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 89.9) confirms very high confidence well-folded structure consistent with stable chaperonin complex assembly. STRING interactions define CCT/TRiC holoenzyme machinery with ultra-high confidence: all seven other CCT subunits CCT5 (0.999), CCT4 (0.999), CCT3 (0.999), TCP1/CCT1 (0.999), CCT8 (0.999), CCT2 (0.999), CCT7 (0.994) forming obligate hetero-octameric ring, plus protein phosphatase 2A subunits PPP2R2C (0.996)/PPP2CA (0.995) as client proteins, and cell cycle regulator CDC20 (0.995) requiring chaperonin-assisted folding. HPA localizes CCT6A to cytosol as essential chaperone/hydrolase involved in protein folding with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue/cancer specificity reflecting housekeeping function. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic variants including extensive 7p22.3-q36.3 copy number gains (chr7:10365-159119707, chr7:43361-159119707 triplications), 7p11.2 loss (chr7:54972516-56172165), TMEM106B-BRAF fusion deletion, and splice site mutations (c.1523+2T>C, c.1451-14T>G, c.615-11T>G). Literature (106 papers) demonstrates emerging oncogenic mechanisms: tumor-derived exosomal CCT6A serves as matchmaker introducing chemokines to tumor-associated macrophages promoting M2 polarization in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (9x citations, 2025), TRIM38 E3 ubiquitin ligase suppresses colorectal cancer progression via enhancing CCT6A ubiquitination to inhibit MYC pathway (7x citations, 2025), CAF-secreted exosomal CCT6A interacts with β-catenin enhancing chemoresistance and tumorigenesis in gastric cancer (8x citations, 2025), CCT6A facilitates lung adenocarcinoma progression and glycolysis via STAT1/HK2 axis (30x citations, 2024). Mechanistic hypothesis: CCT6A functions as integral component of CCT/TRiC hetero-octameric ring (CCT1-8 subunits) undergoing ATP-driven conformational cycling between open (substrate acceptance) and closed (folding chamber) states; equatorial domain binds/hydrolyzes ATP while intermediate domain transmits conformational change to apical domain that captures unfolded clients via hydrophobic interactions; in cancer, CCT6A overexpression enhances folding of oncoproteins (MYC, β-catenin, HK2) stabilizing them against degradation; STAT1 transcriptionally activates CCT6A increasing hexokinase 2 (HK2) folding efficiency driving Warburg effect glycolysis; TRIM38-mediated K48-linked polyubiquitination targets CCT6A for proteasomal degradation suppressing MYC-dependent proliferation; exosomal CCT6A transfer from tumor cells/CAFs to macrophages remodels tumor microenvironment promoting immunosuppressive M2 phenotype; 7p CNVs alter CCT6A dosage affecting global proteostasis and cell cycle progression; ubiquitous expression reflects essential role in folding ~10% of newly synthesized eukaryotic proteins including cytoskeletal components.
