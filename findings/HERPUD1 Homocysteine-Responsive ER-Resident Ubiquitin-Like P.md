## Description
HERPUD1 (Homocysteine-Responsive Endoplasmic Reticulum-Resident Ubiquitin-Like Domain Member 1; UniProt Q15011) is a 391 amino acid ER-resident scaffold protein with 153 publications, representing a moderately-characterized regulator of ER stress responses. Despite its name suggesting homocysteine sensing, HERPUD1 functions primarily as an ER-associated degradation (ERAD) scaffold coordinating ubiquitin-proteasome system recruitment to misfolded proteins during the unfolded protein response (UPR), with emerging roles in cancer progression, metabolic disease, and neurodegeneration.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals HERPUD1 contains a modular domain organization: (1) Ubiquitin-like domain (IPR000626/PF00240/SM00213, residues 10-87, ~78 aa) — N-terminal Ubl fold structurally homologous to ubiquitin (G3DSA:3.10.20.90, SSF54236), potentially mediating proteasome recognition or competing with ubiquitin for binding partners; (2) HERPUD family domain (IPR039751/PTHR12943, residues 1-391) — defines the HERP subfamily distinct from HERPUD2; (3) C-terminal region (residues 88-391, ~303 aa) — largely unstructured (AlphaFold pLDDT 62.2 medium confidence), containing predicted transmembrane segments anchoring HERPUD1 to ER membrane and disordered regions serving as flexible interaction hubs. The partial structure (pLDDT 62.2) indicates a hybrid architecture: structured Ubl domain (~80 aa) connected to intrinsically disordered ERAD scaffold region (~310 aa), typical of proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals high-confidence interactions exclusively with ERAD and UPR machinery: SYVN1 (0.926—highest; Synoviolin/HRD1, E3 ubiquitin ligase core to ERAD), ATF4 (0.895; Activating Transcription Factor 4, master UPR transcription factor), AMFR (0.883; Autocrine Motility Factor Receptor/GP78, E3 ubiquitin ligase alternative ERAD pathway), PRKCSH (0.769; Protein Kinase C Substrate 80K-H, ER glycosidase), SEL1L (0.719; Sel1 Like Adaptor Protein 1, ERAD adaptor bridging substrates to HRD1), EDEM1 (0.611; ER Degradation Enhancing Alpha-Mannosidase Like Protein 1, glycoprotein quality control), HSPA5 (0.595; BiP/GRP78, ER chaperone sensing misfolded proteins), PDIA3 (0.474; Protein Disulfide Isomerase A3, oxidative folding), XBP1 (0.424; X-box Binding Protein 1, UPR transcription factor), and DNAJB9 (0.415; ER J-domain co-chaperone). This interaction profile definitively places HERPUD1 at the center of ERAD: SYVN1/AMFR (E3 ligases), SEL1L/EDEM1 (substrate adaptors), HSPA5/DNAJB9 (chaperones), ATF4/XBP1 (UPR signaling).\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA classifies HERPUD1 as a predicted intracellular and membrane protein with plasma membrane and cytosol subcellular locations—however, literature confirms primary ER membrane localization consistent with ERAD function. RNA distribution shows low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues, reflecting ubiquitous requirement for protein quality control. Low cancer specificity suggests HERPUD1 is stress-inducible rather than oncogene-addicted, but is co-opted in malignancy. Single cell type specificity shows cell type enhanced pattern, likely indicating elevated expression in secretory cells (pancreatic beta cells, plasma cells) with high ER load.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number variations (16q11.2-24.3 x3, 16q12.2-22.1 x1/x3 spanning 5-44 Mb regions), deletions (16q11.2-21 x1, 16q12.2-22.1 del), duplications, and 3' UTR variant (c.*248T>G). No specific monogenic disease is definitively assigned, but 16q CNVs are associated with neurodevelopmental disorders and metabolic phenotypes. Literature reveals extensive cancer and metabolic disease connections: (1) 2025 studies demonstrate lncRNA AC012181.2 targets HERPUD1 regulating MSC metabolic reprogramming in gastric cancer metastasis (2 cites), HERPUD1 mediates palmitic acid-induced UPR sustaining triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) aggressiveness and is destabilized by CK2 inhibition (1 cite), EGR1 regulates phenotypic plasticity via HERPUD1-mediated UPR in breast cancer under metabolic stress (0 cites); (2) 2024 work shows HERPUD1 regulates T2DM implant angiogenesis-osteogenesis by accelerating TMBIM6 ubiquitination (0 cites), and effects in methamphetamine-induced neurotoxicity (truncated); (3) HERPUD1 upregulation correlates with poor prognosis in multiple cancers through enhanced ER stress tolerance; (4) Homocysteine induction links to cardiovascular risk and Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis.\n\nCONSERVATION: HERPUD1 orthologs confirmed across vertebrates (mouse Q9JJK5, 391 aa—100% length conservation) with conserved Ubl domain and ER membrane topology, indicating evolutionary constraint on ERAD function since early vertebrate evolution.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: HERPUD1 functions as an inducible ERAD scaffold through a stress-responsive mechanism: (1) UPR induction—ER stress (homocysteine, palmitic acid, hypoxia) activates ATF6/IRE1/PERK pathways→ATF4 (0.895) and XBP1 (0.424) transcription factors induce HERPUD1 expression; (2) ERAD complex assembly—HERPUD1 Ubl domain (residues 10-87) binds proteasome or competes with ubiquitin, while disordered C-terminal region (88-391) recruits SYVN1/HRD1 (0.926) and AMFR/GP78 (0.883) E3 ligases; (3) Substrate handoff—SEL1L (0.719) adaptor delivers misfolded glycoproteins to HERPUD1-SYVN1 complex; EDEM1 (0.611) trims mannose residues marking substrates for degradation; HSPA5/BiP (0.595) chaperone maintains substrate solubility; (4) Ubiquitination—SYVN1/AMFR polyubiquitinate substrates, targeting them to proteasome via HERPUD1 Ubl-proteasome interaction; (5) Cancer mechanism—HERPUD1 overexpression→enhanced ERAD capacity→cancer cells tolerate proteotoxic stress (palmitic acid, hypoxia)→survival + metastasis (gastric, TNBC); CK2 phosphorylation stabilizes HERPUD1, suggesting therapeutic vulnerability; (6) Metabolic disease—HERPUD1 accelerates TMBIM6 ubiquitination→altered calcium homeostasis→impaired angiogenesis-osteogenesis in T2DM; (7) Neurodegeneration—homocysteine-induced HERPUD1 may clear toxic aggregates (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's) or contribute to ER stress-mediated neuronal death; (8) Disease variants—16q CNVs→HERPUD1 dosage imbalance→impaired ERAD→accumulation of misfolded proteins→neurodevelopmental defects; (9) Therapeutic angle—CK2 inhibitors destabilize HERPUD1 sensitizing TNBC to ER stress; HERPUD1 modulation could enhance proteostasis in neurodegeneration or impair cancer cell stress tolerance.
