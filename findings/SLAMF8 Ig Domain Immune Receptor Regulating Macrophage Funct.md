## Description
SLAMF8 (BLAME/CD353) is a 285 aa type I transmembrane receptor of the CD2/SLAM family (IPR015631) containing two immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR007110, positions 19-130 and 131-221). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 81.3) consistent with stable Ig superfamily architecture. HPA shows lymphoid tissue-enriched expression (30.8 nTPM) with immune cell specificity. STRING reveals interactions with SLAM family members (SLAMF9: 0.730, SLAMF6: 0.638, SLAMF7: 0.539, SLAMF1: 0.579, LY9: 0.452) and immune regulators (CD2: 0.650, CD244: 0.605, FPR3: 0.666, ARHGAP30: 0.579, C1QB: 0.568), positioning it within leukocyte signaling networks. Unlike other SLAM receptors, SLAMF8 lacks classical intracellular signaling motifs (SAP/SLAM-associated protein binding sites), suggesting it signals through adaptor recruitment or heterodimerization. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (gains/losses at 1q23.2), though specific disease associations remain unclear. Recent 2025 literature reveals emerging functions: (1) disrupts epithelial barrier in chronic rhinosinusitis via M1 macrophage polarization, (2) regulates Fc receptor-mediated phagocytosis through PI3K-Akt signaling, (3) promotes neuroinflammation in Alzheimer's disease via TLR4-NFκB pathway with NINJ2, and (4) acts as novel immune checkpoint in oncology. With only 80 publications versus thousands for classical immune receptors, SLAMF8 represents an incompletely characterized immunomodulator.
