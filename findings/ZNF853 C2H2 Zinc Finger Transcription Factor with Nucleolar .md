## Description
ZNF853 encodes a 659 amino acid zinc finger protein (UniProt P0CG23) localized to nucleoli. InterPro analysis reveals multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 485-568, plus a PRDM4/PRDM1/PRDM14-like family domain (IPR050331, residues 127-620), suggesting chromatin regulatory function. The protein contains 3 canonical C2H2 zinc finger motifs (PS00028 signature) that enable DNA-binding transcription factor activity for RNA polymerase II. STRING interactions identify 10 medium-confidence partners including ZNF521 (0.428), ZNF768 (0.425), and ZMIZ2 (0.414) - all zinc finger or transcriptional co-regulator proteins, indicating ZNF853 functions in multi-protein transcriptional complexes. HPA data shows nucleolar localization, cancer-enhanced expression, and detection across many tissues with low specificity. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains on chromosome 7p22, implicating ZNF853 dosage sensitivity in disease. The PRDM-like domain suggests potential histone methyltransferase or chromatin remodeling activity, while C2H2 fingers provide sequence-specific DNA recognition. ZNF853 likely acts as a context-dependent transcriptional repressor or activator recruiting chromatin modifiers to target gene promoters, with dysregulation contributing to oncogenesis through altered gene expression programs.
