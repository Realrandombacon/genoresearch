## Description
CNOT9 encodes CCR4-NOT Transcription Complex Subunit 9 (UniProt Q92600), a 299-amino acid core scaffold subunit of the evolutionarily conserved CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex essential for mRNA decay, transcription regulation, and translation control. The protein contains conserved armadillo-type structural architecture including ARM repeat fold (IPR011989/IPR016024/SSF48371, positions 14-285/30-252) forming curved helical solenoid for protein-protein interactions, CCR4-NOT complex subunit 9 family domain (IPR007216/PTHR12262, positions 15-287) defining complex membership with GO annotations for mRNA catabolic process (GO:0006402) and CCR4-NOT complex (GO:0030014), and Rcd1-like cell differentiation family domain (PF04078, positions 25-283) conserved from yeast to humans, classified within Leucine-rich repeat variant superfamily (G3DSA:1.25.10.10, positions 14-285) indicating essential role in CCR4-NOT-mediated mRNA deadenylation through scaffold assembly and nuclease module recruitment.
