# TMEM144: Brain-Enriched Mitochondrial Nucleotide Sugar Transporter with EmrE Fold

**Quality Score:** 1.5/10  (E=2.3, D=0.7) [POOR]

## Description
TMEM144 (Transmembrane Protein 144, UniProt Q7Z5S9) is a 345 amino acid multi-pass membrane protein localized to mitochondria with strong brain-enriched expression (218.6 nTPM in brain tissue per HPA). InterPro analysis reveals it belongs to the sugar transport protein family (IPR010651, positions 5-344) and contains the TMEM144 transporter domain (IPR012435, PF07857, positions 9-344). Critically, the C-terminal region (240-331) adopts the Multidrug Transporter EmrE superfamily fold (IPR037185, SSF103481), a 4-transmembrane helix bundle typically involved in small molecule efflux. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-confidence partners including multiple solute carriers: SLC35C2 (0.517), SLC35B3 (0.504), SLC35C1 (0.477) - all nucleotide sugar transporters - plus SLC30A1 (0.430, zinc transporter), suggesting TMEM144 functions in coordinated nucleotide sugar transport across mitochondrial membranes. The gene alias SLC35G7 places it within the SLC35 nucleotide sugar transporter family. ClinVar documents 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 4q32.1-q35.2, indicating dosage sensitivity. The combination of EmrE-fold architecture, SLC35 family membership, brain-enriched expression, and mitochondrial localization supports a hypothesis that TMEM144 functions as a mitochondrial nucleotide sugar transporter, potentially importing UDP-glucose or related activated sugars required for mitochondrial glycosylation reactions. Disruption of this transport activity through copy number variation may impair mitochondrial protein glycosylation, affecting neuronal energy metabolism and contributing to neurological phenotypes.
