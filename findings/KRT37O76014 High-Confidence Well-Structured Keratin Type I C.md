## Description
KRT37 (Keratin 37, also known as HA7, K37, KRTHA7, Keratin type I cuticular Ha7) is a 449 amino acid intermediate filament protein (UniProt O76014) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 74.4), characteristic of type I hair keratins that function through heterodimerization with type II keratins to form the structural framework of hair shaft cuticle cells. The protein belongs to the keratin type I family (IPR002957, residues 87-414; PTHR23239, residues 87-414), contains an intermediate filament rod domain (IPR039008, residues 103-415; PF00038, residues 104-414; SM01391, residues 103-414; PS51842, residues 104-415), an intermediate filament conserved site (IPR018039, residues 401-409; PS00226, residues 401-409), and CATH-Gene3D homologous superfamily domains (G3DSA:1.20.5.1160, residues 139-254; G3DSA:1.20.5.170, residues 335-415; G3DSA:1.20.5.500, residues 268-334; SSF64593, coiled coil region). KRT37 is located on chromosome 17q21.2 within the type I hair keratin gene cluster and is predicted to have structural molecule activity (GO:0005198), involved in hair shaft formation and cuticle development.
