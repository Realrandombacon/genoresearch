## Description
SDF4 (Stromal Cell Derived Factor 4), also known as Cab45 (45 kDa Calcium-binding protein), is a 362-amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9BRK5) representing a genuine dark gene with only 37 publications despite its critical roles as a Golgi-localized EF-hand calcium-binding protein regulating pancreatic cancer biomarker secretion, sepsis mortality via ER stress suppression, chemotherapy-induced angiogenesis through CXCR4 signaling, nasopharyngeal carcinoma progression via RAB1B-PA2G4 axis, cardiac injury markers in open-heart surgery patients, and thiol/disulfide homeostasis. Structurally, SDF4 contains canonical calcium-binding protein architecture: EF-hand calcium-binding motifs (IPR002048/PF13202/PF13499/PS00018/PS50222/IPR018247, residues 98-172+233-268+278-313/289-305/101-163/111-123+150-162+246-258/98-133+137-172+233-268+278-313/111-123+150-162+246-258) forming multiple calcium-binding sites for conformational switching, EF-hand domain pairs (IPR011992/G3DSA:1.10.238.10, residues 96-164+193-356/98-166+179-351) enabling cooperative calcium binding, Calcium-binding protein CAB45 EF-hand domain (IPR027240/cd16225, residues 67-347/67-347) defining the functional class, and Calcium-Regulated Endoplasmic Reticulum family (PTHR10823/PTHR10827, residues 22-362) linking to ER/Golgi calcium homeostasis. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.6), consistent with a well-folded calcium-binding protein containing stable EF-hand helix-loop-helix motifs forming defined calcium-coordinating surfaces that undergo conformational changes upon calcium binding to regulate cargo interactions. Functionally, SDF4 localizes exclusively to Golgi apparatus with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (HPA: low tissue specificity, detected in all, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced), classified as a predicted intracellular protein functioning in calcium-dependent cargo sorting—Golgi localization confirms roles in secretory pathway protein trafficking and post-translational modification. STRING interactions reveal connections to diverse calcium-binding proteins: CALML4 (0.833, calmodulin-like protein 4—calcium sensor), CALML5 (0.832, calmodulin-like protein 5—epidermal calcium signaling), CALML3 (0.787, calmodulin-like protein 3—muscle calcium regulation), S100G (0.786, S100 calcium-binding protein G—vitamin D-dependent calcium transport), PVALB (0.662, parvalbumin—muscle relaxation calcium buffer), CALM3 (0.658, calmodulin 3—universal calcium sensor), CALB1 (0.584, calbindin 1—neuronal calcium buffering), S100B (0.500, S100 calcium-binding protein B—astrocyte marker), CALML6 (0.445, calmodulin-like protein 6), and CALB2 (0.445, calretinin—neuronal calcium buffer). This interaction network definitively positions SDF4 within the calcium signaling and buffering machinery, suggesting roles in calcium-dependent conformational switching for cargo recognition in the secretory pathway. Critically, ClinVar documents 135 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including massive copy number losses at 1p36.33-36.23 (chr1:82154-7350122)x1, 1p36.33-36.22 (chr1:849466-10271903)x1, 1p36.33-36.23 (chr1:849466-8966102)x1, 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:849467-3500877)x1, copy number gains at 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:849467-3153423)x3, (chr1:849466-4529103)x3, (chr1:1129319-3615916)x3, and single nucleotide variants (NM_016176.6:c.-147G>A promoter, c.556+61T>G intronic, c.716-12C>G splice region), yet ZERO diseases are formally associated—a profound gap given the validated roles in cancer, sepsis, and cardiovascular disease. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: circulating SDF4 serves as potential early detection biomarker for pancreatic cancer, suggesting tumor-derived secretion or altered clearance reflecting disease state (2025); molecular relationship between SDF4 and thiol/disulfide homeostasis correlates with cardiac injury markers in serum and pericardial fluid of open-heart surgery patients, establishing link to oxidative stress and myocardial damage (2025); LINC00173 facilitates nasopharyngeal carcinoma tumor progression by stimulating RAB1B-mediated PA2G4 and SDF4 secretion, revealing lncRNA-driven secretory pathway hijacking for cancer promotion (7 citations, 2023); fibroblast CEBPD/SDF4 axis mediates chemotherapy-induced angiogenesis through CXCR4, demonstrating stromal-tumor crosstalk where SDF4 acts as chemokine-like factor promoting vascularization (29 citations, 2021); SDF4 is prognostic factor for 28-day mortality in sepsis patients via negatively regulating ER stress, showing protective function through unfolded protein response modulation (12 citations, 2021). The mechanistic hypothesis: SDF4 functions as a calcium-dependent cargo receptor in the Golgi apparatus that selectively binds secretory proteins in a calcium-regulated manner, facilitating their sorting into secretory vesicles. The multiple EF-hand domains (at least 6 calcium-binding sites based on domain architecture) undergo conformational changes upon calcium binding in the Golgi lumen (high calcium concentration ~0.5-1 mM), exposing hydrophobic patches that recognize specific cargo proteins—including pancreatic cancer biomarkers (potentially CA19-9 precursors, mucins, or growth factors), angiogenic factors (VEGF, FGF), and inflammatory mediators. In pancreatic cancer, tumor cells upregulate SDF4 expression enhancing secretion of oncogenic factors detectable in circulation as early biomarkers. In nasopharyngeal carcinoma, LINC00173 lncRNA upregulates RAB1B (small GTPase regulating ER-to-Golgi trafficking) which enhances SDF4 and PA2G4 (proliferation-associated 2G4) secretion, promoting tumor growth and metastasis. In chemotherapy-induced angiogenesis, fibroblast-derived SDF4 binds CXCR4 on endothelial cells acting as a chemokine-like ligand (despite lacking classical chemokine structure) promoting vessel formation and tumor revascularization post-treatment. In sepsis, SDF4 suppresses ER stress by enhancing folding capacity or reducing unfolded protein load through efficient cargo sorting, protecting organs from apoptosis—low SDF4 levels predict mortality due to unchecked ER stress. In cardiac injury, SDF4 release from damaged cardiomyocytes or altered thiol/disulfide balance affects its calcium-binding capacity, correlating with troponin and other injury markers. The calcium-binding protein interaction network (CALML3/4/5, S100G/B, PVALB, CALM3, CALB1/2) suggests SDF4 operates within a broader calcium signaling hub, potentially competing for calcium or cooperating in calcium-dependent processes. The Golgi localization and ubiquitous expression indicate fundamental housekeeping functions in protein secretion with tissue-specific cargo preferences. The absence of formal disease associations despite 135 pathogenic variants suggests SDF4 dysfunction contributes to 1p36 deletion syndrome (developmental delay, seizures, cardiomyopathy), pancreatic cancer susceptibility, sepsis susceptibility/severity, cardiovascular disease, congenital disorders of glycosylation (Golgi trafficking defects), or ER stress-related disorders not yet mapped to Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define SDF4's complete cargo repertoire via proteomics of SDF4 immunoprecipitates from Golgi membranes, determine crystal structure of EF-hand domains with bound calcium and cargo peptides, investigate whether germline SDF4 mutations cause congenital secretory defects or developmental disorders, develop SDF4-blocking antibodies for cancer therapy (inhibiting angiogenesis and biomarker secretion), explore SDF4 as prognostic biomarker in sepsis and pancreatic cancer, and assess SDF4 modulation for treating ER stress-related diseases.
