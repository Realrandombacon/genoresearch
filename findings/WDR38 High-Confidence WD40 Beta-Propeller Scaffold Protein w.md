## Description
WDR38 (WD repeat domain 38, UniProt Q5JTN6) is a 314-amino acid intracellular protein that functions as a WD40 repeat-containing scaffold mediating protein-protein interactions in multiple cellular pathways. InterPro analysis reveals defining WD40 beta-propeller architecture: canonical WD40 domain (cd00200/PF00400/IPR001680/IPR019775/PS00678, aa 20-300/14-133/10-303/36-176/36-50) forming the characteristic 7-bladed beta-propeller structure that provides a rigid platform for assembling multi-protein complexes, classified within WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (G3DSA:2.130.10.10/IPR015943/IPR036322, aa 7-312/7-312/12-298) with specific PAC1/LIS1-like WD-40 repeat signatures (IPR020472/PR00320, aa 78-176/78-176) suggesting functional similarity to microtubule-associated and chromatin-regulatory WD40 proteins. GO annotations confirm protein binding activity (GO:0005515). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 87.5), indicating a well-folded protein with stable beta-propeller architecture — essential for maintaining the rigid circular scaffold required for simultaneous binding of multiple partner proteins through distinct blade surfaces. Human Protein Atlas shows group-enriched expression specifically in fallopian tube (73.1 nTPM top expression) and choroid plexus (42.5 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, group-enriched cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell specificity — subcellular localization in vesicles suggests trafficking through secretory/endosomal pathways. This interactome suggests WDR38 functions at the intersection of epigenetic regulation (via COMPASS complex association) and ciliary structure/function (via radial spoke complex association). ClinVar contains 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting primarily of copy number variations (gains and losses) spanning chromosome 9q regions (9q21.11-34.3, 9p24.3-q34.3) — these large CNVs encompass multiple genes and are associated with developmental disorders, though no specific disease is attributed solely to WDR38. Conservation across primates (Macaca nemestrina A0A2K6CS65, Chlorocebus sabaeus A0A0D9RM93, Saimiri boliviensis A0A2K6U8W6, Mus musculus Q9D994 303 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental cellular processes. WDR38 likely functions as a versatile protein-protein interaction scaffold where the WD40 beta-propeller simultaneously binds chromatin modifiers (RBBP5/ASH2L) to regulate histone H3K4 methylation and gene transcription in reproductive and CNS tissues, while also associating with ciliary radial spoke components (RSPH9/RSPH14) to regulate cilia assembly or motility — with fallopian tube enrichment suggesting roles in oviductal ciliary function for egg transport, choroid plexus enrichment suggesting roles in CSF production/ciliary function, and pathogenic CNVs causing developmental disorders through combined disruption of epigenetic programming and ciliogenesis.
