## Description
NMRAL1 encodes NmrA-like redox sensor 1 (UniProt Q9HBL8), a 299 aa protein functioning as a cellular redox sensor linking NAD(P)H status to innate immunity, DNA damage response, and metabolic regulation. InterPro analysis reveals a complete NmrA-like oxidoreductase family signature (IPR051164, PF05368, PTHR42748) with NAD(P)-binding Rossmann-fold domain (IPR036291, SSF51735, G3DSA:3.40.50.720) spanning residues 4-286 and UDP-galactose 4-epimerase-like domain (G3DSA:3.90.25.10), characteristic of SDR (short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase) superfamily enzymes. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 96.6), indicating a stable, well-folded protein with conserved catalytic triad geometry typical of NAD(P)H-dependent oxidoreductases. STRING interactions show high-confidence connections to mitochondrial respiratory chain components: NDUFS3 (0.874), UQCRFS1 (0.861), CYC1 (0.849), NDUFV1 (0.845), NDUFS8 (0.830), and NDUFA12 (0.815), suggesting functional coupling between redox sensing and oxidative phosphorylation. HPA confirms nucleoplasmic localization with broad tissue expression, consistent with a fundamental regulatory role. ClinVar reports 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily 16p13.3 copy number gains/losses), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (16 papers) establishes roles in: (1) PRRSV inhibition via nitazoxanide targeting (2024); (2) Postherpetic neuralgia risk (2025); (3) Schizophrenia susceptibility through rs2270363 regulation (2022); (4) Diabetic cardiomyopathy protection via STAMP2/NMRAL1/NF-κB axis (2022); (5) Redox-immunity-DNA damage integration (2020). Functional hypothesis: NMRAL1 functions through: (1) NAD(P)H Redox Sensing - binds NAD(P)H via Rossmann fold, undergoing conformational changes that modulate downstream signaling; (2) NF-κB Pathway Regulation - redox-dependent inhibition of NF-κB reduces inflammatory responses in diabetic cardiomyopathy; (3) Mitochondrial Crosstalk - physical association with Complex I subunits suggests retrograde signaling from respiratory chain to nuclear gene expression; (4) Viral Replication Control - serves as host factor targeted by nitazoxanide to inhibit PRRSV, potentially through redox modulation of viral entry/fusion; (5) Neurological Disease Mechanism - 16p13.3 CNVs affecting NMRAL1 dosage may disrupt redox homeostasis in neurons, contributing to schizophrenia and chronic pain conditions. The very high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 96.6) and strong interaction network with mitochondrial complexes provide a structural basis for its role as a metabolic sentinel integrating cellular energy status with immune and stress responses.
