## Description
NCAPH2 (Non-SMC Condensin II Complex Subunit H2, UniProt Q6IBW4) is a 605 aa nuclear protein belonging to the Condensin-2 complex subunit H2 family (IPR031739/PTHR14324, positions 1-604) that functions in chromosome condensation, telomere stability, and erythroid gene expression. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal domain (IPR009378/PF06278, positions 7-124) for condensin complex assembly, a middle domain (IPR031719/PF16869, positions 148-218) for structural scaffolding, and a C-terminal winged helix-turn-helix domain (IPR031737/PF16858, positions 466-594) for DNA binding and chromatin interaction. Literature (29 papers) establishes NCAPH2 roles in: (1) associations between peripheral NCAPH2 promoter methylation and different stages of Alzheimer's disease (2023, 3 citations), (2) condensin II complex subunit NCAPH2 regulates patterns of H4K20me1 occupancy and erythroid gene expression (2023), (3) blood NCAPH2 methylation associated with hippocampal volume in subjective cognitive decline with APOE ε4 non-carriers (2023, 4 citations), (4) NCAPH2 associates with shelterin protein TRF1 and is required for telomere stability (2019, 17 citations), (5) condensin II complex subunit NCAPH2 essential for erythropoiesis and regulates erythroid gene expression (2021). Functional hypothesis: NCAPH2 functions as a structural subunit of the condensin II complex that mediates chromosome condensation during mitosis, telomere stability through TRF1 interaction, and epigenetic regulation of erythroid and neuronal gene expression. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) N-terminal domain binds SMC2-SMC4 heterodimer for condensin II ring assembly, (2) Middle domain provides structural scaffold for complex stability, (3) C-terminal wHTH domain binds DNA at specific chromatin loci for loop extrusion and compaction, (4) NCAPH2-TERF1 interaction protects telomeres from DNA damage response and maintains telomere length, (5) NCAPH2 regulates H4K20me1 histone methylation patterns at erythroid-specific genes for terminal differentiation, (6) NCAPH2 promoter methylation in peripheral blood correlates with hippocampal volume and Alzheimer's disease progression through epigenetic dysregulation of neuronal genes. In Alzheimer's disease, altered NCAPH2 methylation disrupts chromosome architecture in neurons, contributing to cognitive decline. In erythropoiesis, NCAPH2 loss impairs nuclear condensation and enucleation. NCAPH2 represents an understudied condensin II subunit linking chromosome dynamics to telomere maintenance, blood cell development, and neurodegenerative disease.
