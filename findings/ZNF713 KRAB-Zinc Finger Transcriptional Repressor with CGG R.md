# ZNF713: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with CGG Repeat Expansion Linked to Autism Spectrum Disorder

**Date:** 2026-03-28T00:42:31.427925

## Description
ZNF713 (Zinc Finger Protein 713, UniProt Q8N859) is a 443-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor localized to nucleoli. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB-A box repression domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 31-102) that recruits the universal co-repressor TRIM28/KAP1, followed by multiple tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 235-409) arranged in clusters that recognize specific genomic sequences. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT=55.3) with confident zinc finger folds but disordered linker regions, characteristic of transcription factors that require conformational flexibility for chromatin engagement. HPA classifies ZNF713 as a transcription factor with low tissue specificity (detected in many tissues), nucleolar subcellular localization, and immune cell-enhanced expression in blood cells — suggesting roles in both ubiquitous gene regulation and immune-specific transcriptional programs. STRING interaction network reveals functional associations with SUMF2 (0.670, sulfatase-modifying factor), ZNF793 (0.572, related KRAB-ZFP), TRIM28 (0.566, KRAB co-repressor essential for heterochromatin formation), ZNF674 (0.526, X-linked intellectual disability gene), PHKG1 (0.496), ZNF554 (0.489), DIP2B (0.484), SEPTIN14 (0.445), MRPS17 (0.424), and NIPSNAP2 (0.407). The TRIM28 interaction confirms canonical KRAB-ZFP repressor function through recruitment of histone methyltransferases and HP1 to establish heterochromatin. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses across 7p11.2-q36.3 and complex rearrangements. Notably, RefSeq reports CGG-repeat expansions from normal 5-22 repeats to pathogenic 68-450 repeats in the first intron, proposed to disrupt ZNF713 expression and associate with Autism Spectrum Disorder — analogous to FMR1 CGG expansion mechanisms in fragile X syndrome. The combination of KRAB-mediated transcriptional repression, nucleolar localization (suggesting rRNA or ribosome biogenesis regulation), TRIM28 co-repressor binding, and CGG repeat instability implicates ZNF713 in neurodevelopmental gene regulation where dosage disruption through repeat expansion or CNV causes autism spectrum phenotypes through dysregulation of neuronal transcriptional programs.
