# Golgi-Localized Anti-Apoptotic Regulator TMBIM4 Coordinates Calcium Signaling and Innate Immunity

## Description
TMBIM4 (Transmembrane BAX Inhibitor Motif Containing 4), also known as Protein Lifeguard 4, is a 238-amino acid Golgi apparatus-localized anti-apoptotic protein that functions as a critical regulator of cell death, calcium homeostasis, and innate immune signaling. Despite only 29 publications (confirming its 'dark gene' status), emerging evidence reveals sophisticated biological roles. The protein contains a conserved Bax inhibitor-1 domain (IPR006214, PF01027, positions 17-234) and Golgi antiapoptotic protein domain (cd10429, positions 6-236), with AlphaFold predicting a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 92.4). STRING interactions reveal functional connections to SIGMAR1 (0.876), DDX58/RIG-I (0.845), TMEM97 (0.830), ITPR3 (0.814), EIF4E (0.755), and IFIH1/MDA5 (0.746), suggesting TMBIM4 coordinates apoptosis regulation with calcium signaling (via ITPR3), ER stress responses (via SIGMAR1), and antiviral innate immunity (via DDX58/IFIH1). HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with Golgi apparatus localization. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number losses on chromosome 12q14.3), though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. Recent studies (2023-2025) demonstrate TMBIM4 affects left-right patterning during gastrulation, regulates BCR-mediated Ca2+ mobilization in germinal center B cell selection, prevents NLRP3 inflammasome activation in trophoblasts (preeclampsia pathogenesis), and is regulated by miR-125a-3p in lens epithelial cell apoptosis. We hypothesize TMBIM4 functions as a Golgi-ER calcium homeostasis checkpoint that suppresses BAX/BAK-mediated apoptosis while simultaneously modulating RIG-I/MDA5 antiviral signaling, positioning it as a molecular hub integrating cell death decisions with innate immune surveillance.
