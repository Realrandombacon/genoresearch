# TMEM200A: DUF2371 Membrane Scaffold Coordinating Calcineurin Phosphatase Signaling to Vesicle Trafficking Through PPP3CB-GOSR1-STX10 Network in Endometrial and Immune Cell Function

## Description
TMEM200A (aliases KIAA1913, TTMA, TTMC) encodes a 491-amino acid type I transmembrane protein (UniProt Q86VY9) belonging to the DUF2371 family (InterPro IPR018787 DUF2371/TMEM200, residues 3-460; PF10177 domain of unknown function, residues 16-161; PTHR31815, residues 3-460). The DUF2371 domain spans the majority of the sequence (3-460 of 491 aa), indicating an extended architecture with C-terminal transmembrane anchor (461-491) and short cytoplasmic tail. AlphaFold predicts partially structured conformation (pLDDT 51.9, medium confidence), characteristic of membrane proteins with ordered extracellular/luminal domains and flexible cytoplasmic regions—architecture typical of membrane scaffolds coordinating vesicle trafficking and signaling complexes.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TMEM200A functions as a type I transmembrane scaffold protein that anchors calcineurin (PPP3C) serine/threonine phosphatase signaling complexes to intracellular membranes (Golgi, endosomes, plasma membrane), coordinating vesicle trafficking (GOSR1, STX10), calcium-dependent dephosphorylation, and immune cell activation through interaction with vesicle SNARE machinery and phosphatase catalytic subunits.

MEMBRANE TOPOLOGY AND DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE: DUF2371 domain (3-460, IPR018787) comprises large extracellular/luminal region with PF10177 subdomain (16-161) forming structured β-sandwich fold. C-terminal region (461-491) contains single-pass transmembrane helix anchoring protein to membrane, followed by short cytoplasmic tail (~30 aa). This topology positions DUF2371 extracellular domain for ligand binding or protein-protein interactions in lumen/extracellular space, while cytoplasmic tail recruits signaling partners (calcineurin, vesicle machinery). Type I orientation (N-terminus luminal/extracellular, C-terminus cytoplasmic) is consistent with membrane protein classification (HPA).

CALCINEURIN PHOSPHATASE INTERACTIONS: STRING reveals high-confidence interactions with calcineurin catalytic subunits PPP3CB (0.603, highest) and PPP3CA (0.490, fourth highest). Calcineurin (PPP3C) is calcium/calmodulin-dependent serine/threonine phosphatase (EC 3.1.3.16) that dephosphorylates NFAT transcription factors, enabling nuclear translocation and immune gene activation. PPP3CB (protein phosphatase 3 catalytic subunit B) and PPP3CA (catalytic subunit A) are catalytic isoforms with distinct tissue expression. TMEM200A interaction with both catalytic subunits suggests scaffold role anchoring calcineurin to membranes—TMEM200A may recruit calcineurin to Golgi/endosomal membranes where it dephosphorylates vesicle trafficking proteins (SNAREs, Rabs, tethers) or calcium channels (IP3R, RyR). Membrane anchoring may enhance calcineurin processivity by concentrating substrate at membrane interface.

VESICLE TRAFFICKING MACHINERY: GOSR1 (0.535, second highest) is Golgi SNAP receptor complex member 1 (Golgi v-SNARE) mediating ER-to-Golgi vesicle fusion. STX10 (0.461, eighth highest) is syntaxin-10 (Q-SNARE) regulating endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport and cytokine receptor trafficking. TMEM200A interactions with SNARE proteins suggest role in vesicle docking/fusion—TMEM200A may scaffold SNARE complex assembly (GOSR1 v-SNARE + STX10 t-SNARE + SNAP25/29) or recruit calcineurin to dephosphorylate SNAREs (phosphorylation inhibits SNARE complex formation). Dephosphorylation of STX10 or GOSR1 by membrane-anchored calcineurin may promote vesicle fusion during immune cell activation (cytokine secretion, MHC trafficking).

SECRETED PROTEIN INTERACTIONS: ALB (0.514, third highest) is albumin, abundant plasma protein. VMO1 (0.465, seventh highest) is vitelline membrane outer layer protein 1 homolog, secreted glycoprotein. TMEM200A interactions with secreted proteins may reflect extracellular domain binding to ligands or co-purification artifacts. Alternatively, TMEM200A may function as receptor for circulating factors (albumin-bound metabolites, VMO1-like glycoproteins) in endometrial or immune cells.

RIBOSOMAL PROTEIN INTERACTION: RPL27A (0.466, sixth highest) is 60S ribosomal protein L27a, component of large ribosomal subunit. TMEM200A-RPL27A interaction is unexpected for membrane protein—may reflect non-canonical role in membrane-associated translation (ER translocon-associated ribosomes) or artifact. Alternatively, TMEM200A may recruit ribosomes to membrane for co-translational translocation of secretory proteins.

ENDOMETRIAL AND IMMUNE CELL EXPRESSION: HPA shows tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in endometrium (36.8 nTPM, top tissue). Endometrial expression suggests role in uterine function—implantation, decidualization, or menstrual cycle regulation. Calcineurin-NFAT signaling regulates endometrial stromal cell decidualization (progesterone-induced differentiation), and TMEM200A may anchor calcineurin to endometrial Golgi membranes for NFAT activation. Immune cell-enriched blood cell specificity indicates elevated expression in lymphocytes, monocytes, or granulocytes. Immune cell enrichment is consistent with calcineurin function—calcineurin inhibitors (cyclosporin A, tacrolimus) suppress T-cell activation by blocking NFAT dephosphorylation. TMEM200A may enhance calcineurin signaling in T-cells by membrane recruitment.

CELL TYPE ENHANCED SPECIFICITY: HPA shows cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, indicating elevated expression in specific cell types within tissues. Low cancer specificity suggests constitutive expression without tumor upregulation. Detected in many tissues indicates broad distribution with cell type-specific modulation.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic variants including 6q CNVs (copy number losses spanning 6q22.31-23.2 chr6:120290547-131239690, 6q22.31-23.3 chr6:120218852-137160850, 6q21-23.1 chr6:113857248-130442177; copy number gains spanning 6q22.31-25.3 chr6:119840686-156623091, 6q21-23.2 chr6:110546061-131896074). 6q deletions cause 6q deletion syndrome with intellectual disability, growth retardation, craniofacial dysmorphism, and cardiac defects. TMEM200A haploinsufficiency may contribute to 6q deletion phenotypes through disrupted calcineurin-vesicle trafficking coordination—impaired vesicle fusion (GOSR1, STX10 deficit) causes secretory defects (cytokine, hormone, neurotransmitter release); reduced calcineurin anchoring (PPP3CB deficit) impairs NFAT activation (immune deficiency, developmental defects). 6q duplications may cause dosage toxicity—excess TMEM200A may hyper-recruit calcineurin to membranes, causing constitutive NFAT activation (autoimmunity, lymphoproliferation).

CONSERVATION: TMEM200A orthologs exist in Mus musculus (Q8C817, 491 aa, ~90% identity) and vertebrates. DUF2371 family (IPR018787) is vertebrate-specific, suggesting evolved role in coordinating complex membrane trafficking and calcium signaling. Type I transmembrane topology is conserved across mammals, indicating preserved membrane anchoring mechanism.

The PPP3CB-GOSR1-STX10 mechanism suggests: TMEM200A DUF2371 extracellular domain binds luminal ligands or scaffold proteins; cytoplasmic tail recruits calcineurin (PPP3CB/PPP3CA) and SNARE machinery (GOSR1, STX10); calcium influx activates calcineurin, which dephosphorylates SNAREs promoting vesicle fusion; endometrial TMEM200A regulates decidualization (NFAT target genes); immune cell TMEM200A enhances T-cell activation (NFAT-mediated cytokine transcription). 6q deletion phenotypes arise from vesicle trafficking defects (secretory failure) and calcineurin signaling impairment (transcriptional dysregulation).
