## Description
FAM90A26 encodes a 464 amino acid nuclear protein (UniProt D6RGX4) localized to nucleoplasm and nucleoli. InterPro analysis reveals two key domains: (1) FAM90 family signature (IPR039213, PTHR16035) spanning residues 2-462, defining membership in the poorly characterized FAM90 protein family; (2) C4-type zinc knuckle domain (IPR041670, PF15288) at residues 41-82, suggesting nucleic acid binding capability typical of zinc finger proteins. HPA shows restricted expression pattern - not detected in most tissues but cell type enriched in single cell analysis, with nucleolar localization suggesting ribosomal RNA processing or nucleolar stress response functions. STRING identifies one high-confidence interaction with FAM90A1 (score 0.629), indicating functional relationship within the FAM90 family. ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations at chromosome 4p16.3 (deletions and duplications spanning 68kb-12.7Mb), indicating clinical significance though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. Conservation analysis shows orthologs in chimpanzee (462-464 aa, >99% identity expected) and whale (377 aa with conserved zinc knuckle), suggesting functional constraint across mammals. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: FAM90A26 functions as a nucleolar zinc knuckle protein involved in ribosomal RNA metabolism or nucleolar organization. The zinc knuckle domain (C4-type) typically binds single-stranded RNA or DNA, suggesting direct nucleic acid interactions. FAM90 family membership and interaction with FAM90A1 suggests potential hetero-oligomerization or shared pathway involvement. Nucleolar localization combined with zinc knuckle architecture points to roles in rRNA processing, ribosome biogenesis, or nucleolar stress sensing. Pathogenic 4p16.3 CNVs may cause haploinsufficiency affecting nucleolar function, potentially contributing to developmental disorders given the 4p16.3 region association with Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome.
