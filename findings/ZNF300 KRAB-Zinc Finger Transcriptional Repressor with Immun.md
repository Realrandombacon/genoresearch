# ZNF300: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Immune-Enhanced Expression and IRGM Interaction

**Date:** 2026-03-20T15:08:40.734387

## Description
ZNF300 (zinc finger protein 300, Gene ID: 91975) is a 604 amino acid transcriptional repressor located on chromosome 5q33.1 (UniProt: Q96RE9). InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, positions 7-79) that recruits corepressors, plus three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, positions 269-352) for sequence-specific DNA binding, and a PRDM-like zinc finger region (IPR050331, positions 461-600). STRING database identifies 4 protein interactions: IRGM (score 0.831, high confidence), TRIM28/KAP1 (0.584), SLC26A1 (0.446), and IKBKB (0.445). The IRGM interaction suggests potential roles in immunity/autophagy pathways, while TRIM28 confirms canonical KRAB corepressor recruitment. HPA data shows expression in many tissues (low tissue specificity) with immune cell-enhanced expression pattern and nucleoplasmic/nucleolar/nuclear body localization. Classified as cancer-related gene. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses spanning 5q32-35.3 regions (variants 1703528, 441920, 441919, 394550, 149858, 58118, 57483) and deletions (457359, 442431, 59672). The KRAB domain mediates heterochromatin formation via TRIM28 recruitment, while zinc fingers confer DNA sequence specificity. ZNF300 functions as a chromatin-associated transcriptional silencer, potentially regulating immune response genes through IRGM interaction and cell cycle genes. Three transcript variants encode different isoforms. AlphaFold structure prediction and conservation data not computed. While domain architecture is well-defined, specific target genes and disease mechanisms remain unclear.
