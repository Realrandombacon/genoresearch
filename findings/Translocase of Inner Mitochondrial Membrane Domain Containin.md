## Description
TIMMDC1 (Translocase of inner mitochondrial membrane domain containing 1, also known as C3orf1) is a 285 aa mitochondrial inner membrane protein functioning as a critical assembly chaperone for NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase (Complex I) of the electron transport chain. With 37 literature references, TIMMDC1 represents a 'partially characterized' dark gene where its fundamental role in Complex I biogenesis is established, but its specific mechanisms in neurodegenerative disease correction and asthma-related smooth muscle proliferation are emerging (2022-2025). InterPro analysis reveals a conserved Tim17/Tim22/Tim23/Pmp24 family transmembrane domain (PF02466, aa 77-198) embedded within the larger TIMMDC1 family signature (IPR055299, aa 29-275), suggesting a role in membrane protein integration or stabilization rather than simple transport. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 72.4), indicating a well-folded multi-pass transmembrane protein with stable helical bundles typical of mitochondrial import/assembly factors. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, localizing to mitochondria and nucleoplasm, consistent with a housekeeping role in cellular energy metabolism. STRING interactions reveal TIMMDC1 exists within a tightly coordinated Complex I assembly network: it binds strongly to core membrane arm subunits (NDUFA8: 0.999, NDUFA13: 0.999, NDUFB5: 0.785, NDUFB10: 0.669) and dedicated assembly factors (NDUFAF1: 0.985, NDUFAF3: 0.906, NDUFAF4: 0.937, ECSIT: 0.938, ACAD9: 0.717, TMEM126B: 0.710), confirming its role as a scaffold bridging early and late assembly intermediates. ClinVar lists 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.517+5del, c.361-2A>T, c.194+2T>C), nonsense mutations (p.Trp138Ter), frameshifts (p.Lys64fs, p.Leu272fs), and missense changes (p.Arg160Gln, p.Pro4Thr), definitively linking TIMMDC1 loss-of-function to severe infantile neurodegenerative disorders characterized by Leigh-like syndrome, developmental delay, and lactic acidosis. Recent literature (2022-2025) establishes TIMMDC1 as: (1) Target for oligonucleotide-mediated correction of intronic splice variants in patient-derived cells, restoring Complex I activity; (2) Regulator of airway smooth muscle cell (ASMC) proliferation in asthma via the METTL3/m6A axis and lncRNA TCF7/TIMMDC1/Akt pathway; (3) Essential component whose depletion causes complete absence of assembled Complex I while preserving individual subunit synthesis.
