## Description
IRX5 (Iroquois Homeobox 5, P78411) encodes a 483 aa TALE-class homeodomain transcription factor functioning as a master regulator of tissue patterning, cardiac electrophysiology, and metabolic gene networks through DNA binding and chromatin remodeling. Structural architecture reveals canonical homeodomain (IPR001356/PF00046/PS50071/cd00086/G3DSA:1.10.10.60, residues 113-178/117-175/115-174) forming three-helix bundle for major groove DNA recognition, KN homeodomain extension (IPR008422/PF05920, residues 131-170) characteristic of TALE homeobox proteins enabling heterodimerization with PBX/MEIS partners, Iroquois-specific domain (IPR003893, residues 327-344) for protein-protein interactions, and conserved homeobox signature (IPR017970/PS00027, residues 149-172); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 54.3) reflects typical transcription factor architecture with structured DNA-binding homeodomain embedded in largely disordered transactivation domains enabling dynamic chromatin interactions. STRING interactions define developmental and metabolic networks: histone methyltransferase SMYD1 (0.793) linking to epigenetic regulation, basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor HAND2 (0.730) for cardiac development, obesity-associated gene FTO (0.688) connecting to metabolic regulation, potassium channel regulator KCNE4 (0.626)/KCND2 (0.412) for cardiac repolarization, paralog IRX6 (0.540)/IRX3 (0.400) for redundant functions, chromatin remodeler ARID5B (0.501), serum response factor SRF (0.446) for immediate early genes, and GPD1L (0.424) linking to Brugada syndrome. HPA localizes IRX5 to nuclear speckles/microtubules/cytosol as DNA-binding transcription factor involved in sensory transduction/transcription/vision with tissue-enhanced expression in skin (16.1 nTPM) and breast (13.2 nTPM), cancer-enhanced specificity, intellectual disability association. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic variants including extensive 16q11.2-24.3 copy number gains (chr16:46432879-90294753, chr16:46503573-62203182, chr16:46503968-90155062 triplications), deletions (chr16:53635988-55539614), duplications, and missense variant p.Arg168His within homeodomain disrupting DNA binding. Literature (241 papers) demonstrates multifaceted disease roles: IRX5 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma via SREBP1-mediated de novo fatty acid synthesis through HMGN4 interaction (3x citations, 2025), influences macrophage polarization affecting papillary thyroid cancer outcome (7x citations, 2024), suppresses osteogenic differentiation of human bone marrow mesenchymal stem cells by inhibiting protein synthesis favoring adipogenesis in osteoporosis (3x citations, 2024), IRX3/IRX5 double heterozygosity causes sex-dependent bone homeostasis defects (1x citation, 2024), FTO obesity risk variants regulate IRX5/IRX3 expression through long-range enhancer interactions. Mechanistic hypothesis: IRX5 functions as context-dependent transcriptional regulator through multiple mechanisms; homeodomain binds AT-rich DNA sequences (5-TGACAG-3) in target gene enhancers/promoters while KN domain mediates heterodimerization with PBX/MEIS cofactors increasing binding specificity; in cardiomyocytes, IRX5 represses KCND2/KCNE4 encoding Kv4.2 potassium channels establishing transmural repolarization gradient with IRX3 determining ventricular action potential duration; FTO obesity risk SNPs disrupt IRX5/IRX3 regulatory landscape altering adipocyte thermogenesis; in cancer, nuclear IRX5 recruits HMGN4 chromatin modifier to SREBP1 target genes enhancing lipogenic program supporting tumor growth; cytoplasmic IRX5 pool may interact with HAND2-SMYD1 complex affecting cardiac morphogenesis; 16q CNVs alter IRX5 dosage causing intellectual disability through disrupted neurodevelopmental gene networks; p.Arg168His mutation in homeodomain helix III abolishes DNA contact preventing target gene regulation.
