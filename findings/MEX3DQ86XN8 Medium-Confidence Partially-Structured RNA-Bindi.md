## Description
MEX3D (Mex-3 RNA binding family member D, also known as MEX-3D, MEX3, RKHD1, TINO) is a 651 amino acid intracellular RNA-binding protein (UniProt Q86XN8) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 59.9), characteristic of post-transcriptional regulators that function through RNA binding and protein-protein interactions with structured KH domains connected by flexible regions. The protein contains two type I K homology (KH) RNA-binding domains (IPR004087, residues 177-245 and 272-339; IPR004088, residues 181-241 and 275-335; IPR036612, residues 177-253 and 271-339; IPR047226, residues 271-342; IPR047228, residues 179-251; PF00013, residues 181-241 and 275-335; G3DSA:3.30.1370.10, residues 177-253 and 273-339) enabling RNA binding activity (GO:0003723), a RING-type zinc finger domain (IPR001841, residues 600-640; IPR013083, residues 580-651; PF13920, residues 598-645; G3DSA:3.30.40.10, residues 580-651; cd16723, residues 588-651) for protein interactions, and belongs to the RNA-binding protein MEX3 family (IPR047227, residues 1-651). MEX3D is located on chromosome 19p13.3 and enables mRNA 3'-UTR AU-rich region binding activity, predicted to be involved in mRNA destabilization (GO:0048255) and mRNA localization (GO:0051024) resulting in post-transcriptional regulation of gene expression, located in nucleus (GO:0005634) and perinuclear region of cytoplasm (GO:0048471).
