## Description
PLPP5 (Phospholipid Phosphatase 5, UniProt Q8NEB5) is a 264 amino acid vesicle-localized lipid phosphatase with minimal characterization (10 papers, genuine dark gene) that functions as a member of the PAP2 (phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2) family involved in phospholipid metabolism and emerging roles in cancer oncogenesis through gene amplification and fusion events. The protein contains a conserved PAP2 catalytic domain (IPR000326/PF01569/SM00014, residues 89-232/91-232/89-228) within the phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2/haloperoxidase superfamily (IPR036938/G3DSA:1.20.144.10/SSF48317, residues 40-246/18-251/40-246), placing it in the lipid phosphate phosphatase family (PTHR10165/IPR043216, residues 6-244) with phospholipid metabolic process function (GO:0006644). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, vesicular localization, blood cell group enrichment, with biological processes including lipid metabolism and tumor suppressor classification. ClinVar contains 59 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains at 8p11.23-11.1 (chr8:36650289-43776564, 37551624-52934472), 8p21.3-11.1, 8p23.3-11.21, and duplications, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential cancer, metabolic, or developmental phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 91.7) indicates very high confidence with a well-folded transmembrane PAP2 catalytic domain typical of lipid phosphatases.
