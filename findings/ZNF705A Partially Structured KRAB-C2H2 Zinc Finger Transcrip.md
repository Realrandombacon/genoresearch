ZNF705A (Zinc finger protein 705A, UniProt Q6ZN79) is a 300-amino acid intracellular protein that functions as a KRAB domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional repressor involved in sequence-specific DNA binding and epigenetic silencing. GO annotations confirm DNA-binding molecular function and transcription/transcription regulation biological processes. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves medium confidence (pLDDT 64.4), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered zinc finger domains connected by flexible linkers — this is characteristic of KRAB-ZFPs where rigid C2H2 fingers provide sequence-specific DNA recognition while disordered regions enable conformational adaptation.
