# ANKEF1: Pancreas-Enriched Calcium-Binding Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein Regulating Cilia Assembly and Motility via WDR38-TEKT3-DNAH10 Axonemal Interaction Network

## Description
ANKEF1 (Ankyrin Repeat and EF-Hand Domain Containing 1, also ANKRD5) is a 776 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9NU02) with a well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 81.0, high confidence), functioning as a calcium-dependent scaffold protein that regulates cilia assembly, axonemal structure, and ciliary motility through ankyrin repeat-mediated protein-protein interactions and EF-hand calcium sensing.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals ANKEF1 contains multiple canonical Ankyrin Repeat domains (IPR002110/IPR036770/PF12796/G3DSA:1.25.40.20) organized in three clusters: N-terminal repeats (residues 4-312, positions 18-110/113-216/217-282), central EF-hand domain (IPR002048/IPR011992/PS50222/G3DSA:1.10.238.10, residues 328-403/335-369), and C-terminal repeats (residues 488-706). The ankyrin repeats adopt helix-turn-helix folds that mediate protein-protein interactions, while the EF-hand domain confers calcium ion binding activity (GO:0005509). ANKEF1 belongs to the Ankyrin Repeat and EF-hand Domain-containing family (IPR052801, residues 1-772). The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 81.0) confirms stable domain architecture with ordered ankyrin solenoids flanking a central calcium-binding module.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places ANKEF1 within the cilia and flagella assembly network: strongest interactions with WDR38 (0.619, WD repeat-containing protein 38, intraflagular transport protein essential for ciliogenesis), CFAP99 (0.618, cilia and flagella associated protein 99, axonemal component), TSNAXIP1 (0.618, translin-associated factor X-interacting protein 1), TEKT3 (0.616, tectin-3, axonemal microtubule-binding protein), RSPH14 (0.608, radial spoke head protein 14, ciliary motility regulator), DNAH10 (0.606, dynein axonemal heavy chain 10, motor protein for ciliary beating), FBXO36 (0.602, F-box protein 36, ubiquitin ligase), CLBA1 (0.561, clathrin-binding box-containing protein 1), CCDC65 (0.443, coiled-coil domain-containing 65, dynein regulatory complex), and RSPH6A (0.410, radial spoke head protein 6A). The WDR38-TEKT3-DNAH10-RSPH14-RSPH6A-CCDC65 cluster represents the core axonemal machinery, with WDR38 controlling intraflagellar transport, TEKT3 stabilizing microtubule doublets, DNAH10 generating motile force, and RSPH proteins regulating radial spoke assembly for coordinated beating.\n\nHPA expression shows pancreas enrichment (13.2 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced specificity and detection in many tissues, indicating elevated expression in pancreatic cells alongside broader distribution. Subcellular localization includes nucleoli fibrillar center, plasma membrane, and cytosol, suggesting dynamic trafficking between compartments. The cilia interaction network suggests ANKEF1 localizes to basal bodies or ciliary shafts in ciliated cells. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein with calcium ion binding and protein binding functions.\n\nClinVar documents 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including one splice site variant (c.2173-22T>G) and multiple chromosome 20p12.3-12.1 copy number losses and gains. The CNV burden suggests dosage sensitivity, with haploinsufficiency potentially causing ciliopathy phenotypes including primary ciliary dyskinesia, situs inversus, or pancreatic defects through disrupted cilia assembly.\n\nConservation is evident across vertebrates, with ankyrin-EF-hand architecture conserved from fish to mammals, indicating ancient evolutionary conservation of calcium-regulated cilia functions.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: ANKEF1 functions as a calcium-dependent scaffold that coordinates axonemal protein assembly during ciliogenesis. The ankyrin repeat clusters bind multiple ciliary components simultaneously, while the central EF-hand domain senses cytoplasmic calcium levels to regulate complex assembly/disassembly. WDR38 interaction suggests ANKEF1 participates in intraflagellar transport, potentially loading cargo onto IFT trains. TEKT3 and DNAH10 partnerships indicate ANKEF1 stabilizes axonemal microtubule doublets and dynein motor attachment. RSPH14/RSPH6A interactions suggest roles in radial spoke assembly, critical for coordinated ciliary beating. CCDC65 linkage connects ANKEF1 to the dynein regulatory complex that controls motor activity. The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.0) confirms rigid ankyrin solenoids optimized for multi-protein binding. The splice variant (c.2173-22T>G) may disrupt C-terminal ankyrin repeats, impairing scaffold function. CNV-mediated haploinsufficiency causes defective cilia assembly, leading to immotile cilia syndrome phenotypes including chronic respiratory infections, male infertility (immotile sperm flagella), and situs inversus. Pancreatic enrichment suggests ANKEF1 regulates cilia in pancreatic ductal cells, potentially affecting fluid secretion. ANKEF1 represents a candidate gene for primary ciliary dyskinesia and ciliopathy disorders.
