## Description
ZNF474 (Gene ID: 133923, UniProt Q6S9Z5) encodes a 364 aa C2HC-type zinc finger protein mapping to chromosome 5q23.2 (122.1-122.2 Mbp). Multiple evidence lines support classification as a dark zinc finger transcription factor with emerging functional importance: (1) InterPro domains confirm atypical zinc finger architecture: 3× C2HC-type zinc fingers (PF13913, aa 96-116/164-188/221-243) plus classical C2H2-type finger (IPR013087, aa 166-194)—this mixed architecture suggests divergent DNA-binding specificity compared to canonical C2H2 ZNFs; (2) HPA data reveals reproductive tissue-enriched expression: fallopian tube (16.2 nTPM), testis (8.6 nTPM), choroid plexus (7.6 nTPM), with nucleoplasmic and nuclear membrane localization consistent with transcriptional regulation; (3) ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic copy number variants (large 5q deletions/duplications spanning 10-40 Mb), linking ZNF474 dosage to 5q chromosomal instability syndromes; (4) STRING interactions reveal co-expression with ZNF202 (score 0.661)—a known KRAB-ZFP repressor—and cilia-associated proteins CFAP161/CFAP57 (scores 0.501/0.492), suggesting potential roles in ciliogenesis or cilia-dependent signaling; (5) ZNHIT1 interaction (0.461) links to spliceosome assembly. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF474 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional regulator that recognizes degenerate DNA motifs via its atypical C2HC/C2H2 hybrid zinc fingers. The reproductive tissue expression pattern suggests roles in gametogenesis, fertilization, or early embryonic development. Interaction with cilia proteins (CFAP161/CFAP57) raises possibility of dual functions: transcriptional regulation in nucleus plus ciliary localization for Hedgehog or Wnt signaling modulation. The 5q23.2 locus is syntenic with developmental regulators—ZNF474 may co-regulate neighboring genes via chromatin looping. This represents a "dark" transcription factor with tissue-specific functions awaiting target gene identification.
