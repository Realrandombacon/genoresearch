## Description
ZBTB48 (P10074, TZAP/HKR3, 688 aa) is a dual-domain transcriptional regulator combining an N-terminal BTB/POZ dimerization domain (PF00651, residues 16-117) with C-terminal C2H2 zinc fingers (PF00096) for telomeric DNA recognition. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT: 63.8) reveals a partially structured architecture typical of chromatin-associated factors. HPA confirms nucleoplasmic localization with ubiquitous expression across tissues. STRING interactions validate telomeric function through TERF1 binding (0.669) and connect to DNA damage response via DCLRE1B/Artemis (0.545) and STK11IP (0.779). ClinVar documents 55 pathogenic copy number losses spanning 1p36.31-36.23, implicating ZBTB48 haploinsufficiency in 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes including intellectual disability and developmental delay. Only 23 literature citations confirm dark gene status despite critical functions: (1) telomere length homeostasis as TZAP, binding double-stranded telomeric repeats to limit excessive elongation; (2) epitranscriptomic regulation via FTO m6A/m6Am demethylase recruitment to target RNAs; (3) B-cell specific CIITA promoter activation for MHC class II expression. The convergence of telomere biology, RNA methylation, and adaptive immunity positions ZBTB48 as a multi-functional hub whose dosage sensitivity (55 CNV variants) suggests unrecognized roles in aging, cancer, and immunodeficiency.
