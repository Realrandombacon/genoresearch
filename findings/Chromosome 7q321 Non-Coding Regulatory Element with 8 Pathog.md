Functional hypothesis: LOC107986845 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 7q32.1 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a metabolic-immune enhancer coordinating LEP expression in adipocytes (energy homeostasis, appetite regulation) or as a chromatin boundary separating immunity genes (IRF5) from cytoskeleton genes (FLNC). The 8 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. The 7q32.1 deletion/duplication syndromes may present with obesity, autoimmune disorders, cardiomyopathy, cancer predisposition, and skeletal muscle weakness. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (8 CNVs) and LEP-IRF5-FLNC-SMO gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for metabolism-immunity-cytoskeleton- Hedgehog signaling coordination.
