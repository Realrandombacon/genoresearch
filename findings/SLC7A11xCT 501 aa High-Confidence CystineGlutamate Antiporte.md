## Description
SLC7A11 (Q9UPY5, 501 aa), also known as xCT, is the catalytic light chain subunit of system Xc(-), a sodium-independent, chloride-dependent antiporter that exchanges extracellular L-cystine for intracellular L-glutamate in a 1:1 stoichiometry. This transport activity is the rate-limiting step for cellular cysteine uptake, which is subsequently reduced to cysteine and utilized for the synthesis of glutathione (GSH), the cell's primary antioxidant. InterPro identifies a canonical amino acid/polyamine transporter I architecture (IPR002293, PF13520, G3DSA:1.20.1740.10, aa 38-485) belonging to the L-type amino acid transporter family (IPR004760, TIGR00911, aa 1-497), characterized by 12 transmembrane helices forming a specific substrate-binding pocket optimized for bulky anionic amino acids. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.6, residues 1-501), revealing a rigid, well-defined transmembrane bundle with a deeply buried substrate-binding site, consistent with its role as a highly selective gatekeeper for redox homeostasis. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in the brain (12.7 nTPM) and detection in many tissues; localized to vesicles and the plasma membrane; classified as a metabolic protein, FDA-approved drug target, and disease-related gene involved in amino acid transport. STRING reveals 10 extremely high-confidence interactions defining the redox and ferroptosis regulatory network: SLC3A2 (0.999, 4F2hc/CD98, the essential heavy chain partner that stabilizes xCT at the surface), CD44 (0.994, hyaluronan receptor linking transport to signaling), OTUB1 (0.885, deubiquitinase preventing xCT degradation), BECN1 (0.835, Beclin-1, autophagy protein that inhibits xCT), NFE2L2 (0.826, NRF2, master transcriptional regulator of antioxidant response), GPX4 (0.824, glutathione peroxidase 4, the key enzyme preventing ferroptosis), SLC3A1 (0.779), SLC7A6 (0.735), ATF4 (0.728, transcription factor inducing xCT under stress), and SLC7A5 (0.546). The perfect score with SLC3A2 confirms the obligate heterodimeric nature of system Xc(-). ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses (chr4:134952803-144712496, chr4:125166237-142987037) and gains affecting the 4q28 region; although no specific monogenic disease name is explicitly assigned in the summary, the variant burden and interaction with GPX4/NFE2L2 implicate it in neurodegenerative disorders, cancer susceptibility, and sensitivity to oxidative stress. Functional hypothesis: SLC7A11 functions as the critical influx pathway for cystine, maintaining intracellular cysteine pools necessary for GSH synthesis. By sustaining GSH levels, it enables GPX4 to reduce lipid hydroperoxides, thereby preventing the accumulation of toxic lipid ROS and blocking ferroptosis, an iron-dependent form of non-apoptotic cell death. Under conditions of erastin treatment or glutamate accumulation, xCT inhibition leads to rapid GSH depletion, GPX4 inactivation, and catastrophic lipid peroxidation. The interaction with BECN1 suggests a mechanism where autophagy induction can suppress xCT to sensitize cells to ferroptosis, while NRF2 activation upregulates xCT to confer resistance. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 502 aa, rat 502 aa) confirms essential physiological role.
