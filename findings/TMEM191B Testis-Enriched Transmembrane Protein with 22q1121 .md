The C-terminal transmembrane domain topology suggests TMEM191B may function as a membrane scaffold or receptor in spermatogenic cells. Testis-enriched transmembrane proteins often participate in blood-testis barrier maintenance, sperm maturation, or meiotic checkpoint control. The high burden of pathogenic CNVs indicates haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity, consistent with regulatory membrane protein function. TMEM191B paralog TMEM191C (302 aa, 22q11.21) suggests recent gene duplication, potentially enabling subfunctionalization in germ cell development. The combination of testis-specific expression, transmembrane topology, and 22q11.21 dosage sensitivity positions TMEM191B as a candidate regulator of spermatogenesis vulnerable to chromosomal rearrangement.
