# ZNF468: KRAB-C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcription Factor with Ubiquitous Expression and 19q13 Copy Number Variation Associations

**Quality Score:** 3.05/10  (E=5.8, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF468 (Gene ID: 90333, UniProt Q5VIY5) is a 522 amino acid protein located on chromosome 19q13.41 (NC_000019.10, positions 52838009-52857618, ~19.6kb region). InterPro domain analysis reveals a canonical KRAB (Krüppel-associated box) domain (IPR001909, IPR036051, PF01352, positions 8-80) at the N-terminus, characteristic of transcriptional repressors that recruit chromatin-modifying complexes. The protein contains multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, IPR036236, PF00096, PS00028) at positions 195-405, providing sequence-specific DNA-binding capacity. Notably, ZNF468 belongs to the zinc finger PRDM4/PRDM1/PRDM14-like family (IPR050331, positions 323-448), suggesting functional relationship to PRDM family transcriptional regulators involved in cell fate determination. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q5VIY5-F1) shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 64.9), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered zinc finger domains and flexible linker regions typical of transcription factors. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF468 as a transcription factor with low tissue specificity, detected in all surveyed tissues with cell type-enhanced expression patterns, consistent with housekeeping regulatory functions. STRING interactions reveal connections to other KRAB zinc finger proteins: ZNF320 (0.772, high confidence), ZNF816 (0.543), ZNF701 (0.542), ZNF28 (0.517), ZNF845 (0.448), ZNF600 (0.442), and critically TRIM28 (0.427, also known as KAP1), the universal co-repressor for KRAB domain proteins that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins. Additional partners include TFE3 (0.435, transcription factor E3) and KRTAP9-2 (0.444, keratin-associated protein). ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains affecting 19q13 region (multiple chr19:44-59Mb duplications including GRCh37/hg19 19q13.33-13.43 chr19:49625130-57647352x3, chr19:48463931-57095254x3), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Functional hypothesis: ZNF468 functions as a KRAB domain-containing transcriptional repressor that binds specific DNA sequences through its C2H2 zinc finger array and recruits TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor complex to silence target genes via H3K9me3 histone methylation and heterochromatin formation. As a PRDM-family related protein, ZNF468 may regulate developmental gene expression programs, potentially controlling cell differentiation or maintaining cellular identity. The ubiquitous expression suggests involvement in fundamental transcriptional regulation across cell types. Disease mechanism: Copy number gains at 19q13.33-13.43 increase ZNF468 dosage, leading to aberrant transcriptional repression of target genes. Overexpression may cause excessive silencing of developmentally important genes, contributing to neurodevelopmental or growth abnormalities associated with 19q duplication syndromes. Priority experiments include ChIP-seq to identify genome-wide DNA binding sites, identification of TRIM28-dependent target genes via RNA-seq in ZNF468 knockout/knockdown models, investigation of H3K9me3 changes at target loci, and phenotype characterization in patients with focal 19q13 rearrangements to establish genotype-phenotype correlations.
