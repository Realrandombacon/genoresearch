Despite its "coiled-coil" nomenclature, the protein contains a DUF814-related domain (PTHR13049) with RNA-binding capability. ClinVar contains 75 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 8 copy number alterations affecting the 8p21.1 locus.
