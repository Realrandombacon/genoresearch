## Description
RABL2B (RAB, Member of RAS Oncogene Family Like 2B) is a 228 aa small GTPase functioning as a critical regulator of ciliogenesis by recruiting the intraflagellar transport (IFT) machinery to the basal body. InterPro analysis confirms a canonical small GTPase architecture with a Ras-like domain (IPR001806/PF00071, aa 23-176) containing the P-loop nucleotide-binding motif (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, aa 11-222) and specific Rab-like 2 family signatures (IPR041835, cd04124). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 79.5) consistent with a folded G-domain capable of conformational switching between GTP/GDP states. STRING interactions reveal a highly specific functional network centered on ciliary assembly: CEP19 (0.928), the obligate guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that activates RABL2B; SHANK3 (0.951), linking ciliary function to synaptic scaffolding and neurodevelopment; APPL2 (0.901), an endosomal adaptor; and centrosomal proteins FGFR1OP (0.773) and CEP350 (0.702). HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced patterns with maximal expression in the epididymis (84.8 nTPM), consistent with the high demand for motile cilia/flagella in sperm, and subcellular localization to vesicles, centrosomes, and basal bodies. ClinVar contains 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses on chromosome 22q13.33 encompassing the Phelan-McDermid syndrome critical region, suggesting that RABL2B haploinsufficiency contributes to the ciliary, neurological, and reproductive phenotypes observed in this syndrome. Literature (29 papers) establishes RABL2B as essential for binding the IFT-B complex at the ciliary base via the CEP19-RABL2B holoenzyme, initiating anterograde transport required for axoneme extension.
