## Description
ZNF605 encodes a 641 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q86T29) that localizes to nucleoplasm and vesicles with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (HPA). The protein architecture features an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 6-77) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 169-252+ with at least 3 fingers identified). This canonical KRAB-ZNF architecture enables sequence-specific DNA recognition via the zinc finger array while the KRAB domain recruits heterochromatin-forming machinery. STRING interactions reveal strong partnerships with other KRAB-ZNF proteins ZNF234 (0.794) and ZNF419 (0.646), plus the universal KRAB corepressor TRIM28/KAP1 (0.518)—the scaffold protein that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 for H3K9me3 deposition. Additional interactions with ZFP30 (0.495), ZFP14 (0.462), and ZNF432 (0.401) suggest ZNF605 operates within a network of related silencing factors. ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and deletions at 12q24.33, indicating dosage sensitivity where altered ZNF605 expression disrupts epigenetic regulation. Conservation is strong across primates (Papio hamadryas Q86T29 ortholog 640 aa, Cebus imitator 641 aa, Mandrillus leucophaeus 640 aa). AlphaFold structure unavailable (API error). Mechanistically, ZNF605 likely binds specific GC-rich promoter/enhancer motifs via its zinc finger array, then recruits TRIM28 which oligomerizes and recruits SETDB1 for H3K9me3 methylation, establishing facultative heterochromatin. This represses target genes involved in cell differentiation or stress responses. Disease-associated CNVs suggest ZNF605 haploinsufficiency or overexpression causes misregulation of developmental or tumor suppressor genes.
