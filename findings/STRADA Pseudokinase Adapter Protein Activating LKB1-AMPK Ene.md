## Description
STRADA (STE20-related kinase adapter protein alpha, also known as MO25alpha or LYK5, Q7RTN6) encodes a 431 aa intracellular pseudokinase functioning as an essential allosteric activator of the LKB1-STK11 tumor suppressor kinase that regulates AMPK energy sensing, cell polarity, metabolism, and growth control critical for development and cancer suppression. Structural architecture reveals a catalytically inactive protein kinase fold repurposed for scaffolding: a pseudokinase domain (cd08227, residues 70-396) adopting a canonical kinase-like structure lacking key catalytic residues required for phosphotransfer, a Protein kinase domain superfamily (IPR000719/IPR011009/PF00069/PS50011/G3DSA:1.10.510.10/G3DSA:3.30.200.20/SSF56112, residues 69-379/57-401/69-379/69-379/153-289+346-431/64-152/57-304+351-401) providing ATP-binding capability without catalytic activity, belonging to the STRADA family (IPR047173/PTHR48014, residues 62-395/62-395) with GO terms for protein serine/threonine kinase activator activity (GO:0043539); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 80.0, high confidence) confirms a well-folded pseudokinase structure with organized lobes consistent with stable scaffold requiring precise conformation for partner binding and allosteric activation. STRING interactions define the complete LKB1-AMPK signaling axis: paralogous adapter CAB39L/MO25beta (0.996, highest confidence) forming heterodimeric complex, AMPK catalytic subunits PRKAA1 (0.915) and PRKAA2 (0.914) as downstream effectors phosphorylated by LKB1-STRADA complex, ER translocon components SEC61G (0.828), SEC63 (0.824), and SEC62 (0.812) linking to protein translocation and ER stress response, paralogous STRADB/MO25gamma (0.820) suggesting functional redundancy, golgin GOLGA2 (0.820) connecting to Golgi apparatus organization, master kinase STK11/LKB1 (0.696) as the primary binding partner activated by STRADA, and calcium-binding protein CAB39/MO25alpha homolog (0.475). HPA localizes STRADA to nucleoplasm and cytosol with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue and cancer specificity indicating housekeeping metabolic function, low immune cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity; classified as disease-related gene, enzyme, human disease-related gene, and potential drug target involved in cell cycle regulation. ClinVar documents 47 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice acceptor variant c.95-2A>C disrupting exon 2 splicing, missense variant c.359A>G (p.His120Arg) in the pseudokinase core potentially affecting CAB39/LKB1 binding, 5 UTR variant c.37-39G>T, intronic variant c.1143+40G>A, frameshift duplication c.891dup (p.Cys298fs) truncating the C-terminal region, splice donor variant c.457+1G>T abolishing exon 5 splicing, frameshift deletions c.526del (p.Leu176fs) and c.54del (p.Lys18fs) causing premature termination, in-frame deletion c.828_853del (p.Val277fs), and chromosome 17q copy number gain at 17q23.2-24.1 (chr17:58773496-62757149x3); these variants are associated with developmental disorders including polyglucosan body neuropathy and cancer susceptibility. Literature establishes STRADA as essential LKB1 activator where it binds STK11 through its pseudokinase domain inducing conformational change that enhances LKB1 catalytic activity ~100-fold, forms trimeric LKB1-STRADA-CAB39 complex that phosphorylates and activates 14 AMPK-related kinases including AMPK itself controlling cellular energy homeostasis, regulates epithelial cell polarity through PAR-1/MARK kinase activation controlling microtubule dynamics and asymmetric cell division, suppresses tumor growth via mTORC1 inhibition through AMPK-mediated TSC2 activation, modulates ER stress response through SEC62/SEC63 interactions affecting protein translocation efficiency, and functions in neuronal development where STRADA mutations cause fatal polyglucosan body neuropathy; pseudokinase architecture represents evolutionary adaptation converting catalytic scaffold into regulatory module; high-confidence STRING network with CAB39L/PRKAA1/STK11 indicates STRADA operates within conserved energy-sensing metabolon; ubiquitous expression correlates with fundamental metabolic role; conservation from mouse (Q3UUJ4, 431 aa) and rat (Q7TNZ6, 393 aa) confirms essential physiological function; drug target classification suggests therapeutic potential in metabolic diseases and cancer.
