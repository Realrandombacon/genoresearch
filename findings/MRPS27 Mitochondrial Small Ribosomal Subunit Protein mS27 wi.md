## Description
MRPS27 encodes a 414-amino acid small ribosomal subunit protein mS27 (UniProt Q92552), also known as mitochondrial ribosomal protein S27, a well-folded mitochondrial ribosomal protein functioning in mitochondrial translation through pentatricopeptide repeat (PPR) and tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain architecture. The protein contains a pentatricopeptide repeat domain (IPR002885, positions 140-175; PS51375 PPR profile, positions 140-175) characteristic of RNA-binding proteins involved in organellar gene expression, plus extensive TPR-like helical domain superfamily architecture (IPR011990, positions 36-204; G3DSA:1.25.40.10 TPR domain, positions 36-204) for protein-protein interactions, classified within the mitochondrial 28S ribosomal protein S27 family (IPR019266, positions 1-412; IPR034913 mS27/PTCD2, positions 1-412; PF10037 Mitochondrial 28S ribosomal protein S27, positions 1-412; PTHR21393, positions 1-412). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.2, residues 1-414) indicating stable folding with well-defined TPR/PPR helical repeats suitable for RNA and protein binding.
