## Description
Proteasome-associated protein 5 (PSMD5) is a core subunit of the 26S proteasome with a unique dual localization in the nucleoplasm and centrosome, mediated by its Armadillo-like helical (IPR011989) and Leucine-rich repeat variant (G3DSA) domains. Unlike canonical proteasome subunits, PSMD5 exhibits low tissue specificity but high subcellular specificity, suggesting a role in nuclear protein turnover and centrosome maturation. The presence of 24 pathogenic variants without a cataloged disease suggests a novel phenotype related to neurodegeneration or cancer progression via disrupted protein homeostasis. AlphaFold predicts a highly stable structure (pLDDT 93.8), supporting its function as a robust scaffold in the cytosolic/nuclear interface. STRING interactions confirm its role in the proteasome core, while the lack of specific disease associations highlights its 'dark gene' status.
