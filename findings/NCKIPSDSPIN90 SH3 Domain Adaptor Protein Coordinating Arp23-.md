## Description
NCKIPSD encodes a 722 amino acid NCK-interacting protein with SH3 domain (UniProt Q9NZQ3), also known as SPIN90 (SH3 protein interacting with Nck, 90 kDa), that functions as a critical adaptor protein coordinating actin cytoskeleton dynamics through Arp2/3 complex-mediated nucleation. The protein contains an N-terminal SH3 domain (IPR001452/PF00018 at positions 1-58; cd11849 at 1-54; SM00326 at 1-57) enabling protein-protein interactions, and a C-terminal SPIN90/Ldb17 leucine-rich domain (IPR018556/PF09431 at positions 548-682) mediating membrane association and complex assembly.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals NCKIPSD forms an exceptionally tight network with actin cytoskeleton regulators: NCK1 (score 0.995—primary binding partner), ARPC5 (0.987), ARPC4 (0.984), ARPC2 (0.976), ACTR2 (0.971), WAS/WASP (0.919), ARPC1B (0.910), BAIAP2/IRSp53 (0.907), ACTR3/Arp3 (0.697), and ARPC3 (0.640). This positions NCKIPSD at the nexus of NCK1-mediated signaling to the Arp2/3 complex, coordinating actin filament nucleation for cell migration, endocytosis, and growth cone dynamics.\n\nHPA expression data shows NCKIPSD is tissue-enhanced with highest expression in parathyroid gland (114.5 nTPM), detected in all tissues with low cancer specificity. Subcellular localization is dual: nucleoplasm and plasma membrane—consistent with roles in both transcriptional regulation and cytoskeletal remodeling at the cell cortex. Classified as a disease-related proto-oncogene.\n\nClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (3p26.3-q29 duplications spanning megabases), copy number losses (3p21.31 deletions), splice site mutations (c.172-2A>C), and deletions. Despite this variant catalog, ClinVar lists 0 associated diseases—a critical knowledge gap suggesting NCKIPSD dysfunction may cause undiagnosed neurodevelopmental disorders (given actin's role in neuronal morphogenesis) or immunodeficiency syndromes (given WASP pathway connections).\n\nAlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9NZQ3-F1) yields a global pLDDT score of 75.2 (high confidence) across all 722 residues, indicating a well-folded stable structure. The 2019 literature reference 'Nckipsd Coordinates Arp2/3 and Formin Nucleation of Actin Filaments in the Cell Cortex' provides mechanistic validation but remains uncited, highlighting genuine knowledge gaps.\n\nWith only 17 publications (vs. 282 for ABHD5, 588 for TNFSF10), NCKIPSD represents authentic dark biology. Key unknowns include: specific disease phenotypes from loss-of-function mutations, precise mechanism of dual nucleoplasmic/membrane localization, role in cancer progression as a proto-oncogene, and therapeutic potential for actin-related disorders.
