## Description
LOC129390278 (Gene ID: 129390278) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 11p13.1 (34,329,387-34,329,587 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak1251 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic copy number variants involving 11p13-15 region - three deletions (14.4 Mb, 8.9 Mb, 15.6 Mb) and one duplication (2.6 Mb); (4) No HPA expression expected (non-coding); (5) No STRING interactions or InterPro domains (non-coding). The 11p13-15 region is clinically critical - contains WT1 (Wilms tumor suppressor, Denys-Drash syndrome, Frasier syndrome), PAX8 (paired box 8, thyroid development), KCNJ1 (potassium channel), and multiple imprinted growth regulators. 11p13 deletions cause WAGR syndrome (Wilms tumor, Aniridia, Genitourinary anomalies, Retardation) - contiguous gene deletion including WT1 and PAX2. 11p15 rearrangements cause Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome (overgrowth) and Silver-Russell syndrome (growth restriction). Critical 11p region contains: WT1 (transcription factor, podocyte development), PAX2/PAX8 (paired box transcription factors, kidney/thyroid development), HRAS (GTPase, Costello syndrome), and TH (tyrosine hydroxylase, catecholamine synthesis). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390278 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or developmental contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, HDAC-containing complexes, DNA methyltransferases) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF, ZNF proteins, WT1 itself) to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in erythroid/myeloid lineage. 11p13 is gene-dense with tumor suppressors (WT1), developmental transcription factors (PAX2/8), and imprinted growth regulators. Silencer disruption via CNVs alters TAD architecture and long-range enhancer-promoter contacts - affecting WT1 dosage (Wilms tumor predisposition, genitourinary anomalies), PAX8 expression (thyroid dysgenesis), and chromatin domain organization. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements in cancer susceptibility and developmental disorder loci.
