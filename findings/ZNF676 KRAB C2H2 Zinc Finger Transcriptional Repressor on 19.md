## Description
ZNF676 (Gene ID: 163223, UniProt Q8N7Q3) is a 588 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger protein mapping to chromosome 19p12 (22,260,303-22,179,088 bp on GRCh38). This establishes facultative heterochromatin at target gene promoters/enhancers.
