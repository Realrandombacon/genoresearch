## Description

The protein contains a conserved SPATA22 domain (IPR033536, residues 1-362) and shows medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 60.6), suggesting partially ordered regions with potential intrinsically disordered segments that may facilitate dynamic protein-protein interactions during meiosis.

Expression profiling reveals extreme testis specificity (HPA: 215.0 nTPM in testis, tissue-enriched pattern), consistent with its role in gametogenesis. Clinical significance is established with 222 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants, associated with premature ovarian failure (POF25) and spermatogenic defects (SPGF96). The variant burden suggests haploinsufficiency or dominant-negative mechanisms disrupt meiotic checkpoint function. Conservation data and GO annotations (meiotic DNA repair synthesis GO:0000711, homologous chromosome pairing GO:0007129, gamete generation GO:0007276) support mechanistic role in coordinating SPO11-induced double-strand breaks with homology-directed repair during meiosis I.
