## Description
MYO1A (Myosin IA, Q9UBC5), also known as unconventional myosin-Ia or brush border myosin I, encodes a 1043 aa single-headed motor protein functioning as a critical actin-based transporter and tension sensor in the intestinal epithelium, specifically localized to the microvilli of the brush border where it links the actin core bundle to the plasma membrane, drives membrane trafficking, and maintains structural integrity for nutrient absorption, while also exhibiting nucleoplasmic localization suggesting potential roles in nuclear actin dynamics or transcriptional regulation.
