# CCDC91: Golgi-Lysosome Transport Adaptor - Coiled-Coil Scaffolding Protein in Vesicular Trafficking

**Date:** 2026-03-20T09:13:18.119880

**Quality Score:** 3.1/10  (E=5.1, D=1.1) [WEAK]

## Description
CCDC91 encodes a 441 amino acid coiled-coil domain protein that functions as a scaffolding adaptor in Golgi-to-lysosome vesicular transport pathways. The protein is dominated by an extended coiled-coil domain (IPR034592, PTHR35072, residues 2-441) that mediates homo- and hetero-oligomerization, creating a structural platform for vesicle coat assembly. AlphaFold predicts a confident helical bundle structure (pLDDT 74.6/100), consistent with coiled-coil-mediated protein dimerization.

Subcellular localization (HPA) spans nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, acrosome, equatorial segment, and flagellar centriole—reflecting roles in multiple membrane trafficking contexts. The exceptionally strong STRING interaction with GGA1 (score 0.989, near-maximal confidence) reveals functional partnership with Golgi-localized gamma-ear-containing ARF-binding protein 1, a clathrin adaptor that sorts cargo from trans-Golgi network to endosomes/lysosomes. Additional interactions include GGA2 (0.489), ERGIC2 (0.505, ER-Golgi intermediate compartment), and C10orf88 (0.649), placing CCDC91 within the early secretory pathway machinery.

ClinVar documents 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 12p copy number gains, though no specific monogenic disease is yet assigned. The coiled-coil architecture (GO:0005515 protein binding) likely enables CCDC91 to bridge GGA adaptors with cargo receptors or SNARE complexes. Mechanistically, CCDC91 may stabilize GGA1-clathrin lattices during vesicle budding, or recruit specific cargo (glycosylation enzymes, lysosomal hydrolases) to forming transport carriers. Centriolar/flagellar localization suggests additional roles in ciliogenesis or sperm acrosome biogenesis—defects could impair lysosomal enzyme sorting, causing storage phenotypes, or disrupt flagellar assembly, affecting male fertility.
