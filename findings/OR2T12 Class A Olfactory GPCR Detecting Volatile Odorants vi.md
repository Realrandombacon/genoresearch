Conservation across mammals indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental chemosensory mechanisms critical for survival behaviors. OR2T12 likely functions as a volatile odorant detector, using its 7TM binding pocket to recognize specific molecular features of odorant compounds while coupling to Gα_olf to initiate the cAMP signaling cascade that converts chemical detection into electrical signals transmitted to the olfactory bulb — mechanisms essential for smell perception, food selection, predator avoidance, and social communication.
