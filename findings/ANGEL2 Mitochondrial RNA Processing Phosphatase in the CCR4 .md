# ANGEL2: Mitochondrial RNA Processing Phosphatase in the CCR4 Deadenylase Family

## Description
ANGEL2 (Angel Homolog 2) is a partially characterized RNA-processing enzyme with only 17 publications, recently identified (2020-2025) as a CCR4-family deadenylase with 2',3'-cyclic phosphatase activity required for non-canonical mitochondrial RNA processing and TP53 translation modulation in cancer. This 544-amino acid protein (UniProt Q5VTE6) contains an endonuclease/exonuclease/phosphatase domain (PF03372/IPR005135, positions 170-486), CCR4/nocturin mRNA turnover domain (IPR050410, positions 93-542), and angel homolog 2 N-terminal domain (PF19339/IPR045816, positions 23-129). AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 74.4), indicating stable catalytic fold.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal ANGEL2 operates within RNA deadenylation and processing networks: TOE1 (0.836, target of EGR1 phosphatase involved in RNA 3'-end processing), CNOT8 (0.651) and CNOT7 (0.520, CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex subunits), PARN (0.550, poly(A)-specific ribonuclease), PAPOLG (0.543, poly(A) polymerase), plus TATDN3 (0.633, TAT DNA nuclease) and PRMT8 (0.633, protein arginine methyltransferase). This positions ANGEL2 at the intersection of cytoplasmic mRNA deadenylation and mitochondrial RNA maturation.\n\nHPA shows ubiquitous expression with dual localization to nucleoplasm/nuclear bodies AND mitochondria - unusual for a CCR4-family member. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number variations on 1q21-44 and missense mutations (p.Asn444Ser), though specific diseases remain uncharacterized. Conservation across mouse (544 aa), zebrafish (569 aa), and bovine (544 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ANGEL2 is a bifunctional RNA-processing enzyme that coordinates nuclear mRNA deadenylation with mitochondrial RNA maturation. Unlike canonical CCR4 deadenylases that primarily regulate cytoplasmic mRNA stability, ANGEL2's mitochondrial localization and 2',3'-cyclic phosphatase activity suggest it processes tRNA-derived fragments or resolves stalled mitochondrial transcription complexes. The TOE1 interaction (highest confidence partner) links ANGEL2 to RNA 3'-end quality control. Recent evidence showing ANGEL2 modulates TP53 translation suggests it may regulate stress-response transcripts during DNA damage. Pathogenic variants may disrupt mitochondrial RNA processing, potentially contributing to metabolic disorders or cancers with defective p53 responses.
