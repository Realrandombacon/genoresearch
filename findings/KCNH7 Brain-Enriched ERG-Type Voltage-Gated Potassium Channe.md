# KCNH7: Brain-Enriched ERG-Type Voltage-Gated Potassium Channel Modulating Neuronal Excitability and Bipolar Disorder Susceptibility via PAS Domain-Mediated Redox Sensing

## Description
KCNH7 (Potassium Voltage-Gated Channel Subfamily H Member 7, also known as Kv11.3 or ERG3, UniProt Q9NS40) is a 1196 aa voltage-gated potassium channel belonging to the EAG/ERG family that functions in regulating neuronal excitability and cardiac repolarization. The molecular architecture includes an N-terminal PAS domain (IPR000014, positions 37-138) for redox sensing and channel regulation, six transmembrane segments (S1-S6) with voltage-sensing S4 helix, pore loop between S5-S6, C-terminal cyclic nucleotide-binding domain (IPR000595, positions 745-863, CAP_ED superfamily cd00038, positions 745-856), and ERG-specific signature motifs (IPR003967). Literature (40 papers) establishes KCNH7 roles in: (1) KCNH7 variants associated with epilepsy in children identified by whole exome sequencing (2024, 2 citations), (2) KCNH7 p.Arg394His polymorphism associated with bipolar spectrum disorder in Amish families (2014, 56 citations), (3) KCNH7 polymorphisms influencing individual responses to risperidone treatment in schizophrenia (2019, 14 citations), (4) IFIH1-GCA-KCNH7 locus influencing multiple sclerosis risk (2008, 61 citations). HPA confirms brain-enriched expression (2.4 nTPM in brain, 1.4 nTPM in retina) with tissue-enhanced specificity and cell type-enhanced pattern, consistent with neuronal function. STRING interactions reveal channel complex network: GCA (0.890) - GC-rich sequence DNA-binding protein at same genomic locus; IFIH1 (0.705) - interferon-induced helicase at same locus; KCNAB2 (0.630), KCNAB1 (0.612), KCNAB3 (0.430) - potassium channel beta subunits that modulate gating; KCNH2 (0.624) - related ERG1 channel; PRL (0.613) - prolactin; SLC4A10 (0.575) - sodium bicarbonate transporter; KCNG2 (0.506) - potassium channel subfamily G; IGSF21 (0.538) - immunoglobulin superfamily member. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic variants including multiple copy number losses and gains at 2q24.2-24.3 encompassing KCNH7, though specific single-nucleotide variant disease associations remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 66.6) indicating partially ordered transmembrane domains with disordered cytoplasmic regions, typical of voltage-gated channels with regulatory domains. Conservation: mouse Kcnh7 (Q9ER47) is 1195 aa, rat Kcnh7 (O54852) is 1195 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: KCNH7 forms homotetrameric or heterotetrameric channels with other ERG family members (KCNH2/KCNH6) that generate slowly activating, slowly inactivating potassium currents regulating action potential duration and neuronal firing patterns. The PAS domain confers redox sensitivity and oxygen sensing capability, while the cyclic nucleotide-binding domain may mediate cAMP/cGMP modulation. Beta subunits (KCNAB1/2/3) associate with KCNH7 to modulate trafficking and gating kinetics. Pathogenic variants alter channel gating, surface expression, or drug binding, leading to neuronal hyperexcitability (epilepsy), mood dysregulation (bipolar disorder), or altered antipsychotic response. The 2q24.2-24.3 CNVs disrupt KCNH7 dosage, potentially contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes. KCNH7 represents an understudied ERG channel subtype with emerging roles in neuropsychiatric disorders.
