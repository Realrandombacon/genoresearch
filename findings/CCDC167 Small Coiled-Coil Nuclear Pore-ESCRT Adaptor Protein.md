## Description
CCDC167 (alias C6orf129, HSPC265, coiled-coil domain containing 167) encodes a 97 amino acid small well-folded coiled-coil scaffold protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 87.8) containing coiled-coil domain-containing protein 167 family domain (IPR028194, aa 1-97), PF15188 coiled-coil domain (aa 11-93), and PTHR31759 family membership (aa 1-97). HPA shows ubiquitous expression with detected in all tissues distribution, low tissue specificity, predicted membrane protein class, cytosol subcellular location, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all being copy number alterations (6p21.31-21.1 deletion, 6p21.31-21.2 deletion, 6p22.1-q14.1 gain, 6p25.3-q27 gain×2, 6p25.3-12.3 gain) associated with chromosomal instability and dosage sensitivity phenotypes.
