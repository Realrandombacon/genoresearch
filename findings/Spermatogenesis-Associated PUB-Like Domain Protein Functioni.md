## Description
SPATA2L (UniProt Q8IUW3) is a 424 amino acid spermatogenesis-associated protein functioning as a potential regulator of ubiquitin-mediated signaling pathways. The protein architecture features a canonical PUB-like domain (IPR048839/PF21388, positions 70-195) characteristic of the spermatogenesis-associated protein 2 family, embedded within a larger homologous superfamily structure (G3DSA:1.20.58.2190, positions 3-207) and belonging to the PTHR15326 family (positions 1-254). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 72.2) spanning residues 1-424, indicating a well-folded protein with stable tertiary structure suitable for specific protein-protein interactions. HPA classifies SPATA2L as a predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity but detection in many tissues, showing enhanced expression in specific cell types and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, mitochondria, and cytosol, suggesting multifunctional roles potentially linking nuclear transcription regulation with mitochondrial dynamics or apoptosis. STRING interaction network reveals exceptionally strong associations (scores >0.9) with components of the Linear Ubiquitin Chain Assembly Complex (LUBAC) and its regulators: CYLD (0.955, cylindromatosis deubiquitinase), SHARPIN (0.925, LUBAC component), RNF31 (0.922, HOIP E3 ligase), SPATA2 (0.921, close paralog and LUBAC regulator), RBCK1 (0.912, HOIL-1 LUBAC component), plus VPS9D1 (0.797), ABHD8 (0.514), DBNDD1 (0.489), DEF8 (0.449), and CENPBD1 (0.435). This interaction profile strongly suggests SPATA2L functions analogously to SPATA2 as a scaffold or regulator recruiting CYLD deubiquitinase to LUBAC complexes, thereby modulating linear ubiquitination critical for NF-kappaB activation, TNF receptor signaling, and cell death decisions. ClinVar contains 42 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants spanning chromosome 16q regions (e.g., chr16:46432879-90294753 gain, chr16:82865402-90163542 gain, chr16:71641395-90161959 gain, chr16:46503968-90155062 gain, chr16:80386595-90163348 gain; deletions at chr16:89730828-89877489, chr16:89730828-89858975, chr16:89712474-89866066, chr16:89620873-89881041), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though no specific monogenic disease is yet attributed solely to SPATA2L. The mechanistic model positions SPATA2L as a cytosolic/nuclear scaffold that binds LUBAC components via its PUB-like domain, recruits CYLD to edit linear ubiquitin chains on signaling intermediates (RIPK1, NEMO), and fine-tunes inflammatory responses and cell survival, with mitochondrial localization hinting at additional roles in regulating apoptosis or mitophagy through ubiquitin-dependent mechanisms.
