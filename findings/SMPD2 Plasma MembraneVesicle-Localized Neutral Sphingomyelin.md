## Description
SMPD2 (Sphingomyelin Phosphodiesterase 2, also known as Neutral Sphingomyelinase 1 or nSMase1, UniProt O60906) is a 423 amino acid plasma membrane/vesicle/cell junction-localized enzyme with moderate characterization (40 papers) that functions as a neutral sphingomyelinase hydrolyzing sphingomyelin to generate ceramide, a critical lipid second messenger regulating cell stress responses, extracellular vesicle biogenesis, and cancer progression. The protein contains an endonuclease/exonuclease/phosphatase domain (IPR005135/IPR036691/PF03372/G3DSA:3.60.10.10/SSF56219, residues 6-279/14-272) conferring catalytic activity (GO:0003824), belongs to the sphingomyelinase C/SMPD2-like family (IPR038772/PTHR16320, residues 7-243) with sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase activity (GO:0004767), placing it in the sphingolipid metabolism machinery.
