## Description
PIK3C2A (Phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate 3-kinase catalytic subunit type 2 alpha, also known as Class II PI3K-alpha, O00443) encodes a 1686 aa multidomain lipid kinase functioning as a critical regulator of endosomal trafficking, membrane dynamics, and phosphoinositide signaling that catalyzes phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol (PI) to phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate [PI(3)P] and phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate [PI(4)P] to phosphatidylinositol 3,4-bisphosphate [PI(3,4)P2], thereby controlling clathrin-mediated endocytosis, receptor tyrosine kinase trafficking, autophagy initiation, and cytokinesis through spatially restricted phosphoinositide production.
