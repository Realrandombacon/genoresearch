## Description
TLL2 (Tolloid-Like Protein 2, also known as BMP-1/Tolloid-Related Metalloprotease 2, UniProt Q9Y6L7) is a 1015 amino acid secreted metalloprotease with limited characterization (34 papers) that functions as an extracellular matrix (ECM) processing enzyme and growth factor activator, cleaving procollagen C-propeptides and activating BMP/GDF family members through its multi-domain catalytic architecture. The protein contains three CUB domains (IPR000859/cd00041/G3DSA:2.60.120.290 residues 351-463/464-576/620-732) for protein-protein interactions and substrate recognition, two EGF-like calcium-binding domains (IPR000742/IPR001881/cd00054/G3DSA:2.10.25.10 residues 576-617/732-772) for structural stability and calcium-dependent conformational changes, and a Peptidase M12A catalytic domain (IPR001506/cd04281/G3DSA:3.40.390.10 residues 149-349/150-346/150-349, GO:0004222 metalloendopeptidase activity, GO:0006508 proteolysis) containing the zinc-binding motif characteristic of astacin-family metalloproteases.
