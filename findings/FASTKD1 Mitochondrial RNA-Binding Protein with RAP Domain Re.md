## Description

Despite its name suggesting kinase activity, FASTKD1 functions primarily as a post-transcriptional regulator of mitochondrial RNA (mtRNA) homeostasis, utilizing a C-terminal RAP (RNA-associated protein) domain for substrate recognition.\n\nSTRUCTURAL FEATURES: AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT 85.1) across the full 847 residues. Its interaction with pseudouridine synthases suggests it may recruit modification enzymes to stabilize target transcripts.
