## Description
ANKRD36B (UniProt Q8N2N9) encodes a 1353 amino acid cytoplasmic protein characterized by extensive ankyrin repeat architecture and a C-terminal coiled-coil domain. InterPro analysis reveals 8+ ankyrin repeat signatures (IPR002110, IPR036770, PF12796) spanning residues 13-278, forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. The C-terminal region (936-1291) contains a CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (IPR039497, PF14915) predicted to mediate homodimerization or heterodimerization with paralogs. STRING interaction network identifies high-confidence binding to ANKRD36 (score 0.983), ANKRD39 (0.622), and ANKRD36C (0.519), suggesting formation of ankyrin repeat protein complexes. Additional partners include SNAPC2 (0.606), a snRNA-activating protein complex subunit, and cell cycle regulators CDKN2C/CDKN2D (0.48-0.50). ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains at 2q11.1-11.2 encompassing the ANKRD36B locus, indicating dosage sensitivity. The ankyrin repeat scaffold likely functions as a molecular adaptor that bridges protein complexes involved in cell cycle regulation and transcriptional control. The coiled-coil domain enables oligomerization, potentially creating multivalent scaffolding platforms. Disease-associated CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity mechanisms, consistent with scaffold proteins that require precise stoichiometry for complex assembly.
