# ZNF160: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Chromatin Modulation Activity

**Date:** 2026-03-23T10:42:22.847801

**Quality Score:** 2.15/10  (E=4.3, D=0.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF160 encodes an 818 aa Kruppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9HCG1) functioning as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, aa 8-79) that recruits corepressors, followed by three C2H2 zinc finger motifs (PF00096, IPR013087, aa 257-335) for DNA binding. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 68.9) suggests partially ordered conformation with structured zinc finger regions and flexible linkers. HPA localizes ZNF160 to nuclear speckles and cytosol with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity). STRING interactions reveal functional associations with TRIM28 (0.564) - the canonical KRAB cofactor that recruits heterochromatin machinery, ASXL2 (0.578) - a Polycomb-group chromatin regulator, and XRCC3 (0.514) - involved in DNA repair. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 19q13.33-13.43 copy number gains of uncertain significance, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The KRAB-TRIM28 interaction indicates ZNF160 likely silences target genes through H3K9me3 heterochromatin deposition. ASXL2 association suggests potential crosstalk with Polycomb repression (H3K27me3). Ubiquitous expression implies housekeeping regulatory functions, possibly maintaining cell identity by repressing lineage-inappropriate genes. Copy number variants may cause haploinsufficiency or dominant-negative effects disrupting chromatin homeostasis.
