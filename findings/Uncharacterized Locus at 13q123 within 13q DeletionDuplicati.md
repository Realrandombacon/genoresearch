## Description
LOC102723345 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 13q12.3 (NC_000013.11:29647692-29655477) spanning approximately 7.8 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - among the highest burdens observed for any 13q locus in this survey. Variants include massive 13q copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 13q12.11-34 (chr13:19837395-114327173, ~94.5 Mb; chr13:19671934-114340331, ~94.7 Mb; chr13:19833130-114327106, ~94.5 Mb), 13q11-34 (chr13:18862146-114342258, ~95.5 Mb; chr13:18456040-114340285, ~95.9 Mb; chr13:18445862-114327173, ~95.9 Mb), 13q11-13.2 (chr13:18862146-33577351, ~14.7 Mb), 13q12.3-13.3 (chr13:29321454-36995348, ~7.7 Mb; chr13:29073320-36556014, ~7.5 Mb), 13q12.11-14.11 (chr13:19671934-40914767, ~21.2 Mb), and deletions spanning 13q11-34 (chr13:18445862-114327173, ~95.9 Mb) and 13q12.3-13.3 (chr13:29073320-36556014, ~7.5 Mb). The 13q12.3 region is the critical locus for 13q deletion syndrome and 13q duplication syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive tumor suppressors and developmental genes including RB1 (retinoblastoma tumor suppressor, hereditary retinoblastoma), BRCA2 (breast cancer susceptibility 2, homologous recombination DNA repair), FLT1 (fms-like tyrosine kinase 1, VEGF receptor, preeclampsia), DACH1 (dachshund homolog 1, developmental transcription factor), HSPC159, and EDNRB (endothelin receptor B, Hirschsprung disease). LOC102723345 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer or silencer controlling 13q tumor suppressor gene expression, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 13q chromatin architecture, (3) a chromatin boundary element within the 13q12.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a replication origin or fragile site affecting 13q genomic stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (19 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) RB1 haploinsufficiency leading to retinoblastoma predisposition, (2) BRCA2 DNA repair capacity affecting cancer susceptibility, (3) 13q chromatin architecture affecting multiple tumor suppressor expression, or (4) neurodevelopmental programs via DACH1/EDNRB, leading to 13q deletion syndrome (retinoblastoma, intellectual disability, growth retardation, congenital anomalies, Hirschsprung disease) or 13q duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, cancer predisposition).
