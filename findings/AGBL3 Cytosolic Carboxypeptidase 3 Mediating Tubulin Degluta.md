## Description
AGBL3 (AGBL Carboxypeptidase 3, also known as Cytosolic Carboxypeptidase 3 or CCP3, UniProt Q8NEM8) is a 1001 aa cytosolic metalloprotease belonging to the cytosolic carboxypeptidase family (IPR050821/PTHR12756, positions 20-820) that functions in tubulin deglutamylation, complement regulation, and autoinflammatory disease pathogenesis. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal cytosolic carboxypeptidase domain (IPR040626/PF18027, positions 171-296) for substrate recognition, followed by a catalytic M14 peptidase domain (IPR000834/PF00246/PS52035/cd06907/G3DSA:3.40.630.10/SSF53187, positions 299-570/319-570/322-539/291-577) containing zinc-binding motifs for metallocarboxypeptidase activity. Literature (12 papers) establishes AGBL3 roles in: (1) novel AGBL3 variant potentially associated with hypocomplementemic urticarial vasculitis syndrome (HUVS) examining clinical outcomes and therapeutic responses (2025), (2) favorable response to rituximab in patient with hypocomplementemic urticarial vasculitis associated with homozygous frameshift AGBL3 variant (2019), (3) new autoinflammatory phenotype manifesting as hypocomplementemic urticarial vasculitis associated with homozygous AGBL3 variant (2018). HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in epididymis (7.4 nTPM), detected in many tissues, cytosol localization, and classification as enzyme with carboxypeptidase, hydrolase, metalloprotease, and protease functions. STRING interactions reveal complement system network: CD46 (0.724) - membrane cofactor protein for complement regulation; CD55 (0.692) - decay-accelerating factor preventing complement activation; CXADR (0.647) - coxsackievirus and adenovirus receptor; CD59 (0.592) - complement regulatory protein preventing MAC formation; C4B (0.569) and C4A (0.547) - complement component 4 for classical pathway; CCPG1 (0.509) - cell cycle progression 1; AGBL2 (0.502) - cytosolic carboxypeptidase 2 paralog; C3 (0.466) - complement component 3 central to all pathways; PRTN3 (0.425) - proteinase 3 neutrophil enzyme. ClinVar contains 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses at 7q31.32-36.1 (chr7:122190535-149944340)x1, 7q31.31-33 (chr7:120582003-137699953)x1, 7q32.3-36.1 (chr7:131779213-149042734)x1, 7q32.1-34 (chr7:127295698-139524358), 7q31.33-33 (chr7:124103982-134693590), 7q33-35 (chr7:133848099-145814115)x1, 7q32.3-36.3 (chr7:131414604-159126310)x1, deletion NC_000007.13:g.(?_130781014)_(150301047_?)del, copy number gain at 7q33-36.3 (chr7:133851002-159119707)x3, and complex variants, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation beyond HUVS. AlphaFold shows medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 63.8) indicating partially structured protein with ordered catalytic domains and potentially disordered regulatory regions typical of cytosolic carboxypeptidases. Conservation: mouse Agbl3 (Q8CDP0) is 1006 aa, bovine AGBL3 (E1B9D8) is 1003 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: AGBL3 functions as a cytosolic metallo-carboxypeptidase that removes glutamate side chains from polyglutamylated tubulin (deglutamylation) regulating microtubule dynamics, and modulates complement system activity through proteolytic processing of complement regulators. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) M14 catalytic domain binds zinc ion coordinating water molecule for peptide bond hydrolysis, (2) N-terminal domain recognizes polyglutamylated tubulin substrates removing γ-linked glutamate branches, (3) AGBL3-CD46-CD55-CD59 interaction network regulates complement cascade preventing excessive activation, (4) AGBL3 deficiency causes tubulin hyperglutamylation disrupting ciliary function and immune cell motility, (5) Loss-of-function variants trigger autoinflammatory phenotype through complement dysregulation and neutrophil activation. In HUVS, homozygous frameshift AGBL3 variants cause complete enzyme loss, leading to hypocomplementemia, urticarial vasculitis, and systemic inflammation responsive to rituximab B-cell depletion therapy. Pathogenic CNVs at 7q31-36 disrupt AGBL3 dosage, potentially causing complement-mediated tissue damage. AGBL3 represents an understudied cytosolic carboxypeptidase linking tubulin post-translational modification to autoinflammatory vasculitis and complement regulation.
