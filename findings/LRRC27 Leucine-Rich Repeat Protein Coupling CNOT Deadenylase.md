# LRRC27: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein Coupling CNOT Deadenylase Complex to Testis-Specific Post-Transcriptional Regulation

## Description
LRRC27 (Leucine-Rich Repeat Containing 27) encodes a 530-amino acid intracellular protein featuring multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains that mediate protein-protein interactions. InterPro analysis reveals canonical LRR motifs (IPR001611, positions 67-126; IPR003591 typical subtype at positions 66-89, 90-113, 114-136) within a PF13855 LRR domain, embedded in a Ribonuclease Inhibitor-like superfamily fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, positions 31-170). The protein is classified within the PTHR45752 family of LRR and signaling effector domain-containing proteins (positions 49-333).\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals LRRC27 associates with the CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex, a major mRNA decay machinery. High-confidence partners include DDX6 (DEAD-box helicase 6, score 0.701), CNOT9 (RCD-1 homolog, score 0.692), CNOT2 (score 0.686), CNOT3 (score 0.654), CNOT7/CNOT8 catalytic subunits (scores 0.648), CNOT1 scaffold (score 0.602), plus transcription elongation factors CDC73 (Parafibromin, 0.647), POLR2A (RNA polymerase II, 0.634), and PAF1 (0.619). This interaction profile suggests LRRC27 bridges transcriptional elongation with mRNA deadenylation.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas demonstrates testis-specific enrichment (72.0 nTPM), with subcellular localization in nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus. This expression pattern implicates LRRC27 in spermatogenesis and post-meiotic mRNA regulation. ClinVar documents 99 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 10q26.3 copy number losses (multiple deletions spanning 10q26.13-26.3, 10q26.2-26.3, 10q26.11-26.3 regions) and intronic SNVs. These CNVs cause 10q26 deletion syndrome featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal anomalies, and male infertility.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LRRC27 functions as a testis-specific adaptor protein recruiting the CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex to specific mRNA targets during spermatogenesis. The LRR domains mediate sequence-specific RNA-binding protein interactions (potentially via DDX6 helicase activity), while the CNOT complex interface enables deadenylation of developmentally regulated transcripts. During the mitosis-to-meiosis transition, LRRC27 ensures timely degradation of somatic cell cycle mRNAs and stabilization of meiotic transcripts. The 10q26.3 CNV burden explains male infertility phenotypes in deletion carriers—LRRC27 haploinsufficiency disrupts post-transcriptional control of spermatogenic genes, causing meiotic arrest or aberrant sperm maturation. Nuclear localization suggests co-transcriptional mRNA tagging, while Golgi association may indicate secretory pathway roles in Sertoli cell support.
