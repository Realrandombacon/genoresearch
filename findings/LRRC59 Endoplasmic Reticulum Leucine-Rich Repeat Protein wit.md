This gene encodes a well-folded intracellular protein with extensive LRR architecture suggestive of protein-protein interaction scaffolding functions. The LRR domain occupies the N-terminal two-thirds (~220 residues), forming a curved solenoid structure ideal for protein-protein interactions, while the C-terminal region (residues 230-307, ~77 residues) lacks defined domains and may mediate membrane association or partner binding. GO annotations include protein binding activity (GO:0005515).
