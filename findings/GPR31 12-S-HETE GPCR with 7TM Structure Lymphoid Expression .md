# GPR31: 12-S-HETE GPCR with 7TM Structure, Lymphoid Expression, and 54 ClinVar Variants

## Description
GPR31 encodes a 319 amino acid Class A G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) that functions as the high-affinity receptor for 12(S)-hydroxy-5,8,10,14-eicosatetraenoic acid (12-S-HETE), an arachidonate metabolite produced by 12-lipoxygenase. The protein contains canonical 7-transmembrane helical domains (PF00001, IPR000276, IPR017452) spanning residues 31-282, with the hydroxycarboxylic acid receptor family signature (IPR051893) covering residues 4-313. AlphaFold prediction yields high confidence (pLDDT 83.2), indicating a well-folded 7TM bundle typical of rhodopsin-family GPCRs. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with lymphoid tissue at 1.3 nTPM, suggesting immune cell localization. STRING interactions reveal connections to NODAL signaling (NODAL 0.767, CFC1 0.721, ACVR2B 0.588), transcription factor ZIC3 (0.841), and related GPCRs (GPR3 0.496), plus SRC kinase (0.517) and angiotensin pathway components (AGT 0.507, AGTR2 0.499). ClinVar contains 54 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses spanning 6q25-27, though no specific monogenic disease is yet assigned. Conservation extends to mouse ortholog (319 aa, high sequence identity). Functional hypothesis: GPR31 mediates pro-inflammatory lipid signaling in lymphoid tissues by coupling 12-S-HETE binding to Gi/o protein activation, lowering cAMP and triggering downstream effects on cell migration, adhesion, and cytokine production. The NODAL/CFC1 interaction network suggests potential crosstalk with TGF-beta superfamily signaling during development or immune modulation. Elevated 12-S-HETE levels in cancer and inflammation may drive GPR31-dependent metastasis or chronic inflammatory phenotypes, explaining the ClinVar structural variant burden.
