# FAM222A: Intrinsically Disordered Scaffold Protein Linking Homeobox Transcription Factors to Golgi-Mediated Processes

**Date:** 2026-03-19T11:27:15.528698

**Quality Score:** 2.3/10  (E=3.6, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
FAM222A (family with sequence similarity 222 member A, alias C12orf34) encodes a 452 amino acid intrinsically disordered protein (AlphaFold pLDDT=49.2) that functions as a molecular scaffold connecting homeobox transcription factors to Golgi-associated cellular machinery. The protein contains two conserved FAM222 domains (IPR029340, PF15258) spanning residues 34-155 and 322-427. STRING interaction analysis reveals high-confidence partnerships with homeobox transcription regulators: MEIS2 (0.785), MAB21L1 (0.784), PBX3 (0.782), PBX1 (0.780), and MEIS1 (0.680), suggesting FAM222A serves as a co-regulatory scaffold in transcriptional complexes controlling cell fate and development. HPA data shows brain-enriched expression (50.4 nTPM) with Golgi apparatus and midbody ring localization, indicating potential roles in neuronal differentiation and cell division. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses on chromosome 12q23-24, implicating FAM222A dosage sensitivity in disease. The disordered structure (49.2 pLDDT) combined with scaffold domain architecture suggests FAM222A functions through multivalent protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic catalysis, potentially bridging nuclear transcription factors (MEIS/PBX) with Golgi-mediated protein trafficking or cell cycle machinery.
