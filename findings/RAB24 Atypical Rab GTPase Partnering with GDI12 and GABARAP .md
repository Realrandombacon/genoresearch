## Description
RAB24 (Q969Q5) is a 203-aa small GTPase of the Rab subfamily with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 89.1), indicating a well-folded canonical Rab GTPase fold. InterPro analysis reveals Rab GTPase family 24 domain (IPR041828: positions 8-201), small GTPase superfamily domains (IPR001806/PF00071: positions 2-203, 9-171), and P-loop NTPase hydrolase fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.300: positions 4-179), characteristic of nucleotide-dependent molecular switches. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to cytosol, with biological processes including autophagy, protein transport, and vesicular trafficking. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence binding to GDP dissociation inhibitors GDI2 (0.765) and GDI1 (0.763), geranylgeranyltransferase RABGGTA (0.744), plus medium-confidence interactions with autophagy proteins GABARAP (0.678), SMCR8 (0.647), ATG16L2 (0.629), and endolysosomal factors CHM (0.644), VPS16 (0.573), RBSN (0.554). Literature (82 papers) identifies RAB24 as an atypical Rab GTPase involved in autophagosome maturation, endolysosomal degradation, and tumor progression in hepatocellular carcinoma and clear cell renal cell carcinoma. Recent studies link RAB24 to APOE4-to-APOE2 replacement adaptation and Aβ/cholesterol dysregulation in Alzheimer's models. ClinVar contains 62 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 5q35.2-35.3 and splice variants (c.265+5G>A), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. The high-confidence structure and GDI interactions suggest RAB24 cycles between GDP/GTP states like canonical Rabs, while GABARAP/ATG16L2 interactions position it as a regulatory node connecting autophagy machinery to endolysosomal degradation pathways.
