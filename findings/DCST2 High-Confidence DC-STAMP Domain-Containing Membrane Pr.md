## Description
DCST2 (DC-STAMP domain-containing protein 2, UniProt Q5T1A1) is a 773-amino acid integral membrane protein that functions as a cell surface receptor and E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase involved in membrane fusion events and protein ubiquitination during fertilization and potentially immune cell fusion. InterPro analysis reveals defining DCST2 architecture: DC-STAMP-like protein domain (IPR012858/PF07782, aa 354-544) with membrane localization (GO:0016020) characteristic of dendritic cell-specific transmembrane protein family members that mediate cell-cell fusion, Cell Surface Receptor and E3 Ligase Protein family (IPR051856/PTHR21041, aa 4-658) providing dual functionality as both receptor and ubiquitin ligase, E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase DCST1-like C-terminal domain (IPR058842/PF26037, aa 598-644) containing a RING-type zinc finger motif essential for E3 ligase activity enabling transfer of ubiquitin from E2 conjugating enzymes to substrate proteins, Dcst2 domain (PF26039, aa 96-280) representing a DCST2-specific region of unknown function potentially involved in protein-protein interactions — this multi-domain organization places DCST2 within the family of membrane-associated E3 ligases that regulate protein stability and signaling through ubiquitination. GO annotations confirm membrane molecular function. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 73.1), indicating a well-folded protein with stable tertiary structure — this structural confidence reflects conserved residues in both the DC-STAMP domain and RING-type E3 ligase domain enabling reliable modeling of functional interfaces for future mechanistic studies. The SPACA6/IZUMO1/TMEM95/CLGN interactions definitively place DCST2 within the sperm-egg recognition and fusion machinery, suggesting roles in acrosome reaction regulation through ubiquitination of sperm surface proteins. Conservation across vertebrates indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental fertilization mechanisms. DCST2 likely functions as a dual-purpose membrane protein that uses its DC-STAMP domain for cell-cell recognition/adhesion during sperm-egg binding while its C-terminal RING domain ubiquitinates target proteins (potentially IZUMO1, SPACA6, or other acrosomal proteins) to regulate their stability, trafficking, or signaling activity — the DCST1 paralog interaction suggesting functional redundancy or heterodimerization, with the high structural confidence (pLDDT 73.1) enabling future structure-based investigation of substrate recognition and rational design of fertility modulators.
