Recent literature (2025) highlights TLK1 as a therapeutic target: small molecule inhibitors overcome TMZ resistance in glioblastoma by disrupting chromatin repair; targeting the TLK1-MK5 axis suppresses prostate cancer metastasis; Circ-MBOAT2/miR-664b-3p regulates TLK1 in NSCLC. Loss-of-function would cause genomic instability due to defective chromatin assembly; gain-of-function could drive cancer progression by enhancing DNA repair in tumor cells.
