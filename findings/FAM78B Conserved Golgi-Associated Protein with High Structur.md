## Description
FAM78B encodes a 261 amino acid protein (UniProt Q5VT40) belonging to the Protein FAM78 family (IPR029638, PTHR31655) with no characterized functional domains. AlphaFold predicts a highly confident 3D structure (pLDDT 94.8), indicating a well-folded globular protein despite lacking known catalytic or binding motifs. HPA localization places FAM78B in the Golgi apparatus and vesicles, suggesting a role in vesicular trafficking or Golgi maintenance. The protein shows a single medium-confidence STRING interaction with C12orf43 (score 0.550), another uncharacterized protein, hinting at a potential heterodimeric complex or shared pathway. Mouse ortholog Q8BQN5 maintains identical length (261 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains spanning 1q23-25, implicating FAM78B dosage sensitivity in disease though no specific monogenic phenotype is yet defined. The combination of Golgi localization, high structural confidence, and CNV pathogenicity suggests FAM78B may function as a structural scaffold protein in Golgi vesicle formation or cargo sorting, where precise stoichiometry is critical. Tissue expression is "not detected" in bulk RNA-seq but "group enriched" at single-cell level, indicating cell-type-restricted expression that may explain why loss-of-function has not been linked to a specific disease despite clear CNV pathogenicity.
