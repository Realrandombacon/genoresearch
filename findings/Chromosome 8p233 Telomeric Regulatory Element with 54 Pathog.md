## Description
LOC286083 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 8p23.3 (NC_000008.11, positions 1302606-1296033, ~6.5 kb region) with exceptional clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000282734 but no expression data was available. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 54 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (the highest count observed in this study), all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 8p23.3-21.2 region (variants include 3384213, 2579290, 545353, 161038, 161036, 161035, 160883, 155691, 155587, 155440). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, monosomy) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy/tetrasomy) ranging from focal alterations (~1-7 Mb) to whole-arm changes (23+ Mb). The 8p23.3 chromosomal location is clinically notable as this telomeric region contains the beta-defensin gene cluster (DEFB4A, DEFB103A) and is adjacent to the hematopoietic transcription factor GATA1 (X-linked, but 8p rearrangements can affect globin loci) and the cytokine receptor CSF3R (colony stimulating factor 3 receptor, severe congenital neutropenia, chronic neutrophilic leukemia). The region is also associated with 8p23.1 duplication/deletion syndrome (characterized by congenital heart defects, developmental delay, and immune dysregulation). Functional hypothesis: LOC286083 likely represents a telomeric cis-regulatory element or sub-telomeric chromatin boundary controlling the copy-number-variable beta-defensin cluster and neighboring hematopoietic genes. The extraordinary burden of 54 pathogenic CNVs indicates extreme structural instability and dosage sensitivity in this sub-telomeric region. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency or overexpression of beta-defensins (leading to innate immune defects, susceptibility to infections, or inflammatory disorders), dysregulation of CSF3R (affecting neutrophil proliferation and leukemia risk), or altered expression of cardiac developmental genes (GATA4, TBX5) located nearby in the 8p23.1 critical region. The absence of protein-coding features combined with record-breaking ClinVar evidence (54 variants) and immune/cardiac disease association suggests this locus functions as a master regulator of innate immunity and hematopoiesis through long-range control of the dynamic defensin cluster.
