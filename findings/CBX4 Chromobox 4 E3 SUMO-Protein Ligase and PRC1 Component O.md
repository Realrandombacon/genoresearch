## Description
CBX4 (Chromobox 4) is a 560 amino acid dual-function protein that serves as both a core component of canonical Polycomb Repressive Complex 1 (cPRC1) for epigenetic gene silencing and an E3 SUMO-protein ligase mediating post-translational modification of transcription factors, serving as a critical regulator of cellular senescence, hypoxia response, and tumor progression. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic bipartite architecture including N-terminal chromo domain (IPR000953, IPR023780, PF00385, PS00598, PS50013, cd18645, residues 8-69) with chromatin organization modifier function for methylated histone recognition and heterochromatin targeting, chromo-like domain superfamily (IPR016197, G3DSA:2.40.50.40, residues 3-65) providing β-barrel fold for ligand binding, chromo domain subgroup (IPR017984, PRO00504, residues 8-48) with conserved aromatic cage residues (IPR023779, residues 28-48) for methyl-lysine recognition, C-terminal CBX family motif (IPR033773, PF17218, residues 534-559) for PRC1 assembly, and E3 SUMO-protein ligase family signature (IPR043531, residues 1-560) with negative regulation of DNA-templated transcription capability (GO:0045892). AlphaFold structure (AF-O00257-F1) predicts medium confidence (pLDDT 54.0) across the 560 residue sequence, indicating a partially structured protein with ordered N-terminal chromo domain suitable for histone binding and extensive intrinsically disordered regions enabling flexible SUMO ligase activity and protein-protein interactions. HPA data classifies CBX4 as a predicted intracellular protein with bone marrow-enriched expression (123.0 nTPM, tissue enriched), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type-enhanced distribution, and nucleoplasmic/nuclear bodies localization reflecting chromatin association and PRC1 complex assembly. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally tight coupling to the complete cPRC1 machinery: BMI1 (0.999), RNF2/RING1B (0.999), RING1/RING1A (0.999) as core catalytic components; PHC1 (0.998), PHC3 (0.995), PHC2 (0.973) as Polyhomeotic homologs for complex stabilization; CBX2 (0.998), CBX8 (0.989) as alternative chromobox paralogs defining PRC1 variant specificity; PCGF2 (0.997) as Polycomb group RING finger protein; and COMMD3-BMI1 fusion (0.994). ClinVar lists 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 17q24.3-25.3, 17q25.1-25.3, 17q24.1-25.3, 17p13.3-q25.3 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 63-81 Mb), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in developmental disorder or cancer susceptibility. Literature (243 papers) establishes CBX4 as a multifunctional epigenetic and SUMOylation regulator: CBX4 counteracts cellular senescence to desensitize gastric cancer cells to chemotherapy by inducing YAP1 SUMOylation linking epigenetics to drug resistance; CBX4 acetoacetylation serves as inhibitory mechanism of HIF-1α activity revealing metabolic control of hypoxia response; specific cPRC1 form containing CBX4 is co-opted to mediate oncogenic gene repression in diffuse midline glioma with H3K27M mutation; CBX4 suppresses CD8+ T cell exhaustion in tumor microenvironment through SUMOylation-dependent mechanisms; CBX4-mediated SUMOylation regulates DNA damage response and genome stability; and comprehensive reviews detail CBX4 roles in senescence and associated diseases through molecular pathways coordinating PRC1 silencing and SUMO modification. As a well-studied dual-function regulator with defined roles in epigenetics and post-translational modification, CBX4 represents a critical node connecting chromatin remodeling to hypoxia adaptation, cellular aging, and cancer therapeutic resistance through coordinated gene silencing and transcription factor SUMOylation.
