# CCDC138: Coiled-Coil Centrosomal Scaffold Linking Microtubule Polyglutamylation to Ciliogenesis and Meiotic Spindle Assembly

**Date:** 2026-03-18T19:21:08.547235

**Quality Score:** 4.75/10  (E=7.4, D=2.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
CCDC138 encodes a 665-amino acid coiled-coil protein (UniProt Q96M89) localized to the nucleoplasm and cytosol. The protein belongs to the CCDC138 family (InterPro IPR038798, PTHR34523, residues 1-664) featuring a central coiled-coil domain (IPR048751, PF21037, residues 263-321) and a C-terminal globular domain (IPR048750, PF21035, residues 362-651) with a G3DSA:1.20.5.340 fold (residues 241-318). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 72.4, high confidence), indicating stable tertiary architecture suitable for scaffolding functions.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CCDC138 functions as a centrosomal and ciliary scaffold that coordinates microtubule polyglutamylation with ciliogenesis and meiotic spindle assembly. Its interaction network is dominated by centrosomal proteins (CCDC14, CCDC61, CCDC22, OFD1, FOPNL) and tubulin-modifying enzymes (TTLL5, TTLL10), positioning it at the hub of microtubule organization.

CENTROSOMAL SCAFFOLDING: High-confidence interactions with CCDC14 (0.594), CCDC61 (0.591), and CCDC22 (0.559) link CCDC138 to the centrosome and basal body. CCDC61 is essential for centriole cohesion and cilia formation; CCDC22 regulates ciliary trafficking. CCDC138 likely stabilizes the pericentriolar material (PCM) lattice, ensuring proper anchoring of gamma-tubulin ring complexes for microtubule nucleation.

MICROTUBULE POLYGLUTAMYLATION: Strong interactions with TTLL5 (0.576) and TTLL10 (0.569), tubulin tyrosine ligase-like enzymes responsible for initiating and elongating polyglutamate chains on alpha/beta-tubulin, suggest CCDC138 recruits these modifiers to specific microtubule subsets. Polyglutamylation regulates microtubule stability and motor protein binding (kinesin/dynein). CCDC138 may act as a "code reader" or scaffold that concentrates TTLL activity at the basal body or meiotic spindle, fine-tuning microtubule dynamics for ciliary assembly or chromosome segregation.

CILIAGENESIS & MEIOSIS: Interactions with OFD1 (0.548, oral-facial-digital syndrome 1), FOPNL (0.533, FGFR1 oncogene partner), and SSX2IP (0.558, synovial sarcoma X breakpoint 2 interacting protein) connect to ciliogenesis and spindle pole organization. OFD1 controls distal centriole formation; FOPNL anchors microtubules to the centrosome. The testis-enriched expression (10.2 nTPM, HPA tissue enhanced) supports a role in spermatocyte meiosis, where robust spindle assembly and transient cilia-like structures (e.g., manchette) are critical.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 25 pathogenic variants including 2q12.2-21.2 copy number losses (106755586-134302739), 2q12.2-13 deletions (107029681-113127751), and splice region variants (c.736-5_736-3del). Haploinsufficiency disrupts centrosomal integrity, leading to ciliopathies (e.g., Joubert syndrome spectrum), microcephaly, or male infertility due to meiotic arrest.

CONSERVATION: The coiled-coil (263-321) and C-terminal domain (362-651) are conserved across vertebrates, indicating preserved structural roles in microtubule regulation.
