# PELI3: Pellino E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Regulating Innate Immunity via IRAK1/TRAF6 Complex Assembly, NF-κB/MAPK Activation, and Emerging Roles in Cancer Progression, Fibrosis through M2 Macrophage Transition, and Gefitinib Resistance via m6A-Dependent Autophagy

## Description
PELI3 (Pellino E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase Family Member 3, also known as PELI3, UniProt Q8N2H9) is a 469-amino acid scaffold protein and E3 ubiquitin ligase that functions as a critical intermediate signaling component in the innate immune response pathway transmitting signals from Toll-like receptors (TLRs) and interleukin-1 receptors (IL-1R) to downstream NF-κB and MAPK activation through IRAK1/TRAF6 complex assembly and K63-linked polyubiquitination. The protein architecture features a complete Pellino family domain (IPR006800 aa 1-469, PIRSF038886, PTHR12098 E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase pellino-related aa 31-469) with GO terms for ubiquitin protein ligase activity (GO:0061630), protein polyubiquitination (GO:0000209), and regulation of Toll signaling pathway (GO:0008592), an N-terminal FHA domain (IPR048334, PF04710 aa 76-320) mediating phospho-dependent protein-protein interactions with phosphorylated IRAK kinases, and a C-terminal RING domain (IPR048335, PF20723 aa 326-469) providing E3 ubiquitin ligase catalytic activity for substrate ubiquitination. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 81.0), indicating a well-folded stable protein with ordered FHA and RING domains optimal for scaffold assembly and ubiquitin transfer. STRING interaction network places PELI3 at the center of TLR/IL-1R signaling with very high-confidence associations to: IRAK1 (0.997), interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase 1 primary substrate; TRAF6 (0.980), TNF receptor-associated factor 6 E3 ligase partner; IRAK4 (0.908), IRAK family kinase upstream activator; MAP3K7 (0.849), TAK1 kinase activating NF-κB/MAPK pathways; UBE2N (0.803), ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 N providing ubiquitin for transfer; UBE2V1 (0.769), ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 variant 1; TAB1 (0.706), TAK1-binding protein 1 adaptor; MAP3K14 (0.664), NIK kinase in non-canonical NF-κB pathway; IKBKG (0.650), IKKγ/NEMO regulatory subunit of IKK complex; and RIPK2 (0.607), receptor-interacting serine/threonine-protein kinase 2. HPA expression profiles demonstrate tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in brain (38.3 nTPM), detected in all tissues indicating broad immune surveillance function, classification as enzyme/plasma protein/predicted intracellular protein involved in immunity/innate immunity/Ubl conjugation pathway with transferase molecular function, subcellular localization to nucleoplasm/nucleoli/vesicles suggesting nuclear signaling roles beyond cytoplasmic TLR signaling, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced expression patterns. ClinVar records document 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 11q12.1-13.3 (56-69Mb), 11q12.2-13.5 (59-76Mb), 11q13.1-13.2 (65-67Mb), 11p15.5-q25 (0.2-134Mb), duplications spanning 11q (64-70Mb), and deletions at 11q13.1 (64-66Mb) suggesting dosage sensitivity though no specific monogenic syndrome has been definitively assigned potentially due to redundancy with PELI1/PELI2 paralogs. Literature analysis (35 papers) reveals emerging multi-context functionality: FTO m6A demethylase promotes gefitinib-resistance in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) by enhancing PELI3 expression and autophagy through mRNA stability; Mebendazole inhibits PELI3-mediated ubiquitination of TRADD triggering proapoptotic autophagy in NSCLC; PELI3 regulates M2 macrophage-to-myofibroblast transition (MMT) and fibrosis in wound healing via IRF4 transcription factor control; PELI3-mediated epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) correlates with radiation sensitivity in NSCLC; PELI3 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression through NF-κB pathway activation; PELI3 deficiency protects against sepsis-induced acute kidney injury by suppressing NF-κB signaling; PELI3 facilitates osteoclast differentiation via TRAF6-NFATc1 axis. We hypothesize that PELI3 functions through a multi-mechanism ubiquitin signaling model: (1) The FHA domain binds phosphorylated IRAK1/IRAK4 following TLR/IL-1R activation recruiting them to the signaling complex; (2) RING domain catalyzes K63-linked polyubiquitination of IRAK1 and TRAF6 creating docking sites for TAB1-TAK1 (MAP3K7) and IKK complexes; (3) UBE2N/UBE2V1 heterodimer serves as E2 conjugating enzyme accepting ubiquitin from E1 and transferring to PELI3 RING for substrate modification; (4) Nuclear localization suggests PELI3 may ubiquitinate nuclear substrates (transcription factors, chromatin regulators) independent of TLR signaling; (5) In cancer, PELI3 stabilizes oncogenic signaling through constitutive NF-κB activation promoting survival and chemoresistance; (6) M2 macrophage transition regulation via IRF4 suggests PELI3 controls fibrosis through immune cell plasticity; (7) m6A-dependent expression by FTO indicates post-transcriptional regulation layer controlling PELI3 abundance in response to cellular stress; (8) TRADD ubiquitination links PELI3 to TNF receptor signaling and apoptosis regulation; (9) High brain expression suggests specialized roles in neuroinflammation or neuronal survival signaling; (10) Functional redundancy with PELI1/PELI2 may explain lack of monogenic disease despite pathogenic variants. Therapeutic targeting of PELI3-IRAK1 interface or RING domain catalytic activity could treat inflammatory diseases, while PELI3 inhibition may overcome gefitinib resistance in NSCLC and sensitize tumors to radiation therapy.
