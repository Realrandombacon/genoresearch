## Description
BBX (BBX High Mobility Group Box Domain Containing, also known as HBP2, ARTC1, HSPC339, MDS001, UniProt Q8WY36) is a 941-amino acid nuclear transcription factor functioning through an HMG box DNA-binding domain that recognizes specific DNA sequences and induces structural bending to facilitate transcriptional regulation of cell cycle progression, developmental processes, and circadian rhythm genes; recruits histone deacetylase complexes through SIN3A/SIN3B interactions enabling chromatin compaction and transcriptional repression; interacts with CLOCK suggesting circadian clock modulation; and exhibits dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations at 3q13.12 contribute to genomic disorder phenotypes including developmental delay and intellectual disability.
