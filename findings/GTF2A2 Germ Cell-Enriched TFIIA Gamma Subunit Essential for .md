## Description
GTF2A2 (General Transcription Factor IIA Subunit 2, also known as TFIIAγ) encodes a small 109-amino acid highly structured protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 93.1) that functions as the gamma subunit of transcription factor IIA, a critical component of the RNA polymerase II pre-initiation complex. This PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED gene has 12 literature references, with a seminal 2001 paper (46 citations) demonstrating GTF2A2 is encoded by a germ cell-specific gene whose expression is up-regulated with other general transcription factors during spermatogenesis in mouse, suggesting specialized roles in male gametogenesis beyond general transcription. InterPro analysis reveals canonical TFIIA gamma subunit architecture: N-terminal alpha-helical domain (IPR009083/G3DSA:1.10.287.190, residues 2-53) and C-terminal beta-barrel domain (IPR009088/G3DSA:2.30.18.10, residues 52-109), with the complete TFIIA gamma subunit family signature (IPR003194, residues 1-105). Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasmic localization, and classification as an essential protein, consistent with its role in basal transcription machinery. STRING interactions reveal exclusive association with core transcription apparatus: GTF2A1 (0.999 highest confidence, TFIIA alpha subunit), GTF2A1L (0.998, TFIIA alpha-like), GTF2H3 (0.994, TFIIH subunit), TAF9 (0.980, TBP-associated factor), TBP (0.974, TATA-binding protein), GTF2B (0.858, TFIIB), GTF2E1/2 (0.644/0.558, TFIIE), GTF2H2 (0.632, TFIIH), GTF2F1 (0.544, TFIIF), placing GTF2A2 at the heart of the Pol II pre-initiation complex. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number variations spanning 15q21.3-22.31 region including deletions (57544843-64324372, 54020810-62086530, 58088503-62221756, 54713558-62769295) and duplications (48589845-63543438, 48000433-60747551, 22770422-102429112), though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. Functional hypothesis: GTF2A2 serves as a germ cell-enriched variant of the TFIIA gamma subunit that stabilizes TBP-DNA interactions and facilitates Pol II recruitment during spermatogenesis, potentially enabling specialized transcriptional programs required for meiosis and sperm differentiation. Mechanistic proposal: The N-terminal helical domain mediates protein-protein interactions with GTF2A1/GTF2A1L and TBP, while the C-terminal beta-barrel domain contacts DNA and stabilizes the TBP-DNA complex; during spermatogenesis, GTF2A2 upregulation may replace canonical TFIIA subunits to enable transcription of germ cell-specific genes; copy number alterations disrupt stoichiometric balance of transcription machinery, potentially contributing to 15q genomic disorder phenotypes including male infertility or developmental abnormalities.
