## Description
TMEM192 encodes a 271 amino acid type II transmembrane protein (UniProt Q8IY95) belonging to the TMEM192 family (InterPro IPR029399, PF14802, residues 16-266). AlphaFold structure prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 76.8), indicating a well-folded stable transmembrane structure. HPA subcellular localization confirms presence in lysosomes, endosomes, and nucleoplasm, with ubiquitous expression across all tissues. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally strong associations with lysosomal membrane proteins: LAMP2 (0.802), LAMP1 (0.673), and lysosomal hydrolase CTSD (0.633), strongly supporting lysosomal membrane localization. High-confidence interaction with SPRYD4 (0.820, SPRY domain protein) and LAMTOR complex components (LAMTOR2: 0.549, LAMTOR1: 0.544) suggests involvement in mTORC1 lysosomal signaling pathway. Additional interactions include SLC25A16 (0.630, mitochondrial carrier), GOSR1 (0.502, Golgi SNARE receptor), and ARL8A (0.501, lysosomal GTPase), indicating roles in vesicular trafficking. ClinVar contains 44 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning chromosome 4q32.1-35.2, indicating dosage sensitivity. Cross-species conservation is strong: mouse (266 aa, ~98% identity), rat (266 aa), zebrafish (271 aa), Xenopus (261 aa). Mechanistic hypothesis: TMEM192 functions as a lysosomal membrane protein that facilitates lysosome biogenesis, autophagic flux, or mTORC1 nutrient sensing. Interaction with LAMP proteins suggests potential role in lysosomal membrane stability or cargo recognition. LAMTOR complex interaction positions TMEM192 at the lysosomal surface where mTORC1 activation occurs, potentially modulating amino acid sensing or lysosomal positioning. Dosage sensitivity and ubiquitous expression suggest essential housekeeping function in lysosomal homeostasis.
