## Description
DISP3 (Q9P2K9) encodes a 1392 amino acid sterol-sensing domain-containing membrane protein that functions as a regulator of neural stem cell (NSC) fate decisions controlling the balance between self-renewal and differentiation, links thyroid hormone action to cholesterol metabolism, and participates in Hedgehog-related signaling pathways through interaction with sonic hedgehog (SHH), desert hedgehog (DHH), and indian hedgehog (IHH) ligands despite lacking canonical Patched-family receptor activity.
