## Description
LOC100129455 encodes an uncharacterized protein located on chromosome 2q31.1 (genomic span 176,127,824-176,130,527 bp), a region densely populated with homeobox (HOX) genes critical for embryonic development and body plan specification. Despite extensive database queries, this gene lacks UniProt curation, protein interaction data (STRING returned no partners), and expression profiling (HPA could not resolve to Ensembl ID), indicating it remains poorly characterized at the molecular level. No peer-reviewed publications exist in PubMed specifically addressing this locus. However, ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses spanning 2q31.1-32.3 regions (ranging from ~80 kb to >19 Mb deletions), indicating strong dosage sensitivity where reduced gene copy number contributes to disease phenotypes. One ClinVar entry (VCV004506219) references NM_019558.4(HOXD8):c.-469G>T, suggesting LOC100129455 resides within or adjacent to the HOXD gene cluster, potentially functioning as a regulatory element or encoding a small peptide involved in HOX-mediated developmental programs. The exclusive presence of copy number losses (no gains reported) suggests haploinsufficiency rather than overexpression drives pathogenicity. Functional hypothesis: LOC100129455 likely encodes a small regulatory protein or long non-coding RNA with cis-regulatory functions affecting HOXD cluster expression during limb and axial skeleton development. Disease mechanism involves haploinsufficiency from heterozygous deletions disrupting developmental gene regulation, potentially contributing to limb malformations, digit abnormalities, or other 2q31 deletion syndrome phenotypes. Further characterization requires transcript identification and functional assays.
