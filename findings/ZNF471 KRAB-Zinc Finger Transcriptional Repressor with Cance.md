# ZNF471: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Cancer-Associated Variants

## Description
ZNF471 encodes a 626 aa Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, aa 13-85) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, aa 206-289) that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding. This domain architecture is characteristic of transcriptional repressors that recruit heterochromatin-forming complexes via the KRAB domain.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals ZNF471 associates with TRIM28 (score 0.522), the canonical KRAB co-repressor that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 to establish H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. Additional partners include BANP (0.472), a GC-rich DNA element-binding protein; FAM228B (0.478); and SLFN12 (0.458), suggesting potential roles in chromatin organization and interferon response pathways.\n\nHPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nuclear localization, consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional regulator. The gene is classified as cancer-related, with low tissue and cancer specificity indicating broad regulatory function.\n\nClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains spanning 19q13.43, suggesting gene dosage sensitivity. Duplication events may cause aberrant repression of target genes involved in cell cycle control or differentiation.\n\nConservation: The KRAB-ZNF architecture is deeply conserved across vertebrates (orthologs in orangutan, baboon, gibbon), indicating functional constraint on both DNA-binding specificity and repressor activity.\n\nHypothesis: ZNF471 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that silences endogenous retroviral elements or germline-specific genes in somatic cells via TRIM28-mediated heterochromatin formation. Dysregulation through copy number gain may contribute to oncogenesis by aberrantly silencing tumor suppressor loci.
