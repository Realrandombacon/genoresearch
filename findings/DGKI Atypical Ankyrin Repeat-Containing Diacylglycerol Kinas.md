## Description
DGKI (Diacylglycerol Kinase Iota, UniProt O75912) is a 1057 aa cytoplasmic enzyme functioning as an atypical diacylglycerol kinase that converts diacylglycerol (DAG) into phosphatidic acid (PA), regulating the respective levels of these two bioactive lipids and controlling DAG/PA-mediated signal transduction pathways. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a well-folded architecture (pLDDT=72.4, confident) featuring dual C1 phorbol ester/diacylglycerol-binding domains (IPR002219/cd20850/cd20896, aa 168-240 and 249-323) enabling DAG substrate recognition and membrane recruitment, DGK catalytic domain (IPR001206, aa 372-507) with ATP-dependent diacylglycerol kinase activity (GO:0004143), DGK accessory domain (IPR000756, aa 526-683), and C-terminal ankyrin repeat domain (IPR002110/G3DSA:1.25.40.20, aa 898-1044) mediating protein-protein interactions, classified within diacylglycerol kinase family (IPR037607, aa 144-712) and NAD kinase/DGK-like superfamily (IPR016064, aa 373-695). Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to lipid metabolism and signaling with exceptionally high confidence: Ras guanine nucleotide releasing protein RASGRP3 (0.904) linking DGKI to Ras/MAPK signaling, lipoprotein lipase LPL (0.900) in lipid metabolism, glyceronephosphate O-acyltransferase GNPAT (0.852) and glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase GPD2 (0.802) in glycerolipid biosynthesis, myristoylated alanine-rich C-kinase substrate MARCKS (0.755) in PKC signaling, acylglycerol-3-phosphate O-acyltransferase AGPAT5 (0.752) in PA synthesis, phospholipase C isoforms PLCD4 (0.752) and PLCB2 (0.703) generating DAG substrate, CDP-diacylglycerol synthase CDS2 (0.752) in phospholipid biosynthesis, and phospholipase D2 PLD2 (0.694) also producing PA. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with thyroid gland highest at 14.9 nTPM, choroid plexus (11.5 nTPM), retina (6.3 nTPM), detected in some tissues, group enriched cancer specificity, cell type enhanced, classified as enzyme/metabolic protein with kinase/transferase molecular function in lipid metabolism. ClinVar identifies 45 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number losses and gains at 7q33-36.3 (chr7:137521595-159119707), 7q31.32-36.1 (chr7:122190535-149944340), and 7q31.31-33 (chr7:120582003-137699953), though no specific Mendelian disease is directly annotated. Literature (59 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic role: 2025 study demonstrates selective DNMT1/ELK1 disruption induces DGKI re-expression promoting temozolomide sensitivity in MGMT-methylated/DGKI-methylated glioblastoma (1 citation), 2020 research establishes DGKI overexpression in gastric cancer predicting poor prognosis (12 citations), 2019 epigenome analysis identifies DGKI as novel candidate risk gene in multiplex pre-eclampsia families (11 citations), 2014 study shows DGKI methylation status modulates MGMT prognostic value in glioblastoma patients treated with radio-chemotherapy (21 citations). The gene represents a 'semi-dark lipid kinase with oncogenic potential' where canonical C1 domain-mediated DAG binding and catalytic domain-mediated phosphorylation to PA is established, ankyrin repeat domain suggests scaffolding function recruiting DGKI to specific protein complexes, thyroid-enhanced expression indicates specialized role in thyroid lipid metabolism and hormone synthesis, RASGRP3 interaction links DGKI to Ras/MAPK oncogenic signaling, LPL/GNPAT/GPD2 partnerships implicate DGKI in systemic lipid homeostasis, PLC/PLD interactions position DGKI within phosphoinositide signaling cascade regulating DAG/PA balance, and 7q33-36.3 CNV pathogenesis may contribute to cancer and neurodevelopmental phenotypes through DGKI dosage effects on lipid signaling. Therapeutic targeting of DGKI catalytic activity or C1 domain-mediated membrane recruitment could represent novel approach for glioblastoma temozolomide sensitization, gastric cancer treatment, and lipid metabolism disorders, while DGKI methylation status should be considered as biomarker for glioblastoma prognosis and treatment response.
