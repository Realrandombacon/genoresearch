## Description
ADAT1 (UniProt Q9BUB4), also known as HADAT1 (Human ADAT1), encodes a 502 aa high-confidence nucleoplasmic enzyme functioning as a tRNA-specific adenosine deaminase responsible for catalyzing the deamination of adenosine 37 to inosine (A37-to-I37 editing) in eukaryotic tRNAs through integrated adenosine deaminase domain-mediated mechanisms critical for wobble base pairing regulation, translation fidelity control, and potential neurodevelopmental function.
