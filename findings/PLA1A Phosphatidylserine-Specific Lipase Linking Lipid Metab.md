## Description
PLA1A (Phospholipase A1 Member A, UniProt Q53H76) is a 456 aa secreted phospholipase that specifically hydrolyzes the sn-1 acyl bond of phosphatidylserine (PS) and lysophosphatidylserine, generating 2-acyl-lysophosphatidylserine and free fatty acids. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence fold (pLDDT=89.6) featuring the canonical alpha/beta hydrolase fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.1820, IPR029058) with a pancreat_lipase_like domain (cd00707, aa 49-332) and triacylglycerol lipase family signature (IPR000734, PF00151, aa 17-336), characteristic of the LIPH-type lipase subfamily (IPR016272). Functionally, STRING interactions reveal a tight phospholipid metabolism network with PTDSS2 (0.926) and PTDSS1 (0.919) for phosphatidylserine synthesis, PISD (0.925) for phosphatidylethanolamine decarboxylation, MBOAT1/2 (0.920/0.915) for acyltransferase activity, and LPCAT3/4 (0.913/0.912) for lysophospholipid remodeling, positioning PLA1A as a central node in PS turnover. HPA shows reproductive tissue enrichment (epididymis 130.6 nTPM, seminal vesicle 63.3 nTPM) and choroid plexus expression (85.7 nTPM), with cancer-enhanced patterns. ClinVar identifies 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains on 3q13.31-q26.31 and missense mutations (p.Ala454Gly), though no specific disease is annotated, suggesting potential roles in male fertility or neurodevelopment. Literature (44 papers) reveals emerging non-canonical functions: PLA1A facilitates antiviral innate immunity by recruiting TBK1 to mitochondria for IRF3 activation, serves as a diagnostic marker for BRAF-mutant melanoma metastasis and breast cancer lymph node involvement, and mediates tendon healing via macrophage regulation through the Pla1a/Etv1 axis. The gene represents a 'semi-dark' lipid enzyme where canonical lipase structure is established, but specific roles in cancer metastasis, antiviral signaling, and reproductive biology remain underexplored therapeutic targets.
