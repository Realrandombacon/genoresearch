# PHF12: A Dark Gene in the Sin3/HDAC Complex Promoting Lung Cancer via Immune-Mediated Transcriptional Dysregulation

**Date:** 2026-05-07T19:51:52.834651

**Quality Score:** 6/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
PHF12 (Polyhomeotic Homolog 12) is a transcriptional corepressor encoded by a gene on chromosome 17q11.2. Despite being a well-characterized component of the Sin3-type complex and HDAC1/2 complexes (interacting with high confidence scores >0.98), PHF12 remains a \"dark gene\" due to the presence of 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar with no associated specific disease phenotype. While broadly expressed with low tissue specificity, PHF12 shows enhanced expression in immune cells (blood cells). Literature links PHF12 to non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) via EGFR/AKT signaling and nucleolar integrity. \n\n**Functional Hypothesis**: PHF12 acts as a critical transcriptional repressor by recruiting HDACs to silence target genes. The \"dark\" nature of its variants suggests that subtle dysregulation of PHF12 in immune cells (where it is upregulated) may lead to aberrant repression of tumor suppressors or activation of pro-inflammatory pathways, driving NSCLC progression. This positions PHF12 not just as a developmental regulator, but as a potential therapeutic target in immune-related cancers.
