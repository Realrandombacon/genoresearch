## Description
BHLHE23 (Basic helix-loop-helix family member e23, also known as BETA4, BHLHB4, Beta3b) is a 225 amino acid nuclear transcription factor (UniProt Q8NDY6) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 65.9), characteristic of bHLH transcription factors that function through DNA binding and protein dimerization with structured HLH domains connected by flexible regulatory regions. The protein contains a Myc-type basic helix-loop-helix domain (IPR011598, residues 100-160; PF00010, residues 102-152; PS50888, residues 100-154; SM00353, residues 106-160) enabling protein dimerization activity (GO:0046983), belongs to the helix-loop-helix DNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR036638, residues 99-172; G3DSA:4.10.280.10, residues 101-171; SSF47459, residues 99-172), and is classified within the basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor family (IPR050359, residues 55-181; PTHR19290, residues 55-181). BHLHE23 is located on chromosome 20q13.33 and encodes a member of the class E bHLH transcription factor family. Studies of the related mouse gene suggest the encoded protein may function as a transcriptional repressor.
