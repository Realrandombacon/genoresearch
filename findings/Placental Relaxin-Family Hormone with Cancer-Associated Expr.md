## Description
INSL4 (Early Placenta Insulin-Like Peptide) is a 139-amino acid secreted hormone belonging to the relaxin/insulin-like peptide family. InterPro analysis reveals three key domains: IPR022421 (Relaxin, positions 5-21/67-82/88-100), IPR023258 (Placentin, positions 26-42/55-69/114-124), and IPR051042 (Mammalian Reproductive Hormone Insulin-like, positions 1-107), confirming its classification as a reproductive hormone. STRING interaction network shows high-confidence associations with INSL6 (0.979), RLN2 (0.917), INSL3 (0.908), RLN3 (0.715), and INSL5 (0.663), plus medium-confidence links to relaxin receptors RXFP1-4 (0.574-0.599), suggesting coordinated signaling through G-protein coupled receptor pathways. HPA data shows placenta-specific expression at 14.9 nTPM with tissue-enriched RNA specificity and cancer-enriched expression pattern, consistent with its role in placental development and emerging oncogenic functions. ClinVar contains 164 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 9p24.3, though specific disease associations remain undefined. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q14641-F1) shows medium confidence (pLDDT 51.4), indicating partial structural disorder characteristic of secreted hormones requiring proteolytic processing for activation. Literature review identified 67 papers, with recent studies (2018-2021) implicating INSL4 in LKB1-inactivated lung cancer growth, NSCLC proliferation/invasiveness, and epigenetic regulation—suggesting underappreciated oncogenic roles beyond placental development.
