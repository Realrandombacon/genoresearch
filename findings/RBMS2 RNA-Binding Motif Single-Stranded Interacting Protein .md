## Description
RBMS2 (RNA-Binding Motif Single-Stranded Interacting Protein 2, also known as MSSP-2, UniProt Q15434) is a 407 aa intracellular nucleoplasmic/cytosolic protein belonging to the Polyadenylate-binding/CELF RNA-binding family (IPR035979/PTHR24012, positions 38-260) with two tandem RNA recognition motif domains (RRM1: positions 51-134/56-129/58-118, RRM2: positions 135-220/135-220/137-201, IPR000504/PF00076/PS50102/SM00360, G3DSA:3.30.70.330) that functions as an RNA-binding protein regulating translation initiation through EIF4H interaction, ferroptosis control via SLC7A11 transcription-translation regulation in colorectal cancer, and breast cancer chemosensitivity through BMF expression modulation, with unexpected complement system network associations. The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal region (positions 1-37) for protein stability, tandem RRM domains (RRM1: positions 51-134, RRM2: positions 135-220) for single-stranded RNA/DNA binding with nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait fold (IPR012677), and C-terminal region (positions 221-407) for protein-protein interactions and subcellular localization. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 63.8, medium confidence) indicating ordered RRM domains with flexible linker and C-terminal regions—consistent with RNA-binding protein function requiring structured domains for nucleic acid recognition and disordered regions for dynamic protein interactions.
