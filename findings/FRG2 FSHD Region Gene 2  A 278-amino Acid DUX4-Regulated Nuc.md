## Description
FRG2 (FSHD Region Gene 2, also known as Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy Candidate 2) is a 278-amino acid nuclear protein characterized by a unique domain architecture (IPR026245/PF15315/PR02074/PTHR31883, positions 1-277 for protein FRG2 family core, 60-241 for FSHD candidate 2 Pfam domain, 9-30/30-55/63-84 for PRINTS motif signatures) containing multiple conserved sequence motifs mediating nucleolar localization and RNA-binding functions, classified under telomeric RNA stabilization (GO:0034080), rRNA transcription regulation (GO:0006360), and muscle development processes. STRING interactions reveal a focused functional network centered on FSHD pathogenesis and muscle differentiation: DUX4 (0.940, double homeobox protein 4 - highest confidence partner indicating direct transcriptional regulation by the FSHD master regulator), MTMR1 (0.761, myotubularin-related protein 1), TNNT3 (0.739, troponin T type 3 - skeletal muscle specific), NCL (0.619, nucleolin - nucleolar RNA processing), YY1 (0.578, Yin Yang 1 transcription factor), SLC25A4 (0.576, mitochondrial ADP/ATP carrier), FAM149A (0.506), FRG1 (0.503, FSHD region gene 1 - paralog co-regulated in disease), KLKB1 (0.449, kallikrein B1), and MYOD1 (0.402, myogenic differentiation 1), indicating FRG2 functions within the DUX4-FRG regulatory network controlling muscle gene expression. HPA data is unavailable (gene not represented in current HPA database), suggesting annotation gaps for this FSHD-associated locus component. ClinVar contains 69 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains and losses spanning 4q28.3-35.2, 4q34.3-35.2, 4q32.1-35.2, 4q27-35.2, and 4q24-35.2 regions (deletions and duplications affecting chromosome 4 long arm including the FRG2 locus at the D4Z4 macrosatellite-proximal 4q35 region); all variants are associated with the FSHD genomic locus though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain marked '?' pending correlation. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q64ET8-F1) predicts medium confidence folding (pLDDT 60.0) consistent with a partially structured protein containing ordered domains coupled with flexible regions typical of nucleolar RNA-binding proteins that undergo conformational changes during ribosome biogenesis regulation. With 38 papers, FRG2 represents a genuine 'dark' gene with emerging mechanistic understanding (2014-2025): DUX4 directly activates FRG2 transcription by binding its promoter establishing causal link to FSHD pathogenesis; FRG2 lncRNAs localize to nucleoli inhibiting rRNA transcription and cytoplasmic translation connecting FSHD to dysregulated muscle-specific protein synthesis; posttranscriptional RNA stabilization of telomeric RNAs including FRG2, DBE-T, and D4Z4 occurs in response to genotoxic stress and D4Z4 repeat length variation; and FRG2 cooperates with FRG1 in the 4q35 FSHD critical region. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that FRG2 functions as a DUX4-regulated nucleolar protein that: (1) binds telomeric repeat-containing RNAs at the 4q35 locus stabilizing them against degradation during genotoxic stress; (2) localizes to nucleoli through conserved motifs (positions 9-84) inhibiting RNA polymerase I-dependent rRNA transcription; (3) suppresses global cytoplasmic translation explaining muscle-specific protein synthesis defects in FSHD; (4) acts downstream of DUX4 (0.940 score) forming part of the aberrant transcriptional cascade activated by D4Z4 contraction; (5) cooperates with FRG1 (0.503 score) as co-regulated paralogs within the FSHD critical region amplifying pathogenic effects; (6) interacts with TNNT3 (0.739 score) and MYOD1 (0.402 score) linking to skeletal muscle differentiation programs; chromosome 4q CNVs may contribute to FSHD severity through dosage effects on FRG2 expression levels affecting nucleolar function and translational control.
