# FNIP2: Folliculin-Interacting Protein 2 with Tripartite DENN Domain Functioning as Critical mTORC1 Regulator via Rag GTPase Activation and Lysosomal Recruitment in Response to Amino Acid Sensing

**Date:** 2026-06-14T19:17:54.557472

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
FNIP2 (Q9P278) encodes a 1114 amino acid folliculin-interacting protein 2 that serves as an essential co-chaperone and activator for the mTORC1 signaling pathway, bridging amino acid sensing to lysosomal Rag GTPase activation. InterPro analysis reveals a complex multi-domain architecture dominated by a massive tripartite DENN domain (IPR037545/PS51836, positions 42-1105) characteristic of guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs), flanked by specialized FNIP-specific domains: an N-terminal domain (IPR028084/PF14636, positions 46-167) mediating initial complex assembly; a middle domain (IPR028085/PF14637, positions 298-531) providing structural scaffolding; and a C-terminal domain (IPR028086/PF14638, positions 927-1110) crucial for FLCN binding stability. The family-defining motif (IPR026156) appears at three discrete positions (332-352, 462-486, 944-961) suggesting multiple interaction interfaces. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 57.2, medium confidence) consistent with a large GEF containing flexible linker regions required for conformational changes during nucleotide exchange, while core DENN subdomains likely maintain stable folding for catalytic activity. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive high-confidence partnerships with the mTORC1 regulatory machinery: FLCN (0.999), folliculin forming the obligate heterodimeric complex; RRAGA (0.995), RRAGB (0.955), RRAGC (0.997), and RRAGD (0.959), Rag GTPase heterodimers serving as direct substrates for DENN-mediated GDP/GTP exchange; FNIP1 (0.996), the paralogous co-chaperone suggesting functional redundancy or heterocomplex formation; LAMTOR4 (0.954), LAMTOR3 (0.924), and LAMTOR5 (0.580), components of the Ragulator complex anchoring Rags to the lysosomal membrane; SLC38A9 (0.935), the lysosomal arginine sensor transmitting nutrient status to the Rag-Ragulator-FNIP2 axis. HPA shows ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, cancer-enhanced expression pattern reflecting mTORC1 hyperactivation in tumors, centriolar satellite and cytosol localization indicating dynamic trafficking between centrosomal structures and lysosomal surfaces, classified under predicted intracellular proteins with DNA damage biological process involvement. ClinVar contains 38 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants exclusively consisting of large copy number gains (chr4q28.3-35.2 duplications, chr4q27-35.2 amplifications, chr4q24-35.2 gains, chr4p12-q35.2 triplications, chr4q26-32.3 duplications) and losses (chr4q32.1-35.2 deletions, chr4q32.1-35.2 hemizygous losses) spanning the FNIP2 locus on chromosome 4, though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined suggesting haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity effects. Literature (74 papers) defines FNIP2 as a critical mTORC1 regulator: cryo-EM structure of the human FLCN-FNIP2-Rag-Ragulator complex reveals the molecular mechanism of Rag GTPase activation where FNIP2's DENN domain catalyzes nucleotide exchange on RagC/D enabling mTORC1 lysosomal recruitment; direct regulation by MEF2 transcription factors sustains MTORC1 activation driving pancreatic cancer progression; targeting the FNIP2-SERCA2b axis improves metabolic and mitochondrial defects in Ataxia Telangiectasia; lncRNA-FNIP2/miR-24-3p/FNIP2 axis regulates chicken adipogenesis demonstrating conserved metabolic control. The mechanistic hypothesis: FNIP2 functions as the catalytic engine of the FLCN-FNIP2 complex where the tripartite DENN domain (positions 42-1105) acts as a GEF specifically activating RagC/D GTPases by exchanging GDP for GTP, a prerequisite for mTORC1 recruitment to the lysosomal surface upon amino acid sufficiency; medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 57.2) reflects the dynamic nature of GEFs requiring conformational flexibility to engage Rag substrates and facilitate nucleotide release; obligate heterodimerization with FLCN (score 0.999) stabilizes the complex and positions the DENN domain for optimal substrate access; interaction with Ragulator components (LAMTOR3/4/5) anchors the active complex to the lysosome colocalizing enzyme and substrate; SLC38A9 partnership integrates arginine sensing signals to modulate FNIP2 GEF activity ensuring precise metabolic control; ubiquitous expression underscores fundamental role in cellular homeostasis across all tissues while cancer-enhanced expression drives oncogenic mTORC1 signaling promoting proliferation and survival; centriolar satellite localization suggests potential non-canonical roles in centrosome function or ciliogenesis linked to DNA damage responses; copy number variations indicate dosage sensitivity where altered FNIP2 levels disrupt mTORC1 set-points contributing to developmental disorders or cancer predisposition; functional redundancy with FNIP1 (score 0.996) provides robustness but also potential for compensatory mechanisms in disease states.
