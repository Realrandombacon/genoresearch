## Description
CLEC11A (C-type Lectin Domain Family 11 Member A, also known as SCGF/Stem Cell Growth Factor, UniProt Q9Y240) is a 323 aa secreted glycoprotein functioning as a hematopoietic growth factor and osteogenesis regulator. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical C-type lectin-like domain (CTLD) architecture (IPR001304, PF00059, positions 198-321; cd03596, positions 177-321) within the C-type lectin-like/link domain superfamily (IPR016186, G3DSA:3.10.100.10, positions 153-321) with defined C-type lectin fold (IPR016187, positions 179-322), conserved lectin signature (IPR018378, PS00615, positions 296-319), and C-type lectin domain profile (PS50041, positions 183-320). The protein belongs to the C-type lectin and tetranectin domain-containing protein family (IPR051663, PTHR22799, positions 82-322; SM00034, position 177). AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 78.9), indicating a well-folded secreted protein with stable CTLD geometry for carbohydrate/protein recognition. HPA data confirms bone marrow-enriched expression (109.3 nTPM, tissue enhanced), detected in many tissues with low cancer specificity, immune cell enhanced classification, centrosome localization, classified as a plasma protein and predicted secreted protein with growth factor molecular function involved in osteogenesis. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with hematopoietic growth factor receptors and chemokine signaling: KIT (0.993, stem cell factor receptor), KITLG (0.713, stem cell factor ligand), CSF1 (0.705, colony stimulating factor 1), CCL27 (0.698, chemokine ligand 27), PDGFRA (0.636, platelet-derived growth factor receptor alpha), NUDCD1 (0.619), PDGFRB (0.606), IL16 (0.599, interleukin 16), CCL7 (0.587), CSF1R (0.585). ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains at 19q13.33-13.43 affecting the CLEC11A locus. Literature (98 papers) establishes CLEC11A roles in: (1) colorectal cancer lymphatic metastasis via CAF-derived CLEC11A/LGR5-mediated WNT pathway activation (2025, 3 citations), (2) intervertebral disc degeneration genetic pathogenesis via multi-omics analysis (2025, 3 citations), (3) gastric cancer diagnostic serum biomarker in 48-multiplex panel (2025), (4) colon adenocarcinoma prognosis and immunotherapy response prediction (2025), (5) pathological cardiac hypertrophy and fibrosis via fibroblast-derived CLEC11A (2025), and (6) original characterization as stem cell growth factor enhancing hematopoietic progenitor proliferation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CLEC11A serves as a secreted growth factor coordinating hematopoietic stem cell maintenance, osteoblast differentiation, and tumor microenvironment remodeling through CTLD-mediated receptor interactions (KIT, PDGFRs) and WNT pathway activation. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The CTLD domain mediates carbohydrate-independent protein-protein interactions with cell surface receptors (KIT, PDGFRA/B) on hematopoietic progenitors and cancer-associated fibroblasts. CLEC11A secretion from CAFs under hypoxia activates LGR5+ cancer stem cells via WNT signaling, promoting lymphatic metastasis in colorectal cancer. In bone marrow, CLEC11A synergizes with KITLG/SCF to enhance progenitor proliferation. In cardiac tissue, fibroblast-derived CLEC11A drives pathological hypertrophy through paracrine signaling. The 14 ClinVar CNVs suggest CLEC11A dosage sensitivity may contribute to hematopoietic or skeletal disorders, though specific disease associations remain undefined. The secreted nature and plasma protein classification indicate CLEC11A functions as an endocrine/paracrine signaling molecule.
