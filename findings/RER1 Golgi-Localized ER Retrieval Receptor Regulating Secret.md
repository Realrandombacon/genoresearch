## Description
RER1/O15258 (196 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 78.6) type IV transmembrane protein functioning as an essential retrieval receptor that recognizes and returns escaped ER resident proteins from the Golgi apparatus back to the endoplasmic reticulum, thereby maintaining secretory pathway proteostasis and organelle identity. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical Rer1 family architecture (IPR004932/PF03248/PIRSF016013/PTHR10743, residues 5-191/22-185/12-191/5-187) containing four transmembrane domains that form a cargo recognition complex sensitive to transmembrane helix length and sequence motifs, classifying it within the early Golgi retrieval machinery. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, exclusive Golgi apparatus localization consistent with its role as a retrograde transport receptor, and classification as a predicted membrane transporter. STRING interactions define a high-confidence secretory pathway network linking RER1 to gamma-secretase complex component PSENEN (0.803), cargo receptor SURF4 (0.751), ER membrane adaptor BCAP31 (0.707), pre-B-cell leukemia transcription factor interacting protein PREB (0.598), ER-Golgi intermediate compartment proteins ERGIC3 (0.569) and LMAN2 (0.559), Golgi phosphoprotein GOLPH3 (0.500), KDEL receptor KDELR1 (0.440), ER chaperone ERP44 (0.432), and calnexin CANX (0.401), positioning it at the intersection of ER quality control, gamma-secretase assembly, and lipid raft organization. Literature (90 papers) establishes emerging roles in neurodegeneration and immunity: 2025 studies demonstrate RER1 regulation of lipid metabolism in monocytes and macrophages affecting inflammatory responses; 2024 research reveals RER1 interaction with DAP12 retaining it in the secretory pathway before TREM2 complex assembly, critical for microglial function; 2023 landmark studies show glucocorticoid enhancement of presenilin-1-dependent amyloid-beta production at ER-mitochondrial associated membranes through RER1 downregulation in neuronal cells, linking ER retrieval failure to Alzheimer's pathogenesis; 2024 Drosophila studies establish RER1 maintenance of proteostasis as essential for competitive cell survival and Myc-driven overgrowth. ClinVar documents 129 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1p36 regions (1p36.33-36.23, 1p36.33-36.22, 1p36.33-36.32), implicating RER1 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined pending further characterization. The convergence of conserved transmembrane cargo recognition machinery, PSENEN/gamma-secretase partnerships, Golgi retention mechanisms, macrophage lipid metabolism regulation, TREM2/DAP12 immune receptor assembly control, and Alzheimer's amyloidogenic modulation suggests RER1 functions as a secretory pathway checkpoint coordinating ER proteostasis with neurodegenerative disease progression and immune cell metabolic reprogramming, with therapeutic potential in Alzheimer's disease targeting, microglial dysfunction correction, and macrophage-mediated inflammation modulation.
