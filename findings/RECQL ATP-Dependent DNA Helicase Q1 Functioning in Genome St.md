## Description
RECQL (RecQ like helicase, ATP-dependent DNA helicase Q1, P46063) encodes a 649 aa SF2 superfamily helicase functioning as a critical guardian of genome stability through DNA replication fork restart, homologous recombination repair, and suppression of illegitimate recombination. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded multi-domain organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 86.1): DEXH-box helicase domain of RecQ1 (cd18015, residues 74-282) forming the catalytic core for ATP hydrolysis, C-terminal helicase domain of RecQ family (cd18794, residues 283-418) completing the bipartite helicase engine, Winged helix-like DNA-binding domain superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.10.10, residues 483-592) mediating specific DNA substrate recognition and binding, P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 48-482) providing the canonical Walker A/B motifs for ATP coordination, Helicase C-terminal domain-like (IPR001650, residues 300-451), DNA helicase ATP-dependent RecQ type family (IPR004589, residues 81-535) with GO terms for helicase activity (GO:0004386) and DNA recombination (GO:0006310), DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545, residues 93-260) with GO terms for nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676) and ATP binding (GO:0005524), Helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001, residues 88-291), ATP-dependent DNA helicase RecQ zinc-binding domain (IPR032284, residues 421-479) stabilizing the helicase core, and Winged helix DNA-binding domain (IPR036388, residues 485-580) enabling strand separation.
