## Description
APOA4 (UniProt P06727) encodes a 396-amino acid secreted apolipoprotein containing two tandem apolipoprotein A1/A4/E domains (IPR000074/PF01442, aa 61-250 and aa 211-363) within the broader apolipoprotein superfamily (IPR050163/PTHR18976, aa 4-387). The protein adopts the characteristic four-helix bundle apolipoprotein fold (G3DSA:1.20.120.20/SSF58113) with two distinct lipid-binding regions (aa 14-176, aa 177-332) essential for lipoprotein assembly. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT: 80.8), consistent with stable amphipathic helix architecture required for lipid binding (GO:0008289) and transport (GO:0006869).\n\nSTRING interactions position APOA4 within the lipoprotein metabolism network: strongest links to APOA2 (0.994), APOC2 (0.987), APOA1 (0.972), APOC1 (0.969), APOM (0.949), APOE (0.941), clusterin CLU (0.927), APOC3 (0.921), APOH (0.911), and APOB (0.865). HPA confirms extreme intestine specificity (2929.9 nTPM, tissue enriched) with vesicular localization and classification as plasma protein and cardiovascular disease candidate.\n\nWith 582 papers, APOA4 is well-studied for chylomicron secretion and HDL maturation but has emerging 2025 translational applications: (1) PCSK9-APOA4 axis in TMAO-induced cholesterol metabolism and cholelithiasis; (2) diagnostic immunohistochemical biomarker for urothelial bladder carcinoma; (3) cardiometabolic-cognition link via APOA4-APOA5-ZPR1-BUD13 locus in rural Indians. ClinVar contains 37 pathogenic variants (11q23.3 SNVs including p.Leu329Val, p.Asp33Asn, p.Leu66Val). This positions APOA4 as a dual-function lipoprotein regulator with untapped diagnostic potential in oncology and metabolic disease.
