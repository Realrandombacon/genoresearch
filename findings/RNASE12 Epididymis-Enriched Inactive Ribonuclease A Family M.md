## Description

Despite retaining the structural scaffold of active RNases, RNASE12 is classified as 'probable inactive' due to the loss of critical catalytic residues, suggesting a neofunctionalization event where the protein retains nucleic acid binding capability (GO:0003676) but lacks hydrolytic activity.
