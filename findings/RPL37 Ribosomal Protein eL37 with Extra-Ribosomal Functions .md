## Description
RPL37 (Ribosomal Protein L37, UniProt P61927) is a 97 amino acid zinc-binding ribosomal protein with moderate characterization that functions as a structural component of the 60S large ribosomal subunit and exhibits extra-ribosomal roles in p53 tumor suppressor regulation and reproductive biology. The protein contains a large ribosomal subunit protein eL37 domain (IPR001569/PF01907, residues 2-54), eL37/eL43 superfamily fold (IPR011331/G3DSA:2.20.25.30, residues 1-88), zinc-binding ribosomal protein structure (IPR011332/SSF57829, residues 1-55), and conserved eL37 signature motif (IPR018267/PS01077, residues 4-23), placing it in the eukaryotic ribosomal protein eL37 family (PTHR10768, residues 1-95) with roles in translation and ribosome assembly. STRING interactions reveal maximum confidence partnerships (all 0.999) with ribosomal proteins RPL18A, RPL19, RPS12, RPL35, RPL8, RPS11, RPS27A, RPS3, RPL29, and RPS23, confirming its integration into the 60S subunit translation machinery. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, intracellular localization, consistent with essential housekeeping ribosomal function. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 5p13.3-11 and single nucleotide variants, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential developmental, hematologic, or cancer phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 89.5) indicates very high confidence with a well-folded zinc-binding ribosomal protein fold. Functional hypothesis: RPL37 serves dual functions—as a canonical ribosomal protein, it stabilizes 60S subunit structure through zinc-coordination and rRNA binding to facilitate peptide bond formation during translation elongation; as an extra-ribosomal regulator, the landmark 2013 study (174 citations) demonstrated RPL37 binds Mdm2 and MdmX to stabilize p53 tumor suppressor activity, creating ribosomal stress signaling that triggers cell cycle arrest or apoptosis under nucleolar stress, while 2024 research links RPL37 translation dysregulation via YTHDC1 m6A modification to trophoblastic dysfunction and preterm birth in ART conceptions, suggesting RPL37 dosage sensitivity in rapidly dividing cells (embryonic development, cancer), with 5p copy number gains potentially contributing to developmental disorders through ribosomal imbalance (ribosomopathy) and p53 pathway dysregulation, making RPL37 a candidate biomarker for preterm birth risk and therapeutic target in p53-deficient cancers.
