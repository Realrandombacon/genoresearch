## Description
MAGEB1 (Melanoma-Associated Antigen B1) encodes a 347 aa cancer-testis antigen (P43366) with testis-restricted expression and emerging roles in X-linked adrenal hypoplasia congenita. With 27 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined structural features but incompletely understood molecular function.
