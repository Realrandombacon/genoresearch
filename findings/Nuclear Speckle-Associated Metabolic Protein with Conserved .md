## Description
FAM227B encodes a 508 amino acid protein (UniProt: Q96M60) localized to nuclear speckles with ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity) classified as a metabolic and predicted intracellular protein. InterPro analysis reveals the conserved FAM227 domain architecture (IPR029417 spanning positions 30-486; PF14922 protein of unknown function at 160-308; PTHR33560 family at 30-486), indicating this domain is preserved across the majority of the protein length and represents a functionally important structural unit. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 70.7, confident) indicates a well-folded stable 3D structure suitable for specific molecular interactions rather than intrinsically disordered signaling. STRING network identifies 10 interaction partners including LRRIQ3 (score 0.734, leucine-rich repeat and IQ domain-containing protein involved in cytoskeletal organization), VXN (0.615, nexin-dynein regulatory complex component), RNF144A (0.559, E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase), TMEM126B (0.518, transmembrane protein associated with mitochondrial complex I assembly), KIFC3 (0.514, kinesin motor protein), FPGT (0.511, fucose-1-phosphate guanylyltransferase involved in GDP-fucose metabolism), NOL4L (0.498, nucleolar protein 4-like), FAM49A (0.496, actin-regulatory protein), CFAP99 (0.479, cilia and flagella-associated protein), and ROPN1L (0.464, ropporin-1-like protein). The convergence of nuclear speckle localization with interactions spanning cytoskeletal regulators (LRRIQ3, VXN, KIFC3), metabolic enzymes (FPGT), ubiquitination machinery (RNF144A), and mitochondrial proteins (TMEM126B) suggests FAM227B may function as a multifunctional scaffold coordinating nuclear-cytoplasmic signaling or gene expression programs linked to cellular metabolism. ClinVar documents 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (chr15:47392800-52877953, chr15:45043912-51196595, chr15:49390592-56800964 deletions), gains (chr15:22770421-50347130, chr15:48589845-63543438 duplications), and intronic SNVs (c.441+229A>G, c.441+268C>A), indicating bidirectional dosage sensitivity on 15q21 regions. Conservation is complete across mammals: mouse Q9D518 (532 aa), rat Q6AXP3 (573 aa), macaque Q4R7D5 (392 aa). The mechanistic hypothesis proposes FAM227B functions as a nuclear speckle-associated scaffold that integrates metabolic signals (via FPGT/GDP-fucose pathway) with transcriptional regulation and cytoskeletal dynamics, potentially modulating RNA processing or export in response to cellular metabolic state, with pathogenic CNVs disrupting this coordination leading to developmental phenotypes.
