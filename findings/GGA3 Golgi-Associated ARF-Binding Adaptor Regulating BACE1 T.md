# GGA3: Golgi-Associated ARF-Binding Adaptor Regulating BACE1 Trafficking and RET Receptor Recycling in Neurodegeneration and Cancer

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

## Description
GGA3 (Golgi Associated Gamma Adaptin Ear Containing ARF Binding Protein 3, Q9NZ52) encodes a 723 aa clathrin adaptor protein functioning as a critical vesicular trafficking regulator at the trans-Golgi network (TGN) that sorts ubiquitinated cargo into clathrin-coated vesicles. Structural architecture reveals three functional modules: N-terminal VHS domain (IPR002014/cd17008, residues 5-146/6-146) for ubiquitinated cargo recognition with phosphatidylinositol/ubiquitin binding capability, central GAT domain (IPR004152/cd14240, residues 171-299/213-299) for ARF-GTP binding and membrane recruitment with phosphatidylinositol binding, and C-terminal gamma-adaptin ear/GAE domain (IPR008153/IPR008152, residues 594-715/591-715) for clathrin and accessory protein binding. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 69.7) reveals partially structured architecture with ordered VHS/GAT/GAE domains connected by flexible hinge regions characteristic of clathrin adaptors. STRING interactions confirm trafficking machinery network with RABEP1 (0.996, rabaptin-5 endosomal fusion regulator), UBC (0.986, ubiquitin C), ARF1 (0.949, ADP-ribosylation factor 1 GTPase), BACE1 (0.933, beta-secretase 1), RPS27A (0.932, ubiquitin-40S ribosomal protein), GGA1/GGA2 (0.691/0.683, paralogous adaptors), IGF2R/M6PR (0.657/0.645, mannose-6-phosphate receptors). HPA confirms Golgi apparatus localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, classified as transporter involved in protein transport. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic variants including 17q24.1-25.3 CNVs and splice site mutations (c.830-16C>G, c.947-24G>T). Literature (181 papers) demonstrates critical roles in Alzheimer’s disease pathogenesis via BACE1 trafficking regulation (30x citations, 2018; 20x citations, 2020), RET receptor tyrosine kinase recycling for cell migration/invasion (18x citations, 2019), type 2 diabetes (Gga3 KO mice show elevated fasting glucose, 2x citations, 2023), and lens epithelial cell dysfunction via circ-GGA3/miR-497-5p/SMAD4 axis (6x citations, 2021). Mechanistic hypothesis: GGA3 recognizes ubiquitinated BACE1 via VHS domain at TGN, recruits ARF1-GTP via GAT domain for membrane association, and engages clathrin via GAE domain to package BACE1 into vesicles for lysosomal degradation; GGA3 loss causes BACE1 accumulation in axonal swellings/dystrophic neurites, increasing amyloid-β production independent of plaque deposition. Similarly, GGA3 recycles RET receptor from endosomes to plasma membrane, promoting cell migration and cancer invasion.
