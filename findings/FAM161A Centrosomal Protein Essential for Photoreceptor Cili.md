# FAM161A: Centrosomal Protein Essential for Photoreceptor Ciliogenesis

**Date:** 2026-03-18T19:06:16.190101

**Quality Score:** 3.98/10  (E=7.15, D=0.8) [MODERATE]

## Description
FAM161A encodes a 660-amino acid centrosomal protein (UniProt Q3B820) localized to the centrosome, basal body, and Golgi apparatus. The protein belongs to the FAM161 family (InterPro IPR019579, IPR051655, PF10595) with conserved regions spanning residues 12-576. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 66.4), consistent with intrinsically disordered regions common in scaffolding proteins.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: FAM161A functions as a centrosomal scaffolding protein essential for photoreceptor cilium biogenesis and maintenance. Mechanistically, it coordinates chaperonin complex assembly (CCT2/CCT5/CCT7 interactions) for proper folding of ciliary proteins, while recruiting centrosomal components (POC5, POC1B) to the basal body. The strong interaction with LCA5 (0.815, lebercilin) and photoreceptor transcription factors (CRX 0.773, PDE6B 0.788) positions FAM161A at the intersection of ciliogenesis and phototransduction machinery assembly. Loss of FAM161A disrupts outer segment formation in mature photoreceptors, causing retinitis pigmentosa-28.

INTERACTION NETWORK: FAM161A shows exceptionally high-confidence interactions with chaperonin subunits CCT2 (0.979), CCT5 (0.972), and CCT7 (0.841), suggesting roles in protein folding at the centrosome. Centrosomal partners include POC5 (0.940) and POC1B (0.421). Additional interactions with PEX19 (0.852, peroxisomal biogenesis), HSPE1 (0.797, chaperonin), LCA5 (0.815, ciliopathy gene), CRX (0.773, photoreceptor development), and PDE6B (0.788, phototransduction) reveal connections to multiple organelle biogenesis pathways.

EXPRESSION & DISEASE: HPA shows retina-enriched expression (26.9 nTPM), detected in many tissues, classified as disease-related gene for ciliopathy and retinitis pigmentosa. Subcellular locations include nucleoplasm, Golgi, centrosome, and basal body. ClinVar documents 215 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Glu80Ter, p.Ser367Ter, p.Lys115Ter, p.Gln550Ter), frameshifts (p.Arg528fs, p.Cys406fs), splice variants, and chromosome 2p CNVs, confirming loss-of-function pathomechanism in autosomal recessive retinitis pigmentosa-28.

CONSERVATION: Orthologs in mouse (Q8QZV6, 475 aa) and rat (Q6AY14, 474 aa) show shorter isoforms, suggesting species-specific C-terminal extensions in humans. The FAM161 domain architecture is conserved across vertebrates.
