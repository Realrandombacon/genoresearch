## Description
RPH3AL encodes Rab effector Noc2 (UniProt Q9UNE2), a 315 aa protein functioning as a Rab GTPase effector in calcium-dependent exocytosis. InterPro analysis reveals modular domain architecture: N-terminal Rabphilin/Doc2/Noc2 family region (IPR043566, aa 6-229), central Rab-binding domain (IPR010911, PF02318, aa 41-158) for small GTPase binding and intracellular protein transport, and FYVE-type zinc finger (IPR017455, IPR041857, aa 46-158/86-149) mediating membrane localization through phosphoinositide binding. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 67.4), consistent with partially folded protein containing both ordered domains and flexible linker regions typical of Rab effectors. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence connections to exocytosis machinery: DOC2B (0.935, double C2 domain protein), RAB27A (0.893, small GTPase regulating secretory granules), DOC2A (0.758, calcium sensor), plus LIM domain proteins WTIP (0.837), LIMD1 (0.783), and AJUBA (0.737) suggesting scaffolding roles. RAB3A (0.450) and RAB8A (0.437) interactions confirm Rab GTPase effector function. HPA shows pancreas-enriched expression (49.6 nTPM) and pituitary gland expression (42.4 nTPM), with exocytosis annotation and intracellular localization, consistent with regulated secretory pathway function. ClinVar reports 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 17p13.3 copy number losses/gains, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (32 papers) establishes roles in: (1) Prostate cancer pathways (2024); (2) Breast cancer gene alterations (2010); (3) Medulloblastoma tumor suppressor locus (1999); (4) Evolutionary conservation across 46 metazoan genomes as Rab effector family member. Functional hypothesis: RPH3AL functions through: (1) Rab27A Effector - binds active GTP-bound RAB27A on secretory granules via Rab-binding domain, recruiting downstream effectors for granule docking; (2) Calcium-Dependent Exocytosis - DOC2A/DOC2B interactions suggest RPH3AL participates in calcium-triggered vesicle fusion, potentially acting as calcium sensor or scaffold; (3) Membrane Targeting - FYVE zinc finger binds phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P) on endosomal/secretory membranes, localizing RPH3AL-RAB27A complexes to specific subcellular compartments; (4) Pancreatic Beta Cell Function - high pancreas expression suggests role in insulin granule exocytosis, potentially linking to diabetes pathogenesis; (5) Neuroendocrine Secretion - pituitary expression indicates involvement in hormone release (ACTH, growth hormone, prolactin); (6) Cancer Mechanism - 17p13.3 CNVs disrupt RPH3AL dosage, potentially affecting secretory pathway integrity in prostate/breast cancers and medulloblastoma. Disease mechanism: RPH3AL haploinsufficiency from 17p13.3 deletions may impair regulated exocytosis in neuroendocrine tissues, contributing to developmental disorders or metabolic dysfunction. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 67.4) and moderate literature (32 papers) suggest RPH3AL is partially characterized but mechanistic details of calcium sensing, specific cargo selection, and tissue-specific functions remain unclear.
