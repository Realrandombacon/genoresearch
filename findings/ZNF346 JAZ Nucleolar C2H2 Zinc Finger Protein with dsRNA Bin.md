# ZNF346 (JAZ): Nucleolar C2H2 Zinc Finger Protein with dsRNA Binding Specificity and 5q35 Dosage Sensitivity

**Quality Score:** 3.23/10  (E=5.45, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF346 (Gene ID: 23567, UniProt Q9UL40, aliases JAZ, Zfp346) is a 294 amino acid nucleolar zinc finger protein located on chromosome 5q35.2 (NC_000005.10, positions 177022695-177081188, ~59kb genomic region). This gene encodes a nucleic acid-binding protein that preferentially binds double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) or RNA/DNA hybrids rather than DNA alone, with zinc finger domains essential for both dsRNA binding and nucleolar localization. InterPro domain analysis reveals extensive C2H2 zinc finger architecture: three canonical C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR013087/PF12874/SM00355 at positions 73-97, 134-158, 185-209), Matrin/U1-C-like C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR003604/SM00451 at positions 70-104, 131-165, 182-216), C2H2 superfamily domains (IPR036236 at positions 44-111, 120-167, 173-223), and ZNF346/matrin-type protein 4 family signature (IPR051868/PTHR46144 spanning positions 16-290). The triple zinc finger array occupies the central region (~150 residues), suggesting coordinated nucleic acid recognition. GO annotations include nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676) and zinc ion binding (GO:0008270). STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners: MYC (0.987, MYC proto-oncogene, transcription factor regulating cell proliferation), ICE2 (0.948, inner centromere protein interacting protein 2, mitotic regulator), MT-CO1 (0.899, mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase subunit 1), SKP1 (0.841, S-phase kinase-associated protein 1, SCF ubiquitin ligase component), PRH1 (0.720, proline-rich protein HaeIII subfragment 1), PRH2 (0.715), ILF3 (0.531, interleukin enhancer-binding factor 3, dsRNA-binding protein), EIF2AK2 (0.529, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2-alpha kinase 2/PKR, dsRNA-activated kinase), CUL1 (0.441, cullin-1, ubiquitin ligase scaffold), and HBS1L (0.405, HBS1-like translation factor). The interaction profile strongly suggests roles in transcriptional regulation (MYC), protein degradation (SKP1-CUL1 ubiquitin ligase complex), dsRNA sensing/signaling (ILF3, EIF2AK2/PKR), and cell cycle control (ICE2). Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF346 (ENSG00000113761) as a predicted intracellular protein with nucleoplasm subcellular localization and RNA-binding molecular function. RNA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and low single cell type specificity—indicating ubiquitous housekeeping expression rather than tissue-restricted function. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9UL40-F1) yields medium-confidence model (global pLDDT 66.5), indicating a partially structured protein with mixed ordered zinc finger domains and disordered linker regions across all 294 residues—consistent with intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) characteristics common in RNA-binding proteins requiring conformational flexibility for nucleic acid recognition. ClinVar lists 57 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large chromosomal copy number variations spanning chromosome 5q35.2-35.3 regions (e.g., chr5:176144576-178014188 deletion, chr5:176097556-180719789 deletion, chr5:175570678-177414568 triplication, chr5:175470000-177450000 deletion, chr5:176385815-178410738 deletion, chr5:176483840-176588195 deletion); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ZNF346 point mutations, suggesting dosage sensitivity within larger chromosomal syndromes rather than haploinsufficiency. The 5q35 region includes NSD1 (Sotos syndrome), and ZNF346 CNVs may contribute to contiguous gene deletion/duplication phenotypes. Conservation evidenced by orthologs in mouse (Q9R0B7, 294 aa identical length) and orangutan (Q5R4W8, 310 aa). Functional hypothesis: ZNF346 functions as a nucleolar/nucleoplasmic dsRNA-binding protein involved in RNA metabolism, ribosome biogenesis, or innate immune signaling. The triple C2H2 zinc finger array mediates specific dsRNA/RNA-DNA hybrid recognition. Interaction with MYC suggests transcriptional co-regulation roles. SKP1-CUL1 interactions implicate ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover regulation. ILF3 and EIF2AK2/PKR interactions suggest participation in dsRNA sensing pathways, potentially linking nucleolar RNA metabolism to antiviral responses or stress granule formation. Medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 66.5) indicates conformational flexibility consistent with RNA-binding adapter function. Ubiquitous expression suggests housekeeping role in fundamental cellular processes. Disease mechanism: ZNF346 haploinsufficiency or overexpression may contribute to chromosome 5q35 deletion/duplication syndromes. The 57 ClinVar CNVs suggest strong dosage sensitivity; 5q35 deletions cause Sotos syndrome-like phenotypes including intellectual disability, overgrowth, and distinctive facial features. ZNF346 may contribute to neurological phenotypes given nucleolar function in ribosome biogenesis (critical for neuronal development) and dsRNA sensing roles. Triplication may cause aberrant RNA-protein complex assembly while deletion may impair nucleolar RNA processing or dsRNA surveillance. Priority experiments include CLIP-seq to identify endogenous RNA targets, co-IP validation of STRING interactions (especially MYC, SKP1, ILF3, EIF2AK2), nucleolar localization confirmation via immunofluorescence, investigation of ZNF346 role in ribosome biogenesis using polysome profiling, assessment of ZNF346 involvement in antiviral responses via dsRNA transfection assays, structural analysis leveraging AlphaFold model (pLDDT 66.5) to identify zinc finger coordination and RNA-binding surfaces, and generation of knockout mice to assess developmental phenotype.
