## Description
WDR44 (WD repeat domain 44, also known as RAB11BP, RPH11, SYM-4) is a 913 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q5JSH3) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 62.0), characteristic of large scaffolding proteins that function through WD40 repeat-mediated protein-protein interactions and flexible linkers. The protein contains multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680, residues 499-542/596-636/637-677; IPR015943, residues 496-866; IPR020472, residues 526-540/621-635/663-677; IPR036322, residues 509-858; IPR040324, residues 145-907; PF00400, residues 509-538/601-633/639-676; G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 496-538/575-866; PS50082, residues 507-542/603-636/643-677; PS50294; PR00320) enabling protein binding activity (GO:0005515). WDR44 is located on chromosome Xq24 and interacts with the small GTPase Rab11, playing a role in endosome recycling and vesicle trafficking, located in the Golgi apparatus (GO:0005794).
