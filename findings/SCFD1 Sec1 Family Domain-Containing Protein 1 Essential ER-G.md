## Description
SCFD1 (Sec1 Family Domain-Containing Protein 1; UniProt Q8WVM8) is a 642 amino acid cytosolic trafficking regulator with 50 publications, representing a moderately-characterized member of the Sec1/Munc18 (SM) protein family. Despite structural homology to neuronal syntaxin-binding proteins, SCFD1 functions primarily as an essential ER-Golgi trafficking factor coordinating SNARE complex assembly, vesicle tethering, and autophagosome-lysosome fusion through its conserved three-domain arch architecture, with emerging roles in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), dilated cardiomyopathy, and intracellular pathogen susceptibility.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals SCFD1 contains the canonical Sec1/Munc18 SM protein fold: (1) Sec1-like protein family (IPR001619/PF00995/PTHR11679, residues 20-640, ~620 aa) — defines the VPS45/Sec1-type vesicle trafficking proteins; (2) Sec1-like domain 1 (IPR043154/G3DSA:3.40.50.2060, residues 13-145, ~133 aa) — N-terminal domain forming one side of the arch; (3) Sec1-like domain 2 (IPR027482/G3DSA:3.40.50.1910, residues 156-639, ~484 aa) — central scaffold domain comprising the arch core; (4) Sec1-like domain 3a (IPR043127/G3DSA:3.90.830.10, residues 274-538, ~265 aa) — C-terminal domain completing the arch structure; (5) Vesicle trafficking superfamily (PIRSF005715/SSF56815) — conserved across eukaryotes. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 84.6), indicating a well-folded three-domain arch typical of SM proteins that template SNARE complex assembly by binding closed syntaxin conformations.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals exclusive ultra-high confidence interactions with ER-Golgi SNARE machinery: GOSR2 (0.999—highest; Golgi SNAP Receptor Complex Member 2, Golgi Q-SNARE), SEC22B (0.997; SEC22 Family Member B, ER-Golgi R-SNARE), STX5 (0.906; Syntaxin 5, Golgi Qa-SNARE), BET1 (0.904; BET1 Golgi Vesicular Membrane Trafficking Protein, Qb-SNARE), YKT6 (0.881; YKT6 V-SNARE Homolog, lipidated R-SNARE), GOSR1 (0.869; Golgi SNAP Receptor Complex Member 1, Q-SNARE paralog), STX18 (0.848; Syntaxin 18, ER Q-SNARE), NSF (0.833; N-Ethylmaleimide Sensitive Factor, SNARE disassembly ATPase), NAPA (0.723; Alpha-SNAP, NSF adaptor), and USO1 (0.511; USO1 Vesicle Tethering Factor, long coiled-coil tether).
