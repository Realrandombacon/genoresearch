## Description
ALG1L2 is predicted to be a glycosyltransferase involved in glycoprotein synthesis. It interacts with GBE1, ZNF676, AGL, PIGL, and GPI, suggesting a role in carbohydrate metabolism. The protein is localized to the endoplasmic reticulum and nucleoli fibrillar center. Eleven pathogenic variants have been identified, indicating potential disease associations. The protein contains a glycosyl transferase, family 1 domain and is similar to chitobiosyldiphosphodolichol beta-mannosyltransferase ALG1.
