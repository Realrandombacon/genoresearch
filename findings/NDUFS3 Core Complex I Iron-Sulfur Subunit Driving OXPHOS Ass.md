## Description
NDUFS3 (NADH Dehydrogenase [Ubiquinone] Iron-Sulfur Protein 3, Mitochondrial, O75489) encodes a 264 aa core subunit of respiratory Complex I (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase) functioning as an essential structural and catalytic component of the mitochondrial electron transport chain. Structural architecture reveals a conserved 30kDa Complex I subunit domain (IPR001268/PF00329/G3DSA:3.30.460.80, residues 87-207/87-207/36-186) containing the NADH-binding pocket and iron-sulfur cluster coordination sites, plus subunit C family domain (IPR010218/MF_01357, residues 61-214/61-214) mediating assembly into the peripheral arm of Complex I. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 82.4) confirms a well-folded structure consistent with stable integration into the ~1 MDa Complex I holoenzyme. STRING interactions exclusively identify Complex I assembly partners with near-perfect confidence: NDUFB10/NDUFA9/NDUFS6 (0.999), NDUFS7 (0.996), NDUFB5 (0.993), NDUFA10 (0.985), NDUFA2 (0.983), NDUFB4 (0.976), NDUFAF1 (0.976), NDUFB7 (0.974). HPA localizes NDUFS3 to mitochondria with tissue-enhanced expression peaking in skeletal muscle (318.8 nTPM), classified as oxidoreductase/translocase involved in electron transport/respiratory chain; disease involvement includes primary mitochondrial disease. ClinVar documents 41 pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Ser251Arg, p.Arg140Gly, p.Pro197Ala, p.Arg125Cys, p.Gly13Arg, p.Tyr166His) and frameshifts (c.49dup/p.Ala17fs). Literature (190 papers) demonstrates oncogenic metabolic reprogramming: promotes melanoma proliferation via AMPK-mediated PRPS1 phosphorylation increasing purine synthesis (7x citations, 2025), transient silencing reduces RAB7 causing lysosomal dysfunction in pancreatic cancer (3x citations, 2025), alleviates sepsis-induced acute kidney injury by suppressing ferroptosis via AMPK pathway (18x citations, 2024), and Parkinson's disease ligand screening targeting Complex I dysfunction. Mechanistic hypothesis: NDUFS3 integrates into Complex I peripheral arm near the NADH oxidation site
