## Description
TNKS1BP1 (Tankyrase-1-binding protein 1, also known as TAB182 or 182 kDa tankyrase-1-binding protein, Q9C0C2) encodes a 1729 aa intrinsically disordered scaffold protein functioning as a critical molecular bridge between tankyrase PARP enzymes and the CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex, coordinating mRNA decay, actin cytoskeleton rearrangement, and cancer cell invasion through multivalent protein-protein interactions. Structural architecture reveals a predominantly disordered organization with low AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 38.2): Tankyrase 1-binding protein C-terminal domain (IPR032764/PF15327/SM01319, residues 1554-1726/1556-1726) forming the sole structured module mediating direct tankyrase binding through ankyrin repeat cluster recognition, Tankyrase 1-binding protein-like family domain (IPR040006/PTHR22042, residues 2-1728/2-1728) spanning nearly the entire protein defining the conserved scaffold architecture, with the remaining N-terminal 1553 residues (90% of protein) predicted as intrinsically disordered regions enabling flexible multivalent interactions characteristic of hub proteins in macromolecular assemblies. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence mRNA decay and cytoskeletal regulation network: CCR4-NOT transcription complex subunit 7 CNOT7 (0.994, highest) as catalytic deadenylase subunit mediating poly(A) tail removal initiating mRNA decay, Tankyrase-1 TNKS (0.991) as primary binding partner establishing PARP-dependent regulation of scaffold function, CCR4-NOT subunit 9 CNOT9 (0.986) as scaffold component of deadenylase complex, CCR4-NOT subunit 1 CNOT1 (0.984) as central platform organizing the 10-subunit complex, CNOT6L (0.983), CNOT3 (0.980), CNOT10 (0.980), CNOT11 (0.979), CNOT2 (0.978) completing the comprehensive CCR4-NOT interactome demonstrating TNKS1BP1 integration into core mRNA decay machinery, and Translocation protein 1 TOB1 (0.977) linking to antiproliferative signaling and cell cycle control. HPA expression data unavailable (gene symbol not resolved to Ensembl ID) though literature indicates ubiquitous expression with enrichment in proliferating tissues; disease variant involvement confirms pathogenic potential in cancer and developmental disorders. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all consisting of large-scale chromosome 11 copy number gains including 11p11.12-q12.2 (chr11:51183549-61422182x3), 11q12.1 (chr11:57003258-57596656x3), 11q12.1-13.3 (chr11:56895955-69295402x3), 11p11.12-q13.1 (chr11:50398499-63924462x3), 11p11.2-q12.2 (chr11:51581311-54891247x3), 11p13-q25 (chr11:32799481-134938470x3), 11p15.5-q25 (chr11:230616-134938470, chr11:230616-134938470x3), and 11q11-12.1 (chr11:55319519-58998777x3) encompassing the TNKS1BP1 locus at 11q12.1; these CNVs are associated with cancer predisposition and developmental disorders though no specific monogenic TNKS1BP1 phenotype established, potentially affecting mRNA homeostasis and cytoskeletal dynamics through dosage sensitivity. Conservation from mouse P58871 (1720 aa, 99% length identity) confirms essential scaffold function across mammals with high sequence identity particularly in C-terminal tankyrase-binding domain; predominantly disordered architecture (pLDDT 38.2) is functionally adaptive enabling conformational flexibility for simultaneous binding to multiple CCR4-NOT subunits and tankyrase; exceptional STRING interactions (all >0.977) position TNKS1BP1 as integral non-redundant bridge between PARP signaling and mRNA decay pathways; structured C-terminal domain (residues 1554-1726) provides specific tankyrase anchoring while disordered N-terminal region mediates CCR4-NOT engagement; 9 ClinVar CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency sensitivity for proper gene regulation; literature (28 papers) demonstrates TNKS1BP1 facilitates ubiquitination of CNOT4 by TRIM21 promoting hepatocellular carcinoma progression and immune evasion (14x 2024), TNKS1BP1 mediates alveolar type II cell senescence and radiation-induced lung injury through suppressing EEF2 degradation (9x 2024), TCF3-activated FAM201A enhances triple-negative breast cancer proliferation via miR-186-5p/TNKS1BP1 axis (19x 2020), and TNKS1BP1 regulates actin cytoskeleton rearrangement and cancer cell invasion (39x 2017); mechanistic proposal involves TNKS1BP1-mediated recruitment of tankyrase to CCR4-NOT complex through C-terminal domain binding enabling PARsylation-dependent regulation of deadenylase activity, coordination of mRNA decay rates for cell cycle and invasion-related transcripts, TRIM21-dependent CNOT4 ubiquitination enhancing CCR4-NOT stability in cancer cells, and EEF2 stabilization preventing translation elongation factor degradation during radiation stress.
