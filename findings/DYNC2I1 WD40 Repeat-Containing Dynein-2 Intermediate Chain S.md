## Description
DYNC2I1 (Cytoplasmic dynein 2 intermediate chain 1, also known as WDR60 or DNCLI1, Q8WVS4) encodes a 1066 aa intracellular scaffolding protein functioning as an essential intermediate chain component of the cytoplasmic dynein-2 motor complex that drives retrograde intraflagellar transport (IFT) within primary cilia, critical for ciliary signaling, skeletal development, and Hedgehog pathway regulation. Structural architecture reveals a large multi-domain scaffold built around beta-propeller modules: three canonical WD40 repeats (IPR001680/SM00320/SSF50978, positions 686-725/901-938/941-983/640-729/777-988) forming triple beta-propeller structures for protein-protein interactions with GO term for protein binding (GO:0005515), two YVTN repeat-like/Quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase superfamily domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10/IPR015943, positions 614-830/833-1012) providing additional interaction surfaces, belonging to the Cytoplasmic dynein 2 intermediate chain 1 family (IPR042505/PTHR16022, positions 6-1058/6-1058) with GO terms for dynein light chain binding (GO:0045503), dynein heavy chain binding (GO:0045504), and intraciliary transport (GO:0042073); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 63.5, medium confidence) reveals a partially structured protein with organized WD40 beta-propeller domains embedded in extended flexible regions consistent with scaffolding function requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly. STRING interactions define the complete dynein-2 holoenzyme complex: light intermediate chain DYNC2LI1 (0.999, highest confidence) forming stable heterodimeric core, heavy chain DYNC2H1 (0.999) providing AAA+ ATPase motor activity, intermediate chain WDR34 (0.956) as paralogous partner completing the intermediate chain pair, light chains DYNLL1 (0.956), DYNLRB2 (0.956), TCTEX1D2 (0.928), DYNLT3 (0.891), DYNLT1 (0.860), and DYNLRB1 (0.821) assembling into the complete motor complex, and cytoplasmic dynein-1 component DYNC1LI1 (0.801) suggesting potential cross-talk between dynein isoforms. HPA localizes DYNC2I1 to primary cilium, centrosome, basal body, and cytosol with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue and cancer specificity indicating housekeeping ciliary function, low immune cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity; classified as disease-related gene, potential drug target, transporter involved in cilium biogenesis/degradation with ciliopathy and disease variant involvement. ClinVar documents 133 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including critical splice site mutations c.1703-1G>A (acceptor) and c.935+1G>C (donor) abolishing proper splicing, frameshift mutations c.1647dup (p.Glu550fs), c.671del (p.Asp224fs), c.2651_2652dup (p.Ile885fs), and c.1566_1578del (p.Asn522fs) causing premature termination, intronic variant c.1461-18T>A potentially affecting splicing regulation, single allele duplication, and chromosome 7q copy number alterations: gain at 7q36.2-36.3 (chr7:153906860-158923491x3) and loss at 7q33-36.3 (chr7:137521595-159119707x1); these variants cause autosomal recessive short rib-polydactyly syndrome (SRPS), a lethal skeletal ciliopathy characterized by shortened limbs, narrow thorax, visceral anomalies, and polydactyly. Literature search returned only 3 papers confirming genuine dark gene status despite severe disease phenotype: recent 2026 study identified novel homozygous missense variant by clinical exome sequencing in prenatal SRPS diagnosis, 2023 paper demonstrated multiple interactions of dynein-2 complex with IFT-B complex required for effective intraflagellar transport showing DYNC2I1 mediates anterograde dynein-2 transport within cilia before engaging retrograde trafficking, and 2022 study linked sporadic hypothalamic hamartoma to ciliopathy with somatic and bi-allelic contributions implicating dynein-2 dysfunction; triple WD40 repeat architecture enables simultaneous binding to multiple dynein-2 subunits stabilizing the holoenzyme; medium AlphaFold confidence (63.5) reflects intrinsic flexibility required for IFT cargo recognition and release; exceptional STRING scores (0.999) with DYNC2LI1/DYNC2H1 indicate obligate complex membership; ciliary/centrosomal localization positions DYNC2I1 at the base of cilia where dynein-2 loads onto IFT trains; high pathogenic variant burden (133) underscores essential non-redundant function in skeletal development; conservation from mouse (Q8C761, 999 aa) confirms evolutionary constraint.
