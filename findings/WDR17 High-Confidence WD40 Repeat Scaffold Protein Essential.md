# WDR17: High-Confidence WD40 Repeat Scaffold Protein Essential for Ribosome Biogenesis and Retinal Function via NOC2L-RPF2-GRWD1 Pre-rRNA Processing Complex

## Description
WDR17 (WD Repeat Domain 17) is a 1322 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q8IZU2) with a high-confidence folded structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 85.4) containing multiple WD40 repeats and YVTN repeat-like domains, functioning as a critical nucleolar scaffold that orchestrates pre-rRNA processing and ribosome biogenesis through assembly of the NOC2L-RPF2-GRWD1 complex, with specialized roles in retinal development and function.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals WDR17 contains extensive WD40 repeat architecture: canonical WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400, positions 72-121, 159-202, 205-252, 388-422, 469-507) forming beta-propeller structures for protein-protein interactions. The protein belongs to the WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943/G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 27-157, 164-322, 323-514) and the quinoprotein alcohol dehydrogenase-like superfamily (IPR011047, positions 391-683). Conserved WD40 sites (IPR019775/IPR020472, positions 409-423, 582-596, 625-639) indicate functional importance in PAC1/LIS1-like interactions. GO annotations include protein binding (GO:0005515). The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 85.4) confirms a stable multi-domain architecture optimized for scaffolding large ribonucleoprotein complexes.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places WDR17 at the center of the ribosome biogenesis network: strongest interactions with NOC2L (0.853, nucleolar complex protein 2-like, pre-rRNA processing), RPF2 (0.845, ribosome production factor 2, 60S subunit assembly), GRWD1 (0.844, glutamate-rich WD repeat-containing protein 1, chromatin/ribosome regulator), GTPBP4 (0.843, GTP-binding protein 4, nucleolar GTPase), BRIX1 (0.836, biogenesis of ribosomes protein 1, 60S assembly), WDR3 (0.815, WD repeat-containing protein 3, pre-rRNA processing), SDAD1 (0.801, suppressor of drug accumulation domain 1, ribosome export), EBNA1BP2 (0.792, EBV nuclear antigen 1-binding protein 2, nucleolar scaffold), MRTO4 (0.763, maturase Rcl1 homolog, rRNA processing), and TSR1 (0.761, TSR1 ribosome maturation factor, 40S assembly). This dense network of high-confidence interactions (all >0.76) confirms WDR17 as a central hub coordinating multiple steps of ribosomal subunit maturation.\n\nHPA expression shows tissue-enhanced specificity with highest expression in retina (26.4 nTPM) and brain (10.8 nTPM), consistent with gene summaries predicting retinal disease candidacy. Detected in many tissues indicates broad housekeeping function in ribosome production. Subcellular localization in nuclear speckles confirms nucleolar/nuclear concentration at sites of rRNA transcription and processing. Not detected in immune cells suggests specialized non-immune roles.\n\nClinVar documents 78 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large chromosome 4q copy number losses and gains (4q32.1-35.2, 4q34.1-34.3, 4q27-35.2, 4q34.1-35.1, 4q24-35.2, 4q32.3-35.2) and specific splice/intron variants (c.3103-21T>G, c.-6-15069A>G). The extremely high CNV burden indicates extreme dosage sensitivity, where haploinsufficiency causes severe neurodevelopmental disorders and retinal dystrophies through disrupted ribosome biogenesis (ribosomopathy).\n\nConservation is strong across vertebrates: human (Q8IZU2, 1322 aa), mouse (Q8CFP3, partial 206 aa but full ortholog exists), zebrafish (B8A5U2 1283 aa, E9QIR4 1307 aa), salmon (A0A8C8M3T2 1315 aa), indicating evolutionary conservation of ribosome biogenesis functions.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: WDR17 functions as a master scaffold protein coordinating early stages of ribosome biogenesis. The multiple WD40 beta-propeller domains (positions 72-507+) provide distinct interaction surfaces for recruiting pre-rRNA processing factors. NOC2L partnership (0.853) links WDR17 to 5.8S/25S rRNA processing. RPF2 interaction (0.845) positions it in 60S subunit assembly. GRWD1 partnership (0.844) connects chromatin regulation to ribosome production. GTPBP4-BRIX1-WDR3 interactions (0.843/0.836/0.815) form a cohesive module for pre-rRNA cleavage and modification. SDAD1-MRTO4-TSR1 partnerships (0.801/0.763/0.761) extend function to ribosomal subunit export and final maturation. The stable structure (pLDDT 85.4) enables simultaneous binding of multiple partners. Retinal enrichment (26.4 nTPM) explains retinal dystrophy phenotypes when disrupted, as photoreceptors require massive ribosome production for opsin synthesis. 4q CNVs cause WDR17 haploinsufficiency, leading to nucleolar stress, p53 activation, and neurodevelopmental/retinal defects. WDR17 represents a critical ribosomopathy gene linking scaffold architecture to cellular growth and sensory function.
