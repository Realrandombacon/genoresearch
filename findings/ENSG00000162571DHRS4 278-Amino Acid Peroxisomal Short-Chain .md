## Description
DHRS4 (UniProt: Q9BTZ2, aliases Dehydrogenase/reductase SDR family member 4, peroxisomal carbonyl reductase) encodes a 278 amino acid peroxisomal enzyme representing a partially characterized dark gene with 66 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 14q11.2-23.1, DHRS4 is a short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase (SDR) family enzyme catalyzing NAD(P)-dependent reduction of carbonyl substrates in retinoic acid metabolism pathways.
