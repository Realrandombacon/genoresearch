# LRRC14: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein with PRAME Domain Architecture and NF-κB Pathway Associations

**Date:** 2026-03-23T08:57:39.063568

**Quality Score:** 1.6/10  (E=3.2, D=0.0) [POOR]

## Description
LRRC14 encodes a 493-amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) containing protein with PRAME-like domain architecture. InterPro analysis reveals multiple conserved domains: an N-terminal PRAME-like domain (PF27091, IPR060378, residues 12-67), a central LRR domain superfamily (IPR032675, G3DSA:3.80.10.10, residues 170-424) adopting ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold, and a C-terminal PRAME domain (PF27089, IPR060377, residues 193-480). The protein belongs to the LRRC14/PRAME family (IPR050694, PTHR14224). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization, and cell type-enhanced expression patterns. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with IKBKB (score 0.857) and CHUK (0.856)—the catalytic subunits of the IκB kinase (IKK) complex that phosphorylates IκB inhibitors to activate NF-κB signaling—plus E4F1 (0.746), ALG12 (0.735), CUL2 (0.681), LRRC24 (0.655), TRMT2A (0.646), PPP1R16A (0.628), BRAT1 (0.618), and MFSD3 (0.601). This interaction network suggests LRRC14 functions as a scaffold or regulatory component in NF-κB signaling and ubiquitin ligase complexes (CUL2). ClinVar documents 58 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 8q24.3 copy number gains and deletions, indicating dosage sensitivity. The LRR architecture suggests protein-protein interaction functions, potentially modulating IKK complex assembly or substrate recruitment in inflammatory and stress response pathways.
