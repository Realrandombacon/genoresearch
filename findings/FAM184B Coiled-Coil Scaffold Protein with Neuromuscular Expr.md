## Description
FAM184B encodes a 1060 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9ULE4) featuring a prominent N-terminal coiled-coil domain (PF15665, IPR039478, residues 48-251) characteristic of the FAM184A/B family. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (3.9 nTPM), brain (1.8 nTPM), and retina (1.6 nTPM), suggesting roles in neuromuscular tissues. The protein is classified as intracellular with cancer-enhanced expression patterns. Critically, ClinVar documents 42 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 4p, indicating dosage sensitivity and clinical relevance. The coiled-coil domain architecture suggests oligomerization capability, potentially functioning as a scaffolding protein that organizes protein complexes in muscle and neural tissues. Mouse ortholog (Q0KK56, 942 aa) shows moderate conservation. The combination of coiled-coil structural motifs, neuromuscular expression, and pathogenic CNVs suggests FAM184B may function in cytoskeletal organization or signal transduction complexes, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression causing developmental phenotypes.
