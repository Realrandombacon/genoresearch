## Description
CMTM3 (CKLF-like MARVEL Transmembrane Domain-containing protein 3, Q96MX0) encodes a 182 aa type III transmembrane protein functioning as a membrane trafficking regulator that controls endothelial barrier integrity, cytokine signaling, and immune checkpoint dynamics. Structural architecture reveals a canonical MARVEL domain (IPR008253/PF01284/PS51225, residues 36-155/36-149/36-155) consisting of four transmembrane helices that mediate membrane apposition events critical for vesicular transport and tight junction assembly; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 79.8) confirms a well-folded transmembrane structure consistent with stable membrane integration. STRING interactions define a CMTM family network with paralogs CMTM1 (0.975), CMTM7 (0.896), CMTM6 (0.577), CMTM4 (0.426), CMTM2 (0.418), CMTM8 (0.412), plus chemokine-like factor CKLF (0.440) and splicing factor SF3B3 (0.496). HPA localizes CMTM3 to nucleoplasm with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, classified as membrane protein involved in chemotaxis with cytokine molecular function. ClinVar documents 27 pathogenic variants including 16q11.2-24.3 CNVs and promoter variants (c.-474T>A). Literature (93 papers) demonstrates emerging oncogenic and inflammatory roles: regulates vascular endothelial cell dysfunction by influencing pulmonary vascular endothelial permeability and inflammation in ARDS (4x citations, 2025), promotes colitis-associated carcinogenesis via CLTC (clathrin heavy chain) stabilization and VE-cadherin modulation (2x citations, 2025), HECTD3 E3 ligase mediates ubiquitination of AKT-phosphorylated CMTM3 in HER2-overexpressed breast cancer (2025), regulates proliferation and migration of glioblastoma U251 cells via NF-κB signaling pathway (2025), differential expression in right vs left colon tumors. Mechanistic hypothesis: CMTM3 MARVEL domain mediates membrane curvature and vesicle fusion at endothelial cell junctions, regulating VE-cadherin endocytosis/recycling via clathrin-coated vesicles; CLTC interaction stabilizes clathrin machinery controlling adherens junction dynamics; in ARDS, CMTM3 dysregulation increases endothelial permeability allowing inflammatory cell infiltration; in cancer, CMTM3 undergoes AKT-dependent phosphorylation creating phosphodegron for HECTD3-mediated ubiquitination, linking growth factor signaling to CMTM3 turnover; similar to CMTM6 which stabilizes PD-L1, CMTM3 may regulate immune checkpoint trafficking affecting tumor immune evasion; NF-κB activation by CMTM3 promotes pro-inflammatory cytokine production driving colitis-associated carcinogenesis.
