## Description
GSTT2B (Glutathione S-Transferase Theta-2B, UniProt P0CG30) is a 244-amino acid theta-class glutathione S-transferase functioning in phase II detoxification metabolism and oxidative stress protection. InterPro analysis confirms canonical GST theta architecture: GST_N_Theta domain (cd03050/IPR040075, aa 3-78) for glutathione binding; C-terminal alpha helical domain (cd03183/IPR040077, aa 91-216) for substrate specificity; GST N-terminal domain (IPR004045, aa 1-82) with thioredoxin-like superfamily fold (IPR036249, aa 3-86); GST C-terminal domain (IPR004046, aa 127-198; IPR010987, aa 88-224; IPR036282, aa 81-236); and glutathione transferase family membership (IPR040079/IPR051369, aa 2-224/1-223) with GO annotations for glutathione transferase activity and xenobiotic metabolism. Functional hypothesis: GSTT2B serves as a theta-class glutathione S-transferase catalyzing conjugation of reduced glutathione (GSH) to electrophilic xenobiotics, carcinogens, and oxidative stress products, protecting cells from DNA damage and enabling detoxification of environmental toxins, with polymorphic copy number variation affecting cancer susceptibility and autoimmune disease modulation. Mechanistic proposal: The N-terminal thioredoxin-like domain (aa 3-86) binds glutathione via conserved catalytic residues, positioning the GSH thiol for nucleophilic attack; the C-terminal alpha-helical domain (aa 91-216) forms the substrate-binding pocket determining specificity for hydrophobic electrophiles; GSTT2B dimerizes to create two active sites; conjugation reactions detoxify carcinogenic epoxides (generated by CYP1A1/CYP1B1), reactive oxygen species products, and lipid peroxidation aldehydes; coordinated action with EPHX1 (epoxide hydrolase), PRDX6/GPX4 (peroxiredoxins), and GSR (glutathione regeneration) creates comprehensive antioxidant defense; GSTT2B copy number polymorphisms alter enzyme dosage affecting detoxification capacity - deletions increase esophageal cancer risk by reducing carcinogen clearance; in rheumatoid arthritis, GSTT2B-expressing stem cells may exert therapeutic effects through enhanced oxidative stress protection and modulation of inflammatory lipid mediators (prostaglandin metabolism via HPGDS interaction); theta-class specificity suggests roles in metabolizing specific substrate classes distinct from alpha/mu/pi-class GSTs.
