## Description
LMCD1 (UniProt Q9NZU5), also known as Dyxin, encodes a 365 aa LIM-domain and PET (Prickle-Espinas-Testin) domain-containing protein functioning as a transcriptional co-regulator that modulates GATA-family transcription factor activity through direct protein-protein interactions mediated by dual C-terminal LIM zinc-finger domains.
