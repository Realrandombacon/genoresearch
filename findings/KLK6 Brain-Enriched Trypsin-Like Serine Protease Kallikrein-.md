## Description
KLK6 (Kallikrein related peptidase 6, also known as Kallikrein-6 or Zyme, Q92876) encodes a 244 aa secreted trypsin-like serine protease functioning as an extracellular hydrolase that cleaves amyloid precursor protein (APP), alpha-synuclein (SNCA), and corneodesmosin (CDSN) critical for neuronal protein homeostasis, epidermal desquamation, and cancer progression through regulated proteolytic cascades. Structural architecture reveals a canonical chymotrypsin-fold serine protease: Trypsin domain (IPR001254/PF00089, residues 21-242/23-237) forming the catalytic core with characteristic beta-barrel structure, Peptidase S1A chymotrypsin family (IPR001314, residues 48-63/102-116/190-202) containing the catalytic triad, PA clan peptidase superfamily (IPR009003, residues 9-242) indicating evolutionary relationship to trypsin-like enzymes, with conserved histidine active site (IPR018114/PS00134, residues 58-63) and serine active site (IPR033116/PS00135, residues 191-202) essential for catalytic mechanism; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 91.8, very high confidence) confirms an exceptionally well-folded serine protease with organized catalytic domain architecture consistent with stable extracellular enzyme requiring precise active site geometry for substrate recognition and hydrolytic catalysis with GO terms for serine-type endopeptidase activity (GO:0004252) and proteolysis (GO:0006508). STRING interactions define a neurodegeneration and epidermal differentiation network: amyloid precursor protein APP (0.967, highest confidence) as key Alzheimer disease substrate suggesting KLK6-mediated APP processing affecting amyloid-beta production, corneodesmosin CDSN (0.748) as epidermal substrate for desquamation, alpha-synuclein SNCA (0.716) as Parkinson disease-related substrate indicating role in synucleinopathy pathogenesis, related kallikrein KLK7 (0.669) as partner protease in proteolytic cascades, transcription factor YAF2 (0.662) linking to apoptosis regulation, involucrin IVL (0.627) as epidermal differentiation marker, glucose metabolism regulator GLIS1 (0.572), serine protease inhibitor SPINK5 (0.528) as endogenous regulator, small proline-rich protein SPRR1B (0.470) in cornified envelope formation, and alpha-2-macroglobulin-like protein A2ML1 (0.426) as protease inhibitor. HPA localizes KLK6 to nucleoplasm, nuclear membrane, and cytokinetic bridge with tissue-enriched expression highest in brain (492.8 nTPM) reflecting primary neurological function, detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced expression across tumor types particularly ovarian and breast cancer suggesting biomarker utility, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell specificity; classified as cancer-related gene, enzyme, plasma protein, predicted intracellular and secreted protein with hydrolase, protease, and serine protease molecular functions.
