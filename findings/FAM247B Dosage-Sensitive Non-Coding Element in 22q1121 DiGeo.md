## Description
FAM247B (Gene ID: 117751732) maps to chromosome 22q11.21 (18.5-18.5 Mbp), within the well-characterized DiGeorge syndrome critical region. Despite annotation as "family with sequence similarity 247 member B", multiple lines of evidence suggest this may not encode a canonical protein-coding gene: (1) No UniProt accession found after extensive searches; (2) No InterPro domains identifiable; (3) STRING interaction search failed (400 error - gene not in database); (4) HPA expression data absent; (5) No PubMed publications. However, ClinVar contains 145 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variants (CNVs) including deletions and duplications spanning 22q11.21. These CNVs are associated with DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome phenotypes. The high CNV burden suggests FAM247B is dosage-sensitive. Sequence analysis of downloaded FAM247B homologs reveals short ORFs (79-232 aa) with no conserved domains. Conservation data: FAM247A paralog exists on same chromosome, suggesting recent duplication. Mechanistic hypothesis: FAM247B likely functions as a cis-regulatory lncRNA or enhancer RNA rather than protein-coding gene. The pathogenic CNVs disrupt regulatory chromatin architecture in the 22q11.21 locus, affecting expression of neighboring genes (e.g., TBX1, COMT). The "protein" annotation may reflect transcriptional noise or short uORF peptides without stable folded structure. This represents a class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding elements misannotated as protein-coding.
