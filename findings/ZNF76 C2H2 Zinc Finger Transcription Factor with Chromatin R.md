## Description
ZNF76 encodes a 570 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt P36508) localized to the nucleoplasm and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals 3 canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 165-254 and 285-309, characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription regulators. The protein belongs to the PTHR14003 family of diverse zinc finger transcription regulators. AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 57.7), suggesting structured zinc finger DNA-binding regions coupled with intrinsically disordered transactivation domains typical of transcription factors. STRING interaction network identifies 10 high-confidence protein partners: TCP11 (0.975), TBP (0.646), DMAP1 (0.582), MRPS11 (0.570), EZH1 (0.558), GRHL1 (0.555), CMTR1 (0.534), HDAC1 (0.502), UCKL1 (0.487), and ULK3 (0.454). Notable interactions with TBP (TATA-box-binding protein) and HDAC1 (histone deacetylase 1) suggest ZNF76 functions in transcriptional initiation complexes and chromatin remodeling. HPA expression data shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, consistent with a basal transcriptional regulator. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses on chromosome 6p21.31, implicating ZNF76 dosage sensitivity in disease. Conservation analysis is needed but C2H2 zinc finger architecture is deeply conserved across vertebrates. Functional hypothesis: ZNF76 acts as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulator that recruits chromatin modifiers (HDAC1, EZH1) and basal transcription machinery (TBP) to regulate RNA polymerase II-dependent gene expression, potentially functioning in epigenetic silencing or activation depending on cofactor recruitment.
