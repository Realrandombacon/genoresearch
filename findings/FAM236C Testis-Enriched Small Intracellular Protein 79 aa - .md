# FAM236C: Testis-Enriched Small Intracellular Protein (79 aa) - X-Linked Gene with 61 Pathogenic CNV Variants in Xp22.33-q28 Critical Region

## Description
FAM236C (Gene ID: 109729126, UniProt P0DP71) encodes a 79 amino acid small intracellular protein of unknown function mapping to Xq13.1 (72,912,614-72,913,400 bp on GRCh38, ~787 bp genomic span). Human Protein Atlas classification: predicted intracellular protein with testis-enriched expression (2.3 nTPM in testis, tissue-enriched pattern, detected in single tissue). The small protein size (79 aa, ~8.7 kDa) and testis-specific expression suggest specialized function in spermatogenesis or male germline development. STRING interaction network: no protein-protein interactions detected (9606.ENSP00000490543), indicating either: (1) protein functions as monomer without stable complexes; (2) interactions are transient/conditional not captured by STRING; (3) protein is genuinely uncharacterized with unknown binding partners. CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 61 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number variations (CNVs) affecting the Xp22.33-q28 chromosomal region (essentially the entire X chromosome). Variants include large duplications (Xp22.33-q28: 2757837-156030895, 26102-155996431, 10679-156022206, multiple others spanning 150+ Mb) and deletions (Xp22.33-q28: 14245-155999293, 26102-155996431, 10679-156013167, 251879-156004066). These CNVs cause X-linked aneuploidy syndromes including Turner syndrome (45,X), Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY), Triple X syndrome (47,XXX), and X chromosome duplication/deletion syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, reproductive abnormalities, and dysmorphic features. The Xp22.33-q28 critical region contains hundreds of dosage-sensitive X-linked genes including: AR (androgen receptor), FMR1 (fragile X mental retardation 1), MECP2 (methyl-CpG binding protein 2, Rett syndrome), DMD (dystrophin), and numerous X-linked intellectual disability genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: FAM236C functions as testis-specific intracellular regulator potentially involved in: (1) spermatogenesis - testis-enriched expression (2.3 nTPM) suggests role in germ cell development, meiosis, or sperm maturation; (2) X-chromosome inactivation escape - Xq13.1 location may indicate involvement in XCI regulation; (3) chromatin organization - small basic proteins often function as chromatin binders or histone chaperones; (4) ubiquitin-proteasome system - small proteins frequently serve as E3 ligase adaptors or deubiquitinase regulators. The 61 ClinVar CNVs reflect X chromosome dosage sensitivity rather than FAM236C-specific pathogenicity - large X chromosome CNVs cause phenotypes through combined haploinsufficiency/triplosensitivity of multiple X-linked genes. In males (hemizygous), FAM236C deletions may contribute to X-linked intellectual disability or male infertility phenotypes. In females, X chromosome CNVs cause skewed X-inactivation patterns affecting FAM236C expression dosage. Testis-enriched expression predicts male-specific phenotypes when FAM236C function is disrupted: oligospermia, asthenospermia, or non-obstructive azoospermia. Conservation across primates (Macaca fascicularis, Macaca mulatta, Callithrix jacchus UniProt entries) indicates functional constraint. Lack of InterPro domains and STRING interactions suggests: (1) protein may contain intrinsically disordered regions without structured domains; (2) functions through short linear motifs (SLiMs) not captured by domain databases; (3) represents a taxonomically restricted orphan protein with primate-specific function. This represents a "dark gene" class of small testis-enriched proteins with X-linked inheritance and clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
