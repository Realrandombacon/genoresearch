## Description
PP2D1 (Protein phosphatase 2C-like domain-containing protein 1, UniProt A8MPX8) is a 630-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 3p26.2. InterPro analysis reveals canonical PPM-type phosphatase architecture with multiple catalytic domains: PPM-type phosphatase-like domain (IPR001932/SM00332, aa 162-619), protein phosphatase 2C family (IPR015655/PF00481/PTHR13832, aa 178-618/358-473), and PPM-type phosphatase domain profile (PS51746, aa 170-621). The CATH-Gene3D superfamily (G3DSA:3.60.40.10) identifies three distinct phosphatase subdomains (aa 163-321, 356-479, 577-626), suggesting a multi-domain catalytic core characteristic of Mg2+/Mn2+-dependent serine/threonine phosphatases. GO annotations confirm protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity (GO:0004722) and protein dephosphorylation (GO:0006470). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 71.6), indicating a well-folded stable structure with defined catalytic pockets. Human Protein Atlas shows group-enriched expression with testis enhancement (6.9 nTPM) and bone marrow expression (1.8 nTPM), nucleoplasmic and intermediate filament subcellular localization, and cell type enhanced specificity — suggesting roles in spermatogenesis and hematopoiesis. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners spanning transcriptional regulation and mitochondrial function: SAXO1 (0.499, microtubule stabilizer), PPTC7 (0.492, protein phosphatase targeting mitochondrial proteins), HCFC1R1 (0.457, host cell factor regulator), SPINT4 (0.445, serine protease inhibitor), ZMYM5 (0.425, zinc finger MYM-type), MTFMT (0.419, mitochondrial tRNA methyltransferase), and MED31/MED14/MED21 (0.415/0.412/0.406, Mediator complex subunits). This interactome suggests PP2D1 functions at the intersection of transcriptional regulation (Mediator complex) and mitochondrial protein homeostasis (PPTC7, MTFMT). ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number gains across 3p26.3-14.3, 3p26.3-22.3, 3p26.3-24.1 and deletions at 3p25.1-24.2, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting PP2D1 cause disease phenotypes. Conservation across mammals (mouse Q8BVT6, 620 aa; rat D3ZZT7, 625 aa; mouse Ppm1d ortholog Q9QZ67, 598 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental phosphorylation signaling. PP2D1 likely functions as a regulatory phosphatase dephosphorylating transcription factors, Mediator complex components, or mitochondrial proteins, coordinating nuclear-cytoplasmic signaling during germ cell development and hematopoietic differentiation.
