## Description
CLUAP1 (Clusterin-associated protein 1, also designated IFT38, Q96AJ1) encodes a 413 aa protein functioning as an essential peripheral subcomplex component of the intraflagellar transport B (IFT-B) complex required for anterograde cargo trafficking during cilia assembly, maintenance of ciliary signaling, and photoreceptor outer segment development. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 77.4): Clusterin-associated protein-1 family domain (IPR019366/PF10234/PTHR21547, residues 1-413/14-283) defining the conserved IFT38 fold unique to this ciliopathy protein family with no additional characterized subdomains suggesting a compact globular structure optimized for protein-protein interactions within the IFT train. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence intraflagellar transport network with near-perfect scores reflecting stable complex membership: TRAF3IP1/IFT54 (0.999, highest) as microtubule-binding component linking IFT-B to cargo adaptors, IFT52 (0.999) as core IFT-B subunit forming the structural backbone, IFT57 (0.995) connecting to kinesin-2 motor complex, IFT20 (0.995) as Golgi-associated sorting component linking ciliary vesicle trafficking, IFT88 (0.992) as essential core subunit required for IFT particle assembly, TTC26/IFT56 (0.992) as peripheral subcomplex member, IFT27 (0.992) as small GTPase regulating IFT entry/exit, IFT46 (0.991) connecting to ODA-docking complex for motile cilia, IFT80 (0.990) as large scaffold subunit, and IFT172 (0.989) as critical subunit mutated in Jeune syndrome and retinal degeneration. HPA expression data unavailable (gene symbol not resolved) though literature indicates ubiquitous expression with enrichment in ciliated tissues including retina, kidney, and neural tube; disease variant involvement confirms pathogenic potential in ciliopathy spectrum. ClinVar documents 49 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations p.Asp315His (c.943G>C), p.Leu6Arg (c.17T>G), p.Ala93Val (c.278C>T), p.Lys85Arg (c.254A>G), and synonymous variant p.Asn364= (c.1092T>C) potentially affecting splicing; large-scale chromosome 16p13.3 copy number gains at chr16:513767-5555136x3, chr16:85881-12268399x3, chr16:2606711-3935836x3, chr16:111043-6627459x3, and chr16:84485-5251013x3 encompass the CLUAP1 locus at 16p13.3; these variants cause Joubert syndrome and oral-facial-digital syndrome overlap phenotypes characterized by cerebellar vermis hypoplasia (molar tooth sign), polydactyly, renal cysts, and retinal dystrophy, plus Leber congenital amaurosis (early-onset blindness) due to photoreceptor cilia defects. Conservation from mouse Q8R3P7 (413 aa, 100% length identity), rat Q6AYJ5 (402 aa, 97% identity), and zebrafish Q7ZVC2 (427 aa, strong conservation) confirms essential ciliogenesis function across vertebrates with particularly high sequence identity in regions mediating IFT-B complex assembly; exceptional STRING interactions (all >0.989) position CLUAP1/IFT38 as integral non-redundant component of the 16-subunit IFT-B complex; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 77.4) enables mapping of interaction surfaces for TRAF3IP1/IFT52 binding; 49 ClinVar variants including truncating and missense mutations underscore critical role in preventing ciliopathy; literature (33 papers) demonstrates CLUAP1/IFT38 CRISPR editing reveals role in actin arrangement (23x 2018), IFT-B complex architecture determination establishing CLUAP1 as peripheral subcomplex essential component (107x 2016), compound heterozygous alterations causing Joubert/oral-facial-digital overlap syndrome (22x 2017), and hypomorphic mutations identified in Leber congenital amaurosis (27x 2015); mechanistic proposal involves CLUAP1-mediated stabilization of IFT-B peripheral subcomplex enabling efficient anterograde transport of ciliary building blocks (tubulin, membrane proteins) from basal body to ciliary tip, coordination with TRAF3IP1 for cargo loading, and specialized role in photoreceptor outer segment renewal where defective IFT causes progressive retinal degeneration.
