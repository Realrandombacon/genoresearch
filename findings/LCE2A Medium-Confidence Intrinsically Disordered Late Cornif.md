## Description
LCE2A (Late Cornified Envelope 2A, alias LEP9) is a 106 amino acid predicted intracellular structural protein (UniProt Q5TA79) with medium-confidence partially structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 53.4) containing late cornified envelope domains (residues 1-106) with proline-rich repeats (PRORICH, residues 2-14/15-24/30-38), functioning as a component of the cornified cell envelope that regulates epidermal barrier formation and keratinization through proline-rich repeat-mediated protein cross-linking and interactions with LCE1C, LCE2C, LCE2B, LCE6A, LCE1A, LCE4A, LCE1B, LCE1F, LCE1D, and LCE3A.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals LCE2A contains a classic late cornified envelope architecture: Late cornified envelope protein family (IPR028205/PF14672, residues 1-106) with epidermis development function (GO:0008544), and PRORICH proline-rich repeats (PR00021, residues 2-14/15-24/30-38) that serve as substrates for transglutaminase-mediated cross-linking. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 53.4) confirms a partially structured protein with intrinsically disordered regions, characteristic of cornified envelope proteins that require conformational flexibility for extensive protein cross-linking during terminal epidermal differentiation.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places LCE2A within the late cornified envelope protein network with very high-confidence interactions: strongest interaction with LCE1C (0.924, late cornified envelope protein 1C), followed by LCE2C (0.913, late cornified envelope protein 2C), LCE2B (0.908, late cornified envelope protein 2B), LCE6A (0.875, late cornified envelope protein 6A), LCE1A (0.803, late cornified envelope protein 1A), LCE4A (0.797, late cornified envelope protein 4A), LCE1B (0.794, late cornified envelope protein 1B), LCE1F (0.779, late cornified envelope protein 1F), LCE1D (0.755, late cornified envelope protein 1D), and LCE3A (0.732, late cornified envelope protein 3A). The extensive LCE family interaction network (all scores >0.732) confirms LCE2A as a core component of the cornified envelope protein complex that forms the insoluble protein scaffold beneath the plasma membrane of terminally differentiated keratinocytes.\n\nHPA expression shows tissue-enriched specificity with skin enrichment (111.6 nTPM), detected in some tissues, cancer-enriched specificity, not detected in immune cells, and cell type enriched specificity. Protein class includes essential proteins and predicted intracellular proteins. Biological process includes keratinization. The extreme skin enrichment (111.6 nTPM) indicates LCE2A functions specifically in epidermal barrier formation and stratum corneum development. Cancer-enriched expression suggests LCE2A dysregulation in squamous cell carcinomas and other epithelial malignancies.\n\nClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including extensive chromosome 1 copy number losses (1q21.3, chr1:151707888-153275358) and gains (1q21.1-44, 1q21.1-23.1, 1q12-23.1, 1q21.3, 1p36.33-q44, 1q21.1-21.3). The extensive CNV burden indicates extreme dosage sensitivity within the epidermal differentiation complex (EDC) at 1q21, where haploinsufficiency or overexpression causes defective cornified envelope assembly, impaired epidermal barrier function, and potential ichthyosis or psoriasis susceptibility.\n\nConservation is present across primates and mammals, indicating evolutionary conservation of cornified envelope functions in epidermal barrier formation.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: LCE2A functions as a structural component of the cornified cell envelope that forms during terminal keratinocyte differentiation. The proline-rich repeats (residues 2-14/15-24/30-38) serve as substrates for transglutaminase TGM1/TGM3-mediated epsilon-(gamma-glutamyl)lysine cross-links, creating an insoluble protein scaffold. The intrinsically disordered architecture (pLDDT 53.4) enables extensive protein-protein interactions and cross-linking flexibility. LCE1C-LCE2C-LCE2B partnerships (0.924/0.913/0.908) position LCE2A within the core LCE2 subfamily that co-assembles during late epidermal differentiation. LCE6A-LCE1A-LCE4A interactions (0.875/0.803/0.797) link LCE2A to the broader LCE family network that collectively forms the cornified envelope. Skin enrichment (111.6 nTPM) ensures LCE2A expression is restricted to epidermal keratinocytes undergoing terminal differentiation. 1q21 CNVs cause LCE2A dosage alterations within the EDC cluster, leading to defective cornified envelope assembly, impaired epidermal barrier function, increased transepidermal water loss, and susceptibility to ichthyosis vulgaris, psoriasis, and atopic dermatitis. LCE2A represents a critical late cornified envelope protein linking proline-rich repeat architecture to epidermal barrier formation and keratinization.
