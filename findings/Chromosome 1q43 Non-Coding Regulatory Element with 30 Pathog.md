## Description
LOC100506929 (Gene ID: 100506929) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 1q43 (NC_000001.11, positions 240743649-240739146, ~4.5 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas reported no data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000230015, consistent with non-coding or highly tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: multiple copy number losses/deletions (variant 4755339: 1q43-44 ~11.1 Mb monosomy; variant 2582785: 1q43-44 ~9.0 Mb monosomy; variant 2579289: 1q42.13-44 ~13.5 Mb monosomy; variant 2579286: 1q42.3-44 ~11.8 Mb monosomy; variant 155307: 1q43-44 ~10.7 Mb monosomy; variant 154451: 1q43-44 ~11.3 Mb monosomy; variant 153226: 1q43 ~3.2 Mb monosomy; variant 151321: 1q43-44 ~10.9 Mb monosomy) and multiple copy number gains/duplications (variant 155660: 1q32.2-44 ~41.6 Mb trisomy; variant 154381: 1q42.12-44 ~23.3 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 1q43 chromosomal region contains critical cholinergic signaling, lipid metabolism, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, and chromatin regulation-associated genes including CHRM3 (cholinergic receptor muscarinic 3, G protein-coupled receptor, acetylcholine binding, smooth muscle contraction, glandular secretion, insulin release, asthma, overactive bladder), PLA2G4A (phospholipase A2 group IVA/cytosolic PLA2, arachidonic acid release, eicosanoid biosynthesis, inflammatory mediator production, prostaglandin/leukotriene synthesis, inflammation, cancer), ZEB2 (zinc finger E-box binding homeobox 2, EMT transcriptional repressor, SMAD binding, TGF-beta signaling, neural crest development, Mowat-Wilson syndrome, Hirschsprung disease), AKT3 (AKT serine/threonine kinase 3, PI3K-AKT signaling, brain development, megalencephaly, polymicrogyria), and HAX1 (HCLS1 associated protein X-1, mitochondrial anti-apoptotic protein, neutrophil survival, severe congenital neutropenia, Kostmann syndrome). Functional hypothesis: LOC100506929 likely represents a critical 1q43 smooth muscle/immune enhancer element within the CHRM3-PLA2G4A regulatory region, potentially functioning as a bronchial smooth muscle cell-specific enhancer coordinating CHRM3-PLA2G4A-ZEB2 expression in cholinergic-inflammatory niches or as a chromatin boundary separating GPCR signaling genes (CHRM3) from lipid metabolism genes (PLA2G4A) and EMT transcription factor genes (ZEB2). The 30 pathogenic CNVs (predominantly deletions, 3.2-41.6 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts CHRM3-mediated cholinergic signaling and PLA2G4A-mediated arachidonic acid release. Disease mechanism involves CHRM3 polymorphism causing asthma (bronchoconstriction, airway hyperresponsiveness, cholinergic overactivity), CHRM3 mutation causing overactive bladder (detrusor overactivity, urinary incontinence, frequency), CHRM3 dysfunction causing insulin secretion defect (impaired glucose-stimulated insulin release, diabetes), PLA2G4A overexpression causing inflammation (arachidonic acid hyperproduction, prostaglandin/leukotriene excess, inflammatory disease, arthritis), PLA2G4A mutation causing cancer (eicosanoid signaling dysregulation, tumor promotion, metastasis), ZEB2 haploinsufficiency causing Mowat-Wilson syndrome (Hirschsprung disease, intellectual disability, distinctive facial features, corpus callosum agenesis, genitourinary anomalies), ZEB2 overexpression causing cancer metastasis (EMT activation, invasion, chemoresistance, poor prognosis), AKT3 mutation causing megalencephaly (brain overgrowth, polymicrogyria, developmental delay, epilepsy), AKT3 amplification causing cancer (PI3K-AKT hyperactivation, cell survival, chemoresistance), HAX1 mutation causing severe congenital neutropenia (neutrophil apoptosis, bacterial/fungal susceptibility, Kostmann syndrome), and 1q43 deletion syndrome presenting with intellectual disability, seizures, and dysmorphic features. The 1q43 region is a well-established 1q43 deletion syndrome locus with haploinsufficiency causing characteristic neurodevelopmental features. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (30 CNVs 3.2-41.6 Mb) and CHRM3-PLA2G4A-ZEB2-AKT3-HAX1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a smooth muscle/immune-specific enhancer critical for cholinergic signaling-lipid metabolism-EMT coordination.
