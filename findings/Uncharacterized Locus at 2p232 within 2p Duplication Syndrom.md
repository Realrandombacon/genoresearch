## Description
LOC100505774 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 2p23.2 (NC_000002.12:28736204-28709049) spanning approximately 27.2 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains (trisomy) affecting 2p: large duplications spanning 2p25.3-21 (chr2:236816-45983232, ~45.7 Mb), 2p25.3-22.3 (chr2:12770-33711509, ~33.7 Mb), and 2p25.3-16.1 (chr2:66097-55570637, ~55.5 Mb). The 2p23 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including ALK (anaplastic lymphoma kinase, neuroblastoma), MYCN (N-myc proto-oncogene, neuroblastoma amplification), FAM49A (actin cytoskeleton regulator), and ACADS (short-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase, metabolic disorder). LOC100505774 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer or silencer controlling 2p dosage-sensitive gene expression, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 2p gene transcription, (3) a chromatin boundary element within the 2p23 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) an alternative transcript isoform of neighboring genes. The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (3 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus resides within a critical 2p dosage-sensitive genomic region where trisomy disrupts neurodevelopmental processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization. 2p duplication syndrome is characterized by developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and increased neuroblastoma risk via MYCN/ALK dosage effects.
