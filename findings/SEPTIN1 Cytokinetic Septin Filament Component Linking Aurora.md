## Description
SEPTIN1 (Septin-1, UniProt Q8WYJ6) is a 372 aa filament-forming cytoskeletal GTPase that functions as an essential component of the septin hetero-oligomeric complex required for cytokinesis, spindle assembly, and chromosome congression. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a well-folded protein (pLDDT=76.1) featuring a canonical septin GTPase domain (IPR016491/PF00735/cd01850, aa 27-302) within the P-loop containing NTP hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, IPR027417, aa 7-313), with the septin-type guanine nucleotide-binding domain (IPR030379, aa 27-304) enabling GTP-dependent filament formation. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal exclusive high-confidence partnerships with other septin family members: SEPTIN4 (0.986), SEPTIN11 (0.980), SEPTIN10 (0.977), SEPTIN6 (0.975), SEPTIN5 (0.964), SEPTIN9 (0.963), SEPTIN3 (0.962), SEPTIN8 (0.962), SEPTIN12 (0.935), and SEPTIN7 (0.933), consistent with septins forming hetero-oligomeric filaments that serve as diffusion barriers and scaffolds at the cleavage furrow. HPA shows lymphoid tissue-enriched expression (48.5 nTPM) and intestine expression (23.5 nTPM) with actin filament localization, supporting roles in cell division and cytoskeletal organization. ClinVar identifies 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses at 16p11.2-13.3, though no specific Mendelian disease is annotated - these CNVs overlap with the well-characterized 16p11.2 deletion/duplication syndrome region associated with neurodevelopmental disorders. Literature (11 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: SEPTIN1 is required for spindle assembly and chromosome congression in mouse oocytes (2011, 20 citations), interacts with Aurora-B kinase for mitotic regulation (2005, 48 citations), is overexpressed in oral cancer (2007, 16 citations), and is ubiquitinated by Parkin (2007). The gene represents a 'semi-dark' cytoskeletal regulator where canonical septin filament formation is established, but specific mechanisms linking SEPTIN1's unique contributions to cytokinetic fidelity, Aurora-B signaling crosstalk, neurofibrillary tangle formation in Alzheimer's disease, and 16p11.2 CNV-associated neurodevelopmental phenotypes remain underexplored.
