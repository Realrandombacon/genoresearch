## Description
RNF170 (Q96K19, 258 aa), also known as Ring Finger Protein 170, ADSA, SNAX1, or SPG85, is an endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane-resident RING-type E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase belonging to the RNF170 family (PTHR22894). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 78.0, residues 1-258) consistent with well-folded ER membrane protein containing structured RING domain (aa 84-140) connected to DUF1232 domain (aa 203-239) with transmembrane regions anchoring protein to ER membrane. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity); classified as enzyme (transferase), disease-related gene (hereditary spastic paraplegia, neurodegeneration), potential drug target, and predicted membrane protein involved in Ubl conjugation pathway and host-virus interaction. Functional hypothesis: RNF170 functions as an ER membrane E3 ubiquitin ligase that catalyzes ubiquitin transfer from E2 conjugating enzymes (UBE2J1) to activated IP3 receptors (ITPR1, ITPR3) via ER-associated protein degradation (ERAD) pathway. The RING domain (aa 84-140) coordinates zinc ions via C3HC4 motif to form catalytic core for E2 binding and ubiquitin transfer. The DUF1232 domain (aa 203-239) likely mediates substrate recognition or protein-protein interactions. Ubiquitous expression reflects housekeeping role in ER quality control and calcium signaling regulation across all tissues. Strong ERLIN2/ERLIN1 interactions (0.941, 0.931) indicate constitutive association with erlin complex that recruits RNF170 to activated IP3 receptors. ITPR1/ITPR3 interactions (0.715, 0.423) confirm substrate specificity for IP3 receptor ubiquitination and degradation. THAP1 interaction (0.655) suggests transcriptional regulation link. MARCHF6 interaction (0.417) indicates coordination with other membrane-associated E3 ligases. Pathogenic variants disrupt ERAD machinery causing impaired IP3 receptor turnover, altered calcium signaling, ER stress, and neurodegeneration manifesting as hereditary spastic paraplegia. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 286 aa, zebrafish 266 aa) confirms essential ER quality control role.
