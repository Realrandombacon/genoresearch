# ZNF416: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor Linked to Proteasomal Regulation

**Date:** 2026-03-23T04:01:05.979955

**Quality Score:** 3.1/10  (E=5.2, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF416 encodes a 594-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9BWM5) featuring an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 27-111) and three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 242-350). The KRAB domain recruits the corepressor TRIM28 (KAP1), which oligomerizes with heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) and histone methyltransferases to silence target genes via H3K9me3 deposition. STRING interactions confirm high-confidence binding to TRIM28 (0.625) and other KRAB-ZFPs like ZNF211 (0.729), suggesting heteromeric complex formation. HPA data indicates ubiquitous expression with nucleoplasmic and nuclear speckle localization, consistent with transcriptional repression machinery. Functional predictions link ZNF416 to microautophagy and ubiquitin-dependent proteasomal regulation, potentially via cross-talk between chromatin silencing and protein turnover pathways. ClinVar reports 15 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 19q13.43, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease etiology, though no specific monogenic disorder is yet defined. The three zinc fingers likely recognize specific GC-rich motifs, positioning ZNF416 as a sequence-specific silencer of genes involved in cardiac muscle development or stress response.
