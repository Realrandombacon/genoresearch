## Description
TCEA2 encodes Transcription elongation factor A protein 2 (299 aa, Q15560), a TFIIS family member functioning in RNA polymerase II transcription elongation and proofreading. InterPro analysis reveals canonical TFIIS architecture: N-terminal TFIIS domain (IPR003617, cd00183, positions 5-82) for polymerase binding, central S-II domain (IPR003618, G3DSA:1.10.472.30, positions 118-227) for elongation stimulation, and C-terminal zinc ribbon domain (IPR001222, cd13749, positions 252-298) with zinc-coordinating residues for nucleic acid interaction. GO annotations confirm transcription elongation by RNA polymerase II (GO:0006368), nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), and zinc ion binding (GO:0008270). STRING interactions reveal tight integration with transcription machinery: SUPT5H (0.995, SPT5 elongation factor), GTF2B (0.979, TFIIB), LEO1 (0.976, PAF1 complex), RTF1 (0.970, PAF1 complex), CDC73 (0.969, PAF1 complex), ELOA (0.968, elongin A), CTR9 (0.960, PAF1 complex), POLR2B (0.957, RNA Pol II subunit), SUPT4H1 (0.794, DSIF complex), confirming membership in transcription elongation/PAF1 regulatory network. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, centrosome, and basal body - the centrosomal/basal body localization is distinctive and suggests potential roles beyond canonical transcription, possibly in centriole-associated transcription or ciliogenesis. ClinVar documents 37 pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 20q13.33 CNVs (gains and losses) indicating strong dosage sensitivity, though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 80.0) with well-folded architecture typical of TFIIS proteins. Literature (15 papers) reveals testis-specific expression pattern (1997 genomic characterization, 2021 single-cell transcriptome in spermatogenesis), suggesting specialized role in germ cell transcription. Functional hypothesis: TCEA2 functions as a specialized transcription elongation factor with dual roles - canonical RNA Pol II pause-release and proofreading in somatic cells, and potentially unique functions in spermatogenesis given testis-specific expression; centrosomal/basal body localization suggests involvement in centriole-associated transcription programs or ciliary gene regulation. Mechanistic proposal: TFIIS domains enable polymerase backtracking rescue via RNA cleavage stimulation (like TCEA1/SII), while zinc ribbon mediates nucleic acid contacts; PAF1 complex interactions (LEO1, RTF1, CDC73, CTR9) coordinate elongation with histone modification; centrosomal localization may link transcription to cell cycle progression or ciliogenesis. Dosage sensitivity (37 CNVs) suggests haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity could disrupt transcription elongation fidelity, potentially contributing to developmental defects or cancer. This gene represents a partially characterized transcription factor with established molecular function but underexplored tissue-specific roles and disease mechanisms.
