## Description
GOSR1 (UniProt Q95249) encodes a 250 aa high-confidence membrane protein functioning as an essential SNARE component of the Golgi SNAP receptor complex (GNAP/GARP), orchestrating vesicle tethering and fusion between the ER-Golgi intermediate compartment (ERGIC) and cis-Golgi network. InterPro analysis reveals canonical SNARE architecture: full-length GOSR1 family domain (IPR023601, residues 5-250); N-terminal Golgi SNAP receptor complex member domain (IPR060289, residues 8-150) mediating vesicle tethering; and C-terminal SNARE motif anchored in vesicle membrane (PF12352, residues 161-226) facilitating trans-SNARE complex formation for fusion. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 84.3), indicating a well-folded stable protein suitable for precise molecular interactions at the ERGIC-cis-Golgi interface. STRING interactions returned strong evidence of functional partnership: GOSR2 and STX5 with score 0.999, BET1L/BET1 (score 0.669), SCFD1/SEC22B (score 0.998), USO1/GOLGB1/VAMP8/YKT6 indicating core Golgi trafficking complex assembly involving multiple SNAREs and tethering factors. HPA classifies GOSR1 as predicted membrane protein with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues but specifically involved in ER-Golgi transport (GO:0006888), Golgi membrane maintenance (GO:0000139), and cis-Golgi network organization (GO:0005801). ClinVar lists 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains on chr17p13.3-q25.3, deletions at chr17q11.2, and intronic mutations; while no single-gene disorder name is currently assigned, these CNVs strongly suggest undiscovered congenital disorders likely involving Golgi dysmorphogenesis or neurodevelopmental defects given the critical role of GOSR1 in vesicle trafficking essential for secretory pathway function. Literature (19 papers) reveals partial characterization: differential expression in lymph node metastasis in breast cancer; Rab24 interaction and autophagy roles; syntaxin-5 flexibility supporting Golgi functions via COG complex interplay; and GARP dysfunction leading to COPI displacement, depletion of v-SNAREs, and calcium homeostasis protein loss. Functional hypothesis: GOSR1 operates through integrated SNARE-mediated vesicle tethering-fusion mechanisms at ERGIC-cis-Golgi interface involving Rab24/Gosr2/STX5/BET complex assembly to ensure Golgi membrane continuity and cargo sorting; defects in this pathway lead to secretory dysfunction, glycosylation errors, calcium dysregulation, and congenital disorders of the brain or immune system. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: precise structural basis of GOSR1-Gosr2-STX5 trans-SNARE complex formation (which residues contact?), mechanism of Rab24 recruitment by N-terminal domain, impact of 11 ClinVar CNVs on specific phenotypes (neurodevelopmental delay? immunodeficiency?), tissue-specific thresholds for GOSR1 dosage in secretory cells, evolutionary divergence from other SNAREs, crosstalk between GOSR1 and COG complex stability, role in Golgi fragmentation observed in neurodegeneration, biomarker utility in congenital disorders of glycosylation (CDGs), whether homozygous loss causes embryonic lethality or severe developmental delay, specific cargo substrates dependent on GOSR1-mediated transport, contribution to cancer metastasis via altered ERGIC-cis-Golgi trafficking, and potential therapeutic targeting of SNARE complexes in secretory diseases.
