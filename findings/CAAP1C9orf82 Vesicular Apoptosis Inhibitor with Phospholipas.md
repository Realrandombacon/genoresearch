## Description
CAAP1 (alias C9orf82/CAAP, caspase activity and apoptosis inhibitor 1) encodes a 361 amino acid partially structured vesicular protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 61.9) containing a caspase activity and apoptosis inhibitor 1 family domain (IPR038991, aa 1-360; GO:0042981 regulation of apoptotic process) and PF15335 core domain (aa 133-195), belonging to the PTHR14740 family. HPA classifies the protein as predicted intracellular with vesicular localization, ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all), low tissue specificity, apoptosis biological process annotation, and cell type-enhanced single cell expression. ClinVar contains 72 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 9p24.3-q21.13 copy number gains and losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence network with phospholipase and scaffolding proteins: PLAA (phospholipase A2-activating protein, lipid metabolism, 0.737), AKAP17A (A-kinase anchoring protein 17A, cAMP signaling scaffold, 0.703), ZSWIM8 (zinc finger SWIM-type containing 8, CRL E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate receptor, 0.512), ANGEL2 (angel homolog 2, deadenylase, 0.463), GREB1L (estrogen-responsive gene B1-like, developmental regulator, 0.459), SNX13 (sorting nexin 13, endosomal trafficking, 0.451), BFAR (bifunctional apoptosis regulator, 0.450), TMEM244 (transmembrane protein 244, 0.447), JMJD6 (jumonji domain-containing protein 6, histone demethylase/splicing factor, 0.443), and AFTPH (aftiphilin, vesicle trafficking, 0.425). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8VDY9 356 aa, bovine Q2T9W9 362 aa) indicates functional constraint. The apoptosis inhibitor domain architecture combined with vesicular localization and PLAA/AKAP17A/ZSWIM8 interactions strongly suggests CAAP1 functions as a vesicular scaffold protein that coordinates apoptosis regulation with lipid signaling and ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation. PLAA interaction indicates coupling to arachidonic acid release and eicosanoid production during apoptotic signaling. AKAP17A interaction places CAAP1 within cAMP-dependent protein kinase A signaling microdomains on vesicular membranes, potentially regulating phosphorylation-dependent apoptosis checkpoints. ZSWIM8 interaction suggests CAAP1 may serve as substrate for CRL E3 ubiquitin ligase complex, linking apoptosis inhibition to proteasomal degradation pathways. BFAR interaction (bifunctional apoptosis regulator) indicates potential heterodimerization with other apoptosis inhibitors for cooperative caspase suppression. Vesicular localization suggests CAAP1 may sequester pro-apoptotic factors away from mitochondria or regulate apoptotic signal transduction through endosomal/lysosomal compartments. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 61.9) indicates partial disorder consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly. Pathogenic 9p CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting apoptosis regulation and vesicular signaling, potentially contributing to developmental defects, cancer predisposition through impaired cell death, and altered inflammatory responses via disrupted PLAA-mediated lipid signaling.
