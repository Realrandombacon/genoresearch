## Description
LOC101927166 (Gene ID: 101927166) is an uncharacterized genomic locus mapping to chromosome 17q21.32 (48,444,832-48,466,039 bp, ~21.2 kb genomic span). Multiple evidence lines suggest this is a non-coding or regulatory element rather than a canonical protein-coding gene: (1) No UniProt accession identified - no characterized protein product; (2) ClinVar contains 3 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 17q21.32-21.33, 17q23.1-25.1, and 17q21.33-24.2 regions (deletions 1.3 Mb, duplications 13.6-38.6 Mb), associated with 17q rearrangement syndromes. The 17q21.32 region is adjacent to the well-characterized 17q21.31 microdeletion syndrome locus (KANSL1 haploinsufficiency syndrome) featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. The 17q21.31-32 region is a gene-dense neurodevelopmental critical region with extensive long-range chromatin interactions. Pathogenic 17q CNVs disrupt gene dosage and TAD architecture, causing dysregulation of MAPT (tauopathy risk), CRHR1 (stress axis dysfunction), KANSL1 (chromatin regulation), and STAT3 (immune signaling, cancer predisposition). 17q21.31 microdeletions occur in ~1:16,000 live births.
