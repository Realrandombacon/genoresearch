## Description
CCDC47 encodes a 483 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein (UniProt Q96A33) that functions as an essential subunit of the PAT (protein-associated translocon) complex. InterPro analysis identifies a conserved PAT complex domain (IPR012879/PF07946, residues 135-467) with GO annotations for calcium ion binding and ER calcium homeostasis. AlphaFold structure prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 76.8) across the full-length protein, indicating stable folded conformation. HPA localization confirms ER membrane residence with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity). STRING interaction network reveals 10 high-confidence partners forming the PAT complex core: TMCO1 (0.976), SEC61A1 (0.947), NCLN (0.943), WDR83OS (0.909), plus SEC61G (0.845), SEC61B (0.563), and ribosomal proteins RPL38/RPL3/RPL5 (0.83-0.86), indicating coupling of ER translocation with calcium regulation. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants including frameshift mutations (c.563del p.Asn188fs, c.1145del p.Leu382fs), nonsense variants (p.Arg271Ter, p.Arg397Ter), and chromosome 17q CNVs, demonstrating dosage sensitivity. Mouse ortholog Ccdc47 (Q9D024, 483 aa) shows >95% sequence conservation. Functional hypothesis: CCDC47 acts as a calcium-regulated chaperone within the PAT complex, coordinating nascent polypeptide translocation through SEC61 with ER luminal calcium buffering via TMCO1. The coiled-coil domain mediates oligomerization with NCLN and WDR83OS, forming a calcium-sensing gate that prevents misfolded protein accumulation. Pathogenic truncations disrupt complex assembly, causing ER stress and unfolded protein response activation, potentially contributing to developmental disorders and metabolic disease.
