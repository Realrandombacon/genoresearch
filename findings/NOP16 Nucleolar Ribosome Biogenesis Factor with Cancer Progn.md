# NOP16: Nucleolar Ribosome Biogenesis Factor with Cancer Prognostic Value

## Description
NOP16 (UniProt Q9Y3C1) is a 178-amino acid nucleolar protein belonging to the Ribosome Biogenesis Protein Nop16 family (IPR019002/PF09420/PTHR13243, residues 1-174). AlphaFold prediction reveals a well-folded structure (pLDDT 85.5), consistent with its role as a stable component of ribosome assembly complexes. HPA data confirms nucleolar localization with ubiquitous expression across tissues, reflecting its housekeeping function in ribosome biogenesis. STRING interaction analysis places NOP16 within a high-confidence ribosome biogenesis network, interacting with NIP7 (0.823), MRTO4 (0.800), NIFK (0.798), NHP2 (0.758), IMP4 (0.756), WDR12 (0.711), and fibrillarin/FBL (0.449), suggesting it functions as a scaffold coordinating pre-rRNA processing factors. ClinVar records 46 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains at 5q35.2-35.3, implicating NOP16 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders. Literature (29 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic roles: NOP16 is estrogen- and c-Myc-inducible, serves as a poor prognosis marker in breast cancer, promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression via Keap1-Nrf2 signaling and EMT activation, and is implicated in nasopharyngeal carcinoma pathogenesis. We hypothesize NOP16 acts as a structural organizer within the nucleolar ribosome assembly factory, where its overexpression (driven by c-Myc amplification in cancers) accelerates ribosome biogenesis to fuel tumor proliferation, while haploinsufficiency causes developmental defects via impaired ribosome production.
