## Description
GOLGA5 (Golgin subfamily A member 5, also known as Golgin-84) encodes a 731 aa Golgi apparatus membrane-associated coiled-coil protein (Q8TBA6) functioning as a structural scaffold in Golgi organization and vesicular trafficking. With 29 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined cellular localization and emerging oncogenic fusion roles. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: GOLGA5 functions as a Golgi membrane tethering protein that organizes Golgi architecture through coiled-coil mediated oligomerization, facilitates vesicle docking via interactions with the COG (Conserved Oligomeric Golgi) complex, and regulates secretory granule biogenesis through recruitment of HID-1 to the trans-Golgi network. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: GOLGA5 operates through: (1) Golgi structural organization - the C-terminal coiled-coil domain (PF09787, positions 420-724) mediates homodimerization or heterodimerization to form extended rod-like structures that tether Golgi membranes; (2) COG complex interaction - high-confidence STRING interactions with COG7 (0.964), COG3 (0.892), COG6 (0.876), COG8 (0.849), COG5 (0.841) indicate essential role in retrograde vesicular trafficking within the Golgi; (3) Secretory granule biogenesis - recruits HID-1 (hypothetical protein involved in dense-core vesicle formation) to the trans-Golgi network through a novel membrane binding domain (2020 study); (4) Oncogenic fusion partner - GOLGA5-RET fusion in malignant spindle cell sarcoma responsive to selpercatinib (2025, 1 citation), GOLGA5-JAK2 fusion in Philadelphia chromosome-like acute lymphoblastic leukemia with induction failure (2018, 60 citations), suggesting kinase activation through coiled-coil mediated dimerization; (5) Golgi membrane association - interacts with GOLGB1/GM130 (0.923) for Golgi stacking and RAB1A (0.883) for vesicle targeting.
