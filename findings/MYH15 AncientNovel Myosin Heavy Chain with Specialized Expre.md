## Description
MYH15 encodes Myosin-15 (1926 aa, Q9Y2K3), an unconventional class II myosin motor protein functioning in actin-based motility and muscle contraction. InterPro analysis reveals canonical myosin architecture: N-terminal motor/head domain (IPR001609, positions 77-771, cd14929) with ATP binding and actin-binding sites for force generation; myosin tail domain (IPR002928) for cargo binding and dimerization; multiple coiled-coil regions (G3DSA:1.20.5.340/1.20.5.370) for structural stability. GO annotations confirm cytoskeletal motor activity (GO:0003774), ATP binding (GO:0005524), actin-binding, calmodulin-binding, and myosin complex membership. STRING interactions reveal strong myosin light chain network: MYL1 (0.850, myosin light chain 1), MYL12B (0.795), MYL12A (0.773), MYLPF (0.762), MYL9 (0.760), MYL6 (0.734), MYL3 (0.724), MYL5 (0.703), MYL6B (0.692) - indicating integration with contractile apparatus; WHRN (0.694, whirlin) interaction is notable as whirlin mutations cause Usher syndrome and deafness, suggesting potential sensory cell roles. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with low overall levels: retina (3.0 nTPM), tongue (1.9 nTPM); literature reveals highly restricted expression in extraocular muscles and muscle spindles (2009 characterization); subcellular localization to vesicles and cytosol. ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 3q CNVs (gains and losses) and missense variants (e.g., p.Met617Val), indicating dosage sensitivity though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely characterized. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 74.5) with well-folded motor domain and coiled-coil tail architecture typical of class II myosins. Literature (39 papers) reveals specialized roles: 2019 study identifies rare MYH15 variants as ALS risk modifiers in C9orf72 expansion carriers; 2009/2010 characterization establishes MYH15 as ancient/novel myosin expressed in extraocular muscles and muscle spindles alongside MYH7B/MYH14; 2015 association with sudden cardiac death risk; 2025 five-gene signature for acute myeloid leukemia prognosis. Functional hypothesis: MYH15 functions as a specialized contractile myosin isoform with restricted expression in proprioceptive muscle spindles (sensory feedback) and extraocular muscles (precise eye movement control); ALS risk modification suggests motor neuron vulnerability when MYH15 function is compromised; whirlin interaction hints at potential roles in stereocilia or sensory hair cell function. Mechanistic proposal: Motor domain hydrolyzes ATP for actin filament sliding generating contractile force; light chain binding (MYL1/3/6/12) regulates motor activity and calcium sensitivity; coiled-coil tail enables dimerization and cargo binding; muscle spindle expression suggests role in stretch receptor contraction modulating proprioceptive signaling; extraocular muscle expression indicates contribution to fine motor control of eye movements; ALS modification may occur via disrupted axonal transport or neuromuscular junction maintenance; dosage sensitivity (19 CNVs) suggests haploinsufficiency could impair specialized muscle function contributing to motor disorders or cardiac arrhythmias. This gene represents a moderately characterized unconventional myosin with established tissue specificity but incompletely understood physiological substrates and disease mechanisms.
