## Description
LOC123575667 (Gene ID: 123575667) is a validated NANOG-H3K27ac histone modification-marked enhancer element on chromosome 6p25.3 (NC_000006.12, positions 11126705-11127231, ~526 bp region) with functional characterization as an active enhancer in naive human embryonic stem cells despite absence of protein-coding potential. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction, consistent with non-coding regulatory element classification. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with pluripotent stem cell-specific enhancer expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding DNA regulatory function. However, ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 8 copy number gains/duplications (variant 155631: 6p25.3-24.1 ~13.1 Mb trisomy; variant 155430: 6p25.3-12.3 ~46.8 Mb trisomy; variant 155267: 6p25.3-23 ~14.3 Mb trisomy; variant 154190: 6p25.3-24.2 ~11.4 Mb trisomy; variant 151603: 6p25.3-22.3 ~16.9 Mb trisomy; variant 150044: 6p25.2-21.33 ~27.4 Mb trisomy) and 5 copy number losses/deletions (variant 155715: 6p25.3-23 ~13.9 Mb monosomy; variant 154368: 6p25.2-22.3 ~13.8 Mb monosomy; variant 154343: 6p24.2-23 ~3.4 Mb monosomy; variant 146906: 6p25.2-24.1 ~9.2 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 6p25.3 chromosomal region contains critical pluripotency transcription, forkhead development, immune regulation, and phosphatase signaling-associated genes including IRF4 (interferon regulatory factor 4, IRF family transcription factor, lymphoid cell differentiation, multiple myeloma oncogene, autoimmune disease susceptibility), FOXC1 (forkhead box C1, forkhead transcription factor, anterior segment eye development, Axenfeld-Rieger anomaly, primary congenital glaucoma), FOXQ1 (forkhead box Q1, forkhead transcription factor, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, cancer metastasis, gastric cancer), DUSP22 (dual specificity phosphatase 22, JAK-STAT pathway negative regulator, anaplastic large cell lymphoma translocation partner, immune signaling modulation), and FOXF2 (forkhead box F2, forkhead transcription factor, mesenchymal development, cleft lip/palate, lung development). Functional hypothesis: LOC123575667 likely represents a critical 6p25.3 naive hESC-specific enhancer element within the IRF4-FOXC1 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a NANOG-dependent pluripotency enhancer coordinating IRF4-FOXC1-FOXQ1-DUSP22 expression in naive human embryonic stem cell niches (inner cell mass, epiblast, naive hESC colonies) or as a chromatin boundary separating immune transcription factor genes (IRF4) from forkhead development genes (FOXC1/FOXQ1/FOXF2) and phosphatase signaling genes (DUSP22). The H3K27ac histone modification mark indicates active enhancer chromatin state in hESCs, with ChIP-STARR-seq MPRA validation confirming enhancer activity and NANOG transcription factor binding. The 13 pathogenic CNVs (8 duplications 11.4-46.8 Mb, 5 deletions 3.4-13.9 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts IRF4-mediated lymphoid differentiation and FOXC1-mediated anterior segment development. Disease mechanism involves IRF4 overexpression causing multiple myeloma (plasma cell proliferation, survival, bone marrow infiltration, poor prognosis), IRF4 mutation causing immunodeficiency (lymphoid differentiation defect, hypogammaglobulinemia, recurrent infection), IRF4 polymorphism causing autoimmune disease (lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, altered cytokine production), FOXC1 haploinsufficiency causing Axenfeld-Rieger anomaly (anterior segment dysgenesis, iris hypoplasia, glaucoma, dental anomalies, umbilical hernia), FOXC1 mutation causing primary congenital glaucoma (trabecular meshwork defect, intraocular pressure elevation, optic nerve damage, blindness), FOXQ1 overexpression causing cancer metastasis (EMT activation, invasion, chemoresistance, poor prognosis, gastric cancer), FOXQ1 amplification causing epithelial-mesenchymal transition (cadherin switching, motility, stemness), DUSP22 translocation causing anaplastic large cell lymphoma (JAK-STAT dysregulation, T cell malignancy, ALK fusion), DUSP22 deletion causing immune signaling hyperactivation (cytokine storm, autoimmunity), FOXF2 mutation causing cleft lip/palate (craniofacial development defect, mesenchymal migration impairment), FOXF2 dysfunction causing lung development defect (alveolar hypoplasia, respiratory distress), and 6p25.3 duplication/deletion syndrome presenting with anterior segment eye anomalies, developmental delay, and immune dysfunction. The 6p25.3 region is a well-established FOXC1-associated anterior segment dysgenesis locus. The NANOG-H3K27ac enhancer classification combined with ChIP-STARR-seq MPRA validation (naive hESCs), ClinVar evidence (13 CNVs 3.4-46.8 Mb), and IRF4-FOXC1-FOXQ1-DUSP22-FOXF2 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a naive hESC-specific enhancer critical for pluripotency-forkhead development-immune regulation coordination.
