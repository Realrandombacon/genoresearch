## Description
PCSK4 (Proprotein Convertase Subtilisin/Kexin Type 4, also known as PC4, UniProt Q6UW60) is a 755 aa type I membrane-bound serine protease belonging to the subtilisin-like proprotein convertase family that activates precursor proteins through proteolytic cleavage in the secretory pathway. InterPro analysis reveals canonical proprotein convertase domain architecture: Peptidase S8/S53 catalytic domain (IPR000209, positions 149-432; cd04059, positions 118-407; G3DSA:3.40.50.200, positions 120-444) with serine-type peptidase activity (GO:0008236), P domain (IPR002884, positions 449-581; G3DSA:2.10.220.10, positions 590-705) for enzyme stability and substrate recognition, galactose-binding-like domain superfamily (IPR008979, positions 440-581; G3DSA:2.60.120.260, positions 449-578), Furin-like repeats (IPR006212, positions 646-694; cd00064, positions 652-684; G3DSA:3.30.70.850, positions 29-113) for propeptide processing, and growth factor receptor cysteine-rich domain superfamily (IPR009030, positions 595-686). AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 84.3), indicating a well-folded protease with stable catalytic and regulatory domains. HPA data confirms testis-enriched expression (62.5 nTPM, tissue enriched), detected in many tissues, classified as predicted intracellular/membrane protein with hydrolase/protease/serine protease molecular function. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with IGF2 signaling and reproductive biology: IGF2 (0.647, insulin-like growth factor 2 substrate), MBTPS1 (0.640, membrane-bound transcription factor protease), LRRC8D (0.634, volume-regulated anion channel), CFD (0.598, complement factor D), TNFRSF18 (0.527, GITR receptor), ACRBP (0.502, acrosome-binding protein), IGF2BP3 (0.497, IGF2 mRNA binding protein), IHH (0.472, Indian hedgehog), SCG5 (0.467, secretogranin V proprotein convertase inhibitor), AMH (0.425, anti-Müllerian hormone). ClinVar contains 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains at 19p13.3 and deletions affecting the PCSK4 locus. Literature (39 papers) establishes PCSK4 roles in: (1) IGF2/PCSK4 imbalance causing big IGF2 overproduction in solitary fibrous tumors with non-islet cell tumor hypoglycemia (NICTH, 2018, 14 citations), (2) male contraceptive target via sperm antigen inhibition (2019), (3) enediynyl peptide and iso-coumarinyl methyl sulfone inhibitor development (2015, 8 citations), (4) human sperm membrane characterization for immunocontraception candidate development (2015), and (5) proprotein processing in male reproductive tract. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: PCSK4 serves as a testis-enriched proprotein convertase activating precursor proteins (prohormones, growth factors, sperm maturation factors) through site-specific proteolytic cleavage in the secretory pathway, regulating male fertility, IGF2 bioavailability, and reproductive hormone maturation. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The S8 peptidase catalytic domain cleaves precursor proteins at paired basic amino acid recognition sites (Arg/Lys-Arg/Lys-X-Arg/Lys↓), converting inactive proproteins to bioactive forms. The P domain stabilizes the catalytic triad (Asp-His-Ser) and ensures proper folding. Furin-like repeats mediate propeptide autocatalytic removal during enzyme maturation. In testis, PCSK4 processes sperm maturation factors and acrosomal proteins (ACRBP interaction), enabling fertilization competence. In SFT/NICTH, PCSK4 dysregulation causes incomplete IGF2 processing, generating big IGF2 that binds IGF1R/insulin receptor, causing severe hypoglycemia. SCG5 interaction suggests regulatory feedback via proprotein convertase inhibitor binding. The 24 ClinVar CNVs indicate PCSK4 dosage sensitivity may contribute to reproductive disorders or metabolic phenotypes. Male contraceptive development targets PCSK4 inhibition to block sperm maturation without affecting systemic hormone levels.
