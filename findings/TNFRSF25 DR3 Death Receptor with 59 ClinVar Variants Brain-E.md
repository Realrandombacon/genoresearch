# TNFRSF25 (DR3): Death Receptor with 59 ClinVar Variants, Brain-Enriched Expression, and High-Confidence TRADD/TOR1A Interactions

## Description
TNFRSF25 (Death Receptor 3, DR3; UniProt Q93038) is a 417 amino acid type I transmembrane protein belonging to the TNF receptor superfamily. Despite 88 publications, it remains under-characterized regarding its specific non-immune roles and the phenotypic consequences of its 59 ClinVar variants, which currently lack defined disease associations.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis confirms a canonical death receptor topology: an N-terminal extracellular domain containing three TNFR/NGFR cysteine-rich regions (IPR001368/PF00020, residues 33-115) for ligand binding, followed by a transmembrane segment, and a C-terminal intracellular Death Domain (IPR000488/PF00531, residues 321-413) essential for apoptotic signaling. AlphaFold predicts a stable structure (global pLDDT 72.3), with high confidence in the structured domains and expected flexibility in linker regions.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals a robust interaction network centered on apoptosis and cytoskeletal regulation. Key partners include TRADD (0.938) and FADD (0.530), confirming its role in the extrinsic apoptosis cascade via caspase-8 recruitment. Notably, TNFRSF25 shows an exceptionally high-confidence interaction with TOR1A (0.999), a nuclear envelope protein linked to early-onset torsion dystonia, suggesting a potential unexplored link between death receptor signaling and neuronal cytoskeletal integrity. Other partners include TNF (0.987), TNFRSF10A (0.820), and the ligand TNFSF15 (0.544).\n\nEXPRESSION PROFILE: HPA data indicates tissue-enhanced expression, with highest levels in the brain (42.2 nTPM), lymphoid tissue (39.8 nTPM), and pituitary gland (26.7 nTPM). Subcellular localization extends beyond the plasma membrane to the nucleoplasm, actin filaments, and focal adhesion sites, supporting the TOR1A interaction and implying roles in cell migration or structural maintenance.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar lists 59 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses at 1p36 (a critical region syndrome locus) and rare missense changes (e.g., p.Asp237Asn). While no monogenic disease is explicitly attributed solely to TNFRSF25 yet, its location in 1p36 and interaction with dystonia-related TOR1A suggest that specific hypomorphic alleles could contribute to neurodevelopmental disorders or altered immune surveillance.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TNFRSF25 likely functions as a dual-purpose sensor: triggering apoptosis in immune cells upon TL1A (TNFSF15) binding, and regulating neuronal cytoskeletal dynamics via TOR1A in the brain. The high brain expression and nucleoplasmic/actin localization suggest a non-canonical role in neuronal development or stress response, where defective signaling might contribute to the neurological phenotypes observed in 1p36 deletion syndromes.
