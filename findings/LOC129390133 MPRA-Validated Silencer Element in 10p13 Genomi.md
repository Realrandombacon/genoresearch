# LOC129390133: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 10p13 Genomic Region with 10p Deletion Syndrome Association

**Date:** 2026-03-23T07:43:51.432125

## Description
LOC129390133 (Gene ID: 129390133) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 10p13 (15,072,376-15,072,576 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak869 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic copy number variants all involving 10p15.3-12.31 region - deletions (19.3 Mb, 14.2 Mb, 16.3 Mb) and duplications (26.2 Mb, 19.1 Mb, 16.3 Mb); (4) No HPA expression expected (non-coding); (5) No STRING interactions or InterPro domains (non-coding). The 10p13-15.3 region is clinically critical - 10p deletion syndrome (del(10p) OMIM #601379) causes developmental delay, intellectual disability, growth retardation, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features; 10p duplication causes variable neurodevelopmental phenotypes. Critical 10p region contains: GATA3 (GATA-binding factor 3, Th2 differentiation, HDR syndrome - hypoparathyroidism, deafness, renal dysplasia), SRF (serum response factor, actin cytoskeleton, cell proliferation), ZFHX3 (zinc finger homeobox 3, neuronal development), ADIRF (adipogenesis regulatory factor), and multiple developmental transcription factors. 10p rearrangements are recurrent in developmental disorders and hematologic malignancies. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390133 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or developmental contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in erythroid/myeloid lineage. 10p13 is gene-dense with GATA3 (Th2/renal development), SRF (cytoskeletal regulation), and developmental regulators. Silencer disruption via CNVs alters TAD architecture and long-range regulation of GATA3 expression (HDR syndrome phenotypes), SRF dosage (cytoskeletal/proliferation defects), and neighboring developmental genes. Haploinsufficiency of GATA3 causes HDR syndrome; SRF dysregulation causes cardiac/skeletal phenotypes; 10p- syndrome involves multiple gene dosage effects. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes.
