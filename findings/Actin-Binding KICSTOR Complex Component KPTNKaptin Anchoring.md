## Description
KPTN encodes kaptin (KPTN/KICS4), a 436 aa actin-binding protein (UniProt Q9Y664) belonging to the kaptin family that functions as an essential scaffold component of the KICSTOR complex (KPTN-ITFG2-C12orf66-SZT2) which anchors the GATOR1 complex (DEPDC5-NPRL2-NPRL3) to lysosomal surfaces for amino acid-sensitive regulation of mTORC1 signaling critical for cell growth, neuronal development, and cognitive function. InterPro reveals distinctive domain architecture combining actin-binding and integrin-like structural elements: kaptin family signature (IPR029982/PTHR15435, residues 13-420) defining protein classification with associated GO molecular functions including actin binding (GO:0003779), actin filament organization (GO:0007015), and actin cytoskeleton localization (GO:0015629), KICSTOR complex protein kaptin Pfam domain (PF30693, residues 16-402) representing a large repetitive structural scaffold, integrin alpha N-terminal domain superfamily (IPR028994/SSF69318, residues 285-388) suggesting structural homology to integrin adhesion receptors though KPTN functions intracellularly indicating convergent evolution of beta-propeller fold for protein-protein interactions rather than extracellular ligand binding. AlphaFold predicts very high confidence structure (pLDDT 88.9) indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined tertiary structure suitable for precise complex assembly at lysosomal membranes and actin filament interfaces. STRING reveals exceptionally strong functional clustering (all scores >0.8) with KICSTOR complex members and GATOR1-Ragulator-mTORC1 regulatory machinery: NPRL3 (0.999, nitrogen permease regulator-like 3 GATOR1 subunit), SZT2 (0.999, seizure threshold 2 KICSTOR scaffold component), DEPDC5 (0.999, DEP domain-containing 5 GATOR1 catalytic subunit with GAP activity toward RagA/B GTPases), NPRL2 (0.998, nitrogen permease regulator-like 2 GATOR1 subunit), WDR59 (0.998, WD repeat-containing 59 Ragulator complex component), WDR24 (0.998, WD repeat-containing 24 Ragulator scaffold), SEH1L (0.998, SEH1-like nucleoporin/Ragulator component), MIOS (0.998, mitotic progression and mTOR signaling regulator Ragulator component), C12orf66 (0.820, chromosome 12 ORF 66 KICSTOR component), ITFG2 (0.809, integrin alpha-G protein interacting factor 2 KICSTOR component). HPA shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in all tissues reflecting universal requirement for nutrient sensing and growth control, multiple subcellular locations including nucleoplasm vesicles and plasma membrane suggesting dynamic trafficking between compartments, classified as disease-related gene and predicted intracellular protein with actin-binding molecular function, immune cell-enriched pattern suggesting specialized roles in lymphocyte activation and proliferation. ClinVar lists 55 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations creating premature stop codons (c.572_573delinsAA/p.Phe191Ter, c.1195C>T/p.Gln399Ter), splice site mutations disrupting mRNA processing (c.394+1G>T, c.599+1G>T, c.999+1G>T), frameshift duplications altering reading frame (c.644_653dup/p.Leu219fs, c.777_783dup/p.Lys262fs), missense variants affecting protein structure/function (c.26C>T/p.Ala9Val, c.500A>G/p.Asp167Gly), and deletions causing autosomal recessive intellectual disability syndrome (MRT41) characterized by macrocephaly, developmental delay, seizures, and autism spectrum features through disruption of mTORC1-mediated neuronal growth control. Functional mechanism: KPTN operates through KICSTOR-GATOR1 lysosomal anchoring model: (1) Complex assembly - KPTN forms heterotetrameric KICSTOR complex with ITFG2, C12orf66, and SZT2 through coiled-coil and protein-protein interaction domains; (2) Lysosomal recruitment - KICSTOR binds Ragulator complex (WDR59/WDR24/SEH1L/MIOS) at lysosomal surface positioning GATOR1 adjacent to RagA/B GTPases; (3) Amino acid sensing - in low amino acid conditions GATOR1 catalytic subunit DEPDC5 acts as GAP converting RagA/B-GTP to RagA/B-GDP inactive state; (4) mTORC1 inhibition - inactive Rag heterodimer fails to recruit mTORC1 to lysosomes preventing activation; (5) Actin coupling - KPTN actin-binding function (GO:0003779) potentially links mTORC1 signaling to cytoskeletal dynamics coordinating cell growth with structural remodeling; (6) Integrin-like domain function - IPR028994 integrin alpha N-terminal domain (residues 285-388) may mediate specific protein-protein interactions within KICSTOR or with additional partners beyond current STRING data. Disease mechanism: KPTN loss-of-function variants cause intellectual disability through mTORC1 dysregulation: haploinsufficiency in heterozygotes typically asymptomatic while biallelic mutations cause MRT41 syndrome; disrupted KICSTOR assembly prevents proper GATOR1 lysosomal localization leading to constitutive mTORC1 activation independent of amino acid availability; hyperactive mTORC1 drives aberrant neuronal protein synthesis, dendritic spine overgrowth, altered synaptic plasticity, and macrocephaly through excessive cell growth; seizure susceptibility reflects imbalanced excitatory/inhibitory neurotransmission from mTORC1-mediated circuit remodeling; recent literature reveals FBXO2 E3 ligase mediates KPTN ubiquitination promoting amino acid-dependent mTORC1 signaling and tumor growth (2025), OTUD3 deubiquitinase suppresses mTORC1 by removing KPTN ubiquitin chains (2024, 5 citations), and mouse models implicate mTOR signaling in cognitive and overgrowth phenotypes (2023, 9 citations) establishing KPTN as critical negative regulator of nutrient-responsive growth pathway.
