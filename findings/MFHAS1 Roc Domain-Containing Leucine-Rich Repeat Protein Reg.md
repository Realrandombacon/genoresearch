## Description
MFHAS1 encodes a 1052 aa malignant fibrous histiocytoma-amplified sequence 1 (UniProt Q9Y4C4) located at 8p23.1, functioning as a Roc domain-containing scaffold protein that regulates macrophage polarization, inflammatory signaling, and sepsis pathogenesis through MAPK pathway activation. InterPro analysis reveals a complex multi-domain architecture: N-terminal leucine-rich repeat domains (IPR001611/PF13855, aa 46-387 in multiple segments) for protein-protein interactions, central Roc domain (IPR020859, aa 403-649) within the LRRK2-related GTPase superfamily (G3DSA:3.30.70.1390, aa 408-493), and P-loop NTP hydrolase domain (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, aa 495-585) conferring GTPase activity. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.9), indicating a well-folded scaffold protein with stable domain organization suitable for signal transduction. STRING interactions reveal immune and signaling networks: CLDN23 (0.970, tight junction protein), PPP1R3B (0.929, glycogen metabolism regulator), PJA2 (0.738, E3 ubiquitin ligase that ubiquitinates MFHAS1), CAMSAP1 (0.622, microtubule minus-end binding), STK25 (0.524, serine/threonine kinase), ADGRL1/2/3 (0.522, adhesion GPCRs), DAPK1 (0.499, death-associated protein kinase). ClinVar reports 67 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 8p23.1-23.3 copy number losses (x1) and gains (x3), suggesting clinical significance in chromosomal disorder syndromes. Literature (55 papers) details emerging mechanisms: (1) Breast cancer pyroptosis - MFHAS1 silencing inducing JNK-activated NF-κB/Caspase1/GSDMD signaling axis (3 citations, 2023); (2) Macrophage polarization - Praja2-mediated MFHAS1 ubiquitination promoting M1 polarization via JNK and p38 pathway activation (66 citations, 2017); (3) Systemic lupus erythematosus - MFHAS1 locus associated with SLE susceptibility (33 citations, 2018); (4) Sepsis-induced cognitive impairment - MFHAS1 effects on hippocampal dendritic pathology, TNF-α, IL-1β, and amyloid-beta expression (11 citations, 2019); (5) Colitis - circRNA MFHAS1 reducing inflammatory responses (2020). Functional hypothesis: MFHAS1 likely functions through: (1) Scaffold Signaling Platform - LRR domains recruiting signaling complexes (STK25, DAPK1) for MAPK cascade assembly; (2) GTPase-Dependent Regulation - Roc domain GTPase activity controlling conformational switching and signal transduction timing; (3) Ubiquitination Substrate - PJA2-mediated polyubiquitination regulating MFHAS1 stability and M1/M2 macrophage balance; (4) Inflammatory Amplifier - JNK/p38 activation driving NF-κB signaling, cytokine production (TNF-α, IL-1β), and pyroptosis execution via GSDMD; (5) Tight Junction Regulation - CLDN23 interaction suggesting epithelial barrier function in colitis and tissue integrity. The Roc-LRR architecture parallels LRRK2 in Parkinson's disease, suggesting conserved GTPase-scaffold mechanisms in inflammation and neurodegeneration. Disease implications include: sepsis and sepsis-associated encephalopathy, autoimmune diseases (SLE), cancer (breast cancer pyroptosis sensitivity), inflammatory bowel disease, potential neurodegenerative links. Despite 55 papers, MFHAS1's GTPase mechanism, tissue-specific functions, and therapeutic targeting potential remain underexplored.
