## Description
RPRD1A (Regulation of Nuclear Pre-mRNA Domain-Containing Protein 1A, also known as p15RS, UniProt Q96P16) is a 312 amino acid nucleoplasmic/Golgi apparatus scaffold protein with partial characterization (21 papers) that functions as an RNA polymerase II C-terminal domain (CTD) interaction platform coordinating Ser5 dephosphorylation during transcription cycling and transcription termination. The protein contains a CID (CTD-Interacting Domain) domain (IPR006569/IPR047884/PF04818/PS51391/SM00582/cd17011, residues 1-133/4-131/8-125/1-133/8-130/4-131) with RNA polymerase II C-terminal domain binding function (GO:0099122), RPRD1A/B C-terminal domain (IPR032337/PF16566, residues 165-310/165-310), and ENTH/VHS superfamily fold (IPR008942/SSF48464/G3DSA:1.25.40.90, residues 1-137/4-137/1-137), placing it in the essential transcription machinery. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with RNA Pol II subunits and CTD regulators: RPAP2 (0.980, RNA Pol II CTD phosphatase), POLR2A (0.975, RNA Pol II largest subunit), POLR2B (0.965, RNA Pol II second largest subunit), POLR2D (0.906, RNA Pol II subunit D), RPRD1B (0.843, paralog), POLR2J (0.796, RNA Pol II subunit J), DXO (0.749, RNA pyrophosphohydrolase), POLR2M (0.670, RNA Pol II subunit M), POLR2G (0.417, RNA Pol II subunit G), and XRN2 (0.406, 5'-3' exoribonuclease for transcription termination), confirming membership in the complete RNA Pol II CTD phosphorylation cycle machinery. HPA data shows nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus subcellular localization, low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, and classification as predicted intracellular protein. ClinVar contains 48 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 18q11.1-23/18q11.2-23/18p11.32-q23 (chr18:20966775-80255845/19309942-78014123/1-78077248/136226-78014123), copy number losses at 18q12.1-12.2/18q12.1-12.3 (chr18:28752661-34829582/26595964-38643072), deletions, duplications, and single nucleotide variants (c.*4A>C), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—however recent literature links RPRD1A to hepatocellular carcinoma progression. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 83.3) indicates high confidence with well-folded architecture containing structured CID domain and C-terminal dimerization domain. Functional hypothesis: RPRD1A acts as a nuclear scaffold protein that binds Ser5-phosphorylated RNA Pol II CTD heptapeptide repeats (YSPTSPS) through its N-terminal CID domain, recruiting phosphatases (RPAP2) and termination factors (XRN2) to coordinate transcription cycle progression from initiation to termination, where recent 2011-2021 research demonstrates: (1) RPRD1A stabilizes NRF2 and aggravates HCC progression through competing with p62 for TRIM21 binding (2021, 12 citations)—RPRD1A prevents NRF2 ubiquitination by blocking TRIM21-p62 interaction, promoting antioxidant response and tumor growth in hepatocellular carcinoma, (2) p15RS/RPRD1A interacts with HDAC2 in inhibition of Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway (2015, 39 citations)—RPRD1A recruits HDAC2 to deacetylate β-catenin target genes, suppressing Wnt signaling and cell proliferation, (3) RPRD1A and RPRD1B are human RNA Pol II CTD scaffolds for Ser5 dephosphorylation (2014, 86 citations)—RPRD1A/B form homo/heterodimers binding Ser5-P CTD, recruiting phosphatases for CTD reset during transcription termination, (4) Control of RNA Pol II phosphorylation state in promoter regions by CTD interaction domain-containing proteins RPRD1A and RPRD1B (2011, 73 citations)—RPRD1A regulates promoter-proximal Ser5 phosphorylation dynamics affecting transcription initiation and early elongation, (5) STRING partners (RPAP2, POLR2A/B/D/G/J/M, XRN2, DXO) confirm integration into complete RNA Pol II CTD cycle where RPRD1A bridges phosphorylated CTD with phosphatase machinery and termination factors, (6) Nucleoplasm localization with Golgi apparatus detection suggests dual roles in transcription and potential secretory pathway regulation, (7) 48 ClinVar variants at 18q11.1-23 indicate strong disease causality potentially contributing to cancer, developmental disorders, or transcription-related diseases through defective CTD regulation, (8) High confidence structure (pLDDT 83.3) indicates stable CID domain fold for CTD binding connected to C-terminal dimerization domain enabling scaffold assembly, making RPRD1A a potential therapeutic target for HCC through NRF2 pathway modulation and Wnt/β-catenin
