# LOC129390658: MPRA-Validated Chromatin Silencer Element on Chromosome 14q31.3 with Low CNV Burden

**Date:** 2026-03-23T11:29:29.628923

**Quality Score:** 4.47/10  (E=4.65, D=4.3) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129390658 is a non-coding cis-regulatory DNA element classified as a transcriptional silencer, validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This 200bp genomic region (chr14:88766311-88766511, GRCh38) at 14q31.3 demonstrates capacity to repress super core promoter (SCP1) activity, confirming silencer function. As a regulatory DNA element, it lacks protein-coding features: no UniProt accession, no InterPro domains, no STRING interactions, and no HPA expression data. Clinical significance is low-to-moderate: ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (CNVs) spanning this locus—all duplications (x3, 5 variants) with no deletions observed across 14q31.3-32.33 regions. This CNV burden is lower than other silencer elements (LOC129390829: 9 CNVs; LOC129390650: 9 CNVs; LOC129389713: 14 CNVs). Hypothesis: LOC129390658 functions as a chromatin silencer recruiting repressive complexes (Polycomb PRC2, HDACs, or KRAB-ZNF co-repressors) to silence target genes on 14q31.3-32.33, a region containing imprinted loci and dosage-sensitive developmental genes. The exclusive duplication pattern (no deletions) suggests this element may regulate genes where haploinsufficiency is tolerated but overexpression is pathogenic—consistent with 14q32.33 imprinting disorders and Emanuel syndrome. K562 erythroid validation indicates relevance to blood cell development, and 14q rearrangements are associated with T-cell prolymphocytic leukemia and myeloid malignancies. The silencer may coordinate long-range chromatin interactions via CTCF/cohesin-mediated looping to repress multiple target genes in the 14q32 imprinted cluster (DLK1-MEG3 locus), and duplications disrupt this three-dimensional genome architecture leading to dysregulated imprinting.
