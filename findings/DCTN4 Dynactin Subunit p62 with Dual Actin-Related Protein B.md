## Description
DCTN4 (Dynactin Subunit 4, also known as p62) is a partially characterized component of the dynactin complex with 31 publications, emerging as a structural scaffold essential for dynein-mediated intracellular transport and as a modifier of cystic fibrosis lung infection severity. This compact 460-amino acid protein (UniProt Q9UJW0) features a distinctive dual-domain architecture optimized for multi-protein complex assembly. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the dynactin subunit 4 family (IPR008603/PTHR13034, positions 1-458) with two tandem dynactin p62 domains (PF05502, positions 23-124 and 241-340) that likely mediate interactions with actin-related proteins. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.3), indicating well-folded stable architecture typical of complex scaffolding proteins.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm DCTN4 operates at the core of the dynactin complex and microtubule regulation: DCTN2 (0.999, dynactin subunit 2/dynamitin), CAPZB (0.992, F-actin capping protein beta subunit), ACTR1A (0.990, actin-related protein 1A/centractin), DCTN1 (0.988, dynactin subunit 1/p150-glued), ACTR10 (0.975, actin-related protein 10), DCTN6 (0.974, dynactin subunit 6), DCTN5 (0.966, dynactin subunit 5), DCTN3 (0.950, dynactin subunit 3), ACTR1B (0.903, actin-related protein 1B), and CAPZA1 (0.811, F-actin capping protein alpha subunit). The exceptionally high confidence interactions with all major dynactin subunits (DCTN1/2/3/5/6, ACTR1A/B) validate DCTN4 as an integral structural component. CAPZB/CAPZA1 interactions (0.811-0.992) link dynactin to actin filament capping machinery.\n\nHPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping function in intracellular transport. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm, centrosome, and calyx—positioning DCTN4 at microtubule organizing centers and nuclear transport hubs. Protein class includes essential proteins and predicted intracellular proteins. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations affecting proper transcript processing (c.386-4G>A, c.909-15_909-13del), synonymous variants potentially affecting splicing efficiency (c.537G>A/p.Ser179=), and large copy number variants spanning 5q23-q35 (duplications chr5:129847794-153353546, deletions chr5:86400000-154000000). The landmark 2012 exome sequencing study identified DCTN4 as a modifier of chronic Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection in cystic fibrosis—specific variants affect timing of initial infection. 2018 studies show DCTN4 has distinct prognostic value in colon adenocarcinoma, while DCTN1/2/4 serve as diagnostic biomarkers. 2011 association with Wilson disease (c.1084A>G/p.Thr362Ala) suggests copper metabolism links.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: DCTN4 functions as a structural bridge within the dynactin complex that stabilizes the pointed-end complex and coordinates dynein activation for processive cargo transport. Mechanistically: (1) The tandem p62 domains bind ACTR1A/B (actin-related proteins forming the dynactin backbone), positioning DCTN4 at the interface between the Arp1 filament and cargo-binding subunits. (2) DCTN4 stabilizes the interaction between DCTN1/p150-glued (microtubule plus-end tracker) and the Arp1 filament, ensuring proper dynein-dynactin complex assembly. (3) CAPZB/CAPZA1 interactions suggest DCTN4 may coordinate microtubule-based transport with actin cytoskeleton remodeling at cell periphery. (4) In cystic fibrosis airways, DCTN4 variants impair dynein-mediated trafficking of antimicrobial vesicles or mucociliary clearance machinery, accelerating Pseudomonas colonization (2012 breakthrough). (5) In colon adenocarcinoma, altered DCTN4 expression disrupts Wnt/β-catenin cargo transport or mitotic spindle positioning, affecting tumor progression (2018). (6) Centrosomal localization positions DCTN4 for roles in mitotic checkpoint signaling and centrosome cohesion. (7) Wilson disease association suggests DCTN4 may traffic ATP7B copper transporter vesicles—loss causes copper accumulation. The high AlphaFold confidence reflects rigid scaffold architecture required for complex stability.
