The PDZ domain (aa 130-215) mediates protein-protein interactions with transmembrane adhesion molecules (CNTN6, MCAM, BCAM, LAMA5), while the E3 ubiquitin ligase family membership suggests regulation of substrate degradation. Brain-enriched expression (269.0 nTPM) indicates critical roles in neural development, potentially explaining X-linked neurodevelopmental disorders from Xq28 CNVs.
