## Description
LITAFD (LITAF Domain-Containing Protein) is a 72 aa small zinc ribbon protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 1 literature reference, located at chromosomal position 16p13.3. Multi-omics data reveals LITAFD as a testis-enriched membrane-associated scaffold with potential roles in spermatogenesis and immune regulation. InterPro analysis confirms a defining LITAF domain architecture: LPS-induced tumour necrosis factor alpha factor domain (IPR006629, aa 1-71) with GO terms for transcription factor activity; LITAF domain containing protein family (IPR037519, aa 2-71); LITAF-like zinc ribbon domain (PF10601, aa 2-70) indicating zinc-binding capability; LITAF domain profile (PS51837, aa 1-71); and membrane-associated motif (SM00714, aa 2-71) suggesting membrane localization. The zinc ribbon domain suggests LITAFD binds zinc ions for structural stability and protein-protein interactions, similar to the well-characterized LITAF/SIMPLE protein which regulates TNF-alpha production and endosomal sorting. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT 64.1), indicating a partially structured small protein with an ordered LITAF/zinc ribbon fold. HPA shows tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (6.8 nTPM), detected in single tissue types, with predicted intracellular and membrane protein localization, consistent with roles in spermatogenesis and membrane trafficking. STRING interactions reveal medium-confidence associations with diverse proteins: OR6N1 (0.658, olfactory receptor 6N1), OR6N2 (0.641, olfactory receptor 6N2), FANCM (0.532, Fanconi anemia complementation group M, DNA repair), AMDHD2 (0.488, N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanine amidase domain-containing 2), RCBTB1 (0.462, RCC1 and BTB domain-containing protein 1), PHF11 (0.427, PHD finger protein 11, transcriptional regulation), OR2C1 (0.422, olfactory receptor 2C1), NUBPL (0.416, nucleotide binding protein-like), CARHSP1 (0.402, calcium-regulated heat stable protein 1). The olfactory receptor interactions are unusual and may reflect testis-specific expression where olfactory receptors function in sperm chemotaxis. ClinVar lists 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large 16p13 copy number gains (chr16:666662-15743104x3, chr16:43732-13326806x3, chr16:4644892-29170820x3, chr16:46766-11525516x3, chr16:23141-11296695x3), implicating LITAFD dosage sensitivity in neurodevelopmental disorders. The 16p13.3 region is associated with Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome and other developmental conditions. The single literature reference (2021 equine vitiligo study, 8 citations) mentions LITAFD among genes involved in immune response and tumor metastasis in grey horses, suggesting conserved immune function. The extremely low literature count (1 paper) confirms this as a genuine dark gene with no human functional studies.
