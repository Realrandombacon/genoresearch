## Description
ZNF567 (Zinc Finger Protein 567) encodes a 647 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein (UniProt Q8N184) that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 5-77) that recruits the corepressor TRIM28/KAP1 for heterochromatin formation, plus three tandem C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 253-331) for DNA recognition. AlphaFold prediction shows confident structural folding (pLDDT 70.7) with modular domain architecture. HPA data indicates nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity) and cell type-enhanced expression pattern. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners including MAP3K20 (0.834, high confidence), TRIM28 (0.580, the canonical KRAB coreceptor), ZNF675 (0.499, paralogous KRAB-ZNF), ZWILCH (0.504, kinetochore complex), and other zinc finger proteins (ZNF420, SPEF1). ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (deletions and duplications on 19q11-q13.13), indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation across mammals (mouse, rat, bovine orthologs ~647 aa) demonstrates functional constraint. ZNF567 likely functions as a KRAB-ZNF transcriptional silencer that binds specific GC-rich DNA motifs via its C2H2 zinc fingers and recruits TRIM28/KAP1 to establish repressive H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. The strong interaction with MAP3K20 suggests potential crosstalk with MAPK signaling pathways, possibly linking environmental stress responses to epigenetic gene regulation. Pathogenic CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity could disrupt target gene repression programs, potentially contributing to developmental or neurocognitive phenotypes given ZNF567 expression in neural tissues.
