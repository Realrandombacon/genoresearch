## Description
CCDC144A (Coiled-Coil Domain-Containing Protein 144A, UniProt A2RUR9) is a 1427 aa intracellular protein functioning as a calcium oxalate binder that may prevent kidney stone formation through inhibition of calcium oxalate monohydrate (COM) crystallization and attenuation of COM-induced apoptotic injury to renal epithelial cells. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a partially ordered architecture (pLDDT=61.0) featuring three distinct domain regions: an N-terminal CCDC144A/B/C family domain (IPR040118/PTHR22245, aa 21-690), a central CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (IPR039497/PF14915, aa 703-1183) enabling protein-protein interactions through coiled-coil oligomerization, and a C-terminal Domain of Unknown Function DUF3496 (IPR021885/PF12001, aa 1245-1353) representing a conserved but uncharacterized functional module. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to poorly characterized genomic neighbors and transporters: CDRT144A paralog CDRT15L2 (0.599), ankyrin repeat protein ANKRD7 (0.574), zinc finger protein ZNF654 (0.529), solute carriers SLC35G6 (0.507) and SLC35G3 (0.421), olfactory receptor OR3A2 (0.487), leucine-rich repeat protein LRRC3C (0.481), Rab GAP TBC1D28 (0.480), chromosome 17 ORF C17orf64 (0.443), and calcium-binding protein EFCAB13 (0.413). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with retina highest at 11.9 nTPM, low cancer specificity, cell junction and cytosol localization, classified as predicted intracellular/membrane protein. ClinVar identifies 59 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number losses and gains spanning 17p11.2 (chr17:15694772-20582794, chr17:16651292-20450566, chr17:16664739-20370783) overlapping with Potocki-Lupski syndrome and Smith-Magenis syndrome critical regions, though no specific Mendelian disease is directly annotated to CCDC144A alone. Literature search yields only 1 paper (2014 exome sequencing in CLL/MCL family), confirming genuine 'very dark gene' status despite clinically significant CNV burden. The gene represents a 'dark gene with clinical relevance' where calcium oxalate binding function suggests direct role in renal crystallopathy and nephrolithiasis susceptibility, coiled-coil architecture enables scaffolding interactions at cell junctions, and 17p11.2 CNV pathogenesis may contribute to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through haploinsufficiency or dosage effects. Therapeutic targeting of CCDC144A-mediated COM crystallization inhibition could represent novel approach for kidney stone prevention, while CNV screening at 17p11.2 should consider CCDC144A dosage in Potocki-Lupski/Smith-Magenis syndrome patients.
