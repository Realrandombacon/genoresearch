## Description

The testis-restricted expression pattern (4.7 nTPM) confirms CAGE1 as bona fide cancer-testis antigen—silenced in somatic tissues but reactivated in tumors, making it attractive target for cancer immunotherapy. The medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 62.4) suggests CAGE1 contains flexible regions enabling dynamic interactions with multiple trafficking and signaling partners.
