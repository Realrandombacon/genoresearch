## Description
ADAP2 (Arf-GAP with Dual PH Domain-Containing Protein 2, also known as CENTA2, cent-b, UniProt Q9NPF8) is a 381-amino acid cytosolic Arf GTPase-activating protein functioning as a specific GAP for ARF6 that regulates vesicle trafficking and actin cytoskeleton remodeling at the plasma membrane, binds beta-tubulin to increase microtubule stability, translocates to cell membranes through dual pleckstrin homology (PH) domains recognizing phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate (PtdInsP3) and inositol 1,3,4,5-tetrakisphosphate (InsP4), may block entry of certain RNA viruses through Arf6 pathway modulation, and exhibits dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations at 17q11.2 within the neurofibromatosis type 1 critical region contribute to genomic disorder phenotypes.
