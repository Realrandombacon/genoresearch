## Description
LOC143666 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 11p15.5 (NC_000011.10:575884-573807) spanning approximately 2.1 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - a high CNV burden. Variants include large 11p copy number alterations: duplications spanning 11p15.5-15.4 (chr11:198510-3400939, ~3.2 Mb; chr11:196855-2116185, ~1.9 Mb; chr11:196855-5321874, ~5.1 Mb; chr11:61793-10727969, ~10.7 Mb; chr11:446754-18904742, ~18.5 Mb; chr11:218365-3377077, ~3.2 Mb; chr11:196966-3377077, ~3.2 Mb; chr11:196966-4435344, ~4.2 Mb) and one deletion (chr11:499700-5279697, ~4.8 Mb). The 11p15.5 region is the critical imprinted gene cluster for Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome (BWS) and Silver-Russell syndrome (SRS), containing H19 (lncRNA), IGF2 (insulin-like growth factor 2), CDKN1C (cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor 1C), KCNQ1OT1 (lncRNA), and TSSE6 (telomeric endonuclease). This region is subject to genomic imprinting with differential DNA methylation at imprinting control regions (ICR1/H19-IGF2 and ICR2/KCNQ1). LOC143666 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory element (enhancer/silencer) controlling imprinted gene expression, (2) a differentially methylated region (DMR) component of ICR1 or ICR2, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element (CTCF binding site) maintaining imprinting domain architecture, or (4) an alternative transcript isoform of H19 or KCNQ1OT1 lncRNAs. The high pathogenic CNV burden (9 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus represents a critical cis-regulatory element within the 11p15.5 imprinted cluster where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) allele-specific DNA methylation patterns, (2) long-range chromatin interactions between IGF2 enhancers and promoters, (3) CTCF-mediated chromatin insulation, or (4) lncRNA-mediated transcriptional silencing, leading to biallelic IGF2 overexpression (BWS) or silencing (SRS) with consequent overgrowth/undergrowth phenotypes.
