# PIK3R5: Critical Regulatory Subunit of Class IB PI3Ks in Leukemia and Cancer

## Description
PIK3R5 encodes a 101 kD regulatory subunit (UniProt Q8WYR1) of the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) complex, specifically acting as a scaffold for Class IB PI3Ks (PIK3CB, PIK3CG) with high-confidence interaction scores (0.999). Structurally, it is a well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 70.8) containing the p101 adapter domain (PF10486). Functionally, it is localized to the cytosol and centriolar satellite, with high expression in bone marrow (nTPM 84.2). Clinically, PIK3R5 is a 'WELL-STUDIED' gene with 13 pathogenic variants (including CNVs) and is implicated in Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML), Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC), and Steroid-induced Avascular Necrosis. It acts as a critical node in the PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway, regulating cell growth, proliferation, and survival.
