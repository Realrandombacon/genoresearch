## Description
SNRNP27 (Small Nuclear Ribonucleoprotein U4/U6.U5 Subunit 27, also known as 27K, RY1, UniProt Q8WVK2) is a 155-amino acid serine/arginine-rich (SR) protein functioning as an essential component of the 25S U4/U6.U5 tri-small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (tri-snRNP) complex, a major building block of the U2-type spliceosome responsible for pre-mRNA splicing and mature mRNA production. The protein architecture features a conserved U4/U6.U5 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein 27kDa protein domain (IPR013957 aa 98-153) defining family membership, U4/U6.U5 small nuclear ribonucleoproteins Pfam family domain (PF08648 aa 98-153) characteristic of tri-snRNP components, and complete U4/U6.U5 SMALL NUCLEAR RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN 27 KDA PROTEIN family membership (PTHR31077 aa 3-154) with GO term annotation for RNA splicing (GO:0008380). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 59.5), indicating a partially structured protein with potential intrinsically disordered regions—a feature consistent with dynamic spliceosome assembly where conformational flexibility enables rapid protein-protein interaction rearrangements during catalytic cycle transitions between B, Bact, B*, C, and C* spliceosomal complexes. STRING interaction network places SNRNP27 within the core spliceosome machinery with uniformly high-confidence associations to essential splicing factors: USP39 (0.998, U4/U6.U5 tri-snRNP component), SART1 (0.977, spliceosome assembly factor), RBM42 (0.964, RNA-binding protein), SNRNP70 (0.963, U1 snRNP 70kDa protein), SNRPE (0.959, U1 snRNP E protein), SNRPG (0.952, U1 snRNP G protein), LSM7 (0.949, LSM sm-like protein), PRPF4 (0.945, pre-mRNA processing factor 4), SNRNP40 (0.941, U5 snRNP 40kDa protein), and SNRPD3 (0.924, U1 snRNP D3 protein), reflecting obligate membership in the spliceosomal network where SNRNP27 likely bridges U4/U6 di-snRNP with U5 snRNP during tri-snRNP formation.
