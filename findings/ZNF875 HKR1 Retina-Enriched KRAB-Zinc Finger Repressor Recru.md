# ZNF875 (HKR1): Retina-Enriched KRAB-Zinc Finger Repressor Recruiting TRIM28 for Heterochromatin Silencing

**Date:** 2026-03-20T16:38:16.708976

**Quality Score:** 3.35/10  (E=6.2, D=0.5) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF875 (alias HKR1) encodes a 659 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional repressor (UniProt P10072) localized to the nucleoplasm and mitochondria. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 33-104) responsible for recruiting co-repressors, followed by three tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 301-384 that confer sequence-specific DNA recognition. The protein also contains a PRDM9-like zinc finger motif (IPR048414) suggesting potential histone methyltransferase-like chromatin targeting. HPA expression shows striking retina enrichment (130.2 nTPM in retina vs ubiquitous low background), indicating a specialized role in retinal development or photoreceptor maintenance. STRING interactions identify TRIM28 (KAP1, score 0.518) as the primary partner—the obligate co-repressor for KRAB-ZF proteins that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 to establish heterochromatin. Additional partners include MYCL/MYCN (0.499/0.447) and NRAS (0.414), linking ZNF875 to Myc-driven proliferation pathways. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic variants, predominantly 19q11-13.13 copy number gains/losses and a splice site mutation (c.257-1G>A), confirming dosage sensitivity. Mechanistically, ZNF875 likely functions as a retina-specific epigenetic silencer: upon binding target DNA via its zinc fingers, it recruits TRIM28/KAP1 to nucleate heterochromatin formation, repressing genes incompatible with the retinal phenotype. Dysregulation via CNVs may disrupt retinal gene networks or contribute to retinoblastoma via Myc pathway cross-talk.
