## Description
LTAP1 (C1orf43) encodes a 253 amino acid lipid transport auxiliary protein localized to mitochondria, cytosol, and Golgi apparatus. InterPro analysis reveals the NICE-3 protein family domain (PF07406, residues 7-187) and IPR010876 family signature, indicating evolutionary conservation across metazoans. UniProt orthologs show strong mammalian conservation: human Q9BWL3 (253 aa), mouse Q8R092 (253 aa), bovine Q5E943 (253 aa), rat Q5XII8 (235 aa), and C. elegans Q4W5H0 (276 aa), suggesting ~90%+ identity with mouse. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9BWL3-F1) predicts a well-folded conformation with global pLDDT 73.1 (high confidence), indicating stable tertiary structure suitable for protein-protein interactions. STRING analysis identifies 10 interaction partners with medium confidence (0.496-0.658): CHMP2A (0.658) and VPS29 (0.546) link LTAP1 to ESCRT-III vesicle sorting machinery; PSMB2 (0.591) and PSMB4 (0.496) connect to the 20S proteasome core particle; EMC7 (0.598) associates with ER membrane protein complex for membrane protein insertion; CRCT1, S100A7A, KIAA1109, C1orf189, and MRFAP1 represent additional functional partners. HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with housekeeping function. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 1q21 copy number gains/losses, implicating LTAP1 dosage sensitivity in disease. The convergence of mitochondrial localization, lipid transport annotation, ESCRT interactions, and proteasome associations suggests LTAP1 functions as a membrane trafficking adaptor that coordinates lipid vesicle sorting with protein quality control machinery.
