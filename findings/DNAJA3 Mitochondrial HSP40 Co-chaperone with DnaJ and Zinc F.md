## Description
DNAJA3 (UniProt Q96EY1) encodes a 480 aa mitochondrial co-chaperone of the HSP40/DnaJ family, also known as Tumorous Imaginal Disc 1 (Tid1), which functions as a critical regulator of mitochondrial protein homeostasis and metabolic health. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical multi-domain architecture essential for chaperone activity: an N-terminal DnaJ domain (IPR001623, cd06257, aa 92-169) that stimulates ATPase activity of partner HSP70s, a central Cysteine-rich Zinc finger domain (IPR001305, cd10719, aa 223-301) coordinating zinc ions for structural stability and client recognition, and a C-terminal substrate-binding domain (IPR002939, cd10747, aa 210-413) responsible for binding unfolded polypeptides. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 74.2), confirming a stable fold capable of precise interactions within the mitochondrial matrix. STRING interactions define a tight functional cluster with mitochondrial HSP70 machinery: HSPA9 (mtHSP70/Mortalin, 0.994), HSPA8 (0.947), HSPA1A/B (0.913/0.881), and nucleotide exchange factors GRPEL1 (0.832) and GRPEL2 (0.829), plus the protease CLPP (0.444), placing DNAJA3 at the heart of the mitochondrial protein import motor (PAM complex) and quality control system. ClinVar reports 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses at 16p13.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity; specific point mutations include a nonsense variant (p.Gln115Ter) truncating the DnaJ domain. Literature (67 papers) establishes roles in: (1) T-cell development and tumor suppression via modulation of NF-κB and Akt signaling; (2) Skeletal muscle maintenance, where haploinsufficiency drives sarcopenic obesity through imbalanced mitochondrial homeostasis and lipid metabolism; (3) B-cell development and immune function; (4) Antiviral defense, interacting with African Swine Fever Virus proteins to modulate interferon production; (5) NASH-driven hepatocellular carcinoma progression. Functional hypothesis: DNAJA3 functions through: (1) Mitochondrial Protein Import - acting as the primary co-chaperone for HSPA9, it delivers nascent polypeptides from the TIM23 translocon into the matrix, using its DnaJ domain to trigger ATP hydrolysis and substrate trapping; (2) mtDNA Nucleoid Stability - interaction with CLPP and potential links to Twinkle/POLG suggest a role in maintaining mitochondrial DNA integrity, explaining links to metabolic disease; (3) Metabolic Switching - regulation of fatty acid oxidation enzymes via chaperone-mediated folding prevents lipid accumulation in muscle, linking loss-of-function to sarcopenic obesity; (4) Immune Modulation - cytosolic pools may regulate IKK/NF-κB complexes, bridging mitochondrial stress to inflammatory responses; (5) Disease Mechanism - 16p13.3 CNVs disrupting DNAJA3 dosage likely contribute to neurodevelopmental phenotypes and metabolic syndrome, while somatic mutations in cancer may disable its tumor-suppressive checkpoint functions. The conserved domain structure and specific HSPA9 partnership position DNAJA3 as an indispensable 'gatekeeper' of mitochondrial proteostasis.
