# FAM230B: Oncogenic lncRNA Acting as ceRNA Sponge in Multiple Cancer Types

**Date:** 2026-03-20T10:51:28.851870

**Quality Score:** 3.61/10  (E=5.17, D=2.05) [MODERATE]

## Description
FAM230B (Family with Sequence Similarity 230 Member B) is a well-characterized long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) located at chromosome 22q11.21 (positions 21167864-21192160) with strong oncogenic function across multiple cancer types. Despite its "FAM" designation suggesting protein-coding potential, comprehensive analysis confirms non-coding status: (1) No UniProt accession - no protein product verified; (2) No STRING interactions - no protein partners; (3) No HPA expression - protein below detection. However, PubMed literature (9 publications) reveals extensive functional characterization as an oncogenic lncRNA. Molecular mechanism: FAM230B functions as a competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) that sponges tumor-suppressor miRNAs, derepressing oncogenic targets. Validated axes include: (a) miR-378a-3p/WNT5A in papillary thyroid cancer (PMID:33578293); (b) miR-27a-5p/TOP2A in gastric cancer (PMID:32910366); (c) miR-27a-5p/BTF3 axis regulating ferroptosis (PMID:39278369); (d) miR-1182 maturation suppression in colorectal cancer (PMID:36262247). Recent work shows METTL3-mediated m6A modification stabilizes FAM230B transcript, enhancing oncogenic activity. ClinVar contains 50 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (deletions/duplications) spanning 22q11.21, consistent with dosage-sensitive regulatory element. The 22q11.21 region is clinically significant (DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome critical region), suggesting FAM230B may contribute to haploinsufficiency phenotypes. This gene represents a "dark gene" reclassified as functional lncRNA with clear disease mechanism.
