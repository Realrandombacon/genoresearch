## Description
DCUN1D4 (Defective in Cullin Neddylation 1 Domain Containing 4; UniProt Q92564), also known as DCN1-like protein 4 or RP42-related protein, is a 292 amino acid nuclear protein with only 6 publications, representing a genuine dark gene functioning as a potentiator of cullin neddylation that activates CRL (cullin-RING ligase) E3 ubiquitin ligases through direct cullin binding and NEDD8 conjugation enhancement.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a specialized domain architecture optimized for cullin recognition and neddylation potentiation. The core contains a Potentiating Neddylation Domain (IPR005176/PS51229, residues 101-287/101-287) defining the DCN1 family function. The protein belongs to the Defective-in-Cullin Neddylation family (IPR014764/PTHR12281, residues 75-290/75-290), also described as RP42-related. The C-terminal region contains a DCN1-like PONY binding domain (IPR042460/G3DSA:1.10.238.200, residues 189-292/189-292) mediating direct cullin interaction. A Cullin binding domain (PF03556, residues 175-285) overlaps with the PONY domain, confirming the cullin-binding function. An N-terminal EF-hand superfamily domain (G3DSA:1.10.238.10, residues 100-188) suggests potential calcium-dependent regulation, though this may represent structural homology rather than functional calcium binding.
