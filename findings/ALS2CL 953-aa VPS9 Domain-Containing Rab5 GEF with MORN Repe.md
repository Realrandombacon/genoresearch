## Description
ALS2CL (ALS2 C-Terminal-Like Protein; UniProt Q60I27) is a 953 amino acid Rab guanine nucleotide exchange factor with only 21 publications, classifying it as a genuine dark gene despite essential roles in endosome dynamics, Rab5 activation, and neurological disease associations.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture homologous to the C-terminal region of ALS2 (alsin). The N-terminal region contains a Dbl homology (DH) domain (IPR035899/G3DSA:1.20.900.10, residues 7-213) characteristic of Rho/Rab GEFs. A central PH-like domain (IPR057248/PF25383, residues 227-319) mediates membrane phosphoinositide binding. Three MORN repeats (IPR003409/PF02493, residues 356-378/407-428/430-451) enable protein-protein interactions and membrane association. The C-terminal VPS9 domain (IPR003123/IPR037191/PF02204/PS51205/G3DSA:1.20.1050.80, residues 796-942/836-936) confers Rab5-specific GEF activity for endosomal trafficking. The protein belongs to the Alsin family (IPR051984, residues 8-938). AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 80.3), indicating a well-folded multi-domain GEF with flexible linkers between functional modules.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies high-confidence interactions with endosomal trafficking proteins: RAB5A (0.818, Ras-related protein Rab-5A, direct GEF substrate), EEA1 (0.705, early endosome antigen 1, Rab5 effector), plus medium-confidence partners RABGEF1 (0.655, Rab5 GEF), ATXN2L (0.650, ataxin-2-like, RNA granule protein), ARHGAP17 (0.626, RhoGAP), RINL (0.623, Ras-interacting protein-like), GAPVD1 (0.607, GTPase-activating protein and VPS9 domain-containing 1), ALS2 (0.582, alsin, full-length homolog), RIN1 (0.525, Ras-interacting protein 1), and RIN3 (0.446). This interaction profile establishes ALS2CL as a Rab5-specific GEF functioning in early endosome fusion and trafficking, analogous to ALS2 but potentially with distinct regulatory mechanisms.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in esophagus (35.3 nTPM), kidney (31.5 nTPM), and skin (31.1 nTPM). Expression is detected in many tissues but not immune cells. Molecular function is annotated as GTPase activation. Classified as a predicted intracellular protein with cell type-enhanced single cell specificity.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains at 3p26.3-q29 and 3p21.31-21.1 encompassing ALS2CL, copy number losses at 3p21.31, duplications, and coding variants including c.2583del (p.Thr860_Tyr861insTer, frameshift truncation in the VPS9 domain region) and c.1926C>T (p.Cys642=, synonymous). No monogenic disease is yet definitively assigned, but 3p CNVs cause developmental anomalies. The 2004 seminal paper established ALS2CL binds Rab5 and modulates endosome dynamics. A 2007 study showed ALS2CL interacts with ALS2 and modulates ALS2-mediated endosome dynamics. A 2013 study identified rare ALS2CL variants in schizophrenia patients, reflecting genetic heterogeneity of the disease. Additional research explores ALS2CL expression in clear-cell renal cell carcinoma.\n\nCONSERVATION: Orthologs confirmed in mouse (Q60I26, 952 aa) and rat alsin homologs, indicating strong evolutionary constraint on the VPS9-MORN-DH modular architecture across mammals.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ALS2CL functions as a Rab5-specific guanine nucleotide exchange factor that activates Rab5-GDP to Rab5-GTP on early endosomal membranes, driving endosome fusion and maturation. The DH domain may provide additional GEF activity for Rho-family GTPases, coupling endosomal trafficking to cytoskeletal remodeling. MORN repeats mediate membrane association and protein scaffolding, potentially linking ALS2CL to the actin cytoskeleton. The VPS9 domain specifically recognizes Rab5 switch regions, catalyzing nucleotide exchange. ALS2CL interaction with full-length ALS2 suggests heterodimerization or competitive regulation of shared Rab5 substrates—ALS2 mutations cause juvenile amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS2), raising the possibility that ALS2CL variants contribute to motor neuron disease through disrupted endosomal trafficking. In schizophrenia, rare ALS2CL variants may impair synaptic vesicle endocytosis and receptor trafficking in neurons. In renal cell carcinoma, altered ALS2CL expression could affect growth factor receptor endocytosis and signaling. The 12 ClinVar variants warrant investigation for neurodevelopmental disorders with endosomal trafficking defects.
