# IDI2: A Skeletal Muscle-Enriched Isopentenyl-Diphosphate Delta-Isomerase Paralogue Regulating Cholesterol Biosynthesis and Mevalonate Pathway Flux Through NUDIX Hydrolase Domain Catalysis

## Description
IDI2 (Isopentenyl-Diphosphate Delta-Isomerase 2) is a 227-amino acid metabolic enzyme characterized by a canonical NUDIX hydrolase domain architecture (IPR000086/IPR015797/IPR011876/PF00293/PS51462/PTHR10885, positions 49-199 for NUDIX core, 3-214 for isomerase domain) containing the conserved NUDIX motif within the Nucleoside Triphosphate Pyrophosphohydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.90.79.10/SSF55811, positions 1-227 and 19-212), classified under the isopentenyl-diphosphate delta-isomerase type 1 family (TIGR02150/PIRSF018427, positions 21-200 and 3-214) catalyzing reversible isomerization of isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) to dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP). STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network exclusively with mevalonate/cholesterol biosynthesis pathway enzymes: MVD (0.997, mevalonate diphosphate decarboxylase - highest confidence), FDPS (0.988, farnesyl diphosphate synthase), GGPS1 (0.986, geranylgeranyl diphosphate synthase), LSS (0.953, lanosterol synthase), IDI1 (0.904, isopentenyl-diphosphate delta-isomerase 1 - the canonical paralogue), HMGCS1 (0.899, HMG-CoA synthase 1), COQ2 (0.888, coenzyme Q2 homolog), LMO4 (0.844, LIM domain only 4), SQLE (0.787, squalene monooxygenase), and CYP51A1 (0.613, lanosterol 14-alpha-demethylase), indicating IDI2 functions as an integral component of the cholesterol/isoprenoid biosynthetic machinery. HPA data shows tissue-enriched expression exclusively in skeletal muscle (261.6 nTPM), group-enriched cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, group-enriched single cell specificity, subcellular localization to vesicles, classified as an enzyme and metabolic protein involved in cholesterol biosynthesis, cholesterol metabolism, isoprene biosynthesis, lipid biosynthesis, and lipid metabolism with isomerase molecular function. ClinVar contains 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses (10p15.3 deletions) and gains (10p15.3-15.1/13 duplications) spanning the terminal chromosomal region, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the high variant count suggests potential contribution to metabolic disorders when disrupted. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9BXS1-F1) predicts very high confidence folding (pLDDT 95.9) consistent with a well-structured enzyme containing the characteristic NUDIX hydrolase fold with ordered active site residues for substrate binding and catalysis. With 39 papers, IDI2 represents a 'gray' gene: foundational work (2007) identified it as a second IDI paralogue in mammals expressed exclusively in skeletal muscle (unlike ubiquitous IDI1), demonstrated dispensability for early development in mouse knockouts, and showed it is not required for zygotic DNA demethylation; recent studies (2024-2025) focus on the antisense lncRNA IDI2-AS1 regulating interleukin-5 expression and acute myocardial infarction via miR-33b-5p/NR4A2 axis. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that IDI2 functions as a tissue-specific isomerase paralogue that: (1) provides localized IPP/DMAPP pool regulation in skeletal muscle for protein prenylation, coenzyme Q synthesis, and cholesterol-dependent membrane maintenance; (2) acts as a metabolic flux control point responding to muscle energy demands through vesicular localization enabling rapid substrate channeling to downstream enzymes (MVD, FDPS, GGPS1); (3) potentially compensates for IDI1 deficiency under stress conditions given their shared catalytic mechanism but distinct expression patterns; the skeletal muscle enrichment (261.6 nTPM) explains why IDI2 knockout mice show no overt phenotype under normal conditions but may exhibit exercise intolerance or metabolic myopathy under stress, while human CNVs at 10p15.3 may contribute to lipid metabolism disorders or cardiovascular disease susceptibility.
