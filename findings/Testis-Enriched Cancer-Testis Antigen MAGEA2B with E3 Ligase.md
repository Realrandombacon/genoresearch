## Description
MAGEA2B (MAGE Family Member A2B) is a 314-amino acid cancer-testis antigen representing a poorly characterized paralog of the MAGEA family with only 7 publications confirming its dark gene status. The protein contains a conserved MAGE homology domain (IPR002190, positions 109-308; PF01454, positions 116-281) with winged helix WH1 (positions 80-190) and WH2 motifs (positions 193-314), plus an N-terminal disordered region (PF12440, positions 5-95). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 70.8). HPA reveals testis-enriched expression (1.8 nTPM) with cancer-enhanced expression across tumors, consistent with cancer-testis antigen biology. STRING interactions show strong connections to MAGEA2 (0.999), MAGEA3 (0.808), CSAG1 (0.758), GAGE1 (0.685), TRIM28/KAP1 (0.594), TP53 (0.575), and CTAG1B/NY-ESO-1 (0.542), suggesting MAGEA2B functions within the cancer-testis antigen network and potentially regulates p53 via TRIM28-mediated chromatin remodeling. ClinVar documents 186 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily Xq26.3-28 copy number gains/losses), though specific disease associations remain uncharacterized. The single 2010 publication identified MAGEA2B as a novel therapeutic candidate for hormone-refractory prostate cancer. We hypothesize MAGEA2B functions as an E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate recruiter (like other MAGE proteins) that targets transcriptional regulators for degradation in germ cells and aberrantly reactivates in cancers to promote survival, chemoresistance, and immune evasion through TRIM28/p53 pathway modulation.
