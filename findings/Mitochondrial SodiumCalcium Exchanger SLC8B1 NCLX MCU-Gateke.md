## Description
SLC8B1 encodes a 584 amino acid mitochondrial sodium/calcium exchanger (NCLX, UniProt Q6J4K2), a member of the SLC8 family of cation antiporters. InterPro analysis confirms canonical NCX architecture including IPR004837 (Sodium/calcium exchanger membrane region, aa 106-245, 422-572), IPR044880 (NCX central ion-binding domain superfamily, aa 135-258, 336-573), IPR051359 (Calcium:cation antiporter, aa 13-580), and PF01699 (Sodium/calcium exchanger protein, aa 106-245, 422-572), indicating a conserved mechanism of electrogenic Na+ influx coupled to Ca2+ efflux via the characteristic 12-14 transmembrane helix fold. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q6J4K2-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 80.7), consistent with a functional multi-pass membrane transporter essential for mitochondrial calcium homeostasis. HPA expression reveals adrenal gland-enriched specificity (70.6 nTPM) with broad tissue detection, suggesting specialized roles in adrenal steroidogenesis and systemic calcium signaling. STRING interactions identify 10 partners exclusively from the mitochondrial calcium handling complex including MICU1 (0.807, MCU chaperone), MCU (0.790, mitochondrial calcium uniporter), MICU2 (0.774), MCUR1 (0.706), MICU3 (0.667), SMDT1 (0.666), SLC8A3 (0.655), MCUB (0.654), SLC8A2 (0.652), and LETM1 (0.530), forming a coherent MCU-NCLX regulatory network for calcium uptake and efflux balance. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 12q copy number gains and losses (12q23-24, 12p13-q24), though no specific disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature (23 papers) identifies SLC8B1 as critical for mitochondrial calcium efflux in B lymphocytes (antigen receptor stimulation), sudden cardiac death via MCU-NCLX uncoupling in myocardial infarction, and tissue-specific calcium regulation in zebrafish. The combination of NCX domains, high-confidence AlphaFold structure, adrenal enrichment, exclusive MCU/MICU interaction network, 12q CNV burden, and emerging cardiac/immune functions suggests SLC8B1 functions as a mitochondrial calcium efflux regulator coordinating MCU-mediated uptake, apoptosis prevention, and cardiac excitation-contraction coupling, with copy number variations potentially contributing to arrhythmias and metabolic stress phenotypes.
