# ZNF174: SCAN-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Disease-Associated Copy Number Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-20T10:32:33.869509

**Quality Score:** 2.5/10  (E=4.7, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF174 encodes a 407-amino acid zinc finger protein (UniProt Q15697) functioning as a transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal SCAN oligomerization domain (PF02023, IPR003309, residues 42-130) that mediates homodimerization and heterodimerization with related SCAN-family proteins, plus three C-terminal C2H2-type zinc fingers (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 326-404) that enable sequence-specific DNA binding. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT=60.0), consistent with structured domains connected by flexible linkers typical of transcription factors. HPA data confirms nuclear localization (nucleoli fibrillar center, nuclear bodies) with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity). STRING interactions reveal strong co-expression with 10 partner proteins including ZSCAN32 (0.965), ZNF24 (0.864), ZKSCAN4 (0.852), ZSCAN1 (0.832), and ZNF396 (0.820), all SCAN/zinc finger family members, suggesting ZNF174 operates within a transcriptional repression complex. ClinVar documents 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses at 16p13.3, indicating dosage sensitivity and disease relevance. The combination of SCAN-mediated oligomerization, C2H2 DNA recognition, nuclear localization, and widespread expression supports a role as a sequence-specific transcriptional silencer that recruits co-repressor complexes to target gene promoters.
