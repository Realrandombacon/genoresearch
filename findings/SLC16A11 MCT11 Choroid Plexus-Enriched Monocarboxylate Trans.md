# SLC16A11 (MCT11): Choroid Plexus-Enriched Monocarboxylate Transporter Regulating Lipid Metabolism and Insulin Sensitivity via MFS Domain Architecture and Copy Number Variants

## Description
SLC16A11 (UniProt Q8NCK7) encodes a 447 amino acid Monocarboxylate Transporter 11 (MCT11), a member of the Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) responsible for pyruvate and lipid metabolite transmembrane transport. InterPro analysis confirms canonical MFS domains (IPR011701, PF07690, PTHR11360) spanning aa 14-407, consistent with its role in proton-linked monocarboxylate transport. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8NCK7-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 83.2), supporting its function as a functional membrane transporter. HPA expression reveals tissue-specific enrichment in the choroid plexus (nTPM 11.8) and broad detection across many tissues, with group-enriched cancer specificity, suggesting roles in blood-brain barrier function and metabolic regulation. Literature search identified 60 papers, highlighting associations with type 2 diabetes risk haplotypes, fatty acid metabolism alterations, and reduced peripheral insulin sensitivity in mice. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses on 17p13, with no specific monogenic disease annotated, implying involvement in complex metabolic traits. The convergence of choroid plexus expression, MFS domain architecture, high structural confidence, and CNV burden supports a functional hypothesis: SLC16A11 functions as a lipid/pyruvate transporter in the choroid plexus that regulates systemic lipid metabolism and insulin sensitivity; disruption via copy number variations may contribute to metabolic syndrome and type 2 diabetes susceptibility.
