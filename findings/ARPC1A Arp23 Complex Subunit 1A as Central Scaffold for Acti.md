## Description
ARPC1A (Actin-Related Protein 2/3 Complex Subunit 1A, p41-Arc, Q92747) encodes a 370 aa WD40-repeat protein functioning as the central scaffold subunit of the Arp2/3 complex that nucleates branched actin filament networks essential for cell migration, invadopodia formation, and cancer metastasis. HPA localizes ARPC1A to cell junctions/cytosol as actin-binding protein with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic variants including 7q22.1 copy number losses (chr7:98396469-102108193, chr7:98454022-100723798 deletions) and gains (chr7:87477185-100333327, chr7:98693388-159119707 duplications). Literature (48 papers) demonstrates oncogenic roles: ARPC1A promotes NSCLC malignancy via stimulating drug resistance and cell migration (2025), pan-cancer analysis validates ARPC1A/c-Myc axis in non-small cell lung cancer (4x citations, 2025), prognostic relevance in low-grade glioma progression (20x citations, 2024), novel oncogene and therapeutic target in glioblastoma (2024). Mechanistic hypothesis: ARPC1A serves as structural core of Arp2/3 complex positioning ACTR2 and ACTR3 to mimic actin monomer conformation creating template for new filament nucleation at 70-degree angle from existing filaments; WD40 beta-propeller binds ARPC2/ARPC3 stabilizing complex assembly; WASP/WAVE family proteins bind ARPC1A via C-terminal VCA domain releasing autoinhibition and activating nucleation; in cancer cells, ARPC1A upregulation enhances invadopodia formation enabling extracellular matrix degradation and metastatic invasion; ARPC1A/c-Myc axis creates positive feedback loop where c-Myc transcriptionally activates ARPC1A while branched actin networks facilitate c-Myc nuclear import; in glioblastoma, ARPC1A overexpression drives infiltrative growth through white matter tracts; 7q CNVs alter ARPC1A dosage affecting cytoskeletal dynamics and cell motility; ARPC1B paralog provides immune cell-specific functions while ARPC1A maintains ubiquitous housekeeping roles.
