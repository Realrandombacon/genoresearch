## Description
LOC129390735 (Gene ID: 129390735) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 15q26.1 (90,513,522-90,513,722 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak2422 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number gains (duplications) affecting the 15q25-q26 chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 15q26.1-26.3 duplication (90068508-101888837, ~11.8 Mb); (2) 15q23-26.3 duplication (72154949-101920998, ~29.8 Mb); (3) 15q22.2-26.3 duplication (59828460-101920998, ~42.1 Mb); (4) 15q26.1-26.3 duplication (88676575-98364743, ~9.7 Mb); (5) 15q25.3-26.3 duplication (85397539-101888909, ~16.5 Mb); (6) 15q25.3-26.3 tetrasomy (85826665-101920998, ~16.1 Mb, 4 copies); (7) 15q25.2-26.3 duplication (82859676-101810992, ~19.0 Mb); (8) 15q25.2-26.3 duplication (83711377-101843270, ~18.1 Mb); (9) 15q25.3-26.3 duplication (87904735-101843270, ~13.9 Mb); (10) 15q26.1-26.3 duplication (89679237-101978958, ~12.3 Mb). These CNVs cause 15q overgrowth syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and somatic overgrowth. The 15q critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: IGF1R (insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor, growth signaling, somatic overgrowth), CHD2 (chromodomain helicoacetyltransferase DNA binding protein 2, chromatin remodeling, epilepsy), CYFIP2 (cytoplasmic FMR1 interacting protein 2, neuronal development, WAVE complex), and multiple neuronal development genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390735 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Duplications encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of target genes contributing to 15q overgrowth syndrome phenotype; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting normal enhancer-promoter contacts; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting growth signaling (IGF1R) and neurodevelopment (CHD2, CYFIP2). The 11 ClinVar CNVs (all duplications including one tetrasomy) suggest this regulatory element exhibits strong triplosensitivity - increased silencer dosage causes pathological gene repression. IGF1R overactivation causes somatic overgrowth and macrocephaly. CHD2 haploinsufficiency causes epilepsy and neurodevelopmental disorders. CYFIP2 variants cause intellectual disability and epilepsy. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
