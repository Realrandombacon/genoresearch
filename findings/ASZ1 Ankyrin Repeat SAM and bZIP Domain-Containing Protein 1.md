## Description
ASZ1 (Ankyrin Repeat SAM and Basic Leucine Zipper Domain-Containing Protein 1, UniProt Q8WWH4) is a 475 amino acid intracellular protein with limited human characterization (23 papers, predominantly zebrafish and mouse models) representing a genuine dark gene in human reproductive biology that functions as an essential scaffold protein organizing the intermitochondrial cement (IMC) for piRNA biogenesis machinery during mammalian spermatogenesis. The protein contains multiple Ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110/PF00023/PF12796 residues 43-238, with repeats at 45-74/78-110/110-213/130-210) conferring protein-protein interaction capacity (GO:0005515), a Sterile Alpha Motif (SAM) domain (IPR001660/PF07647/IPR042650 residues 272-334) for protein binding and oligomerization, and belongs to the ASZ1 SAM domain subfamily (cd09521) with Ets-1 transcription factor structural homology (G3DSA:1.10.150.50 residues 274-345).
