## Description
CDK13 (Cyclin-Dependent Kinase 13) is a 1512 amino acid serine/threonine protein kinase that functions as a critical regulator of RNA Polymerase II transcriptional elongation, mRNA splicing, and 3'-end processing through phosphorylation of Pol II C-terminal domain (CTD) heptad repeats and interaction with cyclin partners for coordinated gene expression control in neurodevelopmental and proliferating tissues.
