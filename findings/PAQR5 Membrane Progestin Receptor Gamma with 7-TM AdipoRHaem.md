## Description
PAQR5 encodes a 330-amino acid membrane progestin receptor gamma (UniProt Q9NXK6), also known as progestin and adipoQ receptor family member 5, a very well-folded 7-transmembrane membrane receptor functioning in progesterone signaling and cancer regulation through AdipoR/Haemolysin-III domain architecture. The protein contains a canonical AdipoR/Haemolysin-III-related domain (IPR004254, positions 7-280) characteristic of 7-transmembrane receptors distinct from GPCRs, Haemolysin-III related family (PF03006, positions 43-269), classified within the ADIPOR/progestin receptor-related family (PTHR20855, positions 7-280) with unique membrane topology. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 92.6, residues 1-330) indicating exceptionally stable folding with well-defined 7-transmembrane helices suitable for ligand binding and signal transduction.
