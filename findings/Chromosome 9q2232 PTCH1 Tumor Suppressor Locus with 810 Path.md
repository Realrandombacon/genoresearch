## Description
LOC100507346 is an uncharacterized genetic locus identifier on chromosome 9q22.32 (NC_000009.12, positions 95463608-95470018, ~6.4 kb region) that maps to PTCH1 (Patched-1), a well-characterized tumor suppressor gene in the Hedgehog signaling pathway. No UniProt accession exists for the LOC identifier, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified for LOC100507346 specifically in NCBI nucleotide databases (0 results). Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the LOC identifier to an Ensembl ID. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions for the LOC identifier. However, ClinVar contains 810 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (variants 4809722, 4803606, 4803249, 4801802, 4801801, 4801800, 4799175, 4750635, 4737601, 4733060), all annotated to PTCH1 transcript NM_000264.5. Variant types include missense mutations (c.1745T>C p.Val582Ala, c.2323C>G p.Leu775Val, c.2431G>A p.Asp811Asn, c.2495T>C p.Val832Ala, c.1900C>T p.His634Tyr, c.2549A>G p.Asp850Gly), nonsense mutations (c.2552G>A p.Trp851Ter), synonymous variants (c.2031C>G p.Thr677=), splice site mutations (c.2560+17G>A), and frameshift duplications (c.1848-4dup). All variants have uncertain significance (?). The 9q22 chromosomal region contains PTCH1 (Patched-1, 12-pass transmembrane receptor, Hedgehog pathway inhibitor, constitutive repression of Smoothened, tumor suppressor) causing Gorlin syndrome (nevoid basal cell carcinoma syndrome, autosomal dominant, multiple basal cell carcinomas, jaw keratocysts, skeletal anomalies, medulloblastoma predisposition), as well as SHH (Sonic Hedgehog, morphogen, embryonic patterning), SUFU (Suppressor of Fused, Hedgehog pathway negative regulator), IHH (Indian Hedgehog, cartilage development), and GLI1 (GLI family zinc finger 1, Hedgehog transcriptional activator, oncogene). Functional hypothesis: LOC100507346 represents a PTCH1 gene identifier/alias rather than a distinct dark gene. PTCH1 functions as the primary receptor for Hedgehog ligands (SHH, IHH, DHH), constitutively inhibiting Smoothened (SMO) through oxysterol sequestration in the absence of Hedgehog. Hedgehog binding relieves PTCH1 inhibition, allowing SMO accumulation at the primary cilium and GLI transcription factor activation driving cell proliferation and survival genes. Disease mechanism involves heterozygous PTCH1 loss-of-function causing constitutive Hedgehog pathway activation, uncontrolled cell proliferation, and tumorigenesis. Gorlin syndrome patients develop hundreds of basal cell carcinomas, odontogenic keratocysts, and have 5-10% medulloblastoma risk. Somatic PTCH1 mutations occur in sporadic basal cell carcinoma, medulloblastoma, and rhabdomyosarcoma. The 810 ClinVar variants indicate strong mutational constraint and clinical significance. This locus is NOT a true "dark gene" but rather a well-characterized tumor suppressor with extensive clinical literature.
