# LRRC8C: Leucine-Rich Repeat VRAC Subunit Forming Heterohexameric Anion Channels with Lipid Metabolism Links

**Date:** 2026-03-20T13:52:38.944611

**Quality Score:** 3.7/10  (E=6.6, D=0.8) [MODERATE]

## Description
LRRC8C encodes an 803 amino acid volume-regulated anion channel (VRAC) subunit (UniProt Q8TDW0) that obligatorily heteromerizes with LRRC8A to form functional swell-activated chloride channels. The protein contains an N-terminal pannexin-like transmembrane domain (PF12534, residues 1-338, IPR021040) with 6 predicted transmembrane helices, followed by a cytoplasmic leucine-rich repeat domain (IPR001611, residues 588-705) containing 7 LRR motifs that mediate protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 83.4). STRING interactions reveal strong partnerships with other VRAC subunits LRRC8B (0.749), LRRC8D (0.523), and LRRC8A (0.522), confirming hexameric channel assembly. Unexpected interactions with lipid metabolism proteins FABP4 (0.639), LPL (0.548), and SREBF1 (0.541) suggest LRRC8C may couple cell volume regulation to lipid homeostasis. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low specificity) with Golgi apparatus localization. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants including copy number losses and missense mutations (e.g., p.Pro746Gln, p.Val390Leu) linked to dwarfism and intellectual disability, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is high: mouse (Q8R502), rat (Q498T9), and bovine (A5PK13) orthologs all maintain 803 aa length. Mechanistically, LRRC8C likely functions as a regulatory subunit modulating channel gating, substrate selectivity (organic osmolytes vs. inorganic anions), and cGAMP import for STING immune signaling. The LRR domain may serve as a scaffold for metabolic enzyme recruitment, explaining lipid metabolism interactions.
