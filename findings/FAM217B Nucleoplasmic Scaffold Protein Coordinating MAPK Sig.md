## Description
FAM217B encodes a 383-amino acid nucleoplasmic protein containing a conserved FAM217 family domain (IPR029266/PF15344/PTHR22145, residues 1-383) with no catalytic motifs, characteristic of scaffold proteins that organize multiprotein complexes. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=57.2) indicates medium-confidence folding with partially structured regions, consistent with scaffold proteins containing both ordered interaction interfaces and flexible linker regions for conformational adaptability. Protein interaction analysis reveals functional coherence across three interconnected cellular processes: (1) Signal transduction via NCK1 (0.769, NCK adaptor protein 1, SH2/SH3 adaptor linking tyrosine kinases to actin cytoskeleton) and DUSP6 (0.611, dual specificity phosphatase 6, ERK1/2-specific MAPK phosphatase); (2) Vesicle trafficking via RAB11FIP1 (0.591, Rab11 family interacting protein 1, endosomal recycling regulator) and IFT46 (0.481, intraflagellar transport protein 46, ciliary vesicle transport); (3) Ubiquitination/phosphatase regulation via ASB8 (0.558, ankyrin-box SOCS box protein 8, E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate recognition) and PPP1R3D (0.546, protein phosphatase 1 regulatory subunit 3D, glycogen targeting). The NCK1-DUSP6-RAB11FIP1 triad suggests FAM217B functions as a nucleoplasmic scaffold coordinating MAPK signal termination (via DUSP6 recruitment) with vesicle trafficking (via RAB11FIP1), analogous to scaffold proteins that spatially organize kinase-phosphatase-substrate complexes. Tissue expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasmic subcellular localization, and cell type-enhanced expression pattern, indicating housekeeping function in nuclear signaling complex assembly. Clinical significance includes 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants, predominantly copy number gains and deletions spanning 20q13.13-13.33 region, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene amplification or haploinsufficiency disrupts MAPK signaling homeostasis and vesicle trafficking coordination. The combination of FAM217 domain architecture, nucleoplasmic localization, NCK1-DUSP6-RAB11FIP1 interaction network, partial structural disorder, and CNV dosage sensitivity strongly suggests FAM217B functions as a nucleoplasmic scaffold protein that spatially organizes MAPK phosphatase (DUSP6) recruitment to NCK1 adaptor complexes while coordinating endosomal vesicle trafficking, potentially regulating growth factor receptor signaling termination and nuclear-cytoplasmic vesicle communication.
