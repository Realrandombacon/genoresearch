## Description
WDR86 (WD repeat-containing protein 86, UniProt Q86TI4) is a 376-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 7q35 within a gene-dense region. InterPro analysis reveals canonical WD40 repeat architecture: multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400, aa 7-43/47-83/87-123/304-343) forming a beta-propeller structure, WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943/G3DSA:2.130.10.10, aa 3-64/65-244/245-352), and WDR86-specific family membership (IPR044715, aa 7-342). GO annotations indicate protein binding activity (GO:0005515), consistent with WD40 proteins functioning as scaffolds for multi-protein complex assembly. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves very high confidence (pLDDT 90.4), indicating a well-folded stable beta-propeller structure with 7-8 WD40 blades suitable for simultaneous binding of multiple protein partners. Human Protein Atlas shows thyroid gland-enriched expression (27.6 nTPM — highest among all tissues), detected in many tissues, with cancer-enhanced expression pattern and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, plasma membrane, and cytosol — suggesting roles in thyroid hormone synthesis or secretion where protein complex assembly is critical. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners comprising chaperone and ubiquitination machinery: TXNDC9 (0.688, thioredoxin domain-containing protein 9, sperm-specific chaperone), CCT5/CCT3/CCT7 (0.596/0.579/0.525, chaperonin containing TCP1 subunits involved in actin/tubulin folding), PDCL (0.568, phosducin-like protein regulating G-protein signaling), CRYGN (0.518, crystallin gamma N), SKP1 (0.512, S-phase kinase-associated protein 1, SCF ubiquitin ligase component), CDC6 (0.493, cell division control protein 6, DNA replication initiator), and RBX1 (0.403, RING-box protein 1, E3 ubiquitin ligase). This interactome strongly suggests WDR86 functions as a scaffold coordinating protein folding (CCT chaperonins), ubiquitin-mediated degradation (SKP1-RBX1 SCF complex), and cell cycle progression (CDC6). ClinVar contains 78 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number gains and losses across 7q33-36.3, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 7q cause disease phenotypes potentially affecting thyroid function, cell cycle regulation, and protein homeostasis. Conservation across vertebrates indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental cellular processes. WDR86 likely functions as a regulatory scaffold in thyroid epithelial cells, using its WD40 beta-propeller to simultaneously recruit chaperone complexes for protein folding, ubiquitin ligases for targeted degradation, and cell cycle regulators — coordinating proteostasis with thyroid hormone production and secretion.
