## Description
VOPP1 (alias ECOP, GASP, WBP1L2, vesicular overexpressed in cancer prosurvival protein 1, WW domain binding protein) encodes a 172 amino acid partially structured cytoplasmic vesicle membrane protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 65.6) containing a VOPP1 family domain (IPR026229, aa 1-172), VOPP1 N-terminal domain (IPR060429, aa 6-54), VOPP1 C-terminal domain (IPR060430/PF27963, aa 57-90), and belonging to the PTHR14971 family (vesicular overexpressed in cancer prosurvival protein 1, aa 1-172). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in retina (233.4 nTPM), detected in all tissues distribution, predicted intracellular and membrane protein class, cancer-enhanced specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 7p22.3-q36.3, 7p11.2, and 7p14.1-11.2 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes and glioblastoma amplification. STRING interactions reveal network with EGFR trafficking and WW domain-binding proteins: TMEM207 (transmembrane protein 207, Golgi vesicle trafficking, 0.617), SEC61G (SEC61 gamma subunit, ER translocon, 0.613), VWA7 (von Willebrand factor A domain containing 7, extracellular matrix, 0.613), WWOX (WW domain containing oxidoreductase, tumor suppressor, 0.568), VSTM2A (V-set and transmembrane domain containing 2A, oligodendrocyte differentiation, 0.529), C7orf25 (chromosome 7 ORF 25, 0.499), SEPTIN14 (septin 14, cytoskeleton, 0.495), EGFR (epidermal growth factor receptor, receptor tyrosine kinase, 0.475), WBP1 (WW domain binding protein 1, EGFR trafficking, 0.444), and LANCL2 (lanthionine synthetase C-like 2, ABA receptor, 0.422). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The VOPP1 domain architecture combined with gene name "WW domain binding protein" and high-confidence EGFR interaction (0.475) strongly suggests VOPP1 functions as an EGFR endosomal trafficking scaffold protein involved in receptor recycling and prosurvival signaling. WBP1 interaction (0.444) is mechanistically central—WBP1 is a known WW domain-binding protein that interacts with the PY motifs of EGFR cytoplasmic tail and regulates EGFR ubiquitination and lysosomal sorting, suggesting VOPP1 functions as a WBP1 paralog competing for EGFR binding to modulate receptor fate. WWOX association (0.568) extends this role to tumor suppression—WWOX contains WW domains that bind proline-rich motifs and functions as a tumor suppressor by antagonizing EGFR signaling, placing VOPP1 in competitive balance with WWOX for EGFR pathway regulation. TMEM207 interaction (0.617) links to Golgi vesicle trafficking—TMEM207 regulates retrograde transport from endosomes to Golgi, suggesting VOPP1 facilitates EGFR recycling from early endosomes to plasma membrane rather than lysosomal degradation. SEC61G association (0.613) connects to ER translocon function—SEC61G forms the protein-conducting channel for nascent polypeptide entry into ER, suggesting VOPP1 may regulate EGFR biogenesis and quality control. EGFR interaction (0.475) directly places VOPP1 in receptor tyrosine kinase signaling—EGFR amplification is a hallmark of glioblastoma and VOPP1 is co-amplified with EGFR on chromosome 7p11.2, suggesting VOPP1 functions as an EGFR oncogene cofactor enhancing receptor stability and downstream MAPK/PI3K signaling. Retina enrichment (233.4 nTPM) is exceptional—this represents one of the highest tissue expression levels, indicating specialized function in retinal pigment epithelium and photoreceptor cells, consistent with EGFR role in retinal development and photoreceptor survival. Cancer-enhanced expression indicates upregulation in tumor cells, particularly glioblastoma where VOPP1 is co-amplified with EGFR, consistent with prosurvival function through enhanced EGFR recycling and reduced lysosomal degradation. Detected in all tissues indicates ubiquitous basal expression, consistent with housekeeping EGFR trafficking regulation. Cell type-enhanced pattern indicates specific upregulation in epithelial and glial cell lineages. Moderate pLDDT score (65.6) indicates partially structured protein with flexible regions, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational adaptability for WW domain recognition and PY motif binding. Pathogenic 7p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting EGFR trafficking, receptor recycling, and prosurvival signaling, potentially contributing to 7p duplication syndrome and glioblastoma pathogenesis through enhanced EGFR stability and constitutive kinase activation.
