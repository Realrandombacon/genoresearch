## Description
PCBP3 encodes Poly(rC)-binding protein 3 (371 aa, P57721), an RNA-binding protein of the hnRNP K homology (KH) domain family functioning in post-transcriptional gene regulation. InterPro analysis reveals canonical PCBP architecture with three type I KH domains: KH1 (IPR004087/PF00013, positions 47-107), KH2 (positions 131-194), KH3 (positions 295-358) - each domain mediating single-stranded RNA/DNA recognition via conserved GXXG motif; GO annotations confirm RNA binding (GO:0003723) and nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676). STRING interactions reveal moderate-confidence partners: YBEY (0.610, mitochondrial ribonuclease), TSTA3 (0.530, tissue-specific transplantation antigen), RBM11 (0.473, RNA-binding motif protein), PCBP1 (0.465, paralog), RWDD2B (0.453), PDCD2L (0.448), SNRPA (0.445, U1 snRNP), POFUT2 (0.438), VPS53 (0.437), COMMD9 (0.433) - suggesting roles in RNA processing, splicing, and mitochondrial RNA metabolism. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with neural enrichment: retina (75.7 nTPM), brain (59.3 nTPM); immune cell enhanced pattern; subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, vesicles, and mitochondria - the mitochondrial localization is distinctive among PCBP family members. ClinVar documents 93 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 21q22.3 CNVs (gains and losses) indicating strong dosage sensitivity, though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely characterized. AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 67.2) with three structured KH domains connected by flexible linkers, consistent with RNA-binding proteins requiring conformational adaptability. Literature (37 papers) reveals emerging disease roles: 2022 study shows lncRNA A2M-AS1 promotes ferroptosis in pancreatic cancer via PCBP3 interaction; 2021 research identifies PCBP3 in pulmonary fibrosis via PFKFB3-driven glycolysis regulation by anlotinib; 2018 proteomic study identifies PCBP3 as prognostic biomarker for pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Functional hypothesis: PCBP3 functions as an RNA-binding protein regulating mRNA stability, translation, and splicing with tissue-specific roles in neural tissues (retina, brain) and immune cells; mitochondrial localization suggests unique role in mitochondrial RNA metabolism distinguishing it from cytoplasmic PCBP1/2; ferroptosis regulation indicates involvement in iron-dependent cell death pathways relevant to cancer and neurodegeneration. Mechanistic proposal: Three KH domains enable cooperative binding to poly(C) sequences in target mRNAs, regulating stability and translation efficiency; PCBP1 paralog interaction suggests potential heterodimerization or functional redundancy; mitochondrial localization may link nuclear RNA processing to mitochondrial gene expression; ferroptosis regulation via A2M-AS1 lncRNA interaction suggests ceRNA network involvement; immune cell enhancement indicates roles in inflammatory gene regulation; dosage sensitivity (93 CNVs) suggests haploinsufficiency could disrupt RNA regulatory networks contributing to developmental defects or cancer. This gene represents a moderately characterized RNA-binding protein with emerging roles in cancer biology, ferroptosis, and neural/immune function.
