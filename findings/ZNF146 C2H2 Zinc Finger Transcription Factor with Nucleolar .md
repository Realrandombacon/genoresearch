STRING interactions identify 10 high-confidence partners: API5 (0.774), FASTKD2 (0.530), POLR2B (0.485), ZNF770 (0.469), HIPK4 (0.467), and SMARCAD1 (0.464), suggesting involvement in transcription elongation (POLR2B), chromatin remodeling (SMARCAD1), and apoptosis inhibition (API5). Conservation is high: mouse ortholog Q8BQN6 shares identical 292 aa length. The protein belongs to the Krueppel C2H2-type zinc-finger family (PTHR23226), a large class of sequence-specific DNA-binding regulators.
