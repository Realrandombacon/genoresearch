## Description
LACRT (Lacritin) encodes a 138 aa secreted extracellular glycoprotein (Q9GZZ8) belonging to the dermcidin/lacritin family with established roles in tear film homeostasis, salivary function, and emerging cancer biomarker applications. With only 16 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined protein family membership but incomplete mechanistic understanding of its diverse physiological roles.
