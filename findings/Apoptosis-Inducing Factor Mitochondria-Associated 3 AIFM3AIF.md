## Description
AIFM3 (Apoptosis-inducing factor mitochondria-associated 3, also known as AIFL) is a 605 aa mitochondrial enzyme representing a genuine dark gene with only 38 literature references despite essential roles in apoptosis regulation, redox metabolism, and neurodevelopment. Multi-omics data reveals AIFM3 as a well-folded oxidoreductase integrating electron transport, apoptotic signaling, and Ras pathway regulation. InterPro analysis confirms a multi-domain architecture characteristic of Rieske-type oxidoreductases: an N-terminal Rieske [2Fe-2S] iron-sulfur domain (IPR017941/G3DSA:2.102.10.10, aa 68-177) binding 2 iron-2 sulfur clusters for electron transfer, multiple FAD/NAD(P)-binding domains (IPR023753/IPR036188/G3DSA:3.50.50.60, aa 188-502) for redox catalysis, and a C-terminal dimerization domain (IPR016156/G3DSA:3.30.390.30, aa 506-594) enabling homodimer formation. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.4), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme with canonical oxidoreductase architecture. HPA shows striking choroid plexus-enriched expression (136.7 nTPM, highest of all tissues), with mitochondrial subcellular localization and biological processes linked to apoptosis, electron transport, and transport. STRING interactions reveal a functionally coherent apoptotic/metabolic network: AIFM3 associates with LZTR1 (0.716, leucine zipper-like post-translational regulator 1, a Ras pathway negative regulator), DAO (0.652, D-amino acid oxidase), DDO (0.621, D-aspartate oxidase), ACOD1 (0.621, aconitate decarboxylase/immune-responsive gene 1), FDX1 (0.606) and FDX2 (0.606, ferredoxins), PIPOX (0.609, sarcosine oxidase), THAP7 (0.602, zinc finger protein), and MRPS5 (0.579, mitochondrial ribosomal protein). ClinVar lists 357 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 22q11.21 copy number alterations (gains: chr22:18919477-21459713x3, chr22:18161474-21110475x3, chr22:18929330-21110475x3; losses: chr22:19017218-21105423x1, chr22:18929330-21110475x1), suggesting AIFM3 dosage sensitivity in DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome region. Recent literature (2021-2025) identifies AIFM3 as: (1) An LZTR1 interaction partner negatively regulating Ras signaling by downregulating SHOC2 and phosphorylated p90RSK in apoptosis; (2) A pro-apoptotic factor with bilateral functions in the intrinsic apoptosis pathway; (3) A colorectal cancer biomarker with stage-dependent expression; (4) A kidney development gene with spatio-temporal expression patterns. The scarcity of mechanistic literature (38 papers) despite clear disease causality makes AIFM3 a paradigmatic dark gene awaiting comprehensive characterization.
