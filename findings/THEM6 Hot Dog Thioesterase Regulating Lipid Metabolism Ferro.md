## Description

Literature (37 papers) documents THEM6-mediated reprogramming of lipid metabolism supporting treatment resistance in prostate cancer (23 citations), carboplatin sensitivity modulation via ferroptosis through FDFT1 regulation in triple-negative breast cancer (3 citations), prognostic biomarker roles in breast cancer with immune infiltration (2 citations), and bladder cancer molecular subtype prediction (3 citations). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: THEM6 functions as a hot dog fold thioesterase that hydrolyzes specific acyl-CoA or thioester substrates to regulate lipid metabolism pathways critical for cancer cell survival under therapeutic stress. The FDFT1 (farnesyl-diphosphate farnesyltransferase 1) connection suggests THEM6 modulates cholesterol/isoprenoid biosynthesis, which affects membrane composition and ferroptosis sensitivity. Nuclear speckle localization indicates potential roles in RNA processing or transcriptional regulation beyond catalytic activity. The 58 ClinVar variants at 8q24 suggest THEM6 dosage effects contribute to cancer predisposition or developmental disorders through dysregulated lipid metabolism. Medium-confidence interactions with transmembrane proteins (TMC6, MFSD3, TMEM200A, SLC4A7) suggest THEM6 may coordinate metabolic signaling at membrane interfaces. The very high-confidence AlphaFold structure indicates a stable catalytic core suitable for substrate specificity studies and drug targeting in chemotherapy-resistant cancers.
