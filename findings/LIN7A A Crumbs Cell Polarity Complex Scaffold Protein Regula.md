## Description
LIN7A (Lin-7 Homolog A, also known as Veli-1 or hLin-7A) is a 233-amino acid scaffold protein characterized by an N-terminal L27 domain (IPR004172/IPR014775/IPR036892/PF02828/G3DSA:1.10.287.650, positions 25-83) mediating heterodimeric protein-protein interactions within polarity complexes, and a C-terminal PDZ domain (IPR001478/IPR036034/PF00595/PS50106/cd06796/G3DSA:2.30.42.10, positions 108-190) recognizing specific C-terminal motifs on client proteins, classified under the Lin-7 protein family (IPR017365/PIRSF038039, positions 19-210) and membrane-associated multiprotein complex regulator family (IPR051109, positions 16-211). STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network with cell polarity and synaptic scaffolding proteins: CASK (0.999, calcium/calmodulin-dependent serine protein kinase - highest confidence partner indicating core complex membership), PATJ (0.975, PALS1-associated tight junction protein), GRIN2B (0.937, glutamate ionotropic receptor NMDA type subunit 2B), KIF17 (0.802, kinesin family member 17), MPP2 (0.775, membrane palmitoylated protein 2), DLG1 (0.727, discs large MAGUK scaffold protein 1), MPP7 (0.478), MPP5 (0.477), MPP6 (0.446), and APBA1 (0.436, amyloid beta precursor protein binding family A member 1). HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in bone marrow (26.3 nTPM) and retina (24.6 nTPM), detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced specificity, group-enriched blood cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, classified as a predicted intracellular protein involved in exocytosis, protein transport, and transport as a crumbs cell polarity complex component. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses (12q21.1-21.33, 12q21.2-22 deletions), copy number gains (12p13.33-q24.33, 12q21.2-21.31 duplications), and translocations t(12;14)(q14;q13), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in neurodevelopmental disorders and hematologic malignancies. AlphaFold structure (AF-O14910-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 80.2) consistent with a well-structured scaffold protein containing ordered L27 and PDZ domains connected by flexible linker regions typical of polarity complex adaptors. With 44 papers, LIN7A represents a 'gray' gene rapidly transitioning from dark due to emerging clinical associations (2023-2025): LIN7A silencing reduces U87 glioblastoma cell invasion identifying it as a therapeutic target; LIN7A serves as a diagnostic marker for bone non-union via miR-29b-3p axis; DNA methylation of LIN7A predicts decitabine response in t(8;21) acute myeloid leukemia; and LIN7A is differentially expressed in metastatic breast cancer to brain and lymph nodes. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that LIN7A functions as a critical scaffolding component of the Crumbs polarity complex that: (1) uses its L27 domain to heterodimerize with CASK forming a stable core complex recruiting PATJ and other MAGUK proteins to basolateral membranes; (2) employs its PDZ domain to anchor client proteins including GRIN2B (NMDA receptors) and kinesin motors (KIF17) for polarized trafficking in neurons and epithelial cells; (3) regulates exocytosis and protein transport pathways controlling cell migration and invasion in cancer contexts; (4) undergoes epigenetic silencing via DNA methylation in AML making it a biomarker for hypomethylating agent response; its bone marrow (26.3 nTPM) and retina (24.6 nTPM) enrichment aligns with roles in hematopoietic cell polarity and photoreceptor outer segment maintenance, while cancer associations reflect dysregulated cell polarity driving metastatic behavior.
