Despite lacking UniProt accession, HPA expression data, and STRING interaction partners, this gene shows significant clinical relevance with 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar. No PubMed publications directly characterize this gene, confirming its "dark" status. The clustering of pathogenic CNAs in the 8q12 region suggests LOC286177 resides in a dosage-sensitive genomic neighborhood, potentially involved in cell cycle regulation or developmental processes where gene copy number must be tightly controlled.
