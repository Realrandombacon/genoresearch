## Description
TMED7-TICAM2 encodes a 188 aa transmembrane emp24 domain-containing protein 7 (UniProt A0A0A6YYA0), functioning as a critical negative regulator of Toll-like receptor 4 (TLR4) signaling from the endosome that prevents excessive inflammatory responses to bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS). The protein contains a conserved GOLD domain (IPR009038, PF01105, SM01190, aa36-188) belonging to the emp24/gp25L/p24 family (IPR015720, PTHR22811, aa21-188) with G3DSA:2.60.120.680 and IPR036598 homologous superfamilies (aa45-133/52-128), involved in vesicular trafficking and cargo selection. AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 82.2), indicating a well-folded GOLD domain with stable beta-sandwich architecture typical of lipid-binding and vesicle coat proteins. HPA classifies TMED7-TICAM2 as having low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, suggesting ubiquitous expression consistent with a housekeeping role in innate immune regulation across cell types. The 0.847 confidence interaction with TLR4 and 0.821 with TIRAP confirms TMED7-TICAM2 acts at the endosomal checkpoint to inhibit TLR4-MyD88 signaling after LPS internalization. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses on 5q (5q15-23.2, 5q22.2-23.3, 5q22.1-23.1, 5q21.1-23.2, 5q15-22.3, 5q21.3-23.1 deletions), one copy number gain (5q14.3-23.3), and two missense variants (p.Ala7Val, p.Trp10Arg in TMED7 exon), though no specific disease is yet assigned. TMED7-TICAM2 functions as an endosomal brake on TLR4 signaling—the GOLD domain (aa36-188) likely binds lipid cargo or vesicle coats (COPII) to retain TLR4 or its adapters (TIRAP/MYD88) in specific endosomal compartments, preventing downstream NF-kappaB and IRF3 activation. High-confidence TLR4 (0.847) and TIRAP (0.821) interactions suggest TMED7-TICAM2 physically associates with the receptor complex post-internalization to terminate signaling. TICAM2 (0.800) and MYD88 (0.795) connections indicate TMED7-TICAM2 modulates both MyD88-dependent (pro-inflammatory cytokines) and TRIF-dependent (interferon) pathways. The ubiquitous expression pattern ensures broad protection against septic shock and chronic inflammation. RHBDL4-mediated degradation suggests TMED7-TICAM2 levels are dynamically tuned—low levels permit strong immune responses, while high levels enforce tolerance. The 16 ClinVar variants, particularly 5q deletions and N-terminal missense mutations (p.Ala7Val, p.Trp10Arg), may cause haploinsufficiency or destabilize the GOLD domain, leading to hyperactive TLR4 signaling and susceptibility to septic shock, autoimmune diseases, or chronic inflammatory disorders. The well-folded structure (pLDDT 82.2) implies structural intolerance to mutation. TMED7-TICAM2 represents a partially characterized dark gene with clear mechanistic role in innate immune homeostasis and emerging relevance to sepsis, acute lung injury, and lymphoma.
