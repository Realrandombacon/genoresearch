## Description
CALML6 (Calmodulin-Like Protein 6, UniProt Q8TD86) is a 181 amino acid intracellular calcium-binding protein with moderate characterization (20 papers) that functions as a negative regulator of antiviral innate immunity and acute inflammation by suppressing IRF3 dimerization and controlling TAK1 ubiquitination. The protein contains two canonical EF-hand domains (IPR002048 residues 33-101/107-178 with calcium ion binding GO:0005509, cd00051 residues 37-98, G3DSA:1.10.238.10 residues 28-103/104-180) forming a paired EF-hand structure (IPR011992 residues 26-174, PF13499 residues 109-173) characteristic of the calmodulin/myosin light chain/troponin C superfamily (IPR050230 residues 27-174, PTHR23048 residues 27-174), with specific calcium-binding sites at residues 156-168 (IPR018247, PS00018).
