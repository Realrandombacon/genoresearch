## Description
ZNF367 encodes a 350 amino acid Krüppel-like C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q7RTV3) localized to the nucleoplasm. InterPro analysis reveals canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, PF13912) at positions 167-224, characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors. The protein belongs to the KRUEPPEL-LIKE TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR family (PTHR23235), suggesting regulatory roles in development and differentiation.

HPA expression data shows tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in bone marrow (24.6 nTPM), consistent with UniProt annotation suggesting involvement in transcriptional activation of erythroid genes. Subcellular localization is nucleoplasmic, matching its role as a DNA-binding transcriptional activator.

STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-confidence protein partners including UHRF1 (0.531), a ubiquitin-like plant homeodomain RING finger protein involved in epigenetic regulation and DNA methylation maintenance; SMYD1 (0.492), a histone methyltransferase; and TCF19 (0.486), a transcription factor. These interactions suggest ZNF367 may function within chromatin remodeling complexes coordinating transcriptional programs.

BLAST conservation analysis demonstrates strong evolutionary constraint: 90.6% identity with Rattus norvegicus ortholog, 89.7% with Mus musculus, and 66.7% with Danio rerio, indicating functional importance across vertebrates.

ClinVar documents 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses on chromosome 9q22.32, though specific disease associations remain uncharacterized. The variant burden suggests haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity may underlie clinical phenotypes.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ZNF367 acts as a sequence-specific transcriptional activator recruiting chromatin modifiers (UHRF1, SMYD1) to regulate erythroid differentiation genes in bone marrow progenitors. The C2H2 zinc finger array confers DNA-binding specificity while protein interactions enable epigenetic coordination.
