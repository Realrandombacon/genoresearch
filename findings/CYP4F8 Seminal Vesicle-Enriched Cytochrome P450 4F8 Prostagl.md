## Description
CYP4F8 encodes a 520 aa cytochrome P450 family 4 subfamily F member 8 (UniProt P98187), also known as CPF8 or CYPIVF8, functioning as an endoplasmic reticulum-localized monooxygenase that catalyzes 19-hydroxylation of prostaglandin endoperoxides and omega-hydroxylation of arachidonic acid to (18R)-hydroxyarachidonate. The protein contains the canonical cytochrome P450 fold (IPR001128/PF00067, aa52-502) with heme-binding pocket and iron coordination site (IPR017972, aa461-470), belonging to the E-class group I cytochrome P450 family (IPR002401) and CYP4F subfamily (cd20679, aa74-515) within the cytochrome P450 superfamily (IPR036396/G3DSA:1.10.630.10, aa42-519). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.2), indicating a well-folded P450 structure with characteristic helical bundle and substrate-binding channel. HPA shows seminal vesicle-enriched expression (202.7 nTPM) with tissue-enriched specificity, cancer-enhanced pattern, and roles in fatty acid/lipid metabolism. STRING interactions reveal CYP4F8 associates with eicosanoid metabolism enzymes: ALOX15B (0.934, arachidonate 15-lipoxygenase), CYP2J2 (0.918, epoxygenase), CYP4A11 (0.916, omega-hydroxylase), CYP4A22 (0.910), CYP2C8 (0.908), CYP2C9 (0.907), ALOX12 (0.906, 12-lipoxygenase), PTGS2 (0.906, COX-2), CYP2B6 (0.904), and CYP2U1 (0.902). ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 19p13.12-13.11/19p13.3-q13.43 copy number losses and gains. Literature (46 papers) demonstrates CYP4F8 functions as prominent prostaglandin endoperoxide 19-hydroxylase in seminal vesicles; CYP4F8 variants associate with methotrexate toxicity and plasma levels in osteosarcoma patients; CYP4F8 identified as therapeutic target in prostate cancer through arachidonic acid pathway dysregulation; CYP4F8 oxygenates polyunsaturated long-chain fatty acids with catalytic importance of Tyr-125 and Gly-328 residues; CYP4F8 produces anti-inflammatory lipid mediators; and CYP4F8 polymorphisms affect drug metabolism pharmacogenetics. CYP4F8 functions as a specialized eicosanoid-modifying enzyme—its P450 active site positions prostaglandin H2 and arachidonic acid for regioselective 19-hydroxylation, generating 19-hydroxy-PGE2 and 18(R)-HETE with distinct biological activities from parent compounds. Seminal vesicle enrichment reflects reproductive tract-specific eicosanoid production for sperm maturation and seminal fluid composition. Prostate cancer association suggests CYP4F8-derived eicosanoids promote tumor growth through PPAR signaling or inflammation modulation. Osteosarcoma methotrexate toxicity link indicates CYP4F8 variants alter drug clearance or prostaglandin-mediated renal toxicity. Copy number variants may cause dosage effects on eicosanoid balance, potentially affecting inflammatory disease susceptibility and drug response phenotypes.
