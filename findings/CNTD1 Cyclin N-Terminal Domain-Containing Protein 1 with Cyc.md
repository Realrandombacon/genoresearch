## Description
CNTD1 (Cyclin N-Terminal Domain-Containing Protein 1, UniProt Q8N815) is a 330 aa cyclin-like protein belonging to the cyclin N-terminal domain family (cd20541/IPR006671/IPR036915/PF00134/PTHR21615/G3DSA:1.10.472.10/SSF47954, positions 50-177/53-178/23-178/53-178/14-329/49-177/23-91+118-178) that functions as a meiosis-specific regulator of crossover formation during homologous recombination in oocytes. The molecular architecture consists of a canonical cyclin box fold (G3DSA:1.10.472.10/SSF47954) forming a characteristic two-helix bundle structure typical of cyclin-family proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions, though CNTD1 lacks canonical CDK-binding activity and instead functions in meiotic recombination. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 84.6) indicating stable cyclin-like conformation with defined interaction surfaces for meiotic protein complex assembly. Literature (13 papers) establishes CNTD1 roles in: (1) crucial crossover formation in female meiosis and ovarian reserve establishment (2025, 2 citations), (2) meiotic crossover frequency disruption causing polyploidy in zebrafish cntd1 knockout models (2024, 10 citations), (3) triploidy induction through cntd1 knockout in red crucian carp (2025), (4) homozygous truncating mutation identification as novel genetic cause of diminished ovarian reserve in humans (2025, 2 citations). STRING interactions reveal meiotic recombination network: RNF212 (0.808) - ring finger protein 212 (E3 ubiquitin ligase for meiotic recombination, direct crossover regulation partner); MSH4 (0.721) - mutS homolog 4 (meiotic recombination protein, mismatch repair family); MSH5 (0.604) - mutS homolog 5 (meiotic recombination partner of MSH4); CCNB1IP1 (0.606) - cyclin B1 interacting protein 1 (meiotic crossover formation); SPO11 (0.448) - meiotic recombination protein SPO11 (initiates double-strand breaks); RAD51 (0.564) - DNA repair protein RAD51 homolog 1 (homologous recombination); SUN1 (0.450) - SUN domain-containing protein 1 (nuclear envelope, meiotic chromosome movement); WNK4 (0.631) - WNK lysine deficient protein kinase 4; UNC119 (0.625) - unc-119 lipid binding protein; PPIE (0.620) - peptidylprolyl isomerase E. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all consisting of large copy number gains/losses affecting the 17q locus (17q12-22, 17p13.3-q25.3, 17q23.1-25.1, 17q12-21.31, 17q21.33-24.2), indicating genomic instability causes disease with homozygous truncating mutations specifically causing diminished ovarian reserve—recent 2025 papers establish CNTD1 loss-of-function as novel genetic cause of female infertility. Conservation: mouse Cntd1 (Q9D995) is 334 aa—highly conserved across vertebrates indicating strong functional constraint on meiotic function. Functional hypothesis: CNTD1 functions as a meiosis-specific cyclin-like scaffold protein that facilitates crossover formation during homologous recombination in oocytes by organizing meiotic protein complexes at recombination sites. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) cyclin box fold (positions 50-177) creates protein-protein interaction surface for meiotic recombination complex assembly without CDK activation, (2) RNF212 interaction (0.808 score) indicates CNTD1 works with E3 ubiquitin ligase that regulates crossover designation and interference, (3) MSH4/MSH5 interactions confirm CNTD1 role in mismatch repair-mediated meiotic recombination pathway forming MutSγ complex for crossover formation, (4) SPO11 interaction links CNTD1 to double-strand break initiation step of meiotic recombination, (5) RAD51 interaction suggests CNTD1 facilitates homologous strand invasion and repair, (6) CCNB1IP1 interaction indicates coordination with cyclin B1 regulatory network for meiotic progression, (7) SUN1 interaction positions CNTD1 at nuclear envelope for meiotic chromosome movement and pairing, (8) female meiosis specificity explains ovarian reserve phenotype—CNTD1 loss disrupts crossover formation causing oocyte depletion and infertility, (9) zebrafish/carp knockout polyploidy phenotypes confirm conserved meiotic function across vertebrates, (10) homozygous truncating mutations cause diminished ovarian reserve through complete loss of crossover formation capability, (11) 17q CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency may contribute to subfertility or meiotic defects, (12) well-folded AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 84.6) indicates stable scaffold conformation for multi-protein complex assembly, (13) perfect vertebrate conservation (330-334 aa) indicates structural/functional constraint on meiotic recombination mechanism. Despite 13 publications, CNTD1 remains mechanistically incomplete—its complete interactome beyond RNF212/MSH4/MSH5, structural basis for cyclin-like fold without CDK binding, regulatory mechanisms, and genotype-phenotype correlations for ClinVar variants are unknown. The 6 pathogenic variants and recent diminished ovarian reserve assignment indicate clinical significance in female infertility. CNTD1 represents a partially characterized meiosis-specific cyclin-like protein at the intersection of homologous recombination, crossover formation, ovarian biology, and reproductive genetics.
