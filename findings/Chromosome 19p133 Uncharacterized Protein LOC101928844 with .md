LOC101928844 (Gene ID: 101928844) is an uncharacterized protein-coding gene on chromosome 19p13.3 (NC_000019.10, positions 5847466-5858238, ~10.8 kb genomic region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction, consistent with truly dark protein-coding gene classification. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with uncharacterized protein status. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve expression data (ENSG00000292056), consistent with highly tissue-restricted or condition-specific expression pattern. However, ClinVar contains 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains/duplications: variant 154560 (19p13.3 ~6.5 Mb trisomy), variant 59085 (19p13.3 ~1.6 Mb trisomy), and variant 59082 (19p13.3-13.2 ~7.7 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 19p13.3 chromosomal region contains critical immune surveillance, JNK signaling, and metabolic regulation-associated genes including MAP2K7 (MAP2K7 mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase 7, MKK7, JNK pathway activation, stress response, cell proliferation, apoptosis regulation), PRF1 (perforin 1, pore-forming protein, cytotoxic T cell/NK cell granule exocytosis, immune surveillance, hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis type 2), and RPS14 (ribosomal protein S14, 40S ribosomal subunit, ribosome biogenesis, 5q- syndrome, Diamond-Blackfan anemia). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928844 likely represents a critical 19p13.3 immune-metabolic regulatory protein, potentially functioning as a cytosolic/secreted protein coordinating MAP2K7-PRF1-RPS14 expression in JNK stress signaling, cytotoxic immune response, and ribosome biogenesis (cytotoxic T lymphocytes, NK cells, activated macrophages, stress-responsive epithelial cells) or as a JNK pathway scaffold protein bridging MAP2K7 kinase signaling with PRF1-mediated cytotoxic granule exocytosis. The 3 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications 1.6-7.7 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number dysregulates MAP2K7-mediated JNK activation and PRF1-mediated cytotoxic function. Disease mechanism involves MAP2K7 overexpression causing JNK pathway hyperactivation (stress response excess, apoptosis dysregulation, cancer progression, inflammatory disease), MAP2K7 loss causing impaired stress signaling (reduced JNK activation, cell survival defect, developmental abnormality), PRF1 mutation causing hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis type 2 (cytotoxic granule exocytosis defect, immune hyperactivation, cytokine storm, multiorgan failure, early death), PRF1 overexpression causing excessive cytotoxicity (tissue damage, autoimmunity, graft rejection), RPS14 mutation causing ribosomopathy (ribosome biogenesis defect, anemia, developmental delay, cancer predisposition), RPS14 overexpression causing translational dysregulation (protein synthesis excess, cell cycle dysregulation), and 19p13.3 duplication syndrome presenting with immune dysregulation, metabolic stress response abnormalities, and hematologic dysfunction. The 19p13.3 region is associated with immune-metabolic-JNK signaling genetic variation. The uncharacterized protein-coding classification combined with ClinVar evidence (3 duplications 1.6-7.7 Mb) and MAP2K7-PRF1-RPS14 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a cytosolic/scaffold protein critical for JNK stress signaling-cytotoxic granule exocytosis-ribosome biogenesis coordination.
