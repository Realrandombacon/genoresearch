## Description
PIGS (Phosphatidylinositol glycan anchor biosynthesis class S, aliases PIG-S, DEE95, GPIBD18) is a 555 amino acid predicted membrane protein (UniProt Q96S52) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 85.5) containing canonical PIG-S domain architecture including PIG-S family domain (residues 12-552) and PIGS domain (residues 22-546), functioning as a ubiquitously expressed essential subunit of the GPI transamidase complex that regulates GPI-anchor biosynthesis and cell surface protein anchoring through attachment of GPI anchor to protein (GO:0016255), GPI-anchor transamidase complex localization (GO:0042765), and endoplasmic reticulum membrane association via partnerships with PIGK, GPAA1, PIGO, PGAP1, PIGF, PRSS50, PIGU, PIGC, PIGT, and PIGB.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals PIGS contains canonical GPI transamidase architecture: PIG-S family domain (IPR019540, residues 12-552) forming a 541 aa structured module (~97% of protein) characteristic of GPI transamidase subunits with attachment of GPI anchor to protein (GO:0016255) and GPI-anchor transamidase complex (GO:0042765) functions, PIGS Pfam domain (PF10510, residues 22-546) forming a 525 aa module (~95%) providing the conserved PIGS structural core, and GPI transamidase component PIG-S family (PTHR21072, residues 12-552) defining PIGS-specific identity within the GPI biosynthesis pathway. The PIGS domain spans 525-541 amino acids (residues 12-552, ~95-97%), forming the catalytic scaffold for GPI transamidase complex assembly.
