## Description
FBXO3 (UniProt Q9UK99), also known as FBA or FBX3, encodes a 471 aa F-box protein functioning as the substrate recognition component of the SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex mediating phosphorylation-dependent ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of target proteins. InterPro analysis reveals a tripartite domain architecture characteristic of F-box substrate receptors: an N-terminal F-box domain (IPR001810/PF00069/cd22084/G3DSA:1.20.1280.50/IPR036047, residues 10-58/13-61/4-81) providing protein-protein interaction surfaces (GO:0005515) for binding SKP1 adaptor protein and anchoring FBXO3 to the SCF core complex; a central Knr4/Smi1-like domain (IPR018958/G3DSA:3.40.1580.10/IPR037883, residues 121-251/115-258/99-265) of uncertain function potentially involved in substrate recognition or protein-protein interactions; and a C-terminal ApaG domain (IPR007474/PF04379/G3DSA:2.60.40.1470/IPR036767/NF003967, residues 278-408/275-407/295-383/281-405) originally identified in Co2+/Mg2+ efflux proteins but repurposed in FBXO3 for substrate binding or complex assembly. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 88.7) indicating a well-folded multi-domain protein where the F-box forms an L-shaped helical bundle for SKP1 engagement, while ApaG and Knr4/Smi1-like domains create a composite substrate-binding surface. STRING interactions define a high-confidence SCF ubiquitin ligase network: SKP1 (0.994, S-phase kinase-associated protein 1, essential adaptor linking F-box proteins to CUL1), CUL1 (0.989, cullin-1, scaffold protein organizing SCF complex), KIAA1549L (0.872, uncharacterized protein), FBXL2 (0.860, F-box and leucine-rich repeat protein 2, paralogous substrate receptor), POLD2 (0.669, DNA polymerase delta subunit 2, potential substrate), NUDCD1 (0.626, NudC domain-containing protein 1), C11orf91 (0.604), FBXL15 (0.597, F-box and leucine-rich repeat protein 15), RBX1 (0.568, RING-box protein 1, E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme recruiter), and CEACAM1 (0.431, carcinoembryonic antigen-related cell adhesion molecule 1, potential substrate). The ultra-high confidence SKP1/CUL1/RBX1 connections confirm FBXO3 operates as an integral SCF complex component. HPA classifies FBXO3 as low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer/immune specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, involved in host-virus interaction and Ubl conjugation pathway, reflecting housekeeping roles in protein turnover across cell types. ClinVar lists 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting of copy number losses (11p14.1-13x1, 11p14.3-12x1, 11p15.1-12x1, 11p13-12x1), copy number gains (11p13-q25x3, 11p15.3-13x3), and intronic/splice variants (c.359-16T>A, c.679-109A>G, c.725-59G>T) though no single-gene disorder is definitively assigned suggesting contribution to 11p13 deletion syndromes (WAGR syndrome region) or ubiquitination disorders. Functional hypothesis: FBXO3 operates through integrated SCF-mediated ubiquitination mechanisms: (1) SCF complex assembly - F-box domain binds SKP1 positioning FBXO3 within the CUL1-RBX1 scaffold for E2 enzyme recruitment; (2) Substrate recognition - ApaG and Knr4/Smi1-like domains create composite binding surface recognizing phosphorylated degron motifs on target proteins; (3) DUSP9 regulation - Recent 2025 studies show FBXO3 mediates DUSP9 (dual specificity phosphatase 9) ubiquitination reprogramming MAPK signaling to eradicate tyrosine kinase inhibitor-resistant CML stem cells; (4) USP4/Twist1 stabilization - FBXO3 stabilizes USP4 deubiquitinase and Twist1 transcription factor promoting PI3K-mediated breast cancer metastasis through paradoxical stabilization rather than degradation; (5) PGC-1α/Nrf2 activation - Ginsenoside Rg1 reduces FBXO3 stability in m6A-dependent manner activating PGC-1α/Nrf2 signaling alleviating sepsis-induced acute lung injury; (6) Disease mechanism - CNV losses cause haploinsufficiency disrupting substrate ubiquitination leading to accumulation of oncoproteins or cell cycle regulators; gains may cause overexpression altering degradation kinetics; 11p13 location places it near WT1/PAX6 in WAGR syndrome critical region; (7) Conservation - Mouse Fbxo3 (Q9DC63, 480 aa), Bovine FBXO3 (A6H7H7, 469 aa) indicate essential conserved function. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: identification of complete substrate repertoire beyond DUSP9/USP4/Twist1, structural basis for substrate phosphodegron recognition by ApaG/Knr4 domains, mechanism of paradoxical substrate stabilization vs degradation, role in WAGR syndrome phenotypes, genotype-phenotype correlations for 11p13 CNV variants, functional divergence from paralogs (FBXL2, FBXL15), post-translational modifications regulating FBXO3 stability (m6A methylation), therapeutic potential in CML stem cell eradication and breast cancer metastasis prevention, cell-type specific functions beyond housekeeping ubiquitination, and crosstalk with other E3 ligase complexes.
