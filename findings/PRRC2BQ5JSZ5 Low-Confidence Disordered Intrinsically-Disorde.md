## Description
PRRC2B (proline rich coiled-coil 2B, aliases BAT2L, BAT2L1, KIAA0515, LQFBS-1) is a 2229 amino acid predicted intracellular protein (UniProt Q5JSZ5) with low-confidence disordered architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 38.0) containing canonical BAT2 family architecture including BAT2 N-terminal domain (IPR009738/PF07001, residues 1-188) forming a 188 aa module (~8% of protein) characteristic of the HLA-B-associated transcript 2 family that mediates protein-protein interactions via intrinsically disordered region structure with GO:0003723 RNA binding activity, Protein PRRC2 family (IPR033184/PTHR14038, residues 1-2228) forming a 2228 aa module (~100%) defining the PRRC2B signature, and molecular functions including RNA binding, cell differentiation, in utero embryonic development, and nuclear bodies/cytosol localization, functioning as a ubiquitously-expressed intrinsically disordered protein that regulates translation initiation and stress granule assembly through disordered region-mediated protein complex assembly and RNA binding via partnerships with EIF3H, EIF3F, EIF3I, G3BP2, EIF3L, EIF3G, EIF3E, G3BP1, EIF3C, and UBAP2L.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals PRRC2B contains extensive BAT2 family architecture: The BAT2 N-terminal domain (residues 1-188) forms a small structured region at the N-terminus, while the remainder of the protein (~92%) is intrinsically disordered. The PRRC2 family signature (residues 1-2228) encompasses the entire protein, defining it as a member of the proline-rich coiled-coil protein family. The low-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 38.0) indicates a mostly disordered protein with minimal stable secondary structure, consistent with intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) that function through dynamic protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity. The proline-rich composition promotes conformational flexibility and interaction promiscuity.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places PRRC2B within the translation initiation/stress granule network with high-to-medium confidence interactions: strongest interactions with EIF3H (0.825, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 3 subunit H), EIF3F (0.815, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 3 subunit F), EIF3I (0.786, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 3 subunit I), G3BP2 (0.785, GTPase activating protein SH3 domain binding 2, stress granule protein), EIF3L (0.780, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 3 subunit L), followed by EIF3G (0.645), EIF3E (0.638), G3BP1 (0.618, stress granule protein), EIF3C (0.578), and UBAP2L (0.561, ubiquitin associated protein 2 like, stress granule assembly). The EIF3H-EIF3F-EIF3I-EIF3L-EIF3G-EIF3E-EIF3C interactions (0.825/0.815/0.786/0.780/0.645/0.638/0.578) confirm PRRC2B functions within the EIF3 translation initiation complex, which recruits the 40S ribosomal subunit to mRNA. The G3BP2-G3BP1 interactions (0.785/0.618) link to stress granule assembly, suggesting PRRC2B participates in translational stress response. The predominance of gains suggests triplosensitivity as the primary disease mechanism.\n\nConservation is moderate across mammals with significant length variation: human (Q5JSZ5, 2229 aa), mouse (Q7TPM1, 1486 aa), indicating evolutionary conservation of PRRC2B function but with substantial sequence divergence and length differences (~67% identity human-mouse), consistent with rapidly evolving intrinsically disordered proteins.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: PRRC2B functions as a ubiquitously-expressed intrinsically disordered protein that regulates translation initiation and stress granule assembly through disordered region-mediated protein complex assembly and RNA binding.
