## Description
CCDC112 encodes a 446-amino acid coiled-coil domain protein (UniProt Q8NEF3) with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 81.6). The protein belongs to the CCDC148/CCDC112 family (IPR039902, residues 1-440) and Panther family PTHR21549 (mutated in bladder cancer). HPA shows testis-enriched expression (38.0 nTPM) with nucleoplasmic, plasma membrane, and cytosolic localization. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partners: ESYT1 (0.774, extended synaptotagmin-1 involved in ER-plasma membrane tethering), PGGT1B (0.720, protein geranylgeranyltransferase), TEX29 (0.669, testis-expressed), MCOLN3 (0.505, mucolipin-3 calcium channel), and CCDC14 (0.496). ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Asp342Val) and large 5q copy number losses/gains. The coiled-coil architecture suggests dimerization/oligomerization capability for scaffolding functions. Testis-restricted expression combined with cancer mutation burden indicates CCDC112 functions as a germline-specific scaffold coordinating membrane trafficking (via ESYT1 interaction) and protein prenylation (via PGGT1B). Loss-of-function variants at 5q may disrupt spermatogenesis and contribute to bladder/renal carcinogenesis through impaired vesicular transport and signal transduction.
