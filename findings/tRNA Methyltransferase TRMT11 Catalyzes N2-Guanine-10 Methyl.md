## Description
TRMT11 (tRNA Methyltransferase 11 Homolog) is a 463 aa S-adenosylmethionine (SAM)-dependent methyltransferase that catalyzes N2-methylation of guanine at position 10 (m2G10) in tRNAs, a critical modification for tRNA stability, structural integrity, and translational fidelity. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture characteristic of class I methyltransferases: an N-terminal THUMP domain (IPR059073/PF25904, aa 9-180) for tRNA binding and substrate recognition, and a C-terminal catalytic methyltransferase domain (IPR000241/PF01170, aa 190-320) belonging to the AdoMet_MTases superfamily (cd02440, aa 219-377) with the conserved SAM-binding fold (IPR029063/G3DSA:3.40.50.150, aa 136-401). The Vaccinia Virus VP39-like superfamily assignment (G3DSA:3.40.50.150) indicates structural homology to cap-specific mRNA methyltransferases. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.4), confirming a well-folded enzyme with distinct tRNA-binding and catalytic lobes. STRING interactions place TRMT11 at the center of a methyltransferase network with ultra-high confidence partner TRMT112 (0.999), a universal activator protein that stabilizes multiple methyltransferases, plus connections to TRMT1 (0.885, tRNA m1G37 methyltransferase), BUD23 (0.847, 18S rRNA methyltransferase), WDR36 (0.836, ribosome biogenesis), TRMT61A (0.806, tRNA m1A58 methyltransferase), ALKBH8 (0.769, tRNA wobble uridine modifier), and DUS1L (0.617, tRNA dihydrouridine synthase). HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue and cancer specificity, nuclear bodies localization, and classification as a metabolic protein involved in tRNA processing. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 6q (6q21-23.3), implicating TRMT11 haploinsufficiency or overexpression in genomic disorders, though no specific monogenic disease is yet definitively attributed. Literature (21 papers) establishes TRMT11 as: a prognostic marker in advanced castrate-resistant prostate cancer (2015); part of the TRMT112-methyltransferase network consisting of seven partners (TRMT11, TRMT1, BUD23, METTL5, METTL18, N6AMT1, ALKBH8) that require TRMT112 for stability and activity (2021, 24 citations); essential for m2G10 tRNA modification affecting cell proliferation, protein translation, and pre-mRNA splicing (2023, 28 citations); and linked to nicotinamide metabolism in heart failure (2025).
