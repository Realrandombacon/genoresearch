## Description
C1orf198 (UniProt Q9H425) is a 327-amino acid cytosolic protein containing a Domain of Unknown Function (DUF4706/PF15797, IPR031600) spanning residues 31-134. AlphaFold prediction reveals a partially structured protein (pLDDT 60.1), suggesting a mix of ordered and flexible regions that may facilitate dynamic protein interactions. HPA data shows strong brain enrichment (144.0 nTPM) and immune cell enhancement, indicating roles in high-energy-demand tissues. STRING interaction analysis reveals striking connections to mitochondrial respiratory chain components with exceptionally high confidence: COX5A (0.995), COX7C (0.994), UQCRFS1 (0.987), UQCRQ (0.984), UQCRB (0.981), CYC1 (0.980), UQCR10 (0.974), MT-CO3 (0.973), MT-CO2 (0.972), and UQCRC2 (0.966). These partners span Complex III (ubiquinol-cytochrome c reductase) and Complex IV (cytochrome c oxidase), suggesting C1orf198 functions as a regulatory factor or assembly chaperone for oxidative phosphorylation. ClinVar records 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses at 1q42, implicating dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders. Literature confirms dark gene status (only 6 papers), with recent studies identifying C1orf198 as a prognostic biomarker in colorectal cancer, bladder cancer metastasis, and gastric cancer. We hypothesize C1orf198 acts as a cytosolic regulator of mitochondrial respiratory chain assembly or activity, where its brain-enriched expression supports neuronal energy metabolism, and disruption via CNVs causes neurodevelopmental phenotypes through impaired oxidative phosphorylation. The DUF4706 domain may mediate specific binding to respiratory complex subunits.
