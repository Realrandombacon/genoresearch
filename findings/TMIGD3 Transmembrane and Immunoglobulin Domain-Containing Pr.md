## Description
TMIGD3 (Transmembrane and Immunoglobulin Domain-Containing Protein 3, UniProt P0DMS9) is a 266 amino acid type I transmembrane protein with minimal characterization (10 papers) that functions as a CD300 family immune receptor suppressing NF-κB signaling and osteosarcoma malignancy through immunoglobulin domain-mediated homophilic or heterophilic interactions. The protein contains a single immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR013783/G3DSA:2.60.40.10/SSF48726 residues 78-181, IPR036179 residues 92-174) forming the characteristic Ig V-set fold typical of immune recognition receptors, and belongs to the CD300 family immune receptors (IPR050671/PTHR11860 residues 60-237) which regulate myeloid cell activation and immune responses.
