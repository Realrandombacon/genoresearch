## Description
SUDS3 (SDS3 Homolog, SIN3A Corepressor Complex Component, UniProt Q9H7L9) is a 328 amino acid nuclear/cytosolic protein functioning as an essential core subunit of the SIN3-histone deacetylase (HDAC) chromatin repressor complex. With only 18 literature references (genuine dark gene status), SUDS3 has emerging roles in transcriptional repression, early lineage specification, and cancer progression. The protein contains a conserved Sds3-like domain (IPR013907, aa 19-327; PF08598, aa 62-185; SM01401, aa 53-222) characteristic of BRMS1 family corepressors. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 78.0) across all 328 residues, indicating stable folded architecture. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally high-confidence partnerships with SIN3-HDAC complex members: RBBP7 (0.999), SAP18 (0.998), HDAC1 (0.997), HDAC2 (0.997), SIN3A (0.996), RBBP4 (0.987), SAP30 (0.986), SIN3B (0.985), ARID4B (0.957), and ING1 (0.656), confirming integration into the canonical SIN3-HDAC chromatin remodeling machinery. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nuclear bodies and cytosol localization, classified as an essential protein with chromatin regulator and repressor molecular functions. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses across 12q regions and splice site mutations (c.213-5del), though specific SUDS3-associated diseases require further characterization. Key literature findings: (1) 2020 study identified lncRNA SNHG22/miR-324-3p/SUDS3 axis promoting triple-negative breast cancer malignant phenotypes (30 citations), (2) 2013 depletion studies revealed essential role in early lineage specification (24 citations), (3) 2008-2009 work showed SUDS3 regulates BRMS1-mediated phenotypes by affecting SIN3-HDAC complex composition, (4) 2023 screen identified SUDS3 as suppressor of break-induced replication in human cells. Functional hypothesis: SUDS3 functions as a structural scaffold within the SIN3-HDAC complex, stabilizing complex assembly and facilitating recruitment of HDAC1/HDAC2 catalytic subunits to chromatin. The Sds3-like domain likely mediates protein-protein interactions with SIN3A/SIN3B scaffolding proteins and RBBP4/RBBP7 chromatin readers. Through HDAC-mediated histone deacetylation (removing acetyl groups from H3K9, H3K14, H4K16), SUDS3-containing complexes establish transcriptionally repressive chromatin states critical for developmental gene silencing, cell fate decisions, and tumor suppression. The BRMS1 family classification suggests potential roles in metastasis suppression, though 2009 data showed overexpression alone doesn't suppress metastasis. Disease mechanism: SUDS3 dysregulation in cancer (TNBC upregulation via lncRNA sponging) may cause aberrant transcriptional repression of tumor suppressor genes or activation of oncogenic programs through altered chromatin states. The 15 ClinVar variants suggest potential developmental disorders, consistent with the essential lineage specification role.
