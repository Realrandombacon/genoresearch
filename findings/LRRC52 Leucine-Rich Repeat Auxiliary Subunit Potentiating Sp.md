## Description
LRRC52 encodes a 313 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein (UniProt: Q8N7C0) at chromosome 1q24.1 that functions as an auxiliary regulatory subunit of voltage- and calcium-activated potassium channels. The domain architecture features an N-terminal LRR domain (IPR000372, residues 25-57) followed by multiple LRR repeats (IPR001611/IPR003591, residues 76-148; PF13855, residues 79-136) forming a curved solenoid structure (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, residues 23-242). The protein belongs to the voltage and calcium-activated potassium channel auxiliary protein family (IPR051432/PTHR46473, residues 14-283), defining its functional role as a channel modulator.\n\nHPA expression profiling reveals testis-specific enrichment (15.6 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced RNA signature and cancer-enhanced expression. The restricted expression pattern indicates LRRC52 functions primarily in male germ cells, consistent with its role in sperm physiology. Subcellular classification as a predicted membrane protein aligns with potassium channel complex localization.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 10 high-confidence partners dominated by potassium channel subunits: KCNU1 (0.914, potassium channel subfamily U member 1, sperm-specific), KCNMB1 (0.858, calcium-activated potassium channel beta subunit 1), KCNMA1 (0.802, calcium-activated potassium channel alpha subunit 1), and KCNMB4 (0.534). This interactome definitively positions LRRC52 within the sperm-specific potassium channel holoenzyme complex. Additional partners include CATSPER1 (0.554, cation channel of sperm) and MGST3 (0.639, membrane glutathione transferase).\n\nClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants spanning 1q23-25, including both deletions (11 variants) and duplications (5 variants). The 1q23-25 region is associated with developmental disorders and male infertility phenotypes. LRRC52 haploinsufficiency may impair sperm potassium channel function, disrupting membrane potential regulation and motility.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: LRRC52 functions as a tissue-specific auxiliary subunit assembling with KCNU1/KCNMA1 pore-forming subunits in sperm flagellar membranes. The LRR solenoid domain mediates protein-protein interactions stabilizing channel complex assembly and modulating voltage/calcium sensitivity. By potentiating potassium currents, LRRC52 regulates sperm membrane hyperpolarization essential for motility activation and acrosome reaction. Loss of LRRC52 disrupts potassium conductance, impairing sperm function and causing male infertility.
