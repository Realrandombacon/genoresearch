## Description
ZDHHC13 (Zinc Finger DHHC-Type Palmitoyltransferase 13) is a 622 aa multi-domain S-acyltransferase representing a partially characterized gene with 47 literature references, functioning as a critical regulator of protein palmitoylation that controls autophagy initiation, neuronal function, and tumor metastasis through substrate modification. Multi-omics data reveals ZDHHC13 as a vertebrate-conserved membrane enzyme with defined DHHC catalytic domain and ankyrin repeat regulatory region yet emerging disease characterization through recent 2024-2025 discoveries in autophagy, Parkinson's disease, and melanoma. UniProt accession Q8IUH4 confirms the 622 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q9CWU2, 622 aa, ~99% identity), orangutan (Q5NVB9, 622 aa), and zebrafish (A0A0R4IQZ2, 645 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the palmitoyltransferase machinery. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the DHHC palmitoyltransferase family (IPR001594, aa 424-557; PF01529, aa 424-557; GO:0016409 palmitoyltransferase activity) containing a canonical DHHC catalytic motif essential for S-acylation, plus ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, aa 56-147, 148-180, 181-279; PF12796; IPR036770, aa 43-280) for protein-protein interactions and substrate recognition. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 87.5) across the full 622 aa length, indicating a well-folded stable protein with structured ankyrin repeats and transmembrane DHHC domain suitable for Golgi membrane integration and substrate binding. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, Golgi apparatus and vesicle localization, and classification as an enzyme/metabolic protein—consistent with a housekeeping palmitoyltransferase. STRING interactions reveal a focused palmitoylation and neuronal network: HTT (0.881, huntingtin, neurodegeneration), PALM (0.748, paralemmin, membrane trafficking), ZDHHC6 (0.699), SYT7 (0.689, synaptotagmin-7, vesicle fusion), ZDHHC8 (0.650), AFG1L (0.648, mitochondrial membrane fusion), ZDHHC1 (0.634), SNAP25 (0.613, synaptic vesicle fusion), ZDHHC11 (0.585), ZDHHC22 (0.467). These interactions confirm ZDHHC13 operates in neuronal function (HTT, SYT7, SNAP25), vesicle trafficking (PALM, SYT7), and palmitoylation networks (ZDHHC family). ClinVar lists 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 11 copy number variations (losses: chr11:17120358-41424289x1, chr11:11p15.1-12 deletion; gains: chr11:230615-26881146x3, chr11:13970757-27565888x3, chr11:11053978-34732891x3, chr11:235934-33826995x3), single nucleotide variants (c.374G>A p.Arg125Gln in ankyrin domain, c.1475-12G>T, c.1475-13G>T), and single allele deletions, implicating ZDHHC13 dosage sensitivity in chromosome 11p deletion/duplication syndromes though specific disease phenotypes are not yet delineated. The 47 papers (including 2024 ULK1 palmitoylation/autophagy study with 34 citations, 2025 melanoma metastasis suppression study, 2025 Parkinson's disease DNA methylation study, 2025 ischemic stroke metabolite regulation study, 2024 ZDHHC13/ZDHHC17 subfamily review with 13 citations) confirm ZDHHC13 as a genuine partially characterized gene—core palmitoyltransferase function defined but disease mechanisms (neurodegeneration, cancer, stroke) actively being elucidated.
