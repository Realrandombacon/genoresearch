# DNAJA4: HSP40 Co-Chaperone with Ciliogenesis and Cancer Metastasis Roles via PSMD2-Mediated Protein Degradation

## Description
DNAJA4 encodes a 397-amino acid DnaJ heat shock protein family member A4 (UniProt Q8WW22), a well-folded HSP40 co-chaperone functioning in protein folding, ciliogenesis, and cancer metastasis suppression. The protein contains canonical HSP40/DnaJ domains: DnaJ domain (IPR001623, positions 5-79; cd06257, positions 7-57; G3DSA:1.10.287.110, positions 3-105) for HSP70 interaction and ATPase stimulation, cysteine-rich zinc finger domain (IPR001305, positions 122-206; cd10719, positions 135-201; G3DSA:2.10.230.10, positions 135-201) for substrate recognition and client protein binding, and C-terminal substrate binding domain (IPR002939, positions 109-330; cd10747, positions 106-332; G3DSA:2.60.260.20, positions 106-134/202-242/249-331) for unfolded protein binding, classified within the chaperone DnaJ family (IPR012724, positions 3-367; PTHR15686, positions 3-367) with HSP40/DnaJ peptide-binding superfamily fold (IPR008971, positions 107-249/251-334; G3DSA:2.60.260.20). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.4, residues 1-397) indicating stable folding consistent with canonical HSP40 architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals DNAJA4 forms part of the HSP70 chaperone network with high-confidence partners: HSPA8 (0.935, heat shock cognate 71 kDa protein), DNAJA1 (0.921, DnaJ homolog subfamily A member 1), DNAJB1 (0.881, DnaJ homolog subfamily B member 1), HSPA2 (0.874, heat shock 70 kDa protein 2), HSPA1B (0.873, heat shock 70 kDa protein 1B), DNAJA2 (0.851, DnaJ homolog subfamily A member 2), HSPA5 (0.674, GRP78/BiP), HSPA6 (0.599, HSP70B'), HSPA1A (0.548, HSP70-1), and HSPA1L (0.533, HSP70-like protein), confirming its role as an HSP70 co-chaperone stimulating ATPase activity and client protein delivery. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with tongue enrichment (135.1 nTPM, highest expression), detected in all tissues, with striking subcellular localization to nuclear membrane, plasma membrane, primary cilium, centriolar satellite, and basal body - ciliary localization consistent with roles in ciliogenesis and ciliary protein trafficking. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (15q24.1-26.3, 15q24.3-26.3, 15q23-26.3) and losses (15q24.1-25.2), plus splice site variants (c.877+2T>C), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 57 publications, DNAJA4 has emerging cancer biology roles: DNAJA4 suppresses epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and metastasis in nasopharyngeal carcinoma via PSMD2-mediated MYH9 degradation (2023, 18 cit), DNAJA4/DNAJB11/DNAJC10 induce cell transformation by inhibiting p53 and oncogene-induced senescence (2024), DNAJA4 promotes Chinese giant salamander iridovirus replication (2022, 4 cit), DNAJA4 methylation essential for human erythropoiesis (2022, 2 cit), high DNAJA4 expression correlates with poor survival in breast cancer (2022). The functional hypothesis: DNAJA4 serves as an HSP40 co-chaperone recruiting HSP70 (HSPA8) to client proteins for folding, degradation, or trafficking; the DnaJ domain stimulates HSP70 ATPase activity while the zinc finger and C-terminal domains recognize specific substrates; ciliary localization (primary cilium, basal body, centriolar satellite) suggests roles in ciliogenesis and ciliary protein quality control, potentially explaining tongue enrichment given ciliated epithelial cells; PSMD2-mediated MYH9 degradation mechanism indicates DNAJA4 delivers MYH9 (myosin heavy chain 9) to the 26S proteasome via PSMD2 interaction, suppressing EMT and metastasis by regulating cytoskeletal dynamics; dual roles in cancer (metastasis suppression in NPC vs. poor prognosis in breast cancer) suggest tissue-specific substrate preferences or context-dependent functions; viral replication promotion suggests DNAJA4 assists viral protein folding or assembly; erythropoiesis methylation indicates epigenetic regulation of DNAJA4 expression during red blood cell differentiation; 15q CNVs may disrupt DNAJA4 dosage, causing protein homeostasis defects and ciliary dysfunction contributing to developmental disorders.
