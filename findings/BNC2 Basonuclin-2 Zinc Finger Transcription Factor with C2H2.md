## Description
BNC2 (Basonuclin 2, Zinc finger protein basonuclin-2, UniProt Q6ZN30) is a 1099 aa nuclear transcription factor belonging to the DISCONNECTED-RELATED family (PTHR15021/IPR040436, positions 110-1097) with multiple C-terminal C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087/PF00096/PF12874/PS00028/PS50157/SM00355/G3DSA:3.30.160.60, positions 416-494/818-889/1025-1092) that functions as a master regulator of melanocyte pigmentation, leptin-responsive hypothalamic neuron activity for appetite control, and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition in cancer.
