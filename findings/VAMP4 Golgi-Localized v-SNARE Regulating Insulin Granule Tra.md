# VAMP4: Golgi-Localized v-SNARE Regulating Insulin Granule Trafficking, Synaptic Vesicle Release Probability, Autophagic Mitochondria Secretion in HNSCC, and Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury via STX6/STX8/VTI1B SNARE Complex Assembly with 28 Unmapped Pathogenic Variants

## Description
VAMP4 (Vesicle-Associated Membrane Protein 4) is a 141-amino acid predicted membrane protein (UniProt O75379) representing a partially characterized dark gene with 144 publications that has established molecular mechanisms in vesicle trafficking but lacks formal Mendelian disease mapping despite validated roles in insulin granule targeting to lysosomes, synaptic vesicle release probability regulation, autophagic mitochondria secretion promoting tumor-associated macrophage polarization in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC), and ischemia-reperfusion injury alleviation via hypoxic adipose stem cell exosomes. Structurally, VAMP4 contains canonical v-SNARE architecture: synaptobrevin-like domain (IPR001388/PF00957/PR00219/PS00417, residues 57-96+113-132/52-132/57-76+77-96+113-132/70-89) forming the core SNARE motif for membrane fusion, v-SNARE coiled-coil homology domain (IPR042855/PS50892/SSF58038, residues 52-132/52-112/48-113) mediating four-helix bundle assembly with target SNAREs, and VAMP4-specific family membership (IPR042887/PTHR46897, residues 1-141) defining functional specificity distinct from other VAMPs. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.2), consistent with a well-folded coiled-coil protein containing stable helical SNARE motif (residues 50-116, cd15869) forming defined interaction surfaces for partner binding, with N-terminal domain potentially involved in regulatory interactions or subcellular targeting. Functionally, VAMP4 localizes exclusively to Golgi apparatus with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (HPA: low tissue specificity, detected in all, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced), classified as a predicted membrane protein functioning in vesicle-mediated transport and Golgi ribbon formation (GO:0016192, GO:0090161)—Golgi localization confirms roles in retrograde trafficking from endosomes to Golgi and lysosome-related organelle biogenesis. STRING interactions reveal extremely high-confidence connections to core SNARE machinery: STX6 (0.999, syntaxin-6—Golgi target SNARE), STX12 (0.999, syntaxin-12—endosomal fusion), VTI1A (0.999, vesicle transport through interaction with t-SNAREs homolog 1A—Qb SNARE), STX16 (0.999, syntaxin-16—Golgi trafficking), VTI1B (0.998, Qb SNARE for lysosomal trafficking), STX5 (0.998, syntaxin-5—ER-Golgi transport), STX10 (0.995, syntaxin-10—endosome-Golgi), STX4 (0.994, syntaxin-4—plasma membrane), SNAP23 (0.993, synaptosomal-associated protein 23—Qc SNARE), and STX3 (0.993, syntaxin-3—apical membrane). This interaction network definitively positions VAMP4 within multiple SNARE complexes regulating Golgi-endosome-lysosome trafficking pathways. Critically, ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including massive copy number gains at 1q21.1-25.3 (chr1:143550435-185515177)x3, 1q21.1-44 (chr1:143932350-249224684)x3, duplications at 1q24.2-25.3 (NC_000001.10:g.(?_171605065)_(173962123_?)dup), and copy number losses at 1q24.2-25.1 (chr1:170777501-173113232)x1, 1q24.2-31.1 (chr1:167994071-187711459)x1, 1q23.3-25.1 (chr1:164571371-175708060)x1, deletions at 1q24.2-25.3/1q24.2-24.3/1q24.2-25.3, yet ZERO diseases are formally associated—a profound gap given the validated roles in diabetes, neurobiology, cancer, and tissue injury. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: VAMP4 in hypoxic adipose stem cell exosomes alleviates ischemia-reperfusion injury by reducing oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction, suggesting therapeutic potential for transplant medicine (2025); VAMP4/STX8 mediate autophagic secretion of mitochondria promoting tumor-associated macrophage (TAM) polarization in HNSCC under hypoxic conditions, revealing mechanism for tumor microenvironment modulation via mitophagy-derived signaling (2026); VAMP4 regulates insulin levels by targeting secretory granules to lysosomes for degradation rather than secretion, assembling SNARE complex with STX7, STX8, and VTI1B on lysosomal membrane to control insulin granule turnover and prevent hyperinsulinemia (23 citations, 2022); control of synaptic vesicle release probability via VAMP4 targeting to endolysosomes acts as molecular rheostat adjusting neurotransmitter release to neuronal activity and degradative status, with VAMP4 diversion from synaptic vesicles to lysosomes reducing release probability (Pr) and preventing excitotoxicity (33 citations, 2021); VAMP2 and VAMP4 control recycling and intracellular sorting of synaptic vesicle proteins, demonstrating functional redundancy and specialization within v-SNARE family (2021). The mechanistic hypothesis: VAMP4 functions as a v-SNARE that forms four-helix trans-SNARE complexes with specific t-SNARE partners (STX6/STX8/VTI1B for lysosomal targeting; STX6/STX12/VTI1A for Golgi-endosome retrograde transport) to drive membrane fusion events. In pancreatic beta cells, VAMP4 directs insulin granules to lysosomes for degradation via STX7-STX8-VTI1B complex, acting as negative regulator of insulin secretion—loss of VAMP4 causes hyperinsulinemia due to failed granule degradation. In neurons, activity-dependent sorting diverts VAMP4 from synaptic vesicle pathway to endolysosomes, reducing available v-SNARE pool and decreasing release probability (Pr) as homeostatic mechanism preventing hyperexcitability. In HNSCC, hypoxia induces VAMP4 expression enabling autophagic mitochondria secretion via STX8-containing SNARE complexes, releasing mitochondrial DNA/metabolites that polarize macrophages toward pro-tumor M2 phenotype. In ischemia-reperfusion, hypoxic adipose stem cells package VAMP4 into exosomes that fuse with injured endothelial/cardiac cells delivering protective cargo or modulating recipient cell trafficking to reduce oxidative stress. The ubiquitous expression and Golgi localization indicate fundamental housekeeping functions in endosome-Golgi-lysosome trafficking with tissue-specific cargo preferences (insulin granules, synaptic vesicles, mitochondria). The absence of formal disease associations despite 28 pathogenic variants suggests VAMP4 dysfunction contributes to 1q21.1 copy number variation syndromes (developmental delay, autism, schizophrenia), maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY) or congenital hyperinsulinism, epilepsy syndromes (altered Pr), HNSCC susceptibility/progression, or congenital disorders of glycosylation/trafficking not yet mapped to Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define VAMP4's complete cargo repertoire via immunoprecipitation of SNARE complexes from different tissues, determine crystal structure of VAMP4 in four-helix bundle with STX8/VTI1B, investigate whether germline VAMP4 mutations cause familial hyperinsulinism or epilepsy, develop VAMP4 inhibitors for cancer therapy (blocking mitophagic secretion and TAM polarization), explore VAMP4-enhanced exosomes for ischemia-reperfusion treatment, and assess VAMP4 modulation for treating neurodegenerative diseases with synaptic dysfunction.
