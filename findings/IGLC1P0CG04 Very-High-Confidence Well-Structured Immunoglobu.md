## Description
IGLC1 (Immunoglobulin lambda constant 1, also known as IGLC) is a 106 amino acid secreted antibody constant domain (UniProt P0CG04) with very-high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 96.0), characteristic of immunoglobulin C1-set domains that function through beta-sandwich folds to form the constant region of lambda light chains in antibody molecules. The protein belongs to the immunoglobulin C1-set family (IPR003597, residues 9-97; PF07654 Immunoglobulin C1-set domain, residues 9-93; SM00407 Immunoglobulin C-Type), immunoglobulin-like domain family (IPR007110, residues 7-101; PS50835 Ig-like domain profile, residues 7-101), immunoglobulin-like fold superfamily (IPR013783, residues 2-106; IPR036179 Immunoglobulin-like domain superfamily, residues 3-100; G3DSA:2.60.40.10 Immunoglobulins, residues 2-106), MHC/immunoglobulin superfamily (IPR050160, residues 4-102; PTHR19944, residues 4-102), and immunoglobulin light chain constant domain family (cd07699, residues 7-99). IGLC1 also contains immunoglobulin/MHC conserved sites (IPR003006, residues 85-91; PS00290, residues 85-91). IGLC1 is located on chromosome 22q11.22 within the immunoglobulin lambda locus and is predicted to be secreted as part of antibody molecules. The very-high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 96.0) indicates a well-folded immunoglobulin beta-sandwich with stable disulfide-bonded architecture for antigen binding and effector function.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis returned no protein partners for IGLC1, which is expected for immunoglobulin constant regions that function primarily through non-covalent association with variable domains and heavy chains rather than stable protein complexes detectable by STRING. IGLC1 functions by forming disulfide bonds with immunoglobulin lambda variable regions (IGLV) and non-covalently associating with immunoglobulin heavy chains to form complete antibody molecules (IgG, IgM, IgA, IgE, IgD isotypes).\n\nHPA expression reveals IGLC1 has tissue-enhanced expression with extremely high levels in lymphoid tissue (5228.6 nTPM), intestine (3069.3 nTPM), and stomach (2196.9 nTPM), indicating specialized functions in humoral immunity and mucosal immune responses. This restricted expression pattern is consistent with IGLC1's role in antibody production by B cells and plasma cells. RNA tissue distribution is detected in many tissues, reflecting B cell presence across lymphoid organs. Low cancer specificity indicates IGLC1 is not dysregulated in malignancies. Group enriched blood cell specificity indicates expression in B cell lineages. The protein is classified as an immunoglobulin gene, predicted intracellular protein, and predicted secreted protein with antigen binding molecular function (GO:0003823), localized to blood microparticles and extracellular exosomes for immune surveillance.\n\nClinVar documents 87 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including extensive copy number gains (22q11.21-11.23 amplifications 21.8-24.6 Mb x3, 21.9-23.7 Mb x3, 21.8-23.7 Mb x3, 22.9-25.0 Mb x3, 22.9-25.0 Mb x3) and copy number losses (22q11.22-11.23 deletions 23.0-23.7 Mb, 21.9-24.1 Mb, 22.9-24.2 Mb, 22.9-25.0 Mb). The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity with both haploinsufficiency and overexpression effects. While no specific Mendelian disease is annotated, 22q11 CNVs are associated with DiGeorge syndrome, velocardiofacial syndrome, immunodeficiency, and developmental abnormalities. Given IGLC1's role in antibody production, loss-of-function may cause impaired humoral immunity, reduced antibody diversity, or increased susceptibility to infections.\n\nConservation is evident across mammals and vertebrates: human (P0CG04, 106 aa), Mus musculus (A0A0G2JE99, 106 aa), Macaca mulatta (A0AA48P905, 105 aa), Atractosteus spatula (A0A8J7TBX4, 103 aa), indicating the immunoglobulin C1-set domain architecture is highly conserved across vertebrates with nearly identical protein length, suggesting strong purifying selection on antibody structure and function.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: IGLC1 functions as the constant domain of immunoglobulin lambda light chains that heterodimerizes with lambda variable domains (IGLV) through disulfide bonds and non-covalently associates with immunoglobulin heavy chains to form complete antibody molecules. The very-high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 96.0) indicates a well-folded immunoglobulin beta-sandwich with conserved disulfide bonds for structural stability and antigen binding site formation. Through association with IGLV variable domains, IGLC1 contributes to antigen binding specificity and antibody effector functions. Lymphoid tissue enrichment (5228.6 nTPM) indicates IGLC1 is produced by B cells and plasma cells for antibody secretion. High expression in intestine and stomach suggests roles in mucosal immunity through IgA production. 22q11 CNVs cause IGLC1 dosage alterations, potentially disrupting antibody repertoire diversity and contributing to immunodeficiency phenotypes in DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome. IGLC1 represents a well-characterized immunoglobulin constant region gene essential for humoral immunity and may serve as a biomarker for B cell function and immunodeficiency disorders through serum immunoglobulin measurement.
