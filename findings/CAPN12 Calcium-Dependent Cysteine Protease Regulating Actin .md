## Description

AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 86.0), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme capable of precise substrate recognition. HPA expression shows group enrichment in liver (25.9 nTPM) and gallbladder (21.3 nTPM) with cancer enhancement, consistent with roles in hepatocyte metabolism and tumor growth. ClinVar reports 6 pathogenic variants, predominantly large 19q copy number gains (x3), suggesting dosage sensitivity; specific ACTN4-linked variants may disrupt cytoskeletal anchoring. Functional hypothesis: CAPN12 functions through: (1) Cytoskeletal Remodeling - cleaving actin filaments or crosslinking proteins (via ACTN4) to facilitate cell migration and invasion in cancer; (2) Metabolic Regulation - degrading metabolic enzymes or signaling proteins to alter glycolytic flux in liver and tumor cells; (3) Focal Adhesion Signaling - interacting with ACTN4 to transmit mechanical signals and regulate adhesion turnover; (4) Dosage Sensitivity - 19q amplification driving oncogenesis by enhancing migratory capacity. Disease implications include Glioblastoma metastasis, liver cancer progression, and metabolic disorders. Despite clinical variant burden, the precise substrate repertoire for vimentin polymerization and the full extent of its metabolic targets remain partially unexplored.
