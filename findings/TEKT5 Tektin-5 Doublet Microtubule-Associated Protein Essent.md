## Description
TEKT5 (Tektin-5, UniProt Q96M29) is a 485 amino acid intracellular protein with partial characterization (14 papers) that functions as a structural component of doublet microtubules in sperm flagella and cilia, forming part of the tektin filament system that stabilizes axonemal microtubules and regulates flagellar motility essential for sperm function and male fertility.
