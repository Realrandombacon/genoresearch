The extended coiled-coil domain (residues 26-1311) enables multivalent binding, creating a meshwork that concentrates γ-tubulin ring complex components for efficient microtubule nucleation during mitosis. Testis enrichment reflects high centrosomal demand in spermatogenesis. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt scaffold stoichiometry, causing centrosome amplification or fragmentation—phenotypes linked to ciliopathies and microcephaly.
