# Cyclophilin-like protein with potential role in protein folding and genomic stability

## Description
PPIAL4A is a cyclophilin-like protein containing a peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase domain. Its interactions with RanBP1 and RGPD family members suggest a role in protein transport or stability. The presence of 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, mostly copy number gains/losses, implies that PPIAL4A may be involved in genomic stability. Further research is needed to elucidate its precise function.
