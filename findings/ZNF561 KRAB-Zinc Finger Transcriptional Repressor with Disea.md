## Description
ZNF561 encodes a 486 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q8N587) localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 40-80) that recruits transcriptional corepressors, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 197-219, 281-303, and 309-331 that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding. STRING interaction analysis reveals high-confidence partnership with ZNF562 (score 0.795), suggesting heterodimerization or co-regulation of target genes, and medium-confidence interactions with TRIM28 (0.563), the canonical KRAB domain corepressor that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase for H3K9me3 silencing. Additional partners include SETD4 (0.488), ZNF616 (0.459), TEX19 (0.420), and MTHFSD (0.401), placing ZNF561 within a chromatin remodeling network. HPA data shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues and cell types enhanced pattern, consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation. ClinVar documents 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning 19p13.2-13.3, implicating ZNF561 dosage sensitivity in disease etiology. The KRAB-ZNF architecture suggests ZNF561 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that silences target genes through TRIM28-mediated heterochromatin formation, potentially regulating developmental or cell cycle genes. Copy number gains may cause dominant-negative effects or aberrant repression of tumor suppressor loci.
