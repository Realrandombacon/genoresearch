## Description
TRIM55 (Q9BYV6, 548 aa), also known as MuRF-2 (Muscle-specific RING Finger protein 2) or RNF29, is a tripartite motif (TRIM) family E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase localized to cytosol and Golgi apparatus. InterPro identifies canonical TRIM architecture: N-terminal RING-type zinc finger (IPR001841, IPR027370, cd16760, G3DSA:3.30.40.10, aa 26-82) for E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme binding and catalytic activity, B-box type 2 zinc finger (IPR000315, cd19832, G3DSA:3.30.160.60, aa 119-161) for protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal COS domain (IPR017903, aa 269-327) unique to MuRF subfamily for substrate recognition. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 67.4, residues 1-548) consistent with folded RING/B-box domains connected by disordered linkers to COS domain - typical of TRIM family proteins. HPA shows muscle-enriched expression: heart muscle (57.8 nTPM), liver (35.5 nTPM), tongue (31.4 nTPM), skeletal muscle (27.7 nTPM); classified as muscle protein and transferase. STRING reveals 10 interactions forming sarcomere assembly and ubiquitination network: TTN/titin (0.960, ultra-high), SQSTM1/p62 (0.792), NBR1 (0.750), UBE2D3 (0.654, E2 conjugating enzyme), TRIM63/MuRF-1 (0.638), USP39 (0.581), USP13 (0.580, deubiquitinases), BBOX1 (0.517), TCAP/telethonin (0.419), and TRIM23 (0.418). ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses spanning 8q regions, though no specific monogenic diseases are yet assigned. Functional hypothesis: TRIM55/MuRF-2 functions as a sarcomere assembly factor and E3 ubiquitin ligase that transiently associates with titin, myosin, and microtubules during myofibrillogenesis. The RING domain recruits E2~Ub conjugates (UBE2D3 interaction, 0.654) to catalyze K48-linked polyubiquitination of sarcomeric substrates, marking them for proteasomal degradation or K63-linked chains for signaling. Ultra-high TTN interaction (0.960) indicates direct titin binding via COS domain, regulating titin turnover and A-band assembly. SQSTM1/p62 (0.792) and NBR1 (0.750) interactions link to autophagic clearance of ubiquitinated sarcomeric proteins during muscle remodeling. TRIM63/MuRF-1 interaction (0.638) suggests functional redundancy or heterodimerization in muscle atrophy pathways. TCAP/telethonin interaction (0.419) connects to Z-disc assembly regulation. Heart muscle enrichment (57.8 nTPM) aligns with high sarcomere turnover demand. Pathogenic CNVs may disrupt sarcomere integrity causing cardiomyopathy or skeletal myopathy. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 545 aa, rat 545 aa) confirms essential muscle function. NOTE: This gene is well-characterized as MuRF-2 with established role in muscle protein ubiquitination.
