## Description
DDX56 (DEAD-Box Helicase 56, Q9NY93) encodes a 547 aa ATP-dependent RNA helicase functioning as a critical ribosome biogenesis factor and oncogenic driver in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and lung squamous cell carcinoma. Structural architecture reveals canonical DEAD-box helicase organization with N-terminal Q motif (IPR014014, residues 7-35) for ATP recognition, central DEAD-box helicase domain (IPR011545/PF00270/cd17961, residues 32-206/32-206/14-220) containing Walker A/B motifs for ATP hydrolysis and RNA unwinding, C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650/PF00271/cd18787, residues 230-424/244-380/231-389) for RNA substrate binding, and P-loop NTPase fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/IPR027417, residues 2-221/227-423). AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 79.8) reveals confident folded structure characteristic of SF2 helicase superfamily. STRING interactions confirm ribosome biogenesis network with DHX36 (0.996, DEAH-box helicase 36), DDX1 (0.990, DEAD-box helicase 1), RPF2 (0.987, ribosome production factor 2), GTPBP4 (0.978, GTP binding protein 4), WDR74 (0.975, WD repeat protein 74), EBNA1BP2 (0.971, Epstein-Barr virus nuclear antigen 1 binding protein 2), NOC2L (0.971, nucleolar complex protein 2 homolog), PES1 (0.970, pescadillo homolog 1), WDR46 (0.968), and CEBPZ (0.502, CCAAT/enhancer binding protein zeta). HPA confirms nucleolar and mitotic chromosome localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, classified as essential enzyme with helicase/RNA-binding/hydrolase activity involved in rRNA processing and ribosome biogenesis. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic variants including 7p14.3-12.3 CNVs and splice site mutations (c.890+287T>G, c.1220-274A>C, c.1384-5G>A). Literature (40 papers) demonstrates oncogenic roles: promotes EMT and cancer stemness via MELK-FOXM1 axis in HCC (12x citations, 2024), transcriptionally activates MIST1 to facilitate HCC tumorigenesis through PTEN-AKT signaling (24x citations, 2022), modulates post-transcriptional Wnt signaling through miRNAs associated with early recurrence in squamous cell lung carcinoma (25x citations, 2021), and machine learning identification as crucial oncogenic RBP (1x citation, 2025). Mechanistic hypothesis: DDX56 localizes to nucleoli where it unwinds rRNA secondary structures during pre-rRNA processing and ribosome assembly; in cancer, DDX56 is co-opted to regulate mRNA stability/translation of oncogenic transcripts (MELK, FOXM1, MIST1) via helicase-dependent RNA remodeling; PTEN-AKT pathway activation results from DDX56-mediated suppression of PTEN expression or enhancement of AKT phosphorylation; Wnt signaling modulation occurs through DDX56 processing of Wnt pathway component mRNAs or miRNAs targeting Wnt regulators.
