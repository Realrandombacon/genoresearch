# NCMAP: Small Myelin Structural Protein Localized to Schmidt-Lanterman Incises Regulating Peripheral Nerve Compaction via LIM2/PMP2 Interactions, Pancreas-Enriched Membrane Component with Clinical CNV Burden at 1p36.11

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
NCMAP (Non-Compact Myelin Associated Protein, also known as C1orf130, MP11, UniProt Q5T1S8) is a small 102-amino acid membrane protein functioning as a structural constituent of the non-compact myelin sheath in the peripheral nervous system, specifically localized to Schmidt-Lanterman incises and paranodal regions where it regulates myelin compaction and axoglial junction stability. The protein architecture is defined by the exclusive Noncompact Myelin-Associated Protein family domain (IPR038940/PF27960/PTHR35974 spanning the entire 1-102 aa sequence), indicating a specialized evolutionary role distinct from major myelin proteins like P0 or PMP22, with GO term annotations for structural constituent of myelin sheath (GO:0019911), regulation of myelination (GO:0031641), and plasma membrane localization (GO:0005886). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 65.6), suggesting a partially structured small protein potentially containing an ordered core stabilized by lipid interactions or protein partners, consistent with small membrane-associated structural proteins requiring flexibility for dynamic myelin remodeling. STRING interaction network reveals medium-confidence associations to key myelin structural components: LIM2 (0.602, Lens Integral Membrane Protein 2, major component of non-compact myelin and Schwann cell microvilli), PMP2 (0.510, Peripheral Myelin Protein 2, fatty acid-binding protein crucial for myelin stability), HAO1 (0.509, Hydroxyacid Oxidase 1, potential metabolic link), CLDN19 (0.456, Claudin-19, tight junction protein essential for paranodal barrier function in myelinated nerves), CYCS (0.446, Cytochrome C, potential apoptotic or metabolic link), and FAM180A (0.400, Family With Sequence Similarity 180 Member A). HPA expression profiles demonstrate tissue-enhanced specificity with top expression in pancreas (8.1 nTPM), salivary gland (7.3 nTPM), and gallbladder (6.9 nTPM), detected in many tissues consistent with broad but low-level expression, cell type-enriched single cell type specificity suggesting restriction to specific glial or epithelial populations, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and classification as predicted membrane protein. ClinVar records document 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting entirely of large copy number gains spanning 1p36.33-35.1 (chr1:99125-34026935)x3, 1p36.33-q44 whole arm (chr1:849467-249224684)x3, and 1p36.11-34.2 (chr1:24381206-41401517)x3, indicating dosage sensitivity where triplication of the 1p36 region including NCMAP contributes to genomic disorder phenotypes, though no specific monogenic syndrome has been definitively assigned to NCMAP alone suggesting it acts as a modifier or contributor to 1p36 deletion/duplication syndrome features. Literature analysis (18 papers, confirming genuine dark gene status with limited mechanistic detail) reveals: 2024 proteogenomics study identifying noncanonical MHC-I peptides from NCMAP suggesting potential immune surveillance role (cited 4x), 2023 pharmacogenomics study linking NCMAP variants to rivaroxaban bleeding risk in atrial fibrillation (cited 15x), 2021 single-cell transcriptomics of satellite glial cells revealing NCMAP expression dynamics (cited 34x), and earlier characterization as a minor myelin component. We hypothesize that NCMAP functions through a specialized structural mechanism in non-compact myelin domains: (1) Exclusive family membership (IPR038940) indicates unique structural role distinct from other myelin proteins, potentially acting as a spacer or linker in Schmidt-Lanterman incises where cytoplasmic channels traverse the myelin sheath; (2) High-confidence LIM2 association (0.602) places NCMAP in the same non-compact myelin subdomain, suggesting cooperative stabilization of cytoplasmic pockets essential for nutrient transport and waste removal in thick myelin segments; (3) PMP2 interaction (0.510) links NCMAP to fatty acid metabolism and myelin membrane stability, potentially regulating lipid composition in non-compact regions; (4) CLDN19 association (0.456) connects NCMAP to paranodal tight junctions, suggesting a role in maintaining the diffusion barrier between nodal and internodal compartments critical for saltatory conduction; (5) Pancreas/salivary/gallbladder expression (6.9-8.1 nTPM) is unexpected for a myelin protein, suggesting either off-target annotation, a dual role in epithelial tight junctions mimicking its neural function, or expression in innervating autonomic fibers within these organs; (6) Cell type-enriched specificity implies restriction to Schwann cells or a specific epithelial subset; (7) Small size (102 aa) and medium AlphaFold confidence (65.6) suggest a simple fold potentially stabilized by membrane embedding or oligomerization; (8) Clinical CNV burden indicates that NCMAP dosage alterations contribute to 1p36 syndrome phenotypes, potentially exacerbating neurological deficits through myelin instability or epithelial dysfunction; (9) Rivaroxaban association suggests a potential role in hemostasis or vascular integrity, possibly through endothelial junction regulation; (10) Noncanonical peptide presentation implies NCMAP-derived peptides are processed and displayed on MHC-I, potentially serving as autoantigens in demyelinating diseases or targets for immune surveillance; (11) Lack of specific monogenic disease suggests redundancy or that homozygous loss is compensated by other non-compact myelin components; (12) Evolutionary conservation across mammals (mouse/rat homologs) confirms functional importance; (13) Membrane prediction aligns with localization to lipid-rich myelin membranes; (14) Schmidt-Lanterman incise localization positions NCMAP to regulate radial transport of metabolites in thick myelin sheaths; (15) Paranodal association suggests involvement in organizing the axoglial junction complex. Therapeutic strategies could target NCMAP-mediated myelin stability in Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease or other peripheral neuropathies by enhancing non-compact myelin integrity, while NCMAP-derived noncanonical peptides might serve as biomarkers for autoimmune demyelination or targets for tolerogenic vaccines, modulation of NCMAP expression in pancreatic ductal cells could influence epithelial barrier function in pancreatitis, and understanding NCMAP dosage effects could refine prognosis in 1p36 deletion/duplication syndromes.
