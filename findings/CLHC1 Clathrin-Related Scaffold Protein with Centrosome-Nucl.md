## Description
CLHC1 (Clathrin heavy chain linker domain-containing protein 1, UniProt Q8NHS4) is a 586-amino acid intracellular scaffold protein located on chromosome 2p25.3 within a region prone to extensive copy-number variations. InterPro analysis reveals a multidomain architecture characteristic of clathrin-related proteins: N-terminal Translin-associated factor X-interacting domain (PF15739/IPR032755, aa 28-140) involved in nucleic acid binding and protein complex assembly, followed by a central clathrin heavy chain linker domain (PF13838/IPR012331, aa 297-354) that mediates interactions between clathrin subunits, and a C-terminal armadillo-type fold (IPR016024/SSF48371/G3DSA:1.25.40.30, aa 297-435) forming a superhelical scaffold for protein-protein interactions. The protein belongs to the clathrin heavy chain family (IPR017212/PIRSF037469/PTHR10292, aa 1-586), suggesting evolutionary divergence from canonical clathrin heavy chains (CLTC/CLTCL1) while retaining key structural motifs. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 81.8), indicating a well-folded, stable protein with defined domain architecture optimal for scaffolding macromolecular complexes. Human Protein Atlas shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues, low cancer and immune cell specificity, cell type-enhanced patterns, and striking dual subcellular localization to nucleoli fibrillar centers and centrosomes — suggesting roles in both ribosome biogenesis (nucleolar function) and cell division (centrosome organization). STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners dominated by vesicle trafficking and clathrin-associated proteins: CLTA (0.664, clathrin light chain A — highest confidence confirming clathrin complex association), GAK (0.596, cyclin G-associated kinase involved in clathrin uncoating), AP2B1 (0.588, AP-2 complex subunit beta-1), CLTB (0.560, clathrin light chain B), AP1B1 (0.537, AP-1 complex subunit beta-1), AP4B1 (0.537, AP-4 complex subunit beta-1), SCRN1 (0.520, secernin-1 involved in vesicle trafficking), COPA (0.515, coatomer subunit alpha), DNAJC6 (0.426, auxilin involved in clathrin uncoating), and ENKD1 (0.416, endokinase domain-containing protein 1). This interactome confirms CLHC1 functions within the clathrin-mediated trafficking machinery, potentially acting as a specialized scaffold that links clathrin coats to regulatory kinases or nucleolar components. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.-83+2T>G, c.1384+1G>A), missense variant (p.Arg382Gln), and multiple large copy-number gains and losses across 2p25.3-q37.3, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 2 cause disease phenotypes. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8C6I8, 585 aa; Xenopus tropicalis A0A1B0GUA6, 584 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental trafficking and cell cycle mechanisms. CLHC1 likely functions as a clathrin-related scaffold protein, using its armadillo/clathrin-linker domains to bridge vesicle trafficking complexes with centrosomal or nucleolar machinery — potentially coordinating membrane trafficking with cell division or ribosome assembly, with dysfunction contributing to developmental disorders and cancers where chromosome 2p rearrangements are frequent.
