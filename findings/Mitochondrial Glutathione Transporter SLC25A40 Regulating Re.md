## Description
SLC25A40 encodes a 338 aa mitochondrial inner membrane glutathione transporter (UniProt Q8TBP6), functioning as a critical importer of reduced glutathione (GSH) into the mitochondrial matrix for redox homeostasis and iron-sulfur cluster stability. InterPro analysis confirms canonical mitochondrial carrier architecture with three homologous domains (IPR002067, IPR018108, IPR023395, PF00153; aa 11-334), characteristic triple-repeat transmembrane structure (aa 13-134, 139-227, 233-331), and Mtm1-like family membership (IPR045315), consistent with metabolite translocation mechanisms across mitochondrial membranes. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 78.4), indicating a stable, well-folded carrier with canonical 6-transmembrane helix topology per repeat. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and cell type-enhanced detection, consistent with its fundamental role in cellular redox balance. STRING interactions reveal connections to metabolic and translational machinery (RUNDC3B 0.549, TMEM243 0.513, LARS1 0.475, WDR44 0.469, RRBP1 0.466, TXNL1 0.465, SLC6A6 0.450, CROT 0.447), suggesting coordination between mitochondrial GSH availability and protein synthesis/metabolism. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (copy number losses at 7q21.11-21.3, copy number gains, inversions, TMEM106B-BRAF fusion deletions), indicating disease causality though specific phenotypes remain undefined. Recent literature (29 papers) demonstrates SLC25A40 regulates macrophage cytokine production via mitochondrial GSH-dependent iron-sulfur cluster stability (2025), promotes NSCLC growth by enhancing NADPH-mediated lipid synthesis and suppressing ROS accumulation-induced ferroptosis (2025), and facilitates anticancer drug resistance in leukemia K562 cells (2023, 5 citations). As the primary mitochondrial GSH importer, SLC25A40 supplies substrate for mitochondrial antioxidant defense, preventing lipid peroxidation and maintaining electron transport chain function. GSH deficiency impairs iron-sulfur cluster assembly, destabilizing ETC complexes and triggering metabolic crisis. The ferroptosis resistance mechanism links SLC25A40 to cancer metabolism—enhanced mitochondrial GSH consumes lipid peroxides, reducing cell death substrates. The 12 ClinVar variants indicate strong disease causality, with emerging roles in immune regulation and cancer progression. SLC25A40 represents a critical redox node connecting mitochondrial energetics, ferroptosis regulation, and immune metabolism.
