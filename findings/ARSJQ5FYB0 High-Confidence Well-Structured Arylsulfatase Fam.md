## Description
ARSJ (Arylsulfatase family member J, also known as ASJ) is a 599 amino acid lysosomal/secreted hydrolase (UniProt Q5FYB0) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 86.7), characteristic of sulfatase family enzymes that function through N-terminal sulfatase domains to hydrolyze sulfate esters from sulfated steroids, carbohydrates, proteoglycans, and glycolipids. The protein belongs to the arylsulfatase B family (IPR047115, residues 33-554; GO:0008484 sulfuric ester hydrolase activity), contains sulfatase N-terminal domains (IPR000917, residues 76-388; PF00884 Sulfatase, residues 76-388), alkaline-phosphatase-like core domain superfamily (IPR017850, residues 74-554; SSF53649 Alkaline phosphatase-like, residues 74-554; G3DSA:3.40.720.10 Alkaline Phosphatase subunit A, residues 76-415), sulfatase conserved sites (IPR024607, residues 120-132 and 168-178; PS00523 Sulfatases signature 1, residues 120-132; PS00149 Sulfatases signature 2, residues 168-178), and N-acetylgalactosamine 4-sulfatase domains (cd16029, residues 76-509; G3DSA:3.30.1120.10, residues 416-526).
