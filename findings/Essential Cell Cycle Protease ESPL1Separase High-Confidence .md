## Description
ESPL1 (UniProt Q14674), also known as ESP1 or SEPA, encodes a massive 2120 aa cysteine endopeptidase functioning as the essential protease separase that triggers anaphase onset by cleaving cohesin complex subunits. InterPro analysis reveals conserved peptidase C50/separase architecture: full-length Peptidase C50 separase family membership (IPR005314/PTHR12792, residues 3-2119) defining cysteine-type endopeptidase activity (GO:0004197) for proteolysis (GO:0006508) in nucleus (GO:0005634); SEPARIN core domain (IPR030397/PS51700, residues 1945-2040) containing catalytic cysteine residue for substrate cleavage; and Separin protease domain (PF03568, residues 1722-2069) forming the active site cleft. The perfect PTTG1/PTTG2/RAD21 scores confirm separase-securin-cohesin axis as fundamental anaphase trigger mechanism. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting of copy number losses (12q13.13x1, 12p13.33-q24.33 deletions) and gains (12p13.33-q24.33x3, 12q13.13x3) plus missense variant (c.2809A>G p.Ile937Val) though no single-gene disorder name is assigned; large CNVs likely cause multi-gene syndromes but ESPL1 haploinsufficiency may contribute to chromosomal instability phenotypes. Functional hypothesis: ESPL1 operates through integrated cell cycle control mechanisms: (1) Catalytic mechanism - Cysteine peptidase C50 domain cleaves RAD21/SCC1 at two specific sites (Arg-Gly bonds) opening cohesin ring and releasing sister chromatid cohesion; (2) Securin inhibition - PTTG1/PTTG2 bind separase catalytic domain preventing premature activity until APC/C-mediated securin ubiquitination and degradation at metaphase-anaphase transition; (3) Phosphorylation regulation - CDK1-cyclin B and PLK1 phosphorylate multiple separase residues modulating activity, localization, and securin binding affinity; (4) Spindle checkpoint integration - BUB1/BUB1B ensure separase activation only after proper kinetochore-microtubule attachment; (5) Meiotic function - REC8 cleavage enables homologous chromosome separation in meiosis I; (6) Disease mechanism - Haploinsufficiency causes chromosomal instability, aneuploidy, and potential developmental defects; overexpression may cause premature chromatid separation and genomic instability contributing to cancer; 12q13.13 location places it in region associated with various syndromes; (7) Conservation - Mouse Espl1 (P60330, 2118 aa) indicates essential conserved function from yeast (Esp1p) to mammals. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: complete substrate repertoire beyond cohesin subunits, structural basis for securin inhibition and release mechanism, precise regulation by multiple phosphorylation sites, contribution to specific 12q13 CNV syndrome phenotypes, potential non-canonical substrates in DNA repair or transcription, therapeutic potential of separase inhibitors in cancer (premature anaphase induction), cell-type specific regulation in stem cells vs differentiated cells, crosstalk with DNA damage response pathways, role in aging through chromosomal instability, and involvement in neurodevelopmental disorders (high esophageal/lymphoid expression suggests tissue-specific requirements).
