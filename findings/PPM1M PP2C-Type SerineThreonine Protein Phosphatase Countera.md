## Description
PPM1M (Protein Phosphatase Mg2+/Mn2+ Dependent 1M, also known as PP2C-eta, PP2CE, UniProt Q96MI6) is a 459-amino acid manganese-dependent serine/threonine protein phosphatase functioning as a critical negative regulator of LRRK2 kinase signaling through preferential dephosphorylation of phosphoRab12 GTPases, thereby counteracting Parkinson's disease-associated LRRK2 hyperactivation, while also stabilizing EP300 protein levels to suppress epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) in renal proximal tubular epithelial cells and prevent renal fibrosis progression.
