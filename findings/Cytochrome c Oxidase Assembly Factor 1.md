# Cytochrome c Oxidase Assembly Factor 1

## Description
COA1 is a cytochrome c oxidase assembly factor 1, likely involved in the assembly of cytochrome c oxidase in mitochondria. The protein contains InterPro domains IPR014807 and PF08695, and interacts with COX14, NDUFAF5, and TMEM186. COA1 is expressed in all tissues with low tissue specificity and is localized to mitochondria. Pathogenic variants in COA1 have been associated with disease.
