## Description
FAM98A encodes a 518 aa nuclear/cytoplasmic protein (UniProt Q8NCA5) that functions as a key regulator of protein arginine methylation and tRNA splicing. The protein contains a conserved FAM98 domain (IPR018797, residues 3-403) and DUF2465/PF10239 domain (residues 29-327), characteristic of the FAM98 family. FAM98A positively stimulates PRMT1-induced protein arginine methylation, linking it to epitranscriptomic regulation. STRING interactions reveal strong associations with RNA metabolism proteins: DDX1 (0.994), RTRAF (0.985), ATXN2 (0.880), G3BP1 (0.851), and FAM98B (0.687), placing FAM98A within the tRNA-splicing ligase complex. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low specificity) with vesicular subcellular localization and cell type-enhanced expression patterns. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses on chromosome 2p, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The mouse ortholog (Fam98a, 515 aa) shows strong conservation.
