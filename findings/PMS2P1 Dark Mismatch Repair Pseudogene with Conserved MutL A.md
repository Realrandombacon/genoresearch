## Description
PMS2P1 (PMS1 Homolog 2, Mismatch Repair System Component Pseudogene 1, Gene ID: 5379, aliases PMS2L1, PMS2L13, PMS2L6, PMS2L7, PMS2L8, PMS3, PMS8, PMSR1, PMSR2) is a dark gene with only 4 publications on Semantic Scholar, representing an understudied DNA mismatch repair-related protein located at chromosome 7q22.1. Despite its pseudogene annotation, PMS2P1 encodes a 440 amino acid putative postmeiotic segregation increased 2-like protein (UniProt A4D2B8) with predicted involvement in mismatch repair. A 2023 study specifically reported a pathogenic variant reclassified to the PMS2P1 pseudogene in a patient with suspected hereditary cancer, establishing potential clinical relevance in cancer genetics. The canonical PMS2 protein forms the MutLalpha heterodimer with MLH1, essential for recognizing and repairing base-base mismatches and insertion/deletion loops during DNA replication. Defects in PMS2 cause Lynch syndrome (hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer). The dark literature status (4 papers), medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 51.9), conserved MutL ATPase domain architecture, and hereditary cancer case report make PMS2P1 a compelling candidate for functional characterization as a novel mismatch repair modulator with potential clinical significance in cancer genetics.
