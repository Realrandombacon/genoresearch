## Description
CPNE6 (UniProt: O95741, aliases Copine-6) encodes a 557 amino acid calcium-dependent phospholipid-binding protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with 12 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 14q11.2, CPNE6 is a member of the copine family characterized by calcium-dependent membrane binding through C2 domains. InterPro domain analysis reveals canonical copine architecture: two tandem C2 domains (IPR000008/PF00168, first C2 domain residues 2-127/26-124, second C2 domain residues 134-263/159-255, GO:0005544 calcium-dependent phospholipid binding), von Willebrand factor type A domain (IPR002035, residues 304-526), Copine C-terminal domain (IPR010734/PF07002, residues 272-541/325-541), and Copine family signature (IPR045052, residues 19-544). The C2 domains form beta-sandwich structures that bind calcium ions and phospholipids for membrane targeting, while the vWA domain mediates protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 86.4), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined tertiary structure suitable for calcium-regulated membrane binding and complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners spanning neuronal and glial biology: AK5 (adenylate kinase 5, score 0.703 - highest confidence, nucleotide metabolism enzyme), HAPLN2 (hyaluronan and proteoglycan link protein 2, 0.578 - extracellular matrix), OS9 (ER lectin OS-9, 0.542 - ER quality control), PHYHIP (phytanoyl-CoA hydroxylase-interacting protein-like, 0.521), ANKS1B (ankyrin repeat and SAM domain-containing protein 1B, 0.515 - synaptic scaffolding), CPNE4 (copine-4, 0.507 - paralog), HPCA (hippocalcin, 0.506 - neuronal calcium sensor), NRXN3 (neurexin-3, 0.503 - synaptic adhesion molecule), DLGAP1 (DLG-associated protein 1, 0.487 - postsynaptic density scaffold), and GFAP (glial fibrillary acidic protein, 0.423 - astrocyte intermediate filament). This interaction network places CPNE6 within synaptic biology alongside calcium sensors (HPCA), synaptic adhesion molecules (NRXN3), postsynaptic scaffolds (DLGAP1, ANKS1B), and glial markers (GFAP). Human Protein Atlas shows brain-enriched expression (45.5 nTPM - highest), retina expression (22.1 nTPM), seminal vesicle expression (21.4 nTPM), group-enriched RNA tissue specificity, cancer-enhanced expression pattern, no immune cell detection, and subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus and vesicles - consistent with membrane trafficking function. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number losses (deletions) and gains (duplications) spanning 14q11.2-12 to 14q11.2-32.33, though no specific disease names are assigned - variants include large deletions removing the entire CPNE6 locus and duplications causing gene dosage effects. Literature reveals key functional roles: Zhang et al. (2016, cited 72x) demonstrate CPNE6 regulates hippocampal spine structural plasticity and learning and memory through calcium-dependent membrane binding; increased CPNE6 expression observed in refractory epilepsy patients and rat models (2016, cited 18x); CPNE6 detected in human ciliary body proteome (2013, cited 45x); recent 2026 study identifies CPNE6 as prognostic biomarker in prostate cancer. Based on high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 86.4), dual C2 domain architecture, brain-enriched expression (45.5 nTPM), Golgi/vesicle localization, HPCA/NRXN3/DLGAP1 interaction network, and spine plasticity function, CPNE6 functions as a calcium-regulated membrane trafficking protein coordinating dendritic spine remodeling during synaptic plasticity. The two C2 domains bind calcium ions to trigger translocation from cytosol to plasma membrane or intracellular vesicles upon neuronal activity-induced calcium influx. The vWA domain mediates interactions with synaptic scaffolding proteins (DLGAP1, ANKS1B) and adhesion molecules (NRXN3) to couple membrane dynamics to cytoskeletal reorganization. CPNE6 likely operates in concert with hippocalcin (HPCA, calcium sensor) to decode calcium signals into structural changes at excitatory synapses. The Golgi/vesicle localization suggests CPNE6 regulates trafficking of synaptic proteins (receptors, adhesion molecules) to dendritic spines during long-term potentiation (LTP). The AK5 interaction links CPNE6 to local ATP/ADP metabolism at synapses, potentially coordinating energy supply with structural plasticity. The CPNE4 paralog interaction suggests functional redundancy or heteromeric complex formation among copines. The GFAP interaction indicates potential roles in neuron-glia communication. The 11 ClinVar CNVs support clinical significance in neurodevelopmental disorders, though specific disease associations remain to be defined. CPNE6 exemplifies calcium-dependent membrane trafficking proteins that translate neuronal activity into structural synaptic changes underlying learning and memory, with therapeutic potential in epilepsy and cognitive disorders.
