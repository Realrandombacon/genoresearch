## Description
RNF17 (TDRD4) encodes a 1623 amino acid testis-specific protein (UniProt Q9BXT8, HPA 22.7 nTPM in testis) containing an N-terminal RING-type zinc finger (IPR001841, residues 32-75) and five tandem Tudor domains (residues 465-541, 703-798, 945-1026, 1229-1296, 1477-1581) that recognize methylated arginine/lysine marks on piRNA pathway proteins. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners centered on the piRNA biogenesis machinery: PIWIL2 (0.849, highest confidence), PIWIL4 (0.655), PIWIL1 (0.568), MOV10L1 (0.636, RNA helicase), MAEL (0.622, transposon silencer), DDX4/VASA (0.618, DEAD-box helicase), TDRD12 (0.580), TDRD9 (0.579), STK31 (0.673), and DAZL (0.481, germ cell-specific RNA-binding protein). The RING domain confers E3 ubiquitin ligase activity, while the five Tudor domains create a multivalent scaffold binding symmetrically dimethylated arginines (sDMA) on PIWI proteins. ClinVar documents 74 pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.3204+2T>C) and chromosomal 13q CNVs, implicating RNF17 dysfunction in male infertility and spermatogenic failure. The testis-enriched expression (detected in some tissues, cancer-enriched pattern) combined with piRNA pathway interactions suggests RNF17 functions as a molecular scaffold assembling piRNA-induced silencing complexes (piRISCs) at mitochondrial surface nuage/intermitochondrial cement structures, where it coordinates transposon RNA silencing via PIWI endonuclease recruitment and ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover during pachytene spermatogenesis.
