## Description
LOC107984399 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 11q23.3 (NC_000011.10, positions 120168976-120171680, ~2.7 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000176984 but reported no detectable protein expression, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 1 single allele duplication (variant 545140), 9 duplications (variant 161071: 11q23.3-25, chr11:116851395-134998513, ~18.1 Mb; variant 150522: 11q23.3-24.2, chr11:117333952-127709156, ~10.4 Mb; variant 149257: 11q23.3-25, chr11:116806268-135075271, ~18.3 Mb; variant 148508: 11q23.2-25, chr11:112864326-131189315, ~18.3 Mb; variant 148379: 11q23.3-25, chr11:116868935-135075271, ~18.2 Mb; variant 144588: 11q23.3-25, chr11:116851372-134998526, ~18.1 Mb; variant 59772: 11q22.1-25, chr11:100348599-135040246, ~34.7 Mb) and 4 deletions (variant 151354: 11q23.3-24.2, chr11:120080142-125829106, ~5.7 Mb; variant 150032: 11q23.3-25, chr11:119424297-135075271, ~15.7 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 11q23.3 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including APOA1 (apolipoprotein A1, HDL cholesterol structural protein, reverse cholesterol transport, ABCA1 lipid efflux, Tangier disease, cardiovascular disease, amyloidosis), KMT2A/MLL (lysine methyltransferase 2A, H3K4 methylation, transcriptional activation, Wiedemann-Steiner syndrome, acute lymphoblastic/leukemia MLL rearrangements, histone epigenetics), CBL (Casitas B-lineage lymphoma proto-oncogene, E3 ubiquitin ligase, RAS pathway negative regulation, receptor tyrosine kinase ubiquitination, Noonan-like syndrome, juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia), APOA5 (apolipoprotein A5, triglyceride metabolism, lipoprotein lipase activation, hypertriglyceridemia, cardiovascular risk), and H2AFX (H2A.X variant histone, DNA double-strand break repair, gamma-H2AX phosphorylation, ATM/ATR signaling, tumor suppression, ataxia telangiectasia). Functional hypothesis: LOC107984399 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 11q23.3 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a liver-specific enhancer coordinating APOA1/APOA5 expression in hepatocytes (HDL biogenesis, triglyceride clearance) or as a chromatin boundary separating lipid metabolism genes (APOA1/APOA5) from epigenetic regulator genes (KMT2A). The 14 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves APOA1 deficiency causing HDL deficiency and accelerated atherosclerosis (Tangier disease), APOA1 overexpression potentially causing amyloid deposition, KMT2A haploinsufficiency causing Wiedemann-Steiner syndrome (developmental delay, hypertrichosis, immune deficiency), KMT2A rearrangements driving MLL-rearranged acute leukemia (11q23 translocations), CBL loss-of-function causing constitutive RAS activation (JMML, Noonan-like features), CBL overexpression causing enhanced RTK degradation and signaling defects, APOA5 deficiency causing severe hypertriglyceridemia and pancreatitis, and H2AX deficiency impairing DNA damage response (genomic instability, cancer predisposition). The 11q23.3 deletion syndrome (Jacobsen syndrome 11q23 deletion) presents with thrombocytopenia, congenital heart defects, developmental delay, and distinctive facial features. The 11q23.3 duplication syndrome may present with metabolic abnormalities, epigenetic dysregulation, and cancer predisposition. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (14 CNVs ranging 2.7-34.7 Mb) and APOA1-KMT2A-CBL-APOA5-H2AFX gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for lipid metabolism-epigenetics-RAS signaling-DNA repair coordination.
