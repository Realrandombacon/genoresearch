## Description
ECH1 encodes a 328 aa delta(3,5)-delta(2,4)-dienoyl-CoA isomerase, mitochondrial (UniProt Q13011) located at 19q13.2, functioning as an auxiliary enzyme in mitochondrial fatty acid beta-oxidation that enables complete oxidation of unsaturated fatty acids. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved crotonase/enoyl-CoA hydratase architecture: Enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase domain (IPR001753/PF00378, aa 64-318) forming the catalytic core, crotonase-like domain (cd06558, aa 66-253) characteristic of the crotonase superfamily, C-terminal enoyl-CoA hydratase domain (IPR014748/G3DSA:1.10.12.10, aa 266-328), conserved catalytic site (IPR018376/PS00166, aa 164-184) essential for isomerase activity (GO:0016853), ClpP/crotonase-like domain superfamily (IPR029045, aa 61-318), and full-length Delta(3,5)-Delta(2,4)-dienoyl-CoA isomerase Ech1-like family membership (IPR045002, aa 49-328). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.2), indicating a well-folded enzyme with stable crotonase fold suitable for CoA-ester substrate binding and catalysis. Functional hypothesis: ECH1 likely functions through: (1) Auxiliary Beta-Oxidation Enzyme - catalyzing isomerization of 3,5-dienoyl-CoA intermediates to 2,4-dienoyl-CoA during unsaturated fatty acid oxidation, enabling enoyl-CoA hydratase to continue beta-oxidation cycle; (2) Mitochondrial Energy Metabolism - essential for complete oxidation of dietary unsaturated fatty acids (oleate, linoleate, arachidonate) in high-energy tissues (skeletal muscle, heart), preventing accumulation of toxic intermediates; (3) Ferroptosis Regulation - modulating lipid peroxidation susceptibility through fatty acid composition control, with ECH1 deficiency potentially increasing polyunsaturated fatty acid accumulation and ferroptotic cell death in ischemic stroke; (4) Diabetic Kidney Disease - mitochondrial dysfunction in podocytes/tubular cells linked to ECH1 expression changes, affecting fatty acid oxidation capacity and contributing to diabetic nephropathy progression; (5) Circular RNA Regulation - circ-ECH1 acting as miRNA sponge for miR-708-5p, regulating Ntrk2 expression in lung development and bronchopulmonary dysplasia. The crotonase fold and catalytic site conservation parallel ECI2 (peroxisomal paralog) and other enoyl-CoA hydratases (HADH, HADHB), suggesting shared mechanistic features with subcellular specialization. Disease implications include: diabetic nephropathy, ischemic stroke, ferroptosis-related disorders, bronchopulmonary dysplasia, 19q duplication syndromes, potential inborn errors of fatty acid oxidation. Despite 133 papers, ECH1's specific substrate preferences, role in ferroptosis execution, therapeutic targeting in diabetic kidney disease, and circ-ECH1 functions remain underexplored.
