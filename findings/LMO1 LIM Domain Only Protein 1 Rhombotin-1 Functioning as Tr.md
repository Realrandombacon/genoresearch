## Description
LMO1 (LIM domain only protein 1, also known as Rhombotin-1 or RBTN1, P25800) encodes a 156 aa nuclear protein functioning as a critical transcriptional co-regulator that lacks DNA-binding capability but mediates protein-protein interactions through two tandem LIM domains to assemble multi-protein transcription complexes controlling hematopoietic differentiation, T-cell development, and oncogenic transformation. Structural architecture reveals an exceptionally well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 88.2): First LIM domain (IPR001781/PF00412/SM00132/cd09388/G3DSA:2.10.110.10, residues 22-84/24-82/23-77/24-78/24-83) containing the characteristic zinc-binding motif with conserved cysteine/histidine residues coordinating two zinc ions in cross-brace arrangement forming stable scaffold for protein interaction, second LIM domain (IPR001781/PF00412/SM00132/cd09389/G3DSA:2.10.110.10, residues 86-148/88-144/87-141/88-142/84-149) providing additional interaction surface and complex stabilization, LMO/RBTN transcription factor family domain (IPR050945/PTHR45787, residues 7-148/7-148) spanning nearly the entire protein defining the functional unit, LIM zinc-binding domain signature (PS00478, residues 88-123) containing the conserved metal-coordinating residues essential for structural integrity, GO terms DNA-binding transcription factor activity (GO:0003700), zinc ion binding (GO:0008270), and regulation of transcription (GO:0006355). STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence hematopoietic transcription network: LIM domain binding protein 1 LDB1 (0.996, highest) as obligate dimerization partner bridging LMO1 to DNA-binding factors forming the core complex, GATA binding protein 3 GATA3 (0.859) and GATA1 (0.854) as zinc-finger transcription factors controlling T-cell and erythroid differentiation respectively, T-cell leukemia homeobox 1 TLX1 (0.830) and TLX3 (0.659) as homeodomain oncogenes frequently translocated with LMO1 in T-ALL, TAL bHLH transcription factor 2 TAL2 (0.615) and TAL1 (0.481) as basic helix-loop-helix partners in erythroid/T-cell complexes, LIM domain binding protein 2 LDB2 (0.467) as LDB1 paralog, Transcription factor 3 TCF3 (0.439) as E-protein bHLH factor, and LYL1 (0.429) as stem cell leukemia homolog. HPA classifies LMO1 as cancer-related/disease-related/predicted intracellular protein with proto-oncogene disease involvement; tissue-enhanced RNA specificity with highest expression in skeletal muscle (27.5 nTPM) and tongue (11.6 nTPM) indicating roles beyond hematopoiesis in muscle development or maintenance; detected in some tissues reflects restricted expression pattern consistent with lineage-specific transcription factor, cancer-enriched RNA specificity confirms oncogenic function with upregulation in tumors, not detected in immune cells suggests low basal expression in mature lymphocytes though critical in developing T-cells, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity indicates specific progenitor expression, and nuclear subcellular localization confirms transcriptional regulatory function.
