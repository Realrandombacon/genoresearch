## Description
The CPSF4L protein is likely involved in RNA processing, specifically in cleavage and polyadenylation reactions. It interacts with other proteins in the CPSF complex, such as CPSF1, CPSF2, CPSF3, and WDR33, with high confidence scores. The protein contains multiple CCCH-type zinc finger domains, suggesting its role in RNA binding. While its tissue expression is not detected in normal tissues, it shows low cancer specificity. Additionally, 14 pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants have been reported, indicating its potential involvement in diseases.
