# MRI1: Methylthioribose-1-phosphate Isomerase in Methionine Salvage Pathway with Roles in Translation Initiation, Neurodevelopment, and Congenital Heart Defects

## Description
MRI1 (Q9BV20) encodes a 369 amino acid methylthioribose-1-phosphate isomerase that functions as a critical enzyme in the methionine salvage pathway, converting methylthioribose-1-phosphate (MTR-1-P) to methylthioribulose-1-phosphate (MTRu-1-P) for regeneration of methionine from S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) cycle byproducts. InterPro analysis reveals a bimodular translation initiation factor eIF-2B architecture: an N-terminal eIF-2B domain 1 (G3DSA:1.20.120.420, positions 1-157) and a C-terminal eIF-2B domain 2 (G3DSA:3.40.50.10470, positions 160-369), embedded within the initiation factor 2B alpha/beta/delta family (IPR011559/PF01008, positions 33-346) and classified as methylthioribose-1-phosphate isomerase (IPR005251/PTHR43475/MF_01678, positions 2-355) within the NagB/RpiA transferase-like superfamily (IPR037171/G3DSA:3.40.50.1110, positions 3-354). AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally well-folded structure (pLDDT 94.8, very high confidence) consistent with stable enzymatic architecture required for precise substrate positioning in the methionine salvage pathway. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with methionine metabolism and nucleotide processing machinery: APIP (0.992), apoptosis inhibiting protein with methionine salvage functions; MTAP (0.991), methylthioadenosine phosphorylase producing MTR-1-P substrate; ENOPH1 (0.975), enolase-phosphatase E1 in methionine salvage; ADI1 (0.909), acid-inducible protein 1; CYREN (0.890), cell cycle regulator and non-homologous end joining inhibitor; PNP (0.881), purine nucleoside phosphorylase; ACAD10/11 (0.822-0.842), acyl-CoA dehydrogenases; and ADD1/ADD2 (0.796), alpha-adducin cytoskeletal proteins. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, nucleoplasm/nucleoli fibrillar center/cytosol localization consistent with dual roles in translation initiation factor complex assembly and metabolic enzyme function, classified under amino-acid biosynthesis/methionine biosynthesis/isomerase activity biological processes. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses spanning chromosome 19p13.2-13.12 regions (e.g., chr19:12697728-14111313 deletions, chr19:13592592-14717528 losses) and duplications (chr19:260912-58956888 gains), plus missense variants (c.629G>A p.Arg210Gln), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (113 papers) demonstrates MRI1 genetic variations associate with ventricular septal defect in Chinese Tibetan populations through whole-exome sequencing; MRI1 was identified as a candidate locus for infantile epilepsy with severe cystic degeneration of the brain encoding translation initiation factor eIF-2B subunit alpha/beta/delta-like protein. The mechanistic hypothesis: MRI1 functions dually as (1) a metabolic enzyme catalyzing MTR-1-P isomerization in the methionine salvage pathway regenerating methionine from SAM cycle byproducts essential for cellular methylation capacity, and (2) a structural component related to eIF-2B translation initiation factor complex regulating global protein synthesis rates; the nucleolar localization suggests MRI1 participates in ribosome biogenesis coupling methionine availability to translation capacity; APIP and MTAP interactions place MRI1 at the center of methionine recycling preventing toxic MTR-1-P accumulation; CYREN interaction suggests cross-talk between methionine metabolism and DNA double-strand break repair via non-homologous end joining regulation; copy number losses on 19p may cause haploinsufficiency leading to impaired methionine salvage affecting neurodevelopment (infantile epilepsy with cystic brain degeneration) and cardiac development (ventricular septal defects) through disrupted methylation patterns during embryogenesis; exceptionally high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 94.8) indicates rigid active site geometry enabling precise substrate orientation for efficient isomerization; ADD1/ADD2 interactions suggest potential cytoskeletal coupling linking metabolic state to cell shape changes during development.
