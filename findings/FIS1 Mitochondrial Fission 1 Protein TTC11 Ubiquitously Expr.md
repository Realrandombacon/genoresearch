# FIS1 (Mitochondrial Fission 1 Protein, TTC11): Ubiquitously Expressed TPR Domain-Containing Outer Membrane Adaptor Essential for Drp1 Recruitment, Mitochondrial and Peroxisomal Fission, Acute Kidney Injury via Lactylation, Sepsis Pathogenesis, HCC Lenvatinib Resistance, and Osteogenesis Regulation Through DNM1L-TBC1D15-MFF Fission Machinery Assembly

## Description
FIS1 encodes a 152 aa mitochondrial and peroxisomal outer membrane protein (UniProt Q9Y3D6), also known as TTC11, that functions as a critical molecular adaptor recruiting the dynamin-related GTPase Drp1 (DNM1L) to drive mitochondrial fission and peroxisomal division through its tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain scaffold. This well-studied gene (1431 publications) has emerging clinical significance for acute kidney injury (AKI), sepsis-induced organ failure, hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) drug resistance, and radiation-induced bone loss. The protein features conserved TPR architecture: N-terminal TPR repeat (IPR028058/PF14852, residues 34-62) and C-terminal TPR repeat (IPR028061/PF14853, residues 71-123) forming the characteristic helical bundle; cytosolic TPR domain (cd12212/IPR033745, residues 9-123) mediating protein-protein interactions with fission machinery; classified within tetratricopeptide-like helical domain superfamily (IPR011990/G3DSA:1.25.40.10/SSF48452, residues 1-152) and mitochondria fission 1 protein family (IPR016543/PIRSF008835/PTHR13247, residues 2-150) with GO terms for protein binding, mitochondrial fission, and apoptosis. STRING interactions reveal FIS1 operates within extremely high-confidence fission networks: DNM1L (0.999), dynamin-1-like protein (Drp1, primary effector); TBC1D15 (0.999), Rab GTPase activating protein linking fission to ER-mitochondria contacts; DNM1 (0.997), dynamin-1 paralog; BCAP31 (0.996), B-cell receptor-associated protein 31 in ER-mitochondria tethering; DENR (0.995), density-regulated protein; TBC1D17 (0.993), paralog of TBC1D15; MFF (0.989), mitochondrial fission factor alternative receptor; MFN2 (0.988), mitofusin 2 fusion protein; PEX11A (0.972), peroxisomal biogenesis factor 11-alpha; MARCHF5 (0.965), E3 ubiquitin ligase regulating fission protein turnover. HPA confirms low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced, mitochondrial subcellular localization consistent with outer membrane anchoring, classified as plasma/intracellular/membrane protein involved in apoptosis. ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants dominated by large-scale copy number losses across 7q22.1 region (7q22.1 98.4-102.1Mb x1, 7q22.1 100.5-101.9Mb x1, 7q22.1 99.1-101.9Mb x1, 7q22.1-31.31 100.7-119.2Mb x1, 7q22.1-31.1 99.4-111.6Mb x1, 7q22.1 99.6-102.5Mb x1), large deletions (7p22.3-q36.3 0.01-159.1Mb), copy number gains (7p22.3-q36.3 0.01-159.1Mb x3, 7q22.1-36.3 98.7-159.1Mb x3), and complex rearrangements, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined beyond susceptibility to metabolic and neurodegenerative phenotypes. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 77.9), revealing a well-folded TPR domain bundle suitable for scaffolding multiple protein partners. Mechanistically, FIS1 operates through integrated modes: (1) Drp1 recruitment—TPR domain binds DNM1L (Drp1) directly or via adaptors (MFF, TBC1D15) to assemble fission complexes at constriction sites; (2) Peroxisomal division—interacts with PEX11A to drive peroxisomal fission maintaining organelle homeostasis; (3) AKI pathogenesis—2024 study (66 citations) demonstrates Drp1-FIS1 interaction inhibition alleviates aberrant mitochondrial fragmentation in acute kidney injury; (4) Sepsis-induced AKI—2023 study (156 citations) reveals PDHA1 hyperacetylation drives lactate overproduction causing FIS1 lactylation, promoting excessive fission and kidney damage; (5) Oxidative stress sensing—2025 study (10 citations) identifies hidden cysteine in FIS1 preventing excessive fission under oxidative stress; (6) HCC drug resistance—2025 study (3 citations) shows tumor stiffness modulates lenvatinib resistance via FIS1-dependent mitophagy; (7) Osteogenesis regulation—2025 study (3 citations) links Ca2+-NFATc1-FIS1 pathway to MSC mitochondrial dysfunction in radiation-induced bone injury; (8) ER-mitochondria tethering—TBC1D15/TBC1D17 interactions coordinate fission with ER contact sites. Loss-of-function causes defective mitochondrial fragmentation, impaired peroxisomal division, metabolic dysregulation, while overexpression drives pathological fission, apoptosis, and organ injury.
