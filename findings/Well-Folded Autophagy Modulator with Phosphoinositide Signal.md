## Description
TMEM150A encodes a 271 amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt: Q86TG1) with ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity) classified as a predicted intracellular membrane protein and transporter. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the DRAM/TMEM150 autophagy modulator family (IPR050911 spanning positions 6-259; PTHR21324) with a CWH43-like N-terminal domain (IPR019402 at positions 4-231; PF10277 Frag1/DRAM/Sfk1/Cwh43P domain), indicating evolutionary conservation with proteins involved in autophagy regulation and membrane trafficking. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 89.7, high confidence) indicates a well-folded stable 3D structure, distinguishing it from intrinsically disordered proteins and suggesting defined functional domains suitable for specific protein-protein or protein-lipid interactions. STRING network identifies 10 interaction partners including TTC7A (score 0.724, tetratricopeptide repeat protein regulating epithelial polarity and actin cytoskeleton), TTC7B (0.665), EFR3B (0.635, EFR3 homolog B involved in phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase recruitment to plasma membrane), FAM126A (0.632, hyaluronan-mediated motility regulator involved in vesicle trafficking), EFR3A (0.543), FBXL15 (0.507, F-box ubiquitin ligase), PI4KA (0.492, phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase alpha producing PI4P for membrane identity and vesicle budding), ACOX3 (0.482, acyl-CoA oxidase), SH3BGRL (0.463), and CUEDC2 (0.448). The convergence of interactions with EFR3A/B (phosphoinositide signaling), PI4KA (lipid kinase), FAM126A (vesicle trafficking), and TTC7A/B (cytoskeletal organization) strongly suggests TMEM150A functions at the intersection of autophagy, membrane trafficking, and phosphoinositide signaling pathways. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including both copy number losses (chr2:11504318-111365996, chr2:82486900-87322042 deletions) and gains (chr2:1-243199373 duplication) spanning 2p regions, plus one intronic SNV (c.397-71A>T), indicating bidirectional dosage sensitivity. Conservation is complete across mammals: mouse Q91WN2 (271 aa), rat Q9QZE9 (271 aa). The mechanistic hypothesis proposes TMEM150A functions as an autophagy modulator that recruits or stabilizes phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase complexes (via EFR3/PI4KA interactions) at specific membrane compartments, regulating PI4P production required for autophagosome formation or maturation, with pathogenic variants disrupting this lipid signaling hub leading to defective autophagic flux and contributing to chromosomal syndrome phenotypes.
