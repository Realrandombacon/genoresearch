## Description
LOC112577592 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 18p11.23 (NC_000018.10, positions 7461134-7455841, ~5.3 kb region) with exceptional clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000286126 but no expression data was available. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (among the highest counts observed in this study), all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 18p11.32-11.21 region (variants include 4796302, 4526717, 2579191, 160884, 160810, 155410, 155367, 155151, 154106, 154072). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, monosomy) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy/tetrasomy) ranging from focal alterations (~10-15 Mb) to whole-arm changes (80+ Mb). The 18p chromosomal location is clinically exceptional as this region constitutes the 18p deletion syndrome (de Grouchy syndrome type 1) critical region, characterized by intellectual disability, short stature, ptosis, round face, and hypotonia. The region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including SMCHD1 (structural maintenance of chromosomes flexible hinge domain containing 1, chromatin compaction, facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy type 2, Bosniak classification), TGIF1 (TGIF homeobox 1, holoprosencephaly, 18p deletion syndrome), NDUFV2 (NADH deubiquitinase flavoprotein 2, mitochondrial complex I), and CARS2 (cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase 2, mitochondrial translation). Functional hypothesis: LOC112577592 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary controlling expression of neighboring dosage-sensitive genes in the 18p critical region, particularly SMCHD1 which regulates chromatin higher-order structure through DNA methylation and histone modification. The extraordinary burden of 43 pathogenic CNVs indicates extreme structural instability and dosage sensitivity in this sub-telomeric region. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency of SMCHD1 (leading to defective chromatin compaction and epigenetic dysregulation), TGIF1 (causing holoprosencephaly and midline defects through TGF-beta/Nodal signaling disruption), or mitochondrial genes (causing metabolic dysfunction). The absence of protein-coding features combined with record-breaking ClinVar evidence (43 variants) and neurodevelopmental syndrome association suggests this locus functions as a master chromatin regulatory hub controlling brain development and somatic growth through long-range epigenetic regulation.
