HPA shows ubiquitous expression with nucleoplasmic/nucleolar localization, annotated for mRNA/tRNA processing and classified as disease-related gene. Functional mechanism: TSEN15 dimerizes with TSEN34 to form the catalytic core recognizing branched pre-tRNA structures, positioning the intron-exon boundaries for precise endonucleolytic cleavage.
