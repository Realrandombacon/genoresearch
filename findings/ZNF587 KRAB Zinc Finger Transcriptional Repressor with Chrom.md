## Description
ZNF587 encodes a 575 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 15-55) that recruits the corepressor TRIM28/KAP1 complex for heterochromatin formation, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 92-417 that mediate sequence-specific DNA recognition. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=66.6) indicates a partially structured protein with ordered zinc finger regions and flexible linker segments typical of transcriptional regulators. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic and nucleolar localization, consistent with chromatin-associated function. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with TRIM28 (score 0.639), the canonical KRAB coreceptor, plus ZBTB14 (0.598), another zinc finger regulator, and CYSRT1 (0.603), suggesting involvement in epithelial differentiation pathways. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 19q13.43, implicating ZNF587 dosage sensitivity in human disease. The mechanistic model proposes ZNF587 binds specific GC-rich DNA motifs via its zinc finger array, recruits TRIM28/SETDB1 histone methyltransferase complexes through the KRAB domain, and establishes H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks to silence target genes involved in cell differentiation and proliferation control.
