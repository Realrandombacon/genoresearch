## Description
KCNH3 (Potassium voltage-gated channel subfamily H member 3, also known as Kv12.2 or ELK3, Q9ULD8) encodes a 1083 aa voltage-gated potassium channel functioning as a critical regulator of neuronal excitability, seizure threshold, and neurodevelopment through modulation of membrane potential and action potential repolarization in brain tissue.
