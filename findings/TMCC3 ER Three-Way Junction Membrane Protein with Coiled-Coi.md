## Description
TMCC3 (Transmembrane and Coiled-Coil Domain Protein 3, UniProt Q9ULS5) is a 477 aa endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein functioning as a structural regulator of ER three-way junctions essential for maintaining the reticular network architecture of the endoplasmic reticulum. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a partially ordered architecture (pLDDT=69.9, medium confidence, borderline confident) featuring extensive coiled-coil domain (PF10267, aa 67-463) enabling protein-protein interactions and membrane protein oligomerization, classification within TEX28/TMCC family (IPR019394, aa 2-476) and CEREBRAL PROTEIN-11-RELATED family (PTHR17613, aa 2-476), consistent with ER membrane localization and junction-forming capacity. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to ER membrane and junction-associated proteins: testis-specific TEX28 (0.698) as paralogous family member, transmembrane O-mannosyltransferase TMTC4 (0.505) and TMTC2 (0.446) as ER membrane proteins, F-box protein FBXO28 (0.450) in ubiquitination, coiled-coil domain protein CCDC106 (0.450), C-type lectin CLEC17A (0.445), ER membrane protein LSMEM1 (0.438), protein phosphatase 4 regulatory subunit PPP4R4 (0.415), and uncharacterized C3orf62 (0.413). HPA shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, immune cell enhanced expression, cell type enhanced specificity, endoplasmic reticulum and vesicles localization, classified as predicted intracellular/membrane protein and transporter. ClinVar identifies 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including synonymous variant p.Glu318=, missense variant p.Glu328Gln, and copy number gains/losses at 12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:173787-133777902) and 12q21.33-24.11 (chr12:91044318-109133210), though no specific Mendelian disease is directly annotated. Literature (18 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: 2022 study demonstrates 14-3-3γ isoform binds TMCC3 regulating its ER localization for reticular network formation (4 citations), 2021 research establishes TMCC3 regulates breast cancer stem cell maintenance and AKT activation (11 citations), 2019 study shows TMCC3 localizes at ER three-way junctions for proper tubular network formation (6 citations). The gene represents a 'semi-dark ER junction protein' where canonical coiled-coil-mediated ER membrane protein oligomerization and three-way junction localization is established, 14-3-3γ binding suggests phosphorylation-dependent regulation of ER localization, breast cancer stem cell regulation via AKT activation reveals oncogenic potential, and 12p13.33-q24.33 CNV pathogenesis may contribute to neurodevelopmental and cancer phenotypes through TMCC3 dosage effects on ER architecture. Therapeutic targeting of TMCC3-14-3-3γ interaction or TMCC3-mediated AKT activation could represent novel approach for breast cancer stem cell eradication and therapy resistance, while CNV screening at 12p13.33-q24.33 should consider TMCC3 dosage in ER stress-associated disorders.
