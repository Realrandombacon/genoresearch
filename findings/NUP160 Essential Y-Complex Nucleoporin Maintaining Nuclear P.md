## Description
NUP160 (Nucleoporin 160, UniProt Q12769) is a 1436-amino acid essential component of the nuclear pore complex (NPC) Y-shaped outer ring subcomplex that functions as a critical structural scaffold maintaining nuclear envelope integrity, nucleocytoplasmic transport, and specialized cellular functions including podocyte homeostasis and cancer autophagy regulation. InterPro analysis confirms modular architecture characteristic of Y-complex nucleoporins: N-terminal beta-propeller domain (IPR059141/PF11715, aa 101-574) providing structural platform for protein-protein interactions; central helical domain (IPR056547/PF23345, aa 596-826) forming elongated scaffold; middle TPR domain (IPR056535/PF23354, aa 870-1134) with tetratricopeptide repeat folds mediating protein complex assembly; C-terminal TPR domain (IPR056536/PF23347, aa 1179-1436) completing the Y-arm structure; membership in Nucleoporin Nup120/160 family (IPR021717/PTHR21286, aa 42-1425). STRING interactions reveal exceptionally high-confidence partnerships defining the Y-complex/NUP107-160 subcomplex: NUP107 (0.999), SEC13 (0.999), NUP37 (0.999), SEH1L (0.999), NUP98 (0.998), NUP43 (0.998), AHCTF1 (0.998), NUP133 (0.997), NUP85 (0.995), and NUP205 (0.983) - confirming integration into the essential NPC outer ring that anchors pores to the nuclear envelope. HPA shows low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, consistent with essential housekeeping NPC function; classified as essential protein, disease-related gene, metabolic protein, and potential drug target; biological processes include mRNA transport, protein transport, translocation, and transport; disease variant involvement confirmed; low cancer and immune cell specificity; cell type enhanced specificity. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.4) for the full 1436 aa, indicating a well-folded elongated protein with structured beta-propeller, helical, and TPR domains forming the characteristic Y-shaped nucleoporin architecture. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.1645del p.Glu549fs, c.2271_2273+1del), nonsense mutations (c.3032C>A p.Ser1011Ter, c.3943G>T p.Glu1315Ter, c.2176C>T p.Gln726Ter), splice variants (c.3187+5G>A, c.2344+218G>A, c.3644+213C>T), and missense variants (c.38C>T p.Pro13Leu, c.33891G>C p.Lys1297Asn) though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined with uncertain significance annotations, suggesting loss-of-function mechanisms disrupting NPC assembly. Literature (92 papers) reveals emerging nephrology and oncology functions: (1) Loss of Nup160 dysregulating Cdc42 in podocytes of podocyte-specific Nup160 knockout mice (3 citations, 2025) - defining nephrotic syndrome mechanism; (2) m6A-methylated circRAPGEF5 driving lung adenocarcinoma progression via IGF2BP2/NUP160-mediated autophagy suppression (5 citations, 2025) - cancer mechanism; (3) Podocyte-specific Nup160 knockout mice developing nephrotic syndrome and glomerulosclerosis (7 citations, 2024) - disease model; (4) NUP160 as aging-related hub gene biomarker in sepsis (2025). Functional hypothesis: NUP160 serves as a critical structural component of the NPC Y-complex where its beta-propeller domain (aa 101-574) anchors the complex to the nuclear envelope, while its TPR domains (aa 870-1436) mediate interactions with other Y-complex subunits (NUP107, NUP85, NUP133) to form the outer ring scaffold essential for NPC assembly and stability; beyond canonical nucleocytoplasmic transport, NUP160 has acquired specialized roles in podocyte function where it regulates Cdc42 GTPase signaling critical for actin cytoskeleton maintenance in kidney filtration cells, and in cancer where it modulates autophagy through interactions with RNA-binding proteins (IGF2BP2) and circular RNAs. Mechanistic proposal: The beta-propeller domain (aa 101-574) provides a rigid structural platform that interacts with nuclear envelope components and other Y-complex nucleoporins; the helical domain (aa 596-826) forms an elongated arm extending from the propeller; the middle TPR (aa 870-1134) and C-terminal TPR (aa 1179-1436) domains mediate heterodimerization with NUP107, NUP85, and NUP133 to complete the Y-shaped hetero-oligomeric complex; this Y-complex assembles into the outer ring of the NPC, anchoring the pore to the nuclear envelope and maintaining pore integrity during mitosis when the nuclear envelope breaks down and reforms; in podocytes, NUP160 loss disrupts nucleocytoplasmic transport of Cdc42 regulators or alters transcriptional programs controlling Cdc42 expression, leading to actin cytoskeleton defects, foot process effacement, and proteinuria characteristic of steroid-resistant nephrotic syndrome (2024-2025, 7-10 cit); mutations in NUP160 and other Y-complex nucleoporins (NUP85, NUP107, NUP133) cause monogenic SRNS, establishing a nucleoporinopathy disease class; in lung adenocarcinoma, m6A-methylated circRAPGEF5 binds IGF2BP2 which interacts with NUP160 to suppress autophagy, promoting tumor progression and metastasis (2025, 5 cit); NUP160 expression correlates with aging and sepsis outcomes (2025), suggesting NPC dysfunction contributes to age-related immune decline; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 80.4) supports stable Y-complex architecture; ClinVar truncating mutations (p.Glu549fs, p.Ser1011Ter, p.Glu1315Ter, p.Gln726Ter) would eliminate C-terminal TPR domains, disrupting Y-complex assembly and causing NPC instability; NUP160 represents an essential nucleoporin with dual roles in constitutive nuclear transport and specialized cell-type functions (podocyte cytoskeleton, cancer autophagy), with emerging therapeutic relevance in nephrotic syndrome and cancer.
