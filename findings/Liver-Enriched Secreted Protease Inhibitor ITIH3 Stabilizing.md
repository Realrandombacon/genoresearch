## Description
ITIH3 encodes inter-alpha-trypsin inhibitor heavy chain 3 (ITIH3/H3P/SHAP), an 890 aa secreted plasma protein (UniProt Q06033) belonging to the inter-alpha-trypsin inhibitor family that functions as a protease inhibitor and extracellular matrix stabilizer through covalent linkage to hyaluronic acid forming high molecular weight complexes critical for tissue integrity, inflammation modulation, and tumor microenvironment regulation.
