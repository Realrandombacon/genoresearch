## Description
SFXN2 (Sideroflexin-2) is a mitochondrial outer membrane transporter (UniProt Q96NB2) with a high-confidence (pLDDT 85.0) 3D structure. It interacts with key mitochondrial metabolic enzymes (DHODH, ATP5MD, SFXN1/4) and is highly expressed in the kidney and parathyroid gland (nTPM 16.7/11.9). Despite having 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar, no specific disease has been annotated, classifying it as a "dark gene." We hypothesize that SFXN2 regulates a specific mitochondrial metabolite flux critical for renal and parathyroid homeostasis, and its dysfunction causes undiagnosed metabolic or developmental disorders.
