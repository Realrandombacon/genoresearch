## Description
TMEM47 (Transmembrane Protein 47, alias BCMP1/TM4SF10/VAB-9, UniProt Q9BQJ4) is a 181 amino acid integral membrane protein belonging to the PMP-22/EMP/MP20/Claudin superfamily (IPR004031/PF00822, residues 22-170), a conserved family of tetraspan proteins involved in cell junction formation, membrane organization, and barrier function. Additional domain annotations include P53-induced protein family (IPR015664/PTHR14399, residues 1-181) and CATH-Gene3D superfamily G3DSA:1.20.140.150 (residues 15-179), suggesting evolutionary relationship to apoptosis regulators. AlphaFold structure prediction shows high-confidence folding (global pLDDT 86.5), indicating a stable four-transmembrane helix topology characteristic of the PMP-22/Claudin family. HPA expression reveals vascular enrichment with blood vessel showing highest expression at 105.1 nTPM, classified as tissue-enhanced and detected in many tissues, with subcellular localization to the nuclear membrane. STRING interaction network identifies AJM1 (score 0.729, adherens junction molecule) as the top partner, alongside FAM47B (0.581), cadherin CDH17 (0.485), transmembrane proteins TMEM94 (0.480) and TMEM178A (0.474), proline-rich protein PRRG1 (0.467), gap junction protein GJC1/Connexin45 (0.462), annexin ANXA3 (0.446), and zinc finger ZNF227 (0.446). ClinVar contains 150 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning Xp21.1-Xp22.33 regions, indicating strong dosage sensitivity though no specific disease phenotype is yet defined. Mechanistic hypothesis: TMEM47 functions as a tetraspan membrane organizer at cell-cell junctions and nuclear envelope, potentially regulating vascular endothelial barrier integrity and intercellular communication. The PMP-22/Claudin family membership suggests roles in tight junction or adherens junction assembly, supported by interactions with AJM1 (junctional scaffolding), CDH17 (calcium-dependent adhesion), and GJC1 (gap junction channel). Nuclear membrane localization indicates potential additional function in nuclear envelope stability or nucleocytoplasmic transport regulation. Vascular enrichment (105.1 nTPM in blood vessels) parallels expression patterns of genes involved in angiogenesis and endothelial barrier function. The extensive ClinVar CNV burden (150 variants) suggests TMEM47 dosage is critical for normal development, possibly explaining X-linked phenotypes when disrupted. Structural conservation (pLDDT 86.5) and four-transmembrane topology support function as a membrane scaffold organizing junctional protein complexes.
