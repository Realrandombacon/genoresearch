## Description
ZNF461 encodes a 563 amino acid (UniProt Q8TAF7) Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) C2H2 zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 19q13.12. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, residues 5-46; IPR001909, residues 5-77) that recruits transcriptional corepressors, followed by multiple C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096 at residues 273-295, 301-323, 329-351; IPR013087) spanning residues 192-450. This domain architecture is characteristic of sequence-specific transcriptional repressors that silence target genes through heterochromatin formation.\n\nProtein interaction data (STRING-DB) reveals ZNF461 associates with ZNF615 (score 0.796, high confidence), suggesting potential cooperative DNA binding or shared regulatory targets among zinc finger proteins. The interaction with TRIM28 (score 0.522) is mechanistically significant—TRIM28/KAP1 is the canonical coreceptor for KRAB domains, recruiting SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 to establish H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. Additional partners include PHGDH (0.696, serine biosynthesis), TOX2 (0.690, HMG-box transcription factor), and SLA2 (0.613, T-cell signaling adaptor), suggesting ZNF461 may coordinate transcriptional programs linking metabolism, lymphocyte function, and chromatin regulation.\n\nHPA expression profiling shows ubiquitous RNA detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulatory functions. Subcellular localization is predominantly nucleoplasmic with nucleolar and Golgi apparatus signals, supporting its role as a DNA-binding transcription factor. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 19q11-13.33 regions, implicating ZNF461 dosage sensitivity in human disease. While AlphaFold structure prediction failed (API error), the conserved C2H2 zinc finger fold (G3DSA:3.30.160.60) is well-characterized structurally.\n\nFunctional hypothesis: ZNF461 operates as a sequence-specific transcriptional silencer that recognizes GC-rich DNA motifs through its tandem zinc fingers, recruiting the TRIM28/SETDB1 corepressor complex via its KRAB domain to deposit repressive H3K9me3 chromatin marks. Disease-associated copy number variants suggest ZNF461 haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts transcriptional homeostasis, potentially contributing to developmental disorders or cancer through dysregulation of cell cycle, metabolic, or immune response target genes.
