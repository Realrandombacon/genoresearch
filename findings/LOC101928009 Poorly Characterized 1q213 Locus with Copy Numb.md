## Description

Critically, no UniProt accession exists for this locus, preventing InterPro domain analysis, AlphaFold structure prediction, and STRING interaction mapping. HPA expression data is also absent, suggesting either very low expression, tissue-restricted patterns below detection thresholds, or that this may represent a non-coding transcript rather than a protein-coding gene. Despite the lack of protein-level data, ClinVar contains 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: a copy number loss (1q21.3, chr1:151707888-153275358) and a copy number gain (1q21.2-25.2, chr1:149854269-180267197), indicating dosage sensitivity in this genomic region. The 1q21.3 region is known to harbor segmental duplications and is associated with recurrent copy number variants linked to neurodevelopmental disorders, thrombocytopenia, and cardiac defects. Without protein sequence data, functional annotation remains speculative. This locus may represent: (1) a genuine protein-coding gene with extremely low/transient expression, (2) a long non-coding RNA misannotated as protein-coding, or (3) a pseudogene. The ClinVar variants likely affect regulatory elements or neighboring genes rather than LOC101928009 itself. Priority recommendation: obtain RNA-seq data from diverse tissues to confirm transcription, followed by ribosome profiling to assess translation potential.
