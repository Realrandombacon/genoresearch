# CASC9: Oncogenic Long Non-Coding RNA with miRNA Sponge Activity in Multiple Cancers

**Date:** 2026-03-19T12:41:02.301864

**Quality Score:** 4.15/10  (E=8.3, D=0.0) [MODERATE]

## Description
CASC9 (cancer susceptibility 9, Gene ID: 101805492) is a well-characterized long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) located on chromosome 8q21.13, spanning approximately 55kb of genomic sequence. Unlike protein-coding "dark genes," CASC9 lacks open reading frames and UniProt accession, explaining the absence of InterPro domains, STRING interactions, and AlphaFold structures. ClinVar analysis reveals 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses across 8q21.13, indicating clinical relevance in genomic instability syndromes. PubMed literature (82 publications) establishes CASC9 as an oncogenic lncRNA functioning through competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) mechanisms: it sponges tumor-suppressive miRNAs (miR-28-3p, miR-874-3p) to derepress oncogenic targets including BCL-2 and SOX12, activating PI3K/AKT signaling. Documented roles span papillary thyroid cancer, osteosarcoma, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCAL-1 alias), and colorectal cancer. This gene is NOT a "dark gene" but rather a characterized oncogenic lncRNA with established molecular mechanisms and disease associations.
