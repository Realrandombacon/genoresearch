## Description
LYPLA2 (Acyl-protein thioesterase 2, APT-2) is a 231 amino acid intracellular hydrolase that reverses S-palmitoylation modifications on cysteine residues of substrate proteins. The enzyme contains a canonical alpha/beta hydrolase fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.1820) with a phospholipase/carboxylesterase/thioesterase domain (IPR003140, PF02230, positions 13-227). AlphaFold prediction shows exceptional structural confidence (pLDDT 95.7), indicating a well-folded, stable enzyme. HPA data reveals ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, consistent with its role in regulating multiple signaling pathways. STRING interaction network identifies 10 high-confidence partners involved in lipid metabolism: GPCPD1 (0.943), PNPLA6 (0.920), PLAAT3 (0.917), PLB1 (0.915), PNPLA7 (0.902), and GAP43 (0.818). Notably, LYPLA2 interacts with ZDHHC7 (0.612), a palmitoyltransferase, suggesting coordinated regulation of protein palmitoylation cycles. ClinVar reports 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants, though specific disease associations remain undefined. Literature (48 papers) indicates roles in cancer stemness regulation via glycerophosphocholine metabolism and STAT3 palmitoylation cycling in TH17 differentiation. The enzyme likely functions as a regulatory switch controlling membrane association of signaling proteins through depalmitoylation, affecting protein trafficking, stability, and signal transduction in lipid metabolic pathways.
