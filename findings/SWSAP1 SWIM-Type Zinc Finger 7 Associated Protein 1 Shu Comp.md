## Description
SWSAP1 (SWIM-Type Zinc Finger 7 Associated Protein 1, also known as Shu Complex Subunit SWSAP1 or ATPase SWSAP1, UniProt Q6NVH7) is a 229 aa nuclear/cytosolic protein belonging to the Shu complex (SWS1-SWSAP1) that functions as a RAD51 paralog-like factor in homologous recombination (HR) DNA repair, RAD51 filament stabilization, and meiotic recombination. InterPro analysis reveals P-loop NTPase architecture: P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase domain (IPR027417, positions 5-150; G3DSA:3.40.50.300, positions 5-150; SSF52540, positions 6-125; PTHR28653, positions 4-214) for ATP binding and hydrolysis. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 82.0), indicating a well-folded compact ATPase with stable P-loop core. HPA data confirms nucleoplasm/cytosol localization, classified as predicted intracellular protein involved in DNA damage, DNA recombination, and DNA repair with low tissue specificity (detected in many tissues). STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with DNA repair machinery: ZSWIM7/SWS1 (0.996, Shu complex partner), ATM (0.906, ataxia telangiectasia mutated kinase), MSH2 (0.904, DNA mismatch repair protein), ERCC4 (0.903, DNA repair endonuclease XPF), XRCC6 (0.902, Ku70 DNA double-strand break repair factor), PAXX (0.902, paralog of XRCC4 and XLF), ERCC1 (0.900, DNA repair endonuclease), MSH6 (0.900, DNA mismatch repair protein), RCOR1 (0.900, REST corepressor 1), ERCC8 (0.900, Cockayne syndrome protein A). ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 19p13.2 copy number losses/gains and SNV c.250-16C>G. Literature (19 papers) establishes SWSAP1 roles in: (1) premature ovarian insufficiency (POI) as new disease gene through SWS1-complex disruption (2025), (2) distinct homologous recombination pathways controlled by SWS1-SWSAP1-SPIDR complex (2020, 38 citations), (3) RAD51 filament fostering by regulating anti-recombinase FIGNL1 AAA+ ATPase (2018, 62 citations), and (4) early meiotic recombination promotion in mouse germ cells (2018, 47 citations). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SWSAP1 serves as an ATPase-dependent Shu complex subunit that heterodimerizes with ZSWIM7/SWS1 to stabilize RAD51 nucleoprotein filaments on single-stranded DNA, antagonizing the anti-recombinase activity of FIGNL1 AAA+ ATPase to promote homology search and strand invasion during homologous recombination. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The P-loop NTPase domain (positions 5-150) binds and hydrolyzes ATP, providing energy for conformational changes that stabilize SWSAP1-ZSWIM7 heterodimer formation. The Shu complex binds RAD51-coated ssDNA through direct protein-protein interactions, preventing FIGNL1-mediated RAD51 filament disassembly. FIGNL1 is an AAA+ ATPase that acts as anti-recombinase by disrupting RAD51 filaments; SWSAP1 counteracts this by recruiting SPIDR (SWS1-SPIDR-interacting DNA repair protein) to form tripartite SWS1-SWSAP1-SPIDR complex that shields RAD51 filaments from FIGNL1 activity. In meiosis, SWSAP1 promotes DMC1/RAD51-mediated strand exchange during meiotic recombination, essential for crossover formation and chromosome segregation. The 12 ClinVar variants at 19p13.2 suggest SWSAP1 dosage sensitivity contributes to DNA repair deficiency disorders and premature ovarian insufficiency through defective meiotic recombination in oocytes. ATM interaction links SWSAP1 to DNA damage response signaling. MSH2/MSH6 interactions suggest crosstalk between HR and mismatch repair. ERCC1/ERCC4/XRCC6/PAXX interactions indicate coordination with nucleotide excision repair and non-homologous end joining. High AlphaFold confidence (82.0) confirms stable ATPase structure suitable for drug targeting in cancer therapy (HR deficiency sensitizes tumors to PARP inhibitors).
