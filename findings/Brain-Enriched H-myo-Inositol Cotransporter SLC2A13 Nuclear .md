## Description
SLC2A13 encodes a 648 amino acid proton myo-inositol cotransporter (UniProt Q96QE2), a member of the Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) of solute transporters. InterPro analysis confirms canonical MFS transporter architecture including IPR003663 (Sugar/inositol transporter, aa 78-412/510-531/533-545), IPR005828 (Major facilitator sugar transporter-like, aa 84-415/508-609), IPR005829 (Sugar transporter conserved site, aa 180-205/381-397), IPR020846 (MFS domain, aa 84-598), IPR036259 (MFS transporter superfamily, aa 73-430/493-615), and IPR050814 (Myo-inositol Transporter), indicating a conserved proton-coupled inositol transport mechanism via the characteristic 12-transmembrane helix MFS fold. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q96QE2-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 80.1), consistent with a functional multi-pass membrane transporter. HPA expression reveals brain-enhanced specificity (27.2 nTPM) with parathyroid gland expression (21.3 nTPM) and nuclear membrane subcellular localization, suggesting specialized roles in neuronal inositol signaling and nuclear-cytoplasmic metabolite shuttling. STRING interactions identify 10 partners including SLC2A11-2 (0.792), SLC5A3 (0.735, sodium/myo-inositol cotransporter), SLC5A11 (0.595), ISYNA1 (0.476, myo-inositol-1-phosphate synthase), MIOX (0.453, myo-inositol oxygenase), and DHDH (0.466, dehydrogenase), forming a coherent inositol metabolism and transport network. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 12 copy number gains and losses spanning 12p13.33-q24.33, though no specific disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature (30 papers) identifies SLC2A13 as a potential cancer stem cell marker in oral squamous cell carcinoma and as regulated by circSLC2A13 in chicken muscle development via miR-34a-3p sponging. The combination of MFS transporter domains, brain-enriched expression, nuclear membrane localization, inositol metabolism interaction network, 12q CNV burden, and cancer stem cell association suggests SLC2A13 functions as a proton-coupled myo-inositol transporter regulating nuclear inositol signaling pools, with implications for neuronal function, cancer stemness, and developmental phenotypes associated with chromosome 12 copy number variations.
