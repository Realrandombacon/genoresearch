# LOC129390204: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 10q22.1 with Balanced CNV Burden (2 Del:2 Dup)

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:36:23.511968

**Quality Score:** 2.5/10  (E=4.0, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129390204 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 10q22.1 (GRCh38: 74907316-74907516), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a balanced burden with both deletion and duplication events (2:2 ratio). Deletions include 10q21.3-22.2 (~12Mb) and 10q22.1-22.2 (~3Mb); duplications include 10q11.23-23.2 (~36Mb) and 10q21.3-22.3 (~12Mb). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 10q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and skeletal anomalies. The 10q22 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including BMPR1A (Bone Morphogenetic Protein Receptor Type 1A, mutations cause juvenile polyposis syndrome), NECA1, and multiple developmental transcription factors.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129390204 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 10q22 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes. Candidate targets include BMPR1A (TGF-beta signaling receptor essential for gastrointestinal and skeletal development) or neighboring neural crest transcription factors. The silencer likely recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The balanced CNV burden (2 deletions:2 duplications) suggests both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity intolerance where both silencer loss and gain cause derepression or hyper-repression of developmental genes. The K562 validation suggests potential roles in hematopoietic lineage specification. Deletions may cause BMPR1A derepression leading to polyposis spectrum phenotypes while duplications may cause excessive silencing of TGF-beta pathway genes, contributing to 10q deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes.
