## Description
BOC (Brother of CDO, BOC cell adhesion associated oncogene regulated, Q9BWV1) encodes a 1114 aa type I transmembrane glycoprotein functioning as a co-receptor in the Hedgehog (Hh) signaling pathway that binds Sonic Hedgehog (SHH), Indian Hedgehog (IHH), and Desert Hedgehog (DHH) ligands to regulate embryonic patterning, neural tube development, skeletal muscle differentiation, and cell adhesion processes. Structural architecture reveals multi-domain organization characteristic of cell surface receptors: N-terminal signal peptide followed by five immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR003598/IPR003599/IPR007110/IPR013098/IPR013783/IPR036179/G3DSA:2.60.40.10, residues 32-131/132-223/226-318/42-125/141-207/243-306/135-222/237-315/36-123/139-213/235-304/136-210/323-406) forming the ligand-binding extracellular region that directly interacts with Hh proteins through Ig domain surfaces, three fibronectin type III domains (IPR003961/IPR036116/cd00063/G3DSA:2.60.40.10, residues 472-571/606-703/712-812/486-567/606-697/712-809/468-573/605-809) providing structural spacing and protein-protein interaction interfaces for co-receptor complex assembly, single transmembrane helix anchoring BOC to plasma membrane, and short cytoplasmic tail mediating intracellular signaling; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 63.4, medium confidence) reflects partially structured architecture with well-folded Ig/FN3 domains embedded in disordered linker regions and cytoplasmic tail consistent with flexible cell surface receptor requiring conformational dynamics for ligand binding and signal transduction. STRING interactions define complete Hedgehog signaling apparatus: hedgehog ligands DHH (0.998)/IHH (0.998)/SHH (0.996) as direct binding partners, patched receptors PTCH1 (0.993)/PTCH2 (0.974) serving as primary Hh receptors that BOC co-operates with, co-receptor GAS1 (0.982) functioning redundantly with BOC in Hh binding, paralog CDON (0.942) sharing structural homology and overlapping functions in Hh signal reception, LDL receptor-related protein LRP2 (0.916) modulating Hh ligand availability, M-cadherin CDH15 (0.814) linking BOC to cell adhesion machinery in muscle development, and smoothened SMO (0.758) as the signal transducer activated downstream of Hh-BOC-PTCH1 interaction. HPA localizes BOC to plasma membrane, nucleoplasm, and nuclear bodies as predicted membrane protein involved in cell adhesion with expression detected in many tissues, low tissue specificity reflecting broad developmental role, cell type enhanced expression in specific lineages, not detected in immune cells indicating specialized non-hematopoietic functions. Literature reveals emerging mechanisms: BOC forms heterodimeric complexes with CDON enhancing SHH binding affinity compared to either co-receptor alone, BOC-CDON-PTCH1 ternary complex sequesters Hh ligands relieving PTCH1-mediated inhibition of SMO allowing Gli transcription factor activation, during myogenesis BOC expression marks muscle progenitor cells where SHH-BOC signaling drives differentiation through MyoD upregulation, in neural tube BOC patterns ventral cell fates through graded SHH response, CDH15 interaction (0.814 score) links BOC to M-cadherin-mediated myoblast fusion during muscle development, nucleoplasmic/nuclear body localization suggests potential transcriptional co-regulator function beyond membrane signaling, medium AlphaFold confidence (63.4 pLDDT) indicates flexible receptor architecture with structured Ig/FN3 domains connected by disordered linkers enabling conformational changes upon ligand binding, conservation from zebrafish (Q8UVD6, 1032 aa) to mouse (Q6AZB0, 1110 aa) to human (Q9BWV1, 1114 aa) underscores essential role in vertebrate development, ClinVar 3q deletions encompassing BOC cause holoprosencephaly-like phenotypes and muscle defects through disrupted Hh morphogen gradient interpretation.
