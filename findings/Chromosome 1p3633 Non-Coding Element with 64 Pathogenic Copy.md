## Description
LOC105378948 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 1p36.33 (NC_000001.11, positions 1069336-1065634, ~3.7 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 64 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (the highest count observed in this study), all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 1p36.33-36.22 region (variants include 2664871, 1684638, 541215, 160923, 155157, 154687, 154642, 154584, 154548, 154003). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, monosomy) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy) ranging from focal alterations (~700 kb-2 Mb) to whole-arm changes (34+ Mb). The 1p36.33 chromosomal location is clinically exceptional as this region constitutes the 1p36 deletion syndrome critical region, the most common terminal deletion syndrome in humans (occurring in ~1 in 5,000 live births). The region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including SKI (SKI proto-oncogene, TGF-beta signaling antagonist, Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome), PRDM16 (PR/SET domain 16, cardiomyocyte differentiation, dilated cardiomyopathy), GABRD (GABA-A receptor delta subunit, epilepsy, periventricular heterotopia), MMP23B (matrix metalloproteinase 23B), and DFFA (DNA fragmentation factor alpha). Functional hypothesis: LOC105378948 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary controlling expression of neighboring dosage-sensitive genes in the 1p36 critical region, particularly SKI which regulates TGF-beta signaling through SMAD transcription factor inhibition. The extraordinary burden of 64 pathogenic CNVs indicates extreme structural instability and dosage sensitivity in this sub-telomeric region, likely due to replication stress and fragile site characteristics. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency of SKI (leading to craniosynostosis, arachnodactyly, and intellectual disability through dysregulated TGF-beta/BMP signaling), PRDM16 (causing cardiomyopathy through defective cardiac muscle differentiation), or GABRD (causing epilepsy through altered GABAergic inhibition). The 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotype includes moderate-to-severe intellectual disability, speech delay, hypotonia, seizures, distinctive facial features (deep-set eyes, straight eyebrows, midface hypoplasia), and congenital heart defects. The absence of protein-coding features combined with record-breaking ClinVar evidence (64 variants) and neurodevelopmental syndrome association suggests this locus functions as a master regulatory hub controlling brain development, craniofacial morphogenesis, and cardiac formation through long-range gene regulation.
