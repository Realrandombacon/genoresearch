## Description
LBX2 (Ladybird Homeobox 2, also known as Ladybird-like Homeobox 2, UniProt Q6XYB7) is a 198 aa nuclear transcription factor belonging to the LBX family of homeodomain proteins that functions as a DNA-binding transcriptional regulator controlling developmental gene expression programs in liver, intestine, and immune cells through homeodomain-mediated sequence-specific DNA recognition. InterPro analysis reveals canonical homeodomain architecture: Homeodomain (IPR001356/PF00046/SM00389/cd00086, positions 83-147/86-142; GO:0003677 DNA binding), Homeodomain-like superfamily (IPR009057/G3DSA:1.10.10.60/SSF46689, positions 72-145/77-145), and LBX Family Transcription Factors (IPR051892/PTHR24336, positions 42-170). AlphaFold structure shows confident folding (pLDDT 74.4), indicating a stable homeodomain structure suitable for DNA binding. HPA data confirms group-enriched RNA expression with liver enrichment (9.1 nTPM), intestine (5.0 nTPM), stomach (3.0 nTPM), classified as predicted intracellular transcription factor with nucleoplasm and midbody subcellular localization, immune cell enhanced in blood cells. STRING interactions reveal connections to chromatin regulation and structural proteins: WDR54 (0.445, WD Repeat Domain 54 - chromatin-associated), CCDC142 (0.435, Coiled-Coil Domain Containing 142 - ciliary/structural), PCGF1 (0.423, Polycomb Group Ring Finger 1 - core Polycomb Repressive Complex 1 component, E3 ubiquitin ligase). ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 2p13.3-12 copy number losses (chr2:71076472-76368354x1), 2p25.1-q13 deletions (chr2:11504318-111365996x1), 2p25.3-q37.3 copy number gains (chr2:1-243199373, chr2:12771-242783384x3), and single allele deletions/duplications affecting LBX2 dosage. Literature (91 papers) predominantly focuses on LBX2-AS1 lncRNA rather than LBX2 protein: (1) LBX2-AS1 exacerbates LPS-induced periodontal ligament cell stress via TLR2/miR-654-3p axis (2025), (2) LBX2-AS1-miR-491-5p-PKM2 positive feedback loop promotes esophageal cancer radiation resistance through glycolysis enhancement (2025), (3) LBX2-AS1 accelerates colorectal cancer glycolysis and angiogenesis via miR-491-5p/MDK axis (2025), (4) LBX2-AS1 dysregulation in pediatric epilepsy with oxidative stress/inflammation (2025), (5) LBX2 promotes colorectal cancer progression via glycosylation and lactylation positive feedback (2025). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: LBX2 serves as a homeodomain transcription factor that recognizes specific DNA sequences (likely TAAT core motifs characteristic of homeobox proteins) to regulate target gene expression in liver development, intestinal differentiation, and immune cell function. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The homeodomain (positions 83-147) contains three alpha-helices with helix 3 (recognition helix) inserting into the DNA major groove for sequence-specific binding. LBX2 likely regulates developmental genes analogous to Drosophila ladybird genes controlling muscle and CNS development. PCGF1 interaction (STRING 0.423) suggests LBX2 may recruit Polycomb Repressive Complex 1 for transcriptional repression through H2AK119 ubiquitination, linking LBX2 to epigenetic silencing. WDR54 interaction (0.445) suggests chromatin remodeling involvement. Midbody localization indicates potential roles in cell division/cytokinesis regulation. The 13 ClinVar CNVs at 2p suggest LBX2 dosage sensitivity contributes to developmental disorders, potentially through disrupted liver/intestine development or immune dysfunction. Confident AlphaFold structure (74.4) supports stable DNA-binding homeodomain. Despite 91 papers, LBX2 protein function remains understudied compared to literature on LBX2-AS1 lncRNA — the protein represents a moderately dark homeobox transcription factor with emerging roles in cancer metabolism (glycolysis), epilepsy, and chromosomal 2p deletion/duplication syndromes.
