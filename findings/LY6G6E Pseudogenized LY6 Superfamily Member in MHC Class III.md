This co-interaction network suggests co-expression within the MHC region and potential functional redundancy or coordinated regulation.\n\nClinVar reports zero variants, HPA has no expression data, and no human UniProt accession exists — all consistent with pseudogenization in humans. However, the conserved interaction network and cross-species protein existence raise questions about whether LY6G6E represents a true pseudogene or a population-specific functional allele.\n\nFunctional hypothesis: LY6G6E likely underwent pseudogenization in the human lineage after divergence from other mammals. The dense clustering of LY6 genes in the MHC region suggests this locus evolved through gene duplication with subsequent subfunctionalization or pseudogenization.
