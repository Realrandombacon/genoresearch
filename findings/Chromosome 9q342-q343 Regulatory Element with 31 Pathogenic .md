## Description
LOC100506532 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 9q34.2-q34.3 (NC_000009.12, positions 134486267-134545262, ~59 kb region) with exceptional clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000228877 but no expression data was available. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 9q34.13-34.3 region (variants include 3238701, 3238673, 3238671, 3238668, 3238667, 3238666, 3238664, 3238663, 160862, 155409). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, hemizygous) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy) ranging from focal alterations (~200 kb-3 Mb) to whole-arm changes (138+ Mb). The 9q34 chromosomal location is clinically notable as this region is associated with 9q34 duplication syndrome and Kleefstra syndrome (characterized by intellectual disability, distinctive facial features, hypotonia, and behavioral problems). The region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including EHMT1 (euchromatic histone lysine methyltransferase 1, chromatin modification, haploinsufficient, primary driver of Kleefstra syndrome), ABL1 (c-ABL proto-oncogene 1, tyrosine kinase, chronic myeloid leukemia, BCR-ABL fusion), TOR1A (torsin A 1, early-onset torsion dystonia, DYT1), COL5A1 (collagen type V alpha 1, Ehlers-Danlos syndrome), and TUBB (tubulin beta, microtubule cytoskeleton). Functional hypothesis: LOC100506532 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary controlling expression of neighboring dosage-sensitive genes in the 9q34 critical region, particularly EHMT1 which is the primary driver of Kleefstra syndrome through histone methylation and chromatin silencing. The 31 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations disrupt normal transcriptional regulation of chromatin modification and tyrosine kinase signaling genes. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency of EHMT1 (leading to Kleefstra syndrome through H3K9 monomethylation defects), dysregulation of ABL1 (affecting cell proliferation and leukemia susceptibility), or altered TOR1A expression affecting neuronal development and movement disorders. The absence of protein-coding features combined with exceptional ClinVar evidence (31 variants, among highest analyzed) and neurodevelopmental/oncological disease association suggests this locus functions as a master chromatin regulatory hub controlling epigenetic programming, cell cycle control, and neuronal development through long-range gene regulation.
