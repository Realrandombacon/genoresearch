## Description
CLEC4A (C-type Lectin Domain Family 4 Member A, also known as DCIR or Dendritic Cell Inhibitory Receptor) is a 237-amino acid type II transmembrane protein characterized by a canonical C-type lectin-like domain architecture (IPR001304/IPR016186/IPR016187/IPR033989/IPR051379/PF00059/PS00615/PS50041/cd03590/G3DSA:3.10.100.10/PTHR46746, positions 106-232 for CTLD core, 90-237 for mannose-binding protein fold, 66-234 for C-type lectin fold, 1-232 for immune modulator family) containing the carbohydrate recognition domain (CRD) with conserved calcium-binding residues (positions 203-230) mediating pathogen-associated molecular pattern recognition, classified under innate immunity (GO:0045087), adaptive immunity (GO:0002250), and host-virus interaction (GO:0050689). STRING interactions reveal a functional network connecting to immune receptor signaling proteins: LY75 (0.877, lymphocyte antigen 75/DEC-205 endocytic receptor - highest confidence partner indicating shared dendritic cell antigen capture function), LY75-CD302 (0.864, fusion protein), PTPN11 (0.744, protein tyrosine phosphatase non-receptor type 11/SHP-2 phosphatase mediating ITIM signaling), TLR8 (0.717, toll-like receptor 8), TLR2 (0.711), FCER1G (0.693, Fc receptor gamma chain), CLEC7A (0.685, Dectin-1 beta-glucan receptor), SLC2A3 (0.675, glucose transporter 3), ITIH4 (0.651, inter-alpha-trypsin inhibitor heavy chain 4), and TYROBP (0.647, DAP12 signaling adaptor), indicating CLEC4A functions within an integrated pattern recognition receptor network. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (25.3 nTPM) and bone marrow (20.4 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, group-enriched blood cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, classified as a predicted membrane protein and CD marker involved in innate and adaptive immunity. ClinVar contains 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number gains and losses spanning 12p13.33-11.1, 12p13.33-q13.12, and 12p13.33-13.2 regions (duplications and deletions affecting chromosome 12 short arm), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in immune dysregulation and hematologic disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9UMR7-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 84.1) consistent with a well-structured C-type lectin domain containing the characteristic mannose-binding protein fold with calcium-coordinating residues. With 55 papers, CLEC4A represents a 'gray' gene with established immunological function but emerging clinical associations (2020-2025): CLEC4A expression serves as a prognostic biomarker in lung adenocarcinoma correlating with immune cell infiltration patterns; CLEC4A and CLEC12B mediate recognition of Pneumocystis cell wall components linking to opportunistic infection susceptibility; CLEC4A self-interaction through carbohydrate recognition domain elicits ITIM-mediated inhibitory function in murine conventional dendritic cells; differential CLEC4A expression on PBMCs distinguishes early-stage non-small cell lung cancer patients; and porcine tissue expression studies reveal conserved immune regulatory roles. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that CLEC4A functions as an inhibitory immunoreceptor that: (1) uses its CRD to recognize carbohydrate ligands on pathogens (Pneumocystis) and self-antigens triggering conformational changes; (2) signals through cytoplasmic ITIM (immunoreceptor tyrosine-based inhibitory motif) sequences recruiting SHP-2 phosphatase (PTPN11, 0.744 score) to dampen dendritic cell activation and prevent autoimmunity; (3) interacts with LY75/DEC-205 (0.877 score) coordinating antigen capture and presentation balancing tolerance versus immunity; (4) forms homodimers through CRD self-interaction enhancing inhibitory signaling capacity; (5) cooperates with activating receptors (CLEC7A/Dectin-1, TLR2/8, FCER1G) setting activation thresholds for innate immune responses; lymphoid tissue (25.3 nTPM) and bone marrow (20.4 nTPM) enrichment aligns with dendritic cell and myeloid lineage expression, while chromosome 12p CNVs may contribute to immune dysregulation phenotypes including autoimmune disease or cancer susceptibility through dosage effects on inhibitory signaling balance.
