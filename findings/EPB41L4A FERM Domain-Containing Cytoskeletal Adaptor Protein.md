## Description
EPB41L4A encodes a 686 aa band 4.1-like protein 4A (UniProt Q9HCS5), also known as NBL4 (novel band 4.1-like protein 4), functioning as a FERM domain-containing cytoskeletal adaptor protein that links plasma membrane proteins to the actin cytoskeleton and regulates cell migration and polarity. The protein contains a canonical FERM domain (IPR000299, aa11-299) composed of three subdomains: F1 subdomain (cd17107, aa9-102), FERM B-lobe (cd14473, aa111-203), and FERM C-lobe specific to NBL4/5 (cd13186, aa207-296), belonging to the ezrin/radixin/moesin-like family (IPR000798, aa29-48/83-102/126-147) with cytoskeletal protein binding activity (GO:0008092), PH-like domain superfamily (IPR011993, aa206-298), and FERM/acyl-CoA-binding protein superfamily (IPR014352, aa98-205). AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 65.6), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered FERM domains and potentially disordered C-terminal regions typical of cytoskeletal adaptors. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localizing to nucleoplasm and plasma membrane with cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal EPB41L4A associates with membrane and cytoskeletal regulators: TAAR1 (0.813, trace amine-associated receptor 1), PCNX2 (0.494, pecanex-like protein 2), DYNLL2 (0.465, dynein light chain 2), KIAA1755 (0.460, microtubule-associated protein), ALAD (0.451, delta-aminolevulinic acid dehydratase), CCT5 (0.429, chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit 5), MAB21L3 (0.428, male-abnormal 21-like protein 3), ARHGEF37 (0.424, Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor 37), SLC6A7 (0.414, sodium- and chloride-dependent transporter), CTNNB1 (0.409, beta-catenin). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.965+1G>T, c.1933-1G>T), missense variant (p.Arg459Lys), deletions, and multiple 5q15-23.2 copy number losses and gains. Literature (95 papers) primarily focuses on the antisense lncRNA EPB41L4A-AS1 as diagnostic marker for chronic periodontitis, cervical cancer, and sepsis through miR-214-3p/YAP1 and miR-146a-5p axes; the protein-coding gene itself remains less characterized. EPB41L4A functions as a membrane-cytoskeleton linker—its FERM domain binds transmembrane proteins (TAAR1, transporters) while connecting to actin cytoskeleton through ezrin/radixin/moesin-like motifs, regulating cell shape, migration, and polarity. Cancer pathogenesis may reflect EPB41L4A dysregulation: altered expression disrupts membrane-cytoskeleton integrity, enhancing cell migration and invasion through beta-catenin signaling (CTNNB1 interaction). The TAAR1 interaction (highest confidence 0.813) suggests EPB41L4A may regulate GPCR trafficking or signaling at plasma membrane. Copy number variants on 5q may cause EPB41L4A dosage effects contributing to developmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. The nucleoplasm localization indicates potential nuclear functions in transcription regulation or chromatin organization distinct from cytoplasmic cytoskeletal roles.
