## Description
C8orf44-SGK3 (UniProt A0A6I8PL85) represents a naturally occurring readthrough transcription between the neighboring uncharacterized protein C8orf44 (GeneID 56260) and serine/threonine-protein kinase SGK3 (GeneID 23678) on chromosome 8q13.1. The readthrough transcript produces a protein sharing sequence identity with the downstream SGK3 kinase product. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence associations with the SGK signaling pathway: FOXO1 (0.910), NEDD4L (0.809), UBE2G1 (0.776), FOXO4 (0.687), IGF1 (0.606), FOXO3 (0.564), SGK1 (0.517), ASIC1 (0.515), SGK2 (0.445), PDPK1 (0.410), indicating functional integration with FOXO transcription factor regulation and ubiquitination machinery. HPA expression shows intracellular localization to nucleoplasm and vesicles with low tissue specificity but immune cell-enhanced expression. ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including deletions (c.1075-5del, c.979-15del) and large copy number gains/losses on 8p-q regions. AlphaFold structure prediction covers only 32 amino acids with medium confidence (pLDDT 64.2), suggesting this readthrough isoform encodes a truncated peptide rather than full-length kinase domain. HONEST TRIAGE: This locus is NOT a true dark gene — SGK3 is a well-characterized serum/glucocorticoid-regulated kinase involved in ion transport, cell survival, and FOXO phosphorylation. The C8orf44-SGK3 readthrough represents an alternative transcriptional isoform that may produce regulatory peptides or non-coding RNA rather than novel protein function. Research priority should focus on canonical SGK3 (Q9H7F6) rather than this readthrough variant.
