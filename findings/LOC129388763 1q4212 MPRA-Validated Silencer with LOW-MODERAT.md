## Description
LOC129388763 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 1q42.12 (GRCh38: 226882153-226882353), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This locus lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory sequence, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, STRING interaction partners, or Human Protein Atlas expression data (not applicable for non-coding locus).\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region, representing LOW-MODERATE CNV burden (compared to FAM53A: 146 variants, RNF115: 134 variants, LOC101928389: 82 variants, LOC129390867: 53 variants, ZNF500: 30 variants, LOC129389193: 19 variants, LOC129389757: 16 variants, ZNF716: 9 variants, LOC129389639: 8 variants, LOC129390513: 6 variants, LOC129390786: 6 variants, LOC129390507: 4 variants).
