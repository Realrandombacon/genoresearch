## Description
SEMA4G (Semaphorin-4G, UniProt Q9NTN9) is an 838 aa transmembrane protein belonging to the class IV semaphorin family that functions as a guidance cue in neurogenesis and cell differentiation through plexin receptor binding. InterPro analysis reveals canonical semaphorin domain architecture: N-terminal Sema domain (IPR001627, positions 35-505; cd11262, positions 47-506) within WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943, G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 29-506) for ligand-receptor recognition, PSI domain (IPR016201, position 507) for structural stability, Plexin repeat (IPR002165, positions 507-540) for receptor interaction, and C-terminal Immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR003599/IPR007110/IPR013783, positions 567-651; G3DSA:2.60.40.10, positions 569-651) within Ig-like fold superfamily for dimerization. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 79.8), indicating a well-folded transmembrane receptor with stable Sema and Ig domains. HPA data confirms intestine-enriched expression (26.1 nTPM, tissue enhanced), detected in many tissues, vesicles/lipid droplets localization, classified as predicted intracellular/membrane protein involved in differentiation and neurogenesis. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with plexin-B receptors: PLXNB2 (0.978, primary receptor), PLXNB1 (0.943), PLXNB3 (0.939), plus PLXNC1 (0.608), PLXND1 (0.553), and signaling adaptors DLG4 (0.496, PSD-95 synaptic scaffold). ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 10q23.1-26.3 affecting the SEMA4G locus. Literature (31 papers) establishes SEMA4G roles in: (1) thyroid carcinoma susceptibility via rs4919510 polymorphism (2023, 3 citations), (2) granulosa cell proliferation regulation in Yunshang black goats via miR-363-5p targeting (2023, 2 citations), (3) neurogenesis and axon guidance during neural development, and (4) intestinal epithelial differentiation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SEMA4G serves as a transmembrane semaphorin ligand that binds plexin-B receptors (PLXNB1/2/3) to transduce repulsive guidance signals regulating axon pathfinding, cell migration, and tissue differentiation through Rho GTPase signaling cascades. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The Sema domain mediates high-affinity binding to plexin-B receptor extracellular domains, inducing receptor dimerization and intracellular domain activation. The Ig-like domain stabilizes SEMA4G homodimerization and receptor clustering. Plexin-B activation triggers RhoA/ROCK signaling for cytoskeletal reorganization, growth cone collapse, and cell migration inhibition. In thyroid carcinoma, SEMA4G rs4919510 polymorphism alters expression or function, modifying cancer susceptibility. In ovarian biology, miR-363-5p post-transcriptionally represses SEMA4G, controlling granulosa cell proliferation during folliculogenesis. Intestinal enrichment suggests SEMA4G regulates epithelial cell positioning and crypt-villus axis maintenance. The 15 ClinVar CNVs indicate SEMA4G dosage sensitivity may contribute to neurodevelopmental disorders or cancer predisposition. Class IV semaphorin status positions SEMA4G as a bidirectional signaling molecule affecting both ligand-expressing and receptor-expressing cells.
