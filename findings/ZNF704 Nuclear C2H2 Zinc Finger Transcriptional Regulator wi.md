## Description
ZNF704 (Gene ID: 619279, UniProt Q6ZNC4, alias Gig1) is a 412 amino acid zinc finger protein located on chromosome 8q21.13 (NC_000008.11, positions 80884418-80628450, ~256kb region on reverse strand). InterPro domain analysis reveals a C2H2-type zinc finger domain (IPR013087, positions 193-223; PS00028/PS50157) characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors, membership in the CR1/CR2 motif-containing transcription regulator family (IPR052253/PTHR13006, positions 4-412), a C-terminal domain (PF27556, positions 384-412), and a c-clamp domain (SM01366, positions 382-412) that may mediate additional DNA or protein interactions. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q6ZNC4-F1) shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 57.8), indicating a partially structured protein with intrinsically disordered regions typical of transcriptional regulators that undergo conformational changes during complex assembly. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF704 as a predicted intracellular protein localized to nucleoplasm, with functions in transcription and transcription regulation through DNA-binding. Expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues and cell type-enhanced patterns, suggesting ubiquitous but regulated expression. STRING database identifies 10 high-to-medium confidence protein-protein interactions with chromatin remodeling and transcriptional repression complex components: ING2 (score 0.796), FAU (0.796), SAP30 (0.701), ARID4A (0.697), SUDS3 (0.692), RPL21 (0.689), SIN3A (0.680), SAP30L (0.628), SAP130 (0.624), and BRMS1 (0.623). These partners are core components of SIN3/HDAC histone deacetylase complexes involved in transcriptional repression through chromatin modification. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains affecting 8q21 region including duplications (GRCh37/hg19 8p21.2-q21.3 chr8:27024288-89410121x3, 8p23.3-q24.3 chr8:158048-146295771x3, 8q21.12-22.3 chr8:79046933-102008860x3, 8q21.12-24.11 chr8:79409349-119040631x3, 8q13.2-24.3 chr8:68912432-146295771x2, 8q13.2-24.3 chr8:70382990-146295771) and deletions (8q21.11-21.3 chr8:77906471-88917707, 8q21.11-21.13 chr8:75197438-81685526, 8q21.11-21.3 chr8:75904944-87097083x1). Conservation across vertebrates is evidenced by orthologs in mouse (Q9ERQ3, 566 aa), zebrafish (Q1LY51, 501 aa), medaka (A0A3B3INZ5, 513 aa), and cod (A0A8C5D4Y7, 547 aa). Functional hypothesis: ZNF704 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor that recruits SIN3/HDAC chromatin remodeling complexes through interactions with SIN3A, SAP30, SAP30L, SAP130, and ARID4A. The C2H2 zinc finger mediates DNA recognition at specific promoter/enhancer sequences, while the CR1/CR2 motifs and c-clamp domain facilitate protein-protein interactions within repressive complexes. This leads to histone deacetylation and chromatin compaction, silencing target genes involved in development, differentiation, or cell cycle control. Disease mechanism: Dosage sensitivity of ZNF704 contributes to 8q21 duplication/deletion syndrome phenotypes. Overexpression from duplications may cause excessive transcriptional repression of developmental genes, while haploinsufficiency from deletions may lead to inappropriate gene activation. The 8q21.13 region's involvement in multiple CNV syndromes suggests ZNF704 dysregulation contributes to developmental delay, intellectual disability, growth abnormalities, and congenital anomalies observed in patients. Priority experiments include identification of DNA binding targets via ChIP-seq, characterization of SIN3/HDAC complex recruitment mechanisms, assessment of target gene expression changes upon ZNF704 knockdown/overexpression, and investigation of ZNF704's role in specific developmental contexts.
