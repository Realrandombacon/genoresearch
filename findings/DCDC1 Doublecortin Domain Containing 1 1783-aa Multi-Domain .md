## Description

UniProt accession M0R2J8 identifies it as a massive 1783-amino acid intracellular protein. Additionally, DCDC1 contains a Ricin B-type lectin domain with a beta-trefoil fold (cd23427, 1032-1267), suggesting potential carbohydrate-binding or protein-protein interaction capabilities.
