# KCTD8: A BTB/POZ Domain Scaffold Protein Regulating GABAB Receptor Trafficking in Habenula Neurons and Hepatocellular Carcinoma Suppression via PI3K/AKT Signaling

**Date:** 2026-06-28T13:38:22.446610

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
KCTD8 (Potassium Channel Tetramerization Domain Containing 8) is a 473-amino acid BTB/POZ domain-containing scaffold protein characterized by an N-terminal BTB/POZ domain (IPR000210/PF02214/SM00225, positions 44-154) mediating protein-protein interactions and homooligomerization, a potassium channel tetramerisation-type BTB domain (IPR003131, 46-142) within the SKP1/BTB/POZ superfamily (IPR011333/SSF54695, 41-158), and a C-terminal H1 domain (IPR049904/IPR057093/PF23110, 207-321) specific to the KCTD8/12/16 subfamily, classified under the BTB/POZ domain-containing family (PTHR14499, 42-316). STRING interactions reveal functional connections to GABAergic signaling machinery: GABBR1 (0.543, GABA-B receptor subunit 1), GABRG1 (0.482, GABA-A receptor subunit), alongside other KCTD family members KCTD11 (0.680), KCTD20 (0.538), KCTD19 (0.532), KCTD2 (0.458), the E3 ubiquitin ligase scaffold CUL3 (0.578) suggesting potential role in ubiquitination complexes, CEP162 (0.574, centriole protein), BTBD10 (0.481), and SSTR3 (0.460, somatostatin receptor 3). HPA data shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in brain (16.9 nTPM) and retina (6.3 nTPM), cancer-enhanced specificity, not detected in immune cells, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, and subcellular localization to cytoplasmic bodies, classified as predicted intracellular and membrane protein. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains spanning 4p16.3-11, 4p16.3-q12, and 4p16.3-q35.2 regions, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in neurodevelopmental disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q6ZWB6-F1) predicts medium confidence folding (pLDDT 66.0) consistent with a partially structured scaffold protein containing ordered BTB and H1 domains coupled with flexible linker regions. With only 25 papers, KCTD8 represents a 'gray' gene transitioning from dark: breakthrough studies identify critical roles in facilitating axonal expression of GABAB receptors in habenula cholinergic neurons (controlling aversive memory expression), epigenetic silencing promoting hepatocellular carcinoma growth via PI3K/AKT pathway activation, and association with brain growth in adverse intrauterine environments via GWAS. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that KCTD8 functions as a multi-domain scaffold protein that oligomerizes via its BTB/POZ domain to form heteromeric complexes with other KCTD family members (particularly KCTD12/16), recruiting CUL3 E3 ubiquitin ligase machinery to regulate GABAB receptor surface expression and trafficking in specific neuronal populations (habenula cholinergic neurons), while simultaneously modulating PI3K/AKT signaling through protein-protein interactions that control cell proliferation; loss of KCTD8 function leads to reduced GABAB receptor axonal localization impairing presynaptic inhibition and aversive memory processing, combined with hyperactivated PI3K/AKT signaling promoting tumorigenesis in hepatocytes, making it a potential therapeutic target for both neuropsychiatric disorders and liver cancer.
