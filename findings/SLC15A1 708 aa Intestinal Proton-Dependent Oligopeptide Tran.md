## Description
SLC15A1 (P46059, 708 aa), also known as PEPT1 or HPEPT1, is a proton-dependent oligopeptide transporter belonging to the solute carrier family 15 (SLC15/POT family) within the Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) (PTHR11662).
