## Description
LOC101928272 (Gene ID: 101928272) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 10p14 (NC_000010.11, positions 9197365-9295592, ~98 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 5 duplications/gains (variant 154187: 10p15.3-13 ~13.2 Mb; variant 154099: 10p15.3-14 ~7.6 Mb; variant 149407: 10p15.3-12.1 ~26.3 Mb; variant 146220: 10p15.3-14 ~11.6 Mb; variant 146014: 10p15.3-13 ~12.7 Mb; variant 59657: 10p15.3-12.31 ~19.1 Mb) and 5 deletions/losses (variant 155636: 10p15.3-12.31 ~19.3 Mb; variant 150048: 10p15.3-13 ~13.7 Mb; variant 148232: 10p14-12.31 ~14.2 Mb; variant 58717: 10p15.1-13 ~12.0 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 10p15.3-14 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including GATA3 (GATA binding protein 3, T-cell development, Th2 cell differentiation, mammary gland development, HDR syndrome, breast cancer), PTK7 (protein tyrosine kinase 7, Wnt signaling, neural tube closure, colorectal cancer), PRDM12 (PR/SET domain 12, pain sensation, congenital indifference to pain), and MGMT (O-6-methylguanine-DNA methyltransferase, DNA repair, temozolomide resistance in glioblastoma). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928272 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 10p15.3-14 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a T-cell/Thymus-specific enhancer coordinating GATA3 expression during T-cell development (Th2 lineage commitment, IL-4/IL-5/IL-13 production, allergic response regulation) or as a chromatin boundary separating GATA transcription factor genes (GATA3) from DNA repair genes (MGMT) and Wnt signaling genes (PTK7). The 10 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple immune development and DNA repair programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves GATA3 haploinsufficiency causing HDR syndrome (hypoparathyroidism, sensorineural deafness, renal anomalies, T-cell immunodeficiency), GATA3 overexpression promoting breast cancer progression (luminal B subtype, ER+ breast cancer, tamoxifen resistance), PTK7 dysfunction causing neural tube defects (spina bifida, anencephaly, Wnt/PCP pathway disruption), PRDM12 loss causing congenital indifference to pain (nociceptor development failure, self-mutilation, corneal scarring), and MGMT silencing causing temozolomide sensitivity in glioblastoma (DNA alkylation damage accumulation, tumor cell death). The 10p15.3 deletion syndrome and 10p15.3 duplication syndrome may present with immunodeficiency, hypoparathyroidism, deafness, and renal anomalies (HDR syndrome phenotype). The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (10 CNVs ranging 7.6-26.3 Mb) and GATA3-PTK7-PRDM12-MGMT gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for T-cell development-DNA repair-Wnt signaling coordination.
