## Description
ASMTL (UniProt: O95671, aliases ASMTLX/ASMTLY/ASTML) encodes a 621 amino acid bifunctional enzyme located in the pseudoautosomal region 1 (PAR1) of X and Y chromosomes (Xp22.33-Yp11.32), representing a dark gene with only 35 publications in Semantic Scholar despite emerging disease relevance. InterPro analysis reveals a modular architecture supporting dual enzymatic functions: N-terminal Maf-like domain (cd00555, residues 15-208; IPR003697, GO:0047429 nucleoside triphosphate diphosphatase activity) for nucleotide hydrolysis, winged helix DNA-binding domain (G3DSA:1.10.10.10, residues 284-365), dimerization domain (IPR012967, residues 289-367, GO:0046983 protein dimerization activity), and C-terminal methyltransferase domain (cd02440, residues 457-559; IPR001077, GO:0008171 O-methyltransferase activity; IPR016461, GO:0008168 methyltransferase activity) homologous to vaccinia virus VP39 (G3DSA:3.40.50.150). The protein is predicted to hydrolyze dTTP and UTP while possessing weak methyltransferase activity. AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 87.3), indicating a well-folded stable bifunctional enzyme with ordered catalytic domains. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 protein partners: SLC25A6 (adenine nucleotide translocator, score 0.890), TDO2 (tryptophan 2,3-dioxygenase, 0.793), PLCXD1 (phosphatidylinositol-specific phospholipase C X domain containing 1, 0.743), EIF1AX (eukaryotic translation initiation factor 1A X-linked, 0.670), NR1I2 (pregnane X receptor, 0.584), DHRSX (dehydrogenase/reductase SDR family member X, 0.477), P2RY8 (P2Y purinoceptor 8, 0.451), ZBED1 (zinc finger BED-type containing 1, 0.444), AKAP17A (A-kinase anchoring protein 17A, 0.409), and GTPBP6 (GTP binding protein 6, 0.405). These interactions link ASMTL to nucleotide transport (SLC25A6), tryptophan metabolism (TDO2), phospholipid signaling (PLCXD1), translation initiation (EIF1AX), xenobiotic response (NR1I2), and other PAR1-localized proteins (P2RY8, ZBED1, AKAP17A). Human Protein Atlas shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in all tissues, cytosolic localization, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (Xp22.33-11.4) and multiple single nucleotide variants (p.Arg113Gln, splice site variants), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Notably, ASMTL hosts the antisense lncRNA ASMTL-AS1 in its intron, which is extensively studied in cancer: ASMTL-AS1 induces ferroptosis in osteosarcoma by targeting TYRO3 (2025), serves as a biomarker in colorectal cancer (2024), inhibits renal cell carcinoma growth via HuR binding (2023), and is a potential Alzheimer's disease biomarker (2023). Based on domain architecture (bifunctional pyrophosphatase/methyltransferase), interaction partners (nucleotide and metabolic regulators), high-confidence AlphaFold structure, cytosolic localization, and lncRNA associations, ASMTL likely functions as a bifunctional enzyme regulating nucleotide pool balance through dTTP/UTP hydrolysis and potentially methylating nucleic acid substrates, while its genomic locus produces the cancer- and neurodegeneration-associated lncRNA ASMTL-AS1. The PAR1 location suggests dosage-sensitive functions in both sexes, potentially explaining pathogenic copy number variants. The Maf-like domain may also confer DNA-binding capacity for transcriptional autoregulation or chromatin interactions.
