## Description
LHX4 (LIM Homeobox Protein 4; UniProt Q969G2) is a 390 amino acid essential nuclear transcription factor with 280 publications, representing a PARTIALLY DARK gene encoding a critical regulator of pituitary gland development and multiple pituitary hormone deficiency (MPHD). Despite its established role in endocrine development—a process critical for growth, metabolism, and reproduction—LHX4 remains incompletely characterized in human cells regarding its precise DNA target specificity beyond consensus homeobox motifs, dynamic cofactor recruitment mechanisms, and the full spectrum of downstream hormonal pathways it regulates.
