## Description
ANKRD23 (Ankyrin Repeat Domain 23, aliases DARP, MARP3) is a 305 amino acid nuclear protein belonging to the muscle ankyrin repeat protein (MARP) family, functioning as a transcriptional regulator induced during recovery following starvation. The protein contains four tandem ankyrin-like repeats (residues 45-300) classified as Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box family (InterPro: IPR050663, IPR002110, IPR036770; Pfam: PF00023, PF12796; SMART: SM00248; PTHR24193), with GO annotation for protein binding (GO:0005515). Ankyrin repeats mediate protein-protein interactions through curved solenoid structures, enabling ANKRD23 to recruit transcriptional co-regulators or chromatin modifiers to target gene promoters.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals 10 high-confidence partners enriched for sarcomeric and muscle structural proteins: TTN (titin, score 0.926 - highest confidence), MYPN (myopalladin, 0.808), ANKRD39 (ankyrin repeat domain 39, 0.773 - paralog), CAPN3 (calpain-3, 0.678), MYBPC2 (myosin-binding protein C2, 0.672), PPP1R27 (protein phosphatase 1 regulatory subunit 27, 0.604), ACTN3 (alpha-actinin-3, 0.592), TMOD4 (tropomodulin-4, 0.590), MYBPC3 (myosin-binding protein C3, 0.587), and RPL3L (ribosomal protein L3-like, 0.448). This interaction profile strongly suggests ANKRD23 functions within the sarcomeric Z-disk/I-band complex, potentially linking structural proteins to transcriptional regulation through mechanotransduction pathways.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas shows GROUP ENHANCED tissue specificity with skeletal muscle dominance (1347.5 nTPM - very high expression) and secondary expression in tongue (418.8 nTPM - striated muscle). Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm (consistent with transcriptional regulator function), actin filaments, and cytosol. Cancer specificity is enriched, suggesting dysregulation in muscle-derived tumors (rhabdomyosarcoma, leiomyosarcoma). The MARP family includes ANKRD1 (MARP1), ANKRD2 (MARP2), and ANKRD23 (MARP3/DARP) - all stress-inducible transcriptional co-regulators responding to muscle damage, starvation, and mechanical stress.\n\nClinVar analysis identifies 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting ANKRD23, representing MODERATE-HIGH CNV burden (compared to LOC129390381: 40 variants, FAM53A: 146 variants, RNF115: 134 variants). Variant spectrum includes both deletions (4796325, 4755375, 3024612, 1047904, 997821 - all x1 losses spanning 2q11.1-11.2, ~2-7Mb genomic spans) and duplications (4682540, 2685857, 1808615, 1703543 - x3 gains spanning 2p11.2-q11.2 through 2q11.1-11.2, ~5-240Mb spans). The deletion:duplication ratio is approximately 50:50, suggesting balanced dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency (50% activity) and triplosufficiency (200% activity) are pathogenic. This contrasts with LOC129390381 (~80% deletions) and LOC129388831 (~80% gains), indicating ANKRD23 requires precise dosage homeostasis.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ANKRD23 functions as a muscle-specific transcriptional co-regulator within the MARP stress-response pathway, integrating mechanical and metabolic signals to regulate sarcomeric gene expression. The mechanism likely involves: (1) ankyrin repeat-mediated protein-protein interactions with TTN, MYPN, and other Z-disk components, positioning ANKRD23 at the sarcomere-nucleus interface; (2) starvation/stress induction via DARP (Diabetes-Associated Retinopathy Protein) pathway linking metabolic state to muscle gene expression; (3) recruitment of chromatin modifiers (HDACs, HATs, or methyltransferases) to muscle-specific promoters through SOCS box-mediated E3 ubiquitin ligase adaptor activity; (4) mechanotransduction signaling where muscle contraction/stretch alters ANKRD23 localization or activity, coupling physical stress to transcriptional adaptation. The balanced CNV sensitivity (50:50 deletion:duplication) suggests ANKRD23 dosage must be precisely maintained - both underactivity (haploinsufficiency) and overactivity (triplosufficiency) disrupt muscle transcriptional homeostasis, potentially causing myopathy, cardiomyopathy, or metabolic myopathy phenotypes. High skeletal muscle expression (1347.5 nTPM) and interaction with sarcomeric proteins (TTN, MYPN, ACTN3, MYBPC2/3) support a role in Z-disk-to-nucleus signaling analogous to ANKRD1/2 stress-responsive pathways. Cancer enrichment suggests ANKRD23 dysregulation in rhabdomyosarcoma or other muscle tumors through transcriptional reprogramming.
