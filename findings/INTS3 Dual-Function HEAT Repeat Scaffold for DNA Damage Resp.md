## Description
INTS3 encodes a 1043 aa integrator complex subunit (UniProt Q68E01) functioning as a dual-purpose scaffold protein in both DNA damage repair and RNA processing pathways. The protein contains an N-terminal integrator complex domain (IPR019333/PF10189, aa85-492) and C-terminal HEAT repeat region (IPR056518/PF24566, aa583-974) forming an elongated scaffold architecture, plus INTS3 family signatures (IPR045334/PTHR13587, aa35-1001). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 83.1), indicating a well-folded HEAT repeat solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. HPA data unavailable, but literature indicates ubiquitous expression. STRING interactions reveal dual complex membership: SOSS1 DNA repair complex (NABP2/hSSB1 0.999, NABP1/hSSB2 0.999, INIP/SOSSC 0.999) and Integrator snRNA processing complex (INTS7/5/1/2/10/13 all 0.999, INTS6 0.578). ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic variants including 1q21 copy number gains and splice site mutations, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (41 papers) demonstrates INTS3 dimerization is critical for DSB repair, forms heterotrimeric SOSS1 complex binding ssDNA at break sites, recruits ATM/ATR kinases for checkpoint activation, and independently functions in Integrator complex for RNA Pol II CTD interaction and U1/U2 snRNA 3'-end processing. INTS3 likely functions as a molecular switch between DNA repair and transcription regulation—during genotoxic stress, INTS3 prioritizes SOSS1 complex assembly for homologous recombination repair, while under normal conditions participates in Integrator-mediated snRNA maturation. The HEAT repeat architecture enables simultaneous binding of multiple partners (hSSB1/2, INTS subunits), coordinating genome stability maintenance with RNA processing fidelity. Copy number variations at 1q21 may disrupt this balance, contributing to cancer susceptibility through impaired DNA repair capacity.
