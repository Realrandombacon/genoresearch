Disease mechanism: Haploinsufficiency from 6p21 deletions disrupts LOC401261 alongside neighboring MHC genes, potentially contributing to immunodeficiency, autoimmunity, or neurodevelopmental phenotypes observed in 6p deletion syndromes.
