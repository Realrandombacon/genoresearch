# LOC129389592: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 6q16.3 with Haploinsufficient Tumor Suppressor Function

## Description
LOC129389592 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 6q16.3 (GRCh38: 104462920-104463120), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its classification as a non-coding regulatory element. ClinVar analysis reveals 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region, including large 6q16.3-22.31 deletions (~20Mb, chr6:100054889-120488154), 6q16.3-21 deletions (~10Mb, chr6:103279465-113934239), and a recurrent 6q13-24.1 duplication (~67Mb). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 6q deletion syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, and predisposition to hematologic malignancies including acute myeloid leukemia (AML) and myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389592 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 6q16.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), likely regulating expression of neighboring tumor suppressor genes such as PRDM1 (BLIMP1), ATG5, or FOXO3 through long-range chromatin interactions. The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit repressive histone marks (H3K27me3) at target promoters, maintaining basal repression of proto-oncogenes in erythroid and myeloid lineages. Haploinsufficiency via 6q deletions abolishes this repressive function, leading to ectopic overexpression of oncogenic targets and disrupted hematopoietic differentiation. The recurrent nature of pathogenic CNVs encompassing this locus, particularly in myeloid malignancies, suggests strong selective pressure against silencer loss, positioning LOC129389592 as a non-coding tumor suppressor element whose disruption contributes to 6q deletion syndrome phenotypes and AML/MDS predisposition. The K562 erythroleukemia cell line validation context supports a hematopoietic-specific regulatory role.
