## Description
ZNF266 encodes a 549 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q14584) belonging to the PTHR24384 family of C2H2-type zinc-finger domain-containing proteins (residues 256-546). The protein contains multiple characteristic structural domains including IPR001909 (KRAB domain, positions 1-42, associated with GO:0006355 regulation of DNA-templated transcription), IPR013087 (C2H2-type zinc finger, positions 156-183/184-211/212-239), PF00096 (C2H2 zinc finger, positions 212-234/240-262/268-290), and G3DSA:3.30.160.60 (classic zinc finger homologous superfamily, positions 72-145/148-206/207-236). The KRAB domain at the N-terminus is a well-characterized transcriptional repression module that recruits co-repressor complexes including TRIM28/KAP1, suggesting ZNF266 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The AlphaFold predicted structure displays medium confidence folding (global pLDDT 68.6) across the full-length 549 aa protein, consistent with a partially structured protein containing both ordered zinc finger domains and flexible linker regions typical of KRAB-ZFP family members. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF266 as a predicted intracellular transcription factor with nucleoplasmic localization plus additional detection in vesicles and rods & rings structures, low tissue specificity, and expression detected in all tissues examined, indicating ubiquitous regulatory functions. STRING interaction network identifies 9 functional partners revealing potential biological roles: CCDC163 (score 0.594, coiled-coil domain containing 163), ZPR1 (0.559, zinc finger protein 1, involved in ribosome biogenesis and cell proliferation), OR4F3 (0.511, olfactory receptor family 4 subfamily F member 3), TRIM28 (0.507, tripartite motif containing 28/KAP1, critical KRAB-ZFP co-repressor that mediates transcriptional silencing through heterochromatin formation), TRIM41 (0.474, tripartite motif containing 41, E3 ubiquitin ligase), GPR15 (0.437, G protein-coupled receptor 15), SP140L (0.427, SP140 like, nuclear body protein), ZNF559 (0.422, zinc finger protein 559, another KRAB-ZFP), and CLLU1OS (0.414, CLL upregulated 1 opposite strand). The TRIM28 interaction is particularly significant as it confirms ZNF266's role in the canonical KRAB-ZFP repression pathway where TRIM28 recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 to establish repressive chromatin marks. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains spanning chromosome 19p13 regions (chr19:3120160-9732820 x3, chr19:260912-58956888 x3, chr19:8831147-13331227 x3, chr19:1972245-9648879 x3) plus one missense variant NM_001370374.1:c.26G>T (p.Gly9Val) within the KRAB domain that may disrupt co-repressor recruitment, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and overexpression disrupt transcriptional regulation programs. The chromosomal location at 19p13.2-13.3 overlaps with regions associated with developmental disorders and intellectual disability. Given the KRAB domain architecture, TRIM28 interaction, nuclear localization, and chromosome 19p13 CNV-associated pathogenicity, ZNF266 likely functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor regulating gene expression programs during development, with dosage imbalance contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through disrupted epigenetic silencing mechanisms.
