## Description
EIF1AY (eukaryotic translation initiation factor 1A Y-linked) is a 144-amino acid protein encoded on the non-recombining region of chromosome Y (Yq11.223). This gene represents the Y-chromosome homolog of the X-linked EIF1AX and autosomal EIF1A, forming part of the essential translation initiation machinery. InterPro analysis reveals conserved domains including PF01176 (Translation initiation factor 1A/IF-1, positions 32-93), IPR001253 (eIF-1A family), IPR006196 (RNA-binding domain S1/IF1 type, positions 22-96), and an OB-fold nucleic acid-binding superfamily (G3DSA:2.40.50.140). The protein functions in stabilizing initiator Met-tRNA binding to 40S ribosomal subunits during translation initiation. STRING interaction network identifies 10 high-confidence partners: USP9Y (0.999), DDX3Y (0.999), EIF1 (0.987), RPS18 (0.982), RPS15 (0.982), RPS4Y1 (0.681), EIF1B (0.672), RPS3 (0.652), KDM5D (0.510), and RPS11 (0.506), revealing tight clustering with Y-chromosome genes (USP9Y, DDX3Y, RPS4Y1, KDM5D) and core translation machinery. HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced pattern with highest levels in tongue (122.8 nTPM) and heart muscle (113.5 nTPM), cytosolic localization. ClinVar contains 71 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly Y-chromosome copy number losses affecting the AZF (azoospermia factor) region, consistent with literature linking EIF1AY deletion to non-obstructive azoospermia and recent findings (2026) connecting EIF1AY loss to sex differences in multiple myeloma incidence/mortality. Only 81 total papers exist for this gene—substantially fewer than well-characterized genes—confirming its understudied status despite clinical importance.
