## Description
TCEA3 encodes a 348 aa transcription elongation factor A protein 3 (UniProt O75764), functioning as a TFIIS homolog that stimulates RNA polymerase II transcriptional elongation by preventing arrest and promoting readthrough of pause sites through transcript cleavage activation. The protein contains a TFIIS/I domain (cd00183, aa4-80) for Pol II binding, a TFIIS-type zinc finger domain (IPR001222, aa306-347) with zinc ion binding capability for nucleic acid interaction, a TFIIS N-terminal domain (IPR003617, aa7-81), a central S-II domain (IPR003618, aa183-303) for elongation stimulation, and belongs to TFIIS family (IPR006289, aa5-348). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 74.1), indicating a well-folded protein with stable domain architecture suitable for Pol II interaction. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with exceptionally high levels in skeletal muscle (446.3 nTPM—one of the highest tissue-specific expressions observed), localized to nucleoplasm and vesicles, classified as DNA-binding protein involved in transcription regulation. STRING interactions reveal TCEA3 forms the core transcription elongation machinery: POLR2I (0.963, RNA Pol II subunit I - highest confidence), POLR2B (0.956, Pol II subunit 2), SUPT5H (0.956, SPT5 transcription elongation factor - DSIF complex), POLR2A (0.935, Pol II largest subunit RPB1), POLR2D (0.927, Pol II subunit 4), POLR2E (0.923, Pol II subunit 5), POLR2C (0.922, Pol II subunit 3), POLR2K (0.902, Pol II subunit 11), GTF2B (0.840, general transcription factor IIB), SUPT4H1 (0.794, SPT4 homolog). ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including c.819+271A>G and c.820-10T>A splice region variants, 1p36.33-35.1 deletion, and 1p36.33-q44 copy number gains, though no specific disease is yet assigned. Literature (31 papers) documents TCEA3 roles in: (1) gastric cancer suppression via linc01105/miR-650/TCEA3 axis where linc01105 inhibits miR-650 to upregulate TCEA3, suppressing growth and metastasis; (2) osteosarcoma where natural product Eriocalyxin B downregulates TCEA3 to exert anti-tumor effects and sensitize immune checkpoint blockade; (3) lung cancer where tRF-Leu-CAG (tRNA-derived fragment) targets TCEA3 to promote tumorigenesis and autophagy; (4) cardiomyocyte hypertrophy as a novel anti-hypertrophy gene involved in fatty acid oxidation and oxidative stress regulation. TCEA3 functions as a transcription elongation factor—the TFIIS-type zinc finger domain (aa306-347) coordinates zinc ions for structural stability and nucleic acid binding, inserting into the Pol II secondary channel to activate intrinsic transcript cleavage when Pol II arrests. The N-terminal domain (aa7-81) and central domain (aa183-303) mediate Pol II interaction and elongation stimulation. Ultra-high confidence interactions with POLR2A-I subunits (0.922-0.963) confirm direct Pol II binding, while SUPT5H (0.956) and SUPT4H1 (0.794) connections place TCEA3 in the DSIF (DRB sensitivity-inducing factor) elongation complex that regulates pause-release. Exceptional skeletal muscle expression (446.3 nTPM) suggests specialized roles in muscle-specific transcription programs—potentially regulating genes for contractile proteins, metabolism, or muscle development. The anti-hypertrophy function in cardiomyocytes reflects TCEA3's role in maintaining transcriptional homeostasis under stress; TCEA3 loss may cause Pol II arrest at stress-response genes, altering cardiac gene expression programs. Cancer context-dependence (tumor suppressor in gastric cancer, oncogene in osteosarcoma/lung cancer) reflects tissue-specific transcriptional programs—TCEA3 may differentially regulate proliferation vs. differentiation genes. The linc01105/miR-650/TCEA3 and tRF-Leu-CAG/TCEA3 axes represent multi-layer post-transcriptional regulation of elongation capacity. ClinVar variants on 1p36 may cause TCEA3 dosage effects contributing to developmental abnormalities or transcription-related disorders.
