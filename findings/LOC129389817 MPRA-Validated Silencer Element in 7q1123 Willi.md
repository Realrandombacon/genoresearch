## Description
LOC129389817 (Gene ID: 129389817) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 7q11.23 (76,955,098-76,955,298 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak6614 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 7q11.22-21.11, 7q11.23-21.11, and 7p22.3-q36.3 regions (deletions 3.8-105 Mb, duplications 7.0 Mb), associated with Williams-Beuren syndrome (WBS, 7q11.23 microdeletion syndrome) and 7q11.23 duplication syndrome. WBS features: supravalvular aortic stenosis, distinctive facial features ("elfin" facies), intellectual disability, hypersocial personality, and connective tissue abnormalities. The 7q11.23 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including ELN (elastin, haploinsufficiency causes supravalvular aortic stenosis and cutis laxa), LIMK1 (LIM domain kinase 1, actin cytoskeleton regulation, visuospatial cognition), RFC2 (replication factor C subunit 2, DNA replication), HIP2 (huntingtin interacting protein 2, ubiquitination), and multiple neural development genes; (4) No HPA expression (non-coding - Ensembl ID could not be resolved); (5) No STRING interactions (non-coding). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129389817 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or neural crest contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in erythroid/myeloid lineage. The 7q11.23 region forms a topologically associating domain (TAD) with extensive long-range chromatin interactions critical for neural crest and cardiovascular development. Pathogenic 7q11.23 CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and TAD architecture, causing long-range dysregulation of ELN, LIMK1, RFC2, and HIP2. ELN haploinsufficiency causes vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation and arterial stenosis. LIMK1 loss affects actin dynamics and visuospatial cognition. WBS occurs in ~1:7,500-20,000 live births. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions.
