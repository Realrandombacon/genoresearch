Recent 2023-2024 studies established PLCD3 as an inhibitor of apoptosis promoting proliferation, invasion, and migration in gastric cancer [2024, 4 cites], a driver of glycolytic reprogramming via PKC-Rap1 activation in lung cancer [2024, 2 cites], a target of circ_0003747/miR-338-3p axis in thyroid cancer progression [2023, 14 cites], and a component of cuproptosis signatures predicting immune infiltration in osteosarcoma [2023, 15 cites], yet critical mechanistic gaps persist: (1) The specific substrate selectivity and regulatory mechanisms distinguishing PLCD3 from paralogs PLCD1 and PLCD4 remain uncharacterized despite very high-confidence STRING interactions with Protein Kinase C alpha PRKCA (score 0.925), Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor type 3 ITPR3 (0.921, calcium release channel), Inositol polyphosphate 5-phosphatase A INPP5A (0.914), Inositol polyphosphate multikinase IPMK (0.912), and family members PLCD1 (0.911)/PLCD4 (0.910), suggesting tight coupling within the phosphoinositide signaling cascade;
