PABPC5 (Poly(A) binding protein cytoplasmic 5, alias PABP5) is a 382 amino acid predicted intracellular protein (UniProt Q96DU9) with very-high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 91.2) containing canonical poly(A) binding protein architecture with four RNA recognition motif (RRM) domains including RRM1 (residues 22-98), RRM2 (residues 104-179), RRM3 (residues 198-277), RRM4 (residues 303-374), and PABP family domain (residues 18-379), functioning as an ovary-enhanced cytoplasmic poly(A) binding protein that regulates mRNA stability and translation initiation through RNA binding (GO:0003723), nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), and cytoplasmic localization via partnerships with TGIF2LX, TGIF2LY, PAIP1, EIF4G1, TGIF2, PCDH11X, EIF4E, PAIP2, EIF4A1, and PABPN1.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals PABPC5 contains canonical poly(A) binding protein architecture with four tandem RRM domains: RNA recognition motif 1 (RRM1) found in type I polyadenylate-binding proteins (cd12378/IPR034364, residues 22-98) forming a 77 aa structured module (~20% of protein) for poly(A) tail recognition, RRM2 (cd12379, residues 104-179) forming a 76 aa module (~20%) for surface, RNA recognition motif domain (IPR000504, residues 18-96, 106-182, 199-276) providing the conserved RNP1/RNP2 motifs for RNA binding (GO:0003723), eukaryotic-type RRM domain (IPR003954,
