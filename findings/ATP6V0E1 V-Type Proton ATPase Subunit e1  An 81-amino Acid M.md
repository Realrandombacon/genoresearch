## Description

With 23 papers, ATP6V0E1 represents a genuine 'dark' gene with emerging mechanistic understanding (2018-2025): NAT10-mediated ac4C acetylation of ATP6V0E1 mRNA increases lysosomal acidification promoting esophageal cancer metastasis establishing epigenetic regulation of V-ATPase expression; capsaicin alleviates autophagy-lysosomal dysfunction in 3xTg-AD Alzheimer's mice via PPARA-mediated ATP6V0E1 signaling linking metabolic regulation to neuroprotection; miR-107-3p/Atp6v0e1 axis contributes to selenium-containing peptide protection against lead-induced neurotoxicity; and ATP6V0E1 regulates neuroblastoma cell survival and differentiation.
