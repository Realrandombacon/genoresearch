## Description
TMPRSS15 (UniProt: P98073, aliases Enteropeptidase, Enterokinase) encodes a 1019 amino acid type II transmembrane serine protease representing a partially characterized dark gene with 26 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 21q11.2-22.3, TMPRSS15 is the key initiator of intestinal protein digestion, cleaving and activating trypsinogen to trypsin which then activates other pancreatic zymogens. InterPro domain analysis reveals TMPRSS15-specific multi-domain architecture: SEA domain (IPR000082, residues 52-172, protein-protein interactions), two CUB domains (IPR000859, residues 225-334/524-634, complement subcomponents C1r/C1s, embryonic sea urchin protein Uegf, bone morphogenetic protein 1), MAM domain (IPR000998, residues 342-504, meprin/A5/mu phosphatase, membrane localization), two LDLa domains (residues 184-218/643-677, low-density lipoprotein receptor class A, ligand binding), and C-terminal trypsin-like serine protease domain (Tryp_SPc, residues 785-1016, catalytic core). The catalytic triad (His/Asp/Ser) resides within the trypsin domain (G3DSA:2.40.10.10, residues 786-1015). AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 81.5), indicating a well-folded multi-domain protease with defined catalytic cleft suitable for substrate recognition and cleavage. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 partners revealing digestive protease network: PRSS1 (Trypsin-1, score 0.920 - highest confidence, direct substrate), SPINK1 (Serine protease inhibitor Kazal-type 1, 0.822 - endogenous inhibitor), AGRN (Agrin, 0.847 - basement membrane protein), TXN (Thioredoxin, 0.704 - redox regulation), and multiple mucins (MUC1 0.672, MUC20 0.618, MUC7 0.597, MUC12 0.578, MUC17 0.573, MUC13 0.533). This interaction network places TMPRSS15 within intestinal digestive machinery alongside trypsin (PRSS1), its inhibitor (SPINK1), and mucin substrates. ClinVar contains 113 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1428+1G>A, c.2032+2T>C, c.277-2A>G affecting catalytic domain splicing), frameshifts (c.735del p.Lys245fs in CUB domain, c.1496_1497del p.Tyr499fs in MAM domain, c.1837_1840del p.Thr613fs), nonsense mutations (c.2540C>G p.Ser847Ter, c.2419C>T p.Arg807Ter in catalytic domain), deletions (c.2353_2366del p.Lys784_Ile785insTer), and copy number gains (chr21:13644166-44968483), causing autosomal recessive enterokinase deficiency (OMIM #226200) characterized by severe protein malabsorption, hypoproteinemia, edema, and failure to thrive. HPA expression shows intestine-specific enrichment (921.9 nTPM - highest), plasma membrane localization, and classification as disease-related enzyme/protease.
