## Description
SESTD1 (UniProt Q86VW0), also known as Solo, encodes a 696 aa SEC14 lipid-binding domain and spectrin repeat-containing protein functioning as a phospholipid-binding scaffold that regulates calcium channel activity, synaptic development, and cytoskeletal organization through integrated lipid recognition and protein-protein interaction mechanisms.
