## Description
ZNF622 encodes a 477 amino acid cytoplasmic 60S subunit biogenesis factor (UniProt Q969S3) localized to nucleoplasm, nucleoli, Golgi apparatus, and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, IPR003604) at positions 4-28, 67-91, and 252-275, plus a double-stranded RNA-binding zinc finger (IPR022755, PF12171) at residues 68-91. The protein belongs to the ZNF622/Rei1/Reh1 family (IPR040025) conserved across eukaryotes. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 72.2) spanning residues 1-477. STRING interactions reveal ZNF622 functions in a ribosome biogenesis complex with high-confidence partners: EIF6 (0.992), NMD3 (0.990), PA2G4 (0.977), MELK (0.966), and ribosomal proteins RPL10L (0.945), RPL29 (0.923), RPL5 (0.920), RPL24 (0.917). HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping function in ribosome assembly. ClinVar contains 78 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 5p, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The mechanistic model: ZNF622 acts as a ribosome biogenesis factor that binds pre-60S subunits via its zinc finger domains, facilitating nuclear export and cytoplasmic maturation. The dsRNA-binding zinc finger may recognize rRNA structural elements during assembly. Interaction with EIF6 (a known 60S anti-association factor) and NMD3 (60S export adaptor) positions ZNF622 at the late stages of 60S subunit maturation.
