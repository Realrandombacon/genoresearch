## Description
SRL (Sarcalumenin, also known as SAR) is a 473 amino acid sarcoplasmic reticulum membrane protein (UniProt Q86TD4) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 84.2), characteristic of dynamin-like GTPases that regulate membrane trafficking and calcium handling in muscle cells. The protein contains a dynamin-type guanine nucleotide-binding (G) domain (IPR030381/PS51718, residues 90-331) enabling GTP binding activity (GO:0005525), an EH domain-containing protein N-terminal region (IPR031692/PF16880, residues 59-90) involved in protein-protein interactions, an Eps15 homology domain (EHD) C-terminal domain (cd09913, residues 95-336) for membrane remodeling, P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase domains (IPR027417/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 69-346/96-321) for GTP hydrolysis, and belongs to the TRAFAC Dynamin-like GTPase family (IPR051943/PTHR43681, residues 19-473) and dynamin family (PF00350, residues 96-256).
