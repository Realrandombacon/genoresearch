# BPNT1: Nuclear Inositol Monophosphatase - Misannotated Nucleotidase with Pathogenic Variants

## Description
BPNT1 (UniProt O95861) is a small nuclear protein (308 aa) that appears to be misannotated as a '3'(2'),5'-bisphosphate nucleotidase' but is structurally and functionally an **Inositol Monophosphatase (IMPase)**. InterPro domains (PF00459, CysQ family) confirm an IMPase fold with conserved metal-binding and catalytic sites, contrasting with its UniProt name. STRING interactions reveal it partners with **PAPSS1**, **PAPSS2**, and **ACBD3**, key enzymes in phosphoinositide biosynthesis, suggesting a role in nuclear lipid signaling. HPA data places it in **nuclear speckles**, consistent with RNA processing or transcription regulation. ClinVar lists 32 pathogenic variants (e.g., p.Ile265Leu), indicating disease relevance despite low literature count (23 papers). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence (pLDDT 96.4) structure, validating the stable IMPase fold. The data suggests BPNT1 may regulate nuclear lipid metabolism or RNA processing, and its variants likely disrupt this critical nuclear function.
