# CFAP263: Coiled-Coil Scaffold Protein Regulating Ciliogenesis and Ciliary Beating

**Date:** 2026-03-23T03:11:12.720204

**Quality Score:** 1.6/10  (E=3.2, D=0.0) [POOR]

## Description
CFAP263 (UniProt Q9H0I3) is a 377 amino acid coiled-coil domain protein localized to centriolar satellites and ciliary basal bodies. The protein contains two characteristic coiled-coil domains (IPR025254, PF13870, residues 180-353) that enable oligomerization and scaffold formation. InterPro family IPR051885 classifies it as a coiled-coil domain-containing protein involved in ciliogenesis. HPA expression data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in fallopian tube (29.4 nTPM), consistent with ciliated epithelial tissues. ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (deletions and duplications on chromosome 16q), indicating dosage sensitivity. Gene ontology places CFAP263 in cilium assembly pathways, and UniProt annotations describe it as a component of centriolar satellites contributing to primary cilium formation. The protein acts as a regulator of ciliary beating, connecting radial spoke 3 (RS3) to the central apparatus in the ciliary axoneme. CFAP263 aliases include CCDC113 and HSPC065, reflecting its coiled-coil domain architecture.
