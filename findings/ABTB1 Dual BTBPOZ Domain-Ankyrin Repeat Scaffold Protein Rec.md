## Description
ABTB1 (Ankyrin Repeat and BTB Domain Containing 1, also known as BPOZ, BPOZ-2, BTB3, BTBD21, EF1ABP, PP2259, UniProt Q969K4) is a 478-amino acid multi-domain scaffold protein belonging to the BTB/POZ domain family that functions as a critical regulator of protein ubiquitination, viral replication, and cell cycle progression through dual BTB domain-mediated CUL3 E3 ligase recruitment and ankyrin repeat-dependent protein-protein interactions.
