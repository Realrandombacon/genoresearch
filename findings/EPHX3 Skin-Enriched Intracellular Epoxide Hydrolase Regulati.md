## Description
EPHX3 (UniProt Q9H6B9), also known as ABHD9 or EH3, encodes a compact 360-amino acid intracellular enzyme belonging to the alpha/beta hydrolase superfamily. The protein contains a canonical alpha/beta hydrolase fold-1 domain (IPR000073/PF00561, aa 98-339) within the broader G3DSA:3.40.50.1820 superfamily (aa 77-356), with conserved epoxide hydrolase-like motifs (IPR000639, aa 104-122/169-182/183-196) defining its catalytic capacity for epoxide substrate hydrolysis. AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally well-folded structure (pLDDT: 94.4), among the highest confidence predictions and indicative of stable enzymatic architecture.\n\nSTRING interactions position EPHX3 within detoxification and metabolic networks: strongest link to microsomal epoxide hydrolase EPHX1 (0.687), establishing functional parallelism in epoxide metabolism. Additional connections include ketol-acid reductoisomerase ILVBL (0.567), transcription regulator TRIR (0.532), neuropeptide receptor NPBWR1 (0.511), mitochondrial NDUFAB1 (0.495), breast cancer susceptibility protein PALB2 (0.495), core histones H4C7/H4C6 (~0.48), and chaperone HSP90AB1 (0.458). HPA confirms epithelial enrichment with highest expression in skin (73.7 nTPM), esophagus (66.3 nTPM), vagina (35.0 nTPM), cervix (26.2 nTPM), and lymphoid tissue (25.1 nTPM), with nucleoplasmic localization.\n\nClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants, predominantly 19p13.12 copy number losses/gains, though specific disease associations remain undefined. This positions EPHX3 as an understudied intracellular epoxide hydrolase potentially regulating endogenous lipid signaling molecules (epoxyeicosatrienoic acids) and xenobiotic detoxification in barrier tissues, with untapped relevance in dermatological disorders and chemical sensitivity.
