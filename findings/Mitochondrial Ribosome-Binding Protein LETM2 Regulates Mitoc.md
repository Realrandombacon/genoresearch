## Description
LETM2 (Leucine Zipper EF-hand Containing Transmembrane Protein 2) is a 491 aa mitochondrial inner membrane protein functioning as a ribosome-binding factor that regulates mitochondrial protein synthesis, with emerging roles in pancreatic cancer progression, diabetic foot ulcers, and immune cell function. InterPro analysis reveals LETM2 contains a conserved LETM1-like ribosome-binding domain (IPR033122/PF07766, aa 160-390/438) with GO annotation for ribosome binding (GO:0043022), membership in the LETM1/MDM38-like family (IPR044202, aa 8-393) localized to mitochondrial inner membrane (GO:0005743), and a distinctive N-terminal domain (IPR045742/PF19324, aa 1-111) of unknown function. The PTHR14009 family classification links LETM2 to the leucine zipper-EF-hand containing transmembrane protein superfamily. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 61.8) consistent with a partially structured membrane protein containing both ordered domains and intrinsically disordered regions, typical for proteins undergoing conformational changes during ribosome binding. STRING interactions place LETM2 in mitochondrial and signaling networks: DDHD2 (0.556), a phospholipase involved in lipid metabolism and mitochondrial function; GOT1L1 (0.527), a glutamate oxaloacetate transaminase-like protein; RAB11FIP1 (0.514), a Rab11 effector regulating endosomal trafficking; FAM118A (0.506), a poorly characterized nuclear protein; RPS9/RPS2/RPS11 (0.503/0.496/0.489), small ribosomal subunit proteins suggesting cytoplasmic or mitochondrial ribosome interactions; PLPP5 (0.499), a phospholipid phosphatase; BRF2 (0.468), a RNA polymerase III transcription factor; and NSD3 (0.459), a histone methyltransferase. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in testis (22.2 nTPM) and choroid plexus (13.7 nTPM), subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, and cytosol (suggesting dual mitochondrial-nuclear localization), immune cell enrichment in blood, and classification as a transporter and membrane protein. ClinVar contains 61 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains on chromosome 8p (8p11.23-11.1, 8p21.3-11.1, 8p23.3-q24.3) and a 3' UTR variant (c.*24A>T), though no specific LETM2-associated Mendelian disease is definitively established despite clear clinical significance. Literature (22 papers) establishes LETM2 as: regulating mitochondrial function and autophagy in diabetic foot ulcers via oxidative stress pathways (2025); a prognostic and therapeutic target in pancreatic cancer through a LETM2-regulated PI3K-Akt signaling axis (2022, 11 citations); essential for accumulation of mitochondrially encoded ATP synthase proteins in Arabidopsis (2012, 63 citations); and required for fungus-induced antibiotic resistance in plants (2026).
