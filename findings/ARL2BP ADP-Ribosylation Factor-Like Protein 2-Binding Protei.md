## Description
ARL2BP (ADP-Ribosylation Factor-Like Protein 2-Binding Protein, UniProt Q9Y2Y0) is a 163 amino acid cytosolic and ciliary protein with limited characterization (22 papers) that functions as a BART (Buccal and Respiratory Tract) domain-containing effector protein regulating ciliary microtubule structure, photoreceptor outer segment morphogenesis, sperm flagella assembly, and embryonic left-right patterning, with homozygous loss-of-function mutations causing a syndromic ciliopathy featuring retinitis pigmentosa (rod-cone dystrophy), situs inversus, asthenozoospermia/male infertility, and renal agenesis. The protein contains a conserved BART domain (IPR023379/PF11527 residues 20-134) belonging to the BART domain superfamily (IPR042541/G3DSA:1.20.1520.10 residues 9-136) and the ARL2BP family (IPR038849/PTHR15487 residues 1-162), which mediates binding to ARL2/ARL3 small GTPases for ciliary cargo trafficking. STRING interactions reveal extremely high-confidence partnerships defining ciliary structure and function: ARL2 (0.999, ADP-ribosylation factor-like protein 2—GTPase regulating tubulin cofactor and ciliary trafficking), CFAP20 (0.846, Cilia and flagella associated protein 20—axonemal dynein regulator), ARL3 (0.829, ARL3 GTPase—ciliary lipidated cargo release factor), SLC25A4 (0.571, ANT1—mitochondrial ADP/ATP carrier), RSPRY1 (0.562, Ring finger and SPRY domain-containing protein 1—ciliopathy gene), INTS6 (0.551, Integrator complex subunit 6), C8orf37 (0.539, Retinitis pigmentosa protein), FAM76B (0.536), TTC8 (0.514, Bardet-Biedl syndrome protein 8), and RPGR (0.505, Retinitis pigmentosa GTPase regulator), confirming integration into ciliary assembly, photoreceptor maintenance, and sperm flagella pathways.
