## Description
MEMO1 (Mediator of cell motility 1, also known as Mediator of ERBB2-driven cell motility, Q9Y316) encodes a 297 aa intracellular protein functioning as a metal-binding scaffold that coordinates receptor tyrosine kinase signaling with cytoskeletal dynamics critical for cancer metastasis, angiogenesis, and cell migration. Structural architecture reveals a conserved Memo-like domain (IPR002737/PF01875/MF_00055/cd07361, residues 6-296/8-291/6-295/9-294) adopting a LigB-like fold (G3DSA:3.40.830.10, residues 5-297) characteristic of bacterial adhesion proteins repurposed for eukaryotic signaling, belonging to the MEMO1 family (PTHR11060, residues 7-296) and AmmeMemoRadiSam system protein B (TIGR04336, residues 8-292); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 97.6, very high confidence) confirms an exceptionally well-folded globular structure with defined secondary elements consistent with a stable metalloprotein requiring precise coordination geometry for metal binding. STRING interactions define a metastasis signaling network: ribosomal protein RPL32 (0.900, highest confidence) suggesting potential translation regulation or ribosome association, ubiquitin ligase UBE3D (0.624) linking to protein degradation pathways, small GTPase RHOA (0.603) as central cytoskeletal regulator controlling actomyosin contractility, homeobox protein HMBOX1 (0.591) connecting to transcriptional regulation, EGFR (0.557) and ERBB2/HER2 (0.532) as upstream receptor tyrosine kinases initiating MEMO1 activation, microtubule minus-end stabilizer CAMSAP2 (0.533) regulating microtubule organization, kynurenine 3-monooxygenase KMO (0.526) linking to tryptophan metabolism, formin DIAPH1 (0.522) mediating actin polymerization downstream of RHOA, and neuregulin NRG1 (0.513) as ERBB ligand. HPA localizes MEMO1 to vesicles with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue and cancer specificity indicating housekeeping function with context-dependent regulation, low immune cell specificity, and group-enriched single cell type specificity. ClinVar documents 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice acceptor mutations c.658-2A>G and c.658-2A>T disrupting the C-terminal region, in-frame deletion c.438-16_438-15del removing two residues in the core domain, and multiple chromosome 2p copy number alterations: gains at 2p24.1-22.2 (chr2:20938401-37327210x3), 2p25.3-22.3 (chr2:12771-35541353x3, chr2:706460-35523639x3), 2p25.3-q37.3 (chr2:1-243199373), and losses at 2p23.1-21 (chr2:30814984-42798684x1), 2p23.1-22.3 (chr2:32083215-32684402x1), 2p25.1-q13 (chr2:11504318-111365996x1); these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (67 papers) establishes MEMO1 as essential for ERBB2-driven cell motility where it acts downstream of HER2 phosphorylation to activate RHOA-DIAPH1 signaling cascade reorganizing actin cytoskeleton for directional migration, regulates angiogenic VEGF signaling in retinopathy of prematurity through endothelial cell migration control, binds iron and copper metals modulating cellular metal homeostasis in cancer cells affecting TCA cycle enzyme activity and metabolic reprogramming, drives cancer metastasis through circRNA-miRNA regulatory axes (hsa_circ_0007385-MEMO1 axis regulated by miR-485-3p/miR-543/miR-337-3p) in colorectal cancer, and functions in endothelial angiogenic signaling; vesicular localization suggests MEMO1 may traffic with activated receptors or localize to endosomal signaling platforms; high AlphaFold confidence (97.6) reflects evolutionary constraint on metal-coordinating residues; RPL32 interaction hints at potential non-canonical role in translation regulation during stress; conservation from zebrafish (Q803S3, 297 aa) to bovine (Q2HJH7, 285 aa) confirms essential physiological function.
