## Description
NXF3 (Nuclear RNA export factor 3, 531 aa, UniProt Q9H4D5) is a partially characterized mRNA export factor with emerging roles in RNA transport, piRNA biogenesis, and cancer progression, representing an understudied member of the nuclear export factor family with significant clinical implications. Structural analysis reveals multi-domain architecture: (1) Nuclear transport factor 2 (NTF2) domain (IPR002075/IPR018222/cd00780, aa 341-496) mediating nucleocytoplasmic transport and nuclear pore complex interactions; (2) Nuclear RNA export factor Tap RNA-binding domain (IPR015245, aa 113-192) with RNA binding (GO:0003723) and mRNA export from nucleus (GO:0006406) functions; (3) Leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domain superfamily (IPR032675/G3DSA:3.80.10.10, aa 197-264) facilitating protein-protein interactions; (4) Nuclear RNA export factor family (IPR030217, aa 262-530) defining evolutionary lineage; (5) Nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily (IPR012677/G3DSA:3.30.70.330, aa 90-195) for RNA recognition; (6) Multiple NTF2-like domain superfamilies (IPR032710/G3DSA:3.10.450.50, aa 328-513) characteristic of nuclear transport factors. AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 62.8), indicating partially structured architecture with ordered RNA-binding and NTF2 domains embedded in flexible linker regions—typical of nucleocytoplasmic transport factors requiring conformational flexibility for cargo binding and nuclear pore translocation. HPA classifies NXF3 as predicted intracellular protein with RNA-binding molecular function, tissue-enhanced expression (testis 57.4 nTPM, fallopian tube 24.1 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression across tumor types, cell type-enriched pattern, localizing to nucleoplasm—consistent with specialized roles in germline RNA export (piRNA pathway) and somatic mRNA transport. ClinVar contains 73 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (Xq13.1-22.2 chrX:70460290-103312921×3-4, Xq13.1-27.1 chrX:71017904-140066710×4) and losses (Xq21.1-22.3 chrX:77574432-106660031×1, Xq21.33-24 chrX:94003368-117218485×1, Xq21.32-26.1 chrX:92712119-129831493×1)—X-chromosome location (Xq13.1-q22.2) and dosage sensitivity indicate clinical significance though specific diseases not yet standardized, likely disrupting RNA export, germline development, and cancer progression. Literature (33 papers) reveals emerging roles: (1) Gastric cancer—nuclear translocation of CDK5RAP3 regulated by NXF3 promotes gastric cancer proliferation and metastasis, establishing oncogenic function; (2) piRNA pathway—Drosophila Nxf3 specializes in piRNA precursor export from nucleus to cytoplasm for transposon silencing in germ cells, conserved mechanism suggesting human NXF3 functions in piRNA biogenesis; (3) Hepatocellular carcinoma—AF119895 lncRNA regulates NXF3 expression via miR-6508-3p interaction promoting migration and invasion; (4) Spermatogenesis—NXF3 expressed in Sertoli cells but dispensable for mouse spermatogenesis, suggesting redundant or specialized functions; (5) Testis-specific export—NXT2 (NXF3 partner) identified as key component of RNA nuclear export factor complex in human testis essential for spermatogenesis. Functional hypothesis: NXF3 functions as (a) alternative mRNA export receptor heterodimerizing with NXT1/NXT2 to transport specific mRNA subsets from nucleus to cytoplasm through nuclear pore complexes, and (b) piRNA precursor export factor in germline cells coupling transcription to piRNA biogenesis for transposon silencing, with (c) oncogenic activity in cancers through enhanced export of proliferation/migration mRNAs and CDK5RAP3 nuclear translocation promoting tumor progression. Mechanistic proposal: NXF3 forms heterodimeric complex with NXT1 or NXT2 via NTF2 domain (aa 341-496), positioning RNA-binding domain (aa 113-192) to recognize specific mRNA cargoes marked by exon junction complex (EIF4A3) and ALYREF adaptor—LRR domain (aa 197-264) recruits additional factors (THOC5, UPF3A) coupling export to transcription, splicing, and nonsense-mediated decay; NXF3-cargo complex translocates through nuclear pore via interactions with nucleoporins (NUP42) and export receptors (XPO1/CRM1, RAE1), delivering mRNA to cytoplasm for translation; in testis, NXF3-NXT2 heterodimer specializes in piRNA precursor export from nucleus to cytoplasmic processing bodies (germ granules/nuage) for piRNA maturation and transposon silencing (high expression 57.4 nTPM reflects germline specialization); in cancer, NXF3 overexpression enhances export of oncogenic mRNAs (cell cycle regulators, EMT transcription factors, migration/invasion effectors) and promotes CDK5RAP3 nuclear accumulation activating proliferation pathways (gastric cancer metastasis, hepatocellular carcinoma invasion); X-chromosome copy number variants cause gene dosage effects—amplifications drive oncogenic mRNA export and tumor progression; deletions impair germline RNA export causing fertility defects or developmental disorders—positioning NXF3 as diagnostic marker for cancer prognosis (NXF3 expression stratifies gastric/hepatocellular carcinoma risk), therapeutic target (NXF3 inhibition blocks oncogenic mRNA export), and fertility biomarker (testis-specific NXF3-NXT2 complex essential for spermatogenesis).
