## Description
LIN52 (LIM domain-containing protein 52) represents a classic 'Clinical Data Gap' candidate. The gene is involved in cell cycle regulation but lacks a defined human disease syndrome, making it a prime target for discovering novel genetic disorders linked to 14q abnormalities.
