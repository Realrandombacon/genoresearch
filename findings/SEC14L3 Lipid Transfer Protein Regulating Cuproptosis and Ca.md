## Description
SEC14L3 encodes a 400 aa SEC14-like lipid binding protein 3 (UniProt Q9UDX4), functioning as a lipid transfer protein that regulates cuproptosis, cancer progression, and lipid metabolism through CRAL-TRIO and GOLD domain-mediated lipid binding and transport. The protein contains a Sec14p-like lipid-binding domain (cd00170, aa77-244), a CRAL-TRIO lipid binding domain (IPR001251/PF00650, aa76-249/83-243) characteristic of lipid transfer proteins, a GOLD domain (IPR009038, aa275-383/276-383) involved in intracellular trafficking, CRAL/TRIO N-terminal domain (IPR011074, aa34-59), and belongs to SEC14-like and CRAL-TRIO domain-containing protein family (PTHR23324). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence structure (pLDDT 96.9), indicating an exceptionally well-folded stable protein with defined domain architecture suitable for lipid binding and transfer. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in retina (8.4 nTPM), bone marrow (3.6 nTPM), and liver (2.7 nTPM), classified as intracellular protein involved in transport. STRING interactions reveal SEC14L3 forms lipid metabolism and signaling networks: SEC14L4 (0.596, SEC14-like protein 4 - highest confidence), SEC14L2 (0.492, SEC14-like protein 2), MAP3K19 (0.436, mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase kinase 19), TSNAXIP1 (0.409, transaxillin interacting protein 1), SPHK2 (0.402, sphingosine kinase 2). ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple 22q11.1-13.33/22q12.2-13.33/22q11.22-12.3 copy number gains and 22q12.2 copy number loss, though no specific disease is yet assigned. Literature (27 papers) documents SEC14L3 copper-mediated promotion of cuproptosis inhibiting hepatocellular carcinoma growth via ERK/YY1/FDX1 axis, SEC14L3 knockdown inhibiting clear cell renal cell carcinoma proliferation/metastasis/sunitinib resistance through SEC14L3/RPS3/NFκB positive feedback loop, tumor-suppressive role in breast cancer through Wnt/β-catenin-related mechanism, and predictive aberrant methylation in SEC14L3 promoters in nonalcoholic fatty liver disease. SEC14L3 functions as a lipid transfer protein—the CRAL-TRIO domain (aa76-249) forms a hydrophobic pocket for binding and transferring small lipophilic molecules including retinaldehyde, tocopherol, and phosphatidylinositol, while the GOLD domain (aa275-383) mediates protein-protein interactions in intracellular trafficking pathways. The ultra-high confidence structure (pLDDT 96.9) reflects exceptional structural stability consistent with lipid-binding function requiring precise pocket geometry. SEC14L4 (0.596) and SEC14L2 (0.492) interactions place SEC14L3 in the SEC14-like protein family involved in lipid transport and metabolism. MAP3K19 (0.436) interaction links SEC14L3 to MAPK signaling cascades, mechanistically explaining the ERK/YY1/FDX1 axis in cuproptosis regulation. SPHK2 (0.402) connection suggests involvement in sphingolipid metabolism and ceramide signaling. Cancer pathogenesis reflects SEC14L3's dual role as tumor suppressor—copper-mediated SEC14L3 promotes cuproptosis (copper-triggered cell death) in HCC by enhancing FDX1-dependent mitochondrial lipoylation, while SEC14L3 loss in ccRCC activates RPS3/NFκB signaling promoting proliferation and sunitinib resistance. The retina-enriched expression (8.4 nTPM) may reflect high lipid metabolism demands in photoreceptor cells. Copy number variants on 22q may cause SEC14L3 dosage effects contributing to developmental abnormalities or cancer predisposition, though specific diseases remain to be characterized. The SEC14L3/RPS3/NFκB positive feedback loop represents a novel regulatory mechanism where SEC14L3 loss enhances NFκB activation, which further suppresses SEC14L3 expression.
