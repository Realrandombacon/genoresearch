## Description
DDX54 (Q8TDD1) encodes an 881 amino acid DEAD-box RNA helicase belonging to the DBP10/DDX54 family that functions as a critical ribosome assembly factor initiating peptidyl transferase center (PTC) formation during 60S ribosomal subunit biogenesis while simultaneously regulating epitranscriptomic modifications and innate immune signaling. InterPro analysis reveals canonical DEAD-box helicase architecture with multiple functional domains: a Q motif (IPR014014, positions 96-124) characteristic of DEAD-box proteins mediating ATP-dependent RNA binding; a conserved DEAD-box helicase site (IPR000629, positions 245-253) containing the signature Asp-Glu-Ala-Asp motif for ATP hydrolysis; a DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545, positions 120-283) providing core catalytic activity; an ATP-binding P-loop NTPase domain (IPR014001/IPR027417, positions 115-314/70-461) enabling nucleotide-dependent conformational changes; a C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650, positions 324-473) completing the dual RecA-like fold; and a DBP10-specific C-terminal domain (IPR012541, positions 714-774) unique to this subfamily potentially mediating protein-protein interactions within the ribosome assembly machinery. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 70.4) consistent with stable helicase core domains while flexible linker regions enable conformational dynamics required for RNA remodeling during ribosome maturation.
