# HERC6: Interferon-Induced HECT E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Catalyzing ISGylation with Sex-Biased STING Regulation, Hippo Signaling Modulation, and Roles in NAFLD, Lupus Nephritis, and Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia

## Description
HERC6 (Q8IVU3) encodes a 1022 amino acid HECT domain-containing E3 ubiquitin ligase that functions as the primary E3 ligase for ISG15 conjugation (ISGylation), a critical post-translational modification induced by type I interferons during antiviral immune responses. InterPro analysis reveals a bimodular architecture combining regulatory and catalytic domains: N-terminal RCC1 repeats (IPR000408, positions 25-38/42-93/94-149) forming a regulator of chromosome condensation 1/beta-lactamase-inhibitor protein II superfamily scaffold (IPR009091/G3DSA:2.130.10.30, positions 2-404) for protein-protein interactions and substrate recognition; a C-terminal HECT catalytic domain (IPR000569/G3DSA:3.30.2160.10/G3DSA:3.30.2410.10, positions 672-1015) containing the characteristic E3 ligase catalytic machinery for ubiquitin-like transfer classified within the HECT E3 ligase superfamily (IPR035983, positions 659-1011) and ubiquitin ligases/GTPase regulators family (IPR051709, positions 26-1011). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 86.4, high confidence) consistent with stable multi-domain architecture required for coordinated substrate binding and catalytic activity. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive partnerships with interferon-stimulated gene (ISG) machinery: ISG15 (0.941), the ubiquitin-like modifier conjugated by HERC6; IFI44 (0.908) and IFI44L (0.883), interferon-induced proteins; RSAD2 (0.903), radical S-adenosyl methionine domain-containing antiviral protein; DDX60 (0.902), DExD/H-box helicase in RIG-I signaling; OAS2 (0.893) and OAS3 (0.887), 2'-5' oligoadenylate synthetases; MX1 (0.887), myxovirus resistance GTPase; IFIT1 (0.882), interferon-induced protein with tetratricopeptide repeats; and USP18 (0.790), ubiquitin-specific peptidase removing ISG15 conjugates. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, nucleoplasm/cytosol localization consistent with nuclear-cytoplasmic shuttling during ISGylation cycles, classified under Ubl conjugation pathway/transferase molecular function. ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning chromosome 4q21.21-31.21 regions (e.g., chr4:79246602-146469099 duplications, chr4:85139670-96295033 gains) and losses (chr4:70910927-92961027 deletions, chr4:81558759-95965995 losses), plus missense variants (c.1714G>T p.Val572Leu, c.1713G>T p.Lys571Asn), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (90 papers) demonstrates macrophage HERC6 promotes non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) through integrated Mendelian randomization and single-cell transcriptome analyses; HERC6 expression associates with primary ciliary dyskinesia and nasal nitric oxide levels; HERC6 regulates STING activity in a sex-biased manner through modulation of LATS2/VGLL3 Hippo signaling explaining female-skewed interferon responses in human skin; porcine HERC6 acts as major E3 ligase for ISGylation and undergoes auto-ISGylation for effective ISGylation; interferon α-induced HERC6 provokes podocyte dysfunction via mitotic catastrophe in lupus nephritis. The mechanistic hypothesis: HERC6 functions as the master E3 ligase for ISG15 conjugation during type I interferon responses—the RCC1 repeat region binds target proteins while the HECT domain catalyzes ISG15 transfer from E2 conjugating enzymes to substrate lysine residues; ISGylation stabilizes antiviral effectors (MX1, OAS proteins, IFIT1) and modifies signaling intermediates (STING, RIG-I) enhancing interferon amplification; sex-biased STING regulation via LATS2/VGLL3 Hippo pathway explains enhanced female interferon responses providing protection against viral infections but predisposing to autoimmune diseases like lupus nephritis; USP18 interaction enables reversible ISGylation providing temporal control preventing excessive inflammation; copy number gains on 4q may drive autoimmune phenotypes through elevated ISGylation enhancing interferon signaling, while losses may predispose to viral susceptibility through impaired antiviral responses; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 86.4) indicates stable domain organization enabling coordinated substrate recognition (RCC1 repeats) and catalysis (HECT domain); podocyte dysfunction in lupus nephritis suggests HERC6-mediated ISGylation disrupts cell cycle regulation triggering mitotic catastrophe in glomerular cells.
