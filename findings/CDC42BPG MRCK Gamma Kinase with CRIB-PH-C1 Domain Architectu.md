## Description
CDC42BPG (Q6DT37) is a 1551-aa serine/threonine protein kinase (MRCK gamma) with confident AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 72.3), indicating a well-folded multi-domain enzyme optimized for signal transduction. InterPro analysis reveals comprehensive domain architecture characteristic of Rho-effector kinases: CRIB/Cdc42-binding domain (IPR000095/PF00786: positions 1437-1450) enabling Rho GTPase binding and membrane recruitment, protein kinase catalytic domain (IPR000719/IPR011009/PF00069/PS00107/PS50011: positions 71-337) with DMPK-like kinase signature (cd05597: positions 69-399) for serine/threonine phosphorylation, C1 phorbol-ester/diacylglycerol-binding domain (cd20866: positions 879-921) providing lipid second messenger sensing, and pleckstrin homology PH domain (cd01243/G3DSA:2.30.29.30: positions 938-1070) mediating phosphoinositide binding and membrane targeting. HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced pattern with highest expression in skin (31.5 nTPM) and brain (28.8 nTPM), localized to cytosol, functioning as kinase/transferase in signal transduction pathways. STRING interactions reveal functional coupling to Rho GTPase signaling with medium confidence: RHOQ/RhoQ GTPase (0.590), CDC42/cell division cycle 42 (0.493), KRTAP10-6/keratin associated protein (0.418), and FRK/fyn-related kinase (0.407), positioning CDC42BPG within cytoskeletal regulation and cell polarity networks. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains at 11q12.1-13.3, 11q12.2-13.5, 11p13-q25, 11q13.1-13.2 regions, deletions at 11q12, and splice variants c.1488+28C>G, c.1668+79G>T, c.1953+2T>C, c.2865G>A, establishing clinical significance though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature shows only 8 papers (genuine dark gene) with primary association as novel susceptibility locus for hyperuricemia in Japanese population GWAS, plus recent mentions in organelle-specific autophagy regulator screens and phenylketonuria neuronal model transcriptome profiling. The confident multi-domain structure (pLDDT 72.3), CRIB domain for Cdc42/RhoQ binding, PH-C1 lipid-binding modules, kinase catalytic domain, skin-brain enriched expression, and pathogenic variant burden suggest CDC42BPG functions as Rho GTPase effector kinase transducing Cdc42/RhoQ signals to regulate actin cytoskeleton reorganization, cell polarity, and membrane trafficking through phosphorylation of cytoskeletal substrates, with disruption potentially contributing to hyperuricemia, neurological phenotypes, and skin disorders through impaired Rho signaling and cytoskeletal dynamics.
