## Description
PPP4R3A (Protein Phosphatase 4 Regulatory Subunit 3A) is a 'Dark Gene' candidate characterized by a significant clinical data gap. Despite having 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants recorded in ClinVar, no specific human disease syndrome is associated with these variants, indicating an undefined clinical phenotype. The gene is poorly characterized with only 6 literature papers (DARK status). Structurally, it encodes a large (833 aa) regulatory subunit of the Protein Phosphatase 4 (PP4) complex, which is known to regulate cell cycle progression and DNA repair. The presence of copy number variations and single nucleotide variants in the 14q31.1-32.33 region suggests a potential role in undiagnosed genetic disorders, likely related to the PP4 complex's function in synaptic protein synthesis or metabolic decline, yet the specific disease entity remains elusive.
