## Description
HIGD1B (HIG1 domain family member 1B, alias CLST11240, CLST11240-15, HIG1 hypoxia inducible domain family member 1B) is a 99 amino acid predicted intracellular membrane-associated mitochondrial respiratory supercomplex factor (UniProt Q9P298) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 67.8) containing canonical HIG1 domain architecture including Hypoxia induced protein domain (IPR007667/PS51503, residues 1-94) forming a 94 aa structured module (~95% of protein) characteristic of the HIG1 family that mediates mitochondrial respiratory chain assembly and hypoxia response via transmembrane helix structure with GO:0006119 oxidative phosphorylation and GO:0005743 mitochondrial inner membrane localization, Mitochondrial Respiratory Supercomplex Factor 1 family (IPR050355/PTHR12297, residues 4-94) forming a 91 aa module (~92%) defining the respiratory supercomplex assembly factor signature, Hypoxia induced protein conserved region (PF04588, residues 27-79) forming a 53 aa module (~54%) containing the core HIG1 fold, and molecular functions including mitochondrial respiratory chain complex assembly, cytochrome c oxidase activity regulation, and hypoxia response, functioning as a placenta/brain-enriched mitochondrial inner membrane protein that regulates cytochrome c oxidase assembly and hypoxia response through HIG1 domain-mediated protein interactions via partnerships with COX4I2, MT-CO3, MT-CO2, COX5B, COX6A2, COX6A1, FAM162B, GJA4, and C1orf198.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals HIGD1B contains canonical HIG1 domain architecture: The HIG1 domain (residues 1-94) encompasses nearly the entire protein, forming a transmembrane helix structure characteristic of mitochondrial inner membrane proteins.
