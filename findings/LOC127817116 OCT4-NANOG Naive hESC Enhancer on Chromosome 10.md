## Description
LOC127817116 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 10q (genomic coordinates: 4127784-4128423 on NC_000010.11, spanning approximately 639 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in naive human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with two master pluripotency transcription factors: OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU domain class 5 homeobox 1) and NANOG (NK2 homeobox protein). The OCT4-NANOG enhancer signature indicates this element functions in the core pluripotency regulatory network maintaining naive pluripotency (pre-implantation epiblast-like state). A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. The 10q chromosomal region contains critical developmental genes. As a naive hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4 and NANOG, LOC127817116 likely functions to maintain transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and pluripotency maintenance, potentially regulating genes involved in self-renewal and preventing premature differentiation.
