## Description
C1QTNF6 (Complement C1q tumor necrosis factor-related protein 6, 278 aa, UniProt Q9BXI9) is a secreted C1q/TNF-related adipokine (CTRP) family member functioning in immune regulation, antiviral defense, and cancer progression through heteromeric complex formation. Structural analysis reveals: (1) N-terminal collagen-like domain with triple helix repeats (IPR008160/PF01391, aa 97-138, 20 copies) enabling trimerization and extracellular matrix interactions; (2) C-terminal C1q globular domain (IPR001073/PF00386, aa 136-278) for receptor binding and complement activation; (3) Cerebellin synaptic organizer family (IPR050822/PTHR22923, aa 78-276) suggesting neuronal/immune synapse functions; (4) TNF-like domain superfamily (IPR008983/G3DSA:2.60.120.40, aa 134-277) characteristic of cytokine signaling. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 76.6), indicating stable trimeric architecture typical of C1q family proteins. STRING interactions identify immune and metabolic partners: C1QTNF1 (0.794, paralog heteromerization), TAGAP (0.639, T-cell activation GTPase), CLEC16A (0.627, endosomal trafficking), SH2B3 (0.563, lymphoid phosphatase), CTSH (0.502, cathepsin H), PTPN2 (0.501, protein tyrosine phosphatase), BACH2 (0.499, transcriptional repressor), PRKCQ (0.484, protein kinase C theta), UBASH3A (0.447, T-cell signaling inhibitor) - collectively implicating C1QTNF6 in T-cell receptor signaling and immune checkpoint regulation. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression highest in endometrium (29.8 nTPM) and placenta (27.9 nTPM), with nucleoplasm localization and predicted secreted protein classification, consistent with hormone-responsive tissue expression and cytokine-like secretion. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 22q11.1-13.33 and missense variant p.Gly21Val, indicating clinical relevance though specific diseases not yet fully defined. Literature (73 papers) reveals emerging roles: (1) antiviral defense - C1QTNF6-MX2 axis inhibits porcine circovirus type 2 (PCV2) replication in macrophages through interferon-stimulated gene activation; (2) type 1 diabetes - rs229541 polymorphism confers T1D susceptibility with implications for ZnT8 autoantibody production and beta-cell function; (3) lung adenocarcinoma - C1QTNF6 upregulation correlates with immune infiltration, tumor progression, and poor prognosis; (4) bladder cancer - super-enhancer-driven C1QTNF6 expression serves as prognostic biomarker and therapeutic target. Functional hypothesis: C1QTNF6 functions as (a) heteromeric adipokine forming complexes with C1QTNF1 and other CTRP family members to modulate immune cell activation and metabolic signaling, and (b) antiviral effector inducing MX2-dependent restriction of viral replication in macrophages. Mechanistic proposal: Secreted C1QTNF6 trimerizes via collagen-like domain, binds cell surface receptors (potentially ADIPOR1/2 or integrins) through C1q globular domain on T-cells and macrophages, activating downstream signaling cascades including: (i) TAGAP-mediated TCR signaling modulation affecting T-cell activation thresholds; (ii) CLEC16A-dependent endosomal trafficking regulating cytokine receptor turnover; (iii) SH2B3/PTPN2 phosphatase recruitment dampening JAK-STAT signaling; (iv) MX2 induction via interferon regulatory factors restricting viral DNA integration—this multi-pathway regulation positions C1QTNF6 at intersection of metabolic control (adipokine signaling), immune tolerance (T-cell checkpoint modulation), and antiviral defense (MX2 axis), with dysregulation contributing to autoimmune diabetes (T1D susceptibility), cancer immune evasion (lung/bladder tumors), and viral susceptibility—making C1QTNF6 therapeutic target for cancer immunotherapy enhancement, autoimmune disease modulation, and broad-spectrum antiviral strategies.
