## Description
SCYL2 (SCY1-Like Pseudokinase 2; UniProt Q6P3W7) is a 929 amino acid intracellular scaffolding protein with only 29 publications, classifying it as a genuine dark gene despite critical roles in neurodevelopment, secretory trafficking, and cancer progression.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a catalytically inactive pseudokinase architecture: an N-terminal pseudokinase domain (cd14011, residues 38-329; IPR000719/PF00069, residues 32-327) belonging to the protein kinase-like superfamily (IPR011009, residues 28-341) but lacking catalytic activity. The C-terminal region contains an armadillo-like helical fold (IPR011989/IPR016024, residues 337-610) characteristic of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. The protein belongs to the catalytically inactive kinase-related protein family (IPR051177/PTHR12984, residues 21-865). AlphaFold predicts confident structure (global pLDDT 70.8), indicating a stable scaffold optimized for binding partners rather than enzymatic catalysis.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies high-confidence interactions with clathrin trafficking machinery: CLINT1 (0.718, clathrin interactor), plus medium-confidence partners including PCMT1 (0.502, protein carboxyl methyltransferase), COX17 (0.490, cytochrome c oxidase assembly), AFTPH (0.483, aftiphilin), PANK3 (0.463, pantothenate kinase), paralogs SCYL1 (0.445) and SCYL3 (0.442), GNA13 (0.445, G-protein alpha-13), FZD5 (0.418, Frizzled-5 Wnt receptor), and RALGAPB (0.417, Ral GTPase activating protein). This network positions SCYL2 at the intersection of endocytic trafficking, Wnt signaling, and small GTPase regulation.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with fundamental cellular trafficking functions. Classified as an intracellular protein involved in disease processes and as a potential drug target.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including truncating mutations (p.Tyr405Ter, p.Met777fs, p.Cys200fs), missense changes (p.Asp520Gly, p.Leu478Pro, p.Met321Val), and large copy number gains at 12q23.1-24.33. Recent 2024-2026 literature establishes biallelic SCYL2 mutations as causative for arthrogryposis multiplex congenita type 4 (AMC4), a severe neurogenic disorder featuring joint contractures and developmental delay. Additional studies reveal SCYL2's role in attenuating excitotoxicity in neurons and promoting tumorigenesis in adult T-cell leukemia/lymphoma via PTEN phosphorylation and PI3K/AKT pathway activation.\n\nCONSERVATION: Orthologs confirmed in mouse (Q8CFE4, 930 aa) and Arabidopsis (Q9C9H8, 909 aa), indicating strong evolutionary constraint on the pseudokinase-armadillo architecture.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SCYL2 likely functions as a clathrin-associated scaffold that regulates secretory protein trafficking and receptor endocytosis without catalytic activity. By binding CLINT1 and potentially Frizzled receptors, it controls Wnt signaling compartmentalization and turnover. The pseudokinase domain may serve as a regulatory switch—binding ATP or interacting partners without phosphorylation. In neurons, SCYL2 suppresses excitotoxicity by modulating glutamate receptor trafficking. In cancer, it promotes oncogenesis by facilitating PTEN phosphorylation (potentially via recruiting active kinases), leading to PI3K/AKT hyperactivation. The 23 ClinVar variants causing AMC4 suggest haploinsufficiency disrupts neurodevelopmental trafficking programs, particularly at neuromuscular junctions.
