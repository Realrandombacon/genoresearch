# Dishevelled-2 (DVL2): Multi-Domain Scaffold Protein Orchestrating Wnt/β-Catenin and Planar Cell Polarity Signaling

**Date:** 2026-06-17T10:19:10.900372

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
DVL2 encodes a 736 aa cytoplasmic scaffold protein (90-95 kD when phosphorylated) that serves as a central hub in Wnt signal transduction. The protein contains three conserved domains arranged linearly: N-terminal DIX domain (IPR001158, residues 11-93) mediating polymerization and Axin recruitment, central PDZ domain (IPR001478, residues 267-355) binding Frizzled receptors and membrane partners, and C-terminal DEP domain (IPR000591, residues 433-507) required for membrane translocation and planar cell polarity signaling. STRING interactions reveal DVL2 at the core of Wnt machinery with ultra-high confidence partners: AXIN1 (0.999), VANGL1/2 (0.994), LRP6 (0.993), DACT1 (0.992), DVL1/3 paralogs (0.991/0.990), FZD4 (0.985), ARRB2 (0.981), and PRICKLE1 (0.979). HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, and aggresome localization. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains on 17p13.1 associated with developmental disorders. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 58.7) indicates medium confidence with structured domains connected by flexible linkers—consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility. Mechanistically, DVL2 acts as a molecular switch: upon Wnt ligand binding to Frizzled/LRP6, DVL2 recruits to the membrane via DEP-PDZ interactions, polymerizes via DIX domains to form signalosomes, and inhibits the β-catenin destruction complex (AXIN1/APC/GSK3β), allowing β-catenin nuclear translocation for transcriptional activation. In parallel, DVL2-VANGL-PRICKLE complexes regulate planar cell polarity during tissue morphogenesis.
