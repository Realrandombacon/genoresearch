## Description

The TRIM28 interaction confirms canonical KRAB corepressor recruitment mechanism, while interactions with ZNF222/ZNF223 suggest potential heterodimerization within the zinc finger protein family cluster on 19q. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues (detected in all, low tissue specificity) with nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization - dual localization may indicate nucleocytoplasmic shuttling. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic variants including two single nucleotide variants (c.15+91G>T, c.-85-12C>G) and large copy number gains/losses spanning 19q13 regions (variants 685025, 443499, 443498, 253540, 144406) plus duplications (545227). The KRAB domain mediates heterochromatin formation via TRIM28 recruitment, while zinc fingers confer DNA sequence specificity. ZNF155 functions as a chromatin-associated transcriptional silencer, potentially regulating developmentally expressed genes in the 19q13 cluster. The clustered interaction pattern with ZNF222/ZNF223 suggests coordinated regulatory function within this zinc finger gene family. AlphaFold structure prediction and conservation data not computed.
