## Description
PRRG2 encodes a 202 aa single-pass transmembrane protein (UniProt O14669) featuring an N-terminal vitamin K-dependent gamma-carboxyglutamic acid (Gla) domain (IPR000294/PF00594, residues 28-96) and C-terminal proline-rich PY motifs. The Gla domain enables calcium-dependent membrane binding similar to coagulation factors, while PY motifs serve as WW domain-binding sites. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 72.1) with well-folded Gla domain and flexible cytoplasmic region. STRING reveals high-confidence interaction with YAP1 (0.956), the transcriptional coactivator of the Hippo pathway, plus connections to LATS1 (0.520, Hippo kinase), NEDD4 (0.504, E3 ubiquitin ligase), and GGCX (0.684, gamma-glutamyl carboxylase). HPA shows parathyroid-enriched expression (26.6 nTPM), immune cell enhancement, and nucleoplasm/cytosol localization. ClinVar lists 16 pathogenic variants including copy number alterations and nonsense mutation p.Arg135Ter. With only 5 publications, PRRG2 remains poorly characterized despite clear domain architecture suggesting regulatory function.
