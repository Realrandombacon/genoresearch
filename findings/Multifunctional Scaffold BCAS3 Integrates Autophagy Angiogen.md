## Description
BCAS3 (Breast Carcinoma-Amplified Sequence 3, also known as Rudhira or WDR82-interacting protein) is a 928 aa multifunctional scaffold protein functioning as a critical node linking microtubule dynamics, autophagy, and transcriptional regulation. With 91 literature references, BCAS3 represents a 'partially characterized' dark gene where its diverse roles in development and disease are increasingly recognized but mechanistically fragmented. InterPro analysis reveals a complex domain architecture dominated by an N-terminal region of multiple WD40 repeats (IPR036322/PF21034, aa 12-464) forming a β-propeller structure for protein-protein interactions, a central YVTN repeat-like domain (IPR015943, aa 315-466), and a unique C-terminal BCAS3-specific domain (IPR022175/PF12490, aa 716-757) of unknown function that may mediate specific binding or regulation. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence global structure (pLDDT 64.6), consistent with a large scaffold containing structured WD40 domains connected by flexible linkers, enabling conformational adaptability. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, localizing primarily to nucleoli but also implicated in cytoplasmic functions, suggesting dual roles in ribosome biogenesis/transcription and cytoskeletal organization. STRING interactions place BCAS3 at the intersection of autophagy and chromatin remodeling: WIPI2 (0.877, key autophagy initiator), BCAS4 (0.983, frequent co-amplification partner in breast cancer), MTA1 (0.704, chromatin remodeler), VIM (0.698, vimentin intermediate filament), and transcription factors TBX2/TBX4 (0.529/0.697). ClinVar lists 45 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg544Ter, p.Leu129Ter), missense changes (p.Met5Val, p.Gly809Cys), and large copy number alterations on chromosome 17q, implicating BCAS3 dosage sensitivity in neurodevelopmental disorders (intellectual disability) and cancer. Recent literature (2023-2025) establishes BCAS3 as: (1) A target of TET2-mediated m5C methylation regulating podocyte injury and mitophagy in diabetic nephropathy; (2) Essential for neurodevelopment where knockout causes defects in zebrafish; (3) A proto-oncogene amplified in breast cancer driving cell migration; (4) A fusion partner with PPM1D in rare extraskeletal osteosarcoma.
