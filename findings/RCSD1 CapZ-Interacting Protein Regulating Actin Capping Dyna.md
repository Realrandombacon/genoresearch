## Description
RCSD1 encodes a 416 amino acid CapZ-interacting protein (UniProt Q6JBY9) belonging to the WASH complex-associated protein family (PTHR21669). Despite 53 publications, its normal physiological function remains incompletely characterized, with recent focus on its role in RCSD1::ABL2 fusion-driven leukemia.\n\nSTRUCTURAL FEATURES: AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 55.8) across all 416 residues, consistent with intrinsically disordered regions interspersed with folded domains. InterPro analysis reveals two key domains: N-terminal FAM21/CAPZIP domain (IPR029341, PF15255, residues 78-184) mediating interaction with actin capping protein, and central RCSD region (IPR007850, PF05177, residues 228-323) of unknown function but conserved across vertebrates. The low confidence score suggests conformational flexibility important for scaffold function.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies 10 interaction partners revealing dual roles in cytoskeletal regulation and signaling. Actin capping complex: CAPZB (0.781, beta subunit), CAPZA2 (0.747, alpha-2 subunit), CAPZA1 (0.556, alpha-1 subunit)—direct binding partners for actin filament barbed-end regulation. Stress kinase pathway: MAPKAPK3 (0.772), MAPKAPK2 (0.523), MAPK13 (0.579), MAPK12 (0.578)—downstream effectors of p38 MAPK signaling. Oncogenic fusion partner: ABL1 (0.589)—the RCSD1::ABL2 fusion creates constitutively active tyrosine kinase driving B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia. P2RY8 (0.658, purinergic receptor) and NUP214 (0.576, nucleoporin) suggest nuclear/cytosolic shuttling.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (138.5 nTPM) and lymphoid tissue (107.6 nTPM). Detected in many tissues with low cancer specificity but cell type-enhanced expression, consistent with specialized cytoskeletal functions in contractile and immune cells.\n\nCLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: ClinVar reports 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains/losses (1q21.1-25.3, 1q23.3-24.3), duplications, deletions, and splice site variants (c.109-8C>G, c.475-8_475-5del). While specific monogenic diseases are not annotated, the RCSD1::ABL2 fusion defines a distinct B-ALL subtype responsive to tyrosine kinase inhibitors (imatinib).\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: Recent literature reveals emerging roles: (1) RCSD1::ABL2 fusion drives B-ALL via constitutive kinase activity—patients achieve sustained remission with TKI therapy. (2) Pan-cancer analysis links RCSD1 expression to immune infiltration and prognosis across tumor types. (3) Low RCSD1 predicts poor lung adenocarcinoma survival. Mechanistically, RCSD1 likely functions as an actin regulatory scaffold: the FAM21/CAPZIP domain recruits CapZ heterodimers to specific cellular locations, potentially coordinating with the WASH complex for endosomal actin polymerization. In stress conditions, MAPKAPK2/3 phosphorylation may modulate RCSD1's capping activity, coupling cytoskeletal dynamics to p38 signaling. The oncogenic fusion replaces regulatory domains with ABL2's kinase domain, creating uncontrolled actin remodeling that promotes leukemic transformation.
