## Description
DPP3 (Dipeptidyl peptidase 3, also known as Dipeptidyl aminopeptidase 3 or ECP32, Q9NY33) encodes a 737 aa cytosolic zinc-dependent metallopeptidase functioning as an exopeptidase that sequentially cleaves dipeptides from the N-terminus of oligopeptides including opioid peptides (enkephalins, endomorphins), angiotensin peptides, and globin degradation products, serving critical roles in pain modulation, blood pressure regulation, and protein turnover.
