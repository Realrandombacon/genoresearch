## Description
SNTG2 (Gamma-2-syntrophin, 539 aa, UniProt Q9NY99) is a partially characterized PDZ domain-containing scaffold protein representing an understudied component of the dystrophin-glycoprotein complex (DGC) with emerging roles in cytoskeletal organization, membrane scaffolding, cancer prognosis, and bone metabolism, with only 30 published papers confirming understudied status despite important structural function. This gene has clinical significance with 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants at chromosome 2p25.3.
