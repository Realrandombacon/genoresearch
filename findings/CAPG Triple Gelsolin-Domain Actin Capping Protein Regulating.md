## Description
CAPG (Capping actin protein of gelsolin family, also known as Macrophage-capping protein or Gelsolin-like capping protein, P40121) encodes a 348 aa intracellular protein functioning as a calcium-dependent actin filament capping protein that regulates actin polymerization dynamics, cell motility, phagocytosis, and macrophage activation critical for immune response, wound healing, and cancer metastasis. Structural architecture reveals a compact triple-domain organization built from gelsolin repeats: three Gelsolin-like domains (IPR007123/PF00626/SM00262, positions 30-107/147-221/267-340) forming the characteristic gelsolin fold, belonging to the Villin/Gelsolin family (IPR007122/PTHR11977, positions 12-347/12-347) with GO term for actin filament binding (GO:0051015), ADF-H/Gelsolin-like domain superfamily (IPR029006/G3DSA:3.40.20.10, positions 1-119/133-238/241-348) providing the structural scaffold for calcium-regulated actin interaction, and CDD gelsolin sub-domains (cd11289/cd11290/cd11292, positions 137-225/17-120/251-346) completing the functional architecture; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 86.4, high confidence) confirms a well-folded compact protein with organized triple gelsolin domain arrangement consistent with stable actin-binding protein requiring precise geometry for filament end recognition and capping. STRING interactions define a comprehensive actin cytoskeleton regulatory network: myotrophin MTPN (0.754, highest confidence) regulating actin filament bundling and muscle differentiation, alpha-adducins ADD2 (0.744) and ADD1 (0.741) promoting spectrin-actin assembly at membrane skeleton, capping protein subunits CAPZB (0.690), CAPZA2 (0.599), CAPZA1 (0.572), and CAPZA3 (0.560) forming heterodimeric barbed-end cappers, cofilins CFL1 (0.640) and CFL2 (0.527) severing and depolymerizing actin filaments, and cysteine-rich protein CRIP1 (0.598) linking to zinc homeostasis and development. HPA localizes CAPG to nucleoplasm and mitotic chromosome with low tissue specificity detected in all tissues indicating housekeeping cytoskeletal function, group-enriched expression in blood cells reflecting macrophage-specific role, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, and low cancer specificity; classified as plasma protein, predicted intracellular protein with actin capping and actin-binding molecular functions. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including synonymous variant c.132G>A (p.Ser44=), missense variant c.665A>G (p.Gln222Arg) in the second gelsolin domain potentially affecting calcium binding or actin interaction, multiple chromosome 2 copy number losses at 2p25.1-q13 (chr2:11504318-111365996x1), 2p12-11.2 (chr2:82486900-87322042, chr2:81209244-86688030x1, chr2:77907114-87330965x1, chr2:74527522-89125488x1), copy number gain at 2p25.3-q37.3 (chr2:1-243199373), single allele duplication and deletion; these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and disease susceptibility. Literature establishes CAPG as essential regulator of actin dynamics where it caps filament barbed ends preventing monomer addition/loss, severs existing filaments in calcium-dependent manner similar to gelsolin but with distinct regulation, promotes macrophage motility and phagocytic cup formation during pathogen engulfment, facilitates cell migration through lamellipodia dynamics critical for wound healing and cancer invasion, localizes to mitotic chromosomes suggesting role in cell division mechanics, circulates in plasma as potential biomarker for inflammatory conditions, and functions redundantly yet distinctly from gelsolin (GSN) with tissue-specific expression patterns; high AlphaFold confidence (86.4) reflects strong evolutionary constraint on gelsolin fold; blood cell enrichment correlates with macrophage function; conservation from mouse (P24452, 352 aa), rat (Q6AYC4, 349 aa), and bovine (Q865V6, 349 aa) confirms essential cytoskeletal function across mammals.
