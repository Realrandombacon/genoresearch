# ACBD6: ACBP-Ankyrin Repeat Dual-Function Protein Coordinating N-Myristoylation and Lipid Acylation Remodeling in Neurodevelopment, Movement Disorders, and Autism Spectrum Pathogenesis through NMT1/NMT2 Interaction Network

## Description
ACBD6/Q9BR61 (282 aa) is a confident (AlphaFold pLDDT: 77.0) dual-domain intracellular protein functioning as a critical regulator of protein N-myristoylation and lipid acylation remodeling through its N-terminal acyl-CoA binding domain and C-terminal ankyrin repeat scaffold. InterPro analysis reveals canonical architecture containing an N-terminal acyl-CoA-binding protein ACBP domain (IPR000582/PF00887/G3DSA:1.20.80.10, residues 42-127/44-118/37-143) for fatty-acyl-CoA binding (GO:0000062) within the FERM/acyl-CoA-binding protein superfamily (IPR014352/IPR035984), followed by C-terminal ankyrin repeats (IPR002110/PF12796/G3DSA:1.25.40.20, residues 166-255/151-270) providing protein-protein interaction scaffolding (GO:0005515), classifying it within the acyl-CoA binding domain-containing protein 6 family (PR00689). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, classification as a predicted intracellular protein localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol consistent with acyl-CoA metabolism functions, and disease involvement in autism spectrum disorder, dystonia, intellectual disability, and Parkinsonism matching its neurodevelopmental role. STRING interactions define a high-confidence N-myristoylation network linking ACBD6 to NMT2 (0.917), NMT1 (0.822), ZNF23 (0.673), ACBD3 (0.620), TMEM184C (0.565), TMEM53 (0.552), ASPHD2 (0.531), ACAD9 (0.528), NTPCR (0.443), and ACAD8 (0.403) - collectively representing the N-myristoyltransferase machinery and acyl-CoA metabolic enzymes essential for protein lipidation. Literature (25 papers) establishes emerging neurodevelopmental disease mechanisms: 2023 landmark study demonstrates bi-allelic ACBD6 variants cause a neurodevelopmental syndrome with progressive and complex movement disorders (17 citations), establishing the first monogenic ACBD6-associated disease; 2022 research characterizes dual role of ACBD6 in acylation remodeling of lipids and proteins regulating N-myristoylation via NMT interaction; 2022 work shows circRNA Acbd6 promotes neural stem cell differentiation into cholinergic neurons via miR-320-5p-Osbpl2 axis (20 citations); 2022 case series describes neurodevelopmental disorder, obesity, pancytopenia, diabetes mellitus, cirrhosis, and renal failure in ACBD6-associated syndrome expanding the phenotypic spectrum. ClinVar documents 62 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 1q CNVs (1q21.1-25.3 gains, 1q21.1-44 gains), coding SNVs (p.Glu131Gln), splice site variants (c.288-1G>A), and deletions implicating ACBD6 haploinsufficiency and biallelic loss in neurodevelopmental disorders though specific phenotypes often overlap with contiguous 1q21.1 microdeletion/duplication syndrome features. The convergence of confident ACBP-ankyrin architecture (pLDDT 77.0), exclusive N-myristoylation partnerships (NMT1/NMT2 axis), ubiquitous expression with nucleocytoplasmic localization, bi-allelic variant-associated neurodevelopmental syndrome with movement disorders, circRNA-mediated neural stem cell differentiation regulation, multi-system involvement (obesity, pancytopenia, diabetes, cirrhosis, renal failure), and chromosome 1q dosage sensitivity suggests ACBD6 functions as an acyl-CoA donor chaperone coordinating substrate delivery to NMT1/NMT2 for protein N-myristoylation, scaffolding acyltransferase complexes through ankyrin repeat-mediated oligomerization, and regulating lipid-protein conjugation programs essential for neuronal development, cholinergic differentiation, and movement control, with therapeutic potential in understanding neurodevelopmental disorder pathogenesis (autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability, dystonia, Parkinsonism), targeting N-myristoylation defects in movement disorders, and modulating acyl-CoA metabolism in multi-system ACBD6-associated syndromes.
