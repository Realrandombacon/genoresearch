## Description
CELA3B (Chymotrypsin-Like Elastase Family Member 3B, also known as pancreatic elastase 3B or elastase-3B, UniProt P08861) is a 270 aa secreted serine protease belonging to the trypsin/chymotrypsin elastase family that functions in pancreatic protein digestion through peptide bond hydrolysis. InterPro analysis reveals canonical trypsin-fold serine protease architecture: Trypsin domain (IPR001254, positions 28-268; PF00089, positions 29-263; G3DSA:2.40.10.10, positions 43-264) within peptidase S1 PA clan superfamily (IPR009003, positions 25-267), chymotrypsin family signatures (IPR001314, positions 59-74/119-133/210-222; PR00722), elastase subfamily membership (IPR050850, positions 4-267), and conserved catalytic triad residues: histidine active site (IPR018114, positions 69-74; PS00134) and serine active site (IPR033116, positions 211-222; PS00135). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.8), indicating an exceptionally well-folded trypsin-fold β-barrel structure with stable catalytic pocket. HPA data confirms extreme pancreas-enriched expression (46793.9 nTPM, tissue enriched), detected in many tissues, cytosol/secreted localization, classified as enzyme/hydrolase/protease/serine protease with cancer-enriched expression pattern. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with pancreatic digestive enzyme complex: CPB1 (0.909, carboxypeptidase B1), CEL (0.892, bile salt-dependent lipase), CTRL (0.841, chymotrypsin-like protease), CELA2A (0.807, elastase 2A), CTRB1 (0.795, chymotrypsinogen B1), CELA2B (0.770, elastase 2B), CPA1 (0.769, carboxypeptidase A1), PPY (0.761, pancreatic polypeptide), CTRC (0.745, chymotrypsin C), CPA2 (0.462, carboxypeptidase A2). ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 1p36.33-36.12 affecting the CELA3B locus. Literature (39 papers) establishes CELA3B roles in: (1) novel fecal pancreatic elastase-3B isoform-specific immunoassay for exocrine pancreatic insufficiency (EPI) detection with improved sensitivity (2025), (2) highly specific immunostaining marker for pancreatic acinar cell carcinoma diagnosis (2023, 3 citations), (3) loss-of-function variant association with non-alcoholic chronic pancreatitis susceptibility (2022, 13 citations), and (4) childhood pancreatitis risk modulation via CELA3B variant interactions with other pancreatitis-related genes (2023). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CELA3B serves as a pancreas-specific digestive serine protease that hydrolyzes peptide bonds in dietary proteins (preferentially cleaving after small neutral amino acids like alanine, glycine, valine) within the duodenal lumen, contributing to protein digestion and nutrient absorption while serving as a clinical biomarker for pancreatic exocrine function. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The trypsin-fold β-barrel structure positions the catalytic triad (His69, Asp102, Ser211) for nucleophilic attack on peptide carbonyl carbons. CELA3B is synthesized as an inactive zymogen (proelastase) in pancreatic acinar cells, secreted into the pancreatic duct, and activated by trypsin cleavage in the duodenum. The elastase substrate specificity pocket accommodates small neutral amino acid side chains, distinguishing CELA3B from trypsin (basic residues) and chymotrypsin (aromatic residues). In EPI, reduced CELA3B secretion causes decreased fecal elastase levels, enabling non-invasive diagnosis. In acinar cell carcinoma, CELA3B overexpression provides tumor-specific immunostaining for pathological diagnosis. Loss-of-function CELA3B variants impair protein digestion and may trigger pancreatitis through zymogen accumulation and acinar cell stress. The 8 ClinVar CNVs indicate CELA3B dosage sensitivity may contribute to pancreatic disorders. Extreme pancreas enrichment (46793.9 nTPM) confirms CELA3B as a pancreas-specific digestive enzyme with minimal extra-pancreatic expression.
