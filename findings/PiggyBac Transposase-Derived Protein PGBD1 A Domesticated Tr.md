# PiggyBac Transposase-Derived Protein PGBD1: A Domesticated Transposable Element Functioning as a SCAN Domain Zinc Finger Transcriptional Regulator in Neuronal Paraspeckles and Neuropsychiatric Disease

**Date:** 2026-06-28T13:36:40.275113

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
PGBD1 (PiggyBac Transposable Element Derived 1) is an 809-amino acid protein representing a domesticated transposable element that has been repurposed for host cellular functions, characterized by an N-terminal SRCR domain (IPR001190/PS50287, positions 2-44) for potential membrane association or protein-protein interactions, a central SCAN domain (IPR003309/IPR038269/PF02023/PS50804, 40-152; SM00431, 40-152) mediating homo- and hetero-oligomerization with other zinc finger proteins, and a C-terminal PiggyBac transposase domain (IPR029526/PF13843, 418-775) derived from the piggyBac transposable element family (IPR052638/PTHR47055, 55-809) within the IS4 transposase superfamily. STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network with other SCAN domain-containing zinc finger proteins: ZKSCAN4 (0.840), SCAND1 (0.837), ZNF446 (0.835), ZSCAN18 (0.834), ZNF396 (0.831), ZNF24 (0.831), ZSCAN32 (0.828), ZKSCAN3 (0.828), ZSCAN20 (0.787), and ZSCAN1 (0.786), all at high confidence, indicating PGBD1 functions as part of a transcriptional regulatory complex through SCAN-mediated oligomerization. HPA data shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, immune cell-enhanced expression, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol, classified as a predicted intracellular protein. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning 6p25.3-q27 and 6p25.3-12.3 regions, deletions, and intronic variants (c.773-49C>T/A), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in neuropsychiatric disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q96JS3-F1) predicts medium confidence folding (pLDDT 65.8) consistent with a partially structured protein containing ordered SCAN and transposase-derived domains coupled with flexible linker regions typical of transcriptional regulators. With only 20 papers, PGBD1 represents a 'gray' gene transitioning from dark: while GWAS studies have linked the 6p22.1 region containing PGBD1 to schizophrenia and Alzheimer's disease susceptibility (though Japanese cohort studies found no direct association), a breakthrough 2022 study identified PGBD1 as a mammal-specific gene controlling neuronal paraspeckles—nuclear bodies involved in RNA editing and retention—suggesting a role in post-transcriptional gene regulation unique to mammals. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that PGBD1 functions as a domesticated transposase that has lost DNA cleavage activity but retained DNA-binding capability, using its SCAN domain to oligomerize with other zinc finger proteins (ZKSCAN/ZSCAN family) forming sequence-specific transcriptional repressor complexes that regulate target genes involved in neuronal development and immune responses, while its paraspeckle localization suggests additional roles in retaining edited RNAs (particularly A-to-I edited transcripts) within nuclear bodies to control their cytoplasmic availability, with dysfunction contributing to neuropsychiatric disease phenotypes through aberrant gene expression programs and RNA processing defects.
