## Description
LOC127458760 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 8 (genomic coordinates: 14802601-14803333 on NC_000008.11, spanning approximately 732 bp in GRCh38; GRCh37 coordinates: 14660111-14660843). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in naive human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with two master pluripotency transcription factors: OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU domain class 5 homeobox 1) and NANOG (NK2 homeobox protein). The element is marked by canonical active enhancer histone modifications: H3K27ac (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation) and H3K4me1 (histone H3 lysine 4 monomethylation), confirming its identity as a transcriptionally active enhancer in the naive pluripotency state. A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. Chromosome 8 contains critical developmental genes and oncogenes (e.g., MYC at 8q24, multiple tumor suppressors at 8p). As a naive hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4 and NANOG with active histone marks, LOC127458760 likely functions to maintain transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and naive pluripotency maintenance, potentially regulating genes involved in self-renewal, ground state pluripotency, and epigenetic reprogramming.
