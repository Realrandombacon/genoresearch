## Description
SLA2 (Src-Like Adaptor 2, UniProt Q9H6Q3) is a 261 aa cytoplasmic adaptor protein belonging to the Grb2-like family that functions in clathrin-mediated endocytosis, actin cytoskeleton regulation, and immune receptor signaling. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal SH3 domain (IPR001452/PF00018/G3DSA:2.30.30.40, positions 32-92) for proline-rich motif binding and a C-terminal SH2 domain (IPR000980/PF00017/G3DSA:3.30.505.10, positions 92-191) for phosphotyrosine recognition, forming a compact modular signaling scaffold (IPR043539/PR00401/PS50001, positions 27-231). Literature (156 papers) establishes SLA2 roles in: (1) SLA2 as core interaction hub for clathrin light chain and Pan1/End3/Sla1 complex in endocytic mid-coat adaptor function (2025), (2) SLA2 as prognostic marker in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC) correlating with immune cell infiltration in tumor microenvironment (2023, 7 citations), (3) actin-generated force measurement during endocytosis using SLA2-based FRET tension sensors (2021, 23 citations), (4) SLA2 linking membrane to actin cytoskeleton during clathrin-mediated endocytosis. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (17.0 nTPM) and bone marrow (11.7 nTPM), group-enriched blood cell specificity, nucleoplasm/Golgi apparatus/vesicles localization, consistent with immune cell signaling and endocytic trafficking functions. STRING interactions reveal endocytosis and immune signaling network: CBL (0.975) - E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase for receptor downregulation; HIP1R (0.946) - huntingtin-interacting protein 1-related for actin-endocytosis coupling; SLMAP (0.792) - sarcolemma-associated protein; TLN1 (0.785) and TLN2 (0.785) - talin-1/2 for integrin-actin linkage; HTT (0.741) - huntingtin protein; CLTC (0.733) - clathrin heavy chain for vesicle coat formation; EGFR (0.703) - epidermal growth factor receptor; SNAP91 (0.703) - synaptojanin-1-binding protein for endocytosis; ZAP70 (0.702) - zeta-chain-associated protein kinase 70 for T-cell receptor signaling. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 20q11.21-13.12 (chr20:31010829-44560369)x1, 20q11.22-13.12 (chr20:35237946-47631818)x1, 20q11.21-11.23 (chr20:33432363-36821881)x1 and gains at 20p13-q13.33 (chr20:61569-62915555)x3, 20q11.21-11.23 (chr20:29652122-35603726)x3, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 75.7) indicating well-folded SH2-SH3 tandem domain architecture typical of functional adaptor proteins. Conservation: mouse Sla2 (Q8R4L0) is 259 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: SLA2 functions as a modular adaptor that couples phosphotyrosine signaling to actin cytoskeleton remodeling during clathrin-mediated endocytosis and immune receptor activation. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) SH2 domain binds phosphorylated tyrosine residues on activated receptors (EGFR, TCR complex via ZAP70), (2) SH3 domain recruits proline-rich partners including HIP1R for actin polymerization and CBL for ubiquitination-mediated receptor downregulation, (3) SLA2-HIP1R-CLTC complex generates mechanical force via actin polymerization to drive clathrin-coated pit invagination, (4) In T-cells, SLA2-ZAP70 interaction modulates TCR signaling threshold and immune synapse formation, (5) SLA2-HTT interaction links endocytosis to Huntington disease pathways. In HNSCC, SLA2 expression correlates with immune cell infiltration, suggesting role in tumor immune evasion. Pathogenic CNVs at 20q11-13 disrupt SLA2 dosage, potentially causing endocytic defects and immune dysregulation. SLA2 represents an understudied adaptor protein linking endocytosis, actin dynamics, and immune signaling.
