## Description
FAM221B (alias C9orf128, family with sequence similarity 221 member B) encodes a 402 amino acid partially structured intracellular protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 63.7) containing a FAM221A/B family domain (IPR026755, aa 162-401), FAM221A/B Pfam domain (PF14753, aa 241-393), and belonging to the PTHR31214 family (protein FAM221A-related, aa 162-401). HPA shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (8.3 nTPM) and bone marrow (2.7 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, predicted intracellular protein class, cancer-enhanced specificity, immune cell-enhanced blood cell pattern, and group-enriched single cell type. ClinVar contains 72 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 9p24.3-q21.13, 9p24.3-13.1, 9p13.3-13.1, and 9p24.3-q34.3 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence network with autophagy initiation complex proteins: ATG13 (autophagy related 13, autophagosome formation, 0.680), ATG101 (autophagy related 101, ATG13 stabilization, 0.669), TMEM202 (transmembrane protein 202, autolysosome formation, 0.646), ULK1 (unc-51 like autophagy activating kinase 1, autophagy initiation, 0.608), RB1CC1 (RB1 inducible coiled-coil 1, FIP200 scaffold, 0.543), C7orf57 (chromosome 7 ORF 57, 0.527), FNDC8 (fibronectin type III domain containing 8, 0.480), CABCOCO1 (coiled-coil coactivator, 0.477), SPAG8 (sperm associated antigen 8, 0.447), and SPAAR (small peptide upstream of ATG15, 0.445). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The FAM221B domain architecture combined with high-confidence interactions with core autophagy machinery strongly suggests FAM221B functions as an autophagy initiation complex scaffold protein involved in autophagosome formation and nutrient stress response. ATG13 interaction (0.680) is mechanistically central—ATG13 forms a stable complex with ULK1 and FIP200 (RB1CC1) to initiate autophagy, recruiting the kinase complex to isolation membranes. ATG101 association (0.669) reinforces this role—ATG101 stabilizes ATG13 and prevents its degradation, suggesting FAM221B may function as an ATG13 chaperone or complex assembly factor. ULK1 interaction (0.608) links FAM221B to the serine/threonine kinase that phosphorylates autophagy substrates including ATG13 and Beclin-1, placing FAM221B at the proximal signaling node of autophagy induction. RB1CC1 association (0.543) connects to FIP200 scaffold function—FIP200 recruits the ULK1 complex to omegasome precursors, suggesting FAM221B facilitates membrane tethering during phagophore nucleation. TMEM202 interaction (0.646) extends this role to autolysosome formation—TMEM202 regulates lysosomal acidification and autolysosome maturation, suggesting FAM221B coordinates early and late autophagy stages. Testis enrichment (8.3 nTPM) indicates specialized function in spermatogenesis, consistent with autophagy role in germ cell development and sperm maturation through clearance of damaged organelles and protein aggregates. Bone marrow expression (2.7 nTPM) suggests function in hematopoietic progenitor cells, potentially regulating stem cell quiescence and differentiation through autophagy-mediated metabolic adaptation. Cancer-enhanced expression indicates upregulation in tumor cells, consistent with autophagy role in cancer cell survival under nutrient stress and chemotherapy resistance. Immune cell-enhanced pattern suggests function in lymphocytes and myeloid cells, potentially regulating immune cell activation and cytokine production through autophagy-dependent antigen presentation and inflammasome modulation. Group-enriched single cell pattern indicates specific upregulation in proliferating and metabolically active cell lineages. Moderate pLDDT score (63.7) indicates partially structured protein with flexible regions, consistent with intrinsically disordered scaffold proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly at the phagophore assembly site. Pathogenic 9p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting autophagy initiation complex assembly, autophagosome formation, and cellular stress response, potentially contributing to 9p deletion syndrome (9p- syndrome, Alfi syndrome) phenotypes including growth retardation, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, and genitourinary defects through impaired autophagy-mediated protein homeostasis and organelle clearance in developing tissues.
