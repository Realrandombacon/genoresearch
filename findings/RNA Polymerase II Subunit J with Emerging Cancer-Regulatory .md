## Description
POLR2J encodes a 117-amino acid subunit (UniProt P52435) of RNA polymerase II, the enzyme responsible for mRNA synthesis in eukaryotes. This small but essential protein forms a heterodimeric core assembly unit within the 12-subunit Pol II complex. InterPro analysis reveals conserved domains including IPR008193 (Rpb11 conserved site, aa 35-66), IPR009025 (dimerization domain, aa 31-103), and IPR037685 (RNA polymerase RBP11 family, aa 14-105), all associated with DNA-directed RNA polymerase activity (GO:0003899) and transcription by RNA polymerase II (GO:0006366). AlphaFold prediction shows a well-folded structure with very high confidence (pLDDT: 94.2), consistent with its role as a stable core subunit.\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals exclusive high-confidence partnerships (all scores 0.999) with other Pol II subunits: POLR2A (largest catalytic subunit), POLR2B, POLR2C, POLR2D, POLR2F, POLR2G, POLR2H, POLR2I, POLR2K, and POLR2L—confirming its integration into the core transcription machinery. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic localization, consistent with housekeeping transcription functions.\n\nHowever, emerging literature (27 papers) reveals non-canonical roles: POLR2J promotes glioblastoma malignancy via STAT3 signaling and oxidative stress regulation (2024), and POLR2J knockdown induces ROS-mediated DNA damage response and ferroptosis through STAT3-GPX4 axis inhibition in lung adenocarcinoma (2026). ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (e.g., p.Arg106Trp) and copy number variations, suggesting clinical relevance beyond basic transcription. This positions POLR2J as a partially-characterized gene with emerging extra-transcriptional oncogenic functions—a 'moonlighting' subunit that may couple transcriptional machinery to cancer signaling pathways.
