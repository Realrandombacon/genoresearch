## Description
KRTAP7-1 (keratin associated protein 7-1, alias KAP7.1) is a 87 amino acid predicted intracellular keratin-associated protein (UniProt Q8IUC3) with low-confidence intrinsically disordered architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 36.6) containing canonical KRTAP7 family architecture including Keratin-associated protein 7 family domain (IPR020184, residues 1-87) forming a 87 aa module (~100% of protein) characteristic of the KRTAP7 subfamily that mediates keratin filament cross-linking, KRTAP type 7 family Pfam domain (PF15034, residues 1-84) forming a 84 aa module (~97%) defining the KRTAP7 signature, KERATIN-ASSOCIATED PROTEIN 7-1 family (PTHR38504, residues 1-87) encompassing the full protein (100%) placing KRTAP7-1 within the keratin-associated protein superfamily, and molecular functions including intermediate filament association and hair shaft formation, functioning as a skin-enriched intrinsically disordered keratin-associated protein that regulates hair shaft formation through cysteine/serine-rich domain-mediated keratin filament cross-linking via partnerships with KRTAP19-3, KRTAP24-1, KRTAP13-3, KRTAP8-1, KRTAP20-1, KRTAP6-2, KRTAP11-1, KRTAP15-1, KRTAP20-2, and KRTAP13-1.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals KRTAP7-1 contains canonical KRTAP7 architecture: The Keratin-associated protein 7 family domain (residues 1-87) encompasses the entire protein, characteristic of small ultra-high sulfur (UHS) or high sulfur (HS) keratin-associated proteins that are rich in cysteine and serine residues for disulfide bond formation. The low-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 36.6) indicates an intrinsically disordered protein, consistent with KAPs that function as flexible cross-linkers between keratin intermediate filaments rather than forming stable globular domains.
