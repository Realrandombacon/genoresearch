## Description
MOV10 (Moloney Leukemia Virus 10) is a 1003 amino acid Superfamily 1 (SF1) RNA helicase that functions as a critical host restriction factor against retrotransposons and viruses, mediating LINE-1 retrotransposition suppression, RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC) assembly, nonsense-mediated mRNA decay (NMD), and neuronal translation regulation through DEXXQ/H-box helicase-dependent ATP hydrolysis and 5'-3' RNA unwinding activity. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic SF1 helicase architecture including N-terminal MOV-10 domain (IPR049075, residues 11-76) for unique functional specificity, Ig-like domain (IPR049077, residues 114-241) for protein-protein interactions, helical domain (IPR049079, residues 291-357) for structural stability, DEXXQ/H-box helicase domain (IPR026122, cd18038, residues 498-733) performing 5'-3' RNA helicase activity (GO:0032574) and RNA binding (GO:0003723), DNA2/NAM7 helicase domains (IPR041677, IPR041679, residues 499-569/610-690/700-921) providing helicase core scaffold, C-terminal Upf1-like helicase domain (IPR047187, cd18808, residues 734-939) for NMD pathway integration, and P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (IPR027417, G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 481-948) for ATP-dependent unwinding. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9HCE1-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 81.1) across the 1003 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded multi-domain helicase with structured catalytic core suitable for RNA substrate processing and RISC complex integration. HPA data classifies MOV10 as an intracellular enzyme with helicase, hydrolase, and RNA-binding molecular functions involved in host-virus interaction, RNA-mediated gene silencing, transcription, and transcription regulation, cytosolic localization reflecting RISC and NMD machinery, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with post-transcriptional gene regulation. STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to RNA silencing and decay machinery: DICER1 (Dicer 1 Ribonuclease III, 0.991) as primary miRNA biogenesis partner; TNRC6B (Trinucleotide Repeat Containing Adaptor 6B, 0.988) for RISC effector function; UPF1 (Up-Frameshift 1, 0.912) as NMD master regulator; FMR1 (Fragile X Mental Retardation Protein 1, 0.636) for neuronal translation control; APOBEC3G (Apolipoprotein B mRNA Editing Catalytic Polypeptide-Like 3G, 0.532) as antiviral restriction factor; CBX6 (Chromobox 6, 0.522) for Polycomb repression; AGO1 (Argonaute 1, 0.504) and AGO2 (0.482) as RISC core components; PUM2 (Pumilio 2, 0.421) for mRNA stability control; and TNRC6A (0.418) as related adaptor. ClinVar lists 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses on 1p13.3-13.1, 1p21.3-13.2, 1p21.2-12 (spanning 6-27 Mb regions), deletions (c.1296-65_1296del, chr1:112318699-115576848, chr1:111145905-114454813), and copy number gains on 1p13.3-11.2, 1p22.1-11.2, 1p36.33-q44, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in neurodevelopmental disorder and cancer susceptibility. Literature (189 papers) establishes MOV10 as a well-characterized RNA helicase: maximal inhibitory effect on LINE-1 retrotransposition requires both MOV10/LINE-1 association and granule formation revealing restriction mechanism; MOV10 suppresses fear memory and dendritic arborization while regulating microtubule dynamics in hippocampal neurons demonstrating neuronal function; S100A16 stabilizes ITGA3-mediated ECM-receptor interaction pathway to drive lung adenocarcinoma malignant properties via binding MOV10 establishing cancer role; MOV10 interplay with nonsense-mediated mRNA decay factors confirms NMD pathway integration; and MOV10 association with FMRP and AGO2 in postnatal brain links to learning and memory regulation. As a well-studied RNA helicase with multifaceted functions, MOV10 represents a critical node connecting retrotransposon restriction to RNA silencing, NMD, neuronal development, antiviral immunity, and cancer progression through DEXXQ/H-box helicase-dependent RNA unwinding, RISC complex assembly via AGO/TNRC6 interactions, and LINE-1 granule-mediated restriction disrupted in neurodevelopmental disorders and malignancy.
