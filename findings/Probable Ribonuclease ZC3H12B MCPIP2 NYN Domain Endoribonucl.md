## Description
ZC3H12B (Zinc Finger CCCH-Type Containing 12B, also known as MCPIP2) is a large 836-amino acid intracellular ribonuclease with 14 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status as the most enigmatic member of the ZC3H12/MCPIP family. The protein belongs to the ZC3H12/N4BP1 ribonuclease and regulator family (IPR051101, positions 4-830; PTHR12876, positions 4-830) containing a canonical NYN/PIN domain ribonuclease catalytic core (IPR021869, positions 190-345; PF11977, positions 190-345; cd18729, positions 193-323; G3DSA:3.40.50.11980, positions 189-351), CCCH-type zinc finger RNA-binding domain (IPR000571, positions 355-380; PS50103, positions 355-380; GO:0046872 metal ion binding), N-terminal UBA-like domain (IPR040546, positions 108-149; PF18039, positions 108-149) for protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal Regnase-1/ZC3H12 domain (IPR040757, positions 795-830; PF18561, positions 795-830) of unknown function. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 58.6, medium confidence), consistent with ordered catalytic domains interspersed with flexible linker regions typical of RNA-binding ribonucleases. HPA reveals low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, not detected in cancer or immune cells, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, with annotated molecular functions as endonuclease, hydrolase, and nuclease, and subcellular localization to nucleoli fibrillar center and Golgi apparatus. ClinVar documents 121 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly Xq11.2, Xq11.1-21.1, and Xp22.33-q28 copy number losses and gains with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong X-chromosome dosage sensitivity potentially affecting RNA metabolism programs. STRING interactions reveal ZC3H12B functions within the ribonuclease and RNA regulation network with medium confidence connections to RC3H2 (0.550) - ring finger and CCCH-type zinc finger domains protein 2 paralog, RC3H1 (0.470) - ring finger and CCCH-type zinc finger domains protein 1 (Roquin-1), IER3 (0.448) - immediate early response 3 apoptosis regulator, TJAP1 (0.434) - tight junction-associated protein 1, and SARNP (0.404) - SAP domain-containing ribonucleoprotein. Recent studies (2019-2021) established ZC3H12B as active ribonuclease member of ZC3H12 family distinct from Regnase-1/MCPIP1, M2 macrophage-derived exosomal microRNA-155-5p promoting colon cancer immune escape by downregulating ZC3H12B, and extracellular vesicle-derived miR-320a targeting ZC3H12B to inhibit tumorigenesis, invasion, and angiogenesis in ovarian cancer. We hypothesize ZC3H12B functions as an NYN domain endoribonuclease that degrades specific mRNA targets through the catalytic NYN/PIN domain (positions 190-345) with RNA substrate recognition mediated by the CCCH zinc finger (positions 355-380), analogous to the well-characterized Regnase-1/ZC3H12A mechanism. The UBA-like domain (positions 108-149) may mediate protein-protein interactions with ubiquitination machinery or RNA-binding partners. The C-terminal domain (positions 795-830) may regulate catalytic activity or subcellular trafficking. Interaction with RC3H1/Roquin-1 (0.470) suggests functional cooperation in post-transcriptional gene silencing, as Roquin-1 is a known mRNA decay regulator. The nucleolar localization indicates potential roles in ribosomal RNA processing or ribosome biogenesis regulation. The 121 ClinVar X-chromosome CNVs reflect strong purifying selection on this X-linked ribonuclease. ZC3H12B represents an uncharacterized endoribonuclease linking NYN domain catalytic activity with cancer immune escape (miR-155-5p target in colon cancer), ovarian cancer tumorigenesis suppression (miR-320a target), and X-linked dosage-sensitive RNA metabolism through CCCH zinc finger-mediated substrate recognition and mRNA degradation mechanisms.
