## Description
COMMD7 encodes COMM domain-containing protein 7 (200 aa, Q86VX2), a small intracellular protein functioning in transcriptional regulation, NF-κB signaling, and cancer progression as part of the multi-subunit COMMD complex. InterPro analysis reveals conserved COMM domain architecture: COMM domain (IPR017920, positions 131-200; PF07258, positions 131-192; PS51269 profile, positions 133-200) defining family identity and protein-protein interactions; HN domain (PF21672, positions 12-113) forming N-terminal structural module; COMMD7-specific family (IPR037358, positions 19-198); COMMD4/6/7/8 family (IPR047155, positions 5-194) indicating functional clustering within COMMD family; CDD domain (cd04755, positions 19-198) spanning nearly entire protein.
