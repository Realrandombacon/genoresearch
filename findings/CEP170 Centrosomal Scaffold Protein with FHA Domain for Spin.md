## Description
CEP170 encodes a 1584 aa centrosomal protein of 170 kDa (UniProt Q5SW79), also known as KIAA0586-like, functioning as a large centrosomal scaffold protein that organizes microtubule nucleation complexes and regulates spindle orientation during mitosis. The protein contains an N-terminal forkhead-associated FHA domain (IPR000253/PF00498/SM00240, aa22-90) for phosphothreonine-dependent protein-protein interactions, a CEP170 C-terminal domain (IPR029300/PF15308, aa812-1505) unique to centrosomal scaffolds, belonging to the centrosomal and immune signaling modulators family (IPR051176/PTHR15715, aa867-1521) and SMAD/FHA domain superfamily (IPR008984/G3DSA:2.60.200.20, aa5-105). AlphaFold prediction shows low confidence (pLDDT 45.8), indicating a predominantly intrinsically disordered architecture typical of large centrosomal scaffold proteins that adopt structure upon binding partner proteins. HPA shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (85.1 nTPM), bone marrow (49.6 nTPM), and brain (22.8 nTPM), localizing to centrosome, basal body, and cytosol with cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal CEP170 forms the centrosome maturation complex: CEP128 (0.992, centrosomal protein 128), CNTRL/centriolin (0.984, centriole biogenesis), NIN/ninein (0.978, microtubule anchoring), CCDC68 (0.974, centrosomal coiled-coil), CCDC120 (0.972, centrosomal scaffold), KIF2A (0.962, kinesin-13 microtubule depolymerase), KIFC3 (0.897, kinesin-14), ODF2 (0.896, outer dense fiber protein), CNTLN/centriolin (0.879), CCDC61 (0.870, spindle pole body component). ClinVar contains 57 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple 1q43-44 copy number losses and gains, deletions, and splice variants (c.274+91A>G). Literature (89 papers) demonstrates CEP170 enhances clear cell renal cell carcinoma progression by promoting platelet aggregation and angiogenesis; METTL3-mediated m6A modifications of CEP170 regulate spindle orientation and esophageal cancer cell proliferation; NAT10 acetylates CEP170 mRNA enhancing translation efficiency in multiple myeloma; CEP170::RAD51B fusion represents alignment artifact from pseudogene rearrangements requiring diagnostic caution; CEP170 localizes to distal end of mother centrioles recruiting microtubule nucleation factors; CEP170 depletion causes spindle orientation defects and mitotic delay; and CEP170 interacts with microtubule plus-end tracking proteins. CEP170 functions as a centrosomal scaffold—its FHA domain binds phosphorylated centrosomal partners enabling assembly of microtubule nucleation complexes; the large disordered C-terminal region provides multiple interaction surfaces for recruiting kinesins, microtubule anchors, and centriole biogenesis factors. Cancer pathogenesis reflects CEP170 overexpression: elevated CEP170 enhances microtubule dynamics, promotes abnormal spindle orientation, and drives chromosomal instability in ccRCC, esophageal cancer, and multiple myeloma. 1q43-44 microdeletion syndrome may reflect CEP170 haploinsufficiency contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through centrosome dysfunction in neural progenitors. Testis enrichment suggests CEP170 role in spermatogenesis where centrosome-to-basal body conversion is critical. The disordered architecture enables CEP170 to function as a flexible scaffold accommodating multiple binding partners during centrosome maturation and spindle assembly checkpoint signaling.
