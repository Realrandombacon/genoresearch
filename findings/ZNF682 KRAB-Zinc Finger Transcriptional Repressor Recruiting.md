# ZNF682: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor Recruiting TRIM28/KAP1 Corepressor Complex

## Description
ZNF682 (Zinc Finger Protein 682, UniProt O95780) is a 498 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor with a canonical KRAB (Krüppel-associated box) repression domain at the N-terminus (residues 4-75, IPR001909/PF01352). The protein contains three tandem C2H2 zinc finger domains (residues 173-256, IPR013087/PF00096) arranged in a classical DNA-binding scaffold (G3DSA:3.30.160.60), enabling sequence-specific recognition of GC-rich promoter elements. STRING interaction analysis identifies TRIM28 (KAP1, score 0.585) as the primary binding partner - this is the canonical co-repressor that bridges KRAB zinc fingers to heterochromatin formation machinery including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1. A secondary interaction with VAV3 (score 0.400, RhoGEF oncogene) suggests potential crosstalk with Rho GTPase signaling pathways. HPA expression profiling shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with cancer-enhanced expression patterns and unexpected Golgi apparatus localization, potentially indicating non-canonical functions beyond nuclear transcriptional repression. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning 19p13.11-q13.2, indicating strong dosage sensitivity - gene amplification likely causes aberrant silencing of tumor suppressor targets. The mechanistic model proposes that ZNF682 functions as a sequence-specific epigenetic silencer: DNA binding via C2H2 fingers recruits TRIM28/KAP1 complex through the KRAB domain, which then oligomerizes and recruits SETDB1 to deposit H3K9me3 repressive marks, establishing facultative heterochromatin at target loci. Dysregulation through copy number gain may drive oncogenic transcriptional programs by inappropriately silencing cell cycle checkpoints or differentiation genes.
