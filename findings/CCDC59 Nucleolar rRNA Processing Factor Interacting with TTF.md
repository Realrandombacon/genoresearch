# CCDC59: Nucleolar rRNA Processing Factor Interacting with TTF1

## Description
CCDC59 (Coiled-coil domain containing 59, aliases BR22, HSPC128, TAP26) encodes a 241 amino acid essential nuclear protein localized to nucleoplasm and nucleoli rim. UniProt accession Q9P031. InterPro analysis reveals critical functional domains: IPR013730 (Fyv7/TAP26 family) with three repeats at positions 17-40, 44-71, 82-107; PF08524 (rRNA processing domain) spanning residues 125-239; and PTHR15657 (TAP26/FYV7/NST1 family). The rRNA processing domain strongly suggests CCDC59 functions in ribosomal RNA maturation within the nucleolus. STRING interactions confirm functional partnerships with TTF1/NKX2-1 (score 0.905, high confidence), LLPH (0.839), surfactant proteins SFTPB (0.809) and SFTPC (0.764), and ribosomal proteins MRPS31, MRPS18C, BRIX1, RPF1 (all 0.45-0.50 scores). The TTF1 interaction is mechanistically significant - TTF1 is a thyroid/lung transcription factor, and CCDC59 may serve as a co-regulator bridging transcriptional control with ribosome biogenesis. HPA classifies CCDC59 as an "essential protein" involved in transcription regulation, detected in all tissues with nucleolar rim localization consistent with rRNA processing function. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and a coding deletion (c.561_564+4del), indicating dosage sensitivity. The combination of nucleolar localization, rRNA processing domain, ribosomal protein interactions, and TTF1 partnership suggests CCDC59 operates at the interface of ribosome biogenesis and tissue-specific transcriptional programs, particularly in thyroid and lung where TTF1 targets are enriched.
