## Description
SLC66A2 encodes a 271 amino acid solute carrier family 66 member 2 (UniProt Q8N2U9), a member of the SLC66 family of lysosomal/vacuolar amino acid transporters. InterPro analysis confirms canonical PQ-loop transporter architecture including IPR006603 (PQ-loop repeat, aa 18-75 and 171-229), IPR052241 (SLC66/Scramblase ANY1 family, aa 7-267), PF04193 (PQ loop repeat, aa 18-75 and 171-229), PTHR14856 (SLC66 family, aa 7-267), and SM00679 (cystinosin-like repeat motif, aa 31-62 and 184-215), indicating a conserved mechanism for transporting small molecules across membranes via PQ-loop mediated transport. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8N2U9-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 85.6), consistent with a functional membrane-associated protein. HPA expression reveals liver-enhanced specificity (179.0 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced distribution across all tissues, and nucleoplasmic subcellular localization, suggesting a specialized role in hepatic nuclear transport or metabolism. STRING interactions identify 10 partners including ATP9B (0.658, P-type ATPase), FAM89B (0.595), SLC25A46 (0.529, mitochondrial membrane protein), ZNF516 (0.504, zinc finger transcription factor), and CTDP1 (0.498, transcription elongation factor), linking it to transcriptional regulation and organelle membrane dynamics. Critically, ClinVar contains a massive burden of 168 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large chromosome 18q copy number variations (gains and losses spanning 18q11-q23), though no specific disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature search returned only 1 paper (AML gene fusion detection), confirming SLC66A2 as a genuine 'very dark gene' with virtually no functional characterization. The combination of PQ-loop transporter domains, liver-enriched expression, nucleoplasmic localization, transcription factor interactions, and extraordinary 18q CNV burden suggests SLC66A2 functions as a nuclear envelope or nucleoplasmic transporter regulating hepatic gene expression or metabolite shuttling, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression contributing to the severe developmental phenotypes observed in 18q deletion/duplication syndromes.
