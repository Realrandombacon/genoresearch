## Description
MYCBP (Q99417) encodes a 103 amino acid nuclear and mitochondrial protein that functions as a transcription coactivator and c-Myc binding partner regulating MYC stability and transcriptional activity through leucine zipper-mediated heterodimerization, while also serving as an essential factor in germline stem cell renewal and differentiation conserved from Drosophila oogenesis to human reproduction and driving cervical cancer progression through the USP25-KIFC1 regulatory axis. InterPro analysis reveals a compact single-domain architecture built on leucine zipper motifs: a leucine zipper domain found in c-Myc-binding protein and similar proteins (cd21937, positions 42-94) forming a coiled-coil dimerization interface essential for MYC interaction; an Associate of Myc 1 family domain (IPR026060/PTHR13168, positions 7-101/7-101) defining the functional unit with transcription coactivator activity (GO:0003713) and regulation of DNA-templated transcription (GO:0006355); a c-Myc-binding protein domain (PF29056, positions 8-89) confirming direct MYC partnership; and C-Myc-binding protein PRINTS motifs (PR02028, positions 8-18/18-32/32-46) providing sequence signatures for family classification. AlphaFold predicts a high confidence structure (pLDDT 86.1) consistent with a well-folded small protein where the leucine zipper domain (positions 42-94) forms an alpha-helical coiled-coil suitable for heterodimerization with MYC or other bZIP partners, contrasting with intrinsically disordered transcription cofactors.
