# ZNF684: KRAB Domain C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Liver Expression and TRIM28 Interaction

**Date:** 2026-03-20T19:39:41.959423

## Description
ZNF684 encodes a 378-amino acid Kruppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q5T5D7). The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (Pfam PF01352, residues 7-48; InterPro IPR001909, residues 7-79) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (Pfam PF00096, residues 159-181, 187-209, 215-237; InterPro IPR013087). This canonical KRAB-C2H2 architecture mediates sequence-specific DNA recognition and transcriptional repression through heterochromatin recruitment.

HPA data shows liver-enhanced expression (12.0 nTPM), classified as tissue-enhanced with detection across all tissues. Subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol is consistent with nucleocytoplasmic shuttling typical of transcriptional regulators. As a transcription factor, ZNF684 likely functions in RNA polymerase II-mediated transcriptional regulation, with Alliance of Genome Resources predicting involvement in innate immune response and negative regulation of viral RNA replication.

STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners led by TRIM28 (score 0.659), the canonical KRAB domain cofactor that recruits HP1 heterochromatin protein and histone methyltransferases for epigenetic silencing. Additional partners include CCDC17 (0.650), PHETA1 (0.541), FOXD4L6 (0.507), and FBXO48 (0.471), suggesting roles in chromatin remodeling complexes and ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover. Several olfactory receptor interactions (OR5AP2, OR1L8, OR8B4) may reflect co-regulation of chemosensory gene clusters.

ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses and gains spanning 1p34.2 region and one inversion event. The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity of the 1p34 locus, though most variants are large chromosomal rearrangements affecting multiple genes.

Orthologs demonstrate strong conservation: Bos taurus (A0A3Q1MM01, 346 aa), Macaca fascicularis (Q4R6J4, 378 aa), and Propithecus coquereli (A0A2K6GNY5, 378 aa). The conserved KRAB-C2H2 architecture across mammals supports ZNF684 function as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor involved in liver metabolism, innate immunity, and epigenetic regulation via TRIM28-mediated heterochromatin formation.
