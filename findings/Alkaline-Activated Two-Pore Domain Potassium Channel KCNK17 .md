## Description
KCNK17 (UniProt Q96T54) encodes a 332 aa two-pore domain potassium channel (K2P) subfamily K member 17, also known as TASK-4 or TALK-2, functioning as an open rectifier potassium channel primarily passing outward current under physiological K+ concentrations and activated at alkaline pH.
