## Description
TNFRSF1A encodes Tumor Necrosis Factor Receptor 1 (TNFR1/p55), a type I transmembrane receptor for TNF-alpha. It is a central regulator of inflammation, apoptosis, and immune signaling. With 1,279 literature papers and established roles in diseases like IBD, Cystic Fibrosis, and cancer, TNFRSF1A is definitively a 'well-studied' gene and does not meet the criteria for a 'dark gene'.
