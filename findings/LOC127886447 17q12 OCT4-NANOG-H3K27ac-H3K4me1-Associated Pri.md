## Description
LOC127886447 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 17q12 (genomic coordinates: 31424918-31425645 on NC_000017.11, spanning approximately 727 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in primed human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU class 5 homeobox 1, master pluripotency transcription factor) and NANOG (homeobox protein NANOG, core pluripotency transcription factor) transcription factors and is marked by both H3K27ac (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation — active enhancer mark) and H3K4me1 (histone H3 lysine 4 monomethylation — enhancer lineage mark) histone modifications. The combination of OCT4-NANOG co-binding with dual H3K27ac-H3K4me1 marks represents the highest-confidence enhancer signature in this series, indicating direct regulation by the core pluripotency transcriptional network in an active chromatin state. A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. This absence of ClinVar entries is consistent with other hESC enhancers in this series and contrasts with silencers showing strong triplosensitivity or haploinsufficiency patterns. The 17q12 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including ERBB2 (receptor tyrosine kinase erbB-2 - breast cancer, cell proliferation), GRB7 (growth factor receptor-bound protein 7 - cell signaling), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a primed hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4-NANOG and marked by H3K27ac-H3K4me1, LOC127886447 likely functions as a direct target of the core pluripotency network to activate transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and lineage priming. The dual enhancer/silencer activity suggests context-dependent regulation, potentially activating pluripotency factors in primed stem cells while repressing differentiation genes in somatic lineages.
