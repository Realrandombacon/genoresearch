## Description
SPATS1 (Spermatogenesis-Associated Serine-Rich Protein 1, UniProt Q496A3) is a 300 aa intracellular nuclear protein belonging to the spermatogenesis-associated serine-rich protein 1 family (IPR029165/PF15160/PTHR35845, positions 29-299/51-288/29-299) that functions as a testis-enriched nucleoplasmic protein with non-essential role in spermatogenesis and male fertility, interacts with proteasome assembly chaperones and spermatogenesis-associated proteins, is associated with 6p21.1-25.3 copy number variant disorders, and shows evolutionary conservation across vertebrates including mammals and reptiles. The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal region (positions 1-28) for nuclear localization, central SPATS1 domain (positions 29-299, IPR029165) forming serine-rich intrinsically disordered region for protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal region (positions 289-300) for functional regulation. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 57.5, medium confidence) indicating mixed ordered and disordered regions—consistent with intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) that may function through conformational flexibility and dynamic protein interactions rather than rigid structural domains. Literature (10 papers) establishes SPATS1 roles in: (1) SPATS1 not essential for spermatogenesis and fertility in mouse (2021, 6 citations)—demonstrates SPATS1 is dispensable for male reproduction despite testis-enriched expression, (2) SPATS1 expression and characterization in Chinese soft-shelled turtle (Pelodiscus sinensis) with response to E2/MT treatment (2022, 4 citations)—confirms evolutionary conservation and hormonal regulation in reptiles, (3) SPATS1 differential expression in rat male meiotic prophase—establishes germ cell stage-specific expression pattern, (4) SPATS1 in X- and Y-spermatozoa differential expression in Bos indicus bulls (2025)—suggests potential sex chromosome-linked regulation, (5) SPATS1 in gonad transcriptomics of snakeskin gourami (2020, 23 citations)—confirms conserved gonad expression across fish.
