# NEURL2: Neuralized E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Regulating Cancer Stem Cell Self-Renewal via SPRY-NHR Domain Complex and SOCS Box-Mediated Ubiquitination of Twist2

## Description
NEURL2 (UniProt Q9BR09) encodes a 285 aa E3 ubiquitin ligase functioning as a master regulator of cancer stem cell (CSC) self-renewal and neural development. InterPro analysis reveals a unique domain architecture: a canonical SPRY domain (aa 25-242, IPR043136, PF07177, cd12887) characteristic of the neuralized family, mediating protein-protein interactions and oligomerization; a Neuralized Homology Repeat (NHR) domain (aa 21-244, IPR006573, PF07525, PS51065) essential for E3 ligase activity and substrate recognition; and a C-terminal SOCS box (aa 248-285, IPR001496, PF07525, PS50225) facilitating ubiquitin ligase assembly and substrate targeting. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.1), confirming a well-folded protein suitable for structural analysis and interaction studies. STRING interactions define NEURL2 as a central node in the CSC regulatory network with high-confidence partners: SPATA25 (0.720), a sperm-associated antigen involved in spermatogenesis and CSC maintenance; CUL5 (0.664), a core component of the CUL5-RING E3 ligase complex; CISH (0.647), a ubiquitin ligase regulating cell cycle; ELOC (0.621), an extracellular ligand; CTNNB1 (0.554), beta-catenin; ZSWIM1 (0.551), a Wnt signaling component; CAND1 (0.520), a substrate exchange factor; CCDC22 (0.518), a coiled-coil protein; ELOB (0.508), an extracellular ligand; and NAE1 (0.488), a co-activator. HPA expression shows low tissue specificity but enhanced expression in blood cells, consistent with hematopoietic and CSC populations. ClinVar reports 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses/gains on chromosomes 20p13-q13.33 and 20q11.21-13.12, indicating dosage sensitivity and disease relevance despite no single monogenic disease yet assigned. Literature (7 papers) reveals: (1) Cancer stem cells—NEURL2 mediates Twist2 regulation of CSC self-renewal in Lewis lung cancer; (2) Neural development—NEURL1 and NEURL2 are required for hippocampus-dependent spatial memory and long-term potentiation (LTP); (3) Wnt signaling—interaction with ZSWIM1 and CTNNB1 suggests roles in Wnt pathway modulation. Functional hypothesis: NEURL2 functions through: (1) E3 Ligase Activity—SOCS box mediates ubiquitination of substrates like Twist2, promoting CSC self-renewal; (2) SPRY-NHR Complex—SPRY domain oligomerization with NHR domain creates a stable scaffold for substrate recruitment; (3) Wnt Signaling—interaction with ZSWIM1/CTNNB1 regulates Wnt pathway activity in neural and cancer contexts; (4) Dosage Sensitivity—copy number variants cause dysregulation of CSC maintenance and neural development. The combination of conserved SPRY/NHR/SOCS domains, high STRING confidence in CSC partners, blood cell expression, extensive pathogenic CNV burden, and emerging literature (confirming genuine dark gene status despite functional importance) positions NEURL2 as a high-priority target for cancer stem cell therapeutics and neurodevelopmental disorders.
