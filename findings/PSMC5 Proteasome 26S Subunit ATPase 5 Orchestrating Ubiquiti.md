## Description
PSMC5 (Proteasome 26S Subunit ATPase 5) is a 406 amino acid AAA+ ATPase that functions as an essential regulatory subunit of the 26S proteasome 19S cap, mediating ATP-dependent substrate unfolding, gate opening of the 20S core particle, and ubiquitinated protein degradation critical for protein homeostasis, neurodevelopment, and immune regulation through hexameric ring assembly with other AAA+ ATPases and TLR4-dependent microglial activation control. AlphaFold structure (AF-P62195-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 82.1) across the 406 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded AAA+ ATPase with stable OB-fold and catalytic core suitable for hexameric ring assembly and mechanical substrate processing. HPA data classifies PSMC5 as an essential intracellular enzyme and plasma protein involved in proteasome-mediated ubiquitin-dependent protein catabolic process with cytosolic and plasma membrane localization reflecting 19S regulatory particle function, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with housekeeping proteostasis machinery. As an emerging essential proteasomal ATPase with neurodevelopmental disease associations, PSMC5 represents a critical node connecting ubiquitin-dependent protein degradation to neurodevelopment, cognitive function, motor control, and neuroinflammation through AAA+ ATPase-driven substrate unfolding, hexameric ring assembly with PSMC1/PSMC4, USP14-mediated deubiquitination coordination, and TLR4-dependent microglial polarization disrupted in proteasomopathy and neurodegeneration.
