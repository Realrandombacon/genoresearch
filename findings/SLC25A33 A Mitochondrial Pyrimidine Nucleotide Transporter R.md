## Description
SLC25A33 (UniProt Q9BSK2) encodes a 321 amino acid mitochondrial solute carrier belonging to the mitochondrial carrier family (MCF). InterPro analysis confirms canonical MCF architecture with three mitochondrial carrier transmembrane repeats (IPR018108, PF00153, positions 9-120/126-216/231-317), mitochondrial carrier domain superfamily (IPR023395, aa 2-321), and SLC25A33/36-like family membership (IPR049562, aa 6-318) with pyrimidine nucleotide transmembrane transporter activity (GO:0015218). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9BSK2-F1) predicts a highly confident 3D structure (global pLDDT 84.9), consistent with a well-folded mitochondrial inner membrane transporter. HPA expression reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in tongue (25.7 nTPM), detected in all tissues, classified as metabolic proteins/transporters with immune cell-enhanced expression in blood cells, reflecting broad metabolic roles with immune specialization. Literature (22 papers) confirms SLC25A33 as a mitochondrial pyrimidine nucleotide transporter mediating mtDNA synthesis critical for M1 macrophage inflammatory responses through mitochondrial ROS generation and VDAC oligomerization, vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation/migration via cytosolic mtDNA regulation in occlusive vascular diseases, and fibrosis promotion in oral submucous fibrosis via miR-134-3p/methionine metabolism axis. ClinVar lists 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number losses/gains on chromosome 1p36.33-36.12), indicating strong clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to neurodevelopmental disorders (1p36 deletion syndrome region) or metabolic diseases. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners including MTCH1 (0.834), MTCH2 (0.796), TMEM201 (0.596), SLC25A52 (0.568), and SLC25A53 (0.540), placing SLC25A33 within mitochondrial carrier networks involved in outer membrane integration and metabolite transport. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A33 functions as a mitochondrial pyrimidine nucleotide transporter regulating mtDNA synthesis and release, thereby controlling innate immune activation in macrophages and vascular smooth muscle cell behavior; its role in cytosolic mtDNA levels positions it as a critical checkpoint in inflammatory signaling and vascular remodeling. Mechanistic proposal: SLC25A33 imports pyrimidine nucleotides into mitochondria for mtDNA replication; during M1 macrophage polarization, increased SLC25A33 activity elevates mtDNA synthesis leading to VDAC oligomerization and mtDNA release into cytosol, triggering cGAS-STING inflammatory pathways; in vascular smooth muscle cells, cytosolic mtDNA accumulation drives proliferation/migration contributing to occlusive vascular disease; copy number variations disrupting SLC25A33 function may impair mitochondrial nucleotide homeostasis contributing to 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes.
