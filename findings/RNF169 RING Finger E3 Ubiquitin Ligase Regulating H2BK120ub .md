## Description

The combination of RING/UDM1 domains, IDP characteristics, nuclear localization, USP7-MDC1-RNF168 interaction network, 11q CNV burden, and DNA repair/replication functions suggests RNF169 functions as a ubiquitin ligase coordinating H2BK120ub-mediated DNA damage response and replication fork stability, with copy number variations potentially contributing to genomic instability and cancer progression.
