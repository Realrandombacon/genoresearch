## Description
The protein encoded by PDP2 is a catalytic subunit of pyruvate dehydrogenase phosphatase, which plays a crucial role in regulating pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH) activity. PDH is a key enzyme in the conversion of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA, a critical step in cellular energy metabolism. The PDP2 protein contains a PPM-type phosphatase domain (IPR000222) and interacts with several proteins involved in pyruvate metabolism, including PDHB, PDHA1, and PDHX (STRING-DB scores: 0.944, 0.890, and 0.836, respectively). 

  The protein is primarily localized in mitochondria (HPA) and is expressed in all tissues, with low tissue specificity. 

  Several pathogenic variants have been identified in the PDP2 gene (ClinVar), suggesting its importance in human health.

  Based on these findings, we propose that PDP2 likely functions in mitochondrial pyruvate metabolism, regulating PDH activity through dephosphorylation.
