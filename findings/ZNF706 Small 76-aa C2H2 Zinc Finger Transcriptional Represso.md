## Description
ZNF706 (Gene ID: 51123, UniProt Q9Y5V0, aliases HSPC038, PNAS-106, PNAS-113) is a remarkably small 76 amino acid zinc finger protein located on chromosome 8q22.3 (NC_000008.11, positions 101197051-101206221, ~9kb genomic region). Despite its compact size, this gene encodes a functional transcriptional repressor with conserved structural domains. InterPro domain analysis reveals a bipartite architecture: Small EDRK-rich factor-like N-terminal domain (IPR007513/PF04419, positions 1-37, disordered region rich in Glu-Asp-Arg-Lys residues) and canonical C2H2-type zinc finger (IPR013087/PF12874, positions 39-61, ~23 residue DNA/RNA-binding motif). The protein belongs to the ZNF706/At2g23090 superfamily (IPR026939, positions 1-72) and PTHR21213 GEO09665P1-related family (positions 1-76). The N-terminal EDRK-rich region likely mediates protein-protein interactions or subcellular targeting, while the C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger enables nucleic acid recognition. GO annotations include zinc ion binding activity, negative regulation of DNA-templated transcription, and negative regulation of stem cell population maintenance. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-confidence partners: TXNDC5 (0.557, thioredoxin domain-containing protein 5, ER chaperone), IQCF1 (0.539, IQ motif-containing protein F1, ciliary protein), SERPINE3 (0.517, serpin family E member 3, protease inhibitor), ANKRD9 (0.509, ankyrin repeat domain 9), TRIM28 (0.508, tripartite motif-containing protein 28/KAP1, transcriptional corepressor), PRR15L (0.508, proline-rich 15-like), AZIN1 (0.499, antizyme inhibitor 1, polyamine regulator), CLNS1A (0.473, chloride channel-associated protein), RUNDC3A (0.464, RUN domain-containing protein 3A), and WDR81 (0.451, WD repeat-containing protein 81). The TRIM28 interaction is particularly significant—TRIM28/KAP1 is a master transcriptional corepressor that recruits chromatin modifiers, suggesting ZNF706 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding component of repressor complexes. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF706 (ENSG00000120963) as a predicted intracellular protein with unexpected plasma membrane subcellular localization, repressor molecular function, and translation regulation biological process. RNA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, but cell type-enhanced single cell specificity—indicating ubiquitous but cell type-modulated expression. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9Y5V0-F1) yields high-confidence model (global pLDDT 70.4), indicating a well-folded stable architecture across all 76 residues—remarkable for such a small protein and suggesting evolutionary constraint. The structure likely comprises an unstructured N-terminal EDRK-rich region followed by a compact zinc finger fold. ClinVar lists 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including one single nucleotide variant (NM_016096.5:c.136-240T>A, intronic) and 38 large chromosomal copy number gains spanning chromosome 8 regions (e.g., chr8:158048-146295771 triplication, chr8:79409349-119040631 triplication, chr8:68912432-146295771 duplication, chr8:84712253-146295771 triplication); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ZNF706, suggesting dosage sensitivity within larger chromosomal syndromes. Conservation is strong across vertebrates: mouse (Q9D115, 76 aa identical), chicken (Q5ZMM5, 76 aa identical), coho salmon (A0A8C7GMK3, 78 aa), and Xenopus (Q6GM97, 75 aa)—indicating functional importance despite minimal size. Functional hypothesis: ZNF706 functions as a compact transcriptional repressor that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complexes to specific genomic loci via its C2H2 zinc finger. The EDRK-rich N-terminal region may mediate additional protein interactions or post-translational modifications. Plasma membrane localization (per HPA) is unusual for a zinc finger protein and suggests potential dual functionality—perhaps shuttling between nucleus and membrane, or involvement in signal transduction pathways linking membrane events to transcriptional responses. TRIM28 interaction places ZNF706 within heterochromatin formation and epigenetic silencing pathways. Small size (76 aa) and high-confidence structure (pLDDT 70.4) suggest efficient folding and potential for rapid turnover. Negative regulation of stem cell maintenance suggests roles in differentiation programs. Disease mechanism: ZNF706 overexpression from chromosome 8q amplifications may cause aberrant transcriptional repression contributing to oncogenesis or developmental disorders. The 39 ClinVar CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity; 8q24 amplifications are common in cancers (MYC locus nearby). ZNF706 may contribute to phenotypes through ectopic silencing of tumor suppressors or differentiation genes. Priority experiments include ChIP-seq to identify genomic binding sites, co-IP validation of TRIM28 interaction, nuclear vs membrane localization studies via fractionation and immunofluorescence, assessment of ZNF706 role in stem cell differentiation using knockdown models, structural analysis leveraging high-confidence AlphaFold model (pLDDT 70.4) to identify zinc coordination and DNA-binding surfaces, and investigation of EDRK-rich region phosphorylation/acetylation status.
