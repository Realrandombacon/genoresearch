## Description
IPMK encodes a 416-amino acid inositol polyphosphate multikinase (UniProt Q8NFU5) with remarkable catalytic versatility—possessing 3-kinase, 5-kinase, and 6-kinase activities on phosphorylated inositol substrates. InterPro analysis identifies the conserved inositol polyphosphate kinase domain (PF03770, aa 127-410; IPR005522, aa 45-415) within the SAICAR synthase-like superfamily (SSF56104), consistent with its role in synthesizing inositol 1,3,4,5,6-pentakisphosphate. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT: 73.8) spanning the full protein length.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal a specialized network centered on inositol metabolism: ITPKB (0.954), PLCD1 (0.921), INPP5K (0.921), ITPK1 (0.920), and MINPP1 (0.900)—all enzymes controlling inositol phosphate signaling cascades. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with liver predominance (16.9 nTPM), nucleoplasmic and basal body localization, supporting both metabolic and nuclear functions.\n\nCritically, 218 literature papers reveal IPMK as a moonlighting kinase with expanding nuclear roles beyond inositol metabolism: it enhances SRF transcription factor DNA-binding activity (2025), influences genome-wide DNA methylation patterns (2025), modulates ubiquitination in anti-stress responses via UFD1f interaction (2025), and is now a drug target with novel kinase inhibitors in development (2025). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains and inversions at 10q21.1, suggesting clinical relevance. This positions IPMK as a metabolic-epigenetic hub coupling inositol signaling to transcriptional regulation, DNA methylation, and stress responses—a paradigm for how metabolic enzymes acquire regulatory functions in the nucleus.
