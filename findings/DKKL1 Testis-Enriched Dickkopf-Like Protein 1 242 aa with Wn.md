## Description
DKKL1 encodes a 242 aa Dickkopf-like protein 1 (UniProt Q9UK85), also known as Dickkopf-like acrosomal protein 1, functioning as a testis-specific secreted protein with roles in spermatogenesis and sperm function through Wnt signaling pathway modulation. The protein belongs to the Dickkopf-related protein 1-4 family (IPR039863, aa14-241) with a Dickkopf-like protein 1 domain (cd23006, IPR049632, aa77-242) and DICKKOPF3-LIKE 3 family membership (PTHR12113, aa14-241), indicating structural and functional similarity to canonical DKK Wnt antagonists.
