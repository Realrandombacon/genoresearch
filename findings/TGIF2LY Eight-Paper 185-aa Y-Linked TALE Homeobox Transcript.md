## Description
TGIF2LY (TGFB induced factor homeobox 2 like Y-linked, alias TGIFLY, Gene ID: 90655) is a 185-amino acid Y-linked TALE homeobox transcription factor encoded on chromosome Yp11.2 (3579066-3580040) that represents a dark gene with only 8 publications, including recent 2024 studies linking it to male infertility diagnostics. Literature analysis confirms minimal characterization with studies showing TGIF2LY is overexpressed in acute myeloid leukemia cells and implicated in male spermatogenic failure. This gene lies within the male-specific region of chromosome Y in a block resulting from large X-to-Y transposition, with its X homolog being TGIF2LX. UniProt entry Q8IUE0 exists for the 185-aa human protein with conservation in squirrel monkey A0A2K6SNZ8 (168 aa) and Antarctic toothfish A0AAD9B5R3 (200 aa), indicating evolutionary preservation of Y-linked function.
