## Description
ZNF714 (Gene ID: 148206, UniProt Q96N38) is a 554 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein mapping to chromosome 19p12 (21,082,202-21,125,093 bp). The protein contains canonical KRAB-ZFP architecture: N-terminal KRAB repression domain (aa 1-47, IPR001909, PF00096) and multiple C-terminal C2H2-type zinc fingers (aa 116-250+, 3+ fingers, IPR013087, PF00096) for sequence-specific DNA binding. InterPro analysis confirms: KRAB domain (PS50805), classic C2H2 zinc finger superfamily (IPR036236), zinc finger double domain (PF13465), and PTHR24379 family membership. STRING interactions (10 partners, scores 0.415-0.639) reveal functional networks: ZNF850 (0.639, paralogous KRAB-ZFP), TRIM28 (0.522, KAP1 co-repressor, critical for KRAB-mediated silencing), ATAD3C (0.615, mitochondrial inner membrane), ZNF431 (0.545, zinc finger protein), and others. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in ovary (13.9 nTPM) and thyroid gland (13.4 nTPM), detected in many tissues, classified as predicted intracellular protein with nuclear localization. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 1 promoter SNP (c.-174C>T) and 7 large copy number gains spanning 19p19q regions (19p13.11-q13.2 duplications ranging 2-58 Mb). AlphaFold structure (Q96N38) shows global pLDDT 68.2 (medium confidence) consistent with modular zinc finger domains connected by flexible linkers. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF714 functions as sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruiting heterochromatin machinery via KRAB-TRIM28/KAP1 interaction. The KRAB domain (aa 1-47) binds TRIM28/KAP1 which recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase (H3K9me3), HP1 heterochromatin protein, and NuRD complex (HDAC1/2) to establish repressive chromatin. C2H2 zinc fingers (3+ repeats) recognize specific DNA sequences (likely 5-GGG-X-3 variants) in target gene promoters/enhancers. High ovary/thyroid expression suggests roles in reproductive and endocrine transcriptional regulation. Chromosome 19 is gene-dense with clustered ZNF genes (KZNF family expansion); ZNF714 likely co-regulates neighboring genes via chromatin looping. CNVs disrupting 19p13 cause dosage imbalance of multiple ZNF genes and developmental phenotypes. This represents a "dark gene" that is actually a well-characterized KRAB-ZFP family member with emerging clinical significance.
