## Description
LOC100132287 (Gene ID: 100132287) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 1p36.33 (NC_000001.11, positions 495444-490755, ~4.7 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 1 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variant despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 1 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variant: copy number gain/duplication (variant 2664871: 1p36.33-35.1 ~33.9 Mb trisomy). This variant has uncertain significance (?) but is classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 1p36.33 chromosomal region contains critical cell signaling, innate immunity, and epithelial-mesenchymal transition-associated genes including PRKCZ (protein kinase C zeta, atypical PKC, NF-kappaB activation, cell polarity, insulin signaling, glucose transport, cancer cell survival), ISG15 (ISG15 ubiquitin like modifier/interferon-stimulated gene 15, ISGylation, antiviral innate immunity, type I interferon response, viral restriction, protein modification), MIR200B/MIR200A (microRNA 200b/200a, miR-200 family, ZEB1/ZEB2 repression, EMT suppression, epithelial phenotype maintenance, cancer metastasis inhibition, chemoresistance modulation), and ATAD3 (ATPase family AAA domain containing 3, mitochondrial inner membrane, cholesterol transport, mitochondrial nucleoid organization, 1p36.33 duplication syndrome). Functional hypothesis: LOC100132287 likely represents a critical 1p36.33 immune/epithelial enhancer element within the PRKCZ-ISG15 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a type I interferon-responsive enhancer coordinating PRKCZ-ISG15-miR-200 expression in antiviral immune niches (plasmacytoid dendritic cells, macrophages, infected epithelial cells) or as a chromatin boundary separating atypical PKC signaling genes (PRKCZ) from ubiquitin-like modifier genes (ISG15) and microRNA genes (MIR200B/MIR200A). The 1 pathogenic CNV (duplication, 33.9 Mb range) indicates regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number disrupts PRKCZ-mediated NF-kappaB activation and ISG15-mediated ISGylation. Disease mechanism involves PRKCZ overexpression causing cancer (NF-kappaB hyperactivation, cell survival, chemoresistance, poor prognosis), PRKCZ dysfunction causing insulin resistance (impaired GLUT4 translocation, type 2 diabetes, metabolic syndrome), PRKCZ mutation causing cell polarity defect (epithelial barrier dysfunction, cancer invasion), ISG15 overexpression causing autoinflammatory disease (ISG15 accumulation, interferon signature, Aicardi-Goutieres syndrome features, lupus-like autoimmunity), ISG15 deficiency causing viral susceptibility (impaired antiviral ISGylation, intracranial calcification, interferonopathy, viral encephalitis), miR-200 downregulation causing cancer metastasis (ZEB1/ZEB2 derepression, EMT activation, invasion, chemoresistance, poor prognosis), miR-200 overexpression causing epithelial phenotype maintenance (MET induction, metastasis suppression, chemosensitivity), ATAD3 duplication causing 1p36.33 duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, seizures, behavioral abnormalities), and 1p36 deletion syndrome (monosomy 1p36, intellectual disability, hypotonia, seizures, cardiomyopathy, distinctive facial features). The 1p36.33 region is a well-established 1p36 deletion/duplication syndrome locus. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (1 duplication 33.9 Mb) and PRKCZ-ISG15-MIR200B-MIR200A-ATAD3 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as an immune/epithelial-specific enhancer critical for NF-kappaB signaling-antiviral immunity-EMT regulation coordination.
