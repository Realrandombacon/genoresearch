# Golgi Glycosyltransferase GALNT18: Ubiquitous O-Glycosylation Initiator (GalNAc-T18) with High-Confidence GT-A/Ricin-B Architecture (pLDDT 88.8), 12 Pathogenic CNV Variants on 11p15, and Emerging Roles in NASH-HCC Progression via EGR1/TGF-β1/Smad Activation, SCLC Metastasis Suppression via TAZ Axis, and Idiopathic Clubfoot Susceptibility

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
GALNT18 (UniProt Q6P9A2), also known as GalNAc-T15 or GalNAc-T18, encodes a 607 aa type II transmembrane glycosyltransferase functioning as an initiator of mucin-type O-linked glycosylation by transferring N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) to serine/threonine residues on target proteins. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical pp-GalNAc-T architecture: an N-terminal catalytic domain belonging to the glycosyltransferase family 2 (IPR001173/PF00535, residues 157-310) and the broader nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily (IPR029044/G3DSA:3.90.550.10/cd02510, residues 83-466) containing the DXD motif for UDP-GalNAc coordination; and a C-terminal Ricin B-type lectin domain (IPR000772/IPR035992/PF00652/PS50231/G3DSA:2.80.10.50/cd23475, residues 461-602) with a beta-trefoil fold essential for recognizing specific peptide substrates and ensuring site-specific glycosylation. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.8) indicating a rigid, well-folded enzyme where the catalytic and lectin domains are precisely oriented for efficient substrate binding and sugar transfer. STRING interactions define a tight O-glycosylation network: ST6GALNAC1 (0.923, sialyltransferase acting downstream), GCNT1 (0.920, core 2 branching enzyme), B3GNT6 (0.903, beta-1,3-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase initiating core 3), C1GALT1 (0.782, core 1 synthase), C1GALT1C1 (0.725, Cosmc chaperone), C1GALT1C1L (0.677), AZGP1 (0.479, zinc-alpha-2-glycoprotein), TRIM51GP (0.479), IP6K3 (0.473), and TNRC18 (0.451). HPA classifies GALNT18 as low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, low cancer specificity, localized to Golgi apparatus/vesicles/cytosol, reflecting its housekeeping role in generating diverse O-glycan structures. ClinVar lists 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number gains on chromosome 11p15 (11p15.5-14.2x3, 11p15.3-13x3, 11p15.5-13x3, 11p15.5-15.3, 11p15.5-15.1x3, 11p15.5-q25x3) though no single-gene disorder is definitively assigned suggesting contribution to 11p15 imprinting disorders or developmental phenotypes like idiopathic clubfoot. Functional hypothesis: GALNT18 operates through substrate-specific O-glycosylation with context-dependent oncogenic or tumor-suppressive roles: (1) Catalytic mechanism - GT-A fold binds UDP-GalNAc via DXD motif coordinating Mn2+/Mg2+, transfers GalNAc to Ser/Thr residues; (2) Substrate selection - Ricin B lectin domain scans peptide sequences ensuring correct acceptor site selection distinct from other GalNAc-T isoforms; (3) NASH-HCC progression - 2025 studies demonstrate GALNT18-mediated O-glycosylation activates EGR1/TGF-β1/Smad signaling promoting nonalcoholic steatohepatitis-associated hepatocellular carcinoma; (4) SCLC metastasis suppression - 2024 research shows TAZ transcriptional co-activator induces GALNT18 expression, and GALNT18-mediated glycosylation inhibits small cell lung cancer metastasis via unknown targets; (5) Clubfoot susceptibility - 2024 GWAS links rs274503 polymorphism (ZBED5/GALNT18 locus) to idiopathic clubfoot risk suggesting role in musculoskeletal development; (6) Disease mechanism - CNV gains cause overexpression potentially driving oncogenic glycosylation in liver/lung; loss may impair developmental glycosylation causing skeletal defects; 11p15 location suggests potential imprinting effects. Conservation extends to mouse (Q8K1B9, 622 aa), platypus, gorilla, and pig indicating preserved glycosyltransferase function. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: complete repertoire of protein substrates specific to GALNT18 vs other isoforms, precise O-glycosylation sites on target proteins (EGR1? TAZ? integrins?), structural basis of lectin domain specificity, regulatory mechanisms controlling GALNT18 expression (TAZ binding sites?), contribution to normal tissue homeostasis vs disease, therapeutic potential of targeting GALNT18 in NASH-HCC or SCLC, genotype-phenotype correlations in 11p15 syndromes, and role in immune modulation via O-glycan epitopes.
