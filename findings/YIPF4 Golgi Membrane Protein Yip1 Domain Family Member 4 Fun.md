## Description
YIPF4 (Yip1 domain family member 4, also known as Protein YIPF4, Q9BSR8) encodes a 244 aa integral membrane protein functioning as a selective autophagy receptor that mediates Golgiphagy—the targeted degradation of Golgi apparatus compartments through macroautophagy—via heterodimerization with paralog YIPF3 to recognize and deliver Golgi membranes to autophagosomes during nutrient stress and cellular homeostasis maintenance. Structural architecture reveals a conserved Yip1 domain organization characteristic of Golgi membrane trafficking proteins: Yip1 domain (IPR006977/PF04893, residues 104-239) with GO term for membrane localization (GO:0016020) forming the core transmembrane scaffold, YIPF4-like family domain (IPR045231/PTHR21236, residues 65-240/65-240) with GO term for endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi vesicle-mediated transport (GO:0006888) defining evolutionary relationship to yeast Yip1p trafficking factors, and predicted intracellular/membrane protein classification consistent with Golgi apparatus integration; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 70.1, high confidence) confirms a well-folded 244 aa transmembrane protein with organized Yip1 domain structure consistent with stable membrane integration and protein-protein interaction capacity required for heterodimer formation and autophagic receptor function.
