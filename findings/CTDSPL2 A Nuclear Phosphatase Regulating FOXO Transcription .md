## Description
CTDSPL2 (CTD small phosphatase-like protein 2) is a 466 aa nuclear phosphatase belonging to the haloacid dehalogenase (HAD) superfamily (IPR011948, PF03031). Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence (pLDDT=65.6) fold with mixed ordered/disordered regions, consistent with its role in protein-protein interactions. Functionally, it dephosphorylates FOXO1 and FOXO3A, promoting their nuclear retention and transcriptional activation of gluconeogenic genes (PCK1, G6PC1). It interacts with nuclear transport factors (KPNA1, KPNA4) and metabolic regulators (PDIK1L, STK35), suggesting a role in linking nuclear signaling to metabolism. Expression is ubiquitous but low-specificity. ClinVar identifies 10 pathogenic variants (CNVs/deletions), linking it to developmental disorders despite lacking direct disease annotations. Literature (17 papers) highlights its role in osteosarcoma, lung cancer, and necrotizing enterocolitis via PI3K/AKT and FOXO pathways.
