## Description
SLC35D3 (UniProt Q5M8T2) encodes a 416 amino acid solute carrier belonging to the Triose Phosphate/Phosphate Translocator family (IPR050186, PTHR11132, aa 15-306), though functionally characterized as a probable UDP-glucose transmembrane transporter. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q5M8T2-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure (global pLDDT 76.6), consistent with a functional membrane transporter. HPA expression reveals tissue-enriched expression with peak levels in brain (5.7 nTPM), detected in single tissues, classified as sugar transporters with group-enriched RNA cancer specificity, and subcellular localization to centriolar satellites, suggesting a role in centrosome-associated trafficking. Literature (24 papers) confirms SLC35D3 as a critical regulator of white adipose tissue browning via NOTCH signaling, a prognostic marker for colorectal cancer lymph node metastasis, and involved in porcine intramuscular preadipocyte differentiation. ClinVar lists 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number losses and deletions on chromosome 6q23.2-24.3), indicating strong clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to neurodevelopmental disorders or metabolic syndromes. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners including SLC35A3 (0.957), SLC35A1 (0.922), SLC35B4 (0.885), SLC35B3 (0.853), SLC35B2 (0.848), SLC35B1 (0.803), SULT4A1 (0.747), ADGRL1 (0.727), SLC35A2 (0.685), and RAB27A (0.667), placing SLC35D3 within the SLC35 family complex involved in vesicular transport. Functional hypothesis: SLC35D3 functions as a UDP-glucose transporter facilitating the loading of UDP-glucose into synaptic vesicles and platelet dense granules via centriolar satellite localization; its role in NOTCH signaling in adipose tissue suggests a broader function in metabolic differentiation and tissue remodeling. Mechanistic proposal: SLC35D3 mediates UDP-glucose transport from the cytosol to the lumen of synaptic vesicles and platelet dense granules, essential for neurotransmitter packaging and granule maturation; loss-of-function variants disrupt vesicle biogenesis leading to neurodevelopmental phenotypes, while its expression in adipose tissue implies a role in lipid metabolism regulation via NOTCH pathway modulation.
