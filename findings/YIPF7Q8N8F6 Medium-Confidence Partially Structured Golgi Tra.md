In muscle, YIPF7 ensures rapid turnover of sarcolemmal components; its dysregulation leads to trafficking defects characteristic of myopathies. Dysregulation via 4p CNVs disrupts this flow: loss causes ER stress and muscle wasting, while gain creates traffic jams, driving the pathogenesis of 4p syndromes.
