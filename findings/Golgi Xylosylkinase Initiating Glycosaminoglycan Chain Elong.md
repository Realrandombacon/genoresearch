# Golgi Xylosylkinase Initiating Glycosaminoglycan Chain Elongation

## Description
FAM20B encodes a 409 amino acid glycosaminoglycan xylosylkinase (UniProt: O75063) located on chromosome 1q25.2 that catalyzes the phosphorylation of xylose residues on proteoglycan core proteins, representing the second step in glycosaminoglycan (GAG) chain initiation. InterPro analysis identifies a C-terminal kinase domain (cd10470, residues 188-394) and FAM20 family signatures (IPR009581, IPR024869, PF06702) characteristic of the Golgi casein kinase superfamily. STRING interaction network reveals functional associations with GAG biosynthesis enzymes including EXTL2 (0.702), B3GALT6 (0.701), B3GAT3 (0.687), PXYLP1 (0.668), and xylosyltransferases XYLT1/XYLT2 (0.440-0.464), positioning FAM20B within the proteoglycan assembly complex. HPA profiling shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with dual nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus localization, consistent with its metabolic kinase function. The protein is classified as a transferase involved in proteoglycan biosynthetic processes. ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains/losses on 1q24.2-q25.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential roles in connective tissue disorders or skeletal development abnormalities. The kinase domain architecture combined with interaction partners indicates FAM20B functions as the gatekeeper enzyme controlling GAG chain elongation by phosphorylating the xylose linker, thereby regulating extracellular matrix composition, cell signaling through proteoglycan co-receptors, and tissue morphogenesis.
