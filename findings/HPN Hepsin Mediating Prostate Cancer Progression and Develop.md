## Description
HPN (Hepsin) encodes a type II transmembrane serine protease (UniProt P05981) with a confident 3D structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 91.1) featuring a Trypsin-like catalytic domain (IPR001254) and an N-terminal SRCR domain (IPR001190). Functionally, it acts as a key regulator of cell morphology and coagulation, evidenced by high-confidence interactions (0.914) with IGLL5 and (0.810) with HGF, suggesting roles in inflammation and signaling. It is broadly expressed in epithelial tissues (Liver nTPM 849.7, Pancreas 263.1) with cancer-enhanced specificity. Clinically, HPN is a 'WELL-STUDIED' gene (3536 papers) with 16 pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs) linked to developmental disorders and cancer progression (Prostate Cancer). It drives progression by disrupting barrier function and facilitating cell migration via proteolytic activation of PAR-1 and other targets.
