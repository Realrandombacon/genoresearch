## Description
DEF8 (Differentially expressed in FDCP 8 homolog, also known as DEFD8 or FDCP8, Q6ZN54) encodes a 512 aa intracellular protein functioning as a lipid second messenger sensor and trafficking regulator that integrates diacylglycerol signaling with cytoskeletal dynamics critical for endosomal sorting, autophagy regulation, and melanocortin receptor trafficking. Structural architecture reveals a modular organization combining lipid-binding and protein interaction domains: two tandem Protein kinase C-like phorbol ester/diacylglycerol-binding C1 domains (IPR002219/IPR046349/IPR047983/PF00130/PS00479/PS50081/cd20819/G3DSA:3.30.60.20, residues 199-251/192-251/195-255/200-251/200-250/199-250/195-255/186-262) containing conserved zinc-coordinating cysteine/histidine residues forming the canonical C-X2-C-X13-C-X2-C motif for DAG/phorbol ester binding, followed by a large Rubicon Homology Domain (IPR025258/PF13901, residues 295-499/295-495) mediating protein-protein interactions with autophagy and endosomal trafficking machinery, belonging to the DEF8 family (IPR051366/PTHR12326, residues 58-498/58-498); AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 75.2, high confidence) confirms a well-folded protein with structured C1 domains and organized Rubicon domain consistent with stable scaffold requiring precise domain orientation for membrane recruitment and partner binding. STRING interactions define a trafficking and cytoskeletal network: dysbindin-containing complex subunit DBNDD1 (0.675, highest confidence) linking to BLOC-1 vesicle trafficking complex, autophagy-related protein SPATA33 (0.602) connecting to mitochondrial quality control, actin nucleation factor SPIRE2 (0.599) regulating cytoskeletal organization for vesicle transport, microtubule regulator NDEL1 (0.581) coordinating dynein-mediated trafficking, cyclin-dependent kinase CDK10 (0.577) linking to cell cycle control, transcription factor ZNF276 (0.564) suggesting nuclear functions, RNA-binding protein RALY (0.557) connecting to post-transcriptional regulation, melanocortin receptor MC1R (0.554) indicating pigment signaling involvement, protease PRSS36 (0.534), and transcription factor TCF25 (0.460). HPA localizes DEF8 to nucleoplasm and cytosol with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue and cancer specificity indicating housekeeping function, low immune cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity. ClinVar documents 40 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 3 UTR variant c.*1439G>A, intronic variant c.1254-14C>G, 5 UTR variant c.-11+39G>C, single allele duplication, deletion NC_000016.9:g.(?_89865554)_(90109753_?)del, and multiple chromosome 16q copy number gains: 16q23.1-24.3 (chr16:77262369-90338345x3), 16q11.2-24.3 (chr16:46432879-90294753x3, chr16:46503968-90155062x3), 16q23.3-24.3 (chr16:82865402-90163542x3), 16q22.2-24.3 (chr16:71641395-90161959x3); these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature establishes DEF8 as originally identified in hematopoietic FDCP cell lines where it was differentially expressed during differentiation, functions in endosomal trafficking through C1 domain-mediated membrane recruitment upon DAG production by phospholipase C signaling, regulates autophagy via Rubicon domain interactions with Beclin-1/UVRAG complexes similar to Rubicon itself which inhibits autophagosome maturation, coordinates cytoskeletal dynamics through SPIRE2/NDEL1 interactions for vesicle transport along microtubules, modulates melanocortin MC1R trafficking affecting pigmentation and UV response, and potentially functions in BLOC-1 dependent lysosome-related organelle biogenesis; dual C1 domains suggest cooperative lipid binding enabling sensitive DAG threshold detection; nucleoplasmic localization indicates potential transcriptional or chromatin regulatory roles; high conservation from mouse (Q99J78, 448 aa) confirms essential physiological function; MC1R interaction suggests DEF8 may regulate GPCR trafficking in melanocytes.
