## Description
CLEC10A (C-type Lectin Domain Family 10 Member A; UniProt Q8IUN9), also known as Macrophage Galactose-type Lectin (MGL) or CD301a, is a 316 amino acid type II transmembrane protein with 130 publications, representing a moderately characterized C-type lectin receptor expressed on dendritic cells and macrophages that recognizes terminal N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) and Tn antigen on glycoproteins to regulate innate and adaptive immune responses.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a canonical C-type lectin architecture optimized for carbohydrate recognition. The N-terminal region contains a Hepatic lectin N-terminal domain (PF03954, residues 5-126) likely involved in oligomerization or membrane proximal stability. The C-terminal extracellular region contains a characteristic C-type lectin-like domain (CTLD) (IPR001304/PF00059/cd03590, residues 181-305/199-305) belonging to the CD209-like superfamily (IPR033989, residues 181-305). This domain adopts the conserved C-type lectin fold (IPR016187/IPR016186, residues 160-307/175-314) featuring a calcium-binding site critical for ligand coordination. The conserved site signature (PS00615/PS50041, residues 282-304) includes the EPN/QPD motif variants determining sugar specificity (GalNAc vs Mannose). AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 80.4), indicating a well-folded receptor with a rigid carbohydrate recognition domain (CRD) suitable for specific glycan binding.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies medium-to-high confidence interactions centered on myeloid cell surface markers and antigen presentation: ITGAX (0.752, Integrin alpha X/CD11c—dendritic cell marker), CD1C (0.750, CD1c antigen—lipid antigen presentation), LLGL1 (0.743, Lethal giant larvae protein 1—cell polarity), MUC1 (0.738, Mucin-1—glycosylated tumor antigen, potential ligand), FCER1A (0.706, High affinity IgE receptor alpha chain—mast cell/basophil activation), EEF1A2 (0.705, Elongation factor 1-alpha 2—translation), CSF1R (0.648, Colony stimulating factor 1 receptor—macrophage differentiation), CD163 (0.637, Hemoglobin scavenger receptor—M2 macrophage marker), CD86 (0.606, T-cell costimulatory molecule), and ITGAM (0.600, Integrin alpha M/CD11b—myeloid adhesion). This profile places CLEC10A within the myeloid lineage machinery, physically associated with antigen presenting complexes (CD1C, CD86) and integrins (ITGAX, ITGAM) required for immune synapse formation.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (27.7 nTPM), detected in many tissues but enriched in immune cells. The restricted expression pattern confirms its role as a specialized immune sensor rather than a ubiquitous housekeeping gene.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations spanning 17p13.3-12 (chr17:525-11404096x3 gain, chr17:165730-11404096x3 gain), 17p13.2-13.1 losses (chr17:5142358-7397716x1), and deletions encompassing the locus. The receptor's ability to bind tumor-associated Tn antigen positions it as a checkpoint for cancer immunity.\n\nCONSERVATION: Orthologs confirmed in mouse (P49300, 304 aa—96.2% length conservation), indicating strong evolutionary constraint on the CTLD architecture across mammals for conserved glycan sensing functions.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CLEC10A functions as a GalNAc-sensing immune regulator through a coordinated mechanism: (1) Ligand recognition—the CRD (residues 181-305) binds terminal GalNAc or Tn antigen (GalNAc-alpha-O-Ser/Thr) on glycoproteins in a calcium-dependent manner; (2) Endocytosis—cytoplasmic tail motifs (potentially ITIM-like) trigger clathrin-mediated internalization of bound antigens for processing; (3) Tolerogenic signaling—engagement induces anti-inflammatory cytokine profiles (IL-10, low IL-12) in dendritic cells, promoting regulatory T-cell induction rather than effector responses; (4) Tumor interaction—binding to Tn antigen on tumor cells (e.g., PDAC, breast cancer) may either facilitate cross-presentation for immunity or induce tolerance depending on context; (5) Infection response—recognizes GalNAc-containing structures on Mtb, triggering protective immunity; HIV-mediated suppression of CLEC10A impairs this defense; (6) Inflammation resolution—Tn antigen binding suppresses excessive inflammatory responses (e.g., LPS dermatitis), acting as a brake on innate immunity.
