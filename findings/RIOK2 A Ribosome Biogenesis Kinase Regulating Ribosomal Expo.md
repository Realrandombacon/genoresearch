## Description
RIOK2 (RIO kinase 2) is a 552 aa serine/threonine protein kinase (UniProt Q9BVS4) critical for the final maturation steps of cytoplasmic 40S ribosomal subunit export. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence (pLDDT=67.4) fold with a Winged Helix DNA-binding domain (IPR011009) and catalytic RIO kinase domain (IPR000687), suggesting a role in protein-protein interactions and nucleocytoplasmic transport. Functionally, it interacts with ribosomal proteins (RPS2, RPS15, RPS3, RPS5) and translation factors (TSR1, LTV1, NOB1, PNO1, KRR1) with high confidence (scores 0.754–0.999), positioning it as a central hub in the preribosome complex. Expression is ubiquitous but low-specificity, with no strong cancer enrichment. ClinVar identifies 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs), linking it to developmental disorders despite lacking specific disease annotations. Literature (87 papers) confirms its role in ribosome biogenesis, but its specific mechanistic link to stress-induced ribosomal export regulation remains underexplored. This gene represents a 'dark' node in the ribosome biogenesis pathway, where its kinase activity likely regulates the release of pre-40S particles from the nucleus to the cytoplasm, potentially acting as a checkpoint for ribosomal quality control.
