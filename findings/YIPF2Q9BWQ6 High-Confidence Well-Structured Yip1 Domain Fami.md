## Description
YIPF2 (Yip1 domain family member 2, also known as YIPFbeta3B, Yip5C, FinGER2) is a 316 amino acid Golgi membrane transport protein (UniProt Q9BWQ6) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 75.0), characteristic of Yip1 domain family proteins that function through small GTPase binding and vesicle-mediated transport to regulate intracellular trafficking between Golgi apparatus and other organelles. The protein belongs to the Yip1 domain family (IPR006977, residues 102-274; GO:0016020 membrane), the protein Yip5/YIPF1/YIPF2 family (IPR039765, residues 26-283; GO:0031267 small GTPase binding, GO:0016192 vesicle-mediated transport, GO:0005794 Golgi apparatus), contains a Yip1 domain (PF04893, residues 102-274), and belongs to the protein YIPF family (PTHR12822, residues 26-283). YIPF2 is located on chromosome 19p13.2 and is predicted to enable small GTPase binding activity, involved in vesicle-mediated transport, located in Golgi apparatus subcompartment and transport vesicle.
