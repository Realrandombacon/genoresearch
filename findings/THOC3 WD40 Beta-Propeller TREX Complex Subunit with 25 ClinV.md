## Description
THOC3 (THO Complex Subunit 3; UniProt Q96J01) is a 351 amino acid nuclear protein and core component of the TREX (TRanscription-EXport) complex with only 13 publications, classifying it as a genuine dark gene despite emerging roles in cancer progression and mRNA metabolism.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a canonical WD40 repeat beta-propeller fold (IPR001680/PF00400, residues 44-294) with 7 WD40 repeats forming the characteristic propeller structure. The protein belongs to the TREX component Tex1/THOC3 family (IPR040132, residues 26-347) and contains a THOC3-specific beta-propeller domain (PF25174, residues 54-325). AlphaFold predicts exceptional structural confidence (global pLDDT 90.2), indicating a highly stable, well-folded WD40 platform optimized for protein-protein interactions—typical of scaffold proteins that nucleate multi-protein complexes.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals THOC3 embedded in the core TREX mRNA export machinery with uniformly ultra-high confidence interactions: THOC5 (0.999), ALYREF (0.999, mRNA export adaptor), THOC7 (0.999), DDX39B (0.999, DEAD-box RNA helicase), THOC6 (0.999), THOC2 (0.984), SARNP (0.984, SAP domain-containing RNA-binding protein), and THOC1 (0.960). Notably, CYLD (0.989, deubiquitinase) and GLI2 (0.986, Hedgehog transcription factor) interactions suggest regulatory crosstalk between mRNA export and signaling pathways.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping functions in mRNA processing. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm and vesicles, matching its role in nuclear mRNA export. Classified as an essential protein by HPA.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses/gains at 5q35.1-35.3 encompassing THOC3. No monogenic disease is yet defined, but 5q35 deletions cause Sotos syndrome-like phenotypes. Recent 2023-2026 literature establishes THOC3 as an oncogenic driver: it interacts with YBX1 to promote lung squamous cell carcinoma progression through PFKFB4 mRNA modification (48 citations), regulates subtype specification in basal-squamous pancreatic cancer, and functions as an oncogenic RNA processing regulator in lung adenocarcinoma.\n\nCONSERVATION: Orthologs confirmed in mouse (Q8VE80, 351 aa) and cattle (Q29RH4, 351 aa), indicating strong evolutionary constraint on the WD40 architecture.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: THOC3 likely functions as a structural scaffold within the TREX complex, using its stable WD40 beta-propeller to bridge the THO subcomplex (THOC1/2/5/6/7) with the mRNA export adaptor ALYREF and helicase DDX39B. By facilitating efficient mRNA export, THOC3 controls the nuclear-cytoplasmic distribution of specific transcripts—including oncogenic mRNAs like PFKFB4 via YBX1 interaction. The 25 ClinVar CNVs without defined phenotypes suggest haploinsufficiency may contribute to neurodevelopmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. The CYLD interaction hints at regulation by deubiquitination, potentially controlling TREX complex stability or turnover.
