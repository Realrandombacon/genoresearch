## Description
TSPAN3 (Tetraspanin-3, 253 aa, UniProt O60637) is a moderately studied tetraspanin family member with emerging roles in immune protein trafficking, neurodegeneration, and cancer. With 47 papers, TSPAN3 is less characterized than canonical tetraspanins (CD9, CD81, CD63) but has defined functions in MHCII compartment sorting and alpha-synuclein oligomer degradation. InterPro analysis reveals canonical tetraspanin architecture: four transmembrane domains (TM4SF signature IPR018503/PS00421, positions 61-83), large extracellular loop EC2 with tetraspanin EC2 domain superfamily (IPR008952/G3DSA:1.10.1450.10, positions 105-206), and tetraspanin animal family domain (IPR000301/PF00335, positions 10-236). STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners: ITGB1 (integrin beta-1, 0.743), EPHA5 (0.611), ABCG8 (0.605), CD82 (0.573), CLDN11 (0.537), CD81 (0.497), TSPAN33 (0.459) - consistent with tetraspanin web formation and integrin signaling. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity), group enriched in blood cells, subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus - unusual for tetraspanins which typically localize to plasma membrane and endosomes. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (15q24.1-26.3 CNVs, duplications), though no specific diseases defined yet. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 87.1, high confidence) indicates well-folded tetraspanin fold across full length (1-253 aa). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TSPAN3 functions as tetraspanin web organizer that facilitates MHCII trafficking to antigen presentation compartments in B cells and promotes endo-lysosomal degradation of alpha-synuclein oligomers in neurons, potentially protecting against Parkinson's disease pathology. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: Four transmembrane domains anchor TSPAN3 in endosomal/Golgi membranes, while EC2 large extracellular loop (105-206 aa) mediates homophilic/heterophilic interactions with CD81, CD82, and integrins (ITGB1) to form tetraspanin-enriched microdomains (TEMs). These TEMs sort MHCII molecules to specialized compartments for antigen presentation (2024 study on sorting motif) and recruit alpha-synuclein oligomers to endo-lysosomes for degradation (2023 CRISPR screen). Blood cell enrichment suggests immune regulation role, while ubiquitous expression indicates broader cellular function. 14 ClinVar CNVs at 15q24-26 may contribute to neurodevelopmental or immunodeficiency phenotypes via TSPAN3 haploinsufficiency. With 47 publications including 2023-2025 mechanistic studies on MHCII sorting, alpha-synuclein degradation, and cancer (pancreatic, AML), defined tetraspanin architecture, known protein interactions (ITGB1, CD81, CD82), 14 ClinVar variants, and high-confidence structure (pLDDT 87.1), TSPAN3 represents a moderately characterized tetraspanin with emerging disease relevance worthy of further investigation.
