# LRRC26: Leucine-Rich Repeat Potassium Channel Auxiliary Subunit Highly Expressed in Salivary Gland

**Date:** 2026-03-23T11:41:26.385916

**Quality Score:** 2.48/10  (E=3.65, D=1.3) [WEAK]

## Description
LRRC26 encodes a 334 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) membrane protein that functions as an auxiliary subunit of voltage-gated potassium channels. InterPro analysis reveals canonical LRR architecture with multiple LRR repeats (IPR001611, PF13855, positions 72-202), typical LRR subtypes (IPR003591), and a ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, positions 40-256). The protein belongs to the SLIT AND NTRK-LIKE PROTEIN 4-RELATED family (PTHR45773). HPA expression shows exceptional tissue enrichment in salivary gland (285.7 nTPM), cancer-enriched expression, plasma membrane and nucleolar fibrillar center localization, and immune cell enrichment. STRING interactions reveal strong associations with potassium channel complex components: KCNMB1 (0.734, high confidence), LRRC58 (0.704, another LRR channel auxiliary subunit), KCNU1 (0.629), KCNMA1 (0.511, calcium-activated potassium channel), and KCNMB4 (0.491). Additional partners include KRT1 (0.726, keratin) and LRRC38/39 paralogs. ClinVar contains 96 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses at 9q34.3, indicating dosage sensitivity. Mouse ortholog Q91W20 (331 aa) shows strong sequence conservation. Functional hypothesis: LRRC26 serves as a tissue-specific auxiliary subunit that modulates voltage-gated potassium channel gating kinetics and surface expression in salivary gland epithelial cells, potentially regulating ion secretion and fluid homeostasis. The LRR domain likely mediates extracellular protein-protein interactions within the channel complex, analogous to LRRC58 in prostate and LRRC38 in testis.
