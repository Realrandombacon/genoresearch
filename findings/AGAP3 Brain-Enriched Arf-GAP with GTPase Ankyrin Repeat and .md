## Description
AGAP3 (Arf-GAP with GTPase domain, ankyrin repeat and PH domain-containing protein 3, also known as CENTG2 or PIKE, Q96P47) encodes an 875 aa intracellular multi-domain scaffolding protein functioning as an ARF GTPase-activating protein that regulates membrane trafficking, receptor endocytosis, and synaptic plasticity through ARF6-dependent vesicular transport mechanisms critical for neuronal function and cognitive processes. Structural architecture reveals a complex multi-domain organization characteristic of Arf-GAP family regulators: Pleckstrin homology PH domain (IPR001849/cd01250/G3DSA:2.30.29.30, residues 365-609/367-607) mediating phosphoinositide binding and membrane recruitment, Arf GTPase activating protein domain (IPR001164/G3DSA:1.10.220.150/cd08855, residues 626-746/609-743/626-734) providing catalytic GTPase activator activity (GO:0005096) for ARF6 inactivation, Small GTPase domain (IPR001806/cd04103/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 84-375/92-249/75-255) with GTPase activity (GO:0003924) and GTP binding (GO:0005525) enabling nucleotide-dependent conformational switching, Ankyrin repeat domain (IPR002110/G3DSA:1.25.40.20, residues 747-846) mediating protein-protein interactions through stacked helical repeats, and P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 75-255) providing evolutionary context within nucleotide-binding enzymes; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 70.0, medium confidence) indicates a partially structured protein with organized catalytic domains and potentially flexible linker regions consistent with dynamic membrane-cytosol shuttling during vesicular trafficking regulation.
