## Description
PI4KB encodes phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase beta (PI4KB/PI4KIIIβ/NPIK/PIK4CB), an 816 aa lipid kinase (UniProt Q9UBF8) belonging to the phosphatidylinositol 3-/4-kinase family that functions as the primary enzyme generating phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate (PI4P) at the Golgi apparatus, establishing the essential phosphoinositide platform required for vesicle trafficking, lipid transfer, and membrane identity through phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol at the D4 position of the inositol ring. Functional mechanism: PI4KB operates through regulated membrane-associated kinase cycle: (1) Membrane recruitment - NCS1 (0.992) binds PI4KB in calcium-dependent manner recruiting enzyme to Golgi membranes where ARF1-GTP (0.981) further stabilizes association; (2) Scaffold anchoring - ACBD3 (0.450) tethers PI4KB to Golgi via interaction with both PI4KB and Golgi membrane components; (3) Substrate phosphorylation - Catalytic domain (cd05168, 529-816) transfers gamma-phosphate from ATP to D4 position of phosphatidylinositol inositol ring generating PI4P; (4) Downstream signaling - PI4P serves as substrate for PIP5K1A/C (0.971/0.972) generating PI(4,5)P2 or recruits PI4P-binding effectors for vesicle budding; (5) Paralog coordination - PI4K2A/2B (0.978/0.908) produce PI4P at endosomes while PI4KA (0.445) operates at plasma membrane creating compartmentalized phosphoinositide pools; (6) Lipid synthesis coupling - CDIPT (0.944) uses PI4P intermediates for phosphatidylinositol regeneration. Biological roles include: (a) Golgi trafficking - PI4P recruits clathrin adaptors (AP-1, GGA) and lipid transfer proteins (OSBP, CERT) for vesicle formation and lipid exchange; (b) Inner ear development - Precise PI4P levels required for hair cell stereocilia organization and mechanotransduction explaining deafness phenotype; (c) Viral replication - Enteroviruses hijack PI4KB-generated PI4P platforms for replication organelle formation; (d) Lysosome organization - Acts upstream of lysosome positioning and function; (e) Secretory pathway - Essential for protein glycosylation and sorting through Golgi.
