## Description
ZNF436 encodes a 470 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9C0F3) that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 13-54) followed by three tandem C2H2-type zinc fingers (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 138-221) that mediate DNA recognition. AlphaFold prediction shows a well-folded structure with global pLDDT of 77.5, indicating confident structural modeling.

STRING interaction data reveals ZNF436 associates with chromatin modifiers and protein stability regulators: ZUP1 (0.855), UBC (0.820), YOD1 (0.788), SETDB1 (0.700), HDAC6 (0.671), USP44 (0.642), USP5 (0.564), and TRIM28 (0.400). This interaction profile suggests ZNF436 recruits the TRIM28/SETDB1 co-repressor complex for histone H3K9 trimethylation-mediated silencing, while simultaneously engaging deubiquitinase machinery (YOD1, USP44, USP5) that may regulate its own stability or that of target proteins.

HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional regulator. ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and deletions on chromosome 1p36, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation is evident with mouse ortholog (Q8BPP0, 452 aa, ~93% identity) and orangutan ortholog (Q5R5Y7, 470 aa, 100% identity).
