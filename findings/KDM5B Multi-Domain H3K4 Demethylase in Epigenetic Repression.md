## Description
KDM5B (JARID1B/PLU-1) is a 1544 aa histone H3 lysine-4 demethylase containing catalytic JmjN (IPR003349, aa31-73) and JmjC (IPR003347, aa453-619) domains, ARID DNA-binding domain (IPR001606, aa97-188), three PHD zinc fingers (IPR001965, aa311-357/1178-1222/1486-1536), and C5HC2 zinc finger (IPR004198, aa692-744). AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 72.3) across the full length. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (34.5 nTPM) with nucleoplasmic/cytosolic localization. STRING reveals high-confidence interactions with chromatin modifiers KDM1A (0.988), HDAC1 (0.986), MYC (0.966), and mitochondrial ATP synthase subunits ATP5F1A/C/D and MT-ATP6 (0.890 each), plus transcription factors TFAP2C (0.523), FOXG1 (0.468), and corepressor RCOR1 (0.446). This positions KDM5B in transcriptional repression complexes targeting tumor suppressors. ClinVar contains 214 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (Q132*, R1045*, L782*, C1195*, Y1474*, W486*), frameshifts (V1051fs, I657fs), and deletions, associated with intellectual disability. The multi-domain architecture enables chromatin targeting (ARID), reader function (PHD fingers recognizing histone marks), and catalytic demethylation (JmjC Fe(II)/α-KG-dependent). Recent 2025 studies reveal KDM5B hijacking by Epstein-Barr virus in nasopharyngeal carcinoma, USP7-mediated stabilization driving cisplatin resistance via ZBTB16/TOP2A axis, and cooperation with m6A writer KIAA1429 in hepatocellular carcinoma immune evasion through FoxO1 regulation. With 574 publications, KDM5B represents a partially characterized epigenetic regulator with emerging oncology relevance.
