# QARS1: Glutaminyl-tRNA Synthetase 1 Orchestrating Protein Translation Fidelity, Neurodevelopmental Epileptic Encephalopathy, and Microcephaly via Class I Aminoacyl-tRNA Ligase Activity and Multi-Synthetase Complex Assembly in Cerebral/Cerebellar Atrophy and Placental Development

## Description
QARS1 (Glutaminyl-tRNA Synthetase 1) is a 775 amino acid class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase that catalyzes the ATP-dependent ligation of glutamine to its cognate tRNA(Gln), serving as an essential gatekeeper of protein translation fidelity and neurodevelopment through catalytic core-mediated aminoacylation, non-specific RNA-binding domain interactions, and multi-synthetase complex assembly with other aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase architecture including N-terminal non-specific RNA-binding domain (G3DSA:1.10.8.1290, residues 4-115) for tRNA recognition and binding, catalytic core domain of glutaminyl-tRNA synthetase (cd00807, G3DSA:3.40.50.620, residues 222-568) performing glutamine-tRNA ligase activity (GO:0004819), ATP binding (GO:0005524), and nucleotide binding (GO:0000166), class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase conserved site (IPR001412, residues 270-281) with HIGH motif for catalysis, glutamyl/glutaminyl-tRNA synthetase family signatures (IPR000924, residues 267-279/281-292/296-309) defining substrate specificity, glutamine-tRNA synthetase family (IPR004514, residues 264-771) providing functional context, and C-terminal Ribosomal Protein L25-like domains (G3DSA:2.40.240.10, residues 569-682/683-770) for anticodon recognition and tRNA binding specificity. AlphaFold structure (AF-P47897-F1) predicts very high confidence (pLDDT 89.8) across the 775 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded multi-domain enzyme with stable catalytic core and RNA-binding domains suitable for precise tRNA aminoacylation. HPA data classifies QARS1 as an essential disease-related enzyme and metabolic protein involved in protein biosynthesis with cytosolic localization reflecting translation machinery function, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with housekeeping translation apparatus; disease involvement includes epilepsy and primary microcephaly. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally tight coupling to aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase network and multi-synthetase complex: LARS1 (Leucyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.999), KARS1 (Lysyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.999), EPRS1 (Glutamyl-Prolyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.999), IARS1 (Isoleucyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.999), RARS2 (Arginyl-tRNA Synthetase 2, 0.999), AIMP1 (Aminoacyl tRNA Synthetase Complex-Interacting Multifunctional Protein 1, 0.999), MARS1 (Methionyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.971), RARS1 (Arginyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.917), AIMP2 (0.877), and DARS1 (Aspartyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, 0.521) demonstrating integration into the multi-synthetase complex for coordinated translation control. ClinVar lists 202 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.2111del p.Pro704fs, c.14_35del p.Asp5fs), duplications (c.1657dup p.Leu553fs), splice site mutations (c.1758+1G>C), missense mutations (c.1781A>G p.Asn594Ser, c.2192C>G p.Ser731Cys, c.946A>C p.Thr316Pro, c.1596C>G p.Ile532Met, c.2206A>G p.Lys736Glu), and large copy number gains on 3p26.3-14.3 (spanning 52 Mb), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in developmental epileptic encephalopathy and microcephaly. Literature (17 papers) establishes QARS1 as an emerging neurodevelopmental disease gene: QARS1-associated developmental epileptic encephalopathy with first report of rare homozygous missense variant from Pakistan causing nonepileptic phenotype in family of seven patients establishing genotype-phenotype correlation; third trimester diagnosis of compound heterozygous QARS1 mutation based upon microcephaly and abnormal placenta revealing prenatal presentation; homozygous mutation causing developmental epileptic encephalopathy in siblings in Southeast Asian region confirming global disease distribution; and microcephaly, progressive seizures, and cerebral/cerebellar atrophy associated with severe QARS1 phenotype defining clinical spectrum. As an emerging essential aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase with severe neurodevelopmental disease associations, QARS1 represents a critical node connecting protein translation fidelity to brain development, seizure susceptibility, and placental function through catalytic core-mediated glutamine-tRNA ligation, multi-synthetase complex assembly with LARS1/KARS1/EPRS1/AIMP1 partners, and tRNA anticodon recognition disrupted in developmental epileptic encephalopathy, progressive microcephaly, cerebral/cerebellar atrophy, and abnormal placental development.
