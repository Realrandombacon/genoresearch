## Description
KRABD1 (KRAB domain-containing protein 1, UniProt C9JBD0) is a 128-amino acid nuclear protein located on chromosome 3p22.1. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352) spanning residues 14-86, which is characteristic of C2H2 zinc-finger transcriptional repressors. The KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 co-repressor complexes that induce heterochromatin formation via HP1 binding and histone H3K9 methylation. Human Protein Atlas shows strong testis-specific expression (191.9 nTPM) with nucleoplasmic and Golgi apparatus localization, suggesting a role in spermatogenesis-related gene silencing. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 66.9) indicates medium confidence with partial disorder, consistent with transcriptional regulators that require flexibility for partner binding. ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic copy-number gain variants on chromosome 3, implicating KRABD1 dosage sensitivity in disease. STRING database returned no interaction partners, typical for understudied dark genes, but the KRAB domain architecture strongly predicts interaction with KAP1/TRIM28. This gene likely functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor during germ cell development, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression potentially contributing to reproductive disorders or developmental anomalies.
