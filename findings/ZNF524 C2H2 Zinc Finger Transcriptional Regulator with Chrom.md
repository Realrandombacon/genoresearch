## Description
ZNF524 (Gene ID: 147807, UniProt Q96C55) is a 264 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor mapping to chromosome 19q13.42 (55,599,573-55,603,137 bp, ~3.6 kb genomic span). InterPro analysis reveals 3 canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, PF13894, PF13912) at positions 114-197, forming the classic beta-beta-alpha zinc finger fold (G3DSA:3.30.160.60, SSF57667) characteristic of Krüppel-type DNA-binding proteins. The zinc finger array (PS00028 signature, PS50157 profile) mediates sequence-specific double-stranded DNA binding, typically recognizing GC-rich consensus sequences (5'-G/C-G/C-G-N-G/T-3'). HPA expression data shows ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasmic subcellular localization, and classification as transcription factor/predicted intracellular protein. STRING interactions reveal 10 protein partners with medium confidence scores (0.438-0.677): CHD4 (0.455, chromodomain helicase DNA-binding protein 4, NuRD complex chromatin remodeler), YY1AP1 (0.451, YY1-associated protein 1, transcriptional co-regulator), POLD1 (0.535, DNA polymerase delta catalytic subunit, DNA replication/repair), SNRPN (0.533, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide N, splicing), BORCS6 (0.677, BLOC-1 related complex subunit 6, endolysosomal trafficking), THG1L (0.556, tRNA-histidine guanylyltransferase 1-like), VRK3 (0.476, vaccinia-related kinase 3, pseudokinase), SNAPC2 (0.461, snRNA-activating protein complex subunit 2, Pol II/Pol III transcription), RPS6KL1 (0.460, ribosomal protein S6 kinase-like 1), and CPTP (0.438, ceramide-1-phosphate transporter). ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants spanning 19q13.33-13.43 (deletions 55-56 Mb, duplications 49-58 Mb whole arm), associated with 19q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, growth abnormalities, and dysmorphic features. The 19q13.4 region contains dosage-sensitive genes including ZNF524 itself and neighboring zinc finger clusters. Functional hypothesis: ZNF524 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulator that recruits chromatin remodeling complexes (particularly NuRD via CHD4 interaction) to modulate local chromatin structure and gene expression. The C2H2 zinc finger array provides modular DNA recognition capacity, potentially targeting distinct genomic loci through combinatorial finger usage. Interaction with YY1AP1 suggests co-regulatory activity with YY1 (yin yang 1), a bifunctional transcriptional activator/repressor. CHD4 interaction indicates involvement in ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeling and histone deacetylation, positioning ZNF524 in epigenetic silencing complexes. Nuclear localization and ubiquitous expression suggest housekeeping regulatory functions across cell types, potentially maintaining basal transcription programs or responding to cellular stress. Pathogenic 19q CNVs disrupt ZNF524 dosage alongside neighboring zinc finger genes, contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through altered transcriptional regulation of downstream targets. This represents a "dark gene" in the sense that despite clear domain architecture and interaction data, specific DNA binding motifs, target genes, and biological pathways remain uncharacterized in literature.
