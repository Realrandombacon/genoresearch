## Description
TMEM52 (Transmembrane protein 52) encodes a 209 amino acid predicted membrane protein (UniProt Q8NDY8) belonging to the TMEM52 family (IPR038942, residues 2-208; PF14979, residues 46-180; PTHR33955, residues 2-208). The protein contains a conserved transmembrane domain architecture characteristic of integral membrane proteins, though specific topology remains uncharacterized. The AlphaFold predicted structure displays low-to-medium confidence folding (global pLDDT 58.8) across the full-length 209 aa protein, suggesting TMEM52 contains both structured transmembrane regions and flexible intracellular/extracellular domains typical of single-pass or multi-pass membrane proteins. Human Protein Atlas classifies TMEM52 as a predicted intracellular and membrane protein with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and plasma membrane, showing group-enriched RNA expression with exceptionally high levels in skeletal muscle (280.0 nTPM), pancreas (206.6 nTPM), and tongue (193.9 nTPM), suggesting primary roles in striated muscle biology and secretory tissue function. STRING interaction network identifies 8 medium-confidence functional partners revealing potential biological mechanisms: GPATCH4 (score 0.595, G-patch domain-containing protein 4, involved in RNA processing and transcriptional regulation), CFAP74 (0.561, cilia- and flagella-associated protein 74, microtubule-binding protein involved in ciliary function), SINHCAF (0.503, SIN3-HDAC complex-associated factor, transcriptional corepressor), NXPH4 (0.494, neurexophilin 4, neuronal glycoprotein), ANKRD10 (0.488, ankyrin repeat domain 10, ubiquitin ligase substrate recognition), LRRC30 (0.459, leucine-rich repeat-containing protein 30), STEAP2 (0.441, six-transmembrane epithelial antigen of prostate 2, metalloreductase), and CLPSL1 (0.405, colipase-like pancreatic lipase 1, digestive enzyme). The GPATCH4 and CFAP74 interactions are particularly notable, suggesting TMEM52 may function at the intersection of RNA processing, cytoskeletal organization, and ciliary/microtubule dynamics. ClinVar contains 135 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number variations spanning chromosome 1p36 regions (chr1:849467-3153423 x3 gain, chr1:849466-4529103 x3 gain, chr1:849466-8966102 x1 loss, chr1:1129319-3615916 x3 gain, chr1:1182855-2431925 x1 loss, chr1:849467-3500877 x1 loss, chr1:849467-9627901 x1 loss, chr1:99125-34026935 x3 gain) plus one splice region variant (NM_178545.4:c.171-82G>A), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and overexpression disrupt cellular homeostasis. The chromosomal location at 1p36.33-p36.32 overlaps with 1p36 deletion syndrome critical region associated with intellectual disability, developmental delay, cardiomyopathy, and distinctive facial features. Given the transmembrane architecture, muscle-enriched expression (280.0 nTPM in skeletal muscle), chromosome 1p36 CNV-associated pathogenicity, and GPATCH4/CFAP74 interactions, TMEM52 likely functions as a membrane-associated scaffold protein involved in organizing protein complexes at the plasma membrane or nuclear envelope, with particular importance in muscle tissue where membrane integrity and cytoskeletal organization are critical for contractile function. The low-confidence AlphaFold structure suggests conformational flexibility that may enable dynamic interactions with multiple partners during muscle development or regeneration.
