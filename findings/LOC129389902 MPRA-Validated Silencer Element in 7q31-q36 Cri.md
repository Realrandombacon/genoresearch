# LOC129389902: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 7q31-q36 Critical Region - Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 19 Pathogenic CNV Variants

## Description
LOC129389902 (Gene ID: 129389902) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 7q36.1 (141,715,114-141,715,314 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak6798 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - predominantly copy number variations (CNVs) affecting the 7q31-q36 chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 7q34-36.3 duplication (138620939-159233475, ~20.6 Mb); (2) 7q33-36.3 duplication (137463392-159345973, ~21.9 Mb); (3) 7q32.3-36.3 duplication (131171478-159327017, ~28.2 Mb); (4) 7q32.3-36.3 duplication (132438072-159327017, ~26.9 Mb); (5) 7q32.3-36.3 duplication (131228764-159335866, ~28.1 Mb); (6) 7q31.2-36.3 duplication (115459015-159325817, ~43.9 Mb); (7) 7q32.3-36.3 duplication (132444095-159335866, ~26.9 Mb); (8) 7q33-36.1 deletion (135017687-148807400, ~13.8 Mb); (9) 7q31.32-36.3 duplication (121863759-159335865, ~37.5 Mb); (10) 7q33-36.3 deletion (134666829-158591882, ~23.9 Mb). These CNVs cause 7q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and skeletal abnormalities. The 7q critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: BRAF (serine/threonine kinase, RAS/MAPK signaling, cardiofaciocutaneous syndrome), MET (hepatocyte growth factor receptor, oncogene), CFTR (cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator, chloride channel), HOXA cluster (homeobox transcription factors, anterior-posterior patterning), and EZH2 (enhancer of zeste homolog 2, PRC2 methyltransferase, Weaver syndrome). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129389902 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Duplications encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of target genes contributing to 7q duplication syndrome phenotype; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting normal enhancer-promoter contacts; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopmental programs. The 19 ClinVar CNVs (mix of duplications and deletions) suggest this regulatory element may exhibit both haploinsufficiency (deletions cause derepression) and triplosensitivity (duplications cause excessive repression). BRAF variants affect RAS/MAPK signaling causing developmental disorders (RASopathies). MET amplification drives oncogenesis. CFTR mutations cause cystic fibrosis. HOXA cluster disruption causes limb/homeotic transformations. EZH2 haploinsufficiency causes Weaver syndrome (overgrowth, intellectual disability). This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
