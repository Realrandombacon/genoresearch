## Description
SLC36A3 (Solute carrier family 36 member 3, also known as PAT3, Proton-coupled amino acid transporter 3, TRAMD2, tramdorin2) is a 470 amino acid membrane protein (UniProt Q495N2) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 84.2), characteristic of stable transmembrane transporters that function in solute movement across membranes. The protein contains an amino acid transporter transmembrane domain (IPR013057/PF01490, residues 42-450) that forms the core transport machinery with multiple membrane-spanning helices, and belongs to the amino acid/polyamine transporter 2 family (PTHR22950, residues 40-448). These interactions suggest SLC36A3 may link amino acid transport to cytoskeletal organization and cell adhesion, potentially coordinating nutrient uptake with cell migration or structural remodeling.\n\nHPA expression reveals SLC36A3 is testis-enriched with expression of 7.0 nTPM, detected in a single tissue type. This highly restricted expression pattern suggests SLC36A3 plays a specialized role in spermatogenesis or testis-specific metabolism. The protein is classified as a metabolic membrane transporter protein. Not detected in cancer or immune cells indicates SLC36A3 is not dysregulated in tumors or immune responses. Group-enriched single cell type specificity suggests expression in specific testicular cell types, potentially spermatogenic cells or Sertoli cells.\n\nClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all consisting of large copy number variations on chromosome 5q: copy number gains (5q33.1-35.2, 5q32-35.3, 5p15.33-q35.3, 5q21.3-35.3, 5q23.3-33.2, 5q32-35.3 amplifications) and copy number losses (5p15.1-q35.2, 5q21.3-33.2 deletions). Given SLC36A3's testis-enriched expression, loss-of-function may contribute to male infertility or spermatogenic defects through impaired amino acid supply to developing germ cells.\n\nConservation is strong across mammals: human (Q495N2, 470 aa), mouse (Q811P0, 477 aa), rat (Q4V8B1, 477 aa), indicating an ancient and essential function in amino acid transport conserved in mammals.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: SLC36A3/PAT3 functions as a proton-coupled amino acid symporter that transports small neutral amino acids (such as glycine, alanine, proline) across membranes using the proton gradient as an energy source. The well-folded transmembrane domain (pLDDT 84.2) forms a substrate-binding pocket and proton translocation pathway typical of the SLC36/PAT family. Testis-enriched expression (7.0 nTPM) suggests SLC36A3 supplies amino acids to spermatogenic cells during spermatogenesis, a process requiring high protein synthesis. SPATA31A7 interaction supports a role in spermatogenesis. 5q CNVs cause SLC36A3 dosage imbalance, potentially disrupting amino acid homeostasis in testis and contributing to male infertility. SLC36A3 represents a candidate gene for non-obstructive azoospermia and a potential target for male contraception.
