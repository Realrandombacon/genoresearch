## Description
MPV17L (Mpv17-like protein/M-LP) is a 196 aa mitochondrial inner membrane protein (UniProt Q2QL34, AlphaFold pLDDT 85.7) belonging to the Mpv17/PMP22 family (IPR007248 aa5-166, PF04117 aa106-165). Despite only 16 papers in literature, recent studies (2021-2025) reveal MPV17L as an atypical cyclic nucleotide phosphodiesterase (PDE) lacking canonical PDE structural motifs. STRING interactions confirm functional network: MPV17 (0.912), MPV17L2 (0.903), HTRA2/Omi (0.608), PDXDC1 (0.672). HPA has no expression data (likely low/undetectable). ClinVar contains 150 pathogenic variants (chr16p13.11 CNVs, deletions, duplications). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: MPV17L functions as a mitochondrial atypical PDE that regulates cAMP/cGMP signaling within mitochondria, controlling oxidative stress responses and metabolic homeostasis. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: (1) MPV17L activates Omi/HtrA2 protease to protect against mitochondrial oxidative stress and apoptosis (2008, 98 citations), (2) MPV17L deficiency activates Wnt and TGF-β pathways leading to β-cell hyperplasia and improved glucose tolerance (2021), (3) MPV17L knockout induces physiological afferent cardiac hypertrophy (2023), (4) MPV17L knockout alleviates diabetic conditions in mice (2024). The MPV17-MPV17L-MPV17L2 paralog network suggests functional redundancy in mitochondrial membrane integrity. CROSS-DOMAIN REASONING: High AlphaFold confidence (85.7) indicates stable transmembrane structure; 150 ClinVar variants suggest disease relevance despite limited literature; HTRA2 interaction links to Parkinson's disease pathways. MPV17L represents an emerging therapeutic target for diabetes and cardiac disease.
