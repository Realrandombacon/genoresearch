## Description
OSER1 (Oxidative Stress-Responsive Serine-Rich Protein 1, UniProt Q9NX31) is a 292 amino acid nucleoli/nucleoli rim/nuclear speckle-localized protein with partial characterization (35 papers) that functions as a FOXO-regulated oxidative stress response protein extending lifespan in multiple species through oxidative stress reduction. The protein contains a Domain of Unknown Function DUF776 (IPR008494/PF05604, residues 1-292/1-180) and belongs to the oxidative stress-response serine-rich protein 1 family (PTHR31383, residues 1-292), placing it in the oxidative stress response machinery despite the DUF classification indicating incomplete mechanistic characterization. STRING interactions reveal medium-confidence partnerships with immune and RNA processing proteins: C2orf73 (0.474, chromosome 2 open reading frame 73), NCR1 (0.472, natural cytotoxicity triggering receptor 1/NKp46), PSMB9 (0.423, proteasome 20S subunit beta 9/immunoproteasome), and TRMT13 (0.420, tRNA methyltransferase 13), suggesting potential roles in immune regulation and RNA modification. HPA data shows nucleoli, nucleoli rim, and nuclear speckles subcellular localization, low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, and predicted intracellular protein classification. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 20q11.21-13.12 (chr20:31010829-44560369, chr20:35237946-47631818), 20q13.12 (chr20:42778133-43079191), deletions at 20q11.21-13.12 (chr20:42223339-45362473/44638757), and copy number gains at 20p13-q13.33 (chr20:61569-62915555/99557-64277321), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential oxidative stress-related disorders, aging phenotypes, or cancer susceptibility. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 57.9) indicates medium confidence with partially structured/disordered architecture typical of serine-rich regulatory proteins that may function through intrinsically disordered regions. Functional hypothesis: OSER1 acts as a nucleolar/nuclear speckle-localized oxidative stress-responsive protein that is transcriptionally regulated by FOXO transcription factors, where recent 2021-2024 research demonstrates: (1) FOXO-regulated OSER1 reduces oxidative stress and extends lifespan in multiple species (2024, 27 citations)—OSER1 is a direct FOXO target mediating longevity through reactive oxygen species (ROS) scavenging or antioxidant pathway activation, (2) LncRNA OSER1-AS1 regulates inflammation and apoptosis of rheumatoid arthritis fibroblast-like synoviocytes via miR-1298-5p/E2F1 axis (2022, 22 citations)—OSER1 locus produces regulatory lncRNA affecting autoimmune disease progression, (3) LncRNA OSER1-AS1 interacts with miR-612/FOXM1 axis to modulate gefitinib resistance of lung adenocarcinoma (2021, 14 citations)—OSER1 antisense transcript influences cancer chemoresistance through miRNA sponging, (4) STRING partners (NCR1/NKp46, PSMB9/immunoproteasome, TRMT13/tRNA methyltransferase) suggest connections to immune surveillance and RNA modification, with nucleolar/nuclear speckle localization reflecting potential roles in ribosome biogenesis, stress granule formation, or RNA processing under oxidative stress conditions, and 9 ClinVar variants at 20q11.21-13.12/20p13-q13.33 potentially contributing to accelerated aging, oxidative stress disorders, autoimmune diseases, or cancer susceptibility through defective ROS management, making OSER1 a potential therapeutic target for aging-related diseases and chemoresistance through oxidative stress pathway modulation.
