## Description
ARRDC3 encodes a 414 aa arrestin domain-containing protein 3 (UniProt Q96B67), functioning as a tumor suppressor scaffold protein that regulates GPCR trafficking, ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation, and viral restriction through arrestin domain-mediated protein-protein interactions. The protein contains canonical arrestin architecture with N-terminal arrestin domain (IPR011021/PF00339, aa18-165) and C-terminal arrestin domain (IPR011022/PF02752, aa187-314), belonging to the arrestin domain-containing protein family (IPR050357/PTHR11188, aa5-405) with immunoglobulin E-set domain superfamily structure (IPR014756/SSF81296, aa11-343). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 82.2), indicating a well-folded stable arrestin fold typical of scaffold proteins. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a predicted intracellular transporter with immune cell-enhanced and cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal ARRDC3 forms ubiquitin ligase complexes: NEDD4 (0.992, neural precursor cell expressed developmentally down-regulated protein 4 E3 ubiquitin ligase), WWP2 (0.946, WW domain-containing E3 ubiquitin protein ligase 2), ADRB2 (0.934, beta-2 adrenergic receptor GPCR), WWP1 (0.884), NEDD4L (0.855, NEDD4-like E3 ubiquitin ligase), ARRB1 (0.838, arrestin beta-1), ITCH (0.821, E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase Itchy), ARRB2 (0.701, arrestin beta-2), SAG (0.701, S-antigen visual arrestin), TXN (0.696, thioredoxin). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 5q14.3-21.2 copy number losses/gains, splice site mutation (c.613+1G>A), inversions, and deletions. Literature (150 papers) demonstrates ARRDC3 inhibits gastric cancer progression via ARRDC3/ITGB4/PI3K/AKT pathway inactivation; ARRDC3 regulates non-small cell lung cancer cisplatin resistance and autophagy; lncRNA MEG3 suppresses prostate cancer via miR-148a-3p/ARRDC3 signaling axis and macrophage polarization; ARRDC3 promotes lysosome-mediated YAP degradation inhibiting enterovirus D68/A71 replication; ARRDC3 tyrosine phosphorylation functions as molecular switch controlling c-Src versus WWP2 interactions for distinct scaffold functions. ARRDC3 functions as a ubiquitin ligase adaptor—its arrestin domains recruit NEDD4 family E3 ligases (NEDD4/WWP2/WWP1/NEDD4L/ITCH) to ubiquitinate cargo proteins including GPCRs (ADRB2) and integrins (ITGB4) for endosomal sorting and lysosomal degradation. The ultra-high confidence NEDD4 interaction (0.992) indicates ARRDC3 is a primary NEDD4 substrate adaptor. In cancer, ARRDC3 suppresses tumor progression by promoting ITGB4 ubiquitination and degradation, inactivating PI3K/AKT signaling; YAP degradation restricts Hippo pathway oncogenic signaling. The ADRB2 interaction (0.934) places ARRDC3 in GPCR desensitization and trafficking pathways alongside canonical arrestins (ARRB1/ARRB2). Viral restriction reflects ARRDC3-mediated lysosomal degradation of viral components or host factors required for enterovirus replication. Tyrosine phosphorylation creates a molecular switch—phosphorylated ARRDC3 preferentially binds c-Src for signaling versus WWP2 for ubiquitination, enabling context-dependent scaffold functions. Copy number variants on 5q may cause ARRDC3 haploinsufficiency contributing to cancer susceptibility or developmental disorders. The well-folded arrestin structure (pLDDT 82.2) enables ARRDC3 to serve as a stable platform coordinating multiple protein complexes at membrane-cytosol interfaces.
