## Description
FAM9C encodes a 166 aa X-linked protein (UniProt Q8IZT9) containing an XLR/SYCP3/FAM9 domain (IPR006888, PF04803, aa 77-158) with coiled-coil structure, homologous to synaptonemal complex protein 3 (SYCP3) involved in meiotic chromosome pairing. The protein belongs to the X-linked lymphocyte-regulated (XLR) family (IPR051443, PTHR19368) that arose through X chromosome duplication. HPA shows lymphoid tissue-enriched expression (35.9 nTPM) with cancer-enriched specificity, suggesting immune cell functions. STRING interactions reveal associations with testis-expressed proteins TEX12 (0.405), TEX30 (0.532), TEX13C (0.478), TEX29 (0.468) - indicating potential meiotic/reproductive roles, plus OGFOD1 (0.641) involved in tRNA modification and CCDC191/SPHAR (0.621-0.623) of unknown function. ClinVar documents 165 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly Xp22.33-11.4 copy number gains/losses of uncertain significance, suggesting dosage sensitivity on the X chromosome. The SYCP3 homology suggests FAM9C may function in chromosome axis formation or meiotic recombination, while lymphoid enrichment indicates potential B/T-cell specific chromatin regulation. X-linked location and testis-expression pattern implies dual roles in germline meiosis and somatic lymphoid function, possibly as a scaffold protein organizing chromatin loops through coiled-coil dimerization.
