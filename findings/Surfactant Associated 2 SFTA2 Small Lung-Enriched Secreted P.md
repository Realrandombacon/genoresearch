## Description
SFTA2 (Surfactant Associated 2) is a small 78-amino acid secreted protein with 35 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein belongs to the surfactant-associated protein 2 family (IPR028198, positions 1-78; PF15210, positions 20-78; PTHR38500, positions 1-78) with predicted secreted and intracellular localization. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 66.9, medium confidence), consistent with small secreted proteins that may adopt defined structures upon binding partners. HPA reveals highly lung-enriched expression (178.9 nTPM top tissue) with esophagus expression (48.9 nTPM), group enriched RNA specificity, detected-in-some distribution pattern, cancer-enriched RNA specificity, not detected in immune cells, with annotated functions as predicted secreted protein. ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (chromosome 6p22.1-q14.1 and 6p25.3-q27 copy number gains and deletions with uncertain clinical significance), indicating dosage sensitivity potentially affecting lung development. STRING interactions reveal SFTA2 functions within pulmonary surfactant and secretory protein networks with medium confidence connections to SFTA3 (0.649) - surfactant associated 3 paralog, SFTPA1 (0.632) - surfactant protein A1, MUCL3 (0.544) - mucin like 3, SFTPA2 (0.515) - surfactant protein A2, SFTPC (0.505) - surfactant protein C, SCGB3A2 (0.474) - secretoglobin family 3A member 2, SFTPB (0.453) - surfactant protein B, VARS1 (0.447) - valyl-tRNA synthetase 1, SFTPD (0.447) - surfactant protein D, and VARS2 (0.418) - valyl-tRNA synthetase 2. Recent studies (2022-2026) established SFTA2 as a type II pneumocyte-derived biomarker for non-small cell lung cancer prognosis and immune regulation, SFTA2 reduced colorectal cancer ferroptosis promoting metastasis through regulating EMT transition by degradation of Nrf2, CTSH and SFTA2 influencing lung cancer risk through candidate pathway analysis of surfactant proteins, and SFTA2 role in epithelial-mesenchymal transition model revealing novel prognosis implications in non-small-cell lung carcinoma. We hypothesize SFTA2 functions as a lung-enriched secreted surfactant-associated protein that regulates epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and ferroptosis resistance through Nrf2 pathway modulation, potentially through interactions with other surfactant proteins (SFTPA1/A2/B/C/D, SFTA3) in pulmonary surfactant complexes. The small size (78 aa) and medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 66.9) suggest it may function as a peptide regulator or co-factor rather than enzymatic component. Lung enrichment (178.9 nTPM) and cancer-enriched specificity indicate roles in lung homeostasis and oncogenesis. The SFTA3 paralog connection (0.649) suggests functional redundancy within surfactant-associated protein subfamily. SFTA2 represents an uncharacterized lung-enriched secreted protein linking surfactant biology with EMT regulation, ferroptosis resistance, and cancer progression through Nrf2 signaling.
