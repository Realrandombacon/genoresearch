## Description
PHC3 (Q8NDX5) is a 983-aa Polycomb group protein with low-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 49.9), indicating extensive intrinsic disorder characteristic of chromatin regulatory proteins that function through dynamic protein-protein interactions rather than stable globular folds. InterPro analysis reveals Sterile Alpha Motif (SAM) domain (IPR001660/IPR013761/PF00536/PS50105: positions 911-983) enabling open-ended polymerization critical for Polycomb complex assembly, FCS-type zinc finger (IPR012313/IPR038603/PF21319/PS51024: positions 767-813) providing zinc-dependent DNA/protein binding capability, and Polycomb group chromatin remodeling factor family signature (IPR050548/PTHR12247: positions 613-981). HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced pattern with detection in all tissues, highest in bone marrow (48.0 nTPM), localized to nucleoplasm, functioning as developmental protein with DNA-binding molecular activity. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive high-confidence binding to Polycomb Repressive Complex 1 (PRC1) components: PHC1 (0.996), CBX8 (0.994), RNF2/RING1B (0.994), PCGF2/MEL18 (0.993), BMI1/PCGF4 (0.992), RING1 (0.990), CBX4 (0.989), CBX6 (0.989), CBX2 (0.977), CBX7 (0.969), establishing PHC3 as core PRC1 scaffold. Literature (53 papers - moderately studied) documents prion-like properties of short PHC3 isoforms forming self-perpetuating aggregates, SAM linker enabling open-ended PHC3 polymerization essential for chromatin compaction, circ-PHC3 enhancing ovarian cancer progression via miR-497-5p/SOX9 pathway, and PHC3 amplification as cancer-driving alteration in solid tumors. ClinVar contains 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains at 3q22.1-29, 3q13.31-26.31, 3q25.32-26.33, 3q26.2-26.33, 3q26.2-28, 3p26.3-q29 regions, plus splice variants c.15-1G>T and c.181-23T>G, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. The intrinsically disordered architecture (pLDDT 49.9), SAM domain polymerization capability, FCS zinc finger, exclusive PRC1 interaction network, and pathogenic variant burden suggest PHC3 functions as scaffold protein driving Polycomb complex oligomerization through SAM-SAM interactions to compact chromatin and silence developmental genes, with disruption potentially contributing to cancer through altered epigenetic regulation and developmental disorders via impaired chromatin silencing programs.
