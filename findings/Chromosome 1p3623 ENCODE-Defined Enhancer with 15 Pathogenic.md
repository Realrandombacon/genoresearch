## Description
LOC108281189 (Gene ID: 108281189) is an ENCODE-defined enhancer element on chromosome 1p36.23 (NC_000001.11, positions 8173055-8173585, ~530 bp region) located in the intergenic region between RPL7AP18 (ribosomal protein L7a pseudogene 18) and LOC102724539 with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. Defined by ENCODE project as nontranscribed region with regulatory potential based on co-occurrence of H3K27ac and H3K4me1 ChIP-seq marks with absence of capped transcripts by CAGE. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding element), preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with enhancer/tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding regulatory function. However, ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 2 copy number gains/duplications (variant 2664871: 1p36.33-35.1 ~33.9 Mb trisomy; variant 154839: 1p36.31-36.13 ~9.5 Mb trisomy) and 13 copy number losses/deletions (variant 154704: 1p36.31-36.13 ~13.0 Mb monosomy; variant 154548: 1p36.33-36.22 ~8.4 Mb monosomy; variant 153663: 1p36.33-36.22 ~8.7 Mb monosomy; variant 153078: 1p36.33-36.22 ~8.7 Mb monosomy; variant 148885: 1p36.31-36.21 ~9.5 Mb monosomy; variant 148387: 1p36.32-36.21 ~8.0 Mb monosomy; variant 147521: 1p36.33-36.22 ~11.6 Mb monosomy; variant 59884: 1p36.23-36.21 ~5.9 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 1p36.23 chromosomal region contains critical neuroprotection, glycolysis, circadian rhythm, and immune signaling-associated genes including PARK7 (Parkinsonism associated deglycase/DJ-1, oxidative stress sensor, alpha-synuclein aggregation prevention, mitochondrial function, autosomal recessive early-onset Parkinson disease), ENO1 (enolase 1, glycolytic enzyme, plasminogen receptor, cell surface proteolysis, cancer metastasis, autoimmune encephalopathy), PER3 (period circadian regulator 3, circadian clock component, sleep-wake regulation, seasonal affective disorder, cancer chronotherapy), TNFRSF9 (TNF receptor superfamily member 9/4-1BB, T cell costimulation, cancer immunotherapy target, autoimmune regulation), and UTS2 (urotensin 2, vasoconstrictor peptide, cardiovascular function, renal disease, pulmonary hypertension). Functional hypothesis: LOC108281189 likely represents a critical 1p36.23 neuronal/immune enhancer element within the PARK7-ENO1 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a dopaminergic neuron-specific enhancer coordinating PARK7-ENO1-PER3 expression in substantia nigra neurons or as a chromatin boundary separating neuroprotection genes (PARK7) from metabolic genes (ENO1) and circadian genes (PER3). The 15 pathogenic CNVs (predominantly deletions, 5.9-33.9 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts PARK7-mediated oxidative stress defense and ENO1-mediated glycolytic flux. Disease mechanism involves PARK7 mutation causing autosomal recessive early-onset Parkinson disease (oxidative stress accumulation, mitochondrial dysfunction, dopaminergic neuron degeneration, alpha-synuclein aggregation), PARK7 overexpression causing cancer (oxidative stress tolerance, chemoresistance, tumor survival), ENO1 overexpression causing cancer metastasis (plasminogen activation, extracellular matrix degradation, invasion), ENO1 autoantibodies causing cancer-associated retinopathy and autoimmune encephalopathy, PER3 polymorphism causing circadian sleep disorders (delayed sleep phase, seasonal affective disorder, cancer chronotherapy response), PER3 dysregulation causing cancer (cell cycle checkpoint defect, DNA damage response impairment), TNFRSF9 activation causing autoimmunity (T cell hyperactivation, inflammatory disease), TNFRSF9 targeting causing cancer immunotherapy (4-1BB agonist antibodies, T cell expansion), UTS2 overexpression causing cardiovascular disease (vasoconstriction, hypertension, pulmonary arterial hypertension, heart failure), and 1p36 deletion syndrome presenting with intellectual disability, developmental delay, cardiomyopathy, and dysmorphic features. The 1p36.23 region is a well-established 1p36 deletion syndrome locus with haploinsufficiency causing characteristic clinical features including neurodevelopmental abnormalities. The ENCODE enhancer marks (H3K27ac + H3K4me1) combined with ClinVar evidence (15 CNVs 5.9-33.9 Mb) and PARK7-ENO1-PER3-TNFRSF9-UTS2 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a neuronal/immune-specific enhancer critical for neuroprotection-glycolysis-circadian-immune coordination.
