## Description
USP13 (Ubiquitin specific peptidase 13, Ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase 13, Q92995) encodes a 863 aa cysteine protease functioning as a critical deubiquitinating enzyme (DUB) regulating protein stability, autophagy initiation, and tumor suppression through removal of Lys48-linked polyubiquitin chains from key substrates. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded multi-domain organization with moderate-high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 77.1): Peptidase C19 ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase domain (IPR001394/cd02658, residues 336-858/337-859) forming the catalytic core with GO terms for cysteine-type deubiquitinase activity (GO:0004843) and protein deubiquitination (GO:0016579), UBA1 domain (cd14384, residues 652-700) and UBA2 domain (cd14386, residues 728-769) mediating ubiquitin binding and substrate recognition, UBA-like superfamily (IPR009060, residues 723-769) with GO term for protein binding (GO:0005515), Zinc finger UBP-type domain (IPR001607, residues 187-295) with GO term for zinc ion binding (GO:0008270) stabilizing the catalytic domain, RING/FYVE/PHD-type zinc finger superfamily (IPR013083/G3DSA:3.30.40.10, residues 21-124/186-282) providing additional ubiquitin interaction surfaces, DNA helicase RuvA C-terminal domain-like folds (G3DSA:1.10.8.10, residues 654-701/725-777) structurally related to UBA domains, and Cysteine proteinase superfamily (G3DSA:3.90.70.10, residues 329-631) establishing catalytic mechanism.
