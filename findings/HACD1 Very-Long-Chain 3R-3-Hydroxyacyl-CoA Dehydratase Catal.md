# HACD1: Very-Long-Chain (3R)-3-Hydroxyacyl-CoA Dehydratase Catalyzing Third Step of Fatty Acid Elongation Cycle, Mutated in Autosomal Recessive Congenital Myopathy with Fiber-Type Disproportion and Limb-Girdle Weakness

**Quality Score:** 10/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
HACD1 (B0YJ81) encodes a 288 amino acid very-long-chain (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase 1 belonging to the PTPLA/PTPLB protein tyrosine phosphatase-like superfamily that functions as the third enzyme in the four-step fatty acid elongation cycle producing very-long-chain fatty acids (VLCFAs) essential for membrane phospholipids, sphingolipids, and myelin formation. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved catalytic domain architecture: a protein-tyrosine phosphatase-like PTPLA family domain (IPR007482/PF04387, positions 60-279/119-279) classified within the very-long-chain (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase family (PTHR11035, positions 60-279) containing the dehydration active site converting 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA to trans-2-enoyl-CoA substrates during fatty acid chain extension. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 75.1, confident range) consistent with stable enzymatic architecture required for precise substrate positioning during dehydration chemistry. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive partnerships with fatty acid elongation machinery forming the functional elongase complex: ELOVL6 (0.944) and ELOVL4 (0.943), elongation of very-long-chain fatty acids proteins 6 and 4 catalyzing the condensation step; HSD17B12 (0.931), 17-beta-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase 12 performing the reduction step; HACD4 (0.919) and HACD3 (0.907), paralogous dehydratases suggesting functional redundancy or tissue-specific isoforms; TECR (0.917), trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase catalyzing the fourth/final reduction step; SCD (0.917) and SCD5 (0.491), stearoyl-CoA desaturases introducing double bonds into fatty acyl chains; FADS1 (0.606) and ELOVL2 (0.409), fatty acid desaturase 1 and elongase 2 for polyunsaturated fatty acid synthesis. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with heart muscle as top expressing tissue (68.7 nTPM), detected in many tissues, immune cell enriched classification consistent with fatty acid metabolism demands in proliferating immune cells, classified under metabolic proteins/enzymes/disease-related genes with fatty acid biosynthesis, fatty acid metabolism, lipid biosynthesis, and lipid metabolism biological processes, lyase molecular function, and disease variant involvement. ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift mutations (c.312_313del p.His104fs, c.103del p.Arg35fs, c.706del p.Ser235_Ile236insTer disrupting catalytic domain), nonsense mutations (c.133G>T p.Glu45Ter, c.355C>T p.Gln119Ter truncating the protein), large deletions (chr10p14-q26.3 losses, chr10p15.3-12.31 gains), though specific monogenic disease names remain undefined in ClinVar database. Literature (42 papers) demonstrates HACD1 variants cause autosomal recessive congenital myopathy with fiber-type disproportion presenting as delayed motor milestones, slowly progressive adult-onset limb-girdle weakness, and characteristic skeletal muscle MRI findings identified in Middle Eastern, Asian, Iranian, and Brazilian cohorts; comparative studies in large yellow croaker and rainbow trout show HACD1 critical for long-chain polyunsaturated fatty acid (LC-PUFA) biosynthesis explaining species differences in LC-PUFA production capacity. The mechanistic hypothesis: HACD1 functions as an integral membrane enzyme of the endoplasmic reticulum fatty acid elongase complex where it catalyzes the dehydration step (step 3 of 4) converting 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA intermediates to trans-2-enoyl-CoA products through water elimination from the β-carbon; the PTPLA-like domain (positions 60-279) contains catalytic residues coordinating substrate binding and proton abstraction enabling stereospecific dehydration of (3R)-hydroxyacyl substrates; high-confidence STRING interactions with ELOVL4/6 (condensation), HSD17B12 (reduction), and TECR (final reduction) indicate HACD1 operates within a stable multi-enzyme complex channeling fatty acyl intermediates between active sites without diffusion into bulk membrane; heart muscle enrichment (68.7 nTPM) reflects high VLCFA demand for cardiac membrane phospholipids and cardiolipin synthesis essential for mitochondrial function; immune cell enrichment supports rapid membrane biogenesis during lymphocyte proliferation requiring VLCFA-containing sphingolipids; confident AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 75.1) indicates stable transmembrane topology with catalytic site oriented toward cytosolic face for CoA-substrate access; 32 pathogenic variants distributed across the catalytic domain disrupt dehydration activity causing accumulation of 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA intermediates and depletion of VLCFAs necessary for myelin stability, muscle membrane integrity, and neuromuscular junction function explaining congenital myopathy phenotype with fiber-type disproportion; paralogous HACD3/HACD4 interactions suggest potential compensatory mechanisms that may explain variable disease severity; skeletal muscle MRI findings in Brazilian cohort indicate specific muscle group vulnerability correlating with HACD1 expression patterns.
