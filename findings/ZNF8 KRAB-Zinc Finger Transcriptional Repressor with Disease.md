## Description
ZNF8 encodes a 575-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 19q13.43. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 24-96) and three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 257-340), characteristic of transcriptional repressors that recruit KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors to silence target genes via heterochromatin formation. STRING interactions identify 10 protein partners including WEE1 (0.612), ZNF189 (0.610), HINFP (0.487), ZNF827 (0.475), and TSHZ3 (0.457), suggesting roles in cell cycle regulation and chromatin remodeling complexes. HPA data shows nuclear localization with expression in multiple tissues (low tissue specificity), consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains spanning 19q13.41-13.43, implicating ZNF8 dosage sensitivity in disease etiology. The KRAB domain mediates transcriptional silencing through recruitment of heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) and histone methyltransferases, while the C2H2 zinc fingers confer sequence-specific DNA recognition. Mouse ortholog Znf8 (Q8BGV5) shares 572 aa length, indicating strong evolutionary conservation. AlphaFold structure prediction unavailable (API error).
