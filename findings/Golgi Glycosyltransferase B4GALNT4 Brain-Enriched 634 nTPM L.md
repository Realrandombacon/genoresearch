## Description
B4GALNT4 (UniProt Q76KP1), also known as beta-1,4-N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 4 or LacdiNAc synthase, encodes a 1039 aa type II transmembrane glycosyltransferase functioning as the key enzyme synthesizing LacdiNAc (GalNAcβ1-4GlcNAc) glycans on N- and O-linked glycoproteins with critical roles in cancer progression, metabolic regulation, and development. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture characteristic of chondroitin sulfate glycosyltransferases: an N-terminal PA14/GLEYA domain (IPR011658/IPR037524/PS51820/SM00758, residues 108-278) functioning as a lectin module for glycan substrate recognition and binding, a central chondroitin N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase family domain (IPR008428/PF05679, residues 716-1022, GO:0032580) containing the catalytic machinery, a C-terminal GT-A fold glycosyltransferase domain (cd00761/G3DSA:3.90.550.10/SSF53448, residues 807-1037) featuring the canonical DXD motif for UDP-sugar coordination and nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase activity (IPR029044), and assignment to the chondroitin sulfate glycosyltransferase family (IPR037524/IPR051227/PTHR12369, residues 4-1032). AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT 69.8) consistent with a partially structured protein where the C-terminal catalytic GT-A domain forms a stable Rossmannoid fold suitable for UDP-GalNAc binding and glycan transfer while the N-terminal region including the PA14 lectin domain and transmembrane anchor may exhibit flexibility for substrate scanning and Golgi retention. STRING interactions define a tight glycosylation enzyme network: CHST9 (0.925, carbohydrate sulfotransferase 9), FUT8 (0.912, core fucosyltransferase), B4GALT1 (0.910, beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase 1), CHST8 (0.909, carbohydrate sulfotransferase 8), B4GALT2 (0.907), B4GALT3 (0.900), PGGHG (0.707, phosphoglycerate mutase), PILRA (0.595, paired immunoglobulin-like type 2 receptor alpha), B4GALNT3 (0.571, paralogous glycosyltransferase), and NDUFV1 (0.571, mitochondrial complex I subunit suggesting metabolic crosstalk). HPA classifies B4GALNT4 as tissue-enhanced with highest expression in brain (63.4 nTPM) and ovary (23.8 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, localized to nucleoplasm/vesicles/cytosol though predicted Golgi residence based on enzymatic function, reflecting specialized roles in neural glycosylation and reproductive biology. ClinVar lists 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number gains on chromosome 11p15.5 (11p15.5-15.4x10, 11p15.5-14.2, 11p15.5-15.4x7) plus one loss (11p15.5) and intronic variants (c.2715+23G>A, c.444+26G>A) though no single-gene disorder is definitively assigned suggesting contribution to 11p15.5 imprinting disorders (Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome spectrum) or metabolic phenotypes given this imprinted region. Functional hypothesis: B4GALNT4 operates through substrate-specific glycosylation with oncogenic consequences: (1) Catalytic mechanism - GT-A fold binds UDP-GalNAc via DXD motif coordinating Mn2+/Mg2+ cofactors, transfers GalNAc to GlcNAc-terminated acceptors forming LacdiNAc structures on specific glycoprotein substrates; (2) Substrate recognition - PA14 lectin domain scans and binds specific glycan epitopes ensuring correct acceptor selection; (3) PI3K-AKT activation - 2026 studies demonstrate B4GALNT4-mediated glycosylation of PDK1 (3-phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1) stabilizes PDK1 protein enhancing AKT phosphorylation driving prostate cancer progression; (4) Ferroptosis suppression - 2026 research shows B4GALNT4 glycosylates SLC7A11 (cystine/glutamate antiporter) preventing its degradation, maintaining glutathione levels, and blocking lipid peroxidation-induced cell death in lung cancer; (5) Esophageal cancer - 2020 studies link B4GALNT4 overexpression to esophageal squamous cell carcinoma malignant potential via unknown glycoprotein targets; (6) Metabolic programming - 2025 Healthy Start Study reveals B4GALNT4 DNA methylation associates with maternal serum folate/choline concentrations and offspring adiposity suggesting epigenetic regulation of glycosylation affects fetal metabolic programming; (7) Disease mechanism - CNV gains cause B4GALNT4 overexpression enhancing oncogenic glycosylation promoting tumor growth/metastasis; loss may impair neural glycosylation causing neurodevelopmental defects; 11p15.5 imprinting disruption affects B4GALNT4 dosage contributing to Beckwith-Wiedemann/Silver-Russell spectrum phenotypes. Conservation extends to mouse (Q766D5, 1034 aa), duck (A0A493T3P3, 642 aa), and fish (A0A8C6VQZ0, 766 aa) indicating preserved glycosyltransferase function though variable domain architecture. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: complete substrate glycoprotein repertoire beyond PDK1/SLC7A11, precise LacdiNAc sites on target proteins, structural basis of PA14 domain glycan specificity, regulatory mechanisms controlling B4GALNT4 expression/activity (phosphorylation, proteolysis), role in normal brain function given high expression, contribution to ovarian physiology, therapeutic potential of B4GALNT4 inhibitors in cancer (especially prostate/lung), involvement in immune modulation via glycan epitopes, crosstalk with other glycosyltransferases (B4GALT/FUT/CHST enzymes), and genotype-phenotype correlations in 11p15.5 imprinting disorders.
