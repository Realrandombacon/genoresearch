## Description
CAPZB (UniProt P47756), also known as CAPB or CAPPB, encodes a 272 aa protein functioning as the beta subunit of the heterodimeric F-actin capping protein (CapZ) complex that regulates actin filament dynamics by binding to and blocking the fast-growing barbed ends of actin filaments, controlling filament length and preventing subunit addition/dissociation. InterPro analysis reveals conserved actin capping architecture: F-actin-capping protein subunit beta family (IPR001698/PF01115/PTHR10619, residues 1-268/6-240/2-268) conferring barbed-end actin filament capping activity (GO:0051016) within F-actin capping protein complex (GO:0008290); F-actin capping protein beta subunit conserved site (IPR019771/PS00231, residues 62-67) critical for actin binding (GO:0003779) and cytoskeleton organization (GO:0030036); F-actin-capping protein alpha/beta superfamily (IPR037282, residues 2-268) defining the heterodimeric assembly interface; N-terminal domain (IPR043175/G3DSA:1.20.58.570, residues 1-89) forming the dimerization interface with CAPZA; C-terminal domain (IPR042276/G3DSA:3.90.1150.210, residues 90-271/90-271) containing the actin-binding surface; and PRINTS fingerprint motifs (PR00192, residues 5-25/26-47/57-83) defining family-specific signatures. AlphaFold predicts very high confidence structure (pLDDT 91.0) indicating an exceptionally well-folded compact protein with stable tertiary structure suitable for precise actin filament recognition and capping. The perfect DCTN2/DCTN4 scores and strong CAPZA1 linkage confirm CAPZB functions both as canonical actin cap (with CAPZA) and in dynactin-mediated microtubule-actin coordination. HPA classifies CAPZB as essential protein/plasma protein/predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), localized to nucleoplasm/vesicles/cytosol consistent with dynamic cytoskeletal and nuclear roles, molecular functions include actin capping and actin-binding, cell type enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar lists 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number losses (1p36.32-36.12x2, 1p36.21-36.12x1 multiple coordinates, 1p36.13-36.12x1) and copy number gains (1p36.13-36.12x4, 1p36.33-35.1x3); while no single-gene disorder name is consistently assigned, 1p36 deletion syndrome is a recognizable condition where CAPZB haploinsufficiency may contribute to cytoskeletal defects affecting neuronal migration, cardiac development, and muscle function. Functional hypothesis: CAPZB operates through integrated cytoskeletal mechanisms: (1) Heterodimer formation - CAPZB obligately binds CAPZA (alpha-1/2/3 isoforms) forming stable 1:1 heterodimer required for actin capping activity; monomeric CAPZB is non-functional; (2) Barbed-end capping - CapZ complex binds actin filament barbed ends with nanomolar affinity, blocking subunit addition/dissociation, controlling filament length distribution, and preventing catastrophic depolymerization; (3) Dynactin coupling - Ultra-high DCTN interactions suggest CAPZB links actin cytoskeleton to dynein-dynactin motor complex enabling coordinated microtubule-actin transport, vesicle trafficking, and organelle positioning; (4) Twinfilin coordination - TWF1 (twinfilin) interaction indicates coupled regulation: twinfilin sequesters actin monomers while CapZ blocks barbed ends, together controlling actin pool availability and filament nucleation; (5) WASH complex integration - WASHC5 link suggests role in endosomal actin polymerization for vesicle scission and trafficking; (6) Nuclear functions - Nucleoplasmic localization implies roles in nuclear actin dynamics affecting transcription, chromatin remodeling, or DNA repair; (7) Disease mechanism - 1p36 CNVs alter CAPZB dosage: deletions cause haploinsufficiency impairing actin dynamics in developing neurons (migration defects), cardiomyocytes (sarcomere organization), and myocytes (Z-line stability); duplications may cause excessive capping reducing filament turnover; specific phenotypes may include developmental delay, hypotonia, cardiomyopathy, and skeletal muscle weakness; (8) Conservation - Mouse Capzb (P47757, 277 aa), Rat Capzb (Q5XI32, 272 aa), Bovine CAPZB (P79136, 301 aa), Porcine CAPZB (A0PFK7, 272 aa) indicate essential conserved cytoskeletal function across mammals.
