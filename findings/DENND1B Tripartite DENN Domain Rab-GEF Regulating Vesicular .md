# DENND1B: Tripartite DENN Domain Rab-GEF Regulating Vesicular Trafficking in Obesity, Asthma, and Cancer

**Date:** 2026-05-07T11:13:41.736663

**Quality Score:** 7/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH

## Description
DENND1B encodes a 775-amino acid DENN domain-containing protein 1B (UniProt Q6P3S1), a guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that activates Rab GTPases to regulate vesicular trafficking and membrane transport. The protein contains a characteristic tripartite DENN domain architecture (IPR037516, positions 14-395) comprising uDENN (IPR005113, pos 9-89), cDENN (IPR001194, pos 90-291), and dDENN (IPR005112, pos 322-405) subdomains that form the catalytic core for Rab activation. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 66.7, residues 1-775) with partially ordered domains and disordered C-terminal regions, consistent with flexible GEF conformations during Rab binding. STRING interaction analysis reveals DENND1B associates with Rab GEF family member DENND1A (0.792), asthma susceptibility gene ORMDL3 (0.680), clathrin-mediated endocytosis components CLTC (0.669) and RAB35 (0.535), MAP kinase activator MADD (0.677), cell cycle kinases NEK6/NEK7 (0.602-0.650), and 14-3-3 protein YWHAE (0.563), supporting roles in endocytic trafficking and signal transduction. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with nuclear speckle and cytosolic localization, annotated for protein transport and guanine-nucleotide releasing factor activity, with disease involvement in asthma. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 1q25-32 and single nucleotide variants, though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 57 publications, DENND1B is moderately characterized: recent canine GWAS identified DENND1B as obesity gene regulating melanocortin signaling in dogs and humans (2025, 8 cit), circNFIC/miR-30e-3p/DENND1B axis balances inflammation and apoptosis (2021, 15 cit), circRNA DENND1B inhibits tumorigenicity in clear cell renal cell carcinoma (2022), and asthma GWAS consistently implicates DENND1B SNPs in disease risk. The functional hypothesis: DENND1B activates specific Rab GTPases (likely RAB35 or related) to regulate clathrin-mediated endocytosis and vesicular trafficking of signaling receptors (melanocortin receptors, cytokine receptors), with tissue-specific effects explaining obesity (hypothalamic melanocortin signaling), asthma (immune cell cytokine receptor trafficking), and cancer (growth factor receptor endocytosis); disease variants may disrupt GEF catalytic activity or Rab substrate specificity, altering receptor recycling and signal duration.
