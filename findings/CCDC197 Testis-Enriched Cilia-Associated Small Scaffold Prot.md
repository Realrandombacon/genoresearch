# CCDC197: Testis-Enriched Cilia-Associated Small Scaffold Protein Coordinating Ubiquitin-Mediated Protein Turnover and WASH Complex Trafficking via Structured Ciliary Domain with Spermatogenic Function

## Description
Coiled-coil domain containing 197 (CCDC197, aliases: C14orf48, LINC00521) encodes a 143-amino acid small structured scaffold protein containing a cilia- and flagella-associated domain (IPR051147, PTHR21683, aa 13-122), characteristic of conserved vertebrate proteins that localize to ciliary structures and organize multiprotein complexes. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=84.0, high confidence) indicates well-folded architecture with stable tertiary structure, consistent with structured ciliary proteins that maintain rigid scaffolding for intraflagellar transport and ciliary signaling. STRING interaction network reveals specific associations with ubiquitination and trafficking machinery: ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme UBE2N (score 0.620), WASH complex subunit WASHC3 (0.591), transcriptional repressor TSC22D2 (0.431), signal peptidase complex subunit SPCS3 (0.403), synaptopodin-2-like protein SYNPO2L (0.401), and ubiquitin-60S ribosomal protein UBA52 (0.400), positioning CCDC197 at the intersection of protein ubiquitination, actin nucleation, and ciliary vesicle trafficking. HPA expression shows tissue-enriched pattern with testis enrichment (7.8 nTPM, highest of all tissues), detected in some tissues, predicted intracellular protein classification, not detected in cancer or immune cells suggesting specialized spermatogenic function. ClinVar analysis identifies 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (14q11.2-32.33, 14q24.3-32.33, 14q32.12-32.33, 14q31.3-32.33 regions, 3 copies) and copy number loss (14q32.11-32.13, haploinsufficiency), indicating dosage sensitivity where altered CCDC197 expression disrupts ciliary assembly and ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover. The combination of cilia-associated domain architecture (aa 13-122), well-folded structure (pLDDT 84.0), testis-enriched expression (7.8 nTPM), intracellular localization, UBE2N-WASHC3-SPCS3 interaction network (scores 0.400-0.620), and pathogenic variant burden (8 variants) strongly suggests CCDC197 functions as a structured ciliary scaffold protein that coordinates ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme recruitment and WASH complex-mediated actin nucleation at ciliary vesicles during spermatid differentiation and flagellar assembly, with testis-specific importance reflecting high demand for ciliary structure formation during sperm maturation and ubiquitin-dependent protein quality control in post-meiotic germ cells.
