## Description
CCDC187 encodes a 1063 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt A0A096LP49) localized to the centrosome. InterPro analysis identifies IPR028750 domain spanning residues 2-1015, with GO annotations for microtubule binding (GO:0008017), microtubule anchoring (GO:0034453), and centrosome localization (GO:0005813). HPA expression shows testis-specific enrichment (28.3 nTPM) with lower expression in fallopian tube (7.1 nTPM) and pituitary gland (6.7 nTPM), classified as tissue-enhanced and cancer-enhanced. STRING interactions reveal associations with RADIL (0.591), RHOC (0.570), C20orf173 (0.544), CXorf65 (0.507), TEX33 (0.506), TMCO2 (0.505), C12orf50 (0.489), CCDC54 (0.484), FAM209A (0.479), and FAM71F1 (0.447) — several partners are centrosomal or cytoskeletal proteins. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic copy number variants (losses and gains) affecting the 10q25.2 region. BLAST conservation shows 46.2% identity with mouse ortholog (Q8C5V8, 958 aa). Functional hypothesis: CCDC187 serves as a centrosomal scaffold protein that anchors microtubules during cell division, particularly critical in germ cell development given testis-specific expression. The coiled-coil architecture suggests dimerization capability for structural scaffolding. Pathogenic CNVs may disrupt centrosome integrity, potentially contributing to chromosomal instability in cancers and reproductive defects.
