## Description
CENPP (Centromere Protein P, also known as CENP-P) is a 288-amino acid nuclear protein localized to the constitutive centromere characterized by a canonical CENP-P domain architecture (IPR027801/PF13096/PTHR28577, positions 2-287 for centromere protein P family core, 103-279 for CENP-A-nucleosome distal/CAD centromere subunit domain) containing the structured C-terminal region mediating integration into the CCAN complex at the inner kinetochore, classified under chromosome segregation (GO:0007059), mitotic cell cycle (GO:0000278), and CENP-A containing chromatin assembly (GO:0034080). STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network exclusively with other CCAN subunits forming the CENP-O/P/Q/U complex: CENPQ (0.999, centromere protein Q - highest confidence partner indicating obligate heterodimerization), CENPI (0.999), CENPO (0.999), CENPN (0.999), CENPU (0.998), CENPL (0.998), CENPK (0.998), CENPH (0.997), ITGB3BP (0.997, integrin beta-3 binding protein/NME4 linking to metastasis suppression), and CENPM (0.994), indicating CENPP functions as an integral structural component of the 16-subunit CCAN holoenzyme bridging centromeric chromatin to outer kinetochore microtubule attachments. HPA data is unavailable (gene not represented in current HPA database), suggesting potential annotation gaps for this kinetochore component. ClinVar contains 38 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including one nonsense mutation (c.849T>A/p.Cys283Ter truncating the C-terminal domain) and multiple large copy number gains and losses spanning 9p24.3-q34.3, 9p24.3-q34.11, and 9p24.3 regions (deletions and duplications affecting chromosome 9 including the CENPP locus); specific disease associations include autosomal dominant low-frequency sensorineural hearing loss from a Swiss family segregation study, though most variant significance remains marked '?' pending phenotypic correlation. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q6IPU0-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 85.0) consistent with a well-structured centromere protein containing ordered alpha-helical domains typical of CCAN components that form stable coiled-coil interactions. With only 17 papers, CENPP represents a genuine 'dark' gene with emerging mechanistic understanding (2012-2022): first identified in Chinese Banna Mini-pig Inbred Line establishing evolutionary conservation; CDK4 regulates Aurora B and CENPP expression in mouse keratinocytes linking cell cycle control to kinetochore assembly; nomogram based on CENPP expression predicts survival in breast cancer identifying it as an oncogenic biomarker; and a novel nonsense variant segregates with autosomal dominant low-frequency sensorineural hearing loss revealing unexpected extra-mitotic functions. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that CENPP functions as a CCAN scaffold that: (1) forms an obligate heterodimer with CENPQ (0.999 score) creating the CENP-O/P subcomplex essential for kinetochore-microtubule attachment stability; (2) integrates into the CENP-H/I/K/M/N/O/P/Q/U module positioning centromeric nucleosomes relative to outer kinetochore NDC80 complexes; (3) coordinates with CENPI (0.999 score) and CENPO (0.999 score) ensuring proper chromosome alignment during metaphase; (4) exhibits cell cycle-regulated expression through CDK4-Aurora B signaling explaining cancer prognostic value in breast tumors; (5) may possess non-canonical roles in auditory hair cell function given hearing loss association, potentially through ciliary or stereocilia maintenance mechanisms analogous to other centromere proteins with extra-mitotic functions; chromosome 9p CNVs may contribute to developmental phenotypes through dosage effects on kinetochore integrity affecting genomic stability.
