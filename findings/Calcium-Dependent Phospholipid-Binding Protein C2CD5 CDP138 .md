## Description
C2CD5 (UniProt Q86YS7), also known as CDP138 or KIAA0528, encodes a 1000 aa intracellular protein functioning as a calcium-dependent phospholipid-binding regulator of insulin receptor signaling, intracellular protein trafficking, and mitochondrial dynamics. InterPro analysis reveals a complex multi-domain architecture: an N-terminal C2 domain (IPR000008/PF00168/cd08688/G3DSA:2.60.40.150, residues 1-129) mediating calcium-dependent membrane binding (GO:0005544), five YbjQ-like domains distributed throughout the protein (IPR056431/PF23025 at residues 138-246, 345-438, 488-586; IPR056430/PF23028 at residues 701-794) of unknown molecular function but potentially involved in protein-protein interactions or lipid binding, a C-terminal domain (IPR057815/PF23128, residues 877-970) unique to C2CD5, and assignment to the C2CD5 family (IPR038983, residues 1-995). AlphaFold predicts a high confidence structure (pLDDT 74.1) consistent with a well-folded multi-domain protein where the C2 domain forms a canonical beta-sandwich calcium-binding fold while YbjQ-like domains create structured modules connected by flexible linkers enabling conformational changes upon calcium binding. STRING interactions define a network centered on vesicle trafficking, cytoskeletal dynamics, and insulin signaling: CDK5 (0.919, cyclin-dependent kinase 5 regulating neuronal function), FIBP (0.875, FGF-1 binding protein), AKT2 (0.780, key insulin signaling kinase), SLC2A4 (0.629, GLUT4 glucose transporter), STX4 (0.599, syntaxin-4 SNARE protein), STXBP3 (0.590, syntaxin-binding protein 3), MYO5A (0.588, myosin-Va motor protein), RALA (0.587, Ras-related GTPase), SNAP23 (0.584, SNARE complex component), and RAB10 (0.577, vesicle trafficking GTPase). HPA classifies C2CD5 as low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, cancer-enhanced expression pattern, localized to centriolar satellites and cytosol, involved in protein transport, reflecting ubiquitous housekeeping functions with elevated roles in malignancy. ClinVar lists 42 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large copy number gains on chromosome 12p (12p13.33-11.1 chr12:64621-34650483x3, 12p13.33-q13.12 chr12:82453-49847230x3, 12p13.33-11.1 chr12:173787-34835837x3/x4, 12p13.2-11.1 chr12:10155706-34051988x3) plus one intronic variant (c.2877+120T>G) though no single-gene disorder is definitively assigned suggesting contribution to 12p duplication syndromes (potentially Pallister-Killian syndrome spectrum) or cancer predisposition. Functional hypothesis: C2CD5 operates through calcium-regulated membrane recruitment and protein complex assembly: (1) Calcium sensing - C2 domain binds Ca2+ triggering translocation from cytosol to phospholipid membranes (plasma membrane, vesicle membranes); (2) Insulin signaling - Recruited to insulin-responsive compartments where it facilitates GLUT4 (SLC2A4) translocation via interactions with STX4/SNAP23 SNARE machinery and AKT2 phosphorylation cascade; (3) Vesicle trafficking - MYO5A and RAB10 interactions enable motor-driven transport of cargo vesicles along actin filaments; (4) Mitochondrial dynamics - Recent 2021 literature demonstrates C2CD5 loss alters hypothalamic mitochondrial trafficking, structure, and function affecting energy homeostasis; (5) Cancer metabolism - 2025 studies show USP5 deubiquitinase stabilizes C2CD5 driving PI3K/AKT/mTOR/HIF-1α-mediated glycolysis in acute myeloid leukemia; (6) Thermoregulation - 2026 research reveals C2CD5 in noradrenergic neurons regulates thermogenesis and lipid homeostasis via norepinephrine secretion; (7) Disease mechanism - Copy number gains cause C2CD5 overexpression enhancing glycolytic flux in cancer cells and potentially disrupting calcium homeostasis; haploinsufficiency may impair insulin-stimulated glucose uptake contributing to metabolic syndrome; hypothalamic dysfunction links to obesity and feeding behavior (2019 food intake study). Conservation extends to mouse (Q7TPS5, 1016 aa), orangutan (Q5RDC8, 1000 aa), and Xenopus (Q28BX9, 1014 aa) indicating preserved role in vertebrate metabolism. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: precise molecular function of YbjQ-like domains (novel fold?), structural basis of C2 domain calcium coordination and membrane insertion geometry, specific phospholipid species bound (PS, PI, PI(4,5)P2?), role in neuronal synaptic vesicle release beyond hypothalamic function, contribution to insulin resistance in type 2 diabetes, therapeutic potential of targeting USP5-C2CD5 axis in AML, involvement in centriolar satellite function and ciliopathy phenotypes, crosstalk between CDK5 phosphorylation and calcium binding, tissue-specific interactome differences (adipocyte vs neuron vs muscle), and genotype-phenotype correlations explaining clinical variability in 12p duplication syndromes.
