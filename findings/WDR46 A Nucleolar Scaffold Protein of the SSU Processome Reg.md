# WDR46: A Nucleolar Scaffold Protein of the SSU Processome Regulating Ribosome Biogenesis, HBV-Mediated HCC Progression, and Aspirin-Exacerbated Respiratory Disease

**Date:** 2026-04-16T11:21:00.987854

**Quality Score:** 3.5/10  (E=5.9, D=1.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
WDR46 (UniProt O15213) encodes a 610 amino acid WD repeat-containing nucleolar scaffold protein that organizes the granular compartment of the nucleolus and functions as a core component of the small subunit (SSU) processome for 18S rRNA processing and ribosome biogenesis. InterPro analysis confirms canonical WD40 repeat architecture (IPR001680, PF00400, SM00320) with three repeats at aa 226-263, 268-303, and 348-389 forming a beta-propeller structure, plus a BING4 C-terminal domain (IPR012952, PF08149, aa 440-518) characteristic of the WDR46/Utp7 family (IPR040315, PTHR14085). AlphaFold structure (AF-O15213-F1) predicts a highly stable, well-folded protein (global pLDDT 81.2), consistent with scaffold function in ribonucleoprotein complex assembly. HPA expression reveals nucleolar localization with low tissue specificity but detection in all tissues, classified as an essential protein with cell type-enhanced expression. Literature (11 papers) confirms WDR46 determines granular compartment localization of nucleolin (NCL) and DDX21, is stabilized by HBV core protein to upregulate NUSAP1 and promote hepatocellular carcinoma progression, and represents a genetic risk factor for aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (AERD) in Korean populations. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number gains on chr6p25.3-q27), indicating clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to ribosomopathy or immune dysregulation disorders. STRING interactions identify exceptionally high-confidence partners including UTP6 (0.999), UTP15 (0.999), BYSL (0.998), RRP9 (0.998), UTP20 (0.998), UTP18 (0.997), KRR1 (0.997), MPHOSPH10 (0.996), UTP3 (0.995), and NAT10 (0.995), placing WDR46 within the SSU processome complex for ribosome assembly. Functional hypothesis: WDR46 functions as a structural scaffold that organizes SSU processome components in the nucleolar granular compartment to facilitate 18S rRNA maturation; its stabilization by HBV core protein drives NUSAP1-mediated cell proliferation in HCC, while copy number variations may disrupt ribosome biogenesis contributing to AERD susceptibility through altered immune cell function.
