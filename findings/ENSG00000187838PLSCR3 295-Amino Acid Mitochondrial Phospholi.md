## Description
PLSCR3 (UniProt: Q9NRY6, aliases Phospholipid scramblase 3) encodes a 295 amino acid mitochondrial membrane protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with 34 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 17p13.2-13.1, PLSCR3 is a member of the phospholipid scramblase family catalyzing bidirectional translocation of phospholipids between membrane leaflets, specifically mediating cardiolipin externalization from the inner mitochondrial membrane to the outer mitochondrial membrane during mitophagy and apoptosis.
