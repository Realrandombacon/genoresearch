## Description
LOC126861488 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 12p11.21 (genomic coordinates 28023038-28024237, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a BRD4-INDEPENDENT GROUP 4 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors but shows LIMITED OR NO DEPENDENCE on the BRD4 bromodomain protein. This cofactor profile distinguishes it from canonical BET-dependent enhancers and suggests it operates through BRD2-dependent chromatin remodeling mechanisms that bypass BRD4 requirement. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants affecting this locus through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements. STRIKINGLY, this locus shows BIDIRECTIONAL pathogenicity with EXTREME AMPLIFICATION: 9 copy number GAINS including multiple TETRASOMY events (chr12:64621-34650483x3, chr12:82453-49847230x3, chr12:64620-34682902x4, chr12:54427-34608071x4, chr12:121271-133196807x3, chr12:121271-34603261x3, chr12:121255-34603274x3, chr12:212976-33926913x4, chr12:77187-34380176x3) AND 1 copy number LOSS (chr12:25263833-34064528x1). The presence of three tetrasomy (4 copies) events indicates extreme oncogenic amplification pressure. The 12p region contains KRAS (12p12.1), one of the most frequently mutated oncogenes in human cancer (pancreatic, colorectal, lung), as well as CCND2 (cyclin D2) and other cell cycle regulators. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with strong oncogenic relevance — the BRD2/P300/CBP/MED14/CDK7-dependent but BRD4-independent enhancer activity in HCT116 cells combined with bidirectional pathogenicity including tetrasomy events suggests LOC126861488 functions as a dosage-critical enhancer controlling 12p oncogene expression (potentially KRAS or CCND2), where amplification drives oncogenic overexpression and the BRD4-independence suggests this enhancer may remain active even when BET inhibitors target BRD4.
