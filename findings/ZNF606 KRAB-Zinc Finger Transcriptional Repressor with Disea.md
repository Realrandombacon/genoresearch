## Description
ZNF606 encodes a 792 amino acid Kruppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q8WXB4) that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 61-102) which recruits the corepressor TRIM28/KAP1 complex to silence target genes through heterochromatin formation, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 289-506 that mediate sequence-specific DNA recognition. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 59.8) indicates moderate confidence with structured zinc finger regions and potentially disordered linker regions, consistent with KRAB-ZNF family architecture. STRING interaction network identifies TRIM28 (score 0.565) as the primary binding partner, confirming the canonical KRAB-corepressor recruitment mechanism, along with interactions with C3orf20 (0.487), C5orf47 (0.479), OR10S1 (0.447), KIAA1958 (0.429), and RTL8A (0.417). HPA expression profiling shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity and nucleoplasmic localization, consistent with genome-wide regulatory function. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19q13.43 copy number gains affecting ZNF606 dosage, suggesting haploinsufficiency or overexpression phenotypes though no specific Mendelian disease is yet defined. The gene is located at 19q13.43 and aliases include ZNF328. Conservation analysis indicates the KRAB-ZNF architecture is preserved across vertebrates with orthologs in Pan paniscus (407 aa), Macaca fascicularis (792 aa), and Castor canadensis (733 aa).
