## Description
OR52M1 (Olfactory receptor 52M1, UniProt Q8NGK5) is a 317-amino acid G protein-coupled receptor located on chromosome 11p15.5 within the large olfactory receptor gene cluster. InterPro analysis confirms this as a canonical class A rhodopsin-like GPCR containing the olfactory receptor family domain (IPR000725/PF13853, aa 33-308), seven transmembrane helices characteristic of rhodopsin-like receptors (IPR017452, aa 43-295; G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, aa 22-314), and membership in the olfactory receptor subfamily 52M (cd15949, aa 11-303; IPR050402, aa 9-308). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 88.4), indicating a well-folded stable 7-transmembrane bundle architecture typical of functional GPCRs ready for ligand binding and signal transduction. Human Protein Atlas shows no detection in standard tissue panels, which is expected for olfactory receptors expressed exclusively in olfactory sensory neurons of the nasal epithelium not profiled in HPA. This interactome confirms OR52M1 functions through the canonical cAMP-mediated olfactory transduction pathway. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic copy-number gains across 11p15.5-p14.2, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where duplication of the olfactory receptor cluster disrupts normal odorant perception or causes genomic instability. Conservation across mammals (marmoset F7HA83, skua A0A7K9FGC4, penguin A0A8J4LKF6, gull A0A7K5NN57) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to chemosensory function. OR52M1 likely functions as a volatile odorant receptor detecting specific molecular features, activating GNAL to stimulate adenylate cyclase III, increasing cAMP, opening cyclic nucleotide-gated channels, and depolarizing olfactory sensory neurons.
