# TCTN1: Tectonic-1 Ciliary Transition Zone Gatekeeper Regulating Hedgehog Signaling and Ciliopathy Pathogenesis

## Description
TCTN1 (Q2MV58) encodes a 587 amino acid tectonic family protein that functions as a critical component of the ciliary transition zone (TZ), acting as a molecular gatekeeper that controls protein trafficking into and out of the primary cilium. InterPro analysis reveals the canonical Tectonic-1-3 domain architecture (IPR011677, positions 204-368 and 378-560) with an N-terminal DUF1619 domain (PF25752, positions 70-175) characteristic of ciliary membrane proteins. AlphaFold predicts a moderately well-folded structure (pLDDT 77.6) consistent with stable membrane-associated complexes. STRING interactions identify exceptionally high-confidence partners within the ciliopathy network: CC2D2A (0.998), TCTN3 (0.996), TMEM67 (0.994), B9D1 (0.987), TMEM216 (0.987), CEP290 (0.984), TCTN2 (0.979), MKS1 (0.979), B9D2 (0.970), and TMEM231 (0.880), indicating TCTN1 functions within the MKS (Meckel syndrome) and JBTS (Joubert syndrome) protein complexes at the ciliary pore. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in choroid plexus (31.9 nTPM), localized to nucleoplasm, actin filaments, and primary cilium, consistent with roles in cilium biogenesis and developmental signaling. ClinVar contains 92 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (p.Gly378fs, p.Leu388fs, p.Leu502fs) and nonsense mutations (p.Gln86Ter, p.Gln137Ter, p.Glu172Ter) causing autosomal-recessive ciliopathies including Joubert syndrome, Meckel-Gruber syndrome, and oral-facial-digital syndrome. Literature (49 papers) demonstrates TCTN1 regulates Hedgehog signaling pathway activity through ciliary gatekeeping, with truncated variants disrupting ciliogenesis and causing developmental defects. The mechanistic hypothesis: TCTN1 forms a heteromeric complex with TCTN2/TCTN3 and MKS/B9D proteins at the ciliary transition zone, creating a diffusion barrier that selectively permits entry of signaling components (e.g., Smoothened, Gli transcription factors) while excluding others; loss-of-function variants compromise this barrier, leading to aberrant Hedgehog signaling, defective ciliogenesis, and the pleiotropic developmental phenotypes characteristic of ciliopathies affecting brain, kidney, and skeletal development.
