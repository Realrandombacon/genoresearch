## Description
BIN2 (Bridging Integrator 2, UniProt Q9UBW5) is a 565 amino acid plasma membrane-associated protein with moderate characterization that functions as a BAR domain-containing membrane curvature sensor regulating B-cell receptor signaling and cytoskeletal dynamics.
