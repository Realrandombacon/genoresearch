## Description
ADAM29 encodes an 820 aa disintegrin and metalloproteinase domain-containing protein 29 (UniProt Q9UKF5), functioning as a membrane-anchored metalloendopeptidase that mediates proteolytic cleavage of cell surface proteins and regulates cell-cell/cell-matrix interactions through its multi-domain architecture. The protein contains an N-terminal propeptide domain (IPR002870, aa34-107) that maintains the enzyme in latent form until proteolytic activation, a catalytic reprolysin/metalloprotease M12B domain (IPR001590, IPR034027, cd04269, G3DSA:3.40.390.10, aa198-386/194-388) with zinc-binding motif for metalloendopeptidase activity (GO:0004222) and proteolysis (GO:0006508), a disintegrin domain (IPR001762, IPR036436, G3DSA:4.10.70.10, PF00200, aa397-483/406-478) for integrin binding and cell adhesion regulation, and a C-terminal cysteine-rich domain (IPR006586, aa482-618) for protein-protein interactions and substrate specificity, belonging to the ADAM/reprolysin peptidase M12B family (IPR024079). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 77.1), indicating a well-folded multi-domain architecture with structured catalytic and adhesion domains consistent with functional metalloprotease activity. The disintegrin domain (aa397-483) binds integrins (potentially αVβ3, α5β1) to regulate cell adhesion, migration, and signaling independent of proteolytic activity.
