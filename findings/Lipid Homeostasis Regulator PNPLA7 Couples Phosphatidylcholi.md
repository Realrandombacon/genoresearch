## Description
PNPLA7 (Patatin-like Phospholipase Domain Containing 7, also known as NTE-related protein or iPLA2γ-like) is a massive 1317 aa membrane-associated hydrolase functioning as a critical regulator of phosphatidylcholine (PC) turnover, choline supply for the methionine cycle, and mitochondrial quality control (mitophagy). Despite 30 literature references, its precise substrate specificity and integration into metabolic signaling networks remain partially obscure, classifying it as a 'partially characterized' dark gene. InterPro analysis reveals a unique modular architecture: three N-terminal cyclic nucleotide-binding (CNB) domains (IPR000595, aa 145-272, 458-578, 581-696) featuring a 'Jelly Roll' fold (IPR014710), which likely serve as regulatory or scaffolding modules rather than cAMP sensors given the lack of canonical binding residues; and a C-terminal catalytic patatin-like phospholipase domain (IPR002641, aa 928-1094) containing the characteristic GXSXG lipase motif and catalytic dyad (Ser-Asp) essential for hydrolyzing PC and lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC). AlphaFold predicts a confident global structure (pLDDT 71.2), indicating a stable multi-domain enzyme where the CNB domains may form a rigid platform positioning the catalytic domain near membrane interfaces. HPA shows broad expression with enhancement in immune cells and certain cancers, localizing to the cytosol and centriolar satellites, suggesting roles in cell division and lipid droplet dynamics. STRING interactions place PNPLA7 in a tight network of lipid metabolic enzymes: GPCPD1 (0.926, glycerophosphocholine phosphodiesterase), PNPLA6 (0.923, neuropathy target esterase), PLAAT3 (0.917), PLB1 (0.915), and lysophosphatidylcholine acyltransferases LPCAT3/4 (0.907/0.908), confirming its central role in the Lands' cycle and PC remodeling. ClinVar lists 132 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses/gains on chromosome 9q34.3, implicating PNPLA7 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders, though specific monogenic phenotypes are not yet fully defined. Recent literature (2023) explicitly links PNPLA7 (alongside PNPLA8) to hepatic PC catabolism that supplies endogenous choline for S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) production, while 2025 studies identify PNPLA7 as a mediator of Parkin recruitment to mitochondria in adipose tissue, inhibiting beige fat browning by regulating mitophagy.
