## Description
ITPRIPL1 (Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor-interacting protein-like 1, UniProt Q6GPH6) is a 555-amino acid membrane-associated protein located on chromosome 2q11.1 within a region prone to extensive copy-number variations. The protein belongs to the ITPRIP/MAB-21 nucleotidyltransferase family (PTHR10656, aa 149-545) and contains ITPRIP-specific signature motifs (IPR026250/PR02107, aa 313-327/394-407/413-429) that define this subfamily. GO annotations are limited but protein class indicates predicted membrane localization. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest expression in testis (26.1 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, immune cell-enhanced pattern, and group-enriched single cell type specificity — suggesting roles in both spermatogenesis and immune function. This interactome suggests ITPRIPL1 functions within metabolic, scaffolding, and immune regulatory networks. ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic variants consisting predominantly of large copy-number gains and losses across 2q11.1-11.2, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 2 cause disease phenotypes. Conservation across mammals (mouse A2ASA8, 547 aa; rat Q66H52, 547 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental calcium signaling or nucleotide metabolism mechanisms. ITPRIPL1 likely functions as an inositol trisphosphate receptor-interacting scaffold protein, using its MAB-21 domain to modulate IP3R-mediated calcium release from endoplasmic reticulum stores — critical for sperm capacitation, acrosome reaction, and immune cell activation, with dysfunction potentially contributing to male infertility and immune disorders where chromosome 2q rearrangements disrupt ITPRIPL1 dosage.
