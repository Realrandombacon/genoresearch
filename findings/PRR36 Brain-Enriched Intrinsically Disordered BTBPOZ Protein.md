## Description

The BTB/POZ domain mediates protein-protein interactions and homodimerization. GO annotations are limited, reflecting the protein's uncharacterized status. EVI5L partnership (0.507) links PRR36 to cell cycle regulation, suggesting roles in neurogenesis. The disordered architecture (pLDDT 42.2) enables binding multiple partners simultaneously. Nuclear speckle localization suggests PRR36 regulates alternative splicing of neuronal genes. CNV-mediated overexpression causes neurodevelopmental defects through aberrant protocadherin clustering. PRR36 represents a critical neuronal scaffold linking protocadherin-mediated adhesion, nuclear organization, and brain development.
