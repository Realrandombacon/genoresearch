## Description
PCOLCE (Procollagen C-Endopeptidase Enhancer 1) is a 449 amino acid secreted glycoprotein that functions as a critical extracellular matrix regulator by binding to the C-propeptide region of procollagen substrates and accelerating their cleavage by BMP1/Tolloid-like metalloproteinases, serving as an essential cofactor for collagen fibrillogenesis and tissue integrity. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic modular architecture including two tandem CUB domains (IPR000859, PF00431, PS01180, residues 37-149/159-273) mediating procollagen substrate recognition, NTR/Netrin domain (IPR001134, IPR018933, IPR035814, PF01759, PS50189, residues 318-437) for protein-protein interactions, Netrin module non-TIMP type (IPR018933, residues 329-435), TIMP-like OB-fold superfamily homology (IPR008993, G3DSA:2.40.50.120, residues 312-442), and spermadhesin CUB domain superfamily (IPR035914, G3DSA:2.60.120.290, residues 35-147/154-279). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q15113-F1) predicts confident folding (pLDDT 79.6) across the 449 residue sequence, indicating a stable multi-domain secreted protein suitable for extracellular substrate binding. HPA data classifies PCOLCE as a plasma protein and predicted secreted protein involved in extracellular matrix organization, with Golgi apparatus and vesicle localization reflecting secretory pathway trafficking, low tissue specificity (detected in many tissues), cell type-enriched expression, and implications in connective tissue disorders. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling to the collagen biosynthesis machinery: exceptionally strong associations with fibrillar collagens COL3A1 (0.976), COL1A2 (0.963), COL5A1 (0.928), COL5A2 (0.857), COL1A1 (0.813), COL11A2 (0.800); collagen-processing proteases BMP1 (Bone morphogenetic protein 1, 0.888) and TLL2 (Tolloid-like 2, 0.917) which PCOLCE enhances; collagen-associated proteoglycan BGN (Biglycan, 0.829); and COL6A2 (0.833) for basement membrane organization. ClinVar lists 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal deletions on 7q22.1 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 98-102 Mb), splice site mutations (c.1012+1G>T), and complex rearrangements, though no specific monogenic syndrome is fully defined, suggesting potential roles in connective tissue fragility or fibrosis susceptibility. Literature (102 papers) establishes PCOLCE as a rate-limiting enhancer of procollagen processing: LNP-targeted Pcolce mRNA delivery synergizes with calcium supplementation to attenuate osteoporosis through enhanced bone matrix deposition; SOX9 transcriptionally upregulates PCOLCE promoting Collagen VI secretion in neurofibroma pathogenesis; high PCOLCE expression indicates poor prognosis in glioma and pan-cancer analyses reveal oncogenic roles across multiple tumor types; TWIST1-mediated upregulation drives osteosarcoma metastasis; and PCOLCE functions as a biomarker for fibrotic diseases including liver fibrosis and pulmonary fibrosis where excessive collagen deposition occurs. As a well-studied ECM regulator with emerging roles in cancer progression and fibrosis, PCOLCE represents a critical node connecting procollagen maturation to tissue mechanics, wound healing, and pathological matrix remodeling through acceleration of BMP1/TLL2-mediated C-propeptide cleavage.
