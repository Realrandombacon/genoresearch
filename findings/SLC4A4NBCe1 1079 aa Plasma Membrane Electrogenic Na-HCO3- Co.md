## Description
SLC4A4 (Q9Y6R1, 1079 aa), also known as NBCe1 (Electrogenic Sodium Bicarbonate Cotransporter 1), is a critical membrane transporter belonging to the SLC4 bicarbonate transporter family (PTHR11827). InterPro identifies a complex multi-domain architecture: a large N-terminal cytoplasmic domain containing Band 3 cytoplasmic domain features (IPR013769, aa 107-387) for protein interactions, followed by the core bicarbonate transporter integral membrane domain (IPR003020, IPR011531, PF00955, aa 441-1006) forming 10-14 transmembrane helices, with specific sodium bicarbonate cotransporter signatures (IPR003024, aa 138-144, 328-336, 369-377). The structure belongs to the phosphotransferase/anion transporter superfamily (IPR016152, G3DSA:3.40.930.10, aa 102-412). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 70.6, residues 1-1079), consistent with a stable transmembrane protein featuring extensive cytoplasmic regulatory domains. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in pancreas (245.3 nTPM) and kidney (111.6 nTPM); localized to plasma membrane and cytosol; classified as a metabolic protein, disease-related gene, and potential drug target involved in ion/sodium transport and symport; associated with retinitis pigmentosa. STRING reveals 10 interactions forming an acid-base regulatory network: AHCYL1 (0.749, adenosylhomocysteinase-like 1), SLC26A6 (0.741, chloride/bicarbonate exchanger PAT1), SLC26A3 (0.616, chloride/bicarbonate exchanger DRA), SLC12A2 (0.579, Na-K-2Cl cotransporter NKCC1), CA2 (0.503, carbonic anhydrase 2), SLC9A3 (0.475, Na+/H+ exchanger NHE3), CFTR (0.456, cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator), SLC9A1 (0.437, Na+/H+ exchanger NHE1), CA4 (0.430, carbonic anhydrase 4), and SLC9A2 (0.420, Na+/H+ exchanger NHE2). The strong links to SLC26A6/A3 and carbonic anhydrases confirm its role in coordinated bicarbonate handling. ClinVar contains 44 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.551-2A>G, c.1903+2T>A), frameshifts (c.1677_1681del/p.Trp560fs, c.853del/p.Ser285fs), nonsense mutations (c.277C>T/p.Arg93Ter), missense variants (c.2773C>T/p.Arg925Cys, c.1015G>A/p.Glu339Lys), and large copy number losses (chr4:67406178-76619632), causing proximal renal tubular acidosis (pRTA) with ocular abnormalities (band keratopathy, cataracts, glaucoma) and dental enamel defects. Functional hypothesis: SLC4A4/NBCe1 functions as an electrogenic sodium-bicarbonate cotransporter (typically 1 Na+: 2-3 HCO3- stoichiometry) responsible for basolateral bicarbonate extrusion in renal proximal tubule cells and pancreatic duct cells, enabling systemic pH regulation and bicarbonate reabsorption/secretion. In the kidney, it works in concert with apical NHE3 (SLC9A3) and luminal carbonic anhydrases (CA4) to reclaim filtered bicarbonate, while in the pancreas it drives alkaline fluid secretion. The large cytoplasmic N-terminal domain serves as a regulatory hub, binding carbonic anhydrase II (CA2, score 0.503) to form a 'bicarbonate transport metabolon' that channels substrate directly from CO2 hydration to the transporter, and interacting with SLC26A6/A3 for coordinated Cl-/HCO3- exchange. Disruption via mutations causes proximal RTA (type 2) characterized by urinary bicarbonate wasting, metabolic acidosis, growth retardation, and ocular/dental defects due to impaired bicarbonate-dependent mineralization. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 1079 aa, bovine 1079 aa) confirms essential physiological role. NOTE: While mechanistically well-defined, this gene's regulatory complexity and tissue-specific isoforms (NBCe1-A in kidney, NBCe1-B in pancreas) represent areas of ongoing research.
