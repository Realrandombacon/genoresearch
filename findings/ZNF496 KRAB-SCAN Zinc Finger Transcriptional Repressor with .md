## Description
ZNF496 encodes a 587 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 1q44. The protein contains three key structural elements: (1) an N-terminal SCAN oligomerization domain (IPR003309, residues 38-149) enabling protein-protein interactions and heterodimerization with other ZNF family members; (2) a central KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909/PF00096, residues 221-291) that recruits transcriptional corepressors; and (3) three C-terminal C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 406-575) recognizing specific GC-rich DNA sequences. AlphaFold prediction (Q96IT1) shows medium confidence (pLDDT 54.3), consistent with intrinsically disordered regions common in transcriptional regulators. STRING analysis reveals high-confidence interactions with NSD1 (histone H3-K36 methyltransferase, score 0.781), ARR3 (arrestin, 0.709), YY1AP1 (0.647), and multiple paralogous ZNF proteins (ZNF483, ZSCAN22, ZKSCAN4, TRIM28), suggesting ZNF496 functions within a chromatin-modifying complex. ClinVar contains 73 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains at 1q43-44, indicating dosage sensitivity and disease relevance. The mouse ortholog (Q5SXI5, 585 aa) shows strong conservation. Functionally, ZNF496 likely acts as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 and histone modifiers to silence target genes, potentially regulating neurodevelopmental or cell cycle pathways given its 1q44 location and interaction profile.
