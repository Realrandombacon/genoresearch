# SPATA21: Testis-Enriched EF-Hand Calcium-Binding Protein with Spermatogenesis Function

**Date:** 2026-03-20T09:33:48.678301

**Quality Score:** 2.98/10  (E=4.65, D=1.3) [WEAK]

## Description
SPATA21 encodes a 469 amino acid spermatogenesis-associated protein (UniProt Q7Z572) containing a canonical EF-hand calcium-binding domain (IPR002048, PF00036, residues 226-290) with the conserved calcium-binding loop motif (residues 268-280). The protein belongs to the spermatogenesis-associated protein 21 family (IPR043520) spanning residues 50-469. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 59.2) indicates low-medium confidence with the EF-hand domain likely structured while flanking regions may be intrinsically disordered, consistent with calcium-regulated conformational switching. HPA data reveals testis-enriched expression (7.3 nTPM) with vesicular and cytosolic localization, strongly implicating function in spermatogenesis. STRING interactions show moderate associations with spermatogenesis factors SPATA3 (0.627) and SPATA24 (0.503), plus calcium/calmodulin-related protein CASK (0.439), suggesting participation in calcium-dependent spermatogenic complexes. ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1p36, indicating dosage sensitivity where altered SPATA21 copy number disrupts spermatogenesis. The EF-hand domain enables calcium ion binding (GO:0005509), proposing a mechanistic role as a calcium sensor that undergoes conformational change upon calcium binding to regulate downstream spermatogenic processes such as acrosome reaction, sperm motility, or meiotic progression. Conservation across primates (Pan troglodytes, Pan paniscus homologs) suggests functional constraint on reproductive function.
