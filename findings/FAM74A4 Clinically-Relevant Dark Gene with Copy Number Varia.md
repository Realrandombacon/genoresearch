# FAM74A4: Clinically-Relevant Dark Gene with Copy Number Variant Pathogenicity but No Protein Evidence

## Description
FAM74A4 (Family with sequence similarity 74 member A4, Gene ID: 401508) is an uncharacterized locus on chromosome 9q12 (genomic coordinates NC_000009.12: 61205259-61212372, ~7.1 kb region) that presents a striking disconnect between clinical significance and molecular characterization. Despite having 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar, comprehensive proteomic analysis reveals essentially no evidence for a functional protein product. UniProt search returned zero human entries (only distant fish homologs from Dissostichus eleginoides), STRING-DB found no protein entry for human species 9606, and Human Protein Atlas has no expression data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000293075. Zero PubMed publications confirm no experimental characterization exists. All 20 ClinVar variants are large-scale copy number gains affecting broad chromosomal regions (9p24.3-q34.3 spanning up to 138 Mb), suggesting FAM74A4 resides in a dosage-sensitive chromosomal segment rather than being directly causative. The pathogenic CNVs include: 9p24.3-q21.13 (72 Mb gain), 9p24.3-q34.3 (138 Mb gain), 9p24.3-q21.31 (80 Mb gain), and multiple other large duplications. Without a human UniProt accession, domain architecture analysis (InterPro) and structure prediction (AlphaFold) are impossible. Functional hypotheses: (1) FAM74A4 may encode a non-coding RNA misannotated as protein-coding, where CNVs affect regulatory function; (2) it may produce a small, unstable peptide below standard proteomic detection; (3) the gene may be expressed only under highly specific developmental or stress conditions not captured by standard assays; (4) pathogenic CNVs may primarily affect neighboring genes with FAM74A4 as a bystander marker. The complete absence of protein-level evidence across all major databases despite clinical variant reports makes this a priority candidate for transcript-level validation.
