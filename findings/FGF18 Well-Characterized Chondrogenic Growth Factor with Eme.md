## Description
FGF18 (UniProt O76093) encodes a 207-amino acid secreted fibroblast growth factor with a canonical beta-trefoil fold (cd23324, aa 53-178; PF00167, aa 53-175) within the FGF family (IPR002209, aa 17-187) and cytokine IL1/FGF superfamily (IPR008996, aa 39-178). AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT: 87.6) consistent with stable growth factors. STRING interactions reveal exclusive high-confidence partnerships with all four FGF receptors: FGFR2 (0.999), FGFR1 (0.999), FGFR3 (0.998), FGFR4 (0.996), plus co-receptor Klotho (KL: 0.992), heparan sulfate proteoglycan HSPG2 (0.970), and binding protein FGFBP1 (0.946). HPA shows heart muscle-enriched expression (15.1 nTPM) with secretion annotation and RAS pathway association. ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 5q35.1 and missense mutations (p.Arg71Cys). With 563 papers, FGF18 is NOT a dark gene—it is the most clinically advanced FGF for cartilage regeneration. Sprifermin (recombinant FGF18) completed Phase II trials showing dose-dependent cartilage thickness increases in knee osteoarthritis. Recent 2024-2025 breakthroughs include mRNA-LNP delivery systems protecting cartilage via senescence alleviation (28 citations), injectable microgels with chondrocyte-targeted gene editing (141 citations), and enhanced midbrain dopamine neuron derivation for Parkinson's disease models (6 citations). FGF18 signals primarily through FGFR3 to promote chondrocyte proliferation while inhibiting hypertrophy, making it uniquely therapeutic among FGFs. This represents a 'light' gene—fully characterized with active clinical translation.
