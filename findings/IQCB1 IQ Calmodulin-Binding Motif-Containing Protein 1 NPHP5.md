# IQCB1: IQ Calmodulin-Binding Motif-Containing Protein 1 (NPHP5) Orchestrating Ciliary Transport, Photoreceptor Maintenance, and Renal Tubule Integrity via CEP290 Complex Assembly and Calcium-Calmodulin Signaling in Senior-Løken Syndrome

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
IQCB1 (IQ Motif Containing B1), also known as NPHP5, is a 598 amino acid ciliary protein featuring dual IQ calmodulin-binding motifs that serves as a critical component of the CEP290-centered ciliary transition zone complex, mediating calcium-calmodulin signaling for ciliary transport, photoreceptor outer segment maintenance, and renal tubule integrity through calmodulin-dependent protein interactions. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic ciliopathy protein architecture including two IQ calmodulin-binding motifs (IPR000048, PF00612, PS50096, SM00015, residues 293-315/386-408) with conserved isoleucine-glutamine residues for calcium-dependent calmodulin binding (GO:0005516), armadillo-type fold superfamily (IPR016024, SSF48371, residues 49-265) providing structural scaffold for protein-protein interactions, P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase homology (IPR027417, residues 255-333) for potential ATP-dependent regulation, IQ calmodulin-binding motif-containing protein 1 family signature (IPR028765, PTHR15673, residues 1-597) with cilium assembly capability (GO:0060271), and G3DSA:1.20.5.190 structural homology (residues 291-345/381-441). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q15051-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 82.8) across the 598 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded ciliary protein with structured IQ motifs and armadillo repeats suitable for calmodulin binding and CEP290 complex assembly. HPA data classifies IQCB1 as a disease-related intracellular protein involved in cilium biogenesis/degradation with calmodulin-binding molecular function, low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, microtubules, cytokinetic bridge, mitotic spindle, and primary cilium reflecting cell cycle and ciliary functions, and disease involvement in ciliopathy, Leber congenital amaurosis, nephronophthisis, and Senior-Løken syndrome. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally tight coupling to the ciliopathy disease network: CEP290 (Centrosomal protein 290 kDa, 0.999) as the primary interaction partner forming the ciliary transition zone complex; RPGR (Retinitis Pigmentosa GTPase Regulator, 0.989) for photoreceptor ciliary transport; SDCCAG8 (Serologically Defined Colon Cancer Antigen 8, 0.965) as a nephronophthisis-associated protein; NPHP4 (Nephrocystin-4, 0.953), NPHP3 (0.951), and NPHP1 (0.943) as nephronophthisis complex components; and calmodulin family members CALML3 (0.949), CALML5 (0.937), CALM3 (0.532), CALML6 (0.532) as calcium signal transducers binding IQ motifs. ClinVar lists 134 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.292_296del p.Glu98fs), duplications (c.751dup p.Ser251fs), nonsense mutations (c.745A>T p.Arg249Ter, c.859C>T p.Gln287Ter), splice site mutations (c.394-2A>G, c.1278+2del), missense changes (c.1759T>A p.Leu587Ile), and 3'UTR variants (c.*295G>A), causing Senior-Løken syndrome type 5 characterized by juvenile nephronophthisis progressing to end-stage renal disease and early-onset retinal dystrophy/Leber congenital amaurosis. Literature (76 papers) establishes IQCB1 as a well-characterized ciliopathy gene: long-read technologies identify hidden LINE-1/ERV1 insertion in IQCB1 as causative variant for Senior-Løken syndrome revealing complex mutational mechanisms; IQCB1/NPHP5 mutation causes adult-onset Senior-Løken syndrome expanding phenotypic spectrum beyond childhood diagnosis; pathogenic variants in CEP290 or IQCB1 cause earlier onset retinopathy in Senior-Løken syndrome compared to INVS, NPHP3, or NPHP4 establishing genotype-phenotype correlations; IQCB1-retinopathy clinical and genetic characterization defines natural history of retinal degeneration; IQCB1 localizes to connecting cilium of photoreceptors and interacts with CEP290 and calmodulin for ciliary gate function; and comprehensive reviews detail IQCB1 roles in ciliary transport and calcium signaling for photoreceptor and renal tubule homeostasis. As a well-studied ciliopathy protein with defined monogenic disease, IQCB1 represents a critical node connecting calcium-calmodulin signaling to ciliary gate function, photoreceptor outer segment renewal, and renal tubule maintenance through CEP290 complex assembly and IQ motif-mediated protein interactions disrupted in Senior-Løken syndrome.
