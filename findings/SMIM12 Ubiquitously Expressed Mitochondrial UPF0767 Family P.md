## Description

SMIM12 is the founding member of the SMALL INTEGRAL MEMBRANE PROTEIN 12 family (PTHR28599, residues 1-92). The UPF0767 designation indicates this is a protein of unknown function that is highly conserved, suggesting essential cellular roles.
