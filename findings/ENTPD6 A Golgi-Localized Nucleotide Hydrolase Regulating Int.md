# ENTPD6: A Golgi-Localized Nucleotide Hydrolase Regulating Intracellular Nucleotide Pools and Glycosylation

## Description
ENTPD6 (Ectonucleoside triphosphate diphosphohydrolase 6) is a 484-amino acid enzyme that hydrolyzes nucleoside triphosphates and diphosphates in a divalent cation-dependent manner, with strong preference for GDP. InterPro and Pfam analysis confirm it belongs to the GDA1/CD39 family (PF01150, IPR0000), containing four conserved apyrase domains (cd2455) and a nucleotide-binding domain (3.30.420.15). The AlphaFold model (O75354) shows high structural confidence (pLDDT: 83.4), indicating a stable, folded protein localized to the Golgi apparatus (HPA data). This localization aligns with functional parallels to plant Golgi-localized APYRASE1 (AtAPY1), which regulates nucleotide sugar homeostasis and glycosylation. ENTPD6 interacts with multiple ectonucleotidases (ENPP1/3, ENTPD1/3/4/8) and nucleotide metabolism enzymes (ITPA, CANT1, UCKL1) at high STRING confidence (>0.90), suggesting a role in a coordinated nucleotide surveillance network. HPA data show ubiquitous RNA expression with cell-type enhanced specificity, and ClinVar reports 20 pathogenic variants (including copy number gains), implicating ENTPD6 in disease despite limited literature. We hypothesize that ENTPD6 maintains Golgi nucleotide homeostasis by hydrolyzing excess nucleoside diphosphates, thereby preventing substrate imbalance that could disrupt glycosyltransferase activity and protein glycosylation. This function is critical for proper secretion and membrane protein maturation, with dysregulation potentially contributing to glycosylation disorders.
