# Lipase Maturation Factor 1 (LMF1) Function

**Date:** 2026-06-17T17:23:15.619733

**Quality Score:** 6/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
LMF1 likely functions as a chaperone for lipase maturation, playing a crucial role in lipid metabolism. The protein interacts with several key lipases and lipid-related proteins, including LIPC, LPL, APOC2, and GPIHBP1. LMF1 is widely expressed across tissues, with low tissue specificity, and has been implicated in various diseases through ClinVar variants.
