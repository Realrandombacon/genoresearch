# TMEM223: Mitochondrial Inner Membrane Assembly Factor for Cytochrome C Oxidase Biogenesis

**Date:** 2026-03-23T04:36:00.168108

**Quality Score:** 1.65/10  (E=3.0, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
TMEM223 (transmembrane protein 223, UniProt A0PJW6) is a 202-amino acid mitochondrial inner membrane protein that functions as an assembly factor for cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV) biogenesis. The protein contains a conserved TMEM70/TMEM186/TMEM223 family domain (PF06979, IPR045325, residues 32-198) shared with related mitochondrial membrane proteins involved in oxidative phosphorylation complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis reveals TMEM223 associates with COA3 (0.475), a known cytochrome c oxidase assembly factor, as well as multiple transmembrane proteins including SMIM4 (0.629), TMEM101 (0.624), TMEM53 (0.605), and TMEM238 (0.524), suggesting it operates within a network of small membrane scaffolds facilitating respiratory chain maturation. Additional interactions with PIGS (0.488, GPI anchor biosynthesis) and TMEM147 (0.478, ribosome/mitochondria association) indicate potential coordination between protein import, membrane insertion, and cofactor assembly. Human Protein Atlas data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and dual localization to mitochondria and nucleoplasm, consistent with essential housekeeping function in oxidative phosphorylation. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains affecting 11q12.3, demonstrating dosage sensitivity and disease association. The gene alias Mrx15 suggests potential links to X-linked intellectual disability phenotypes when disrupted. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 199 aa, zebrafish 248 aa) indicates strong functional constraint on mitochondrial respiratory chain assembly machinery. TMEM223 likely acts as a membrane-anchored chaperone recruiting nascent COX subunits or facilitating heme A insertion during Complex IV maturation.
