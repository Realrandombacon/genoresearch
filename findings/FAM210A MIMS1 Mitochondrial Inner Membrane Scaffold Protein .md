## Description
FAM210A (Family with sequence similarity 210 member A), officially named MIMS1 (mitochondrial inner membrane scaffold 1, UniProt Q96ND0), encodes a 272-amino acid protein localized to mitochondria, nucleoplasm, and Golgi apparatus, functioning as a structural scaffold protein that may regulate muscle and bone integrity through mitochondrial membrane organization and protein complex assembly. The protein contains a conserved FAM210A/B-like domain (IPR009688, PF06916, residues 124-210) belonging to the FAM210A/B-like family (IPR045866, PTHR21377, residues 94-227) and a C-terminal homologous superfamily domain (G3DSA:6.10.140.1430, residues 227-271) from CATH-Gene3D. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 64.6), indicating a partially structured protein with both ordered domains and intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with a scaffolding protein that functions through flexible protein-protein interactions. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression pattern with highest expression in tongue (30.9 nTPM), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced pattern, with subcellular localization in nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and mitochondria, supporting its role as a mitochondrial membrane scaffold. STRING interaction network reveals medium-confidence associations with ATAD3A (0.585, ATPase family AAA domain-containing protein 3A, mitochondrial inner membrane AAA-ATPase involved in mitochondrial cristae organization and cholesterol transport), GFM1 (0.546, elongation factor G, mitochondrial, mitochondrial translation elongation factor), SLC25A13 (0.521, solute carrier family 25 member 13, mitochondrial aspartate/glutamate carrier), HIBADH (0.573, 3-hydroxyisobutyrate dehydrogenase, mitochondrial enzyme), CD300C (0.594, CMRF35-like molecule 6, immune receptor), ZBTB40 (0.592, zinc finger and BTB domain-containing protein 40, transcriptional repressor), DCDC1 (0.527, doublecortin domain-containing protein 1, microtubule-binding protein), CPED1 (0.507, caudal-related homeobox transcription factor 1), SPTBN1 (0.493, spectrin beta chain, non-erythrocytic 1, cytoskeletal protein), and STARD3NL (0.404, START domain-containing protein 3 N-terminal like). The strong interaction with ATAD3A places FAM210A within the mitochondrial inner membrane protein complex, likely functioning as a scaffold that stabilizes ATAD3A oligomers involved in cristae organization and mitochondrial DNA nucleoid anchoring. Association with GFM1 suggests FAM210A may couple mitochondrial membrane structure to mitochondrial translation machinery, potentially coordinating protein synthesis with membrane biogenesis. Interaction with SLC25A13 indicates potential role in mitochondrial metabolite transport regulation. ClinVar contains 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning chromosome 18p11.21-32 regions (e.g., chr18:136227-15181209 duplications, chr18:136226-14352648 deletions), implicating FAM210A dosage in disease pathogenesis though specific phenotypes remain undefined; the high burden of structural variants suggests haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity mechanisms. Evolutionary conservation is evidenced by orthologs in mouse (Q8BGY7, 273 aa), bovine (Q05B67, 273 aa), rat (Q5XIJ4, 273 aa), and chicken (Q5ZML6, 275 aa), indicating functional constraint across vertebrates. Amino acid composition shows moderate hydrophobicity (37.5%, 102 residues) and charged residue content (26.8%, 73 residues), with leucine (28, 10.3%), lysine (26, 9.6%), valine (24, 8.8%), threonine/serine/proline/glutamate (18 each, 6.6%), and alanine/glycine/glutamine (16 each, 5.9%) as most abundant residues, consistent with a membrane-associated scaffolding protein with moderate structural flexibility. The mechanistic hypothesis proposes that FAM210A functions as a mitochondrial inner membrane scaffold protein that stabilizes ATAD3A oligomeric complexes involved in cristae organization and mitochondrial DNA nucleoid anchoring, while simultaneously coupling membrane structure to mitochondrial translation through GFM1 interaction. The FAM210A/B-like domain mediates protein-protein interactions within the inner membrane supercomplex, while the intrinsically disordered regions provide flexible tethering for dynamic assembly of respiratory chain complexes, metabolite carriers (SLC25A13), and translation machinery. FAM210A may also function in muscle and bone tissue integrity by maintaining mitochondrial cristae architecture in high-energy-demand tissues, explaining the tissue-enhanced expression pattern with highest levels in tongue (striated muscle). Dosage sensitivity evidenced by 39 ClinVar CNVs suggests that precise FAM210A stoichiometry is critical for mitochondrial membrane scaffold function, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupting cristae organization and leading to tissue-specific pathologies in muscle and bone.
