# EZH1: A SET Domain Histone H3K27 Methyltransferase Driving PRC2-Mediated Transcriptional Silencing in Stem Cell Maintenance

**Date:** 2026-06-26T17:10:53.901955

**Quality Score:** 10/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
EZH1 (Enhancer of Zeste 1, UniProt Q92800), also known as KMT6B, is a 747-amino acid catalytic subunit of noncanonical Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2) that trimethylates histone H3 at lysine 27 (H3K27me3), establishing facultative heterochromatin and silencing developmental genes. InterPro analysis reveals a modular methyltransferase architecture: an N-terminal EZH1-specific domain (IPR021654, aa 39-68) for complex assembly; two SANT/Myb domains (IPR001005, aa 135-263, 430-478) that bind modified histone tails and recruit chromatin readers; a CXC domain (IPR026489, IPR033467, aa 504-606) that positions the substrate; and a C-terminal SET domain (IPR001214, IPR044438, cd00167, cd19217, G3DSA:2.170.270.10, aa 608-743) containing the catalytic core for S-adenosylmethionine (SAM)-dependent methyl transfer. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows confident folding (pLDDT 74.6), consistent with a stable enzymatic scaffold. STRING interaction data places EZH1 at the heart of the PRC2 epigenetic machinery with near-perfect associations to: EZH2 (0.989), the paralogous catalytic subunit; SUZ12 (0.987) and EED (0.972), essential PRC2 core subunits; PHF19 (0.971), a PRC2 recruitment factor recognizing H3K36me3; RBBP4/RBBP7 (0.940-0.956), histone-binding proteins; AEBP2 (0.952), a DNA-binding PRC2 stabilizer; JARID2 (0.729), which recruits PRC2 to CpG islands; and RNF2/RING1 (0.601-0.602), components of PRC1 that recognize H3K27me3. HPA expression profiles show ubiquitous detection with enhancement in retina (123.7 nTPM), nucleoplasmic localization, and classification as a cancer-related chromatin regulator. ClinVar records document 31 pathogenic variants including missense mutations in critical domains (p.Pro559Ser in CXC, p.Arg443Leu in SANT, p.Gly656Arg in SET), frameshifts (p.Val738fs), and splice defects (c.1660+1G>A), implicating EZH1 in developmental disorders and malignancies. We hypothesize that EZH1 functions as the catalytic engine of noncanonical PRC2: the SANT domains anchor EZH1 to nucleosomes via histone tail recognition; the CXC domain positions the H3 tail into the SET domain active site; SAM binding enables sequential methylation to H3K27me3; this mark recruits PRC1 (via RNF2/RING1) for ubiquitination of H2AK119, establishing compacted chromatin; unlike EZH2 which drives proliferation in cycling cells, EZH1 maintains silencing in quiescent and differentiated cells, explaining its role in stem cell pluripotency and tissue homeostasis; loss of EZH1 disrupts epigenetic memory, causing derepression of developmental genes and contributing to oncogenesis.
