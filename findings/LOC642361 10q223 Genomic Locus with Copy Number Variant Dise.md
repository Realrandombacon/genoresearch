## Description

This locus spans approximately 1,700 base pairs and remains completely uncharacterized at the protein level—no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis, AlphaFold structure prediction, and STRING protein interaction mapping. The CNV burden suggests LOC642361 may function as a dosage-sensitive regulatory element, enhancer RNA, or non-coding RNA. Interestingly, NCBI gene search reveals proximity to NUTM2B-AS1 (NUTM2B antisense RNA 1), suggesting LOC642361 may operate in antisense regulation or chromatin modulation. The exclusive CNV variant pattern (no point mutations) indicates selection against structural rearrangements rather than specific amino acid changes, consistent with regulatory or non-coding function.
