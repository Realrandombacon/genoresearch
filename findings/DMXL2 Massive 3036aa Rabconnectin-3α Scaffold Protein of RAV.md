## Description
DMXL2 (DmX-Like Protein 2, also known as Rabconnectin-3α, UniProt Q8TDJ6) is a massive 3036 amino acid cytoplasmic/vesicular scaffold protein with moderate characterization (73 papers) that functions as a critical component of the RAVE (Regulator of ATPase of Vacuoles and Endosomes) complex regulating V-ATPase proton pump acidification and Rab3 GTPase cycling essential for synaptic vesicle neurotransmission and ribbon synapse function. The protein contains multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400/PS50082/PS50294, residues 43-84/100-136/159-198/170-197/2896-2937/2938-2979) with protein binding function (GO:0005515), RAVE complex protein Rav1 C-terminal domains (IPR022033/PF12234, residues 1144-1376/1497-1903), YVTN repeat-like domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10/IPR015943/IPR036322, residues 30-308/2748-2873/2874-3024/24-670/2764-3018), and belongs to the DmX-like/RAVE complex component family (IPR052208, residues 2-3022), placing it in the essential vesicle acidification and Rab GTPase regulatory machinery. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with Rab GTPase regulators and V-ATPase subunits: RAB3GAP1 (0.913, Rab3 GTPase activating protein catalytic subunit), RAB3GAP2 (0.900, Rab3 GTPase activating protein non-catalytic subunit), MADD (0.848, MAP kinase activating death domain protein, Rab3 GEF), ATP6V1E1 (0.730, V-ATPase subunit E1), ATP6V1B2 (0.722, V-ATPase subunit B2), ATP6V1H (0.698, V-ATPase subunit H), ATP6V1G1 (0.684, V-ATPase subunit G1), WDR7 (0.646, WD repeat protein 7), ROGDI (0.523, protein rogdi homolog, epilepsy gene), and RAB3A (0.466, Ras-related protein Rab-3A), confirming membership in the RAVE complex regulating V-ATPase assembly and Rab3-mediated synaptic vesicle exocytosis. HPA data shows vesicles and cytosol subcellular localization, low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, low cancer specificity, and classification as disease-related gene/potential drug target/transporter with disease involvement including deafness, diabetes mellitus, epilepsy, and hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. ClinVar contains 145 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg233Ter, p.Gln219Ter, p.Arg1393Ter, p.Gln2184Ter, p.Trp910Ter, p.Cys1897Ter), frameshifts (p.His580fs, p.Asn2571fs), deletions (c.7710del, c.6222_6223del), and complex insertions, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—however recent literature definitively links DMXL2 to autosomal dominant non-syndromic hearing impairment DFNA71. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.4) indicates medium confidence with partially structured architecture typical of large scaffold proteins containing multiple WD40 β-propeller domains connected by flexible linkers.
