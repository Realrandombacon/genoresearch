# SLC4A8 (NDCBE): A Brain-Enriched Sodium-Bicarbonate Exchanger Regulating Neuronal pH Homeostasis and Hearing via USH2A Interactions

## Description
SLC4A8 (UniProt Q2Y0W8) encodes a 1093 amino acid electroneutral sodium-bicarbonate exchanger (NDCBE), a member of the SLC4 family characterized by canonical bicarbonate transporter domains (IPR003020, PF00955) spanning aa 109-1032, with specific sodium-binding motifs (IPR003024) at aa 146-152 and 344-352. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q2Y0W8-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable transporter with high confidence (global pLDDT 72.4), consistent with its role in active transmembrane exchange. HPA expression reveals strong enrichment in the brain (12.0 nTPM), pituitary gland (16.9 nTPM), and testis (11.0 nTPM), with predicted plasma membrane localization. Literature search (52 papers) confirms extensive characterization in acid-base homeostasis, salt reabsorption, and neuronal pH regulation, though no specific monogenic disease is yet annotated, suggesting involvement in complex neurodevelopmental or auditory disorders. ClinVar lists 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (including c.2700+4A>T and multiple CNVs on chr12), predominantly copy number gains/losses, though no specific disease is yet annotated, implicating potential roles in syndromes involving hearing loss or developmental delay. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners USH2A (0.599), SLC26A4 (0.718), and SLC9A3 (0.592), placing SLC4A8 within the United Hearing Syndrome 2 (USH2) complex and other ion transport networks. Functional hypothesis: SLC4A8 functions as a critical neuronal pH regulator that maintains intracellular bicarbonate levels via sodium-coupled exchange; its interaction with USH2A (a key component of theUSH2 complex) suggests a role in stereocilia function and mechanotransduction, where pH dysregulation contributes to hearing loss and potential neurodevelopmental phenotypes.
