## Description
CCDC106 encodes a 280 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt Q9BWC9) localized to the nucleoplasm and cytosol. The protein contains a conserved coiled-coil domain (IPR031591, PF15794, positions 56-276) characteristic of scaffold proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions. HPA expression data shows highest expression in brain tissue (68.7 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced RNA specificity and detection across all tissues examined. STRING interaction analysis reveals 10 medium-confidence protein partners including CCDC154 (0.622), CCDC34 (0.605), ATF4 (0.605), RSPRY1 (0.594), and notably TP53 (0.450) - suggesting potential involvement in transcriptional regulation or stress response pathways. The interaction with ATF4 (activating transcription factor 4) and TP53 (tumor protein 53) positions CCDC106 within cellular stress response networks. ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses on chromosome 19q13.42-13.43, indicating dosage sensitivity. Mouse ortholog (Q3ULM0, 279 aa) shows strong conservation (~99% identity), supporting functional importance. The coiled-coil architecture suggests CCDC106 functions as a dimerization or oligomerization scaffold, potentially assembling nuclear protein complexes involved in transcriptional regulation, DNA damage response, or chromatin organization. Disease-associated copy number variants suggest haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity mechanisms.
