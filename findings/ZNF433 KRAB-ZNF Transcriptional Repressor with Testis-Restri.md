## Description
ZNF433 (zinc finger protein 433) is a 673 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger transcription factor encoded on chromosome 19p13.2 (GRCh38: 12014714-12035678). UniProt accession Q8N7K0 confirms this as a canonical protein-coding gene with well-defined domain architecture.\n\nInterPro domain analysis reveals canonical KRAB-ZNF architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 6-85) that recruits co-repressor complexes, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 142-345+) that confer sequence-specific DNA recognition. The KRAB domain (cd07765, residues 7-46) mediates heterochromatin formation through HP1 recruitment. This domain architecture classifies ZNF433 as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor targeting major groove DNA via zinc finger recognition helices.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 6 medium-confidence partners: TRIM28 (score 0.570, tripartite motif-containing protein 28, universal KRAB co-repressor that recruits SETDB1 H3K9 methyltransferase and HP1), TMEM220 (0.475), SPATA31E1 (0.447), OR2D3 (0.432), RCCD1 (0.425), and CYLC2 (0.407). The TRIM28 interaction (KAP1/TIF1β) is functionally critical - TRIM28 is the universal adaptor for KRAB-ZNF repressors, forming heterochromatin via H3K9me3 deposition.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas reveals RESTRICTED EXPRESSION PROFILE: RNA tissue specificity is "tissue enhanced" with top expression in TESTIS (20.4 nTPM), detected in all tissues but strongly enriched in germline. Subcellular location is nuclear bodies (PML bodies or heterochromatin foci). Protein class: transcription factors, predicted intracellular proteins. This testis-restricted pattern suggests ZNF433 functions in spermatogenesis, meiotic silencing, or transposon repression in male germline.\n\nClinVar analysis identifies 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 6 copy number losses (19p13.2 deletions ~0.5-3.5 Mb), 5 copy number gains (19p13.2-q13.43 duplications ~3-58 Mb), 1 single nucleotide variant (c.131-6T>A intronic), and 1 unclassified deletion. The CNV ratio shows ~5:6 duplication:deletion (~45% duplications, 55% deletions), indicating MODERATE BALANCED dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and triplication cause disease. This contrasts with extreme biases seen in silencer elements (LOC129390117 ~30:1 deletion, LOC129389033 ~7:1 duplication).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ZNF433 functions as a testis-specific KRAB-ZNF transcriptional repressor that silences transposable elements (LINE-1, ERV retrotransposons) or meiosis-specific genes during spermatogenesis. The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1, which scaffolds SETDB1 (H3K9me3 methyltransferase), HP1 (heterochromatin protein), and NuRD complex to establish facultative heterochromatin at target loci. Testis-restricted expression (20.4 nTPM) aligns with known biology: male germline requires stringent transposon silencing to maintain genomic integrity across generations. ZNF433 may target young primate-specific retrotransposons or regulate meiotic checkpoint genes. The balanced CNV burden (13 variants, ~1:1 ratio) suggests ZNF433 dosage must be precisely maintained - both loss (reduced transposon silencing, genomic instability) and gain (ectopic repression of essential spermatogenesis genes) cause male infertility or developmental defects. TRIM28 interaction confirms canonical KRAB-ZNF repression mechanism. Nuclear body localization suggests concentration in PML bodies or pericentromeric heterochromatin. ZNF433 represents the "dark" majority of ~350 human KRAB-ZNFs with uncharacterized target specificity but conserved repression mechanism.
