# ZC3HC1: Nuclear Basket-Associated SCF(NIPA) E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Component Regulating Cyclin B1 Degradation and Cell Cycle Progression with Links to Hypertension and Cancer

**Date:** 2026-06-14T18:39:22.238484

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
ZC3HC1 (Q86WB0) encodes a 502 amino acid zinc finger C3HC-type protein that functions as a critical component of the SCF(NIPA) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex, regulating cell cycle progression through targeted degradation of cyclin B1 (CCNB1) during G2/M transition. InterPro analysis reveals a bimodular NuBaID (Nuclear Basket Anchoring Domain) architecture: an N-terminal NuBaID domain (IPR012935/PF07967, positions 73-199) and a C-terminal NuBaID domain (IPR013909/PF08600, positions 248-470), both containing zinc ion binding capability (GO:0008270), embedded within the SCF(NIPA) complex component family (PTHR15835, positions 21-289); this domain organization anchors ZC3HC1 to the nuclear basket of the nuclear pore complex while enabling substrate recognition for ubiquitination. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 68.3, medium confidence) consistent with flexible linker regions between ordered NuBaID domains enabling conformational adaptation during substrate binding and nuclear basket association. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with cell cycle and ubiquitination machinery: CUL1 (0.904), the scaffold protein of SCF complexes; RBX1 (0.617), the RING component recruiting E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes; SKP1 (0.503), the adapter linking F-box proteins to CUL1; CCNB1 (0.434), the cyclin B1 substrate; NPM1 (0.576), nucleophosmin involved in ribosome biogenesis and stress response; ALK (0.600), anaplastic lymphoma kinase; MAPK1 (0.439), ERK2 signaling kinase; and PHACTR1 (0.400), linking to vascular biology. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, nucleoplasm and nuclear membrane localization consistent with nuclear basket positioning, classified under cell cycle/cell division/mitosis/Ubl conjugation pathway biological processes. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses spanning chromosome 7q31.32-36.1 regions (e.g., chr7:122190535-149944340 deletions, chr7:120582003-137699953 deletions) and duplications (chr7:109251060-159119707 gains), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (52 papers) demonstrates ZC3HC1 has functions distinct from TPR and is dispensable for TPR localization to the nuclear basket; an evolutionarily conserved bimodular NuBaID domain anchors ZC3HC1 to the nuclear basket; the rs11556924 missense variant has a protective effect against peripheral artery disease risk in women; ZC3HC1 gene variants increase essential hypertension risk in Portuguese populations; HROB induces lung adenocarcinoma progression via ZC3HC1-CCNB1 axis regulation and cell cycle dysregulation. The mechanistic hypothesis: ZC3HC1 functions as the substrate recognition component of SCF(NIPA), recruiting phosphorylated cyclin B1 for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation during interphase, preventing premature mitotic entry; the bimodular NuBaID architecture anchors ZC3HC1 to nuclear basket filaments formed by TPR, positioning the SCF complex near nuclear pores for efficient substrate processing; copy number losses on 7q may cause haploinsufficiency leading to cyclin B1 accumulation and genomic instability, potentially contributing to cancer predisposition; the hypertension and peripheral artery disease associations suggest ZC3HC1 variants alter vascular smooth muscle cell cycle control or endothelial function; partial disorder (pLDDT 68.3) enables ZC3HC1 to function as a flexible substrate receptor adapting to different phosphorylation states of cyclin B1; interaction with ALK and MAPK1 suggests cross-talk between growth factor signaling and cell cycle regulation through ZC3HC1-mediated ubiquitination.
