## Description
ERO1B (Endoplasmic Reticulum Oxidoreductase 1 Beta, also known as ERO1-Like Protein Beta, UniProt Q86YB8) is a 467 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum (ER) resident enzyme with limited characterization (11 papers) that functions as an FAD-dependent oxidoreductase generating disulfide bonds in nascent secretory and membrane proteins through oxidative protein folding, serving as a paralogue of ERO1A with tissue-specific expression patterns. The protein contains an Endoplasmic Reticulum Oxidoreductin 1 family domain (IPR007266/PF04137/PTHR12613 residues 16-466/56-454/16-466, GO:0015035 protein-disulfide reductase activity, GO:0016972 thiol oxidase activity, GO:0071949 FAD binding), ERO1-like superfamily (IPR037192/SSF110019 residues 44-464), and Endoplasmic Oxidoreductin family (PIRSF017205 residues 30-463), defining the conserved ERO1 oxidoreductase family that transfers electrons from protein thiols to molecular oxygen via FAD. STRING interactions reveal high-to-medium confidence partnerships with ER protein folding machinery: PDIA4 (0.984, protein disulfide-isomerase A4—direct electron acceptor), ERO1A (0.961, ERO1 alpha—paralogous oxidoreductase), TXNDC5 (0.959, thioredoxin domain-containing protein 5—ER oxidoreductase), PDIA6 (0.955, protein disulfide-isomerase A6), JCHAIN (0.833, joining chain of IgA/IgM—client protein), HSPA5 (0.794, heat shock 70kDa protein 5/BiP—ER chaperone), ERP29 (0.621, ER protein 29), P4HB (0.590, protein disulfide-isomerase), PDIA2 (0.526), and ERP44 (0.434, ER resident protein 44—disulfide bond regulator), confirming integration into the complete oxidative folding pathway.
