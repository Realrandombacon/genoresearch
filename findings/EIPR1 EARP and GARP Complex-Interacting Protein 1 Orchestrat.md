## Description
EIPR1 (EARP and GARP Complex-Interacting Protein 1) is a 387 amino acid WD40 repeat-containing scaffolding protein that functions as a critical regulator of endolysosomal trafficking and dense-core vesicle (DCV) cargo retention through beta-propeller-mediated protein-protein interactions, EARP complex localization control, and GARP complex assembly for retrograde transport from endosomes to trans-Golgi network.
