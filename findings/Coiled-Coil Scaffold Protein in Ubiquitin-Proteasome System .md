## Description
CCDC74B (Q96LY2) encodes a 380 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein that functions as a scaffold component of the ubiquitin-proteasome system. InterPro analysis identifies two characteristic coiled-coil domains: an N-terminal domain (IPR039496/PF14916, positions 51-100) and a C-terminal domain (IPR029422/PF14917, positions 263-377), placing it in the CCDC74A/74B/92 family (IPR040370/PTHR14882). The AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 59.2), consistent with coiled-coil proteins that form extended helical structures. STRING interactions reveal CCDC74B within a proteasome-associated network with high-confidence partners: UCHL5/UCH37 (0.841, deubiquitinating enzyme), ADRM1/Rpn13 (0.835, proteasome activator), and multiple 19S regulatory particle subunits including PSMD1/p66 (0.834), PSMD2/p69 (0.834), PSMD14/Rpn11 (0.830), PSMD10/gankyrin (0.607), PSMD7/S8 (0.499), PSMC1/TBP7 (0.601), and PSMC4/Sug2 (0.591). The moderate interaction with paralog CCDC74A (0.586) suggests functional redundancy. HPA expression shows testis-enriched RNA (51.4 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, centrosome, basal body, and cytosol—positions critical for both proteasomal degradation and ciliary assembly. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (deletions and duplications spanning 2q21.1), though no specific disease phenotype has been definitively assigned. The convergence of proteasome interactions, centrosomal/basal body localization, and testis enrichment suggests CCDC74B may regulate protein turnover during spermatogenesis or ciliogenesis, potentially coordinating ubiquitin-dependent degradation of cell cycle regulators at the centrosome.
