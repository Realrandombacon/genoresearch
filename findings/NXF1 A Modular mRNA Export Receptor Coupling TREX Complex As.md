# NXF1: A Modular mRNA Export Receptor Coupling TREX Complex Assembly to Nuclear Pore Translocation via NTF2-UBA Domain Architecture

**Date:** 2026-06-26T16:54:46.166522

**Quality Score:** 10/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
NXF1 (Nuclear RNA Export Factor 1, UniProt Q9UBU9), also known as TAP (Tip-associated protein) or MEX67, is a 619-amino acid essential mRNA export receptor that mediates the bulk transport of spliced messenger RNAs from nucleus to cytoplasm through nuclear pore complexes (NPCs). InterPro analysis reveals a modular domain architecture optimized for RNA binding and nucleocytoplasmic trafficking: an N-terminal RNA-binding domain (RBD, IPR015245, aa 96-201) with a nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait fold that recognizes mRNAs; four leucine-rich repeats (LRRs, IPR001611, aa 266-313) that mediate protein-protein interactions; a central NTF2-like domain (IPR002075, cd00780, aa 382-538) that heterodimerizes with NXT1/p15 to form the active export complex; and a C-terminal ubiquitin-associated domain (UBA, IPR009060, cd14342, aa 567-617) that binds phenylalanine-glycine (FG) repeat nucleoporins. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 79.4), confirming a stable multi-domain scaffold. STRING interaction data places NXF1 at the heart of the mRNA export and surveillance machinery with near-perfect associations to exon junction complex (EJC) components UPF3A (0.998), UPF1 (0.998), RNPS1 (0.998), and RBM8A (0.998)—linking export to nonsense-mediated decay; the TREX complex adaptor ALYREF (0.984) that recruits NXF1 to spliced mRNAs; the obligate heterodimer partner NXT1 (0.894); and nucleoporins NUP98 (0.563) and RAE1 (0.425) that facilitate NPC translocation. HPA expression profiles confirm ubiquitous presence ('Detected in all') with nucleoplasmic localization, consistent with essential housekeeping function. ClinVar records document 12 pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses at 11q12.3, implying dosage sensitivity. We hypothesize that NXF1 functions as the terminal adaptor in the mRNA export pathway: after splicing, the TREX complex (ALYREF, THOC subunits) is deposited on mRNAs; ALYREF recruits NXF1-NXT1 heterodimers via the LRR domain; the RBD contacts the mRNA while the UBA domain engages FG-nucleoporins lining the NPC channel; and the NTF2 domain provides structural stability for repeated translocation cycles. This positions NXF1 as a molecular bridge between processed mRNAs and the nuclear pore, ensuring only properly spliced transcripts reach the cytoplasm. Dysregulation of NXF1 may disrupt global gene expression or impair NMD surveillance, contributing to the phenotypes associated with 11q12.3 CNVs.
