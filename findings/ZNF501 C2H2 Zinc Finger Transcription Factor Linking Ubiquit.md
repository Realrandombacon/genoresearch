# ZNF501: C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcription Factor Linking Ubiquitin Signaling with Chromatin Regulation and Golgi Organization

**Date:** 2026-03-27T13:22:08.180037

**Quality Score:** 4.65/10  (E=8.2, D=1.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
ZNF501 encodes a 271 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q96CX3) located on chromosome 3p21.31. InterPro analysis reveals three tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096, PS00028, PS50157, SM00355) at positions 22-49, 50-77, and 78-105, forming the classic beta-beta-alpha zinc finger fold (G3DSA:3.30.160.60, SSF57667) that enables sequence-specific DNA recognition through alpha-helix insertion into the major groove. Additional Kruppel-type zinc finger family domain (PTHR24381, aa 93-271) extends through the C-terminus. AlphaFold structure prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.8), indicating a well-folded stable structure typical of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors. STRING interaction network reveals connections to ubiquitin signaling and chromatin modification pathways: UBC (polyubiquitin, score 0.821), YOD1 (OTU-family deubiquitinase, 0.788), SETDB1 (histone H3K9 methyltransferase, 0.696), HDAC6 (histone deacetylase, 0.671), USP44/USP5 (deubiquitinases, 0.642/0.564), ZUP1 (zinc finger protein, 0.548), ZPR1 (zinc finger RNA-binding protein, 0.527), OTUD7A (deubiquitinase, 0.448), and TRIM28 (transcriptional corepressor/KAP1, 0.400). ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning 3p26.3-21.31 regions and one missense variant (p.Gln41His within the first zinc finger), suggesting both dosage effects and structural disruption contribute to disease. Conservation is evident across primates (orangutan Q5RF70, macaque A0A2K6ASR9) and mammals (bear A0A384D5V0, beluga whale A0A7F8KEW5), all maintaining 271 aa length. Functional hypothesis: ZNF501 acts as a sequence-specific transcriptional regulator that coordinates gene expression programs linking chromatin state with ubiquitin-dependent protein turnover, potentially recruiting SETDB1-mediated H3K9 methylation and HDAC6-mediated deacetylation to silence target genes while simultaneously regulating their ubiquitination status through YOD1/USP interactions. The Golgi organization role suggests ZNF501 may regulate genes involved in secretory pathway function or membrane trafficking. Disease mechanism involves both haploinsufficiency from deletions and dominant-negative effects from missense mutations disrupting zinc finger DNA binding, contributing to developmental disorders associated with 3p deletions/duplications.
