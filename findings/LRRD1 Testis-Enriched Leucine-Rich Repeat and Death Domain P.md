## Description
LRRD1 (Leucine-rich repeat and death domain-containing protein 1, UniProt A4D1F6) is a 860-amino acid intracellular scaffold protein located on chromosome 7q21.2 within a region prone to extensive copy-number variations. InterPro analysis reveals a multidomain architecture combining leucine-rich repeat (LRR) and death domain (DD) motifs: N-terminal LRR region (IPR001611/PF00560/SM00369/PS51450, aa 189-280) belonging to the LRR domain superfamily (IPR032675/G3DSA:3.80.10.10, aa 41-430) that mediates protein-protein interactions, followed by a C-terminal death domain (IPR000488/SM00381/PS50017, aa 782-852) belonging to the death-like domain superfamily (IPR011029/G3DSA:1.10.533.10, aa 755-850) that typically mediates apoptosis signaling and caspase recruitment. The protein also contains a disease resistance R13L4/SHOC-2-like LRR domain (IPR055414, aa 175-291/557-659) and belongs to the LRRD1 death domain family (IPR056869, aa 763-852). GO annotations confirm roles in protein binding and signal transduction. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 81.1), indicating a well-folded, stable protein with defined LRR solenoid and DD helical bundle domains — architecture consistent with signaling scaffold proteins that integrate multiple interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched expression with highest expression in testis (13.8 nTPM), detected in single tissue, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and cell type-enhanced specificity — suggesting primary function in male germ cell development. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners including TERB1 (0.951, highest confidence — telomere repeat-binding bouquet formation protein 1 essential for meiotic telomere attachment), C2orf83 (0.774, chromosome 2 open reading frame 83), MRAS (0.563, muscle RAS oncogene homolog involved in MAPK signaling), ADGRL1/2/3 (0.526 each, adhesion G protein-coupled receptors involved in cell adhesion), SPATA31A7 (0.521, spermatogenesis-associated protein 31A7), CCDC27 (0.437, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 27), and SLC36A3 (0.436, solute carrier family 36 member 3). This interactome confirms LRRD1 functions within spermatogenesis networks, particularly in meiotic chromosome dynamics through TERB1 interaction. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic variants consisting of single nucleotide variants, deletions, and large copy-number gains and losses across 7q21.11-22.1, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 7 cause disease phenotypes. Conservation across mammals (mouse Q8C0R9, 853 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental reproductive mechanisms. LRRD1 likely functions as a signaling scaffold in spermatogenesis, using its LRR domain to recruit partners like TERB1 for meiotic telomere attachment and its death domain to potentially regulate apoptosis during germ cell development — mechanisms critical for male fertility, with dysfunction potentially contributing to infertility and 7q deletion syndromes where LRRD1 haploinsufficiency disrupts testis-specific signaling pathways.
