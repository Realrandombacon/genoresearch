## Description
CDC40 encodes a 579-amino acid pre-mRNA-processing factor 17 (UniProt O60508), also known as cell division cycle 40 homolog, a well-folded WD40-repeat protein functioning as an essential component of the catalytic step 2 spliceosome complex. The protein contains multiple WD40 repeat domains (IPR001680, positions 277-318/321-404/407-446; PF00400, positions 279-316/323-360/365-404; PS00678, positions 304-318; PS50082, positions 284-318/328-369/414-446) forming a characteristic beta-propeller structure (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 263-579; IPR015943, positions 263-579; IPR036322, positions 275-579) that serves as a scaffold for protein-protein interactions within the spliceosome, classified within the pre-mRNA-processing factor 17 family (IPR032847, positions 7-579) with mRNA splicing via spliceosome and catalytic step 2 spliceosome GO annotations. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.8, residues 1-579) indicating stable folding consistent with WD40 beta-propeller architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals CDC40 forms part of the core spliceosome network with ultra-high confidence interactions: DHX38 (0.999, DEAH-box helicase 38), PRPF19 (0.996, pre-mRNA processing factor 19), RBM22 (0.994, RNA-binding motif protein 22), SNRPA1 (0.993, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide A'), SYF2 (0.989, pre-mRNA-splicing factor SYF2), SNRPD3 (0.926, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein D3), DHX8 (0.911, DEAH-box helicase 8), SNRPF (0.903, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein F), SNRNP40 (0.892, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein 40 kDa), and AQR (0.849, intron-binding protein aquarius), confirming its central role in spliceosome assembly and catalytic activation. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasm subcellular localization consistent with spliceosome function, annotated for mRNA processing and mRNA splicing biological processes, classified as essential protein and disease-related gene with intellectual disability involvement. ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including single nucleotide variants (c.964C>T p.Arg322Trp in WD40 domain, c.1505T>G p.Phe502Cys) and copy number losses (6q21-22.31, 6q15-22.2, 6q14.3-22.31, 6q16.1-21, 6q16.1-22.1) and gains (6q21-23.2, 6q16.3-22.1, 6p25.3-q27), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined beyond intellectual disability association. With 40 publications, CDC40 has emerging cancer biology roles: CDC40 suppression induces CDCA5 splicing defects and anti-proliferative effects in lung cancer cells (2025, 2 cit), miR-422a directly targets CDC40 modulating gastric cancer cell proliferation (2020, 4 cit), HBx-induced miR-1269b upregulates CDC40 to promote proliferation and migration in hepatoma cells via NF-κB pathway (2016, 39 cit). The functional hypothesis: CDC40 serves as a WD40-repeat scaffold protein stabilizing the catalytic step 2 spliceosome complex through protein-protein interactions with core spliceosomal components; the beta-propeller structure provides multiple binding surfaces for recruiting spliceosome assembly factors (PRPF19 complex), RNA helicases (DHX38, DHX8), and snRNP components (SNRPA1, SNRPD3, SNRPF, SNRNP40); WD40 repeats mediate conformational changes during spliceosome activation and catalytic transitions between step 1 and step 2 of splicing; cancer associations suggest CDC40 dysregulation causes aberrant splicing of cell cycle regulators (CDCA5) and oncogenes, promoting tumor proliferation and metastasis; miR-1269b-mediated upregulation in HBV-associated HCC indicates viral oncogenesis through splicing factor overexpression; miR-422a targeting in gastric cancer suggests tumor suppressor miRNA loss leads to CDC40 overexpression and oncogenic splicing programs; intellectual disability association implies CDC40 haploinsufficiency disrupts neuronal gene splicing, potentially affecting synaptic proteins or neurodevelopmental transcripts; 6q CNVs may alter CDC40 dosage causing widespread splicing defects contributing to developmental disorders and cancer; essential protein classification indicates CDC40 is required for cell viability through constitutive pre-mRNA splicing.
