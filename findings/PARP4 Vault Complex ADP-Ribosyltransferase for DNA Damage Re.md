## Description
PARP4 encodes a 1724 aa protein mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase PARP4 (UniProt Q9UKK3), also known as ARTD4 or vault PARP, functioning as a component of the cytoplasmic vault organelle that regulates DNA damage repair, alternative splicing, and apoptosis. The protein contains multiple functional domains: N-terminal BRCT domain (IPR001357/cd17726, aa5-94) for protein-protein interactions and DNA damage response, PARP catalytic domain (IPR012317/cd01437, aa251-565) with NAD+ poly-ADP-ribosyltransferase activity (GO:0003950), PARP regulatory domain (IPR004102, aa242-370), VIT domain (IPR013694, aa607-735) of unknown function, and von Willebrand factor type A domain (IPR002035, aa874-1046) for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 69.2), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered catalytic domains and flexible linker regions. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a predicted intracellular protein with cell type-enhanced specificity localized to nucleoplasm, microtubules, mitotic spindle, primary cilium, and cytosol. STRING interactions reveal PARP4 forms the vault ribonucleoprotein complex: MVP (0.999, major vault protein), TEP1 (0.998, telomerase-associated protein 1), plus caspase interactions (CASP3 0.915, CASP7 0.913, CASP9 0.911) linking to apoptosis, and PARP family members (PARP15 0.745, PARP6 0.737, PARP9 0.638, PARP16 0.405). ClinVar contains 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1449-6A>T, c.2345+9C>G), missense variants (c.2355G>C p.Leu785Phe), and 13q copy number gains/deletions. Literature (80 papers) demonstrates PARP4 deficiency enhances melanoma sensitivity to ATM inhibitors by impairing DNA double-strand break repair; PARP4 interacts with hnRNPM to regulate alternative splicing during lung cancer progression; PARP4 structural studies reveal NAD+ binding roles in vault cage assembly; PARP4 BRCT domain binds nucleic acids; PARP4 is cleaved by caspases during apoptosis. PARP4 functions as a dual-purpose enzyme: in the vault complex, the ultra-high confidence MVP/TEP1 interactions (0.999/0.998) indicate PARP4 is an integral vault component where its ADP-ribosyltransferase activity may modify vault proteins or associated RNAs for nucleocytoplasmic transport; in DNA damage response, the BRCT domain recruits PARP4 to double-strand breaks where mono-ADP-ribosylation of repair factors facilitates homologous recombination. The caspase interactions (0.915/0.913/0.911) suggest PARP4 is a apoptosis substrate—caspase cleavage may inactivate PARP4 or generate pro-apoptotic fragments. hnRNPM-mediated splicing regulation links PARP4 to cancer progression through alternative splicing of oncogenic transcripts. Melanoma ATM inhibitor sensitivity reflects PARP4's role in DSB repair—PARP4 loss creates synthetic lethality with ATM inhibition. Copy number variants on 13q may cause PARP4 dosage effects contributing to cancer susceptibility or developmental disorders. The medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 69.2) enables PARP4 to function as a flexible scaffold coordinating vault assembly, DNA repair complex formation, and spliceosome recruitment.
