## Description
FAM156A (Family with Sequence Similarity 156 Member A, UniProt Q8NDB6) encodes a 213-amino acid intracellular protein containing a PGC7/Stella/Dppa3 domain (InterPro IPR029096, Pfam PF15549 residues 1-170, Panther PTHR31577 residues 1-197). This domain family is associated with developmental pluripotency and epigenetic regulation, particularly in protecting DNA methylation marks during early embryogenesis. Gene ontology predicts methylated histone binding activity and nuclear envelope localization. Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue and cancer specificity, classified as cell type enhanced at single-cell resolution. STRING interaction network identifies 5 partners: RBM41 (0.479), TMEM164 (0.436), TMEM120A (0.428), SLX1B (0.404), and DYDC2 (0.400). Notably, TMEM120A is an inner nuclear membrane protein involved in nuclear envelope organization, supporting the predicted nuclear envelope localization. RBM41 is an RNA-binding protein suggesting potential roles in nuclear RNA processing. ClinVar documents 134 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly X chromosome copy number losses and gains spanning Xp11.22 where FAM156A resides, indicating strong dosage sensitivity. The X-chromosomal location combined with ubiquitous expression and nuclear envelope association suggests FAM156A may function in chromatin organization or nuclear envelope integrity across cell types. The PGC7/Stella domain homology implies potential involvement in epigenetic mark protection, possibly binding methylated histones to maintain chromatin states. X-linked CNVs disrupting FAM156A dosage could affect nuclear envelope function and epigenetic regulation, contributing to developmental or neurological phenotypes commonly associated with X-chromosome aneuploidies.
