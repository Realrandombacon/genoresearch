## Description
TUBA3D (Tubulin alpha 3d, also known as H2-ALPHA, KTCN9, TUBA2) is a 450 amino acid intracellular structural protein (UniProt P0DPH8) with very-high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 91.3), characteristic of alpha-tubulin family members that function through GTP-dependent heterodimerization with beta-tubulins to form microtubule polymers. The protein belongs to the alpha-tubulin family (cd02186, residues 2-435; IPR000217, residues 2-439; IPR002452, residues 18-33/45-58/88-101; IPR017975, residues 142-148), contains a Tubulin/FtsZ GTPase domain (IPR003008, residues 3-246; G3DSA:3.40.50.1440, residues 1-268) enabling GTP binding activity (GO:0005525), a Tubulin/FtsZ C-terminal domain (IPR008280, residues 246-437; G3DSA:3.30.1330.20, residues 269-383), and a 2-layer sandwich domain (IPR018316, residues 248-393; G3DSA:1.10.287.600, residues 384-436). TUBA3D is located on chromosome 2q21.1 and enables structural constituent of cytoskeleton activity (GO:0005200), involved in microtubule-based processes (GO:0007017), located in microtubules (GO:0005874) and primary cilium.
