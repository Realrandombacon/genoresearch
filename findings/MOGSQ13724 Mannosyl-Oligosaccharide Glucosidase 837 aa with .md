## Description
MOGS (Mannosyl-Oligosaccharide Glucosidase, UniProt Q13724) encodes an 837 aa type II transmembrane glycoside hydrolase representing a MODERATELY CHARACTERIZED dark gene with defined enzymatic function but incomplete disease mechanism understanding. MOGS catalyzes the first step in N-glycan processing within the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), removing the terminal α1,2-linked glucose residue from Glc3Man9GlcNAc2 oligosaccharides transferred to nascent polypeptides, initiating the calnexin/calreticulin folding cycle.
