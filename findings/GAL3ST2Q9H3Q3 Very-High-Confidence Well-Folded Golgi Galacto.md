## Description
GAL3ST2 (galactose-3-O-sulfotransferase 2, aliases GAL3ST-2, GP3ST) is a 398 amino acid predicted Golgi membrane sulfotransferase enzyme (UniProt Q9H3Q3) with very-high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 92.6) containing canonical sulfotransferase architecture including Galactose-3-O-sulfotransferase family (IPR009729/PF06990/PTHR14647, residues 1-384) forming a 384 aa structured module (~96% of protein) characteristic of the galactose sulfotransferase family that catalyzes sulfate transfer to C-3 hydroxyl of beta-galactosyl residues, P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/SSF52540, residues 51-308/52-191) forming a 140-258 aa module (~35-65%) with characteristic P-loop fold for nucleotide binding and catalysis, and molecular functions including galactosylceramide sulfotransferase activity (GO:0001733), glycolipid biosynthetic process (GO:0009247), and membrane localization (GO:0016020), functioning as a Golgi-localized sulfotransferase that regulates glycolipid biosynthesis and carbohydrate sulfation through PAPS-dependent sulfate transfer to type 1/type 2 oligosaccharides and core 1 glycans via partnerships with NEU4, DTYMK, CHST5, NEU2, FUT3, SULT1C3, SULT1C4, SULT1B1, and TFF3.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals GAL3ST2 contains canonical sulfotransferase architecture: The Galactose-3-O-sulfotransferase family domain (IPR009729, residues 1-384) spans 384 aa (~96%) with characteristic sulfotransferase fold that catalyzes transfer of sulfate from 3'-phosphoadenosine 5'-phosphosulfate (PAPS) to the C-3 hydroxyl of non-reducing beta-galactosyl residues in glycolipids and glycoproteins. The enzyme acts on both type 1 (Galβ1-3GlcNAc-R) and type 2 (Galβ1-4GlcNAc-R) oligosaccharides with similar efficiency, and on core 1 glycans (Galβ1-3GalNAc). The P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 51-308) forms 258 aa (~65%) providing the nucleotide-binding fold for PAPS recognition and catalysis. The SSF52540 domain (residues 52-191) forms 140 aa (~35%) representing the core P-loop motif. The very-high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 92.6) indicates an exceptionally well-folded enzyme with stable catalytic geometry suitable for precise substrate positioning and sulfate transfer chemistry.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places GAL3ST2 within the glycosylation/sulfation network with high-to-medium confidence interactions: strongest interaction with NEU4 (0.904, sialidase 4, glycosidase that removes sialic acid), followed by DTYMK (0.840, deoxythymidylate kinase, nucleotide metabolism), CHST5 (0.728, carbohydrate sulfotransferase 5, another sulfotransferase), NEU2 (0.681, sialidase 2, cytosolic glycosidase), FUT3 (0.668, fucosyltransferase 3, glycosyltransferase), SULT1C3 (0.588, sulfotransferase family 1C member 3, cytosolic sulfotransferase), SULT1C4 (0.581, sulfotransferase family 1C member 4), SULT1B1 (0.580, sulfotransferase family 1B member 1, thyroid hormone sulfotransferase), and TFF3 (0.414, trefoil factor 3, secreted mucosal protein). The NEU4 interaction (0.904) is extremely strong, linking GAL3ST2 to sialidase-mediated glycan remodeling—sialidases remove sialic acid caps to expose galactose residues for sulfation.
