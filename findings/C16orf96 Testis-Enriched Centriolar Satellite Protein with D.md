## Description
C16orf96 encodes a large uncharacterized protein (1141 amino acids, UniProt A6NNT2) with testis-enriched expression (9.7 nTPM) and centriolar satellite localization. The protein contains a conserved Domain of Unknown Function DUF4795 (IPR032013/PF16043, residues 767-940) within a larger PTHR47080 family scaffold. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence structure (pLDDT 55.5), suggesting a partially ordered protein with intrinsically disordered regions typical of scaffold proteins. STRING interactions reveal associations with zinc finger proteins (ZNF142, score 0.634; ZFP69B, 0.462), endogenous retrovirus elements (EBLN1, 0.505), and other uncharacterized ORF proteins (C2orf16, C3orf20). ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses at 16p13.3, indicating clinical significance though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Mouse homolog (E9QMW4, 1145 aa) shows strong evolutionary conservation across mammals. The combination of testis-specific expression, centriolar satellite localization, and zinc finger protein interactions suggests C16orf96 functions as a scaffolding regulator in germ cell centrosome biology, potentially coordinating ciliogenesis or meiotic spindle assembly during spermatogenesis.
