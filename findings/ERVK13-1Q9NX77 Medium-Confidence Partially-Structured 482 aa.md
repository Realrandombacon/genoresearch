## Description
ERVK13-1 (Endogenous retrovirus group K member 13-1, also known as HERV-K13-1) is a human endogenous retrovirus (HERV) envelope protein located on chromosome 16p13.3 (NC_000016.10: 2673438-2658388), encoding a 482 amino acid Env polyprotein (UniProt Q9NX77) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 61.6), characteristic of viral envelope glycoproteins that function through membrane fusion, receptor binding, and structural roles rather than enzymatic activity. ERVK13-1 represents a remnant of ancient HERV-K retroviral integration events in the human genome, part of the HERV-K (HML-2) family that integrated during primate evolution and may retain functional properties derived from ancestral viral envelope proteins.
