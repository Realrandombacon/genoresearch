# SYNJ2BP: Mitochondrial PDZ Domain Scaffold Protein Regulating MAM Formation, Mitochondrial Fission, and Lipid Homeostasis via SYNJ2-COX16-DRP1 Complex Assembly

## Description
SYNJ2BP (Synaptojanin-2-Binding Protein, UniProt P57105) is a 145 aa mitochondrial outer membrane scaffold protein belonging to the synaptic scaffolding LAP/MAGUK family that functions in mitochondria-associated endoplasmic reticulum membrane (MAM) formation, mitochondrial dynamics, and lipid homeostasis. The molecular architecture consists of a single PDZ domain (IPR001478/PF00595, positions 13-100; cd06709 SYNJ2BP PDZ, positions 12-97) within the PDZ superfamily (IPR036034, positions 1-112; SSF50156, positions 10-103) that mediates protein-protein interactions at mitochondrial contact sites. Literature (46 papers) establishes SYNJ2BP roles in: (1) SYNJ2BP ameliorating intervertebral disc degeneration by facilitating MAM formation and mitochondrial Zn2+ homeostasis in nucleus pulposus cells (2023, 5 citations), (2) hepatic SYNJ2BP expression regulating wrappER-mitochondria contact extent to maintain lipid homeostasis and VLDL synthesis in mouse liver (2022, 18 citations), (3) SUMOylation of SYNJ2BP-COX16 fusion promoting breast cancer progression through DRP1-mediated mitochondrial fission (2022, 23 citations), (4) SYNJ2BP-COX16 readthrough fusion protein functioning as mitochondrial complex IV assembly factor (2018). HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and mitochondrial localization, consistent with housekeeping role in mitochondrial dynamics and ER-mitochondria communication. STRING interactions reveal mitochondrial scaffold network: RRBP1 (0.989) - ribosome-binding protein 1 at ER-mitochondria interface; SYNJ2 (0.921) - synaptojanin-2 phosphoinositide phosphatase (original binding partner); SYNJ2BP-COX16 (0.801) - readthrough fusion with cytochrome c oxidase assembly protein; RALA (0.782) - Ras-related GTPase regulating mitochondrial fission; PEX26 (0.702) - peroxin-26 for peroxisome biogenesis at MAMs; AMPH (0.672) - amphiphysin involved in membrane curvature; SYNJ1 (0.671) - synaptojanin-1 phosphatase; ACVR2A (0.416) - activin receptor. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants consisting of copy number gains and losses at 14q11.2-32.33 encompassing SYNJ2BP locus, though specific single-nucleotide variant disease associations remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 78.8) indicating well-folded PDZ domain with stable β-sandwich core and α-helical extensions, typical of functional PDZ scaffolds. Conservation: mouse Synj2bp (Q9D6K5) is 145 aa, rat (Q9WVJ4) is 145 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: SYNJ2BP functions as a PDZ domain-mediated scaffold that anchors the mitochondrial outer membrane to ER contact sites (MAMs) by binding SYNJ2 phosphoinositide phosphatase and recruiting DRP1 fission machinery. The PDZ domain recognizes C-terminal motifs of partner proteins including SYNJ2, COX16, and potentially other mitochondrial outer membrane proteins. At MAMs, SYNJ2BP facilitates calcium transfer, lipid exchange, and mitochondrial fission by organizing protein complexes that include DRP1, RALA GTPase, and peroxisomal proteins (PEX26). The SYNJ2BP-COX16 readthrough fusion additionally functions in Complex IV assembly, linking mitochondrial respiration to fission dynamics. SUMOylation regulates SYNJ2BP activity and oncogenic potential in breast cancer. In liver, SYNJ2BP controls wrappER-mitochondria contact extent, regulating VLDL synthesis and lipid metabolism. In intervertebral disc degeneration, SYNJ2BP maintains mitochondrial Zn2+ homeostasis through MAM formation, preventing NP cell dysfunction. Pathogenic CNVs at 14q disrupt SYNJ2BP dosage, potentially contributing to mitochondrial diseases and metabolic disorders. SYNJ2BP represents an understudied mitochondrial scaffold linking ER-mitochondria communication, lipid metabolism, and cancer progression.
