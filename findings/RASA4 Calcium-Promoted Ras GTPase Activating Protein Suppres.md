## Description
RASA4 (RAS p21 Protein Activator 4, also known as CAPRI or Ca2+-Promoted Ras Inactivator, O43374) encodes an 803 aa calcium-sensitive Ras GTPase-activating protein functioning as a tumor suppressor that terminates Ras-MAPK signaling through GTP hydrolysis acceleration while integrating calcium signaling via membrane recruitment mechanisms. Structural architecture reveals dual N-terminal C2 domains (IPR000008/G3DSA:2.60.40.150/cd04054/cd04025, residues 1-105/116-234/6-126/134-257) mediating Ca2+-dependent phospholipid binding for plasma membrane translocation, central Ras-GAP catalytic domain (IPR001936/G3DSA:1.10.506.10/cd05395, residues 244-605/265-550/321-557) containing arginine finger motif for Ras-GTP hydrolysis stimulation, C-terminal pleckstrin homology PH domain (IPR001849/G3DSA:2.30.29.30/cd13372, residues 566-675/542-681/562-713) binding phosphatidylinositol lipids for membrane anchoring, and Btk-type zinc finger (IPR001562, residues 675-711) stabilizing PH domain conformation; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 85.8) confirms high confidence well-folded multi-domain structure enabling coordinated calcium sensing and Ras inactivation. STRING interactions define Ras regulatory network: guanine nucleotide exchange factor RASGRP1 (0.827) as functional antagonist activating Ras, canonical Ras family members HRAS (0.779), NRAS (0.773), MRAS (0.711), KRAS (0.497), RRAS (0.492), RRAS2 (0.416) as substrates for GAP activity, paralog RASA1 (0.660) sharing Ras-inactivating function, scaffolding protein SCAF11 (0.634) linking to MAPK cascade organization, and Rab GDP dissociation stimulator RABIF (0.625) coordinating parallel small GTPase pathways. HPA localizes RASA4 to vesicles and cell junctions as intracellular GTPase activator with group-enriched expression in skeletal muscle (223.0 nTPM) and tongue (76.1 nTPM), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity reflecting broad regulatory role. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic variants including extensive 7q22.1-36.3/7q22.1-31.31/7q22.1 copy number losses (chr7:98693388-159119707, chr7:100676872-119156160, chr7:99417471-111586308, chr7:99593346-102470275 deletions) reducing gene dosage, 7p22.3-q36.3/7q22.1-36.3 copy number gains (chr7:10365-159119707, chr7:43361-159119707, chr7:98847725-102472176 triplications) altering regulation, and TMEM106B-BRAF fusion deletion disrupting genomic context. Literature (28 papers) demonstrates tumor suppressor mechanisms: integrated methylome analysis identifies epigenetically silenced RASA4 in small cell lung cancer driving chemo-resistance (0x citations, 2025), Treponema denticola-induced RASA4 upregulation mediates cytoskeletal dysfunction and MMP-2 activity in periodontal fibroblasts (17x citations, 2021), RASA4 inhibits HIFα signaling pathway suppressing cervical squamous cell carcinoma proliferation (8x citations, 2021), TRPC3 calcium channel regulates triple-negative breast cancer proliferation/apoptosis resistance through TRPC3/RASA4/MAPK pathway axis (55x citations, 2019). Mechanistic hypothesis: RASA4 functions as calcium-regulated molecular switch terminating oncogenic Ras signaling; resting state maintains cytosolic localization via autoinhibitory PH domain-C2 domain interactions; upon calcium influx through TRPC3 channels or store-operated calcium entry, dual C2 domains bind phosphatidylserine at inner leaflet plasma membrane recruiting RASA4 to proximity with membrane-anchored Ras-GTP; Ras-GAP domain inserts arginine finger into Ras active site stabilizing transition state accelerating intrinsic GTP hydrolysis 100-1000 fold converting Ras-GTP to inactive Ras-GDP terminating MAPK cascade (RAF-MEK-ERK); in cancer, promoter hypermethylation silences RASA4 expression enabling sustained Ras-MAPK signaling driving proliferation/chemo-resistance in SCLC; HIFα inhibition occurs via reduced ERK-mediated HIF1α stabilization under normoxia; periodontal pathogen T. denticola exploits RASA4 upregulation altering cytoskeletal dynamics through Rac1/Cdc42 crosstalk enhancing MMP-2 secretion for tissue invasion; skeletal muscle enrichment (223.0 nTPM) suggests specialized roles in excitation-contraction coupling where rapid Ras signal termination prevents aberrant hypertrophy; 7q CNVs alter RASA4 dosage disrupting Ras homeostasis potentially contributing to developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility.
