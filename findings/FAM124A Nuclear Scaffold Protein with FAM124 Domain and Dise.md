## Description
FAM124A encodes a 546 amino acid nuclear protein (UniProt Q86V42) containing a conserved FAM124 domain (IPR046365/PF15067, residues 47-278) that spans ~42% of the protein sequence. The AlphaFold structure predicts medium confidence folding (pLDDT 62.2), suggesting a partially structured scaffold with potential intrinsically disordered regions flanking the core domain. HPA data reveals predominant expression in neural tissues: brain (28.1 nTPM), retina (23.8 nTPM), and choroid plexus (21.4 nTPM), with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-confidence partners including FAM167B (0.567), WDR49 (0.513), and SERPINE3 (0.499), suggesting roles in multiprotein complexes. ClinVar documents 60 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants spanning 13q14.3, implicating FAM124A dosage sensitivity in disease. The FAM124 domain family (IPR029380) is vertebrate-specific, with no clear yeast ortholog, suggesting evolution of novel regulatory functions. Neural enrichment combined with nuclear localization supports a hypothesis that FAM124A functions as a chromatin-associated scaffold protein, potentially recruiting regulatory complexes to specific genomic loci. Copy number gains/losses may disrupt stoichiometric balance of these complexes, leading to transcriptional dysregulation in neural tissues. Medium-confidence AlphaFold structure and lack of enzymatic domains further support a scaffolding/regulatory rather than catalytic role.
