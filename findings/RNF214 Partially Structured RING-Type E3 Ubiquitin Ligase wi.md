## Description

Additionally, RNF214 contains two TTC3/DZIP3-like helical domains: N-terminal helical domain (IPR056872/PF24525, aa 228-442) and C-terminal helical domain (IPR056870/PF24905, aa 555-609) suggesting structural and functional relationship to TTC3 (tetratricopeptide repeat protein 3) and DZIP3 (DAZ interacting protein 3) family members involved in protein-protein interactions and RNA binding. GO annotations from PANTHER classify this within E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase/RNA-binding family (PTHR15727, aa 19-703).
