## Description
MRPS11 encodes a 194-amino acid small ribosomal subunit protein uS11m (UniProt P82912), a structural component of the mitochondrial 28S small ribosomal subunit essential for mitochondrial protein synthesis. The protein contains the conserved ribosomal protein S11 domain (IPR001971, PF00411, positions 85-192) with a signature motif (IPR018102, PS00054, positions 162-184) critical for rRNA binding, belonging to the uS11 superfamily (IPR036967, positions 59-194) within translational machinery components (SSF53137, positions 80-194). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 82.9, residues 1-194) indicating stable folding consistent with ribosomal protein architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals MRPS11 forms part of the mitochondrial ribosome with ultra-high confidence interactions: MRPS5 (0.999), DAP3 (0.999), MRPS2 (0.999), MRPS10 (0.998), MRPS7 (0.996), MRPS9 (0.996), MRPS18C (0.995), MRPS35 (0.994), MRPS25 (0.981), and large subunit component MRPL17 (0.975), confirming its central role in mitoribosome assembly and function. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with exclusive mitochondrial localization, annotated as ribosomal protein with ribonucleoprotein molecular function, consistent with housekeeping role in oxidative phosphorylation. ClinVar contains 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.412-1G>A, c.282-17T>G) and copy number variations at 15q24-26 (gains and losses), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 23 publications, MRPS11 is partially characterized: recent studies identify MRPS11 in shared gene signatures between polycystic ovary syndrome and endometrial cancer (2022, 21 cit), MRPS proteins indicating putative role in tumorigenic cellular processes (2021, 14 cit), co-expression networks in ankylosing spondylitis arthritis (2021, 13 cit), and feed efficiency regulation in pigs (2020, 10 cit). The functional hypothesis: MRPS11 binds mitochondrial 12S rRNA within the small subunit decoding center, stabilizing mRNA-tRNA interactions during mitochondrial translation; disease variants disrupt mitoribosome assembly or function, causing defective synthesis of 13 mitochondrially-encoded OXPHOS subunits, leading to respiratory chain deficiency and multi-system mitochondrial disease phenotypes; cancer associations may reflect altered mitochondrial translation supporting tumor metabolic reprogramming.
