## Description
LOC114004388 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 9303 located on chromosome 5q31 (genomic coordinates: 141734881-141735175 on NC_000005.10). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element (silencer) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 3 copy number gains (triplifications) and 2 copy number losses (deletions), indicating balanced dosage sensitivity where both under-dosage and over-dosage disrupt cellular homeostasis. The moderate variant count (5 vs 21 for LOC114004412, 2 for LOC121725039) suggests intermediate clinical penetrance. The 5q31 chromosomal region is gene-dense and contains the Th2 cytokine cluster (IL5, IL4, IL13 at 5q31.1, critical for allergic/immune responses), CDH1 (E-cadherin tumor suppressor at 5q31.1), APC (adenomatous polyposis coli at 5q22.2), and MEF2C (transcription factor at 5q14.3, haploinsufficiency causes intellectual disability). As a repressive enhancer/silencer, this element likely suppresses expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 5q domain. The balanced pathogenicity pattern (3 gains, 2 losses) suggests this silencer regulates genes requiring precise expression levels - both haploinsufficiency and overexpression cause disease. This element may contribute to 5q deletion syndromes (5q- syndrome in myelodysplastic disorders), cancer predisposition, or immune dysregulation through dysregulation of critical 5q genes requiring tight transcriptional repression.
