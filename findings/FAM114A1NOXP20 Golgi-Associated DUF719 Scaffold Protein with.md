## Description
FAM114A1 (alias NOXP20, family with sequence similarity 114 member A1) encodes a 563 amino acid partially structured nucleoplasmic/Golgi/cytosolic protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 63.6) containing a domain of unknown function DUF719 (IPR007998/PF05334, aa 1-560/126-299) and belonging to the PTHR12842 family (aa 1-560). HPA shows ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all) with nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and cytosol localization, low tissue specificity, group-enriched blood cell expression, and cell type-enhanced single cell expression pattern. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 4p16.3-11/4p16.3-q13.1 copy number gains associated with chromosomal duplication syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with Rab GTPases and membrane trafficking regulators: RAB2A (Ras-related protein Rab-2A, ER-to-Golgi transport, 0.640), RAB2B (Ras-related protein Rab-2B, vesicular trafficking, 0.530), UNC119B (unc-119 lipid chaperone B, ciliary protein trafficking, 0.436), and POSTN (periostin, extracellular matrix organization, 0.425). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q9D281 569 aa) indicates functional constraint. The DUF719 domain architecture combined with Golgi apparatus localization and RAB2A/RAB2B interactions strongly suggests FAM114A1 functions as a Golgi-associated scaffold protein involved in ER-to-Golgi vesicular trafficking and membrane protein sorting. Rab GTPases are master regulators of vesicle budding, transport, tethering, and fusion. RAB2A/RAB2B specifically regulate COPII vesicle formation and ER-to-Golgi anterograde transport. The Golgi localization places FAM114A1 at the critical interface between ER export and Golgi processing. UNC119B interaction suggests potential role in ciliary protein trafficking, linking FAM114A1 to ciliogenesis and ciliary membrane protein sorting. POSTN interaction indicates potential coupling to extracellular matrix remodeling through secretory pathway regulation. The nucleoplasmic localization suggests additional nuclear functions, potentially coordinating transcription with secretory pathway capacity. Blood cell-enriched expression indicates specialized function in hematopoietic cells, potentially regulating cytokine secretion, immune receptor trafficking, or platelet granule biogenesis. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.6) indicates partial disorder with structured DUF719 core, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly. Pathogenic 4p CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting ER-to-Golgi trafficking, secretory pathway function, and ciliary protein sorting, potentially contributing to 4p duplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental defects, immune dysfunction, and ciliopathy-like features through impaired vesicular transport and protein secretion.
