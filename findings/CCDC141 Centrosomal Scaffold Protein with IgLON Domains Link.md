## Description
CCDC141 (coiled-coil domain containing 141, Gene ID: 285025, UniProt Q6ZP82) is a large 1530 amino acid scaffold protein located on chromosome 2q31.2 with predicted roles in brain development and centrosome localization. DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals multiple structural domains including spectrin repeats (PF00435, IPR002017, positions 52-127) associated with protein binding (GO:0005515), and a C-terminal Immunoglobulin I-set domain (PF07679, IPR013098, positions 1409-1498). The protein belongs to the IgLON domain-containing protein family (IPR50876), suggesting involvement in cell adhesion or neuronal recognition. STRUCTURAL CONFIDENCE: AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with global pLDDT score of 71.8 (confident range), modeling all 1530 residues. INTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies 10 high-confidence interaction partners, most notably DISC1 (score 0.897) - a schizophrenia-associated protein critical for neurodevelopment, and TTN/titin (score 0.795) - the giant sarcomeric protein. Additional partners include SLC35F1 (0.579), KLHL38 (0.504), SYT10 (0.476), CCSER2 (0.460), KIAA1755 (0.450), IGSF10 (0.450), PROKR2 (0.447), and FEZF1 (0.447). TISSUE EXPRESSION: Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched RNA expression with highest levels in heart muscle (69.9 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, immune cell enhancement in blood, and nucleoplasmic subcellular localization. CLINICAL VARIANTS: ClinVar contains 45 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Tyr102Cys, p.Leu612Val, p.Glu107Lys, p.Arg468Gln, p.Lys377Asn), truncating variants (p.Tyr1479Ter, p.Tyr466fs, p.Leu1107fs), and large chromosomal deletions. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CCDC141 functions as a centrosomal scaffold protein that bridges neurodevelopmental and cardiac processes through its DISC1 interaction. The spectrin repeats provide flexible structural scaffolding while the C-terminal IgLON domain mediates protein-protein interactions at the centrosome. High cardiac expression combined with DISC1 binding suggests dual roles in cardiomyocyte organization and neuronal migration during cortical development. Pathogenic variants likely disrupt centrosome positioning during radial glial cell migration, contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders.
