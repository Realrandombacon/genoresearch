## Description
NDST4 encodes a 872 aa bifunctional heparan sulfate N-deacetylase/N-sulfotransferase 4 (UniProt Q9H3R1), functioning as a Golgi-resident enzyme that catalyzes both N-deacetylation and N-sulfation of glucosamine residues in heparan sulfate biosynthesis, regulating extracellular matrix composition, growth factor signaling, and tumor suppression through heparan sulfate proteoglycan modification. The protein contains an N-terminal heparan sulfate N-deacetylase domain (IPR021930/PF12062, aa301-505) with hydrolase activity (GO:0016787) for heparan sulfate N-sulfotransferase activity (GO:0015016), a C-terminal sulfotransferase domain (IPR000863/PF00685, aa595-844) with sulfotransferase activity (GO:0008146), an N-terminal domain (IPR056793/PF25119, aa76-291) of unknown function, and belongs to the heparan sulfate sulfotransferase family (IPR037359/PTHR10605, aa121-857) with P-loop NTP hydrolase superfamily structure (IPR027417/G3DSA:3.40.50.300/SSF52540, aa569-872). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.1), indicating an exceptionally well-folded stable bifunctional enzyme structure. HPA shows no detected expression in bulk tissues, classified as a predicted intracellular metabolic enzyme with group-enriched cell type specificity, suggesting restricted expression to specific cell populations. STRING interactions reveal NDST4 forms heparan sulfate biosynthesis networks: HS2ST1 (0.691, heparan sulfate 2-O-sulfotransferase 1), GPC5 (0.656, glypican-5), GPC3 (0.639, glypican-3), GPC2 (0.636, glypican-2), HS6ST3 (0.635, heparan sulfate 6-O-sulfotransferase 3), GPC4 (0.631, glypican-4), GPC6 (0.612, glypican-6), SDC2 (0.594, syndecan-2), GLCE (0.585, glucuronic acid C5-epimerase), GPC1 (0.462, glypican-1). ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 4q24-35.2/4p12-q35.2/4q24-31.21/4q12-31.21/4q22.1-35.2/4q25-35.2/4p16.3-q35.2 copy number gains, 4q26-28.3 copy number loss, and large chromosomal rearrangements. Literature (33 papers) demonstrates NDST4 is a novel candidate tumor suppressor gene at 4q26 with genetic loss predicting adverse prognosis in colorectal cancer; NDST4 loci associated with circulating resistin levels in GWAS; NDST4 deficiency enhances acute colitis and colitis-associated tumorigenesis in mice; NDST4 is the fourth member of vertebrate heparan sulfate/heparin GlcNAc N-deacetylase/GlcN N-sulfotransferase family with bifunctional enzymatic activity. NDST4 functions as a bifunctional Golgi enzyme—its N-deacetylase domain removes acetyl groups from N-acetylglucosamine residues while the sulfotransferase domain adds sulfate groups to generate N-sulfoglucosamine, creating sulfation patterns essential for heparan sulfate biological activity. The medium-confidence HS2ST1/HS6ST3 interactions (0.691/0.635) indicate NDST4 operates within a coordinated sulfotransferase network where different enzymes create distinct sulfation patterns (2-O, 6-O, N-sulfation) determining heparan sulfate binding specificity for growth factors (FGF, VEGF, Wnt), chemokines, and morphogens. The GPC1-6 interactions (0.656-0.462) link NDST4 to glypican core protein modification—heparan sulfate chains on glypicans regulate morphogen gradient formation and signaling. SDC2 interaction (0.594) connects NDST4 to syndecan proteoglycan biosynthesis affecting cell adhesion and migration. GLCE interaction (0.585) links NDST4 to glucuronic acid epimerization completing heparan sulfate modification. Tumor suppression reflects NDST4 loss altering heparan sulfate sulfation patterns, disrupting growth factor sequestration and enhancing oncogenic signaling in colorectal cancer. Colitis enhancement upon NDST4 deficiency suggests heparan sulfate modifications regulate intestinal barrier function and immune cell trafficking. Copy number variants on 4q may cause NDST4 haploinsufficiency contributing to cancer susceptibility or inflammatory disorders. The very high confidence structure (pLDDT 91.1) and restricted expression pattern enable NDST4 to function as a stable bifunctional enzyme coordinating heparan sulfate diversity in specific cell types.
