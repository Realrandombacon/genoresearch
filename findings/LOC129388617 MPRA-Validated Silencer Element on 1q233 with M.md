# LOC129388617: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 1q23.3 with Moderate Balanced CNV Burden (4 Del:3 Dup)

## Description
LOC129388617 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 1q23.3 (GRCh38: 165715017-165715217), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a moderate balanced burden with both deletion and duplication events (4 deletions:3 duplications ratio). Deletions span 1q23.3-25.1 (~12-14Mb regions) while duplications span 1q21.2-25.2 (~30Mb regions). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 1q21.2-25 deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, thrombocytopenia, and immune dysfunction. The 1q23-25 region is gene-dense, containing critical dosage-sensitive genes such as F5 (Coagulation Factor V, mutations cause thrombophilia), FCGR cluster (Fc Gamma Receptors, immune function), BCL9 (B-Cell Lymphoma 9, Wnt signaling), and PITX1 (Paired-Like Homeodermic Transcription Factor 1, limb development).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129388617 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 1q23-25 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental and immune genes. Candidate targets include PITX1 (hindlimb development, clubfoot susceptibility) or FCGR cluster genes (immune complex clearance). The silencer likely recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The moderate balanced CNV burden (4:3 ratio) suggests both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity intolerance where both silencer loss and gain cause derepression or hyper-repression of developmental/immune genes. The K562 validation suggests potential roles in hematopoietic/immune lineage specification, consistent with 1q23-25 enrichment for Fc receptors and coagulation factors. Deletions may cause PITX1 or FCGR derepression leading to limb/immune phenotypes while duplications may cause excessive silencing, contributing to 1q21.2-25 syndrome with thrombocytopenia and developmental delay.
