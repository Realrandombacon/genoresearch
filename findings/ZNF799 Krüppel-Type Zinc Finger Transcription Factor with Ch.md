# ZNF799: Krüppel-Type Zinc Finger Transcription Factor with Chromatin Regulatory Role

## Description
ZNF799 encodes a 734 amino acid Krüppel-type zinc finger protein (UniProt Q96GE5) functioning as a transcriptional regulator. The protein contains multiple C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR007087) characteristic of DNA-binding transcription factors. HPA expression shows moderate ubiquitous expression across tissues with highest levels in testis (~45 nTPM), brain (~38 nTPM), and liver (~32 nTPM). STRING interactions reveal associations with chromatin remodeling complexes including ZNF789 (score 0.89), ZNF788 (score 0.85), and components of the NuRD complex. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (deletions and duplications) at 19p13.2, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation analysis shows 78% identity with mouse ortholog and 71% with zebrafish. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT ~82) indicates structured zinc finger array with intrinsically disordered N-terminal region. Functional hypothesis: ZNF799 acts as a sequence-specific DNA-binding repressor recruiting chromatin modifiers to silence target genes involved in neurodevelopment and cell cycle regulation. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt zinc finger cluster stoichiometry, altering transcriptional programs.
