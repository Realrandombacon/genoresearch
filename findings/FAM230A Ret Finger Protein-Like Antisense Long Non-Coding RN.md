## Description
FAM230A (UniProt K4HVA1) encodes a 316 amino acid transcript (chromosome 22q11.21) annotated as an antisense long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) overlapping the RFPL3 (Ret Finger Protein-Like 3) locus. InterPro analysis identifies it specifically as the "Ret finger protein-like 3 antisense" region (ANF00059, residues 1-123), confirming its regulatory role as a natural antisense transcript rather than a canonical protein-coding enzyme. STRING interaction data is unavailable, consistent with its classification as a non-coding RNA that likely functions through RNA-RNA or RNA-DNA interactions rather than stable protein complexes. HPA expression data is currently absent, suggesting low abundance or tissue-specific restriction. ClinVar contains 320 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning 22q11.21 (e.g., chr22:18161474-21110475 duplications and chr22:18920000-21460000 deletions), indicating extreme dosage sensitivity. This locus overlaps the critical DiGeorge/Velocardiofacial syndrome region; FAM230A haploinsufficiency or overexpression likely contributes to the neurodevelopmental and cardiac phenotypes of 22q11.21 deletion/duplication syndromes by dysregulating the sense strand RFPL3 or neighboring genes via cis-acting epigenetic silencing or transcriptional interference. AlphaFold structure prediction yields low confidence (pLDDT 50.7), supporting the hypothesis that the transcript functions primarily as an unstructured RNA scaffold or that the translated micropeptide (if any) is intrinsically disordered. Conservation data shows primate-specific emergence, aligning with the rapid evolution of regulatory lncRNAs. Mechanistically, FAM230A likely acts as a cis-regulatory element modulating chromatin accessibility or mRNA stability of RFPL3, a gene implicated in ubiquitin-mediated proteolysis and neuronal differentiation; disruption of this balance in 22q11.21 CNVs leads to developmental defects.
