# CCDC166: Testis-Enriched Coiled-Coil Protein with DUF4515 Domain Linked to 8q Copy Number Disorders

**Date:** 2026-03-23T02:55:49.501825

**Quality Score:** 4.72/10  (E=4.65, D=4.8) [MODERATE]

## Description
CCDC166 (coiled-coil domain containing 166) encodes a 439-amino acid intracellular protein containing a DUF4515 domain of unknown function (IPR032777, PF14988, residues 72-259) and is classified within the basal body-orientation factor 1 family (PTHR14845, residues 2-304). HPA data reveals testis-enriched expression (3.9 nTPM) with tissue-specific distribution, detected only in testis and not in cancer tissues or immune cells. STRING analysis identifies 5 medium-confidence interaction partners including KIAA1211L (0.507), BTBD11 (0.447), MEIG1 (0.447), FOXI2 (0.414), and ZNF704 (0.410). MEIG1 (meiosis-expressed gene 1) is a known spermatogenesis factor, suggesting CCDC166 functions in germ cell development. ClinVar documents 61 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 8q24.3 copy number gains and losses, indicating dosage sensitivity. The testis-specific expression pattern combined with MEIG1 interaction and basal body-orientation factor classification suggests CCDC166 functions as a coiled-coil scaffold protein involved in spermatid differentiation and flagellar assembly. The DUF4515 domain likely mediates protein-protein interactions within the manchette or acrosomal structures during spermiogenesis. Chromosome 8q amplifications commonly occur in cancers, and CCDC166 dosage alterations may contribute to oncogenic phenotypes through dysregulation of cell division machinery.
