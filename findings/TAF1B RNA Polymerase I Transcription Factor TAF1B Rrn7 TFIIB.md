## Description
TAF1B (TATA-Box Binding Protein Associated Factor RNA Polymerase I Subunit B; UniProt Q53T94) is a 588 amino acid nucleolar transcription factor with 51 publications, representing a moderately-characterized essential component of the RNA Polymerase I (Pol I) pre-initiation complex. Unlike general transcription factors, TAF1B (also known as Rrn7 in yeast) serves as the structural bridge between the Pol I-specific Selectivity Factor 1 (SL1/TIF-IB) and the polymerase itself, driving ribosomal RNA (rRNA) synthesis and linking ribosome biogenesis to cell growth, stress response, and cancer progression through its unique TFIIB-like architecture.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals TAF1B contains a distinctive multi-domain organization mimicking the general transcription factor TFIIB but adapted for Pol I specificity: (1) N-terminal Zinc-finger domain (IPR021752/PF11781, residues 7-39, ~33 aa) — C2H2-type zinc finger potentially mediating DNA contacts or protein-protein interactions within SL1; (2) N-terminal Cyclin domain (IPR048540/PF20644, residues 204-253, ~50 aa) — cyclin-fold module likely involved in regulatory partner binding; (3) C-terminal Cyclin domain (IPR048538/PF20645, residues 270-460, ~190 aa) — second cyclin-fold completing the core scaffold; (4) Full-length family (IPR033599/PTHR31576, residues 5-583) — defines the Rrn7/TAF1B family across eukaryotes. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 78.7), indicating a well-folded protein with stable cyclin domains connected by structured linkers, consistent with a rigid scaffold role positioning Pol I at the rDNA promoter. The dual cyclin fold resembles the core of TFIIB but with distinct surface features enabling Pol I specificity.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals ultra-high confidence interactions exclusively with the Pol I transcription machinery: TAF1A (0.999—highest; TATA-Box Binding Protein Associated Factor 1A, core SL1 subunit), TAF1C (0.999; TAF1C, core SL1 subunit), TAF1D (0.999; TAF1D, core SL1 subunit), UBTF (0.888; Upstream Binding Transcription Factor/UBF, rDNA architectural protein bending promoter), NOC2L (0.825; Nucleolar Complex Associated 2 Homolog, repressor of Pol I/II), CD3EAP (0.822; CD3e Antigen Associated Protein, nucleolar protein linked to cancer), THAP7 (0.808; THAP Domain Containing 7, transcriptional repressor), ASTE1 (0.806; Astrotactin 1, uncharacterized nuclear protein), RRN3 (0.792; RNA Polymerase I Specific Transcription Initiation Factor, Pol I recruitment factor), and TBP (0.718; TATA-Binding Protein, general factor).
