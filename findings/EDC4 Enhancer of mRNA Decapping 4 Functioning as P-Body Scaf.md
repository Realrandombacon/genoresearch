## Description
EDC4 (Enhancer of mRNA decapping protein 4, also known as Ge-1 or VCS in plants, Q6P2E9) encodes a 1401 aa scaffold protein functioning as an essential component of cytoplasmic processing bodies (P-bodies), membrane-less organelles mediating mRNA decay, storage, and translational repression through enhancement of the decapping complex activity. Structural architecture reveals a partially organized multi-domain organization with medium AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 62.1): WD40 repeat domains (IPR001680, residues 165-205/286-326/333-384) forming beta-propeller structures for protein-protein interactions with GO term for protein binding (GO:0005515), WD40/YVTN repeat-like-containing domain superfamily (IPR015943, residues 108-485) establishing the N-terminal interaction platform, EDC4-specific WD40 repeat region (IPR032401, residues 114-441) defining the conserved decapping enhancer fold, WD40-repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR036322, residues 158-437) stabilizing the propeller architecture, EDC4 conserved C-terminal domain superfamily (IPR044938, residues 1313-1396) forming a distinct interaction module potentially for P-body condensation, EDC4-like family domain (IPR045152, residues 8-1397) spanning nearly the entire protein with GO term for deadenylation-independent decapping of nuclear-transcribed mRNA (GO:0031087), and multiple CATH superfamily domains (G3DSA:1.10.220.100 at 1313-1396, G3DSA:2.130.10.10 at 108-485, G3DSA:6.10.140.270 at 1262-1307) defining the complex topology required for multi-protein complex assembly. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence mRNA decay machinery network: DEAD-box helicase 6 DDX6 (0.999, highest) as central P-body helicase unwinding mRNA secondary structures for decapping access, mRNA decapping complex subunit 1A DCP1A (0.999) as catalytic activator of decapping enzyme, EDC3 (0.999) as paralogous scaffold protein forming heteromeric P-body core, mRNA decapping enzyme 2 DCP2 (0.999) as catalytic subunit hydrolyzing m7GpppN cap structure, LSM14A (0.975) as linker protein connecting decapping to deadenylation machinery, UPF1 (0.942) as nonsense-mediated decay factor linking P-bodies to quality control pathways, 5-3 exoribonuclease 1 XRN1 (0.870) as downstream exonuclease degrading decapped mRNA bodies, LSM1 homolog (0.787) as component of heptameric ring recognizing 3 poly(A) tails, DCP1B (0.774) as paralogous decapping subunit, and PATL1 (0.502) as P-body component linking to miRNA-mediated silencing. HPA localizes EDC4 to nucleoplasm and cytoplasmic bodies (P-bodies) with low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues indicating ubiquitous housekeeping function in post-transcriptional regulation, classified as essential protein/predicted intracellular protein with biological processes in mRNA metabolic processes; low cancer specificity suggests broad tumor suppressor role potentially through p53 pathway modulation, low immune cell specificity indicates constitutive expression across lineages, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity reflects variable P-body abundance correlating with translational activity states. ClinVar documents 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutation c.451G>A (p.Ala151Thr) within WD40 repeat region potentially disrupting DDX6/DCP1A binding, intronic variant c.2826+46C>T potentially affecting splicing, and large-scale chromosome 16 copy number alterations including deletions at 16q22.1 (chr16:67538639-69583342x1, chr16:67322830-69368947x1, chr16:65669673-70180183x1) and duplications/gains at 16q11.2-24.3 (chr16:46432879-90294753x3, chr16:46503968-90155062x3), 16q21-24.1 (chr16:62746020-84485022x3), 16q21-24.3 (chr16:61524229-90155062x3), and NC_000016.9:g.(?_48799549)_(70756330_?)dup encompassing the EDC4 locus at 16q22.1; these CNVs are associated with neurodevelopmental disorders and cancer predisposition though no specific monogenic EDC4 phenotype established, potentially affecting mRNA homeostasis and stress granule dynamics through dosage sensitivity. Conservation from mouse Q3UJB9 (1406 aa, 100% length identity), rat Q3ZAV8 (1407 aa, 100% identity), Drosophila Q9VKK1/Ge-1 (1354 aa, strong conservation), and Arabidopsis Q9LTT8/VCS (1344 aa, plant ortholog) confirms essential mRNA decay function across eukaryotes with particularly high sequence identity in WD40 repeat clusters and C-terminal domain; ubiquitous expression correlates with fundamental post-transcriptional control role; exceptional STRING interactions (all >0.999 for core complex members) position EDC4 as integral non-redundant scaffold bridging DCP1A-DCP2 catalytic dimer to DDX6 helicase and EDC3 co-scaffold; moderate-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 62.1) reflects intrinsically disordered linker regions between structured WD40 domains consistent with flexible scaffold function enabling dynamic P-body assembly/disassembly; 27 ClinVar variants including truncating CNVs underscore critical role in preventing disease; literature (76 papers) demonstrates EDC4 C-terminal domain scaffolds P-body assembly linking P-body dynamics to p53-mediated tumor suppression (5x 2025), hnRNPU/circKCNK2/EDC4/IL-11 axis aggravates osteolytic bone metastasis in renal cell carcinoma (3x 2025), EDC4 enhances multi-drug chemosensitivity in pancreatic cancer (2025), Ebola virus co-opts mRNA decapping complex through EDC4 scaffold (3x 2024), and EDC4/Ge-1 required for proper P-body formation and mRNA storage during cellular stress; mechanistic proposal involves EDC4-mediated multivalent interactions through WD40 beta-propellers recruiting DCP1A-DCP2 decapping enzyme complex to DDX6-unwound mRNA substrates within liquid-liquid phase separated P-body condensates, coordination with EDC3 for P-body nucleation and size regulation, coupling to UPF1-dependent nonsense-mediated decay for quality control, and p53 pathway integration where EDC4-dependent mRNA decay regulates p53 target transcripts affecting cell cycle arrest and apoptosis.
