## Description
PRRC2C (Proline-Rich Coiled-Coil 2C, also known as BAT2 Family Member, UniProt Q9Y520) is a 2896 aa intracellular cytosolic protein belonging to the BAT2 HLA-B-Associated Transcript 2 family (IPR033184/PTHR14038, positions 525-2896) with BAT2 N-terminal domain (IPR009738/PF07001, positions 1-160) that functions as an intrinsically disordered scaffold protein promoting translation initiation through eIF3 complex association and stress granule localization, regulating leaky scanning of upstream open reading frames (uORFs) on approximately half of animal mRNAs, with pathogenic variants and 1q21 amplifications driving hepatocellular carcinoma proliferation and metastasis.
