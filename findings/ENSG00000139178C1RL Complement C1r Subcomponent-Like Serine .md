## Description
C1RL (UniProt: Q9NZP8, aliases C1RL1/C1RLP/C1r-LP/CLSPa) encodes a 487 amino acid complement system serine protease representing a moderately studied gene with 49 publications in Semantic Scholar, though most research focuses on the long non-coding RNA C1RL-AS1 rather than the protein-coding gene itself. Located on chromosome 12p13.31, C1RL is predicted to enable serine-type endopeptidase activity and participate in zymogen activation within extracellular exosomes. InterPro domain analysis reveals canonical complement protease architecture: IPR000859/cd00041 (CUB domain, residues 39-163, GO:0005509 calcium ion binding), IPR001254 (trypsin-like serine protease domain, residues 244-484, GO:0004252 serine-type endopeptidase activity, GO:0006508 proteolysis), IPR001314 (peptidase S1A chymotrypsin family), IPR009003 (peptidase S1 PA clan), IPR033116 (serine active site, residues 430-441), G3DSA:2.10.70.10 (complement module domain 1, residues 185-236), and IPR035976 (Sushi/SCR/CCP superfamily). The CUB domain mediates protein-protein interactions and calcium-dependent binding, while the trypsin-like domain contains the catalytic triad for proteolytic activity. AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 77.1), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined tertiary structure. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners: CD163 (scavenger receptor cysteine-rich type 1 protein M130, score 0.783 - highest confidence), C1S (complement C1s subcomponent, 0.642), C1R (complement C1r subcomponent, 0.555), APRT (adenine phosphoribosyltransferase, 0.546), C8A (complement component 8 alpha chain, 0.543), C4A (complement component 4A, 0.513), WWOX (WW domain-containing oxidoreductase, 0.488), PRTN3 (proteinase 3, 0.429), HPX (hemopexin, 0.416), and LCAT (lecithin-cholesterol acyltransferase, 0.410). The strong CD163 interaction suggests C1RL functions in macrophage-mediated clearance pathways, while C1S/C1R interactions place it within the classical complement cascade. Human Protein Atlas shows liver-enriched expression (134.2 nTPM - exceptionally high, ~122x higher than BPY2's 1.1 nTPM), tissue-enhanced RNA specificity, detected in all tissues, immune cell-enhanced expression, and classification as plasma protein, enzyme, protease, and serine protease involved in complement pathway and innate immunity. UniProt function annotation specifies C1RL mediates proteolytic cleavage of HP/haptoglobin in the endoplasmic reticulum, linking it to hemoglobin scavenging and iron homeostasis. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains (duplications) and deletions spanning 12p13.33-11.1, though no specific disease names are assigned - variants include c.301-3C>G splice region variant and large chromosomal rearrangements. Recent literature (2025-2026) focuses on lncRNA C1RL-AS1 roles in influenza A virus pneumonia via miR-16-5p/LAMP3 axis, lung adenocarcinoma progression through miR-424-5p/adrenoceptor β2 axis with immune infiltration, ocular Behcet's disease susceptibility, gastric cancer malignancy via AKT/β-catenin/c-Myc pathway suppression, and glioma immunological characteristics in 2120 patients. Based on domain architecture (CUB + trypsin-like serine protease), high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 77.1), liver-specific high expression (134.2 nTPM), CD163/C1S/C1R interaction network, haptoglobin cleavage function, and complement pathway classification, C1RL functions as a complement system serine protease that cleaves haptoglobin in the endoplasmic reticulum, facilitating hemoglobin scavenging and iron recycling. The CUB domain enables calcium-dependent protein-protein interactions with complement components and scavenger receptors, while the trypsin-like catalytic domain executes proteolytic cleavage. C1RL likely operates at the intersection of innate immunity (complement activation) and metabolic homeostasis (haptoglobin-hemoglobin clearance), with CD163 interaction suggesting roles in macrophage-mediated clearance of hemoglobin-haptoglobin complexes. Liver-specific expression aligns with the organ's central role in acute-phase protein synthesis and iron metabolism. Copy number variants may disrupt complement regulation or haptoglobin processing, potentially contributing to inflammatory disorders, though specific disease associations require further characterization. C1RL exemplifies a complement protease with dual roles in immunity and metabolic homeostasis.
