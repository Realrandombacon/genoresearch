## Description
PPP2R3A encodes a 1150 aa regulatory subunit (UniProt Q06190), also known as PR130 or PR72, that serves as the calcium-sensing targeting component of the protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) heterotrimeric holoenzyme. The protein contains multiple EF-hand calcium-binding domains (IPR002048, PF13499, PF17958, PF21161, residues 731-1007) including a canonical calcium-binding site (PS00018, PS50222, residues 985-997) that confers calcium-dependent regulation of phosphatase activity. These EF-hands belong to the PP2A regulatory subunit B'' family (IPR041534, IPR048855, PTHR14095, residues 197-1113) and are organized in pairs (IPR011992, residues 729-872/884-1049) typical of calcium sensor proteins. STRING interactions place PPP2R3A at the core of the PP2A holoenzyme assembly with ultra-high confidence partnerships: PPP2CA (0.996), the catalytic C subunit; PPP2R1A (0.993) and PPP2R1B (0.939), the structural A scaffolding subunits; PPP2R5C (0.955), PPP2R2A (0.951), PPP2R2D (0.922), and PPP2R5A (0.864), alternative regulatory B subunits indicating mutually exclusive holoenzyme compositions. Critically, PPP2R3A shows strong interaction with RYR2 (0.964), the cardiac ryanodine receptor calcium release channel, and BCL2 (0.912), the apoptosis regulator. HPA shows group-enriched expression peaking in skeletal muscle (168.9 nTPM), tongue (110.0 nTPM), and heart muscle (47.9 nTPM) with subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus, flagellar centriole, and sperm midpiece—consistent with roles in calcium-regulated processes in excitable tissues and ciliary function. ClinVar lists 21 pathogenic variants including large copy number gains/losses spanning chromosome 3q and deletions, implicating PPP2R3A dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though no specific disease phenotype has been definitively assigned. AlphaFold structure prediction yields low-medium confidence (global pLDDT 58.2), consistent with a large scaffold protein containing structured EF-hand domains embedded in extensive flexible linker regions—a hallmark of regulatory proteins requiring conformational adaptability for calcium-dependent partner binding. Mechanistically, PPP2R3A functions through calcium-regulated substrate targeting: (1) The EF-hand domains bind calcium ions during cellular calcium transients (e.g., muscle contraction, neuronal firing), inducing conformational changes that modulate PP2A catalytic activity toward specific substrates; (2) The RYR2 interaction suggests PPP2R3A targets PP2A to dephosphorylate the ryanodine receptor, regulating calcium release from sarcoplasmic reticulum and preventing calcium overload-induced arrhythmias; (3) Golgi localization indicates roles in secretory pathway regulation, potentially dephosphorylating cargo proteins or trafficking machinery; (4) Centriole/midpiece localization suggests functions in ciliogenesis or sperm motility through calcium-dependent phosphorylation control of axonemal proteins. Disruption of PPP2R3A would cause dysregulated PP2A substrate specificity, leading to hyperphosphorylation of calcium handling proteins, defective excitation-contraction coupling, and potentially cardiomyopathy or myopathy phenotypes.
