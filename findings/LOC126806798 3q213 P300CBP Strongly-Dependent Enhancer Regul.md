## Description
LOC126806798 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 3q21.3 (genomic coordinates 125231830-125233163, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a P300/CBP STRONGLY-DEPENDENT GROUP 1 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors with STRONG DEPENDENCE on P300/CBP (histone acetyltransferases that acetylate histone H3K27 to open chromatin and recruit transcriptional machinery). This cofactor profile suggests it operates through chromatin remodeling and histone acetylation mechanisms. ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants — notably, ALL variants map to ZNF148 (Zinc Finger Protein 148), including missense mutations (p.His547Arg, p.Ser674Phe, p.Asn619Ser, p.Glu604Lys, p.Ala621Gly), frameshift deletions (p.Thr750fs, p.Tyr737fs, p.Gln555fs), and nonsense mutations (p.Asp532Ter). This indicates LOC126806798 functions as an enhancer regulating ZNF148 expression. ZNF148 is a C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor involved in cell cycle regulation, ribosomal RNA transcription, and has been implicated in colorectal cancer and other malignancies. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with strong clinical relevance — the P300/CBP-dependent enhancer activity combined with 28 ZNF148 pathogenic variants suggests LOC126806798 functions as a critical enhancer controlling ZNF148 transcription factor expression, where disruption causes dysregulation of ZNF148 target genes involved in cell cycle and cancer.
