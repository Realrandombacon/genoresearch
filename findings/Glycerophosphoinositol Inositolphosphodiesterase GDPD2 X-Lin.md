## Description
GDPD2 (Glycerophosphodiester Phosphodiesterase Domain Containing 2) is a 539-amino acid lipid metabolic enzyme with only 14 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein contains conserved glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase domains including glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase domain of mammalian GDE3-like proteins (cd08609, positions 202-512), PLC-like phosphodiesterase TIM beta/alpha-barrel domain superfamily (IPR017946, positions 213-480) with phosphoric diester hydrolase activity (GO:0008081) and lipid metabolic process (GO:0006629) functions, glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase domain (IPR030395, positions 224-479), glycerophosphoryl diester phosphodiesterase family domains (PF03009, positions 229-356; PF13653, positions 444-471), GP-PDE domain profile (PS51704, positions 224-479), and glycerophosphoryl diester phosphodiesterase family architecture (PTHR23344, positions 8-538). AlphaFold predicts a very well-folded structure (pLDDT 90.4), consistent with stable enzymatic TIM barrel architecture typical of phosphodiesterases. Recent studies (2024) established Myb73-GDPD2-GA2ox1 transcriptional regulatory module conferring phosphate deficiency tolerance in soybean, and 2023 studies identified NO-GDPD2 axis hindering club cell proliferation during allergic airway inflammation. We hypothesize GDPD2 functions as a membrane-associated glycerophosphoinositol phosphodiesterase that hydrolyzes glycerophosphoinositol and related glycerophospholipids to generate inositol phosphates and glycerol, linking phospholipid turnover with phosphate homeostasis and inositol signaling. The TIM barrel catalytic domain (positions 213-480) provides the phosphodiesterase active site for substrate hydrolysis. X-chromosome location with 121 ClinVar variants indicates GDPD2 dosage sensitivity potentially contributing to X-linked metabolic disorders, consistent with essential roles in glycerophospholipid catabolism. Lymphoid tissue enrichment (58.9 nTPM) suggests specialized functions in immune cell lipid metabolism. GDPD3 paralog connection (0.465) indicates functional redundancy within the GDPD enzyme subfamily. The GPD2 interaction (0.627) connects GDPD2 to glycerolipid metabolism pathways. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests potential roles in tumor lipid metabolism reprogramming. GDPD2 represents an uncharacterized X-linked glycerophosphodiester phosphodiesterase essential for phospholipid turnover and phosphate homeostasis.
