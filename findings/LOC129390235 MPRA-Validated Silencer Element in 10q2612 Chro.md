# LOC129390235: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 10q26.12 Chromatin Regulatory Region

## Description
LOC129390235 (Gene ID: 129390235) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 10q26.12 (121,382,681-121,382,881 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak1117 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 10q24.31-10q26.3 region (deletions 119-133 Mb, 120-133 Mb, 120-123 Mb; duplications 100-133 Mb, 108-133 Mb, 111-133 Mb, 117-133 Mb, 119-133 Mb), associated with 10q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, growth abnormalities, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac/renal defects, and skeletal anomalies. The 10q26 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including PTEN (phosphatase and tensin homolog, PI3K-AKT signaling, tumor suppressor), SBF2 (SET binding factor 2, Charcot-Marie-Tooth neuropathy 4B2), and NEBL (nebulette, cardiac Z-disc protein); (4) No HPA expression or STRING interactions (non-coding). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390235 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or neural contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). Pathogenic 10q CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and topologically associating domain (TAD) architecture, causing long-range dysregulation of PTEN, SBF2, and other 10q genes leading to neurodevelopmental phenotypes, tumor predisposition (PTEN haploinsufficiency causes Cowden syndrome), and peripheral neuropathy. The 10q26 deletion syndrome critical region spans ~10-20 Mb - altered silencer function may contribute to position effects and chromatin topology disruption. PTEN is a critical tumor suppressor - silencer disruption may alter PTEN expression contributing to cancer predisposition in 10q deletion carriers. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions. The MPRA validation provides direct functional evidence of regulatory activity in erythroid lineage.
