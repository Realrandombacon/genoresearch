## Description
FAM236A (Family with Sequence Similarity 236 Member A) is a 79-amino acid small protein encoded on the X chromosome (Xq13.1). Human Protein Atlas data shows strong testis-specific expression at 144.8 nTPM, classified as 'tissue enriched' and 'cancer enriched', suggesting a role in germ cell development or spermatogenesis. The protein is predicted intracellular with no detected expression in immune cells. AlphaFold structure prediction (A0A1B0GUQ0) yields a medium-confidence model (pLDDT 62.3), indicating partial folding with potentially disordered regions—characteristic of intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) that often function in signaling or regulatory complexes. STRING interaction analysis returned no partners, consistent with poor characterization. Critically, ClinVar documents 61 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning Xp22.33-q28, implicating FAM236A dosage sensitivity in human disease. The gene has aliases DMRTC1-AS1 and LINC00684, suggesting possible regulatory RNA functions or antisense relationships with DMRTC1 (a germ cell development gene). Conservation across primates (orthologs in Pongo abelii, Pan troglodytes) indicates functional constraint. Combined evidence suggests FAM236A functions as a testis-specific regulatory scaffold or cofactor in spermatogenic processes, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression contributing to X-linked disorders.
