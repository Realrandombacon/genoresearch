## Description
FKBP15 (FK506-Binding Protein 15) is a 1219 aa large FKBP-type peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 17 literature references, functioning as a component of the WASH complex regulating endosomal actin polymerization and retromer-mediated cargo sorting. Multi-omics data reveals FKBP15 as a scaffolding isomerase coordinating protein folding within the endosomal trafficking machinery. InterPro analysis confirms defining FKBP domain architecture: FKBP-type peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase domain (IPR001179/PF00254/PS50059, aa 194-290) with GO term for peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase activity (GO:0003755); FKBP15-like domain (IPR056598/PF23649, aa 557-779) representing a coiled-coil region for protein-protein interactions; FKBP-like superfamily (SSF54534/G3DSA:3.10.50.40/IPR046357, aa 75-291) indicating structural homology to other FKBP proteins; and membership in FK506-binding domain-containing protein family (PTHR44927, aa 8-1210). The FKBP domain catalyzes cis-trans isomerization of proline peptide bonds, accelerating protein folding. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT 63.5), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered FKBP domains and potentially disordered regions for flexible partner binding. HPA shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, immune cell enhanced pattern, nucleoli and cytosol subcellular localization, and predicted roles in endocytosis, transport, and actin-binding - consistent with endosomal trafficking functions. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence associations with WASH complex and retromer components: WASHC2C (0.886, WASH complex subunit 2C/Strumpellin), WASHC4 (0.810, WASH complex subunit 4/SWIP), VPS29 (0.799, vacuolar protein sorting 29, retromer complex), VPS35 (0.793, vacuolar protein sorting 35, retromer cargo recognition), WASHC5 (0.736, WASH complex subunit 5/FAM21), WASHC1 (0.683, WASH complex subunit 1), VPS26A (0.588, vacuolar protein sorting 26A, retromer), WASHC2A (0.544, WASH complex subunit 2A), TBC1D5 (0.432, TBC1 domain family member 5, retromer regulator), CAPZA1 (0.407, capping actin protein of muscle Z-line alpha subunit 1). The WASH complex (WASHC1/2/4/5) and retromer (VPS26/29/35) interactions place FKBP15 within the endosomal actin polymerization machinery that drives cargo sorting and vesicle scission. ClinVar lists 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.53+2T>G, c.2223+5G>C), synonymous variants (c.534G>A/p.Leu178=), intronic variants (c.1008-29A>G, c.1173+87C>G), and large copy number variations (chr9:203861-141020389x3 gain, chr9:203861-131603223x3 gain, chr9:109265628-117650999 loss), implicating FKBP15 dosage sensitivity in disease. The 17 literature references include studies on: (1) m6A demethylase regulation of dental follicle stem cell osteogenesis via miR-7974/FKBP15 pathway (2023, 10 citations); (2) Arabidopsis FKBP15-1/2 repression of vacuolar invertase VIN2 affecting lateral root development (2020, 13 citations); (3) wheat TaFKBP15-5B essential for starch synthesis and grain weight (2026, 0 citations). Human FKBP15 function remains poorly characterized despite clear WASH complex association.
