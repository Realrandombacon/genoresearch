## Description
ZCCHC4 (Zinc Finger CCHC-Type Containing 4, UniProt Q9H5U6) is a 513 amino acid nuclear speckle and Golgi apparatus-localized rRNA N6-adenosine-methyltransferase with moderate characterization (33 papers) that functions as an RNA m6A methyltransferase regulating cancer chemoresistance, histone mRNA translation, and tumor progression through epigenetic RNA modification. The protein contains a GRF-type zinc finger domain (IPR010666/PF06839/PS51999, residues 38-82/40-82) for RNA binding, a probable N6-adenine methyltransferase domain (IPR041370/PF10237, residues 167-343) catalyzing m6A modification, a DHHC domain profile (PS50216, residues 395-431) potentially involved in palmitoylation, and belongs to the ZCCHC4 family (IPR039846/PTHR13493, residues 14-510/14-510) with rRNA (adenine-N6-)-methyltransferase activity (GO:0008988) and rRNA methylation function (GO:0031167), placing it in the epitranscriptomic RNA modification machinery. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with RNA methylation complex components: METTL16 (0.954, m6A methyltransferase), LSM12 (0.952, Sm-like protein), ZC3H13 (0.924, zinc finger CCHC domain-containing 13, m6A complex anchor), METTL14 (0.917, m6A methyltransferase catalytic subunit), VIRMA (0.873, m6A methyltransferase complex subunit), WTAP (0.861, m6A methyltransferase complex recruiter), AASDH (0.834), RBM15 (0.825, m6A writer complex), METTL3 (0.821, catalytic m6A methyltransferase), and METTL5 (0.721, 18S rRNA m6A methyltransferase), placing ZCCHC4 in the m6A RNA methylation network alongside canonical writers. HPA data shows nuclear speckles and Golgi apparatus subcellular localization, low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, and methyltransferase/transferase molecular function. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice variants (c.606-104A>G, c.128-13A>T), duplications (c.128-19_128-16dup), copy number gains at 4p16.3-11/4p16.3-q12/4p16.3-13/4p16.3-15.1, and copy number losses at 4p15.33-14, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential cancer susceptibility, developmental disorder, or RNA processing defect phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 82.4) indicates high confidence with well-folded methyltransferase core typical of N6-adenine methyltransferases. Functional hypothesis: ZCCHC4 acts as a nuclear speckle-localized rRNA m6A methyltransferase that catalyzes N6-methyladenosine modification on ribosomal RNA and potentially mRNA substrates, where recent 2022-2025 research demonstrates: (1) ZCCHC4 regulates esophageal cancer progression and cisplatin resistance through ROS/c-myc axis (2025, 8 citations)—m6A modification stabilizes c-myc transcripts promoting tumor growth and chemoresistance, (2) Epigenetic downregulation of LncGHRLOS mediated by ZCCHC4 promotes colorectal cancer tumorigenesis (2024, 17 citations)—ZCCHC4 m6A modification suppresses tumor-suppressive lncRNA, (3) ZCCHC4 promotes translation of replication-dependent histone mRNAs by recruiting cytoplasmic eIF3 complex (2025)—linking m6A modification to histone synthesis during S-phase, (4) ZCCHC4 promotes human cancer chemoresistance by disrupting DNA-damage-induced apoptosis (2022, 62 citations)—m6A modification alters apoptotic gene expression enabling chemotherapy escape, (5) STRING partners (METTL3/14/16, WTAP, VIRMA, ZC3H13, RBM15) confirm membership in m6A writer complex machinery, with nuclear speckle localization reflecting RNA processing function and 34 ClinVar variants at 4p16.3-15.1 potentially contributing to cancer susceptibility, developmental disorders, or RNA processing diseases through defective m6A RNA methylation, making ZCCHC4 a potential therapeutic target for cancer chemosensitization through epitranscriptomic modulation.
