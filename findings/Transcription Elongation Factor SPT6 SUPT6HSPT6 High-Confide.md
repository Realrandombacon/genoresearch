## Description
SUPT6H (UniProt Q7KZ85), also known as SPT6 or emb-5, encodes a 1726 aa multi-domain histone chaperone and transcription elongation factor functioning as an essential component of the RNA polymerase II (Pol II) elongation complex that controls transcriptional elongation, chromatin reassembly behind transcribing polymerase, and maintenance of histone H3 lysine 36 trimethylation (H3K36me3) epigenetic marks. InterPro analysis reveals complex multi-domain architecture: dual SH2 (Src Homology 2) domains (cd09918/cd09928/G3DSA:3.30.505.10/IPR000980, residues 1331-1415/1424-1515/1317-1423/1425-1519/1325-1431) mediating phospho-CTD recognition of RNA Pol II; S1 RNA-binding domain (cd00164/IPR003029/G3DSA:2.40.50.140, residues 1227-1274/1221-1282) providing nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676); YqgF/RNase H-like domain (G3DSA:3.30.505.10/IPR006641, residues 780-937) for catalytic or structural functions; RuvA domain 2-like (G3DSA:1.10.10.650, residues 305-478) for DNA binding; helix-hairpin-helix domain (G3DSA:1.10.150.850, residues 950-1040) for nucleic acid interaction; Tex N-terminal region-like (G3DSA:1.10.3500.10, residues 523-779); Death-like domain (G3DSA:1.10.10.2740, residues 1043-1137); and nucleic acid-binding protein regions (G3DSA:2.40.50.140, residues 1140-1177/1227-1287).
