# LOC129390650: MPRA-Validated Chromatin Silencer Element on Chromosome 14q24.3 with Moderate CNV Burden

**Date:** 2026-03-23T11:27:39.858818

**Quality Score:** 4.25/10  (E=4.5, D=4.0) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129390650 is a non-coding cis-regulatory DNA element classified as a transcriptional silencer, validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This 200bp genomic region (chr14:77583851-77584051, GRCh38) at 14q24.3 demonstrates capacity to repress super core promoter (SCP1) activity, confirming silencer function. As a regulatory DNA element, it lacks protein-coding features: no UniProt accession, no InterPro domains, no STRING interactions, and no HPA expression data. Clinical significance is moderate: ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (CNVs) spanning this locus—including both duplications (x3, 5 variants) and deletions (x1, 4 variants) across 14q24.3-32.33 regions. This CNV burden is intermediate compared to other silencer elements (LOC129390829: 9 CNVs; LOC129389713: 14 CNVs; LOC129389488: 1 CNV). Hypothesis: LOC129390650 functions as a chromatin silencer recruiting repressive complexes (Polycomb PRC2, HDACs, or KRAB-ZNF co-repressors) to silence target genes on 14q24.3-32.33, a region containing dosage-sensitive genes involved in development and hematopoiesis. The moderate CNV burden suggests this element regulates genes where disruption contributes to 14q deletion/duplication syndromes—potentially causing developmental delay, growth abnormalities, or hematologic dysfunction. K562 erythroid validation indicates relevance to blood cell development, and 14q rearrangements are associated with myeloid malignancies. The silencer may coordinate long-range chromatin interactions via CTCF/cohesin-mediated looping to repress multiple target genes, and CNVs disrupt this three-dimensional genome architecture.
