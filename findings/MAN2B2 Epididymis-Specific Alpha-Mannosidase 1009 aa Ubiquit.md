## Description
MAN2B2 (Mannosidase Alpha Class 2B Member 2, also known as Epididymis-Specific Alpha-Mannosidase, UniProt Q9Y2E5) is a 1009 amino acid secreted enzyme with moderate human characterization (26 papers, predominantly recent congenital disorder of glycosylation and autoimmune disease studies from 2020-2025 with limited enzymatic mechanism characterization) representing a genuine dark gene in human biology that functions as a glycoside hydrolase family 38 enzyme catalyzing alpha-1,6-mannoside hydrolysis in N-glycan processing while regulating immune homeostasis, sperm maturation, and cancer progression. The protein contains an N-terminal glycoside hydrolase family 38 domain (IPR000602 residues 27-353) with alpha-mannosidase activity (GO:0004559) and mannose metabolic process function (GO:0006013), a galactose mutarotase-like domain superfamily (IPR011013 residues 434-1007) for carbohydrate binding (GO:0030246) and metabolic process (GO:0005975), belonging to the glycoside hydrolase/deacetylase beta/alpha-barrel superfamily (IPR011330 residues 25-351) with molecular functions as glycosidase and hydrolase.
