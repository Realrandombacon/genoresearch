## Description
DDX19A (DEAD-Box Helicase 19A, UniProt Q9NUU7) is a 478 amino acid nucleoplasmic RNA helicase with minimal characterization (17 papers, genuine dark gene) that functions as a nuclear pore-associated mRNA export factor regulating MCL1 mRNA localization, intrinsic apoptosis, and cancer progression through PI3K/AKT pathway activation. The protein contains a DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545/PF00270, residues 116-279) with ATP binding and nucleic acid binding function (GO:0005524/GO:0003676), a Q motif for RNA helicase activity (IPR014014, residues 91-119, GO:0003724), helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001, residues 110-309), C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650/PF00271, residues 305-473/318-433), and belongs to the P-loop containing NTP hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/IPR027417, residues 88-297/303-478/88-478), placing it in the DEAD-box RNA helicase family involved in mRNA export and translation regulation. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with nuclear pore and mRNA export machinery: NUP214 (0.970, nucleoporin 214kDa), MIF4GD (0.932, mRNA export factor), DDX19B-2 (0.919, paralog), CTIF (0.906, CBP80/20-dependent translation initiation factor), GLE1 (0.889, mRNA export factor), NUP88 (0.848, nucleoporin 88kDa), NLRP3 (0.825, inflammasome component), GOSR1 (0.764, Golgi SNARE), NUP62 (0.715, nucleoporin 62kDa), and DDX25 (0.445), placing DDX19A in the nuclear pore complex mRNA export pathway. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, nucleoplasmic localization, and classification as enzyme/transporter with helicase, hydrolase, and RNA-binding molecular functions. ClinVar contains 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 16q11.2-24.3/16q21-24.1/16q12.2-22.2/16p13.3-q24.3, deletions at 16q22.1-22.3/16q22.1-23.1, and duplications, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential cancer, developmental, or hematologic phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 80.7) indicates high confidence with well-folded DEAD-box helicase core typical of RNA helicases. Functional hypothesis: DDX19A acts as an ATP-dependent RNA helicase localized to the nuclear pore complex where it remodels mRNA-protein complexes (mRNPs) for nuclear export through its DEAD-box catalytic core, where recent 2023-2026 research demonstrates: (1) DDX19A promotes gastric cancer cell proliferation and migration by activating the PI3K/AKT pathway (2024, 4 citations), (2) DDX19A/B modulate selinexor (XPO1 inhibitor) sensitivity by regulating MCL1 mRNA nuclear export in leukemia cells—DDX19A depletion traps MCL1 mRNA in nucleus reducing anti-apoptotic MCL1 protein (2024, 3 citations), (3) genome-wide CRISPR screens identify DDX19A/DDX19B as critical regulators of intrinsic apoptosis through MCL1 mRNA cellular localization (2023), (4) DDX19A dependency exploited to induce transcriptional catastrophe in Ewing sarcoma driven by EWS/FLI1 oncofusion (2026), (5) DDX19A promotes cervical squamous cell carcinoma metastasis by inducing NOX1-mediated ROS production (2021, 7 citations), with STRING partners (NUP214, NUP88, NUP62, GLE1, MIF4GD, CTIF) confirming nuclear pore mRNA export function and 24 ClinVar variants at 16q22.1 potentially contributing to cancer predisposition or developmental disorders through mRNA export defects, making DDX19A a potential therapeutic target for selinexor-sensitive leukemias, gastric cancer, and Ewing sarcoma through mRNA export inhibition or PI3K/AKT pathway modulation.
