## Description
RAE1 (Ribonucleic Acid Export 1, also known as mRNA export factor RAE1 or NUP98-RAE1 fusion partner, UniProt P78406) is a 368 aa essential nuclear protein belonging to the WD40 repeat family that functions as an mRNA export factor and nuclear pore complex (NPC) component. InterPro analysis reveals canonical WD40 repeat architecture: multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680, positions 28-70/76-166/250-310; PF00400, positions 85-114/125-157/275-301) within WD40/YVTN repeat-like-containing domain superfamily (IPR015943, positions 1-368; G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 1-368) with conserved WD repeat signatures (IPR019775, positions 101-115/144-158; PS00678, positions 101-115/144-158; PS50082, positions 82-123/123-166) and PAC1/LIS1-like WD-40 repeats (IPR020472, positions 101-115/144-158/288-302). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 92.9), indicating an exceptionally well-folded β-propeller WD40 domain structure for protein-protein interactions. HPA data confirms testis-enriched expression (96.1 nTPM, tissue enhanced), detected in all tissues, nucleoplasm/nucleoli fibrillar center localization, classified as essential predicted intracellular protein/transporter involved in cell cycle, cell division, mitosis, transport, and host-virus interaction. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with mRNA export machinery and nuclear pore complex: NXF1 (0.998, nuclear RNA export factor 1), NUP153 (0.975, nucleoporin 153 kDa), NXF2B (0.973), SEH1L (0.964, SEH1-like nucleoporin), NXF5 (0.960), NUP42 (0.802, nucleoporin 42 kDa), NUP98 (0.708, nucleoporin 98 kDa, fusion partner in t(7;11) leukemia), NUP214 (0.701), NXF3 (0.578), GLE1 (0.410, RNA export mediator). ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains at 20q13.13-13.33 and deletions affecting the RAE1 locus. Literature (290 papers) establishes RAE1 roles in: (1) RAE1-armoured dendritic cell vaccines boosting NKG2D-CAR-T cell anti-solid tumor treatment (2025, 2 citations), (2) Nup98/Rae1 nuclear pore subcomplex vital for maternal-to-zygotic transition during early embryonic development (2025), (3) mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint control and chromosome segregation, (4) NKG2D ligand expression on tumor cells enabling immune recognition, and (5) mRNA nuclear export regulation through NXF1/TAP interaction. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: RAE1 serves as a WD40 β-propeller scaffold mediating mRNA export complex assembly and nuclear pore targeting through interactions with NXF1/TAP export receptor and NUP98 nucleoporin, coordinating nucleocytoplasmic mRNA transport and mitotic spindle checkpoint signaling. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The WD40 β-propeller structure provides multiple interaction surfaces for binding mRNA export adaptors (NXF1), nucleoporins (NUP98, NUP153, SEH1L), and mitotic regulators. RAE1 binds mRNA-bound NXF1 complexes and targets them to nuclear pore complexes via NUP98 interactions, facilitating mRNA translocation through the NPC central channel. During mitosis, RAE1 localizes to kinetochores and spindle apparatus, ensuring proper chromosome alignment and segregation. In cancer immunotherapy, RAE1 expression on tumor cells serves as an NKG2D ligand, enabling CAR-T cell recognition and killing. The NUP98-RAE1 fusion in t(7;11)(p15;p15) acute myeloid leukemia creates an oncogenic transcription factor disrupting normal mRNA export and cell cycle control. The 16 ClinVar CNVs indicate RAE1 dosage sensitivity may contribute to developmental disorders or cancer predisposition. Essential protein classification confirms fundamental role in cell viability through mRNA export and mitotic fidelity.
