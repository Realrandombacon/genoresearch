## Description
TMF1 (TATA element modulatory factor 1) is a well-studied transcription coactivator and nuclear androgen receptor binding protein (UniProt P82094). Structurally, it is a partially structured protein (AlphaFold pLDDT: 64.9) containing conserved Zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR022092) and TATA-binding domains (IPR022091), with high-confidence interactions (STRING >0.7) with Golgi apparatus proteins (GOLGA4, GOLGA1) and vesicle trafficking factors (RAB6A, RAB6B), suggesting a role in intracellular transport and transcription regulation. Expression is ubiquitous with low tissue specificity but enriched in blood cells. Clinically, it is associated with 15 pathogenic copy number variants (gains/losses) on chromosome 3, linked to poor outcomes in cervical cancer and chronic granulomatous disease. Functionally, it is upregulated by insulin and required for sustained glucose homeostasis, positioning it as a critical node in metabolic signaling and cancer progression.
