## Description
UGT3A2 (UDP Glycosyltransferase Family 3 Member A2, UniProt Q3SY77) is a 523 aa phase II metabolic enzyme that catalyzes glucuronidation of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and xenobiotics through UDP-glucuronic acid conjugation, functioning in tobacco carcinogen detoxification and Ewing sarcoma drug sensitivity pathways. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a highly confident well-folded glycosyltransferase architecture (pLDDT=90.7) featuring a canonical UDP-glycosyltransferase domain (IPR002213/PF00201, aa 24-508) within the UDP-glucuronosyl/UDP-glucosyltransferase family (IPR035595/PTHR48043, aa 34-495), with conserved catalytic site residues (PS00375, aa 351-394) and glycogen phosphorylase B-like folds (G3DSA:3.40.50.2000/SSF53756, aa 20-468) enabling sugar donor binding and substrate recognition. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to xenobiotic metabolism and transport: the UDP-sugar transporter SLC35A2 (0.973) supplying UDP-glucuronic acid substrate, organic anion transporter SLCO1A2 (0.675) mediating substrate uptake, phase II enzyme NAT2 (0.582) for acetylation reactions, paralog UGT3A1 (0.559) sharing substrate specificity, and metabolic enzymes PGM2 (0.640), RUBCNL (0.629), and CWC25 (0.642). HPA shows lymphoid tissue-enriched expression (13.6 nTPM), skin expression (9.7 nTPM), and bone marrow expression (6.1 nTPM) with nucleoplasmic, Golgi apparatus, and cytosolic localization, classified as cancer-enhanced. ClinVar identifies 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 5p13.3-11 (multiple breakpoints) and splice variants (c.196+1G>C), though no specific Mendelian disease is annotated - these CNVs overlap with developmental delay and hematologic disorder regions. Literature (20 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: EWS-FLI1 fusion oncoprotein drives UGT3A2 upregulation unlocking drug sensitivity pathways in Ewing sarcoma (2024), missense variants alter PAH metabolism affecting lung cancer susceptibility (2020, 5 citations), and UGT3A2 detoxifies tobacco carcinogens via alternative sugar conjugation (2018-2019). The gene represents a 'semi-dark' atypical UGT where canonical glucuronidation function is established, but specific substrate repertoire beyond PAHs, mechanisms linking UGT3A2-mediated detoxification to Ewing sarcoma therapeutic vulnerability, and tissue-specific roles in lymphoid malignancy remain underexplored therapeutic targets.
