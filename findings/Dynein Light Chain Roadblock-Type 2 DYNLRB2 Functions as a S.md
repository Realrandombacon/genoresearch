## Description
DYNLRB2 (Dynein light chain roadblock-type 2) is a small 96 aa accessory subunit of the cytoplasmic dynein motor complex that regulates microtubule-based cargo transport and spindle assembly. With 34 literature references, DYNLRB2 represents a 'partially characterized' dark gene where its role as a dynein light chain is established, but its specific functions in male meiosis, ciliopathies, and metabolic disease are emerging (2023-2025). InterPro analysis reveals a compact Roadblock/LC7 domain architecture (IPR004942/PF03259, aa 4-93) embedded within the cytoplasmic dynein light chain LC7-type family (IPR016561/PIRSF009998, aa 1-96), forming a conserved β-grasp fold (G3DSA:3.30.450.30/SSF103196) optimized for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally high-confidence structure (pLDDT 95.1), indicating a rigid, evolutionarily constrained scaffold essential for dynein complex assembly. HPA shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (157.8 nTPM), fallopian tube (57.1 nTPM), and choroid plexus (54.7 nTPM), consistent with roles in cell division and ciliated epithelia. STRING interactions reveal DYNLRB2 functions within the dynein machinery: it binds other light chains (DYNLL1: 0.956, DYNLL2: 0.946), intermediate chains (DYNC1I1: 0.760, DYNC1I2: 0.602), the heavy chain motor (DYNC2H1: 0.891), and ciliopathy-associated WDR proteins (WDR60: 0.923, WDR34: 0.616) that regulate intraflagellar transport. ClinVar lists 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 16q11.2-24.3, implicating DYNLRB2 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders, though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. Recent literature (2023-2025) establishes DYNLRB2 as: (1) Essential regulator of male meiotic spindle bipolarity distinct from DYNLRB1's mitotic role; (2) Component of lncRNA DYNLRB2-AS1 axis promoting gemcitabine resistance in nasopharyngeal carcinoma via DHX9 stabilization; (3) Autophagy enhancer inhibiting macrophage foam cell formation in atherosclerosis; (4) Biomarker for ischemic stroke risk via circulating lncRNA expression.
