In bone, DUSP18 couples hormonal signals (Kisspeptin) to Src inactivation, limiting osteoclast activity. In cancer, hypoxia-induced DUSP18 may selectively dephosphorylate p38 while sparing JNK/ERK, creating a pro-migratory signaling bias. The 18 ClinVar variants suggest dosage sensitivity, where DUSP18 overexpression (via copy gain) could suppress tumor immunity by altering cholesterol metabolism.
