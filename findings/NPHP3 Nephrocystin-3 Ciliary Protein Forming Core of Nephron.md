## Description
NPHP3 (Nephrocystin 3, also known as CFAP31, MKS7, NPH3, RHPD, RHPD1, SLSN3, UniProt Q7Z494) is a 1330-amino acid ciliary protein functioning as a core component of the nephronophthisis protein complex localized to the primary cilium transition zone and cilium tip, regulating ciliary development and renal tubular morphogenesis while also modulating Wnt signaling pathway activity and contributing to Senior-Loken syndrome pathogenesis when mutated.
