# DDX12P: Pseudogene-Encoded DEAD-Box Helicase with Conserved Structural Domains and Potential Regulatory Function

## Description
DDX12P (Gene ID: 440081) is annotated as a pseudogene on chromosome 12p13.31 but encodes a substantial 950 amino acid protein (UniProt Q92771) with well-conserved helicase domains. InterPro analysis reveals multiple functional domains: DEAD-box helicase domain (IPR006554, positions 39-456), ATP-dependent helicase C-terminal domain (IPR006555, positions 712-899), RAD3-like helicase domain (IPR010614, positions 251-434), and P-loop NTP hydrolase superfamily domains (IPR014013, IPR013020). GO terms predict DNA helicase activity (GO:0003678), ATP binding (GO:0005524), and nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676). AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q92771-F1) shows high confidence folding (global pLDDT: 76.2) across the full 1-950 aa range, indicating a stable 3D structure despite pseudogene annotation. Literature search reveals only 4 publications, confirming dark gene status. Notably, two papers link DDX12P to lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA networks in Primary Open Angle Glaucoma (POAG), suggesting potential regulatory RNA functions. Two other papers discuss DDX11 (the functional paralog) in Warsaw Breakage Syndrome, a cohesinopathy involving sister chromatid cohesion. STRING-DB, HPA, and ClinVar returned no data, consistent with poor characterization. The presence of conserved helicase domains and confident AlphaFold structure suggests DDX12P may escape typical pseudogene silencing and produce a functional protein, potentially acting as: (1) a regulatory non-coding RNA modulating DDX11 expression via miRNA sponging, (2) a truncated helicase with dominant-negative effects on DNA repair pathways, or (3) a tissue-specific helicase active in contexts not captured by HPA. The glaucoma association warrants investigation into ocular tissue expression and DNA damage response roles.
