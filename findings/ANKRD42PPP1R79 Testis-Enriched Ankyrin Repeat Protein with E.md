# ANKRD42/PPP1R79: Testis-Enriched Ankyrin Repeat Protein with EF-Hand Calcium Binding and Wnt Signaling Network Connections

## Description
ANKRD42 (also known as PPP1R79 or SARP) encodes a 389 amino acid ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein (UniProt Q8N9B4) that functions as a protein-protein interaction scaffold with potential calcium-dependent regulation. InterPro analysis reveals three tandem ankyrin repeat motifs (IPR002110, PF12796) spanning residues 47-331, forming a curved solenoid structure for protein binding, plus a C-terminal EF-hand calcium-binding site (IPR018247, PS00018, aa 329-341). The protein is classified in the 'Ankyrin Repeat and Cyclin-Dependent Kinase Inhibitor' family (IPR050776, PTHR24201), consistent with Gene Ontology predictions of CDK inhibitor activity. AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 77.5), indicating a well-folded stable structure.\n\nHPA expression demonstrates testis enrichment (34.8 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced specificity and multi-compartment localization (nucleoplasm, plasma membrane, cytosol). This distribution suggests ANKRD42 may shuttle between compartments or exist in distinct pools regulating different processes—nuclear roles in cell cycle control via CDK inhibition and membrane roles in signal transduction.\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals connections to Wnt signaling antagonists: FRZB (frizzled-related protein, 0.654), SFRP1/2/5 (secreted frizzled-related proteins, 0.41-0.47), plus ciliogenesis factor DNAAF1 (0.599) and transcriptional regulator ZBBX (0.594). This places ANKRD42 in developmental signaling pathways, potentially modulating Wnt/Frizzled receptor activity through its ankyrin-mediated scaffolding.\n\nClinVar contains 14 pathogenic copy number variants at 11q14.1, predominantly large deletions. Given the testis-specific expression and Wnt pathway connections, ANKRD42 haploinsufficiency may disrupt spermatogenesis and embryonic development through aberrant Wnt signaling and cell cycle dysregulation.
