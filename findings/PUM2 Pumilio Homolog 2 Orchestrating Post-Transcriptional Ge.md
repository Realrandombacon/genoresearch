## Description
PUM2 (Pumilio Homolog 2) is a 1066 amino acid RNA-binding protein that functions as a critical regulator of post-transcriptional gene silencing, neuronal survival, and germ cell development through C-terminal Pumilio homology domain (PUM-HD)-mediated sequence-specific RNA recognition, NANOS-DAZ complex assembly for translational repression, and m6A-dependent alternative polyadenylation control in neural and germline tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic PUF family architecture including Pumilio-family RNA binding domain (cd07920, residues 721-1044) for target mRNA recognition, Pumilio RNA-binding repeats (IPR001313, PF00806, PS50302, residues 726-764/762-797/798-835) forming the signature eight-repeat PUM-HD structure with RNA binding function (GO:0003723), Pumilio homology domain (IPR033133, PS50303, residues 706-1048) providing sequence-specific RNA recognition scaffold, Pumilio RNA binding domain (IPR033712, residues 721-1044) for UGUAHAUA motif binding, armadillo-like helical superfamily (IPR011989, IPR016024, G3DSA:1.25.10.10, residues 705-1056/707-1045) forming the curved solenoid structure for RNA interaction, leucine-rich repeat variant (G3DSA:1.25.10.10, residues 705-1056) providing structural framework, and Pumilio RNA-binding family (PTHR12537, residues 394-1044) defining evolutionary relationship to Drosophila Pumilio. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8TB72-F1) predicts medium confidence (pLDDT 55.2) across the 1066 residue sequence, indicating a partially structured protein with ordered C-terminal PUM-HD RNA-binding domain connected to intrinsically disordered N-terminal regions suitable for dynamic protein-protein interactions and flexible RNA target recognition. HPA data classifies PUM2 as a predicted intracellular protein involved in translation regulation with RNA-binding molecular function, cytosolic subcellular localization reflecting mRNA processing roles, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and low cell type specificity consistent with housekeeping RNA regulator function. STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to germ cell and neuronal RNA regulatory networks: NANOS1 (Nanos C2HC-Type Zinc Finger 1, 0.997) as primary interaction partner for germ cell development; DAZ1 (Deleted in Azoospermia 1, 0.977) for spermatogenesis; ATXN1L (Ataxin-1-Like, 0.904) for spinocerebellar ataxia pathway; MOV10 (Moloney Leukemia Virus 10, 0.891) as RNA helicase partner; FMR1 (Fragile X Mental Retardation Protein, 0.851) for neuronal RNA metabolism; EIF4ENIF1 (Eukaryotic Translation Initiation Factor 4E Nuclear Import Factor 1, 0.848) for translation control; DAZL (0.485) and BOLL (0.440) as germ cell regulators; AGO2 (Argonaute-2, 0.432) for RNA interference; and ATXN1 (Ataxin-1, 0.424) for neurodegeneration pathway. ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 2p25.3-23.1, 2p25.3-22.3, 2p25.2-24.1, 2p25.3-q37.3 (spanning 3-243 Mb regions), copy number losses on 2p25.1-q13, 2p24.3-24.1, 2p24.1-23.3 (multiple independent CNV events), splice site variants (c.1292-2A>C, c.2622+6T>C), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in neurodevelopmental disorders and infertility. Literature (257 papers) establishes PUM2 as an emerging RNA regulator with disease associations: miR-299-5p modulates LPS-induced inflammation and osteogenic differentiation of periodontal stem cells by targeting PUM2 revealing inflammatory bone disease mechanism; SENP1 inhibits aerobic glycolysis in Aβ1-42-incubated astrocytes by promoting PUM2 deSUMOylation establishing Alzheimer's disease metabolic dysfunction link; IGF2BP2 binding to CPSF6 facilitates m6A-mediated alternative polyadenylation of PUM2 and promotes malignant progression in ovarian cancer demonstrating epitranscriptomic cancer mechanism; PUM2 axis disruption aggravates neuronal damage following cerebral ischemia-reperfusion showing neuroprotection role; and PUM2 functions in post-transcriptional gene silencing confirming fundamental RNA regulatory mechanism. As an emerging PUF family RNA-binding protein with multifaceted disease associations, PUM2 represents a critical node connecting post-transcriptional regulation to neurodegeneration, germ cell development, and cancer progression through C-terminal PUM-HD-mediated UGUAHAUA motif recognition, NANOS1-DAZ1 complex assembly for translational repression in germline, ATXN1/ATXN1L interaction for spinocerebellar ataxia pathway integration, FMR1-MOV10 cooperation for neuronal RNA metabolism, and IGF2BP2-CPSF6-mediated m6A-dependent alternative polyadenylation disrupted in spinocerebellar ataxia, Alzheimer's disease astrocyte dysfunction, ovarian cancer progression, cerebral ischemia-reperfusion injury, and male infertility.
