## Description
LOC129388455 (Gene ID: 129388455) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 1p36.33 (14,362,459-14,362,659 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak78 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - ALL are copy number variations (CNVs) affecting the 1p36 chromosomal region. Variants include large copy number losses (1p36.31-36.13 deletions: 5363826-18360302, 10264397-15780840, 9428538-15815791, 13400935-15356944, 6303641-15799093, 12724785-16034788; 1p36.22-36.21: 10264397-15780840, 9428538-15815791) and copy number gains (1p36.33-35.1 duplications: 99125-34026935, 6554885-16056011, 11021751-15236671; duplication: 10115497_16283149dup). These large CNVs cause 1p36 deletion syndrome - the most common terminal deletion disorder in humans featuring: severe intellectual disability, developmental delay, hypotonia, seizures, cardiomyopathy, distinctive facial features (deep-set eyes, midface hypoplasia), and growth retardation. The 1p36 critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: PRDM16 (PR/SET domain 16, cardiomyopathy, craniofacial development), SKI (SKI proto-oncogene, myogenesis, TGF-beta signaling), GABRD (GABA-A receptor delta subunit, epilepsy), MMP23B (matrix metalloproteinase), and multiple tumor suppressor genes (CHD5 - chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 5, neuroblastoma). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129388455 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Deletions encompassing this element may cause: (1) loss of silencer function leading to ectopic/overexpression of target genes contributing to 1p36 deletion phenotype; (2) disruption of topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries altering long-range enhancer-promoter contacts; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopmental and cardiac programs. Duplications may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of target genes; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting normal gene expression patterns. The 16 ClinVar CNVs indicate this regulatory element resides in a dosage-sensitive genomic region where copy number changes cause severe neurodevelopmental, cardiac, and craniofacial phenotypes. Lack of point mutations in ClinVar suggests: (1) silencer sequence may be tolerant to single nucleotide variation due to degenerate transcription factor binding motifs; (2) clinical significance primarily manifests through large structural variants affecting multiple regulatory elements and genes simultaneously; (3) functional redundancy with other silencers in the 1p36 region. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
