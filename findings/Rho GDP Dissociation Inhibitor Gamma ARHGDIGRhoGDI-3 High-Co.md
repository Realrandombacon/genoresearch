## Description
ARHGDIG (UniProt Q99819), also known as RhoGDI-3 or Rho GDP-dissociation inhibitor gamma, encodes a 225 aa high-confidence intracellular protein functioning as a specific regulator of Rho family GTPases that inhibits GDP dissociation and maintains Rho proteins in an inactive cytosolic state through integrated GDP-dissociation inhibition-mediated RHOA/CDC42 sequestration mechanisms critical for cytoskeletal dynamics, cell migration, macrophage extracellular trap (MET) formation, and ferroptosis suppression.
