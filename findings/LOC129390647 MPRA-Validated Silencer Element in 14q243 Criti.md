# LOC129390647: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 14q24.3 Critical Region - Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 7 Pathogenic CNV Variants

## Description
LOC129390647 (Gene ID: 129390647) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 14q24.3 (75,697,731-75,697,931 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak2202 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA would not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) STRING would find no protein interactions (non-coding); (5) No InterPro/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all are large copy number variations affecting the 14q chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 14q11.2-32.33 duplication (20043514-106877229, ~86.8 Mb); (2) 14q24.3-31.1 deletion (73343213-78835059, ~5.5 Mb); (3) 14q11.2-32.33 duplication (20151149-106855263, ~86.7 Mb); (4) 14q24.1-31.1 deletion (69562099-81975384, ~12.4 Mb); (5) 14q24.3-32.33 duplication (73655772-106879298, ~33.2 Mb); (6) 14q24.3-31.1 deletion (75489052-79610332, ~4.1 Mb); (7) 14q24.3 deletion (73877072-78042422, ~4.2 Mb). These CNVs cause 14q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, seizures, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, growth retardation, and skeletal anomalies. The 14q critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: AKT1 (RAC-alpha serine/threonine-protein kinase, PI3K-AKT signaling pathway), IGF1R (insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor, growth signaling), PAX9 (paired box 9, tooth/orofacial development), NKX2-1 (thyroid transcription factor-1, lung/thyroid/brain development), TTR (transthyretin, thyroid hormone transport), and RHEB (Ras homolog enriched in brain, mTOR signaling). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390647 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Deletions encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer loss leading to derepression of target genes including potential oncogenes (AKT1 pathway activation) or developmental regulators; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting 14q gene regulation; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopment and hematopoiesis. Duplications may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of tumor suppressors or developmental genes; (2) disrupted topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries affecting 14q gene expression programs. The 7 ClinVar CNVs suggest this regulatory element exhibits both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity. AKT1 dysregulation causes Proteus syndrome and cancer predisposition. IGF1R variants cause growth retardation. PAX9 mutations cause tooth agenesis. NKX2-1 haploinsufficiency causes brain-lung-thyroid syndrome. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
