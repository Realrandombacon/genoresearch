## Description
ATP13A1 (Q9HD20) encodes a 1204 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane-localized P5A-type ATPase that functions as a critical protein translocase and topogenesis factor facilitating the proper insertion, folding, and orientation of multi-spanning membrane proteins with poorly hydrophobic transmembrane domains (pTMDs) that are difficult for the canonical SEC61 translocon to recognize, while also preventing ER-associated degradation (ERAD) of folding-competent but mislocalized proteins and regulating MAVS-mediated antiviral innate immunity. InterPro analysis reveals canonical P-type ATPase architecture distributed across the full-length protein: a P-type cation-transporting ATPase domain similar to yeast Spf1p (cd07543, positions 214-1134) defining the catalytic core; a P-type ATPase subfamily V domain (IPR006544, positions 54-1192) classifying it as P5A-type with P-type transmembrane transporter activity (GO:0140358) and membrane localization (GO:0016020); a P-type ATPase family domain (IPR001757, positions 444-556/833-885) with ATP binding (GO:0005524) and ATP hydrolysis activity (GO:0016887); calcium-transporting ATPase cytoplasmic transduction domain A (G3DSA:2.70.150.10, positions 264-430) forming the actuator (A) domain; cytoplasmic domain N (G3DSA:3.40.1110.10, positions 540-717) forming the nucleotide-binding (N) domain; HAD superfamily domain (IPR023214/G3DSA:3.40.50.1000, positions 718-892) forming the phosphorylation (P) domain; P-type ATPase A domain superfamily (IPR008250, positions 298-429); phosphorylation site (IPR018303, positions 533-539) containing the conserved DKTGT motif where aspartate residue becomes phosphorylated during catalytic cycle; and transmembrane domain superfamily (IPR023298) forming 10 transmembrane helices creating the substrate translocation pathway.
