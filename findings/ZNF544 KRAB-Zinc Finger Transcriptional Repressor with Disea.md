## Description
ZNF544 encodes a 715 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein contains a canonical KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 13-85) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 380-458, enabling sequence-specific DNA recognition. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT=62.9), consistent with structured zinc finger regions connected by flexible linkers. HPA data indicates ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity (nTPM data available), suggesting housekeeping transcriptional regulatory function. STRING interactions reveal functional associations with TRIM28 (score 0.628), the canonical KRAB co-repressor that recruits heterochromatin machinery, plus NDEL1 (0.519), PAAF1 (0.507), and PEX2 (0.438), suggesting roles in nuclear organization and potentially peroxisome biogenesis. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains spanning 19q13.41-13.43, implicating ZNF544 dosage sensitivity in human disease. The KRAB-TRIM28 interaction mechanism suggests ZNF544 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor, recruiting KAP1/TRIM28 to silence target genes through histone methylation (H3K9me3) and heterochromatin formation. Copy number gains may cause dominant-negative effects or ectopic gene silencing.
