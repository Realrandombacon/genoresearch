## Description
POLDIP3 (Polymerase Delta-Interacting Protein 3, also known as SKAR/S6K1 Aly/REF-like target, UniProt Q9BY77) is a 421 aa nucleocytoplasmic protein functioning as a molecular bridge between DNA replication and RNA metabolism through its conserved RNA recognition motif (RRM) domain that enables binding to CBC-cap-bound spliced mRNAs during pioneer round translation and recruitment to mRNA export complexes. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a partially ordered architecture (pLDDT=61.9, medium confidence) featuring a canonical RRM domain (IPR000504/PF00076/SM00360/cd12681, aa 280-351) within the RNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR035979/SSF54928, aa 275-355) and ALYREF mRNA export adapter family (IPR051229/PTHR19965, aa 1-404), enabling RNA binding (GO:0003723), nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), and protein-protein interactions characteristic of mRNA export adaptors. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to core mRNA export and processing machinery with exceptionally high confidence: DEAD-box helicase DDX39B (0.994), translation initiation factor EIF4A3 (0.977), nuclear cap-binding protein NCBP1 (0.940), mRNA export adaptor ALYREF (0.929), THO complex subunit THOC1 (0.791), mRNA-splicing factor SARNP (0.743), mRNA export factor FYTTD1 (0.720), DDX39A (0.700), chromatin regulator CHTOP (0.649), and ribosomal kinase RPS6KB1 (0.433). HPA shows low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer and immune cell specificity, nuclear speckles and cytoplasmic bodies localization, classified as predicted intracellular protein with RNA-binding molecular function and roles in mRNA transport, translation regulation, and transport. ClinVar identifies 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses at 22q13.1-13.33 (chr22:39044105-51244066) overlapping with Phelan-McDermid syndrome critical region, though no specific Mendelian disease is directly annotated to POLDIP3 alone. Literature (37 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: 2023 study demonstrates broad coronavirus nsp5 antagonism through POLDIP3 cleavage to evade host antiviral responses (24 citations), 2022 review establishes POLDIP3 at crossroad of RNA and DNA metabolism (2 citations), 2020 research shows RTEL1 helicase associates with POLDIP3 to facilitate replication stress responses and R-loop resolution (34 citations), 2017 study identifies alternative POLDIP3 transcript promoting hepatocellular carcinoma progression (22 citations), and 2012 landmark paper demonstrates POLDIP3 splicing alteration associated with TDP-43 loss of function in ALS-affected tissues (90 citations). The gene represents a 'semi-dark RNA/DNA metabolism regulator' where canonical RRM-mediated RNA binding and mRNA export complex association is established, but specific mechanisms linking POLDIP3-mediated pioneer translation to DNA replication stress responses, RTEL1-dependent R-loop resolution pathways, coronavirus susceptibility through nsp5 cleavage, and 22q13 CNV contribution to Phelan-McDermid syndrome neurodevelopmental phenotypes remain underexplored. Therapeutic targeting of POLDIP3-RTEL1 interaction could represent novel approach for replication stress-associated cancers and R-loop-mediated genomic instability disorders, while POLDIP3 splicing modulation may offer therapeutic avenue for ALS and related TDP-43 proteinopathies.
