# LOC121852968: Dosage-Sensitive Enhancer Element on 18q with Strong Gain-Predominant Pathogenicity

## Description
LOC121852968 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 3246 located on chromosome 18q (genomic coordinates: 57998476-57998770 on NC_000018.10). This 294 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional enhancer (not repressor) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA expression). ClinVar analysis identified 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - substantially more than typical regulatory regions. Of the variants sampled, approximately 80% are copy number gains (triplifications) while 20% are copy number losses, indicating strong gain-predominant dosage sensitivity. The 18q region contains numerous clinically important genes including tumor suppressors (SMAD4 at 18q21.1, mutated in pancreatic cancer), cell adhesion molecules (CDH19, CDH20), and neurological disease genes (TSHZ1 at 18q22.1 associated with autism). As an enhancer, this element likely drives expression of one or more target genes in cis. Amplification leads to over-activation of oncogenes or developmental regulators, while deletion causes haploinsufficiency. The high variant count (29 vs 3-11 for other LOC elements) suggests this enhancer regulates particularly dosage-sensitive targets. The gain predominance indicates cellular homeostasis is more disrupted by enhancer over-dosage than by reduction - consistent with enhancer-mediated oncogene activation being a common cancer mechanism. This element may contribute to 18q syndromes, cancer predisposition, or developmental disorders through dysregulation of critical 18q genes.
