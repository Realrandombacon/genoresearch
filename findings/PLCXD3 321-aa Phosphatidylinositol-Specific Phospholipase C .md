## Description
PLCXD3 (UniProt Q63HM9) encodes a 321-amino acid phosphatidylinositol-specific phospholipase C X domain-containing protein belonging to the PI-PLC X domain family (IPR000909/PF00388/PS50007/SM00148, aa 22-196 for X domain; IPR017946/G3DSA:3.20.20.190/SSF51695, aa 12-312 for TIM beta/alpha-barrel phosphodiesterase domain). The protein adopts the canonical PLC catalytic fold (IPR042158/PTHR13593/IPR051057, aa 13-312) with AlphaFold predicting high-confidence structure (pLDDT: 88.9) consistent with enzymatically active phosphodiesterase.\n\nSTRING interactions are limited but specific: ASMTL (0.458, acetylserotonin O-methyltransferase-like protein involved in melatonin synthesis) and TSACC (0.411, tetratricopeptide repeat and ankyrin repeat containing coiled-coil protein). HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in heart muscle (31.0 nTPM), ovary (26.4 nTPM), and brain (20.6 nTPM); subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus; functional annotation in lipid degradation/metabolism and signal transduction.\n\nWith 28 papers, PLCXD3 represents a partially characterized dark gene with emerging clinical significance. Recent 2025 literature reveals: (1) PLCXD3-ALK novel fusion oncogene in lung squamous cell carcinoma responsive to ALK inhibitors; (2) SPINT1-AS1 lncRNA promotes gastric cancer apoptosis via miR-656-3p/PLCXD3 axis; (3) PLCXD3 downregulation enhances gastric cancer stemness and invasiveness as early diagnostic biomarker; (4) reduced PLCXD3 disrupts glucose sensing and insulin signaling in pancreatic β-cells (2019, 24 citations); (5) genetic variants associated with metabolic syndrome risk in Emirati population. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic variants including multiple chromosome 5p copy number gains. This positions PLCXD3 as an understudied lipid signaling enzyme linking metabolic regulation with oncogenic transformation.
