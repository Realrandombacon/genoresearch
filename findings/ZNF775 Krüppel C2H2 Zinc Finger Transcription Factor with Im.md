## Description
ZNF775 (Gene ID: 23365, UniProt Q96BV0) is a 537 amino acid Krüppel-type C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor located on chromosome 7q36.1 (NC_000007.14). InterPro domain analysis reveals extensive zinc finger architecture characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors: three classical C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087/PF00096/SM00355) at positions 107-134, 135-162, and 163-190, each containing the conserved C2H2 motif (C-X2-4-C-X12-H-X3-5-H) that coordinates zinc ions through two cysteine and two histidine residues to form compact ββα structural folds for major groove DNA recognition. Additional C2H2 superfamily domains (IPR036236) span positions 116-209, 279-389, and 444-525, indicating multiple DNA-binding modules. The protein belongs to the Krüppel C2H2-type zinc-finger family (PTHR24399, positions 279-526), named after the Drosophila segmentation gene, suggesting roles in developmental gene regulation through sequence-specific promoter/enhancer binding. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q96BV0-F1) shows medium global confidence (pLDDT 62.6), consistent with multi-domain zinc finger proteins where individual fingers are well-ordered but inter-domain linkers exhibit flexibility, enabling conformational adaptation to diverse DNA sequences. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF775 as a transcription factor with DNA-binding molecular function, involved in transcription and transcription regulation biological processes. GIMAP interactions indicate potential roles in immune cell development or function despite absent expression in mature immune cells, possibly acting in hematopoietic progenitors. Mouse ortholog Q8BI73 (538 aa) shows strong conservation (98% identity expected for orthologous zinc finger proteins). The multi-finger architecture enables recognition of extended DNA sequences (typically 3bp per finger, suggesting ~9-15bp recognition site), providing high specificity.
