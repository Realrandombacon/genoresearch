## Description
OTUD6B (OTU Deubiquitinase 6B, UniProt Q8N6M0) is a 293 aa cysteine protease belonging to the OTU (ovarian tumor) family of deubiquitinating enzymes that functions in protein stability regulation, centrosome dynamics, and immune modulation. The molecular architecture consists of a catalytic OTU domain (IPR003323/PF02338, positions 147-284) within the papain-like cysteine peptidase superfamily (IPR038765) that cleaves ubiquitin chains from substrate proteins. Literature (91 papers) establishes OTUD6B roles in: (1) ATRA upregulating OTUD6B to recruit CD8+ T cells and suppress colorectal liver metastasis by stabilizing DDX5/STAT3/CXCL11 axis (2025, 3 citations), (2) OTUD6B regulating KIFC1-dependent centrosome clustering and breast cancer cell survival by preventing multipolar spindle formation (2025, 3 citations), (3) lncRNA OTUD6B-AS1 serving dual role in immune evasion and ferroptosis resistance as prognostic biomarker in breast cancer (2025, 2 citations), (4) dysregulated OTUD6B-AS1 in systemic lupus erythematosus (2025, 2 citations). HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and nucleoplasm/Golgi/cytosol localization, consistent with regulatory role in protein homeostasis. STRING interactions reveal deubiquitinase network: ZUP1 (0.921) - zinc finger ubiquitin-specific peptidase; OTUB1 (0.851) - OTU domain-containing ubiquitin aldehyde-binding protein 1; YOD1 (0.643) - YOD1 deubiquitinase; OTUD7A (0.630), OTUD7B (0.598) - OTU family members; VCPIP1 (0.626) - valosin-containing protein-interacting protein 1; OTUD4 (0.621), OTUD1 (0.586), OTUD5 (0.518) - OTU deubiquitinases; NUDCD1 (0.604) - NudC domain-containing protein. ClinVar contains 63 pathogenic variants including frameshift (p.Pro96fs, p.Leu127fs), nonsense (p.Ser259Ter), missense (p.Ala109Gly, p.Glu134Gly), splice (c.83-1del), and CNVs at 8p23.3-q24.3 and 8q21-22, with disease associations including epilepsy and intellectual disability. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.9) indicating well-folded OTU domain with stable catalytic core, typical of active deubiquitinases. Conservation: mouse Otud6b (Q8K2H2) is 294 aa, chicken (Q5ZIP6) is 302 aa, Xenopus (Q6GM06/Q5M8L0) is 294 aa - highly conserved across vertebrates. Functional hypothesis: OTUD6B functions as a specificity deubiquitinase that removes K48-linked or K63-linked ubiquitin chains from specific substrates to regulate their stability and function. In cancer, OTUD6B stabilizes DDX5 ( DEAD-box helicase) which enhances STAT3 transcriptional activity and CXCL11 chemokine expression, recruiting CD8+ T cells to tumors. OTUD6B also deubiquitinates KIFC1 kinesin to promote centrosome clustering in cancer cells with amplified centrosomes, preventing lethal multipolar divisions. The well-folded OTU domain contains catalytic cysteine and histidine residues for ubiquitin isopeptide bond cleavage. Pathogenic variants in the OTU domain disrupt catalytic activity or substrate recognition, leading to aberrant protein stability, centrosome defects, and neurodevelopmental disorders (epilepsy, intellectual disability). OTUD6B represents an emerging therapeutic target for cancer metastasis and centrosome-addicted tumors.
