# EIF4G3: Ubiquitous eIF4F Scaffold Protein with MIF4G-MA3-W2 Domain Architecture Orchestrating Cap-Dependent Translation Initiation and Cancer Progression

## Description
EIF4G3 (Eukaryotic Translation Initiation Factor 4 Gamma 3, UniProt O43432) is a 1585 aa scaffolding protein belonging to the eIF4G family that functions as a central component of the eIF4F complex in cap-dependent translation initiation. The molecular architecture includes multiple protein-protein interaction domains: N-terminal region, MIF4G domain type 3 (IPR003890/PF02854, positions 755-983) for eIF4A helicase binding, MA3 domain (IPR003891/PF02847, positions 1221-1343) for eIF4E cap-binding protein interaction, and C-terminal W2 domain (IPR003307, positions 1416-1585) for PABPC1 poly(A)-binding protein association. Literature (81 papers) establishes EIF4G3 roles in: (1) circular RNA EIF4G3 suppressing gastric cancer progression through inhibition of β-catenin by promoting δ-catenin ubiquitin degradation and upregulating SIK1 (2022, 56 citations), (2) miR-375-EIF4G3 axis regulating cell apoptosis and tumor growth in lung squamous cell carcinomas with chidamide and radiotherapy treatment (2021, 11 citations), (3) EIF4G3 and EIF4G4 initiation factors from Leishmania showing dissimilar modes of action during translation (2026), (4) essential role in global protein synthesis and cell proliferation. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and cytosolic localization, consistent with housekeeping role in translation. STRING interactions reveal core translation initiation network: EIF4E (0.999) - cap-binding protein; EIF4G1 (0.972), EIF4G2 (0.968) - paralogous scaffolding proteins; EIF4E1B (0.960), EIF4E3 (0.945), EIF4E2 (0.434) - cap-binding protein family; EIF4B (0.887) - RNA-binding protein stimulating eIF4A; EIF4A1 (0.762), EIF4A2 (0.761) - DEAD-box RNA helicases; PABPC1 (0.440) - poly(A)-binding protein. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Ala1495Val), frameshift duplication (p.Lys1609fs), splice variants (c.3772+1G>A, c.2253+900T>C, c.2623-12G>A), and multiple CNVs at 1p36.32-36.12, though specific disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 55.3) indicating partially ordered domains with extensive intrinsically disordered regions, characteristic of flexible scaffolding proteins that undergo conformational changes during eIF4F assembly. Conservation: mouse Eif4g3 (Q80XI3) is 1579 aa - highly conserved across vertebrates. Functional hypothesis: EIF4G3 serves as an alternative scaffolding component of the eIF4F complex that bridges the 5' cap-bound eIF4E to the 43S preinitiation complex via eIF3, while simultaneously recruiting eIF4A helicase through the MIF4G domain and circularizing mRNA through W2 domain interaction with PABPC1. The disordered regions enable conformational flexibility for dynamic complex assembly and regulation by 4E-BP competitive inhibitors. Unlike the more studied EIF4G1, EIF4G3 may have specialized roles in stress conditions, specific mRNA subsets, or developmental stages. Overexpression in cancers (gastric, lung squamous) drives oncogenic translation programs including Wnt/β-catenin pathway activation. Pathogenic variants likely disrupt eIF4F complex assembly, impairing global translation and contributing to developmental disorders associated with 1p36 deletions.
