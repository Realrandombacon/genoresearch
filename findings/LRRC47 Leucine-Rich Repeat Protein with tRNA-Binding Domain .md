# LRRC47: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein with tRNA-Binding Domain, Nucleolar Localization, Ribosome Biogenesis Interactions, and 1p36 Deletion Syndrome Association

**Date:** 2026-03-20T13:32:04.202683

**Quality Score:** 2.55/10  (E=4.4, D=0.7) [WEAK]

## Description
LRRC47 encodes a 583 amino acid leucine-rich repeat-containing protein (UniProt Q8N1G4) with a distinctive hybrid domain architecture combining protein-protein interaction and RNA-binding modules. InterPro analysis reveals multiple N-terminal leucine-rich repeat domains (IPR001611, IPR003591, IPR025875, IPR032675, PF12799, residues 19-262) that mediate protein-protein interactions, followed by a C-terminal B3/B4 tRNA-binding domain (IPR005146, IPR020825, IPR045060, residues 314-525) with structural homology to phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase beta subunit—suggesting direct tRNA recognition capability. HPA data indicates ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity and distinctive nucleoli fibrillar center localization—the site of rRNA transcription and early ribosome subunit assembly—strongly implicating LRRC47 in ribosome biogenesis. STRING interactions identify 10 protein partners with remarkably high confidence scores for ribosomal proteins and translation factors: RIOK1 (0.954, ribosomal biogenesis kinase), RPS13 (0.932), EIF1AD (0.926), RPS15 (0.917), RPS3 (0.527), RPS11 (0.403), and aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases FARS2 (0.554, mitochondrial phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase), YARS1 (0.552, tyrosyl-tRNA synthetase), FARSA (0.533, cytoplasmic phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase)—this interaction network places LRRC47 at the intersection of ribosome assembly and tRNA charging machinery. ClinVar documents 87 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses spanning 1p36.33-36.22 (chr1:849467-9627901) and 1p36.33-36.31 regions, which overlap the critical 1p36 deletion syndrome locus—the most common terminal deletion syndrome in humans characterized by developmental delay, cardiomyopathy, and hypotonia. The combination of LRR-mediated protein scaffolding, tRNA-binding capability, nucleolar localization, and ribosome biogenesis interaction partners suggests LRRC47 functions as a molecular adaptor that coordinates tRNA delivery to nascent ribosomal subunits during ribosome assembly, with haploinsufficiency via 1p36 deletions disrupting ribosome biogenesis leading to nucleolar stress and p53 activation underlying 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes.
