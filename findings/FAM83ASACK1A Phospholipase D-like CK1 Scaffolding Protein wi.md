## Description
FAM83A (alias SACK1A, BJ-TSA-9, scaffolding CK1 anchoring protein A, family with sequence similarity 83 member A) encodes a 434 amino acid moderately folded nucleoplasmic/cytosolic protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 72.4) containing a phospholipase D-like N-terminal domain (cd09181, aa 24-298), scaffolding anchor of CK1 domain (IPR012461/PF07894, aa 20-295), and belonging to the FAM83 family (IPR050944/PTHR16181, aa 9-360) with endonuclease-like fold (G3DSA:3.30.870.10, aa 120-304) and phospholipase D/nuclease superfamily (SSF56024, aa 144-296). HPA shows tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in esophagus (59.6 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, nucleoplasm/cytosol subcellular localization, cancer-enhanced specificity, proto-oncogene disease involvement, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 8q23.2-24.3, 8q24.11-24.13, and 8q21.2-24.3 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with casein kinase 1 regulators and cellular trafficking proteins: CSNK1A1 (casein kinase 1 alpha, Wnt signaling negative regulator, 0.599), TBC1D31 (TBC1 domain family member 31, ciliogenesis regulator, 0.512), MRPL23 (mitochondrial ribosomal protein L23, translation, 0.501), RAB5IF (RAB5 interaction factor, endosomal trafficking, 0.470), TP53I13 (tumor protein p53 inducible 13, apoptosis regulation, 0.463), CSNK1E (casein kinase 1 epsilon, circadian rhythm/Wnt signaling, 0.452), DERL1 (derlin 1, ERAD protein degradation, 0.438), ZHX3 (zinc fingers and homeoboxes 3, transcriptional repression, 0.425), and DHX35 (DEAH-box helicase 35, RNA splicing, 0.421). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The FAM83 domain architecture with N-terminal phospholipase D-like fold combined with CK1 scaffolding domain (IPR012461) strongly suggests FAM83A functions as a casein kinase 1 regulatory scaffold protein involved in EGFR signaling pathway modulation and keratinocyte proliferation. CSNK1A1 interaction (0.599) is mechanistically central—casein kinase 1 alpha phosphorylates multiple substrates in Wnt signaling, circadian rhythm, and DNA damage response pathways, and FAM83A serves as an anchoring protein recruiting CK1α to specific subcellular compartments or substrate complexes. CSNK1E association (0.452) extends this regulatory role to casein kinase 1 epsilon, which phosphorylates PER/CRY circadian clock proteins and Dishevelled in Wnt signaling, suggesting FAM83A coordinates multiple CK1 isoforms. The phospholipase D-like domain (cd09181, aa 24-298) is catalytically degenerate but retains structural homology to PLD enzymes, suggesting potential lipid-binding or membrane association function despite loss of catalytic activity. Esophagus enrichment (59.6 nTPM) indicates specialized function in stratified squamous epithelium, consistent with FAM83A role in keratinocyte proliferation and epidermal differentiation. Cancer-enhanced expression and proto-oncogene designation indicate oncogenic function—FAM83A overactivation promotes EGFR signaling, cell proliferation, and tumor growth through CK1α-mediated phosphorylation of pathway components. Nucleoplasm/cytosol localization suggests shuttling between compartments, potentially coordinating nuclear CK1 substrates (transcription factors, chromatin regulators) with cytoplasmic signaling (EGFR, Wnt). Tissue-enriched pattern indicates specific upregulation in epithelial lineages. Moderate pLDDT score (72.4) indicates generally folded structure with some flexible regions, consistent with scaffolding proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly. Pathogenic 8q CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting CK1 signaling regulation, EGFR pathway modulation, and epithelial proliferation control, potentially contributing to 8q duplication syndrome (8q24 amplification in cancer) phenotypes including developmental defects, epithelial hyperplasia, and oncogenic transformation through constitutive CK1α activation and enhanced EGFR/Wnt signaling.
