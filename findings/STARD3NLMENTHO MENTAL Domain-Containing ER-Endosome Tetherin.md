## Description
STARD3NL (STARD3 N-Terminal Like, also known as MENTHO, MLN64N, UniProt O95772) is a 234-amino acid late-endosomal membrane protein belonging to the STARD3 family that functions as a critical cholesterol transport regulator and ER-endosome tethering factor through MENTAL domain-mediated lipid binding and VAP protein interactions. The protein architecture features MENTAL domain (IPR019498/PS51439 aa 48-218) forming the characteristic MLN64 N-terminal fold for cholesterol recognition and binding, complete STARD3 family membership (IPR051869/PTHR46121 aa 1-224) indicating evolutionary relationship to steroidogenic acute regulatory protein-related lipid transfer proteins, Cholesterol-capturing domain (PF10457 aa 49-214) enabling sterol sequestration from endosomal membranes, and GO term annotations consistent with transporter molecular function and vesicle-associated biological processes. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 62.2), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered MENTAL domain core surrounded by flexible regions—consistent with membrane-associated tethering proteins requiring conformational flexibility for dynamic organelle contact formation.
