# CCDC24: Coiled-Coil Scaffold Protein with Nucleolar Localization and Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
CCDC24 encodes a 307-amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt Q8N4L8) characterized by an extended coiled-coil structural motif spanning residues 21-206 (PF15669, IPR031367). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 66.1), consistent with coiled-coil proteins that form elongated helical bundles suitable for protein-protein interactions and scaffolding functions. HPA expression profiling reveals low tissue specificity with detection across many tissues, and subcellular localization to nucleoli, plasma membrane, and cytosol—suggesting potential roles in ribosome biogenesis (nucleolar function) and membrane-associated signaling complexes. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (1p34.2-34.1 deletions), copy number gains, and structural rearrangements, indicating dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and triplosomy may cause disease phenotypes. The coiled-coil architecture suggests CCDC24 functions as a molecular scaffold that oligomerizes or bridges protein complexes, potentially involved in nucleolar organization, pre-ribosomal particle assembly, or cell cycle regulation given the chromosomal instability associated with CNVs. The specific interaction partners, upstream regulators, and downstream effectors remain uncharacterized. The blastocyst hatching annotation from Alliance of Genome Resources suggests possible developmental roles in early embryogenesis, though this requires experimental validation.
