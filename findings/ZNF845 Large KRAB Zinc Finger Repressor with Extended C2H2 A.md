## Description
ZNF845 encodes a 970 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 8-48) that recruits the TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex for heterochromatin formation, followed by an extended array of C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) spanning residues 186-674 that mediate sequence-specific DNA recognition. The large size (970 aa vs typical 400-600 aa KRAB-ZFPs) suggests additional regulatory domains or extended linker regions. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=70.2) indicates a well-folded protein with confident structural modeling across the zinc finger array. HPA data shows expression in many tissues with low tissue specificity and predicted nuclear localization, consistent with chromatin-associated transcriptional regulation function. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with TRIM28 (score 0.427), the canonical KRAB coreceptor, plus related zinc finger proteins ZNF816 (0.543), ZNF235 (0.464), and ZNF468 (0.448), suggesting cooperative DNA binding or shared target gene regulation. Additional partners include RFC2 (0.442), a replication factor, and RNF166 (0.427), an E3 ubiquitin ligase, hinting at roles in DNA replication-coupled chromatin assembly. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 19q13.33-13.43, implicating ZNF845 dosage sensitivity in human disease. The mechanistic model proposes ZNF845 binds specific DNA motifs via its extended zinc finger array, recruits TRIM28/SETDB1 histone methyltransferase complexes through the KRAB domain, and establishes H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks to silence target genes involved in cell cycle progression and differentiation.
