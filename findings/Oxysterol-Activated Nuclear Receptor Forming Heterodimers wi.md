## Description
NR1H2 encodes Liver X Receptor beta (LXRβ), a 460 aa nuclear receptor (UniProt P55055) belonging to the NR1H subfamily of ligand-activated transcription factors. InterPro reveals canonical nuclear receptor architecture: N-terminal C4-type zinc finger DNA-binding domain (IPR001628, residues 84-161) recognizing AGGTCA direct repeat motifs, and C-terminal ligand-binding domain (IPR000536, residues 222-460) binding oxidized cholesterol derivatives (oxysterols). AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 80.2) consistent with stable nuclear receptor fold. STRING reveals obligate heterodimerization with RXRA (0.998) and paralog NR1H3/LXRα (0.915), plus coactivators NCOA1 (0.923)/NCOA2 (0.868), O-linked glycosyltransferase OGT (0.903), and target genes ABCA1 (0.468)/ABCG5 (0.524) for cholesterol efflux. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasm/vesicle localization, classified as transcription factor and cancer-related gene. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 19q13.33-13.43. Functional mechanism: LXRβ senses intracellular oxysterol levels (24(S)-hydroxycholesterol, 27-hydroxycholesterol), undergoes conformational change, recruits coactivators via AF-2 domain, and induces transcription of ABC transporters (ABCA1, ABCG1, ABCG5/8) promoting reverse cholesterol transport. Also regulates fatty acid synthesis (SREBP1c), inflammation (NF-κB suppression), and glucose metabolism. Unlike inducible LXRα (liver-enriched), LXRβ is constitutively expressed, making it attractive therapeutic target for atherosclerosis, Alzheimer\'s (brain cholesterol)
