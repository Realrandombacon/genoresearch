## Description
LUC7L (Q9NQ29) encodes a 371 amino acid nucleoplasmic and mitochondrial RNA-binding protein that functions as a core U1 snRNP spliceosome component regulating mRNA splice site recognition and alternative splicing programs, controls skeletal muscle growth and development through alternative splicing regulation identified via nanopore and Illumina sequencing integration, modulates diabetic nephropathy progression through the circ-Luc7l/miR-205-5p/Tgfbr1 competitive endogenous RNA axis reducing mesangial cell proliferation inflammation and extracellular matrix accumulation, and drives myeloproliferative neoplasms with eosinophilia through novel LUC7L::NUTM1 fusion oncogene formation.
