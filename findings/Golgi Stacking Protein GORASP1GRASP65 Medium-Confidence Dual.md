## Description
GORASP1 (UniProt Q9BQQ3), also known as GRASP65 or GOLPH5, encodes a 440 aa peripheral membrane protein functioning as the primary structural organizer of cis-Golgi stack formation and mitotic Golgi fragmentation. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved dual PDZ domain architecture characteristic of GRASP family proteins: two tandem GRASP-type PDZ domains (IPR024958/PF04495/PS51865/IPR036034/G3DSA:2.30.42.10, residues 15-105/111-199/69-204/1-210/12-77/95-181) mediating homotypic and heterotypic protein-protein interactions (GO:0005515) essential for Golgi cisternal stacking; membership in GRASP55/65 family (IPR007583/PTHR12893, residues 1-425) indicating evolutionary conservation from yeast to mammals; and PDZ superfamily classification confirming role as scaffold protein organizing membrane protein complexes. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT 63.1) indicating a partially structured protein with ordered PDZ domains connected by flexible linker regions and intrinsically disordered C-terminal tail consistent with peripheral membrane association requiring conformational flexibility for stacking interactions. STRING interactions define an ultra-high confidence Golgi apparatus network: GOLGA2 (0.999, golgin subfamily A member 2/gm130, cis-Golgi matrix protein forming complex with GORASP1), BLZF1 (0.980, basic leucine zipper nuclear factor 1, Golgi localization factor), GOLGB1 (0.931, golgin subfamily B member 1/giantin, trans-Golgi membrane protein), SEC24B (0.877, SEC24 homolog B, COPII vesicle coat component for ER-to-Golgi transport), SMU1 (0.835, suppressor of mec-8 and unc-52 homolog, spliceosome component with Golgi functions), STK25 (0.829, serine/threonine kinase 25, phosphorylates GORASP1 regulating Golgi morphology), GOLGA3 (0.527, golgin subfamily A member 3), USO1 (0.514, universal stress protein 1/vesicle docking protein), PLK1 (0.494, polo-like kinase 1, mitotic kinase phosphorylating GORASP1 triggering Golgi fragmentation), and STX5 (0.413, syntaxin 5, SNARE protein for Golgi vesicle fusion). The perfect GOLGA2 score confirms GORASP1-GM130 complex as fundamental cis-Golgi stacking module. HPA classifies GORASP1 as low tissue specificity detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, exclusively localized to Golgi apparatus, involved in protein transport reflecting housekeeping roles in secretory pathway across all cell types. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting of large copy number gains (3p26.3-14.3x3, 3p24.3-21.31x2,3, 3p26.3-q29x3, 3p22.2-21.31x3, 3p26.3-22.1x3, 3p22.3-22.1x3) and deletions (3p26.3-14.3del) plus single allele duplication though no single-gene disorder name is assigned; critically, 2025 literature identifies first biallelic variant causing novel "Golgipathy" with glycosylation defects and mitotic progression abnormalities confirming GORASP1 haploinsufficiency causes human disease. Functional hypothesis: GORASP1 operates through integrated Golgi organization mechanisms: (1) Cis-Golgi stack formation - Dual PDZ domains mediate homotypic GORASP1-GORASP1 trans-oligomerization bridging adjacent cisternae while heterotypic interaction with GOLGA2/GM130 anchors complex to cis-Golgi membrane via myristoylated N-terminus; (2) Unconventional protein secretion - SEC24B interaction suggests role in COPII vesicle formation for ER-to-Golgi transport of cargo proteins; (3) Mitotic Golgi fragmentation - PLK1 phosphorylates conserved C-terminal serine residues during prophase triggering GORASP1 dissociation and Golgi unstacking ensuring equal partitioning to daughter cells; (4) Kinase regulation - STK25 phosphorylation controls interphase Golgi morphology and cell migration; (5) Disease mechanism - Biallelic loss causes Golgipathy with defective glycosylation (impaired processing of N-linked glycans due to disrupted Golgi architecture) and mitotic defects (aberrant Golgi fragmentation causing cell cycle arrest); CNV gains may cause overexpression altering Golgi dynamics; 3p22.2 location places it in region associated with developmental disorders; (6) Conservation - Mouse Gorasp1 (Q91X51, 446 aa), Rat Gorasp1 (O35254, 451 aa) indicate essential conserved function across mammals. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: complete substrate repertoire for PDZ domain interactions, structural basis for homotypic vs heterotypic PDZ binding specificity, mechanism of unconventional protein secretion beyond canonical secretory pathway, contribution to specific Golgipathy phenotypes beyond glycosylation/mitotic defects, genotype-phenotype correlations for 3p22.2 CNV variants, functional divergence from paralog GORASP2/GRASP55 (trans-Golgi stacking), post-translational modifications beyond phosphorylation (ubiquitination, acetylation), therapeutic potential in congenital disorders of glycosylation and cancer (Golgi disruption sensitizes to apoptosis), cell-type specific Golgi organization requirements, crosstalk with autophagy (GORASP2 mediates autophagosome-lysosome fusion), and role in viral replication (many viruses hijack Golgi for assembly).
