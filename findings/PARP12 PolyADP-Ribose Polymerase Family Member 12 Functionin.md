## Description
PARP12 (Q9H0J9) encodes a 701 amino acid Golgi-associated mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase that functions as a critical regulator of cell death checkpoints controlling the balance between necroptosis and apoptosis through RIPK1 modification, coordinates antiviral innate immunity via RIG-I (DDX58) and MDA5 (IFIH1) pathway activation, drives osteoarthritis cartilage degradation by inhibiting PINK1/Parkin-dependent mitophagy through ISG15-mediated attenuation of MFN1/2 ubiquitylation and SUMOylation, and regulates breast cancer cell fate through AKT activation and DNA damage response modulation.
