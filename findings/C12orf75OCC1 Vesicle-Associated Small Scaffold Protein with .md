# C12orf75/OCC1: Vesicle-Associated Small Scaffold Protein with BTRC/IRS4 Interaction Network Implicated in 12q Copy Number Disorders and Colon Carcinoma

## Description
C12orf75 (alias OCC1, AGD3, overexpressed in colon carcinoma 1) encodes a 63 amino acid partially structured vesicle-associated protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 68.4) containing an OCC1 family domain (IPR029133/PF15506, aa 1-62) and belonging to the PTHR38502 family (aa 1-63). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in blood vessel (129.2 nTPM) and seminal vesicle (102.9 nTPM), detected in all tissues distribution, vesicles subcellular localization, cancer-enhanced specificity, immune cell-enhanced blood cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 12q23.1-24.33 and 12p13.33-q24.33 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with ubiquitination and insulin signaling regulators: BTRC (beta-transducin repeat containing E3 ubiquitin protein ligase, NF-kB pathway, 0.557), IRS4 (insulin receptor substrate 4, PI3K-AKT signaling, 0.555), LUZP6 (leucine zipper protein 6, cytoskeletal organization, 0.470), C6orf132 (chromosome 6 ORF 132, 0.468), ELAVL1 (ELAV like RNA binding protein 1, mRNA stability, 0.468), UNC119 (unc-119 lipid chaperone, ciliary trafficking, 0.422), and INSYN2B (insyn-2b, synaptic scaffolding, 0.407). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The OCC1 domain architecture combined with vesicle localization and BTRC/IRS4 interactions strongly suggests C12orf75 functions as a vesicle-associated scaffold protein involved in ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation and insulin/PI3K-AKT signaling. BTRC is an F-box E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate receptor that targets phosphorylated substrates for proteasomal degradation, including NF-kB inhibitors and cell cycle regulators. The BTRC interaction (0.557) places C12orf75 at the ubiquitination machinery interface, potentially serving as an adaptor protein recruiting specific substrates. IRS4 interaction (0.555) links C12orf75 to insulin receptor signaling cascade, potentially modulating PI3K-AKT activation through scaffold assembly at vesicular compartments. Vesicle localization indicates function in endosomal/secretory vesicle trafficking, potentially coordinating receptor turnover with signaling output. Cancer-enhanced expression (colon carcinoma name origin) suggests oncogenic role through dysregulated ubiquitination or growth factor signaling. Blood vessel enrichment (129.2 nTPM) indicates specialized function in endothelial cells, potentially regulating angiogenesis through VEGF receptor trafficking or NF-kB-mediated inflammatory responses. Immune cell enhancement suggests role in immune receptor signaling or cytokine secretion. Medium pLDDT score (68.4) indicates partial disorder with structured core, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly. Pathogenic 12q CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting ubiquitin-mediated proteostasis, insulin signaling, and vesicular trafficking, potentially contributing to 12q duplication/deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental defects, metabolic dysfunction, and cancer predisposition through impaired protein degradation and growth factor signaling coordination.
