# ZNF132: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Chromatin Regulatory Function

**Date:** 2026-03-23T04:11:36.893772

## Description
ZNF132 encodes a 706-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt P52740) that functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 37-78) which recruits the corepressor KAP1/TRIM28 and associated heterochromatin machinery including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 proteins. The C-terminal region harbors multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) arranged in tandem repeats at positions 114-141, 142-169, 236-263, 291-313, 347-369, and 375-397, providing sequence-specific DNA recognition capability. HPA expression profiling shows low tissue specificity with detection across multiple tissues, localized to vesicles and intermediate filaments. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains spanning 19q13.41-13.43, suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential disease associations though specific phenotypes remain undefined. As a KRAB-ZFP family member, ZNF132 likely silences endogenous retroviral elements and repetitive sequences through H3K9me3 deposition, maintaining genomic stability and epigenetic regulation. The specific DNA binding motifs, target gene repertoire, and tissue-specific regulatory networks remain uncharacterized, representing key knowledge gaps.
