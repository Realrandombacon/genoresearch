## Description
NR4A1 (NUR77/TR3) is a well-studied nuclear transcription factor belonging to the steroid-thyroid hormone-retinoid receptor superfamily. Structurally, it is a partially structured protein (AlphaFold pLDDT: 66.1) containing conserved Zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR001628) and a Ligand-binding domain (IPR000536). Functionally, it acts as a key regulator of inflammatory response and transcription, forming high-confidence complexes with BCL2 (0.993), AKT1 (0.956), and EP300 (0.849) to modulate cell survival and metabolism. Expression is enriched in blood cells and ubiquitous, with low tissue specificity. Clinically, it is associated with 6 pathogenic copy number variants (gains/losses) on chromosome 12, suggesting a role in developmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. Recent literature (1683 papers) highlights its critical roles in hepatic lipid metabolism, cardiac remodeling, platelet activation, and T cell exhaustion.
