# Chromosome 22q11.21 CTCF Insulator Element with 31 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants

## Description
LOC108491837 is a characterized non-coding regulatory element on chromosome 22q11.21 (NC_000022.11, positions 22559161-22559475, ~314 bp region) designated as "IGL region enhancer-blocking element 22-2" with exceptional clinical significance. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding element), preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases, and Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding regulatory function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 22q11.21-11.23 region (variants include 155200, 153062, 152705, 151375, 150486, 150466, 148861, 148699, 148615, 148420). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, hemizygous) and duplications (copy number gain) ranging from focal alterations (~1 Mb) to large deletions (19+ Mb). The 22q11.21 chromosomal location is clinically notable as this region is the critical locus for 22q11.2 deletion syndrome (DiGeorge syndrome/velocardiofacial syndrome, characterized by conotruncal cardiac defects, palatal abnormalities, hypoparathyroidism, thymic hypoplasia, and neurodevelopmental disorders). The region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including TBX1 (T-box transcription factor 1, major driver of 22q11.2 syndrome phenotypes), COMT (catechol-O-methyltransferase, neuropsychiatric susceptibility), DGCR8 (DiGeorge syndrome critical region gene 8, microRNA processing), and the immunoglobulin lambda locus (IGL) genes. Functional hypothesis: LOC108491837 functions as a CTCF-binding chromatin insulator element that establishes topological domain boundaries regulating expression of TBX1 and neighboring 22q11.21 genes. The 31 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations disrupt chromatin architecture and long-range gene regulation. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency of TBX1 through enhancer disruption or topological domain alteration leading to classic DiGeorge syndrome features, or dysregulation of DGCR14 and IGL locus affecting immune development. The element overlaps the 5′ ends of PRAME (preferentially expressed antigen in melanoma, cancer-testis antigen) and LOC648691 (antisense lncRNA), suggesting bidirectional regulatory function. The absence of protein-coding features combined with exceptional ClinVar evidence (31 variants, highest among analyzed loci) and developmental/immunological disease association suggests this locus functions as a master chromatin boundary element controlling embryonic patterning, cardiac development, and lymphocyte differentiation through CTCF-mediated loop extrusion and enhancer blocking.
