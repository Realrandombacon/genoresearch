## Description

Structurally, it features a canonical Ribosomal L1 domain (IPR016095, PF00687) with an alpha/beta-sandwich fold, but also contains disordered regions (AlphaFold pLDDT 66.8), enabling flexible interactions with partners like BOP1, NOP58, and NIP7. Functionally, it localizes to the nucleolus, mitotic chromosomes, and primary cilium, suggesting a role in RNA processing and ciliary signaling. Clinically, it exhibits a 'dark gene' profile with 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily CNVs/Duplications) but zero explicitly tagged diseases in ClinVar. This suggests a dosage-sensitive mechanism where excess copies disrupt nucleolar architecture or cause ribosomal stress, leading to cellular senescence or apoptosis. HPA expression confirms essential protein status with broad tissue distribution.
