## Description
KCNIP2 (Kv Channel Interacting Protein 2) is a 270 aa calcium-binding regulatory protein representing a partially characterized gene with 51 literature references, functioning as a critical auxiliary subunit of voltage-gated potassium channels (Kv4.x) that controls cardiac action potential repolarization, neuronal firing patterns, and spinal cold sensory processing through calcium-dependent modulation. Multi-omics data reveals KCNIP2 as a vertebrate-conserved EF-hand protein with defined calcium-binding domains yet emerging disease characterization through recent 2022-2025 discoveries in cardiac electrophysiology, tumorigenesis, and somatosensory processing. UniProt accession Q9NS61 confirms the 270 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q9JJ69, 270 aa, ~99% identity), rat (Q9JM59, 270 aa), and ferret (Q8WN03, 270 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the potassium channel regulatory machinery across mammals. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the recoverin family (IPR028846, aa 84-261; GO:0005509 calcium ion binding) containing multiple EF-hand calcium-binding domains (IPR002048, aa 140-259; IPR011992, aa 89-268; IPR018247, aa 153-165/189-201/237-249; PF13499, aa 179-252; PF13833, aa 141-166; SM00054) that confer calcium sensitivity to channel modulation. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 69.3) across the full 270 aa length, indicating a partially structured protein with ordered EF-hand domains and potentially disordered N-terminal regions—consistent with calcium-sensing regulatory proteins requiring conformational flexibility. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with top expression in heart muscle (286.7 nTPM), brain (247.2 nTPM), and adipose tissue (213.5 nTPM), with vesicle subcellular localization and cancer-enhanced/immune cell-enhanced RNA specificity—consistent with roles in cardiac electrophysiology and neuronal function. These interactions confirm KCNIP2 operates as an auxiliary subunit of A-type potassium channels (KCND family).
