## Description
GPR19 encodes a 415 amino acid class A rhodopsin-like G protein-coupled receptor (UniProt Q15760) with seven transmembrane helices characteristic of the rhodopsin family. InterPro analysis reveals canonical 7TM architecture (PF00001, IPR000276, IPR017452) spanning residues 67-341, with conserved GPCR signature motifs across all seven helical domains. AlphaFold prediction yields a confident structure (global pLDDT 74.4) with well-defined transmembrane bundle topology. HPA expression shows neural-endocrine tropism: brain (7.8 nTPM), pituitary gland (7.4 nTPM), and testis (3.7 nTPM), consistent with neuromodulatory function. STRING interactions reveal connections to transcriptional regulators (ETV6, score 0.736), cell cycle control (CDKN1B, score 0.567), and related GPCRs (GPR82, GPR22, GPR88, scores 0.467-0.515), suggesting integration of GPCR signaling with proliferative pathways. ClinVar documents 45 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 12p copy number alterations, implicating GPR19 dosage sensitivity in disease. Conservation analysis shows orthologs in mouse (Q61121, 100% length conservation) and rat (P70585), indicating strong evolutionary constraint. I propose GPR19 functions as an orphan GPCR modulating neuroendocrine cell proliferation through cAMP/cell cycle cross-talk, with ETV6 and CDKN1B interactions suggesting direct coupling to transcriptional and cell cycle machinery.
