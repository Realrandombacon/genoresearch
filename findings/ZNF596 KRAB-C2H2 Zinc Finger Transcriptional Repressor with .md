## Description
ZNF596 encodes a 504 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 8p23.3. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical domain architecture: an N-terminal KRAB box (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 6-78) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 196-273) arranged in tandem repeats. The KRAB domain is a well-characterized transcriptional repression module that typically recruits the corepressor KAP1/TRIM28, which in turn recruits heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) and histone methyltransferases to silence target genes through H3K9me3 deposition. The C2H2 zinc fingers provide sequence-specific DNA binding, with each finger recognizing ~3 base pairs, suggesting ZNF596 targets specific genomic loci. AlphaFold predicts a confident 3D structure (pLDDT 70.3) across the full 504 aa length, indicating a well-folded protein. Human Protein Atlas data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity (detected in all), but with enhanced expression in specific cell types and dual subcellular localization to nucleoli and mitochondria. The nucleolar localization is particularly notable, as this compartment is the site of ribosome biogenesis and rRNA processing, suggesting ZNF596 may regulate ribosomal RNA transcription or processing. ClinVar contains 130 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 8p23.3, indicating dosage sensitivity and disease relevance. The combination of KRAB repression domain, multiple DNA-binding zinc fingers, nucleolar localization, and widespread expression suggests ZNF596 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that may regulate ribosome biogenesis genes or nucleolar organizer regions. The high variant burden implies that ZNF596 dysfunction contributes to disease phenotypes, potentially through dysregulation of cell proliferation or stress response pathways.
