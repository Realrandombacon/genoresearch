## Description
RIC1 (Guanine Nucleotide Exchange Factor Subunit RIC1, UniProt Q4ADV7) is a 1423 aa Golgi-associated protein belonging to the Rab6A-GEF complex partner protein family (IPR040096/PTHR22746, positions 1-1361/1-1361) with multiple WD40/beta-propeller domains (IPR015943/IPR036322/SSF50978/PF25440, positions 205-480/13-379/13-96+137-379/474-823) and C-terminal alpha solenoid region (IPR009771/PF07064, positions 829-987/829-987) that functions as an essential component of the Ric1-Rgp1 guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor complex for Rab6 GTPase activation and Golgi-to-TGN membrane trafficking. The molecular architecture consists of two beta-propeller domains (positions 13-379 and 474-823) connected by linker regions to a C-terminal alpha solenoid (positions 829-987), forming an extended scaffold for protein-protein interactions characteristic of GEF regulatory subunits. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded protein (pLDDT 72.9, confident) indicating stable 3D structure with organized beta-propeller and alpha solenoid domains—typical architecture for trafficking regulators requiring structural rigidity for complex assembly. Literature (92 papers) establishes RIC1 roles in: (1) structural basis for Rab6 activation by Ric1-Rgp1 complex with mechanistic insights into nucleotide exchange (2024, 6 citations), (2) SLC1A1-RIC1 fusion sensitive to asparaginase-based therapy in natural killer/T-cell lymphoma (2023, 1 citation), (3) genome-wide CNV analysis identifying RIC1 as cleft lip/palate gene (2022, 18 citations), (4) Ric1-Rgp1 GEF complex structure and function in Golgi trafficking.
