# Uncharacterized Locus at 6p24.1 within PHACTR1/FOXC1 Regulatory Region with Pathogenic Variants Linked to Vascular and Craniofacial Development

## Description
LOC100130357 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 6p24.1 (NC_000006.12:13295585-13273093) spanning approximately 22.5 kb. UniProt search identified one homologous readthrough protein (A0A8T1SHP2: TBC1D7-LOC100130357) from snapping turtle (Chelydra serpentina, 293 aa), but no human UniProt accession was found, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners for human ortholog, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000215022 but found no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, comprising both copy number losses (chr6:12020008-22846454, ~10.8 Mb; chr6:156974-13855925, ~13.7 Mb) and single nucleotide variants/indels within PHACTR1 (phosphatase actin regulator 1: c.1606C>G p.Arg536Gly, c.1551G>T p.Lys517Asn, c.1555C>G p.Leu519Val, c.1510-6_1529del, c.1713T>G p.Ser571Arg, c.1454A>G p.Asn485Ser, c.1499T>C p.Leu500Pro, c.1561C>T p.Arg521Cys). PHACTR1 is a critical regulator of endothelial cell migration, actin cytoskeleton organization, and vascular development, with genome-wide association studies linking PHACTR1 variants to coronary artery disease, migraine, and fibromuscular dysplasia. The 6p24.1 region also contains FOXC1 (forkhead box C1), a transcription factor essential for anterior eye segment development and cerebellar morphogenesis; FOXC1 haploinsufficiency causes Axenfeld-Rieger anomaly and Dandy-Walker malformation. LOC100130357 likely functions as part of the 6p24.1 cis-regulatory landscape, potentially containing enhancer elements or encoding a long non-coding RNA that modulates PHACTR1 and/or FOXC1 expression through chromatin looping or transcriptional interference. The presence of 19 pathogenic variants including both structural and sequence-level alterations indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive and sequence-constrained genomic region where variation disrupts vascular development, endothelial function, and craniofacial morphogenesis via dysregulation of PHACTR1-mediated actin dynamics and FOXC1-dependent transcriptional programs.
