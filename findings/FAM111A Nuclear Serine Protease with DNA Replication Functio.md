## Description
FAM111A encodes a 611-amino acid nuclear serine protease containing a canonical trypsin-like peptidase domain (IPR009003/PF13365, residues 370-555) and a FAM111-specific N-terminal domain (IPR060141/PF26749, residues 73-267). The catalytic triad architecture is conserved within the peptidase S1 PA clan (G3DSA:2.40.10.10), indicating active proteolytic function. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=76.7) indicates confident folding throughout both domains, consistent with a stable enzymatic scaffold. Protein interaction analysis reveals high-confidence partnerships with DNA replication and repair machinery: TBCE (0.950, tubulin folding cofactor E, paralog interaction), BRCA1 (0.590, breast cancer DNA repair protein), SPRTN (0.528, DNA-dependent metalloprotease for DNA-protein crosslink repair), GCNA (0.507, germ cell nuclear antigen), and cullin ubiquitin ligases CUL7/CUL9 (0.468/0.464). The presence of a PCNA-interacting peptide (PIP) box enables co-localization with proliferating cell nuclear antigen at replication forks. Tissue expression is ubiquitous (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity), consistent with essential housekeeping function in DNA replication. Subcellular localization in nucleoplasm and nucleoli fibrillar center supports roles in ribosomal DNA processing and chromatin maintenance. Clinical significance includes 39 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants, including missense mutations (p.Ile488Thr, p.Arg569Cys, p.Ser466Cys, p.Ala484Asp) within the peptidase domain and copy number variations, associated with Kenny-Caffey syndrome (hypoparathyroidism, dwarfism, osteosclerosis) and growth retardation. Disease variant mapping to catalytic regions suggests loss of protease function disrupts DNA replication fork progression. The combination of trypsin-like protease architecture, PCNA interaction, DNA damage/repair annotations, and disease-associated mutations strongly suggests FAM111A functions as a replication-coupled protease that cleaves protein barriers (histones, DNA-bound proteins) at stalled replication forks, analogous to SPRTN but with distinct substrate specificity, enabling fork restart and genome stability maintenance.
