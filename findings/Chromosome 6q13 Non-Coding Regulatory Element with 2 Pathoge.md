## Description
LOC101928516 (Gene ID: 101928516, Ensembl: ENSG00000223786) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 6q13 (NC_000006.12, positions 74069450-74690726, ~621 kb region) with clinical significance evidenced by 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID (ENSG00000223786) but no quantitative expression data (nTPM unavailable). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 copy number loss/deletion (variant 154460: 6q12-14.2, chr6:64549655-83426791, ~18.9 Mb) and 1 copy number gain/duplication (variant 150937: 6q13-24.1, chr6:74382807-142040500, ~67.7 Mb). Both variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 6q13 chromosomal region contains critical translation, innate immunity, and extracellular matrix-associated genes including EEF1A1 (eukaryotic translation elongation factor 1 alpha 1, GTP-dependent aminoacyl-tRNA delivery, protein synthesis, cell proliferation, cancer progression), MIR30A (microRNA 30a, post-transcriptional gene silencing, tumor suppression, epithelial-mesenchymal transition regulation), CGAS (cyclic GMP-AMP synthase, cytosolic DNA sensor, cGAS-STING pathway, type I interferon production, antiviral immunity, autoinflammation), CD109 (CD109 molecule, GPI-anchored cell surface protein, TGF-beta signaling, platelet activation, squamous cell carcinoma), and COL9A1 (collagen type IX alpha 1 chain, cartilage extracellular matrix, intervertebral disc structure, Stickler syndrome, multiple epiphyseal dysplasia). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928516 likely represents a critical 6q13 enhancer element within the EEF1A1-CGAS regulatory region, potentially functioning as an immune cell-specific enhancer coordinating EEF1A1-CGAS-MIR30A expression in hematopoietic tissues (lymphocytes, monocytes, dendritic cells) or as a chromatin boundary separating translation machinery genes (EEF1A1) from innate immunity genes (CGAS) and cartilage structural genes (COL9A1). The 2 pathogenic CNVs (deletion ~18.9 Mb, duplication ~67.7 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts EEF1A1-mediated protein synthesis and CGAS-mediated cytosolic DNA sensing. Disease mechanism involves EEF1A1 overexpression causing cancer (translation hyperactivation, cell proliferation, tumor growth, chemoresistance), EEF1A1 mutation causing neurodevelopmental disorders (protein synthesis defect, intellectual disability, developmental delay), CGAS activation causing autoinflammatory disease (type I interferonopathy, Aicardi-Goutières syndrome, systemic lupus erythematosus, interferon signature), CGAS deficiency causing viral susceptibility (impaired DNA sensing, herpesvirus infection, HIV progression), MIR30A dysregulation causing cancer metastasis (EMT promotion, invasion, poor prognosis), CD109 overexpression causing squamous cell carcinoma (TGF-beta signaling modulation, tumor progression), COL9A1 mutation causing skeletal dysplasia (cartilage defect, intervertebral disc degeneration, Stickler syndrome, multiple epiphyseal dysplasia, hearing loss, retinal detachment), and 6q13 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with immune dysfunction and skeletal abnormalities. The 6q13 region is associated with translation and innate immunity genetic variation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (2 CNVs 18.9-67.7 Mb) and EEF1A1-CGAS-MIR30A-CD109-COL9A1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a hematopoietic/immune-specific enhancer critical for protein synthesis-cytosolic DNA sensing-microRNA regulation coordination.
