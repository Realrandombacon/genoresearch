## Description
CHCHD1 (UniProt Q96BP2), also known as mS37, encodes a compact 118-amino acid mitochondrial ribosomal small subunit protein. It features a defining Coiled-Coil-Helix-Coiled-Coil-Helix (CHCH) domain (IPR010625/PF06747/PS51808, aa 42-84) characteristic of mitochondrial intermembrane space proteins, embedded within the metazoan-specific mS37 family (IPR033620/PTHR31278, aa 5-115). The protein adopts a stable cysteine alpha-hairpin motif fold (IPR009069/SSF47072, aa 44-84). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT: 92.4), consistent with its role as a structured ribosomal component.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm exclusive association with the mitochondrial ribosome small subunit (SSU), showing high-confidence links to MRPS11 (0.993), MRPS16 (0.993), MRPS21 (0.993), MRPS2 (0.992), MRPS6 (0.992), MRPS23 (0.991), MRPS7 (0.988), MRPS24 (0.987), MRPS28 (0.986), and MRPS18C (0.978). HPA classifies CHCHD1 as an essential protein with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) and dual localization to mitochondria and nucleoli fibrillar centers, functioning as a ribonucleoprotein.\n\nWith only 8 papers, CHCHD1 remains a genuine dark gene despite its fundamental role. Foundational work (2013, 84 citations) identified it as a core member of the mammalian mitochondrial ribosome essential for synthesizing OXPHOS subunits. Emerging 2025 research reveals a non-canonical oncogenic function: CHCHD1 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression by regulating Transforming Growth Factor Beta Receptor 1 (TGFBR1) within the tumor immune microenvironment. Additional studies identify it as a reference gene for hypoxia in cervical cancer. ClinVar lists 14 pathogenic variants, primarily large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 10q, linking genomic instability at this locus to disease. This positions CHCHD1 as a critical but understudied node connecting mitochondrial translation fidelity with cancer immune evasion.
