## Description
SIKE1 encodes a 207-amino acid suppressor of IKBKE 1 (UniProt Q9BRV8), a small coiled-coil protein functioning as an integral component of the STRIPAK (STRiatin-Interacting Phosphatase And Kinase) supramolecular complex. The protein contains a SIKE family domain (IPR008555, PF05769, positions 1-202/2-187) with a spectrin repeat structural motif (SSF46966, positions 99-191) characteristic of scaffold proteins mediating protein-protein interactions through coiled-coil oligomerization. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.4, residues 1-207) indicating stable folding consistent with coiled-coil scaffold architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals SIKE1 forms ultra-high confidence interactions exclusively with STRIPAK complex members: SLMAP (0.999, sarcolemmal membrane-associated protein), MOB4 (0.998, Mps one binder kinase activator), STRN3 (0.998, striatin-3), STRIP1 (0.998, striatin-interacting protein 1), PDCD10 (0.998, programmed cell death 10), STRN4 (0.997, striatin-4), STK25 (0.996, serine/threonine kinase 25), STRN (0.978, striatin), STRIP2 (0.909, striatin-interacting protein 2), and FGFR1OP2 (0.851, FGFR1 oncogene partner 2), confirming its exclusive role as STRIPAK scaffold component. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, subcellular localization to vesicles and focal adhesion sites consistent with STRIPAK complex roles in cytoskeletal organization and cell adhesion. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.265+6T>G, c.265+3_265+6del) and copy number variations (1p13.3-13.1 losses, 1p22.1-11.2 gains), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With only 18 publications, SIKE1 is genuinely understudied: crystal structures of SLMAP-SIKE1 complex, STRN3-SIKE1 coiled-coil domain, and second coiled-coil domain were solved in 2019 (0 citations each), with one withdrawn paper on hepatic ischemia/reperfusion injury (2018, 1 cit), and recent GWAS linking SIKE1 region to nevus count and melanoma susceptibility (2025-2026). The functional hypothesis: SIKE1 serves as a coiled-coil scaffold adapter within the STRIPAK complex, bridging striatin family proteins (STRN3/4) with kinase/phosphatase modules (STK25, PP2A) to regulate Hippo pathway signaling, cell polarity, and cytoskeletal dynamics; the spectrin repeat domain mediates heterodimerization with SLMAP and other STRIPAK components, assembling a supramolecular machine that coordinates dephosphorylation of Hippo kinases (MST1/2, LATS1/2) and controls organ size, cell proliferation, and tumor suppression; disease variants may disrupt STRIPAK complex assembly, altering Hippo signaling output and contributing to melanoma susceptibility through dysregulated cell growth control; vesicle and focal adhesion localization suggests roles in trafficking and cell-matrix adhesion coordination.
