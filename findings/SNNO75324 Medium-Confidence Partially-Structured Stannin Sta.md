## Description
SNN (Stannin) is an 88 amino acid membrane protein (UniProt O75324) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 65.7), characteristic of small transmembrane proteins that function through membrane anchoring and protein-protein interactions. The protein contains a stannin transmembrane domain (IPR015135, residues 2-33; PF09049, residues 2-33) for membrane integration, a stannin unstructured linker region (IPR015136, residues 34-59; PF09050, residues 34-59) providing conformational flexibility, and a stannin cytoplasmic domain (IPR015137, residues 61-87; PF09051, residues 61-87) for protein interactions. SNN belongs to the stannin superfamily (IPR027435, residues 1-88; G3DSA:4.10.280.20, residues 1-88) and stannin family (IPR038747, residues 1-88; PTHR28564, residues 1-88; cd20257, residues 4-88), with subcellular localization to mitochondrial outer membrane (GO:0005741) and membrane (GO:0016020). SNN is located on chromosome 16p13.13 and enables metal ion binding activity, predicted to be involved in response to toxic substance. These interactions suggest SNN functions within oxidative stress response and MAPK signaling pathways, with TXNDC11 as the highest-confidence partner (0.556) reflecting potential redox regulation roles.
