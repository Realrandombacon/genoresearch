## Description
ZNF229 encodes an 825 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9UJW7) with characteristic domain architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 33-108) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 349-432). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT=63.0) consistent with KRAB-ZFP proteins containing structured zinc finger clusters connected by flexible linkers. HPA shows cancer-enhanced expression and immune cell-enhanced specificity, with detection across many tissues but low tissue specificity, suggesting context-dependent regulation. Seven pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar include copy number gains and losses spanning 19q13.31, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease. STRING interactions identify PDE3B (cGMP-specific phosphodiesterase, score 0.527) and OR4F6 (olfactory receptor, score 0.506), suggesting potential roles in cAMP/cGMP signaling modulation and cell-type specific regulation. The KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors to silence target genes via histone methylation (H3K9me3), while C2H2 zinc fingers confer sequence-specific DNA recognition. ZNF229 likely functions as a transcriptional repressor in immune cells and cancer contexts, potentially regulating inflammatory responses or cell proliferation genes. Copy number alterations suggest haploinsufficiency or overexpression phenotypes contribute to disease pathogenesis through dysregulated target gene networks.
