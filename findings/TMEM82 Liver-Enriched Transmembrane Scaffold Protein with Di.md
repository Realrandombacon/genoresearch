## Description
TMEM82 encodes a 343-amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt A0PJX8) with a conserved transmembrane domain (IPR031648, PF15816, residues 24-321). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 77.9), indicating stable 3D conformation. Conservation: mouse ortholog (Q8R115, 356 aa, 89% identity) and Xenopus (B0BMG8, 344 aa) indicate strong vertebrate conservation. Hypothesis: TMEM82 functions as a liver-specific membrane scaffold protein that stabilizes protein complexes at the hepatocyte plasma membrane or endoplasmic reticulum, potentially regulating lipid metabolism or bile acid transport. Disease mechanism: CNV-mediated loss disrupts membrane integrity, contributing to 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes.
