## Description
TLCD3A (TLC Domain-Containing Protein 3A) is a 257 amino acid integral membrane protein (UniProt Q8TBR7) with minimal characterization (2 papers) that functions in lipid transport, membrane homeostasis, and skin barrier integrity. The protein contains a canonical TRAM/LAG1/CLN8 homology (TLC) domain (IPR006634/PF03798/SM00724, residues 33-249) found in lipid transporters and ceramide synthases, indicating capacity for lipid binding and transmembrane lipid movement. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with GGT7 (0.927), a glutathione hydrolase involved in redox homeostasis, SLC3A2 (0.852), the heavy chain of heteromeric amino acid transporters, GEMIN4 (0.609), a component of the SMN complex, VPS53 (0.596), part of the GARP vesicle tethering complex, and MFSD12 (0.505), a lysosomal membrane transporter implicated in pigmentation. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skin (70.8 nTPM), detected in all tissues, and immune cell enhancement, consistent with barrier and immune functions. ClinVar contains 63 pathogenic copy number variants (losses and gains) at 17p13.3, though no specific diseases are yet assigned—suggesting potential dermatological or immunological phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 92.9) indicates a very well-folded protein with stable transmembrane architecture typical of lipid transporters. Functional hypothesis: TLCD3A acts as a membrane lipid transporter or flippase that facilitates transbilayer movement of glycosphingolipids or ceramides in epidermal keratinocytes and immune cells, regulating skin barrier formation, membrane composition, and inflammatory responses through lipid-mediated signaling, with loss-of-function potentially causing skin barrier defects, immunodeficiency, or lysosomal storage disorders through disrupted lipid homeostasis.
