## Description
RABL3 (RAB, member of RAS oncogene family like 3) is a 236 aa atypical small GTPase functioning as a critical regulator of KRAS prenylation, membrane trafficking, and oncogenic signaling, with emerging roles in pancreatic cancer, ciliogenesis, and gastric cancer progression. InterPro analysis confirms RABL3 as a canonical small GTPase with a conserved P-loop NTPase fold: the G3DSA:3.40.50.300 superfamily (aa 3-207) and IPR027417 homologous superfamily (aa 3-207) define the Rossmann-like nucleotide-binding domain, while specific domains include cd04102 (Rab GTPase-like family 3, aa 7-210), SM00175 (Rab subfamily, aa 7-187), and PS51419 (small GTPase Rab1 family profile, aa 1-236). The Roc domain (PF08477, aa 8-115) is characteristic of Ras-of-Complex proteins, suggesting potential dimerization or regulatory functions. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.7) consistent with a well-folded GTPase that undergoes conformational switching between active (GTP-bound) and inactive (GDP-bound) states. STRING interactions place RABL3 in trafficking and signaling networks: RAP1GDS1 (0.650), a guanine nucleotide dissociation stimulator for RAP1 and other small GTPases; GTF2E1 (0.589), a general transcription factor subunit; RABL6 (0.571), a paralogous atypical GTPase; RHOA (0.569), a Rho family GTPase regulating cytoskeleton; METTL14 (0.538), an m6A RNA methyltransferase; RAB3GAP1 (0.502), a Rab3 GTPase-activating protein; CLPB (0.496), a mitochondrial ATPase; RABGEF1 (0.490), a Rab guanine nucleotide exchange factor; RABGGTA (0.489), a Rab geranylgeranyltransferase subunit; and MAPK8 (0.488), a c-Jun N-terminal kinase. HPA shows ubiquitous RNA expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, endoplasmic reticulum (ER) subcellular localization, and classification as a disease-related and disease-variant protein, consistent with its role in KRAS processing. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Gly128Glu, p.Gly16Ser, p.Leu32Met), splice variants (c.607-10T>A), and copy number losses/gains on 3q11.1-21.2, though no specific RABL3-associated Mendelian disease is definitively established despite clear clinical significance. Literature (27 papers) establishes RABL3 as: mutated in hereditary pancreatic cancer (2019, 48 citations); regulating KRAS trafficking and activity (2024); interacting with RAB11 to control early ciliogenesis (2022, 3 citations); promoting gastric cancer progression via miR-145 sponging (2021, 14 citations); and suppressing oral squamous cell carcinoma via FAK/AKT pathway (2021, 2 citations).
