## Description
PRG2 (Proteoglycan 2, also known as Eosinophil Major Basic Protein or EMBP, UniProt P13727) is a 222 amino acid secreted protein with moderate-to-high human characterization (96 papers, predominantly immunology and emerging 2019-2020 neuronal growth/placental pathology studies with established but incomplete mechanistic understanding in non-immune contexts) representing a partially-characterized gene in human biology that functions as a potent cytotoxic toxin stored in eosinophil granules, driving helminth immunity, allergic inflammation, and placental tissue remodeling while recently discovered to regulate axonal branching via PTEN inhibition.
