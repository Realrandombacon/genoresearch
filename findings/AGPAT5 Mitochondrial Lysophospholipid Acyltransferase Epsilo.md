## Description
AGPAT5 encodes a 364 aa 1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase epsilon (UniProt Q9NUQ2) located at 8p23.3, functioning as a mitochondrial lysophospholipid acyltransferase (LPLAT) that catalyzes the acylation of lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) to phosphatidic acid (PA) in glycerophospholipid biosynthesis. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved acyltransferase architecture: LCLAT1-like lysophospholipid acyltransferase domain (cd07990, aa 63-263) characteristic of the LPLAT superfamily, central phospholipid/glycerol acyltransferase catalytic domain (IPR002123/PF01553/SM00563, aa 83-212) conferring acyltransferase activity (GO:0016746), C-terminal acyltransferase domain (IPR032098/PF16076, aa 251-326) for substrate recognition, and full-length 1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase family membership (PTHR10983, aa 7-350). AlphaFold predicts very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 94.1), indicating a well-folded enzyme with stable catalytic core suitable for membrane-associated lipid metabolism. STRING interactions reveal a specialized glycerophospholipid biosynthesis network: AGPAT1/2/3/4 (0.925-0.965, paralogous acyltransferases), MBOAT1/2 (0.928-0.950, membrane-bound O-acyltransferases), CDS1/2 (0.930-0.938, CDP-diacylglycerol synthases converting PA to CDP-DAG), PLPP4/5 (0.921-0.922, phospholipid phosphatases). HPA expression shows mitochondrial localization with tissue-enhanced RNA expression, highest in brain (19.4 nTPM), detected in all tissues with low cancer and immune cell specificity. ClinVar reports 66 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large 8p23 copy number losses (x1) and gains (x3), frameshift deletion c.859del (p.Ile287fs) truncating the C-terminal domain, suggesting clinical significance in chromosomal disorder syndromes affecting metabolism. Literature (40 papers) details emerging metabolic functions: (1) Glucose homeostasis - liver-specific Agpat5 deletion protecting against liquid sucrose-induced hyperinsulinemia and glucose intolerance in mice (0 citations, 2024); (2) Hypoglycemia sensing - Agpat5 in AgRP-neurons required for insulin-induced hypoglycemia sensing and glucagon secretion counterregulatory response (15 citations, 2022); (3) Obesity-associated hyperinsulinemia - Agpat5 promoting obesity-induced hyperinsulinemia in mouse genetic reference populations (0 citations, 2020); (4) Colorectal cancer - low AGPAT5 expression associated with advanced clinical stage and poor prognosis, contributing to tumor progression (2 citations, 2022). Functional hypothesis: AGPAT5 likely functions through: (1) Glycerophospholipid Biosynthesis - catalyzing the committed step converting LPA to PA using acyl-CoA donors, determining fatty acid composition of downstream phospholipids (phosphatidylcholine, phosphatidylethanolamine, cardiolipin); (2) Mitochondrial Cardiolipin Remodeling - mitochondrial localization suggesting role in cardiolipin biosynthesis/remodeling critical for electron transport chain function and mitochondrial membrane integrity; (3) Hypothalamic Glucose Sensing - AgRP-neuron expression enabling detection of insulin-induced hypoglycemia through lipid signaling intermediates (PA, DAG) that modulate neuronal excitability and glucagon secretion; (4) Insulin Sensitivity Regulation - hepatic AGPAT5 activity influencing insulin signaling through lipid intermediate accumulation (PA, DAG) that activate PKCε and impair insulin receptor substrate phosphorylation; (5) Cancer Metabolism - altered AGPAT5 expression in colorectal cancer affecting membrane phospholipid composition, cell proliferation, and metastatic potential through lipid signaling pathways. The conserved acyltransferase fold and mitochondrial targeting parallel AGPAT2 (linked to congenital generalized lipodystrophy), suggesting overlapping yet non-redundant functions in tissue-specific lipid metabolism. Disease implications include: 8p23 deletion/duplication syndromes, type 2 diabetes, obesity, hypoglycemia unawareness, colorectal cancer progression, potential cardiolipinopathies.
