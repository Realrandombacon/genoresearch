## Description
KCTD9 (Potassium channel tetramerization domain containing 9, UniProt Q7L273, 389 aa) is a well-characterized E3 ubiquitin ligase component with 33 publications, functioning as a scaffold for CUL3-based E3 ligase complexes that regulate TOP2A ubiquitination and colorectal carcinoma metastasis. InterPro analysis reveals a modular architecture: KHA domain (IPR021789, cd17073, positions 2-82/2-66) mediating dimerization and doublecortin-like interactions; BTB/POZ domain (IPR000210, cd18368, IPR003131, positions 89-192/89-188/92-181) facilitating protein homooligomerization and protein binding; and a C-terminal E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase SopA-like domain (G3DSA:2.160.20.80, IPR036572 homologous superfamily, positions 211-386) responsible for ubiquitin transfer. STRING interactions (10 total) confirm its role in the CUL3-ROC1-RBX1 complex (score 0.893 with CUL3) and regulation of DNA topoisomerase II (TOP2A, score 0.734; TOP2B, score 0.735), linking it to cell cycle control and genomic stability. HPA expression data indicates low tissue specificity but cell-type enhanced expression, with subcellular localization in the nucleoplasm, intermediate filaments, and cytosol, consistent with its role in Ubl conjugation pathways. ClinVar identifies 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations (CNVs) spanning 8p23.3-11.21 (chr8:158048-41600696), including gains and losses, suggesting a critical role in chromosomal stability and potential involvement in developmental disorders or cancer predisposition. AlphaFold (AF-Q7L273-F1, pLDDT 89.8) predicts a high-confidence, well-folded structure, supporting the functional integrity of the BTB/POZ and KHA domains. Mechanistically, KCTD9 acts as a critical hub: its interaction with CUL3 recruits TOP2A for ubiquitination; loss of KCTD9 function or CNV-mediated disruption of the 8p23.3-11.21 region likely leads to accumulation of TOP2A, DNA replication stress, and genomic instability, driving metastasis in colorectal carcinoma. This positions KCTD9 not merely as a structural scaffold but as a central regulator of the ubiquitin-proteasome system and DNA topology, with clinical relevance in cancer progression and potential therapeutic targets for modulating TOP2A levels.
