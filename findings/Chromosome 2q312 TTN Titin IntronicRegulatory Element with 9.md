## Description
LOC101927055 (Gene ID: 101927055) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 2q31.2 (NC_000002.12, positions 178776925-178779962, ~3 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 92 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 92 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: ALL variants map to TTN (titin, NM_001267550.2) including nonsense mutations (c.4619C>A p.Ser1540Ter, c.4510A>T p.Lys1504Ter, c.4834G>T p.Glu1612Ter), frameshift mutations (c.4739_4740insTT p.Gly1581fs, c.4854_4858del p.Ser1619fs, c.4634dup p.Leu1545fs, c.3850del p.Ser1284fs), splice site mutations (c.4815-1G>A), and missense mutations (c.4412G>A p.Gly1471Glu). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 2q31.2 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including TTN (titin, sarcomere elastic filament, cardiac/skeletal muscle contraction, dilated cardiomyopathy, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, limb-girdle muscular dystrophy, centronuclear myopathy), NFE2L2/NRF2 (NFE2 like bZIP transcription factor 2, oxidative stress response, cytoprotection, cancer chemoresistance), PRKRA (protein activator of interferon induced protein kinase EIF2AK2/PKR, antiviral response, stress granule formation, dystonia), HNRNPA3 (heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein A3, RNA splicing, telomere maintenance, ALS/FTD), and AGPS (alkylglycerone phosphate synthase, plasmalogen biosynthesis, peroxisomal lipid metabolism, rhizomelic chondrodysplasia punctata). Functional hypothesis: LOC101927055 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the TTN gene, potentially functioning as an intronic enhancer coordinating TTN alternative splicing in cardiac and skeletal muscle (titin isoform switching, fetal-to-adult TTN transition, cardiac compliance regulation) or as a chromatin boundary separating TTN sarcomere genes from oxidative stress genes (NFE2L2) and antiviral response genes (PRKRA). The 92 pathogenic TTN variants indicate extreme dosage sensitivity where altered copy number or sequence disrupts titin expression programs. Disease mechanism involves TTN truncating variants causing haploinsufficiency (dilated cardiomyopathy DCM, left ventricular dysfunction, heart failure, arrhythmia, sudden cardiac death), TTN missense variants altering sarcomere elasticity (hypertrophic cardiomyopathy HCM, restrictive cardiomyopathy, diastolic dysfunction), TTN splicing defects causing isoform imbalance (centronuclear myopathy, skeletal muscle weakness, hypotonia, respiratory insufficiency), NFE2L2 dysregulation impairing oxidative stress defense (cardiomyocyte damage, ischemia-reperfusion injury), PRKRA alteration causing dystonia (DYT16, basal ganglia dysfunction), HNRNPA3 mutation causing ALS/FTD (TDP-43 pathology, neurodegeneration), and AGPS deficiency causing plasmalogen defects (rhizomelic chondrodysplasia punctata, peroxisomal disorder, skeletal dysplasia). The 2q31.2 TTN region is the most common genetic cause of dilated cardiomyopathy (~25% familial DCM). The presence of non-coding features (no UniProt, 92 TTN ClinVar variants) combined with TTN-NFE2L2-PRKRA-HNRNPA3-AGPS gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a muscle-specific intronic regulatory element critical for TTN splicing-sarcomere assembly-cardiac contractility coordination.
