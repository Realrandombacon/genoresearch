## Description
ARL6IP5 (ADP-Ribosylation Factor-Like GTPase 6-Interacting Protein 5, UniProt O75915) is a 188-amino acid PRA1 (Prenylated Rab Acceptor 1) family protein that functions as an endoplasmic reticulum membrane-shaping factor and selective ER-phagy regulator. The protein contains a conserved PRA1 domain (IPR004895, PF03208, positions 2-159) with four predicted transmembrane helices that embed it in the ER membrane. AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT 89.9) consistent with a compact membrane-integral fold. ARL6IP5 is ubiquitously expressed (HPA: detected in all tissues) with ER localization. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with SLC1A1/excitatory amino acid transporter 3 (0.955, exceptionally high confidence), ER-phagy receptor RNF185 (0.684), reticulon RTN4 (0.672), ER-shaping protein ESYT1 (0.640), and ERAD components DERL1/DERL2 (0.635/0.583). Mechanistically, ARL6IP5 modulates ER tubule morphology and promotes reticulophagy (selective ER autophagy) to clear misfolded proteins including pathogenic prion aggregates (PrPSc). Recent 2025 literature establishes ARL6IP5 as bidirectional in cancer contexts and essential for ER stress resolution. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic variants (mostly chromosomal 3p CNVs), though no monogenic disease is definitively mapped. With only 49 literature references despite clear mechanistic roles in ER homeostasis, neurodegeneration, and cancer, ARL6IP5 represents an underexplored ER membrane regulator with therapeutic potential in prion disease and ER stress-related disorders.
