## Description
UBXN2A (UBX domain-containing protein 2A, also known as UBXD8 or UBX domain protein 2A, P68543) encodes a 259 aa ubiquitin-like regulatory protein functioning as a tumor suppressor in colorectal cancer by targeting the Rictor-mTORC2 complex for proteasomal degradation through its UBX domain-mediated interaction with the VCP/p97 ATPase machinery. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded bipartite organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 73.4): UBX domain (IPR001012/PF00789/PS50033/SM00166, residues 166-247/171-246/169-246) forming the characteristic ubiquitin-like fold essential for binding VCP/p97 AAA+ ATPase and recruiting substrate proteins to the proteasomal degradation machinery, SEP domain (IPR012989/PF08059/PS51399, residues 57-150/65-138/60-124) establishing an additional protein-protein interaction module of the NSFL1 cofactor p47 superfamily (IPR036241/G3DSA:3.30.420.210, residues 53-149) mediating specific substrate recognition, ubiquitin-like domain superfamily (IPR029071/G3DSA:3.10.20.90, residues 133-246/125-248) providing structural homology to ubiquitin for proteasome engagement, and UBX domain-containing protein family (PTHR23333, residues 13-246) spanning most of the protein. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence VCP/p97 cofactor network: Transitional endoplasmic reticulum ATPase VCP/p97 (0.999, highest) as primary binding partner and master regulator of ubiquitin-dependent protein degradation, NSFL1 cofactor p47-like protein NSFL1C (0.999) as paralogous VCP cofactor suggesting functional redundancy in ER-associated degradation, UBX domain-containing protein 10 UBXN10 (0.997) as family member indicating cooperative assembly in VCP complexes, Beta-lactamase-like protein LACTB (0.994) linking to mitochondrial metabolism regulation, UBX domain-containing protein 8 UBXN8 (0.965) as another UBX family cofactor, Small VCP-interacting protein SVIP (0.957) as competitive VCP regulator, UBXN6 (0.714), UBXN1 (0.584), ASPSCR1 (0.512), and VCPIP1 (0.440) completing the comprehensive VCP cofactor interactome demonstrating UBXN2A integration into ubiquitin-proteasome system. HPA localizes UBXN2A to centrosomes as predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues indicating ubiquitous housekeeping function in protein quality control and cell division; low cancer specificity suggests constitutive expression though literature demonstrates tumor suppressor activity, low immune cell specificity indicates broad lineage expression, and low single cell type specificity reflects universal proteostasis role. ClinVar documents 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutation c.287+3G>T potentially disrupting exon splicing and large-scale chromosome 2 copy number alterations including gains at 2p25.3-23.1 (chr2:12771-30565600x3), 2p24.1-22.2 (chr2:20938401-37327210x3), 2p25.3-22.3 (chr2:12771-35541353x3, chr2:706460-35523639x3), 2p25.3-q37.3 (chr2:1-243199373 gain) and losses at 2p25.1-q13 (chr2:11504318-111365996x1), 2p24.1-23.3 (chr2:22439520-25608211x1, chr2:19905995-24762790x1) encompassing the UBXN2A locus at 2p25.3; these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer though no specific monogenic UBXN2A phenotype established, potentially affecting proteostasis and mTORC2 signaling through dosage sensitivity. Conservation from mouse Q99KJ0 (258 aa, 99% length identity) and rat D3ZID8 (258 aa, 99% identity) confirms essential VCP cofactor function across mammals with high sequence identity in both UBX and SEP domains; centrosomal localization correlates with cell division regulation function; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 73.4) enables mapping of both UBX VCP-binding surface and SEP substrate-recognition interface; 18 ClinVar variants suggest haploinsufficiency sensitivity for proper protein quality control; literature demonstrates UBXN2A suppresses Rictor-mTORC2 signaling pathway inhibiting colorectal cancer progression and metastasis (2023/2025), UBXN2A enhances CHIP-mediated proteasomal degradation of oncoprotein mortalin-2 in cancer cells (2018), UBXN2A functions as tumor suppressor via selective suppression of mTORC2-AKT pathway in patient-derived xenograft models (2025), and UBXN2A alters proteins in mTORC2 pathway through direct Rictor targeting (2022); mechanistic proposal involves UBXN2A-mediated recruitment of VCP/p97 ATPase through UBX domain binding enabling extraction of ubiquitinated Rictor from mTORC2 complex, SEP domain-dependent substrate specificity ensuring selective targeting of oncogenic proteins, coordination with CHIP E3 ligase for polyubiquitination prior to VCP engagement, centrosomal localization positioning UBXN2A for cell cycle-regulated proteasomal degradation of mitotic regulators, and tumor suppressor activity through downregulation of AKT phosphorylation at Ser473 (mTORC2-specific site) inhibiting cancer cell proliferation and metastasis.
