## Description
HES6 (Gene ID: 55502) encodes a well-studied (209 papers) basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factor (UniProt Q96HZ4, 224 aa) with a confident AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 70.3) featuring a bHLH domain (IPR011598, PF00010) and an Orange domain (IPR003650, PF07527) essential for DNA binding and dimerization. Functionally, it acts as a transcriptional cofactor regulating cell differentiation and development, evidenced by high-confidence interactions (0.929) with TLE1 and (0.817) with MSC, suggesting roles in transcriptional repression and complex formation. It is broadly expressed in tissues with cancer-enhanced specificity (HPA), showing top expression in pituitary gland (nTPM 61.7) and brain (nTPM 56.2). Clinically, HES6 is associated with 82 pathogenic variants, predominantly Copy Number Variants (CNVs) affecting the 2q37 region, linked to developmental disorders and cancer predisposition via dosage sensitivity. Literature highlights its dual roles in cancer immunity (promoting CD8+ T cell infiltration via oxidative phosphorylation) and hematopoiesis (interacting with GATA1), confirming its status as a key regulator of Notch signaling and cell fate decisions.
