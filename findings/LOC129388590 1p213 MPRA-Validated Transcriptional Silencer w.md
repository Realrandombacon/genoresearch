## Description
LOC129388590 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 1p21.3 (genomic coordinates: 114702833-114703033 on NC_000001.11, spanning approximately 200 bp). This genomic region was experimentally validated as a transcriptional silencer using STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells, demonstrating ability to repress activity of super core promoter SCP1. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element. The accessible chromatin subregion annotation indicates active regulatory function in hematopoietic cell lineages. ClinVar analysis identified 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants with pure triplosensitivity: BOTH variants are copy number gains (duplications, 100%), with 0 deletions, indicating extreme triplosensitivity where increased copy number is the exclusive pathogenic mechanism. This 100% duplication bias matches the strongest triplosensitivity signals observed in this silencer series (LOC129390835, LOC129390366, LOC129390151). The 1p21-13 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including PEX11B (peroxisome biogenesis factor 11 beta - peroxisome proliferation, lipid metabolism), MLLT3/AF9 (myeloid/lymphoid translocation partner 3 - leukemia translocations, chromatin regulation), ITGA4 (integrin alpha-4 - leukocyte adhesion, immune cell trafficking), and PEX16 (peroxisome membrane protein). As an experimentally validated silencer, LOC129388590 likely functions to repress transcription of target genes in cis during erythroid differentiation and potentially peroxisome biogenesis regulation. Duplication of this silencer causes excessive repression of target genes (potentially PEX11B or MLLT3), contributing to 1p21-13 duplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, metabolic disturbances (via peroxisome pathway), and potential hematologic abnormalities (via MLLT3 pathway). The pure triplosensitivity pattern suggests this element regulates peroxisome biogenesis factors or chromatin regulators where precise dosage is critical for cellular metabolism.
