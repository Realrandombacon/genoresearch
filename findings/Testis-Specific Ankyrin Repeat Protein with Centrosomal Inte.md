# Testis-Specific Ankyrin Repeat Protein with Centrosomal Interaction Network

**Date:** 2026-03-20T17:36:23.568194

**Quality Score:** 2.75/10  (E=4.5, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ANKRD7 encodes a 254 amino acid ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein (UniProt Q92527) with strong testis-specific expression (803.6 nTPM in HPA). InterPro analysis reveals multiple ankyrin repeat domains: IPR002110 (positions 29-123, 124-219), PF12796 (3 copies at 29-122, 129-217), and PTHR24147 family domain (12-222), with GO annotation for protein binding (GO:0005515). The ankyrin repeat superfamily (IPR036770, SSF48403) forms a curved solenoid structure ideal for protein-protein interactions. HPA localizes ANKRD7 to nucleoplasm with tissue-enriched expression pattern. STRING interactions identify partners including CCDC144A (0.574), CFAP99 (0.565), ASZ1 (0.522), ASB12 (0.505), ANKRD46 (0.477), NFKBIE (0.470), TSACC (0.456), C2CD6 (0.445), CCDC54 (0.439), and CCDC24 (0.431) — several are coiled-coil or centrosomal proteins. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains, deletions, and complex rearrangements affecting 7q31.31. Conservation data shows mouse ortholog Q9D504 (279 aa) and rat A0A0G2KA76 (279 aa). Functional hypothesis: ANKRD7 functions as a testis-specific scaffold protein that mediates protein-protein interactions during spermatogenesis via its ankyrin repeat domains. The extreme testis enrichment (803.6 nTPM vs negligible elsewhere) suggests role in germ cell development. Interaction with centrosomal partners (CCDC144A, CFAP99, CCDC54) and nucleoplasmic localization indicate involvement in nuclear-cytoskeletal coordination during meiosis. Pathogenic CNVs may disrupt ankyrin-mediated interaction networks, potentially causing male infertility or chromosomal instability.
