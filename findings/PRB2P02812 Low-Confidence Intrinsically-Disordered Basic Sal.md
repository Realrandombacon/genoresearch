## Description
PRB2 (Proline rich protein BstNI subfamily 2, also known as Basic salivary proline-rich protein 2, IB-9, PRPPRB1, Ps, cP7) is a 416 amino acid secreted salivary glycoprotein (UniProt P02812) with low-confidence intrinsically-disordered architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 46.7), characteristic of proline-rich proteins that function through flexible tandem repeat domains to mediate oral lubrication, calcium binding, tannin precipitation, and enamel pellicle formation. The protein belongs to the proline-rich protein family (IPR026086, residues 1-233 and 234-416; PTHR23203 PROLINE-RICH PROTEIN, residues 320-416; SM01412 Proline-rich domain, residues 1-233 and 234-416) and contains proline-rich disordered regions (PF15240 Proline-rich protein, residues 1-135 and 319-416). PRB2 is located on chromosome 12p13.2 within the salivary proline-rich protein gene cluster and is predicted to be secreted from parotid glands. The preproprotein undergoes proteolytic processing to generate one or more mature isoforms before secretion.
