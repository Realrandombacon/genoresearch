## Description
BRPF3 encodes a 1205 aa nuclear scaffold protein (UniProt Q9ULD4) that functions as an essential regulatory subunit of the MOZ/MORF (MYST3/MYST4) histone acetyltransferase (HAT) complexes, specifically stabilizing MYST2 (KAT7) and KAT6A/B catalytic subunits while preventing their HUWE1-mediated ubiquitin-dependent degradation. This partially characterized gene (23 publications) has emerging clinical significance for cardiac fibrosis, ovarian cancer drug resistance, and migraine. The protein features multi-domain chromatin reader architecture: N-terminal extended PHD finger (EPHD, IPR001965/cd15572/cd15703, residues 212-386/269-386) recognizing unmodified H3K4 and facilitating complex assembly; central bromodomain (IPR001487/cd05512/G3DSA:1.20.920.10, residues 587-695/593-690) binding acetylated lysines on histone tails (H3K14ac, H4K12ac); C-terminal PWWP domain (IPR000313/cd20158/G3DSA:2.30.30.140, residues 1074-1186/1074-1191) recognizing H3K36me3 marks at actively transcribed gene bodies; classified within zinc finger PHD-type and bromodomain families with GO terms for chromatin regulation, protein binding, and positive regulation of DNA replication. STRING interactions reveal BRPF3 operates within extremely high-confidence HAT complex networks: MEAF6 (0.997), essential scaffold subunit shared across MOZ/MORF complexes; ING5 (0.990)/ING4 (0.908), inhibitor of growth proteins providing H3K4me3 recognition; BRD1 (0.987)/BRPF1 (0.964), paralogous bromodomain-PHD scaffolds; KAT6A (0.861)/KAT6B (0.453), MYST family HAT catalytic subunits; KAT7 (0.463)/KAT5 (0.581), additional MYST HATs; TP53 (0.551), tumor suppressor p53 linking to cell cycle control. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, nucleoplasm and mitochondria subcellular localization, classified as plasma protein/chromatin regulator. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large-scale copy number losses (6p21.31-21.1 35-42Mb x1, 6p21.31-21.2 34-38Mb), copy number gains (6p22.1-q14.1 29-81Mb, 6p21.31-21.2 34-36Mb x3, 6p25.3-q27 0.15-170Mb x3, 6p25.3-12.3 0.15-46Mb x3), intronic variant (c.3279+118A>T), and whole gene deletion, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 67.1), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered bromodomain, PHD fingers, and PWWP domain connected by flexible linker regions characteristic of modular chromatin readers requiring conformational adaptability. Mechanistically, BRPF3 operates through integrated modes: (1) MYST2 stabilization—2020 study (13 citations) demonstrates BRPF3-HUWE1 axis prevents MYST2 degradation, essential for embryonic stem cell differentiation and cell-cycle progression; (2) Cardiac fibrosis feedback loop—2025 study (2 citations) identifies CELF1/circ-BRPF3/BRPF3/KAT7 regulatory circuit driving fibroblast activation and ECM deposition; (3) PARP inhibitor resistance—2021 study (4 citations) shows BRPF3 targeting moderately reverses olaparib resistance in high-grade serous ovarian carcinoma by impairing DNA repair capacity; (4) Migraine susceptibility—2024 Mendelian randomization study (25 citations) identifies BRPF3 as potential therapeutic target for migraine; (5) Chromatin targeting—PWWP domain anchors complex to H3K36me3-marked gene bodies while bromodomain engages acetylated histones at enhancers/promoters; (6) Complex assembly—scaffold bridges MEAF6, ING5, and catalytic KAT subunits enabling coordinated H3/H4 acetylation. Loss-of-function causes destabilization of MYST2/KAT7, impaired HAT activity, defective embryonic stem cell differentiation, enhanced cardiac fibrosis, increased PARP inhibitor sensitivity, and altered neuronal gene expression contributing to migraine.
