## Description
ANKRD27 (also known as VARP) encodes a 1050 amino acid peripheral membrane protein (UniProt Q96NW4) that functions as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) for endosomal small GTPases. The protein contains a canonical VPS9 domain (PF02204, IPR003123, residues 266-364) which mediates Rab activation, followed by two Zn-fingernail motifs (residues 429-468 and 706-747) and extensive ankyrin repeat arrays (IPR002110, PF00023) spanning residues 463-895 that mediate protein-protein interactions. STRING interaction network reveals high-confidence binding to RAB32 (score 0.994) and RAB38 (score 0.453), consistent with its GEF specificity for melanocyte-specific Rabs involved in pigment granule biogenesis. Additional partners include retromer complex components VPS29 (0.940), VPS26A (0.644), VPS35 (0.531), Golgi marker GOLGA4 (0.871), kinesin KIF5A (0.755), and SNARE VAMP7 (0.508), positioning ANKRD27 at the intersection of endosomal recycling, retrograde transport, and lysosomal trafficking. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with vesicular/cytosolic localization, supporting its housekeeping role in endolysosomal homeostasis. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses on chromosome 19q, with implications for eosinophilic esophagitis. Conservation is strong: mouse ortholog (Q3UMR0) shares 1048/1050 residues (~99.8% identity), indicating strong purifying selection across 90+ million years of evolution.
