# CIMIP6/C2orf73: Testis-Enriched Ciliary Scaffold Protein with Microtubule Inner Protein Domain Implicated in 2p Copy Number Disorders and Male Fertility

## Description
CIMIP6 (alias C2orf73, ciliary microtubule inner protein 6) encodes a 287 amino acid partially structured intracellular protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 53.5) containing a ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 family domain (IPR031365/PF15667, aa 1-286/38-284) and belonging to the PTHR35087 family (aa 1-286). HPA shows testis-enriched tissue expression (46.3 nTPM, tissue enriched category), detected in some tissues distribution, nucleoplasm/plasma membrane/cytosol localization, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell type expression pattern. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 2p25.3-q37.3 and 2p22.3-16.1 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING database returned no interaction partners, consistent with specialized ciliary protein with limited interactome data. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q5SPV6 233 aa) indicates functional constraint despite sequence divergence. The ciliary microtubule inner protein domain architecture combined with testis-enriched expression (46.3 nTPM) strongly suggests CIMIP6 functions as a structural component of ciliary/flagellar microtubules, particularly in sperm flagella where ciliary proteins are essential for motility. Ciliary microtubule inner proteins localize to the lumen of axonemal microtubules and stabilize microtubule doublets through interactions with tubulin and microtubule-associated proteins. Testis enrichment indicates specialized function in spermatogenesis, potentially required for sperm flagella assembly, stability, or motility. The nucleoplasmic and plasma membrane HPA localizations suggest additional non-ciliary functions, potentially in nuclear envelope organization or plasma membrane microdomain scaffolding. Low pLDDT score (53.5) indicates substantial intrinsic disorder, characteristic of ciliary scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for microtubule lattice integration and post-translational modification regulation. Absence of STRING interactions suggests CIMIP6 may function as a structural component rather than signaling hub, or that ciliary protein interactomes remain under-characterized. Pathogenic 2p CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting ciliary microtubule stability and sperm flagella function, potentially contributing to 2p deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes including male infertility, developmental defects through impaired ciliary signaling (nodal cilia), and respiratory ciliary dysfunction through compromised airway cilia motility. The PF15667 domain represents a cilia-specific structural motif conserved across ciliated organisms, indicating ancient evolutionary origin tied to ciliogenesis.
