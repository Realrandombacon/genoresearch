# LOC127889230: 18q12.1 OCT4-NANOG-H3K27ac-Associated Naive hESC Enhancer with No Clinical Variants — Core Pluripotency Regulatory Element

**Date:** 2026-03-19T03:13:58.952467

## Description
LOC127889230 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 18q12.1 (genomic coordinates: 47967740-47968468 on NC_000018.10, spanning approximately 728 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in naive human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU class 5 homeobox 1, master pluripotency transcription factor) and NANOG (homeobox protein NANOG, core pluripotency transcription factor) transcription factors and is marked by the H3K27ac histone modification (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation — canonical active enhancer mark distinguishing active from poised enhancers). The OCT4-NANOG co-binding signature combined with H3K27ac indicates direct regulation by the core pluripotency transcriptional network in an active enhancer state. A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. This absence of ClinVar entries is consistent with other hESC enhancers in this series (LOC127894026: 0 variants; LOC127892927: 0 variants; LOC127892406: 0 variants; LOC127891630: 0 variants; LOC127889588: 0 variants) and contrasts with silencers showing strong triplosensitivity or haploinsufficiency patterns. The 18q12 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including KATNB1 (kinesin-associated protein 3 - microtubule severing, mitotic spindle formation), SMAD2 (SMAD family member 2 - TGF-beta signaling, embryonic development), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a naive hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4-NANOG and marked by H3K27ac, LOC127889230 likely functions as a direct target of the core pluripotency network to activate transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and naive pluripotency maintenance. The dual enhancer/silencer activity suggests context-dependent regulation, potentially activating pluripotency factors in naive stem cells while repressing differentiation genes in somatic lineages. The absence of ClinVar variants suggests either functional redundancy, essentiality (deleterious variants are embryonic lethal), or that this element regulates genes with buffered dosage sensitivity in post-embryonic development.
