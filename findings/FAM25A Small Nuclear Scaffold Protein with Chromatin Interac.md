## Description
FAM25A encodes an 89 amino acid small protein belonging to the FAM25 family (IPR023243, PF15825, PTHR34994), conserved across residues 1-89. Despite its small size, STRING-DB identifies 10 interaction partners with medium confidence scores (0.40-0.59), including ANTXRL (0.594), CRCT1 (0.560), NUTM2D (0.528), AGAP9 (0.519), and AGAP6 (0.480). The interaction profile suggests dual functional associations: NUTM2D (Nuclein Mid1-2 domain-containing protein) implicates chromatin or nuclear organization roles, while AGAP family members (ArfGAP with GTPase-like domain and Ankyrin repeat proteins) suggest vesicle trafficking or membrane dynamics involvement. This positions FAM25A as a potential adaptor bridging nuclear and endomembrane compartments. ClinVar documents 46 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning 10q23.2, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity comparable to known haploinsufficient genes. The 10q23 region is associated with developmental phenotypes when disrupted. HPA expression data was unavailable, but the interaction network and small protein architecture suggest FAM25A may function as a transient scaffold or regulatory module rather than a stable structural component. The combination of small size, diverse interaction partners spanning nuclear and trafficking functions, and extreme CNV pathogenicity supports a model where FAM25A serves as a dosage-sensitive regulatory hub coordinating chromatin-vesicle crosstalk, with copy number alterations disrupting nuclear envelope integrity or nucleocytoplasmic transport fidelity.
