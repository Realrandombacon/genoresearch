## Description
SH3GL3 (UniProt: Q99963, aliases Endophilin-A3, SH3 domain containing GRB2 like 3) encodes a 347 amino acid BAR domain-containing protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with 44 publications in Semantic Scholar. Located on chromosome 15q25.2, SH3GL3 is an endophilin family member functioning in clathrin-mediated endocytosis, synaptic vesicle recycling, and tumor suppression via STAT3 signaling inhibition.
