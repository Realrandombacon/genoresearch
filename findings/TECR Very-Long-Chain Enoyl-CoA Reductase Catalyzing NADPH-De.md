## Description
TECR (Trans-2,3-Enoyl-CoA Reductase, Very-long-chain enoyl-CoA reductase, Q9NZ01) encodes a 308 aa microsomal oxidoreductase functioning as the fourth and final enzyme in the fatty acid elongation cycle that extends saturated and monounsaturated fatty acyl-CoAs by two carbons through NADPH-dependent reduction of trans-2,3-enoyl-CoA intermediates to saturated acyl-CoAs essential for very long-chain fatty acid (VLCFA) biosynthesis.
