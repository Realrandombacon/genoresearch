DLEU1 is located on chromosome 13q14.2-q14.3 within the critical deleted region in chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL). The gene is annotated as both a protein-coding gene (leukemia-associated protein 1, 78 aa) and a long non-coding RNA (LINC00021/NCRNA00021), reflecting ongoing debate about whether DLEU1 produces a functional micropeptide or functions primarily as a lncRNA.
