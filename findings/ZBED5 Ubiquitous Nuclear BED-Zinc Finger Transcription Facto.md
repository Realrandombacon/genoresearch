# ZBED5: Ubiquitous Nuclear BED-Zinc Finger Transcription Factor with RNase-H-like Fold Regulating Gene Expression via ZBED6/ZMYM1 Interaction Network

**Date:** 2026-04-06T13:53:40.443992

**Quality Score:** 2.88/10  (E=4.65, D=1.1) [WEAK]

## Description
ZBED5 (Zinc Finger BED-Type Containing 5, also Buster1) is a 693 amino acid nuclear protein (UniProt Q49AG3) with a well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 81.4, high confidence), functioning as a transcription factor derived from an ancient Charlie-like DNA transposon that has been domesticated for mammalian gene regulation. The protein localizes to the nucleoplasm and nucleoli fibrillar center, with additional Golgi apparatus detection suggesting potential dual roles in transcription and RNA processing or secretion.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals ZBED5 contains an N-terminal BED-type zinc finger domain (IPR003656/PF02892/PS50808, residues 108-164/127-157) that mediates DNA binding (GO:0003677), characteristic of hAT family transposases but repurposed here for sequence-specific genomic recognition. The C-terminal region adopts a Ribonuclease H-like superfamily fold (IPR012337/SSF53098, residues 271-664) composed of two subdomains: FAM200/ZBED5 RNase-like (PF27073, residues 190-360) and FAM200/GTF2IRD2 RNase-like helical (PF27046, residues 371-519). While structurally homologous to RNase H, catalytic residues may be degenerate, suggesting a role in protein-protein interactions or non-catalytic nucleic acid binding rather than enzymatic cleavage. The protein belongs to the EPM2A-interacting protein 1 family (PTHR45913, residues 103-693).\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals ZBED5 functions within a network of zinc finger transcription factors and chromatin regulators: strongest interactions with ZBED6CL (0.571, zinc finger BED domain-containing protein 6C-like), TMPRSS11D (0.479, transmembrane protease serine 11D), ZMYM1 (0.465, zinc finger MYM-type protein 1, transcriptional repressor), TTC23L (0.447, tetratricopeptide repeat domain 23-like), ZBED6 (0.447, zinc finger BED domain-containing protein 6, IGF2 regulator), ZNF235 (0.444), ZSCAN30 (0.442), ZNF770 (0.441), ZNF850 (0.424), and MAGEB17 (0.405). The ZBED6 partnership is particularly significant as ZBED6 regulates IGF2 expression; ZBED5 may act as a co-factor or competitive regulator at similar genomic loci. ZMYM1 interaction links ZBED5 to histone deacetylase complexes.\n\nHPA expression shows ubiquitous low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, consistent with a housekeeping transcription factor maintaining basal gene expression programs. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm, nucleoli fibrillar center, and Golgi apparatus. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein and transcription factor with cell type-enhanced single cell specificity.\n\nClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large chromosome 11p15 copy number gains (spanning 11p15.5-14.2 to 11p15.5-q25) and one 11p15.4-15.2 deletion. These CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity potentially causing developmental disorders, overgrowth syndromes (Beckwith-Wiedemann spectrum), or intellectual disability through dysregulated transcription of imprinted genes in the 11p15 cluster.\n\nConservation is restricted to mammalian Laurasiatheria (human, dog, cow), indicating relatively recent evolutionary domestication from a transposon ancestor, with no orthologs in rodents or distant vertebrates.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: ZBED5 acts as a domesticated transposase-derived transcription factor that binds specific DNA sequences via its BED zinc finger domain, recruiting chromatin modifiers through its RNase-H-like scaffold. The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.4) suggests a stable platform for assembling multi-protein complexes. ZBED5 may regulate imprinted gene clusters at 11p15, modulating IGF2/H19 expression in concert with ZBED6. The RNase-H-like fold, while likely catalytically inactive, may bind RNA or DNA intermediates during transcription or repair. Nucleolar localization suggests involvement in rRNA processing or ribosome biogenesis regulation. Golgi detection implies a subset of ZBED5 may undergo unconventional secretion or function in Golgi-to-nucleus signaling. CNV-mediated overexpression disrupts stoichiometry of transcriptional complexes, causing aberrant activation/repression of growth regulators. The restriction to Laurasiatheria suggests ZBED5 acquired lineage-specific functions in placental development or metabolic regulation.
