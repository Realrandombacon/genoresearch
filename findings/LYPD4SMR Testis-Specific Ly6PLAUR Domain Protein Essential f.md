## Description
LYPD4 (UniProt Q6UWN0), also known as SMR, encodes a 246-amino acid testis-specific glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored membrane protein containing two tandem Ly6/PLAUR domains (IPR016054/PF00021, aa 54-117 and aa 139-216) within the snake toxin-like superfamily (IPR045860/SSF57302, aa 139-207). The protein belongs to the fertilization and immune response mediator family (IPR051899/PTHR16529, aa 46-243) with two distinct extracellular domains (ECD1: aa 26-128, ECD2: aa 138-225) characteristic of Ly6 family proteins involved in cell surface recognition events. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded three-finger toxin-like structure (pLDDT: 84.8), consistent with stable protein-protein interaction platform required for sperm-egg binding.\n\nSTRING interactions position LYPD4 within the sperm acrosome reaction network: strongest links to Ly6 family member LY6L (0.630), meiosis regulator DMRTC2 (0.629), prostate/testis-expressed PATE family members PATE3 (0.593)/PATE4 (0.588)/PATE2 (0.543), sperm acrosome protein PINLYP (0.585), Ly6H (0.543), LYPD2 (0.538), sperm acrosome-associated SPACA4 (0.480), and cancer/testis antigen LY6K (0.418). HPA confirms extreme testis specificity (99.5 nTPM, tissue enriched) with single-tissue distribution pattern.\n\nWith only 8 papers, LYPD4 represents a genuinely dark gene with critical reproductive function. Mouse homolog studies (2020, 23 citations) demonstrate LYPD4 is essential for sperm fertilizing ability and male fertility through acrosome-mediated zona pellucida recognition. The Ly6/PLAUR architecture suggests GPI-anchored cell surface localization enabling protein-protein interactions during gamete recognition, analogous to uPAR-mediated protease recruitment. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic variants (19q13.2 CNVs including duplications). This positions LYPD4 as a critical sperm surface receptor for egg binding with potential implications for male infertility diagnosis.
