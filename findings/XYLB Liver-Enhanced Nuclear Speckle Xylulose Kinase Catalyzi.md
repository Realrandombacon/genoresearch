## Description
XYLB (Xylulose Kinase, UniProt O75191) is a 536 amino acid nuclear speckle metabolic enzyme with substantial characterization (225 papers, though many focus on bacterial homologs) that functions as an FGGY-family carbohydrate kinase catalyzing the ATP-dependent phosphorylation of D-xylulose to D-xylulose-5-phosphate, a critical step connecting xylose metabolism to the pentose phosphate pathway. The protein contains FGGY carbohydrate kinase family domains (IPR000577/PF00370/PF02782/IPR018484/IPR018485, residues 9-525/132-287/296-479/132-287/296-479) with kinase activity (GO:0016301) and carbohydrate metabolic process function (GO:0005975), D-xylulose kinase family (IPR042024, residues 9-519) with D-xylulokinase activity (GO:0004856) and D-xylose metabolic process (GO:0042732), nucleotide-binding domain (cd07776, residues 9-519), and ATPase nucleotide binding domain superfamily (IPR043129/G3DSA:3.30.420.40, residues 10-287/290-523/11-287/464-498/289-463/499-520), placing it in the essential pentose phosphate pathway machinery.
