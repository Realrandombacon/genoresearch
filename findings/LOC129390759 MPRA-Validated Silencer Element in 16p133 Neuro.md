# LOC129390759: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 16p13.3 Neurodevelopmental Critical Region

**Date:** 2026-03-23T07:16:35.990982

**Quality Score:** 4.1/10  (E=5.0, D=3.2) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129390759 (Gene ID: 129390759) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 16p13.3 (5,678,474-5,678,674 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak2482 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 16p13.3-13.11, 16p13.3-13.12, 16p13.3-11.2, 16p13.3-13.13, 16p13.3-13.2, and 16p13.3 regions (deletions 0.4-4.3 Mb, duplications 0.01-15.7 Mb whole chromosome arm), associated with 16p13.3 deletion/duplication syndromes featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, autism spectrum disorder, epilepsy, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and skeletal anomalies. The 16p13.3 region is a neurodevelopmental critical region containing multiple dosage-sensitive genes including NDE1 (nude neurodevelopment gene 1, microcephaly, lissencephaly, centrosome function), MYH11 (myosin heavy chain 11, smooth muscle contraction, thoracic aortic aneurysm), ABCC6 (ATP binding cassette subfamily C member 6, pseudoxanthoma elasticum, mineralization), PKD1 (polycystic kidney disease 1, renal cyst formation), TSC2 (tuberous sclerosis complex 2, mTOR signaling, tumor suppression), and CREBBP (CREB binding protein, Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome, histone acetyltransferase); (4) No HPA expression (non-coding); (5) No STRING interactions (non-coding). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390759 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or neural progenitor contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in hematopoietic lineage. The 16p13.3 region forms a topologically associating domain (TAD) with extensive long-range chromatin interactions critical for neurodevelopment. Pathogenic 16p13.3 CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and TAD architecture, causing long-range dysregulation of NDE1, MYH11, ABCC6, PKD1, TSC2, and CREBBP. NDE1 haploinsufficiency causes microcephaly and lissencephaly. TSC2 loss causes tuberous sclerosis complex with mTOR hyperactivation. CREBBP dysregulation affects histone acetylation and transcriptional programs. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions. The MPRA validation provides direct functional evidence of regulatory activity in erythroid lineage. 16p13.3 rearrangements are recurrent in neurodevelopmental disorders affecting ~1 in 10,000-20,000 live births - silencer disruption may contribute to phenotypic severity through dysregulation of neurodevelopmental gene expression.
