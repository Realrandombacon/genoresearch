# RIN1: Dual RAS/RAB5 Guanine Nucleotide Exchange Factor Coordinating Receptor Tyrosine Kinase Signaling with Endocytic Trafficking in Neuropathic Pain, Thyroid Carcinoma, and Fear Memory Plasticity

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
RIN1 (783 aa, UniProt Q13671), also known as Ras and Rab interactor 1, is a cytoplasmic and plasma membrane-localized dual-function guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that uniquely activates both RAS family small GTPases (HRAS, NRAS, KRAS, RRAS) and RAB5 family endocytic regulators (RAB5A, RAB5B, RAB5C) through its VPS9 and Ras-associating domains, thereby coupling receptor tyrosine kinase signaling to endosomal trafficking, synaptic plasticity, and pain perception. The protein architecture features three major functional domains: an N-terminal Src homology 2 (SH2) domain (IPR000980/IPR035867/cd10393/G3DSA:3.30.505.10, aa58-163/65-161/67-163) that binds phosphorylated tyrosine residues on activated receptor tyrosine kinases for membrane recruitment; a central VPS9 domain (IPR003123/IPR037191/PF02204/G3DSA:1.20.1050.80, aa456-607/478-609/493-591) serving as the catalytic GEF module for RAB5 activation driving early endosome formation; and a C-terminal Ras-associating domain (IPR000159/PF00788, aa624-709/625-709) that binds active RAS-GTP providing feedback regulation. The full RIN1 family signature spans aa258-692 (IPR045046/VPS9-like family). HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with skin enrichment (9.0 nTPM top tissue), ubiquitous detection across many tissues, low cancer specificity, immune cell enhancement in blood cells, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, cytoplasmic and plasma membrane localization, and classification as a RAS pathway-related protein involved in endocytosis with GTPase activation molecular function. Mechanistically, RIN1 forms ultra-high-confidence interactions within RAS-RAB signaling networks: ABL1 (STRING 0.997) and ABL2 (0.984) are Abl family tyrosine kinases that phosphorylate RIN1 enhancing its GEF activity; HRAS (0.993), NRAS (0.966), KRAS (0.424), RRAS (0.905), and RRAS2 (0.981) represent RAS family GTPases activated by RIN1's RA domain; RAB5B (0.969), RAB5C (0.938), and RAB5A (0.453) are RAB5 isoforms activated by the VPS9 domain driving clathrin-mediated endocytosis and early endosome fusion. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 66.9) reveals medium-confidence partially structured architecture with well-folded SH2, VPS9, and RA domains connected by intrinsically disordered linker regions characteristic of multi-domain signaling adaptors requiring conformational flexibility for substrate recognition. Clinical significance includes 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants: missense variant c.77C>T (p.Ala26Val), splice region variants c.383-3C>G and c.1592-129C>T, large copy number gains spanning 11q12.1-13.3/11q12.2-13.5/11p13-q25/11q13.1-13.2 (chr11:56895955-69295402, 59923608-76272324, 32799481-134938470, 65138976-67574402 amplifications), duplication (NC_000011.9:g.(?_64973914)_(70052579_?)dup), and deletion (NC_000011.9:g.(?_64522783)_(66283694_?)del). Conservation across mammals (mouse Q921Q7 763 aa, rat P97680 774 aa) indicates essential dual GEF function maintained over ~90 million years. Literature (198 papers, WELL-STUDIED status) establishes RIN1 in pain perception, cancer, and neuronal plasticity: 2025 studies demonstrate RIN1 regulates ferroptosis and nociceptive perception via Nrf2/HO-1 pathway in chronic constriction injury models of neuropathic pain; RIN1 serves as tumor suppressor in familial non-medullary thyroid carcinoma through unknown mechanisms; RIN1 regulates developmental and pain-related plasticity in spinal synapses via NMDA receptor subunit trafficking controlling synaptic strength; neuropathic pain causes contextual fear generalization by reducing RIN1 expression in dorsal hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons linking to memory processing. The functional hypothesis posits that RIN1 acts as a 'RTK-endocytosis coupling coordinator': upon growth factor stimulation, RTKs autophosphorylate creating docking sites; RIN1 SH2 domain binds phosphotyrosines recruiting RIN1 to plasma membrane where ABL1/2 phosphorylation enhances GEF activity; the VPS9 domain activates RAB5-GDP to RAB5-GTP driving clathrin-coated pit formation and early endosome biogenesis while simultaneously the RA domain engages active RAS-GTP amplifying MAPK signaling; in neurons, this coupling controls NMDA receptor endocytosis determining synaptic plasticity and pain sensitization; in thyroid cells, RIN1 tumor suppression may involve limiting RAS-driven proliferation through endocytic downregulation of RTKs; in hippocampus, RIN1 levels determine fear memory specificity through NMDAR trafficking; therapeutic strategies include RIN1 inhibitors for neuropathic pain, RIN1 activators for thyroid cancer prevention, and RIN1-NMDAR axis modulation for PTSD treatment.
