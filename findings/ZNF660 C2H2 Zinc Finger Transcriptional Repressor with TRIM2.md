## Description
ZNF660 (Gene ID: 285349, UniProt Q6AZW8) is a 331 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor mapping to chromosome 3p21.31 (44,584,963-44,599,693 bp, ~14.7 kb genomic span). InterPro analysis reveals 3 canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 50-133, forming the classic beta-beta-alpha zinc finger fold (G3DSA:3.30.160.60, SSF57667) characteristic of Krüppel-type DNA-binding proteins. The zinc finger array (PS00028 signature, PS50157 profile) mediates sequence-specific double-stranded DNA binding, typically recognizing GC-rich consensus sequences. AlphaFold structure prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 82.8) for the full-length protein (residues 1-331), indicating a well-folded stable structure. STRING interactions reveal 4 protein partners with medium confidence scores (0.522-0.609): TRIM28 (0.522, tripartite motif-containing protein 28, KRAB zinc finger corepressor, heterochromatin formation via HP1 recruitment), CCDC181 (0.609, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 181, centrosomal protein), ST6GALNAC3 (0.580, ST6 alpha-2,6-sialyltransferase 3, glycosylation enzyme), and HAPLN3 (0.544, hyaluronan and proteoglycan link protein 3, extracellular matrix). ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants spanning 3p21.31-26.3 (deletions 41-44 Mb, duplications 2-55 Mb whole arm), associated with 3p deletion/duplication syndromes featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, growth abnormalities, and dysmorphic features. The 3p21.31 region contains a cluster of zinc finger genes including ZNF660, ZNF197 (readthrough transcription partner), and neighboring ZNF genes. RefSeq notes naturally-occurring readthrough transcription between ZNF660 and downstream ZNF197 (GeneID: 110354863), producing a fusion transcript. Functional hypothesis: ZNF660 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor that recruits the KRAB/KAP1 silencing complex via TRIM28 interaction. The C2H2 zinc finger array provides modular DNA recognition capacity, targeting distinct genomic loci. TRIM28 (KAP1/TIF1β) is the universal corepressor for KRAB domain-containing zinc fingers, recruiting SETDB1 histone methyltransferase (H3K9me3), HP1 heterochromatin protein, and NuRD complex to establish transcriptional silencing. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 82.8) suggests stable folding of the zinc finger array, enabling precise DNA recognition. Readthrough transcription with ZNF197 may produce extended isoforms with altered DNA-binding specificity or regulatory capacity. Interaction with CCDC181 suggests potential centrosomal/chromatin coordination during cell division. Pathogenic 3p CNVs disrupt ZNF660 dosage alongside neighboring zinc finger cluster genes, contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through altered epigenetic silencing programs. This represents a "dark gene" where domain architecture and interaction data suggest clear molecular function (KRAB-mediated repression), but specific DNA binding motifs, target genes, and biological pathways remain uncharacterized in literature.
