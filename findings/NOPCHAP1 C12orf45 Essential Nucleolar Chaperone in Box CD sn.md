## Description
NOPCHAP1 (NOP protein chaperone 1, formerly C12orf45, UniProt Q8N5I9) is a 185-amino acid essential intracellular protein located on chromosome 12q24.31. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the NOP protein chaperone 1 family (IPR027921/PF15370/PTHR28674, aa 21-184/68-152) with GO annotation for box C/D snoRNP assembly (GO:0000492), defining its role in small nucleolar ribonucleoprotein biogenesis. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 70.2), indicating a well-folded stable structure consistent with chaperone proteins that require defined binding surfaces for client protein recognition. Human Protein Atlas annotates NOPCHAP1 as an essential protein detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity and low cancer specificity, immune cell enriched, with subcellular localization in nucleoplasm and nuclear membrane — consistent with snoRNP assembly occurring in the nucleolus and Cajal bodies. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners comprising the R2TP/Prefoldin-like complex and snoRNP machinery: RPAP2 (0.977, RNA polymerase II-associated protein 2, core R2TP component), MIPOL1 (0.779, mirror image polydactyly 1), RUVBL1/RUVBL2 (0.770, AAA+ ATPases forming the R2TP core), KIAA1328 (0.665, uncharacterized protein), ZNHIT3/ZNHIT2 (0.575/0.572, zinc finger HIT-type proteins), ASTN2 (0.559, astrotactin 2), FRK (0.557, fyn-related kinase), and NOP58 (0.408, core box C/D snoRNP component). This interactome positions NOPCHAP1 within the R2TP chaperone complex that assembles box C/D snoRNPs by loading NOP58 and other core proteins onto snoRNAs. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number gains across 12q23.1-24.33, 12p13.33-q24.33, and losses at 12q23.2-24.11, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts snoRNP biogenesis causing disease phenotypes. Conservation across deuterostomes (chimpanzee A0A6D2WK35, 211 aa; macaque H9EYC5, 183 aa; amphioxus A0A8K0A4J4/A0A8K0A1W4, 191-203 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental RNA processing machinery. NOPCHAP1 likely functions as a specialized chaperone within the R2TP complex, facilitating the assembly of box C/D snoRNPs by stabilizing NOP58-NOP56-SNU13 complexes during their loading onto C/D box snoRNAs, which guide 2'-O-methylation of ribosomal RNAs critical for ribosome function.
