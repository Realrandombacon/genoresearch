## Description
RALGAPA2 (Ral GTPase-Activating Protein Subunit Alpha-2) is a 1873-amino acid intracellular protein with 14 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status as an emerging member of the RalGAP family of GTPase activating proteins. The protein belongs to the RalGAPA2 family (IPR027107, positions 1-1869; GO:0005096 GTPase activator activity, GO:0043547 positive regulation of GTPase activity, GO:0005634 nucleus) containing a canonical C-terminal Rap/Ran-GAP catalytic domain (IPR000331, positions 1635-1843; PF02145, positions 1664-1835; PS50085, positions 1635-1843; G3DSA:3.40.50.11210, positions 1643-1823; IPR035974, positions 1527-1857) responsible for GTPase activation, an Armadillo-type fold domain (IPR016024, positions 931-1310) for protein-protein interactions, an N-terminal RALGAPA1-like domain (IPR060415, positions 2-235; PF27567, positions 2-235), and a RALGAPB N-terminal domain (IPR046859, positions 535-657; PF20412, positions 535-657). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 72.2, high confidence), consistent with stable multi-domain architecture typical of large GTPase regulatory proteins. HPA reveals low tissue specificity with expression detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced single cell type specificity, with annotated functions as GTPase activation and RAS pathway related protein, subcellular localization to plasma membrane and cytosol. ClinVar documents 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly chromosome 20p13-11.21 and 20p11.23-q11.22 copy number gains and losses, all with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong dosage sensitivity potentially affecting Ral GTPase signaling and tumor suppression programs. Recent studies (2013-2020) established RALGAPA2 as downregulated in bladder cancer promoting tumor invasion and metastasis (2013, 53 citations), downregulation promoting invasion of prostatic epithelial cells and progression from intraepithelial neoplasia to cancer during prostate carcinogenesis (2019, 14 citations), RalGAPα2 and NLRP3 orchestrating tumor invasion in colitis-associated cancers (2019), dysregulation in hepatocellular carcinoma (2020), and the interaction between MALAT1 target miR-143-3p and RALGAPA2 affected by functional SNP rs3827693 in breast cancer (2020, 25 citations). We hypothesize RALGAPA2 functions as the catalytic alpha subunit of the heterodimeric RalGAP complex that accelerates GTP hydrolysis on RalA and RalB small GTPases through the C-terminal Rap/Ran-GAP domain (positions 1635-1843), converting active GTP-bound Ral to inactive GDP-bound state and thereby suppressing Ral-driven oncogenic signaling. The AKT2 interaction (0.752) suggests cross-talk with PI3K-AKT signaling pathway. The NKIRAS2 interaction (0.790) indicates coordination with NF-κB regulatory networks. The plasma membrane and cytosol localization indicates RALGAPA2 operates at membrane-cytosol interface where Ral GTPases cycle. RALGAPA2 represents a partially characterized RalGAP catalytic subunit linking small GTPase regulation, tumor invasion suppression, inflammation-associated cancer, and post-transcriptional control through miR-143-3p/MALAT1 axis mechanisms.
