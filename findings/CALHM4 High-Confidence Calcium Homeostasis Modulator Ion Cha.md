## Description
CALHM4 (Calcium homeostasis modulator protein 4, UniProt Q5JW98) is a 314-amino acid integral membrane protein that functions as a calcium-permeable ion channel regulating intracellular calcium homeostasis. InterPro analysis reveals defining CALHM family architecture: calcium homeostasis modulator family domain (IPR029569/PTHR32261, aa 8-305) within the calcium homeostasis modulator Pfam family (PF14798, aa 12-255), characteristic of a recently discovered family of voltage-gated ion channels that form hexameric or octameric pores permeable to Ca2+ and other monovalent cations, structurally related to connexins and pannexins but with distinct topology featuring four transmembrane helices and cytoplasmic N- and C-termini. GO annotations from HPA confirm ion channel molecular function, ion transport and transport biological processes, and plasma membrane subcellular localization. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 74.5), indicating a well-folded protein with stable transmembrane architecture — essential for maintaining proper pore geometry and gating mechanisms required for selective ion conductance. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched expression specifically in placenta (21.9 nTPM top expression), detected in some tissues distribution, not detected in cancers or immune cells, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity — placental enrichment suggests critical roles in trophoblast calcium signaling, nutrient transport, or barrier function during pregnancy.
