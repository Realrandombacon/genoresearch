## Description
CDK18 encodes a 474 aa serine/threonine protein kinase (UniProt Q07002), also known as PCTAIRE-3 or PCTK3, that functions as an atypical cyclin-dependent kinase regulating DNA damage response, replication stress signaling, cell cycle progression, myelination, and vascular remodeling. This well-studied gene (63 publications) has significant clinical implications for cancer therapy resistance and cardiovascular disease. The protein features canonical kinase architecture: PCTAIRE-3 kinase catalytic domain (cd07871, residues 138-425) defining the PCTAIRE subfamily of CDKs; protein kinase domain (IPR000719/PF00069, residues 144-425) with ATP binding site (IPR017441/PS00107, residues 150-173) and serine/threonine kinase active site signature (IPR008271/PS00108, residues 261-273); classified within cyclin-dependent kinase family (IPR050108, residues 142-425) with transferase/phosphotransferase domain folds (G3DSA:1.10.510.10/G3DSA:3.30.200.20, residues 141-459). STRING interactions reveal CDK18 operates within DNA damage response and cell cycle networks: TDRD7 (0.799), tudor domain containing 7 in RNA granule formation; CABLES1 (0.765), Cdk5 and Abl enzyme substrate 1 linking to cell cycle regulation; CCNH (0.654), cyclin H activating CAK complex; CCNI2 (0.631), cyclin I2 potential regulatory partner; RAD9A (0.599), RAD9 checkpoint clamp component in DNA damage sensing; CCNB1 (0.558), cyclin B1 in G2/M transition; CDC6 (0.465), cell division cycle 6 in replication initiation; CDK17 (0.440)/CDK15 (0.440), related PCTAIRE family kinases; CCNY (0.414), cyclin Y in Wnt signaling. HPA confirms group-enriched expression with brain enrichment (367.7 nTPM), heart muscle (116.7 nTPM), detected in all tissues, cancer-enriched expression, not detected in immune cells, actin filaments/mitotic spindle/primary cilium/basal body/cytosol subcellular localization, classified as enzyme/kinase/serine-threonine-protein kinase/transferase. ClinVar lists 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large-scale duplications (1q21.1-44 143-249Mb x3, 1q25.3-32.3 181-213Mb x3, 1q31.3-44 197-249Mb x3, chromosome 1 duplication spanning 130-248Mb), deletions (1q?_190-216Mb_?), 3-prime UTR variant (c.*437G>C), and intronic variants (c.1221+17C>T, c.456+205C>T), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 73.2), revealing a well-folded kinase structure with properly positioned catalytic residues and ATP-binding pocket characteristic of active serine/threonine kinases. Mechanistically, CDK18 operates through integrated modes: (1) Replication stress signaling—2019 study (101 citations) demonstrates MYC-targeted CDK18 promotes ATR activation and homologous recombination repair mediating PARP inhibitor resistance in glioblastoma, representing major therapeutic resistance mechanism; (2) PARPi resistance—CDK18 phosphorylates key HR factors enabling BRCA-intact tumors to survive PARP inhibition; (3) Pulmonary arterial hypertension—2026 study shows TBX5-GATA4 transcriptional complex co-activates CDK18 driving pulmonary artery smooth muscle cell proliferation in PAH pathogenesis; (4) Ischemic stroke in sickle cell disease—2023/2026 GWAS studies identify CDK18 as risk locus for early ischemic stroke in SCD patients (11% of children affected before age 20); (5) Myelination regulation—gene summary confirms positive regulation of myelination, potentially through oligodendrocyte differentiation signaling; (6) Atypical CDK function—unlike canonical CDKs requiring cyclin binding for activation, PCTAIRE kinases may have distinct regulatory mechanisms; (7) Subcellular versatility—localization to actin filaments, mitotic spindle, primary cilium, and basal body suggests roles in cytoskeletal dynamics, cell division, and ciliary signaling. Loss-of-function causes defective replication stress response, impaired homologous recombination, increased PARP inhibitor sensitivity in cancer, attenuated pulmonary artery smooth muscle proliferation, reduced ischemic stroke risk in SCD, and defective myelination.
