## Description
LOC113939933 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 1346 located on chromosome 12q24.21 (genomic coordinates: 118645243-118645537 on NC_000012.12). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element (silencer) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all 4 are copy number gains (duplications), indicating strong duplication-predominant dosage sensitivity. The variant count (4 vs 8 for LOC113939947, 7 for LOC113939953, 3 for LOC113960610, 11 for LOC113939970, 21 for LOC114004412) suggests moderate-to-low clinical penetrance. The 12q24 chromosomal region contains critical genes including TBX3 (T-box transcription factor 3 - mutations cause ulnar-mammary syndrome), TBX5 (T-box transcription factor 5 - mutations cause Holt-Oram syndrome), KRT8 (keratin 8 - expressed in liver epithelium), and multiple metabolic enzyme genes. As a repressive enhancer/silencer, this element likely suppresses expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 12q domain. The duplication-predominant pathogenicity pattern (4 gains, 0 losses) is distinctive compared to other LOC regulatory elements - suggests this silencer normally represses tumor suppressors or differentiation genes where increased silencer dosage leads to target gene underexpression. This element may contribute to 12q duplication syndromes, cardiac/limb developmental defects through TBX pathway dysregulation, or liver pathology through KRT8 pathway effects.
