## Description
PINX1 encodes a 328 aa PIN2/TERF1-interacting telomerase inhibitor 1 (UniProt Q96BK5) located at 8p23.1, functioning as a dual-specificity regulator of telomere maintenance and ribosome biogenesis. InterPro analysis reveals a G-patch domain architecture (IPR000467/PF01585/PS50174/SM00443, aa 24-72) conferring nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), within the PIN2/TERF1-interacting telomerase inhibitor family (IPR050656/PTHR23149, aa 18-327). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 64.8), indicating a partially ordered protein with flexible regions enabling dynamic RNA-protein interactions. STRING interactions reveal a ribosome biogenesis and telomere network: DHX15 (0.983), WDR46 (0.976), SNU13 (0.962), EIF4A3 (0.962) - RNA helicases and spliceosome components; TERF1 (0.953) - telomere binding protein; SURF6 (0.872), FBLL1 (0.865), FBL (0.549), NOM1 (0.470), BYSL (0.424) - nucleolar ribosome assembly factors. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression in vagina (4.6 nTPM) and cervix (1.6 nTPM), with nuclear speckle and Golgi apparatus localization. ClinVar reports 62 pathogenic variants, predominantly 8p23.1-23.3 copy number losses and gains, suggesting clinical significance in chromosomal disorder syndromes. Literature (210 papers) details emerging dual functions: (1) Nutrient starvation response - acetylation of PINX1 orchestrating ribosome biogenesis via RNA polymerase I preinitiation complex (2 citations, 2025); (2) Nasopharyngeal carcinoma suppression - PINX1 inhibiting proliferation and cisplatin resistance by promoting ILF3 ubiquitination (0 citations, 2025); (3) Radiotherapy enhancement - PINX1 silencing enhancing radiosensitivity and antitumor immunity in non-small cell lung cancer (0 citations, 2024); (4) Telomerase inhibition - PINX1 binding TERF1 to limit telomere elongation (established mechanism). Functional hypothesis: PINX1 likely functions through: (1) Telomere Length Regulation - G-patch domain binding telomeric RNA/DNA, TERF1 interaction limiting telomerase access; (2) Ribosome Biogenesis Control - nucleolar localization coordinating RNA Pol I transcription during nutrient stress via acetylation-dependent mechanism; (3) RNA Processing - G-patch domain recruiting RNA helicases (DHX15, EIF4A3) for pre-rRNA processing; (4) Tumor Suppression - ubiquitination of ILF3 and other oncogenic RNA-binding proteins. The dual telomere-ribosome axis represents a critical nexus between cellular aging and metabolic adaptation. Disease implications include: cancer susceptibility (telomere dysfunction), chemosensitivity modulation, developmental disorders from 8p23 CNVs. Despite 210 papers, PINX1's ribosome biogenesis role and acetylation-dependent nutrient sensing remain underexplored.
