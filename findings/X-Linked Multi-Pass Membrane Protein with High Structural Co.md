## Description
TMEM187 (transmembrane protein 187, Gene ID: 8269) encodes a 261 amino acid multi-pass membrane protein localized to the X chromosome (Xq28, NC_000023.11:153972753-153983193). The protein contains the TMEM187 family domain (IPR028066, PF15100, residues 9-245) and shows very high AlphaFold structural confidence (pLDDT 92.1), indicating a well-folded stable 3D structure. STRING interaction analysis reveals 10 protein partners with moderate-to-high confidence scores: L1CAM (0.762), LAGE3 (0.679), MECP2 (0.574), AKAP4 (0.540), CXorf21 (0.533), UBL4A (0.518), BOLA1 (0.517), HCFC1 (0.514), IRAK1 (0.503), and RBM25 (0.499). These interactions suggest TMEM187 may function in X-chromosome associated protein complexes, potentially involving neural cell adhesion (L1CAM), chromatin regulation (MECP2), or immune signaling (IRAK1). Human Protein Atlas data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, subcellular localization to nucleoli and cytosol, and classification as a predicted membrane protein/transporter. ClinVar documents 273 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large Xq28 copy number gains/losses and point mutations (e.g., c.665G>T p.Cys222Phe), implicating this gene in X-linked disorders. The Xq28 region contains multiple disease-associated genes including MECP2 (Rett syndrome) and L1CAM (L1 syndrome). Given its transmembrane topology, ubiquitous expression, and interaction with neural/chromatin proteins, TMEM187 likely functions as an X-linked membrane scaffold or transporter involved in nucleolar-cytoplasmic trafficking, potentially modulating chromatin-associated protein complexes or X-chromosome dosage compensation mechanisms.
