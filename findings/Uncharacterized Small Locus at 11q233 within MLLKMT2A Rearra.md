The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (3 variants) with all copy number gains suggests: (1) this locus may be dosage-sensitive for KMT2A regulation, (2) duplications may disrupt 11q23.3 chromatin architecture leading to MLL overexpression or aberrant fusion events, (3) copy number gains may alter hematopoietic enhancer activity contributing to acute myeloid leukemia (AML) or acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) pathogenesis, or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus with leukemogenic regulatory effects. The 11q23.3 region is critical for hematopoietic differentiation and epigenetic regulation, with KMT2A rearrangements being among the most common oncogenic drivers in infant leukemias and therapy-related AML.
