## Description
BMPR1B (O00238) encodes a 502 amino acid transmembrane serine/threonine kinase receptor that functions as a type I receptor in the TGF-β superfamily signaling pathway, transducing bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) and growth differentiation factor (GDF) signals to regulate chondrogenesis, skeletal development, digit patterning, and reproductive tissue homeostasis through SMAD-dependent and independent pathways.
