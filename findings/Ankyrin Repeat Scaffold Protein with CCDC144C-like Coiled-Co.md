## Description
ANKRD36B encodes a 1353 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q8N2N9) featuring extensive ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110/PF12796 at positions 13-278 with multiple repeat units at 19-48, 52-81, 85-114, 118-150, 123-215) that mediate protein-protein interactions, plus a large C-terminal CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (IPR039497/PF14915, residues 936-1291) suggesting dimerization or multi-protein complex assembly capabilities. The protein belongs to the ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein family (PTHR24147, residues 739-1311) with the ankyrin repeat-containing domain superfamily architecture (IPR036770, residues 13-278; G3DSA:1.25.40.20, residues 17-278). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence folding (global pLDDT 59.2) across the full-length 1353 aa protein, consistent with a partially structured scaffold containing ordered ankyrin repeat bundles connected by flexible linker regions typical of large scaffolding proteins. Human Protein Atlas has no expression data for this gene, suggesting either low/conditional expression or technical limitations in detection. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners revealing two functional clusters: (1) paralogous ankyrin repeat proteins ANKRD36 (score 0.983), ANKRD39 (0.622), and ANKRD36C (0.519) suggesting functional redundancy or shared pathway membership within the ANKRD36 family; (2) cell cycle regulators CDKN2D (0.499, cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor 2D/p19INK4d) and CDKN2C (0.480, p18INK4c) — both INK4 family inhibitors that block CDK4/6-cyclin D complexes to arrest G1-S transition — plus SNAPC2 (0.606, snRNA activating complex subunit 2 involved in transcription initiation). This interaction pattern strongly suggests ANKRD36B functions in cell cycle regulation potentially as a scaffold that organizes INK4 family CDK inhibitors or modulates their activity through ankyrin-mediated protein interactions. ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number variations (gains and losses) spanning chromosome 2q11.1-11.2 regions (e.g., chr2:95435458-97633175 deletions, chr2:95773428-102550061 duplications), suggesting dosage sensitivity where altered ANKRD36B expression disrupts cell cycle control programs. The combination of ankyrin repeat protein-binding architecture, CCDC144C-like coiled-coil oligomerization domain, specific interactions with INK4 family cell cycle inhibitors, and chromosome 2 CNV-associated pathogenicity suggests ANKRD36B functions as a multi-domain scaffold protein that regulates G1-S cell cycle checkpoint progression through organization of CDK inhibitor complexes, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression causing disease through disrupted cell cycle control.
