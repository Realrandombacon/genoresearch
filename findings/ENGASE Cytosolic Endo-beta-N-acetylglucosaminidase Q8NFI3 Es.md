## Description
ENGASE (Cytosolic Endo-beta-N-acetylglucosaminidase; UniProt Q8NFI3) is a 743 amino acid essential glycoside hydrolase with 149 publications, representing a PARTIALLY DARK gene encoding a critical cytosolic enzyme that cleaves the beta-1,4 linkage between the two core N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) residues of N-linked glycans on misfolded glycoproteins and free oligosaccharides.
