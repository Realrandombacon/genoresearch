## Description
CCDC38 (Coiled-Coil Domain-Containing Protein 38, UniProt Q502W7) is a 563-amino acid intracellular protein characterized by a conserved domain of unknown function (DUF4200) functioning primarily in ciliary/flagellar assembly within spermatogenic cells. The protein belongs to the cilia- and flagella-associated domain-containing protein family (IPR051147/PTHR21683, residues 24-561) with a central DUF4200 domain (IPR025252/PF13863, residues 112-230) that likely mediates specific protein-protein interactions within axonemal or peri-axonemal structures. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded architecture (pLDDT=73.0, high confidence) consistent with stable coiled-coil and globular domain structures required for incorporation into multi-protein ciliary complexes. HPA reveals striking testis-enriched expression (15.7 nTPM, highest among all tissues) with tissue-enhanced specificity, detection in some tissues only, absence from cancer and immune cells, cell type enhancement, and explicit annotation for spermatogenesis and differentiation biological processes — indicating highly specialized roles in male germ cell development and sperm flagellum formation. STRING interaction network reveals exclusive associations with ciliary/flagellar proteins: CFAP73 (0.632, cilia and flagella-associated protein 73, essential for ciliary microtubule stability), ARMC2 (0.605, armadillo repeat-containing protein 2, ciliary assembly factor), CCDC96 (0.590, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 96, ciliary protein), C4orf47/CFAP107 (0.581, cilia and flagella-associated protein 107), SPATA9 (0.572, spermatogenesis-associated protein 9), CCDC116 (0.562, ciliary coiled-coil protein), C7orf31/CFAP221 (0.548, cilia and flagella-associated protein 221), LRMDA (0.512, leucine-rich repeats and melanoma antigen family containing, retinal ciliopathy gene), CCDC146 (0.483, ciliary coiled-coil protein), and CCDC42 (0.441, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 42, sperm annulus component). The uniformity of ciliary interaction partners places CCDC38 definitively within the ciliogenesis machinery. ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal duplications spanning 12p13.33-q24.33 encompassing the CCDC38 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity causes disease phenotypes potentially involving male infertility, ciliopathies, or developmental defects given the testis-specific expression and ciliary protein network. The combination of testis-enriched expression, spermatogenesis annotation, exclusive ciliary protein interactions (CFAP73, ARMC2, CCDC96, CFAP107, CFAP221, LRMDA), DUF4200 domain architecture, well-folded structure, and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: CCDC38 functions as a structural component of sperm flagellar axonemes or accessory structures (fibrous sheath, outer dense fibers), potentially bridging microtubule doublets (via CFAP73) with armadillo-repeat scaffolds (via ARMC2) during spermiogenesis, where it stabilizes ciliary microtubule arrays required for sperm motility, with dosage disruption causing male infertility through impaired flagellar assembly or function analogous to primary ciliary dyskinesia phenotypes.
