## Description
FAM186A encodes a large 2351 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt A6NE01) with testis-enriched RNA expression (14.2 nTPM) and nucleoplasmic, vesicular, and plasma membrane localization. InterPro analysis reveals three distinct structural features: an N-terminal domain (IPR049144/PF20870, residues 39-280), a C-terminal domain (IPR049146/PF20865, residues 2102-2330), and multiple PQQAQ low-complexity repeats (IPR049147/PF20869, residues 1102-1285) characteristic of intrinsically disordered regions that mediate protein-protein interactions. STRING-DB identifies 10 medium-confidence interaction partners including FAM47C (0.573), GOLGA6L2 (0.517), FAM170B (0.475), FER1L6 (0.447), TMPRSS15 (0.435), ZNF850 (0.434), UTP23 (0.433), and DIP2B (0.408), suggesting roles in nuclear organization and membrane trafficking. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 50.3) consistent with partial folding and intrinsic disorder typical of scaffold proteins. ClinVar documents 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Thr1161Pro, p.Thr1401Pro) and copy number gains/losses spanning 12q13.12-13.13, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation data shows orthologs in mouse (Q9D9R9, 1790 aa) and California sea lion (2354 aa), confirming evolutionary preservation despite size variation. The combination of testis-specific expression, nuclear localization, low-complexity repeats, and protein interaction network suggests FAM186A functions as a chromatin-associated scaffold protein in spermatogenesis, potentially organizing nuclear subdomains or facilitating transcriptional complexes during germ cell development. The PQQAQ repeats may enable multivalent weak interactions with chromatin modifiers or RNA-processing machinery.
