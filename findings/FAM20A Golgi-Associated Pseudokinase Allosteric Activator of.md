## Description
FAM20A (FAM20A golgi associated secretory pathway pseudokinase, alias AI1G, AIGFS, FP2747) encodes a 541-amino acid secreted pseudokinase belonging to the FAM20 family (IPR024869/PTHR12450, residues 4-526) that functions as an essential allosteric activator of the bona fide kinase FAM20C in the secretory pathway. The protein contains a C-terminal kinase-like domain (IPR009581/PF06702/cd10469, residues 305-522) that lacks catalytic residues but retains structural homology to active kinases, enabling regulatory function through protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT=85.2), indicating a well-folded globular protein with stable tertiary structure characteristic of functional scaffold proteins. HPA classifies FAM20A as a disease-related gene with tissue-enhanced expression (liver 23.6 nTPM top, also expressed in salivary gland, kidney, and other secretory tissues), dual intracellular/secreted localization, and biomineralization function, consistent with roles in enamel formation. Gene ontology annotations include biomineralization and amelogenesis, positioning FAM20A within dental development pathways. STRING interactions reveal strong coupling to enamel matrix proteins and amelogenesis machinery: ENAM (0.774, enamelin), FAM83H (0.744, keratin filament organization, amelogenesis), AMELX (0.720, amelogenin, major enamel protein), MXRA8 (0.715, matrix remodeling), WDR72 (0.708, WD-repeat protein, amelogenesis), AMBN (0.705, ameloblastin), ODAPH (0.665, odontogenesis), AMTN (0.640, amelotin), FAM20C (0.569, secretory pathway kinase, S-xE/pS motif phosphorylase), and PRSS23 (0.462, serine protease). The FAM20C interaction is mechanistically critical: FAM20A forms a heteromeric complex with FAM20C in the Golgi, inducing conformational activation of FAM20C kinase activity toward secretory pathway substrates including enamel matrix proteins. ClinVar documents 72 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (p.Arg437fs, p.Ser115fs, p.Leu95fs), nonsense mutations (p.Gln451Ter, p.Arg156Ter), splice site variants (c.720-1G>C, c.719+1G>T), and missense changes (p.Arg127Trp), causing amelogenesis imperfecta with gingival hyperplasia syndrome (AIGFS), demonstrating strict requirement for dental development. Conservation is strong across mammals: mouse (Q8CID3, 541 aa), rat (I7LRF5, 541 aa). Mechanistically, FAM20A functions as a non-catalytic regulatory subunit that binds and allosterically activates FAM20C kinase in the Golgi apparatus, enabling phosphorylation of secretory pathway substrates (S-xE/pS motifs) including enamel matrix proteins (amelogenin, ameloblastin, enamelin), dentin matrix proteins, and biomineralization regulators. Loss of FAM20A abolishes FAM20C activity, causing hypophosphorylation of enamel proteins, defective mineralization, and amelogenesis imperfecta. The pseudokinase domain mediates heterodimerization with FAM20C rather than catalysis, representing a paradigm of kinase regulation through pseudokinase scaffolding.
