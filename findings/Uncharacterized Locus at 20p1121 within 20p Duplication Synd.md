## Description
LOC284798 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 20p11.21 (NC_000020.11:25148789-25140797) spanning approximately 8.0 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000230725 but reported no detectable protein expression data, indicating this locus is not represented in the HPA protein atlas. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all copy number gains (trisomy) affecting 20p: whole-arm duplications spanning 20p13-11.1 (chr20:80927-26324843, ~26.2 Mb), 20p13-q13.33 (chr20:99557-64277321, ~64.2 Mb whole chromosome), 20p13-q11.1 (chr20:80106-30227427, ~30.1 Mb), and focal duplications of 20p11.22-q11.1 (chr20:22061586-30285812, ~8.2 Mb), 20p12.2-q12 (chr20:9811433-39316956, ~29.5 Mb), and 20p13-11.21 (chr20:89939-25697564, ~25.6 Mb). These structural variants are associated with 20p duplication syndrome, characterized by developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal anomalies, and cardiac defects. The 20p11.21 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including MACROD2 (mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase diphosphatase 2, autism spectrum disorder), DNMT3B (DNA methyltransferase 3B, ICF syndrome), and ADRB2 (beta-2 adrenergic receptor). LOC284798 likely functions as: (1) part of the 20p11.21 cis-regulatory landscape controlling dosage-sensitive gene expression, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 20p gene transcription, (3) a chromatin boundary element within the 20p11.21 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) an alternative transcript isoform of neighboring genes. The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (6 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus resides within a critical 20p dosage-sensitive genomic region where trisomy disrupts neurodevelopmental processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization.
