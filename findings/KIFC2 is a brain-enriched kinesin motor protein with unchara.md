# KIFC2 is a brain-enriched kinesin motor protein with uncharacterized neurodevelopmental disease associations and oncogenic potential

**Date:** 2026-05-08T04:02:00.148427

**Quality Score:** 5/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
KIFC2 (Q96AC6) is a kinesin-like motor protein primarily expressed in the brain. It interacts with other kinesins and contains a kinesin motor domain. Despite its brain specificity, KIFC2 has been implicated in cancer through CDK4/p65 regulation. ClinVar records 58 pathogenic variants but no specific disease associations. The AlphaFold structure confirms a medium-confidence model for residues 1-838.
