# MB21D2: Cytosolic Nucleotidyltransferase with Mab-21 Domain Regulating Cadherin-Mediated Cell Adhesion

## Description
MB21D2 (Mab-21 Domain Containing 2, also C3orf59, D2A, hMB21D2) is a 491 amino acid cytosolic protein (UniProt Q8IYB1) with a well-folded nucleotidyltransferase structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 83.6, high confidence), characteristic of the Mab-21 family involved in developmental signaling and cell adhesion regulation.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals MB21D2 contains a canonical Mab-21 nucleotidyltransferase domain (IPR024810/SM01265, residues 120-405) with two subdomains: a nucleotidyltransferase catalytic domain (IPR046903/PF03281, residues 136-298; G3DSA:3.30.460.90, residues 103-259) and an HhH/H2TH-like DNA-binding domain (IPR046906/PF20266, residues 325-400; G3DSA:1.10.1410.40, residues 276-413). The protein belongs to the ITPRIP/mab-21 nucleotidyltransferase family (PTHR10656).\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals MB21D2 functions in cell adhesion and developmental regulation: strongest interactions with MAB21L3 (0.601, Mab-21 cell lineage abnormality protein-like 3), TOX4 (0.548, thymocyte selection-associated protein), ITPRIPL2 (0.462, inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor interacting protein-like 2), MROH2B (0.451, maestro heat-like repeat family member 2B), TBC1D12 (0.443, Rab GTPase activating protein), CDH1 (0.433, E-cadherin), CDH17 (0.424, cadherin-17), NOVA1 (0.420, neuro-oncological ventral antigen 1), C3orf70 (0.418), and OR52J3 (0.418, olfactory receptor). The cadherin interactions (CDH1, CDH17) support the gene's annotated cadherin binding activity.\n\nHPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity and cytosolic subcellular localization, consistent with housekeeping cell adhesion functions. The protein is classified as intracellular with nucleotidyltransferase and transferase molecular functions. Blood cell analysis shows immune cell enhanced expression.\n\nClinVar documents 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large chromosome 3q copy number variations (gains and losses spanning 3q22.1-q29 regions including duplications and deletions). While specific MB21D2 point mutations are not yet catalogued, these CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity potentially causing developmental disorders through impaired cell adhesion and signaling.\n\nConservation is evident from the Mab-21 family across metazoans, with human paralogs MAB21L1, MAB21L2, and MAB21L3, indicating functional importance in developmental processes.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: MB21D2 acts as a cytosolic nucleotidyltransferase that modifies signaling molecules or RNA to regulate cadherin-mediated cell adhesion during development. The nucleotidyltransferase domain may synthesize cyclic nucleotide second messengers or modify RNA substrates that control cadherin expression or trafficking. Interaction with CDH1 (E-cadherin) and CDH17 suggests roles in epithelial cell adhesion and barrier function. The HhH/H2TH-like domain may bind nucleic acids to coordinate transcriptional responses with adhesion status. Immune cell enhanced expression suggests roles in leukocyte trafficking or immune synapse formation. CNV-mediated dosage imbalance disrupts cell adhesion signaling, causing developmental phenotypes through impaired tissue morphogenesis or cell migration.
