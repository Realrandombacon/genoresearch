## Description
TRMT61B encodes tRNA (adenine(58)-N1)-methyltransferase mitochondrial (TRMT61B), a 477 aa enzyme (UniProt Q9BVS5) belonging to the S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)-dependent methyltransferase superfamily that functions as the catalytic subunit responsible for installing N1-methyladenosine at position 58 (m1A58) in the T-loop of mitochondrial tRNAs, a critical post-transcriptional modification essential for tRNA structural stability, proper codon-anticodon interaction, and efficient mitochondrial protein synthesis required for oxidative phosphorylation.
