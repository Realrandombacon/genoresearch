## Description
COPS6 encodes a 327 aa essential subunit of the COP9 signalosome (CSN), an evolutionarily conserved 8-subunit complex structurally and functionally analogous to the 19S regulatory particle of the 26S proteasome. InterPro reveals characteristic MPN (Mpr1p-Pad1p N-terminal) domain architecture: JAB1/MPN/MOV34 metalloenzyme domain (IPR000555/PF01398/SM00232, residues 39-173) mediating protein-protein interactions within the CSN holoenzyme, and EIF3F/CSN6-like C-terminal domain (IPR024969/PF13012, residues 194-307) contributing to complex stability. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.9) consistent with stable folded conformation. STRING demonstrates near-perfect co-complex interactions with all CSN subunits: COPS2 (0.973), COPS3 (0.982), COPS4 (0.999), COPS5 (0.999), COPS7A (0.999), COPS7B (0.999), COPS8 (0.999), COPS9 (0.985), GPS1 (0.999), plus NEDD8 (0.575) representing the deneddylation substrate. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic localization, classified as essential protein involved in host-virus interaction. ClinVar lists 24 pathogenic variants including large deletions and copy number losses at 7q22.1. Functional mechanism: COPS6 integrates into CSN complex to catalyze NEDD8 removal from cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligases (particularly SCF-type), thereby regulating ubiquitin-proteasome degradation of cell cycle regulators (cyclins, CDK inhibitors), transcription factors (NF-κB, HIF-1α), and tumor suppressors. Recent literature (30 papers) links COPS6 to cancer progression via MAPK signaling, CD8+ T cell infiltration suppression through IL-6 repression, and m6A modification-mediated immune escape in colorectal cancer.
