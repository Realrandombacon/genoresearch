## Description
ZNF786 encodes a 782 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q8N393) localized to the nucleus with enrichment in nucleoplasm, nuclear speckles, and nuclear bodies. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 8-80) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096) at positions 187-209, 240-267, 369-396, 425-447, and 453-475, characteristic of sequence-specific transcriptional repressors. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional regulator role. STRING interactions identify 10 partners including USP49 (0.640), TRIM28 (0.522), and fellow zinc finger protein ZNF223 (0.497), suggesting ZNF786 functions within a TRIM28/KAP1-mediated heterochromatin silencing complex. TRIM28 is a known KRAB zinc finger co-repressor that recruits histone methyltransferases and chromatin remodelers. ClinVar documents 65 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 7q33-36.3, implicating ZNF786 dosage sensitivity in chromosomal instability disorders. The KRAB domain architecture combined with nuclear speckle localization and TRIM28 interaction suggests ZNF786 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruiting heterochromatin machinery to silence target genes, potentially regulating cell cycle or differentiation pathways. Disruption via CNVs may alter repression of oncogenic or tumor suppressor targets, contributing to disease phenotypes.
