MED6 (Mediator of RNA Polymerase II Transcription Subunit 6, UniProt O75586) is a 246 aa essential nuclear protein belonging to the Mediator complex head module that functions as a transcriptional coactivator bridging gene-specific transcription factors with RNA polymerase II machinery. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: MED6 serves as a structural core subunit of the Mediator complex head module that stabilizes Mediator-Pol II interactions and facilitates transcriptional preinitiation complex assembly, regulating global gene expression programs in development and cancer. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: MED6 integrates into the Mediator head module through interactions with MED7/MED8/MED17/MED21, forming a stable platform for Pol II CTD binding and transcription factor recruitment. The MED6-MED7 heterodimer forms a structural core that nucleates head module assembly. MED6 dysfunction impairs Mediator complex integrity, reducing Pol II recruitment and transcriptional output. In lung adenocarcinoma, MED6 overexpression drives oncogenic transcription programs promoting tumor growth and poor prognosis. In bladder cancer, MED6 expression levels correlate with patient survival, serving as a prognostic biomarker.
