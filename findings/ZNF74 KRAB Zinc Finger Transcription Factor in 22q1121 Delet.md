## Description
ZNF74 encodes a 644 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein (UniProt Q16587) that functions as a transcriptional repressor and chromatin regulator. InterPro analysis reveals canonical KRAB-ZFP architecture: N-terminal KRAB-A box (IPR001909, PF01352, aa 42-114) for transcriptional repression via KAP1/TRIM28 recruitment, followed by three C2H2 zinc finger motifs (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 248-275, 276-303, and 304-331 for sequence-specific DNA binding. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 65.3), consistent with structured zinc finger domains connected by flexible linkers.\n\nHPA expression demonstrates dual subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and actin filaments, with broad tissue distribution. This unusual cytoskeletal association suggests ZNF74 may bridge transcriptional regulation with cytoskeletal dynamics or cell motility pathways.\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals connections to transcriptional machinery: MED15 (Mediator complex subunit, 0.850), TRIM28/KAP1 (KRAB co-repressor, 0.848), and HIRA (histone chaperone, 0.663). Notably, ZNF74 interacts with multiple 22q11.21 deletion syndrome genes: DGCR6 (0.603), DGCR6L (0.598), DGCR2 (0.583), and TBX1 (0.464), placing it within the DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome critical region protein network.\n\nClinVar contains 347 pathogenic copy number variants at 22q11.21, predominantly deletions and duplications associated with DiGeorge syndrome spectrum disorders. ZNF74 haploinsufficiency likely contributes to the neurodevelopmental, cardiac, and craniofacial phenotypes of 22q11.21 deletion syndrome through disrupted transcriptional repression and chromatin remodeling during embryonic development.
