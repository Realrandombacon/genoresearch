## Description
SLC7A4 (UniProt O43246, 635 aa), also known as Cationic Amino Acid Transporter 4 (CAT4), is a partially characterized solute carrier with 28 literature papers suggesting emerging roles in cancer metastasis and velocardiofacial syndrome. InterPro reveals canonical amino acid/polyamine transporter architecture (IPR002293, PF13520, aa 37-420) with dual G3DSA:1.20.1740.10 domains (aa 32-432, 467-605) forming the characteristic 12-transmembrane helix fold of the APC superfamily. The C-terminal domain (IPR029485/PF13906, aa 540-590) likely regulates substrate gating or pH sensing, consistent with recent structural findings on pH-responsive transport. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (global pLDDT 80.8), indicating a stable transporter capable of alternating access mechanisms. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in testis (14.6 nTPM) and choroid plexus (13.2 nTPM), suggesting specialized roles in spermatogenesis and cerebrospinal fluid amino acid homeostasis; subcellular localization includes plasma membrane, nucleoplasm, and vesicles. STRING interactions reveal moderate-confidence partners including COQ7 (0.724, coenzyme Q biosynthesis), THAP7 (0.643, transcriptional regulation), AIFM3 (0.589, apoptosis), and other transporters (SLC3A1 0.529, SLC3A2 0.505, SLC7A6 0.484, SLC7A7 0.489), hinting at metabolic coupling or heteromeric complex formation. ClinVar contains 348 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses spanning 22q11.21 (the DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome region), though specific SLC7A4 point mutations remain unclassified (?), suggesting dosage sensitivity rather than single-gene Mendelian pathology. Conservation is strong across vertebrates: mouse Q8BLQ7 (635 aa, ~90% identity), zebrafish (implied by family conservation), indicating evolutionary constraint on cationic amino acid transport. Functional hypothesis: SLC7A4 functions as a pH-regulated cationic amino acid transporter (arginine, lysine, ornithine) operating via the proton antiport mechanism typical of CAT family members. Its enrichment in testis suggests a role in providing cationic amino acids for protamine synthesis during spermiogenesis, while choroid plexus expression implies regulation of CSF arginine/lysine levels critical for neuronal nitric oxide synthase (NOS) activity and polyamine synthesis. The interaction with COQ7 (ubiquinone biosynthesis) and AIFM3 (apoptosis) suggests potential coupling between amino acid flux, mitochondrial respiration, and cell survival pathways. The 22q11.21 CNVs implicate SLC7A4 haploinsufficiency or overexpression in the neurodevelopmental and immunological phenotypes of DiGeorge syndrome, possibly via disrupted arginine metabolism affecting T-cell development and neural crest migration. The pH-responsive mechanism (recently structurally characterized) may allow SLC7A4 to act as a metabolic sensor in acidic tumor microenvironments (brain metastasis) or inflamed tissues. Priority research: (1) electrophysiological characterization of pH-dependent arginine/lysine uptake, (2) phenotype correlation in 22q11.21 deletion/duplication patients stratified by SLC7A4 copy number, (3) investigation of SLC7A4-SLC3A2 heterodimerization, (4) role in blood-CSF barrier function and spermatogenesis.
