# FAM90A20: Primate-Specific Nuclear Zinc Knuckle Protein with Intrinsically Disordered Structure and 8p23 Deletion Syndrome Associations

## Description
FAM90A20 (Gene ID: 728430, UniProt A6NIJ5, alias FAM90A20P) is a 464 amino acid protein located on chromosome 8p23.1 (NC_000008.11, positions 7295013-7298023, ~3kb region). This gene belongs to subfamily II of the primate-specific FAM90A gene family, which originated from multiple duplications and rearrangements during primate evolution (Bosch et al., 2007). InterPro domain analysis reveals membership in the FAM90 family (IPR039213, positions 2-462) with a zinc knuckle domain (IPR041670/PF15288, positions 41-82), a CCHC-type zinc finger motif typically involved in nucleic acid binding. The PANTHER family classification (PTHR16035) groups it with FAM90A1 proteins. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-A6NIJ5-F1) shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 50.4), indicating a partially structured protein with significant intrinsically disordered regions - characteristic of proteins involved in dynamic regulatory interactions. Human Protein Atlas classifies FAM90A20 as a predicted intracellular protein localized to nucleoplasm and nucleoli, though RNA expression was not detected in surveyed tissues, blood cells, or single cell types, suggesting highly restricted or condition-specific expression. Despite UniProt entry existence, STRING database returns no protein-protein interactions, consistent with poor characterization. ClinVar contains 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses affecting 8p23 region including deletions (GRCh38/hg38 8p23.1 chr8:7296514-12636756x1, 8p23.3-21.2 chr8:449893-23854904x1, 8p23.3-23.1 chr8:241530-7895064x1, 8p23.2-21.1 chr8:2475295-27504279x1, 8p23.3-23.1 chr8:241530-10458484x1) and duplications (8p23.3-23.1 chr8:241530-7895064x3, 8p23.3-23.1 chr8:226452-12698554x3, 8p23.3-q24.3 chr8:226452-145068712x3), plus one single nucleotide variant (NC_000008.11:g.7296399T>C). The 8p23.1 region is associated with 8p23.1 deletion syndrome featuring congenital heart defects, developmental delay, intellectual disability, and behavioral abnormalities. Functional hypothesis: FAM90A20 functions as a nuclear regulatory protein involved in transcription or RNA processing through its zinc knuckle domain, which typically mediates RNA/DNA binding in contexts including retroviral nucleocapsid proteins, transcription factors, and RNA chaperones. The intrinsically disordered nature (pLDDT 50.4) suggests conformational flexibility enabling interactions with multiple partners in dynamic complexes. Nuclear/nucleolar localization implies roles in ribosome biogenesis, pre-mRNA splicing, or transcriptional regulation. As a primate-specific gene, FAM90A20 may contribute to lineage-specific adaptations in gene regulation. Disease mechanism: Haploinsufficiency from 8p23.1 deletions disrupts FAM90A20 dosage alongside neighboring genes including GATA4 (cardiac development), contributing to congenital heart defects and neurodevelopmental phenotypes observed in 8p23.1 deletion syndrome. The zinc knuckle domain's role in nucleic acid binding suggests that reduced FAM90A20 levels may impair transcriptional programs or RNA processing critical for cardiac and neural development. Priority experiments include identification of RNA/DNA binding targets via CLIP-seq, characterization of interaction partners through co-immunoprecipitation, assessment of expression patterns in developing heart and brain tissues, and functional studies in cardiomyocyte differentiation models.
