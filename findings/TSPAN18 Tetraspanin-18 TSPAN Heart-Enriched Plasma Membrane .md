## Description
TSPAN18 encodes a 248 aa plasma membrane protein (UniProt Q96SJ8) that functions as a member of the tetraspanin family organizing membrane microdomains (tetraspanin-enriched microdomains, TEMs) to regulate angiogenesis, cancer metastasis, calcium signaling, and thromboinflammation through scaffold interactions with partner proteins. The protein features canonical tetraspanin architecture: four transmembrane domains forming the characteristic transmembrane-4 superfamily topology; a large extracellular loop 2 (EC2) containing uroplakin_I_like_LEL domain (cd03156/G3DSA:1.10.1450.10/SSF48652/IPR008952, residues 105-218) mediating homophilic and heterophilic interactions; classified within animal tetraspanin family (IPR000301/PF00335/PIRSF002419/PTHR19282/IPR018499, residues 3-247/10-245) with conserved tetraspanin signature motif (PS00421/PR00259, residues 62-84) and GO term for membrane localization. STRING interactions reveal TSPAN18 operates within tetraspanin web and calcium signaling networks: PTGFRN (0.645), prostaglandin F2 receptor negative regulator connecting to lipid mediator signaling; ADAM10 (0.495), disintegrin and metalloproteinase domain-containing protein 10 involved in Notch processing and ectodomain shedding; ORAI1 (0.482), calcium release-activated calcium channel protein 1 linking to store-operated calcium entry (SOCE); TSPAN13 (0.481), TSPAN32 (0.478), other tetraspanins suggesting TEM formation; NKAPL (0.468), NK cell activation protein-like; EPCAM (0.464), epithelial cell adhesion molecule in cell-cell junctions; CD44 (0.454), hyaluronan receptor in cell migration; TSG101 (0.407), tumor susceptibility gene 101 in ESCRT complex and exosome biogenesis; and ZKSCAN4 (0.400), zinc finger protein. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with heart muscle enrichment (78.2 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer and immune cell specificity, classified as predicted membrane protein/transporter. ClinVar lists 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including intronic variant (c.-11+167C>A), extensive copy number losses (11p13-11.2 36-45Mb x1, 11p11.2 43-44Mb, 11p12-11.2 40-46Mb x1, 11p12-11.2 39-44Mb x1, single allele deletion) and gains (11p11.2-q12.2 51-54Mb x3, 11p13-q25 32-134Mb x3, 11p15.5-q25 0.2-134Mb x2/x3), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. AlphaFold structure prediction yields very high confidence (global pLDDT 90.6), revealing a well-folded tetraspanin structure with properly positioned four transmembrane helices and structured EC2 domain. Mechanistically, TSPAN18 operates through integrated modes: (1) Angiogenesis regulation—gene summary confirms role in sprouting angiogenesis, potentially through VEGF signaling modulation or endothelial cell migration; (2) Prostate cancer bone metastasis—2023 study (31 citations) demonstrates TSPAN18 facilitates bone metastasis by protecting STIM1 from TRIM32-mediated ubiquitination, stabilizing this ER calcium sensor to enhance SOCE and promote metastatic colonization; (3) Thromboinflammation—2026 study shows lncRNA LINC00607 epigenetically regulates endothelial TSPAN18 to promote hypoxia-induced thromboinflammation, linking to coagulation cascade activation; (4) Calcium signaling—ORAI1 interaction suggests role in store-operated calcium entry modulation, consistent with STIM1 stabilization mechanism; (5) Membrane organization—tetraspanin web formation with TSPAN13/TSPAN32 creates TEMs organizing signaling complexes for cell adhesion (EPCAM, CD44), proteolysis (ADAM10), and vesicle trafficking (TSG101/exosomes); (6) Angular limb deformity—2022/2023 studies identify TSPAN18 variants associated with ALD in Rambouillet rams, suggesting conserved role in skeletal development. Loss-of-function causes defective sprouting angiogenesis, impaired STIM1-mediated calcium signaling, reduced prostate cancer bone metastasis potential, attenuated hypoxia-induced thromboinflammation, disrupted tetraspanin web organization, and skeletal developmental abnormalities.
