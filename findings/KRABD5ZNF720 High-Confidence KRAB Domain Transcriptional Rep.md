## Description
KRABD5 (KRAB box domain containing 5, also known as ZNF720/KRBOX5, UniProt Q7Z2F6) is a 126-amino acid intracellular protein that functions as a member of the KRAB (Krüppel-associated box) domain-containing zinc finger protein family of transcriptional regulators. InterPro analysis reveals defining KRAB architecture: N-terminal KRAB domain (IPR001909/PF01352/cd07765/SM00349/PS50805, aa 4-75/4-44/4-64/2-59) characteristic of transcriptional repression modules that recruit co-repressor complexes, classified within Krueppel C2H2-type zinc-finger families (IPR036051/IPR050169/PTHR23232, aa 2-59/2-79). GO annotations confirm role in regulation of DNA-templated transcription (GO:0006355). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 70.1), indicating a well-folded protein with stable KRAB domain architecture — essential for maintaining the hydrophobic interface required for TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor binding. Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and low cell type specificity — subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm (consistent with transcriptional function), plasma membrane, and cytosol. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners led by TRIM28 (0.661, tripartite motif-containing protein 28/KAP1 — the canonical KRAB domain co-repressor that recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins), FAM86C1 (0.479), FLYWCH2 (0.473, zinc finger transcription factor), MFSD11 (0.462), PPP1R21 (0.450), MOSMO (0.425), WDR90 (0.411), EFCAB6 (0.409), ERI2 (0.406), and RSC1A1 (0.405). This interactome definitively places KRABD5 within the canonical KRAB-ZFP silencing pathway through TRIM28 recruitment. ClinVar contains 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, demonstrating high genetic tolerance — consistent with functional redundancy across the ~400 human KRAB-ZFP genes where loss of individual repressors is compensated by overlapping target specificities. Conservation data indicates this is a primate-specific or recently evolved KRAB-ZFP. KRABD5 likely functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor where the KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor complex which in turn recruits SETDB1 histone H3 lysine 9 methyltransferase and heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1), establishing repressive chromatin marks at target gene promoters — potentially regulating endogenous retroelements, developmental genes, or lineage-specific transcriptional programs with ubiquitous expression suggesting housekeeping repression functions across multiple tissue types.
