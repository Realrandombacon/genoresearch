## Description
MCAM encodes melanoma cell adhesion molecule (MCAM/CD146/MUC18/MelCAM/HEMCAM/METCAM), a 646 aa type I transmembrane glycoprotein (UniProt P43121) belonging to the immunoglobulin superfamily that functions as a calcium-independent cell adhesion molecule localized to endothelial cell junctions, regulating vascular integrity, angiogenesis, glomerular filtration, and vascular wound healing through homophilic and heterophilic interactions while also serving as a laminin receptor and co-receptor for Wnt signaling.
