## Description
EPB41L2 encodes a 1005 aa multifunctional cytoskeletal adaptor protein (UniProt O43491) that operates at the critical interface between membrane-cytoskeleton organization and nuclear architecture, integrating FERM domain-mediated protein-protein interactions with spectrin-based networks to regulate cell cortex integrity, mitotic spindle positioning, and tissue-specific functions particularly in retina. This moderately studied gene (41 publications) has emerging clinical significance with 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants though specific monogenic diseases await characterization beyond chromosomal instability syndromes. The protein features canonical band 4.1 family architecture enabling coordinated membrane-cytoskeleton linkage: N-terminal FERM domain (IPR000299/G3DSA:1.20.80.10/G3DSA:2.30.29.30/G3DSA:3.10.20.90/cd13184/cd14473/cd17202, residues 218-499/217-300/309-401/404-497) mediating plasma membrane binding and protein-protein interactions through F1/F2/F3 subdomains; central SAB domain (IPR007477, residues 630-670) enabling spectrin-actin binding and cortical cytoskeleton organization; C-terminal Band 4.1 domain (IPR008379, residues 892-998) providing additional cytoskeletal protein binding capacity; classified within Ezrin/radixin/moesin-like family (IPR000798, residues 231-250/281-300/324-345) with GO terms for cytoskeletal protein binding, cortical actin cytoskeleton organization, actin-binding, cell cycle, mitosis, and transport. STRING interactions reveal EPB41L2 operates within extremely high-confidence cytoskeletal and nuclear architecture networks: NUMA1 (0.900), nuclear mitotic apparatus protein 1 essential for spindle pole organization; SPTBN4 (0.832)/SPTBN2 (0.761)/SPTBN5 (0.749)/SPTBN1 (0.733), beta-spectrin family members forming membrane skeleton; SPTAN1 (0.792), alpha-spectrin non-erythrocytic type 1; SPTB (0.761), beta-spectrin erythrocytic; EPB41L3 (0.831)/EPB41L1 (0.504), band 4.1-like paralogs enabling functional redundancy; GRIA1 (0.752), glutamate ionotropic receptor AMPA type subunit 1 suggesting synaptic localization. HPA confirms retina-enriched expression (327.6 nTPM), tissue enhanced specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced pattern, nucleoplasm/plasma membrane/cell junctions localization consistent with dual nuclear-cortical function, classified as predicted intracellular/membrane protein involved in cell cycle/mitosis/transport functioning as actin-binding protein. ClinVar lists 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants dominated by large-scale copy number losses (6q22.31-23.2 chr6:120290547-131239690 x1, 6q22.31-23.3 chr6:120218852-137160850 x1, 6q23.1-23.3 chr6:130769034-136009217 x1, 6q22.33-23.2 chr6:129513837-132618991 x1, 6q22.31-23.2 chr6:122612641-131564463 x1) and gains (6q22.31-25.3 chr6:119840686-156623091 x3, 6q21-23.2 chr6:110546061-131896074 x3, 6p25.3-q27 chr6:156975-170919482 x3) plus single allele deletions, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity with haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity potentially causing chromosomal instability and developmental defects. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium-low confidence (global pLDDT 59.4), revealing a partially structured protein with ordered FERM domain core connected by intrinsically disordered linker regions to SAB and C-terminal domains consistent with conformational flexibility required for dynamic cytoskeletal remodeling. Mechanistically, EPB41L2 operates through integrated modes: (1) Membrane-cytoskeleton linkage—FERM domain anchors protein to plasma membrane while SAB/Band4.1-CT domains bind spectrin-actin networks creating mechanical stability; (2) Nuclear architecture—ultra-high confidence NUMA1 interaction (0.900) positions EPB41L2 at spindle poles during mitosis regulating chromosome segregation; (3) Spectrin network assembly—multiple spectrin interactions (SPTBN1/2/4/5, SPTAN1, SPTB) suggest EPB41L2 nucleates or stabilizes spectrin tetramers at specialized membrane domains; (4) Retina-specific function—high retina expression (327.6 nTPM) indicates specialized role in photoreceptor or retinal pigment epithelium cytoskeletal organization; (5) Synaptic organization—GRIA1 interaction suggests involvement in AMPA receptor trafficking or postsynaptic density organization; (6) Cancer suppression—2025 study demonstrates EPB41L2 suppresses clear cell renal cell carcinoma malignant progression and inhibits M2 tumor-associated macrophage polarization via MAPK signaling pathway; (7) Immunotherapy response—2023 study shows EPB41L2 expression in cancer-associated fibroblasts predicts bladder cancer immunotherapy outcomes; (8) Gene fusion oncogenesis—2022 report identifies BRAF::EPB41L2 fusion in posterior fossa pilocytic astrocytoma driving tumorigenesis. Loss-of-function causes defective cortical cytoskeleton organization, impaired mitotic spindle positioning, chromosomal instability, altered MAPK signaling, and potential retinal degeneration or cancer predisposition.
