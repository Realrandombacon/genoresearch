## Description
ZC3H15 (Gene ID: 55854) encodes a 426 amino acid CCCH-type zinc finger protein (UniProt Q8WU90) localized to the cytosol with ubiquitous tissue expression (HPA: detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity). InterPro analysis reveals two canonical CCCH zinc finger domains (IPR000571, PF00642, positions 99-126 and 174-212) conferring RNA-binding capability, plus a ZC3H15/TMA46 family C-terminal domain (IPR032378, PF16543, positions 229-313) characteristic of DRG family regulatory proteins. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 71.1) indicates a well-folded, stable 3D conformation. STRING interaction network identifies ZC3H15 as part of a ribosome-associated quality control complex with high-confidence partners: RWDD1 (RWD domain-containing 1, score 0.917), FAU (ubiquitin-like protein, 0.884), ribosomal proteins RPS15/RPL23A/RPS2/RPS12 (0.728-0.876), chaperone DNAJC2 (0.844), and translation regulator RACK1 (0.829). Notably, ZC3H15 interacts with DRG1 (0.674) and DRG2 (0.418), developmentally-regulated GTPases involved in ribosome biogenesis and cell cycle control. ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number losses/gains on 2q32.1), though no specific Mendelian disease is yet assigned. Functional hypothesis: ZC3H15 operates as an RNA-binding adaptor in ribosome quality control (RQC), recruiting ubiquitin machinery (FAU, RWDD1) to stalled ribosomes and coordinating nascent chain degradation via interaction with DRG GTPases. The CCCH zinc fingers likely recognize specific RNA sequences or structures in aberrant mRNAs, while the TMA46 domain mediates protein-protein interactions within the RQC complex. Ubiquitous expression and structural conservation (mouse/zebrafish orthologs 426 aa) suggest essential housekeeping function in proteostasis.
