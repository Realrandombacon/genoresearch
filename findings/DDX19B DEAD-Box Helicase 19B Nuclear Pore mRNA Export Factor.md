## Description
DDX19B (DEAD-Box Helicase 19B; UniProt Q9UMR2) is a 479 amino acid nuclear RNA helicase with only 26 publications, representing a genuinely dark gene in the DEAD-box helicase family. Unlike its cytoplasmic paralog DDX19A (DDX19/Dbp5), DDX19B localizes to the nucleoplasm and functions primarily at the nuclear pore complex (NPC) where it remodels messenger ribonucleoprotein (mRNP) complexes during export, couples export to perinuclear translation via CTIF interaction, and regulates intrinsic apoptosis through MCL1 mRNA localization.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals DDX19B contains the canonical DEAD-box helicase bipartite fold: (1) Q motif (IPR014014, residues 92-120, ~29 aa) — unique DEAD-box signature conferring ATP binding specificity and regulatory control; (2) DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545/PF00270, residues 117-280, ~164 aa) — RecA-like ATP-binding core containing DEAD motif (Asp-Glu-Ala-Asp); (3) Helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001, residues 111-310, ~200 aa) — P-loop NTPase fold; (4) C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650/PF00271, residues 319-434, ~116 aa) — RecA-like domain completing the ATPase cleft; (5) P-loop containing NTP hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 89-479) — global structural classification. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 81.6), indicating a well-folded two-domain RecA-like architecture typical of DEAD-box helicases, with the ATP-binding cleft between domains suitable for RNA-dependent ATP hydrolysis and mRNP remodeling.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals medium-to-high confidence interactions exclusively with nuclear pore and mRNA processing machinery: NUP42 (0.866—highest; Nucleoporin 42 kDa, FG-nucleoporin at cytoplasmic NPC face), CTIF (0.662; CBP80/20-Dependent Translation Initiation Factor, links CBC-bound mRNA to perinuclear translation), MIF4GD (0.650; MIF4G Domain Containing, translation initiation factor), UBALD1 (0.587; UBL Domain Containing 1, ubiquitin-like modifier), BOP1 (0.587; Block Of Proliferation 1, ribosome biogenesis), NOP58 (0.555; Nucleolar Protein 58, box C/D snoRNP component), PES1 (0.555; Pescadillo Ribosomal Biogenesis Protein 1), WDR12 (0.548; WD Repeat Protein 12, ribosome biogenesis), NUP214 (0.501; Nucleoporin 214 kDa, cytoplasmic NPC component), and GLE1 (0.483; GLE1 RNA Export Mediator, activates DDX19B via IP6 cofactor). This interaction profile definitively places DDX19B at the NPC-cytoplasm interface: NUP42/NUP214 anchor DDX19B to cytoplasmic filaments; GLE1-IP6 stimulates ATPase activity for mRNP remodeling; CTIF couples exported mRNA to perinuclear translation.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA classifies DDX19B as a predicted intracellular protein with nucleoplasmic subcellular location, consistent with nuclear pore association.
