## Description
CPNE5 (Copine-5) is a partially characterized dark gene with ~35 publications, representing a critical calcium-dependent membrane trafficking regulator with emerging roles in cardiac physiology and neuroprotection. UniProt accession Q9HCH3 identifies it as a 593-amino acid intracellular protein. InterPro scan reveals a defining tripartite architecture: two N-terminal C2 domains (IPR000008/PF00168; C2A: residues 2-134, C2B: 161-284) responsible for calcium-dependent phospholipid binding, followed by a large C-terminal von Willebrand Factor type A (vWA) domain (IPR002035/cd01459, residues 298-554) characteristic of the Copine family. This arrangement classifies CPNE5 as a modular sensor that translocates to membranes upon calcium influx to mediate vesicle fusion or protein stabilization. The family classification (IPR045052) explicitly links it to calcium-dependent phospholipid binding (GO:0005544).\n\nAlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 84.9), indicating a rigid, well-folded multi-domain protein.
