## Description
ZNF492 (Zinc finger protein 492) encodes a 531 amino acid KRAB domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q9P255) belonging to the zinc finger protein C2H2-type family (IPR050758, residues 265-447). The protein contains characteristic structural domains including IPR001909 (Krueppel-associated box/KRAB domain, positions 1-43), IPR036051 (KRAB domain superfamily, positions 1-28), IPR013087 (C2H2-type zinc finger, positions 141-168/169-196/197-224), IPR001909 (classic zinc finger C2H2, positions 132-165/166-195/196-221), PF00096 (C2H2 zinc finger, positions 141-163/169-191/197-219), and PS50157 (C2H2 zinc finger profile, positions 141-168/169-196/197-224). The N-terminal KRAB domain serves as a potent transcriptional repression module that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complexes, which in turn recruit SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins to establish repressive chromatin marks (H3K9me3) at target gene promoters. The C-terminal tandem C2H2 zinc finger array provides sequence-specific DNA binding capability, recognizing specific genomic sequences through the zinc-coordinating beta-beta-alpha fold structure. The AlphaFold predicted structure displays high confidence folding (global pLDDT 73.0) across the full-length 531 aa protein, consistent with a well-structured transcription factor containing ordered zinc-coordinating domains and structured KRAB repression module.
