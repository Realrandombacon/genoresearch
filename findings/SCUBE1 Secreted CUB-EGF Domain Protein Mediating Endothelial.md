## Description
SCUBE1 (Signal Peptide, CUB and EGF-like Domain-Containing Protein 1, UniProt Q8IWY4) is a 988 aa secreted modular protein belonging to the SCUBE family (IPR038975/PTHR13782) that functions in endothelial dysfunction, BMP signaling modulation, and complement cascade activation. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal signal peptide for secretion, followed by multiple calcium-binding EGF-like domains (IPR000742/IPR001881/G3DSA:2.10.25.10, positions 33-73/77-116/120-157/362-402) for protein-protein interactions, CUB domain (IPR000859/cd00041/G3DSA:2.60.120.290, positions 798-910/798-909/802-911) for ligand binding, and TNFR-like cysteine-rich domains (G3DSA:2.10.50.10, positions 639-697/698-750/751-801) forming an elongated extracellular scaffold. Literature (145 papers) establishes SCUBE1 roles in: (1) differential levels and biological roles of eNOS and SCUBE1 in endothelial dysfunction systematic review (2025, 1 citation), (2) dorsomedial striatal Scube1 expression correlating with perseveration in rat reversal-learning task modeling obsessive-compulsive disorder (2025), (3) predictive value of serum SCUBE1, CLEC-2 and UCP2 in acute ischemic stroke after intravenous thrombolysis (2025), (4) SCUBE1 and SCUBE2 gene expression differences in Type II diabetes mellitus patients with dyslipidemia (2025), (5) SCUBE1 expression in atherosclerosis plaque buildup. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in ovary (24.6 nTPM), detected in many tissues, classified as predicted secreted and intracellular protein, consistent with extracellular signaling and endothelial functions. STRING interactions reveal complement and BMP signaling network: SCUBE3 (0.758) - signal peptide CUB EGF-like domain-containing protein 3, family paralog; ARID4B (0.486) - AT-rich interactive domain-containing protein 4B for transcriptional repression; C1R (0.483) - complement C1r subcomponent for classical complement pathway initiation; C1S (0.470) - complement C1s subcomponent for C4/C2 cleavage; BMP2 (0.452) - bone morphogenetic protein 2 for osteogenic signaling; GCC2 (0.400) - GRIP and coiled-coil domain-containing protein 2 for Golgi trafficking. ClinVar contains 46 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses at 22q13.2-13.33 (chr22:43451317-50307583)x1, 22q13.2-13.33 (chr22:43107363-51156692)x1, 22q13.2-13.31 (chr22:43451316-46662660), 22q13.2-13.33 (chr22:42972719-51197838), 22q13.2-13.33 (chr22:42321321-51244066)x1 and gains at 22q13.1-13.31 (chr22:39044105-45794212)x3, 22q13.2-13.33 (chr22:43436847-51188164)x3, plus splice variant c.728-247G>A, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 77.3) indicating well-folded modular architecture with stable CUB and EGF domains typical of secreted signaling proteins. Conservation: mouse Scube1 (Q6NZL8) is 1018 aa - highly conserved across mammals. Functional hypothesis: SCUBE1 functions as a secreted modular scaffold that modulates BMP2 signaling and complement activation in endothelial cells, linking vascular homeostasis to metabolic and neurological disorders. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) EGF-like domains bind calcium ions stabilizing protein conformation for receptor interactions, (2) CUB domain binds BMP2 ligand modulating osteogenic and endothelial signaling, (3) SCUBE1-C1R-C1S interaction activates classical complement pathway contributing to inflammation in atherosclerosis and stroke, (4) SCUBE1-eNOS coupling regulates nitric oxide production for vascular tone, (5) In Type II diabetes, dysregulated SCUBE1 expression contributes to endothelial dysfunction and dyslipidemia, (6) Striatal SCUBE1 expression in OCD models suggests role in cortico-striatal circuitry and reversal learning. In acute ischemic stroke, elevated serum SCUBE1 predicts thrombolysis outcomes reflecting endothelial damage. In atherosclerosis, SCUBE1 expression in plaques indicates role in vascular inflammation. Pathogenic CNVs at 22q13 disrupt SCUBE1 dosage, potentially causing complement dysregulation and vascular defects. SCUBE1 represents an understudied secreted scaffold linking endothelial function to stroke, diabetes, and neuropsychiatric disorders.
