# ANKRD22: High-Confidence Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein with Immune-Related Interaction Network and Gastrointestinal Expression

**Date:** 2026-03-20T18:26:13.211278

**Quality Score:** 3.7/10  (E=6.3, D=1.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
ANKRD22 encodes a 191-amino acid ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein that functions as a protein-protein interaction scaffold. The protein consists almost entirely of ankyrin repeat motifs (PF12796, IPR002110, residues 10-166) forming three tandem ankyrin repeats that create a curved solenoid structure for binding partner proteins. AlphaFold structure prediction yields very high confidence (pLDDT 93.8/100), indicating a well-folded, stable tertiary structure typical of canonical ankyrin repeat domains. HPA expression profiling reveals tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in gastrointestinal tissues: stomach (28.4 nTPM), skin (23.5 nTPM), and esophagus (18.0 nTPM), plus immune cell-enhanced expression in blood cells, suggesting roles in mucosal barrier immunity. Subcellular localization is nucleoplasmic, consistent with scaffold functions in nuclear protein complexes. STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners including FCGR1B (score 0.613), an Fc gamma receptor involved in antibody-mediated phagocytosis; STAMBPL1 (0.609), a deubiquitinating enzyme regulating NF-kappaB signaling; ETV7 (0.574), an ETS-family transcriptional repressor; and GBP5 (0.573), a guanylate-binding protein in interferon gamma responses. This interaction profile strongly implicates ANKRD22 in innate immune signaling pathways, potentially serving as an adaptor protein that bridges pattern recognition receptors to downstream transcriptional machinery. ClinVar documents 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on 10q23.31, indicating dosage sensitivity where altered ANKRD22 copy number disrupts immune homeostasis and contributes to disease phenotypes.
