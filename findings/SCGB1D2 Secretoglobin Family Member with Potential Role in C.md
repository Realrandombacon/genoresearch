# SCGB1D2: Secretoglobin Family Member with Potential Role in Cancer

**Date:** 2026-06-27T22:41:33.512179

**Quality Score:** 5/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
SCGB1D2, a member of the secretoglobin family, is highly expressed in cervix and breast tissues. Its expression is enriched in cancerous tissues, suggesting a potential role in tumorigenesis. The protein interacts with lipases (LIPA, LIPC, LIPF) and other secretoglobin family members (SCGB2A1, SCGB1D1), possibly modulating lipid metabolism in the extracellular environment. The presence of 9 pathogenic variants in ClinVar indicates potential disease implications. While its precise function remains unclear, SCGB1D2 may play a role in regulating cellular processes through secreted protein interactions.
