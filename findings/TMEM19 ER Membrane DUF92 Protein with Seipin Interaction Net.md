## Description
TMEM19 (Transmembrane Protein 19) is a 336 amino acid type II integral membrane protein encoded on chromosome 12q21.1 that contains a conserved Domain of Unknown Function 92 (DUF92, IPR002794/PF01940, residues 50-332) characteristic of endoplasmic reticulum membrane proteins. AlphaFold prediction yields exceptional confidence (pLDDT 91.4), indicating a highly stable folded transmembrane architecture suitable for membrane scaffolding or transport functions. Human Protein Atlas classifies TMEM19 as a predicted membrane protein with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity), consistent with a housekeeping role in fundamental cellular membrane processes. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners with BSCL2 (seipin) showing the highest confidence (0.813), establishing a mechanistic link to ER lipid droplet biogenesis and adipocyte differentiation; additional partners include DTD2 (tRNA modification), COX18 (mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase assembly), CDIPT/CDP-ethanolamine synthase (phospholipid biosynthesis), CRLS1 (cardiolipin synthase), and SLC30A5 (zinc transporter), positioning TMEM19 at the intersection of ER membrane lipid metabolism and mitochondrial function. The BSCL2-seipin connection is particularly significant as BSCL2 mutations cause Berardinelli-Seip congenital lipodystrophy type 2, suggesting TMEM19 may function as a seipin cofactor in lipid droplet formation or ER membrane phospholipid homeostasis. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses on 12q15-21.1 and a missense variant p.Gly283Trp within the DUF92 domain, indicating dosage sensitivity and functional constraint; while no specific monogenic disease is yet assigned, the variant burden combined with seipin interaction and phospholipid biosynthesis partners implicates TMEM19 in lipodystrophy spectrum disorders or metabolic membrane defects. The DUF92 domain architecture with ubiquitous expression and ER membrane protein network supports a mechanistic model where TMEM19 acts as a seipin-associated membrane scaffold facilitating lipid droplet nucleation or phosphatidylcholine/phosphatidylethanolamine trafficking at ER-mitochondria contact sites.
