## Description
ELF5 (E74 Like ETS Transcription Factor 5, Q9UKW6) encodes a 265 aa ETS-family transcription factor functioning as a master regulator of mammary epithelial cell differentiation, alveolar development, and lactation. Structural architecture reveals bipartite DNA-binding organization: N-terminal Pointed/SAM domain (IPR003118/IPR013761/cd08538, residues 43-129/32-128/49-132) mediating protein-protein interactions and transcriptional co-regulation, and C-terminal Ets winged helix DNA-binding domain (IPR000418/IPR036390/G3DSA:1.10.10.10, residues 172-258/165-260/134-265) recognizing GGAA/T core motifs in target gene promoters. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 70.8) reveals confident folded structure with structured Ets DNA-binding domain and flexible transactivation regions characteristic of ETS factors. STRING interactions confirm epithelial differentiation network with WFDC5 (0.826, WAP four-disulfide core domain protein 5), CDX2 (0.789, caudal type homeobox 2), GATA3 (0.756, GATA binding protein 3 - key mammary regulator), EOMES (0.747, eomesodermin), TEAD4 (0.734, Hippo pathway effector), ITGB3 (0.669, integrin beta-3), SIRT6 (0.599, sirtuin deacetylase), CSN2 (0.540, beta-casein), TFAP2C (0.473, AP-2 gamma), and GCM1 (0.446, glial cells missing homolog). HPA shows group-enriched expression in breast (140.5 nTPM) and salivary gland (138.9 nTPM) with nucleoplasm localization, classified as transcriptional activator with DNA-binding function; cancer-enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar documents 18 pathogenic variants including 11p14.1-13, 11p15.1-13, and 11p13-q25 CNVs disrupting the locus. Literature (342 papers) demonstrates critical roles in milk lipid synthesis in goat mammary epithelial cells (4x citations, 2025), keratinocyte proliferation/differentiation (2x citations, 2025), buffalo lactation regulation (2025), Brown Swiss cattle milk production polymorphisms (2025), and casein synthesis modulation. Mechanistic hypothesis: ELF5 binds GGAA/T motifs in promoters of milk protein genes (CSN2/casein, WFDC5) and lipid metabolism enzymes; cooperates with GATA3 and CDX2 through Pointed domain-mediated protein interactions to form enhanceosome complexes; TEAD4/Hippo pathway integration links mechanical cues from expanding alveoli to transcriptional output; SIRT6 interaction suggests epigenetic regulation via histone deacetylation. ELF5 loss disrupts alveolar differentiation program, blocking lactogenesis.
