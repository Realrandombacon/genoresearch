## Description
P2RX5/Q93086 (444 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 81.6) trimeric ATP-gated ion channel functioning as a purinergic receptor that mediates extracellular ATP-dependent cation flux regulating adipocyte differentiation, immune cell targeting, and tumor progression. InterPro analysis reveals canonical P2X receptor architecture with extracellular ATP-binding domain (IPR001429/IPR027309/IPR059116/PF00864/PTHR10125/TIGR00863, residues 1-390/14-375/1-388/1-373) containing the conserved ATP-binding signature (IPR053792/PS01212, residues 252-278) and P2X5-specific motifs (IPR003048/PR01312, residues 1-13/58-68/119-128), classifying it within the P2X purinoceptor family (G3DSA:1.10.287.940/G3DSA:2.60.490.10, residues 34-58/344-358/59-343) that forms non-selective cation channels upon ATP binding. HPA data confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (73.8 nTPM) and intestine (23.7 nTPM), plasma membrane and cytosolic localization, and group-enriched blood cell expression consistent with roles in immune cell function and purinergic signaling. STRING interactions define an ultra-high-confidence P2X receptor network linking P2RX5 to P2RX3 (0.986), P2RX1 (0.982), P2RX4 (0.977), P2RX2 (0.976), P2RX6 (0.971), P2RX7 (0.948), and NMDA receptor subunits GRIN1 (0.909), GRIN2D (0.908), GRIN2A (0.907), and GRIN2C (0.906), positioning it within the broader ligand-gated ion channel superfamily mediating excitatory signaling. Literature (61 papers) establishes emerging metabolic and immunotherapeutic roles: 2024 studies identify P2RX5 as critical for brown adipocyte differentiation and energy homeostasis; ancestral haplotype of P2RX5 yields novel B-cell surface marker enabling multi-lineage immunotherapy targeting; P2RX5-directed CAR-T cells proposed for genotype-guided treatment of lymphoid and plasma cell malignancies in patients of African ancestry; 2026 research demonstrates high P2RX5 expression predicts poor prognosis and promotes tumor progression in endometrial cancer; 2014 landmark study identifies ASC-1, PAT2 and P2RX5 as novel cell surface markers for white, beige and brown adipocytes (182 citations). ClinVar documents 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 17p regions (17p13.3-13.2, 17p13.3-11.2, 17p13.3-12), implicating P2RX5 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. The convergence of trimeric ATP-gated ion channel architecture, P2X receptor family partnerships, lymphoid-enriched expression, brown adipocyte differentiation regulation, B-cell surface marker function enabling CAR-T immunotherapy, and endometrial cancer progression suggests P2RX5 functions as a metabolic-immune purinergic sensor coordinating ATP-mediated cation signaling with adipogenesis and lymphoid malignancy targeting, with therapeutic potential in obesity/energy homeostasis modulation, genotype-guided CAR-T therapy for African ancestry patients, and endometrial cancer prognostication.
