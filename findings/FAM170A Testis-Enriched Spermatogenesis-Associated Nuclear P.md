## Description
FAM170A (Gene ID: 340069, UniProt A1A519, alias ZNFD) is a 330 amino acid protein located on chromosome 5q23.1 (NC_000005.10, positions 119629557-119635821, ~6.3kb region). InterPro domain analysis reveals membership in the spermatogenesis-associated protein 46-like family (IPR040879, positions 1-302), a SPATA46 domain (PF17734, positions 217-266), and relationship to the FAM170B-related protein family (PTHR33517, positions 1-302). AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-A1A519-F1) shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 56.7), indicating a partially structured protein with intrinsically disordered regions characteristic of proteins involved in dynamic regulatory interactions. Human Protein Atlas classifies FAM170A as a predicted intracellular protein with functions in transcription and transcription regulation through DNA-binding and activator activity. Critically, FAM170A shows testis-enriched expression (13.4 nTPM) with tissue-enriched specificity and detection in single tissue/cell types, strongly suggesting a specialized role in spermatogenesis or male germ cell development. STRING database identifies 10 medium-confidence protein-protein interactions including ZMYND19 (score 0.651), TUBB2A (0.650), TUBB (0.648), SPC24 (0.621), SPC25 (0.596), DEAF1 (0.587), VAPA (0.575), CSNK1A1L (0.558), MCHR1 (0.428), and TUBA4A (0.426). Interaction partners include tubulin components (TUBB2A, TUBB, TUBA4A) suggesting potential cytoskeletal associations, kinetochore proteins (SPC24, SPC25) implicating roles in chromosome segregation during cell division, and ZMYND19 (zinc finger MYND-type containing 19) which is also testis-expressed and involved in chromatin regulation. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including one single nucleotide variant (NM_001367956.1:c.71-20T>G) and multiple large copy number losses affecting 5q23 region (GRCh37/hg19 5q15-23.2 chr5:93828571-123711334x1, 5q22.2-23.3 chr5:112557391-128106299x1, 5q21.1-23.2 chr5:100607918-125900735, 5q21.3-23.1 chr5:108304806-121335239x1, 5p15.1-q35.2 chr5:17628741-154000000del; GRCh38/hg38 5q23.1 chr5:119555413-119745470) and duplications (5q14.3-23.3 chr5:89949118-129317455x3, 5p15.33-q35.3 chr5:113577-180719789). Conservation across mammals is evidenced by orthologs in mouse (Q66LM6, 333 aa) and crab-eating macaque (Q66LM5, 283 aa). Functional hypothesis: FAM170A functions as a testis-specific nuclear regulatory protein involved in spermatogenesis, potentially acting at the intersection of transcriptional regulation and chromosome dynamics during germ cell development. The SPATA46 domain association suggests roles in sperm development or function. Interactions with tubulin components and kinetochore proteins (SPC24/SPC25) suggest involvement in meiotic chromosome segregation or spermatid structural organization. The nuclear localization and DNA-binding predictions indicate transcriptional regulatory functions controlling expression of genes required for spermatogenesis. Disease mechanism: Haploinsufficiency from 5q23 deletions disrupts FAM170A dosage, potentially contributing to male infertility, spermatogenic failure, or developmental abnormalities observed in 5q deletion syndromes. The testis-enriched expression pattern makes FAM170A a strong candidate gene for reproductive phenotypes in patients with 5q23 copy number variations. Priority experiments include immunohistochemical localization in testis sections across spermatogenic stages, identification of DNA binding targets via ChIP-seq in germ cells, characterization of interactions with tubulin and kinetochore components, assessment of fertility phenotypes in Fam170a knockout mice, and investigation of FAM170A expression in infertile men with oligospermia or azoospermia.
