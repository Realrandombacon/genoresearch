## Description
RGL2 (Ral Guanine Nucleotide Dissociation Stimulator-Like 2) is a 777-amino acid guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) with critical distinction: 185 literature papers primarily describe Arabidopsis DELLA protein RGL2 (gibberellin signaling repressor), NOT human RGL2 which remains a genuine dark gene with minimal direct characterization. The human protein contains conserved Ras-GEF domains including RasGEF domain (cd00155, positions 239-509) catalyzing GDP-GTP exchange on RAS family GTPases, REM domain (cd06224, positions 96-214) regulating GEF activity, Ras-associating domain (IPR000159, positions 648-735) mediating protein-protein interactions and membrane recruitment, Ras-like GEF catalytic domain (IPR001895, positions 239-514) with guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity (GO:0005085) and small GTPase-mediated signal transduction (GO:0007264), and Ras-like GEF family architecture (IPR008937, positions 85-520). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 71.4), consistent with stable multi-domain GEF architecture. HPA returned no data (gene not in database), reflecting limited antibody-based characterization. ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (all chromosome 6p copy number gains with uncertain clinical significance), suggesting potential dosage sensitivity. STRING interactions reveal RGL2 functions within RAS-MAPK signaling networks with very high confidence connections to HRAS (0.980), MRAS (0.941), NRAS (0.932), PFDN6 (0.881) - prefoldin subunit 6 involved in actin cytoskeleton organization, RPS18 (0.874) - 18S ribosomal protein, plus medium confidence interactions with RALB (0.532) and RALA (0.479) - Ral GTPase substrates, KRAS (0.469), RRAS (0.446), and RRAS2 (0.416). We hypothesize RGL2 functions as a RAS-family GEF that activates RAL GTPases (RALA/RALB) downstream of RAS signaling, bridging RAS-MAPK pathways with RAL-dependent effector functions including vesicle trafficking, cytoskeletal reorganization, and transcriptional regulation. The RasGEF catalytic domain (positions 239-509) mediates nucleotide exchange on RAL GTPases, while the RA domain (positions 648-735) likely recruits RGL2 to membrane compartments via interaction with activated RAS proteins. The REM domain (positions 96-214) may provide autoinhibitory regulation. PFDN6 interaction (0.881) suggests coupling to actin cytoskeleton dynamics, consistent with RAL GTPase roles in vesicle trafficking and cell migration. The 6 ClinVar variants indicate potential dosage sensitivity, with 6p copy number gains potentially disrupting RAS-RAL signaling balance contributing to developmental disorders or cancer. RGL2 represents an uncharacterized RAS-family GEF linking RAS activation to RAL-dependent downstream effectors in signal transduction cascades.
