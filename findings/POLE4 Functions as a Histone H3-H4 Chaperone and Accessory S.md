## Description
POLE4 (DNA Polymerase Epsilon Subunit 4) is a 117 aa accessory subunit of the DNA polymerase epsilon complex representing a partially characterized gene with 44 literature references, functioning as a critical histone H3-H4 chaperone that maintains chromatin integrity during DNA replication and regulates replicative gap accumulation. Multi-omics data reveals POLE4 as a vertebrate-conserved replication factor with a defined histone-fold domain yet emerging functional characterization through recent 2018-2024 discoveries linking it to PARP inhibitor sensitivity and BRCA1-independent synthetic lethality. UniProt accession Q9NR33 confirms the 117 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q9CQ36, 118 aa, ~98% identity) and Drosophila (Q9W256, 155 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the histone-chaperone function within the replisome. The structural similarity to archaeal histones and NF-Y suggests POLE4 functions by wrapping or shielding DNA/histones.
