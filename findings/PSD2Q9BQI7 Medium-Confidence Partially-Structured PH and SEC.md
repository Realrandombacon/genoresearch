## Description
PSD2 (Pleckstrin and Sec7 domain-containing protein 2, also known as EFA6C, exchange factor for ARF6 C) is a 771 amino acid plasma membrane protein (UniProt Q9BQI7) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 61.6), characteristic of ARF guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs) that activate small GTPases for membrane trafficking and cytoskeletal reorganization. The protein contains a Sec7 domain (IPR000904/cd00171/G3DSA:1.10.1000.11/IPR035999/IPR023394, residues 275-464/297-464/304-472/355-472) enabling guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity (GO:0005085) and regulation of ARF protein signal transduction (GO:0032012), multiple pleckstrin homology (PH) domains (IPR001605/IPR001849/cd13295/G3DSA:2.30.29.30/IPR011993, residues 508-632/510-633/512-627/515-534/580-597/600-618) involved in protein binding (GO:0005515) and phospholipid binding (GO:0005543), and the Exchange Factor for ARF6 PH domain (cd13295, residues 510-633) that targets PSD2 to plasma membrane.
