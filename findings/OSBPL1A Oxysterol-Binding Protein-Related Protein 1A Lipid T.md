## Description
OSBPL1A (Oxysterol-Binding Protein-Related Protein 1A, UniProt Q9BXW6) is a 950 amino acid intracellular lipid-binding protein with moderate characterization (32 papers) that functions as an oxysterol-binding protein regulating cholesterol homeostasis, lipid transport, and membrane contact site formation between endoplasmic reticulum and endosomes through PH domain-mediated membrane targeting and ORD-mediated lipid transfer. The protein contains N-terminal ankyrin repeats (IPR002110 residues 10-112/146-216 with protein binding GO:0005515, G3DSA:1.25.40.20 residues 1-147/148-236, IPR036770 residues 1-236) for protein-protein interactions, central pleckstrin homology domain (IPR001849 residues 235-336, cd13285 residues 229-352, G3DSA:2.30.29.30 residues 238-341, IPR011993 residues 238-341) for phosphoinositide binding and membrane targeting, and C-terminal oxysterol-binding protein domain (IPR000648 residues 237-942 with lipid binding GO:0008289, IPR018494 residues 647-657, IPR037239, G3DSA:2.40.160.120 residues 632-809, G3DSA:3.30.70.3490 residues 878-941) forming the lipid-binding/transfer module characteristic of the OSBP/ORP family.
