## Description
CFHR2 encodes a 270 aa complement factor H-related protein 2 (UniProt P36980), also known as FHR2, functioning as a complement regulatory protein that modulates the alternative pathway of the complement cascade through competition with factor H for C3b binding and surface deposition. The protein contains three canonical Sushi/SCR/CCP (short consensus repeat) domains (IPR000436/PF00084/cd00033, aa23-83, aa85-142, aa147-205) forming the complement control protein module architecture (G3DSA:2.10.70.10, aa19-204) characteristic of complement regulators, plus a C-terminal complement system regulator domain (IPR051503/PTHR45785, aa145-269) mediating oligomerization and target recognition. AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.6), indicating a well-folded elongated structure with three tandem SCR domains connected by flexible linkers. HPA shows liver-enriched expression (1262.2 nTPM) with tissue-enriched specificity and cancer-enhanced pattern, consistent with acute-phase protein synthesis. STRING interactions reveal CFHR2 associates with complement cascade components: CFI (0.665, factor I protease), CFP (0.639, properdin), C3 (0.507, central complement component), C4BPB (0.631, C4b-binding protein), plus CFHR family paralogs CFHR1/4/5 (all 0.646-0.647), myosin-binding protein MYBPH (0.724), DGKE (0.633, diacylglycerol kinase), and RBMS2 (0.622, RNA-binding protein). ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 1q25.3-32.1/1q21.1-44 copy number gains, 1q31.2-32.1/1q31.1-32.1 copy number losses, splice site mutations (c.254-6C>G, c.59-12T>C), and missense variants (p.Lys27Glu, p.Arg69Leu). Literature (73 papers) demonstrates CFHR2 dimerization enhances C3b binding affinity, CFHR2 polymorphisms associate with age-related macular degeneration (AMD) protection, CFHR2 autoantibodies occur in atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS), CFHR2 supplementation mitigates neuromyelitis optica (NMOSD) pathology by regulating complement activation, urinary CFHR2 serves as biomarker for diabetic kidney disease progression, and CFHR2 inhibits LPS-induced macrophage pro-inflammatory responses. CFHR2 functions as a complement amplification regulator—its SCR domains bind C3b/C3d on cell surfaces, competing with factor H for regulatory sites while lacking factor H's cofactor activity for factor I-mediated C3b cleavage. This creates a rheostat mechanism: CFHR2 homo/heterodimers (with CFHR1/CFHR5) fine-tune complement activation thresholds, preventing excessive alternative pathway amplification on host tissues while permitting pathogen clearance. Disease-associated variants disrupt this balance: copy number gains increase CFHR2 dosage causing excessive complement inhibition (infection susceptibility), while losses permit uncontrolled complement activation (aHUS, dense deposit disease, AMD). Liver enrichment reflects acute-phase induction during inflammation, positioning CFHR2 as a dynamic regulator coupling innate immune activation to complement homeostasis.
