## Description
FAM117B encodes a 589 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q6P1L5) localized to the nucleoplasm and centrosome according to Human Protein Atlas data. The protein contains the FAM117 family domain (IPR026642, residues 2-583) and a disordered PF15388 region (residues 215-528), suggesting an intrinsically disordered scaffold function. STRING interaction analysis reveals 10 high-confidence protein partners, with DCAF7 (DDB1- and CUL4-associated factor 7, score 0.779) as the strongest interactor, followed by DYNLL1 (0.695), ICA1L (0.655), NBEAL1 (0.654), and TSC22D2 (0.637). Notable interactions include dynein light chains (DYNLL1/DYNLL2) and DYRK kinases (DYRK1A/DYRK1B), suggesting roles in microtubule-based transport and cell cycle regulation. ClinVar documents 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 2q32.3-34 copy number losses and gains, implicating FAM117B haploinsufficiency or duplication in genomic disorders. The centrosomal localization combined with DCAF7 interaction (a CUL4-DDB1 E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate receptor) suggests FAM117B may function as a centrosomal scaffold recruiting ubiquitination machinery for cell cycle checkpoint control or centrosome duplication regulation. Low tissue specificity with expression across many tissues indicates a constitutive housekeeping role rather than tissue-restricted function.
