## Description
UBAC1 (Ubiquitin-associated domain-containing protein 1) is a 405 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein featuring a modular domain architecture critical for ubiquitin-dependent cellular processes. The protein contains two tandem UBA domains (IPR015940: residues 187-238 and 288-328) that enable polyubiquitin chain binding, an N-terminal ubiquitin-like domain (IPR029071: residues 8-96) for proteasomal recognition, and a heat shock chaperonin-binding domain (IPR006636: residues 353-392) suggesting cochaperone activity. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with global pLDDT 74.8, indicating stable tertiary conformation. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with tissue enhancement in skeletal muscle (290.9 nTPM) and tongue (159.7 nTPM), localizing to Golgi apparatus, plasma membrane, and cytosol. STRING analysis reveals high-confidence interactions with RNF123 (E3 ubiquitin ligase, score 0.999), VIM (vimentin intermediate filament, 0.874), CAMSAP1 (cytoskeletal regulator, 0.710), and USP48 (deubiquitinase, 0.625), positioning UBAC1 at the intersection of ubiquitination, cytoskeletal organization, and protein quality control. ClinVar documents 68 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and missense mutations (e.g., p.Leu326Val), though specific disease associations remain uncharacterized. The dual UBA architecture suggests UBAC1 functions as a ubiquitin chain-length sensor or scaffold that recruits specific E3 ligases and deubiquitinases to substrate complexes, potentially regulating proteasomal degradation kinetics of cytoskeletal and membrane-associated proteins.
