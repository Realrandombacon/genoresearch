# FAM91A1: Golgi Vesicle Tethering Scaffold with Dual-Domain Architecture and Disease-Associated Variants

## Description
FAM91A1 encodes an 838 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q658Y4) localized to the trans-Golgi network and cytoplasmic vesicles, functioning in vesicle tethering and intracellular protein transport. The protein contains two conserved structural domains: an N-terminal FAM91 domain (IPR028091/PF14647, residues 8-312) and a C-terminal FAM91 domain (IPR028097/PF14648, residues 374-820), forming a bipartite architecture spanning most of the protein length (IPR039199). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with global pLDDT=77.0, indicating confident structural modeling. STRING interaction analysis reveals high-confidence partnerships with C17orf75 (score 0.965), TBC1D23 (0.819), NUDCD1 (0.652), and golgin family member GOLGA4 (0.534), suggesting integration into Golgi-associated trafficking complexes. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic and microtubule localization. Critically, ClinVar documents 53 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutation p.Gly678Ter and multiple copy number variations, indicating disease relevance though specific phenotypes require further characterization. Conservation extends to mouse ortholog Fam91a1 (Q3UVG3, 837 aa, ~99% identity).
