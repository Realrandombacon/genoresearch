## Description
CORO7 (UniProt P57737) encodes Coronin-7, a 925 aa atypical coronin family protein that functions as a regulator of actin cytoskeleton dynamics and vesicle trafficking, distinguished from other coronins by its unique tandem β-propeller architecture and Golgi apparatus localization. InterPro analysis reveals an exceptional multi-domain structure: two large YVTN repeat-like/WD40 β-propeller domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, IPR015943) spanning aa 1-345 and aa 467-815, creating a dual-propeller scaffold; multiple canonical WD40 repeats (IPR001680, PF00400) at positions 66-107, 115-154, 157-205, and 545-572 for protein-protein interactions; and two DUF1899 domains (IPR015048, PF08953) at aa 3-64 and 466-532, unique to coronins and implicated in membrane association. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 86.2), confirming a stable, well-folded tandem β-propeller architecture capable of simultaneous multi-protein binding. STRING interactions define functional networks in vesicle trafficking and actin regulation: PAM16 (0.860), a mitochondrial presequence translocase component suggesting cross-organelle coordination; EPS15 (0.848), an endocytic adaptor linking CORO7 to clathrin-mediated trafficking; CDC42 (0.670) and WASL/N-WASP (0.482), key actin nucleation factors placing CORO7 at the intersection of Rho GTPase signaling and Arp2/3-mediated actin polymerization; KLHL20 (0.413), a BTB-CUL3 ubiquitin ligase substrate adaptor suggesting proteasomal regulation. HPA classifies CORO7 as tissue-enhanced with ubiquitous expression, highest in bone marrow (65.6 nTPM), localized intracellularly with actin-binding and protein transport functions. Recent literature (23 papers) reveals: (1) Arp2/3 complex branch disassembly—CORO7 directly inhibits Arp2/3-mediated actin nucleation, controlling branched actin network turnover; (2) CCT/TRiC chaperonin-dependent folding—the tandem β-propellers require specialized chaperone assistance for proper folding; (3) Megakaryocyte proplatelet formation—high 17β-estradiol suppresses CORO7, impairing platelet production in immune thrombocytopenia during pregnancy. ClinVar reports 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large 16p13.3 CNVs, suggesting dosage sensitivity though no monogenic disease is yet assigned. Functional hypothesis: CORO7 functions through: (1) Actin Network Remodeling—unlike other coronins that stabilize Arp2/3 branches, CORO7 promotes branch disassembly, enabling rapid cytoskeletal reorganization during vesicle budding and cell migration; (2) Golgi-to-Plasma Membrane Trafficking—dual β-propellers may simultaneously bind cargo vesicles (via EPS15) and actin nucleation machinery (via CDC42/WASL), coordinating vesicle movement along actin tracks; (3) Megakaryocyte Differentiation—bone marrow enrichment and estradiol sensitivity suggest CORO7 regulates proplatelet extension through localized actin remodeling; (4) Chaperone-Dependent Maturation—CCT/TRiC requirement indicates CORO7 folding is a regulatory checkpoint, potentially linking proteostasis to cytoskeletal function; (5) Disease Mechanism—16p13.3 CNVs disrupting CORO7 dosage may contribute to hematologic abnormalities (thrombocytopenia) and neurodevelopmental phenotypes via altered vesicle trafficking and actin dynamics. The unique tandem β-propeller architecture, Arp2/3 disassembly activity, and bone marrow enrichment position CORO7 as a specialized coronin with distinct mechanistic roles from its family members.
