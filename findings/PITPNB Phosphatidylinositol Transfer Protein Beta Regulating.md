## Description
PITPNB (Phosphatidylinositol transfer protein beta) is a 271 aa cytosolic lipid transfer protein (UniProt P48739, AlphaFold pLDDT 95.4) belonging to the Class I phosphatidylinositol transfer protein (PITP) family. With 27 papers in literature, PITPNB is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with emerging roles in lipid signaling and neurodevelopment. InterPro domains confirm lipid transfer function: IPR001666 phosphatidylinositol transfer protein (aa3-260) with phospholipid transporter activity (GO:0005548), cd08888 Lipid-binding SRPBCC domain (aa3-259), PF02121 PITP domain (aa2-252), IPR055261 PITP N-terminal domain (aa2-252). STRING interactions reveal lipid metabolism network: PITPNM3 (0.676, phosphatidylinositol transfer protein membrane-associated), PITPNA (0.441, PITP alpha isoform), CERT1 (0.488, ceramide transfer protein), OSBP (0.456, oxysterol-binding protein), VAPA (0.464, vesicle-associated membrane protein). HPA shows ubiquitous expression with vesicle and cytosol localization, consistent with lipid transport function. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants (22q12.1-12.2 CNVs, deletions). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: PITPNB functions as a soluble lipid transfer protein that shuttles phosphatidylinositol (PI) and phosphatidylcholine (PC) between membrane compartments, maintaining Golgi PI4P homeostasis and regulating Golgi stress response pathways. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: (1) PITPNB cooperates with PITPNA in maintaining neural stem cell (NSC) self-renewal via GOLPH3-dependent Notch signaling during brain development (2018), (2) PITPNB regulates PI4P levels in trans-Golgi regions, activating mammalian Golgi stress response (2023-2024), (3) SRPBCC domain provides hydrophobic cavity for PI/PC binding and intermembrane transfer, (4) CERT1 and OSBP interactions place PITPNB in coordinated sphingolipid/sterol/PI transfer network at ER-Golgi contact sites, (5) VAPA interaction suggests PITPNB functions at membrane contact sites for non-vesicular lipid transport. CROSS-DOMAIN REASONING: Very high AlphaFold confidence (95.4) indicates stable SRPBCC lipid-binding fold; ubiquitous expression suggests housekeeping lipid metabolism function; 13 ClinVar variants imply disease relevance potentially in neurodevelopmental disorders; PITPNB/PITPNA paralog network suggests functional redundancy; Golgi stress response link connects to neurodegeneration pathways. PITPNB represents an emerging therapeutic target for lipid metabolism disorders and neural development diseases.
