## Description
NMRK2 encodes nicotinamide riboside kinase 2 (NMRK2/NRK2/MIBP/ITGB1BP3), a 230 aa nucleoside kinase (UniProt Q9NPI5) belonging to the nicotinamide riboside kinase family that catalyzes the phosphorylation of nicotinamide riboside (NR) to nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) as a critical rate-limiting step in the NAD+ salvage pathway essential for cellular energy metabolism, redox homeostasis, and sirtuin-mediated signaling particularly in high-energy demand tissues.
