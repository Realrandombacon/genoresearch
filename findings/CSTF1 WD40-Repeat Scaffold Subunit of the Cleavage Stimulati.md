# CSTF1: WD40-Repeat Scaffold Subunit of the Cleavage Stimulation Factor Complex Essential for mRNA 3'-End Processing with DNA Damage Response Links

## Description
CSTF1 (Cleavage stimulation factor subunit 1) is a 431-amino acid nuclear protein that serves as the foundational scaffold subunit of the cleavage stimulation factor (CstF) complex, essential for pre-mRNA 3'-end cleavage and polyadenylation. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture: an N-terminal dimerization domain (IPR032028, IPR038184; residues 8-60) that mediates homodimerization and complex assembly, followed by seven WD40 repeats (IPR001680, IPR015943, IPR019775; residues 97-424) forming a beta-propeller structure that serves as a protein-protein interaction platform. The protein belongs to the CSTF1-like family (IPR044633) with GO-annotated mRNA 3'-end processing activity (GO:0031124) and protein binding (GO:0005515). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.4) with a rigid WD40 beta-propeller core consistent with its scaffolding role.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals CSTF1 operates in two tightly coordinated functional modules: (1) The canonical CstF trimeric complex with CSTF2 (0.999) and CSTF3 (0.999), plus the testis-specific paralog CSTF2T (0.996); (2) The cleavage and polyadenylation specificity factor (CPSF) complex with CPSF1 (0.966), CPSF2 (0.998), CPSF3 (0.982), and CPSF4 (0.974), indicating direct coupling of cleavage and polyadenylation machinery. Notably, CSTF1 shows ultra-high confidence interactions with BRCA1 (0.974) and BARD1 (0.997), the breast cancer susceptibility E3 ubiquitin ligase complex, suggesting an unappreciated role in DNA damage response. Additional partner FIP1L1 (0.941) links CSTF1 to the cleavage factor Im complex. Human Protein Atlas classifies CSTF1 as an 'essential protein' detected in all tissues with nucleoplasmic localization, reflecting its housekeeping role in gene expression. ClinVar reports 12 pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains at 20q13.2-13.33, implicating CSTF1 dosage sensitivity in disease.\n\nLiterature analysis (14 papers) reveals emerging roles beyond canonical mRNA processing: CSTF1 is upregulated in hepatocellular carcinoma as a prognostic biomarker correlating with immune infiltration, shows altered expression in late-onset Alzheimer's disease peripheral blood, and is implicated in breast cancer DNA damage responses. We hypothesize that CSTF1 functions as a molecular 'hub' that coordinates transcriptional output with genome integrity: the BRCA1-BARD1 interaction suggests CSTF1 may recruit DNA repair factors to actively transcribed genes via the mRNA processing machinery, creating a 'transcription-coupled repair' checkpoint. The WD40 beta-propeller likely serves as a docking platform for multiple partners simultaneously, with the N-terminal dimerization domain enabling higher-order complex assembly. CSTF1 dosage sensitivity (evidenced by 20q13 CNVs) suggests that precise stoichiometry of the CstF complex is critical for proper 3'-end processing fidelity, and that overexpression may drive oncogenic alternative polyadenylation programs in cancer. The CSTF2T interaction indicates specialized functions in germ cells where alternative polyadenylation regulates meiotic gene expression.
