## Description
NUP42 encodes a 423 aa nucleoporin (UniProt O15504), also known as NUPL2, CG1, NLP1, or UIP1, that functions as a critical component of the cytoplasmic filaments of the nuclear pore complex (NPC) mediating mRNA export and nuclear protein translocation through its unique CCCH-type zinc finger and conserved NUP42/Amo1 domains. This partially characterized gene has emerging clinical significance for nuclear transport disorders and cancer susceptibility. The protein features specialized nucleoporin architecture: N-terminal CCCH-type zinc finger domain (IPR000571/SM00356/PS50103, residues 1-25/1-24) providing RNA-binding capability through Cys3His1 zinc coordination; NUP42/Amo1 N-terminal domain (IPR063609/PF27634, residues 119-196) mediating protein-protein interactions within NPC cytoplasmic filaments; NUP42 C-terminal domain (IPR060306/PF27899, residues 392-423) completing the scaffold; classified within nucleoporin NUP42 domain-containing protein family (IPR051767/PTHR46527, residues 1-421/1-421) with GO terms for metal ion binding, mRNA transport, protein transport, translocation, and transport. STRING interactions reveal NUP42 operates within extremely high-confidence nuclear export networks: GLE1 (0.998), inositol hexakisphosphate kinase essential for mRNA export termination; NXF1 (0.970), nuclear RNA export factor 1 primary mRNA export receptor; NUP214 (0.968), nucleoporin 214 forming cytoplasmic filament complex; XPO1 (0.966), exportin-1/CRM1 mediating protein export; RAE1 (0.964), mRNA export factor homolog; NUP88 (0.944), nucleoporin 88 scaffolding cytoplasmic filaments; NUP35 (0.935), nucleoporin 35/Nup53 in NPC assembly; SEH1L (0.933), SEH1-like protein in Y-complex; RANBP2 (0.928), Ran-binding protein 2 at cytoplasmic face; AAAS (0.548), aladin protein linked to triple A syndrome. HPA confirms low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced, subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nuclear membrane, and cytosol consistent with nucleocytoplasmic trafficking function, classified as metabolic protein/intracellular protein/transporter involved in mRNA transport, protein transport, translocation, and transport. ClinVar lists 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.610-2A>G affecting acceptor site, c.609+3G>A affecting donor site, c.523-17C>G in intronic region), missense variant (p.Tyr174Cys within NUP42/Amo1 N-terminal domain potentially disrupting protein interactions), and large-scale copy number alterations across 7p region (7p22.3-14.3 0.02-29.9Mb x3 gain, 7p21.1-15.3 18.1-25.4Mb loss, 7p21.2-15.2 16.4-27.5Mb x3 gain, 7p21.1-14.3 17.7-30.7Mb loss, 7p21.3-15.2 13.1-27.5Mb loss, complex rearrangements), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined beyond susceptibility to nuclear transport defects and neurodevelopmental phenotypes. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 55.3), revealing a partially structured protein characteristic of nucleoporins with folded zinc finger and domain cores connected by intrinsically disordered regions enabling flexible interactions within dynamic NPC architecture. Mechanistically, NUP42 operates through integrated modes: (1) mRNA export—CCCH zinc finger binds mRNA substrates while GLE1-NXF1-RAE1 interactions facilitate transcript release to cytoplasm; (2) Cytoplasmic filament assembly—interacts with NUP214-NUP88 complex anchoring NPC cytoplasmic face; (3) Protein translocation—XPO1 interaction coordinates protein export alongside mRNA transport; (4) NPC assembly—NUP35/SEH1L interactions integrate NUP42 into Y-complex scaffold during pore biogenesis; (5) Ran gradient sensing—RANBP2 proximity enables coupling to RanGTPase cycle for directional transport; (6) Triple A syndrome connection—AAAS interaction suggests potential involvement in alacrima-achalasia-adrenal insufficiency pathology. Loss-of-function causes defective mRNA export, nuclear accumulation of transcripts, impaired protein translocation, disrupted NPC assembly, while overexpression may alter nucleocytoplasmic transport kinetics.
