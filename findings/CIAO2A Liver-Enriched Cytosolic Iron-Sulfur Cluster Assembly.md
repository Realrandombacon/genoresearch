## Description
CIAO2A (Cytosolic Iron-Sulfur Assembly Component 2A, also known as FAM96A, UniProt Q9H5X1) is a 160 amino acid nucleoplasmic/cytosolic protein with very limited characterization (3 papers) that functions as an essential component of the CIA (Cytosolic Iron-sulfur cluster Assembly) targeting complex responsible for maturation of cytosolic and nuclear iron-sulfur (Fe-S) enzymes. The protein contains a MIP18 family-like domain (IPR002744/PF01883, residues 39-103) for Fe-S cluster assembly, belongs to the Fe-S cluster assembly domain superfamily (IPR034904/G3DSA:3.30.300.130/SSF117916, residues 28-138/28-124/38-138), and is part of the MIP18 family (IPR039796/PTHR12377, residues 35-157/35-157) with protein maturation function (GO:0051604), placing it in the essential Fe-S protein biogenesis machinery. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with CIA complex components: CIAO1 (0.999, cytosolic iron-sulfur assembly component 1), MMS19 (0.996, MMS19 nucleotide excision repair homolog, CIA scaffold), CIAO2B (0.965, cytosolic iron-sulfur assembly component 2B paralog), NUBP1 (0.791, NUBP iron-sulfur cluster assembly protein), CIAPIN1 (0.620, cytokine-induced apoptosis inhibitor 1), NDOR1 (0.534, NADPH-dependent diflavin oxidoreductase 1), ABCB7 (0.527, mitochondrial iron exporter), APAF1 (0.524, apoptotic protease-activating factor 1), IREB2 (0.445, iron-responsive element-binding protein 2), and CIAO3 (0.440, cytosolic iron-sulfur assembly component 3), confirming membership in the CIA targeting complex. HPA data shows nucleoplasm and cytosol subcellular localization, tissue-enhanced specificity with exceptional liver expression (245.2 nTPM), detected in all tissues distribution, low cancer specificity, and involvement in chromosome partition. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 86.6) indicates high confidence with well-folded architecture typical of Fe-S assembly factors.
