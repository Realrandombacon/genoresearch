Interaction with TMEM120A/B (recently identified as mechanosensitive ion channels/adipocyte regulators) and WD-repeat ciliogenesis proteins (WDR37, WDR72) suggests potential roles in membrane dynamics, organelle biogenesis, or ciliary trafficking pathways. Ubiquitous expression indicates fundamental cellular function, possibly in maintaining Golgi integrity or regulating vesicle budding/fusion events. Dosage sensitivity indicated by ClinVar CNVs suggests precise TMEM60 levels are critical for normal membrane trafficking homeostasis.
