# RNF217: An RBR-Type E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Regulating Ferroptosis Resistance, KEAP1-NRF2 Signaling, and Parathyroid Function via Ubiquitin-Mediated Protein Degradation

## Description
RNF217 (UniProt Q8TC41) encodes a 542 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase belonging to the RBR (RING-between-RING) family (IPR031127, aa 179-469). InterPro analysis reveals a complex domain architecture characteristic of RBR ligases including a variant RING finger HC subclass (cd16622, aa 261-309), IBR domain (IPR002867, aa 328-396/403-474), BRcat domain (cd20342, aa 338-412), Rcat domain (cd20350, aa 416-483), and TRIAD supradomain (IPR044066, aa 259-478), consistent with E2-dependent ubiquitin transfer activity. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8TC41-F1) predicts a partially structured protein (global pLDDT 61.2), with ordered catalytic domains and potentially disordered regulatory regions typical of ubiquitin ligases. HPA expression reveals tissue-enhanced expression with peak levels in parathyroid gland (39.0 nTPM), detected in all tissues, classified as enzymes/metabolic proteins involved in Ubl conjugation pathway with transferase activity. Literature (26 papers) confirms RNF217 as a critical regulator of ferroptosis resistance in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma via KEAP1-NRF2 feedback loop, therapeutic resistance to chemoradiotherapy, lncRNA RNF217-AS1-encoded peptide inhibiting stomach cancer tumorigenesis and macrophage recruitment, and angiogenesis inhibition in dental pulp stem cells through mitochondrial respiration depression. ClinVar lists 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 6q21-23.3), indicating strong clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to cancer susceptibility or endocrine disorders. STRING interactions identify medium-confidence partners including TPD52L1 (0.644), HAX1 (0.616), FHL5 (0.610), SLC35E3 (0.486), ACTA2 (0.464), SLCO5A1 (0.452), RNF19B (0.419), and UBE2O (0.409), placing RNF217 within ubiquitin ligase networks. Functional hypothesis: RNF217 functions as an RBR-type E3 ubiquitin ligase that regulates ferroptosis resistance and therapeutic response in cancer via KEAP1-NRF2 pathway modulation; its high parathyroid expression suggests a role in endocrine function and calcium homeostasis. Mechanistic proposal: RNF217 accepts ubiquitin from E2 conjugating enzymes and transfers it to KEAP1 or NRF2 substrates, stabilizing NRF2 and inhibiting ferroptosis; in esophageal cancer, this confers chemoradiotherapy resistance; the lncRNA RNF217-AS1-encoded peptide may act as a dominant-negative regulator; copy number variants disrupting RNF217 function may impair ubiquitin-mediated protein homeostasis contributing to cancer susceptibility or parathyroid dysfunction.
