## Description
ALDH1L2 (Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 1 Family Member L2, UniProt Q3SY69) is a 923 aa mitochondrial enzyme that catalyzes the NADP(+)-dependent conversion of 10-formyltetrahydrofolate to tetrahydrofolate and CO2, serving as the mitochondrial counterpart to cytosolic ALDH1L1 in one-carbon metabolism. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence fold (pLDDT=92.8) featuring a bifunctional architecture with an N-terminal hydrolase domain (cd08647, aa 23-225; G3DSA:3.40.605.10 formyl transferase N-terminal), a C-terminal hydrolase subdomain (cd08703, aa 228-327; G3DSA:3.10.25.10 formyl transferase C-terminal), and a C-terminal ALDH dehydrogenase domain (cd07140, aa 439-923; G3DSA:3.40.309.10/3.40.50.170), plus a phosphoribosylglycinamide formyltransferase active site (IPR001555, aa 153-176). Functionally, STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to the one-carbon metabolism network with MTHFD1 (0.976) and MTHFD2 (0.975) for folate interconversion, SHMT1 (0.974) and SHMT2 (0.971) for serine-glycine conversion, MTHFD2L (0.969), DHFR (0.952), ALDH1L1 (0.950), MTR (0.943), and GART (0.940), positioning ALDH1L2 as a critical node balancing mitochondrial vs. cytosolic folate pools. HPA shows pancreas-enriched expression (38.3 nTPM) and salivary gland expression (12.2 nTPM) with strict mitochondrial localization. ClinVar identifies 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 12q23-24 and intronic variants, though no specific Mendelian disease is annotated. Literature (82 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic roles: ALDH1L2 drives hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) progression through tumor-associated macrophage (TAM) polarization (2024), regulates ferroptosis in pulmonary endothelial cells via the FOXO1/ALDH1L2 axis in sepsis-associated acute lung injury (2025), and serves as a CT imaging biomarker in canine HCC (2025). The gene represents a 'semi-dark' metabolic enzyme where canonical folate chemistry is established, but specific mechanisms linking mitochondrial one-carbon flux to cancer immunometabolism, ferroptosis sensitivity, and gastrointestinal disease pathogenesis remain underexplored therapeutic targets.
