## Description
CISD3 (CDGSH iron-sulfur domain-containing protein 3, mitochondrial / MiNT, UniProt P0C7P0, 127 aa) is a 'PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED' gene with 32 papers in literature, representing a mitochondrial iron-sulfur protein of the NEET (CISD) family that regulates mitochondrial complex I function and iron-sulfur cluster homeostasis with emerging roles in liver disease and muscle maintenance. InterPro domains identify characteristic CISD3 architecture: Iron-binding zinc finger CDGSH type (IPR018967/PF09360/SM00704, aa44-78 and aa82-119) for 2Fe-2S cluster binding (GO:0051537), CDGSH iron-sulfur domain mitoNEET-type (G3DSA:3.40.5.90/IPR042216, aa40-79 and aa80-124) for iron-sulfur cluster coordination, and CDGSH iron-sulfur domain-containing protein family membership (IPR052950/PTHR46491, aa31-125). STRING interactions reveal exclusive partnerships with NEET family members and mitochondrial ribosomal proteins: CISD1/mitoNEET (0.992), CISD2/NAF1 (0.962), MRPL24 (0.704), MRPS33 (0.603), MRPL21 (0.594), MRPS16 (0.593), MRPL13 (0.586), MRPL47 (0.583), MRPL17 (0.572), FDX1/ferredoxin-1 (0.555). HPA expression data unavailable. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (17q CNVs and SNV c.217G>A p.Gly73Ser in CDGSH domain) though no specific disease named. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 87.6) shows high confidence with well-folded CDGSH domain architecture typical of iron-sulfur cluster proteins. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CISD3 functions as a mitochondrial 2Fe-2S cluster-binding protein that regulates iron-sulfur cluster transfer to mitochondrial complex I and other Fe-S proteins, maintaining mitochondrial respiratory chain integrity, cellular iron homeostasis, and metabolic function through reversible cluster binding/release mechanisms characteristic of NEET protein family. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: (1) Two CDGSH domains (aa44-78, aa82-119) each coordinate one 2Fe-2S cluster through conserved 3Cys-1His motif, enabling reversible cluster binding/release for iron-sulfur cluster trafficking, (2) 2024 landmark study demonstrates CISD3/MiNT is required for complex I function, mitochondrial integrity, and skeletal muscle maintenance - CISD3 deficiency causes complex I deficiency, mitochondrial fragmentation, and sarcopenia, (3) STRING partners CISD1 (0.992) and CISD2 (0.962) are fellow NEET family members - three CISD proteins form functional network regulating mitochondrial iron-sulfur homeostasis with overlapping and distinct roles, (4) 2025 literature shows CISD3 regulates NRF2-ATP7B axis to ameliorate alcohol-induced liver mitochondrial dysfunction and cuproptosis - CISD3 protects against copper-dependent cell death through antioxidant signaling, (5) 2024 study demonstrates nicotine aggravates high-fat diet-induced NAFLD via CISD3 inhibition - smoking exacerbates fatty liver disease through mitochondrial dysfunction, (6) MRPL/MRPS ribosomal protein interactions (0.572-0.704) link CISD3 to mitochondrial translation - iron-sulfur cluster delivery to mitochondrial ribosome components or coordination of respiratory complex assembly, (7) FDX1 partnership (0.555) connects CISD3 to ferredoxin-mediated iron-sulfur cluster biogenesis - FDX1 reduces iron for cluster assembly, CISD3 transfers clusters to target proteins, (8) 11 ClinVar variants including p.Gly73Ser in CDGSH domain suggest functional disruption - Gly73 likely critical for cluster coordination, variants may cause mitochondrial disease phenotypes, (9) High AlphaFold confidence (87.6) supports stable CDGSH fold enabling reliable iron-sulfur cluster binding, (10) 2025 biostatistical modeling proposes TI-A-CISD3 fusion to restore FAK signaling and mitochondrial function in age-related sarcopenia - The 2024-2025 literature on complex I function, cuproptosis regulation, and sarcopenia therapy suggests emerging research interest, but specific CISD3 client proteins and cluster transfer mechanisms remain largely uncharacterized.
