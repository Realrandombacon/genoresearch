## Description
CLIP1 (CAP-Gly Domain-Containing Linker Protein 1) is a 1438 amino acid microtubule plus-end tracking protein (+TIP) that functions as a critical regulator of microtubule dynamics, intracellular transport, and oncogenic signaling through dual CAP-Gly domain-mediated microtubule end recognition, dynactin complex assembly, and EB1/CLASP interaction for cargo delivery and spindle orientation during cell division. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic +TIP architecture including two CAP-Gly-rich domains (IPR000938, PF01302, PS00845, PS50245, SM01052, G3DSA:2.30.30.190, SSF74924, residues 60-125/214-279/36-164/184-336) for microtubule plus-end binding and EEY/F motif recognition on tubulin C-termini, CAP-Gly domain signature sequences (PS00845, residues 78-109/232-263) with conserved glycine residues for structural integrity, CLIP1 zinc knuckle domains (IPR032108, PF16641, residues 1376-1393/1416-1432) for nucleic acid or protein interactions, CLIP1/2 coiled-coil domain (IPR060109, PF27241, residues 905-1073) for dimerization and partner recruitment, CLIP1-like domain (PF27241) providing structural scaffold, and microtubule dynamics and stability regulators family (PTHR18916, residues 1-1040) defining functional context. AlphaFold structure (AF-P30622-F1) predicts confident folding (pLDDT 71.2) across the 1438 residue sequence, indicating a large multi-domain +TIP protein with structured CAP-Gly domains and coiled-coil regions connected by flexible linkers suitable for dynamic microtubule end tracking and protein complex assembly. HPA data classifies CLIP1 as a cancer-related gene and plasma protein involved in transport with microtubule ends, cytosol, mid piece, and principal piece subcellular localization reflecting microtubule-associated function and sperm flagellar roles, tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (283.5 nTPM), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated microtubule dynamics function. STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to microtubule motor and +TIP network: DCTN1 (Dynactin Subunit 1, 0.997) as primary dynactin complex partner for dynein-mediated transport; CDC42 (0.979) for Rho GTPase signaling and cell polarity; TUBA1A (Tubulin Alpha-1A, 0.934) as direct microtubule binding partner; CLASP2 (0.770) and CLASP1 (0.532) as related +TIP proteins for microtubule stabilization; CLASRP (CLASP-Related Protein, 0.541) as paralog; MAPRE3 (Microtubule Associated Protein RP/EB Family Member 3, 0.521) and MAPRE1 (0.417) as EB1 family +TIPs for plus-end tracking; MTOR (0.521) for mTOR signaling integration; and IQGAP1 (0.485) for cytoskeletal organization. ClinVar lists 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 12q23.1-24.33, 12q24.21-24.33, 12q24.22-24.33, 12q24.31-24.33 (spanning 15-34 Mb regions), deletions on 12q24 (g.(?_121416034)_(122985387_?)del), 5'UTR variants (c.-106-5T>G), intronic variants (c.3967-19T>A), and 3'UTR variants (c.*897C>T), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (108 papers) establishes CLIP1 as an emerging oncogenic driver: hypothermic oxygenated perfusion inhibits CLIP1-mediated TIRAP ubiquitination via TFPI2 to reduce ischemia-reperfusion injury of fatty liver revealing transplant medicine application; LTK mutations responsible for resistance to lorlatinib in NSCLC harboring CLIP1-LTK fusion establishing targeted therapy resistance mechanism; CLIP1-LTK fusion identified as oncogenic driver in non-small cell lung cancer with dramatic clinical response to lorlatinib confirming therapeutic target; KIF20A promotes cervical cancer progression by interacting with CLIP1 demonstrating mitotic kinase cooperation; and CLIP1 roles in microtubule dynamics and intracellular transport confirming fundamental cellular function. As an emerging +TIP with oncogenic fusion potential, CLIP1 represents a critical node connecting microtubule dynamics to cancer progression, ischemia-reperfusion injury, and targeted therapy response through dual CAP-Gly domain-mediated microtubule plus-end tracking, dynactin-DCTN1 complex assembly for dynein-mediated cargo transport, EB1/CLASP/MAPRE interaction for spindle orientation, and CLIP1-LTK fusion-driven oncogenic signaling disrupted in non-small cell lung cancer, cervical cancer progression, fatty liver transplantation injury, and lorlatinib resistance.
