## Description
DENND5B (DENN/MADD Domain-Containing Protein 5B) is a partially characterized Rab guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) with only 29 publications, emerging as a critical regulator of intracellular membrane trafficking with dual roles in lipid metabolism and neurodevelopment. This massive 1274-amino acid protein (UniProt Q6ZUT9) features an elaborate multi-domain architecture characteristic of DENN family Rab activators. InterPro analysis reveals a tripartite DENN domain module—uDENN (upstream DENN, IPR005113, positions 18-120), cDENN (central DENN, IPR001194, positions 187-375), and dDENN (downstream DENN, IPR005112, positions 498-592)—that together form the catalytic Rab-GEF core, two RUN domains (IPR004012/cd17691/cd17693, positions 727-932 and 1091-1269) for membrane recruitment and protein-protein interactions, and a PLAT/LH2 domain (IPR001024/G3DSA:2.60.60.20, positions 936-1044) for phospholipid binding. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 76.4), indicating a well-folded extended architecture with discrete functional modules connected by flexible linkers.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm DENND5B operates within the membrane trafficking network: SLC35D1 (0.625, solute carrier involved in nucleotide sugar transport for glycosylation), SLC35D2 (0.520, paralog), SMAP1 (0.520, ArfGAP regulating clathrin-coated vesicle formation), VOPP1 (0.468, vesicular integral-membrane protein in EGFR trafficking), DENND4B (0.460, paralogous DENN family Rab GEF), SLC24A3 (0.459, sodium/potassium/calcium exchanger), DENND1C (0.454, another DENN paralog), C5orf63 (0.454), ITFG1 (0.432, integrin-alphaFGAP1 in cytoskeletal regulation), and ZMAT4 (0.400). The moderate-confidence interactions with other DENN family members (DENND4B, DENND1C) suggest functional redundancy or cooperative activation of specific Rab GTPases. SMAP1 interaction links DENND5B to clathrin-mediated endocytosis.\n\nHPA shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, consistent with essential housekeeping function in vesicular transport. Subcellular localization includes microtubules—positioning DENND5B at cytoskeletal tracks for vesicle movement. Molecular function is guanine-nucleotide releasing factor (Rab GEF activity). Immune cell enhancement suggests specialized roles in immune synapse formation or cytokine secretion. ClinVar contains 78 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including missense mutations affecting critical functional residues (p.Asp542Gly in cDENN domain, p.Arg683Thr in RUN1-PLAT linker, p.Val26Met in uDENN, p.Arg587Gly in dDENN), nonsense mutations causing truncation (p.Trp1006Ter, p.Arg175Ter), and large copy number gains on chromosome 12p13.33-q13.12. The 2024 landmark study identified de novo DENND5B variants as causative for a novel neurodevelopmental disorder featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, and dysmorphic features. 2025 breakthroughs reveal DENND5B's critical role in triglyceride-rich lipoprotein (TRL) secretion from human enterocytes and hepatocytes—disruption attenuates TRL release, potentially protective against atherosclerosis.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: DENND5B functions as a specific Rab GTPase activator that coordinates vesicle budding, cargo loading, and microtubule-based transport through spatially restricted GEF activity. Mechanistically: (1) The uDENN-cDENN-dDENN triad forms a catalytic cradle that binds inactive Rab-GDP and catalyzes nucleotide exchange to Rab-GTP, analogous to other DENN family members (DENND1 activates Rab35, DENND3 activates Rab12). DENND5B likely activates a specific Rab involved in late endosome-to-Golgi or Golgi-to-plasma membrane trafficking. (2) The dual RUN domains recruit DENND5B to specific membrane compartments via interactions with phosphoinositides or adaptor proteins—RUN1 may bind early endosomes while RUN2 targets Golgi membranes. (3) The PLAT/LH2 domain anchors DENND5B to phospholipid-rich microdomains, positioning the GEF near its Rab substrate. (4) In enterocytes/hepatocytes, DENND5B activates Rabs required for chylomicron/VLDL assembly and secretion—loss causes intracellular lipid accumulation and reduced plasma triglycerides. (5) In neurons, DENND5B regulates synaptic vesicle recycling or neurotrophin receptor trafficking (TrkB, EGFR via VOPP1 interaction)—de novo missense variants in DENN domains impair GEF catalysis, disrupting axonal transport and synaptic plasticity underlying neurodevelopmental phenotypes. The microtubule localization suggests DENND5B may couple Rab activation to kinesin/dynein motor recruitment for long-range vesicle transport.
