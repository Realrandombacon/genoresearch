# FAM107A/DRR1: Actin-Associated Nuclear Scaffold Protein with Tumor Suppressor Function

**Date:** 2026-03-23T08:45:19.274110

**Quality Score:** 3.4/10  (E=4.8, D=2.0) [WEAK]

## Description
FAM107A (also known as DRR1/TU3A) encodes a 144-amino acid actin-associated protein (UniProt O95990) localized to nuclear speckles. The protein contains a DUF1151 domain (PF06625, residues 27-133) within the FAM107 family (IPR009533). HPA data shows strong brain-enriched expression (1181.3 nTPM in brain, 331.5 nTPM in choroid plexus) with cancer-enriched RNA specificity. STRING interactions reveal associations with HOPX (0.644), FKBPL (0.610), LIFR (0.447), CRYAB (0.442), and PTPRZ1 (0.429), suggesting roles in chromatin regulation and stress response. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic copy number variants (deletions/amplifications at 3p14-21), implicating FAM107A loss in disease. The actin-binding molecular function combined with nuclear speckle localization suggests FAM107A serves as a nucleocytoplasmic scaffold linking cytoskeletal dynamics to transcriptional regulation. High brain expression and cancer enrichment support a tumor suppressor role, particularly in renal cell carcinoma where DRR1 was originally identified as downregulated. The protein likely coordinates actin-dependent chromatin remodeling through interactions with homeobox regulators (HOPX, TBR1) and stress-response chaperones (CRYAB).
