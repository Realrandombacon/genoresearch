## Description
SAXO1 encodes a 474 aa stabilizer of axonemal microtubules 1 (UniProt Q8IYX7), also known as FAM154A, functioning as a cilia-specific microtubule-associated protein that stabilizes axonemal microtubules in sperm flagella and motile cilia. The protein belongs to the SAXO1/2 family (IPR033336, PF05217, aa1-474) with PTHR31516 homology (aa2-452), has GO annotation for microtubule binding (GO:0008017), and is classified as a predicted intracellular protein.
