## Description
ANAPC10 (Anaphase-Promoting Complex Subunit 10, also known as DOC1, UniProt Q9UM13) is a 185 aa essential core subunit of the APC/C E3 ubiquitin ligase complex that mediates substrate ubiquitination during cell cycle progression. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical APC10/DOC domain architecture (IPR004939, PF03256, positions 5-182; SM01337, positions 22-184; cd08366, positions 23-160) within the galactose-binding-like domain superfamily (IPR008979, G3DSA:2.60.120.260, SSF49785, positions 11-160) with DOC domain profile (PS51284, positions 1-185). The protein belongs to the anaphase-promoting complex subunit APC10/Doc1 family (IPR016901, PTHR12936, positions 2-185; PIRSF028841, positions 13-185) with GO annotations for anaphase-promoting complex-dependent catabolic process (GO:0031145) and anaphase-promoting complex (GO:0005680). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.2), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined DOC domain geometry for APC/C core assembly and substrate recognition. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity), nucleoplasm/Golgi apparatus localization, classified as essential predicted intracellular protein involved in cell cycle, cell division, mitosis, and Ubl conjugation pathway. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with APC/C complex core subunits at maximum confidence: ANAPC2 (0.999, catalytic core), ANAPC1 (0.999, scaffold), CDC16 (0.999, tetratricopeptide repeat subunit), CDC20 (0.999, activator), CDC26 (0.999, core subunit), ANAPC11 (0.996, RING-H2 finger catalytic subunit), ANAPC5 (0.950, cullin scaffold), plus cell cycle regulators BUB1B (0.964, spindle assembly checkpoint kinase), CCNB1 (0.797, cyclin B1 substrate), MAD2L1 (0.669, spindle checkpoint protein). ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 4q24-35.2 affecting the ANAPC10 locus. Literature (21 papers) establishes ANAPC10 roles in: (1) oral squamous cell carcinoma poor prognosis biomarker promoting tumor growth via PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway (2025), (2) Sox21 transcription factor regulation determining ectodermal organ fate in tooth development (2020, 21 citations), (3) NLRP3 inflammasome activation orchestration during cell cycle control (2021), and (4) novel meiotic gene in mice (2012, 6 citations). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ANAPC10/DOC1 serves as a structural core subunit stabilizing the APC/C complex architecture while mediating substrate recognition and processive ubiquitination during mitotic exit and G1 phase maintenance. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The DOC domain mediates interactions with APC/C core subunits (ANAPC1/2/5/11) and activators (CDC20/CDH1), positioning substrates (cyclin B1, securin) for ubiquitination by the RING-H2 catalytic subunit ANAPC11. ANAPC10 dysfunction impairs APC/C activity, causing mitotic arrest and genomic instability. In OSCC, ANAPC10 overexpression activates PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling independent of canonical APC/C function, promoting tumor growth. During tooth development, Sox21-mediated ANAPC10 regulation controls epithelial cell proliferation/differentiation balance. The NLRP3 inflammasome connection suggests ANAPC10 links cell cycle progression to innate immune activation, possibly through APC/C-mediated degradation of inflammasome regulators. The 27 ClinVar CNVs indicate ANAPC10 dosage sensitivity may contribute to developmental disorders or cancer predisposition. Essential protein classification confirms fundamental role in cell viability.
