# LOC129389339: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 5q21.3 with Critical Role in 5q Deletion Syndromes

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:06:02.587070

**Quality Score:** 1.45/10  (E=2.6, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
LOC129389339 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 5q21.3 (GRCh38: 108701034-108701234), functionally characterized as a transcriptional silencer via STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter or enhancer chromatin signatures but demonstrates potent repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it possesses no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. However, ClinVar analysis identifies 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic structural variants disrupting this region, predominantly large-scale copy number losses spanning 5q14.3-23.3 (e.g., chr5:91411708-131319567, ~40Mb; chr5:108308463-125777797, ~17.5Mb) and rare duplications (chr5:106547519-110687501). These CNVs are hallmarks of 5q deletion syndromes (5q- syndrome), associated with myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS), acute myeloid leukemia (AML), and developmental anomalies. \n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389339 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 5q31.1 topologically associating domain (TAD), likely regulating the expression of key tumor suppressor genes such as EGR1, IRF1, or CTNNA1 located in the commonly deleted region. By recruiting co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, HDACs) to target promoters, it maintains basal repression of proto-oncogenes or cell cycle regulators in hematopoietic progenitors. Haploinsufficiency of this silencer due to 5q deletions leads to aberrant overexpression of target genes, driving clonal expansion and leukemogenesis. The high frequency of pathogenic CNVs encompassing this locus underscores its essential role in genomic stability and hematopoietic differentiation, positioning it as a non-coding driver of 5q- syndrome pathophysiology.
