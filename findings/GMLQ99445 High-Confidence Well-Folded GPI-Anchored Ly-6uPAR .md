## Description
GML (Glycosylphosphatidylinositol-anchored molecule-like protein, also known as LY6DL) is a 158 amino acid plasma membrane protein (UniProt Q99445) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 76.3), characteristic of Ly-6/uPAR family members.
