## Description
PRKAR2B (Protein Kinase cAMP-Dependent Type II Regulatory Subunit Beta) is a 418 amino acid regulatory subunit of protein kinase A (PKA) that binds cAMP and controls the activity of catalytic subunits, serving as a critical regulator of lipolysis, adipocyte differentiation, and glucose metabolism through compartmentalized cAMP signaling. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic regulatory subunit architecture including N-terminal dimerization/docking domain (IPR003117, cd12104, G3DSA:1.20.890.10, residues 3-45) for R-subunit dimerization and A-kinase anchoring protein (AKAP) binding, two tandem cyclic nucleotide-binding domains (IPR000595, cd00038, residues 154-274/276-402) with conserved cAMP-binding sites (IPR018488, residues 181-197/221-238/303-319) for allosteric activation, jelly roll fold structural homology (IPR014710, G3DSA:2.60.120.10, residues 114-409) providing cyclic nucleotide recognition scaffold, and cAMP-dependent protein kinase regulatory subunit family signature (IPR012198, residues 18-411) with regulator activity (GO:0008603). AlphaFold structure (AF-P31323-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 78.7) across the 418 residue sequence, indicating a stable regulatory protein with distinct dimerization and cAMP-binding domains suitable for PKA holoenzyme assembly and compartmentalized signaling. HPA data classifies PRKAR2B as a predicted intracellular protein with tissue-enhanced expression predominantly in adipose tissue (159.6 nTPM), cancer-enhanced distribution, detected in many tissues, cell type-enhanced specificity, and Golgi apparatus, centrosome, basal body, and cytosol localization reflecting AKAP-mediated subcellular targeting. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling to the PKA signaling network: exceptionally strong associations with catalytic subunits PRKACB (0.999), PRKACA (0.999), PRKACG (0.999); AKAP scaffolding proteins AKAP5 (0.996) and AKAP1 (0.981) for subcellular anchoring; other regulatory subunits PRKAR1B (0.968), PRKAR1A (0.954), PRKAR2A (0.905) for holoenzyme diversity; LIPE (Hormone-sensitive lipase, 0.906) as a key PKA substrate in lipolysis; and PRKX (0.680) as a related cAMP-dependent kinase. ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses on 7q22.3-31.1, 7q22.1-31.31, 7q22.1-31.1 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 99-123 Mb), deletions (c.(?_102937907)_(108155935_?)del), promoter variants (c.-4C>T), and complex rearrangements, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in metabolic disorder or cancer susceptibility. Literature (124 papers) establishes PRKAR2B as a context-dependent metabolic regulator: circSAMD4A promotes porcine adipocyte differentiation via targeting miR-127/PRKAR2B ceRNA axis linking non-coding RNA to adipogenesis; glomerular mesangial derived extracellular vesicles deteriorate diabetic kidney disease via miR-3147/PRKAR2B axis; miR-34c-3p regulates PKA activity independent of cAMP by dicing prkar2b transcripts in Theileria annulata-infected leukocytes revealing parasite manipulation of host signaling; HHEX-PRKAR2B axis-mediated PKA activation drives glucose metabolism-dependent progression of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma; and PRKAR2B functions as the predominant type II regulatory subunit in adipocytes controlling hormone-stimulated lipolysis through PKA-mediated phosphorylation of perilipin and hormone-sensitive lipase. As a well-studied PKA regulatory subunit with defined roles in metabolism and cancer, PRKAR2B represents a critical node connecting cAMP signaling to lipid mobilization, adipocyte biology, and tumor metabolism through AKAP-directed spatial control of PKA activity.
