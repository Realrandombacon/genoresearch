## Description
CCDC180 encodes a 1701 amino acid coiled-coil domain protein (UniProt Q9P1Z9) with two domains of unknown function: DUF4455 (PF14643, aa 175-641) and DUF4456 (PF14644, aa 1389-1592). AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 69.1), suggesting partially ordered coiled-coil regions with flexible linkers. HPA expression reveals enrichment in ciliated tissues: choroid plexus (13.3 nTPM), testis (12.3 nTPM), fallopian tube (6.0 nTPM), and retina (6.0 nTPM), with lower brain expression (3.7 nTPM). STRING interactions identify 10 high-confidence partners including TDRD7 (0.926), CDK17 (0.829), CFAP221 (0.621), SPAG17 (0.609), and LRGUK (0.603) — many are cilia/flagella-associated proteins (CFAP= cilia and flagella associated protein, SPAG= sperm associated antigen). ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses on chromosome 9q, though no specific monogenic disease is yet assigned. A SNP in CCDC180 has been associated with Behcet\'s disease susceptibility (PMID: 19442274). The PTHR21444 family annotation links it to STRA6 (retinol uptake receptor)
