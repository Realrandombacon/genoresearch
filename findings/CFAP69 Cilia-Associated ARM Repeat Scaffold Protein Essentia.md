# CFAP69: Cilia-Associated ARM Repeat Scaffold Protein Essential for Sperm Axoneme Assembly

**Date:** 2026-03-20T10:43:26.764779

**Quality Score:** 4.15/10  (E=6.2, D=2.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
CFAP69 encodes a 941 amino acid cilia- and flagella-associated protein (UniProt A5D8W1) localized to cytoplasm and sperm midpiece. InterPro analysis reveals extensive ARM (armadillo) repeat domains (IPR016024, PF21049) spanning residues 46-763, forming a solenoid scaffold for protein-protein interactions. The protein belongs to the CFAP69 family (IPR048732) with a conserved ARM repeat domain (IPR048733). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 85.1) across residues 1-941, consistent with stable scaffolding function. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with peak levels in choroid plexus (50.5 nTPM) and involvement in spermatogenesis, sensory transduction, and olfaction. STRING interactions reveal CFAP69 functions within a cilia/axoneme assembly complex with partners: CFAP44 (0.800), DNAH1 (0.703, dynein heavy chain), FSIP2 (0.692), ARMC2 (0.679), SPEF2 (0.635), CFAP43 (0.625), QRICH2 (0.609), CFAP70 (0.589), MAATS1 (0.585), and WDR66 (0.460). ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg868Ter, p.Trp216Ter, p.Gln255Ter) and frameshifts (p.Ile114fs, p.Leu357fs) causing spermatogenic failure 24 (SPGF24), plus chromosome 7q CNVs. The mechanistic model: CFAP69 acts as a large ARM repeat scaffold that nucleates axonemal dynein complexes during sperm flagellum assembly. ARM repeats mediate interactions with dynein heavy chains (DNAH1) and other ciliopathy proteins (CFAP43/44/70). Loss-of-function variants disrupt axoneme microtubule organization, causing asthenozoospermia and male infertility. Choroid plexus expression suggests potential additional roles in ependymal cilia function.
