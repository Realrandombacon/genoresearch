## Description
CORO2A (UniProt Q92828) encodes a 525-amino acid member of the coronin family, characterized by an N-terminal DUF1899 domain (IPR015048/PF08953, aa 5-70) followed by a series of WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400) forming a beta-propeller structure essential for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT: 85.4), consistent with stable WD40 scaffolds. STRING interactions reveal CORO2A's dual role: it links to the nuclear receptor co-repressor complex via NCOR1 (0.762) and GPS2 (0.479), while simultaneously connecting to cytoskeletal regulators including COP1 (0.756), WDR1 (0.580), ACTB (0.544), and CFL2 (0.466). HPA data shows intestine-enriched expression (33.0 nTPM) with ubiquitous distribution, localizing to vesicles, plasma membrane, basal bodies, and cytosol, confirming its actin-binding function.\n\nDespite only 27 papers, emerging literature defines CORO2A as a critical driver of cancer metastasis. Recent studies (2021-2026) demonstrate that CORO2A facilitates migration and invasion in oral squamous cell carcinoma and breast cancer by remodeling the actin cytoskeleton. A 2026 study identifies a novel host-targeted mechanism where apple pectic oligosaccharides repair intestinal barriers in colitis by modulating the HSP90β/miRNA/CORO2A axis, linking it to inflammatory bowel disease. ClinVar contains 33 pathogenic variants, primarily large copy number gains/losses and deletions at 9q22.33, though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. This positions CORO2A as a 'transitional dark gene'—its structural role in the N-CoR complex and actin dynamics is established, but its specific transcriptional targets and full disease spectrum remain poorly explored.
