## Description
UBE2J2/Q8N2K1 (259 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 84.4) endoplasmic reticulum-associated ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme functioning as a critical E2 component of the ERAD (ER-associated degradation) machinery that sensitizes ubiquitination cascades to membrane lipid saturation changes and regulates apoptosis in cancer therapy response. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical UBC catalytic core domain architecture (IPR000608/PF00179/PS50127, residues 12-162/16-125/12-162) containing the signature E2 ubiquitin-conjugating active site cysteine and UBC fold (G3DSA:3.10.110.10/SSF54495, residues 1-185/13-166), classifying it within the E2 J1/J2 subfamily (cd23799, residues 15-147) specialized for ER membrane-associated protein quality control. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classification as an enzyme/metabolic protein involved in Ubl conjugation pathway and unfolded protein response, and cell type-enhanced expression pattern consistent with housekeeping ERAD functions. STRING interactions define a high-confidence ERAD and membrane protein quality control network linking UBE2J2 to endocytosis adaptors NECAP1 (0.881) and NECAP2 (0.874), AAA ATPase VCP/p97 (0.870) essential for ERAD substrate extraction, ER-resident E3 ligase TMEM129 (0.858), selenoprotein SELENOS (0.835) involved in ER stress response, E1 activating enzyme UBA1 (0.801), ERAD component DERL1 (0.646), glycosyltransferase B3GALT6 (0.594), related E2 enzyme UBE2G2 (0.452), and mitochondrial E3 ligase MARCHF6 (0.401), positioning it at the intersection of ER protein quality control, vesicular trafficking, and stress signaling. Literature (41 papers) establishes emerging roles in ER homeostasis, cancer therapy, and reproduction: 2025 landmark studies demonstrate UBE2J2 sensitizes the ERAD ubiquitination cascade to changes in membrane lipid saturation, establishing it as a lipid sensor coupling membrane composition to proteostasis, and reveal UBE2J2 is essential for meiosis prophase I progression during spermatogenesis in mice through ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover; 2024/2023 research identifies the UBE2J2/UBE2K-MARCHF5 ubiquitination machinery as a critical regulator of apoptosis in response to venetoclax in acute myeloid leukemia, suggesting therapeutic targeting potential; 2026 proteome analyses reveal ER stress-induced changes in protein abundance associated with Ube2j2 deficiency in human cell culture. ClinVar documents 133 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large chromosome 1p CNVs (1p36.33-36.23 losses, 1p36.33-36.32 gains, 1p36.33-35.1 gains) implicating UBE2J2 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined due to contiguous gene effects. The convergence of high-confidence UBC catalytic structure, VCP/TMEM129/DERL1 ERAD partnerships, lipid saturation sensing mechanisms, venetoclax response regulation in AML, and meiotic progression requirements suggests UBE2J2 functions as an ER membrane-proximal ubiquitination checkpoint coordinating protein quality control with lipid homeostasis, chemotherapeutic efficacy, and germ cell development, with therapeutic potential in enhancing venetoclax sensitivity in AML, modulating ER stress in proteostasis disorders, and understanding male infertility mechanisms.
