## Description
LOC128706666 encodes a 50 amino acid mitochondrial membrane microprotein (UniProt V9GZ13, ENSG00000285508) with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 77.6) and substantial clinical significance evidenced by 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants. The protein is described as "MKKS centrosomal shuttling protein" localized to mitochondrial membrane with function in repression of translation of downstream molecular chaperone MKKS (chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit homolog). Human Protein Atlas reports low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues but not in single cell types. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, likely due to small size and recent annotation. InterPro domain analysis failed. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 10 duplications (variant 2579201: 20p13-11.21, chr20:87153-23635465, ~23.5 Mb; variant 155359: 20p13-11.23, chr20:80928-18688031, ~18.6 Mb; variant 153731: 20p13-11.1, chr20:80927-26324843, ~26.3 Mb; variant 149704: 20p13-12.1, chr20:80106-13029401, ~13 Mb; variant 146596: 20p13-q13.33, chr20:99557-64277321, ~64.2 Mb whole-arm; variant 144514: 20p13-q11.1, chr20:80106-30227427, ~30.2 Mb; variant 59195: 20p12.2-q12, chr20:9811433-39316956, ~29.5 Mb) and 4 deletions/losses (variant 652394: 20p12.2 deletion; variant 146031: 20p12.2-12.1, chr20:9550975-12083434, ~2.5 Mb; variant 145966: 20p12.3-12.1, chr20:6617695-13392559, ~6.8 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 20p12.2 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including JAG1 (jagged canonical Notch ligand 1, Notch signaling pathway, Alagille syndrome, tetralogy of Fallot, arterial-vertebrobasilar stenosis), SNAP25 (synaptosome associated protein 25, SNARE complex, synaptic vesicle fusion, neurotransmitter release, epilepsy, intellectual disability), MKKS (chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit homolog, protein folding, ciliogenesis, McKusick-Kaufman syndrome, Bardet-Biedl syndrome, hydrometrocolpos, polydactyly, congenital heart disease), and PTDSS1 (phosphatidylserine synthase 1, phospholipid metabolism, Lenz-Majewski syndrome, hyperostotic dwarfism). Functional hypothesis: LOC128706666 encodes a mitochondrial membrane microprotein functioning as a translational repressor of MKKS chaperonin mRNA through upstream open reading frame (uORF) or antisense mechanism, coordinating chaperone capacity with mitochondrial protein import demands. The 50 aa length and high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 77.6) suggest a stable folded domain potentially interacting with mitochondrial ribosomes or MKKS mRNA 5 UTR. Disease mechanism involves LOC128706666 haploinsufficiency causing MKKS overexpression and chaperone imbalance (protein aggregation, ciliary defects, Bardet-Biedl features), LOC128706666 duplication causing MKKS repression and chaperone deficiency (protein misfolding, mitochondrial dysfunction), JAG1 dosage alteration causing Notch signaling defects (Alagille syndrome, cardiac outflow tract anomalies), SNAP25 copy number change causing synaptic transmission defects (epilepsy, neurodevelopmental delay), and MKKS mutation causing ciliopathy (polydactyly, congenital heart disease, obesity). The 20p12.2 deletion syndrome presents with developmental delay, dysmorphic features, and cardiac defects. The 20p12.2 duplication syndrome may present with metabolic abnormalities, chaperone dysregulation, and neurodevelopmental phenotypes. The presence of protein-coding features (50 aa, mitochondrial localization, AlphaFold structure) combined with ClinVar evidence (14 CNVs ranging 2.5-64.2 Mb) and MKKS-JAG1-SNAP25-PTDSS1 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a mitochondrial-nuclear coordination microprotein critical for chaperone-Notch-synaptic-phospholipid homeostasis.
