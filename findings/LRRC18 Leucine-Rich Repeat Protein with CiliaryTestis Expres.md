## Description
LRRC18 (Leucine-Rich Repeat-Containing Protein 18, UniProt Q8N456) is a 261-amino acid intracellular protein characterized by multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) motifs functioning in protein-protein interactions within specialized cellular contexts including ciliated epithelia and germ cells. The protein contains canonical LRR motifs (IPR001611/PF23598/SM00369, residues 49-71, 72-95, 120-142) arranged in a solenoid architecture typical of the ribonuclease inhibitor-like superfamily (G3DSA:3.80.10.10/SSF52058, residues 5-232/30-198), forming a curved horseshoe-shaped scaffold that mediates specific protein binding interactions (GO:0005515). LRRC18 belongs to a conserved vertebrate family (IPR050216/PTHR48051, residues 30-198) with a disease resistance R13L4/SHOC-2-like LRR domain (IPR055414, residues 32-133) suggesting potential roles in cellular stress responses or innate immunity pathways. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT=82.1, high confidence) consistent with stable LRR solenoid folds required for sustained protein complex assembly. HPA reveals highly restricted group-enriched expression pattern with highest levels in choroid plexus (23.2 nTPM), testis (12.8 nTPM), and fallopian tube (6.2 nTPM), cytosolic subcellular localization, and group-enriched cell type specificity — suggesting specialized roles in ciliated epithelia (choroid plexus produces cerebrospinal fluid via ependymal cilia, fallopian tube uses cilia for oocyte transport) and spermatogenesis. STRING interaction network reveals associations with autophagy and cytoskeletal regulators: WDFY4 (0.727, WD repeat and FYVE domain-containing protein 4, essential for selective autophagy and aggrephagy), ATP6AP1L (0.643, ATPase H+ transporting accessory protein 1-like, lysosomal/vacuolar acidification), LRGUK (0.636, leucine-rich repeat and guanylate kinase domain-containing protein, microtubule organization and ciliogenesis), DRG2 (0.568, developmentally regulated GTP-binding protein 2, vesicle trafficking), and RNF141 (0.548, ring finger protein 141, E3 ubiquitin ligase in spermatogenesis). The WDFY4 interaction is particularly informative as WDFY4 mediates selective autophagy of protein aggregates, while LRGUK links microtubule dynamics to ciliary assembly. ClinVar documents 63 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal deletions and duplications spanning 10q11.22-11.23 encompassing the LRRC18 locus, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity causes disease phenotypes potentially involving ciliary dysfunction, impaired autophagy, or reproductive defects. The combination of LRR protein-protein interaction capacity, ciliary tissue expression (choroid plexus, fallopian tube, testis), autophagy/microtubule interaction partners (WDFY4, LRGUK), cytosolic localization, and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: LRRC18 functions as a scaffolding protein within autophagy-microtubule networks, potentially bridging WDFY4-mediated selective autophagy with LRGUK-regulated microtubule dynamics during ciliogenesis and spermatogenesis, with dosage disruption causing disease through impaired protein quality control, ciliary assembly defects, or germ cell developmental abnormalities.
