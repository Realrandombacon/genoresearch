## Description
ZNF841 (Zinc Finger Protein 841, UniProt Q6ZN19) is an 808-amino acid Krüppel-associated C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor functioning in transcriptional regulation through recruitment of chromatin-modifying complexes. The protein contains multiple tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, residues 145-172, 173-200, 201-228) arranged in clusters for sequence-specific genomic recognition, classified within the C2H2-type zinc finger superfamily (IPR036236, residues 126-294). Notably, ZNF841 harbors a distinctive PRDM4/PRDM1/PRDM14-like domain (IPR050331/PTHR16515, residues 628-789) characteristic of PR/SET domain histone methyltransferases that catalyze repressive H3K9 methylation — suggesting dual functionality as both DNA-binding factor and potential chromatin modifier. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured architecture (pLDDT=69.6, medium confidence) consistent with ordered zinc finger folds embedded within flexible linker regions required for chromatin scanning and multi-protein complex assembly. HPA classifies ZNF841 as a transcription factor with DNA-binding activity, ubiquitous expression (low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues), and cell type-enhanced expression pattern — indicating fundamental gene regulatory roles across diverse cellular contexts. STRING interaction network reveals associations with chromatin regulation machinery: SPDYE2 (0.542, speed homolog involved in transcription elongation), DMAP1 (0.470, DNA methyltransferase-associated protein 1 recruiting HDACs for transcriptional repression), SUPT5H (0.460, transcription elongation factor SPT5), SETDB2 (0.408, SET domain bifurcated histone lysine methyltransferase adding repressive H3K9me3 marks), and TADA3 (0.401, transcriptional adapter 3 component of GCN5/PCAF histone acetyltransferase complexes). This interaction profile suggests ZNF841 functions at the intersection of transcription elongation control and epigenetic silencing, potentially recruiting DMAP1-HDAC complexes for deacetylation and SETDB2 for H3K9 methylation at target loci. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains across 19q13.33-13.43 encompassing the ZNF841 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene amplification may contribute to disease phenotypes. The combination of C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding capacity, PRDM-like methyltransferase domain, chromatin modifier interactions (DMAP1, SETDB2, TADA3), ubiquitous expression, and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: ZNF841 functions as a sequence-specific transcription factor that recruits multi-enzyme chromatin remodeling complexes to establish repressive histone marks (H3K9me3 via SETDB2, deacetylation via DMAP1-HDAC) at target genes, balancing transcriptional activation versus silencing programs during development and differentiation, with dosage disruption causing disease through epigenetic dysregulation of critical gene networks.
