## Description
ANKRD1 (Ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein 1, also known as CARP/Cardiac Ankyrin Repeat Protein, UniProt Q15327) encodes a 319 amino acid stress-responsive sarcomeric protein belonging to the muscle ankyrin repeat protein (MARP) family alongside Ankrd2 and DARP. The protein contains canonical ankyrin repeat architecture mediating protein-protein interactions: multiple ankyrin repeats (IPR002110/Ankyrin repeat at residues 126-217 and 218-283, IPR036770/Ankyrin repeat-containing domain superfamily at residues 120-315, PF12796/Ankyrin repeats 3 copies at residues 126-216 and 220-282, SM00248/ankyrin repeats at residues 152-181, 185-214, 218-247) plus PTHR24126/Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box family membership (residues 43-309). The AlphaFold predicted structure displays high-confidence folding (global pLDDT 82.8) across the full-length 319 aa protein, indicating stable ankyrin repeat stack formation characteristic of protein interaction scaffolds. Human Protein Atlas classifies ANKRD1 as a predicted intracellular protein with striking heart muscle-specific expression (6125.5 nTPM, tissue enriched), cancer-enhanced expression, not detected in immune cells, with subcellular localization in nucleoplasm and nucleoli fibrillar center indicating dual sarcomeric and nuclear functions. STRING interaction network identifies 10 high-to-medium confidence functional partners revealing connections to sarcomere structure and mechanotransduction: TTN (score 0.988, highest confidence partner, titin, giant sarcomeric elastic filament protein), MYPN (0.802, myopalladin, Z-disk protein), CCN1 (0.772, cellular communication network factor 1/CYR61, immediate early gene), CCN2 (0.770, connective tissue growth factor), YBX1 (0.753, Y-box binding protein 1, transcriptional regulator), YAP1 (0.722, Yes-associated protein 1, Hippo pathway effector), CSRP3 (0.700, cysteine and glycine-rich protein 3/MLP, Z-disk protein), FHL2 (0.696, four and a half LIM domains protein 2, transcriptional coactivator), NEBL (0.695, nebulette, Z-disk protein), and MYOZ2 (0.541, myozenin 2, Z-disk calcium-binding protein). The TTN interaction (0.988) is mechanistically paramount as it positions ANKRD1 within the titin spring element of the sarcomere where it functions as a mechanosensor detecting stretch-induced conformational changes. The MYPN-CSRP3-NEBL-MYOZ2 cluster (scores 0.802, 0.700, 0.695, 0.541) confirms Z-disk localization and integration with costamere complexes transmitting contractile force. The CCN1-CCN2-YAP1-YBX1 network (scores 0.772, 0.770, 0.722, 0.753) links ANKRD1 to transcriptional regulation pathways where cleaved ANKRD1 fragments translocate to the nucleus to modulate stress-response gene expression. ClinVar contains 87 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number variations (10q23.1-25.1 duplication chr10:87456174-107789979, 10q23.2-24.31 deletion chr10:88755921-102461203, 10q23.2-23.32 deletion chr10:88121043-93641582, plus multiple other CNVs spanning 10q regions) and coding mutations including truncating variant c.337G>T (p.Glu113Ter) and missense variants c.739G>A (p.Ala247Thr), c.485T>G (p.Leu162Trp), demonstrating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplication disrupts sarcomeric integrity. Given the ankyrin repeat scaffold architecture defining it as a protein interaction hub, extreme heart muscle specificity (6125.5 nTPM, highest among all tissues) consistent with cardiac-restricted function, high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 82.8) indicating stable folded ankyrin solenoid, TTN partnership (0.988) placing ANKRD1 within titin-based mechanotransduction complexes at the I-band region, Z-disk protein interactions (MYPN/CSRP3/NEBL/MYOZ2) confirming costamere integration, nucleoplasmic/nucleolar localization matching dual structural-transcriptional roles, CCN/YAP signaling connections linking mechanical stress to gene expression, and clinical variant burden (87 pathogenic variants) confirming cardiomyopathy association, ANKRD1 functions as a bidirectional mechanotransducer that: (1) anchors to titin springs in relaxed sarcomeres sensing diastolic stretch, (2) recruits Z-disk proteins (MYPN, CSRP3, MYOZ2) stabilizing costamere-cytoskeleton linkages during contraction, and (3) undergoes proteolytic cleavage under pathological stress releasing nuclear-localized fragments that interact with YBX1/YAP1 to activate hypertrophic gene programs via CCN1/CCN2 signaling, where loss-of-function through 10q deletions or truncating mutations causes dilated cardiomyopathy through impaired stress sensing and aberrant transcriptional responses to hemodynamic load.
