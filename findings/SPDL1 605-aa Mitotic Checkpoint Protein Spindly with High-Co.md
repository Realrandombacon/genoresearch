## Description
SPDL1 (Spindle Apparatus Coiled-Coil Protein 1 / Spindly; UniProt Q96EA4) is a 605 amino acid intracellular protein with 73 publications, representing a moderately-characterized mitotic regulator functioning as a critical dynein motor adapter that recruits cytoplasmic dynein to kinetochores during prometaphase/metaphase to strip checkpoint proteins (Mad1/Mad2, RZZ complex) from attached kinetochores, enabling spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) silencing and anaphase onset.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals SPDL1 belongs to the Protein Spindly chordate family (IPR028593, residues 1-605) with GO annotations for mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint signaling (GO:0007094) and protein localization to kinetochore (GO:0034501). The core functional region is the Spindly/BICDR Dynein Motor Adapter domain (IPR051149/PTHR32123, residues 3-468) which mediates dynein binding. A Pfam Spindly domain (PF28219, residues 5-458) encompasses the structured core. HAMAP family MF_03041 spans the full length (1-605). AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 74.3), indicating a well-folded protein with stable secondary structure—characteristic of scaffold proteins requiring defined interfaces for dynein recruitment and kinetochore binding.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies exclusively high-confidence interactions with core mitotic checkpoint and kinetochore machinery: BUB1B (0.958—highest confidence; Budding Uninhibited By Benzimidazoles 1 homolog beta, SAC kinase), CENPF (0.950; Centromere Protein F, kinetochore scaffold), SKA3 (0.947; Spindle And Kinetochore Associated Complex Subunit 3), SKA1 (0.946; Ska1 subunit), SKA2 (0.927; Ska2 subunit), BOD1 (0.917; Bipolar Order Defect 1, kinetochore phosphatase regulator), PLK1 (0.889; Polo-Like Kinase 1, master mitotic regulator), CDC20 (0.888; Cell Division Cycle 20, APC/C activator), HOXD13 (0.868; Homeobox D13, developmental transcription factor), and NDC80 (0.777; NDC80 kinetochore complex component).
