# KRT28: Skin-Enriched Type I Acidic Keratin Forming Heteropolymeric Intermediate Filaments with Partner Keratins for Epithelial Mechanical Integrity

## Description
KRT28 (Keratin 28, also K25IRS4, KRT25D) is a 464 amino acid cytosolic intermediate filament protein (UniProt Q7Z3Y7) with a well-folded coiled-coil architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 74.6, high confidence), characteristic of type I (acidic) keratins that obligately heterodimerize with type II (basic) keratins to form the 10-nm intermediate filament network providing mechanical resilience to epithelial cells.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals KRT28 contains the canonical intermediate filament rod domain (IPR039008/PF00038/PS51842/SM01391/SSF64593, residues 85-401/85-398/86-401/85-400/84-118+318-396) composed of four alpha-helical segments (1A, 1B, 2A, 2B) separated by linkers, flanked by non-helical head and tail domains. The protein belongs to the type I keratin family (IPR002957/PTHR23239/PR01248, residues 25-434/25-434/168-263) with structural molecule activity (GO:0005198). The rod domain mediates coiled-coil dimerization via heptad repeat patterns, while head/tail domains regulate filament assembly and interactions with desmosomes/hemidesmosomes.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals KRT28 functions exclusively within the keratin intermediate filament network: strongest interactions with KRT25 (0.582, keratin 25, type I partner), KRT35 (0.495, keratin 35, type II), KRT26 (0.484, keratin 26, type I), KRT39 (0.483, keratin 39, type II), KRT40 (0.482, keratin 40, type II), KRT27 (0.479, keratin 27, type I), KRT38 (0.440, keratin 38, type II), KRT37 (0.434, keratin 37, type II), KRT33A (0.418, keratin 33A, type I), and KRT31 (0.415, keratin 31, type I). These interactions confirm KRT28 as part of the 'inner root sheath' or 'hair shaft' keratin cohort, likely pairing with specific type II partners (KRT35/39/40/37/38) in hair follicle epithelia.\n\nHPA expression shows skin-enriched specificity (10.6 nTPM in skin 1) with detection in a single tissue, consistent with a specialized epithelial keratin restricted to hair follicles or epidermal appendages. The protein is classified as predicted intracellular with group enriched single cell specificity and cancer-enhanced expression.\n\nClinVar documents 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large chromosome 17q copy number variations (gains spanning 17q12-25.3 and losses at 17q12-22). These CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity potentially causing skin fragility disorders, hair shaft defects, or ectodermal dysplasias through impaired intermediate filament assembly.\n\nConservation is evident from mouse (A6BLY7, 462 aa) ortholog, indicating ancient evolutionary conservation of epithelial cytoskeletal architecture.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: KRT28 acts as a type I acidic keratin that obligately heterodimerizes with a specific type II basic keratin partner (likely KRT35, KRT39, or KRT40 based on STRING scores) to form intermediate filaments in hair follicle inner root sheath or cuticle cells. The coiled-coil rod domain (residues 85-401) forms parallel in-register dimers that further assemble into tetramers, unit-length filaments, and mature 10-nm filaments. This network provides tensile strength to withstand mechanical stress during hair growth and shaft emergence. The head domain regulates filament dynamics through phosphorylation-dependent disassembly/reassembly cycles. Tail domain interactions anchor filaments to desmosomal plaques (via plakoglobin/plakophilin) and hemidesmosomes (via plectin/integrin α6β4), coupling the cytoskeleton to cell-cell and cell-matrix junctions. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests reactivation in squamous cell carcinomas or trichilemmomas. CNV-mediated haploinsufficiency disrupts filament stoichiometry, causing dominant-negative effects or aggregate formation similar to epidermolysis bullosa simplex or monilethrix phenotypes. The 17q21 keratin cluster organization facilitates coordinated expression of type I/type II pairs.
