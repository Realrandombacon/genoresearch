# LRRC28: Leucine-Rich Repeat Scaffold Protein Modulating Adhesion GPCR Signaling in Hepatic and Neural Tissues

## Description
LRRC28 encodes a 367 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) scaffold protein with a highly confident AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 90.2). InterPro analysis reveals multiple LRR domains (IPR001611, IPR003591, PF13855, PF23598) spanning residues 40-189, forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. The G3DSA:3.80.10.10 Ribonuclease Inhibitor homologous superfamily (residues 3-243) suggests structural similarity to known LRR-containing regulatory proteins.\n\nHPA expression data shows LRRC28 is tissue-enhanced with highest expression in liver (44.0 nTPM), detected in all tissues, and localized to nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and mitochondria. This multi-compartment distribution suggests roles in both nuclear signaling and secretory pathway regulation.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal connections to adhesion G-protein coupled receptors (ADGRL1/2/3, scores 0.514), extracellular matrix proteins (PRELP 0.509, LUM 0.496, KERA 0.479), and the retinal protein NYX (0.603). This interaction network suggests LRRC28 functions as a bridging scaffold between cell surface adhesion receptors and intracellular signaling machinery.\n\nClinVar contains 85 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 15q26.3, indicating dosage sensitivity. The combination of LRR domains, adhesion GPCR interactions, and haploinsufficiency phenotype suggests LRRC28 modulates ADGRL-mediated signal transduction pathways critical for tissue development and homeostasis.\n\nConservation is evident from orthologs in mouse (Q3TX51, 367 aa) and bovine (Q32KX5, 367 aa) with identical protein length, indicating strong evolutionary constraint on the LRR scaffold architecture.
