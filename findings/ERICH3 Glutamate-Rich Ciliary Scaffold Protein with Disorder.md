# ERICH3: Glutamate-Rich Ciliary Scaffold Protein with Disordered Structure and Disease-Associated Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-19T10:24:39.436005

**Quality Score:** 4.17/10  (E=6.05, D=2.3) [MODERATE]

## Description
ERICH3 (glutamate rich 3, alias C1orf173) encodes a large 1530-amino acid intrinsically disordered protein (AlphaFold pLDDT=43.6) localized to the primary cilium, nuclear membrane, nucleoli, and endoplasmic reticulum. The protein contains a DUF4590 domain (IPR048257/PF15257, residues 299-411) embedded within a glutamate-rich low-complexity region. HPA RNA-seq shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in testis (22.6 nTPM), fallopian tube (16.3 nTPM), and brain (10.4 nTPM), consistent with ciliary function in these tissues. STRING interactions reveal strong associations with ciliary/axonemal proteins: TSPAN5 (0.742), CFAP44 (0.625, cilia formation factor), SAXO2 (0.514, microtubule stabilizer), TEKT1 (0.483, tektin cytoskeletal protein), and ODF3L2 (0.447, outer dense fiber protein). This interaction network strongly supports a role in cilium assembly and maintenance. ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses on chromosome 1p, suggesting haploinsufficiency causes disease phenotypes. The low structural confidence (pLDDT 43.6) indicates extensive intrinsic disorder, characteristic of scaffold proteins that function through multivalent protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic catalysis. Conservation data shows orthologs in mouse (Q92876, 1637 aa, ~85% identity) and macaque, indicating evolutionary constraint despite sequence divergence.
