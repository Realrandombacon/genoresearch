# LRRC24: Leucine-Rich Repeat Scaffold Protein with Immunoglobulin Domains Localized to Pituitary Gland

## Description
LRRC24 encodes a 513 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) scaffold protein with multiple protein-protein interaction domains. InterPro analysis reveals 9 distinct domain signatures including LRR N-terminal domain (IPR000372), canonical LRR repeats (IPR001611, positions 76-204), typical LRR subtypes (IPR003591), cysteine-rich flanking region (IPR000483, positions 204-258), and immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR007110, IPR003598, positions 260-371). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 77.0) across the full-length sequence, indicating a well-folded stable protein. HPA expression data shows tissue-enhanced RNA expression with peak expression in pituitary gland (12.5 nTPM), cancer-enhanced specificity, and vesicular subcellular localization. STRING interactions reveal 10 medium-confidence partners (scores 0.514-0.576) including adhesion GPCRs (ADGRL1/2/3), transcription factor GLI4, and paralog LRRC14. ClinVar contains 56 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and deletions spanning 8q24.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Mouse ortholog Q8BHA1 (521 aa) shows strong conservation. Functional hypothesis: LRRC24 acts as an extracellular/membrane scaffold protein that bridges adhesion GPCR signaling with transcriptional regulation via GLI4, potentially modulating pituitary hormone secretion and cell proliferation. The LRR-Ig domain architecture resembles known cell surface recognition proteins, suggesting a role in cell-cell communication or matrix sensing.
