## Description

The protein belongs to the DNA-binding transcription factor superfamily with GO annotations for DNA binding (GO:0003677) and DNA-templated transcription (GO:0006351). This positions POLR1E as a critical Pol I assembly factor bridging ribosome biogenesis to cancer.
