## Description
PRELID3A encodes a 172 aa mitochondrial intermembrane space protein (UniProt Q96N28) located at 18p11.32, functioning as a critical regulator of lipid transport, mitochondrial membrane integrity, and immune cell metabolism. InterPro analysis confirms its canonical PRELI/MSF1 domain architecture (IPR006797, aa 1-172) belonging to the Slowmo/Ups family (IPR037365, aa 1-169, PF04707), with GO annotations for mitochondrial intermembrane space localization (GO:0005758) and lipid transport functions. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 78.8), indicating a stable, well-folded protein typical of lipid-binding/transfer proteins. STRING interactions reveal a specialized mitochondrial network: TRIAP1 (0.961, TP53-regulated inhibitor of apoptosis 1, primary partner forming heterodimeric complex), PRELID1 (0.783, paralog with overlapping functions), TP53 (0.499, tumor suppressor linking to apoptosis regulation), AKAIN1 (0.480, kinase), and L3MBTL4 (0.405, chromatin regulator). HPA expression shows low tissue specificity but detection in many tissues with immune cell enhancement, consistent with roles in mitochondrial metabolism across diverse cell types. ClinVar reports 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large 18p copy number gains (x3, x4) and losses (x1) spanning 18p11.32-11.21, suggesting extreme dosage sensitivity; specific intronic variants (e.g., c.466-166T>A) may affect splicing. Literature (only 3 papers) confirms this is a VERY DARK gene with minimal characterization. Functional hypothesis: PRELID3A likely functions through: (1) Lipid Transfer Complex - forming a heterodimer with TRIAP1 to transport phospholipids (particularly cardiolipin or phosphatidic acid) between mitochondrial membranes, analogous to yeast Ups1/2 system; (2) Membrane Stability - maintaining inner mitochondrial membrane architecture required for cristae formation and respiratory chain assembly; (3) Immune Metabolism - supporting mitochondrial function in activated immune cells with high energetic demands; (4) Apoptosis Regulation - potential interaction with TP53 pathway via TRIAP1 to modulate cell survival decisions; (5) Dosage Sensitivity - 18p amplification causing PRELID3A overexpression potentially disrupting lipid homeostasis and membrane dynamics, while deletion impairs mitochondrial function contributing to 18p deletion syndrome phenotypes. Disease implications include 18p deletion/duplication syndromes (developmental delay, intellectual disability), potential roles in immune deficiencies, and metabolic disorders. Despite clinical variant burden, the precise lipid substrates, structural mechanism of transfer, and therapeutic potential of PRELID3A-TRIAP1 complex modulation remain completely unexplored — a genuine dark gene opportunity.
