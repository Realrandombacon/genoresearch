## Description
TPTE2P1 (TPTE2 pseudogene 1, Gene ID: 646405) is a 138-amino acid putative protein encoded on chromosome 13q12.13 that represents a dark gene (7 papers) with well-characterized lncRNA function but unexplored protein-coding potential. Literature analysis reveals 7 publications establishing TPTE2P1 as an oncogenic lncRNA functioning as a competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) across multiple cancer types: colorectal cancer (2018, 2024; cited 9x and 1x), thyroid carcinoma (2020; cited 3x, inhibits miR-520c-3p), hepatocellular carcinoma (2019; cited 4x, promotes migration/invasion), and gallbladder cancer (2015; cited 14x). Despite pseudogene annotation, UniProt entry Q5T6R2 exists for a 138-aa putative phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate 3-phosphatase. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical C2 domain architecture homologous to PTEN tumor suppressor: IPR014020 tensin phosphatase C2 domain (positions 1-75), PF10409 C2 domain of PTEN (positions 41-72), PS51182 C2 tensin-type domain profile (positions 1-75), and G3DSA:2.60.40.1110 homologous superfamily (positions 37-76). C2 domains are calcium-dependent lipid-binding modules that target proteins to cell membranes, suggesting TPTE2P1 may retain lipid-binding capability despite pseudogene status. AlphaFold structure prediction shows low-medium confidence (global pLDDT 51.2), indicating a partially structured protein with significant disordered regions, consistent with regulatory/scaffolding function. STRING database returned no protein interactions, reflecting limited proteomic characterization. HPA has no expression data, and ClinVar contains no variants, suggesting TPTE2P1 has no established monogenic disease associations. The well-documented ceRNA mechanism (sponging miR-520c-3p in thyroid cancer) combined with PTEN-like C2 domain architecture suggests dual functionality: (1) as a lncRNA, TPTE2P1 acts as a miRNA sponge regulating oncogenic pathways; (2) if translated, the C2 domain could enable membrane localization and potential lipid phosphatase activity analogous to PTEN, negatively regulating PI3K/AKT signaling. The 13q12.13 location and multi-cancer oncogenic activity position TPTE2P1 as a pseudogene-derived oncogene worthy of investigation for both RNA and protein-level functions. This represents a dark gene with established lncRNA oncogenic function but unexplored protein-coding potential.
