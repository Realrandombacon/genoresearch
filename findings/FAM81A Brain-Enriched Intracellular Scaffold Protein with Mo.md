# FAM81A: Brain-Enriched Intracellular Scaffold Protein with Molecular Condensate Activity and Synaptic Trafficking Partners

## Description
FAM81A encodes a 368 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q8TBF8) localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol. InterPro analysis identifies the FAM81 family domain (IPR029619, PTHR22420) spanning nearly the entire protein (residues 4-368) plus a PFAM FAM81B domain (PF28217, residues 33-359), indicating a conserved scaffold architecture without catalytic motifs. HPA shows brain-enriched expression (26.3 nTPM in brain, tissue-enriched specificity) with immune cell enhancement, consistent with a neuronal/immunological role. STRING interactions reveal partnerships with olfactory receptors OR5C1 (0.596) and OR52B2 (0.591)—unexpected for a cytosolic protein, suggesting potential GPCR trafficking regulation—plus transmembrane proteins TMEM179 (0.475), TMEM88B (0.460), LMBR1L (0.419), and phosphatase PTPN20 (0.530). This interaction profile suggests FAM81A functions in membrane protein trafficking or synaptic vesicle dynamics. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Gly220Val), splice site, and 15q21.3-22.31 copy number alterations, indicating dosage sensitivity. Cross-species conservation is strong: mouse Q3UXZ6 (364 aa, 93% identity) and rat D4A7T8 (364 aa, 92% identity). The predicted molecular condensate scaffold activity combined with brain enrichment suggests FAM81A may nucleate postsynaptic density complexes or regulate activity-dependent phase separation at excitatory synapses. Mechanistically, FAM81A likely serves as a multivalent interaction hub recruiting transmembrane cargo (ORs, TMEMs) to trafficking machinery via PTPN20-mediated dephosphorylation events.
