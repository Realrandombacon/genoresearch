## Description
NECAP2 (NECAP Endocytosis Associated 2, alias Adaptin Ear-Binding Coat-Associated Protein 2) is a 263 amino acid predicted membrane protein (UniProt Q9NVZ3) with medium-confidence partially structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 69.3) containing a canonical PHear domain (residues 6-162) with PH-like fold and DUF1681 family signature, functioning as an adaptin ear-binding coat-associated protein that regulates clathrin-mediated endocytosis through PHear domain-mediated AP2 complex assembly and interactions with AP2B1, AP2M1, AP2S1, NECAP1, EPS15, AP1G1, ITSN1, AP2A1, AP1B1, and AP2A2.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals NECAP2 contains a classic PHear domain architecture: NECAP PHear domain (IPR012466/PF07933/DUF1681, residues 6-162/6-162/6-162) defining the conserved adaptin ear-binding protein family with endocytosis function (GO:0006897) and membrane localization (GO:0016020), NECAP PH fold with ear-like function domain (cd13228, residues 8-127) forming the characteristic PH-like fold adapted for ear domain binding, PH-like domain superfamily (IPR011993/G3DSA:2.30.29.30/SSF50729, residues 10-132/10-132/3-131) confirming structural homology to pleckstrin homology domains, and Adaptin Ear-Binding Coat-Associated family (PTHR12847, residues 2-245) marking NECAP2-specific conservation. The PHear domain spans 157 amino acids (residues 6-162) within the compact 263 aa protein, with the majority of the protein (~60%) being structured PHear domain and the remainder (~40%) being N-terminal and C-terminal extensions. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 69.3) confirms a partially structured protein with ordered PHear domain core and flexible terminal regions, consistent with adaptor protein function requiring conformational flexibility for coat complex assembly.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places NECAP2 within the clathrin-mediated endocytosis network with exceptionally high-confidence interactions: strongest interaction with AP2B1 (0.975, adaptor related protein complex 2 subunit beta 1, AP2 complex), followed by AP2M1 (0.970, adaptor related protein complex 2 subunit mu 1, AP2 complex), AP2S1 (0.955, adaptor related protein complex 2 subunit sigma 1, AP2 complex), NECAP1 (0.925, NECAP endocytosis associated 1, paralog), EPS15 (0.847, epidermal growth factor receptor pathway substrate 15, endocytic adaptor), AP1G1 (0.841, adaptor related protein complex 1 subunit gamma 1, AP1 complex), ITSN1 (0.752, intersectin 1, endocytic scaffold), AP2A1 (0.739, adaptor related protein complex 2 subunit alpha 1, AP2 complex), AP1B1 (0.560, adaptor related protein complex 1 subunit beta 1, AP1 complex), and AP2A2 (0.545, adaptor related protein complex 2 subunit alpha 2, AP2 complex). The AP2B1-AP2M1-AP2S1 partnerships (0.975/0.970/0.955) extremely strongly link NECAP2 to the AP2 clathrin adaptor complex core. NECAP1 interaction (0.925) confirms functional redundancy within the NECAP family. EPS15-ITSN1 interactions (0.847/0.752) connect NECAP2 to the endocytic machinery and clathrin coat assembly. AP1G1-AP1B1 partnerships (0.841/0.560) link NECAP2 to AP1 complex for Golgi-endosome trafficking.\n\nHPA expression data is not available for NECAP2 in the Human Protein Atlas database, suggesting either low expression levels or technical limitations in antibody detection. However, the strong conservation and extensive interaction network suggest ubiquitous or tissue-specific expression in endocytosis-active tissues.\n\nClinVar documents 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including chromosome 1 copy number losses (1p36.32-36.12 chr1:4436802-22782007, 1p36.21-36.12 chr1:16194137-20561434, 1p36.31-36.13 chr1:6758933-19287770, 1p36.13 chr1:16773001-20221073, 1p36.33-36.13 chr1:849466-17525065, 1p36.13-36.12 chr1:16785250-23491592), gains (1p36.33-35.1 chr1:99125-34026935), deletions (chr1:4481271_20530242del), and splice site variants (c.299-11T>G) plus missense variants (c.109C>G p.Leu37Val). The extensive CNV burden at 1p36 indicates extreme dosage sensitivity, where haploinsufficiency or overexpression causes defective AP2 complex assembly, impaired clathrin-mediated endocytosis, disrupted receptor internalization, and potential 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, and neurological disorders.\n\nConservation is strong across vertebrates: human (Q9NVZ3, 263 aa), rat (Q6P756, 263 aa), indicating evolutionary conservation of PHear domain functions in endocytosis.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: NECAP2 functions as an adaptin ear-binding protein that regulates clathrin-mediated endocytosis through PHear domain-mediated AP2 complex assembly and membrane recruitment. The PHear domain (residues 6-162) with PH-like fold binds to the ear domain of AP2 complex subunits (particularly AP2B1 beta-appendage), positioning NECAP2 at clathrin-coated pit assembly sites. AP2B1-AP2M1-AP2S1 interactions (0.975/0.970/0.955) confirm NECAP2 binds directly to the AP2 heterotetrameric core complex, potentially stabilizing AP2 conformation or recruiting accessory factors. NECAP1 partnership (0.925) suggests functional redundancy and potential heterodimerization within the NECAP family for coordinated endocytic regulation. EPS15-ITSN1 interactions (0.847/0.752) connect NECAP2 to the broader endocytic machinery, with EPS15 serving as an early clathrin coat assembly factor and ITSN1 as a scaffold for dynamin recruitment. AP1G1-AP1B1 partnerships (0.841/0.560) link NECAP2 to AP1 complex function at the Golgi and endosomes, suggesting roles in both plasma membrane endocytosis and intracellular trafficking. The partially structured architecture (pLDDT 69.3) provides flexible PHear domain scaffold for dynamic AP2 interactions. 1p36 CNVs cause NECAP2 dosage alterations, leading to defective AP2 complex assembly, impaired clathrin-mediated endocytosis, disrupted receptor internalization (EGFR, transferrin, LDL), aberrant synaptic vesicle recycling, and potential 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay and neurological disorders. NECAP2 represents a critical PHear domain adaptor protein linking AP2 complex binding to clathrin-mediated endocytosis regulation.
