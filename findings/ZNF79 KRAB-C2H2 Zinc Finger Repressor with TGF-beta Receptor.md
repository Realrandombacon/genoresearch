# ZNF79: KRAB-C2H2 Zinc Finger Repressor with TGF-beta Receptor Interactions, Ubiquitous Expression, and Chromosomal Rearrangement Disease Association

## Description
ZNF79 encodes a 498 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q15937). InterPro analysis reveals canonical domain architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 38-109) that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex for heterochromatin formation via HP1 binding and histone methylation, followed by three tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 193-276) conferring sequence-specific genomic targeting. HPA data indicates ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity and dual nucleoplasmic/plasma membrane localization—suggesting potential non-canonical functions beyond nuclear transcriptional repression. STRING interactions identify 10 protein partners including TRIM28 (0.525, canonical KRAB cofactor), ZNF17 (0.635, related KRAB-ZFP), and notably TGF-beta superfamily receptors ACVRL1 (0.602), ENG (0.587), and BMPR2 (0.561)—this interaction pattern suggests ZNF79 may couple transcriptional repression to TGF-beta/BMP signaling pathways, potentially mediating growth factor-responsive gene silencing during development or tissue homeostasis. ClinVar documents 36 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses/gains spanning 9q33.3-34.11 and 9p24.3-q34.3, indicating strong dosage sensitivity and disease association through chromosomal rearrangement mechanisms. The combination of KRAB-mediated repression, TGF-beta receptor interactions, and ubiquitous expression suggests ZNF79 functions as a developmental transcriptional silencer that integrates growth factor signaling with epigenetic gene regulation, with pathogenic CNVs disrupting this regulatory circuit leading to developmental disorders.
