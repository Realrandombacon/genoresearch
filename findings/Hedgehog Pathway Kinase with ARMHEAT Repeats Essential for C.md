## Description
STK36 (Serine/Threonine Kinase 36, also known as Fused homolog) is a 1315 amino acid kinase critical for primary cilium assembly and Hedgehog signal transduction. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite architecture: an N-terminal catalytic serine/threonine kinase domain (IPR000719, PF00069, residues 4-254) with a conserved active site motif (IPR008271, positions 121-133) and ATP-binding pocket (IPR017441, positions 10-33), followed by extensive ARM/HEAT repeat regions (IPR011989, IPR016024, PF13646) spanning residues 65-724 and 1078-1287 that mediate protein-protein interactions. The protein belongs to the protein kinase-like superfamily (IPR011009) and contains leucine-rich repeat variant folds (G3DSA:1.25.10.10). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9NRP7-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 80.9) across all 1315 residues, indicating a stable multi-domain scaffold. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression with cytosolic localization and specific involvement in cilium biogenesis/degradation pathways; disease associations include ciliopathy and primary ciliary dyskinesia. STRING interactions place STK36 at the core of Hedgehog signaling: SUFU (0.998), KIF27 (0.970), GLI3 (0.927), GLI2 (0.865), KIF7 (0.837), GLI1 (0.806), SMO (0.694), PTCH2 (0.698), and SHH (0.663), demonstrating functional coupling to the suppressor of fused complex and GLI transcription factor regulation. ClinVar lists 38 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.2761+1G>T), frameshifts (c.2758del p.Gln920fs), and large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 2q, though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. Mechanistically, STK36 likely phosphorylates GLI transcription factors to modulate their nuclear translocation and transcriptional activity, while its ARM/HEAT repeats scaffold the SUFU-KIF7 complex at the ciliary tip, making it essential for vertebrate Hedgehog signaling and ciliogenesis.
