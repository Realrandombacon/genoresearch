## Description
LOC100507472 (Gene ID: 100507472) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 15q26.3 (NC_000015.10, positions 101307250-101309303, ~2 kb region) with extraordinary clinical significance evidenced by 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: approximately 15 duplications/gains (variant 4796084: 15q26.1-26.3 ~11.8 Mb; variant 155459: 15q26.3 ~3.6 Mb; variant 154952: 15q23-26.3 ~29.8 Mb; variant 154848: 15q22.2-26.3 ~42.1 Mb; variant 154512: 15q26.3 ~2.5 Mb; variant 153554: 15q25.3-26.3 ~16.5 Mb; plus 9 additional gains) and approximately 16 deletions/losses (variant 155620: 15q26.2-26.3 ~7.5 Mb; variant 155109: 15q26.2-26.3 ~4.2 Mb; variant 155021: 15q26.2-26.3 ~5.9 Mb; variant 154661: 15q26.3 ~3.4 Mb; plus 12 additional losses). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 15q26.3 chromosomal region contains critical growth and development-associated genes including IGF1R (insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor, receptor tyrosine kinase, PI3K-AKT-mTOR signaling, cell proliferation, cancer, intrauterine growth restriction), MEF2A (myocyte enhancer factor 2A, MADS-box transcription factor, cardiac muscle differentiation, coronary artery disease, neuronal development), ALDH1A3 (aldehyde dehydrogenase 1 family member A3, retinoic acid synthesis, stem cell maintenance, cancer stemness), SELENOS (selenoprotein S, ERAD, inflammation, oxidative stress response), and PCSK6 (proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 6, TGF-beta activation, left-right asymmetry). Functional hypothesis: LOC100507472 likely represents a critical IGF1R distal enhancer element within the 15q26.3 subtelomeric region, potentially functioning as a growth hormone-responsive enhancer coordinating IGF1R-MEF2A expression in developing tissues (cardiac muscle, skeletal muscle, neuronal progenitors) or as a chromatin boundary separating growth signaling genes (IGF1R) from transcription factor genes (MEF2A) and metabolic genes (ALDH1A3). The 31 pathogenic CNVs (mix of duplications and deletions, 2.5-42.1 Mb range) indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts IGF1R-mediated growth signaling and MEF2A-mediated cardiac development. Disease mechanism involves IGF1R haploinsufficiency causing intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR, small for gestational age, postnatal growth failure), IGF1R overexpression promoting cancer (breast cancer, prostate cancer, colorectal cancer, PI3K-AKT hyperactivation), MEF2A mutation causing coronary artery disease (premature atherosclerosis, myocardial infarction), MEF2A dysfunction causing cardiomyopathy (dilated cardiomyopathy, heart failure), ALDH1A3 overexpression maintaining cancer stemness (chemoresistance, tumor recurrence), and 15q26.3 deletion syndrome presenting with growth retardation, cardiac defects, and developmental delay. The 15q26.3 region is associated with overgrowth syndrome (duplication) and growth restriction syndrome (deletion). The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (31 CNVs 2.5-42.1 Mb) and IGF1R-MEF2A-ALDH1A3 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a growth hormone-responsive enhancer critical for IGF1R-mediated PI3K-AKT signaling and MEF2A-mediated cardiac development.
