## Description
HSPBP1 (Hsp70-Binding Protein 1; UniProt Q9NZL4) is a 359 amino acid cytosolic protein with limited dedicated literature, representing a GENUINE DARK gene encoding an essential nucleotide exchange factor (NEF) for the Hsp70 chaperone system. Unlike co-chaperones that inhibit Hsp70 (e.g., BAG family), HSPBP1 functions as a dedicated NEF that accelerates the release of ADP from Hsp70, enabling rapid ATP binding and substrate release, thereby driving the chaperone cycle essential for protein folding, aggregation prevention, and stress response. Its unique Armadillo-type fold and high brain expression suggest specialized roles in neuronal proteostasis and neurodegeneration protection.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals HSPBP1 contains a distinctive domain organization characteristic of eukaryotic Hsp70 NEFs: (1) N-terminal region (residues 1-83, ~83 aa) — largely unstructured or flexible linker potentially involved in regulation or localization; (2) Core Armadillo-type fold (IPR016024/G3DSA:1.25.10.10/IPR011989/SSF48371, residues 91-350/84-359, ~260-275 aa) — extended solenoid structure composed of stacked alpha-helical repeats providing a large concave surface for Hsp70 binding; (3) SIL1/FES1/HPBP1 family domain (IPR050693/PTHR19316, residues 40-348, ~308 aa) — defines the functional class of Hsp70 nucleotide exchange factors, conserved from yeast (SIL1/FES1) to humans; (4) C-terminal SIL1/FES1 repeat (PF28415, residues 136-260, ~124 aa) — specific sub-domain within the Armadillo scaffold critical for catalytic activity. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 83.1), indicating a well-folded, stable protein with a rigid helical solenoid architecture, consistent with a structural scaffold designed to bind and induce conformational changes in Hsp70.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals ultra-high confidence interactions exclusively with the Hsp70 chaperone machinery and associated regulators: HSPA8 (0.998—highest; Heat Shock Protein Family A Member 8/Hsc70, constitutive cytosolic Hsp70), HSPA4 (0.998; Heat Shock Protein Family A Member 4/Hsp110, large Hsp70 homolog acting as NEF itself), HSPA1B (0.995; Heat Shock Protein Family A Member 1B/Hsp70-2, inducible stress Hsp70), FAU (0.983; Finkel-Biskis-Reilly Murine Sarcoma Virus-Associated Ubiquitously Expressed Protein, ribosomal protein S30 fusion), BAG1 (0.973; BCL2-Associated Athanogene 1, competing NEF promoting proteasomal degradation), HSPA1A (0.969; Heat Shock Protein Family A Member 1A/Hsp70-1, major inducible Hsp70), HSPH1 (0.963; Heat Shock Protein Family H Member 1/Hsp105, large Hsp70-related protein), BAG2 (0.958; BCL2-Associated Athanogene 2, another competing NEF), DNAJB1 (0.879; DnaJ Heat Shock Protein Family Member B1/Hsp40, co-chaperone stimulating ATPase), and STUB1 (0.532; STUB1/CHIP, E3 ubiquitin ligase linking chaperones to degradation).
