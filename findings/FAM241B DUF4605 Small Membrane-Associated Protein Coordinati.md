FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: FAM241B functions as a small membrane-associated scaffold protein that bridges Wntless (WLS) Wnt ligand secretion machinery to phospholipid scramblase (PLSCR5) lipid asymmetry regulation, coordinating Wnt morphogen secretion, centrosome function, and ER-Golgi membrane trafficking through interaction with ARL6IP5 small GTPase regulator, TEX26 ER membrane protein, and TMEM170A/TMEM208 ER transmembrane adaptors in neuronal development and brain function.
