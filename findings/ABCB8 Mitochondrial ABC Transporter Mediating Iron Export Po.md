# ABCB8: Mitochondrial ABC Transporter Mediating Iron Export, Potassium Channel Function, and Protection Against Doxorubicin Cardiotoxicity

## Description
ABCB8 (Q9NUT2) is a 735-aa mitochondrial ATP-binding cassette transporter with confident AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 78.1), indicating a stable well-folded membrane protein with characteristic ABC transporter architecture. InterPro analysis reveals ABC transporter ATP-binding domain (IPR003439: positions 472-709), ABC transporter type 1 transmembrane domain (IPR011527: positions 149-437), AAA+ ATPase domain (IPR003593: positions 499-686), P-loop NTPase superfamily (IPR027417: positions 470-715), and conserved ABC transporter signature (IPR017871: positions 612-626), characteristic of ATP-dependent transporters. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to mitochondria, nucleoplasm, and nucleoli, functioning in ion/potassium transport. STRING interactions demonstrate very high-confidence binding to CCDC51 (0.995), plus high-confidence interactions with mitochondrial metal transporters SLC25A28 (0.828), SLC25A37 (0.774), other ABC transporters ABCA2 (0.805), ABCD4 (0.802), ABCG5 (0.800), ABCG8 (0.744), ABCB6 (0.475), and ATPases ATP1A3 (0.756), ATP12A (0.725). Literature (84 papers) establishes ABCB8 as mitochondrial inner membrane transporter mediating iron export from mitochondria to cytosol, with loss-of-function disrupting mitochondrial-cytosolic iron homeostasis. Recent 2022-2026 studies document ABCB8 downregulation promoting cardiac doxorubicin retention and cardiotoxicity through iron accumulation, exercise-induced ABCB8 upregulation protecting against doxorubicin cardiotoxicity, lncRNA NEAT1/miR-485-5p/ABCB8 axis driving multiple myeloma transformation, and exosomal miR-3677-3p targeting ABCB8 to induce neuronal ferroptosis in perioperative neurocognitive disorders. ClinVar contains 80 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1218-2A>G, c.1217+73G>A) and copy number gains/losses at 7q34-36.3, though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. The confident structure (pLDDT 78.1) and transporter domain architecture suggest ABCB8 functions as ATP-dependent exporter moving iron and/or potassium ions from mitochondrial matrix to intermembrane space, maintaining mitochondrial iron homeostasis and preventing iron-mediated oxidative damage, explaining its protective role against doxorubicin-induced cardiotoxicity and involvement in ferroptosis regulation.
