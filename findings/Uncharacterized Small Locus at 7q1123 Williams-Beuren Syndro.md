## Description
LOC101927243 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 7q11.23 (NC_000007.14:77415197-77425443) spanning approximately 10.2 kb within the critical Williams-Beuren syndrome (WBS) microdeletion region. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - predominantly copy number losses (deletions): (1) 7q11.22-21.11 deletion (chr7:72179092-79164071, ~7 Mb), (2) 7q11.23-21.11 deletion (chr7:74377395-82031742, ~7.7 Mb), (3) 7p22.3-q36.3 deletion (chr7:54185-159282390, ~159.2 Mb), (4) 7q11.23-21.11 deletion (chr7:75496701-78375575, ~2.9 Mb), (5) 7q11.23-21.11 deletion (chr7:73873420-83988860, ~10.1 Mb), (6) 7q11.22-11.23 deletion (chr7:69382353-77823832, ~8.4 Mb), (7) 7q11.22-21.11 deletion (chr7:71225344-81735657, ~10.5 Mb), plus 1 duplication (7q11.22-21.11). The 7q11.23 region is the critical Williams-Beuren syndrome locus containing dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental genes including ELN (elastin, connective tissue integrity, supravalvular aortic stenosis), LIMK1 (LIM domain kinase 1, actin cytoskeleton, visuospatial cognition), RFC2 (replication factor C, DNA replication), GTF2I/GTF2IRD1 (general transcription factors, WBS cognitive phenotype), and multiple neuronal enhancer elements. LOC101927243 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 7q11.23 ELN/LIMK1 expression in neural crest and endothelial cells, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in neurodevelopment, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 7q11.23 WBS topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 7q11.23 microdeletion susceptibility. The high pathogenic CNV burden (8 variants) with predominantly deletions suggests: (1) this locus is highly dosage-sensitive for WBS region gene regulation, (2) deletions may disrupt 7q11.23 chromatin architecture leading to ELN haploinsufficiency (supravalvular aortic stenosis) and LIMK1 deletion (visuospatial cognitive deficits), (3) copy number losses may alter elastin signaling and neuronal development contributing to Williams-Beuren syndrome phenotype (elfin facies, intellectual disability, hypersocial personality, cardiovascular anomalies), or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the WBS critical region with potent neurodevelopmental regulatory effects. The 7q11.23 region is one of the most well-characterized microdeletion syndromes, with ~1.5 Mb deletions causing classic WBS phenotype.
