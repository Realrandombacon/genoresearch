## Description
IPPK (Inositol-pentakisphosphate 2-kinase, UniProt Q9H8X2) is a 491 aa metabolic enzyme that catalyzes the final step in IP6 (inositol hexakisphosphate) biosynthesis by phosphorylating Ins(1,3,4,5,6)P5 at the 2-position. InterPro analysis identifies the canonical inositol-pentakisphosphate 2-kinase family domain (IPR009286, PF06090, positions 13-456) with an N-terminal lobe (IPR043001, G3DSA:3.30.200.110, positions 2-126) for ATP binding and catalysis. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 88.2), indicating a well-folded kinase with stable catalytic core. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in esophagus (30.7 nTPM), ubiquitous detection across tissues, cytosolic localization. STRING interactions confirm functional clustering within inositol phosphate metabolism: IPMK (0.985, inositol polyphosphate multikinase), PPIP5K1 (0.982), PPIP5K2 (0.949, diphosphoinositol pentakisphosphate kinases), IP6K1/2/3 (0.967/0.964/0.960, IP6 kinases producing IP7), MINPP1 (0.691, inositol phosphatase), GLE1 (0.678, mRNA export factor requiring IP6), ITPK1 (0.521), CDIPT (0.500). ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 9p24.3-q34.3. Literature (62 papers) reveals IPPK produces IP6 essential for mRNA export via GLE1-DBP5/DDX19 complex activation at nuclear pore complexes, contributes to ventral nerve cord assembly in C. elegans, and generates inositol pyrophosphates (IP7/IP8) for cellular signaling. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: IPPK serves as the gatekeeper enzyme controlling cellular IP6 pools, which function both as metabolic intermediates for higher inositol pyrophosphates and as essential cofactors for mRNA export machinery. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: IPPK binds ATP and Ins(1,3,4,5,6)P5 in its catalytic cleft, transferring the gamma-phosphate to produce IP6. IP6 then binds GLE1 (STRING 0.678), inducing conformational changes that activate the DEAD-box helicase DBP5/DDX19 for mRNA remodeling during nuclear export. IPPK also supplies substrate for IP6K1/2/3 to generate 5-IP7 (diphosphoinositol pentakisphosphate), a high-energy signaling molecule regulating insulin signaling, telomere length, phosphate homeostasis, and vesicle trafficking. The 29 ClinVar variants suggest IPPK dysfunction causes developmental disorders, potentially through impaired mRNA export in neural tissues (consistent with C. elegans ventral nerve cord phenotypes). The high esophagus expression may reflect specialized mRNA export demands in stratified epithelium.
