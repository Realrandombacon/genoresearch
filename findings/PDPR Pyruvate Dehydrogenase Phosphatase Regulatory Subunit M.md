## Description
PDPR (Pyruvate Dehydrogenase Phosphatase Regulatory Subunit, Mitochondrial; UniProt Q8NCN5) is an 879 amino acid essential mitochondrial protein with 188 publications, representing a PARTIALLY DARK gene encoding a critical regulatory subunit controlling pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDC) activity—the gatekeeper enzyme linking glycolysis to the TCA cycle. Despite its fundamental role in cellular energy metabolism—a process critical for all metabolically active tissues—PDPR remains incompletely characterized in human cells regarding its precise regulatory mechanism, with much understanding extrapolated from bacterial homologs and biochemical studies.
