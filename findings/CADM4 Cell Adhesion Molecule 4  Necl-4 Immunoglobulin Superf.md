## Description
CADM4 (Cell Adhesion Molecule 4, also known as Necl-4, SynCAM4, UniProt Q8NFZ8) is a 388 aa type I transmembrane protein belonging to the immunoglobulin superfamily cell adhesion molecule CADM family (IPR003599/IPR013783) with three extracellular immunoglobulin domains (V-set: positions 30-122/PF07686, I-set: positions 121-220/cd05885, C2-set: positions 130-206/PF08205, G3DSA:2.60.40.10 at positions 25-123/124-226/227-314) and cytoplasmic neurexin/syndecan/glycophorin C motif (IPR003585, positions 344-362) that functions as a ZDHHC3-palmitoylated myelin membrane scaffold regulating Schmidt-Lanterman incisure formation in peripheral nerves, modulates GABAergic synaptic strength on inhibitory neurons via ErbB4 activation, assembles MAGUK complexes with MPP6/CADM3, serves as tumor suppressor in gastric carcinoma, modifies SPG35 fatty acid 2-hydroxylase deficiency phenotypes, and is associated with 19q11-13.33 copy number variant disorders. The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal signal peptide (positions 1-24) for ER targeting, three extracellular Ig domains (positions 25-314) mediating homophilic and heterophilic cell-cell adhesion, single transmembrane helix (positions 315-343) anchoring protein in plasma membrane, and C-terminal cytoplasmic tail (positions 344-388) containing PDZ-binding motif for MAGUK scaffold recruitment and palmitoylation sites for membrane microdomain targeting.
