## Description
LOC101926964 (Gene ID: 101926964, Ensembl: ENSG00000231252) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 1p32.1-p31.3 (NC_000001.11, positions 60825583-60659630, ~166 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID (ENSG00000231252) but no quantitative expression data (nTPM unavailable). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 inversion (variant 2672311, single allele), 2 duplications/gains (variant 153325: 1p32.3-31.3 ~14.6 Mb; variant 58079: 1p32.3-31.1 ~24.2 Mb), and 8 deletions/losses (variant 150647: 1p32.3-31.3 ~10.6 Mb; variant 147765: 1p32.1-22.3 ~27.3 Mb; variant 144714: 1p32.1-31.2 ~10.3 Mb; variant 144228: 1p32.1-31.1 ~11.8 Mb; variant 59971: 1p32.1-31.1 ~17.1 Mb; variant 59970: 1p32.2-31.1 ~14.0 Mb; variant 59968: 1p32.3-31.3 ~7.7 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 1p32.1 chromosomal region contains critical oncogene and signaling-associated genes including JUN (Jun proto-oncogene, AP-1 transcription factor subunit, bZIP DNA binding, cell proliferation, transformation, stress response, cancer progression), TACSTD2 (tumor associated calcium signal transducer 2/TROP2, calcium signaling, cell adhesion, cancer stem cell marker, solid tumor overexpression), CYP2J2 (cytochrome P450 family 2 subfamily J member 2, arachidonic acid epoxygenase, cardiovascular protection, cancer metabolism, EET production), HOOK1 (hook microtubule tethering protein 1, vesicle trafficking, microtubule organization, intracellular transport), and MYSM1 (Myb-like SWIRM and MPN domains 1, histone H2A deubiquitinase, chromatin remodeling, hematopoiesis, tumor suppression). Functional hypothesis: LOC101926964 likely represents a critical 1p32.1 enhancer element within the JUN proto-oncogene regulatory region, potentially functioning as a proliferation-specific enhancer coordinating JUN-TACSTD2-CYP2J2 expression in rapidly dividing tissues (epithelial cells, hematopoietic progenitors, cancer cells) or as a chromatin boundary separating oncogenes (JUN, TACSTD2) from metabolic enzymes (CYP2J2) and chromatin regulators (MYSM1). The 11 pathogenic CNVs (mix of deletions and duplications, 7.7-27.3 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts JUN-mediated AP-1 transcriptional activation and TACSTD2-mediated calcium signaling. Disease mechanism involves JUN amplification causing cancer (AP-1 hyperactivation, cell cycle progression, transformation, solid tumors, leukemia), JUN overexpression promoting tumor progression (proliferation, invasion, metastasis, chemoresistance), TACSTD2 overexpression causing cancer stemness (TROP2 marker, solid tumor progression, poor prognosis, antibody-drug conjugate target), CYP2J2 overexpression causing cardiovascular disease (EET production, vasodilation, cardioprotection, cancer metabolism), MYSM1 mutation causing hematologic disorders (histone H2A deubiquitination defect, hematopoiesis impairment, bone marrow failure, anemia), and 1p32.1 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with cancer predisposition and developmental abnormalities. The 1p32.1 region is a well-established cancer susceptibility locus with JUN germline variants associated with cancer risk. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (11 CNVs 7.7-27.3 Mb) and JUN-TACSTD2-CYP2J2-HOOK1-MYSM1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as an epithelial/hematopoietic-specific enhancer critical for AP-1 oncogenic signaling-calcium transduction-chromatin remodeling coordination.
