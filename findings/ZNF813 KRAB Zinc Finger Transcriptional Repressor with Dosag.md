# ZNF813: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
ZNF813 encodes a 538-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 8-81) that recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors and histone methyltransferases to silence target genes through heterochromatin formation. Three canonical C2H2 zinc finger clusters (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 215-349 provide DNA-binding specificity to GC-rich promoter elements. Human Protein Atlas data shows low tissue specificity with expression detected across many tissues (vesicular subcellular localization), suggesting broad regulatory roles. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with related zinc finger proteins: ZNF525 (0.766), ZNF816 (0.698), ZNF320 (0.437), and chromatin regulators KAZN (0.477), HOXB9 (0.422), MAPK6 (0.416). ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains (9 CNV entries) affecting 19q13.33-13.43, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene amplification disrupts transcriptional networks. Conservation analysis shows moderate sequence preservation across vertebrates. The combination of KRAB repression domain, multiple DNA-binding zinc fingers, protein-protein interactions with chromatin modifiers, and pathogenic CNVs supports a model where ZNF813 maintains epigenetic homeostasis at specific genomic loci, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression causing dysregulated gene expression programs.
