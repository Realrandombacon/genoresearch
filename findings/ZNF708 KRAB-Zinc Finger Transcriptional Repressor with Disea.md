## Description
ZNF708 encodes a 563 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt P17019) functioning as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 3-75) at the N-terminus which recruits the corepressor complex, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 140-246) that mediate sequence-specific DNA recognition. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with a general transcriptional regulatory role. STRING interactions reveal functional association with TRIM28 (score 0.522), the canonical KRAB domain coreceptor that recruits heterochromatin protein 1 and histone methyltransferases for epigenetic silencing, plus paralogous zinc finger proteins ZNF675 (0.520), ZNF253 (0.489), ZNF107 (0.484), and ZNF431 (0.477), suggesting participation in a coordinated zinc finger repressor network. ClinVar documents 7 pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning chromosome 19 regions containing ZNF708, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene duplication disrupts normal transcriptional repression programs. The KRAB-ZNF architecture positions ZNF708 as an epigenetic silencer that binds specific genomic loci and recruits TRIM28/KAP1 to establish repressive chromatin marks (H3K9me3), regulating target gene expression in development and differentiation.
