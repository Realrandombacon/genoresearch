## Description
FAM83A (SACK1A/BJ-TSA-9) encodes a 434 amino acid scaffolding CK1 anchoring protein (UniProt Q86UY5) with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 72.4). InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal phospholipase D-like domain (cd09181, aa24-298) within the FAM83 family scaffold (IPR050944/PTHR16181, aa9-360) and a Scaffolding anchor of CK1 domain (IPR012461/PF07894, aa20-295), indicating phospholipid-binding and kinase scaffolding capability. HPA data reveals tissue-enriched expression with esophagus showing highest expression (59.6 nTPM), subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol, cancer-enhanced expression pattern, and proto-oncogene classification. STRING interactions identify 10 partners (0.421-0.599 scores): CSNK1A1 (casein kinase 1 alpha, 0.599-medium confidence), TBC1D31 (TBC1 domain family member 31, 0.512), MRPL23 (mitochondrial ribosomal protein L23, 0.501), RAB5IF (RAB5 interaction factor, 0.470), TP53I13 (tumor protein p53 inducible protein 13, 0.463), CSNK1E (casein kinase 1 epsilon, 0.452), DERL1 (derlin-1, 0.438), ZHX3 (zinc fingers and homeoboxes 3, 0.425), and DHX35 (DEAH-box helicase 35, 0.421). This network positions FAM83A at the intersection of Wnt/beta-catenin signaling (CSNK1A1/1E), vesicle trafficking (TBC1D31, RAB5IF), mitochondrial translation (MRPL23), and ER-associated degradation (DERL1). ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses spanning 8q23.2-24.3 and 8q24.11-24.13 regions, with 8q24 amplification being a known cancer driver locus. The 8q24.13 chromosomal location and esophageal-enriched expression suggests epithelial proliferation control role. Mechanistic hypothesis: FAM83A functions as a phospholipase D-like scaffold protein that anchors casein kinase 1 (CK1α/ε) to phosphatidylinositol-enriched membrane microdomains to regulate Wnt/beta-catenin signaling amplitude in esophageal epithelial cells; its PLD-like domain (aa24-298, pLDDT 72.4) provides a stable folded core for membrane recruitment while the CK1 anchoring motif (aa20-295) positions kinase complexes for substrate phosphorylation including beta-catenin and p53; overexpression in cancer cells enhances CK1-dependent beta-catenin stabilization and epithelial proliferation, while haploinsufficiency from 8q24 deletions impairs Wnt signaling homeostasis, explaining the dosage-sensitive phenotypes associated with 8q24 copy number variants in cancer and developmental disorders.
