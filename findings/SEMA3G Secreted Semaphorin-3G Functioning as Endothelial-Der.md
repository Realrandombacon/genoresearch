## Description
SEMA3G (Q9NS98) encodes a 782 amino acid secreted semaphorin-3G that functions as a class 3 semaphorin signaling molecule regulating axon guidance, angiogenesis, immune cell migration, and tumor-stromal communication through neuropilin-plexin receptor complexes.
