## Description
OR2AK2 (Olfactory receptor family 2 subfamily AK member 2, UniProt Q8NG84) is a 335-amino acid integral membrane protein located on chromosome 1q44. InterPro analysis reveals canonical class A G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) architecture: olfactory receptor domain (IPR000725/PF13853, aa 48-320) embedded within rhodopsin-like 7-transmembrane helix bundle (IPR000276/IPR017452/G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, aa 36-332/41-65/74-95/119-141) forming the characteristic serpentine fold with extracellular N-terminus and intracellular C-terminus. GO annotations confirm olfactory receptor activity (GO:0004984), G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway (GO:0007186), and membrane localization (GO:0016020). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 87.1), indicating a well-folded stable 7TM structure optimal for odorant binding and G protein activation. Human Protein Atlas shows not detected expression in bulk tissues but cell type-enhanced specificity — consistent with restricted expression in olfactory sensory neurons of the nasal epithelium where individual ORs exhibit monoallelic, neuron-specific expression patterns. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners representing the canonical olfactory signaling cascade: GNAL (0.739, Gαolf subunit — highest confidence interaction confirming direct G protein coupling), ARRB2/ARRB1 (0.679 each, arrestins mediating receptor desensitization), GNG7/GNG13 (0.678/0.566, Gβγ subunits), PRKACA/PRKACB (0.650/0.649, cAMP-dependent protein kinase catalytic subunits), GRK2/GRK3 (0.569/0.547, G protein-coupled receptor kinases), and GNB1 (0.503, Gβ1 subunit). This interactome confirms OR2AK2 functions within the canonical cAMP-mediated olfactory transduction pathway. ClinVar contains 70 pathogenic variants consisting predominantly of large copy-number gains and losses across 1q41-q44, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 1q cause disease phenotypes potentially affecting olfactory function and neurodevelopment. Conservation across primates (macaque A0A7N9IB07, 375 aa; snub-nosed monkey A0AAJ7IMP9, 329 aa) indicates evolutionary origin tied to fundamental chemosensory mechanisms. OR2AK2 likely functions as an odorant-activated GPCR, using its 7TM bundle to bind specific volatile ligands in the olfactory epithelium while activating Gαolf to stimulate adenylyl cyclase, increase cAMP, open cyclic nucleotide-gated channels, and depolarize olfactory sensory neurons — initiating action potentials that transmit smell information to the olfactory bulb, with potential extra-nasal functions in non-olfactory tissues where ectopic OR expression regulates processes like sperm chemotaxis, wound healing, and cancer metastasis.
