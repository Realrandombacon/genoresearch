## Description
CDH24 (Cadherin-24, 819 aa, UniProt Q86UP0) is a partially characterized type II classical cadherin with emerging roles in gastrointestinal cancers, CNS development, and cell adhesion. Only 11 publications exist, confirming genuine partial characterization. InterPro analysis reveals canonical cadherin architecture: three cadherin tandem repeat domains (cd11304, positions 51-146/154-255/264-368), cadherin-like domains (IPR002126, positions 54-150/150-270/260-374), Y-type LIR-motif (IPR000233, positions 751-812), and catenin-binding domain (G3DSA:4.10.900.10/IPR027397, positions 721-816) for intracellular scaffold recruitment. STRING interactions confirm high-confidence partnerships with CTNND1/p120-catenin (0.796), CTNNB1/β-catenin (0.762), CTNNA1/α-catenin (0.609), CTNNA2/αT-catenin (0.586), CTNNA3/αC-catenin (0.660), JUP/plakoglobin (0.589), and other cadherins CDH17 (0.592), CDH12 (0.589), CDH13 (0.585), DCHS1 (0.590) - all canonical adherens junction components. HPA shows low tissue specificity (detected in many tissues), not detected in immune cells, cell type enhanced pattern, with vesicle/cytosol localization (note: cadherins typically membrane-localized, suggesting dynamic trafficking). ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including SNVs and chromosome 14q CNVs (copy number gains and deletions), though specific disease associations remain undefined. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 76.2, high confidence) indicates well-folded protein with stable cadherin extracellular domains and structured catenin-binding region. Literature reveals: frameshift mutations in gastric/colorectal cancers with high microsatellite instability (2014, 40 citations - most cited paper); differential spatiotemporal expression in CNS segmentation and brain nuclei formation during mouse development (2021, 28 citations); junctional gene associations with lung adenocarcinoma survival (2024); cadherin family transcriptional profiling in gastric cancer therapeutics (2024); cfDNA methylation biomarker in rectal cancer radiochemotherapy response (2024). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CDH24 functions as type II classical cadherin mediating calcium-dependent homophilic cell-cell adhesion in gastrointestinal epithelium and developing CNS, with catenin-binding domain recruiting adherens junction scaffolds (p120-catenin, β-catenin, α-catenin) to link adhesion to actin cytoskeleton and WNT signaling. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: CDH24 extracellular cadherin repeats (EC1-EC3) mediate calcium-dependent homophilic binding between adjacent cells, while cytoplasmic catenin-binding domain (721-816) recruits p120-catenin (stabilization), β-catenin (WNT signaling), and α-catenin (actin linkage) to form mature adherens junctions. Frameshift mutations in MSI-high gastrointestinal cancers suggest tumor suppressor role via adhesion loss. CNS expression pattern indicates role in rhombomere segmentation and neural circuit formation. With only 11 papers, defined cadherin domain architecture, known catenin interactions, 14 ClinVar variants, and emerging cancer/CNS roles, CDH24 represents a genuine partially-characterized dark gene worthy of further investigation.
