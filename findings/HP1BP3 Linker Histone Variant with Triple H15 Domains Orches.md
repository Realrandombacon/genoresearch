## Description
HP1BP3 encodes a 553 amino acid linker histone variant (UniProt Q5SSJ5), also known as Heterochromatin Protein 1-Binding Protein 3, that functions as a chromatin architectural regulator distinct from canonical H1 histones. With 51 publications, HP1BP3 is emerging from the 'dark' zone, particularly regarding its role in glioma metabolic vulnerability and epigenetic therapy resistance.\n\nSTRUCTURAL FEATURES: AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 65.3) across all 553 residues, indicating a partially structured protein with both ordered domains and flexible linker regions characteristic of histone proteins.
