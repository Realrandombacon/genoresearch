# LOC129390807: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 16q21-q23 Critical Region - Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 10 Pathogenic CNV Variants

**Quality Score:** 2.9/10  (E=2.9, D=2.9) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129390807 (Gene ID: 129390807) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 16q22.1 (69,687,052-69,687,252 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak2631 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - predominantly copy number gains (duplications) affecting the 16q21-q24 chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 16q21-23.3 duplication (65957829-83611443, ~17.7 Mb); (2) 16q12.2-24.3 duplication (52899183-90088654, ~37.2 Mb); (3) 16q21-24.3 duplication (64389378-90081985, ~25.7 Mb); (4) 16q22.1-23.3 duplication (69053457-83274681, ~14.2 Mb); (5) 16q21-24.3 duplication (65511483-90096995, ~24.6 Mb); (6) 16q21-24.1 duplication (62925929-84585795, ~21.7 Mb); (7) 16q21-24.3 duplication (65313395-90081985, ~24.8 Mb); (8) 16q21-23.1 duplication (58456122-74708723, ~16.3 Mb); (9) 16q13-22.2 duplication (56883592-71279975, ~14.4 Mb); (10) 16q22.1-23.1 deletion (68698941-74353205, ~5.7 Mb). These CNVs cause 16q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, behavioral abnormalities, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and renal anomalies. The 16q critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated gene, demethylase), IRF8 (interferon regulatory factor 8, immune development), CYLD (cylindromatosis tumor suppressor, deubiquitinase), and multiple neuronal development genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390807 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Duplications encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of target genes contributing to 16q duplication syndrome phenotype; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting normal enhancer-promoter contacts; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopmental programs. The 10 ClinVar CNVs (9 duplications, 1 deletion) suggest this regulatory element may exhibit triplosensitivity - increased silencer dosage causes pathological gene repression. FTO variants affect energy homeostasis and neurodevelopment. IRF8 haploinsufficiency causes immunodeficiency. CYLD loss predisposes to cylindromatosis tumors. Deletion of silencer may cause derepression of oncogenes or developmental genes. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
