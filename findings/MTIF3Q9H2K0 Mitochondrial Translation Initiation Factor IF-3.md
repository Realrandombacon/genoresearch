## Description

MTIF3 functions as the mitochondrial homolog of bacterial translation initiation factor IF-3, ensuring fidelity of mitochondrial protein synthesis by promoting dissociation of mitochondrial ribosome subunits, preventing premature subunit association, and facilitating correct initiator tRNA selection on mitochondrial mRNAs.
