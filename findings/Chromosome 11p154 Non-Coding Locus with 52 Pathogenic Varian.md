## Description
LOC101928008 is an uncharacterized genetic locus on chromosome 11p15.4 (NC_000011.10, positions 9839141-9929262, ~90 kb region) with substantial clinical significance linked to SBF2 (SET binding factor 2). No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases (0 results). Human Protein Atlas resolved the gene to Ensembl ID ENSG00000255476 but reports no detectable protein expression across tissues. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 52 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (variants 4808114, 4732510, 3731533, 3731532, 3678971, 3659277, 3604745, 2665001, 2178831, 2124663), all annotated to SBF2 transcript NM_030962.4. Variant types include frameshift deletions (c.1907del p.Pro636fs, c.2329del p.Trp777fs), nonsense mutations (c.2581G>T p.Glu861Ter, c.2911C>T p.Gln971Ter, c.2331G>A p.Trp777Ter), missense variants (c.3110G>A p.Arg1037His, c.2513A>G p.Lys838Arg), duplications (c.1902_1905dup p.Pro636fs, c.2287dup p.Thr763fs), and splice site mutations (c.3257-5_3257-1del). All variants have uncertain significance (?). The 11p15 chromosomal region contains SBF2 (SET binding factor 2, pseudophosphatase, guanine nucleotide exchange factor for Rab GTPases, endosomal trafficking, peripheral nerve myelination) causing Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 4B2 (CMT4B2, autosomal recessive demyelinating neuropathy with myelin outfolding, early-onset foot deformities, distal muscle weakness, sensory loss, reduced nerve conduction velocity). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928008 likely represents a non-coding regulatory element within or adjacent to the SBF2 locus, potentially functioning as an intronic enhancer, 5/UTR regulator, or alternative promoter controlling SBF2 expression in Schwann cells and peripheral glia. The 52 pathogenic variants indicate strong functional constraint where loss-of-function mutations disrupt SBF2-mediated endosomal sorting and myelin membrane homeostasis. Disease mechanism involves biallelic SBF2 loss-of-function causing defective Rab GTPase activation, impaired endolysosomal trafficking in Schwann cells, accumulation of abnormal myelin outfolding structures, and progressive demyelination. SBF2 pseudophosphatase domain scaffolds protein complexes regulating phosphoinositide metabolism critical for myelin sheath maintenance. CMT4B2 patients present with childhood-onset distal weakness, foot deformities (pes cavus, clubfoot), reduced compound muscle action potentials, and characteristic myelin outfolding on nerve biopsy. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (52 variants in SBF2) and neuropathy disease association suggests LOC101928008 functions as a cis-regulatory element controlling SBF2 transcriptional activity in peripheral nerve glia through enhancer RNA production, chromatin looping to SBF2 promoter, or transcriptional interference modulation.
