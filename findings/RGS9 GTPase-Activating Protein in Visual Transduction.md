## Description
The RGS9 gene (ID: 8787) encodes Regulator of G-protein signaling 9 (UniProt: O75916), a 674 amino acid GTPase-activating protein (GAP) anchored to photoreceptor membranes in retinal cells. Located on chromosome 17 (17q24.1), this protein accelerates the deactivation of G-proteins in the rod and cone phototransduction cascades, specifically targeting transducin (Gtalpha). Mutations in RGS9 are causally linked to bradyopsia (dim-light vision deficiency).
