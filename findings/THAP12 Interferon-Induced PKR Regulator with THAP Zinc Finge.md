# THAP12: Interferon-Induced PKR Regulator with THAP Zinc Finger Domain Forming ZFP574 Complex in B Cell Malignancies and Epileptic Encephalopathy

## Description
THAP12 (O43422) is a 761-aa nuclear protein with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 83.8), indicating a stable well-folded transcription factor. InterPro analysis reveals THAP-type zinc finger domain (IPR006612/PF05485/SM00980: positions 1-92), HAT C-terminal dimerization domain (IPR008906/PF05699: positions 636-728) with protein dimerization activity (GO:0046983), ZMYM1-like RNase-like domain (IPR025398/PF14291: positions 158-338), Ribonuclease H-like superfamily (IPR012337/SSF53098: positions 249-729), and Interferon-induced PKR regulator family signature (IPR052958/PTHR46289: positions 1-757). HPA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, localized to cytosol, classified as transcription factor with DNA-binding molecular function. STRING interactions demonstrate very high-confidence binding to ER chaperone DNAJC3 (0.907) and THAP family members THAP11 (0.905), THAP4 (0.905), plus medium-confidence interaction with EIF2AK2/PKR (0.728), the very kinase THAP12 represses, and additional THAP family members THAP5 (0.648), THAP9 (0.611), THAP7 (0.573), THAP1 (0.532), THAP10 (0.530), THAP3 (0.507). Literature (only 6 papers - genuine dark gene) establishes THAP12 as 52 kDa repressor of PKR inhibitor that forms complex with zinc finger protein ZFP574 to regulate B cell malignancies, with 2024 study showing ZFP574-THAP12 complex disruption suppresses B cell cancers in mice. Critically, 2026 study documents ultra-rare biallelic THAP12 variants causing loss of function underlying severe epileptic encephalopathy, establishing first monogenic disease association. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutation p.Ala679Ser (c.2035G>T) within HAT dimerization domain and multiple copy number gains/losses at 11q12.2-13.5 and 11p13-q25 regions. The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.8), THAP zinc finger DNA-binding domain, HAT dimerization domain, and PKR regulator family classification suggest THAP12 functions as transcriptional regulator that modulates interferon-induced PKR kinase activity through direct protein-protein interactions, coupling innate immune signaling to transcriptional programs in B cells and neurons, explaining its role in B cell malignancy progression and epileptic encephalopathy pathogenesis when disrupted.
