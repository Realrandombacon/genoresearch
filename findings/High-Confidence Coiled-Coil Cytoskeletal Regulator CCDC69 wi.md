## Description
CCDC69 (UniProt A6NI79) is a 296 amino acid intracellular coiled-coil domain-containing protein representing an understudied but structurally well-characterized member of the MTUS1/CCDC69 microtubule-associated tumor suppressor family with emerging roles in cytoskeletal organization, genome stability, and muscle physiology. The protein exhibits characteristic coiled-coil domain architecture optimized for protein-protein interactions and cytoskeletal scaffolding: (1) N-terminal first coiled-coil domain (PF27559) spanning residues 50-137, providing dimerization interface and structural scaffold for complex assembly, (2) C-terminal second coiled-coil domain (IPR060408/PF27558) at positions 172-242, forming extended helical bundle for partner protein recruitment, (3) Full-length MTUS1/CCDC69 family signature (IPR051293/PTHR24200) spanning residues 54-288 (79% of sequence), confirming membership in microtubule-associated tumor suppressor superfamily implicated in cell division control and cancer suppression. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 84.6) spanning the full 1-296 residue length, classifying CCDC69 as a well-folded protein with stable tertiary structure—this high confidence score (>80) strongly suggests CCDC69 adopts defined coiled-coil conformations enabling precise molecular interactions with cytoskeletal components and DNA repair machinery. Low cancer specificity indicates CCDC69 maintains relatively stable expression across malignant and normal tissues, consistent with housekeeping cytoskeletal functions rather than oncogene/tumor suppressor dysregulation.
