## Description

InterPro analysis reveals extensive C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, PF13909) spanning positions 130-180, 274-323, and 345-367, plus a Krüppel C2H2-type zinc-finger domain-containing protein family signature (IPR051967, PTHR45925) covering residues 56-1295. The protein contains an unstructured conserved region between zinc fingers (PF16606, residues 657-737), characteristic of transcriptional regulatory proteins. AlphaFold prediction shows low global confidence (pLDDT 46.8), indicating intrinsic disorder typical of transcription factors that function through protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic catalysis. HPA expression data demonstrates brain-enriched expression (30.4 nTPM) with secondary expression in retina (8.8 nTPM), classified as tissue-enhanced and cancer-enhanced. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses affecting 19q11-13, plus missense variant c.3143C>T (p.Ala1048Val). Conservation is high across vertebrates with mouse ortholog Q8K083 (1302 aa, 99% identity) and gorilla ortholog G3RCS2 (1300 aa, 100% identity).
