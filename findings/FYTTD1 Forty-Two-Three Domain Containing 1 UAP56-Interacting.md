## Description
FYTTD1 (Forty-Two-Three Domain Containing 1, also known as UAP56-Interacting Factor or UIF) is a 318 amino acid nuclear protein that functions as a critical adaptor in the TREX (TRanscription-EXport) complex, mediating mRNA export from nucleus to cytoplasm through interaction with the DEAD-box helicase DDX39B/UAP56 and the export receptor NXF1/TAP for coupling transcription to nuclear export and preventing nuclear retention of transcripts in proliferating and differentiated tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic UAP56-interacting factor architecture including UAP56-interacting factor family (IPR009782, residues 1-318) defining evolutionary relationship to mRNA export adaptors with mRNA binding capability (GO:0003729) and nuclear export function (GO:0006406), Forty-two-three protein Pfam domain (PF07078, residues 12-318) forming the structured core for protein-protein interactions, and 40-2-3 protein-related Panther family (PTHR21038, residues 1-318) establishing orthology across eukaryotes. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q96QD9-F1) predicts low-to-medium confidence (pLDDT 57.9) across the 318 residue modeled sequence, indicating a partially structured protein with flexible regions suitable for dynamic TREX complex assembly, RNA binding, and conformational adaptability during mRNA handover to NXF1. HPA data classifies FYTTD1 as a predicted intracellular protein with nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and nuclear speckles subcellular localization reflecting transcription/export roles, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated mRNA transport function; biological processes annotated as mRNA transport and general transport. ClinVar lists 48 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 3q22.1-29 (chr3:132561657-197851986x3), 3q29 (chr3:194814799-197817520x3, chr3:195106447-197846145x3), 3q26.31-29 (chr3:174764228-197851986x3), 3q26.33-29 (chr3:179313373-197851444x3), and deletions on 3q29 (chr3:?_197401889_197682644_?del), though no specific monogenic syndrome name is defined, literature establishes 3q29 microdeletion/duplication syndromes featuring neurodevelopmental delay, autism spectrum disorder, schizophrenia susceptibility, and cardiac defects. As an emerging TREX component with neurodevelopmental and cardiovascular associations, FYTTD1 represents a critical node connecting transcription to nuclear export through DDX39B-SARNP-NXF1 network integration for mRNP assembly and nuclear pore targeting, UAP56-interacting domain-mediated helicase recruitment for ATP-dependent RNA remodeling, 3q29 copy number variation disorder pathogenesis via dosage sensitivity affecting neurodevelopment and cardiac morphogenesis, and cancer fusion/NMD disruption in lung adenocarcinoma, cardiovascular disease, and environmental stress responses.
