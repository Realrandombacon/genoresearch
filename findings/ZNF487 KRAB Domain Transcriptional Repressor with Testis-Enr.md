## Description
ZNF487 (Zinc Finger Protein 487, alias KRBO1) is a 207-amino acid KRAB domain-containing zinc finger transcription factor located on chromosome 10q11.21. InterPro analysis reveals two key functional domains: an N-terminal Krueppel-associated box (KRAB, IPR001909, positions 1-43) which mediates transcriptional repression through recruitment of KAP1/TRIM28 and heterochromatin-forming complexes, and a C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger domain (IPR036236, positions 150-200) providing sequence-specific DNA binding. Human Protein Atlas shows testis-enriched expression at 26.2 nTPM with tissue-enhanced pattern across all tissues, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nuclear membrane, and cytosol—consistent with transcriptional regulatory function. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners including other zinc finger proteins (ZNF772 score 0.516, ZNF816 score 0.455, ZNF200 score 0.455, ZNF493 score 0.447) and FAM71E1 (score 0.570), suggesting participation in zinc finger protein complexes. AlphaFold structure prediction (B1APH4) yields low global confidence (pLDDT 37.7), indicating intrinsic disorder typical of KRAB-ZNF proteins with flexible interdomain linkers that facilitate chromatin scanning. ClinVar documents 4 pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning 10q11.21-q21.3, implicating ZNF487 dosage sensitivity in human disease. The combination of KRAB repression domain, testis-enriched expression, and zinc finger interaction partners suggests ZNF487 functions as a transcriptional silencer regulating germ cell development or spermatogenesis genes through sequence-specific DNA recognition and heterochromatin recruitment.
