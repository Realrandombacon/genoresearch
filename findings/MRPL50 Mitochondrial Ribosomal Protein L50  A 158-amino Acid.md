## Description

STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally high-confidence functional network exclusively composed of mitochondrial large subunit ribosomal proteins: MRPL47 (0.989, 39S ribosomal protein L47 - highest confidence partner indicating direct mitoribosome assembly), MRPL43 (0.981, 39S ribosomal protein L43), MRPL4 (0.979, 39S ribosomal protein L4), MRPL39 (0.961, 39S ribosomal protein L39), MRPL57 (0.956, 39S ribosomal protein L57), MRPL15 (0.955, 39S ribosomal protein L15), MRPL49 (0.946, 39S ribosomal protein L49), MRPL32 (0.912, 39S ribosomal protein L32), MRPL53 (0.868, 39S ribosomal protein L53), and MRPL44 (0.861, 39S ribosomal protein L44), indicating MRPL50 functions as an essential structural component of the mitochondrial translation machinery with no extra-ribosomal interactions detected. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8N5N7-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 80.3) consistent with a well-structured mitochondrial ribosomal protein containing ordered alpha/beta topology typical of large subunit components with defined RNA-binding surfaces. With only 6 papers, MRPL50 represents a genuine 'dark' gene with critical disease mechanism elucidated (2023): biallelic MRPL50 deficiency causes autosomal recessive syndromic premature ovarian insufficiency (POI) establishing role in ovarian follicle development; MRPL50 mutations cause Perrault syndrome phenotype linking mitochondrial translation defects to hearing loss and gonadal dysgenesis; and yeast mitochondrial ribosome isolation identified MRPL50 as conserved ribosomal component. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that MRPL50 functions as a mitochondrial ribosomal protein that: (1) integrates into the 39S large subunit through interactions with MRPL47 (0.989 score), MRPL43 (0.981 score), and MRPL4 (0.979 score) forming structural core of mitoribosome; (2) enables mitochondrial translation of oxidative phosphorylation subunits encoded by mtDNA (ND1-6, COX1-3, CYTB, ATP6/8); (3) localizes to mitochondria via N-terminal targeting sequence cleaved upon import; (4) binds mitochondrial rRNA through the PF10501 domain (positions 60-139) stabilizing peptidyl transferase center; (5) shows ubiquitous expression reflecting essential housekeeping function in cellular energy production; (6) ovarian-specific phenotype arises from high mitochondrial demand during folliculogenesis and oocyte maturation; (7) chromosome 9q31.1 CNVs cause haploinsufficiency affecting mitochondrial protein synthesis capacity; (8) missense variant p.Val112Asp likely disrupts protein folding or ribosome assembly; (9) represents therapeutic target for Perrault syndrome and POI through mitochondrial translation enhancement or hormone replacement strategies.
