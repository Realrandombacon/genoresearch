## Description
UTP14A (U3 small nucleolar RNA-associated protein 14 homolog A, UniProt Q9BVJ6) is a 771-amino acid nucleolar protein functioning as an essential component of the small-subunit (SSU) processome required for 18S rRNA synthesis and ribosome biogenesis. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the Utp14 family (IPR006709, aa 29-760; PF04615, aa 44-735; PTHR14150, aa 29-760) with GO annotations for rRNA processing (GO:0006364) and small-subunit processome (GO:0032040). STRING interactions reveal exceptionally high-confidence partnerships (0.997-0.999) with SSU processome components WDR46, UTP3, UTP20, UTP6, UTP4, UTP18, MPHOSPH10, BMS1, plus nucleolar proteins NOP58 and DCAF13, confirming integral role in the 90S pre-ribosome assembly machinery. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleolar and cytosolic localization consistent with ribosome biogenesis function. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 66.2) for the full 771 aa, indicating partially structured protein with ordered domains interspersed with flexible regions typical of large ribosome assembly factors. ClinVar documents 176 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains on chromosome Xq13-28 and single nucleotide variants/intronic changes, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (26 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic functions: (1) P16INK4A drives RB1 degradation via UTP14A-catalyzed K810 ubiquitination in cancer cells (1 citation, 2024); (2) Oncogenic functions in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (2024); (3) Modulator of cardiac angiogenesis in obesity-induced cardiac injury with DKC1, DDX10, PinX1, ESF1 (9 citations, 2022); (4) Promoter of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma proliferation and metastasis via PERK/eIF2α/GRP78 unfolded protein response signaling pathway (7 citations, 2021). Functional hypothesis: UTP14A serves dual roles as (a) essential ribosome biogenesis factor in the SSU processome enabling 18S rRNA maturation, and (b) context-dependent oncogenic driver linking ribosome production to stress response pathways (UPR/PERK) and cell cycle regulation (RB1 ubiquitination). Mechanistic proposal: UTP14A scaffolds SSU processome assembly through interactions with UTP proteins and WDR46, facilitating pre-rRNA cleavage; in cancer contexts, UTP14A may be co-opted to enhance translational capacity while simultaneously promoting RB1 degradation via K810 ubiquitination and activating PERK/eIF2α/GRP78 signaling to support tumor survival under metabolic stress, explaining its association with obesity-induced cardiac injury and ESCC metastasis.
