## Description
LOC112268114 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 13q32.1 (NC_000013.11:94713442-94712719) spanning approximately 0.7 kb - one of the smallest annotated loci in this survey. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - the highest burden observed for any locus in this survey. Variants include massive 13q copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 13q12.11-34 (chr13:19837395-114327173, ~94.5 Mb; chr13:19671934-114340331, ~94.7 Mb; chr13:19833130-114327106, ~94.5 Mb), 13q11-34 (chr13:18862146-114342258, ~95.5 Mb; chr13:18456040-114340285, ~95.9 Mb), 13q31.1-34 (chr13:78964223-114340331, ~35.4 Mb; chr13:83288131-114342258, ~31.1 Mb; chr13:78999318-114327106, ~35.3 Mb), 13q31.3-34 (chr13:89779269-114338054, ~24.6 Mb), 13q31.2-32.3 (chr13:87944479-99866542, ~11.9 Mb), and deletions spanning 13q31.3-34 (chr13:89779269-114338054, ~24.6 Mb) and 13q31.2-32.3 (chr13:87944479-99866542, ~11.9 Mb). The 13q32.1 region is the critical locus for 13q deletion syndrome and 13q duplication syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive tumor suppressors and developmental genes including RB1 (retinoblastoma tumor suppressor, hereditary retinoblastoma), BRCA2 (breast cancer susceptibility 2, homologous recombination DNA repair), FLT1 (fms-like tyrosine kinase 1, VEGF receptor), DACH1 (dachshund homolog 1, developmental transcription factor), and multiple microRNA clusters (MIR17HG, MIR106B~25). LOC112268114 likely functions as: (1) a small cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 13q tumor suppressor gene expression, (2) a microRNA or small non-coding RNA (sncRNA) with post-transcriptional regulatory function, (3) a chromatin boundary element within the 13q32.1 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 13q genomic stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (27 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus represents the most critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element identified in this survey where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) RB1 haploinsufficiency leading to retinoblastoma predisposition, (2) BRCA2 DNA repair capacity affecting cancer susceptibility, (3) 13q chromatin architecture affecting multiple tumor suppressor expression, or (4) neurodevelopmental programs, leading to 13q deletion syndrome (retinoblastoma, intellectual disability, growth retardation, congenital anomalies, Hirschsprung disease) or 13q duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, cancer predisposition).
