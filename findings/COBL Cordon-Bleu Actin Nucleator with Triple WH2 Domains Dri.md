## Description
COBL (Cordon-bleu, O75128) encodes a large 1261 aa intracellular protein functioning as a potent actin nucleator that utilizes three tandem Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome homology 2 (WH2) domains to bind G-actin monomers and initiate filament assembly critical for neuronal morphogenesis, enamel formation, and embryonic patterning. Structural architecture reveals an N-terminal ubiquitin-like domain (IPR019025/PF09469, residues 144-235) potentially mediating protein stability or specific interactions, followed by a long unstructured linker region, and a C-terminal actin-binding module containing three distinct WH2 repeats: Wa (cd21799/IPR003124/PF02205, residues 1099-1131/1107-1134), Wb (cd21800/IPR003124/PF02205, residues 1145-1188/1147-1175), and Wc (cd21801/IPR003124/PF02205, residues 1233-1258/1236-1258) which cooperatively recruit actin monomers to form a nucleation seed; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 50.6, medium confidence) indicates a predominantly intrinsically disordered protein with localized structured WH2 motifs embedded in flexible linkers, a conformational plasticity essential for dynamic cytoskeletal remodeling and rapid response to signaling cues. STRING interactions define a specialized actin regulatory network: alpha-actin ACTA1 (0.914, highest confidence) as the primary substrate for filament nucleation, BIN/amphiphysin/Rvs (BAR) domain proteins PACSIN3 (0.897), PACSIN1 (0.891), and PACSIN2 (0.562) suggesting COBL recruits membrane-curving partners to coordinate actin polymerization with plasma membrane deformation during neurite outgrowth or vesicle trafficking, ITPRID2 (0.863) as a scaffolding protein linking to phosphoinositide signaling, growth factor receptor binder GRB10 (0.819) connecting to insulin/IGF1 pathways, planar cell polarity protein VANGL2 (0.784) essential for neural tube closure and oriented cell division, tropomodulin TMOD3 (0.659) capping actin filaments to regulate length, cardiac actin ACTC1 (0.644), and Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome protein WAS (0.429) indicating crosstalk with Arp2/3-mediated branching. HPA localizes COBL to plasma membrane and cell junctions consistent with its role in cortical actin dynamics, with tissue-enhanced expression highest in skeletal muscle (124.0 nTPM) and tongue (100.7 nTPM), reflecting high demand for actin turnover in contractile tissues, detected in many tissues, immune cell enriched, and cell type enhanced. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice-affecting mutations c.3504+1G>A and c.245+84T>G disrupting exon recognition, missense variant c.1096G>C/p.Val366Leu in the ubiquitin-like domain potentially destabilizing the protein, deletion c.686-5del near splice acceptor, large copy number gains spanning 7p22.3-q36.3 (chr7:10365-159119707x3, chr7:43361-159119707x3) duplicating the locus, and a TMEM106B-BRAF fusion event; these variants are associated with neural tube defects (craniorachischisis) and potential developmental delays due to failed actin-driven morphogenetic movements. Literature (178 papers) establishes COBL as a key driver of emerging adulthood cortical dendritic arborization where it expands the dendritic tree via de novo filament nucleation, essential for proper amelogenesis where COBL-mediated actin networks shape ameloblast Tomes\' processes for enamel matrix secretion
