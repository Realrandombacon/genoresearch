## Description
VASH2 encodes a 355 aa tubulinyl-Tyr carboxypeptidase (UniProt Q86V25) with a confident 3D structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 80.4) containing the Vasohibin domain (PF14822). Functionally, it acts as a critical regulator of α-tubulin detyrosination, evidenced by high-confidence interactions (0.942) with TUBA1A and SVBP. It is localized to the cytosol with enhanced expression in testis (nTPM 6.8) and cancer tissues. Clinically, VASH2 is a 'WELL-STUDIED' gene with 18 pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs) and is implicated in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) metastasis, Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma (LUSC) chemoresistance, and Systemic Sclerosis (SSc) endothelial senescence. It drives cancer progression by enhancing KIF3C-mediated EGFR-endosomal recycling.
