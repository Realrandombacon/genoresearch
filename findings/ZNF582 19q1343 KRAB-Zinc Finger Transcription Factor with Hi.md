## Description
ZNF582 (Zinc Finger Protein 582, UniProt Q96NG8, Gene ID: 147948) is a 517 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor encoded on chromosome 19q13.43 (GRCh38: 56382750-56393533). InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 6-77) at the N-terminus, responsible for transcriptional repression via co-repressor recruitment, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 171-277) mediating sequence-specific DNA binding.
