# LRRC20: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein with Extreme Skeletal Muscle Expression and Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
LRRC20 (Leucine-rich repeat-containing protein 20) is a 184 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q8TCA0) characterized by a canonical leucine-rich repeat (LRR) architecture. InterPro analysis reveals multiple LRR domains including IPR001611 (LRR), IPR003591 (LRR typical subtype, 3 repeats at positions 73-142), and PF13855 (LRR, positions 75-132), embedded within a ribonuclease inhibitor-like superfamily fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, positions 9-164). This domain architecture suggests protein-protein interaction scaffolding capacity, typical of LRR-containing proteins that mediate signal transduction or complex assembly. HPA expression profiling reveals exceptionally high tissue-specific expression: skeletal muscle shows 286.3 nTPM (extremely elevated), tongue 116.7 nTPM, classified as "group enriched" and "detected in all" tissues. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm and cytosol. The extraordinary muscle specificity (286.3 nTPM ranks among highest tissue-specific expressions) strongly implies a critical role in skeletal muscle biology—potentially sarcomere organization, muscle differentiation, or metabolic regulation. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 10 (e.g., chr10:68735254-78885714 deletion, chr10:69040366-93194993 duplication), demonstrating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is evidenced by mouse ortholog Lrrc20 (Q8CI70, 184 aa, identical length). STRING interactions could not be resolved, consistent with limited characterization of this dark gene. Functional hypothesis: LRRC20 functions as a muscle-specific LRR scaffold protein that organizes protein complexes in skeletal muscle cells, potentially interacting with sarcomeric or signaling proteins through its LRR interface; dosage disruption via CNVs leads to myopathic phenotypes or muscle dysfunction.
