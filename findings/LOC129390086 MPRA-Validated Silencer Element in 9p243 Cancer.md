# LOC129390086: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 9p24.3 Cancer Amplification Region

**Date:** 2026-03-23T07:11:31.272696

**Quality Score:** 3.8/10  (E=4.4, D=3.2) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC129390086 (Gene ID: 129390086) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 9q21.12 (70,514,438-70,514,638 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak7263 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 9p24.3-q34.3, 9p24.3-q21.13, 9q21.11-21.13, 9p24.3-q21.31, 9p24.3-q22.1, 9q21.12-31.3, and 9p11.2-q34.3 regions (deletions 2-138 Mb, duplications 0.02-138 Mb whole chromosome arm), associated with 9p24.3/9q21 rearrangement syndromes featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and predisposition to multiple cancers. The 9p24.3 region contains the JAK2 (Janus kinase 2, cytokine signaling, myeloproliferative neoplasms), PD-L1/PD-L2 (programmed death-ligand 1/2, immune checkpoint, cancer immunotherapy), and DMRT1 (doublesex and mab-3 related transcription factor 1, sex determination) locus - a critical cancer amplification region. The 9q21 region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including TSC1 (tuberous sclerosis complex 1, mTOR signaling, tumor suppression), ABL1 (c-abl oncogene 1, tyrosine kinase, chronic myeloid leukemia t(9;22) Philadelphia chromosome), and JAK2 (V617F mutation in myeloproliferative neoplasms); (4) No HPA expression (non-coding); (5) No STRING interactions (non-coding). Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390086 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or immune contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests activity in hemat/erythroid lineage. The 9p24.3 region is frequently amplified in classical Hodgkin lymphoma, primary mediastinal B-cell lymphoma, and other cancers - amplification causes PD-L1/PD-L2 overexpression enabling immune evasion. JAK2 amplification activates STAT signaling promoting proliferation. Pathogenic 9p24.3/9q21 CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and TAD architecture, causing long-range dysregulation of JAK2, PD-L1/PD-L2, TSC1, ABL1, and DMRT1. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions. The MPRA validation provides direct functional evidence of regulatory activity in erythroid lineage. 9p24.3 amplifications occur in ~40-50% of classical Hodgkin lymphomas and ~20% of primary mediastinal B-cell lymphomas - silencer disruption may contribute to oncogenic immune evasion through PD-L1/PD-L2 dysregulation.
