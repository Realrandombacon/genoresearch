## Description
FAM185A encodes a 392 amino acid protein (UniProt Q8N0U4) localized to mitochondrion and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved DUF4097/Toastrack domain (IPR025164, PF13349) spanning residues 213-342, plus a G3DSA superfamily fold (68-242). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with high confidence (pLDDT 78.9). STRING interactions show strong enrichment for peroxisomal biogenesis factors: PEX13 (0.896), PEX7 (0.741), PEX5 (0.729), PEX5L (0.729), PEX2 (0.628), PEX19 (0.603), PEX12 (0.544), PEX6 (0.539). HPA reports cytosolic localization with expression detected across many tissues (low tissue specificity). ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on 7q22.1, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation: mouse ortholog (Q7TPD2) shares 378/392 aa (~96% identity). The convergence of mitochondrial localization, peroxisomal protein interactions, and clinical CNVs suggests FAM185A functions as a scaffold at mitochondria-peroxisome membrane contact sites, potentially coordinating lipid metabolism or reactive oxygen species handling between these organelles. The DUF4097 domain may mediate protein-protein interactions with PEX complex components.
