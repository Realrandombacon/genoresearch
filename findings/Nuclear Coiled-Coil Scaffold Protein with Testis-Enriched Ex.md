## Description
CCDC150 encodes a 1101 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt: Q8NCX0) localized to the nucleoplasm with tissue-enhanced RNA expression in testis (4.6 nTPM) and kidney (2.4 nTPM). InterPro analysis identifies membership in the CCDC150 family (IPR038807, positions 12-1101; PTHR35352), indicating an extensive coiled-coil architecture spanning nearly the entire protein length. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 74.2, high confidence) reveals a well-folded structure consistent with stable coiled-coil helical bundles that typically mediate protein-protein interactions and oligomerization. STRING network identifies 10 interaction partners including C18orf54 (score 0.596), ARMH2 (0.581), GTF3C3 (0.571, general transcription factor IIIC subunit involved in RNA polymerase III transcription), C3orf14 (0.559), CCDC34 (0.549), CCDC77 (0.545), C9orf40 (0.510), NEMP1 (0.487, nuclear envelope membrane protein), CEP85 (0.468, centrosomal protein), and DEPDC1B (0.445, cell cycle regulator). The interaction with GTF3C3 suggests potential involvement in transcriptional machinery, while CEP85 and NEMP1 connections implicate roles in nuclear/centrosomal organization. ClinVar documents 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains spanning 2q31-36 regions, indicating dosage sensitivity. The combination of nuclear localization, testis-enriched expression, coiled-coil scaffolding architecture, and interactions with transcription factors and centrosomal proteins suggests CCDC150 functions as a nuclear scaffold organizing transcriptional complexes or chromatin architecture, potentially with specialized roles in germ cell development. Pathogenic CNVs disrupting CCDC150 may cause haploinsufficiency affecting nuclear organization and transcriptional programs, though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized.
