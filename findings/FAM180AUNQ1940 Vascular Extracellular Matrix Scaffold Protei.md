## Description
FAM180A (alias UNQ1940, family with sequence similarity 180 member A) encodes a 173 amino acid well-folded secreted protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 86.6) containing a FAM180 family domain (IPR029170, aa 3-170), FAM180 family Pfam domain (PF15173, aa 28-163), and belonging to the PTHR34034 family (protein FAM180A-related, aa 3-170). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in blood vessel (35.4 nTPM), gallbladder (15.0 nTPM), and heart muscle (14.9 nTPM), detected in many tissues distribution, vesicles subcellular localization, predicted secreted protein class, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell type. ClinVar contains 33 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 7q31.32-36.1, 7q31.31-33, 7q32.3-36.1, 7q32.1-34, and 7q33-36.3 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with extracellular matrix and vesicle trafficking proteins: SLC25A29 (solute carrier family 25 member 29, mitochondrial transporter, 0.517), ANKS1A (ankyrin repeat and SAM domain containing 1A, scaffold protein, 0.514), COL28A1 (collagen type XVIII alpha 1 chain, basement membrane, 0.510), KLHL35 (kelch-like family member 35, ubiquitin ligase substrate adaptor, 0.485), GLT6D1 (glycosyltransferase 6 domain 1, protein glycosylation, 0.479), TCP11 (t-complex protein 11, sperm antigen, 0.473), GCC2 (GRIP domain coiled-coil protein 2, Golgi vesicle tethering, 0.452), PI15 (peptidase inhibitor 15, protease regulation, 0.448), C17orf67 (chromosome 17 ORF 67, 0.437), and FANCE (Fanconi anemia complementation group E, DNA repair, 0.409). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The FAM180 domain architecture combined with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 86.6) and predicted secreted protein classification strongly suggests FAM180A functions as a vascular extracellular matrix scaffold protein involved in basement membrane organization and endothelial cell adhesion. COL28A1 interaction (0.510) is mechanistically central—COL28A1 is a transmembrane collagen localized to basement membranes and endothelial cell junctions, suggesting FAM180A functions as a collagen-binding protein stabilizing vascular basement membrane integrity. ANKS1A association (0.514) extends this role to scaffold function—ANKS1A contains ankyrin repeats and SAM domains mediating protein-protein interactions, placing FAM180A in multiprotein complexes at cell-matrix interfaces. GCC2 interaction (0.452) links to Golgi vesicle tethering—GCC2 regulates retrograde vesicle trafficking from endosomes to Golgi, suggesting FAM180A undergoes regulated secretion through the Golgi apparatus. GLT6D1 association (0.479) indicates potential glycosylation—GLT6D1 catalyzes protein glycosylation, suggesting FAM180A may be glycosylated for extracellular matrix incorporation. Blood vessel enrichment (35.4 nTPM) is striking—this represents the highest expression level among all tissues, indicating specialized function in vascular endothelial cells and vascular smooth muscle cells. Heart muscle expression (14.9 nTPM) suggests role in cardiac basement membrane and cardiomyocyte adhesion. Gallbladder expression (15.0 nTPM) indicates function in epithelial barrier maintenance. Vesicles subcellular localization indicates secretory vesicle residence prior to extracellular release, consistent with secreted protein classification. Low cancer specificity suggests housekeeping function rather than oncogenic role. Not detected in immune cells indicates restricted expression to structural cell lineages. Group-enriched single cell pattern indicates specific upregulation in endothelial and epithelial cell types. High pLDDT score (86.6) indicates stable folded structure with defined secondary and tertiary elements, consistent with secreted proteins requiring structural stability for extracellular matrix incorporation and protease resistance. Pathogenic 7q CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting vascular basement membrane organization, endothelial cell adhesion, and extracellular matrix integrity, potentially contributing to 7q deletion syndrome (7q- syndrome) phenotypes including growth retardation, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, and cardiovascular defects through impaired vascular development and basement membrane dysfunction.
