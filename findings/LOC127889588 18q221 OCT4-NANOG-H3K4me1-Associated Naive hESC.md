# LOC127889588: 18q22.1 OCT4-NANOG-H3K4me1-Associated Naive hESC Enhancer with No Clinical Variants — Core Pluripotency Regulatory Element

## Description
LOC127889588 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 18q22.1 (genomic coordinates: 76065085-76065584 on NC_000018.10, spanning approximately 499 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in naive human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it physically associates with OCT4 (POU5F1 - POU class 5 homeobox 1, master pluripotency transcription factor) and NANOG (homeobox protein NANOG, core pluripotency transcription factor) transcription factors and is marked by the H3K4me1 histone modification (histone H3 lysine 4 monomethylation — enhancer lineage mark). The OCT4-NANOG co-binding signature indicates direct regulation by the core pluripotency transcriptional network, distinguishing this element from other hESC enhancers lacking transcription factor ChIP evidence. A subregion was identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. This absence of ClinVar entries is consistent with other hESC enhancers in this series (LOC127894026: 0 variants; LOC127892927: 0 variants; LOC127892406: 0 variants; LOC127891630: 0 variants) and contrasts with silencers showing strong triplosensitivity or haploinsufficiency patterns. The 18q22 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including TSHZ1 (teashirt zinc finger homeobox 1 - hindbrain development, congenital aural atresia), MYO5B (myosin VB - vesicle trafficking, microvillus inclusion disease), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a naive hESC-specific enhancer bound by OCT4-NANOG and marked by H3K4me1, LOC127889588 likely functions as a direct target of the core pluripotency network to activate transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and naive pluripotency maintenance. The dual enhancer/silencer activity suggests context-dependent regulation, potentially activating pluripotency factors in naive stem cells while repressing differentiation genes in somatic lineages. The absence of ClinVar variants suggests either functional redundancy, essentiality (deleterious variants are embryonic lethal), or that this element regulates genes with buffered dosage sensitivity in post-embryonic development.
