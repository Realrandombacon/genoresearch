## Description
SNX7 (Sorting Nexin-7) is a 387-amino acid vesicle-associated trafficking protein (UniProt Q9UNH6) representing a genuine dark gene with only 29 publications despite its critical emerging roles regulating retromer-mediated endosomal trafficking, autophagy inhibition in prostate cancer via CFLIP activation, glioblastoma pathogenesis, hepatocellular carcinoma biomarker function, maternal exercise-programmed placental metabolism, and 1p21-22 copy number variation disorders. Structurally, SNX7 contains defining sorting nexin architecture combining membrane targeting and curvature sensing modules: N-terminal PX (Phox homology) domain (IPR001683/PF00787/PS50195/cd07284/IPR042130, residues 27-151/32-147/63-146/30-151) mediating phosphatidylinositol binding (GO:0035091) for endosomal membrane recruitment, C-terminal BAR (Bin/Amphiphysin/Rvs) domain (IPR027267/G3DSA:1.20.1270.60/cd07666/IPR042131, residues 131-373/179-376) sensing and inducing membrane curvature for tubule formation, PX domain superfamily (IPR036871/G3DSA:3.30.1520.10, residues 24-159) providing structural scaffold, and Sorting Nexin family (PTHR45949, residues 2-371) defining functional class within SNX subfamily. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: SNX7 mediating inhibition of autophagy in prostate cancer via activation of CFLIP expression demonstrating direct regulation of cell death pathways (1 citation, 2025); SNX7 as therapeutic target in glioblastoma pathogenesis linked to mitophagy dysregulation (0 citations, 2024); maternal exercise programming placental miR-495-5p-mediated Snx7 expression and kynurenic acid metabolic pathway induced by prenatal high-fat diet establishing transgenerational metabolic effects (4 citations, 2024); comprehensive analysis validating SNX7 as novel biomarker for diagnosis, prognosis, and prediction of chemotherapy and immunotherapy response in hepatocellular carcinoma (8 citations, 2023). The mechanistic hypothesis: SNX7 functions as a membrane-remodeling trafficking protein that recognizes phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate (PI3P) on early endosomes via its PX domain and induces membrane curvature via BAR domain dimerization to form retrieval tubules. The PX domain (residues 27-151) contains a conserved phosphoinositide-binding pocket coordinating PI3P headgroup through basic residues, recruiting SNX7 to endosomal membranes. The BAR domain (residues 131-373) forms a crescent-shaped dimer that scaffolds membrane curvature, promoting tubule extension for retrograde transport. In prostate cancer, SNX7 inhibits autophagy by activating CFLIP (cellular FLICE-like inhibitory protein), blocking caspase-8 activation and promoting tumor survival. In glioblastoma, SNX7 dysfunction impairs mitophagy leading to mitochondrial accumulation and tumor aggressiveness. In hepatocellular carcinoma, SNX7 expression levels predict chemotherapy and immunotherapy response, potentially through altered growth factor receptor trafficking (EGFR, MET). The retromer interactions (VPS29, VPS26A/B, VPS35) suggest SNX7 participates in cargo retrieval (Wntless, CI-M6PR, SorLA) from endosomes to Golgi. The BLOC1S2 connection links SNX7 to lysosome-related organelle biogenesis. The SNX4/SNX6/SNX32 paralogs indicate functional redundancy or specialization within sorting nexin subfamilies. The maternal exercise-miR-495-5p-SNX7 axis reveals epigenetic programming: exercise increases miR-495-5p suppressing Snx7, altering placental kynurenic acid metabolism and protecting offspring from high-fat diet-induced metabolic disease. The 1p21-22 CNV dosage changes alter SNX7 expression: deletion reduces retromer function causing cargo mistrafficking (growth factor receptor degradation defects, lysosomal enzyme mis-sorting); duplication increases endosomal retrieval potentially enhancing oncogenic signaling. The vesicle/cell junction localization suggests SNX7 regulates epithelial polarity and barrier function. The absence of formal disease associations despite 9 pathogenic variants suggests SNX7 dysfunction contributes to neurodevelopmental disorders (retromer defects link to Parkinson's, Alzheimer's), cancer susceptibility (prostate, liver, brain), metabolic syndrome, or congenital disorders of endosomal trafficking not yet mapped to Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define SNX7's phosphoinositide specificity via lipid binding assays with PI3P, PI(3,5)P2, other phosphoinositides, determine crystal structure of SNX7 PX-BAR tandem alone and in complex with membranes to identify autoinhibitory interfaces, investigate SNX7 mutations in cancer patients with altered autophagy markers, assess SNX7 role in placental nutrient transport using knockout models, develop SNX7 inhibitors for blocking retromer-dependent oncogenic trafficking, explore SNX7 biomarker potential in hepatocellular carcinoma treatment stratification, and test whether SNX7 levels predict response to autophagy inhibitors in prostate cancer.
