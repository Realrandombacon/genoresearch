## Description
STARD4 encodes StAR-Related Lipid Transfer Protein 4 (UniProt Q96DR4), a 205-amino acid lipid transfer protein essential for intracellular cholesterol transport and lipid homeostasis. The protein contains a conserved START domain (IPR002913, positions 1-205; PF01852, positions 18-204; SM00234, positions 9-205; PS50848, positions 1-205) classified within the START-like domain superfamily (IPR023393, positions 8-205; G3DSA:3.30.530.20, positions 8-205; SSF55961, positions 9-204) and StAR-related lipid transfer protein 4 family (IPR042555, positions 1-205; PTHR47006, positions 1-205) with annotated lipid binding (GO:0008289), cholesterol binding (GO:0015485), and intracellular cholesterol transport (GO:0032367) functions, indicating essential role in non-vesicular sterol trafficking between intracellular membranes. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 95.9, residues 1-205) indicating extremely stable folding with well-defined START domain architecture featuring a curved beta-sheet backbone forming a hydrophobic pocket suitable for cholesterol/sterol binding and membrane extraction/insertion.
