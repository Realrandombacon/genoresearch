## Description
LOC100996637 is an uncharacterized genetic locus on chromosome 2p25.3 (NC_000002.12, positions 495812-496635, ~824 bp region) with substantial clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases, and Human Protein Atlas found no expression data (ENSG00000284452). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 2p25.3 region (variants include 4526718, 160975, 155615, 154817, 154668, 153520, 153475, 153441, 152888, 152693). These CNVs range from focal deletions to multi-megabase duplications (30 kb to 46 Mb). The 2p25.3 chromosomal location is clinically notable as this region contains MYCN (N-myc proto-oncogene), whose amplification drives neuroblastoma pathogenesis. Functional hypothesis: LOC100996637 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary controlling MYCN expression or neighboring genes in the 2p25.3 amplicon. The 25 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations disrupt normal transcriptional regulation. Disease mechanism likely involves MYCN dysregulation through enhancer amplification, deletion of repressive elements, or chromatin topology disruption. The absence of protein-coding features combined with strong ClinVar evidence and oncogenic neighborhood suggests this locus functions in cancer predisposition through position effects on MYCN or adjacent oncogenes/tumor suppressors.
