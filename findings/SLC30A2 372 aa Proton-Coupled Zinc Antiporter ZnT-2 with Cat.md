## Description
SLC30A2 (Q9BRI3, 372 aa), also known as ZnT-2 (Zinc Transporter 2), is a proton-coupled zinc antiporter belonging to solute carrier family 30 (CDF/ZnT family) within the Cation Diffusion Facilitator (CDF) superfamily. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 77.9, residues 1-372) consistent with 6-transmembrane helix topology with cytoplasmic N- and C-termini typical of ZnT family transporters. HPA shows pancreas-enriched expression (79.4 nTPM, tissue enriched); localized to vesicles; classified as disease-related gene, potential drug target, and transporter involved in zinc transport, antiport, and ion transport. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants including single nucleotide variants (p.Arg165Gln, p.Arg218Ter), frameshift deletions (p.Phe221fs, p.Thr177fs), duplications, and copy number gains (chr1:99125-34026935, chr1:849467-249224684, chr1:24381206-41401517), though no specific monogenic diseases are yet assigned. Functional hypothesis: SLC30A2/ZnT-2 functions as a vesicular zinc exporter that transports cytoplasmic Zn2+ into intracellular vesicles (secretory granules, lysosomes, milk fat globules) via proton antiport mechanism (Zn2+/H+ exchange). The 6-TM transmembrane domain forms the zinc transport pore with conserved metal-binding residues, while the cytoplasmic C-terminal domain (aa 282-357) mediates protein-protein interactions and regulatory functions. Pancreas enrichment (79.4 nTPM) reflects critical role in zinc sequestration into insulin secretory granules of beta cells, where zinc coordinates insulin hexamer formation and storage. Vesicular localization confirms function in intracellular zinc compartmentalization. Strong SLC39A7 interaction (0.739) indicates coordination with ER/Golgi zinc importer ZIP7 for zinc flux between compartments. Multiple SLC39 family interactions (ZIP1, ZIP4, ZIP10, ZIP12, ZIP14) suggest systemic zinc homeostasis network where ZnT-2 export balances ZIP import. SLC30A9 interaction (0.582) indicates functional redundancy with other ZnT exporters. Pathogenic variants disrupt zinc transport causing transient neonatal zinc deficiency in breastfed infants (maternal ZnT-2 deficiency reduces milk zinc), pancreatic dysfunction, and potential neurodegeneration. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 371 aa, rat 359 aa, bovine 375 aa) confirms essential zinc homeostasis role. NOTE: This gene is well-characterized as ZnT-2 with established role in zinc secretion into breast milk and pancreatic function.
