## Description
AMN1 (Antagonist of Mitotic Exit Network 1 Homolog, UniProt Q8IY45) is a 258 aa centrosome-localized intracellular protein belonging to the F-box/Leucine-Rich Repeat (LRR) protein family that functions as a potential substrate recognition component of SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes, regulating cell cycle exit, centrosome dynamics, and immune cell function. InterPro analysis reveals characteristic LRR architecture: Antagonist of mitotic exit network protein 1 domain (cd09293, positions 37-257), Leucine-rich repeat cysteine-containing subtype (IPR006553/SM00367, positions 60-84, 114-139, 140-165 - three LRR motifs), Leucine-rich repeat domain superfamily (IPR032675/G3DSA:3.80.10.10, positions 5-254), F-box/LRR-repeat protein 15-like LRR domain (IPR057207, positions 51-242; PF25372, positions 51-242), and F-box/Leucine-Rich Repeat family (PTHR13318, positions 48-241). The Ribonuclease Inhibitor-like superfamily (G3DSA:3.80.10.10/SSF52047, positions 5-254/61-242) indicates a curved solenoid structure typical of LRR proteins for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.2), confirming a well-folded LRR solenoid suitable for substrate binding. HPA data confirms tissue-enhanced expression with testis enrichment (52.9 nTPM), centrosome subcellular localization, detected in all tissues, immune cell enhanced classification. STRING interactions reveal connections to ubiquitin-proteasome and trafficking machinery: TRIM75P (0.634, Tripartite Motif Containing 75 Pseudogene), FBXO33 (0.510, F-Box Protein 33 - another F-box protein), SKP1 (0.480, S-Phase Kinase Associated Protein 1 - core SCF complex component), TFF3 (0.463, Trefoil Factor 3), PSMF1 (0.447, Proteasome Inhibitor), IFT81 (0.424, Intraflagellar Transport Protein 81 - ciliary/centrosomal), ORAI2 (0.418) and ORAI3 (0.418) (calcium channel subunits), PEX19 (0.407, Peroxin 19 - peroxisomal trafficking). ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 12p13.33-11.1 copy number gains (chr12:64621-34650483x3, chr12:82453-49847230x3, chr12:173787-34835837x3/x4), duplications, and single allele variants. Literature (62 papers) is predominantly yeast-focused: AMN1 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae governs post-mitotic cell separation (2018, 24 citations), downregulates Tem1 GTPase after cytokinesis via nuclear import and SCF-mediated degradation (2021), and directed evolution studies show selection to deactivate AMN1 in haploids (2026). Human AMN1 homolog remains poorly characterized despite conserved domain architecture. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: Human AMN1 serves as an F-box/LRR substrate recognition component of SCF E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes that targets cell cycle regulators for proteasomal degradation during mitotic exit, analogous to yeast AMN1 antagonism of the Mitotic Exit Network (MEN). The LRR domain (positions 51-242) recognizes specific phosphorylated substrates, while the N-terminal region potentially interacts with SKP1 (STRING score 0.480) to assemble the SCF complex. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The three cysteine-containing LRR motifs (positions 60-84, 114-139, 140-165) form a curved solenoid structure that binds phosphorylated degron motifs on target proteins. AMN1 localizes to centrosomes (HPA), positioning it to regulate centrosome duplication/separation and spindle dynamics. Interaction with SKP1 suggests AMN1 functions as an F-box protein adaptor, recruiting substrates to the CUL1-RBX1 catalytic core for ubiquitination. Potential substrates include Tem1-like GTPases (conserved from yeast), cyclins, or centrosomal regulators. ORAI2/3 interactions suggest calcium signaling coupling, potentially linking cell cycle exit to calcium-dependent processes. IFT81 interaction connects AMN1 to ciliary/centrosomal trafficking. The 32 ClinVar CNVs at 12p suggest AMN1 dosage sensitivity contributes to developmental disorders through disrupted cell cycle regulation. Very high AlphaFold confidence (91.2) supports stable LRR structure for drug targeting. AMN1 represents an understudied human homolog of a well-characterized yeast cell cycle regulator with emerging roles in centrosome biology and immune cell function.
