## Description
B4GALNT3 (Beta-1,4-N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 3, UniProt Q6L9W6) is a 998-amino acid Golgi-resident glycosyltransferase that synthesizes the LacdiNAc (GalNAcβ1-4GlcNAc) glycan structure on N- and O-glycans. Unlike typical glycosyltransferases, B4GALNT3 contains a unique PA14 lectin domain (IPR011658, PF07691, positions 117-278) that binds sulfated glycan ligands, enabling substrate recognition and specificity. The catalytic domain (IPR008428, PF05679, positions 668-986) belongs to the chondroitin N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase family within the nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily (IPR029044). B4GALNT3 shows stomach-enriched expression (HPA: 57.6 nTPM in stomach, 22.4 nTPM in heart muscle) with Golgi apparatus localization. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with glycosylation enzymes: CHST9 (0.935), FUT8 (0.932), B4GALT2 (0.924), B4GALT3 (0.921), B4GALT1 (0.921), CHST8 (0.909), and B4GALNT4 (0.909). Mechanistically, B4GALNT3 regulates sclerostin (SOST) glycosylation, directly impacting bone mineral density and fracture risk - GWAS signals link B4GALNT3 variants to circulating sclerostin levels. The WNK1-B4GALNT3 fusion occurs in papillary thyroid carcinoma (61 citations). ClinVar documents 68 pathogenic variants (mostly 12p CNVs). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 71.2). With only 34 literature references despite clear roles in bone metabolism, cancer, and glycan biology, B4GALNT3 represents an underexplored glycosyltransferase with therapeutic potential in osteoporosis and oncology.
