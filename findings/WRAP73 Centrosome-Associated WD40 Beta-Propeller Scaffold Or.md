## Description

(2) Ciliary vesicle docking—WRAP73 localizes to the distal end of mother centrioles where it facilitates docking of ciliary vesicles, the membrane source for ciliary axoneme extension. (3) Centriole stabilization—interaction with CEP135 and SPICE1 stabilizes the centriolar cartwheel structure essential for nine-fold symmetry. (4) Flagellar assembly—mid piece/principal piece localization indicates roles in sperm flagellum formation, explaining potential male infertility phenotypes. Mechanistically, the seven-bladed beta-propeller creates multiple interaction surfaces for simultaneous binding to vesicle trafficking machinery (SSX2IP), extracellular matrix components (MEGF6), and centriole structural proteins (CEP135/SPICE1). Loss-of-function causes Microspherophakia through defective lens fiber cell cilia during development; broader ciliopathy phenotypes may include retinal degeneration, renal cysts, and skeletal abnormalities typical of ciliary dysfunction. The antisense relationship to TP73 (tumor protein p73) suggests potential regulatory cross-talk between ciliogenesis and p53-family tumor suppression pathways.
