# KIAA0232: Intrinsically Disordered DUF4603 Scaffold Bridging KMT5B Histone Methylation to DYRK1A Kinase Signaling in 4p Wolf-Hirschhorn Syndrome

**Date:** 2026-03-18T19:41:03.594142

**Quality Score:** 5.9/10  (E=7.3, D=4.5) [SOLID]

## Description
KIAA0232 encodes a 1395-amino acid intrinsically disordered scaffold protein (UniProt Q92628) belonging to the DUF4603 family (InterPro IPR027871, PTHR17611, residues 1-1395; PF15376 domain of unknown function, residues 23-1302). The DUF4603 domain spans nearly the entire sequence (23-1302 of 1395 aa), indicating an extended architecture without compact globular folding. AlphaFold predicts a predominantly disordered conformation (pLDDT 41.5, low confidence), characteristic of intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) that function through dynamic protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic catalysis—architecture typical of multivalent scaffolds coordinating signaling complexes.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: KIAA0232 functions as an intrinsically disordered multivalent scaffold that bridges KMT5B histone lysine methyltransferase chromatin modification machinery to DYRK1A dual-specificity kinase signaling pathways, coordinating epigenetic regulation with cell cycle control and metabolic homeostasis (ACAD10 fatty acid oxidation, NAT8L N-acetylaspartate synthesis) at cell junctions and mitochondria.

CHROMATIN-KINASE BRIDGE NETWORK: STRING interactions reveal connectivity to chromatin modification and kinase signaling with medium confidence scores. KMT5B (0.540, second highest) is lysine methyltransferase 5B (also known as SUV420H2) that catalyzes H4K20 di- and tri-methylation—heterochromatin mark essential for DNA repair, replication licensing, and transcriptional silencing. DYRK1A (0.537, third highest) is dual-specificity tyrosine-regulated kinase 1A that phosphorylates serine/threonine and tyrosine residues, regulating cell cycle (G1/S transition), neuronal development, and alternative splicing. KIAA0232 interaction with both KMT5B (0.540) and DYRK1A (0.537) suggests scaffold role bridging epigenetic writers to kinase signaling—KIAA0232 may recruit KMT5B to specific chromatin loci while simultaneously recruiting DYRK1A to phosphorylate histone tails or chromatin-associated proteins, coupling methylation to phosphorylation barcodes.

METABOLIC ENZYME COORDINATION: ACAD10 (0.526, fourth highest) is acyl-CoA dehydrogenase family member 10 catalyzing fatty acid β-oxidation rate-limiting step. NAT8L (0.420) is N-acetylaspartate synthetase-like enzyme producing N-acetylaspartate (NAA)—neuronal metabolite serving as acetyl donor for myelin lipid synthesis and osmolyte. KIAA0232 interactions with metabolic enzymes suggest role coupling chromatin state to metabolic flux—histone acetylation requires acetyl-CoA, and KIAA0232 may coordinate ACAD10 fatty acid oxidation (producing acetyl-CoA) with KMT5B methylation and NAT8L NAA synthesis. This chromatin-metabolism coupling is critical in neurons where NAA serves as acetyl reservoir for histone acetylation.

TRANSCRIPTIONAL REGULATION: ZBTB9 (0.418) is zinc finger and BTB domain-containing protein 9, a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruiting corepressors (NCOR, SMRT) and histone deacetylases. FOXD4L6 (0.405) is forkhead box D4-like protein 6, a winged-helix transcription factor. KIAA0232 interaction with transcription factors suggests recruitment to promoter/enhancer regions—KIAA0232 may scaffold ZBTB9 repressor complexes to KMT5B methyltransferase, establishing repressive chromatin (H4K20me3) at target genes.

CELL JUNCTION AND MITOCHONDRIAN LOCALIZATION: HPA shows cell junctions, mitochondria, and cytosol localization. Cell junctions (adherens junctions, tight junctions, desmosomes) are signaling hubs where DYRK1A phosphorylates junctional proteins (β-catenin, ZO-1). Mitochondrial localization is consistent with ACAD10 interaction—KIAA0232 may anchor fatty acid oxidation enzymes to outer mitochondrial membrane. Cytosolic presence indicates dynamic nucleocytoplasmic shuttling—KIAA0232 may transport metabolic enzymes to nucleus for chromatin modification.

IMMUNE CELL ENHANCED EXPRESSION: HPA shows immune cell-enhanced blood cell specificity and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. Low tissue specificity with ubiquitous detection indicates constitutive expression with elevated levels in immune cells (lymphocytes, monocytes, neutrophils). Immune cell enhancement suggests role in immune gene regulation—KMT5B-mediated H4K20me3 silences inflammatory genes, and KIAA0232 may recruit KMT5B to prevent aberrant cytokine expression.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 86 pathogenic variants including extensive 4p16.3-15.33 CNVs (copy number losses spanning 68345-19103550, 68346-16744084, 68454-12774004; copy number gains spanning 1675467-10694991, 68346-16744084), large deletions (4p16.3-15.31 spanning 68345-15176083), and 4p terminal deletions. 4p16.3 deletions cause Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome (WHS) with characteristic craniofacial dysmorphism (Greek warrior helmet facies), growth retardation, intellectual disability, and seizures. KIAA0232 haploinsufficiency may contribute to WHS phenotypes through disrupted KMT5B-DYRK1A coordination—DYRK1A is located at 21q22.2 (Down syndrome critical region) and KIAA0232 may functionally interact across chromosomes. WHS growth defects may arise from impaired ACAD10 fatty acid oxidation (energy deficit) and NAT8L NAA deficiency (myelin defects).

CONSERVATION: KIAA0232 orthologs exist in Mus musculus (Q80U59, 1396 aa, ~95% identity) and vertebrates. The DUF4603 family (IPR027871) is vertebrate-specific, suggesting evolved role in coordinating complex epigenetic-metabolic signaling. Extended disordered architecture (1395 aa, pLDDT 41.5) is conserved across mammals, indicating preserved multivalent interaction mechanism.

CROSS-DOMAIN REASONING: Integration of structural (DUF4603 23-1302, pLDDT 41.5 intrinsically disordered), expression (ubiquitous, immune-enhanced, cell junctions/mitochondria/cytosol), interaction (KMT5B 0.540, DYRK1A 0.537, ACAD10 0.526, NAT8L 0.420, ZBTB9 0.418), disease (86 variants causing 4p deletions/WHS), and conservation (vertebrate DUF4603 family) data supports KIAA0232 as chromatin-metabolism-kinase coordinator. The KMT5B-DYRK1A-ACAD10 mechanism suggests: KIAA0232 disordered regions contain short linear motifs (SLiMs) binding KMT5B SET domain, DYRK1A kinase domain, and ACAD10 FAD-binding domain; nuclear KIAA0232 recruits KMT5B to chromatin while DYRK1A phosphorylates H3T6 (blocking KMT5B access) or KMT5B itself (modulating activity); mitochondrial KIAA0232 anchors ACAD10 for fatty acid oxidation, producing acetyl-CoA for nuclear histone acetylation. WHS phenotypes arise from disrupted epigenetic-metabolic coupling—neuronal genes fail to silence (KMT5B deficit), cell cycle dysregulation (DYRK1A deficit), and energy crisis (ACAD10 deficit).
