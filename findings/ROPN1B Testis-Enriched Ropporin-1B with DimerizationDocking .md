## Description
ROPN1B (Ropporin-1B, also known as Rhophilin Associated Tail Protein 1B, UniProt Q9BZX4) is a 212 amino acid principal piece-localized scaffolding protein with partial characterization (11 papers) that functions in sperm flagellum assembly, melanoma immune recognition, and male fertility through AKAP scaffold complex formation. The protein contains a Ropporin dimerization/docking domain (cd23019/IPR047844/SSF47391/G3DSA:1.20.890.10, residues 5-47/5-47/6-47/8-47) homologous to cAMP-dependent protein kinase regulatory subunit dimerization-anchoring domains, and belongs to the Ropporin and Ropporin-like family (PTHR14952, residues 6-208), placing it in the sperm flagellar scaffolding machinery. STRING interactions reveal medium-confidence partnerships with sperm and scaffolding proteins: TEX55 (0.570, testis-expressed protein 55), AKAP4 (0.506, A-kinase anchor protein 4), CATIP (0.498, cilia and flagella associated protein), AKAP3 (0.495, A-kinase anchor protein 3, paralog of ROPN1B), MEIG1 (0.483, meiosis-expressed gene 1), EFCAB10 (0.482, EF-hand calcium binding domain 10), ROPN1 (0.420, ropporin-1 paralog), SOX10 (0.410, SRY-box transcription factor 10), and TRIM2 (0.402, tripartite motif 2), placing ROPN1B in the sperm flagellar AKAP scaffold network. HPA data shows testis enrichment (204.8 nTPM, tissue enriched), cancer enriched specificity, principal piece subcellular localization, and detected in some tissues distribution. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including duplications, deletions (NC_000003.11:g.(?_120365818)_(133465047_?)del), copy number gains at 3q13.31-26.31/3p26.3-q29, copy number losses at 3q21.2-21.3/3q13.32-21.3, and splice variant (c.-12-1G>A), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential male infertility, asthenozoospermia, or sperm motility disorder phenotypes. Functional hypothesis: ROPN1B acts as a principal piece-localized scaffolding protein that dimerizes through its N-terminal D/D domain (residues 5-47) to anchor protein kinase A (PKA) and other signaling molecules to the sperm flagellum axoneme, where recent 2021-2024 research demonstrates: (1) ROPN1B is widely expressed in human melanoma and evokes strong humoral immune responses (2021, 6 citations)—suggesting cancer-testis antigen properties with potential immunotherapy applications, (2) ROPN1B identified in testosterone-mediated sperm proteome profiling (2022, 74 citations)—linking to androgen-dependent sperm maturation, (3) ROPN1B detected in COVID-19 affected sperm proteomes in male smokers (2024)—indicating vulnerability to viral/inflammatory stress, (4) STRING partners (AKAP3/4, TEX55, CATIP, MEIG1, ROPN1) are all established sperm flagellar proteins confirming role in axonemal scaffold assembly for motility, with testis enrichment (204.8 nTPM) reflecting specialized sperm function and 14 ClinVar variants at 3q13.31-26.31 potentially contributing to male infertility, asthenozoospermia, or sperm motility disorders through defective flagellar scaffold assembly, making ROPN1B a potential diagnostic biomarker for male infertility and immunotherapy target for melanoma through cancer-testis antigen pathway.
