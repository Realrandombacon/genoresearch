## Description
SPATA9 encodes a 254 amino acid spermatogenesis-associated protein localized to mitochondria with testis-enriched expression (23.0 nTPM). The protein belongs to the SPATA9 family (IPR031659, PF15824, PTHR35669) spanning residues 1-252, representing a conserved spermatogenesis-specific domain architecture. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 58.1), suggesting a partially structured protein with potential intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with mitochondrial membrane association. STRING interactions reveal functional associations with CCDC38 (0.626), LRMDA (0.581), ARMC2 (0.572), SPATC1 (0.514), and SPATA46 (0.497) - multiple partners are spermatogenesis-related factors including SPATC1 (spermatogenesis and centriole associated) and SPATA46, suggesting participation in a testis-specific protein complex. HPA data confirms mitochondrial localization and testis-enriched RNA expression, supporting a role in spermatogenic cell differentiation and energy metabolism. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations (deletions and duplications) spanning 5q14.3-23.3, indicating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts spermatogenesis. The convergence of testis-specific expression, mitochondrial localization, spermatogenesis-related interaction partners, and disease-associated CNVs supports a model where SPATA9 functions as a mitochondrial membrane factor essential for spermatogenic differentiation, potentially regulating energy production or apoptotic signaling during germ cell development, with pathogenic CNVs causing male infertility through disrupted mitochondrial function.
