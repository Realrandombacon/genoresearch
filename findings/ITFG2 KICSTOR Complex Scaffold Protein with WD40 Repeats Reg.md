## Description
ITFG2 encodes a 447-amino acid KICSTOR complex protein ITFG2 (UniProt Q969R8), also known as Integrin alpha FG-GAP repeat-containing protein 2, a well-folded scaffold protein functioning as an essential component of the KICSTOR (KPTN-ITFG2-C12orf66-SZT2) complex that anchors the GATOR1 complex to lysosomes for mTORC1 regulation. The protein contains an N-terminal integrin alpha N-terminal domain (IPR028994, positions 2-156; G3DSA:2.130.10.130, positions 2-50 and 85-156; SSF69318, positions 11-148) characteristic of FG-GAP repeat proteins, plus extensive WD40-repeat architecture (IPR036322, positions 34-381; SSF50978, positions 34-175, 204-216, 275-381) forming a beta-propeller scaffold for protein-protein interactions, classified within the ITFG2 family (IPR031793, positions 1-446; PF15907 FG-GAP repeat, positions 49-386; PTHR16317, positions 1-446). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 86.4, residues 1-447) indicating stable folding with well-defined WD40 beta-propeller suitable for multi-protein complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis reveals ITFG2 forms the core KICSTOR complex with ultra-high confidence interactions: DEPDC5 (0.999, GATOR1 component), SZT2 (0.999, KICSTOR scaffold), NPRL2 (0.999, GATOR1 component), C12orf66/KICS2 (0.999, KICSTOR subunit), KPTN (0.999, KICSTOR subunit), WDR24 (0.998, GATOR1 regulator), WDR59 (0.998, GATOR1 regulator), plus LAMP1 (0.994, lysosomal membrane protein), BMT2 (0.982), and NPRL3 (0.873, GATOR1 component), confirming ITFG2's role as a critical bridge between KICSTOR and GATOR1 complexes for amino acid sensing and mTORC1 inhibition. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), Golgi apparatus and vesicles subcellular localization consistent with lysosomal trafficking and mTORC1 signaling platform assembly, annotated for predicted intracellular proteins class. ClinVar contains 68 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (12p13.33-11.1, 12p13.33-q13.12, 12p13.33-13.2), copy number losses (12p13.33-13.31), and splice site variants (c.848-1G>A), with biallelic pathogenic variants causing syndromic megalencephalic neurodevelopmental syndrome (2023). With only 20 publications, ITFG2 has emerging roles in cardiac and neurodevelopmental pathology: ITFG2 targets ATP5b (mitochondrial ATP synthase beta subunit) to regulate ATP energy metabolism and maintain mitochondrial function in myocardial infarction (2024, 4 cit), ITFG2 acts as NEDD4-2 inhibitor preserving calcium homeostasis to prevent myocardial ischemic injury (2024, 1 cit), ITFG2 modulates T-cell immune response and negatively regulates mTORC1 signaling (2024), ITFG2 specifically targets ATP5b to regulate ATP metabolism and maintain mitochondrial membrane potential in ischemic ventricular arrhythmias (2025, 1 cit), lncRNA ITFG2-AS1 elevated in gastric cancer patients as diagnostic biomarker (2025). The functional hypothesis: ITFG2 serves as a structural scaffold within the KICSTOR complex through its WD40-repeat beta-propeller domain that provides multiple protein-binding surfaces for simultaneous interaction with KPTN, C12orf66, and SZT2, while the N-terminal integrin alpha FG-GAP repeat domain may mediate lysosomal membrane association or cargo recognition; ultra-high confidence interactions with all GATOR1 components (DEPDC5, NPRL2, NPRL3) and regulators (WDR24, WDR59) indicate ITFG2-KICSTOR recruits GATOR1 to lysosomal surfaces where it acts as a GAP (GTPase-activating protein) toward RagA/B GTPases, inhibiting mTORC1 activation under amino acid starvation; Golgi/vesicles localization suggests ITFG2 participates in lysosomal trafficking and positioning required for mTORC1 signaling platform assembly; ATP5b targeting reveals a non-canonical mitochondrial function where ITFG2 may shuttle between lysosomes and mitochondria to coordinate nutrient sensing with ATP production, explaining cardiac vulnerability when ITFG2 is disrupted; NEDD4-2 inhibition suggests ITFG2 prevents ubiquitin-mediated degradation of calcium channels or transporters, preserving calcium homeostasis during ischemic stress; megalencephalic neurodevelopmental syndrome indicates ITFG2 dosage affects brain growth through mTORC1-dependent neural progenitor proliferation and differentiation; 12p CNVs may disrupt ITFG2 dosage causing mTORC1 hyperactivation and metabolic dysregulation affecting multiple organ systems; high structural confidence (pLDDT 86.4) indicates stable WD40 beta-propeller suitable for protein-protein interaction mapping and potential small molecule disruption of KICSTOR-GATOR1 assembly.
