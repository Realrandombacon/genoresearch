## Description
ELMOD3 (ELMO domain-containing protein 3, UniProt Q96FG2) is a 381 aa GTPase-activating protein featuring a conserved ELMO domain (IPR006816, positions 157-324; PF04727) characteristic of the engulfment and cell motility family (IPR050868). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 75.8). HPA classifies it as disease-related with ubiquitous expression and GTPase activation function. STRING reveals 10 interaction partners including Arf-family GTPase regulator ARL2 (0.549), related family members ELMO1 (0.494), ELMO2 (0.499), ELMO3 (0.514), ELMOD1 (0.444), plus SH2D6 (0.697), TGOLN2 (0.556), UBOX5 (0.532), RASA1 (0.509), and SNX15 (0.437), positioning ELMOD3 within Arf GTPase regulatory networks. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutation p.Gln371Ter, missense variants p.Gly141Arg/p.Arg66His/p.Glu376Lys/p.Thr113Asn, splice variant c.739-2A>C, and multiple copy number alterations at 2p11.2. Literature (19 papers) reveals: (1) causative role in autosomal dominant/recessive non-syndromic hearing loss (DFNA81/DFNB88) with localization to stereocilia, kinocilia, and cuticular plate of developing cochlear hair cells; (2) regulation of gastric cancer growth/migration via β-catenin signaling and F-actin cytoskeleton disruption (2024); (3) ELMOD3-Rab1A-Flotillin2 cascade controlling lumen formation (2023); (4) ELMOD3-ALK fusion responding to alectinib in lung adenocarcinoma (2025); (5) iPSC models with CRISPR-corrected c.512A>G mutation for studying inherited hearing loss mechanisms. Mechanistically, ELMOD3 likely functions as an Arf GAP coordinating cytoskeletal remodeling in sensory hair cells through Arf GTPase cycling, with loss-of-function causing progressive hearing impairment via defective stereocilia maintenance.
