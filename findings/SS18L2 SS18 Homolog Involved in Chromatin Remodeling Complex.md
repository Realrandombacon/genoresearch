## Description
SS18L2 (SS18-like protein 2, UniProt Q9UHA2) is a 77-amino acid nuclear protein located on chromosome 3p22.1. Despite being annotated as 'uncharacterized,' this gene shows strong homology to SS18, a core component of BAF/SWI-SNF chromatin remodeling complexes. The protein contains a conserved SS18 N-terminal domain (IPR007726, positions 13-72) and SSXT protein family domain (PF05030), suggesting it functions as a scaffold protein within multiprotein chromatin regulatory assemblies.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals high-confidence connections to SSX family proteins (SSX4B: 0.750, SSX2B: 0.750, SSX4: 0.720, SSX1: 0.668, SSX2: 0.638) and chromatin regulators including SMARCA4 (0.632), DPF2 (0.642), YY1AP1 (0.625), and GON4L (0.618). This interaction profile strongly suggests SS18L2 participates in chromatin remodeling and transcriptional regulation, potentially as an alternative or regulatory subunit of BAF complexes.\n\nHPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and mitochondria. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 74.8), supporting stable protein function. ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, primarily copy number variations affecting 3p22.1, indicating clinical relevance though specific disease associations remain undefined.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: SS18L2 functions as a regulatory paralog of SS18 in BAF/SWI-SNF chromatin remodeling complexes. Its interaction with SSX proteins (the fusion partners in synovial sarcoma t(X;18) translocations) suggests it may modulate SS18-SSX oncogenic activity or serve as a tumor suppressor. The small size (77 aa) and conserved N-terminal domain indicate it may act as a competitive inhibitor or allosteric regulator of SS18-containing complexes, potentially influencing cell fate decisions and contributing to sarcoma pathogenesis when dysregulated.
