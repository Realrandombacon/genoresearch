## Description
GIMAP1 (GTPase of Immunity-Associated Protein Family Member 1) is a partially characterized lymphocyte survival factor with 30 publications, representing a critical regulator of adaptive immune homeostasis. This 306-amino acid protein (UniProt Q8WWP7) features a conserved AIG1-type GTPase domain architecture optimized for nucleotide-dependent conformational switching and protein-protein interactions at the endoplasmic reticulum. InterPro analysis reveals an AIG1-type guanine nucleotide-binding (G) domain (IPR006703/PF04548, positions 25-229) characteristic of the immunity-associated GTPase superfamily, membership in the P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (IPR027417/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, positions 24-253), and the GTPase GIMA/IAN/Toc family (IPR045058/PTHR10903, positions 29-234) with GO annotations for GTP binding (GO:0005525). AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 87.1), indicating a well-folded GTPase core with stable switch regions essential for regulatory function.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm GIMAP1 operates within the GIMAP family cluster with high confidence scores: GIMAP8 (0.893, GTPase IMAP family member 8), GIMAP7 (0.833), GIMAP6 (0.827), GIMAP4 (0.818), GIMAP5 (0.710), CLEC10A (0.540, C-type lectin domain family 10 member A), AIG1 (0.532, Androgen-induced gene 1 protein/paralog), GIMAP2 (0.456), FLI1 (0.446, Friend leukemia integration 1 transcription factor), and FYB1 (0.425, FYN-binding protein 1/ADAP). These interactions (0.425-0.893) validate GIMAP1 as part of the GIMAP gene cluster on chromosome 7q36.1, functioning cooperatively in lymphocyte regulation.\n\nHPA shows tissue-enhanced specificity with top expression in lymphoid tissue (23.3 nTPM), detected in many tissues but enriched in immune compartments. Subcellular localization is explicitly the Endoplasmic Reticulum, positioning GIMAP1 at the site of protein folding and calcium signaling critical for lymphocyte activation. Protein class includes predicted membrane proteins. Single cell type specificity indicates cell type enrichment, consistent with lineage-specific function. ClinVar contains 73 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations on chromosome 7q including losses (chr7:137521595-159119707 x1, chr7:142941022-156102711 x1, chr7:142961178-155604817 x1, chr7:147773827-159119707 x1, chr7:142491993-159119707 del) and gains/duplications (chr7:142491993-159119707 x3, chr7:141690279-159119707 x3, chr7:137456457-159119707 x3, chr7:150324807-150706375 dup). While no specific monogenic disease is yet named, 7q deletions are associated with immunodeficiency and developmental disorders. Conservation across vertebrates (Mouse Gimap1 277 aa) indicates strong functional constraint on the G domain.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: GIMAP1 functions as an ER-localized GTPase that acts as a molecular switch controlling lymphocyte survival thresholds through TMX1 interaction and B/T cell homeostasis maintenance. Mechanistically: (1) The AIG1-type G domain (positions 25-229) cycles between GTP/GDP states to regulate conformational exposure of effector interfaces. (2) High-confidence interactions with GIMAP4/5/6/7/8 (0.710-0.893) suggest homo/hetero-oligomerization forming a functional complex at the ER membrane, potentially coordinating survival signals. (3) In mature T cells (2016 landmark 22 citations), GIMAP1 is intrinsically required for peripheral survival—loss leads to rapid apoptosis independent of external stimuli. (4) In B cells (2015 study 34 citations), GIMAP1 is essential for both naïve and activated B cell survival in vivo, linking it to humoral immunity maintenance. (5) In lung adenocarcinoma (2025 breakthrough 2 citations), GIMAP1 interacts with TMX1 (thioredoxin-related transmembrane protein 1) to improve prognosis by modulating the tumor immune microenvironment, likely enhancing cytotoxic T cell infiltration or function. (6) AIG1 paralog connection (0.532) suggests functional redundancy or divergence within the ancient AIG1 GTPase family involved in stress responses. (7) FLI1/FYB1 interactions (0.425-0.446) link GIMAP1 to T cell receptor signaling adapters and transcriptional regulation, potentially coupling survival to activation status. (8) ER localization positions GIMAP1 to monitor protein folding stress (UPR) and couple it to survival decisions. (9) High AlphaFold confidence (87.1) reflects a rigid GTPase fold with defined switch I/II regions ready for effector binding. (10) 7q CNVs affecting the entire GIMAP cluster may cause haploinsufficiency leading to combined immunodeficiency phenotypes.
