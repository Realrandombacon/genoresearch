## Description
ECM1 (Extracellular matrix protein 1, Q16610) encodes a 540 aa secreted glycoprotein functioning as a multifunctional extracellular matrix organizer that regulates basement membrane assembly, angiogenesis, and tissue mineralization through direct interaction with perlecan (HSPG2) and modulation of matrix metalloproteinase activity.
