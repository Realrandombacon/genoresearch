InterPro domain scan and AlphaFold structure prediction failed due to API errors, but the low sequence complexity (no canonical domains expected) suggests an intrinsically disordered scaffold architecture. Functional hypothesis: SPATA12 acts as a testis-specific protein scaffold that recruits chromatin remodeling complexes (via CHD2 interaction) and RNA processing factors (via RNASE12) to establish epigenetic programs essential for meiotic progression and sperm differentiation. Upregulation by UV-C radiation reported in RefSeq suggests potential stress-response coordination in germ cells.
