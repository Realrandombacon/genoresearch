## Description
LOC100652758 (Gene ID: 100652758, Ensembl: ENSG00000275765) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 5q33.1 (NC_000005.10, positions 151769792-151772175, ~2.4 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction, consistent with non-coding regulatory element classification. Human Protein Atlas reported no data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000275765, consistent with highly tissue-restricted or cell-type-specific expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 3 copy number gains/duplications (variant 149858: 5q23.3-33.2 ~23.5 Mb trisomy; variant 58118: 5q32-35.3 ~31.6 Mb trisomy; variant 57483: 5q23.3-33.2 ~24.5 Mb trisomy) and 1 copy number loss/deletion (variant 59672: 5q21.3-33.2 ~49.5 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 5q33.1 chromosomal region contains critical extracellular matrix remodeling, stress granule assembly, immune antigen presentation, neurotransmission, and antioxidant defense-associated genes including SPARC (secreted protein acidic and cysteine rich/osteonectin, calcium-binding matricellular protein, ECM remodeling, angiogenesis inhibition, cancer metastasis, osteogenesis), G3BP1 (G3BP stress granule assembly factor 1, RNA-binding protein, stress granule nucleation, antiviral innate immunity, mRNA stability regulation), CD74 (CD74 molecule/MHC class II invariant chain, antigen peptide loading, MHC class II trafficking, B cell activation, macrophage activation, cancer immunotherapy target), GLRA1 (glycine receptor alpha 1, ligand-gated chloride channel, inhibitory neurotransmission, startle disease/hyperekplexia, neuronal inhibition), and GPX3 (glutathione peroxidase 3/extracellular GPx, selenocysteine enzyme, extracellular ROS scavenging, antioxidant defense, cardiovascular protection, tumor suppressor). Functional hypothesis: LOC100652758 likely represents a critical 5q33.1 immune/neuronal enhancer element within the SPARC-CD74 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a macrophage/B-lymphocyte/neuronal enhancer coordinating SPARC-G3BP1-CD74-GLRA1-GPX3 expression in immune and neuronal niches (germinal center B cells, tissue macrophages, brainstem inhibitory neurons) or as a chromatin boundary separating ECM remodeling genes (SPARC) from immune genes (CD74) and neurotransmission genes (GLRA1). The 4 pathogenic CNVs (3 duplications 23.5-31.6 Mb, 1 deletion 49.5 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts SPARC-mediated ECM remodeling and CD74-mediated antigen presentation. Disease mechanism involves SPARC overexpression causing cancer metastasis (EMT activation, invasion, chemoresistance, poor prognosis in glioblastoma/pancreatic cancer), SPARC loss causing osteogenesis defect (bone mineralization impairment, osteoporosis), G3BP1 overexpression causing stress granule hyperassembly (antiviral defense enhancement, mRNA storage dysregulation), G3BP1 dysfunction causing stress granule defect (viral susceptibility, neurodegeneration, ALS/FTD pathology), CD74 overexpression causing autoimmune activation (MHC class II hyperactivation, B cell hyperproliferation, B cell lymphoma), CD74 loss causing immunodeficiency (antigen presentation defect, recurrent infection), CD74 targeting causing cancer immunotherapy response (antibody-drug conjugate efficacy), GLRA1 mutation causing hyperekplexia/startle disease (glycine receptor loss, exaggerated startle reflex, muscle rigidity, apnea, sudden death), GLRA1 dysfunction causing inhibitory neurotransmission defect (neuronal hyperexcitability, seizures, movement disorder), GPX3 loss causing oxidative stress susceptibility (extracellular ROS accumulation, cardiovascular disease, thrombosis, cancer progression), GPX3 hypermethylation causing tumor suppressor silencing (epigenetic inactivation, cancer epigenetics), and 5q33.1 duplication/deletion syndrome presenting with immune dysregulation, neurological abnormalities, and cancer predisposition. The 5q33.1 region is associated with extracellular matrix-immune-neurotransmission-antioxidant genetic variation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (4 CNVs 23.5-49.5 Mb) and SPARC-G3BP1-CD74-GLRA1-GPX3 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as an immune/neuronal-specific enhancer critical for ECM remodeling-stress granule-antigen presentation-inhibitory neurotransmission-antioxidant coordination.
