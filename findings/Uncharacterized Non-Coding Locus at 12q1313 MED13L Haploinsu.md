## Description
LOC100240735 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 12q13.13 (NC_000012.12:54081822-54078838) spanning approximately 3.0 kb. Comprehensive database analysis reveals no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified (precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction), STRING database returned no protein interaction partners (confirming absence of characterized interactome), and Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000291180 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number variations affecting 12q13.13 and broader 12q/p regions. Representative variants include: (1) 12q13.13-13.3 duplication (chr12:53420606-56202942, ~2.8 Mb, 3 copies), (2) 12p13.33-q24.33 whole-arm duplication (chr12:121271-133196807, ~133.1 Mb, 3 copies), (3) 12q13.13 deletion (chr12:53224024-54222450, ~1.0 Mb, hemizygous). The 12q13.13 region is the critical MED13L haploinsufficiency syndrome locus containing dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental genes including MED13L (mediator complex subunit 13L, transcriptional regulation, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder), KRT8 (keratin 8, intermediate filament, epithelial integrity), and multiple neuronal enhancer elements. LOC100240735 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 12q13.13 MED13L expression in neural progenitors and cortical development, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in 12q13 chromatin architecture, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 12q13.13 topologically associating domain (TAD) separating MED13L regulatory landscape from adjacent genes, or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 12q13.13 microdeletion/duplication susceptibility. The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (3 variants vs. 54 for LOC401442, 43 for LOC389602, 26 for LOC728158, 3 for LOC100289511) with both gains and losses suggests: (1) this locus is dosage-sensitive for 12q13.13 gene regulation, (2) copy number alterations disrupt 12q13.13 chromatin architecture leading to MED13L dysregulation, (3) gains/losses may alter transcriptional mediator complex function and neuronal development contributing to MED13L haploinsufficiency syndrome phenotype (intellectual disability, developmental delay, autism spectrum disorder, congenital heart defects, dysmorphic features), or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the 12q13.13 MED13L critical region with neurodevelopmental regulatory effects. MED13L haploinsufficiency syndrome is a well-characterized monogenic disorder caused by MED13L loss-of-function variants.
