The Speedy/RINGO family is known to bind and activate CDKs directly without cyclin requirement, accelerating cell cycle progression. SPDYE6's testis-enriched expression aligns with known roles of other Speedy family members in meiotic cell cycle control and spermatogenesis. The high-confidence interactions with CDK1, CDK2, and CDC25C suggest SPDYE6 functions as a non-canonical CDK activator in G2/M transition and DNA damage response. The 7q22.1 location within a CNV-susceptible region (shared with SPDYE2B) suggests SPDYE6 dosage alterations may contribute to 7q-associated developmental disorders through cell cycle dysregulation. This represents a very dark gene with strong mechanistic predictions based on conserved domain architecture and interaction network, but minimal direct experimental validation.
