## Description
RPF1 (Ribosome production factor 1 homolog, UniProt Q9H9Y2) is a 349-amino acid nucleolar protein located on chromosome 1p21.3. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical Brix domain architecture (IPR007109/PF04427/SM00879, aa 142-325; G3DSA:3.40.50.10480, aa 142-315) characteristic of ribosomal biogenesis factors, classified within the U3 snoRNP protein/RPF1 family (IPR044281/PTHR22734, aa 55-349). The protein carries GO annotations for rRNA binding (GO:0019843), rRNA primary transcript binding (GO:0042134), and rRNA processing (GO:0006364), defining its role in ribosome assembly. Conservation across vertebrates (zebrafish Q6IQU6, 330 aa; orangutan Q5R631, 349 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental translation machinery.
