## Description
SEL1L3 (SEL1L Family Member 3; UniProt Q68CR1) is a 1132 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum-associated protein with 30 publications, representing a partially-characterized component of the ER-associated degradation (ERAD) machinery. Unlike the well-studied SEL1L adaptor for HRD1 E3 ligase, SEL1L3 functions as a lectin-like scaffold recognizing glycoprotein substrates through its concanavalin A domain, driving ER quality control, STING stabilization, and immune surveillance through its unique Sel1 repeat architecture and high-confidence folded structure.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals SEL1L3 contains a distinctive multi-domain organization characteristic of ERAD lectin adaptors: (1) N-terminal region (residues 1-282, ~282 aa) — unstructured extension potentially containing signal peptide and regulatory motifs; (2) Concanavalin A-like lectin/glucanase domain (IPR013320/SSF49899/G3DSA:2.60.120.10, residues 283-426, ~143 aa) — carbohydrate-binding fold recognizing N-glycan structures on misfolded glycoproteins; (3) Central linker (residues 427-569, ~142 aa) — connecting lectin and Sel1 repeat regions; (4) Sel1-like repeats (IPR006597/PF08238/SM00671, residues 570-761, ~191 aa across 3-4 repeats: 570-604, 606-642, 689-725, 731-761) — tetratricopeptide-like helical repeats mediating protein-protein interactions with ERAD complex components; (5) C-terminal TPR domain region (G3DSA:1.25.40.10/IPR011990/SSF81901, residues 645-1029, ~384 aa across three domains: 645-776, 777-877, 878-1029) — extended helical scaffold positioning lectin domain for substrate capture; (6) Full-length family (IPR042756/PTHR44444, residues 49-1130, ~1081 aa) — defines SEL1L3 orthologs across vertebrates. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 81.3), indicating a well-folded elongated scaffold with stable lectin and Sel1/TPR domains, consistent with a rigid platform for simultaneous substrate recognition and ERAD complex assembly.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals medium-to-high confidence interactions exclusively with ERAD machinery and ER-Golgi trafficking proteins: ERGIC2 (0.788—highest; ER-Golgi Intermediate Compartment Protein 2, cargo receptor linking ER to Golgi), DERL3 (0.724; Derlin-3, ER membrane channel for substrate retrotranslocation), SYVN1 (0.713; Synoviolin/HRD1 E3 ligase, ubiquitinates ERAD substrates), DERL2 (0.688; Derlin-2, parallel retrotranslocation channel), ERLEC1 (0.686; ER Lectin 1/XTP3-B, glycoprotein quality control lectin), AMFR (0.671; Autocrine Motility Factor Receptor/gp78, E3 ligase for ERAD), OS9 (0.670; Osteosarcoma Amplified 9, lectin recognizing misfolded glycoproteins), PLTP (0.653; Phospholipid Transfer Protein, lipid metabolism), VCP (0.517; Valosin-Containing Protein/p97, AAA+ ATPase extracting substrates), and DERL1 (0.428; Derlin-1, third retrotranslocation channel).
