## Description
LRRC7 (UniProt Q96NW7) is a 1537 amino acid leucine-rich repeat-containing protein representing a well-characterized synaptic scaffolding protein with critical roles in postsynaptic density organization and glutamatergic neurotransmission. The protein architecture features an extensive array of leucine-rich repeats (LRRs): IPR001611 LRR domains at positions 46-102, 139-195, 254-275; IPR003591 typical LRR subtypes at 48-68, 91-113, 137-159; plus IPR032675 LRR superfamily spanning residues 15-419 forming a curved solenoid structure typical for protein-protein interaction platforms. A C-terminal PDZ domain (IPR001478/PF00595, positions 1447-1535) provides peptide-binding capability for anchoring transmembrane receptors and signaling molecules. The IPR050614 Synaptic Scaffolding LAP/MAGUK Families domain (positions 11-1535) confirms membership in the membrane-associated guanylate kinase scaffold family. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (global pLDDT 51.9) spanning residues 1-1537, consistent with a protein containing ordered LRR/PDZ domains connected by flexible linker regions—characteristic of scaffolding proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly. HPA classifies LRRC7 as a predicted intracellular protein with tissue-enhanced RNA expression, highest in brain (7.9 nTPM), with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, plasma membrane, centrosome, and cytosol—the plasma membrane localization aligns with postsynaptic density function. Cancer-enhanced RNA specificity suggests potential dysregulation in neoplastic contexts. STRING interaction network reveals exceptionally high-confidence associations defining a coherent synaptic signaling module: CAMK2A (0.995, calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II alpha, primary postsynaptic kinase), CAMK2B (0.792, CaMKII beta isoform), GRIN2B (0.756, NMDA receptor GluN2B subunit), DLG1 (0.735, discs large MAGUK scaffold protein 1), GRIA1 (0.731, AMPA receptor GluA1 subunit), DLG4/PSD-95 (0.730, primary postsynaptic density scaffold), PKP4 (0.718, plakophilin-4/armadillo protein), CNKSR2 (0.700, connector enhancer of kinase suppressor of Ras 2, synaptic MAGUK-associated protein), CACNA1D (0.444, L-type voltage-gated calcium channel), CTNND2 (0.426, catenin delta-2, neuronal adherens junction). This interaction profile positions LRRC7 as a central hub connecting NMDA/AMPA glutamate receptors to CaMKII signaling cascades via PDZ-mediated scaffolding—a mechanism essential for long-term potentiation (LTP) and memory formation. ClinVar contains 63 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple large copy number losses at chromosome 1p31.3-22.3 (chr1:66341994-88260975, chr1:61397219-85940743, chr1:65412037-95735764) and single nucleotide variants (c.887T>C p.Leu296Pro, c.281A>G p.Asn94Ser). The clustering of CNVs on 1p suggests LRRC7 haploinsufficiency contributes to 1p deletion syndrome phenotypes featuring neurodevelopmental delay, intellectual disability, and seizures—consistent with disrupted synaptic scaffolding. Evolutionary conservation across mammals (mouse Q80TE7, 1490 aa; rat P70587, 1490 aa; cow F1MCA7, 1536 aa) indicates strong functional constraint. The mechanistic model positions LRRC7 as a postsynaptic density scaffold that uses LRR domains for structural organization and the PDZ domain for recruiting NMDA receptors (GRIN2B), AMPA receptors (GRIA1), and CaMKII (CAMK2A/B) into signaling nanodomains. Upon NMDA receptor activation and calcium influx, LRRC7 facilitates CaMKII autophosphorylation and downstream phosphorylation of AMPA receptors to enhance synaptic transmission—a core mechanism underlying learning and memory. Loss-of-function mutations or dosage reduction would disrupt this molecular architecture, causing synaptic dysfunction manifesting as neurodevelopmental disorders.
