## Description
PRRC2A (Proline-Rich Coiled-Coil 2A) is a massive 2157-amino acid intrinsically disordered scaffold protein with 59 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein contains BAT2 N-terminal domain (IPR009738, positions 1-188; PF07001, positions 1-188) and belongs to the PRRC2 family (IPR033184, positions 1-2145; PTHR14038, positions 1-2145), but lacks defined catalytic domains consistent with scaffold/regulatory function. AlphaFold predicts a mostly disordered structure (pLDDT 39.8, low confidence), characteristic of intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) that function through dynamic protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity. HPA returned no data (gene not in database), reflecting limited antibody-based characterization typical of large disordered proteins. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (chromosome 6p21.33-25.3 copy number gains/losses and single nucleotide variants with uncertain clinical significance), indicating dosage sensitivity. STRING interactions reveal PRRC2A functions within m6A RNA methylation and protein quality control networks with very high confidence connections to BAG6 (0.844) - BCL2-associated athanogene 6 chaperone involved in protein quality control and membrane protein biogenesis, high confidence interactions with UBAP2L (0.760) - ubiquitin-associated protein 2-like, ABHD16A (0.755) - abhydrolase domain containing 16A lysophosphatidylserine lipase, and multiple m6A machinery components including YTHDF2 (0.736), YTHDF3 (0.736), YTHDC1 (0.732), METTL3 (0.710) - m6A methyltransferase complex, YTHDC2 (0.657), and YTHDF1 (0.426) - all m6A reader proteins, plus GPANK1 (0.659) - G-patch domain and ankyrin repeat containing 1. Recent studies (2023-2025) established PRRC2A as an m6A reader promoting colorectal cancer progression via CK1ε-mediated activation of WNT and YAP signaling pathways, essential role in meiosis I completion during spermatogenesis, microglial PRRC2A regulating microglia-neuron interactions in cerebellum and motor functions, and PRRC2A as potent epilepsy biomarker identified through transcriptome-derived artificial neural networks. We hypothesize PRRC2A functions as a large intrinsically disordered scaffold protein that bridges m6A RNA methylation machinery with downstream signaling pathways (WNT/YAP), potentially through recruitment of m6A readers (YTHDF family, YTHDC1/2) to specific RNA targets or protein complexes. The BAT2 N-terminal domain (positions 1-188) may mediate interactions with BAG6 complex components for protein quality control coupling. The disordered architecture (pLDDT 39.8) enables multivalent interactions typical of phase-separated condensates involved in RNA processing. BAG6 interaction (0.844) suggests PRRC2A participates in protein quality control pathways. The 8 ClinVar variants indicate 6p dosage sensitivity potentially contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders. PRRC2A represents an uncharacterized m6A reader scaffold protein linking epitranscriptomic regulation with WNT/YAP signaling in cancer, spermatogenesis, and neuronal function.
