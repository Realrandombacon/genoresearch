BEAN1 (Brain Expressed Associated with NEDD4 1, UniProt Q3B7T3) is a 259 aa intracellular protein representing a genuine dark gene with limited functional characterization despite disease associations. The gene name reflects brain expression pattern and NEDD4 association, not legume biology — literature search reveals 91 total papers but most concern agricultural bean research (lima bean, castor bean, common bean) rather than human BEAN1 protein function, indicating genuine knowledge gap for human biology.
