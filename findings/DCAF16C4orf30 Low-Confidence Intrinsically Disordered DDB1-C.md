## Description
DCAF16/C4orf30 (DDB1 and CUL4 associated factor 16, alias C4orf30) is a 216 amino acid predicted intracellular protein (UniProt Q9NXF7) with low-confidence largely disordered architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 38.2) containing a single defining DCAF16 family domain (IPR028216/PF15349/PTHR16194, residues 1-216), functioning as a ubiquitously expressed plasma membrane/cytosolic substrate adaptor for the CRL4 (Cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligase 4) complex that regulates protein ubiquitination and degradation through DDB1 binding and CUL4A/B recruitment. The protein lacks defined globular domains, suggesting it functions as an intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) or flexible linker within the ubiquitin-proteasome system.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals DCAF16 consists entirely of the DCAF16 family domain (IPR028216/PF15349/PTHR16194, residues 1-216), forming a 216 aa module (100%) unique to this DCAF subfamily. Unlike many other DCAFs which contain WD40 repeats or other structured domains for substrate recognition, DCAF16 lacks these canonical motifs, indicating a potentially distinct mechanism of substrate engagement or a role as a scaffold/regulatory subunit. The low-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 38.2) confirms the protein is largely intrinsically disordered, a feature often associated with hub proteins in ubiquitination pathways that require conformational flexibility to engage multiple partners or undergo rapid turnover.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places DCAF16 within the core CRL4 E3 ubiquitin ligase network with very high confidence interactions: strongest interaction with DDB1 (0.970, DNA damage-binding protein 1, core CRL4 adaptor), followed by CUL4A (0.965, Cullin-4A, scaffold), CUL4B (0.951, Cullin-4B, scaffold), DET1 (0.936, DE-etiolated 1, DDB1-associated), DCAF5 (0.914, DDB1-CUL4 associated factor 5, paralog), DTL (0.914, DTL CDT2 DCAF, substrate receptor), DDA1 (0.913, DDB1-det1 associated 1, complex stabilizer), CRBN (0.909, Cereblon, DCAF substrate receptor), DCAF15 (0.900, DDB1-CUL4 associated factor 15), and DCAF12 (0.900, DDB1-CUL4 associated factor 12).
