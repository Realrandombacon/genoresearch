## Description
DHX40 (DEAH-Box Helicase 40) is a 779 amino acid ATP-dependent RNA helicase that functions as a critical negative regulator of antiviral RLR signaling through DEXH-box catalytic domain-mediated RNA unwinding and OB-fold nucleic acid binding for viral RNA sensing modulation, protein stability control via USP7 interaction, and susceptibility to colorectal and hematological neoplasia in proliferating tissues.
