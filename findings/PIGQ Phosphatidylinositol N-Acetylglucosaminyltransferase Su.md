## Description
PIGQ (Phosphatidylinositol N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase subunit Q, also known as GPI1, Q9BRB3) encodes a 760 aa integral membrane protein functioning as an essential catalytic subunit of the GPI-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase (GPI-GnT) complex that catalyzes the first committed step in glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI) anchor biosynthesis—the transfer of N-acetylglucosamine from UDP-GlcNAc to phosphatidylinositol in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Structural architecture reveals a conserved GPI biosynthetic enzyme organization: PIGQ/GPI1 family domain (IPR007720, residues 1-595) with GO terms for GPI anchor biosynthetic process (GO:0006506) and membrane localization (GO:0016020) forming the catalytic core, N-acetylglucosaminyl transferase component Gpi1 domain (PF05024, residues 247-510) defining the glycosyltransferase active site region, and PIGQ-related family (PTHR21329, residues 1-595) establishing evolutionary conservation across eukaryotes; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 64.0, medium confidence) confirms a partially structured 760 aa protein with ordered catalytic domains interspersed with flexible regions consistent with membrane-integrated multi-subunit complex assembly requiring conformational adaptability for substrate access and product release. STRING interactions define an exceptionally tight GPI biosynthesis machinery network with near-perfect confidence scores reflecting obligate complex formation: PIGP (0.999), PIGH (0.999), PIGC (0.999), PIGA (0.999), PIGY (0.999) as core GPI-GnT complex subunits forming the catalytic holoenzyme, DPM2 (0.999) linking to dolichol-phosphate mannose synthesis pathway providing mannose donors for later GPI remodeling steps, PIGL (0.996) as deacetylase acting downstream in GPI maturation, PIGW (0.825) and PIGO (0.814) as ethanolamine phosphate transferases for GPI side-chain modifications, and CDIPT (0.813) as CDP-diacylglycerol synthase providing phosphatidylinositol precursor substrate. HPA localizes PIGQ to nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and vesicles consistent with ER-to-Golgi trafficking of GPI-anchored proteins and quality control compartments, with GPI-anchor biosynthesis as primary biological process, disease variant and epilepsy involvement confirming neurological pathogenesis, low tissue specificity and detection in all tissues indicates ubiquitous housekeeping function essential for cell surface protein anchoring, low cancer specificity suggests broad physiological role, low immune cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity; classified as disease related gene, metabolic protein, and predicted intracellular/membrane protein. ClinVar documents 131 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations p.Gln271Ter, p.Trp66Ter, p.Gln445Ter causing complete loss-of-function, frameshift deletions p.Leu334fs and duplications p.Phe365fs disrupting catalytic domain integrity, splice site variants c.1069+2T>A, c.1336-1G>T, c.1223+2T>G affecting mRNA processing, and missense variant p.His368Asn potentially impairing catalytic activity; these variants cause Multiple Congenital Anomalies-Hypotonia-Seizures Syndrome Type 4 (MCAHS4, OMIM 618548) and early infantile epileptic encephalopathy through biallelic inheritance disrupting GPI anchor biosynthesis and cell surface protein expression critical for neural development and synaptic function. Literature establishes PIGQ as partially characterized with 22 total papers demonstrating two novel MCAHS4 cases expanding genotype-phenotype spectrum with GestaltMatcher diagnostic evaluation (1x 2025), integrative genomic analysis of homozygous frameshift variant in MCAHS4 (1x 2026), seven new subjects with early infantile epileptic encephalopathy expanding phenotypic spectrum (23x 2020), nonprogressive congenital ataxia phenotype (5x 2021), and cross-species validation implicating PIGQ as conserved regulator of sleep duration (1x 2022); conservation from mouse Q9QYT7 (581 aa) confirms essential GPI biosynthetic function; nucleoplasm/Golgi/vesicle localization correlates with ER membrane integration and secretory pathway trafficking; exceptional STRING network (0.999 scores) indicates obligate hexameric GPI-GnT complex formation with PIGA/PIGC/PIGH/PIGP/PIGY; catalytic Gpi1 domain (residues 247-510) positioned for UDP-GlcNAc binding and phosphatidylinositol glycosylation; 131 ClinVar variants demonstrate extreme mutational intolerance reflecting essential cellular function; MCAHS4 pathogenesis mechanism involves defective GPI anchoring of cell surface proteins including neuronal adhesion molecules, neurotransmitter receptors, and complement regulatory proteins leading to neurodevelopmental defects, hypotonia, and seizure susceptibility.
