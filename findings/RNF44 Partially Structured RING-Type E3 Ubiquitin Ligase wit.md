## Description
RNF44 (RING finger protein 44, UniProt Q7L0R7) is a 432-amino acid intracellular protein that functions as an E3 ubiquitin ligase mediating substrate-specific protein ubiquitination and degradation. InterPro analysis reveals defining RING finger architecture: RING-type zinc finger domain (IPR001841/PF13639/PS50089/SM00184, aa 379-421/380-420) within the RING/FYVE/PHD-type zinc finger superfamily (IPR013083/G3DSA:3.30.40.10/SSF57850, aa 354-431/360-426), specifically classified as RING finger H2 subclass found in RNF44 (cd16680, aa 371-432) — this C-terminal RING domain characteristic of E3 ubiquitin-protein ligases that catalyze transfer of ubiquitin from E2 conjugating enzymes to substrate proteins, marking them for proteasomal degradation or altering their function/localization. GO annotations confirm predicted intracellular protein class with nucleoplasmic subcellular localization. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves medium-low confidence (pLDDT 55.8), indicating a partially structured protein with the C-terminal RING domain likely well-folded (zinc-coordinating residues provide structural stability) while the N-terminal region (~350 aa) remains disordered — this architecture is typical of E3 ligases where flexible regions enable substrate recognition and binding while the structured RING domain provides the catalytic interface for E2 enzyme engagement. Human Protein Atlas shows low tissue specificity expression (ENSG00000146083) with detection in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enriched specificity, with nucleoplasmic subcellular localization — ubiquitous expression suggests fundamental housekeeping role in nuclear protein quality control or transcriptional regulation through targeted protein degradation. STRING interaction network reveals 5 partners with functional coherence centered on ubiquitination machinery: UBE2K (0.620, ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 K — E2 enzyme that pairs with RING E3 ligases), UBE2O (0.419, ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 O — hybrid E2/E3 enzyme), NREP (0.434, neuronal regeneration-related protein — potential substrate), DCAF12L2 (0.415, DDB1 and CUL4 associated factor 12-like protein 2 — CRL4 complex substrate receptor), and GLI4 (0.402, GLI family zinc finger 4 — transcription factor, potential ubiquitination substrate). The UBE2K/UBE2O interactions definitively establish RNF44 as a functional E3 ubiquitin ligase operating within the ubiquitin-proteasome system. ClinVar contains 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting predominantly of copy number variations (deletions and duplications) spanning chromosome 5q35.2-35.3 region (5q35.2-35.3, 5q35.1-35.2, 5q35.1-35.3) — these CNVs encompass multiple genes and are associated with developmental disorders including Sotos syndrome spectrum (5q35 microdeletion syndrome features), though no specific disease is attributed solely to RNF44, suggesting haploinsufficiency contributes to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through disrupted nuclear protein homeostasis. Conservation across vertebrates (Danio rerio Q08CG8 448 aa, Mus musculus Q3UHJ8 407 aa, Rattus norvegicus Q4V7B8 350 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental ubiquitination processes. RNF44 likely functions as a nuclear E3 ubiquitin ligase that recognizes specific substrates through its disordered N-terminal region, recruits E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes (UBE2K/UBE2O) via its C-terminal RING domain, and catalyzes ubiquitin transfer to target proteins — regulating transcription factor stability (potentially GLI4), chromatin remodeling complexes, or DNA repair factors, with 51 pathogenic CNVs causing developmental disorders through combined disruption of ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation and co-deleted genes essential for neurodevelopment.
