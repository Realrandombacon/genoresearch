## Description
LOC105373352 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 2p25.3 telomere (NC_000002.12:558200-562092) spanning approximately 3.9 kb. This represents the most telomeric annotated feature on chromosome 2p. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000233633 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - the highest burden observed for any 2p locus in this survey. Variants include massive 2p copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 2p25.3-21 (chr2:236816-45983232, ~45.7 Mb), 2p25.3-22.3 (chr2:12770-33711509, ~33.7 Mb), 2p25.3-23.3 (chr2:12770-25039694, ~25.0 Mb), 2p25.3-24.1 (chr2:17019-20001056, ~20.0 Mb), 2p25.3-25.2 (chr2:30341-4642399, ~4.6 Mb; chr2:17019-4957745, ~4.9 Mb), plus deletions spanning 2p25.3 (chr2:131730-2713517, ~2.6 Mb; chr2:12770-2748672, ~2.7 Mb; chr2:17019-2305267, ~2.3 Mb) and a large interstitial deletion (chr2:100000-1793802, ~1.7 Mb). The 2p25.3 telomeric region is the critical locus for 2p duplication syndrome and 2p deletion syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive proto-oncogenes MYCN (N-myc, neuroblastoma amplification), ALK (anaplastic lymphoma kinase, neuroblastoma/inflammatory myofibroblastic tumor), FAM49A (actin cytoskeleton), and ACADS (metabolic enzyme). LOC105373352 likely functions as: (1) a subtelomeric cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 2p25.3 oncogene expression, (2) a telomere position effect (TPE) boundary element modulating telomeric chromatin silencing, (3) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) regulating 2p25.3 gene transcription in cis, (4) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 2p25.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (5) a telomere-associated repeat element affecting chromosome end stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (24 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this telomeric locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) telomere position effect regulating subtelomeric gene expression, (2) 3D genome architecture of the 2p25.3 oncogene cluster, (3) MYCN/ALK allele-specific expression balance, or (4) telomere capping function affecting chromosome stability, leading to 2p duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, neuroblastoma predisposition) or 2p deletion syndrome (growth retardation, dysmorphic features, developmental delay).
