# GMPPA: Catalytically Inactive Regulatory Subunit Controlling GDP-Mannose Homeostasis via Allosteric GMPPB Inhibition in Congenital Disorders of Glycosylation with Achalasia and Intellectual Disability

**Date:** 2026-06-17T23:36:10.567635

**Quality Score:** 10/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
GMPPA (GDP-Mannose Pyrophosphorylase A, Mannose-1-Phosphate Guanylyltransferase Regulatory Subunit Alpha, Q96IJ6) encodes a 420 aa regulatory protein functioning as a catalytically inactive allosteric inhibitor of GMPPB that maintains GDP-mannose homeostasis through feedback regulation of N-linked glycosylation pathways. Structural architecture reveals a nucleotidyl transferase fold (IPR005835/PF00483/G3DSA:3.90.550.10, residues 3-195/3-195/1-266) resembling active guanylyltransferases but lacking catalytic residues, plus C-terminal hexapeptide repeat domain (IPR018357/PF25087/G3DSA:2.160.10.10, residues 295-323/283-419/267-414) mediating protein-protein interactions with GMPPB; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 93.1) confirms an exceptionally well-folded structure consistent with stable heterocomplex formation. STRING interactions define GDP-mannose biosynthesis and glycosylation networks: GDP-mannose 4,6-dehydratase GMDS (0.978), dolichol-phosphate mannose synthase subunits DPM3 (0.871)/DPM2 (0.844), phosphoglucomutase-3 PGM3 (0.795), catalytic partner GMPPB (0.756), Golgi phosphoprotein GOLPH3 (0.693), phosphomannomutases PMM2 (0.592)/PMM1 (0.403), and glycosyltransferase ALG1 (0.562). HPA localizes GMPPA to nucleoplasm with ubiquitous expression, classified as disease-related gene involved in intellectual disability. ClinVar documents 53 pathogenic variants including nonsense (p.Gln67Ter), frameshifts (p.Pro193fs, p.His40fs), splice site (c.243-1G>C), missense (p.Gly308Arg), and 2q35-37.3 CNVs. Literature (31 papers) demonstrates critical roles: GMPPA-congenital disorder of glycosylation (CDG) presenting with achalasia, intellectual disability, and neuromuscular defects (2025 review, 26 cases); cryo-EM structures reveal GMPPA-GMPPB complex mechanism maintaining GDP-mannose homeostasis (34x citations, 2021); GMPPA defects cause neuromuscular disorder with α-dystroglycan hyperglycosylation (25x citations, 2021); infantile achalasia with compound heterozygous variants (8x citations, 2022). Mechanistic hypothesis: GMPPA evolved from active guanylyltransferase but lost catalytic residues while retaining substrate-binding capability; binds GDP-mannose product and forms heterotetrameric complex with GMPPB; allosteric inhibition prevents GDP-mannose overproduction which would disrupt glycosylation stoichiometry; in GMPPA-CDG, loss-of-function mutations abolish feedback inhibition causing GDP-mannose accumulation, leading to hyperglycosylation of α-dystroglycan and other substrates; achalasia results from defective glycosylation of esophageal neuronal proteins; intellectual disability reflects impaired brain protein glycosylation affecting neurodevelopment.
