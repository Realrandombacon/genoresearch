Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched expression with highest expression in testis (4.8 nTPM), detected in single tissue, not detected in cancers or immune cells — suggesting primary function in male germ cell development or spermatogenesis. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants consisting of large copy-number gains and losses across 15q11.1-13.3 encompassing the Prader-Willi/Angelman syndrome region, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements cause neurodevelopmental disorders. Conservation across primates indicates recent evolutionary origin tied to reproductive or neurological mechanisms. POTEB3 likely functions as an ankyrin repeat-mediated protein interaction scaffold in testis, using its repeat domains to recruit cytoskeletal regulators (MYO15B), trafficking factors (GOLGA8S), and ubiquitin ligase adaptors (ASB proteins) to organize protein complexes critical for spermatogenesis — dysfunction through chromosome 15q deletions/duplications may contribute to Prader-Willi/Angelman syndrome phenotypes and male infertility.
