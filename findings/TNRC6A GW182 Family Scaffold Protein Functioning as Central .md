## Description
TNRC6A (Q8NDV7), also known as GW182, encodes a massive 1962 amino acid intrinsically disordered scaffold protein that functions as the central effector of miRNA-mediated gene silencing, bridging Argonaute proteins loaded with miRNAs to mRNA decay machinery including deadenylases, decapping enzymes, and exonucleases to repress target gene expression post-transcriptionally. InterPro analysis reveals a modular domain architecture characteristic of GW182 family proteins distributed across an otherwise disordered scaffold: an Argonaute hook domain (IPR019486/PF10427, positions 1071-1200) forming a structured module that directly binds PIWI domains of Argonaute proteins anchoring TNRC6A to the RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC); a TNRC6-PABC binding domain (IPR032226/PF16608, positions 1505-1778) classified as disordered mediating interactions with poly(A)-binding protein cytoplasmic 1 (PABPC1) to initiate deadenylation; an RNA recognition motif (IPR034924/cd12711/G3DSA:3.30.70.330/SSF54928, positions 1753-1866/1775-1866) providing sequence-specific RNA binding capacity; a nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily (IPR012677, positions 1775-1862) underlying the RRM fold; and GW182 domain-containing protein family classification (IPR052068/PTHR13020, positions 63-1962) confirming membership in this essential silencing factor family with RNA-binding molecular function (GO:0003676). AlphaFold predicts a low confidence structure (pLDDT 37.9) consistent with a predominantly intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) where functional domains are embedded within long disordered linkers enabling conformational flexibility for multivalent interactions with diverse partners typical of large scaffold proteins coordinating macromolecular complexes. Literature (129 papers) defines TNRC6A as a multifunctional silencing hub: prefrontal TNRC6A mediates anxiety-like behavior by regulating corticotropin-releasing factor (CRF) through maintenance of miR-21-3p stability establishing role in emotional regulation; hypoxic bone marrow mesenchymal stem cell-derived exosomal miR-652-3p promotes hepatocarcinoma proliferation and metastasis via targeting TNRC6A demonstrating tumor suppressor function; lncRNA NORAD/miR-144-3p axis promotes myocardial ischemia-reperfusion injury by targeting TNRC6A linking to cardiac pathology; m5C modification of LINC01082 inhibits osteosarcoma progression by modulating miR-543-TNRC6A axis revealing epigenetic regulation layer.
