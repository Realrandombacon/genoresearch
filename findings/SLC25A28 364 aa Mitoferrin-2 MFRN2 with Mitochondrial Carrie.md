## Description
SLC25A28 (Q96A46, 364 aa), also known as Mitoferrin-2 (MFRN2), MRS3/4, or MRS4L, is a mitochondrial iron importer belonging to solute carrier family 25 (mitochondrial carrier family, MCF). InterPro identifies comprehensive mitochondrial carrier architecture: mitochondrial carrier protein (PF00153, IPR018108, three repeats at aa 70-160/168-254/259-355), mitochondrial carrier domain superfamily (IPR023395, G3DSA:1.50.40.10, aa 51-362), Solcar repeat profile (PS50920, three repeats at aa 70-158/168-252/259-352), and mitochondrial carrier family (PTHR45758, SSF103506, aa 54-357/72-349). AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 72.9, residues 1-364) consistent with canonical 6-transmembrane helix mitochondrial carrier fold with three homologous domains forming a central substrate-binding cavity typical of SLC25 family transporters. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity); classified as metabolic protein, predicted membrane protein, and transporter involved in ion transport and iron transport. STRING reveals 10 interactions forming mitochondrial iron metabolism network: NARS2 (0.897, asparaginyl-tRNA synthetase 2), FXN/frataxin (0.816, iron-sulfur cluster assembly), FECH/ferrochelatase (0.806, heme biosynthesis enzyme), MTCH1 (0.773, mitochondrial carrier homolog 1), MTCH2 (0.692, mitochondrial carrier homolog 2), ABCB7 (0.675, Fe-S cluster exporter), METTL1 (0.673, tRNA methyltransferase), NFS1 (0.662, cysteine desulfurase for Fe-S assembly), ABCB10 (0.453, mitochondrial ABC transporter), and ABCB8 (0.430). ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic variants including large copy number gains (chr10:87456174-107789979, chr10:95078198-135427143, chr10:94346520-135427143, chr10:93283493-135427143, chr10:100027-135427143) and losses (chr10:88755921-102461203, chr10:94283369-101820913, chr10:12829206-135427143, chr10:100177321-101611388), though no specific monogenic diseases are yet assigned. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A28/Mitoferrin-2 functions as a mitochondrial inner membrane ferrous iron (Fe2+) importer that transports cytosolic iron into the mitochondrial matrix via antiport mechanism (likely Fe2+/H+ or Fe2+/metabolite exchange). The 6-TM mitochondrial carrier fold with three Solcar repeats creates a central substrate-binding cavity with conserved charged residues enabling iron recognition and transport. Ubiquitous expression reflects essential housekeeping role in mitochondrial iron homeostasis across all tissues. Strong FXN interaction (0.816) links to frataxin-mediated iron-sulfur cluster assembly machinery. FECH interaction (0.806) connects to terminal step of heme biosynthesis (iron insertion into protoporphyrin IX). ABCB7 interaction (0.675) indicates coordination with Fe-S cluster exporter for cytosolic Fe-S protein biogenesis. NFS1 interaction (0.662) links to cysteine desulfurase providing sulfur for Fe-S clusters. MTCH1/MTCH2 interactions (0.773, 0.692) suggest functional redundancy within mitochondrial carrier network. ABCB10/ABCB8 interactions (0.453, 0.430) connect to mitochondrial ABC transporters for heme/peptide export. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt mitochondrial iron import causing impaired heme synthesis, Fe-S cluster deficiency, sideroblastic anemia, and potential neurodegeneration. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 364 aa, zebrafish 376 aa) confirms essential mitochondrial role. NOTE: This gene is moderately characterized as Mitoferrin-2 with established role in mitochondrial iron import, distinct from Mitoferrin-1 (SLC25A37) which is erythroid-specific.
