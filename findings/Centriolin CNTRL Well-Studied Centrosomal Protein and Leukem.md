# Centriolin (CNTRL): Well-Studied Centrosomal Protein and Leukemia Driver

## Description
Centriolin (CNTRL) is a large (2325 aa) centrosomal protein essential for microtubule organization and cell division. While well-studied (163 papers), it exhibits significant clinical relevance in cancer. Notably, it forms a fusion oncogene with FGFR1 via t(8;9)(p12;q33) translocation, driving biphenotypic leukemia. Aberrant CNTRL expression is also linked to poor prognosis in glioma. Its disruption leads to cell cycle arrest in rhabdomyosarcoma. Despite its canonical role, the specific mechanisms of the CNTRL-FGFR1 fusion and its oncogenic drivers remain active areas of research.
