## Description
LOC129389983 (Gene ID: 129389983) is not a protein-coding gene but a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 8p11.21 (42,983,252-42,983,452 bp, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak7007 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic copy number variants (large 8p-8q rearrangements spanning 5-140 Mb), associated with 8p deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, congenital heart defects, and genitourinary anomalies. The 8p11.21 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including FGFR1 (fibroblast growth factor receptor 1, Kallmann syndrome, skeletal development), WRN (Werner syndrome RecQ helicase, DNA repair, premature aging), CSF3R (colony stimulating factor 3 receptor, neutrophil differentiation, leukemia predisposition), and multiple neurodevelopmental regulators. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129389983 functions as a transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic or neural contexts. The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (e.g., Polycomb repressive complex 2, NuRD, or HDAC-containing complexes) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins to establish repressive chromatin (H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation). Pathogenic 8p CNVs disrupt silencer-enhancer balance and chromatin topology, causing FGFR1 haploinsufficiency (Kallmann syndrome, skeletal dysplasia), WRN dysfunction (genomic instability, premature aging), or CSF3R alterations (neutropenia, leukemia). The 8p11.21 region is a known neurodevelopmental critical region—8p11.21 deletions cause severe intellectual disability and behavioral abnormalities. Altered silencer function may contribute to neurodevelopmental, hematologic, and aging-related phenotypes. This represents a critical class of "dark genes" that are actually functional non-coding regulatory elements misannotated as genes due to automated pipeline predictions. The MPRA validation provides direct functional evidence of regulatory activity in erythroid lineage.
