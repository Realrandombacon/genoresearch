# FAM89B: Leucine Repeat Adapter Protein Linking TGF-beta Signaling to Anaphase-Promoting Complex

**Date:** 2026-03-20T13:18:24.656825

**Quality Score:** 2.17/10  (E=4.35, D=0.0) [WEAK]

## Description
FAM89B encodes a 189 amino acid leucine repeat adapter protein (UniProt Q8N5H3, LRAP25) that functions as a molecular bridge between TGF-beta signaling and cell cycle machinery. The protein contains a conserved leucine-rich adaptor domain (IPR039499/PF14854, positions 85-140) and is classified as a TGF-beta signaling regulator (PTHR46949). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured conformation (pLDDT 68.3) consistent with adapter proteins that undergo conformational changes upon binding partners. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal striking high-confidence associations with anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) subunits: ANAPC5 (0.850), ANAPC4 (0.850), ANAPC1 (0.848), ANAPC2 (0.847), CDC16 (0.845), and CDC23 (0.830), suggesting FAM89B recruits or stabilizes APC/C assembly. Additional partners include HSPB11 (0.646) and SLC25A45 (0.614). ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic variants including copy number gains and deletions at 11q13.1. Orthologs exist in mouse (Q9QUI1) and rat (Q566R4) with identical 189 aa length, indicating strong conservation. The lamellipodium localization and cell migration annotation, combined with APC/C interactions, positions FAM89B as a regulator coordinating TGF-beta-mediated epithelial-mesenchymal transition with cell cycle progression through APC/C modulation.
