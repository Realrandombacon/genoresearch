# Uncharacterized Locus at 12q23.1 within 12q24 Deletion/Duplication Syndrome Region with Pathogenic Copy Number Gains

**Date:** 2026-03-19T03:46:21.133181

**Quality Score:** 2.7/10  (E=3.2, D=2.2) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC101928937 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 12q23.1 (NC_000012.12:99093358-99105010) spanning approximately 11.7 kb. No UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000258039 but found no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all copy number gains (duplications): chr12:91044318-109133210 (~18.1 Mb), chr12:121271-133196807 (~133.1 Mb, whole arm), and chr12:97394550-101410225 (~4.0 Mb). These large structural variants encompass the 12q23.1-24.3 region and are associated with 12q24 deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, and congenital heart defects. The 12q24 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including TBX3 (T-box transcription factor 3) and TBX5, whose haploinsufficiency causes Holt-Oram syndrome, and duplication of which leads to distinct developmental anomalies. LOC101928937 likely functions as part of the 12q24 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose duplication contributes to 12q24 duplication syndrome pathogenesis through gene dosage effects or disruption of topologically associating domains (TADs). The absence of protein-coding features combined with pathogenic CNV burden suggests this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where copy number alteration disrupts normal developmental processes, particularly cardiac and limb morphogenesis governed by TBX family transcription factors.
