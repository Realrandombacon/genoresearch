## Description
CIMIP6 (C2orf73) encodes a 287 amino acid small partially structured intracellular-ciliary protein (UniProt Q8N5S3) with medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 53.5) indicating mixed ordered/disordered architecture consistent with intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) scaffolding function in ciliary microtubule organization. InterPro analysis reveals comprehensive domain architecture: ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 family (IPR031365, aa1-286) defining this conserved domain across vertebrates with GO annotation for cilium localization, ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 Pfam family (PF15667, aa38-284) providing the core structural scaffold for axonemal microtubule inner junction assembly, and similar to hypothetical protein FLJ40298 family (PTHR35087, aa1-286) marking evolutionary conservation. HPA data reveals predicted intracellular protein classification, nucleoplasm plasma membrane and cytosol subcellular localization indicating dynamic distribution between nuclear and ciliary compartments, tissue enriched RNA expression with testis as top tissue (46.3 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells blood cell pattern, and group enriched single cell type specificity. STRING interactions returned no direct partners, suggesting CIMIP6 may act as a stable structural component of the ciliary axoneme rather than a transient signaling scaffold, though functional annotation links it to ciliary microtubule inner junction complexes. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large 2p copy number losses (chr2:11504318-111365996x1) and gains (chr2:1-243199373, chr2:34792916-56676541x3, chr2:12771-242783384x3, chr2:27861707-60790985x3, chr2:40738282-57863821x3, chr2:66097-55570637x3), demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity at the 2p16.2 locus with both triplosensitivity and haploinsufficiency. The 2p16.2 chromosomal location, ciliary microtubule inner designation, testis-enriched expression (46.3 nTPM), nucleoplasm-cytosol-ciliary localization, and ciliary microtubule inner protein domain architecture suggests sperm flagellar axoneme scaffolding role.
