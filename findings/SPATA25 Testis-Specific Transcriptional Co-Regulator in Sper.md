# SPATA25: Testis-Specific Transcriptional Co-Regulator in Spermatogenesis

## Description
SPATA25 (spermatogenesis-associated 25) encodes a 227-amino acid protein (UniProt Q9BR10) with testis-specific expression (HPA: 22.9 nTPM in testis, Golgi apparatus localization). The protein contains a conserved spermatogenesis-associated domain (InterPro IPR029192, Pfam PF15218, Panther PTHR36857) spanning residues 1-227. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 56.7), suggesting partial intrinsic disorder that may enable flexible protein-protein interactions. STRING analysis reveals 10 interaction partners with medium confidence scores: ZSWIM1 (0.634) and multiple TAF transcription factors (TAF4, TAF3, TAF11, TAF13, TAF5, TAF6, TAF8, TAF4B all at 0.610), plus NEURL2 (0.551). The TAF interaction network strongly suggests SPATA25 functions as a transcriptional co-regulator within the TFIID complex during spermatogenesis. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (deletions and duplications on chromosome 20q13.12), indicating dosage sensitivity and clinical relevance for reproductive disorders. Conservation with mouse ortholog Q9DA57 (226 aa, 99% identity) confirms strong evolutionary pressure. Mechanistically, SPATA25 likely serves as a testis-specific chromatin modifier that recruits or stabilizes TFIID components at spermatogenesis gene promoters, with its disordered regions enabling dynamic interactions with multiple transcriptional partners.
