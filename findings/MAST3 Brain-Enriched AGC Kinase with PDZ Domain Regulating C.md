## Description
MAST3 (Microtubule-Associated Serine/Threonine-Protein Kinase 3, UniProt O60307) is a 1309 aa multidomain kinase belonging to the MAST family of AGC kinases that functions in cytoskeletal regulation, neuronal signaling, and immune responses. The molecular architecture includes an N-terminal region, catalytic serine/threonine kinase domain (IPR000719, positions 367-640), AGC-kinase C-terminal extension (IPR000961, positions 641-712), and C-terminal PDZ domain (IPR001478, positions 950-1038) for protein-protein interactions. Literature (54 papers) establishes MAST3 roles in: (1) MAST3 nonkinase domain phosphosites regulating cytoskeletal organization (2025, 1 citation), (2) pathogenic MAST3 variants in the STK kinase domain associated with epilepsy and intellectual disability (2021, 17 citations), (3) MAST3 modulating inflammatory response and proliferation of fibroblast-like synoviocytes in rheumatoid arthritis (2019, 18 citations), (4) miR-125a-3p targeting MAST3 to inhibit cell proliferation via Wnt/β-catenin and NF-κB pathways in rheumatoid arthritis (2021, 18 citations). HPA confirms brain-enriched expression (44.9 nTPM in brain) with tissue-enhanced specificity and nuclear speckle localization, consistent with neuronal function and transcriptional regulation. STRING interactions reveal scaffold complex network: MAGI3 (0.763) - membrane-associated guanylate kinase scaffolding protein; PTEN (0.694) - phosphatase tumor suppressor; SNTB2 (0.643) - syntrophin beta-2; ARPP19 (0.632) - cAMP-regulated phosphoprotein; DLG1 (0.416) - discs large homolog scaffolding protein. This positions MAST3 within MAGI-PTEN scaffold complexes regulating cell polarity and signaling. ClinVar contains 76 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Glu1317Gly, p.Asp253Gly, p.Pro1226Gln, p.Asn202Ile, p.Glu1067Gln, p.Glu393Gln), frameshift (p.Cys570fs, p.Ser975fs), and splice variants, with disease associations including epilepsy and intellectual disability. AlphaFold shows medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 57.1) indicating partially ordered domains with intrinsically disordered regions, typical of scaffold-associated kinases. Conservation: mouse Mast3 (Q3U214) is 1321 aa, Xenopus mast3 (Q6AX33) is 1482 aa - conserved across vertebrates. Functional hypothesis: MAST3 acts as a scaffold-associated kinase that phosphorylates cytoskeletal and signaling proteins within MAGI3-PTEN complexes at neuronal synapses, regulating microtubule dynamics, cell polarity, and synaptic plasticity. The PDZ domain anchors MAST3 to specific subcellular locations while the kinase domain phosphorylates substrates including ARPP19 and syntrophins. Pathogenic variants in the kinase domain disrupt phosphorylation of neuronal substrates, leading to hyperexcitability and epilepsy. The disordered regions may enable conformational flexibility for scaffold binding and substrate recognition. MAST3 represents an understudied node linking cytoskeletal dynamics, scaffold organization, and neuronal excitability disorders.
