## Description
RBBP8P1 (RBBP8 pseudogene 1) is located at chromosome Xq24 and encodes a 435 amino acid protein derived from the RBBP8/CtIP DNA endonuclease family. Despite pseudogene annotation, InterPro analysis detects IPR019518 (DNA endonuclease Ctp1 N-terminal domain, residues 36-151) and PF10482 (Tumour-suppressor protein CtIP N-terminal coiled-coil domain), suggesting retention of functional domain architecture from the parental gene. However, RBBP8P1 shows markedly reduced structural confidence with AlphaFold pLDDT=60.9 (medium/low), indicating substantial intrinsically disordered regions compared to well-folded CtIP. STRING found no interaction partners, HPA has no expression data, and ClinVar documents zero variants—consistent with pseudogene status or extremely low expression. Zero Semantic Scholar publications confirm this is a genuine dark gene. Conservation in marmoset (Callithrix jacchus, 468 aa) suggests evolutionary retention beyond humans. The partial domain preservation raises intriguing questions: RBBP8P1 may represent a 'functional pseudogene' producing truncated regulatory proteins that modulate CtIP activity through dominant-negative mechanisms, or it may function as a ceRNA competing for miRNA binding sites. The X-chromosome location warrants investigation into sex-specific expression patterns and potential roles in X-linked DNA repair disorders.
