## Description
JAM3 (Junctional adhesion molecule C, also known as JAM-C or CD322, Q9BX67) encodes a 310 aa type I transmembrane protein functioning as a critical component of tight junctions in endothelial and epithelial cells that regulates paracellular permeability, leukocyte transendothelial migration (TEM), platelet activation, and spermatogenesis through homophilic and heterophilic interactions with integrins and other junctional adhesion molecules.
