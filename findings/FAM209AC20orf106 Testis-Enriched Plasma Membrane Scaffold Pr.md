## Description
FAM209A (alias C20orf106, dJ1153D9.3, family with sequence similarity 209 member A) encodes a 171 amino acid partially structured plasma membrane protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 58.8) containing a FAM209 family domain (IPR027943/PF15206, aa 20-168) and belonging to the PTHR35157 family (aa 1-171). HPA shows testis-enriched expression (150.9 nTPM, tissue enriched category), detected in some tissues distribution, plasma membrane/cell junctions subcellular localization, not detected in cancer, not detected in immune cells, and cell type-enriched single cell pattern. Biological processes include differentiation and spermatogenesis. ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 20q13.2-13.33 and 20p13-q13.33 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with cancer-testis antigen and ubiquitin signaling regulators: MAGEB16 (melanoma antigen family B 16, cancer-testis antigen, 0.625), C9orf131 (chromosome 9 ORF 131, 0.590), UBASH3B (ubiquitin associated and SH3 domain containing B, T-cell receptor signaling negative regulator, 0.571), C12orf40 (chromosome 12 ORF 40, 0.545), C9orf43 (chromosome 9 ORF 43, 0.543), TMCO5A (transmembrane coiled-coil domain 5A, 0.533), PKDREJ (polycystic kidney disease and receptor for egg jelly, sperm-egg recognition, 0.517), SPATA16 (spermatogenesis associated 16, acrosome formation, 0.514), ZFAND4 (zinc finger AN1-type containing 4, ubiquitin binding, 0.475), and TULP2 (tubulin like protein 2, photoreceptor outer segment trafficking, 0.461). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The FAM209 domain architecture combined with testis-enriched expression (150.9 nTPM) and plasma membrane/cell junction localization strongly suggests FAM209A functions as a testis-specific plasma membrane scaffold protein involved in spermatogenesis and sperm-egg recognition. MAGEB16 interaction (0.625) places FAM209A in the cancer-testis antigen network—MAGE proteins are normally expressed only in germ cells but reactivated in cancers, suggesting FAM209A shares germline-restricted expression pattern. UBASH3B interaction (0.571) links FAM209A to ubiquitin-mediated signaling regulation, potentially modulating T-cell receptor or growth factor receptor pathways through SH3 domain-mediated protein-protein interactions. PKDREJ association (0.517) is particularly informative—PKDREJ is a sperm membrane protein required for sperm-egg binding and zona pellucida recognition, placing FAM209A at the fertilization machinery interface. SPATA16 interaction (0.514) reinforces spermatogenesis role—SPATA16 is essential for acrosome formation during spermiogenesis. Plasma membrane/cell junction localization indicates function in cell-cell adhesion or receptor signaling at the cell surface, potentially coordinating sperm maturation or egg recognition events. Testis enrichment (150.9 nTPM) indicates specialized function in male germ cell development, potentially regulating spermatid differentiation, acrosome biogenesis, or sperm membrane remodeling. Cell type-enriched pattern suggests specific upregulation in spermatogenic cell lineages. Medium pLDDT score (58.8) indicates partial disorder with structured FAM209 core, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly at the plasma membrane. Not detected in cancer suggests tight germline-specific silencing in somatic tissues, with potential oncogenic reactivation through epigenetic dysregulation. Pathogenic 20q CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting spermatogenesis, sperm membrane organization, and fertilization capacity, potentially contributing to 20q duplication/deletion syndrome phenotypes including male infertility, developmental defects, and impaired germ cell differentiation.
