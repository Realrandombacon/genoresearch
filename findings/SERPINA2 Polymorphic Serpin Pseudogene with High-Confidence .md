## Description
SERPINA2 (serpin family A member 2) is located at chromosome 14q32.13 within the serpin gene cluster and encodes a 421 amino acid alpha-1-antitrypsin-related protein. Despite annotation as a polymorphic pseudogene (with non-functional alleles predominant in some populations), multiple lines of evidence suggest functional protein production in specific contexts. InterPro analysis confirms canonical serpin architecture including IPR000215 (Serpin family), PF00079 (Serpin domain, residues 57-418), cd19550 (serpin family A member 2 protease inhibitor 1-like, residues 56-418), and conserved serpin signature PS00284 (residues 391-401). The AlphaFold predicted structure (P20848) shows high confidence folding (pLDDT=84.2), indicating a stable serpin fold with characteristic beta-sheet A and reactive center loop topology. Critically, HPA reports tissue-enriched expression in epididymis (101.0 nTPM) and immune cell-enhanced expression in blood, demonstrating active transcription in reproductive and immune tissues. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—predominantly copy number losses and gains affecting 14q31-32 (e.g., accession 3391923: chr14:79886061-96870809 deletion; accession 2684743: chr14:88580184-107285437 duplication)—suggesting dosage sensitivity. Literature (8 publications) indicates SERPINA2 has divergent function from its paralog SERPINA1 (alpha-1-antitrypsin), with evidence for natural selection favoring pseudogenization in some populations. STRING found no interaction partners, consistent with limited characterization. The epididymal enrichment suggests potential roles in sperm maturation or male reproductive immunity, while the polymorphic nature implies population-specific functional variation warranting investigation into serine protease inhibition specificity and disease associations.
