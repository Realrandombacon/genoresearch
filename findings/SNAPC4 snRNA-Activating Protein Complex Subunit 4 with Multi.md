## Description
SNAPC4 (snRNA-Activating Protein Complex Subunit 4, also known as Small Nuclear RNA Activating Complex Polypeptide 4 or SNAP45, UniProt Q5SXM2) is a 1469 amino acid nuclear protein with partial characterization (23 papers) that functions as the largest subunit of the SNAPc (snRNA-Activating Protein complex), a critical transcription factor required for RNA polymerase II and III-mediated transcription of small nuclear RNA (snRNA) genes including U1, U2, U4, U5 snRNAs essential for spliceosome assembly and pre-mRNA splicing. The protein contains multiple SANT/Myb DNA-binding domains (IPR001005/SANT/Myb domain residues 180-290/289-343/344-397, IPR017884/SANT domain residues 404-445/451-500, IPR017930/Myb domain residues 289-345/396-451/452-503, PF13921/Myb-like DNA-binding residues 297-357/404-464, PTHR46621/Myb-like DNA-binding residues 1-1404) with homeodomain-like superfamily architecture (IPR009057/G3DSA:1.10.10.60 residues 288-339/340-399/400-451/291-503) enabling sequence-specific DNA recognition at snRNA gene promoters through the proximal sequence element (PSE).
