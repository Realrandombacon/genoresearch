## Description
PPP2R2C encodes a 447 aa regulatory subunit of protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) heterotrimeric holoenzyme (UniProt Q9Y2T4), providing substrate specificity and subcellular localization to the core catalytic machinery. The protein contains the PR55/B55 regulatory subunit family domain (IPR000009, aa4-441) with seven WD40 repeats (IPR001680, aa13-52/79-119/161-200) forming a β-propeller scaffold for protein-protein interactions, plus YVTN repeat-like domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, aa52-443) characteristic of PP2A regulatory subunits. AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 89.5), indicating a well-folded WD40 β-propeller structure enabling multiple substrate recognition interfaces. HPA shows brain-enriched expression (112.0 nTPM) with cytosolic localization and cancer-enhanced specificity, suggesting specialized roles in neuronal signaling and tumor suppression. STRING interactions reveal tight PP2A holoenzyme membership: PPP2R1A (scaffold, 0.999), PPP2CA (catalytic, 0.995), plus CCT chaperonin complex partners (CCT5/8/3/2 all 0.994) for proper folding/assembly, and other PP2A subunits (PPP2R1B 0.606, CCT6A 0.495, CCT7 0.409). ClinVar contains 51 pathogenic variants (primarily 4p16 copy number losses/gains), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (74 papers) demonstrates roles in LRRK2 dephosphorylation in Parkinson's disease, telomere DNA damage response attenuation via TRF2 regulation, and immune response modulation in airway epithelial cells. PPP2R2C likely functions as a substrate-recruitment subunit directing PP2A catalytic activity toward specific phospho-proteins involved in cell cycle control, neuronal signaling, and DNA damage response. The brain-enriched expression (112 nTPM vs typical 2-20 nTPM) suggests specialized roles in synaptic plasticity, tau dephosphorylation, or LRRK2 regulation relevant to Parkinson's pathogenesis, while cancer-enhanced expression indicates potential tumor suppressor functions through cell cycle phosphatase activity.
