## Description
ALDH1L1 (Aldehyde dehydrogenase 1 family member L1, also known as Cytosolic 10-formyltetrahydrofolate dehydrogenase or FDH, O75891) encodes a 902 aa homodimeric enzyme functioning as a critical regulator of one-carbon metabolism that catalyzes the NADP+-dependent conversion of 10-formyltetrahydrofolate to tetrahydrofolate and CO2, thereby controlling folate pool distribution, purine synthesis, methylation reactions, and cellular redox balance through integration of formate metabolism with nucleotide biosynthesis pathways. Structural architecture reveals an exceptionally well-folded organization with outstanding AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 94.0): Three distinct functional domains forming a bifunctional enzyme architecture—N-terminal hydrolase domain (cd08647/G3DSA:3.40.50.170/IPR002376, residues 1-203/1-205/1-180) containing the phosphoribosylglycinamide formyltransferase active site (IPR001555, residues 131-154) for formyl group transfer, C-terminal hydrolase subdomain (cd08703/G3DSA:3.10.25.10/IPR005793, residues 206-306/206-307) completing the hydrolase module, linker domain (G3DSA:1.10.1200.10, residues 318-398) connecting hydrolase and dehydrogenase modules enabling conformational flexibility, and C-terminal aldehyde dehydrogenase domain (cd07140/G3DSA:3.40.309.10+3.40.605.10, residues 417-902/425-675+676-867+868-897) containing the catalytic cysteine and NADP+ binding site for oxidative decarboxylation, GO term biosynthetic process (GO:0009058) and oxidoreductase molecular function.
