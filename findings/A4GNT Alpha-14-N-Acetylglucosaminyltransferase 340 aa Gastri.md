## Description
A4GNT (Alpha-1,4-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase, UniProt Q9UNA3) is a 340 amino acid Golgi-resident glycosyltransferase with limited characterization (23 papers) that functions as a key enzyme in O-glycan biosynthesis, specifically adding α1,4-linked N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) residues to the core protein of gastric mucin MUC6, thereby shaping the gastric microbiome and regulating mucosal inflammation. The protein contains a conserved glycosyltransferase DXD sugar-binding motif (IPR007577/PF04488 residues 64-179, GO:0008375 acetylglucosaminyltransferase activity, GO:0005525 GTP binding), an Alpha 1,4-glycosyltransferase domain (IPR007652/PF04572 residues 197-321, GO:0008375), and belongs to the nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily (IPR029044/SSF53448 residues 38-321, GO:0016758 transferase activity), forming the Alpha-1,4-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase family (IPR051981/PTHR12042 residues 1-325). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with mucins and glycosyltransferases: MUC6 (0.843, gastric mucin—structural scaffold), GCNT4 (0.725, core 2 beta-1,6-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase—O-glycan branching), MUC5AC (0.659, gel-forming mucin), MUC20 (0.606), C1GALT1 (0.577, core 1 synthase—Tn antigen formation), MUC1 (0.569), GKN1 (0.550, gastrokine 1—mucosal repair), MUC2 (0.535), GCNT3 (0.533), and GCNT1 (0.506), confirming integration into the gastric mucin glycosylation network. HPA data shows tissue-enriched expression with top expression in stomach (22.3 nTPM), detected in some tissues, group-enriched cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, group-enriched single cell specificity, classification as metabolic proteins and predicted intracellular proteins, with glycosyltransferase/transferase molecular functions. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 3q22.1-29/3q13.31-26.31/3p26.3-q29 (chr3:132561657-197851986x3/116620308-172042292x3/61892-197851986x3), copy number losses at 3q22.1-25.1/3q22.1-24 (chr3:131235568-150065289x1/132716978-144784743x1), and deletions (NC_000003.11:g.(?_137781658)_(138665815_?)del), though no specific diseases are directly assigned despite associations with gastric dysplasia and inflammation. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 88.8) indicates very high confidence with a well-folded GT-A fold typical of nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferases. Functional hypothesis: A4GNT acts as a gastric mucosal gatekeeper where recent research demonstrates: (1) A4GNT deficiency alters gastric microbiome composition in mice (2025, 1 citation)—direct role in host-microbe interactions, (2) Trefoil factor 2 (TFF2) interacts with A4GNT to regulate inflammation and protect against influenza-induced pneumonia (2025, 0 citations)—mucosal immunity link, (3) A4GNT knockout mice spontaneously develop gastric adenocarcinoma (2018, 24 citations)—tumor suppressor role, (4) Euglena gracilis and paramylon suppress gastric dysplasia in A4GNT KO mice (2021, 12 citations)—dietary modulation of A4GNT-related pathology, (5) STRING partner MUC6 (0.843) is the primary acceptor substrate for A4GNT-mediated GlcNAc addition, (6) DXD motif (residues 64-179) coordinates Mn2+ and UDP-GlcNAc for catalytic transfer, (7) Stomach enrichment (22.3 nTPM) confirms gastric-specific function in mucin glycosylation, (8) 19 ClinVar CNVs at 3q22 indicate dosage sensitivity affecting gastric homeostasis, (9) Mechanistic proposal: A4GNT localizes to the Golgi apparatus where it transfers GlcNAc from UDP-GlcNAc to serine/threonine residues of MUC6 via its catalytic domain (residues 197-321), creating α1,4-GlcNAc-terminated O-glycans that: (a) serve as selective antimicrobial barriers by preventing pathogen adhesion, (b) maintain gastric epithelial integrity by promoting proper mucin gel formation, (c) regulate immune cell recruitment through glycan-dependent signaling, with A4GNT deficiency leading to dysbiosis, chronic inflammation, and spontaneous gastric adenocarcinoma, while A4GNT overexpression may protect against infection and tumorigenesis, making A4GNT a therapeutic target for gastric cancer prevention (enzyme replacement) and microbiome modulation.
