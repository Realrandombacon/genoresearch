## Description
TMEM41B (Transmembrane Protein 41B, UniProt Q5BJD5) is a 291-amino acid integral membrane protein functioning as a phospholipid scramblase critical for autophagosome formation and intracellular lipid trafficking. The protein contains a defining VTT domain (IPR032816/PF09335, residues 129-248) characteristic of the TMEM41A/B family (IPR045014, residues 49-289), which mediates phospholipid bidirectional movement across membrane bilayers. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT=82.2, high confidence) consistent with a stable transmembrane architecture required for membrane remodeling activities. HPA classifies TMEM41B as an essential membrane transporter involved in autophagy, lipid transport, host-virus interactions, and neurogenesis, with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity) and peroxisomal subcellular localization — positioning it at critical membrane contact sites between organelles. STRING interaction network reveals functional associations with autophagy and membrane trafficking machinery: WDR46 (0.768, WD-repeat protein regulating ciliogenesis), VMP1 (0.644, vacuole membrane protein 1 essential for autophagosome formation), C19orf54 (0.592), GOLT1B (0.564, golgi transport protein), APOB (0.557, apolipoprotein B for lipid particle assembly), ATG2A (0.556, autophagy-related protein 2A mediating lipid transfer at phagophore assembly sites), TMEM106B (0.539, lysosomal membrane protein linked to frontotemporal dementia), GLDC (0.533), NADK (0.530), and SMN1 (0.526, survival motor neuron protein). The VMP1 and ATG2A interactions place TMEM41B within the core autophagy machinery where phospholipid scrambling enables phagophore membrane expansion, while APOB association suggests roles in lipoprotein biogenesis. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8K1A5 291aa, zebrafish A1A5V7 282aa) indicates strong evolutionary constraint on this function. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains across 11p15.5-p14.2, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene amplification causes disease. The combination of VTT-mediated scramblase activity, peroxisomal/ER localization, autophagy protein interactions (VMP1, ATG2A), and essential protein classification supports a mechanistic model: TMEM41B functions at mitochondria-associated ER membranes (MAMs) and peroxisomal contact sites to redistribute phospholipids during autophagosome nucleation and lipid droplet formation, with disruption causing defective autophagy, lipid accumulation, and potential neurodegenerative or metabolic disease phenotypes.
