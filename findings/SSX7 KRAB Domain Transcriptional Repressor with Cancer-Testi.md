## Description
SSX7 (Synovial Sarcoma X breakpoint 7, UniProt Q7RTT5) is a 188-amino acid nuclear protein located on chromosome Xp11.22. InterPro analysis reveals dual functional domains: an N-terminal KRAB domain (IPR001909, aa 23-82; IPR003655, aa 20-83) characteristic of C2H2 zinc-finger transcriptional repressors, and a C-terminal SSXRD motif (IPR019041/PF09514, aa 158-187) unique to the SSX family. The KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 co-repressor complexes that induce heterochromatin via HP1 binding and H3K9me3 methylation, while the SSXRD motif mediates protein-protein interactions within Polycomb repressive complexes. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 67.6) indicates medium confidence with partial disorder, consistent with transcriptional regulators requiring conformational flexibility. Human Protein Atlas shows nucleoplasmic and nucleolar localization but no detectable RNA expression in standard tissues, typical of cancer-testis antigens restricted to germ cells and malignancies. STRING interactions reveal 10 partners including SSX3 (0.703), SSX2 (0.613), IGSF22 (0.584), CTAG1B/NY-ESO-1 (0.491), and SPANX family members (0.430-0.471), placing SSX7 within a network of germline-restricted transcriptional regulators frequently reactivated in tumors. ClinVar contains 147 pathogenic variants including copy-number losses and gains across Xp11.22-q28, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity. SSX7 likely functions as an epigenetic silencer during spermatogenesis, with aberrant expression in synovial sarcoma and other cancers driving oncogenic transcriptional programs through Polycomb complex recruitment.
