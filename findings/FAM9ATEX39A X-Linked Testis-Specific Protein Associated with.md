# FAM9A/TEX39A: X-Linked Testis-Specific Protein Associated with Synaptonemal Complex and Meiotic Chromosome Pairing

## Description
FAM9A (family with sequence similarity 9 member A, alias TEX39A) encodes a 332-amino acid X-linked protein belonging to the X-linked lymphocyte-regulated/synaptonemal complex protein 3 family (IPR051443/PTHR19368, residues 40-197). AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT=65.8), indicating a partially folded protein with both ordered and disordered regions, characteristic of meiotic chromosome-associated proteins. HPA RNA-seq shows testis-enriched expression (4.1 nTPM) with protein localization to the sperm midpiece, consistent with a role in spermatogenesis and meiotic chromosome dynamics. STRING interactions strongly support meiotic function: SYCP3 (0.641, synaptonemal complex protein 3, essential for meiotic chromosome pairing), OGFOD1 (0.641, ribosomal protein hydroxylase), ARSF (0.614, arylsulfatase F, X-linked), ANOS1 (0.571, anosmin-1, X-linked KAL1), and NOLC1 (0.438, nucleolar and coiled-body phosphoprotein). The SYCP3 interaction is particularly significant as SYCP3 forms the lateral elements of the synaptonemal complex during meiotic prophase I. ClinVar documents 187 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome Xp, indicating strong dosage sensitivity and potential contribution to X-linked infertility or developmental disorders. Conservation data shows FAM9A is part of an X-chromosome gene family arising through duplication, with paralogs FAM9B and FAM9C. The testis-specific expression pattern, synaptonemal complex family membership, and physical interaction with SYCP3 strongly suggest FAM9A functions in meiotic chromosome synapsis, potentially stabilizing homologous chromosome pairing or regulating meiotic recombination. Disruption of FAM9A function through CNVs may cause meiotic arrest and azoospermia.
