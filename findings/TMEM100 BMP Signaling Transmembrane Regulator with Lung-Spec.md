# TMEM100: BMP Signaling Transmembrane Regulator with Lung-Specific Expression and Disease-Associated Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-23T01:08:55.202551

**Quality Score:** 2.0/10  (E=3.7, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
TMEM100 encodes a 134-amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt Q9NV29) localized to the endoplasmic reticulum, perikaryon, and perinuclear region. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved transmembrane domain (IPR032536, PF16311, positions 1-133) belonging to the Transmembrane and Phosphoinositide-interacting Regulators superfamily (PTHR16100), suggesting membrane anchoring and potential lipid interaction capabilities. Human Protein Atlas data shows TMEM100 is highly expressed in lung tissue (179.9 nTPM), with tissue-enhanced and cancer-enhanced expression patterns, indicating roles in pulmonary development and oncogenesis. The gene participates in BMP (bone morphogenetic protein) signaling pathways, consistent with its classification as a developmental protein involved in differentiation processes. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning 17q21-25, implicating TMEM100 dosage sensitivity in disease etiology. The nucleoplasmic localization combined with transmembrane topology suggests a dual-compartment regulatory mechanism, potentially shuttling between membrane and nuclear compartments to modulate BMP signal transduction. This protein likely functions as a BMP pathway co-regulator, where altered expression or structural disruption contributes to developmental abnormalities and cancer progression through dysregulated TGF-beta superfamily signaling.
