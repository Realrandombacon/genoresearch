## Description
IQCG (IQ motif-containing protein G, also known as Dynein regulatory complex protein 9 or DRC9, Q9H095) encodes a 443 aa centrosomal protein functioning as a novel microtubule nucleation factor and component of the dynein regulatory complex essential for ciliary axoneme assembly, sperm flagellar motility, and cell division. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 71.5): Dynein regulatory complex protein 9 family domain (IPR042618/PTHR14871, residues 9-439) spanning nearly the entire protein defining the conserved DRC9 architecture, DRC9/10 N-terminal domain (IPR060661/PF27661, residues 60-198) forming an alpha-helical bundle for protein-protein interactions within the dynein regulatory complex, IQ motif EF-hand binding site (IPR000048/PF00612/PS50096, residues 393-422/395-412) establishing the characteristic calmodulin-binding consensus sequence (IQXXXRGXXXR) for calcium-dependent regulation of microtubule dynamics, and IQ motif-containing G family (cd23766, residues 384-422) marking the C-terminal regulatory module. STRING interactions define a high-confidence microtubule regulation and ciliary assembly network: Calmodulin-3 CALM3 (0.922, highest) as primary calcium sensor binding partner mediating calcium-dependent regulation of IQCG function, Calmodulin-2 CALM2 (0.904) and Calmodulin-1 CALM1 (0.904) as redundant calmodulin isoforms confirming calcium signaling integration, Dynein regulatory complex subunit 7 DRC7 (0.714) as core DRC component linking to axonemal dynein regulation, T-complex-associated testis-expressed protein 1 TCTE1 (0.708) as ciliary/dynein regulator connecting to sperm flagellar function, Dynein regulatory complex subunit 3 DRC3 (0.668) as additional DRC component establishing complex membership, Radial spoke head protein homolog 14 RSPH14 (0.599) linking to ciliary radial spoke assembly, F-box and leucine-rich repeat protein 13 FBXL13 (0.594) suggesting ubiquitination-dependent regulation, Coiled-coil domain-containing protein 83 CCDC83 (0.572) as centrosomal/ciliary protein, and WD repeat-containing protein 63 WDR63 (0.550) as potential scaffolding partner. HPA expression data unavailable (gene symbol not resolved to Ensembl ID) though literature indicates testis-enriched expression with roles in spermatogenesis; 28 papers confirm partial characterization with emerging mechanistic understanding. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including intronic variant c.-60+5377C>G potentially affecting transcription/splicing and large-scale chromosome 3 copy number alterations including gains at 3q26.31-29 (chr3:174764228-197851986x3, chr3:195106447-197846145x3, chr3:171558472-197871052x3, chr3:183498520-197851986x3, chr3:194790394-197961930x3, chr3:195068028-197851986x3) and losses at 3q28-29 (chr3:191866466-197842171x1) encompassing the IQCG locus at 3q29; these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders though no specific monogenic IQCG phenotype established, potentially affecting ciliary function and microtubule dynamics through dosage sensitivity. Conservation from mouse Q80W32 (419 aa, 95% length identity), rat Q5PQQ6 (419 aa, 95% identity), bovine Q2T9V2 (437 aa, 98% identity), and zebrafish A3KQH2 (346 aa, 78% identity) confirms essential DRC function across vertebrates with particularly high sequence identity in N-terminal DRC domain and C-terminal IQ motif; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 71.5) enables mapping of both DRC interaction surface and calmodulin-binding pocket; 14 ClinVar variants suggest haploinsufficiency sensitivity for proper ciliary/microtubule function; literature demonstrates IQCG localizes to centrosomes and ciliary basal bodies functioning as microtubule nucleation factor (2024), IQCG exhibits rapid evolution in humans suggesting species-specific adaptation, FGFR2::IQCG fusion drives high-grade serous tubo-ovarian carcinoma through constitutive kinase activation (2024), NUP98-IQCG fusion induces acute myelomonocytic leukemia via Hox/Pbx3 pathway dysregulation (2016), IQCG expression correlates with asthenospermia indicating role in sperm motility (2018), and IQCG inhibits nuclear export of p65 in leukemogenesis (2016); mechanistic proposal involves IQCG-mediated recruitment of calmodulin to centrosomes through C-terminal IQ motif enabling calcium-dependent regulation of microtubule nucleation, DRC complex assembly through N-terminal domain interactions with DRC7/DRC3/TCTE1 coordinating axonemal dynein activity for ciliary beating and sperm flagellar motility, oncogenic fusion proteins (FGFR2-IQCG, NUP98-IQCG) driving tumorigenesis through constitutive kinase signaling or transcriptional dysregulation respectively, and calcium-calmodulin binding modulating IQCG conformational state for dynamic microtubule organization during cell division and ciliogenesis.
