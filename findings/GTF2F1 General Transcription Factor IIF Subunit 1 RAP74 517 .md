## Description
GTF2F1 (General Transcription Factor IIF Subunit 1, also known as RAP74, UniProt P35269) is a 517 amino acid protein representing a partially characterized gene with only 21 literature references despite its fundamental role in RNA polymerase II transcription initiation.
