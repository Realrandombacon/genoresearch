## Description
PHTF1 (Putative homeodomain transcription factor 1, also known as PHTF) is a 762 amino acid large membrane-associated protein (UniProt Q9UMS5) with medium-confidence partially-disordered architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 61.5), characteristic of proteins with structured domains embedded in flexible scaffolding regions.
