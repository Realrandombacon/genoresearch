## Description
AHCYL2 (Adenosylhomocysteinase Like 2, also known as Long-IRBIT, Q96HN2) encodes a 611 aa metabolic enzyme functioning as an S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH) hydrolase-like protein that regulates one-carbon metabolism, bicarbonate transport, and DNA methylation. Structural architecture reveals canonical adenosylhomocysteinase organization with N-terminal catalytic domain (IPR000043/PF05221/SM00996, residues 178-611/185-320/185-610) containing the substrate-binding pocket, central NAD-binding Rossmann fold domain (IPR015878/PF00670/G3DSA:3.40.50.720, residues 370-531/370-530/376-529) coordinating the essential NAD+ cofactor, and conserved catalytic sites (IPR020082/PS00738/PS00739, residues 257-271/392-409) for SAH hydrolysis. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 79.2) confirms a well-folded structure consistent with enzymatic activity. STRING interactions define a comprehensive one-carbon metabolism and methylation network: cystathionine beta-synthase CBS (0.975), methionine synthase MTR (0.972), cystathionine gamma-lyase CTH (0.971), paralog AHCYL1 (0.964), betaine-homocysteine methyltransferases BHMT/BHMT2 (0.923/0.482), canonical SAH hydrolase AHCY (0.921), kynurenine aminotransferase KYAT1 (0.920), and DNA methyltransferases DNMT1 (0.919)/DNMT3A (0.521). HPA localizes AHCYL2 to nucleoplasm, vesicles, and cytosol with tissue-enhanced expression peaking in choroid plexus (258.8 nTPM) and intestine (166.5 nTPM), classified as hydrolase enzyme involved in one-carbon metabolism. ClinVar documents 27 pathogenic variants including 7q31-36 CNVs and splice site mutations (c.1206+3G>T). Literature (36 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: miR-665-mediated AHCYL2/BVES regulation in recurrent implantation failure (2x citations, 2024), FGFR2-AHCYL2 fusion gene in intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma (2019), long-IRBIT isoform upregulating electrogenic Na+-HCO3- cotransporter NBCe1-B via PEST domain serine residues (6x citations, 2016), and oncogenic deletion in splenic marginal zone lymphoma. Mechanistic hypothesis: AHCYL2 hydrolyzes SAH to adenosine and homocysteine, maintaining cellular methylation potential by preventing SAH accumulation which inhibits methyltransferases; interaction with DNMT1 suggests direct coupling of SAH clearance to DNA methylation machinery; long-IRBIT isoform contains extended N-terminal region enabling protein-protein interactions with NBCe1-B bicarbonate transporter, regulating pH homeostasis and cell volume; in cancer, AHCYL2 supports rapid proliferation by sustaining one-carbon flux for nucleotide synthesis and maintaining epigenetic plasticity via DNMT1 association; FGFR2-AHCYL2 fusion may constitutively activate both kinase signaling and metabolic reprogramming.
