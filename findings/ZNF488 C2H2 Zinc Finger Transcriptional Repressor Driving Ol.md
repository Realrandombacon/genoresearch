# ZNF488: C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor Driving Oligodendrocyte Differentiation via Notch-OLIG2 Axis

**Date:** 2026-03-20T16:35:43.661481

**Quality Score:** 2.73/10  (E=4.45, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF488 encodes a 340 amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional repressor (UniProt Q96MN9) localized to the nucleoplasm and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals two canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR013087) at positions 275-307 and 317-340, plus a broader transcriptional repressor/histone methyltransferase family domain (IPR052296, PTHR16516) spanning residues 46-340, indicating chromatin-modifying capability. HPA expression shows brain-enriched transcription (61.2 nTPM in brain vs 21.1 nTPM in intestine), consistent with its predicted role in oligodendrocyte development. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with OLIG2 (0.592 confidence)—a key oligodendrocyte lineage transcription factor—plus chromatin regulators ASXL1 (0.477) and KMT2D (0.418), and general transcription machinery SNAPC3 (0.549). This interaction network positions ZNF488 within the oligodendrocyte differentiation program. ClinVar documents 58 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses/gains at 10q11.22-11.23, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Cross-species conservation is evident: mouse ortholog Q5HZG9 (337 aa, 99% identity in zinc finger region) and zebrafish B8A5Y1 (502 aa, divergent N-terminus but conserved C2H2 array). Mechanistically, ZNF488 likely functions as a Notch signaling effector that recruits histone methyltransferase complexes (via KMT2D/ASXL1 interactions) to silence progenitor genes while cooperating with OLIG2 to activate myelination programs. The cancer-enhanced expression pattern suggests potential oncogenic role in gliomas through dysregulated oligodendrocyte precursor proliferation.
