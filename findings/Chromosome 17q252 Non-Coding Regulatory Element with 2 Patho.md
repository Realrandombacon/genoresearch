## Description
LOC105371899 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 17q25.2 (NC_000017.11, positions 76969198-76950473, ~18.7 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000267568 but reported no detectable protein expression across any tissue, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, both duplications: variant 155275 (17q24.3-25.3, chr17:69916435-83102552, ~13.2 Mb) and variant 58703 (17q24.3-25.3, chr17:69209079-83086677, ~13.9 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 17q25.2 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including SNHG20 (small nucleolar RNA host gene 20, long non-coding RNA, nucleolar organization, ribosome biogenesis, cancer progression, cell cycle regulation, Wnt/beta-catenin signaling), MGAT5B (alpha-1,6-mannosylglycoprotein 6-beta-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase B, N-glycan branching, cell surface receptor glycosylation, immune regulation, cancer metastasis, TGF-beta signaling), CYCSP40 (cytochrome c pseudogene 40, processed pseudogene, apoptosis pathway remnant), MIR6516 (microRNA 6516, post-transcriptional gene regulation, tumor suppression), and SCARNA16 (small Cajal body-specific RNA 16, spliceosomal RNA modification, telomerase RNA assembly, nucleolar function). Functional hypothesis: LOC105371899 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 17q25.2 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a nucleolus-specific enhancer coordinating SNHG20 lncRNA expression in proliferating cells (ribosome biogenesis, cell cycle progression) or as a chromatin boundary separating glycosylation genes (MGAT5B) from non-coding RNA genes (SNHG20/SCARNA16). The 2 pathogenic CNVs indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves SNHG20 overexpression driving cancer progression (cell cycle dysregulation, Wnt activation, metastasis), SNHG20 haploinsufficiency impairing ribosome biogenesis (nucleolar stress, p53 activation), MGAT5B alteration causing N-glycan branching defects (immune dysfunction, cancer metastasis, TGF-beta resistance), MIR6516 loss promoting tumorigenesis (oncogene derepression), and SCARNA16 dysfunction impairing spliceosome assembly (splicing defects, telomerase dysfunction). The 17q25.2 duplication syndrome may present with cancer predisposition, immune dysregulation, and developmental abnormalities. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (2 duplications 13-14 Mb) and SNHG20-MGAT5B-CYCSP40-MIR6516-SCARNA16 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for ribosome biogenesis-glycosylation-cell cycle coordination.
