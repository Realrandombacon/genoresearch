## Description
LRRC8B encodes an 803 amino acid volume-regulated anion channel (VRAC) subunit that forms heteromeric chloride channels essential for cell volume homeostasis. The protein contains an N-terminal pannexin-like transmembrane domain (PF12534, residues 1-334) that anchors it in the plasma membrane, followed by multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains (IPR001611, IPR003591, PF13306, PF13855) spanning residues 481-714 that mediate protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with high confidence (pLDDT 81.0/100), indicating stable tertiary architecture suitable for channel assembly.

Expression profiling (HPA) shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with enhancement in brain (20.9 nTPM), consistent with VRAC's role in neuronal volume regulation during osmotic stress. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with other LRRC8 paralogs (LRRC8A: 0.511, LRRC8C: 0.504, LRRC8D: 0.479, LRRC8E: 0.634) confirming heteromeric channel assembly
