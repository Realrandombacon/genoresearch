## Description
PPP2R5B (Protein Phosphatase 2 Regulatory Subunit B'beta, also known as B56-beta) is a 497 amino acid regulatory subunit that directs the substrate specificity and subcellular localization of the PP2A heterotrimeric holoenzyme. InterPro analysis reveals a characteristic architecture dominated by an extended Armadillo-like helical fold (IPR011989, IPR016024, residues 69-467; G3DSA:1.25.10.10) comprising multiple HEAT repeats that form a curved solenoid structure for protein-protein interactions, specifically defining the B56 family (IPR002554, PF01603, residues 33-470). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q15173-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 86.1) across the entire sequence, confirming a stable scaffold capable of simultaneous binding to the PP2A catalytic core and diverse substrates. HPA data shows tissue-enriched expression with exceptionally high levels in the brain (112.8 nTPM), suggesting critical roles in neuronal signaling, alongside ubiquitous detection in other tissues. STRING interactions confirm its central role in the PP2A complex: near-perfect association with the catalytic subunits PPP2CA (0.999) and PPP2CB (0.966), and the scaffolding subunits PPP2R1A (0.999) and PPP2R1B (0.980); strong links to other B-family regulators PPP2R2A (0.916) and PPP2R3A (0.906); and specific connections to chromosome segregation factors SGO1 (Shugoshin-1, 0.960) and REC8 (0.907), as well as the apoptosis regulator BCL2 (0.906). ClinVar lists 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1346+3G>C) and large copy number gains on chromosome 11q, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease. Literature (40 papers) identifies PPP2R5B as a critical node in insulin signaling (dephosphorylating Akt substrates), viral entry (regulating ANPEP for coronavirus infection), and spermatogenesis (controlling lactate secretion in Sertoli cells). Its specific recruitment of PP2A to Shugoshin protects centromeric cohesin during mitosis, preventing aneuploidy. As a dark gene with defined structural roles but underexplored therapeutic potential, PPP2R5B represents a master regulator of phospho-signaling networks linking metabolism, cell division, and neurobiology.
