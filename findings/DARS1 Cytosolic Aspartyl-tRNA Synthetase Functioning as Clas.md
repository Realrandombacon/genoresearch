## Description
DARS1 (Aspartyl-tRNA synthetase 1, P14868) encodes a 501 aa cytosolic enzyme functioning as a class II aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase that catalyzes the ATP-dependent ligation of aspartate to its cognate tRNA(Asp), an essential step in protein translation fidelity, with emerging non-canonical roles in cancer progression through SAGA-MYC signaling modulation and neurological disease through hypomyelination disorders, representing a dual-function enzyme bridging fundamental translation machinery to disease pathogenesis.
