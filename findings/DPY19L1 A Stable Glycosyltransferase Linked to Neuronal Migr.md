## Description
DPY19L1 (UniProt Q2PZI1) is a 675 aa C-mannosyltransferase located on chromosome 7p14.2. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a highly stable, well-folded domain (pLDDT 88.5), consistent with its role as a membrane-bound glycosyltransferase. Functionally, it localizes to vesicles and interacts with partners like MANBAL and UNC5A, suggesting a role in the secretory pathway and axonal signaling. Clinically, it is associated with 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number losses/gains on chr7), indicating a link to developmental disorders or cancer. Literature (22 papers) confirms its role in radial migration of glutamatergic neurons in the developing neocortex and distinct C-mannosylation of Netrin receptor thrombospondin type 1 repeats. While not a 'dark gene', DPY19L1 represents a partially characterized gene where structural stability and CNV sensitivity may drive its pathogenicity in neuronal development.
