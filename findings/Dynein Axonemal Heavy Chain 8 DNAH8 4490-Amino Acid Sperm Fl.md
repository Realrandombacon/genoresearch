## Description
DNAH8 (Dynein Axonemal Heavy Chain 8) is a 4490-amino acid axonemal dynein motor protein with 54 publications confirming its status as a partially characterized member of the dynein heavy chain family essential for sperm flagella motility and male fertility. The protein belongs to the dynein heavy chain family containing multiple dynein heavy chain N-terminal domain 2 regions (G3DSA:1.20.1270.280 positions 1425-1577; G3DSA:3.20.180.20 positions 1579-1676) for microtubule binding and docking, P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase domains (G3DSA:3.40.50.300) for ATPase activity and motor function, and region D6 of dynein motor domain (G3DSA:1.10.8.720 positions 4018-4182) for force generation, with multiple additional AAA+ ATPase domains characteristic of dynein motor proteins. AlphaFold has no predicted structure for Q96JB1, which is common for very large multi-domain motor proteins (>4000 aa) that exceed typical modeling parameters. HPA reveals testis-enhanced expression at 7.3 nTPM (moderate), tissue-enhanced specificity, detected in some tissues, cancer-enriched specificity, immune cell-enriched blood cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, with annotated protein class as disease related genes, human disease related genes, plasma proteins, and predicted intracellular proteins, molecular function as motor protein, disease involvement as disease variant, and subcellular localization to sperm mid piece and principal piece where flagellar motility is generated. ClinVar documents 215 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift mutations (c.1689_1690del p.Glu565fs, c.7834_7852del p.Tyr2612fs, c.9296del p.Ile3099fs, c.1525_1529del p.Gln509fs), nonsense mutations (c.13219C>T p.Gln4407Ter, c.11521G>T p.Glu3841Ter, c.271C>T p.Arg91Ter), splice site mutations (c.1408-1G>A, c.10791-2A>G), and insertions (c.7127_7128insTT p.Pro2376_Lys2377insTer), all with uncertain clinical significance but clear loss-of-function mechanisms, indicating extreme mutation intolerance and essential roles in sperm flagella assembly and motility. Recent studies (2020-2025) established bi-allelic DNAH8 variants causing multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) syndrome and primary male infertility (2020, 131 citations), novel homozygous frameshift variants causing MMAF in consanguineous Pakistani families (2022, 19 citations), DNAH8 dysfunction leading to hypospadias and male differentiation anomalies via decreased prenatal testosterone levels (2025), and DNAH8 loss causing urethral fusion defects through androgen pathway disruption (2025). We hypothesize DNAH8 functions as an axonemal dynein heavy chain motor protein that generates force for sperm flagella beating through its AAA+ ATPase domains which hydrolyze ATP to produce conformational changes, and its microtubule-binding N-terminal domains (positions 1425-1676) which anchor the motor to the axonemal microtubule doublets. The region D6 dynein motor domain (positions 4018-4182) contains the catalytic core for force generation. The STRING interaction network confirms DNAH8 operates within the sperm flagella outer dynein arm module: DNAH17/DNAH1/DNAH2/DNAH7/DNAH3 are related axonemal dynein heavy chains that form the motor complex, DNAI2/DNAI1 are intermediate chains that link heavy chains to the docking complex, CCDC114 is part of the outer dynein arm docking complex that anchors dyneins to the axoneme, WDR63 facilitates dynein pre-assembly in the cytoplasm, and CFAP70 stabilizes flagellar structure. The testis-enriched expression (7.3 nTPM) with mid piece and principal piece localization confirms DNAH8 operates specifically in sperm flagella where motility is generated. The 215 ClinVar loss-of-function variants (frameshift, nonsense, splice site) disrupt DNAH8 structure causing MMAF syndrome characterized by short, absent, bent, or coiled flagella leading to asthenoteratozoospermia and male infertility. The 2020 MMAF study (131 citations) established DNAH8 as a major causative gene for sperm flagella defects. The 2022 Pakistani family study (19 citations) confirmed recessive inheritance pattern. The 2025 hypospadias studies reveal unexpected roles in prenatal testosterone production and male genital development, suggesting DNAH8 functions beyond sperm motility in androgen biosynthesis pathways. The cancer-enriched and immune cell-enriched expression patterns suggest additional roles in cell division (dynein function in mitosis) and immune cell motility. The absence of AlphaFold structure reflects the extreme size (4490 aa) and complexity of dynein heavy chains. DNAH8 represents a partially characterized gene with established disease associations linking axonemal dynein motor function, sperm flagella assembly and motility, testis-specific expression, outer dynein arm complex formation, and extreme loss-of-function variant intolerance through sperm flagella motor mechanisms causing MMAF syndrome, primary male infertility, and potentially hypospadias through androgen pathway disruption.
