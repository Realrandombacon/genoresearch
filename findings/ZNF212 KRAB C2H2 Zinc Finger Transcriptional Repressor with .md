# ZNF212: KRAB C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Extensive Pathogenic Copy Number Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-20T14:47:34.018091

**Quality Score:** 3.15/10  (E=4.5, D=1.8) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF212 encodes a 495-amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional regulator (UniProt Q9UDV6) featuring an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 141-219) followed by three C2H2 zinc finger arrays (PF00096, IPR013087 at positions 316-338, 369-394, 428-449). The KRAB domain mediates transcriptional silencing through recruitment of TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex, which scaffolds SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins for H3K9me3 deposition. STRING interactions reveal strong partnerships with KRAB-ZFP family members ZNF282 (0.840), ZNF746 (0.837), ZNF398 (0.829), ZNF783 (0.725), and ZNF408 (0.707), plus canonical corepressor TRIM28 (0.459), consistent with heteromeric zinc finger regulatory complexes. HPA data shows nucleoplasmic localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity), indicating a housekeeping transcriptional regulatory role. ClinVar documents 68 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number losses and gains spanning 7q34-36.3, suggesting extreme dosage sensitivity; the large CNV burden implies ZNF212 haploinsufficiency or triplosufficiency contributes to developmental phenotypes though specific disease associations remain uncharacterized. The three zinc finger arrays likely recognize distinct DNA sequence motifs (5-GTG-3 variants) enabling locus-specific targeting, while the KRAB domain provides epigenetic silencing effector function.
