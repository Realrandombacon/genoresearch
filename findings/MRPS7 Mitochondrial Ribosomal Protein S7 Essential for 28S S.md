## Description
MRPS7 (Q9Y2R9) encodes a 242 amino acid mitochondrial ribosomal protein S7 (uS7m) that serves as an essential structural and functional component of the mitochondrial 28S small ribosomal subunit required for mitochondrial protein synthesis and oxidative phosphorylation. InterPro analysis reveals a conserved ribosomal protein architecture with multiple overlapping domain classifications: a mammalian mitochondrial homolog of ribosomal protein S7 domain (cd14870, positions 35-240) specific to vertebrate mitochondrial ribosomes; a small ribosomal subunit protein uS7 family domain (IPR000235/PF00177, positions 58-235/82-234) representing the bacterial S7p/eukaryotic S5e homolog adapted for mitochondrial translation; a uS7 domain superfamily (IPR023798/IPR036823/SSF47973, positions 35-240/82-234) indicating evolutionary conservation from bacteria to mitochondria; and G3DSA ribosomal protein S7 domain (G3DSA:1.10.455.10, positions 72-240) reflecting the characteristic beta-barrel fold of small subunit ribosomal proteins. AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT 86.8) consistent with a stable beta-barrel ribosomal protein fold required for precise rRNA binding and subunit assembly. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive partnerships with mitochondrial ribosome components at maximum confidence: MRPL17 (0.999), large subunit protein bridging small and large subunits; MRPS9 (0.999), MRPS11 (0.999), MRPS16 (0.999), MRPS5 (0.999), MRPS2 (0.999), and MRPS10 (0.998), small subunit proteins forming the 28S particle; TUFM (0.999), mitochondrial elongation factor Tu delivering aminoacyl-tRNAs; DAP3 (0.999), death-associated protein 3 essential for mitochondrial translation initiation; MRPL27 (0.992), large subunit protein indicating intersubunit contacts during translation. HPA shows ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity reflecting fundamental role in cellular energy production, exclusive mitochondrial localization consistent with mitochondrial ribosome function, classified under disease-related genes/essential proteins/human disease-related proteins/ribosomal proteins/predicted intracellular proteins with ribonucleoprotein and ribosomal protein molecular functions, primary mitochondrial disease involvement confirming pathogenic variant consequences. ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations affecting conserved residues (c.704C>T p.Ala235Val at C-terminal boundary, c.701G>A p.Arg234His disrupting rRNA contacts, c.536G>A p.Arg179His in core domain, c.373A>T p.Lys125Ter causing truncation), promoter variants (c.-176G>A affecting transcription), and multiple copy number gains spanning chromosome 17q24.1-25.3 (chr17:70161447-81041938x3, chr17:72718277-74142256x3, chr17:73261871-78608763x3, chr17:62778720-81041938x3, chr17:64159738-74891024x3) though specific disease names beyond mitochondrial disease phenotypes remain incompletely annotated in ClinVar database. Literature (26 papers) defines MRPS7 as critical for mitochondrial function: homozygous mutation causes congenital sensorineural deafness, progressive hepatic and renal failure, and lactic acidemia demonstrating multi-organ mitochondrial dysfunction; integral role in ovarian function with MRPS7 variants causing syndromic premature ovarian insufficiency linking mitochondrial translation to reproductive aging; co-targeting MRPS7-23 synergistically enhances cisplatin efficacy in nasopharyngeal carcinoma suggesting therapeutic vulnerability in tumors dependent on mitochondrial translation.
