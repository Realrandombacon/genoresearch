## Description
CARNS1 (Carnosine synthase 1, also known as ATP-dependent beta-alanine-histidine dipeptide synthase, A5YM72) encodes a 827 aa mitochondrial enzyme functioning as the dedicated ATP-dependent ligase that catalyzes the final step in carnosine (beta-alanyl-L-histidine) biosynthesis by condensing beta-alanine and L-histidine into the dipeptide, thereby regulating intracellular carnosine pools critical for pH buffering, antioxidant defense, metal ion chelation, anti-glycation activity, and neuromodulation in excitable tissues.
