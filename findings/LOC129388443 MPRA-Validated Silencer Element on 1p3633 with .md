# LOC129388443: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 1p36.33 with High Burden of 14 Recurrent Pathogenic CNVs

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:24:23.438388

**Quality Score:** 2.4/10  (E=3.8, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129388443 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 1p36.33 (GRCh38: 9768016-9768216), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a substantial burden among characterized silencer elements. Variants include 12 deletions (1p36.33-35.1, 1p36.31-36.13, 1p36.22-36.21, 1p36.31-36.21, 1p36.32-36.21, 1p36.33-36.22, 1p36.23-36.22, 1p36.23-36.21, 1p36.23-36.13 spanning ~1.5-34Mb) and 2 duplications. These recurrent CNVs are associated with 1p36 deletion syndrome—the most common terminal deletion disorder—featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, hypotonia, seizures, cardiomyopathy, craniofacial dysmorphism (deep-set eyes, midface hypoplasia), and skeletal abnormalities.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129388443 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 1p36.33 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental genes. Candidate targets include PRDM16 (transcriptional regulator, haploinsufficiency causes cardiomyopathy and cognitive defects), TP73 (tumor suppressor, neurodevelopment), GABRD (GABA receptor delta subunit, seizure susceptibility), or KCNAB2 (potassium channel, cardiac arrhythmia). The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The high CNV burden (14 variants) indicates dosage sensitivity—silencer loss causes derepression of neurodevelopmental regulators, while silencer gain causes co-repressor titration and TAD disruption. The 1p36.33 telomeric location near PRDM16 and neurodevelopmental genes explains the 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes (seizures, cardiomyopathy, intellectual disability). K562 erythroleukemia validation suggests potential hematopoietic regulatory roles.
