# MFSD10 (SLC75A1): Major Facilitator Superfamily Transporter Regulating Apoptosis and Drug Efflux via High-Confidence Structural Fold and 129 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants

**Date:** 2026-04-16T10:39:37.425716

## Description
MFSD10 (UniProt Q14728) encodes a 455 amino acid Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) transporter protein localized to the nuclear membrane and nucleoplasm (HPA). InterPro analysis confirms canonical MFS transporter domains (IPR011701, PF07690, PTHR23504) spanning aa 28-412, consistent with its predicted function in organic anion transport and apoptosis regulation. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q14728-F1) predicts a highly stable, well-folded 3D structure with very high confidence (global pLDDT 91.9), supporting its role as a functional membrane-associated transporter despite nuclear localization. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low cancer specificity, yet ClinVar reveals 129 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on chromosome 4p16.3, with no specific monogenic disease annotated, suggesting a role in developmental regulation or susceptibility to complex traits. The convergence of MFS domain architecture, nuclear membrane localization, high structural confidence, and significant CNV burden supports a functional hypothesis: MFSD10 functions as a nuclear membrane-associated transporter that regulates the efflux of organic anions (including anti-inflammatory drugs like indomethacin) and potentially modulates apoptotic signaling; disruption of this transporter via copy number variations may impair cellular homeostasis or drug response, contributing to disease susceptibility.
