## Description
GOLGA3 (Golgin Subfamily A Member 3, also known as Mea2 in mice, UniProt Q08378) is a massive 1,498 amino acid golgin representing a genuine dark gene with only 34 literature references despite its critical role in spermatogenesis and Golgi architecture. GOLGA3 contains a defining Microtubule and Golgi organization protein domain (IPR051841, positions 103-1490; PTHR18902, positions 103-1490) and a CATH-Gene3D superfamily fold (G3DSA:1.10.287.1490, positions 866-1034), characteristic of long coiled-coil proteins that tether vesicles and organize the Golgi ribbon. The AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 67.8), reflecting the expected architecture of golgins: a mix of structured coiled-coil bundles interspersed with flexible, intrinsically disordered linkers that allow dynamic tethering.
