# CLPB (Caseinolytic Mitochondrial Matrix Peptidase Chaperone Subunit B): AAA+ Hexameric Disaggregase Resolving Protein Aggregates via Ankyrin Repeat Substrate Recognition and Dual ATPase Domains, with Pathogenic Variants Causing 3-Methylglutaconic Aciduria Type VII and Neutropenia-Epilepsy-Cataract Syndrome

## Description
CLPB encodes a 707 aa mitochondrial AAA+ disaggregase (UniProt Q9H078), homologous to bacterial ClpB and yeast Hsp104, that functions as a critical protein quality control enzyme resolving aggregated proteins within the mitochondrial matrix. The protein features a sophisticated multi-domain architecture characteristic of type II AAA+ ATPases: N-terminal ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, residues 130-198 and 258-323; G3DSA:1.25.40.20) mediating substrate recognition and protein-protein interactions; two catalytic AAA+ ATPase domains (IPR003593/IPR003959, residues 373-565) forming the hexameric ring structure essential for ATP-dependent unfolding activity; a ClpA/B family domain (IPR001270, residues 377-470) containing conserved ATP-binding motifs; and a C-terminal Clp ATPase domain (IPR019489, residues 571-660; G3DSA:1.10.8.60) completing the motor apparatus. STRING interactions reveal CLPB operates within an extensive mitochondrial chaperone network: CLPX (0.971), the AAA+ unfoldase partnering in substrate processing; HSPA4/HSP90AA1/HSP90AB1 (0.874-0.941), cytosolic heat shock proteins suggesting cross-compartment coordination; HSPD1/HSPE1 (0.826-0.931), the mitochondrial Hsp60/Hsp10 chaperonin complex for protein folding; GRPEL1/GRPEL2 (0.745-0.910), nucleotide exchange factors regulating Hsp70 activity; CLPP (0.549), mitochondrial caseinolytic protease for degradation; and DNAJB1 (0.545), J-domain co-chaperone recruiting Hsp70. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with testis enrichment (120 nTPM), immune cell enhancement, and disease involvement in cataract, epilepsy, and intellectual disability. ClinVar lists 145 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (c.1492_1493insATTTG p.Val498fs, c.788del p.Leu263fs, c.319del p.Val107fs), missense mutations (c.1879G>C p.Asp627His, c.1759T>C p.Tyr587His, c.797A>G p.Asn266Ser, c.331G>A p.Ala111Thr), and splice variants, causing 3-methylglutaconic aciduria type VII (MGCA7) characterized by severe neutropenia, epilepsy, developmental delay, and congenital cataracts. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 71.8), revealing well-folded hexameric ring architecture with central pore for substrate translocation. Mechanistically, CLPB operates through: (1) Hexamer assembly—six CLPB monomers form ring-shaped homo-hexamer powered by ATP hydrolysis; (2) Aggregate recognition—ankyrin repeats bind exposed hydrophobic patches on aggregated proteins; (3) Threadase activity—AAA+ domains thread substrates through central pore using ATP-driven conformational changes; (4) Disaggregation—mechanical force unfolds aggregates allowing refolding by HSPD1/HSPE1 or degradation by CLPP; (5) Mitochondrial proteostasis—maintains protein quality under stress conditions preventing aggregation-induced dysfunction. Loss-of-function causes accumulated protein aggregates triggering mitochondrial stress, impaired oxidative phosphorylation, neutrophil apoptosis (neutropenia), neuronal hyperexcitability (epilepsy), and lens protein aggregation (cataracts).
