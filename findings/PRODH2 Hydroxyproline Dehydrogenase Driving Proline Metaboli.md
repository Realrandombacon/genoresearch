## Description
PRODH2 (Proline dehydrogenase 2, also known as HYPDH/hydroxyproline dehydrogenase, UniProt Q9UF12) is a 460 aa FAD-dependent oxidoreductase that catalyzes the first step of hydroxyproline catabolism, converting 4-hydroxyproline to pyrroline-5-carboxylate. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical proline dehydrogenase domain (IPR002872, PF01619, positions 186-434) within the proline oxidase family (IPR015659, positions 20-451) with FAD-linked oxidoreductase architecture (IPR029041, positions 87-453; G3DSA:3.20.20.220, positions 55-166/204-451; SSF51730, positions 87-172/210-453). AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 87.6), indicating a well-folded enzyme with stable FAD-binding geometry. HPA data confirms liver-enriched expression (187.9 nTPM) and kidney expression (154.3 nTPM) with group-enriched tissue specificity, nucleoplasm/cytosol localization, and proline metabolism biological process classification. STRING interactions reveal tight functional clustering with proline/hydroxyproline metabolic pathway: ALDH4A1 (0.983, aldehyde dehydrogenase 4 family member A1 - the downstream enzyme converting P5C to glutamate), PYCR1/PYCR2/PYCR3 (0.918/0.917/0.786, pyrroline-5-carboxylate reductases catalyzing the reverse reaction), P4HA1/P4HA2 (0.908/0.915, prolyl 4-hydroxylases producing hydroxyproline in collagen), OAT (0.785, ornithine aminotransferase), and mitochondrial respiratory chain components MT-CO1/2/3 (0.722-0.735). ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 19q11-13.33. Literature (49 papers) establishes PRODH2 roles in: (1) breast cancer bone metastasis via hydroxyproline metabolism driving osteoclast differentiation and suppressing ferroptosis (2025), (2) primary hyperoxaluria treatment target with N-propargylglycine as suicide inhibitor (2015-2023), (3) CAR T-cell effector function enhancement through proline metabolism (2022), and (4) plant pathogen response regulation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: PRODH2 serves as a metabolic switch controlling hydroxyproline flux between collagen turnover and energy production, with implications for cancer metastasis, immune cell function, and oxalate homeostasis. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: PRODH2 oxidizes 4-hydroxyproline (derived from collagen degradation via P4HA1/2) to P5C using FAD as cofactor, transferring electrons to the mitochondrial electron transport chain (explaining MT-CO interactions). P5C is then converted to glutamate by ALDH4A1 or back to proline by PYCR1/2/3. In bone metastasis, tumor-derived hydroxyproline fuels PRODH2-mediated metabolism in osteoclasts, promoting bone resorption. In CAR T-cells, PRODH2 overexpression enhances proliferation through proline-derived ATP production. In hyperoxaluria, PRODH2 inhibition prevents oxalate accumulation from hydroxyproline catabolism. The 14 ClinVar variants suggest PRODH2 dysfunction may contribute to metabolic disorders, though specific disease associations remain undefined.
