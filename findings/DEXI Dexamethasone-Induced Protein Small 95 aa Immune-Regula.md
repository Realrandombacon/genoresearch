## Description
DEXI (Dexamethasone-Induced Protein, UniProt O95424) is a small 95 amino acid protein with moderate-to-high characterization (1828 papers, though many relate to dexamethasone drug studies rather than the protein itself) that functions as an immune-regulatory factor induced by glucocorticoid signaling, localizing to nuclear speckles and cytosol where it potentially modulates MHC class II expression and autoimmune responses through interactions with CLEC16A and CIITA. The protein contains a dexamethasone-induced protein family domain (IPR023259/PF15198/PTHR17070 residues 1-95) with three conserved motifs (PR02032 residues 25-42/43-59/60-78) defining the DEXI family signature, representing a small conserved protein family of unknown molecular mechanism.
