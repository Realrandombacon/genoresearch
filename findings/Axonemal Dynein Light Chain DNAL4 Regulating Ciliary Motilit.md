## Description
DNAL4 (Dynein Axonemal Light Chain 4, UniProt O96015) is a 105-amino acid component of axonemal dynein complexes functioning in ciliary/flagellar motility and intracellular transport. InterPro analysis confirms canonical dynein light chain architecture: dynein light chain type 1/2 family (IPR001372/PF01221, aa 15-104/21-104) with dynein light chain-like domain (cd21453, aa 21-103), dynein light chain superfamily fold (IPR037177/G3DSA:3.30.740.10/SSF54648, aa 10-104), and GO annotations for microtubule-based process (GO:0007017) and dynein complex membership (GO:0030286). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with dynein complex components: DYNLT1 (0.782, dynein light chain Tctex-type 1), FHL5 (0.776, four and a half LIM domains 5), DYNLT3 (0.761, dynein light chain Tctex-type 3), DYNLL2 (0.739, dynein light chain LC8-type 2), DYNLRB1 (0.736, dynein light chain roadblock-type 1), DNAI2 (0.731, dynein axonemal intermediate chain 2), DNAI1 (0.715, dynein axonemal intermediate chain 1), HOXA4 (0.712, homeobox A4), DYNLRB2 (0.687), and DNALI1 (0.675, dynein axonemal light intermediate chain 1), confirming integration into axonemal dynein motor complexes. HPA shows low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues) with cell type-enriched expression pattern and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nucleoli, primary cilium transition zone, centrosome, and basal body - consistent with ciliary/centrosomal functions. AlphaFold predicts very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.4) for the full 105 aa, indicating a well-folded compact dynein light chain fold with stable dimerization interface. ClinVar documents 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number variations on chromosome 22q11.1-13.33 (gains and losses) and splice variants (c.69+293C>T), though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely defined. Literature (16 papers) reveals emerging functions: (1) DNAL4 associated with congenital mirror movements (CMM) - 2024 study defining genetic landscape in 80 affected individuals (13 citations); (2) No DNAL4 mutations found in 17 CMM index cases suggesting genetic heterogeneity (9 citations, 2014); (3) Porcine DNAL4 SNP g.1007A>G affects sperm motility traits and midpiece abnormalities (7 citations, 2015); (4) Recent 2025 studies identify DNAL4 among CMM-associated genes alongside DCC, NTN1, RAD51, ARHGEF7. Functional hypothesis: DNAL4 serves as a light chain component of axonemal dynein motors regulating ciliary beating, sperm flagellar motility, and neuronal axon guidance during brain development, with loss-of-function causing congenital mirror movements through disrupted corpus callosum wiring and ipsilateral corticospinal tract projections. Mechanistic proposal: The dynein light chain domain (aa 15-104) forms a conserved dimeric fold that binds cargo proteins and intermediate chains (DNAI1, DNAI2) within the axonemal dynein complex; DNAL4 integrates into outer dynein arms of cilia/flagella where it regulates dynein motor activity through light chain-mediated cargo recognition and motor complex assembly; in sperm, DNAL4 dysfunction impairs flagellar beating causing midpiece abnormalities and reduced motility; in developing brain, DNAL4-dependent dynein transport regulates axon guidance cues and corpus callosum formation - loss causes failure of contralateral corticospinal tract decussation resulting in mirror movements where voluntary unilateral movements trigger involuntary contralateral movements; centrosomal/basal body localization suggests roles in ciliogenesis and cell division; copy number variants on 22q may disrupt DNAL4 dosage affecting dynein complex stoichiometry and ciliary function.
