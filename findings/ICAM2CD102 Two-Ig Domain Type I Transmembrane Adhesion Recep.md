# ICAM2/CD102: Two-Ig Domain Type I Transmembrane Adhesion Receptor Binding LFA-1 Integrin in Lymphocyte Recirculation, Lupus Nephritis, Gastric Cancer Chemoresistance, and Leptomeningeal Metastasis

## Description
ICAM2 (275 aa, UniProt P13598), also known as CD102 or intercellular adhesion molecule 2, is a type I transmembrane glycoprotein residing in the plasma membrane that functions as a critical ligand for leukocyte function-associated antigen-1 (LFA-1/ITGAL-ITGB2 integrin) mediating lymphocyte recirculation, transendothelial migration, and immune cell adhesion through its two N-terminal immunoglobulin-like C2-type domains. The protein architecture features canonical ICAM domain organization: an N-terminal I-set immunoglobulin domain (cd20995/IPR013768/G3DSA:2.60.40.10/IPR036179, aa27-109/24-114/25-109/25-110) forming the primary LFA-1 binding interface containing critical adhesive recognition determinants; a second C2-set immunoglobulin domain (cd05755/G3DSA:2.60.40.10/IPR036179, aa111-211/110-218/108-206) providing structural stability and potential secondary binding sites; ICAM/VCAM N-terminal motifs (IPR003987, aa35-51+109-122+126-141) and intercellular adhesion molecule signatures (IPR003988, aa121-134+200-216) defining the adhesion molecule family; immunoglobulin-like fold superfamily (IPR013783) characteristic of cell surface receptors. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with lymphoid tissue enrichment (156.5 nTPM top tissue), detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, plasma membrane and cytosolic localization, and classification as CD marker/plasma protein/predicted membrane protein with cell-cell adhesion molecular function. Mechanistically, ICAM2 forms ultra-high-confidence interactions within the leukocyte adhesion network: ITGAM (STRING 0.996) and ITGAL (0.994) are integrin alpha M and alpha L chains forming Mac-1 and LFA-1 heterodimers that bind ICAM2's Ig domains; RDX (0.992), EZR (0.974), and MSN (0.458) are radixin/ezrin/moesin ERM family proteins linking ICAM2 cytoplasmic tail to actin cytoskeleton controlling membrane dynamics and microvilli formation; ICAM1 (0.729) and ICAM3 (0.602) are related adhesion molecules with overlapping ligand specificity; F11R (0.616) is junctional adhesion molecule-A involved in tight junction formation; CD209 (0.612) is DC-SIGN C-type lectin on dendritic cells; ITGB2 (0.479) is integrin beta-2 chain pairing with ITGAL/ITGAM. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 87.1) reveals high-confidence well-folded two-domain immunoglobulin structure with N-terminal Ig domain exposed for LFA-1 binding connected by short linker to second Ig domain, single transmembrane helix, and short cytoplasmic tail consistent with crystallographic structures of ICAM family members showing Ig domain beta-sandwich folds. Clinical significance includes 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants: nonsense mutation c.61G>T (p.Gly21Ter) truncating the first Ig domain abolishing LFA-1 binding, splice region variant c.649+35T>G affecting mRNA processing, large copy number gains spanning 17q23.2-24.1/17q23.1-24.2/17q22-24.2/17p13.3-q25.3/17q21.31-25.3/17q22-23.3/17q23.1-25.1 (chr17:58773496-62757149, 57869604-67078443, 57357088-66306668, 526-81041938, 42580684-81085615, 56321134-62080001, 36449220-75053130 amplifications). Conservation across mammals (mouse P35330 277 aa, gorilla Q5NKV1 275 aa, chimpanzee Q5NKV2 275 aa) indicates essential adhesion function maintained over ~90 million years. Literature (203 papers, WELL-STUDIED status) establishes ICAM2 in autoimmune disease, cancer metastasis, and chemoresistance: 2025 proteomics identifies ICAM2 as novel serum biomarker of proliferative lupus nephritis enabling non-invasive diagnosis; 2026 studies demonstrate ICAM2 loss drives 5-fluorouracil resistance via TGF-beta/Smad/SP1/PTN-dependent apoptosis evasion and macrophage remodeling in gastric cancer; 2023 research shows ICAM2 initiates trans-blood-CSF barrier migration and stemness properties in leptomeningeal metastasis of triple-negative breast cancer; 2026 work identifies ICAM2 promoting endocrine resistance via dynein-mediated OXPHOS activation in ER-positive breast cancer; 2022 studies show artemisitene suppresses rheumatoid arthritis progression via modulating METTL3-mediated N6-methyladenosine modification of ICAM2 mRNA in fibroblast-like synoviocytes. The functional hypothesis posits that ICAM2 acts as a 'LFA-1 adhesion rheostat controlling immune cell trafficking and tumor cell dissemination': on endothelial cells, ICAM2 presents two Ig domains optimally spaced for high-affinity LFA-1 binding on circulating lymphocytes; ERM proteins anchor ICAM2 cytoplasmic tail to cortical actin stabilizing adhesive contacts during transendothelial migration; in lymphoid tissues, ICAM2 enrichment (156.5 nTPM) facilitates lymphocyte recirculation through high endothelial venules; in lupus nephritis, elevated serum ICAM2 reflects endothelial activation and immune complex deposition; in gastric cancer, ICAM2 loss activates TGF-beta/Smad/SP1 pathway upregulating pleiotrophin (PTN) promoting apoptosis evasion and M2 macrophage polarization conferring 5-FU resistance; in breast cancer leptomeningeal metastasis, ICAM2 enables tumor cell crossing of blood-CSF barrier through LFA-1 engagement on meningeal macrophages while maintaining stemness properties; therapeutic strategies include ICAM2-blocking antibodies for autoimmune disease, ICAM2 restoration for gastric cancer chemosensitivity, and ICAM2-LFA1 axis inhibition for preventing CNS metastasis.
