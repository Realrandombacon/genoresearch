## Description

Critically, no UniProt accession was identified despite comprehensive database searches, suggesting this locus may not encode a canonical protein or represents a pseudogene/lncRNA rather than a protein-coding gene. Functional hypothesis: LOC101929662 may represent a non-coding RNA (lncRNA or miRNA precursor) with regulatory functions, or a pseudogene with transcriptional activity. Alternatively, it could encode a small micropeptide below detection thresholds of current proteomics. The clinical CNV burden suggests this locus resides in a haploinsufficient/triplosensitive genomic neighborhood. Without protein evidence, this locus should be prioritized for transcriptome characterization (RNA-seq, Ribo-seq) to determine coding potential before functional assignment.
