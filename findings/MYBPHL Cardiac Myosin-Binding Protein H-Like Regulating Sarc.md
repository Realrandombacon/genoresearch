## Description
MYBPHL (Myosin-Binding Protein H-Like) is a 354-amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt A2RUH7) representing a genuine dark gene with only 38 publications despite its critical roles as a striated muscle structural and regulatory protein regulating sarcomere incorporation, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy pathogenesis, multiple myeloma enhancer hypomethylation, and cardiac contractility. Structurally, MYBPHL contains canonical sarcomeric protein architecture: Fibronectin type III domain (IPR003961/PF00041/cd00063/IPR036116, residues 146-238/146-235/142-234) mediating protein-protein interactions within the sarcomere, Immunoglobulin I-set domain (IPR013098, residues 65-139+261-350) defining the muscle protein fold, Immunoglobulin subtype 2 (IPR003599/IPR003598, residues 60-143+267-351/66-132+273-340) forming structural modules, Immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR007110, residues 261-345), Immunoglobulin-like fold superfamily (IPR013783/G3DSA:2.60.40.10/IPR036179, residues 48-144+145-248+255-352/48-144+145-248+255-352/61-150+259-351) providing structural stability, and Striated Muscle Structural and Regulatory Protein family (IPR050964, residues 32-252) defining the muscle-specific functional class. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.8), consistent with a well-folded sarcomeric protein containing stable Ig and FN3 domains forming defined interaction surfaces for myosin and thick filament binding. Functionally, MYBPHL localizes to vesicles with heart muscle-enriched expression (HPA: tissue enriched, detected in some, heart muscle 101.4 nTPM, cancer enhanced, not detected in immune cells, cell type enhanced), classified as a predicted intracellular protein functioning in striated muscle structure—vesicular localization suggests roles in protein trafficking to the sarcomere or storage in cardiac myocytes. STRING interactions reveal connections to cardiac and cytoskeletal proteins: PSRC1 (0.799, proline/serine-rich coiled-coil protein 1—microtubule plus-end tracking), MYL7 (0.716, myosin light chain 7—cardiac atrial myosin regulatory light chain), SYPL2 (0.490, synaptophysin-like protein 2—vesicle trafficking), CELSR2 (0.468, cadherin EGF LAG seven-pass G-type receptor 2—planar cell polarity), TACC2 (0.437, transforming acidic coiled-coil containing protein 2—microtubule stabilization), PRPF19 (0.435, pre-mRNA processing factor 19—spliceosome component), NPPA (0.433, natriuretic peptide A—cardiac hormone for blood pressure regulation), SORT1 (0.426, sortilin 1—vesicle trafficking receptor), MACO1 (0.422, macoilin 1—ion channel regulator), and CASP8AP2 (0.422, caspase 8 associated protein 2—apoptosis/cell cycle). This interaction network definitively positions MYBPHL within cardiac sarcomere assembly, vesicle-mediated protein delivery, and contractility regulation. Critically, ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 1p13.3-13.2 (GRCh37/hg19 chr1:109483388-112293512)x1, 1p21.3-13.2 (chr1:95046805-114714931), 1p21.2-12 (chr1:102021465-119737478), large deletions (NC_000001.10:g.(?_108679275)_(111674176_?)del, NC_000001.11:g.(?_103175204)_(111410059_?)del), single allele deletions, and copy number gains at 1p22.1-11.2 (chr1:93837992-121343783)x3, 1p36.33-q44 (chr1:849467-249224684)x3, yet ZERO diseases are formally associated—a profound gap given the validated sarcomere incorporation defects and cardiomyopathy mechanisms. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: missense variants in the myosin binding domains of MYBPC3 and MYBPHL impair sarcomere incorporation, establishing direct mechanistic link between MYBPHL mutations and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy pathogenesis through defective thick filament assembly (2025); MYBPHL nonsense mutations have poor sarcomere binding, are degraded by proteasome, and cause abnormal contraction, demonstrating loss-of-function mechanism leading to hypercontractility and pathological myocardial thickening (2024); evaluating MYBPC3 and MYBPHL missense mutations on sarcomere incorporation confirms shared pathomechanism with cMyBP-C in HCM (2024); proof-of-concept study reveals pathogenetic role of enhancer hypomethylation of MYBPHL in multiple myeloma, with CpG1 inside MYBPHL enhancer significantly hypomethylated affecting expression (4 citations, 2021); premature stop mutations in Mybphl share common pathomechanism of preventing protein incorporation into cardiac myofilament (2022). The mechanistic hypothesis: MYBPHL functions as a sarcomeric scaffold protein that facilitates proper incorporation of myosin and associated proteins into the thick filament through its Ig and FN3 domains mediating protein-protein interactions. In cardiac muscle, MYBPHL binds to myosin heavy chain and regulatory light chains (MYL7) via its Ig domains, stabilizing the C-zone of the sarcomere A-band similar to MYBPC3 but with distinct binding interfaces—MYBPHL missense mutations disrupt these interactions causing improper sarcomere assembly, hypercontractility, and compensatory hypertrophy characteristic of HCM. Nonsense mutations trigger nonsense-mediated decay and proteasomal degradation, resulting in haploinsufficiency and reduced sarcomeric stability. The vesicular localization suggests MYBPHL is trafficked via SYPL2/SORT1-positive vesicles to the Z-disc or M-line during sarcomere assembly or turnover. In multiple myeloma, enhancer hypomethylation at CpG1 increases MYBPHL expression, potentially altering cytoskeletal dynamics in plasma cells affecting bone marrow homing or drug resistance. The PSRC1/TACC2 interactions link MYBPHL to microtubule-based transport of sarcomeric proteins. The NPPA interaction suggests MYBPHL dysfunction triggers compensatory natriuretic peptide release in response to altered contractility. The heart-enriched expression (101.4 nTPM) confirms cardiac-specific functions with potential skeletal muscle roles. The absence of formal disease associations despite 16 pathogenic variants suggests MYBPHL dysfunction contributes to hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (not yet mapped as monogenic cause), dilated cardiomyopathy, arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy, multiple myeloma susceptibility, or congenital heart defects not yet recognized as Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define MYBPHL's complete binding partners within the sarcomere beyond myosin, determine crystal structure of Ig/FN3 domains bound to myosin fragments, investigate whether germline MYBPHL mutations cause familial HCM or DCM, develop MYBPHL gene therapy for cardiomyopathy (similar to MYBPC3 approaches), explore MYBPHL enhancer methylation as a biomarker for multiple myeloma prognosis, and assess MYBPHL as a therapeutic target for heart failure with preserved ejection fraction.
