# LOC129389350: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 5q22.3 with Exceptional Burden of Recurrent Pathogenic CNVs

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:23:00.383932

**Quality Score:** 1.85/10  (E=3.4, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
LOC129389350 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 5q22.3 (GRCh38: 117404139-117404339), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its classification as a non-coding regulatory element.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—the highest burden among all characterized silencer elements in this study. Variants include 11 deletions (5q14.3-31.1, 5q21.3-23.2, 5q14.3-23.1, 5q22.2-23.1, 5q14.3-23.3, 5q22.1-23.3, 5q21.3-23.1, 5q22.1-23.2, 5q22.3-23.2 spanning ~1.6-40Mb) and 1 duplication. These recurrent CNVs are associated with 5q deletion syndromes featuring myelodysplastic syndrome (5q- syndrome), developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal abnormalities, and hematologic defects including thrombocytopenia and neutropenia.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389350 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 5q22.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive hematopoietic and developmental genes. Candidate targets include RPS14 (ribosomal protein S14, haploinsufficiency causes 5q- syndrome phenotype), CTNNA1 (catenin alpha-1, cell adhesion), or APC (adenomatous polyposis coli, Wnt signaling regulator). The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The exceptional CNV burden (12 variants, highest observed) indicates extreme dosage sensitivity—silencer loss causes derepression of proto-oncogenes or developmental regulators, while silencer gain causes co-repressor titration and TAD disruption. The 5q22.3 location near RPS14 and hematopoietic genes explains the 5q- syndrome phenotypes (refractory anemia, thrombocytopenia) in deletion carriers. K562 erythroleukemia validation confirms hematopoietic regulatory roles.
