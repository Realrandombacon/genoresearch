## Description
PPP6R2 (O75170) is a 966-aa serine/threonine-protein phosphatase 6 regulatory subunit with medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 64.6), suggesting flexible architecture with both ordered and disordered regions. InterPro analysis reveals SIT4 phosphatase-associated protein family domain (IPR007587/PF04499: positions 129-531) and multiple armadillo-type folds (IPR011989/IPR016024/SSF48371: positions 23-400), characteristic of scaffold proteins that bridge phosphatases to substrates. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to cytosol and cytoplasmic bodies. STRING interactions identify high-confidence binding to PPP2CA (0.811), ANKRD44 (0.801), ANKRD28 (0.752), and USP10 (0.712), plus medium-confidence interaction with catalytic subunit PPP6C (0.665). Literature (17 papers) demonstrates PPP6R2/SAP190 forms holoenzyme with PPP6C to act as T-loop phosphatase for Aurora A kinase, essential for spindle formation and chromosome alignment during mitosis. Loss of PPP6R2 function disrupts mitotic progression. ClinVar contains 142 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and deletions at 22q13.33, though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Recent studies identify PPP6R2::DNAJB1 fusion in eccrine porocarcinoma and PPP6R2 as candidate gene for developmental dysplasia of the hip. The armadillo repeat architecture suggests PPP6R2 serves as molecular scaffold positioning PP6C near Aurora A and other mitotic kinases, with disordered regions (pLDDT 64.6) enabling dynamic complex assembly during cell cycle progression.
