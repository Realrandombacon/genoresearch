## Description
GPRC5C (aliases: RAIG-3, RAIG3) encodes a 441 amino acid orphan G protein-coupled receptor (UniProt Q9NQ84) belonging to the class C group 5 family. Unlike classical metabotropic glutamate receptors, GPRC5C lacks a large extracellular Venus flytrap domain, possessing instead a short N-terminus and a canonical 7-transmembrane helix bundle (residues 44-299, PF00003). InterPro analysis confirms its classification within the retinoic acid-inducible GPCR family (IPR017978, IPR051753), suggesting regulation by retinoid signaling pathways. STRING interactions link GPRC5C to GRM2 (metabotropic glutamate receptor 2, score 0.833) and other orphan GPCRs (GPR158, GPR156), implying potential heterodimerization or shared downstream effectors. Although HPA RNA-seq data is unavailable for this specific entry, recent literature (56 papers) reveals critical physiological roles: (1) GPRC5C maintains hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) dormancy via hyaluronic acid signaling, preventing exhaustion; (2) In acute myeloid leukemia (AML), GPRC5C drives branched-chain amino acid (BCAA) metabolism by upregulating transporters SLC7A5 and SLC43A1, promoting leukemogenesis; (3) MLH1 suppresses pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma metastasis by downregulating GPRC5C; (4) Facilitates angiotensin II-induced smooth muscle contraction. ClinVar lists 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large copy number gains at 17q24-q25, indicating that gene dosage amplification may be oncogenic or developmentally disruptive. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 70.8) predicts a confident 7TM fold with a structured intracellular C-terminus for G-protein coupling. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: GPRC5C acts as a context-dependent metabolic sensor in stem cells and cancer, where it couples extracellular matrix cues (hyaluronic acid) or undefined ligands to Gi/o-mediated suppression of cAMP, thereby enforcing quiescence in normal HSCs but hijacking BCAA uptake to fuel proliferation in AML. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: Upon activation, GPRC5C recruits Gi/o proteins to inhibit adenylyl cyclase, reducing PKA activity and stabilizing HIF-1α or Myc transcription factors that drive SLC7A5/SLC43A1 expression; in smooth muscle, it may cross-talk with AT1R to enhance calcium sensitization.
