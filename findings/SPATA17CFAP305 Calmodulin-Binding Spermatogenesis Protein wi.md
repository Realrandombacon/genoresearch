## Description
SPATA17 (Spermatogenesis-Associated Protein 17, aliases CFAP305, FAP305, IQCH, MOT17, MSRG11) is a 361-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 1q41. InterPro analysis reveals three tandem IQ calmodulin-binding motifs (IPR000048/PF00612, positions 31-112) characteristic of calcium-dependent regulatory proteins, plus membership in the Abnormal Spindle-like Microcephaly-associated protein family (IPR051185/PTHR22706, positions 7-177) and P-loop NTP hydrolase superfamily (IPR027417, positions 14-114). Human Protein Atlas shows testis-enriched expression at 9.5 nTPM with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and cytosol—consistent with roles in germ cell development and nuclear-cytoplasmic trafficking. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners including cilia/flagella-associated proteins CCDC180 (score 0.682), SPAG6 (0.666), and SPAG17 (0.609), plus chromatin regulators KDM1B (0.614) and DPY30 (0.528), suggesting dual functions in spermatogenesis and epigenetic regulation. AlphaFold structure (Q96L03) shows high confidence (pLDDT 81.6), indicating stable folded domains suitable for calmodulin binding. ClinVar documents 34 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 1q41 and intronic SNVs. The combination of IQ motifs, testis expression, and ciliopathy protein interactions suggests SPATA17 functions as a calcium-calmodulin dependent regulator of flagellar assembly during spermiogenesis, potentially coordinating cytoskeletal dynamics through EF-hand mediated conformational switching.
