## Description
TOR3A (UniProt Q9H497), also known as ADIR or ADIR2, encodes a 397 aa AAA+ ATPase family member functioning as an endoplasmic reticulum (ER) luminal chaperone that regulates antiviral immunity, ER stress responses, and nuclear envelope dynamics through ATP-dependent conformational remodeling mechanisms characteristic of the torsin protein family. InterPro analysis reveals canonical torsin architecture: N-terminal signal peptide directing ER lumen localization; central P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase domain (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/SSF52540/IPR027417, residues 119-318/123-370/119-370) forming the AAA+ ATPase catalytic core with ATP binding (GO:0005524) and hydrolysis (GO:0016887) activities; C-terminal torsin-1A domain (IPR049337/PF21376, residues 338-395) mediating substrate recognition and protein-protein interactions; and full-length torsin family membership (IPR010448/PF06309/PTHR10760, residues 12-396/106-234/12-396) defining ER quality control functions.
