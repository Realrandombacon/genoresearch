## Description
GGPS1 (UniProt O95749), also known as GGPPS, GGPPS1, MDHLO, or MUDHLOV, encodes a 300 aa very high-confidence prenyltransferase enzyme functioning as the sole human geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP) synthase that catalyzes GGPP synthesis from farnesyl diphosphate (FPP) and isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) through integrated trans-isoprenyl diphosphate synthase domain-mediated condensation mechanisms critical for protein geranylgeranylation, mevalonate pathway flux control, and isoprenoid biosynthesis. InterPro analysis reveals canonical polyprenyl synthetase architecture: N-terminal trans-isoprenyl diphosphate synthase head-to-tail domain (cd00685/G3DSA:1.10.600.10/IPR000092/IPR008949/PF00348, residues 10-293/1-300/10-250) defining prenyltransferase activity (GO:0004659) and isoprenoid biosynthetic process (GO:0008299); two conserved catalytic signatures (PS00723 residues 61-75, PS00444 residues 180-192) containing aspartate-rich motifs for substrate binding and Mg2+ coordination; full-length geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate synthase family membership (PTHR12001/SFLDG01017/SFLDS00005/SSF48576, residues 7-292/8-293/9-294) establishing terpenoid synthase superfamily classification. AlphaFold predicts very high confidence structure (pLDDT 94.6) indicating an exceptionally well-folded stable enzyme suitable for constitutive mevalonate pathway incorporation and dynamic substrate processing during isoprenoid biosynthesis. STRING interactions define near-perfect confidence mevalonate pathway network: FDPS (0.996, farnesyl diphosphate synthase, upstream enzyme producing FPP substrate), MVD (0.992, mevalonate diphosphate decarboxylase, upstream mevalonate pathway), IDI1 (0.987, isopentenyl-diphosphate delta isomerase 1, IPP/DMAPP interconversion), FDFT1 (0.986, farnesyl-diphosphate farnesyltransferase 1/squalene synthase, cholesterol branch point), DHDDS (0.967, dehydrodolichyl diphosphate synthase, dolichol synthesis), FNTA (0.962, farnesyltransferase alpha subunit, protein farnesylation), PDSS1 (0.961, decaprenyl diphosphate synthase subunit 1, coenzyme Q synthesis), NUS1 (0.952, dehydrodolichyl diphosphate synthase complex subunit), IDI2 (0.602, isopentenyl-diphosphate delta isomerase 2, peroxisomal), and FNTB (0.420, farnesyltransferase beta subunit).
