# LOC129390601: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element in 13q11-q34 Critical Region - Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 28 Pathogenic CNV Variants

**Quality Score:** 3.25/10  (E=3.3, D=3.2) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC129390601 (Gene ID: 129390601) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 13q14.2 (69,677,163-69,677,363 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak2077 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - mix of large copy number variations affecting the 13q chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 13q12.3-31.3 duplication (31363472-90575292, ~59.2 Mb); (2) 13q12.11-34 duplication (19837395-114327173, ~94.5 Mb); (3) 13q11-34 duplication (18862146-114342258, ~95.5 Mb); (4) 13q12.11-34 duplication (19671934-114340331, ~94.7 Mb); (5) 13q21.31-31.1 deletion (63713365-79638415, ~15.9 Mb); (6) 13q11-34 duplication (18456040-114340285, ~95.9 Mb); (7) 13q12.11-34 duplication (19833130-114327106, ~94.5 Mb); (8) 13q21.31-22.1 duplication (64065900-73693578, ~9.6 Mb); (9) 13q13.1-31.1 duplication (32531486-86757044, ~54.2 Mb); (10) Large deletion 13q12.3-31.3 (46968080_87381985, ~40.4 Mb). These CNVs cause 13q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and retinoblastoma risk. The 13q critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: RB1 (retinoblastoma tumor suppressor, cell cycle regulation), FLT1 (VEGF receptor 1, angiogenesis), COL4A1/COL4A2 (collagen type IV, vascular development), PCDH9 (protocadherin-9, neuronal adhesion), and multiple neuronal development genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390601 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Deletions encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer loss leading to derepression of target genes including potential oncogenes; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting RB1 regulation increasing retinoblastoma risk; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopment. Duplications may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of tumor suppressors or developmental genes; (2) disrupted topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries affecting 13q gene expression programs. The 28 ClinVar CNVs (mix of deletions and duplications) suggest this regulatory element exhibits both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity. RB1 loss causes retinoblastoma. FLT1 variants affect vascular development. COL4A1/COL4A2 mutations cause porencephaly and vascular disorders. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
