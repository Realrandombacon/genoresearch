## Description
DNAJC27 (DnaJ Heat Shock Protein Family Member C27, also known as RBJ) encodes a 273 aa dual-domain protein (Q9NZQ0) combining chaperone and GTPase functions with established roles in obesity and metabolic disease. With 29 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined domain architecture and emerging metabolic functions requiring mechanistic elucidation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: DNAJC27 functions as a dual-function chaperone-GTPase protein that regulates metabolic homeostasis through protein folding quality control and GTP-dependent signaling, potentially acting as a co-chaperone for HSP70 family members while simultaneously regulating GTPase-dependent pathways involved in energy balance, adipogenesis, or insulin signaling.
