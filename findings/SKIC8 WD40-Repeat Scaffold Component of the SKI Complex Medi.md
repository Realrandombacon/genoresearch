# SKIC8: WD40-Repeat Scaffold Component of the SKI Complex Mediating RNA Surveillance and Antiviral Defense

**Date:** 2026-05-07T11:37:24.825790

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

## Description
SKIC8 encodes a 305-amino acid superkiller complex protein 8 (UniProt Q9GZS3), a WD40-repeat scaffold protein functioning as an essential component of the SKI (superkiller) complex involved in RNA surveillance and degradation. The protein contains seven WD40 repeats (IPR001680, positions 4-48/53-134/137-305; PF00400, positions 13-47/57-92/109-125) forming a characteristic beta-propeller structure for protein-protein interactions, classified within the WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943, positions 5-305) and PAC1/LIS1-like WD-40 repeat subfamily (IPR020472, positions 79-93/205-219/247-261), with conserved Trp-Asp signatures (PS00678, PS50082) defining the G-beta repeat architecture. AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally high-confidence structure (pLDDT 96.4, residues 1-305) indicating stable beta-propeller folding characteristic of WD40 scaffold proteins. STRING interaction analysis returned no results, suggesting SKIC8 interactions may be transient, condition-specific, or understudied due to the gene's dark status. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasm and cytosol localization consistent with RNA processing machinery, annotated as an essential protein involved in transcription regulation and Wnt signaling pathway. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (15q24.1-26.3, 15q24.3-26.3, 15q23-26.3) and losses (15q24.1-25.2), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With only 1 publication, SKIC8 is genuinely a dark gene: recent work identifies the mammalian SKI complex as a broad-spectrum antiviral drug target that upregulates cellular cholesterol to inhibit viral replication (2025, 0 cit). The functional hypothesis: SKIC8 serves as a WD40-repeat scaffold within the SKI complex (SKI2-SKI3-SKI8 in yeast; SKIV2L-TTC37-SKIC8 in humans), bridging the RNA helicase SKIV2L with regulatory subunits to facilitate 3'-to-5' exosomal RNA degradation, maintain RNA quality control, and restrict viral RNA accumulation; the beta-propeller structure provides multiple interaction surfaces for assembling the surveillance complex, analogous to yeast Ski8p roles in mRNA turnover and meiotic recombination; antiviral activity may involve SKI complex-mediated degradation of viral RNAs or cholesterol-dependent membrane remodeling that blocks viral entry; ClinVar CNVs disrupting SKIC8 dosage may impair RNA surveillance, leading to aberrant RNA accumulation, dysregulated Wnt signaling, and potential developmental defects; nucleoplasmic/cytosolic dual localization suggests roles in both nuclear transcription regulation and cytoplasmic RNA decay pathways.
