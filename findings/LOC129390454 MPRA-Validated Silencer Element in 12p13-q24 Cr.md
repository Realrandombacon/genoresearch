## Description
LOC129390454 (Gene ID: 129390454) is a functionally validated cis-regulatory silencer element mapping to chromosome 12p13.31 (49,766,371-49,766,571 bp on GRCh38, ~200 bp span). RefSeq annotation explicitly states: "MPRA-validated peak1728 silencer" - validated by STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays in K562 erythroleukemia cells as repressing super core promoter SCP1 activity. Multiple evidence lines confirm non-coding regulatory function: (1) No UniProt accession (not a protein); (2) Genomic span of 200 bp is far too small for protein-coding gene; (3) HPA could not resolve gene (non-coding, no protein product); (4) No InterPro/STRING/AlphaFold data applicable (non-coding). CRITICAL FINDING: ClinVar contains 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - both copy number gains (duplications) affecting the 12p13.33-q24 chromosomal region. Variants include: (1) 12p13.33-q13.12 duplication (82453-49847230, ~49.8 Mb) encompassing nearly entire p-arm; (2) 12p13.33-q24.33 duplication (121271-133196807, ~133.1 Mb) encompassing entire chromosome 12. These CNVs cause 12p duplication syndromes (Pallister-Killian syndrome tetrasomy 12p) featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, craniofacial dysmorphism, hypotonia, and skeletal abnormalities. The 12p critical region contains dosage-sensitive genes including: KRAS (GTPase, RAS/MAPK signaling), SOX5 (transcription factor, chondrogenesis), PAX9 (paired box 9, tooth development), and multiple neuronal development genes. Mechanistic hypothesis: LOC129390454 functions as transcriptional silencer that represses gene expression in hematopoietic contexts (validated in K562 erythroleukemia cells). The element likely recruits co-repressor complexes (Polycomb repressive complex 2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3, NuRD complex with HDAC activity, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A/B) via sequence-specific DNA-binding proteins (CTCF insulator protein, KRAB zinc finger proteins, erythroid-specific transcription factors GATA1/KLF1) to establish repressive chromatin architecture. Duplications encompassing this element may cause: (1) silencer dosage increase leading to excessive repression of target genes contributing to 12p duplication syndrome phenotype; (2) altered chromatin looping architecture disrupting normal enhancer-promoter contacts; (3) position effects on neighboring gene regulation affecting neurodevelopmental programs. The 2 ClinVar CNVs (both duplications) suggest this regulatory element may exhibit triplosensitivity - increased silencer dosage causes pathological gene repression. KRAS variants affect RAS/MAPK signaling causing developmental disorders (RASopathies). SOX5 haploinsufficiency causes skeletal dysplasia. PAX9 mutations cause oligodontia. This represents a "dark gene" class of functional non-coding regulatory elements with clear clinical significance through chromosomal rearrangements.
