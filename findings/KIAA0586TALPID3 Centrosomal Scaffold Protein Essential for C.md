## Description
KIAA0586 (TALPID3) encodes a 1533 aa centrosomal protein (UniProt Q9BVV6) critical for ciliogenesis and hedgehog signaling pathway regulation. The protein contains the Hedgehog signalling target domain (PF15324, aa101-1342) and TALPID3 family domain (IPR029246, aa1-1533), functioning as a scaffold for centrosome-cilium transition zone assembly. AlphaFold prediction shows low confidence (pLDDT 47.2), indicating predominantly disordered structure typical of scaffold proteins that function through protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with cell type-enhanced specificity, consistent with fundamental ciliary function. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with centrosomal proteins: CCP110 (0.876), CEP290 (0.775), CNTLN (0.655), SPATA5 (0.652), and moderate interactions with OFD1 (0.588), PCM1 (0.581), and SHH (0.409), placing KIAA0586 at the centrosome-cilium interface. ClinVar contains 272 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants causing Joubert syndrome 23 (JBTS23), short-rib thoracic dysplasia 14 (SRTD14), and related ciliopathies. Recent literature (58 papers) demonstrates KIAA0586/TALPID3 variants cause defects in both primary and motile cilia, with expanding phenotypic spectrum including left isomerism. The protein likely functions as a molecular scaffold bridging centriolar satellites (PCM1), transition zone components (CEP290, OFD1), and hedgehog signaling machinery (SHH), with disorder enabling conformational flexibility for multiple binding partners.
