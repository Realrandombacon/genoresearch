# CIMIP6/C2orf73: Testis-Enriched Ciliary Microtubule Inner Protein with Partially Structured DUF4706-like Domain Implicated in 2p Copy Number Disorders

**Date:** 2026-03-19T14:07:37.737501

**Quality Score:** 4.2/10  (E=4.9, D=3.5) [MODERATE]

## Description
CIMIP6 (alias C2orf73, ciliary microtubule inner protein 6) encodes a 287 amino acid partially structured protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 53.5) containing a ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 family domain (IPR031365/PF15667, aa 38-284) spanning most of the protein sequence. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (46.3 nTPM) with nucleoplasm, plasma membrane, and cytosolic localization, consistent with ciliary/flagellar function in spermatogenesis. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 2p25.3-16.1 copy number gains/losses and deletions associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions were not detected, suggesting the protein may function as a structural component rather than a transient interaction partner. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q5SPV6 233 aa) indicates functional constraint. The ciliary microtubule inner protein domain architecture combined with testis-enriched expression and ciliary gene ontology strongly suggests CIMIP6 functions as a structural component of axonemal microtubule doublets within motile cilia and flagella. The protein likely localizes to the inner lumen of microtubule B-tubules, stabilizing protofilament interactions and maintaining ciliary integrity during mechanical stress. Testis-specific expression reflects the requirement for functional sperm flagella, while low-level somatic expression may support primary cilia function in specialized tissues. Pathogenic 2p CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting ciliary microtubule stability, potentially contributing to ciliopathy phenotypes including male infertility (flagellar dysfunction), laterality defects (nodal cilia), and retinal degeneration (photoreceptor connecting cilia).
