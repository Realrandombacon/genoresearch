## Description
DOLPP1 (Dolichyldiphosphatase 1, alias LSFR2) is a 238 amino acid predicted membrane protein (UniProt Q86YN1) with very high-confidence exceptionally well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 89.4) containing canonical PAP2 dolichyldiphosphatase domains (residues 16-180), Phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2/haloperoxidase domains (residues 23-184/60-184/63-180/88-197), and lipid modification enzyme family signature (residues 15-211), functioning as a ubiquitous dolichyl pyrophosphate phosphatase that regulates N-linked glycosylation through PAP2 domain-mediated dolichyl pyrophosphate (Dol-P-P) dephosphorylation and interactions with DOLK, DPAGT1, DPM3, DPM2, STT3A, TUSC3, RPN1, STT3B, RPN2, and ALG5.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals DOLPP1 contains a classic PAP2 phosphatase architecture: PAP2 dolichyldiphosphatase (cd03382, residues 16-180) with dolichyldiphosphatase activity for Dol-P-P dephosphorylation, Phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2/haloperoxidase (IPR000326/PF01569/SM00014, residues 60-184/63-180) with hydrolase activity (GO:0016787) for lipid phosphate hydrolysis, Phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2/haloperoxidase superfamily (IPR036938/G3DSA:1.20.144.10, residues 23-184/88-197) confirming the PAP2 fold for membrane lipid metabolism, Dolichyldiphosphatase PAP2 domain (IPR039667, residues 16-180) defining DOLPP1 specificity for dolichol phosphate recycling, Lipid modification and degradation enzyme (PTHR11247, residues 15-211) placing DOLPP1 within lipid metabolism enzymes, and Acid phosphatase/Vanadium-dependent haloperoxidase (SSF48317, residues 23-184) confirming the catalytic scaffold. The PAP2 domain spans 164 amino acids (residues 16-180) within the 238 aa protein (~69%), with transmembrane regions for ER membrane integration. DOLPP1 represents a critical PAP2 phosphatase linking dolichol phosphate recycling to N-glycosylation and protein homeostasis.
