## Description
DNAJC7 (DnaJ Heat Shock Protein Family Member C7, also known as TPR2, TTC2, DJ11, DJC7, UniProt Q99615) is a 494-amino acid co-chaperone protein belonging to the DNAJ/Hsp40 family that functions as a molecular bridge between Hsp70 and Hsp90 chaperone systems through its tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domains mediating client protein recognition and its C-terminal J-domain stimulating Hsp70 ATPase activity. The protein architecture features an N-terminal TPR-like helical domain superfamily (IPR011990 aa 26-378) containing multiple TPR repeats (IPR019734/PF13181/13414/13432 aa 28-61/62-95/96-129/189-242/294-325/329-355) forming a superhelical structure for protein-protein interactions with GO term protein binding (GO:0005515), C-terminal DnaJ domain (IPR001623/PF00226 aa 380-451) containing the conserved HPD motif essential for Hsp70 interaction, Chaperone J-domain superfamily membership (IPR036869 aa 375-489), and DnaJ domain homologous superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.287.110 aa 379-483). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 90.3), indicating an exceptionally well-folded protein with stable three-dimensional architecture consistent with structured TPR superhelix and compact J-domain fold required for precise chaperone interactions. STRING interaction network reveals extremely high-confidence associations to heat shock protein chaperones: HSPA8 (0.961, heat shock cognate 71 kDa protein constitutive Hsp70), HSPA1B (0.873, heat shock 70 kDa protein 1B inducible Hsp70), TOMM34 (0.867, translocase of outer mitochondrial membrane 34 kDa TPR-containing co-chaperone), HSPA2 (0.861, heat shock 70 kDa protein 2 testis-specific Hsp70), HSP90AB1 (0.848, heat shock protein 90 alpha family class B member 1), BAG5 (0.775, BAG cochaperone 5 regulating Hsp70 nucleotide exchange), HSP90AA1 (0.732, heat shock protein 90 alpha family class A member 1), HSPA4 (0.722, heat shock 70 kDa protein 4), BAG2 (0.571, BAG cochaperone 2), and HSPA1L (0.533, heat shock 70 kDa protein 1-like). HPA expression profiles demonstrate low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues consistent with fundamental housekeeping chaperone function required for protein homeostasis in all cell types, nucleoplasm and cytosol subcellular localization matching dual roles in nuclear protein quality control and cytoplasmic folding, classification as predicted intracellular protein, chaperone molecular function, low cancer specificity though literature indicates oncogenic roles, low immune cell specificity, and low cell type specificity. (7) Ubiquitous expression reflects housekeeping chaperone function required for proteostasis maintenance across all tissues; (8) Nucleoplasm and cytosol localization suggests dual roles in nuclear protein quality control (transcription factor folding, DNA repair complex assembly) and cytoplasmic proteostasis; (10) TDP-43 pathology connection suggests DNAJC7 normally facilitates TDP-43 folding or disaggregation through Hsp70/Hsp90 collaboration; (11) HSF1 modulation (2024) indicates DNAJC7 regulates heat shock response transcriptional program potentially through chaperone-mediated HSF1 activation or stability; Therapeutic strategies could target DNAJC7-Hsp70 interaction in ALS through small molecules enhancing co-chaperone activity to improve TDP-43 clearance, while DNAJC7 inhibitors might sensitize hepatocellular carcinoma cells to proteotoxic stress by disrupting chaperone-mediated survival pathways, and HSF1 activators leveraging DNAJC7-mediated regulation could boost proteostasis capacity in neurodegenerative diseases.
