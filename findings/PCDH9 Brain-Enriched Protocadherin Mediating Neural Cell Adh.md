## Description
PCDH9 (UniProt Q9HC56) encodes a 1237 aa type I cadherin superfamily cell adhesion molecule functioning as a brain-enriched protocadherin with emerging roles in neural circuit assembly, neurodevelopmental disorders, and cancer metastasis. InterPro analysis reveals a characteristic protocadherin domain architecture: six extracellular cadherin (EC) repeats (positions 29-574, IPR002126, IPR013164, IPR015919, PF00028, PF08266) mediating calcium-dependent homophilic cell-cell adhesion (GO:0007156, GO:0005509); a protocadherin-specific domain (aa 777-999, IPR013585) distinguishing it from classical cadherins; and conserved cadherin sites (IPR020894) at positions 240-250, 457-467, 663-673 critical for calcium coordination and adhesive function. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 67.9), consistent with a large transmembrane protein containing ordered extracellular cadherin repeats and intrinsically disordered cytoplasmic regions suitable for dynamic protein-protein interactions and signaling complex assembly. STRING interactions define PCDH9 as a node in the neural adhesion network with medium-confidence partners: ATP7B (0.653), a copper-transporting ATPase linked to Wilson disease; DPP10 (0.514) and DPP6 (0.460), dipeptidyl peptidase-like proteins regulating Kv4 potassium channels in neurons; GPM6A (0.483), a neuronal membrane glycoprotein; CLDN11 (0.466), a tight junction protein in oligodendrocytes; CDH10 (0.456), another cadherin involved in neural development; UNC5D (0.456), a netrin receptor; NRXN1 (0.435), a neurexin synaptic adhesion protein; SORCS3 (0.432), a VPS10 domain receptor; and CNTNAP2 (0.429), a contactin-associated protein linked to autism spectrum disorder. HPA expression shows brain-enriched expression (38.4 nTPM), cancer-enriched specificity, nucleoplasm and centrosome subcellular localization, and group-enriched expression in blood cells, suggesting roles in neural development, cell division, and immune function. ClinVar reports 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 13q copy number losses/gains and a missense variant (p.Gly916Val), though no single monogenic disease is yet definitively assigned, suggesting PCDH9 contributes to heterogeneous neurodevelopmental and renal phenotypes. Literature (167 papers) reveals emerging functional characterization: (1) Neural development—PCDH9 regulates neuronal migration, dendritic arborization, and synaptic specificity through homophilic adhesion; (2) Neurodevelopmental disorders—PCDH9 polymorphisms associate with major depressive disorder, autism spectrum disorder, and schizophrenia; (3) Gastric cancer—nuclear translocation of cleaved PCDH9 impairs metastasis by downregulating CDH2 (N-cadherin) expression, revealing a tumor suppressor function; (4) CAKUT pathogenesis—PCDH9 expression in developing kidney suggests roles in congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract; (5) Synaptic organization—interaction with DPP6/DPP10 complex regulates neuronal excitability. Functional hypothesis: PCDH9 functions through: (1) Homophilic Adhesion—extracellular EC repeats mediate calcium-dependent cell-cell recognition, establishing neural circuit specificity; (2) Synaptic Organization—interaction with DPP6/DPP10/Kv4 complex regulates membrane excitability and synaptic transmission; (3) Nuclear Signaling—proteolytic cleavage releases intracellular domain that translocates to nucleus, regulating CDH2 and other adhesion genes; (4) Neurodevelopment—spatiotemporal expression patterns guide neuronal migration and dendritic patterning; (5) Disease Mechanism—loss-of-function variants disrupt neural adhesion and synaptic organization, contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders; copy number variants cause dosage-sensitive phenotypes in brain and kidney development. The combination of conserved protocadherin domains, brain-specific expression, extensive pathogenic variant burden, and moderate literature (confirming genuine dark gene status despite functional importance) positions PCDH9 as a high-priority target for neurodevelopmental disorder therapeutics and cancer metastasis inhibition.
