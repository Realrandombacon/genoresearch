## Description
CUL2 encodes a 745 aa nucleoplasmic scaffold protein (UniProt Q13617) that functions as the central structural component of the Cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligase (CRL) complex, specifically assembling the VHL-Elongin B/C (VBC) complex to target hypoxia-inducible factor 1-alpha (HIF-1α) for proteasomal degradation under normoxic conditions. This well-studied gene (1356 publications) has critical clinical significance for cancer metabolism, HPV-driven oncogenesis, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), embryonic stem cell pluripotency, and innate immune defense. The protein features conserved cullin architecture: N-terminal cullin domain (IPR001373/IPR016159, residues 14-487/10-380) mediating binding to substrate recognition modules (VHL); Cullin homology domain (IPR016158/IPR036317, residues 386-618/382-654) providing structural rigidity; C-terminal neddylation domain (IPR019559, residues 672-739) serving as the site for NEDD8 conjugation which activates the ligase; Winged helix-like DNA-binding domain superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.10.10, residues 657-745) though primarily protein-binding; three Cullin repeats (G3DSA:1.20.1310.10, residues 1-160/161-274/275-385) forming the elongated scaffold; classified within cullin-RING ubiquitin ligase complex (GO:0031461) with GO terms for ubiquitin protein ligase binding and ubiquitin-dependent protein catabolic process. STRING interactions reveal CUL2 operates within extremely high-confidence ubiquitination networks: ELOC (0.999), elongin C; HIF1A (0.999), hypoxia-inducible factor 1-alpha (primary substrate); RBX1 (0.930), RING-box protein 1 recruiting E2; ELOB (0.929), elongin B; COMMD1 (0.888), copper metabolism MURR1 domain containing 1; ZER1 (0.605), meiosis-specific protein; RNF7 (0.603), ring finger protein 7; NEDD8 (0.586), neural precursor cell expressed developmentally down-regulated 8; LRR1 (0.505), leucine rich repeat containing 1; VHL (0.430), von Hippel-Lindau tumor suppressor. HPA confirms low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), low cancer specificity, nucleoplasm localization, classified as essential cancer-related protein involved in host-virus interaction and Ubl conjugation. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations in cullin repeat regions (c.603G>T p.Lys201Asn, c.1012C>G p.Leu338Val), intronic variants (c.1171-58G>C, c.1300-9T>A, c.1887+4T>G), 3-prime UTR variant (c.*320A>T), C-terminal missense (c.2192T>C p.Ile731Thr within neddylation domain vicinity), and large-scale copy number alterations (10p14-q26.3 gain, 10p13-q26.3 loss, 10p15.3-q11.23 gain), demonstrating dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined beyond cancer susceptibility. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 85.8), revealing a well-folded elongated scaffold with properly positioned domains for VHL and RBX1 binding. Mechanistically, CUL2 operates through integrated modes: (1) HIF-1α regulation—scaffolds VHL-Elongin complex to ubiquitinate hydroxylated HIF-1α, preventing angiogenic gene expression under normoxia; (2) HPV oncogenesis—2024 study (18 citations) demonstrates HPV16 E6/E7 oncoproteins dysregulate the PHD2-VHL-CUL2-ELOC axis to stabilize HIF-1α driving metabolic reprogramming in cervical/head-neck/colorectal cancers; (3) Pluripotency establishment—2024 study (4 citations) shows CUL2 (with PRAMEL7) destabilizes NuRD complex to enable ground-state pluripotency in embryonic stem cells; (4) COPD biomarker—2025 study (2 citations) identifies CUL2 as ubiquitination-related biomarker in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; (5) Innate immunity—2025 Drosophila study (3 citations) establishes CUL2 as essential for IMD signaling-mediated antimicrobial defense, suggesting conserved immune role; (6) Neddylationswitch—NEDD8 conjugation induces conformational change activating E2 recruitment via RBX1. Loss-of-function causes HIF-1α stabilization, pseudo-hypoxic state, enhanced tumorigenesis, defective pluripotency, impaired immune response, and altered protein homeostasis.
