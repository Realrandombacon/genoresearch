## Description
MAGEB2 (UniProt O15479) encodes a 319-amino acid Type II melanoma-associated antigen and cancer-testis (CT) protein. InterPro analysis reveals the canonical MAGE homology domain (IPR002190/PF01454, aa 111-310) containing two winged helix motifs (WH1: aa 81-192, WH2: aa 196-317; G3DSA:1.10.10.1200/1210) and an N-terminal disordered region (PF12440/IPR021072, aa 3-95). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT: 70.9) consistent with stable MAGE family proteins that function as substrate adaptors for E3 ubiquitin ligases.\n\nSTRING interactions link MAGEB2 to chromatin regulators HDAC1 (0.603) and protein disulfide isomerase PDILT (0.609), alongside CT antigen family members GAGE1 (0.601), GAGE12G (0.538), PAGE2B (0.495), and SPANXD (0.502). HPA confirms testis-restricted expression (27.2 nTPM) with cancer-enriched patterns across tumors, cytosolic localization, and annotation to the Ubl conjugation pathway.\n\nRecent literature (56 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: MAGEB2 mediates EGR1 degradation to regulate spermatogonial stem cell proliferation and apoptosis (2023, 11 citations), interacts with TGFβ1 signaling in cancer progression (2025), and its downregulation by ponicidin via FOXO4 suppresses gallbladder cancer metastasis (2023, 21 citations). Unlike Type I MAGE proteins that are immunogenic, Type II MAGEB2 functions through protein-protein interactions rather than antigen presentation. ClinVar contains 168 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at Xp21.2. This positions MAGEB2 as a transitional dark gene—moving from orphan CTA to characterized regulator of ubiquitin-mediated transcription factor turnover in both fertility and cancer.
