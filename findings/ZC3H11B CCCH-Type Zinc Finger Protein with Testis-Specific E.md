## Description
ZC3H11B (zinc finger CCCH-type containing 11B, Gene ID: 643136) is an 805-amino acid protein encoded on chromosome 1q41 that belongs to the CCCH-type zinc finger family implicated in RNA metabolism and post-transcriptional regulation. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical N-terminal CCCH-type zinc finger domain architecture: three tandem CCCH-type zinc fingers (IPR000571, positions 2-29, 31-56, 60-86; IPR041686 positions 1-108; PF15663 positions 1-108) with C3H1-type zinc-coordinating signatures (PS50103, positions 2-29) and classification within zinc finger CCCH domain-containing proteins (PTHR15725, positions 2-782). The CCCH domain adopts the characteristic fold (G3DSA:4.10.1000.10) distinct from C2H2 zinc fingers, typically mediating RNA binding rather than DNA binding. AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 55.3), consistent with proteins containing structured zinc finger domains connected by intrinsically disordered regions—typical of RNA-binding proteins that undergo conformational changes during RNA recognition. HPA data reveals testis-enriched expression (5.4 nTPM) with nuclear speckle subcellular localization, strongly suggesting roles in RNA processing, splicing, or mRNA export. STRING database identifies 10 medium-confidence interaction partners (scores 0.418-0.574): SNTB1 (0.574, syntrophin beta-1), ZNRF3 (0.506, E3 ubiquitin ligase), ZNF644 (0.490, C2H2 zinc finger), CMSS1 (0.482), PRSS56 (0.479, serine protease), ALPG (0.476, alkaline phosphatase), ASH1L (0.444, histone methyltransferase), GJD2 (0.436, connexin), LAMA2 (0.420, laminin), and KCNQ5 (0.418, potassium channel). The SNTB1 interaction is notable as both genes are co-associated with myopia in GWAS studies. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (all copy number variations: deletions and duplications spanning 1q32-44 region), though no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ZC3H11B. Literature analysis shows 18 papers (PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED), with multiple GWAS studies linking ZC3H11B polymorphisms to myopia severity, axial length, refraction, and corneal biomechanical properties in Chinese populations. The nuclear speckle localization, CCCH domain architecture, and predicted involvement in poly(A)+ mRNA export from nucleus suggest ZC3H11B functions as an RNA-binding protein regulating mRNA processing, stability, or nucleocytoplasmic transport—potentially affecting expression of eye development genes. The testis-enriched expression pattern suggests additional roles in spermatogenesis or male germline RNA metabolism. The myopia association may reflect ZC3H11B-mediated regulation of extracellular matrix genes (LAMA2 interaction) or transcriptional regulators (ASH1L, ZNF644 interactions) critical for ocular development and axial elongation. This represents a partially characterized dark gene with clear molecular function predictions and clinically relevant phenotypic associations.
