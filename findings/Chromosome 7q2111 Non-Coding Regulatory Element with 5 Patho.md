# Chromosome 7q21.11 Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 5 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants in HGF-SEMA3A-CD36 Angiogenesis-Axon Guidance-Lipid Uptake Locus

## Description
LOC101927269 (Gene ID: 101927269) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 7q21.11 (NC_000007.14, positions 80374438-80371516, ~2.9 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas reported no data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000232667, consistent with non-coding or highly tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 4 copy number losses/deletions (variant 254280: single allele deletion; variant 154313: 7q11.23-21.11 ~7.7 Mb monosomy; variant 60244: 7q11.23-21.11 ~10.1 Mb monosomy; variant 57096: 7q11.22-21.11 ~10.5 Mb monosomy) and 1 large copy number loss (variant 146075: 7p22.3-q36.3 ~159.3 Mb monosomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 7q21.11 chromosomal region contains critical angiogenesis, axon guidance, and lipid metabolism-associated genes including HGF (hepatocyte growth factor/c-Met ligand, receptor tyrosine kinase activation, cell proliferation, motility, angiogenesis, liver regeneration, cancer metastasis), CD36 (CD36 molecule/scavenger receptor class B member 3, fatty acid uptake, oxidized LDL clearance, thrombospondin-1 receptor, angiogenesis inhibition, atherosclerosis, malaria susceptibility), SEMA3A (semaphorin 3A, class 3 semaphorin, growth cone collapse, axon repulsion, vascular patterning, neuropilin-1 co-receptor, cancer suppression), GNAI1 (G protein subunit alpha i1, inhibitory G protein alpha subunit, adenylate cyclase inhibition, cAMP reduction, signal transduction), and SEMA3C (semaphorin 3C, axon guidance, cardiac outflow tract development, neural crest cell migration). Functional hypothesis: LOC101927269 likely represents a critical 7q21.11 endothelial/neuronal enhancer element within the HGF-SEMA3A regulatory region, potentially functioning as a liver sinusoidal endothelial cell-specific enhancer coordinating HGF-SEMA3A-CD36 expression in angiogenic niches or as a chromatin boundary separating growth factor signaling genes (HGF) from axon guidance genes (SEMA3A, SEMA3C) and lipid transport genes (CD36). The 5 pathogenic CNVs (predominantly deletions, 2.9-159.3 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts HGF-mediated c-Met activation and SEMA3A-mediated axon repulsion. Disease mechanism involves HGF overexpression causing cancer metastasis (c-Met hyperactivation, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, invasion, poor prognosis), HGF deficiency causing liver regeneration defect (hepatocyte proliferation impairment, fulminant hepatic failure), CD36 deficiency causing atherosclerosis (oxidized LDL accumulation, foam cell formation, plaque instability), CD36 mutation causing malaria susceptibility (Plasmodium falciparum cytoadherence defect), SEMA3A loss causing vascular patterning defects (aberrant angiogenesis, hemorrhage, tumor vascularization), SEMA3A mutation causing neurodevelopmental disorders (axon guidance error, olfactory bulb hypoplasia, Kallmann syndrome features), GNAI1 dysfunction causing signal transduction defects (cAMP dysregulation, platelet activation defect, bleeding diathesis), SEMA3C mutation causing congenital heart disease (outflow tract malformation, tetralogy of Fallot, persistent truncus arteriosus), and 7q21.11 deletion syndrome presenting with developmental delay, dysmorphic features, and organ-specific defects. The 7q21.11 region is associated with angiogenic and neurodevelopmental genetic variation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (5 CNVs 2.9-159.3 Mb) and HGF-SEMA3A-CD36-GNAI1-SEMA3C neighborhood suggests this locus functions as an endothelial/neuronal-specific enhancer critical for angiogenesis-axon guidance-lipid uptake coordination.
