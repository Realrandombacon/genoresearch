## Description
UBR7 (Q8N806) encodes a 425 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase belonging to the UBR box-containing protein family that functions as a chromatin-modifying enzyme catalyzing histone H2BK120 monoubiquitination while simultaneously regulating metabolic reprogramming and immune signaling in cancer. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture characteristic of N-recognin E3 ligases: a UBR-box zinc finger domain (IPR003126/PF02207, positions 44-116/46-112) mediating substrate recognition through the N-end rule pathway; a PHD-type zinc finger domain (IPR064051/PF30811, positions 129-196/134-185) recognizing specific histone marks and recruiting chromatin modifiers; additional zinc/RING finger domains (IPR011011/IPR013083, positions 121-197/132-190) providing structural stability; and a C-terminal region (IPR063242, positions 357-417) potentially involved in protein-protein interactions or catalytic activity. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 75.4, high confidence) consistent with stable domain architecture required for precise substrate positioning during ubiquitin transfer chemistry. STRING interactions demonstrate exclusive partnerships with chromatin regulators and ubiquitination machinery: H3C1 (0.829) and H3C12 (0.655), histone H3 variants indicating direct chromatin targeting; NASP (0.723), nuclear autoantigenic sperm protein functioning as histone chaperone; UBR3 (0.714), UBR1 (0.662), UBR2 (0.648), UBR5 (0.648), and UBR4 (0.584), paralogous UBR-box E3 ligases suggesting functional redundancy or cooperative complex formation; H2BC3 (0.638), histone H2B type C confirming H2B substrate specificity; UBE2E1 (0.606), ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 E1 serving as the cognate E2 for ubiquitin transfer. HPA shows ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, nucleoplasm localization consistent with chromatin-associated function, classified under disease-related genes/enzymes/metabolic proteins/potential drug targets with Ubl conjugation pathway biological process and transferase molecular function, disease variant involvement including intellectual disability. ClinVar contains 38 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (c.227T>G p.Leu76Ter truncating the UBR-box domain, c.1207C>T p.Gln403Ter removing the C-terminal region), missense mutations (c.113T>C p.Leu38Pro disrupting the UBR-box zinc coordination), splice variants (c.961-8C>T, c.441+197A>G affecting intron-exon boundaries), copy number losses (chr14q31.1-32.2 deletions, chr14q31.3-32.13 losses), copy number gains (chr14q31.3-32.33 amplifications, chr14q23.1-32.33 duplications), and in-frame deletions (c.1240AGA[2] p.Arg416del), though specific monogenic disease names beyond intellectual disability remain incompletely defined in ClinVar database. Literature (49 papers) demonstrates UBR7 functions as an oncogenic epigenetic regulator in multiple cancer types: interaction with PRMT5 drives pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma resistance to gemcitabine by regulating glycolysis and immune microenvironment remodeling; partnership with EZH2 inhibits TGF-β signaling leading to extracellular matrix remodeling in triple-negative breast cancer; miR-10b-5p regulation of neuronal autophagy and apoptosis induced by spinal cord injury through UBR7 targeting; suppression of interferon-β mediated immune signaling by targeting Sp110 nuclear body protein in hepatitis B virus-induced hepatocellular carcinoma; direct catalysis of histone H2BK120 monoubiquitination establishing UBR7 as a bona fide chromatin-modifying E3 ligase. The mechanistic hypothesis: UBR7 functions as a dual-function E3 ubiquitin ligase where the UBR-box domain (positions 44-116) recognizes N-degron substrates through the N-end rule pathway while the PHD finger (positions 129-196) binds specific histone modifications recruiting the enzyme to chromatin; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 75.4) indicates stable domain arrangement enabling simultaneous substrate recognition and catalytic activity; ubiquitous expression reflects fundamental role in chromatin regulation across cell types; nucleoplasm localization positions UBR7 at sites of active transcription where H2BK120 monoubiquitination regulates RNA polymerase II elongation and transcriptional activation; oncogenic activity operates through multiple mechanisms: PRMT5 interaction creates positive feedback loop where UBR7-mediated ubiquitination stabilizes PRMT5 enhancing arginine methylation of glycolytic enzymes and immune regulators promoting gemcitabine resistance; EZH2 partnership coordinates H3K27me3 deposition with H2BK120ub establishing repressive chromatin states silencing TGF-β target genes enabling ECM remodeling and metastasis; Sp110 targeting suppresses interferon signaling allowing viral persistence and hepatocarcinogenesis; paralogous UBR1/2/3/4/5 interactions suggest UBR7 may form heteromeric complexes expanding substrate repertoire; 38 pathogenic variants distributed across functional domains disrupt ubiquitin ligase activity causing aberrant chromatin states and transcriptional dysregulation underlying intellectual disability phenotype; NASP interaction indicates coordination with histone chaperone machinery ensuring proper nucleosome assembly during DNA replication and repair; UBE2E1 partnership defines the specific E2~Ub conjugate used for ubiquitin transfer establishing mechanistic specificity within the ubiquitination cascade.
