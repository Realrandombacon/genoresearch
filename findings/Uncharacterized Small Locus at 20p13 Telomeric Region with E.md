## Description
LOC105372493 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 20p13 (NC_000020.11:1118447-1117851) spanning approximately 0.6 kb - one of the smallest annotated loci in this survey, located at the telomeric terminus of chromosome 20p. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - an extreme burden indicating profound clinical relevance. Variants include massive 20p copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 20p13-11.21 (chr20:87153-23635465, ~23.5 Mb), 20p13-11.1 (chr20:80927-26324843, ~26.2 Mb), 20p13-12.3 (chr20:80928-18688031, ~18.6 Mb; chr20:84402-6159078, ~6.1 Mb; chr20:80093-6386012, ~6.3 Mb), 20p13-12.1 (chr20:80106-13029401, ~13.0 Mb), and deletions spanning 20p13 (chr20:89939-1852477, ~1.8 Mb; chr20:80927-1806080, ~1.7 Mb; chr20:80106-1246891, ~1.2 Mb; chr20:80093-1246766, ~1.2 Mb). The 20p13 region is the critical telomeric locus for 20p deletion syndrome and 20p duplication syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive developmental genes including MACROD2 (mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase diphtheria toxin-like 2, neurodevelopment), DNTTP (deoxynucleoside triphosphate triphosphohydrolase), and multiple subtelomeric lncRNAs. LOC105372493 likely functions as: (1) a telomeric cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 20p developmental gene expression, (2) a telomeric long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 20p chromatin architecture, (3) a subtelomeric chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 20p13 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a telomere-adjacent recombination hotspot affecting 20p genomic stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (21 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this telomeric locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) MACROD2 haploinsufficiency leading to neurodevelopmental defects, (2) 20p subtelomeric chromatin architecture affecting developmental gene expression, (3) anterior-posterior patterning programs, or (4) telomere capping function, leading to 20p deletion syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac anomalies, growth retardation) or 20p duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, neurological abnormalities).
