## Description
OSGEPL1 (O-Sialoglycoprotein Endopeptidase Like 1, also known as Qri7, UniProt Q9H4B0) is a 414-amino acid mitochondrial enzyme functioning as the catalytic subunit of the mitochondrial tRNA N6-threonylcarbamoyladenosine (t6A) biosynthesis complex that modifies position 37 of ANN-decoding tRNAs (tRNA-Arg, tRNA-Ile, tRNA-Lys, tRNA-Met, tRNA-Thr) essential for accurate codon-anticodon recognition and prevention of frameshifting during mitochondrial protein synthesis; defects cause CO2-sensitive mitochondrial dysfunction through labile t6A modification leading to respiratory chain complex deficiency, developmental delay, hypotonia, and lactic acidosis; recent multi-omics analysis identifies OSGEPL1 as an oncogene in hepatocellular carcinoma where t6A modification irregularities drive malignant transformation through enhanced mitochondrial gene expression and metabolic reprogramming.
