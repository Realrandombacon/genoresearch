## Description
IGLC6 (immunoglobulin lambda constant 6) is a 106-amino acid protein encoded on chromosome 22q11.22, representing one of the immunoglobulin lambda light chain constant region genes. InterPro analysis reveals extensive immunoglobulin domain architecture: PF07654 (Immunoglobulin C1-set domain, positions 9-93), IPR003597 (Immunoglobulin C1-set, positions 9-97), IPR007110 (Immunoglobulin-like domain, positions 7-101), IPR013783 (Immunoglobulin-like fold), IPR036179 (Immunoglobulin-like domain superfamily, positions 3-99), cd07699 (Immunoglobulin light chain Constant domain, positions 6-104), and G3DSA:2.60.40.10 (Immunoglobulins superfamily, positions 2-106). The protein contains the conserved immunoglobulin signature motif (PS00290, positions 85-91) characteristic of IgSF members. IGLC6 functions as the constant region of lambda-6 type immunoglobulin light chains, forming the structural scaffold that pairs with variable domains (IGLV) to create functional antibody light chains. Unlike variable region genes that undergo V(D)J recombination for antigen diversity, constant region genes like IGLC6 provide structural stability and determine antibody class/subclass properties. STRING database returned no interaction partners, which is typical for immunoglobulin constant regions that function primarily through covalent assembly with variable domains rather than transient protein-protein interactions. HPA has no expression data for IGLC6 (ENSG00000222037 not in database), and ClinVar contains zero variants—no known pathogenic mutations or clinical associations. Critically, only 5 total papers exist for IGLC6 (DARK status), with most literature focused on equine/cattle immunoglobulin organization rather than human IGLC6 specifically. This contrasts sharply with IGHV genes (hundreds of thousands to millions of papers). The lack of disease associations may reflect functional redundancy among the 7 human IGLC genes (IGLC1-7), where loss of one constant region can be compensated by others. IGLC6 likely functions in B cell receptor assembly and antibody secretion, contributing to the structural framework that positions antigen-binding variable domains. The gene's location within the immunoglobulin lambda locus on 22q11.22 places it in a region susceptible to copy number variations, though no specific IGLC6 deletions have been clinically characterized. This represents a genuine dark gene within the well-studied immunoglobulin superfamily—the constant regions remain understudied compared to variable regions despite their essential structural role.
