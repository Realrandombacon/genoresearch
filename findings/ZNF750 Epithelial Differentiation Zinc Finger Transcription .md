# ZNF750: Epithelial Differentiation Zinc Finger Transcription Factor Recruiting Chromatin Remodeling Complexes via KLF4-GRHL3 Network

## Description
ZNF750 (UniProt Q32MQ0) encodes a 723 amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (chromosome 17q25.3) with strong epithelial differentiation signatures. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the zinc finger protein 750 family (IPR039363, residues 4-723; PTHR14678, residues 4-723) with an N-terminal zinc finger domain (IPR039064, residues 6-57; PF15269, residues 6-57) characteristic of DNA-binding transcriptional regulators. STRING interactions show high-confidence associations with epithelial differentiation and chromatin remodeling pathways: KLF4 (0.855, Krüppel-like factor 4, epidermal differentiation master regulator), GRHL3 (0.737, grainyhead-like transcription factor 3, epithelial barrier formation), CTBP1 (0.705, C-terminal binding protein 1, transcriptional corepressor), KDM1A (0.672, lysine-specific histone demethylase 1A, chromatin modifier), CARD14 (0.627, psoriasis susceptibility factor), OVOL1 (0.583, epithelial development transcription factor), FAT1 (0.573, protocadherin), GRHL1 (0.532), RCOR1 (0.517, REST corepressor), and FAT2 (0.438). HPA expression shows skin-enriched transcription (135.0 nTPM) with elevated esophagus (93.4 nTPM) and vagina (50.6 nTPM) expression; classified as tissue-enhanced with cancer-enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses on 17q25.3 spanning the ZNF750 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity. AlphaFold structure prediction shows low confidence (pLDDT 48.0) across the 723 aa sequence, consistent with intrinsically disordered transcription factor architecture containing flexible linkers between structured zinc finger domains. Conservation data shows strong vertebrate conservation: mouse ortholog (703 aa, ~97% identity), zebrafish ortholog (607 aa, ~84% identity). The convergence of C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domain, interaction with KLF4/GRHL3 epithelial differentiation master regulators, recruitment of chromatin modifiers (CTBP1 corepressor, KDM1A demethylase), skin/esophagus-enriched expression, and cancer-enhanced pattern suggests ZNF750 functions as a sequence-specific transcription factor that orchestrates terminal keratinocyte differentiation by recruiting chromatin remodeling complexes to epidermal differentiation gene promoters. Its interaction network places ZNF750 within the GRHL3-KLF4 regulatory axis controlling epidermal barrier formation; cancer-enhanced expression and 17q25.3 CNV pathogenicity implicate ZNF750 as a context-dependent oncogene in squamous cell carcinomas and epithelial malignancies.
