## Description
CHST1 (UniProt O43916), also known as Carbohydrate sulfotransferase 1 or Keratan sulfate galactose 6-O-sulfotransferase, encodes a 411 aa high-confidence Golgi membrane-associated enzyme functioning as a critical catalyst in keratan sulfate biosynthesis through transfer of sulfate groups to the C6 position of galactose residues in glycosaminoglycan chains, thereby regulating extracellular matrix organization, corneal transparency, cartilage development, and neural tissue homeostasis.
