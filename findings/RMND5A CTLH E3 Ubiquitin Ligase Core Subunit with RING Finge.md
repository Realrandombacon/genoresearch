# RMND5A: CTLH E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Core Subunit with RING Finger Domain

**Date:** 2026-03-19T10:16:03.330744

**Quality Score:** 2.55/10  (E=4.0, D=1.1) [WEAK]

## Description
RMND5A (Required for Meiotic Nuclear Division 5 homolog A) encodes a 391 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase that serves as a core catalytic component of the CTLH (C-terminal to LisH) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex. The protein contains multiple conserved domains: an N-terminal LIS1 homology motif (IPR006594, aa 114-146), a CTLH C-terminal LisH motif domain (IPR006595/PF10607, aa 153-296), a CRA domain (IPR013144, aa 208-302), and a C-terminal RING-type zinc finger (IPR027370/PF13445, aa 336-375) with Gid-type E3 ligase activity (IPR044063). The RING finger domain (aa 332-391) mediates ubiquitin transfer from UBE2H E2 conjugating enzyme to substrate proteins, targeting them for proteasomal degradation.

STRING interaction analysis reveals RMND5A forms a stable multiprotein complex with WDR26 (score 0.997), ARMC8 (0.996), MKLN1 (0.990), RANBP10 (0.977), MAEA (0.969), YPEL5 (0.943), and ZMYND19 (0.907), confirming its role as a scaffold within the CTLH complex. HPA expression data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity (nTPM detected in all), nucleoplasmic localization, and cell type-enhanced single cell expression patterns.

ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning 2p11.2-q11.2, suggesting haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity causes disease phenotypes. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9H871-F1) shows high confidence folding (pLDDT 89.9), indicating a stable tertiary structure. Orthologs are conserved in mouse (Q80YQ8, 100% length conservation) and Xenopus (Q640V2, Q6GLP4), demonstrating evolutionary constraint across vertebrates.
