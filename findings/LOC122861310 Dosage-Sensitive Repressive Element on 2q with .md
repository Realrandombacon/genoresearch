# LOC122861310: Dosage-Sensitive Repressive Element on 2q with Gain-Predominant Pathogenicity

## Description
LOC122861310 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 726 located on chromosome 2q (genomic coordinates: 231621023-231621411 on NC_000002.12). This 388 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA expression). ClinVar analysis identified 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 9 are copy number gains (triplifications) spanning 2q32.2-37.3 regions, while 2 are copy number losses. The strong bias toward gains (9:2 ratio) indicates that over-dosage of this repressive element is more pathogenic than haploinsufficiency. This suggests the element normally functions to silence target genes in cis on 2q, and extra copies lead to excessive transcriptional repression. The 2q36-37 region contains genes involved in development and tumor suppression (including SATB2 at 2q33.1, a chromatin organizer). Amplification of this repressor may contribute to aberrant silencing of tumor suppressors or developmental regulators. The presence of both gain and loss pathogenic variants indicates bidirectional dosage sensitivity, though gains predominate—suggesting cells are more sensitive to excess repressive activity than to its reduction. This pattern is consistent with regulatory elements that fine-tune expression of dosage-sensitive target genes.
