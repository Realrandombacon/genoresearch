## Description
SPATC1L (Spermatogenesis and Centriole Associated 1 Like) encodes a 340 aa intracellular speriolin-like protein (Q9H0A9) with critical roles in spermatogenesis and sperm head-tail junction formation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SPATC1L functions as a structural scaffold protein at the sperm head-tail junction (connecting piece), maintaining integrity between the sperm head and flagellum during spermiogenesis. The protein likely mediates centriole-associated protein complex assembly through its bipartite speriolin domain architecture. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: SPATC1L operates through: (1) Sperm head-tail junction assembly - SPATC1L localizes to the connecting piece region where it stabilizes the junction between the sperm head nuclear envelope and the flagellar axoneme, preventing acephalic spermatozoa formation; (2) Centriole-associated complex formation - interacts with centriolar proteins (CEP112, score 0.464) and sperm-specific structural proteins (SUN5 score 0.531, PMFBP1 score 0.580, SPATA6 score 0.594) to form the sperm head-tail coupling apparatus; (3) Spermatid maturation - required for proper sperm tail attachment during late spermiogenesis, with biallelic mutations causing acephalic spermatozoa syndrome characterized by decapitated sperm and severe male infertility. PROTEIN ARCHITECTURE: Speriolin N-terminal domain (IPR029385, PF15058, positions 3-153), Speriolin C-terminal domain (IPR029384, PF15059, positions 195-340), Speriolin family (IPR026715, PTHR22192, positions 101-340) - unique bipartite domain structure specific to spermatogenesis. INTERACTION NETWORK: STRING partners include PM20D1 (0.626), SPATA6/spermatogenesis-associated 6 (0.594), PMFBP1/polyamine-modulated factor 1-binding protein 1 (0.580), SUN5/sad1 and UNC84 domain-containing 5 (0.531), KRTCAP3/keratinocyte-associated protein 3 (0.510), TSGA10 (0.489), C21orf58 (0.479), METTL27 (0.469), FSD2 (0.465), CEP112/centrosomal protein 112 (0.464) - sperm head-tail junction and centriole-associated protein network. TISSUE EXPRESSION: Tissue enhanced (HPA), testis-specific (47.4 nTPM), plasma membrane and cytosol subcellular localization, cell type enhanced in spermatogenic cells. CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: 97 ClinVar pathogenic variants (chromosome 21q22.3 CNVs), biallelic mutations cause acephalic spermatozoa syndrome (OMIM phenotype) with severe male infertility, balanced chromosomal rearrangements implicate SPATC1L in non-obstructive oligoasthenozoospermia and recurrent miscarriage, PTSD-associated DNA methylation changes at SPATC1L locus (26 citations, 2020). RESEARCH STATUS: 20 publications total - key discoveries include SPATC1L maintains sperm head-tail junction integrity (42 citations, 2018), biallelic mutations cause acephalic spermatozoa syndrome (16 citations, 2021), chromosomal rearrangements linked to male infertility (1 citation, 2023). SPATC1L represents a moderately characterized male fertility gene with defined structural role but incompletely understood molecular mechanism - the speriolin domains lack homology to known structural motifs, suggesting novel protein-protein interaction mechanism at the sperm connecting piece.
