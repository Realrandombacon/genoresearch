## Description
DCBLD1 (Discoidin, CUB and LCCL Domain-Containing Protein 1; UniProt Q8N8Z6) is a 715 amino acid multi-domain membrane-associated scaffold protein with 50 publications, representing a moderately-characterized regulator of receptor tyrosine kinase signaling and cellular metabolism. Despite its structural homology to coagulation factors and neuropilins, DCBLD1 functions primarily as an oncogenic scaffold coordinating RTK signaling, metabolic reprogramming, and stress responses through its modular extracellular domain architecture, with emerging roles in cervical cancer, Parkinson's disease, and inflammatory bowel disease.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals DCBLD1 contains a tripartite extracellular domain organization typical of modular recognition proteins: (1) CUB domain (IPR000859/PF00431/SM00213, residues 41-150, ~110 aa) — complement C1r/C1s, Uegf, Bmp1 domain mediating protein-protein interactions and ligand binding; (2) LCCL domain (IPR004043/PF03815, residues 152-248, ~97 aa) — Limulus clotting factor C, Coch-5b2, Lgll domain involved in carbohydrate/lipid binding; (3) F5/8 type C domain (IPR000421/PF00754, residues 248-412, ~165 aa) — coagulation factor 5/8 C-terminal discoidin-like domain mediating membrane association and phospholipid binding; (4) C-terminal region (residues 413-715, ~302 aa) — largely unstructured (AlphaFold pLDDT 67.6 medium confidence), potentially containing transmembrane segment and cytoplasmic tail for signal transduction. AlphaFold predicts partially structured architecture (global pLDDT 67.6), indicating stable extracellular domains (~400 aa) connected to flexible intracellular regions (~300 aa) suitable for dynamic scaffold assembly.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals moderate-confidence interactions reflecting receptor signaling and scaffold functions: ROS1 (0.614—highest; ROS Proto-Oncogene 1, receptor tyrosine kinase frequently fused in cancers), CRKL (0.500; CRK Like Proto-Oncogene, SH2/SH3 adaptor protein in RTK signaling), GOPC (0.407; Golgi-Associated PDZ and Coiled-Coil Motif Containing, scaffold protein linking receptors to trafficking), SLC35F1 (0.524; Solute Carrier Family 35 Member F1, nucleotide sugar transporter), LMBRD1 (0.448; LMBR1 Domain Containing 1, lysosomal cobalamin transporter), FAM229B (0.446; uncharacterized protein), MRAP2 (0.414; Melanocortin Receptor Accessory Protein 2, GPCR chaperone), and HLA-DPA1 (0.414; MHC class II antigen).
