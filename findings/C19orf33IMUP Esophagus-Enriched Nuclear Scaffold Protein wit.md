## Description
Chromosome 19 open reading frame 33 (C19orf33), also known as Immortalization Up-regulated Protein (IMUP), encodes a 106-amino acid uncharacterized nuclear scaffold protein containing the Immortalization Up-regulated Protein domain (IPR026621, aa 1-104) and PF15761 family membership (aa 1-104), characteristic of vertebrate-specific proteins that function through multivalent protein-protein interactions in nuclear organization and transcriptional regulation. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT=62.4, medium confidence) indicates partially structured architecture with mixed ordered and disordered regions across the modeled range (1-106), consistent with proteins that undergo conformational flexibility during macromolecular complex assembly while maintaining stable interaction interfaces. STRING interaction network reveals associations with serine protease inhibition, protein phosphatase 1 regulation, and mucin signaling: serine protease inhibitor SPINT2 (score 0.882, high confidence), protein phosphatase 1 regulatory subunit PPP1R14A (0.663), olfactory receptor OR14A16 (0.433), protein phosphatase 1 regulatory subunit PPP1R12A (0.428), protein phosphatase 1 catalytic subunit PPP1CB (0.425), and mucin MUC21 (0.408), positioning C19orf33 at the intersection of protease signaling, phosphorylation-dependent regulation, and epithelial barrier function. HPA expression shows esophagus-enriched pattern (689.2 nTPM in esophagus, 440.6 nTPM in skin), tissue enhanced classification with detection in many tissues, predicted intracellular protein localization with nucleoplasm/plasma membrane/cytosol distribution, low cancer specificity, and immune cell enriched blood cell specificity. ClinVar analysis identifies 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including extensive copy number gains (19q11-13.2, 19p13.11-q13.2, 19q11-13.33, 19p13.3-q13.43, 19q13.12-13.2 regions, 3 copies) and one splice region variant (c.202-11C>G), indicating strong dosage sensitivity where altered C19orf33 expression disrupts SPINT2-PPP1R14A-PPP1R12A-PPP1CB regulatory network assembly and is implicated in placental development disorders including pre-eclampsia and endometrial carcinoma pathogenesis. The combination of immortalization up-regulated domain architecture (aa 1-104), partially structured conformation (pLDDT 62.4), esophagus-skin enriched expression (689.2/440.6 nTPM), nuclear-cytosolic-membrane localization, SPINT2-PPP1R14A-OR14A16-PPP1R12A-PPP1CB-MUC21 interaction network (scores 0.408-0.882), and pathogenic variant burden (9 variants predominantly CNVs) strongly suggests C19orf33 functions as a nuclear scaffold protein that coordinates serine protease inhibitor (SPINT2) signaling, protein phosphatase 1 regulatory complex (PPP1R14A, PPP1R12A, PPP1CB) assembly, and mucin-mediated (MUC21) epithelial barrier function during placental trophoblast development and epithelial cell immortalization, with esophagus-skin enrichment reflecting high demand for scaffold protein-mediated protease-phosphatase balance during epithelial turnover and barrier maintenance, and dosage sensitivity through copy number gains disrupting SPINT2-PPP1R14A-PPP1R12A-PPP1CB scaffold organization and contributing to pre-eclampsia through placental trophoblast dysfunction and endometrial carcinoma through epithelial cell immortalization.
