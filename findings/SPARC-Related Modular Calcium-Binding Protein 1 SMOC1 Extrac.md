## Description
SMOC1 (SPARC-Related Modular Calcium-Binding Protein 1) is a 434-amino acid secreted extracellular matrix glycoprotein with 171 publications confirming its partially characterized status within the SPARC/osteonectin family. The protein contains multiple modular domains: N-terminal Kazal-type serine protease inhibitor domain (IPR002350, positions 37-89; IPR036058, positions 43-88; cd00104, positions 47-87; G3DSA:3.30.60.30, positions 44-87) for protein-protein interactions, two thyroglobulin type-1 domains (IPR000716, positions 92-162 and 224-296; G3DSA:4.10.800.10, positions 88-158 and 221-292; cd00191, positions 94-158 and 226-292) mediating protein binding and ECM organization, and C-terminal EF-hand calcium-binding domain pair (IPR011992, positions 313-431; IPR018247 calcium-binding sites at positions 372-384 and 409-421; IPR019577 SPARC/Testican calcium-binding domain, positions 359-424; G3DSA:1.10.238.10, positions 315-432; cd16240, positions 315-429) for calcium-dependent conformational regulation. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 73.3, high confidence), consistent with stable modular domain architecture. HPA reveals tissue-enhanced expression with brain (62.5 nTPM) and liver (58.0 nTPM) as top tissues, detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced RNA specificity, immune cell enriched blood cell specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, with annotated functions in differentiation and developmental processes, plasma membrane subcellular location, and disease involvement in microphthalmia. ClinVar documents 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (c.230del p.Pro77fs, c.661del p.Ser221fs, c.445del p.Ser149fs), nonsense mutations (c.274C>T p.Gln92Ter, c.809G>A p.Trp270Ter), missense variants (c.406T>G p.Cys136Gly), intronic variants (c.858-104T>G, c.478+279C>T), and chromosome 14q23.1-32.33 copy number gains, indicating strong dosage sensitivity affecting oculofaciocardiodental development. STRING interactions reveal SMOC1 functions within the extracellular matrix and developmental signaling network with high confidence connections to FST (0.777) - follistatin, SPARC (0.709) - secreted protein acidic and cysteine rich family member, medium confidence interactions with SFMBT2 (0.627) - Scm-like with four MBT domains protein 2, SLC38A4 (0.606) - solute carrier family 38 member 4 amino acid transporter, JADE1 (0.520) - jade family PHD finger 1, SPOCK1 (0.515) - SPARC/osteonectin CWCV and Kazal-like domains proteoglycan 1, FKBP10 (0.488) - FKBP prolyl isomerase 10 collagen chaperone, SALL1 (0.481) - spalt like transcription factor 1, CLDN14 (0.478) - claudin 14 tight junction protein, and VTN (0.475) - vitronectin ECM glycoprotein. Recent studies (2023-2025) established SMOC1 roles in human pancreatic α-cell heterogeneity and β-cell dedifferentiation in type 2 diabetes, endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition during cardiac repair after myocardial infarction, SMOC1 proteomics network controlling signaling modalities and brain homeostasis in Alzheimer's disease fly models, SMOC1 colocalization with Alzheimer's neuropathology delaying Aβ aggregation, and SMOC1 mutations causing Waardenburg anophthalmia syndrome. We hypothesize SMOC1 functions as a calcium-dependent extracellular matrix modulator that regulates tissue development and repair through the Kazal domain (positions 37-89) mediating protease inhibition and protein interactions, thyroglobulin type-1 domains (positions 92-162, 224-296) organizing ECM architecture, and EF-hand calcium-binding domain (positions 313-431) enabling calcium-sensitive conformational switching. The interaction with SPARC (0.709) suggests functional redundancy within the SPARC family for ECM remodeling. FST interaction (0.777) indicates potential TGF-β/BMP signaling modulation. SPOCK1 interaction (0.515) suggests cooperative ECM organization. The 31 ClinVar variants including frameshifts and nonsense mutations confirm loss-of-function pathogenicity causing oculofaciocardiodental disorders. SMOC1 represents an uncharacterized ECM glycoprotein linking calcium-dependent domain conformational changes with β-cell identity maintenance, cardiac endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition, Alzheimer's Aβ aggregation delay, and ocular development through modular domain-mediated protein interactions and ECM organization.
