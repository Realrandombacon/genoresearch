# ASB10: Ankyrin-SOCS Box E3 Ligase Adapter Regulating Cardiac Hypertrophy and Glaucoma via HSP70 Stabilization and CUL5 Recruitment

## Description
ASB10 (Ankyrin repeat and SOCS box-containing protein 10, UniProt Q8WXI3, alias GLC1F) is a 467 aa E3 ubiquitin ligase adapter featuring three N-terminal ankyrin repeats (IPR002110, positions 115-362; PF00023/PF12796) for substrate recognition and a C-terminal SOCS box (IPR001496, positions 420-461; PF07525) that recruits the Cullin-5/Rbx2 complex. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 79.2). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression highest in skeletal muscle (73.0 nTPM) and heart muscle (25.0 nTPM), with classification as a disease-related gene for glaucoma. STRING reveals 10 interaction partners including strong links to glaucoma-associated proteins WDR36 (0.908), MYOC (0.573), OPTN (0.400), and CYP1B1 (0.708), plus core ubiquitination machinery CUL5 (0.779) and ELOC (0.609). ClinVar contains 91 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses/gains at 7q36.1 and specific missense mutations linked to Primary Open Angle Glaucoma (POAG) and Normal-Tension Glaucoma. Literature (27 papers) establishes: (1) causal role in POAG (GLC1F locus) with variants disrupting protein stability; (2) recent 2025 discovery that ASB10 accelerates pathological cardiac remodeling by stabilizing HSP70, preventing its degradation and promoting hypertrophic signaling; (3) functional coupling of SOCS box to elongin BC/CUL5 complex to target specific substrates for proteasomal degradation. Mechanistically, ASB10 acts as a substrate-specific adapter: its ankyrin repeats bind targets like HSP70 or ocular stress factors, while the SOCS box recruits the CUL5-RBX2 E3 ligase complex to modulate their turnover, balancing cardiomyocyte stress responses and trabecular meshwork homeostasis.
