## Description

InterPro analysis reveals canonical BTB-Kelch architecture: N-terminal BTB/POZ domain (IPR000210, aa 10-136) mediating CUL3 binding and homodimerization, BACK domain (cd18485, aa 128-227) linking BTB to Kelch repeats, and C-terminal Kelch-type beta-propeller (IPR015915/IPR006652, aa 262-610) with four Kelch repeats (aa 386-436/437-492/493-547) forming a substrate-binding platform.
