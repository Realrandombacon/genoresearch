## Description
TMEM244 encodes a 128-amino acid integral membrane protein belonging to the SYS1-related family (IPR019185, residues 7-125), which shares homology with SYS1, a Golgi membrane protein involved in vesicular trafficking and retrograde transport from endosomes to the Golgi apparatus. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (pLDDT 84.3/100), indicating a well-folded transmembrane topology consistent with integral membrane protein function. HPA expression profiling reveals striking retina-enriched RNA expression (59.4 nTPM) with detection in only some tissues, suggesting a specialized role in photoreceptor or retinal pigment epithelium cell biology. The tissue-restricted expression pattern parallels other SYS1-family members involved in intracellular protein sorting. STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners including SMLR1 (score 0.574), a small nuclear RNA localization factor; PUSL1 (0.559), a mitochondrial pseudouridine synthase; RASGEF1C (0.525), a Rab guanine nucleotide exchange factor regulating vesicle trafficking; and SNAP47 (0.514), a SNARE-associated protein involved in membrane fusion events. This interaction profile strongly implicates TMEM244 in intracellular vesicular transport pathways, potentially functioning as a Golgi or endosomal membrane adaptor that recruits trafficking machinery to specific cargo vesicles in retinal cells. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 6q22.33, indicating that TMEM244 dosage is critical for retinal function and that haploinsufficiency or overexpression may contribute to retinal dystrophies or macular degeneration phenotypes. The combination of retina-specific expression, vesicular trafficking interactions, and disease-associated variants positions TMEM244 as a candidate gene for inherited retinal disorders.
