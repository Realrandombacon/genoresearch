# ZNF670: KRAB-C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with 1q44 CNV Disease Association

## Description
ZNF670 (Gene ID: 93474, UniProt Q9BS34) encodes a 389-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor. Domain architecture: N-terminal KRAB domain (residues 3-90, IPR001909, PF01352) serves as transcriptional repression module; C-terminal tandem C2H2 zinc fingers (residues 103-331, IPR013087, PF00096, 3+ fingers) mediate sequence-specific DNA binding to GC-rich motifs. Multiple evidence lines support repressor function: (1) InterPro confirms canonical KRAB-ZNF architecture with GO term "regulation of DNA-templated transcription"; (2) STRING interactions reveal TRIM28 (KAP1/TIF1β, score 0.585) as top partner — TRIM28 is the obligate KRAB domain co-repressor that recruits heterochromatin machinery (HP1α, SETDB1 histone methyltransferase, NuRD complex) to establish H3K9me3 repressive chromatin; additional partners CSNK2A2 (casein kinase II, 0.491) and TBC1D3G (RabGAP, 0.476) suggest phosphorylation-dependent regulation and potential roles in vesicular trafficking; (3) HPA shows nucleoplasmic localization consistent with transcription factor function, low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues; (4) ClinVar contains 73 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 1q43-44, including deletions and duplications associated with 1q44 deletion syndrome featuring intellectual disability, developmental delay, corpus callosum agenesis, seizures, and dysmorphic features. The 1q44 critical region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including AKT3 (PI3K-AKT signaling, brain development), HNRNPU (nuclear scaffold, neurodevelopment), and ZNF670 itself; (5) AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 68.2) shows medium confidence with ordered zinc finger domains and partially disordered linker regions typical of KRAB-ZNF proteins. Functional hypothesis: ZNF670 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that recruits TRIM28/KAP1 co-repressor complex to silence target genes via heterochromatin formation (H3K9me3, DNA methylation). The zinc finger array recognizes specific DNA sequences (likely 5-GCC(A/T)GGC-3 or similar GC-rich motifs) in promoters/enhancers of genes involved in lipid metabolism (per Alliance of Genome Resources annotation) and neurodevelopment. Haploinsufficiency from 1q44 deletions reduces ZNF670 dosage, causing derepression of target genes and contributing to 1q44 deletion syndrome neurodevelopmental phenotypes. TRIM28 interaction suggests ZNF670 may function in embryonic stem cell maintenance, genomic imprinting, or retrotransposon silencing. This represents a "dark gene" with clear domain architecture and disease association but no experimentally validated DNA binding motif or target genes.
