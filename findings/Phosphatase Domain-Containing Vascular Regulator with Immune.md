## Description
PALD1 (Paladin, Phosphatase Domain-Containing Protein 1) is an 856 aa intracellular protein functioning as a putative protein tyrosine phosphatase with multiple catalytic domains. InterPro analysis reveals three protein tyrosine phosphatase catalytic domains (IPR003595, positions 262-384) and two inositol hexakisphosphate-binding domains (PF14566, positions 161-329 and 545-677), suggesting dual functionality in dephosphorylation and phosphoinositide signaling. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 83.4, high confidence) spanning the full 1-856 aa range. STRING interactions identify medium-confidence partners including TLR9 (0.572), IRF7 (0.562), PALLD (0.507), ADAMTS14 (0.495), LASP1 (0.483), and CDH5/VE-cadherin (0.432), linking PALD1 to innate immunity, cytoskeletal organization, and endothelial junction stability. HPA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, immune cell enhancement, and subcellular localization to vesicles and cytosol. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily copy number variations with uncertain significance), suggesting potential disease associations. Literature (14 papers) documents PALD1 as a vascular-specific gene where female Pald1-knockout mice exhibit endothelial cell apoptosis and emphysema, PALD1 regulates endothelial sprouting and VE-cadherin junction stability, and a low-frequency Amerindian-specific variant associates with Type 2 Diabetes as a negative regulator of insulin signaling. Recent 2025 work reveals PALD1 involvement in ZBED3/PIP2-dependent calcium homeostasis during oocyte maturation.
