## Description
UGT8 (UniProt Q16880), also known as CGT or UGT4, encodes a 541-amino acid endoplasmic reticulum membrane-bound glycosyltransferase belonging to the UDP-glycosyltransferase superfamily (IPR002213/PF00201, aa 21-503) with conserved catalytic signature (IPR035595/PS00375, aa 340-383). The enzyme adopts the GT-B fold glycosyltransferase architecture (G3DSA:3.40.50.2000/SSF53756, aa 21-460) characteristic of UDP-sugar dependent enzymes, with two Rossmann-like domains forming the catalytic cleft for UDP-galactose binding and ceramide substrate recognition. AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally well-folded structure (pLDDT: 89.8), among the highest confidence predictions indicating stable enzymatic architecture essential for myelin lipid synthesis.\n\nSTRING interactions position UGT8 within the sphingolipid biosynthesis pathway: strongest links to glucosylceramide synthase UGCG (0.969), galactose-3-O-sulfotransferase GAL3ST1 (0.964), ceramide synthases CERS2 (0.945)/CERS4 (0.932), acid ceramidase ASAH1 (0.942), galactosylceramidase GALC (0.937), sphingosine-1-phosphate phosphatase SGPP1 (0.935), sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase SMPD2 (0.895), ceramide kinase CERK (0.600), and arylsulfatase A ARSA (0.581). HPA confirms brain-enriched expression (244.3 nTPM, tissue enriched) with mitochondrial localization and cancer-enhanced specificity.\n\nUGT8 catalyzes the final step in galactocerebroside biosynthesis by transferring galactose from UDP-galactose to ceramide, producing galactosylceramide—the predominant glycosphingolipid in myelin sheaths of central and peripheral nervous systems. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic variants (4q26 CNVs including c.*203T>A and promoter variant c.-2-109A>T). Dysregulation links to Krabbe disease pathology (via GALC interaction), multiple sclerosis demyelination, and emerging roles in cancer cell membrane remodeling. This positions UGT8 as a critical myelin maintenance enzyme with therapeutic potential in leukodystrophies and neurodegenerative disorders.
