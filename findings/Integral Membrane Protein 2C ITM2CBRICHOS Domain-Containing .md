## Description
ITM2C (Integral Membrane Protein 2C) is a 267-amino acid type II transmembrane protein with 36 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status as an emerging member of the ITM2/BRICHOS protein family. The protein belongs to the integral membrane protein 2 family (IPR040145, positions 1-265; PTHR10962, positions 1-265) containing a canonical C-terminal BRICHOS domain (IPR007084, positions 136-230; PF04089, positions 138-229; PS50869, positions 136-230; SM01039, positions 136-230; G3DSA:3.30.390.150, positions 133-229) known for chaperone activity and amyloid inhibition. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 76.2, high confidence), consistent with stable transmembrane architecture typical of type II integral membrane proteins. ClinVar documents 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly chromosome 2q33.3-37.3, 2q35-37.3, 2q36.2-37.3 copy number gains and losses, splice variant c.713-12T>G, all with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong dosage sensitivity potentially affecting neuronal development and protein processing programs. Recent studies (2019-2026) established ITM2C as promoter of poly I:C-induced inflammatory response in airway epithelial cells via NF-κB pathway activation, ITM2C gene signature component for early detection of colorectal cancer using RNA-Seq and machine learning, Lnc-ITM2C-1 and GPR55 as proviral host factors for Hepatitis C Virus replication, C-terminus domain responsible for Golgi localization and recycling of mouse Itm2C, and high brain expression in embryonic development. We hypothesize ITM2C functions as a type II transmembrane chaperone-like protein that regulates protein folding and processing through the BRICHOS domain (positions 136-230) which prevents amyloid formation and protein aggregation analogous to BRI2/ITM2B family members. The BACE1 interaction (0.508) suggests ITM2C modulates amyloid-beta precursor protein processing relevant to Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis. The FURIN (0.589) and PCSK7 (0.750) interactions indicate coordination with proprotein convertase machinery for substrate cleavage and activation. The NF-κB activation mechanism (2026 study) indicates ITM2C regulates inflammatory signaling in airway epithelium during viral mimic stimulation. The brain-enriched expression (1020.9 nTPM) suggests specialized neuronal function potentially in synaptic protein homeostasis. The Golgi localization and recycling (2005 study) indicates dynamic trafficking between Golgi and cell surface. The HCV proviral factor function (2019 study) suggests ITM2C facilitates viral replication machinery assembly. The colorectal cancer signature (2023 study) indicates diagnostic biomarker potential. The 30 ClinVar chromosome 2q CNVs reflect dosage sensitivity affecting this genomic region. ITM2C represents an uncharacterized BRICHOS domain protein linking neuronal protein homeostasis, inflammatory signaling, viral replication, and cancer detection through chaperone-mediated protein folding regulation and proprotein convertase network coordination mechanisms.
