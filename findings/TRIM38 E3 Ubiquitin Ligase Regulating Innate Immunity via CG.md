## Description
TRIM38 encodes a 465 aa RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase (UniProt O00635) with canonical TRIM architecture: N-terminal RING zinc finger (aa 16-63, IPR001841), B-box zinc finger (aa 88-129, IPR000315), and C-terminal SPRY/B30.2 domain (aa 274-465, IPR001870). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 87.3), indicating stable folded conformation. HPA shows low tissue specificity with expression across many tissues, localizing to plasma membrane, cell junctions, and centrosome. STRING interactions reveal functional connections to innate immunity signaling: TAB2 (0.767), CGAS (0.631), STAT1 (0.627), TRAF6 (0.624), and other TRIM proteins (TRIM56 0.579, TRIM32 0.552). ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutation p.Arg164Ter and copy number alterations. The SPRY domain mediates substrate recognition while the RING domain recruits E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes. TRIM38 likely functions as a negative regulator of innate immune responses by ubiquitinating and degrading signaling adaptors (TAB2, TRAF6) and DNA sensor CGAS, preventing excessive inflammation. Interaction with STAT1 suggests role in interferon signaling modulation. Centrosomal localization implicates potential function in cell cycle regulation. Disease-associated variants may disrupt ubiquitin ligase activity or substrate binding, leading to immune dysregulation.
