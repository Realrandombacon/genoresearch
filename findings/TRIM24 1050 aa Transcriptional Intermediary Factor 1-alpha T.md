## Description
TRIM24 (O15164, 1050 aa), also known as TIF1α (Transcriptional Intermediary Factor 1-alpha), PTC6, or RNF82, is a nuclear E3 ubiquitin ligase and transcriptional co-regulator belonging to the TRIM (Tripartite Motif) family and TIF1 subfamily. Functional hypothesis: TRIM24 functions as a context-dependent transcriptional co-regulator that bridges nuclear receptors (ERα, RAR, VDR) with chromatin machinery via its modular domain structure. The N-terminal TRIM motif (RING+B-boxes) mediates E3 ubiquitin ligase activity and homodimerization. The central region acts as a flexible scaffold. The C-terminal PHD-bromodomain cassette reads epigenetic marks: PHD binds unmodified H3K4 while bromodomain recognizes acetylated lysines (H3K14ac, H4K12ac), recruiting TRIM24 to active enhancers/promoters. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 1051 aa) confirms essential regulatory role. NOTE: This gene is well-characterized as TIF1α with established roles in nuclear receptor signaling and cancer.
