## Description

Located in the AZFc (azoospermia factor c) interval of Yq11.23 in the nonrecombining portion of the Y chromosome, BPY2B is one of three nearly identical copies on chromosome Y, with two copies embedded in a palindromic region - this record represents the more centromeric copy. The encoded protein interacts with ubiquitin protein ligase E3A (UBE3A) and may be involved in male germ cell development and male infertility.
