## Description
PPWD1 (Peptidylprolyl isomerase domain and WD repeat-containing protein 1) is a 646 aa nuclear protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 18 literature references despite essential roles in pre-mRNA splicing and disease pathogenesis. Multi-omics data reveals PPWD1 as a bifunctional scaffold enzyme integrating protein folding catalysis with spliceosome assembly. InterPro analysis confirms a bipartite domain architecture: an N-terminal region containing multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680, positions 80-117/122-170/211-251) forming a β-propeller scaffold for protein-protein interactions, and a C-terminal cyclophilin-type peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase domain (IPR002130/IPR044666, aa 490-645) catalyzing proline isomerization. The YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943/G3DSA:2.130.10.10, aa 78-470) provides additional protein-binding surfaces. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.9), indicating a well-folded protein with stable WD40 β-propeller and cyclophilin fold. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low specificity) with nuclear bodies subcellular localization and biological processes linked to mRNA processing and splicing. STRING interactions reveal a highly specific spliceosome network: PPWD1 associates with CDC40 (0.976, pre-mRNA splicing factor), SYF2 (0.969, spliceosome component), PPIL1 (0.968, peptidylprolyl isomerase-like 1), DHX38 (0.964, DEAH-box helicase), PRPF8 (0.943, pre-mRNA processing factor 8), CRNKL1 (0.911, crooked neck-like protein 1), CDC5L (0.897, cell division cycle 5-like), CWC22 (0.891, CWC22 spliceosome-associated), PPIG (0.871, peptidylprolyl isomerase G), and SNRPA1 (0.840, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein A1). ClinVar lists 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 5q12.3-13.2 copy number alterations (losses: chr5:64364710-72835471x1, chr5:64111112-67101123x1, chr5:65413763-66430722x1; gains: chr5:58966132-68847066x4), suggesting PPWD1 dosage sensitivity. Recent literature (2019-2023) identifies PPWD1 as: (1) A KDM4A demethylation target inhibiting osteogenic differentiation of BMSCs in postmenopausal osteoporosis via integrin/FAK signaling; (2) A PMO-associated gene identified by WGCNA; (3) A cervical carcinoma gene regulated by lncRNA RP11-284F21.9/miR-769-3p axis as ceRNA; (4) A crystallographically characterized PPIase with solved structure (2008, 24 citations). The scarcity of mechanistic literature (18 papers) despite clear spliceosome function makes PPWD1 a paradigmatic dark gene awaiting comprehensive characterization.
