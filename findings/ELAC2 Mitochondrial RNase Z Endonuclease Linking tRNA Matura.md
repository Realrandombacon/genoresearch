## Description
ELAC2 (ElaC Ribonuclease Z 2) is an 826 amino acid zinc phosphodiesterase that functions as the mitochondrial RNase Z endonuclease responsible for 3'-end processing of precursor tRNAs, an essential step in mitochondrial tRNA maturation required for oxidative phosphorylation. InterPro analysis identifies canonical RNase Z domains including N-terminal MBL-fold metallo-hydrolase domain (cd16296, residues 60-311), C-terminal MBL-fold metallo-hydrolase domain (cd07718, residues 483-688), tRNase Z endonuclease signature (IPR027794, PF13691, residues 61-120) with tRNA processing capability (GO:0008033), and 3'-tRNA processing endoribonuclease activity (IPR047151, PTHR12553, residues 57-792, GO:0042781). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9BQ52-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 82.8) across the 826 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded metalloenzyme with dual MBL-fold domains suitable for zinc-dependent RNA cleavage. HPA data classifies ELAC2 as a disease-related enzyme and essential protein involved in tRNA processing, with low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), cell type-enhanced expression, nucleoplasmic localization, and implications in primary mitochondrial disease and prostate cancer. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with mitochondrial RNA processing machinery: SUPV3L1 (ATP-dependent RNA helicase, 0.864) for mecciRNA degradation; EXOG (Exonuclease G, 0.857) and ENDOG (Endonuclease G, 0.853) for mitochondrial DNA/RNA metabolism; DCLRE1A (SNM1A DNA repair nuclease, 0.824); RNASEL (Ribonuclease L, 0.766); PRORP (Protein-only RNase P, 0.728) for 5'-tRNA leader removal; TRMT10C (tRNA methyltransferase 10C, 0.552) for tRNA modification; ELAC1 (cytoplasmic paralog, 0.529); CPSF3 (Cleavage and polyadenylation specificity factor, 0.484); and TRNT1 (tRNA nucleotidyltransferase, 0.433) for 3'-CCA addition. ClinVar lists 149 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (p.Leu133fs, p.Ser736fs, p.Val460fs), nonsense mutations (p.Cys311Ter, p.Trp115Ter, p.Gln330Ter), splice variants (c.679+4A>G, c.680-1G>A), and missense changes (p.Leu763Pro), causing Combined Oxidative Phosphorylation Deficiency with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, sensorimotor polyneuropathy, pulmonary hypertension, and lactic acidosis; germline variants also confer familial prostate cancer susceptibility. Literature (191 papers) establishes ELAC2 as critical for mitochondrial pre-tRNA 3'-trailer processing following PRORP-mediated 5'-leader removal, with recent work demonstrating ELAC2/SUPV3L1-mediated degradation of mitochondria-encoded circular RNAs (mecciRNAs) providing therapeutic opportunities for heart failure; variants cause severe pediatric-onset mitochondrial dysfunction primarily presenting as hypertrophic cardiomyopathy; and association with familial pancreatic cancer. As a well-studied gene with established disease mechanisms but emerging roles in non-canonical RNA regulation, ELAC2 represents a critical node connecting mitochondrial tRNA maturation to energy metabolism, cardiac function, and cancer susceptibility through both canonical translation fidelity and mecciRNA turnover pathways.
