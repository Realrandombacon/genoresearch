## Description
RASL12 (UniProt Q9NYN1), or Ras-like protein family member 12, encodes a compact 266-amino acid intracellular GTPase belonging to the Ras superfamily (IPR001806/IPR005225/IPR051065/PTHR45704). The protein architecture features a canonical P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase domain (G3DSA:3.40.50.300/IPR027417, aa 18-202) with a specific Ras family core (PF00071/SM00173, aa 23-183) and Rab/Ras domain profiles (PS51419/PS51421, aa 16-260), enabling GTP binding and hydrolysis to act as a molecular switch. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (global pLDDT: 78.4), indicating a well-folded G-domain with stable switch I/II regions critical for effector binding, consistent with active small GTPases.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals RASL12's integration into cytoskeletal and vesicular trafficking networks: ARHGEF17 (0.568, Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor), FHL2 (0.562, four and a half LIM domains protein 2), ITSN2 (0.540)/ITSN1 (0.537, intersectin scaffolds linking endocytosis to actin), RASL10A (0.497)/RASL10B (0.426, close paralogs), ARL5A (0.474, ADP-ribosylation factor-like GTPase), ZNF853 (0.409), and CHRNA7 (0.404, nicotinic acetylcholine receptor). This partner profile positions RASL12 at the intersection of Rho-family signaling, endocytic trafficking, and potentially cholinergic modulation.\n\nWith only 19 papers, RASL12 qualifies as a genuine dark gene with emerging disease associations in 2024-2026. Recent studies reveal: (1) RASL12 is identified as a disease-associated neurotoxic astrocyte marker in Alzheimer's disease single-nucleus RNA-seq datasets, suggesting a role in glial activation; (2) variants near RASL12 are associated with cerebral small vessel disease (CSVD), implicating it in vascular integrity and blood-brain barrier function; (3) machine learning identifies RASL12 as a shared diagnostic biomarker for age-related hearing loss and sarcopenia, linking it to sensory-neural degeneration and muscle aging; (4) RASL12 is part of cancer-associated fibroblast (CAF) signature genes predicting prognosis in hepatocellular carcinoma, indicating a role in tumor microenvironment remodeling. HPA data confirms vascular enrichment (blood vessel: 273.6 nTPM top tissue) with cancer-enhanced expression, classified as a hydrolase/enzyme. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic variants including chromosome 15q11.2-26.3 copy number gains and specific SNVs (p.Gly13Arg, p.Met67Val, p.Arg63Lys) affecting the G-domain, confirming functional dosage sensitivity. We hypothesize RASL12 functions as a specialized vascular GTPase regulating endothelial barrier integrity and pericyte communication: activated by ARHGEF17-mediated nucleotide exchange, it recruits intersectin scaffolds (ITSN1/2) to coordinate clathrin-mediated endocytosis with actin remodeling, maintaining vessel tone and permeability—the CSVD and Alzheimer's astrocyte links suggest that RASL12 dysfunction leads to impaired clearance of toxic metabolites (e.g., amyloid-beta) across the blood-brain barrier, while its CAF association implies a parallel role in activating fibroblastic contractility and ECM deposition in liver tumors.
