## Description
BCDIN3D (BCDIN3 domain containing RNA methyltransferase) encodes a 292 aa cytoplasmic RNA methyltransferase (Q7Z5W3) that monomethylates the 5'-monophosphate of specific RNAs including tRNAHis and regulates microRNA processing. With 29 publications, this gene is PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED with defined biochemical activity but incompletely understood disease mechanisms. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: BCDIN3D functions as an RNA phospho-methyltransferase that modifies 5'-ends of cytoplasmic tRNA fragments and select mRNAs, regulating RNA stability, translation efficiency, and microRNA biogenesis to control glycolytic metabolism and cell proliferation. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: BCDIN3D operates through: (1) tRNA fragment processing - the 2019 biochemical study (32 citations) demonstrated BCDIN3D regulates tRNAHis 3' fragment processing through 5'-monophosphate methylation, affecting fragment stability and function; (2) Glycolysis regulation - BCDIN3D stimulates Aldolase C (ALDOC) expression through let-7 microRNA modulation (26 citations, 2021), linking RNA methylation to glycolytic flux in breast cancer cells; (3) Cancer progression - overexpressed in breast cancer with poor prognosis, targeted by miR-195-3p in cervical cancer (14 citations, 2020), miR-195-3p/BCDIN3D axis inhibits cell proliferation; (4) Female fertility - Drosophila studies (14 citations, 2019) show BCDIN3D crucial for ovarian miRNA/mRNA profiles and fertility, conserved reproductive function; (5) Obesity network - STRING interactions with obesity-associated genes SEC16B (0.691), TMEM18 (0.600), SH2B1 (0.598), NEGR1 (0.571), MTCH2 (0.581), GNPDA2 (0.653) suggest unexpected metabolic regulation role.
