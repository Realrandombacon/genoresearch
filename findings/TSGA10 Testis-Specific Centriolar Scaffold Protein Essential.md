## Description
TSGA10 encodes a 698-amino acid testis-specific structural protein (UniProt Q9BZW7) that localizes to the flagellar centriole and sperm fibrous sheath. InterPro analysis reveals a centriole and basal body structural protein domain (IPR051877, PTHR20544) spanning residues 2-684, indicating its role as a scaffold for axonemal assembly. AlphaFold prediction shows a well-folded structure with high confidence (pLDDT 81.6), consistent with a stable structural protein. HPA data demonstrates testis-enriched expression at 48.2 nTPM with specific localization to flagellar centrioles. STRING interactions identify 10 protein partners including BRDT (0.600), CEP112 (0.592), SUN5 (0.571), PMFBP1 (0.570), ODF4 (0.556), and AKAP3 (0.541) — all known components of the sperm flagellar apparatus and manchette structure. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants associated with spermatogenic failure 26 (SPGF26), confirming clinical relevance. The protein shows moderate conservation across mammals (mouse ortholog Q6NY15, 697 aa; rat Q9Z220, 712 aa). TSGA10 likely functions as a molecular scaffold that bridges centriolar microtubules with outer dense fibers during spermiogenesis, coordinating the assembly of the sperm motility apparatus through interactions with CEP proteins and outer dense fiber components.
