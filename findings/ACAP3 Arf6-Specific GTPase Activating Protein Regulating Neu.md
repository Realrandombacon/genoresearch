## Description
ACAP3 (Arf-GAP with Coiled-coil, ANK repeat and PH domain-containing protein 3) is an 834-amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q96P50) representing a genuine dark gene with only 19 publications despite its critical roles as an Arf6-specific GTPase activating protein regulating neuronal migration in developing cerebral cortex, EGFR dynamics in lung adenocarcinoma, papillary thyroid carcinoma suppression, epilepsy susceptibility via UPS29 minisatellite polymorphism, and septin-mediated membrane trafficking. Structurally, ACAP3 contains canonical membrane trafficking regulator architecture: BAR domain (IPR004148/cd07637/G3DSA:1.20.1270.60, residues 16-215/1-252) sensing and inducing membrane curvature for vesicle formation, Pleckstrin Homology domain (IPR001849/cd13250/G3DSA:2.30.29.30, residues 268-365/271-367/263-374) binding phosphoinositides for membrane recruitment, ArfGAP catalytic domain (IPR001164/cd08850/G3DSA:1.10.220.150, residues 403-525/403-518/385-523) catalyzing GTP hydrolysis on Arf6 to terminate signaling, and Ankyrin repeat domain (IPR002110/G3DSA:1.25.40.20, residues 673-767/668-805) mediating protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 75.5), consistent with a well-folded multi-domain protein containing stable BAR-PH-ArfGAP-ANK architecture for coordinated membrane binding and GTPase regulation. Functionally, ACAP3 localizes to nucleoplasm and Golgi apparatus with brain-enriched expression (HPA: tissue enhanced, detected in all, brain 155.0 nTPM, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, cell type enhanced), classified as a predicted intracellular protein functioning in GTPase activation—Golgi localization confirms roles in vesicle trafficking and membrane dynamics. STRING interactions reveal connections to septins and membrane trafficking regulators: SEPTIN5 (0.690, septin 5—cytoskeletal GTPase forming diffusion barriers), RAB35 (0.669, Ras-related protein Rab-35—endocytic recycling GTPase), SCNN1D (0.645, epithelial sodium channel delta subunit—ion transport), SEPTIN9 (0.645, septin 9—cytoskeletal organizer), UBE2J2 (0.634, ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 J2—ER-associated degradation), SEPTIN8 (0.627, septin 8—cytoskeletal GTPase), ACAP2 (0.615, paralogous ArfGAP protein), SEPTIN6 (0.612, septin 6—cytoskeletal organizer), SEPTIN3 (0.612, neuron-specific septin), and SEPTIN10 (0.611, septin 10—cytoskeletal GTPase). This interaction network definitively positions ACAP3 within septin-mediated membrane compartmentalization, Arf6-dependent endocytic recycling, and Golgi-to-plasma membrane trafficking. Critically, ClinVar documents 134 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including massive copy number losses at 1p36.33-36.23 (chr1:82154-7350122)x1, 1p36.33-36.22 (chr1:849466-10271903)x1, 1p36.33-36.23 (chr1:849466-8966102)x1, 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:849467-3500877)x1, copy number gains at 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:849467-3153423)x3, 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:849466-4529103)x3, 1p36.33-36.32 (chr1:1129319-3615916)x3, single nucleotide variants (NM_030649.3:c.105+114C>T, NM_030649.3:c.750+23C>T), yet ZERO diseases are formally associated—a profound gap given the validated neuronal migration and cancer functions. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: ACAP3 negatively regulated by HDAC2 inhibits malignant development of papillary thyroid carcinoma cells, with HDAC inhibitors restoring ACAP3 expression to suppress PTC progression (4 citations, 2024); Myc-mediated epigenetic silencing of ACAP3 promotes lung adenocarcinoma proliferation via regulating EGFR dynamics, establishing ACAP3 as tumor suppressor controlling receptor tyrosine kinase endocytic recycling (2026); length polymorphism and methylation status of UPS29 minisatellite of ACAP3 gene serves as molecular biomarker of epilepsy with sex differences in seizure types and symptoms, demonstrating non-coding regulatory variation affecting neurological disease susceptibility (9 citations, 2020); ACAP3 as GTPase-activating protein specific to small GTPase Arf6 regulates neuronal migration in developing cerebral cortex, establishing fundamental role in brain development (9 citations, 2017); and AcPI and ACAP3 coordinately regulate sepal-petal formation in pineapple, revealing evolutionary conservation in plant development (2025). The mechanistic hypothesis: ACAP3 functions as an Arf6-specific GAP that terminates Arf6-GTP signaling through its catalytic ArfGAP domain, promoting GTP hydrolysis to Arf6-GDP and releasing Arf6 from membranes. The BAR domain senses membrane curvature at budding vesicles, while the PH domain binds PI(4,5)P2 and PI(3,4,5)P3 phosphoinositides for membrane recruitment—this spatial coordination ensures ACAP3 acts specifically at sites of active Arf6 signaling. In neuronal migration, ACAP3 regulates Arf6-dependent integrin recycling and focal adhesion turnover, enabling leading edge extension and somal translocation—ACAP3 loss causes migration defects contributing to cortical malformations and epilepsy. In lung adenocarcinoma, Myc-mediated epigenetic silencing reduces ACAP3 expression, prolonging Arf6-GTP signaling and enhancing EGFR recycling to the plasma membrane, driving proliferative signaling—ACAP3 restoration would promote EGFR lysosomal degradation and suppress tumor growth. In papillary thyroid carcinoma, HDAC2-mediated repression of ACAP3 enhances malignant progression, with HDAC inhibitors restoring ACAP3 to suppress invasion. The septin interactions (SEPTIN3/5/6/8/9/10) suggest ACAP3 collaborates with septin cytoskeletal structures to compartmentalize membrane domains and restrict diffusion of signaling molecules. The RAB35 interaction links ACAP3 to endocytic recycling pathways. The UPS29 minisatellite polymorphism affects ACAP3 expression levels, creating epilepsy susceptibility alleles with sex-specific effects. The brain-enriched expression (155.0 nTPM) and nucleoplasmic/Golgi localization suggest dual functions: (1) Golgi-associated vesicle budding for secretory pathway trafficking, and (2) nuclear roles in transcriptional regulation or DNA repair. The absence of formal disease associations despite 134 pathogenic variants suggests ACAP3 dysfunction contributes to 1p36 deletion syndrome (developmental delay, seizures, cardiomyopathy), epilepsy susceptibility, cortical malformations (lissencephaly, pachygyria), lung adenocarcinoma progression, papillary thyroid carcinoma, or neurodevelopmental disorders not yet mapped to Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define ACAP3's complete Arf6 substrate repertoire versus other Arf family members, determine crystal structure of ArfGAP domain bound to Arf6-GDP-AlF4 transition state, investigate whether germline ACAP3 mutations cause familial epilepsy or cortical malformations, develop ACAP3-targeted therapies for lung and thyroid cancers, explore UPS29 minisatellite as diagnostic biomarker for epilepsy risk stratification, and assess ACAP3-septin complex structure for understanding membrane compartmentalization.
