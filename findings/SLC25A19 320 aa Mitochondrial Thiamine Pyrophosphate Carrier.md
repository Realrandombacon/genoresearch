## Description
SLC25A19 (Q9HC21, 320 aa), also known as MTPPT (Mitochondrial Thiamine Pyrophosphate Carrier), DNC (DeoxyNucleotide Carrier), MCPHA, MUP1, THMD3, THMD4, or TPC, is a mitochondrial transporter belonging to solute carrier family 25 (mitochondrial carrier family, MCF). HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity); localized to mitochondria; classified as metabolic protein, disease-related gene (neuropathy), and potential drug target involved in antiport and transport. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A19/MTPPT functions as a mitochondrial inner membrane thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP/coenzyme B1) importer that transports cytosolic TPP into the mitochondrial matrix via antiport mechanism (likely TPP/OH- or TPP/metabolite exchange).
