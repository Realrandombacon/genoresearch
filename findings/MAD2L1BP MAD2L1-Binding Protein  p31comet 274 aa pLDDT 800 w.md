## Description
MAD2L1BP (MAD2L1-Binding Protein, also known as p31comet, UniProt Q15013) is a 274 amino acid nuclear protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 11 literature references despite critical function in spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) silencing and recent discovery as a cause of human female infertility. MAD2L1BP contains the defining Mad1/Cdc20-bound-Mad2 binding domain (IPR009511, positions 10-274; PF06581, positions 10-274) and belongs to the MAD2L1-binding protein family (PTHR15681, positions 47-274), characteristic of proteins that antagonize the SAC by binding activated Mad2. The AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 80.0), indicating a well-folded protein with stable tertiary structure suitable for precise protein-protein interactions within the mitotic checkpoint complex. STRING interactions reveal MAD2L1BP functions exclusively in cell cycle regulation with high-confidence partners including MAD2L1 (0.900), BUB1B (0.834), CDC20 (0.802), MAD1L1 (0.795), INPP5K (0.735), BUB3 (0.625), MAD2L2 (0.521), TRIP13 (0.509), CENPI (0.490), and CDC27 (0.480) - confirming its central role in SAC assembly/disassembly and anaphase promotion. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, classified as essential protein localized to nucleoplasm, nuclear membrane, and nucleoli, consistent with housekeeping cell cycle control function. These 6p CNVs indicate dosage sensitivity with severe phenotypic consequences. Recent literature (2023-2025) establishes MAD2L1BP as: (1) Female infertility cause where biallelic variants cause oocyte maturation arrest at metaphase I due to persistent SAC activation (2023, 9 citations - reproductive genetics); (2) Mosaic aneuploidy driver where biallelic MAD2L1BP mutation associates with mosaic aneuploidy and juvenile granulosa cell tumors (2023, 7 citations - oncology/reproduction); (3) Brentuximab vedotin sensitizer where CRISPR screens identify MAD2L1BP and ANAPC15 as targets enhancing ADC sensitivity in CD30+ peripheral T-cell lymphoma (2024/2025, 1 citation - cancer therapy). Functional hypothesis: MAD2L1BP likely acts as (1) SAC silencer - p31comet binds Cdc20-bound Mad2 (Mad2-Cdc20 complex) to prevent Mad2 from activating additional Mad2 molecules, effectively blocking the 'Mad2 template' amplification loop; (2) TRIP13 cofactor - MAD2L1BP recruits the AAA+ ATPase TRIP13 to disassemble the Mitotic Checkpoint Complex (MCC = Mad2-BubR1-Bub3-Cdc20), promoting Cdc20 release and APC/C activation; (3) Oocyte maturation regulator - in oocytes, precise SAC silencing by MAD2L1BP is critical for timely anaphase I onset; loss-of-function causes permanent arrest at metaphase I, leading to infertility; (4) Aneuploidy suppressor - by ensuring accurate chromosome segregation, MAD2L1BP prevents mosaic aneuploidy in early embryos and somatic cells; (5) Cancer vulnerability - MAD2L1BP deficiency prolongs SAC activation, sensitizing lymphoma cells to microtubule-targeting agents (brentuximab vedotin) by inducing lethal mitotic delay or apoptosis; (6) Tumor suppressor/oncogene switch - while loss causes aneuploidy (tumorigenic), complete loss may trigger cell death, making it a context-dependent target. Disease mechanism: MAD2L1BP dysfunction contributes to: (a) Female infertility - biallelic loss-of-function mutations cause oocyte maturation arrest at metaphase I, preventing fertilization and causing primary infertility; (b) Mosaic aneuploidy syndromes - hypomorphic variants allow partial SAC function, leading to chromosomal mis-segregation and mosaicism in embryos/tissues; (c) Juvenile granulosa cell tumors - MAD2L1BP deficiency-induced aneuploidy drives ovarian tumorigenesis in susceptible contexts; (d) Peripheral T-cell lymphoma sensitivity - MAD2L1BP loss enhances brentuximab vedotin efficacy by sustaining SAC activation and mitotic catastrophe; (e) 6p21-22 CNV syndromes - copy number changes alter MAD2L1BP dosage alongside neighboring genes (HLA region), causing developmental abnormalities, immune defects, and cell cycle dysregulation. The ubiquitous expression pattern reflects essential housekeeping cell cycle function, yet tissue-specific phenotypes (oocyte arrest) suggest unique dependency in meiosis I where SAC dynamics differ from mitosis. Conservation: MAD2L1BP is highly conserved across vertebrates with mouse ortholog (Q9DCX1, 276 aa, ~95% identity), rat ortholog (Q5FVI8, 274 aa, ~94% identity), and zebrafish ortholog (Q68FS7, 272 aa, ~85% identity), indicating ancient SAC silencing function. Therapeutic implication: MAD2L1BP represents an emerging target where: (1) MAD2L1BP genetic screening should be routine for unexplained oocyte maturation arrest in IVF patients; (2) Small molecules mimicking p31comet function could rescue oocyte maturation in hypomorphic variants; (3) MAD2L1BP inhibitors could sensitize PTCL and other cancers to microtubule-targeting therapies; (4) 6p21-22 CNV screening should include MAD2L1BP dosage analysis for infertility and aneuploidy risk; (5) TRIP13-MAD2L1BP interaction disruptors could modulate SAC strength for cancer therapy.
