## Description
GPR155 (UniProt Q7Z3F1), also known as LYCHOS or DEPDC3, encodes an 870 aa multi-domain lysosomal membrane protein functioning as a cholesterol sensor that regulates mTORC1 signaling, amino acid starvation responses, and cellular cholesterol homeostasis through integrated transmembrane transport and intracellular signaling mechanisms.
