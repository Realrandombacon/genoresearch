# FNDC4: Secreted Fibronectin Type III Domain Protein Modulating Metabolic Inflammation, Cancer Immune Evasion, and Tissue Repair via FN1 Interaction and AMPKα Signaling in Liver, Adrenal, and Pancreatic Microenvironments

## Description
FNDC4 (Fibronectin type III domain-containing protein 4, Q9H6D8) encodes a small 234 aa secreted protein functioning as a metabolic and inflammatory regulator that protects against hepatocyte cell death, drives cancer metastasis, and modulates immune responses in tissue microenvironments. Structural architecture is dominated by a single Fibronectin type III domain (IPR003961/PF00041/PS50853/SM00060/cd00063, residues 47-140/47-127/49-134) forming an immunoglobulin-like β-sandwich fold (G3DSA:2.60.40.10/IPR013783/IPR036116/SSF49265, residues 46-141/43-141) characteristic of extracellular matrix proteins involved in protein-protein interactions; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 69.8, medium confidence) indicates a partially structured protein with a well-folded FN3 core embedded in flexible N-terminal and C-terminal regions, consistent with a secreted signaling molecule requiring conformational plasticity for receptor engagement. STRING interactions reveal ECM and trafficking connections: fibronectin FN1 (0.659, highest confidence) as the primary binding partner suggesting FNDC4 integrates into fibronectin matrices or competes for integrin binding sites, adhesion GPCR ADGRF5 (0.633) as a potential membrane receptor mediating FNDC4 signaling, zinc finger transcription factor ZNF512 (0.594) linking to transcriptional regulation, translation initiation factor EIF2B4 (0.583) connecting to stress response pathways, sorting nexin SNX17 (0.507) regulating endosomal recycling, intraflagellar transport protein IFT172 (0.438) suggesting ciliary involvement, and structural proteins CCDC61 (0.547), KRTCAP3 (0.564), ATRAID (0.557), C2orf16 (0.570). HPA localizes FNDC4 to nucleoplasm, aggresome, and cytosol with tissue-enhanced expression highest in liver (125.5 nTPM) reflecting its role in metabolic liver disease protection, adrenal gland (124.6 nTPM) indicating stress hormone regulation, cancer-enhanced expression across multiple tumor types, group-enriched single cell specificity, and not detected in immune cells suggesting indirect immune modulation. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of chromosome 2p copy number alterations: gains at 2p25.3-23.1 (chr2:12771-30565600x3), 2p24.1-22.2 (chr2:20938401-37327210x3), 2p25.3-22.3 (chr2:12771-35541353x3, chr2:706460-35523639x3), 2p25.3-q37.3 (chr2:1-243199373, chr2:12771-242783384x3), and 2p23.3-21 (chr2:24601818-43466284x3, chr2:24881528-43460021x3), plus loss at 2p25.1-q13 (chr2:11504318-111365996x1); these CNVs are associated with developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (52 papers) establishes FNDC4 as a protective factor in MASLD/MASH where it reduces hepatocyte inflammatory cell death via AMPKα activation preventing progression to steatohepatitis, drives pancreatic cancer metastasis and immune evasion by reprogramming tumor microenvironment, serves as prognostic biomarker in lung adenocarcinoma predicting immunotherapy response, prevents aging-related cardiac dysfunction, and is associated with alcohol use disorder altering glutamatergic/GABAergic neurogenesis; FN1 interaction suggests FNDC4 may modulate integrin signaling or ECM remodeling during tissue repair; ADGRF5 represents an orphan adhesion GPCR that could serve as the cognate receptor translating FNDC4 binding into intracellular signals; high liver/adrenal expression correlates with metabolic and stress response functions; cancer-enhanced expression indicates oncogenic potential through immune suppression mechanisms.
