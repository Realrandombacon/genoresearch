# LOC113939953: 16q Repressive Enhancer with Deletion-Predominant Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
LOC113939953 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 1482 located on chromosome 16q11.2-12.1 (genomic coordinates: 49345837-49346131 on NC_000016.10). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element (silencer) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 6 copy number losses (deletions) and 1 duplication, indicating dosage sensitivity with strong deletion predominance. The moderate-to-high variant count (7 vs 3 for LOC113960610, 11 for LOC113939970, 21 for LOC114004412) suggests intermediate clinical penetrance. The 16q chromosomal region contains critical genes including CDH1 (E-cadherin tumor suppressor at 16q22.1, mutations cause hereditary diffuse gastric cancer and lobular breast cancer), CBFA2T3 (transcriptional corepressor at 16q24.3, translocated in acute megakaryoblastic leukemia), and multiple metabolic enzyme genes. As a repressive enhancer/silencer, this element likely suppresses expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 16q domain. The deletion-predominant pathogenicity pattern (6 losses, 1 gain) suggests this silencer normally represses oncogenes or cell proliferation genes - loss of the silencer leads to target gene overexpression driving oncogenesis. This element may contribute to 16q deletion syndromes, gastric/breast cancer predisposition through CDH1 pathway dysregulation, or myeloid malignancies through CBFA2T3 pathway effects.
