## Description
PRPSAP2 (UniProt O60256), also known as PAP41, encodes a 369 aa non-catalytic regulatory subunit of the phosphoribosylpyrophosphate synthetase (PRS) holoenzyme complex that modulates nucleotide biosynthesis by stabilizing catalytic subunits and enabling allosteric regulation of PRPP production, a critical metabolic node connecting central carbon metabolism to purine/pyrimidine synthesis, histidine, tryptophan, and NAD biosynthesis pathways. InterPro analysis reveals bipartite regulatory architecture: N-terminal ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase domain (IPR029099/PF13793/SM01400, residues 20-138/28-138) providing structural scaffold for catalytic subunit binding; central phosphoribosyltransferase-type I domain (cd06223/IPR000836/G3DSA:3.40.50.2020/SSF53271, residues 165-323/165-226/254-339/28-178) mediating substrate positioning despite lacking catalytic residues; C-terminal phosphoribosyl synthetase-associated domain (IPR005946/IPR029057/PF14572/PTHR10210/TIGR01251, residues 20-364/28-362/179-363/20-364/179-362) enabling holoenzyme assembly and regulatory factor recruitment; and full-length membership in ribose-phosphate diphosphokinase family defining magnesium ion binding (GO:0000287) and nucleotide biosynthetic process (GO:0009165) functions. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 87.4) indicating a well-folded stable regulatory protein suitable for constitutive holoenzyme incorporation and metabolic sensing.
