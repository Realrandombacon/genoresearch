ClinVar identifies 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses at 3p21.31-26.3, though no specific Mendelian disease is annotated. Literature (20 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: TCAIM is a mitochondrial DNAJ co-chaperone that reduces OGDH protein levels (2025, cited 8x), controls effector T cell generation by preventing mito-ER contact site-initiated cholesterol biosynthesis (2021), suppresses renal carcinoma growth and enhances sunitinib sensitivity (2022), and is linked to lipid metabolism (2025). The gene represents a 'semi-dark' metabolic regulator where the canonical DNAJ chaperone function is emerging, but specific mechanisms linking OGDH proteostasis to T-cell effector differentiation, tumor metabolism, and immune checkpoint regulation remain underexplored therapeutic targets for cancer immunotherapy and autoimmune disease.
