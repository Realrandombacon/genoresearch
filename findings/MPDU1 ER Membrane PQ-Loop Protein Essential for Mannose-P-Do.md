# MPDU1: ER Membrane PQ-Loop Protein Essential for Mannose-P-Dolichol Utilization in N-Glycosylation and Congenital Disorder of Glycosylation

**Date:** 2026-05-07T11:16:49.684087

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
MPDU1 encodes a 247-amino acid mannose-P-dolichol utilization defect 1 protein (UniProt O75352), an endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein essential for N-linked glycosylation through mannose-P-dolichol (Man-P-Dol) utilization. The protein contains two PQ-loop repeats (IPR006603, PF04193, positions 42-101 and 167-211), a transmembrane transport motif found in cystinosin and other membrane transporters, and belongs to the MPDU1/LEC35 family (IPR016817, positions 10-246). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.8, residues 1-247) indicating stable folding consistent with membrane protein architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals MPDU1 forms part of the N-glycosylation machinery with ultra-high confidence interactions: DPM1 (0.967), DPM2 (0.931), DPM3 (0.762) forming the dolichol-P-mannose synthase complex, plus glycosyltransferases ALG6 (0.741), ALG12 (0.652), ALG3 (0.642), phosphatase DOLPP1 (0.704), and GPI-anchor biosynthesis enzymes PIGV (0.634), PIGO (0.622), confirming its central role in glycosylation pathway. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with endoplasmic reticulum and mitochondrial localization, annotated for transport biological process, with disease involvement in congenital disorder of glycosylation (CDG). ClinVar contains 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.389-2A>G, c.389-2A>T), missense variants (p.Ser101Arg), nonsense mutations (p.Tyr23Ter), frameshifts (p.Ile65fs, p.Cys223fs), and copy number variations at 17p13, causing MPDU1-CDG (formerly CDG-Ip). With 44 publications, MPDU1 is moderately characterized: recent studies identify novel compound heterozygous variants causing CDG with erythrokeratodermia variabilis (2024, 1 cit), severe ciliopathy-like phenotype in infants with missense variants (2023, 3 cit), severe ichthyosis presentation (2018, 10 cit), and MPDU1 regulating CEACAM1 cell adhesion (2018, 2 cit). The functional hypothesis: MPDU1 acts as a membrane transporter or flippase facilitating Man-P-Dol translocation across ER membrane for use by ALG glycosyltransferases in lipid-linked oligosaccharide (LLO) assembly; PQ-loop repeats form the transport pore while transmembrane domains anchor the protein; disease variants disrupt Man-P-Dol availability causing hypoglycosylation of N-glycoproteins, explaining multi-system CDG phenotypes including skin (ichthyosis), neurological (ciliopathy-like), and developmental defects through impaired protein folding and trafficking.
