## Description
SLC46A1 (Q96NT5, 459 aa) is a proton-coupled folate transporter (PCFT) belonging to the Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS) of secondary active transporters. InterPro identifies complete MFS architecture: MFS domain (IPR011701, PF07690, aa 91-405), MFS superfamily (IPR036259, IPR020846, aa 28-448), with conserved sugar transporter signature (IPR005829, PS00216, aa 104-121) and MFS profile (PS50850, aa 28-448). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.1, residues 1-459) consistent with canonical 12-transmembrane helix MFS fold with central substrate-binding cavity. HPA shows intestine-enriched expression (88.2 nTPM) with ubiquitous tissue distribution, plasma membrane and cytosol localization; classified as essential protein, disease-related, and potential drug target. STRING reveals 10 interactions forming folate/heme metabolism network: QDPR (0.819), FOLR1 (0.815), SLC11A2/DMT1 (0.704), FOLR3 (0.666), FLVCR1 (0.545), SLC19A1/RFC1 (0.537), CYBRD1 (0.526), GART (0.480), HMOX1 (0.456), and FOLR2 (0.424). ClinVar contains 49 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Glu9Ter, p.Tyr46Ter), frameshifts (p.Ala163fs, p.Ser393fs, p.Leu418fs, p.Cys66fs), and splice variants causing hereditary folate malabsorption. Functional hypothesis: SLC46A1 functions as a proton-dependent symporter mediating intestinal folate absorption and choroid plexus folate transport into CNS. The MFS 12-TM structure alternates between outward- and inward-facing conformations, coupling folate uptake to proton electrochemical gradient (optimal at acidic pH 5.5-6.0). Strong interactions with folate receptors (FOLR1, FOLR2, FOLR3) and RFC1 (SLC19A1) suggest coordinated folate homeostasis. QDPR interaction (0.819) links to tetrahydrobiopterin regeneration. SLC11A2/DMT1 (0.704) and FLVCR1 (0.545) interactions indicate dual role as heme transporter in duodenal enterocytes. Pathogenic variants disrupt transporter folding, trafficking, or proton coupling, causing severe folate deficiency with megaloblastic anemia and neurological deficits. NOTE: This gene is well-characterized as disease-causing (hereditary folate malabsorption) with established clinical significance.
