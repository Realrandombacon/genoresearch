## Description
PPP1R13B (Apoptosis-stimulating of p53 protein 1, ASPP1, UniProt Q96KQ4) is a 1090 aa multi-domain scaffold protein with a characteristic domain architecture: N-terminal Ras-associating (RA) domain (IPR028319, positions 2-86) for membrane recruitment and Ras signaling integration, central ankyrin repeat region (IPR002110, positions 893-985) for protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal SH3 domain (IPR001452, positions 1019-1081) for binding proline-rich motifs. AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 60.0), indicating a partially structured protein with flexible linker regions between domains, consistent with scaffold protein behavior. HPA data reveals ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, annotated to apoptosis biological process. STRING interactions confirm functional clustering with p53 pathway components: TP53 (0.986), TP73 (0.728), TP53BP2 (0.726), protein phosphatase 1 catalytic subunits PPP1CA (0.938), PPP1CC (0.870), PPP1CB (0.738), Hippo pathway kinase LATS2 (0.687), and Ras signaling regulators RASSF8 (0.924), RASSF7 (0.919), HIF1AN (0.901). ClinVar contains 59 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 14q32.2-32.33. Literature (31 papers) reveals ASPP1 enhances p53 transactivation of proapoptotic genes, predicts therapy resistance in acute myeloid leukemia (preferentially expressed in hematopoietic cells), regulates myoblast proliferation via estrogen-mediated miR-485-5p targeting, promotes skeletal muscle satellite cell proliferation/differentiation through circular RNA formation targeting miR-9-5p, and mediates lung fibrotic response in silicosis via ER stress. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ASPP1 acts as a molecular bridge between p53 family transcription factors and protein phosphatase 1 (PP1), modulating p53 phosphorylation status and transcriptional activity in response to cellular stress. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The RA domain recruits ASPP1 to membranes upon Ras activation. Ankyrin repeats bind p53/TP73, positioning them for dephosphorylation by PP1 catalytic subunits (PPP1CA/B/C). The SH3 domain may recruit additional regulators via proline-rich motifs. In cancer, ASPP1 overexpression enhances p53-mediated apoptosis but also confers therapy resistance through unknown mechanisms. In muscle, circular PPP1R13B RNA sponges miR-9-5p, promoting satellite cell proliferation. In lung fibrosis, ASPP1 mediates ER stress responses. The 59 ClinVar variants suggest ASPP1 dysfunction contributes to developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility.
