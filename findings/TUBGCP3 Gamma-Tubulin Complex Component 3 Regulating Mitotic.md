## Description
TUBGCP3 encodes a 907 aa protein (UniProt Q96CW5) located at 13q32.1, functioning as an essential structural subunit of the γ-tubulin ring complex (γ-TuRC) and small complex (γ-TuSC) that nucleates microtubule assembly at the centrosome. InterPro analysis confirms its canonical architecture: N-terminal domain (PF17681, aa 251-552) and C-terminal domain (PF04130, aa 555-886) forming the core γ-tubulin binding interface (IPR007259), with specific subdomains for microtubule cytoskeleton organization (GO:0000226) and nucleation (GO:0007020). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 73.7), indicating a stable, well-folded protein critical for centrosomal anchoring. STRING interactions reveal a tight, high-confidence complex with core γ-TuRC components: MZT1 (0.999, microtubule-associated protein 1), TUBGCP5 (0.999, γ-TuRC subunit), TUBGCP2 (0.999, γ-TuRC subunit), MZT2B (0.972, microtubule-associated protein 2), NEDD1 (0.971, ubiquitin ligase), TUBGCP6 (0.915, γ-TuRC subunit), TUBG1 (0.888, tubulin gamma-1), ACTB (0.864, actin, cytoskeletal support), and TUBGCP4 (0.836, γ-TuRC subunit). HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection ('Detected in all') with low tissue specificity, consistent with its role in fundamental cell division processes across all tissues. ClinVar reports 76 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including copy number losses (chr13:89779269-114338054) and single nucleotide variants (e.g., c.1555+127A>G), all linked to mitotic defects and developmental disorders. Functional hypothesis: TUBGCP3 acts as a central scaffold for γ-TuRC assembly, recruiting tubulin dimers to initiate microtubule growth at the centrosome. Its interaction with MZT1/2B and NEDD1 ensures proper complex stability and post-translational modification. Disruption leads to spindle assembly failure, aneuploidy, and impaired retinal progenitor cell proliferation (as seen in zebrafish models). Disease implications include microcephaly, intellectual disability, and potential roles in cancer progression via uncontrolled mitosis.
