## Description
LOC126806036 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 1q42.12 (genomic coordinates 226992591-226993790, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a BRD4-INDEPENDENT GROUP 4 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, P300/CBP, MED14, and CDK7 cofactors but shows LIMITED OR NO DEPENDENCE on the BRD4 bromodomain protein. This cofactor profile distinguishes it from canonical BET-dependent enhancers and suggests it operates through BRD2-dependent chromatin remodeling mechanisms that bypass BRD4 requirement. This bidirectional pattern indicates strict dosage requirements. The 1q42 region contains critical neuronal development genes including DISC1 (disrupted in schizophrenia 1, 1q42.13, major psychiatric disorder locus), TSNAX (translin-associated factor X), and multiple other neurodevelopmental genes.
