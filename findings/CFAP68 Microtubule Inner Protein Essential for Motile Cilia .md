# CFAP68: Microtubule Inner Protein Essential for Motile Cilia Beating and Sperm Motility

**Date:** 2026-03-20T15:41:18.765856

**Quality Score:** 2.65/10  (E=3.0, D=2.3) [WEAK]

## Description
CFAP68 (Cilia- and Flagella Associated Protein 68, alias C11orf1) encodes a 150-amino acid microtubule inner protein (MIP) that integrates into the dynein-decorated doublet microtubules (DMTs) of the cilia axoneme. This protein is required for motile cilia beating and flagellated sperm motility (GO:0030317). InterPro analysis reveals four conserved domain signatures: IPR009524 (positions 2-149), IPR037662 (positions 20-150), PF06608 (positions 2-149), and PTHR31180, all defining the CFAP68/107 family. Human Protein Atlas data shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, nucleoplasmic subcellular localization, and cell type-enriched single cell specificity. ClinVar contains 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variants (deletions and duplications spanning 11q22.3-23.3), suggesting haploinsufficiency may cause disease though specific phenotypes remain uncharacterized. Mouse ortholog Q9D131 (166 aa) and bovine ortholog Q2T9Q3 (169 aa) indicate moderate evolutionary conservation. The protein functions as a structural MIP component within the axonemal DMT lattice, likely stabilizing dynein motor complexes for coordinated microtubule sliding during ciliary beating. Disruption would impair mucociliary clearance and sperm motility, potentially causing primary ciliary dyskinesia or male infertility.
