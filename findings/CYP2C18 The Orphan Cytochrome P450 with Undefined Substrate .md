# CYP2C18: The Orphan Cytochrome P450 with Undefined Substrate Specificity Despite High Hepatic Expression

**Date:** 2026-06-27T23:48:11.617851

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
CYP2C18 (UniProt P33260) encodes a 490-amino acid cytochrome P450 monooxygenase localized to the endoplasmic reticulum. InterPro analysis confirms canonical P450 architecture: the cytochrome P450 family domain (IPR001128, aa 30-487), E-class group I signature (IPR002401), heme-binding conserved site (IPR017972, aa 428-437), and the P450 superfamily fold (IPR036396, aa 19-490). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT: 93.8), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme with intact catalytic machinery including the heme-binding cysteine motif.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal exclusive partnerships with other drug-metabolizing CYPs: CYP3A7 (0.967), CYP1A1 (0.959), CYP4A11 (0.950), EPHX1 (0.946), CYP4A22 (0.942), CYP1A2 (0.942), CYP26A1/B1 (retinoic acid metabolism), and CYP3A5 (0.914). HPA data shows liver-enriched expression (184.2 nTPM) with additional expression in intestine (96.6 nTPM) and stomach (63.5 nTPM), consistent with first-pass drug metabolism. Subcellular localization includes vesicles and plasma membrane.\n\nCritically, despite 205 papers and membership in the well-characterized CYP2C family (which includes major drug metabolizers CYP2C9 and CYP2C19), CYP2C18 remains functionally orphaned—its endogenous substrate has never been definitively identified. A 2022 review explicitly titles it 'the orphan in the CYP2C family.' Recent 2025 literature links CYP2C18-derived epoxyeicosatrienoic acids (EETs) to ferroptosis in arsenic-induced hepatic insulin resistance, suggesting arachidonic acid metabolism. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 10q23.33. This positions CYP2C18 as a 'dark enzyme'—structurally characterized but functionally undefined, representing a significant gap in pharmacogenomics and lipid metabolism research.
