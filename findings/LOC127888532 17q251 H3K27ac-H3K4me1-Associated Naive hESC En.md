# LOC127888532: 17q25.1 H3K27ac-H3K4me1-Associated Naive hESC Enhancer with No Clinical Variants — Putative Pluripotency Regulatory Element

**Date:** 2026-03-19T03:14:44.579599

**Quality Score:** 2.35/10  (E=2.0, D=2.7) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC127888532 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 17q25.1 (genomic coordinates: 82081016-82081901 on NC_000017.11, spanning approximately 885 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in naive human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it is marked by both H3K27ac (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation — active enhancer mark) and H3K4me1 (histone H3 lysine 4 monomethylation — enhancer lineage mark) histone modifications. The dual histone mark signature indicates a robust active enhancer state in naive pluripotent stem cells (pre-implantation epiblast-like state). A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. This absence of ClinVar entries is consistent with other hESC enhancers in this series (LOC127894026: 0 variants; LOC127892927: 0 variants; LOC127892406: 0 variants; LOC127891630: 0 variants; LOC127889588: 0 variants; LOC127889230: 0 variants; LOC127889218: 0 variants) and contrasts with silencers showing strong triplosensitivity or haploinsufficiency patterns. The 17q25 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including TCF4 (transcription factor 4 - Wnt signaling, Pitt-Hopkins syndrome), RAB34 (RAB34 member RAS oncogene family - vesicle trafficking), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a naive hESC-specific enhancer marked by H3K27ac-H3K4me1, LOC127888532 likely functions to activate transcription of target genes in cis during early embryonic development and naive pluripotency maintenance. The dual enhancer/silencer activity suggests context-dependent regulation, potentially activating pluripotency factors in naive stem cells while repressing differentiation genes in somatic lineages. The absence of ClinVar variants suggests either functional redundancy, essentiality (deleterious variants are embryonic lethal), or that this element regulates genes with buffered dosage sensitivity in post-embryonic development.
