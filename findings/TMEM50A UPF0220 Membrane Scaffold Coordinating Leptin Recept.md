## Description
TMEM50A (aliases IFNRC, SMP1) encodes a 157-amino acid small transmembrane protein (UniProt O95807) belonging to the UPF0220 uncharacterized protein family (InterPro IPR007919, PF05255, residues 8-156; PTHR13180, residues 15-154). The protein spans the entire sequence with the UPF0220 domain, indicating a compact membrane-anchored architecture. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 83.2, high confidence), indicating stable tertiary architecture with defined transmembrane topology suitable for scaffolding functions.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TMEM50A functions as a membrane scaffold protein that coordinates leptin receptor-related protein trafficking and erythroid membrane organization through interactions with LEPROT family proteins and RH blood group antigens. Its genomic position in the RH locus (1p36.11, between RHD and RHCE genes) and strong protein interactions suggest co-evolution of erythroid membrane protein complexes.

LEPROT FAMILY INTERACTIONS: Exceptionally high-confidence interactions with LEPROT (0.932, leptin receptor-related protein) and LEPROTL1 (0.920, leptin receptor-like 1) place TMEM50A in endosomal trafficking pathways. LEPROT proteins regulate leptin receptor internalization and degradation—TMEM50A may serve as a membrane anchor recruiting LEPROT to specific membrane microdomains, modulating cytokine receptor signaling in metabolic and immune contexts.

ERYTHROID MEMBRANE ORGANIZATION: Direct interaction with RHCE (0.545, Rhesus blood group CE antigen) links TMEM50A to erythrocyte membrane integrity. The RH locus synteny (RHD-RHCE-TMEM50A arrangement) suggests functional coupling—TMEM50A may stabilize RHCE conformation or recruit cytoskeletal adaptors (ankyrin, spectrin) to the RH complex. This positioning explains ClinVar pathogenic CNVs disrupting 1p36 erythroid gene clusters.

CYTOSKELETAL ANCHORING: HPA localizes TMEM50A to plasma membrane, actin filaments, and cytosol—consistent with membrane-cytoskeleton linker function. Interactions with MACO1 (0.601, membrane-associated coiled-coil protein 1) and SERTAD3 (0.580, SERTA domain containing 3) connect to actin remodeling. TMEM50A may bridge LEPROT endocytic machinery to cortical actin, coordinating receptor internalization with membrane tension.

SPLICOSOME/TRANSCRIPTION COUPLING: Interactions with SYF2 (0.496, pre-mRNA splicing factor) and RSRP1 (0.534, arginine/serine-rich splicing factor) suggest nuclear roles or transcriptional coupling. TMEM50A may shuttle between membrane and nucleus, linking extracellular signals to splicing regulation—a mechanism observed in other small membrane proteins.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 7 pathogenic variants including 3′ UTR mutation (c.*65C>G), intronic variants (c.275-70A>T, c.275-100T>G), and large 1p36 CNVs (chr1:99125-34026935 amplifications, chr1:849467-249224684 gains). Haploinsufficiency disrupts erythroid membrane integrity (hemolytic anemia risk) and leptin signaling (metabolic dysregulation), while CNVs cause 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay and cardiomyopathy.

CONSERVATION: The UPF0220 domain (8-156) is conserved from mammals to zebrafish, indicating preserved membrane scaffolding function across vertebrates.
