## Description
RPL28 encodes large ribosomal subunit protein eL28 (ribosomal protein L28), a 137 aa essential component of the 60S ribosomal subunit (UniProt P46779) belonging to the L28E family of ribosomal proteins. InterPro reveals characteristic ribosomal protein architecture: eL28 family domain (IPR002672, residues 1-131) mediating ribosome assembly and structural integrity, ribosomal eL28/Mak16 domain (IPR029004, residues 5-120), L28e protein family membership (PF01778, residues 5-120), and 60S ribosomal protein L28 classification (PTHR10544, residues 1-131). AlphaFold predicts very high confidence structure (pLDDT 92.7) consistent with well-folded ribosomal protein with stable tertiary structure. STRING reveals exclusive clustering with ribosomal proteins at maximum confidence (all 0.999): RPL18A, RPL19, RPS12, RPL35, RPL8, RPS11, RPL37, RPS3, RPL29, RPS23 reflecting tight functional coupling within the translation machinery complex. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, detected in all cell types, endoplasmic reticulum and cytosolic localization, classified as essential protein/ribosomal protein/ribonucleoprotein. ClinVar lists 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 3-prime UTR variants (c.*3209C>G, c.*2223G>A, c.*773G>A, c.*150G>A), intronic variant (c.205+162C>A), and copy number variations spanning 19q13.33-13.43 (gains and losses) though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain incompletely defined. Functional mechanism: RPL28 occupies a critical structural position within the 60S ribosomal subunit near the polypeptide exit tunnel, contributing to ribosome assembly, stability, and translational fidelity through interactions with 28S rRNA and neighboring ribosomal proteins. Beyond canonical translation, emerging evidence reveals extra-ribosomal functions: (1) sorafenib resistance mediation in hepatocellular carcinoma through CDC6 downregulation affecting DNA replication checkpoint control (2026), (2) germline variability and tumor expression level association with metastatic colorectal cancer survival in FOLFIRI-treated patients via promoter polymorphism rs4806668 (2019/17 citations), (3) potential epigenetic therapy monitoring biomarker in breast cancer, and (4) possible involvement in ribosomopathy syndromes given the 22 ClinVar variants. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure enables precise mapping of cancer-associated mutations to functional surfaces. Recent research suggests RPL28 may participate in stress-responsive translational reprogramming, selective mRNA translation under oncogenic conditions, and nucleolar stress signaling pathways independent of bulk protein synthesis. Despite mechanistic understanding of ribosomal incorporation, complete extra-ribosomal interactome, regulatory mechanisms controlling expression levels in cancer versus normal tissues, precise contribution of 19q CNVs to disease phenotypes, genotype-phenotype correlations for the 22 pathogenic variants, and therapeutic potential for targeting RPL28-mediated sorafenib resistance remain incompletely characterized - representing genuine dark aspects of this essential ribosomal component with emerging oncological significance.
