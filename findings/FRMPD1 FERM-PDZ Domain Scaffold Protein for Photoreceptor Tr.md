## Description
FRMPD1 encodes a 1578 aa FERM and PDZ domain-containing protein 1 (UniProt Q5SYB0), functioning as a large multi-domain scaffold protein that orchestrates protein trafficking in photoreceptors and regulates cell proliferation through Hippo pathway signaling. The protein contains canonical FERM domain (IPR000299, aa181-496) composed of three subdomains: F1 subdomain (cd17168, aa179-268), FERM B-lobe (cd14473, aa279-393), and FERM C-lobe specific to FRMPD1/3/4 (cd13183, aa397-501), plus a PDZ domain (IPR001478, aa57-135) for protein-protein interactions, and an LGN tetratricopeptide repeat-binding domain (cd21942, aa901-938) for G-protein signaling modulator recruitment. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 52.5), indicating a partially structured scaffold with ordered FERM/PDZ domains and extensive intrinsically disordered regions typical of large signaling adaptors. HPA shows tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in retina (63.9 nTPM), classified as predicted intracellular protein with group-enriched single cell type specificity. STRING interactions reveal FRMPD1 forms a photoreceptor trafficking complex: GPSM2 (0.823, G-protein signaling modulator 2/LGN), GPSM1 (0.716, G-protein signaling modulator 1), FBXO10 (0.673, F-box protein E3 ubiquitin ligase), SLC25A51 (0.570, mitochondrial NAD transporter), GCNA (0.509, germ cell nuclear antigen), TMEM51 (0.502, transmembrane protein 51), RIC8A (0.500, resistance to inhibitors of cholinesterase 8 homolog A), CPNE5 (0.468, copine 5), MCTP2 (0.457, multiple C2 domains transmembrane protein 2), FLAD1 (0.407, FAD synthase). ClinVar contains 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple 9p24.3-q21.13 copy number gains and losses, duplications, and deletions. Literature (22 papers) demonstrates FRMPD1 facilitates transducin (Gαt) trafficking in rod photoreceptors critical for light adaptation and synaptic transmission; FRMPD1 activates Hippo pathway via WWC3 interaction suppressing lung cancer cell proliferation and invasiveness; FBXO10 drives hepatocellular carcinoma proliferation through K63-linked ubiquitination and stabilization of FRMPD1; FRMPD1 expression is regulated by NRL and CRX transcription factors in photoreceptors. FRMPD1 functions as a dual-purpose scaffold: in retina, the FERM domain binds membrane proteins while the LGN-binding domain recruits GPSM2/LGN complex for transducin trafficking between inner and outer segments during light adaptation; in cancer, FRMPD1-WWC3 interaction activates Hippo kinase cascade phosphorylating YAP/TAZ transcriptional coactivators, suppressing proliferation. FBXO10-mediated K63-linked ubiquitination stabilizes FRMPD1, creating a regulatory node where ubiquitination status determines FRMPD1 tumor suppressor activity. Copy number variants on 9p may cause FRMPD1 dosage effects contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. The large size (1578 aa) and disordered architecture enable FRMPD1 to serve as a flexible platform coordinating multiple protein complexes at membrane-cytoskeleton interfaces.
