# HEATR5B: Large HEAT Repeat Scaffold Protein with AP-1 Complex Interactions and Golgi-Vesicle Trafficking Function

## Description
HEATR5B (HEAT repeat-containing protein 5B, UniProt Q9P2D3) is a massive 2071-amino acid intracellular scaffold protein located on chromosome 2p24.1 within a region prone to copy-number variations. InterPro analysis reveals extensive HEAT/armadillo repeat architecture characteristic of large scaffolding proteins: multiple armadillo-type folds (IPR016024/SSF48371, aa 18-1265 and 1245-2037) organized into three leucine-rich repeat variant domains (G3DSA:1.25.10.10, aa 11-276/342-602/673-1140) belonging to the Laa1/Sip1/HEATR5 family (IPR040108/PTHR21663, aa 1-2070). Two dedicated HEAT repeat regions (PF20210/IPR046837, aa 1288-1493 and PF25468, aa 1559-1771) form the canonical helical hairpin structures that mediate protein-protein interactions in transport complexes. GO annotations confirm roles in protein transport and general transport processes. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 81.2), indicating a well-folded stable elongated scaffold structure optimal for bridging multiple protein partners simultaneously. Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue, cancer, and immune cell specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, vesicles, and cytosol — consistent with roles in constitutive vesicle trafficking between Golgi and endosomes where HEAT repeat scaffolds coordinate coat protein assembly. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners dominated by AP-1 adaptor complex subunits and trafficking regulators: AP1M2 (0.768, AP-1 medium subunit mu-2 — highest confidence confirming direct AP-1 complex association), AP1S2 (0.670, AP-1 sigma-2 subunit), TRAPPC11 (0.643, trafficking protein particle complex subunit 11 involved in ER-Golgi transport), AP1G1 (0.598, AP-1 gamma-1 subunit), AFTPH (0.592, aftiphilin involved in endosomal trafficking), TTC17 (0.516, tetratricopeptide repeat protein), SYNRG (0.499, synergin gamma coiled-coil protein), AP1S3 (0.492, AP-1 sigma-3 subunit), MON2 (0.484, MON2 homolog involved in Golgi organization), and SLC22A18AS (0.473). This interactome confirms HEATR5B functions within the AP-1 clathrin adaptor complex machinery mediating Golgi-to-endosome cargo sorting. ClinVar contains 29 pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1585-3C>T, c.1696+165T>A, c.2697-7T>G), missense variants (p.Gly1811Val), deletions, and copy-number gains across 2p24.1-22.2, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting chromosome 2p cause disease phenotypes. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8C547, 2070 aa; Xenopus tropicalis A1A5F2, 2052 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental vesicle trafficking mechanisms. HEATR5B likely functions as a large HEAT repeat scaffold protein, using its extended armadillo/HEAT repeat superhelix to physically bridge AP-1 adaptor complex subunits with cargo receptors and regulatory proteins at the Golgi membrane — coordinating clathrin-coated vesicle formation, cargo selection, and vesicle budding for lysosomal enzyme sorting and endosomal trafficking, with dysfunction potentially contributing to congenital disorders of glycosylation and neurodevelopmental diseases where Golgi trafficking is impaired.
