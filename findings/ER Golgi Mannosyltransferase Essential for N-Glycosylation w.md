## Description
ALG2 (Alpha-1,3/1,6-mannosyltransferase ALG2) is a 416-amino acid glycosyltransferase localized to the Golgi apparatus, catalyzing critical steps in N-linked glycosylation by transferring mannose from GDP-Man to Man1GlcNAc2-PP-Dol lipid intermediates. InterPro analysis reveals multiple conserved domains: IPR001296 (Glycosyl transferase family 1, positions 218-392), IPR027054 (Mannosyltransferase ALG2, positions 12-414), IPR028098 (Glycosyltransferase subfamily 4-like N-terminal domain, positions 26-189), PF00534 (Glycosyl transferases group 1), and PF13439 (Glycosyltransferase Family 4), with CDD domain cd03805 spanning positions 17-407. STRING interaction network identifies high-confidence partners in the glycosylation pathway: ALG11 (0.969), ALG3 (0.913), ALG6 (0.520), ALG1 (0.415), plus ESCRT-related PDCD6IP (0.800), vacuolar ATPase ATP6V0A2 (0.928), and DNA repair protein MUS81 (0.793), suggesting coordination between glycosylation, membrane trafficking, and genome stability. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with Golgi apparatus localization, classified as essential metabolic protein with disease involvement in congenital disorder of glycosylation (ALG2-CDG, OMIM 607906) and congenital myasthenic syndrome. ClinVar contains 53 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Glu272Ter, p.Ala119_Cys120insTer), frameshifts (p.Asp73fs), missense variants (p.Phe398Ser, p.Asn324Ser, p.Asp197Gly, p.Arg251Leu), and copy number gains on 9p24.3-q34.3. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9H553-F1) shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.0), indicating a well-folded enzyme with stable UDP-glycosyltransferase/glycogen phosphorylase fold (SSF53756). Recent literature (2024-2025) describes ALG2's role in COPII-mediated microautophagy of ER exit sites, novel ALG2-CDG genotypes with heptasaccharide biomarkers, and ophthalmic manifestations—suggesting emerging functions beyond canonical glycosylation.
