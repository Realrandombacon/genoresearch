The KRAB domain serves as a potent transcriptional repression module that recruits the universal corepressor TRIM28/KAP1, which subsequently recruits heterochromatin-inducing complexes including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 proteins to silence target genes through H3K9me3 deposition.
