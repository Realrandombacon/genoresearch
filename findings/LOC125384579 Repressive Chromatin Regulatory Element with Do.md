## Description
LOC125384579 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 6934 located on chromosome 20p (genomic coordinates: 10578600-10578894 on NC_000020.11). This 294 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element using Sharpr-MPRA (Systematic high-resolution activation and repression profiling with reporter tiling using massively parallel reporter assays) in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure). ClinVar analysis identified 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains spanning 20p13-11.21, 20p13-11.23, 20p13-11.1, 20p13-12.1, 20p13-q13.33, 20p13-q11.1, and 20p12.2-q12, plus copy number losses at 20p12.2-12.1 and 20p12.3-12.1, and one deletion event. The high number of pathogenic CNVs indicates strong dosage sensitivity—both amplification and deletion of this regulatory region cause disease phenotypes. As a validated repressive element, LOC125384579 likely functions as a transcriptional silencer or enhancer-blocking element that regulates expression of target genes in cis. The chromatin state predictions from ENCODE suggest this region maintains repressive histone marks (likely H3K27me3 or H3K9me3). Disruption of this element through CNVs may lead to derepression of oncogenes or developmental genes on chromosome 20p, contributing to disease pathogenesis.
