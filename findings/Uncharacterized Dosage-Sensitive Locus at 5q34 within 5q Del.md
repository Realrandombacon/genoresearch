## Description
LOC102546299 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 5q34 (NC_000005.10:164470278-164542983) spanning approximately 72.7 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000241956 but reported no detectable protein expression data, indicating this locus is not represented in the HPA protein atlas. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all large copy number alterations: deletions (chr5:152443869-166104392, ~13.7 Mb; chr5:152761187-167248053, ~14.5 Mb) and duplications (chr5:164386701-181269805, ~16.9 Mb; chr5:156825512-181269805, ~24.4 Mb; chr5:149714592-181272151, ~31.6 Mb). These structural variants are associated with 5q deletion syndrome (5q- syndrome, myelodysplastic syndrome with del(5q)) and 5q duplication syndrome featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal anomalies, and hematologic abnormalities. The 5q34 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including RPS14 (ribosomal protein S14, haploinsufficiency causes 5q- syndrome erythroid defect), NDRG2 (N-myc downstream regulated gene 2, tumor suppressor), and MEF2C (myocyte enhancer factor 2C, critical for neurodevelopment, haploinsufficiency causes MEF2C syndrome). LOC102546299 likely functions as part of the 5q34 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to 5q deletion/duplication syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology, topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries, or long-range dysregulation of dosage-sensitive hematologic and neurodevelopmental genes. The presence of pathogenic CNV burden (5 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where both hemizygosity and trisomy disrupt developmental and hematologic processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization.
