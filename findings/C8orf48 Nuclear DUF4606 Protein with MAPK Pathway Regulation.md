## Description
C8orf48 (UniProt Q96LL4) is a 319-amino acid nuclear/cytosolic protein containing a Domain of Unknown Function (DUF4606/PF15379, IPR027932, residues 166-268) and belonging to the C8orf48 family (PTHR35256, residues 8-273). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 65.0), suggesting a mix of ordered and flexible regions that may facilitate dynamic protein-DNA or protein-protein interactions. HPA confirms nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization with tissue-enhanced expression, highest in testis (6.5 nTPM), and cancer-enhanced expression patterns. STRING interaction analysis reveals connections to transcriptional regulators: ZNF878 (0.578, zinc finger protein), LONRF1 (0.547, E3 ubiquitin ligase), TRMT9B (0.453, tRNA methyltransferase), ZNF14 (0.447, zinc finger protein), and VXN (0.435, vinculin-associated protein), suggesting roles in transcriptional regulation and chromatin modification. ClinVar records 89 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains at 8p23-21, implicating dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders. Literature (5 papers) reveals C8orf48 functions as a tumor suppressor in colorectal cancer by inhibiting tumorigenesis through MAPK signaling pathway regulation, with additional studies linking it to familial thyroid cancer susceptibility. We hypothesize C8orf48 acts as a nuclear transcriptional modulator where its DUF4606 domain mediates interactions with zinc finger proteins and chromatin modifiers to regulate MAPK pathway gene expression. The cancer-enhanced expression pattern combined with tumor suppressor activity suggests C8orf48 may function in a feedback loop restraining oncogenic MAPK signaling, while haploinsufficiency via 8p deletions disrupts this regulation contributing to developmental phenotypes and cancer predisposition.
