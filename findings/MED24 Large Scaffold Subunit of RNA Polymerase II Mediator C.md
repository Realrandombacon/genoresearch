## Description
MED24/O75448 (989 aa) is a highly confident (AlphaFold pLDDT: 84.1) large scaffold protein functioning as an essential subunit of the RNA polymerase II Mediator complex that bridges transcription factors to the basal transcriptional machinery for gene activation. InterPro analysis reveals canonical Mediator complex architecture containing the Mediator complex subunit Med24 family domain (IPR021429/PF11277/PTHR12898, residues 1-987/1-987/1-987) with GO annotations for mediator complex (GO:0016592), transcription regulation, and activator molecular function, classifying it within the multi-subunit Mediator coactivator complex essential for Pol II transcription. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity but cell type-enhanced specificity pattern, classification as plasma proteins/predicted intracellular proteins/predicted membrane proteins consistent with nucleocytoplasmic shuttling, and biological process annotation for transcription/transcription regulation matching its core Mediator function. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence Mediator complex network linking MED24 exclusively to Mediator subunits: MED28 (0.999), MED15 (0.999), MED1 (0.999), MED14 (0.999), MED16 (0.999), MED18 (0.999), MED12 (0.999), MED29 (0.999), MED27 (0.997), and MED23 (0.997), positioning it as an integral scaffold within the Mediator holocomplex that coordinates transcription factor recruitment and Pol II engagement. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 17q12-21.2 copy number gains, 17q12-22 losses, 17p11.2-q21.2 gains, and coding SNVs (p.Arg763Gln, p.Thr416Ser) implicating MED24 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined due to contiguous gene effects on chromosome 17q. The convergence of confident large scaffold structure (989 aa, pLDDT 84.1), exclusive Mediator subunit partnerships, ubiquitous expression with cell type specificity, ERBB2/Pten/Smad4 pathway integration in lung cancer, bipolar disorder genetic risk, and chromosome 17q dosage sensitivity suggests MED24 functions as a transcriptional hub coordinating signal-dependent gene activation programs in cancer progression (particularly ERBB2-driven lung tumors), neuropsychiatric disease susceptibility, and cell type-specific transcriptional responses, with therapeutic potential in targeting Mediator complex assembly in oncology and understanding bipolar disorder genetic architecture.
