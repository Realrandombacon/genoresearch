This locus lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory sequence, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, STRING interaction partners, or Human Protein Atlas expression data (not applicable for non-coding locus).\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region, representing MODERATE CNV burden.
