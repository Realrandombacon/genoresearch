## Description
LOC497256 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 9q22.1 (NC_000009.12:87939259-87867672) spanning approximately 71.6 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, comprising predominantly copy number gains (duplications: chr9:193412-138179445, ~138 Mb whole-arm trisomy; chr9:203862-138125937, ~138 Mb; chr9:203861-88130444, ~88 Mb; chr9:203861-138125937, ~138 Mb; chr9:69627642-111454304, ~41.8 Mb; chr9:193412-138159073, ~138 Mb; chr9:193412-138159073, ~138 Mb; chr9:193412-138124524, ~138 Mb; chr9:68420430-106579493, ~38.2 Mb) and one copy number loss (deletion: chr9:86079851-91827221, ~5.7 Mb). These structural variants encompass the 9q22 region and are associated with 9q22 microdeletion syndrome (Gorlin syndrome phenotype) and 9q22 microduplication syndrome featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, skeletal anomalies, and predisposition to basal cell carcinoma. The 9q22.1 region contains PTCH1 (patched homolog 1), a tumor suppressor and receptor for sonic hedgehog (SHH) signaling pathway whose haploinsufficiency causes nevoid basal cell carcinoma syndrome (Gorlin syndrome), and FANCC (Fanconi anemia complementation group C), essential for DNA interstrand crosslink repair. LOC497256 likely functions as part of the 9q22.1 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to 9q22 syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology, topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries, or long-range regulation of dosage-sensitive genes like PTCH1 and FANCC. The absence of protein-coding features combined with pathogenic CNV burden (14 variants) indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where structural variation disrupts hedgehog signaling, DNA repair, and developmental processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization.
