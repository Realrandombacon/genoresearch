# G Protein Signaling Modulator 2 (GPSM2/LGN): TPR-GoLoco Scaffold Regulating Mitotic Spindle Orientation and Gαi Signaling

**Date:** 2026-06-17T10:17:47.873596

**Quality Score:** 7/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH

## Description
GPSM2 (also known as LGN or PINS) encodes a 684 aa intracellular scaffold protein that orchestrates mitotic spindle orientation and asymmetric cell division through dual-domain architecture. The N-terminal region contains 10 tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) motifs (IPR019734, PF13176/13181/13374/13424) spanning residues 17-385 and 458-651, mediating protein-protein interactions with NUMA1 and INSC. The C-terminal half harbors 3 GoLoco motifs (IPR003109, PF02188) at positions 489-511, 544-566, and 594-616 that function as guanine nucleotide dissociation inhibitors (GDIs) for Gαi subunits. STRING interactions confirm high-confidence partnerships with GNAI1/2/3 (scores 0.887-0.956), PARD3B (0.909), FRMPD4 (0.905), and NUMA1 (0.644), positioning GPSM2 at the intersection of polarity and division machinery. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression peaking in tongue (50.8 nTPM) with centrosome/cytosol localization. ClinVar contains 107 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants associated with autosomal recessive deafness (DFNB82). AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 66.9) reveals medium-confidence folding consistent with TPR helical bundles interspersed with flexible linkers. Mechanistically, GPSM2 recruits dynein-dynactin complexes via NUMA1 to cortical sites, generating pulling forces on astral microtubules for proper spindle positioning—a process critical for neurogenesis and inner ear hair cell development.
