## Description
PSMD6 (UniProt Q15008), also known as Rpn7, S10, p42A, or p44S10, encodes a 389 aa non-ATPase regulatory subunit of the 26S proteasome lid complex that functions as an essential scaffold protein coordinating substrate recognition, deubiquitination, and proteolytic degradation through integrated PCI (Proteasome-COP9 signalosome-Initiation factor 3) domain-mediated protein-protein interaction mechanisms. InterPro analysis reveals canonical proteasome lid architecture: central PCI domain (IPR000716/PF01399/PS50250/SM00088, residues 193-373/278-357/193-361/284-366) defining proteasome-COP9-eIF3 superfamily membership and enabling multi-subunit complex assembly; N-terminal tetratricopeptide-like helical domain (IPR011990/G3DSA:1.25.40.570, residues 87-212/9-340) mediating protein binding (GO:0005515) and substrate positioning; full-length 26S proteasome regulatory subunit Rpn7/COP9 signalosome complex subunit 1 family membership (IPR019585/PTHR14145, residues 7-389/7-389) establishing conserved proteasomal function; N-terminal RPN7 domain (IPR045135/PF10602, residues 66-239/66-239) for lid complex integration; C-terminal helix (IPR049549/PF21154, residues 365-387/365-387) stabilizing quaternary structure; and winged helix DNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR036390, residues 284-366) suggesting potential nucleic acid interactions. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 82.4) indicating a well-folded stable scaffold suitable for constitutive 26S proteasome incorporation and dynamic substrate processing.
