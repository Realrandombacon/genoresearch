# CCDC43: PWI-like Coiled-Coil Scaffold Coupling Transcription Elongation to DNA Repair via P-TEFb Complex

**Date:** 2026-03-20T14:36:26.142583

**Quality Score:** 2.05/10  (E=4.1, D=0.0) [WEAK]

## Description
CCDC43 encodes a 224 amino acid cytosolic coiled-coil protein (UniProt Q96MW1) with a distinctive domain architecture: an N-terminal PWI-like domain (IPR058771/PF26091, residues 21-89) homologous to RNA-binding motifs found in splicing factors, followed by a predicted coiled-coil region and a conserved C-terminal domain (IPR060469/PF27916, residues 169-224). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with enhanced expression in skeletal muscle (68.8 nTPM), consistent with a housekeeping function. STRING interactions reveal CCDC43 associates with transcription and DNA repair machinery: GTF2F2 (0.633) and LEO1 (0.622) are subunits of the P-TEFb transcription elongation complex, PNKP (0.622) is a bifunctional DNA kinase/phosphatase required for DNA strand break repair, KLHL2 (0.563) is a CUL3 ubiquitin ligase substrate adaptor, GOPC (0.548) mediates Golgi-vesicle trafficking, and WASHC3 (0.487) nucleates actin on endosomes. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on chromosome 17q, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The PWI-like domain suggests RNA or protein-binding capacity, while the coiled-coil mediates oligomerization. CCDC43 likely functions as a molecular scaffold bridging transcription elongation complexes (P-TEFb) with DNA repair machinery (PNKP), potentially coordinating transcription-coupled repair or facilitating recruitment of repair factors to sites of transcriptional stress. Disruption of CCDC43 copy number could impair this coupling, leading to genomic instability.
