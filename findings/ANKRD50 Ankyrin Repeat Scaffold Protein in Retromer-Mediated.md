# ANKRD50: Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein in Retromer-Mediated Endocytic Recycling

**Date:** 2026-03-20T16:19:06.349160

**Quality Score:** 2.2/10  (E=4.4, D=0.0) [WEAK]

## Description
ANKRD50 (Q9ULJ7) encodes a 1429 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein featuring multiple ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, PF00023) positioned at residues 495-809, forming a curved protein-protein interaction surface. The N-terminal region contains a Nephrocystin 3-like domain (IPR056884, PF24883, aa 74-199) and TANC1-like domains including an AAA+ ATPase lid (IPR058018, PF25520, aa 255-339) and winged helix-turn-helix DNA-binding domain (IPR058056, PF25521, aa 340-464). High-confidence STRING interactions reveal ANKRD50 functions within the retromer complex pathway: C12orf57 (score 0.994), VPS29 (0.851), VPS35 (0.698), VPS26A (0.478), SNX27 (0.439), and SNX1 (0.401) - all core retromer or sorting nexin components governing endosome-to-Golgi cargo retrieval. HPA data shows low tissue specificity with expression across many tissues, immune cell enhancement, and nucleoplasmic/cytosolic localization consistent with endosomal trafficking. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses across 4q24-q35.2, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The ankyrin repeat array likely scaffolds retromer assembly while the winged helix domain may enable nucleic acid interactions, positioning ANKRD50 as a bifunctional adaptor coordinating endocytic recycling with potential transcriptional regulation.
