## Description
PAXBP1 encodes a 917 aa PAX3- and PAX7-binding protein 1 (UniProt Q9Y5B6), functioning as a nuclear spliceosome-associated factor that regulates pre-mRNA splicing critical for immune cell development, epidermal homeostasis, and thymocyte survival through GCFC2-like domain-mediated RNA processing. The protein contains an intron large complex component GCFC2-like domain (IPR012890, aa1-911) with DNA binding (GO:0003677) and mRNA splicing via spliceosome (GO:0000398) functions localized to nucleus (GO:0005634), a GCF C-terminal domain (IPR022783, aa596-807), belongs to GC-rich sequence DNA-binding factor-like protein family (PF07842, aa596-807), and is classified within GC-rich sequence DNA-binding factor family (PTHR12214, aa1-911). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 71.8), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined domain architecture suitable for spliceosome assembly. HPA has no expression data available. STRING interactions reveal PAXBP1 forms spliceosomal networks: RSRC1 (0.844, arginine/serine-rich coiled-coil 1 - highest confidence), SNRNP40 (0.761, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein U5 subunit 40), SYNJ1 (0.750, synaptojanin 1), DDX23 (0.722, DEAD-box helicase 23), TSSC4 (0.644, tumor suppressing subtransferable 4), TFIP11 (0.624, Tuftelin interacting protein 11), CD2BP2 (0.514, CD2 cytoplasmic tail binding protein 2), SMIM11A (0.503, small integral membrane protein 11A), MAP6D1 (0.502, microtubule associated protein 6 domain containing 1), SNRPF (0.440, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide F). ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 21q11.2-22.3/21q22.11-22.3 copy number gains, 21q22.11/21q11.2-22.3 copy number losses, and coding variants c.650G>T (p.Gly217Val) and c.973C>T (p.Gln325Ter - nonsense). Literature (20 papers) documents PAXBP1 vital role in ILC2 homeostasis and activation through pre-mRNA splicing regulation, indispensable function for hair follicle homeostasis, peripheral CD4 T cell homeostasis maintenance, epidermal homeostasis, and CD4/CD8 double-positive thymocyte survival. PAXBP1 functions as a spliceosome-associated regulator—the GCFC2-like domain (aa1-911) structurally resembles intron large complex components involved in RNA processing, while the GCF C-terminal domain (aa596-807) may mediate GC-rich DNA sequence recognition. The ultra-high confidence RSRC1 interaction (0.844) is mechanistically significant: RSRC1 is an SR-related protein that regulates alternative splicing, and PAXBP1 likely coordinates spliceosome assembly through this interaction. SNRNP40 (0.761) and DDX23 (0.722) interactions place PAXBP1 directly in the U5 snRNP and spliceosomal helicase machinery, while SNRPF (0.440) connection confirms involvement in core spliceosome function. TFIP11 (0.624) interaction links PAXBP1 to mRNA export and splicing factor regulation. Immune cell pathogenesis reflects PAXBP1 loss disrupting pre-mRNA splicing of genes critical for ILC2 activation, T cell receptor signaling, and thymocyte development—splicing defects lead to aberrant isoform production compromising immune cell homeostasis. Epidermal and hair follicle phenotypes suggest PAXBP1 regulates splicing of keratinocyte differentiation genes. The high confidence structure (pLDDT 71.8) reflects stable domain organization suitable for large protein complex assembly. Copy number variants on 21q may cause PAXBP1 dosage effects contributing to developmental abnormalities or immune deficiencies, though no specific disease is yet assigned in ClinVar.
