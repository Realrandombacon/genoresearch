# POU2AF3: OCA Domain-Containing Transcriptional Co-Activator Dysregulated in Fusion-Driven Sarcomas

## Description
POU2AF3 (POU Class 2 Homeobox Associating Factor 3), also known as COLCA2 (Colorectal Cancer-Associated Protein 2), is a 251-amino acid nuclear protein with only 13 publications confirming its dark gene status. The protein contains a conserved OCA domain (IPR047571, positions 5-27) implicated in DNA binding and POU domain interactions, plus membership in the POU2AF3 family (IPR043265) and COLCA2 protein family (PTHR36689). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 55.5), consistent with intrinsically disordered regions common in transcriptional co-regulators. HPA reveals cancer-enhanced expression across multiple tissues with nucleoplasm and cytosol localization, supporting transcriptional regulatory function. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily chromosome 11q22.3-23.3 copy number losses and gains), though specific disease phenotypes beyond sarcoma remain uncharacterized. STRING returned no interactions, reflecting limited experimental characterization. Critically, recent studies (2023-2025) identified POU2AF3 as the 3' fusion partner in EWSR1::POU2AF3 and FUS::POU2AF3 fusion oncogenes defining a novel aggressive sarcoma entity with sinonasal/head-and-neck predilection and undifferentiated round/spindle cell morphology. We hypothesize POU2AF3 functions as a transcriptional co-activator that normally associates with POU-domain transcription factors via its OCA domain to regulate cell differentiation programs, but when fused to EWSR1/FUS transactivation domains, drives oncogenic transcription in sarcomas. The cancer-enhanced expression pattern and fusion oncogene status suggest POU2AF3 may also function in epithelial-mesenchymal transition and tumor progression independent of fusion events.
