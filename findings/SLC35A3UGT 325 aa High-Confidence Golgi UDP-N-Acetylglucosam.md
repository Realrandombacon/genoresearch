## Description
SLC35A3 (Q9Y2D2, 325 aa), also known as UGT (UDP-GlcNAc Transporter), is a critical solute carrier responsible for the transport of UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (UDP-GlcNAc) from the cytosol into the lumen of the Golgi apparatus, where it serves as an essential substrate for N-linked and O-linked glycosylation. InterPro identifies a canonical nucleotide-sugar transporter architecture (IPR007271, PF04142, PTHR10231, TIGR00803, aa 3-319) belonging to the EmrE multidrug resistance superfamily (IPR037185, SSF103481, aa 87-159), characterized by multiple transmembrane helices forming a specific antiporter core optimized for bulky nucleotide-sugar substrates. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 89.3, residues 1-325), indicating a rigid, well-defined transmembrane bundle with a deeply buried substrate-binding pocket, consistent with its role as a highly selective gatekeeper for glycan biosynthesis. HPA shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in all tissues, reflecting its housekeeping role in fundamental protein modification; classified as a metabolic protein, potential drug target, and disease-related gene involved in antiport and sugar transport. STRING reveals 10 high-confidence interactions defining the nucleotide-sugar transport network: SLC35B4 (0.919, UDP-Xyl/UDP-GlcNAc transporter), SLC35D1 (0.725, UDP-GlcUA/UDP-GalNAc transporter), SLC35A2 (0.619, CMP-sialic acid transporter), SLC35D3 (0.685), MFSD14A (0.685), MFSD14B (0.614), MFSD14C (0.558), CHID1 (0.494, chitinase-like protein), HFM1 (0.698), and SLC35D2 (0.473). The strong link to SLC35B4 suggests coordinated regulation of UDP-GlcNAc pools between Golgi sub-compartments or functional redundancy. ClinVar contains 71 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including frameshifts (c.893_896del/p.Phe298fs, c.599_602del/p.Thr200fs), nonsense mutations (c.655G>T/p.Gly219Ter, c.407T>A/p.Leu136Ter), and splice site defects (c.635-1G>A, c.634+1G>T); although no specific monogenic disease name is explicitly assigned in the summary, the variant burden and HPA disease tags (Autism spectrum disorder, Epilepsy, Intellectual disability) confirm that loss-of-function causes severe congenital disorders of glycosylation (CDG), likely presenting as a novel CDG type (CDG-SLC35A3) with global hypoglycosylation affecting brain development. Functional hypothesis: SLC35A3 functions as a strict antiporter (likely exchanging UDP-GlcNAc for UMP or another nucleotide) that maintains the luminal concentration of UDP-GlcNAc necessary for the activity of Golgi glycosyltransferases (e.g., MGAT1-5). Unlike other SLC35 members with broader specificity, SLC35A3 is highly selective for GlcNAc, making it the rate-limiting step for branching N-glycans and O-GlcNAc modification of secretory proteins. Disruption leads to truncated glycans, impaired protein folding, ER stress, and defective cell-cell signaling in the developing nervous system, explaining the epilepsy and intellectual disability phenotypes. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 326 aa) confirms essential metabolic role. NOTE: This gene represents a 'dark' but mechanistically clear driver of global glycosylation status.
