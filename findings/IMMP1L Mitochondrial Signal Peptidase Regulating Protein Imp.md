# IMMP1L: Mitochondrial Signal Peptidase Regulating Protein Import Quality Control

## Description
Inner mitochondrial membrane peptidase subunit 1 (IMMP1L) is the catalytic subunit of the IMP complex, a serine-type peptidase (S26 family, PF10502) essential for proteolytically removing the mitochondrial targeting presequence of nuclear-encoded proteins. Structural analysis (AlphaFold pLDDT 88.1) confirms a well-folded, stable catalytic domain. High-confidence STRING interactions (0.933 with DNAJC24, 0.929 with ELP4) link it to mitochondrial protein import and quality control machinery. Despite 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar, no specific disease is cataloged, suggesting a 'dark gene' phenotype of mitochondrial dysfunction leading to neurodegeneration or metabolic defects. The lack of cancer specificity and broad tissue expression point to a housekeeping role in mitochondrial biogenesis.
