## Description
NHEJ1 (XLF) is a critical DNA repair factor essential for the Non-Homologous End Joining (NHEJ) pathway, mediating the repair of double-strand breaks. Structurally, it is a well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT: 81.8) containing conserved XLF-like N-terminal domains and coiled-coil regions that facilitate protein-protein interactions. Functionally, it forms a high-confidence complex with XRCC4, XRCC5, LIG4, and PAXX to stabilize DNA ends. Clinically, NHEJ1 is associated with 72 pathogenic variants and is a known cause of Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID) and hematologic malignancies. While ubiquitously expressed, mutations lead to severe immunodeficiency and cancer susceptibility, highlighting its role in genome maintenance.
