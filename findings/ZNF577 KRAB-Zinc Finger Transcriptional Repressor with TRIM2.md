# ZNF577: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with TRIM28 Recruitment and Disease-Associated Copy Number Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-20T10:17:08.684847

**Quality Score:** 2.17/10  (E=4.05, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF577 encodes a 485 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q9BSK1) that functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 22-94) at the N-terminus which serves as a transcriptional repression module, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 158-241) that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (pLDDT 63.7), consistent with structured zinc finger DNA-binding domains connected by flexible linker regions. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and nucleoli, supporting a housekeeping transcriptional regulatory role. STRING interactions reveal functional associations with TRIM28 (score 0.522), the canonical KRAB domain co-receptor that recruits heterochromatin machinery including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 proteins. Additional interactors include C12orf42 (0.505), COMMD2 (0.457), MBTD1 (0.434), and CFAP74 (0.410), suggesting potential roles in chromatin remodeling complexes. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19 copy number gains encompassing the ZNF577 locus, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease etiology. Conservation analysis shows orthologs in Chrysochloris asiatica (391 aa, 80% identity in zinc finger region), Xenopus laevis (655 aa), and Polypterus senegalus (608 aa), indicating ancient evolutionary conservation of the KRAB-ZFP architecture. Mechanistically, ZNF577 likely binds specific GC-rich DNA sequences via its zinc finger array and recruits TRIM28/KAP1 to establish H3K9me3 heterochromatic marks, silencing target genes involved in cell cycle regulation or stress response pathways.
