# LRRC23: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein Essential for Sperm Flagellar Radial Spoke Assembly with 50 Pathogenic Variants

## Description
LRRC23 encodes a 343 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein (UniProt: Q53EV4) at chromosome 12p13.31 that is essential for sperm flagellar motility through radial spoke assembly. The domain architecture features an N-terminal SDS22/Internalin LRR-containing family domain (IPR050836, residues 16-321) with multiple LRR repeats (IPR001611 at positions 92-113, 201-222, 223-244; PF14580 at 185-317). The LRR solenoid structure (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, residues 50-315) provides a curved protein-protein interaction surface characteristic of scaffold proteins in macromolecular complexes.\n\nHPA expression profiling reveals fallopian tube enrichment (95.7 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced RNA signature and ubiquitous detection across tissues. Subcellular localization to nucleoli and nucleolar rim suggests potential roles in ribosome biogenesis or RNA processing, though the primary function appears in sperm flagella. The alias SPGF92 (spermatogenic failure 92) directly implicates LRRC23 in male infertility.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 10 partners enriched for cilia/flagella components: CFAP45 (0.535, cilia and flagella associated protein), CFAP74 (0.530), RSPH14 (0.523, radial spoke head protein), CCDC113 (0.480, coiled-coil domain cilia protein), CFAP52 (0.508), and LRRC34 (0.619, LRR paralog). This interactome positions LRRC23 within the radial spoke complex that regulates axonemal dynein activity for coordinated flagellar beating.\n\nClinVar contains 50 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large 12p copy number gains (35 variants), deletions (4 variants), and coding SNVs: c.376C>T (p.Arg126Ter) nonsense mutation truncating the LRR domain, and c.621+1G>A splice site variant. The variant spectrum demonstrates extreme dosage sensitivity and haploinsufficiency. Spermatogenic failure 92 phenotype results from defective radial spoke assembly impairing sperm motility.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: LRRC23 functions as a structural scaffold protein within the sperm flagellar radial spoke complex, using its LRR solenoid domain to mediate protein-protein interactions between radial spoke head and stalk components. The curved LRR surface may template radial spoke assembly during spermiogenesis. Loss of LRRC23 disrupts 9+2 axoneme architecture, abolishing progressive sperm motility and causing male infertility.
