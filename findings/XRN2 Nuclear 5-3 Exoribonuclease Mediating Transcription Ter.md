## Description
XRN2 (5'-3' Exoribonuclease 2, UniProt Q9H0D6) is a 950 aa nuclear exonuclease belonging to the Xrn1/XRN2 family (IPR017151/PIRSF037239, positions 1-887) that functions in RNA polymerase II transcription termination, R-loop resolution, and mRNA quality control. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal Xrn1 domain (IPR004859/PF03159, positions 1-254) containing a FEN-like PIN catalytic domain (cd18673, positions 51-313) for 5'-3' exonuclease activity, followed by an extensive helical domain (IPR041412/PF17846, positions 326-786) forming a processivity channel for RNA substrate threading. Literature (250 papers) establishes XRN2 roles in: (1) lncRNA GDIL scaffolding CHAC1-XRN2 complex to promote platinum resistance in colorectal cancer through glutathione degradation inhibition (2025, 12 citations), (2) NSCLC-derived exosomal hsa_circ_0003026 promoting tumor growth via miR-1183/XRN2 axis and macrophage M2 polarization (2025, 5 citations), (3) HELQ helicase and XRN2 cooperation in R-loop resolution preventing DNA damage (2025), (4) XRN2-deficient cancer cell sensitivity to PARP inhibition revealing synthetic lethality (2024), (5) XRN2 torpedo model of transcription termination where exonuclease degrades nascent RNA downstream of poly(A) site to dislodge RNA polymerase II. HPA confirms essential protein classification with nucleoplasm and nucleoli localization, low tissue specificity detected in all tissues, and functions in mRNA processing, transcription, and transcription termination. STRING interactions reveal RNA processing network: CDKN2AIP (0.933) - CDKN2A-interacting protein for cell cycle regulation; DCP2 (0.901) - mRNA decapping enzyme 2 for 5'-3' decay; RPRD1A (0.883) and RPRD1B (0.843) - RNA polymerase II regulatory proteins for CTD phosphatase recruitment; DXO (0.876) - decapping exoribonuclease for RNA quality control; CDKN2AIPNL (0.868) - CDKN2A-interacting protein-like; XRN1 (0.809) - cytoplasmic 5'-3' exoribonuclease paralog; SKIV2L (0.760) - superkiller complex helicase for exosome-mediated decay; ERI2 (0.685) - exoribonuclease involved in rRNA processing; SETX (0.654) - senataxin helicase for R-loop resolution. ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1936+216T>G, c.1666-69G>T, c.701-6T>G), large deletions at 20p13-11.21 (chr20:68351-23860313)x3, 20p13-11.21 (chr20:87153-23635465)x3, and multiple copy number gains/deletions spanning 20q11-13, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 77.6) indicating well-folded catalytic core with processive exonuclease architecture typical of XRN family enzymes. Conservation: mouse Xrn2 (Q9DBR1) is 951 aa, Drosophila Rat1 (Q9VM71) is 908 aa, C. elegans xrn-2 (Q9U299) is 975 aa, Arabidopsis XRN2 (Q9FQ02) is 1012 aa - highly conserved across eukaryotes. Functional hypothesis: XRN2 functions as a nuclear torpedo exonuclease that degrades nascent RNA transcripts downstream of polyadenylation sites to terminate transcription and prevent transcriptional interference. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) XRN2 PIN domain catalyzes 5'-3' phosphodiester bond hydrolysis using divalent metal ions (Mg2+/Mn2+), (2) helical domain forms arch over RNA substrate enabling processive degradation without dissociation, (3) RPRD1A/B recruit XRN2 to phosphorylated RNA polymerase II CTD at gene 3' ends, (4) XRN2-HELQ-SETX complex resolves R-loops by degrading RNA in RNA-DNA hybrids preventing DNA damage, (5) XRN2-DCP2-DXO pathway coordinates nuclear RNA quality control. In colorectal cancer, GDIL lncRNA scaffolds XRN2-CHAC1 complex inhibiting glutathione degradation conferring platinum resistance. In NSCLC, circ_0003026 sponges miR-1183 upregulating XRN2 promoting M2 macrophage polarization. XRN2 deficiency creates synthetic lethality with PARP inhibitors due to accumulated R-loops and transcription-replication conflicts. Pathogenic CNVs at 20p13 disrupt XRN2 dosage, potentially causing transcription termination defects and genome instability. XRN2 represents an understudied nuclear exonuclease linking transcription termination to cancer drug resistance and genome stability.
