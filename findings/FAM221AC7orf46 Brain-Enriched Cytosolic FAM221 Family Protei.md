## Description
FAM221A (alias C7orf46, family with sequence similarity 221 member A) encodes a 298 amino acid well-folded cytosolic protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 83.5) containing a FAM221A/B family domain (IPR026755/PF14753, aa 20-213) and belonging to the PTHR31214 family (aa 2-256). HPA shows brain-enhanced expression (30.1 nTPM) with cytosolic localization, ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all tissues), and predicted intracellular topology. ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 7p22.3-14.3 copy number gains and losses plus complex rearrangements associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with sorting nexins and chromatin regulators: SNX2 (sorting nexin 2, endosomal trafficking, 0.636), SNX6 (sorting nexin 6, retromer complex, 0.616), SNX5 (sorting nexin 5, endosomal sorting, 0.459), STK31 (serine/threonine kinase 31, cell cycle regulator, 0.621), ZNF713 (zinc finger protein 713, transcriptional repressor, 0.484), ZNF793 (zinc finger protein 793, transcriptional regulator, 0.460), FAM219B (family with sequence similarity 219 member B, paralog, 0.479), C4orf19 (chromosome 4 ORF 19, uncharacterized, 0.458), and olfactory receptors OR51M1 (0.571) and OR6T1 (0.430). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse Q8C790 301 aa) indicates functional constraint. The FAM221 domain architecture combined with cytosolic localization and SNX2/SNX6/SNX5 sorting nexin interactions strongly suggests FAM221A functions as a cytosolic adaptor protein involved in endosomal vesicle trafficking and membrane protein sorting. Sorting nexins recognize phosphoinositide lipids and mediate cargo selection for retromer-dependent retrograde transport from endosomes to the trans-Golgi network. The brain-enriched expression pattern suggests a specialized role in neuronal endosomal trafficking, potentially regulating synaptic vesicle recycling or neurotransmitter receptor trafficking. Interaction with STK31 (cell cycle kinase) indicates potential coupling of endosomal trafficking to cell cycle progression. ZNF713/ZNF793 interactions suggest potential nuclear signaling functions or chromatin regulation through endosomal pathway crosstalk. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 83.5) indicates a stable folded domain suitable for protein-protein interactions. Pathogenic 7p CNVs likely cause haploinsufficiency disrupting endosomal sorting in neuronal tissues, potentially contributing to neurodevelopmental defects through impaired receptor trafficking, synaptic dysfunction, and altered intracellular signaling cascades.
