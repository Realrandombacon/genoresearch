## Description
WDR45 (Q9Y484, 360 aa), also known as WIPI4 (WD repeat domain phosphoinositide-interacting protein 4), is a PROPPIN family member functioning in autophagosome formation and phosphoinositide binding. InterPro identifies complete PROPPIN architecture: PROPPIN family domain (IPR048720, PF21032, aa 5-345), seven-bladed WD40 β-propeller with four canonical WD40 repeats (IPR001680, SM00320, aa 1-33, 92-127, 181-221), and YVTN repeat-like superfamily (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, IPR015943, IPR036322, aa 7-340). AlphaFold predicts very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.5, residues 1-360) consistent with stable seven-bladed β-propeller fold characteristic of PROPPIN proteins. STRING reveals 10 high-confidence interactions forming autophagy machinery: ATG2A (0.998), ATG2B (0.997), OTUD7B (0.849), ATG5 (0.779), ATG12 (0.779), C19orf12 (0.739), GABARAP (0.733), PANK2 (0.717), COASY (0.716), and EPG5 (0.547). HPA has no expression data (gene not in database). ClinVar contains 427 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Ser209Ter, p.Glu35Ter), frameshifts (p.Phe332fs, p.Met1fs, p.Ile188fs), and splice variants (c.235+1G>T, c.726-2A>G) causing neurodegeneration with brain iron accumulation type 4 (NBIA4/BPAN). Functional hypothesis: WDR45/WIPI4 functions as a phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P) and phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate [PI(3,5)P2] effector that recruits ATG2A/ATG2B lipid transfer complexes to omegasome membranes during autophagosome initiation. The seven-bladed β-propeller creates a positively charged pocket for phosphoinositide headgroup binding, while blade surfaces mediate protein-protein interactions. Near-perfect ATG2A/ATG2B interactions (0.998, 0.997) indicate obligate complex formation for lipid transfer from ER to phagophore. ATG5-ATG12 interaction (0.779) links to autophagy ubiquitin-like conjugation system. PANK2 (0.717) and COASY (0.716) interactions connect to coenzyme A biosynthesis and pantothenate metabolism, explaining iron accumulation in BPAN. C19orf12 (0.739) interaction links to another NBIA-causing protein. Pathogenic variants disrupt β-propeller folding, phosphoinositide binding, or ATG2 recruitment, causing defective autophagy and iron homeostasis in basal ganglia. NOTE: This gene is NOT a true 'dark gene' - it is well-characterized as causative for BPAN (Beta-propeller protein-associated neurodegeneration), a form of NBIA with extensive literature.
