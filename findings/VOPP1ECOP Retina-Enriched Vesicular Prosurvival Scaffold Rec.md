## Description
VOPP1 (ECOP/GASP/WBP1L2) encodes a 172 amino acid vesicular prosurvival protein (UniProt Q96AW1) with medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 65.6) indicating partial folding with structured domains and disordered regions. InterPro analysis reveals VOPP1 family architecture (IPR026229/PTHR14971, aa1-172) with N-terminal domain (IPR060429/PF27964, aa6-54) and C-terminal domain (IPR060430/PF27963, aa57-90), no catalytic domains suggesting scaffolding function. HPA data reveals retina-enriched expression with exceptionally high levels (233.4 nTPM), cancer-enhanced expression pattern, subcellular localization to cytoplasmic vesicle membrane, late endosome, and lysosome, classification as predicted intracellular and membrane protein, involvement in transcription regulation. STRING interactions identify 10 partners (0.422-0.617 scores): TMEM207 (transmembrane protein 207, 0.617-medium confidence), SEC61G (SEC61 translocon subunit gamma, 0.613), VWA7 (von Willebrand factor A domain containing 7, 0.613), WWOX (WW domain containing oxidoreductase, 0.568), VSTM2A (V-set and transmembrane domain containing 2A, 0.529), C7orf25 (chromosome 7 open reading frame 25, 0.499), SEPTIN14 (septin 14, 0.495), EGFR (epidermal growth factor receptor, 0.475), WBP1 (WW domain binding protein 1, 0.444), and LANCL2 (lanthionine synthetase C-like 2, 0.422). This network positions VOPP1 at the intersection of EGFR signaling (EGFR, WWOX, WBP1), vesicular trafficking (SEC61G, TMEM207), and membrane organization (SEPTIN14). ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses spanning 7p22.3-q36.3 and 7p11.2 regions, plus TMEM106B-BRAF fusion, indicating strong dosage sensitivity and oncogenic potential. The 7p11.2 chromosomal location (EGFR amplicon) and cancer-enhanced expression suggests oncogenic driver role. Mechanistic hypothesis: VOPP1 functions as a vesicular membrane scaffold that recruits WW domain proteins (WWOX, WBP1) to late endosomal compartments for EGFR signaling modulation; its partially structured conformation (pLDDT 65.6, aa1-172) provides flexible interaction surfaces for assembly of WWOX tumor suppressor complexes and EGFR endosomal sorting machinery; TMEM207 and SEC61G interactions anchor VOPP1 to vesicular membranes for receptor trafficking; WWOX interaction couples VOPP1 to apoptotic signaling and metabolic regulation; SEPTIN14 interaction organizes membrane microdomains for signalosome assembly; 7p11.2 amplification in glioblastoma and squamous cell carcinoma drives VOPP1 overexpression, enhancing EGFR endosomal signaling and blocking WWOX-mediated apoptosis, explaining the prosurvival phenotype in VOPP1-amplified cancers.
