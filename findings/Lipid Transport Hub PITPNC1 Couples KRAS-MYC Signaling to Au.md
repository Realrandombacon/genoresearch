## Description
PITPNC1 (Phosphatidylinositol Transfer Protein Cytoplasmic 1, also known as RdgBβ2 or MTDH-interacting protein) is a 332 aa cytosolic lipid transfer protein functioning as a critical regulator of membrane phosphoinositide homeostasis, oncogenic signaling, and autophagy. With 44 literature references, PITPNC1 represents a 'partially characterized' dark gene where the mechanistic links between lipid transport and disease phenotypes are actively being elucidated. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical Class IIB phosphatidylinositol transfer protein architecture: an N-terminal SRPBCC lipid-binding domain (cd08890, aa 2-250) belonging to the START-like superfamily (IPR023393) that binds phosphatidylinositol (PI) and phosphatidic acid (PA), and a C-terminal extension (aa 251-332) of unknown function that may mediate protein-protein interactions or membrane targeting. AlphaFold predicts a confident global structure (pLDDT 82.1), indicating a well-folded lipid-binding pocket consistent with its transferase function. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with brain enhancement (24.4 nTPM), localizing to nucleoplasm and cytosol, suggesting roles in both nuclear signaling and cytoplasmic membrane trafficking. STRING interactions place PITPNC1 in a functional network with lipid metabolism and vesicle trafficking: PITPNM3 (0.915, membrane-bound PI transfer protein), RAB1B (0.913, Golgi-associated small GTPase), AGTRAP (0.838, angiotensin receptor-associated protein), and protein kinase A subunits (PRKACB: 0.459, PRKACA: 0.455), suggesting integration of lipid signaling with phosphorylation cascades. ClinVar lists 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.683-4G>A, c.683-5383G>A) and large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 17q, implicating PITPNC1 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders, though no specific monogenic disease is yet named. Recent literature (2023-2025) establishes PITPNC1 as: (1) A KRAS-MYC axis effector that prevents autophagy in lung and pancreatic cancer by maintaining PI4P levels at Golgi membranes (2023, 22 citations); (2) A suppressor of CD8+ T cell function that promotes radioresistance in rectal cancer via FASN/CD155 modulation (2024, 17 citations); (3) A cardioprotective factor in ischemia-reperfusion injury through phospholipid metabolism (2025); (4) A prognostic biomarker in lung adenocarcinoma with immune infiltration correlations (2024).
