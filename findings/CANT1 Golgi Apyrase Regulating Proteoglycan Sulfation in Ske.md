## Description
CANT1 (calcium-activated nucleotidase 1) is a 401 aa soluble apyrase (IPR009283, IPR036258, PF06079) localized to the Golgi apparatus. AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT 89.4) consistent with stable enzymatic fold. The enzyme displays calcium-dependent nucleotidase activity with substrate preference UDP > GDP > UTP > GTP, positioning it in nucleotide sugar metabolism critical for proteoglycan biosynthesis. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with Golgi localization. STRING reveals strong interactions with ectonucleotidase family members ENTPD6 (0.942), ENTPD8 (0.937), ENTPD5 (0.929), ENTPD1 (0.925), ENTPD3 (0.910), plus nucleotide metabolism enzymes ADSS2 (0.925), UCKL1 (0.920), RRM2B (0.914), ATIC (0.620), and RRM1 (0.495), placing CANT1 in coordinated nucleotide homeostasis networks. ClinVar contains 76 pathogenic variants including nonsense (S117*, W295*, W147*), frameshift (D89fs, Q120fs), and missense mutations (A371P, S156F) causing Desbuquois dysplasia type 1 (DBQD1), an autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia with short stature and hand anomalies. The mechanistic link: CANT1 hydrolyzes UDP released during glycosaminoglycan chain elongation on decorin and other proteoglycans; loss of function disrupts dermatan/chondroitin sulfate attachment, impairing collagen fibrillogenesis in tendons and cartilage. Recent 2025 studies reveal emerging roles: (1) collagen fibrogenesis regulation via dermatan/chondroitin sulfate synthesis on decorin, (2) pan-cancer prognostic biomarker potential, (3) WTAP-mediated m6A modification stabilizing CANT1 in lung adenocarcinoma via IGF2BP2, and (4) genetic association with scoliosis. With only 99 publications versus thousands for classical metabolic enzymes, CANT1 represents an incompletely characterized Golgi nucleotidase bridging skeletal dysplasia and tumor metabolism.
