## Description
LOC112267991 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 7p22.3 (NC_000007.14:431800-427048) spanning approximately 4.8 kb at the telomeric terminus of chromosome 7p. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals a high 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating significant clinical relevance. Variants include both copy number losses and gains affecting 7p22.3: large deletions spanning 7p22.3 (chr7:54185-1843584, ~1.8 Mb; chr7:54165-725325, ~0.7 Mb; chr7:54185-1331046, ~1.3 Mb; chr7:54165-3258775, ~3.2 Mb; chr7:54185-3324143, ~3.3 Mb), and duplications spanning 7p22.3-15.2 (chr7:43360-27196404, ~27.2 Mb; chr7:45130-25221165, ~25.2 Mb; chr7:54185-26827634, ~26.8 Mb), 7p22.3-21.3 (chr7:45130-7252065, ~7.2 Mb), and 7p22.3-22.1 (chr7:54185-6638027, ~6.6 Mb). The 7p22.3 region is the critical telomeric locus for 7p deletion syndrome and 7p duplication syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive developmental genes including HOXA cluster genes (HOXA1, HOXA2, HOXA3, HOXA4, HOXA5, HOXA6, HOXA7, HOXA9, HOXA10, HOXA11, HOXA13 - homeobox transcription factors, limb/organ development), EVARs (euchromatic variant repeats), and multiple lncRNAs. LOC112267991 likely functions as: (1) a telomeric cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 7p HOXA cluster expression, (2) a telomeric long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) modulating 7p chromatin architecture, (3) a subtelomeric chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 7p22.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a telomere-adjacent recombination hotspot affecting 7p genomic stability. The high pathogenic CNV burden (17 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this telomeric locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) HOXA cluster haploinsufficiency leading to limb malformations (split hand/foot, polysyndactyly), (2) 7p subtelomeric chromatin architecture affecting developmental gene expression, (3) anterior-posterior patterning programs, or (4) telomere capping function, leading to 7p deletion syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, limb defects, cardiac anomalies) or 7p duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, dysmorphic features, growth abnormalities).
