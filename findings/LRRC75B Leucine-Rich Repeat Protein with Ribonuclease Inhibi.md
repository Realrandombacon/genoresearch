# LRRC75B: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein with Ribonuclease Inhibitor-like Fold and Brain-Enriched Expression

**Date:** 2026-03-23T06:08:20.948047

**Quality Score:** 2.9/10  (E=4.8, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
LRRC75B encodes a 315 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein with a canonical LRR domain superfamily (IPR032675, residues 110-257) and PTHR39654 family membership. The protein adopts a ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, SSF52047) with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT=71.6), indicating a stable solenoid structure typical of LRR proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions. HPA data shows brain-enriched expression (8.6 nTPM) with tissue-enhanced specificity, dual nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization, and detection across multiple tissues. ClinVar documents 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains spanning 22q11.23, implicating LRRC75B dosage sensitivity in human disease—particularly relevant given the 22q11.23 region association with DiGeorge/velocardiofacial syndrome phenotypes. The LRR structural architecture suggests LRRC75B functions as a protein interaction scaffold, potentially binding partner proteins through its curved solenoid surface. The RNI-like fold homology raises the possibility of ribonuclease binding or regulation, though no catalytic residues are predicted. Brain-enriched expression combined with LRR-mediated interaction capacity suggests roles in neuronal protein complex assembly, potentially in synaptic scaffolding or RNA-binding protein regulation. Copy number gains may disrupt stoichiometric balance of neuronal protein complexes.
