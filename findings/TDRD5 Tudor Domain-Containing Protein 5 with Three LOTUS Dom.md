## Description
TDRD5 (Tudor Domain-Containing Protein 5) is a partially characterized germline-specific scaffolding protein with 50 publications, representing a critical component of the piRNA pathway essential for transposon silencing and spermatogenesis. This large 981-amino acid protein (UniProt Q8NAT2) features a multi-domain architecture optimized for protein-protein interactions within germ granules and piRNA processing complexes. InterPro analysis reveals three LOTUS/OST-HTH domains (IPR025605/IPR037982, positions 4-98/132-201/295-369) with GO annotations for germ cell development (GO:0007281), a central Tudor domain (IPR002999/cd20419, positions 472-594) characteristic of piRNA pathway proteins, LOTUS domain-like homologous superfamilies (G3DSA:3.30.420.610, positions 1-79/132-207/293-364), and SNase-like OB-fold superfamily (IPR035437, positions 475-657).
