## Description

The CXXC motif (Cys-X-X-Cys) coordinates structural zinc ions stabilizing the protein fold, while the exceptionally high pLDDT score (97.0) reflects evolutionary constraint on structured interaction surfaces. Disease mechanism: Copy number variants disrupt CZIB stoichiometry, impairing ribosome assembly fidelity (nucleolar stress), compromising mitochondrial copper homeostasis (oxidative damage), and destabilizing mitotic spindle integrity (aneuploidy). The 1p32.3 genomic region shows recurrent rearrangement hotspots, with CZIB dosage sensitivity contributing to chromosomal instability syndromes. Ubiquitous expression pattern predicts multi-system phenotypes rather than tissue-restricted disease.
