# DMAC1: Mitochondrial Inner Membrane Chaperone Essential for Distal Membrane Arm Assembly of Respiratory Complex I

## Description
DMAC1 (distal membrane arm assembly component 1, alias C9orf123, TMEM261) encodes a 116-amino acid mitochondrial inner membrane protein belonging to the DMAC1/Dmo2 family (IPR053117/PTHR36469, residues 19-115) that functions as a specialized chaperone for respiratory Complex I (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase) assembly. The protein contains a conserved DMAC1-like domain (IPR028036/PF15055, residues 47-92) characteristic of membrane-embedded assembly factors. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT=59.6), indicating a partially folded transmembrane protein with structured core domain and flexible termini typical of dynamic assembly chaperones. HPA classifies DMAC1 as a predicted membrane protein with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) and low tissue specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping function in oxidative phosphorylation across all metabolically active cells. Gene ontology annotations include mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I assembly, positioning DMAC1 within energy metabolism pathways. STRING interactions reveal strong coupling to Complex I assembly machinery and mitochondrial inner membrane organization: DMAC2 (0.732, distal membrane arm assembly component 2, paralog), FOXRED1 (0.613, FAD-dependent oxidoreductase, Complex I assembly factor), RIC1 (0.527, RICE1, mitochondrial Complex I assembly), SPATA6L (0.476, spermatogenesis-associated protein, mitochondrial), TMEM186 (0.457, transmembrane protein, mitochondrial), OXA1L (0.448, oxidase assembly 1-like, mitochondrial inner membrane insertase), NDUFA3 (0.448, Complex I subunit), ARMC7 (0.444, armadillo repeat protein, mitochondrial), KLHDC4 (0.431, kelch domain protein), and C15orf61 (0.431, mitochondrial Complex I assembly factor). The DMAC2 interaction is particularly significant as a paralog partnership, suggesting coordinated function in distal membrane arm module assembly. ClinVar documents 146 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 9p24.3-q21.13 copy number gains/losses and deletions, indicating dosage sensitivity that may contribute to mitochondrial encephalomyopathy or metabolic disorders. Conservation is strong across mammals: mouse (Q9CQ00, 111 aa), rat (D4AEL3, 111 aa). Mechanistically, DMAC1 likely functions as a membrane-embedded chaperone that stabilizes the distal membrane arm module of Complex I during stepwise assembly in the inner mitochondrial membrane. The protein may act as a scaffold that recruits hydrophobic membrane subunits (ND2, ND3, ND4L, ND6) or assembly factors (FOXRED1, OXA1L) to the assembling holoenzyme. The DMAC1-DMAC2 paralog pair may coordinate sequential assembly steps, with DMAC1 initiating distal arm nucleation and DMAC2 completing module integration. Loss of DMAC1 disrupts Complex I biogenesis, causing reduced NADH oxidation, impaired oxidative phosphorylation, increased ROS production, and metabolic crisis in high-energy tissues (brain, muscle, heart).
