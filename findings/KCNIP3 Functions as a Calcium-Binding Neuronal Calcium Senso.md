## Description
KCNIP3 (Potassium Voltage-Gated Channel Interacting Protein 3, also known as Calsenilin or DREAM—Downstream Regulatory Element Antagonist Modulator) is a 256 aa calcium-binding protein representing a genuine partially characterized dark gene with only 25 literature references, functioning as a multifunctional neuronal calcium sensor (NCS) that regulates voltage-gated potassium channel trafficking, presenilin-mediated amyloid processing, and transcriptional repression through EF-hand calcium sensing mechanisms. Multi-omics data reveals KCNIP3 as a vertebrate-conserved recoverin family protein with defined EF-hand calcium-binding domains yet emerging disease characterization through recent 2018-2022 discoveries in pain sensation, thyroid cancer, and mucin secretion. UniProt accession Q9Y2W7 confirms the 256 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q9QXT8, 256 aa, ~98% identity), rat (Q9JM47, 256 aa), and bovine (Q17QD9, 256 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the calcium sensor machinery across mammals. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the recoverin family (IPR028846, aa 69-247) containing multiple EF-hand calcium-binding domains (IPR002048, aa 126-245; IPR011992 EF-hand domain pair, aa 75-252; IPR018247 EF-Hand 1 calcium-binding site, aa 175-187 and 223-235; PF13499/PF13833 EF-hand domain pairs; PS00018 EF-hand calcium-binding motif; PS50222 EF-hand profile) that mediate calcium-dependent conformational changes and target protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 69.0) across the full 256 aa length, indicating a partially structured protein with ordered EF-hand domains and potentially flexible linker regions—consistent with calcium sensor proteins requiring conformational flexibility for calcium-dependent target regulation. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with top expression in brain (30.4 nTPM), parathyroid gland (25.1 nTPM), and lymphoid tissue (22.9 nTPM), vesicle subcellular localization, and classification as predicted intracellular protein/transporter—consistent with roles in neuronal signaling and ion channel regulation. The KCND2/KCND3/KCND1 interactions confirm KCNIP3 operates as a potassium channel auxiliary subunit. The PSEN1/PSEN2 interactions confirm roles in amyloid precursor protein processing. ClinVar lists 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 2 copy number variations (gains: chr2:85898497-97671333x3, chr2:95773428-102550061x3, chr2:95341388-100340514x3, chr2:1-243199373; loss: chr2:11504318-111365996x1), implicating KCNIP3 dosage sensitivity in chromosome 2 deletion/duplication syndromes though specific disease phenotypes are not yet delineated. The 25 papers (including 2022 KCNIP3 silence promoting papillary thyroid carcinoma proliferation/EMT via Wnt/β-catenin pathway with 6 citations, 2019 Kcnip3 global knockout enhancing pain sensitivity and exacerbating negative emotions in rats with 19 citations, 2018 KChIP3 coupled to Ca2+ oscillations exerting tonic brake on baseline mucin release in colon with 19 citations, 2016 PAX8 target genes in papillary thyroid carcinoma with 20 citations) confirm KCNIP3 as a genuine dark gene—core calcium sensor function defined but disease mechanisms (pain disorders, anxiety/depression, thyroid cancer, neurodegeneration) and full clinical spectrum awaiting characterization.
