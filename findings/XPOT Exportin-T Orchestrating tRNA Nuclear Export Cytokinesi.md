## Description
XPOT (Exportin for tRNA, Exportin-T) is a 962 amino acid nuclear transport receptor that functions as a critical regulator of tRNA nuclear export, cytokinesis control, and cancer progression through C-terminal armadillo fold-mediated RanGTP-tRNA binding, specific tRNA nuclear exportation control, and TTC19 expression regulation for cell division fidelity in proliferating tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic exportin architecture including Exportin-T family (IPR040017, PTHR15952, residues 9-961) for tRNA binding (GO:0000049) and tRNA export from nucleus (GO:0006409), Importin-beta N-terminal domain (IPR001494, PF03810, SM00913, residues 21-89/26-86) for small GTPase binding (GO:0031267) and intracellular protein transport (GO:0006886), Exportin-1/Importin-beta-like domain (IPR013598, PF08389, residues 99-247/99-247) forming the transport receptor core, Exportin-T C-terminal domain (IPR045546, PF19282, residues 318-960/318-960) providing tRNA recognition interface, armadillo-like helical superfamily (IPR011989, IPR016024, SSF48371, G3DSA:1.25.10.10, residues 4-942/4-857) forming the curved solenoid structure for cargo binding, and leucine-rich repeat variant (G3DSA:1.25.10.10, residues 4-942) providing structural scaffold. AlphaFold structure (AF-O43592-F1) predicts very high confidence (pLDDT 91.4) across the 962 residue sequence, indicating an exceptionally well-folded armadillo repeat solenoid suitable for RanGTP-dependent tRNA recognition and nuclear pore translocation. HPA data classifies XPOT as an essential protein and transporter involved in transport with RNA-binding and tRNA-binding molecular functions, nucleoplasm and cytosol subcellular localization reflecting nucleocytoplasmic shuttling, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and low cell type specificity consistent with housekeeping tRNA export function. STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to nuclear transport machinery: RAN (Ran GTPase, 0.999) as primary GTPase partner for transport cycle; XPO1 (Exportin-1, 0.953) as related exportin; RANGAP1 (Ran GTPase Activating Protein 1, 0.878) for RanGTP hydrolysis; NCBP1 (Nuclear Cap-Binding Protein Subunit 1, 0.872) for mRNA export coordination; ADGRD1 (Adhesion G Protein-Coupled Receptor D1, 0.857) for signaling integration; IPO7 (Importin-7, 0.830) as import partner; RANBP2 (Ran Binding Protein 2, 0.579) for nuclear pore complex anchoring; XPO5 (Exportin-5, 0.533) for pre-miRNA export; CSE1L (Cell Segregation 1-Like, 0.462) for importin-alpha recycling; and XPO4 (Exportin-4, 0.436) as alternative exportin. ClinVar lists 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1308-1G>A, c.201-150A>G), large deletions on 12q14.1-15 (chr12:61755618-70035424, chr12:64609458-70352103), copy number losses on 12q14.1-14.3 (chr12:60907151-66568077), and copy number gains on 12p13.33-q24.33 (multiple independent CNV events spanning entire chromosome 12 arm), with recent identification of XPOT deficiency causing a human disorder through impaired tRNA nuclear export confirming disease causality. Literature (45 papers) establishes XPOT as an emerging cancer target and disease gene: XPOT inhibition promotes breast cancer cell pyroptosis to suppress cancer progression revealing cell death mechanism; XPOT disruption suppresses TNBC growth through inhibition of specific tRNA nuclear exportation and TTC19 expression to induce cytokinesis failure establishing mitotic control pathway; XPOT deficiency causes a human disorder through impaired tRNA nuclear export confirming disease etiology; unconventional tonicity-regulated nuclear trafficking of NFAT5 mediated by KPNB1, XPOT and RUVBL2 revealing osmotic stress response role; and integrative analyses identify XPOT in cuproptosis-related gene signatures. As an emerging exportin with cancer and developmental disease associations, XPOT represents a critical node connecting tRNA metabolism to cancer progression, cytokinesis fidelity, and human development through armadillo fold-mediated RanGTP-tRNA ternary complex formation, specific tRNA nuclear exportation control for translation capacity regulation, TTC19 expression modulation for mitochondrial complex III assembly and cytokinesis completion, NFAT5 tonicity-responsive trafficking for osmotic stress adaptation, and pyroptosis suppression in breast cancer disrupted in triple-negative breast cancer progression, cytokinesis failure, human tRNA export disorder, osmotic stress response defects, and cuproptosis-related cell death pathways.
