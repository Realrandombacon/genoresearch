## Description
ECE2 (P0DPD6) encodes an 811 amino acid type II transmembrane metalloprotease that functions as a critical enzyme in the endothelin signaling pathway by cleaving big endothelin-1 (big ET-1) into the potent vasoactive peptide endothelin-1 (ET-1), while also serving as a cancer-enriched biomarker driving tumor progression through multiple oncogenic axes and forming a readthrough fusion protein with EEF1AKMT4 creating a multifunctional enzyme with both protease and methyltransferase activities. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical M13 peptidase architecture: a Peptidase M13 family domain (IPR000718/PF01431/PF05649/PR00786, positions 115-811/607-810/162-548) defining the catalytic core with metalloendopeptidase activity (GO:0004222) and proteolysis function (GO:0006508); a Peptidase M13 N-terminal domain (IPR008753, positions 162-548) forming part of the substrate-binding cleft; a Peptidase M13 C-terminal domain (IPR018497, positions 599-810) completing the active site; neutral endopeptidase domain 2 (G3DSA:1.10.1380.10, positions 188-496/549-594) and collagenase catalytic domain superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.390.10, positions 151-187/497-548/595-811) providing structural scaffolding; metallopeptidase catalytic domain superfamily (IPR024079, positions 151-811) confirming zinc-dependent catalysis; and Peptidase M13 domain 2 homologous superfamily (IPR042089, positions 188-594) stabilizing the fold. AlphaFold predicts a high confidence structure (pLDDT 87.1) consistent with a well-folded enzyme where the catalytic domains form a compact globular structure with a deep active site cleft typical of M13 metallopeptidases, suitable for substrate recognition and zinc coordination.
