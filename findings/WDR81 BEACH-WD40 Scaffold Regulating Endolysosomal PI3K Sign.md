## Description
WDR81 encodes a 1941 amino acid transmembrane scaffold protein (UniProt Q562E7) with a distinctive domain architecture: an N-terminal BEACH domain (IPR000409/PF02138, residues 337-619) followed by C-terminal WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400, residues 1637-1768). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 67.9) spanning the full length, suggesting partially ordered domains connected by flexible linkers. STRING interactions reveal WDR81 operates in endolysosomal trafficking complexes with high-confidence partners: MAP1LC3C (0.799), BECN1 (0.696), CCZ1/CCZ1B (0.607/0.604), and PIK3C3 (0.602). UniProt annotation indicates WDR81 functions as a negative regulator of PI3K activity on endosomal membranes via BECN1, a core subunit of the Class III PI3K complex. ClinVar documents 176 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants causing autosomal recessive cerebellar ataxia with cognitive disability and disequilibrium (CAMRQ2 syndrome), including missense mutations (p.Pro1536Ala, p.Thr1924Pro, p.Phe987Ser) and copy number variants. The BEACH domain (shared with WDR91, score 0.545) mediates endosomal membrane association, while WD40 repeats provide protein-binding scaffolding. WDR81 likely coordinates PI3P turnover during endosome maturation by recruiting the BECN1-PIK3C3 complex and modulating its lipid kinase activity, with disruption causing cerebellar neuronal trafficking defects.
