## Description
SLC25A27 (O95847, 323 aa), also known as Uncoupling Protein 4 (UCP4), is a critical brain-enriched mitochondrial transporter that functions as a master regulator of neuronal energy metabolism, reactive oxygen species (ROS) homeostasis, and ferroptosis resistance. InterPro identifies a canonical mitochondrial carrier architecture with three repeated solute carrier domains (IPR018108/PF00153, aa 20-119, 124-219, 226-317) forming the transmembrane pore, belonging to the Mitochondrial Metabolite Transporter family (IPR050391, PTHR45618). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 81.1, residues 1-323), revealing a compact bundle of six transmembrane helices characteristic of the SLC25 family, poised for proton or anion transport. HPA classifies it as a predicted mitochondrial membrane protein with low tissue specificity but detected in all tissues, reflecting its fundamental role in cellular bioenergetics; however, literature confirms highest expression in the central nervous system. STRING reveals 10 interactions defining its role in oxidative stress management: UCP2 (0.660, paralogous uncoupling protein), GPX8 (0.580), GPX5 (0.575), GPX2 (0.571), GPX3 (0.565), GPX7 (0.523), GPX6 (0.516) (all glutathione peroxidases involved in lipid peroxide detoxification), ACO2 (0.553, mitochondrial aconitase), PARK7 (0.530, DJ-1 Parkinson disease protein), and PPIF (0.412, cyclophilin F regulating permeability transition). The strong cluster of GPX interactions suggests a coordinated defense against lipid peroxidation, while the link to PARK7 connects it directly to Parkinson's disease pathology. ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses on chromosome 6p-q (e.g., chr6:43636308-64947206 x3, chr6:43636308-50947320 x1); while no specific monogenic syndrome name is assigned, the variant burden and brain expression implicate it in neuropsychiatric disorders. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A27 acts as a mild uncoupler in neuronal mitochondria, dissipating the proton gradient to reduce membrane potential and limit superoxide production by the electron transport chain. By lowering ROS, it prevents the accumulation of lipid peroxides, thereby inhibiting ferroptosis—a form of iron-dependent cell death implicated in neurodegeneration and cancer. Recent studies indicate SLC25A27 is a direct transcriptional target of STAT3; in hepatocellular carcinoma, artesunate overcomes lenvatinib resistance by suppressing the STAT3/SLC25A27 axis, inducing ferroptosis. Conversely, in the brain, a specific haplotype of SLC25A27 is associated with ultra-resistant schizophrenia, likely due to altered mitochondrial efficiency affecting neurotransmitter synthesis or synaptic plasticity. The interaction with PARK7 suggests a shared pathway in protecting dopaminergic neurons from oxidative stress. Conservation across vertebrates confirms its essential evolutionary role in neuronal survival. NOTE: This gene represents a 'dark' but mechanistically pivotal node linking mitochondrial physiology, psychiatric disease, and cancer therapy resistance.
