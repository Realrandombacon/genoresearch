# ZNF891: KRAB-C2H2 Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Ubiquitous Expression and Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
ZNF891 (Zinc Finger Protein 891, UniProt A8MT65) is a 544 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein with canonical transcriptional repressor architecture. InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal KRAB-A box domain (PF01352, residues 42-82; IPR001909, residues 42-113; IPR036051 superfamily, residues 37-98) that mediates transcriptional repression through recruitment of TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complexes, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 320-347, 348-375, 376-403, 432-454, and 460-482 providing sequence-specific DNA binding capability. The protein also contains an Ikaros C2H2-type zinc-finger family signature (IPR050589, residues 339-478). AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (global pLDDT 56.4), consistent with KRAB-ZFP architecture featuring ordered zinc finger domains connected by flexible intrinsically disordered linkers. HPA expression demonstrates low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues and cell type-enhanced pattern, with predicted nucleoplasmic and vesicular localization. STRING interaction network identifies CTAGE8 (score 0.503), ZCCHC2 (0.485), FBXO33 (0.479), FAM86B2 (0.446), DPH5 (0.437), GLYCTK (0.434), BRWD3 (0.427), CEP250 (0.411), WNK3 (0.404), and USP27X (0.404) as interaction partners. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning chromosome 12q24.22-24.33 regions, indicating strong dosage sensitivity though no specific disease associations are yet defined. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF891 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that binds target gene promoters/enhancers via its C2H2 zinc finger array and recruits the TRIM28-SETDB1 histone methyltransferase complex through its KRAB domain, establishing H3K9me3-marked heterochromatin. Ubiquitous expression suggests housekeeping regulatory roles across multiple cell types, potentially silencing retrotransposons or regulating developmental gene networks. The diverse interaction partners spanning chromatin regulators (BRWD3), centrosomal proteins (CEP250), kinases (WNK3), and deubiquitinases (USP27X) suggest ZNF891 may integrate transcriptional control with cell cycle progression, signaling pathways, and protein stability regulation. Dosage sensitivity indicated by ClinVar CNVs suggests precise expression levels are critical for normal cellular function.
