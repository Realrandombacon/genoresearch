# FAM53C: Dosage-Sensitive Transcriptional Co-Regulator Enriched in Immune Cells

## Description
FAM53C encodes a 392 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9NYF3) belonging to the FAM53 family (InterPro IPR029356, Pfam PF15242). The protein contains a conserved FAM53 domain spanning residues 1-307, suggesting structured functional core. HPA classifies FAM53C as a predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity but immune cell enrichment in blood cells, indicating potential immunoregulatory function. Critically, ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains, losses, duplications, and deletions spanning 5q31.2, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity. The RefSeq annotation states FAM53 family members bind transcriptional regulators that modulate cell proliferation, positioning FAM53C as a transcriptional co-regulator. The combination of immune cell enrichment, transcriptional regulator binding, and haploinsufficiency phenotype suggests FAM53C functions as a chromatin-associated co-factor regulating proliferation genes in hematopoietic lineages. Pathogenic CNVs indicate both loss and gain of function are deleterious, consistent with stoichiometric requirement in transcriptional complexes. The conserved FAM53 domain across vertebrates (mouse 393 aa, 99% identity) indicates essential functional constraint.
