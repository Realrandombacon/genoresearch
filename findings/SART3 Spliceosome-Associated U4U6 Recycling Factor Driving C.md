## Description
SART3 (Spliceosome Associated Factor 3, Squamous Cell Carcinoma Antigen Recognized by T-cells 3, Q15020) encodes a 963 aa nuclear protein functioning as an essential U4/U6 snRNP recycling factor that promotes spliceosome assembly, resolves DNA-RNA hybrids at double-strand breaks, and drives chemotherapy resistance through transcriptional regulation. Structural architecture reveals N-terminal tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain (G3DSA:1.25.40.10/IPR011990, residues 101-279/304-613) forming helical scaffold for protein-protein interactions, central HAT (Half-A-TPR) repeats (IPR003107, residues 126-158/164-195/201-237) stabilizing complex assembly, two C-terminal RNA recognition motifs RRM1/RRM2 (IPR000504/cd12391/cd12392/G3DSA:3.30.70.330, residues 704-782/801-878) binding U6 snRNA stem-loop for recycling, and C-terminal LSM-interacting domain (IPR008669, residues 944-962) anchoring to LSM2-8 heptameric ring; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 77.4) confirms high-confidence well-folded structure with distinct functional modules enabling dual roles in splicing and DNA repair. STRING interactions define spliceosome and deubiquitination networks: LSM ring components LSM2 (0.998), LSM8 (0.996), LSM4 (0.995), LSM5 (0.990), LSM6 (0.728), LSM7 (0.439) forming core binding partners, pre-mRNA processing factor PRPF3 (0.968) linking to tri-snRNP assembly, deubiquitinases USP15 (0.609)/USP4 (0.444) regulating SART3 stability, methylphosphate capping enzyme MEPCE (0.430) for 7SK snRNA stability. HPA localizes SART3 to nucleoplasm as essential RNA-binding protein involved in mRNA processing/splicing with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low tissue/cancer specificity. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic variants including 12q23.1-24.33 copy number gains (chr12:99532287-133777902 triplication), missense mutations p.Arg252Gln/p.Arg519Gly/p.Pro718Leu/p.Ser216Pro/p.Arg493Trp/p.Arg767Trp/p.Glu211Lys affecting TPR/RRM domains, nonsense mutation p.Arg253Ter causing truncation, and splice variant c.1670-9T>A. Literature (142 papers) demonstrates oncogenic mechanisms: SART3 promotes homologous recombination repair by stimulating DNA-RNA hybrid removal and DNA end resection via DDX1 interaction (3x citations, 2025), activates CD36 transcription by recruiting FOXM1 and activating PARP to augment cisplatin resistance in NSCLC (2025), single nucleotide substitution rs2072580 damages CREB1 binding site in bidirectional SART3/ISCU promoter altering expression (1x citation, 2025), structural basis of U6 snRNA recognition by RRM domains elucidated (2025). Mechanistic hypothesis: SART3 functions through coordinated mechanisms in splicing and genome stability; TPR/HAT domains scaffold assembly with LSM2-8 ring while RRM1/RRM2 specifically bind U6 snRNA 5 stem-loop displacing it from LSm ring enabling U4/U6 di-snRNP formation; in DNA repair, SART3 recruited to DSBs interacts with DDX1 helicase resolving R-loops preventing transcription-replication conflicts and facilitating EXO1-mediated end resection for homologous recombination; in cisplatin resistance, nuclear SART3 recruits FOXM1 to CD36 promoter enhancing fatty acid uptake while PARP activation repairs platinum-induced DNA damage; USP15/USP4 deubiquitination stabilizes SART3 protein levels under stress; 12q CNVs alter SART3 dosage affecting global splicing fidelity and repair capacity; rs2072580 variant disrupts CREB1-mediated transcriptional control potentially contributing to metabolic disease susceptibility.
