## Description
JRK (Jrk helix-turn-helix protein, also known as jerky, JH8) is a 556 amino acid nuclear DNA-binding protein (UniProt O75564) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 70.8), characteristic of centromere and transposable element-derived proteins that function through DNA binding and chromatin organization to regulate gene expression and maintain genomic stability. The protein belongs to the centromere and transposable element-derived protein family (IPR050863, residues 15-416; PTHR19303, residues 15-416), contains a CENP-B type helix-turn-helix DNA-binding domain (IPR006600, residues 77-149; PF04218, residues 14-66; PS51253, residues 77-149; SM00674), a Psq-type HTH DNA-binding domain (IPR007889, residues 11-66; PF03221, residues 87-147; PS50960, residues 11-62), a DDE superfamily endonuclease domain (IPR004875, residues 213-382; PF03184, residues 213-382; GO:0003676 nucleic acid binding), and homeodomain-like superfamily domains (G3DSA:1.10.10.60, residues 13-64/85-143; IPR009057, residues 14-71/81-144). JRK is located on chromosome 8q24.3 and is predicted to enable DNA-binding activity, involved in chromatin organization, located in nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and nuclear bodies. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein with DNA-binding molecular function.
