## Description
ATP5MC1 (ATP Synthase Membrane Subunit C Locus 1, also known as ATP Synthase F(0) Complex Subunit C1) is a partially characterized mitochondrial membrane protein with only 13 publications despite its fundamental role in cellular energy production. This compact 136-amino acid protein (UniProt P05496) forms the rotating c-ring rotor of the F(0) proton channel, converting electrochemical gradient energy into mechanical rotation driving ATP synthesis. InterPro analysis reveals the conserved ATP synthase F0 complex subunit C family architecture (IPR000454/PF00137, positions 3-136/70-132) with characteristic hairpin fold containing two transmembrane helices connected by an acidic loop. Critical functional features include the DCCD-binding site (IPR020537, positions 98-119) containing the essential glutamate residue (Glu61 in mature protein) that undergoes protonation/deprotonation during rotary catalysis. Additional domains include V-ATPase proteolipid subunit C-like domain (IPR002379, positions 70-132), F1F0 ATP synthase subunit C superfamily (G3DSA:1.20.20.10/IPR038662, positions 63-134), and membership in F/V-ATP synthase subunit C superfamily (IPR035921, positions 64-135).
