## Description
TMEM139 encodes a 216-amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt Q8IV31) localized to the plasma membrane and focal adhesion sites. InterPro analysis identifies a conserved transmembrane protein 139 family domain (IPR038805, PTHR36294) spanning the entire protein sequence (residues 1-216). AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (pLDDT 64.5), suggesting a partially structured membrane protein with possible intrinsically disordered regions. Human Protein Atlas data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in intestine (31.8 nTPM) and kidney (30.1 nTPM), plus cancer-enhanced expression pattern. STRING interaction network reveals 6 medium-confidence partners: RPS6KA3 (0.562), PROSER3 (0.528), THTPA (0.506), CCDC189 (0.480), SLC4A1 (0.478), and TRIM68 (0.469). The RPS6KA3 connection is particularly notable—this serine/threonine kinase regulates cell proliferation and differentiation via MAPK/ERK signaling. ClinVar contains 49 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains across 7q34-36.3, indicating dosage sensitivity. No single nucleotide variants suggest the gene tolerates point mutations but is sensitive to copy number changes.
