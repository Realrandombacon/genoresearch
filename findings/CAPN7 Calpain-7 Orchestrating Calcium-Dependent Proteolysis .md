## Description
CAPN7 (Calpain-7) is an 813 amino acid atypical calcium-dependent cysteine protease that functions as a non-classical calpain lacking the small regulatory subunit requirement, mediating substrate-specific proteolytic cleavage critical for embryo adhesion, endometrial stromal cell decidualization, and cancer cell migration through MIT domain-dependent ESCRT-III complex recruitment and FoxO1/AKT1 signaling pathway modulation. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic calpain architecture including N-terminal tandem MIT domains (IPR007330, cd02681, IPR036181, residues 3-77/83-159) for ESCRT-III interaction and membrane remodeling, central CysPc catalytic domain (IPR001300, cd00044, PF00648, G3DSA:3.90.70.10, residues 214-547) performing calcium-dependent cysteine-type endopeptidase activity (GO:0004198) and proteolysis (GO:0006508), papain-like cysteine peptidase superfamily (IPR038765, residues 203-550) providing catalytic scaffold, C-terminal domain III (IPR022682, IPR022683, IPR036213, G3DSA:2.60.120.380, residues 686-810/693-799) for substrate recognition and regulatory interactions, and PalB/RIM13 calpain-like family signature (IPR051297, residues 4-800) defining the atypical calpain subfamily. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9Y6W3-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 88.5) across the 813 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded protease with stable catalytic core and structured MIT domains suitable for membrane association and substrate processing. HPA data classifies CAPN7 as an intracellular enzyme with hydrolase, protease, and thiol protease molecular functions, centrosomal and cytosolic localization reflecting ESCRT and cytoskeletal functions, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with housekeeping proteolytic regulation. STRING interactions reveal functional coupling to calpain regulatory machinery and cytoskeletal organizers: OXNAD1 (Oxidoreductase NAD Binding Domain Containing 1, 0.896) for mitochondrial function; XIRP1 (Xin Actin Binding Repeat Containing 1, 0.841) for actin cytoskeleton organization; CAPNS1 (Calpain Small Subunit 1, 0.830) and CAPNS2 (0.820) as calpain regulatory subunits; IST1 (IST1 ESCRT-III Associated Factor, 0.762) for ESCRT-III complex assembly; CAST (Calpastatin, 0.756) as endogenous calpain inhibitor; TTR (Transthyretin, 0.637) for protein transport; CAV3 (Caveolin-3, 0.562) for membrane microdomain organization; LRGUK (Leucine Rich Repeat And Guanylate Kinase Domain Containing, 0.536) for cytoskeletal dynamics; and TFAP2A (Transcription Factor AP-2 Alpha, 0.513) for transcriptional regulation. ClinVar lists 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 3p26.3-14.3, 3p26.3-22.3, 3p26.3-24.1, 3p25.1-24.2 (spanning 2-55 Mb regions), duplications, and splice site variants (c.2297+35C>G, c.2205-15C>G, c.2074-76A>T, c.1286+68C>T), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in female infertility and developmental disorders. Literature (26 papers) establishes CAPN7 as an emerging atypical calpain: miR-29c-3p targeting TUG1 affects bladder cancer cell migration and invasion by regulating CAPN7 expression revealing tumor suppressor function; CAPN7 negatively regulates embryo adhesion via cleaving HOXA10 establishing role in implantation; CAPN7 negatively regulates human endometrial stromal cell decidualization in endometriosis by promoting FoxO1 nuclear exclusion via hydrolyzing AKT1 demonstrating mechanistic pathway control; and CAPN7 involvement in wild boar muscle growth and protein transformation confirming conserved proteolytic function. As a partially characterized atypical calpain with emerging disease associations, CAPN7 represents a critical node connecting calcium-dependent proteolysis to embryo implantation, endometrial decidualization, cancer metastasis suppression, and ESCRT-mediated membrane remodeling through MIT domain-dependent IST1 recruitment and FoxO1/AKT1 pathway regulation disrupted in bladder cancer progression and female infertility.
