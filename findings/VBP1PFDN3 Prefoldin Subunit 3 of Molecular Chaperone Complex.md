## Description
VBP1 (also known as PFDN3) encodes Prefoldin Subunit 3 (UniProt P61758), a 197-amino acid molecular chaperone essential for protein folding and cytoskeletal assembly. The protein contains a conserved prefoldin alpha-like domain (IPR004127, positions 60-182; PF02996, positions 60-182; cd23156, positions 52-180) classified within the prefoldin superfamily (IPR009053, positions 44-185; SSF46579, positions 50-179; G3DSA:1.10.287.370, positions 44-185) with annotated protein folding (GO:0006457) and prefoldin complex (GO:0016272) functions, indicating essential role in nascent polypeptide capture and delivery to CCT/TRiC chaperonin for folding. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 86.0, residues 1-197) indicating stable folding with well-defined coiled-coil prefoldin architecture suitable for substrate binding and chaperone complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis reveals VBP1 forms an extremely high-confidence prefoldin and CCT/TRiC network with all complex subunits: PFDN6 (0.999; prefoldin subunit 6), PFDN2 (0.999; prefoldin subunit 2), PFDN5 (0.999; prefoldin subunit 5), PFDN4 (0.999; prefoldin subunit 4), CCT8 (0.982; chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit 8), CCT2 (0.974; chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit 2), CCT4 (0.972; chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit 4), TCP1 (0.971; chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit alpha), PFDN1 (0.958; prefoldin subunit 1), and CCT6A (0.931; chaperonin containing TCP1 subunit 6A), confirming VBP1's central integration into the hexameric prefoldin complex that delivers unfolded actin and tubulin to the CCT/TRiC chaperonin for ATP-dependent folding. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity detected in all tissues, vesicles/cytosol subcellular localization consistent with chaperone function in cytoplasmic protein folding, annotated for chaperone molecular function, classified as essential protein and predicted intracellular protein. With 64 publications, VBP1 is a moderately studied chaperone with emerging roles in cancer and hypoxia signaling: VBP1 promotes tumor proliferation as part of hypoxia-related signature in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (2024, 4 cit), VBP1 negatively regulates CHIP and selectively inhibits HIF-1α but not HIF-2α activity (2023, 16 cit), VBP1 modulates Wnt/β-catenin signaling by mediating TCF/LEF transcription factor stability (2020, 13 cit), VBP1 represses cancer metastasis by enhancing HIF-1α degradation induced by pVHL (2018, 31 cit).
