## Description
CPAMD8 (C3 and PZP-like alpha-2-macroglobulin domain-containing protein 8) encodes a large 1885 amino acid secreted protease inhibitor (UniProt Q8IZJ3) belonging to the alpha-2-macroglobulin family. InterPro analysis reveals a complex multi-domain architecture: alpha-2-macroglobulin domains (IPR001599, PF00207, residues 750-841 and 1141-1429), Kazal-type serine protease inhibitor domain (IPR002350, residues 1711-1756), immunoglobulin-like beta-sandwich folds (G3DSA:2.60.40.10, multiple positions), and A2M receptor-binding domain (G3DSA:2.60.40.690, residues 1505-1666). This domain organization suggests CPAMD8 functions as a broad-spectrum protease trap with tissue-specific regulatory capacity.\n\nAlphaFold prediction shows confident structure (pLDDT 72.7) across the full 1885 aa length, indicating a stable, well-folded extracellular protein. The multi-domain architecture with immunoglobulin-like folds suggests protein-protein interaction capability beyond simple protease inhibition.\n\nSTRING interactions identify 10 partners at medium confidence (0.400-0.551): FURIN (0.551), TRIM58 (0.503), GAA (0.458), ADAMTSL4 (0.454), GPATCH3 (0.438), PRSS48 (0.425), IQCC (0.422), MYOC (0.407), PROSER3 (0.402), and P3H2 (0.400). Notably, FURIN is a proprotein convertase that processes many secreted proteins, suggesting CPAMD8 undergoes proteolytic maturation. ADAMTSL4 and MYOC are both extracellular matrix proteins associated with glaucoma and ocular development, supporting CPAMD8's role in eye morphogenesis.\n\nHPA expression reveals CPAMD8 is tissue-enhanced with highest expression in seminal vesicle (34.6 nTPM), cancer-enhanced, and localized to focal adhesion sites. The focal adhesion localization suggests involvement in cell-ECM interactions and mechanotransduction. Subcellular classification as both secreted and intracellular indicates potential dual functionality.\n\nClinVar reports 53 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants causing anterior segment dysgenesis, congenital microcoria, and ectopia lentis. The variant spectrum includes frameshifts (p.Asn757fs), nonsense mutations (p.Gln1518Ter), and missense changes (p.Gly1183Arg, p.Thr1386Asn), indicating dosage sensitivity and structural requirements for function.\n\nWith 54 publications, CPAMD8 occupies a borderline space — disease-associated but mechanistically underexplored. Recent work (2025) confirms striking expression in fetal lens and distal neural retina, with biallelic variants causing Marfan-like syndromic features.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: CPAMD8 functions as a developmental protease regulator that controls extracellular matrix remodeling during ocular anterior segment formation. Its Kazal domain provides specific serine protease inhibition, while the A2M domains enable broad-spectrum protease trapping. CPAMD8 likely regulates ADAMTS family metalloproteases (via ADAMTSL4 interaction) and FURIN-mediated processing of ocular development factors. The focal adhesion localization suggests CPAMD8 couples ECM integrity to cellular signaling, where loss causes dysregulated proteolysis leading to iris hypoplasia (microcoria) and lens dislocation. Seminal vesicle enrichment suggests additional reproductive tract functions, potentially protecting sperm from proteolytic damage.
