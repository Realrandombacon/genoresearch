## Description
MYCT1 (Myc Target 1) is a 235-amino acid intracellular protein originally identified as a transcriptional target of the MYC oncoprotein, representing a family-specific architecture (IPR029180/Myc-target-protein-1, positions 49-235; PF15179, 49-234; PTHR14869, 49-235) with no homology to known functional domains, suggesting it may function as a scaffold or adaptor protein. STRING interactions reveal connections to endothelial and adhesion signaling pathways: ADGRL4/EMR2 (0.636, adhesion G-protein coupled receptor E4 involved in angiogenesis), EMCN (0.516, endomucin regulating leukocyte trafficking), CLEC14A (0.486, C-type lectin in endothelial cells), CKAP4 (0.431, cytoskeleton-associated ER membrane protein), SPTLC2 (0.429, serine palmitoyltransferase for sphingolipid synthesis), and DDX42 (0.425, DEAD-box RNA helicase), suggesting roles in vascular biology, cell adhesion, and lipid metabolism. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (46.7 nTPM), cancer-enhanced specificity, immune cell enrichment, group-enriched single cell type specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, and vesicles, classified as both a predicted intracellular and membrane protein. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning 6q24.2-27 regions and deletions, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in hematologic malignancies and developmental disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8N699-F1) predicts medium confidence folding (pLDDT 60.6) consistent with a partially structured protein containing both ordered regions and flexible linkers typical of scaffold/adaptor proteins. With 72 papers, MYCT1 represents a 'gray' gene transitioning from dark: recent breakthrough studies (2024-2025) identify critical roles in hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) environmental sensing and engraftment, colorectal cancer metastasis suppression via ARHGAP29/GSK3β and ZO-1 signaling, acute myeloid leukemia progression through MARCH1-mediated degradation, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma via RUNX1 transcription suppression, and hypoxia-induced dysfunction alleviation through pericyte reprogramming. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that MYCT1 functions as a MYC-responsive molecular hub that integrates environmental cues (hypoxia, adhesion signals via ADGRL4/EMCN) with transcriptional programs controlling HSC quiescence vs. activation, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, and immune cell trafficking, potentially by scaffolding signaling complexes at nuclear bodies and vesicular compartments to coordinate sphingolipid metabolism (SPTLC2) with cytoskeletal dynamics (CKAP4) and RNA processing (DDX42), making it a promising target for enhancing HSC transplantation efficiency and blocking cancer metastasis.
