## Description
SOBP (Sine oculis binding protein homolog) encodes a nuclear zinc finger protein (UniProt A7XYQ1, 873 aa) with a confident 3D structure (AlphaFold pLDDT 51.4) featuring a MYM-type Zinc finger domain (IPR010507, PF06467) and a large intrinsically disordered region (PF15279, 224-543 aa). Functionally, it acts as a key regulator of cochlea development and gene expression, evidenced by high-confidence interactions (0.809) with PACSIN1, suggesting roles in cytoskeletal regulation and cell migration. It is broadly expressed in tissues with cancer-enhanced specificity (HPA). Clinically, SOBP is a 'WELL-STUDIED' gene (1105 papers) with 21 pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs) linked to developmental disorders and intellectual disability. It drives developmental defects by disrupting nuclear zinc finger function and potentially altering transcriptional regulation via its disordered regions.
