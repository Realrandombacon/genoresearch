# GEMIN7: Essential Protein Involved in mRNA Processing

**Date:** 2026-06-16T13:31:04.744807

**Quality Score:** 4/10 (MODERATE)

**Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM

## Description
GEMIN7 is an essential protein involved in mRNA processing and splicing. It interacts with other GEMIN proteins and SMN1 to form a complex crucial for small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (snRNP) assembly. GEMIN7 shows low tissue specificity and is detected in all tissues. The protein contains a SUZ-C domain and is localized to the nucleoplasm and cytosol. Nine pathogenic variants have been reported, including copy number gains and single nucleotide variants.
