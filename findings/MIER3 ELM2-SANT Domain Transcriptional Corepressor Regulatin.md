## Description
MIER3 (Mesoderm Induction Early Response Protein 3, UniProt Q7Z3K6) is a 550 aa nuclear transcriptional corepressor belonging to the MIER family that functions in chromatin remodeling and gene silencing through histone deacetylase complex recruitment. InterPro analysis reveals canonical MIER domain architecture: ELM2 domain (IPR000949, positions 174-272; PF01448, positions 176-224; PS51156, positions 174-272) for protein-protein interactions and corepressor complex assembly, SANT/Myb DNA-binding domain (IPR001005/IPR017884, positions 277-329; PF00249, positions 281-325; PS51293, positions 277-329; G3DSA:1.10.10.60, positions 274-343) for chromatin binding and transcription factor recruitment, MIER3 C-terminal domain (IPR045787, positions 336-550; PF19426, positions 336-550) for family-specific functions, and MIER family domain (IPR040138, positions 16-509). AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 57.9), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered ELM2-SANT domains and intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with transcriptional corepressor flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity), nucleoplasm localization, classified as predicted intracellular protein/transcription factor with repressor molecular function involved in transcription regulation. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with chromatin remodeling and histone deacetylation machinery: HDAC2 (0.832, histone deacetylase 2), HDAC1 (0.801, histone deacetylase 1), BAHD1 (0.796, heterochromatin-binding protein), SETD9 (0.617, histone methyltransferase), C16orf87 (0.566), H2AZ1 (0.521, histone H2A variant), MIER1 (0.449, family homolog), DYTN (0.441, dynein light chain), H2BC1 (0.420, histone H2B). ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 5q11.1-12.1 and 5p15.33-q35.3 affecting the MIER3 locus. Literature (23 papers) establishes MIER3 roles in: (1) esophageal cancer biomarker and therapeutic target identification (2025), (2) chicken W chromosome gene expression in embryonic gonads (2023), (3) breast cancer epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and aggressiveness via HDAC1/HDAC2/Snail corepressor complex formation (2021, 19 citations), (4) lung cancer cell proliferation and EMT inhibition via mesenchymal stem cell exosome miR-486-5p targeting (2022, 2 citations), and (5) non-small cell lung cancer progression suppression (2020). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: MIER3 serves as an ELM2-SANT domain transcriptional corepressor that recruits HDAC1/HDAC2 histone deacetylases and Snail transcription factor to target gene promoters, silencing epithelial genes and promoting EMT in cancer progression while functioning as a tumor suppressor in lung cancer through context-dependent mechanisms. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The ELM2 domain mediates HDAC1/HDAC2 binding and corepressor complex assembly, while the SANT domain binds chromatin and recruits additional silencing factors. MIER3 forms a tripartite complex with HDAC1/HDAC2 and Snail, deacetylating histones at epithelial gene promoters (E-cadherin/CDH1) to induce EMT and enhance breast cancer metastasis. In lung cancer, MIER3 expression is post-transcriptionally repressed by miR-486-5p carried in mesenchymal stem cell exosomes, reducing cell proliferation and EMT. The dual role (oncogene in breast cancer, tumor suppressor in lung cancer) suggests tissue-specific cofactor recruitment or target gene selectivity. BAHD1 interaction indicates heterochromatin spreading and gene silencing functions. SETD9 interaction suggests crosstalk between histone deacetylation and methylation pathways. The 8 ClinVar CNVs indicate MIER3 dosage sensitivity may contribute to developmental disorders or cancer predisposition. Medium AlphaFold confidence reflects intrinsic disorder common in transcriptional corepressors for dynamic complex assembly.
