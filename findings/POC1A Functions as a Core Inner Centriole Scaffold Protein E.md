## Description
POC1A (POC1 Centriolar Protein Homolog A) is a 407 aa essential centriolar scaffold protein representing a partially characterized gene with 48 literature references, functioning as a critical WD40-repeat component of the inner centriole scaffold required for centriole integrity, cilia formation, and chromosome segregation. Multi-omics data reveals POC1A as a vertebrate-conserved structural protein with defined WD40 domains yet emerging functional characterization through recent 2024-2025 cancer metastasis and centriole architecture discoveries. UniProt accession Q8NBT0 confirms the 407 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q8JZX3, 405 aa, ~99% identity) and cattle (Q2TBP4, 407 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the centriolar scaffold architecture. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the WD40 repeat family (IPR001680, aa 8-299; PF00400, multiple repeats) containing seven canonical WD40 blades that form a beta-propeller structure essential for protein-protein interactions within the centriole lumen. The POC1A/POC1B family (IPR050505, aa 16-383) indicates functional specialization in centriole assembly. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.7) across the full 407 aa length, with the WD40 domain (aa 8-299) showing very high confidence (>90 locally), indicating a stable beta-propeller fold suitable for scaffolding interactions. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with top expression in testis (16.5 nTPM) and lymphoid tissue (11.9 nTPM), consistent with roles in spermatogenesis (flagellar centrioles) and cell division. Subcellular localization includes flagellar centriole, mid piece, and principal piece. Biological process annotation includes cilium biogenesis/degradation. Disease involvement lists ciliopathy, dwarfism, and hypotrichosis. STRING interactions reveal a focused centriole/cell cycle network: POC1B (0.823, heterodimer partner), CENPW (0.738, centromere protein W), CKAP2L (0.633), CDC20 (0.609, cell cycle regulator), CDCA5 (0.605), RBBP5 (0.596), ASH2L (0.595), CEP44 (0.577), CORO1A (0.443), PLK4 (0.400, centriole duplication kinase). These interactions confirm POC1A operates in centriole integrity (POC1B, CEP44, PLK4) and potentially chromatin regulation (RBBP5, ASH2L). ClinVar lists 49 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including frameshift deletions (c.53del p.His18fs, c.483_484del p.Ser162fs, c.689_696del p.Ala230fs), nonsense mutations (c.55C>T p.Arg19Ter), splice site variants (c.104-2del, c.104-2A>C), missense variants (c.719C>T p.Ser240Leu, c.445C>T p.Arg149Cys), and large copy number variations (chr3 gains/losses). These variants implicate POC1A in severe ciliopathies, dwarfism, and hypotrichosis, though specific syndrome names are not yet fully delineated in ClinVar summaries. The 48 papers (including 2025 triple-negative breast cancer/STAT3/EMT studies with 2 citations, 2024 centriole inner scaffold/POC1A-POC1B heterodimer crosslinks study with 13 citations, 2025 prostate cancer/CENPM/EMT studies) confirm POC1A as a genuine partially characterized gene—core centriole function defined but cancer metastasis mechanisms and full disease spectrum awaiting characterization.
