## Description
TRAF3IP3 (TRAF3-interacting protein 3, also known as T3JAM) is a 551 amino acid scaffold protein that serves as a critical component of the STRIPAK (Striatin-interacting phosphatase and kinase) complex, linking PP2A phosphatase activity to JNK signaling pathways. The protein contains multiple coiled-coil domains including an N-terminal CC1 domain (cd21912, positions 189-233) and a STRIPAK-associated coiled-coil domain (PF27557, positions 192-259) that mediate homo- and hetero-oligomerization within the complex. AlphaFold prediction shows high structural confidence (pLDDT 74.0), indicating a well-folded scaffold with stable interaction interfaces. HPA data reveals tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (121.5 nTPM), vesicular subcellular localization consistent with signalosome assembly functions, detected in many tissues. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners defining the STRIPAK complex architecture: striatin family members STRN3 (0.996), STRN4 (0.995), STRN (0.995); scaffolding proteins MOB4 (0.996), STRIP1 (0.996); PP2A regulatory subunit PPP2R1A (0.995); GCKIII kinases STK25 (0.995), STK24 (0.995); negative regulator SIKE1 (0.995); and fusion partner FGFR1OP2 (0.600). ClinVar reports 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants though specific disease phenotypes remain under investigation. Literature (63 papers) reveals emerging disease biology: induces ER stress-mediated apoptosis with protective autophagy to inhibit lung adenocarcinoma proliferation; regulates tumor-associated macrophage M1/M2 phenotypes in breast cancer via SNORA5A; blocks mitophagy exacerbating myocardial ischemia-reperfusion injury; forms oncogenic TRAF3IP3-FGFR1 fusion in aggressive acute myeloid leukemia. TRAF3IP3 likely functions as a molecular scaffold bridging PP2A phosphatase to GCKIII kinases through coiled-coil mediated STRIPAK assembly, enabling coordinated dephosphorylation events that regulate JNK signaling, autophagy flux, and apoptotic thresholds—with dual roles as tumor suppressor in solid cancers and oncogenic driver when fused to FGFR1 in hematologic malignancies.
