## Description
TC2N encodes a 490 aa tandem C2 domain nuclear protein (UniProt Q8N9U0) localized to the nucleus and cytoplasmic membranes, functioning as a critical regulator of cancer stemness, ferroptosis, and membrane trafficking. InterPro analysis confirms its canonical tandem C2 architecture: C2A domain (IPR037786/cd08684, aa 240-342) and C2B domain (IPR037788/cd08692, aa 353-487), both belonging to the C2 domain superfamily (IPR035892, IPR000008, PF00168) with calcium-dependent lipid binding activity and nuclear localization (GO:0005634). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 67.6), indicating partially ordered C2 domains connected by flexible linkers typical of calcium-sensing proteins. STRING interactions reveal a membrane trafficking network: CATSPERB (0.690, cation channel sperm associated), SCARA5 (0.596, scavenger receptor), STAB2 (0.573, stabilin-2), MYO5A (0.531, myosin-Va motor protein), DOC2B (0.446, double C2 domain beta), and STXBP5 (0.466, syntaxin binding protein). HPA expression data unavailable. ClinVar reports 19 pathogenic variants, predominantly large 14q31-q32 copy number gains (x4) and losses (x2), suggesting dosage sensitivity; specific SNVs include synonymous changes (p.Ser172=, p.Gln300=) potentially affecting splicing or stability. Literature (40 papers) confirms emerging oncogenic roles: (1) Lung Cancer - TC2N maintains stem cell-like characteristics by blocking DUSP3 (dual specificity phosphatase 3), accelerating carcinogenesis; (2) Breast Cancer - TC2N inhibits distant metastasis and stemness via blocking fatty acid synthesis; (3) Head and Neck Cancer - TC2N promotes proliferation and invasion with p53-R175H mutant; (4) Ferroptosis - copper-driven lncRNA-TCONS-6251/miR-novel-100/TC2N axis regulates ferroptosis in kidney. Functional hypothesis: TC2N functions through: (1) Calcium-Dependent Membrane Binding - C2 domains binding phospholipids in response to Ca2+ signals to regulate vesicle trafficking; (2) DUSP3 Inhibition - blocking MAPK phosphatase to sustain ERK signaling for stemness maintenance; (3) p53 Mutant Interaction - cooperating with p53-R175H to drive invasion; (4) Ferroptosis Regulation - modulating lipid metabolism and iron-dependent cell death. Disease implications include Lung Cancer, Breast Cancer, Head and Neck Squamous Cell Carcinoma, and Ferroptosis-related disorders. Despite clinical variant burden and emerging cancer literature, the precise calcium-dependent substrates and full signaling mechanisms remain partially unexplored.
