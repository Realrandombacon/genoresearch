## Description
IFT25 (Intraflagellar Transport Protein 25; UniProt Q9Y547) is a compact 144 amino acid essential component of the intraflagellar transport (IFT) machinery with limited literature coverage, classifying it as a genuine dark gene despite critical roles in ciliary assembly, spermatogenesis, and Hedgehog signaling.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals IFT25 contains a canonical F5/8 type C domain (IPR000421/PF00754, residues 18-125) within the galactose-binding domain-like superfamily (IPR008979/G3DSA:2.60.120.260, residues 5-136). The protein is a definitive member of the IFT25 family (IPR033558/PTHR33906, residues 1-140), optimized for protein-protein interactions within the IFT particle. AlphaFold predicts exceptional structural confidence (global pLDDT 94.0), indicating an extremely stable, rigid fold—among the highest confidence predictions observed for dark genes, suggesting strong evolutionary constraint on this compact scaffold.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies ultra-high confidence interactions (all scores 0.996-0.999) exclusively with IFT Complex B components: IFT27 (0.999, forms obligate heterodimer with IFT25), IFT46 (0.999), IFT22 (0.999), IFT52 (0.999), IFT81 (0.998), IFT74 (0.998), IFT88 (0.998), IFT57 (0.997), TRAF3IP1 (0.996, ciliary trafficking adaptor), and TTC30B (0.996). This exclusive interaction profile confirms IFT25's role as an integral, non-exchangeable component of the anterograde IFT machinery that transports cargo from the ciliary base to the tip.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA shows ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with the universal requirement for cilia in vertebrate cells. Notably, protein localization is restricted to sperm flagellar compartments (mid piece, principal piece, end piece), reflecting the specialized role of IFT in spermatogenesis and flagellar assembly. Classified as an intracellular protein involved in differentiation, protein transport, spermatogenesis, and stress response.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses at 1p32-36 encompassing IFT25. No monogenic disease is yet definitively assigned, but 1p deletions cause developmental anomalies. Given the essential role of IFT25 in ciliogenesis, variants likely contribute to ciliopathy phenotypes including skeletal dysplasias, retinal degeneration, renal cysts, and male infertility. Mouse knockout studies demonstrate embryonic lethality with severe Hedgehog signaling defects.\n\nCONSERVATION: Orthologs confirmed in mouse (Q9D6H2, 143 aa) and Chlamydomonas reinhardtii (B8LIX8, 189 aa), indicating extreme evolutionary conservation from green algae to mammals, reflecting the ancient origin of the IFT machinery.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: IFT25 functions as an ultra-stable scaffold that forms an obligate heterodimer with IFT27 (a small GTPase), serving as a critical adaptor within IFT Complex B. The F5/8 type C domain likely mediates specific protein-protein interactions that link the IFT particle to cargo proteins destined for ciliary transport, including membrane receptors (e.g., Smoothened for Hedgehog signaling) and structural components. The exceptionally high AlphaFold confidence (94.0 pLDDT) suggests minimal conformational flexibility, positioning IFT25 as a rigid structural bridge rather than a regulatory switch. In spermatogenesis, IFT25 is essential for flagellar assembly, explaining its sperm tail localization. The 13 ClinVar variants warrant investigation for contributions to syndromic ciliopathies and male infertility.
