## Description
LOC107986723 encodes a transmembrane protein 68-like paralog (chromosome 7q11.23) homologous to TMEM68 (chromosome 8q11.21), functioning as a DGAT1/2-independent enzyme for storage lipid synthesis. The 324 amino acid protein (UniProt Q96MH6 ortholog) contains multiple conserved acyltransferase domains including MGAT-like region (cd07987, residues 103-310), phospholipid/glycerol acyltransferase signature (IPR002123, residues 111-234), and PF01553 acyltransferase family motif (residues 111-234), placing it in the diacylglycerol acyltransferase-related enzyme family (PTHR22753, residues 94-323) within the glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase superfamily (SSF69593, residues 41-306). AlphaFold prediction reveals high-confidence folding (pLDDT 88.1) consistent with stable enzymatic structure. HPA data shows ubiquitous tissue expression with vesicular subcellular localization, classified as predicted membrane protein and transporter involved in lipid metabolism with acyltransferase/transferase molecular function. STRING interactions identify 10 partners (scores 0.486-0.744) including high-confidence PSMD11 (0.744, proteasome regulatory particle), SDR16C5 (0.678, oxidoreductase), CHCHD7 (0.613, mitochondrial coiled-coil), PRDX6 (0.596, peroxiredoxin antioxidant), and LYPLA1 (0.525, lysoplipase), suggesting coordination with proteostasis, redox regulation, and lipid hydrolysis pathways. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic variants predominantly as chromosome 8 copy number alterations, indicating disease relevance. Evolutionary conservation spans Mus musculus (329 aa), Bos taurus (334 aa), and Gallus gallus (332 aa), confirming functional constraint across vertebrates. This paralog likely provides redundant or tissue-specific capacity for neutral lipid esterification independent of canonical DGAT enzymes.
