# ZNF232: SCAN Domain Zinc Finger Transcription Factor Forming Heteromeric Repressor Complexes with Paralogous ZNF Partners

**Date:** 2026-03-20T16:36:33.882791

**Quality Score:** 1.55/10  (E=3.1, D=0.0) [POOR]

## Description
ZNF232 encodes a 444 amino acid SCAN domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q9UNY5) localized to nucleoplasm, vesicles, and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal SCAN oligomerization domain (IPR003309, PF02023, residues 75-188) mediating protein-protein interactions, plus three tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 303-386 enabling sequence-specific DNA recognition. This domain architecture places ZNF232 within the SCAN-ZF subfamily of Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger proteins. STRING interactions show strong partnerships with paralogous SCAN-ZF transcription factors: ZSCAN32 (0.825), ZNF446 (0.793), ZNF397 (0.789), ZNF24 (0.782), ZSCAN20 (0.779), and ZKSCAN3/4/5 (0.727-0.768), suggesting ZNF232 functions within heteromeric repressor complexes where SCAN domains mediate oligomerization and zinc fingers confer DNA target specificity. HPA shows broad expression across tissues with low specificity, consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation. ClinVar documents 23 pathogenic variants, predominantly 17p13.2 copy number alterations, indicating dosage sensitivity. The SCAN domain (residues 75-188) adopts a helical bundle fold enabling selective dimerization with specific ZNF partners, while the C-terminal zinc finger array (residues 289-386) recognizes GC-rich promoter elements. Mechanistically, ZNF232 likely recruits co-repressor complexes (potentially KAP1/TRIM28) to silence target genes involved in cell cycle regulation and differentiation, with paralogue-specific heterodimerization expanding regulatory repertoire.
