## Description
DMXL1 (Dmx like 1, also known as DmX-like protein 1) is a 3027 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q9Y485) with multi-domain architecture characteristic of RAVE complex components that regulate V-ATPase assembly and vesicular trafficking. The protein contains multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680, residues 100-136/158-197/220-268; PF00400, residues 164-196/2921-2956; PS50082, residues 2924-2965; PS50294 circular profile) enabling protein binding activity (GO:0005515), two RAVE complex protein Rav1 C-terminal domains (IPR022033, residues 1114-1394 and 1451-1876; PF12234, residues 1114-1394 and 1451-1876) for V-ATPase regulation, YVTN repeat-like/Quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase superfamily domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 26-270/2630-2828/2858-3012; IPR015943, residues 26-270/2630-2828/2858-3012; IPR036322, residues 45-267/2743-3004) for protein-protein interactions, and belongs to the DmX-like/RAVE complex component family (IPR052208, residues 2-3008). DMXL1 is located on chromosome 5q23.1 and is expressed in many tissue types including several types of eye tissue, associated with ocular phenotypes. The protein is upregulated in cultured cells that overexpress growth factor independence 1B (GFI1B), a transcription factor essential for hematopoiesis. Subcellular localization includes nucleoli, nucleoli fibrillar center, and cytosol.
