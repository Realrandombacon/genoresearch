## Description
P2RX6 (P2X Purinoceptor 6, UniProt O15547) is a 441 amino acid ATP-gated ion channel predominantly expressed in skeletal muscle (31.7 nTPM) and tongue (11.1 nTPM) per Human Protein Atlas. Despite only 12 literature references (partially characterized status), P2RX6 has emerging roles in cancer biology and myopathy. The protein contains canonical P2X receptor domains (IPR001429, IPR003049, PF00864) forming an extracellular ATP-binding domain (aa 68-344) and transmembrane regions (aa 23-376) characteristic of ligand-gated cation channels. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 82.9) consistent with the trimeric P2X receptor architecture. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with other P2X family members (P2RX2: 0.945, P2RX4: 0.921, P2RX1: 0.913, P2RX5: 0.906, P2RX7: 0.852) and NMDA receptor subunits (GRIN1: 0.912, GRIN2A: 0.910, GRIN2C: 0.905, GRIN2D: 0.902), suggesting potential heterotrimeric assembly and neuronal crosstalk. ClinVar documents 353 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations in the 22q11.21 region, though specific P2RX6-associated diseases remain to be fully defined. Recent literature (2019, 105 citations) demonstrates m6A-suppressed P2RX6 activation promotes renal cancer cell migration/invasion through ATP-induced Ca2+ influx modulating ERK1/2 phosphorylation and MMP9 signaling. A 2024 study (6 citations) implicates P2RX6 in myopathy via next-generation sequencing. The gene is p53-regulated and associates with VE-cadherin at endothelial adherens junctions, suggesting roles in vascular integrity and tumor microenvironment.
