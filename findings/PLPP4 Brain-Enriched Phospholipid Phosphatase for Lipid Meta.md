## Description
PLPP4 encodes a 271 aa integral membrane phospholipid phosphatase (UniProt Q5VZY2) belonging to the PAP2 (phosphatidic acid phosphatase type 2) superfamily, critical for lipid metabolism and membrane signaling regulation. The protein contains the canonical PAP2 catalytic domain (IPR000326/PF01569, aa83-226) with five conserved transmembrane regions characteristic of lipid phosphate phosphatases, plus the PAP2_containing_1_like domain (cd03390, aa37-225). AlphaFold prediction shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.1), indicating a well-folded transmembrane enzyme structure with stable catalytic core. HPA shows brain-enhanced expression (2.0 nTPM) with nucleoplasm and nucleoli rim localization, suggesting roles in neuronal lipid metabolism and potentially nuclear lipid signaling. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling with lipid metabolism enzymes: DGAT1 (0.956), LPIN1 (0.947), MOGAT1 (0.932), PLPP2 (0.830), PNPLA3 (0.760), DGAT2 (0.659), AGPAT1 (0.657), placing PLPP4 within the coordinated triacylglycerol and phospholipid biosynthesis network. ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic variants (primarily 10q26 copy number losses/gains), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (20 papers) demonstrates PLPP4 drives oncogenic and adipogenic potential in breast cancer, regulates ferroptosis in glioblastoma, and functions through CAPRIN1 transcriptional activation. PLPP4 likely functions as a diacylglycerol diphosphate phosphatase converting phosphatidic acid to diacylglycerol, regulating lipid second messenger levels (DAG, PA) for membrane signaling, triglyceride synthesis, and ferroptosis sensitivity. The brain-enriched expression suggests specialized roles in neuronal membrane homeostasis, myelination, or synaptic vesicle cycling, while nucleolar localization may indicate nuclear lipid signaling functions.
