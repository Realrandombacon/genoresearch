## Description
CCDC92 encodes a 331 amino acid coiled-coil domain protein (UniProt Q53HC0) localized to centrosome, centriole, and nucleoplasm. InterPro analysis reveals conserved domains: IPR039496 (N-terminal domain, aa 24-78), IPR040370 (CCDC92/74 family, aa 17-290), and PF14916 (coiled-coil DUF). STRING interactions show high-confidence binding (scores 0.81-0.87) to 10 proteasome subunits including PSMD8 (0.875), ADRM1 (0.835), PSMD10 (0.832), PSMC1 (0.832), PSMD14 (0.829), PSMD2 (0.829), PSMD13 (0.825), PSMC6 (0.824), PSMD12 (0.823), and PSMC5 (0.814), suggesting direct proteasome holoenzyme association. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest brain expression (138 nTPM), detected in all tissues, and cell type-enhanced specificity. ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses on 12q24.31-24.33, indicating dosage sensitivity. The coiled-coil structure (aa 17-290) likely mediates oligomerization and proteasome scaffolding. Subcellular localization at centrosome positions CCDC92 to coordinate cell cycle-dependent proteasome activity with antiviral defense responses.
