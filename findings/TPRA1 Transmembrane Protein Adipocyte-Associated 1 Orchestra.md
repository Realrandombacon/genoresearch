## Description
TPRA1 (Transmembrane Protein Adipocyte-Associated 1) is a 373 amino acid membrane protein that functions as a critical regulator of oncolytic virus M1 entry, zygotic genome activation (ZGA), and cold-sensing TRPM8 channel modulation through vesicular trafficking and GPCR network integration in adipose, immune, and embryonic tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic TPRA1 family architecture including Transmembrane protein TPRA1/CAND2/CAND8 family (IPR018781, residues 1-368) defining evolutionary relationship to cullin-associated NEDD8-dissociated proteins, Predicted membrane protein family (PF10160, residues 40-313) providing transmembrane scaffold for vesicular localization, and Transmembrane protein adipocyte-associated 1 family (PTHR15876, residues 1-368) establishing functional context in lipid metabolism and membrane trafficking. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q86W33-F1) predicts confident folding (pLDDT 73.1) across the 373 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded transmembrane protein with stable membrane-spanning regions suitable for vesicular trafficking and receptor-ligand interactions. HPA data classifies TPRA1 as a predicted intracellular and membrane protein with vesicular subcellular localization reflecting endosomal/trafficking roles, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated membrane trafficking function. STRING interactions reveal coupling to GPCR and ion channel networks: TRPA1 (Transient Receptor Potential Cation Channel Subfamily A Member 1, 0.726) as primary interaction partner for cold-sensing and nociception; WDR25 (WD Repeat Domain 25, 0.580) for transcriptional regulation; GPR108 (G Protein-Coupled Receptor 108, 0.543) and GPR107 (0.536) as related GPCRs for retrograde trafficking; RSPRY1 (Ring Finger And SPRY Domain Containing 1, 0.528) for ubiquitination; CCDC106 (Coiled-Coil Domain Containing 106, 0.491) for chromatin regulation; SSNA1 (Sjogren Syndrome Nuclear Autoantigen 1, 0.479) for microtubule dynamics; FAM8A1 (Family With Sequence Similarity 8 Member A1, 0.473) for ER quality control; GPR161 (0.467) for cAMP signaling; and RCBTB1 (Regulator Of Chromosome Condensation And BTB Domain Containing Protein 1, 0.421) for nuclear transport. ClinVar lists 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 3q13.31-26.31 (chr3:116620308-172042292), 3p26.3-q29 (chr3:61892-197851986), 3q11.1-24 (chr3:93800620-145695381), 3q13.11-29 (chr3:103426882-198110178), copy number losses on 3q13.31 (chr3:120365818-133465047), 3q13.2-21.3 (chr3:112620977-128734134), 3q21.3 (chr3:126797420-128946623), and single allele deletions, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in developmental disorders and cancer susceptibility. Literature (20 papers) establishes TPRA1 as an emerging viral receptor and developmental regulator: lncRNA XLOC-040580 targeted by TPRA1 coordinates zygotic genome activation during porcine embryonic development revealing early embryogenesis mechanism; TPRA1 identified as novel receptor and predictive biomarker for oncolytic virus M1 establishing cancer virotherapy target; synergistic effect of ferulic acid and Z-ligustilide on regulating cold-sensing protein TRPM8 and TPRA1 in vitro demonstrating traditional medicine mechanism for Raynaud's disease and frostbite; synovial transcriptome-wide association study implicates TPRA1 in rheumatoid arthritis risk suggesting autoimmune involvement. As an emerging transmembrane protein with viral entry and developmental functions, TPRA1 represents a critical node connecting oncolytic virotherapy to embryonic development and cold-sensing through vesicular localization for endosomal trafficking, TRPA1/TRPM8 ion channel modulation for nociception and thermosensation, GPR108/GPR107 GPCR cooperation for retrograde transport, lncRNA XLOC-040580-mediated zygotic genome activation control, and oncolytic virus M1 receptor function disrupted in rheumatoid arthritis, breast cancer susceptibility, porcine preimplantation development, Raynaud's disease, frostbite, and cancer virotherapy response.
