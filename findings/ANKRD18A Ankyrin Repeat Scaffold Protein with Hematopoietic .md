# ANKRD18A: Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein with Hematopoietic Expression and Clinical Variants

**Date:** 2026-03-23T00:03:31.389319

**Quality Score:** 4.9/10  (E=6.0, D=3.8) [MODERATE]

## Description
ANKRD18A (Ankyrin Repeat Domain-containing protein 18A) is a 992 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein characterized by multiple ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, PF00023) spanning residues 38-198, organized in 3 tandem repeats. The protein contains a large coiled-coil region (PF14915, residues 204-668) suggesting oligomerization capacity, and a C-terminal DUF3496 domain (PF12001, residues 864-968) of unknown function. HPA expression profiling reveals tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in bone marrow (17.6 nTPM) and testis (7.2 nTPM), indicating potential roles in hematopoietic cell differentiation or spermatogenesis. The protein is classified as intracellular with no immune cell detection. ClinVar analysis identified 66 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses on chromosome 9 and splice site mutations (e.g., c.2964+1G>A), demonstrating clinical relevance despite limited disease annotation. The ankyrin repeat architecture (G3DSA:1.25.40.20) suggests protein-protein interaction scaffolding function, potentially organizing signaling complexes in bone marrow progenitor cells. The coiled-coil domain may mediate homodimerization or heterodimerization with partner proteins. Conservation across vertebrates is implied by domain architecture preservation. Functional hypothesis: ANKRD18A serves as a molecular scaffold in hematopoietic cells, organizing protein complexes through ankyrin-mediated interactions, with disruption leading to hematological or developmental phenotypes.
