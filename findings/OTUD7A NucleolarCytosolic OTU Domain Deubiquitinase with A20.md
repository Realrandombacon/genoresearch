## Description
OTUD7A (OTU Domain-Containing Protein 7A, UniProt Q8TE49) is a 926 amino acid nucleolar/cytosolic deubiquitinating enzyme with moderate characterization (54 papers) that functions as an OTU-family cysteine protease regulating cardiac hypertrophy, ferroptosis sensitivity in lung cancer, and neurodevelopmental disorders through specific ubiquitin chain editing. The protein contains a UBA-like domain (IPR060539/PF28552/cd14347, residues 26-75/26-68) for ubiquitin binding, a catalytic OTU domain (IPR003323/IPR051346/PF02338/PS50802/cd22773, residues 199-374/12-685/206-368/174-373) conferring thiol protease activity, and a C-terminal A20-type zinc finger (IPR002653/PF01754/PS51036, residues 884-919/889-912) for substrate recognition or stability, placing it in the OTU deubiquitinase family involved in Ubl conjugation pathways. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with zinc finger proteins and ubiquitin modifiers: ZUP1 (0.843, zinc finger UPF-type protein 1), MTMR10 (0.808, myotubularin-related protein 10, phosphatase), ZNF629 (0.740), ZNF501 (0.740), ZNF44 (0.739), ZNF763 (0.739), ZNF569 (0.739), ZNF436 (0.739), UBC (0.727, ubiquitin C), and CHRFAM7A (0.544, cholinergic receptor nicotinic alpha 7 partial duplication), suggesting roles in transcriptional regulation and ubiquitin signaling. HPA data shows nucleoli and cytosol subcellular localization, tissue-enhanced specificity with top expression in brain (9.8 nTPM), detected in some tissues distribution, low cancer specificity, and involvement in intellectual disability and cancer. ClinVar contains 199 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses at 15q13.2-13.3 (chr15:30094195-32151843, chr15:30913573-32914239, chr15:30935396-32444881), copy number gains at 15q13.2-13.3/15q11.2-13.3, frameshift deletion (c.1146del/p.Glu382fs), and splice variants (c.331+62C>T), strongly associated with 15q13.2 microdeletion syndrome, intellectual disability, epilepsy, and schizophrenia. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 64.8) indicates medium confidence with partially structured architecture typical of multi-domain deubiquitinases containing flexible linkers. Functional hypothesis: OTUD7A acts as a nucleolar/cytosolic deubiquitinase that edits ubiquitin chains on specific substrates to regulate signal transduction, where recent 2023-2025 research demonstrates: (1) OTUD7A accelerates pathological cardiac hypertrophy via TAK1 activation (2025, 1 citation)—OTUD7A removes inhibitory ubiquitin chains from TAK1 (MAP3K7), enhancing its kinase activity and driving hypertrophic gene programs, (2) Suppressing the OTUD7A/KDM5B/GABPA axis enhances cisplatin sensitivity through inducing ferroptosis in KRAS-mutant lung adenocarcinoma (2025)—OTUD7A stabilizes KDM5B histone demethylase, which represses GABPA-mediated transcription of ferroptosis drivers, conferring chemoresistance, (3) OTUD7A-Ankyrin pathway identified as disease mechanism for 15q13.2 microdeletion syndrome (2023)—haploinsufficiency disrupts neuronal development and synaptic function, (4) Genetic counseling challenges for prenatal 15q13.2q13.3 microdeletions encompassing CHRNA7 and OTUD7A with variable penetrance (2025)—OTUD7A loss contributes to neurodevelopmental phenotypes alongside CHRNA7, (5) STRING partners (ZUP1, MTMR10, multiple ZNFs) suggest coordination with chromatin modifiers and phosphatases, with nucleolar localization indicating potential roles in ribosome biogenesis surveillance or stress response, and 199 ClinVar variants at 15q13.2-13.3 confirming critical role in neurodevelopment, making OTUD7A a potential therapeutic target for heart failure, chemoresistant lung cancer, and 15q13.2 microdeletion syndrome through modulation of ubiquitin-dependent signaling.
