Despite being annotated as a pseudogene (OR9H1P), InterPro analysis confirms intact canonical olfactory receptor architecture with IPR000276 (GPCR rhodopsin-like), IPR000725 (Olfactory receptor), IPR017452 (7TM), PF13853 domains, and cd15419 (OR subfamily 9K2-related) spanning residues 23-301.
