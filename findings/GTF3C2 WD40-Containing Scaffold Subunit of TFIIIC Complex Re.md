# GTF3C2: WD40-Containing Scaffold Subunit of TFIIIC Complex Regulating RNA Polymerase III Transcription

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
GTF3C2 (General transcription factor IIIC subunit 2, UniProt Q8WUA4) is a 911-amino acid nuclear protein that serves as an essential scaffold component of the transcription factor TFIIIC complex, which recruits RNA polymerase III to target gene promoters. InterPro analysis reveals multiple WD40 repeat domains (IPR001680, positions 456-644) arranged in a beta-propeller structure, along with a YVTN repeat-like domain (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 370-676) that mediates protein-protein interactions within the multi-subunit TFIIIC holoenzyme. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 72.4) across the WD40 repeat region, consistent with a stable scaffolding role. STRING interaction data confirms GTF3C2's central position in the Pol III machinery, with strongest partnerships to other TFIIIC subunits GTF3C5 (score 0.994), GTF3C4 (0.993), and GTF3C6 (0.966), as well as direct connections to the polymerase catalytic subunit POLR3A (0.862) and POLR3G (0.807). HPA expression profiling shows ubiquitous nucleoplasmic localization with low tissue specificity, consistent with housekeeping functions in tRNA and 5S rRNA synthesis. ClinVar records document 18 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 2p23.3 and missense mutations (p.Pro82Leu, p.Arg318Gln), though specific disease associations remain undefined. Recent literature (18 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic roles: GTF3C2 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma proliferation through the USP21/MEK2/ERK1/2 pathway and is regulated by Sp1 transcription factor. We hypothesize that GTF3C2's WD40 beta-propeller serves as a dynamic interaction hub that coordinates TFIIIC assembly and Pol III recruitment, with dysregulation contributing to both developmental disorders (via CNVs) and cancer progression (via MEK/ERK signaling crosstalk).
