## Description
LOC100506444 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 4q12 (NC_000004.12, positions 53695900-53734357, ~38.5 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000249341 but reported no detectable protein expression across any tissue, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 5 duplications (variant 2579174: 4p16.3-q12, chr4:85624-57073230, ~57 Mb whole-arm; variant 58030: 4q12-13.1, chr4:51899860-59984479, ~8.1 Mb; variant 58029: 4q12-22.3, chr4:51831622-97505618, ~45.7 Mb; variant 58028: 4q12-13.2, chr4:51831622-66991489, ~15.2 Mb; variant 57074: 4p13-q13.1, chr4:44356201-62245882, ~17.9 Mb) and 3 deletions/losses (variant 1098574: 4q12-21.1, chr4:51891814-76009719, ~24.1 Mb; variant 59423: 4q12-13.1, chr4:52639018-59984479, ~7.3 Mb; variant 57309: 4q12, chr4:51870025-55102392, ~3.2 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 4q12 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including KIT (KIT proto-oncogene receptor tyrosine kinase, stem cell factor receptor, hematopoiesis, melanogenesis, gastrointestinal stromal tumors, mastocytosis, piebaldism), KDR (kinase insert domain receptor/VEGFR2, vascular endothelial growth factor receptor 2, angiogenesis, endothelial cell proliferation, diabetic retinopathy, cancer metastasis), PDGFRA (platelet derived growth factor receptor alpha, mesenchymal cell proliferation, gastrointestinal stromal tumors, hypereosinophilic syndrome), ERVMER34-1 (endogenous retrovirus group MER34 envelope, retroviral envelope protein, placental development, immunomodulation), and CLOCK (clock circadian regulator, circadian rhythm transcription factor, sleep disorders, metabolic syndrome, cancer progression). Functional hypothesis: LOC100506444 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 4q12 dosage-sensitive receptor tyrosine kinase region, potentially functioning as a hematopoietic/endothelial cell-specific enhancer coordinating KIT-KDR-PDGFRA expression in progenitor cells (stem cell factor signaling, VEGF angiogenesis, PDGF mesenchymal proliferation) or as a chromatin boundary separating angiogenesis genes (KDR) from hematopoiesis genes (KIT) and circadian genes (CLOCK). The 8 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple receptor tyrosine kinase expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves KIT haploinsufficiency causing piebaldism (melanocyte migration defects, white forelock, vitiligo-like patches), KIT overexpression/activation driving gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GAST, constitutive kinase signaling, imatinib sensitivity), KIT germline mutations causing familial GIST (multiple tumors, mastocytosis), KDR deficiency impairing angiogenesis (vascular development defects, retinopathy), KDR overexpression promoting tumor angiogenesis (metastasis, anti-VEGF therapy targets), PDGFRA mutation causing hypereosinophilic syndrome (eosinophil proliferation, imatinib-sensitive), PDGFRA amplification driving GIST (kinase activation, oncogenesis), ERVMER34-1 dysregulation affecting placental development (pregnancy complications), and CLOCK dysfunction causing circadian disruption (sleep disorders, metabolic syndrome, cancer progression). The 4q12 duplication syndrome may present with GIST predisposition, angiogenesis abnormalities, and hematopoietic defects. The 4q12 deletion syndrome may present with piebaldism, vascular defects, and immune dysregulation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (8 CNVs ranging 3.2-57 Mb) and KIT-KDR-PDGFRA-ERVMER34-1-CLOCK gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific super-enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for receptor tyrosine kinase signaling-hematopoiesis-angiogenesis-circadian coordination.
