## Description
TPRG1L encodes a 272-amino acid tumor protein p63-regulated gene 1-like protein (UniProt Q5T0D9), also known as Mover, a well-folded inositol phosphatase functioning in synaptic vesicle dynamics and lipid droplet association. The protein contains an inositol phosphatase catalytic domain (IPR022158, PF12456, positions 63-168) and hSac2 domain (IPR034753, PS51791, positions 65-238) characteristic of phosphoinositide-metabolizing enzymes that regulate membrane trafficking through lipid second messenger modulation, classified within the TPRG1L family (IPR040242, PTHR31108, positions 1-272). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 70.7, residues 1-272) indicating stable folding consistent with enzymatic function. STRING interaction analysis reveals TPRG1L forms part of a vesicle trafficking and RNA processing network with medium-confidence partners: SYP (0.548, synaptophysin - synaptic vesicle glycoprotein), SRSF8 (0.540, serine/arginine-rich splicing factor 8), SRSF2 (0.520, splicing factor 2), SEC23B (0.508, COPII vesicle coat protein), LIN37 (0.494, DREAM complex component), ARHGEF16 (0.470, Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor), WRAP73 (0.465, centrosomal protein), ABCF1 (0.457, ATP-binding cassette transporter), GPR153 (0.436, G-protein coupled receptor), and HAPLN4 (0.429, hyaluronan and proteoglycan linker). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, vesicle and plasma membrane subcellular localization consistent with synaptic vesicle and trafficking functions. ClinVar contains 90 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses (1p36.33-36.32, 1p36.33-36.23, 1p36.33-36.31, 1p36.33-36.22) and gains (1p36.33-36.32, 1p36.33-35.1), though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With only 9 publications, TPRG1L is genuinely a dark gene: tRF-3013b inhibits gallbladder cancer proliferation by targeting TPRG1L (2022, 25 cit), synaptic vesicle protein Mover/TPRG1L associated with lipid droplets in astrocytes (2022, 6 cit), TPRG1L as novel microRNA-21 target in liver fluke-associated cholangiocarcinoma (2014, 0 cit). The functional hypothesis: TPRG1L/Mover functions as an inositol phosphatase regulating phosphoinositide metabolism at synaptic vesicles and lipid droplets, modulating membrane curvature and vesicle fusion through dephosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol phosphates (PIPs); the hSac2 domain suggests homology to yeast Sac2 phosphatase involved in endosomal trafficking; synaptic vesicle localization and SYP (synaptophysin) interaction indicate roles in neurotransmitter release and presynaptic function; astrocyte lipid droplet association suggests metabolic roles in brain lipid homeostasis, potentially linking neuronal activity with astrocyte lipid storage and release; vesicle/plasma membrane localization and SEC23B interaction implicate TPRG1L in COPII-mediated ER-to-Golgi trafficking; cancer associations (gallbladder, cholangiocarcinoma) through miR-21 and tRF-3013b regulation suggest oncogenic roles potentially through dysregulated phosphoinositide signaling affecting cell proliferation and vesicle-mediated growth factor receptor trafficking; 1p36 CNVs may disrupt TPRG1L dosage, altering synaptic function and lipid metabolism contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders; p63 regulation suggests roles in epithelial development and differentiation parallel to TPRG1.
