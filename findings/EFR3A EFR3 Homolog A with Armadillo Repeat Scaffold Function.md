## Description
EFR3A (EFR3 Homolog A, UniProt Q14156) is an 821 aa plasma membrane peripheral protein belonging to the armadillo repeat superfamily (IPR016024/IPR049152/IPR051851/PF21052/PTHR12444/SSF48371, positions 75-493/48-144/4-821/48-144/4-821/75-346+373-493) that functions as an essential scaffolding component anchoring the PI4KIIIα (PI4KA) lipid kinase complex to the plasma membrane for phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate (PI4P) production and oncogenic KRAS signaling. The molecular architecture consists of multiple armadillo (ARM) repeats forming a superhelical structure characteristic of scaffold proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions at membrane interfaces. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded protein (pLDDT 80.6, high confidence) indicating stable 3D structure with organized armadillo repeat domains—typical architecture for membrane-associated scaffold proteins requiring structural rigidity for complex assembly. Literature (38 papers) establishes EFR3A roles in: (1) plasma membrane localization of PI4KIIIα/PI4KA complex and G-protein coupled receptor responsiveness regulation (2025), (2) oncogenic KRAS dependency on EFR3A-PI4KA signaling axis for potent tumorigenic activity (2021, 40 citations), (3) circular RNA EFR3A promoting nasopharyngeal carcinoma progression via miR-654-3p/EFR3A axis (2023, 4 citations), (4) raft domain organizing protein interacting with flotillins and MPP1 (2023, 7 citations), (5) autism and autism spectrum disorder disease involvement (HPA). STRING interactions reveal PI4KIIIα complex membership and membrane trafficking network: TTC7A (0.900) - tetratricopeptide repeat protein 7A (PI4KIIIα complex subunit); PI4KA (0.869) - phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase alpha (catalytic subunit, direct partner); TTC7B (0.788) - tetratricopeptide repeat protein 7B (PI4KIIIα complex paralog); FAM126A (0.705) - family with sequence similarity 126 member A (PI4KIIIα complex subunit, hyccin); FAM126B (0.668) - family with sequence similarity 126 member B (PI4KIIIα complex paralog); TMEM150A (0.543) - transmembrane protein 150A (membrane protein); SYT6 (0.489) - synaptotagmin-6 (calcium sensor, vesicular trafficking); PI4KB (0.477) - phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase beta (Golgi PI4K, paralog); PPP1R17 (0.474) - protein phosphatase 1 regulatory subunit 17 (phosphatase regulation). HPA shows retina-enriched expression (112.6 nTPM highest), plasma membrane and cytosol localization, tissue-enhanced specificity detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, autism/ASD disease involvement.
