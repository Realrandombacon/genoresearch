## Description
ZNF823 encodes a 610 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt P16415) localized to the nucleus. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (IPR001909/PF01352, residues 3-97) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096) at positions 164-191, 192-219, 220-247, 471-493, and 583-607, characteristic of sequence-specific transcriptional repressors. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and cell type enhanced expression, consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional regulator role. STRING interactions identify 10 partners including fellow KRAB zinc fingers ZNF443 (0.628) and ZNF57 (0.554), scaffold attachment factors SAFB (0.459) and SAFB2 (0.410), transcriptional corepressor SPEN (0.438), and translation initiation factor EIF3G (0.568), suggesting ZNF823 functions within a chromatin-associated transcriptional repression complex. SAFB proteins scaffold nuclear matrix attachment and recruit corepressors, while SPEN mediates Notch signaling repression. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains spanning 19p13.2-13.12, implicating ZNF823 dosage sensitivity in chromosomal instability disorders. The KRAB domain architecture combined with ubiquitous expression and SAFB/SPEN interactions suggests ZNF823 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruiting heterochromatin machinery via scaffold attachment to nuclear matrix, potentially regulating cell cycle, differentiation, or stress response pathways. Disruption via CNVs may alter repression of target genes, contributing to disease phenotypes associated with 19p13.2 rearrangements.
