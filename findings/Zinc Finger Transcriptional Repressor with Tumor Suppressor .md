# Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Tumor Suppressor Activity

**Date:** 2026-03-20T00:39:24.287312

**Quality Score:** 3.15/10  (E=6.0, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF415 encodes a 603 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger protein (UniProt Q09FC8) that functions as a transcriptional repressor. The protein contains three canonical C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 292-370, forming a DNA-binding module characteristic of Krüppel-type zinc fingers. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 58.9), suggesting a partially structured protein with ordered zinc finger domains and intrinsically disordered regions. HPA data indicates nuclear localization with enrichment in nucleoli fibrillar centers and microtubules, consistent with transcriptional regulation function. Expression is ubiquitous across tissues with low tissue specificity. STRING interactions reveal associations with TRIM28 (score 0.518), ZNF610 (0.513), ANHX (0.429), and ZNF347 (0.427), suggesting participation in chromatin remodeling complexes alongside other zinc finger proteins. UniProt annotation indicates ZNF415 suppresses transcriptional activities of AP-1 and p53/TP53, implicating it in cell cycle regulation and stress response pathways. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains on chromosome 19q13, suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential disease association. Conservation analysis shows the C2H2 zinc finger architecture is highly conserved across vertebrates. Mechanistically, ZNF415 likely functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding repressor that recruits co-repressor complexes (potentially via TRIM28 interaction) to silence target genes involved in proliferation and apoptosis, positioning it as a candidate tumor suppressor whose dysregulation through copy number alterations may contribute to oncogenesis.
