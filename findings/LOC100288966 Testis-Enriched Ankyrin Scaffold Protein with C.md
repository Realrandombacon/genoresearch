# LOC100288966: Testis-Enriched Ankyrin Scaffold Protein with Cancer-Testis Antigen Potential

**Date:** 2026-03-22T23:53:14.035614

## Description
LOC100288966 (POTED/POTE ankyrin domain family member D) encodes a 584 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q86YR6) characterized by multiple ankyrin repeat domains and a C-terminal coiled-coil region. InterPro analysis reveals 3+ ankyrin repeat copies (IPR002110, PF12796) spanning residues 143-416, which mediate protein-protein interactions (GO:0005515), plus a CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (PF14915) at residues 470-582 suggesting oligomerization capability. Human Protein Atlas shows testis-enriched expression (2.8 nTPM) with tissue-specific RNA distribution, consistent with POTE family members functioning as cancer-testis antigens. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 68.6) indicates medium-confidence folding with structured ankyrin domains and potentially disordered linkers. ClinVar documents 55 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (gains and losses on chromosome 21), indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is evidenced by orthologs in chimpanzee (566 aa, ~97% length identity) and rat (363 aa). The combination of ankyrin-mediated protein binding, testis-specific expression, coiled-coil oligomerization, and CNV-associated pathogenicity supports a functional hypothesis: POTED acts as a testis-specific protein scaffolding hub that organizes multiprotein complexes during spermatogenesis, with aberrant copy number disrupting stoichiometric complex assembly and contributing to reproductive disorders or cancer susceptibility through dysregulated protein interaction networks.
