## Description
The BTBD19 gene encodes a protein containing a BTB/POZ domain and a BACK domain, suggesting a role in protein-protein interactions and potentially in ubiquitination or transcription regulation. The protein shows medium-confidence interactions with several mitochondrial ribosomal proteins (MRPLs), indicating a possible link to mitochondrial function. Expression data from the Human Protein Atlas shows that BTBD19 is a predicted intracellular protein with low tissue specificity, detected in many tissues and not specifically enriched in immune cells or cancer tissues. The presence of 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar suggests that BTBD19 may be associated with certain diseases, although the specific functions and disease mechanisms remain unclear. Based on these findings, a functional hypothesis for BTBD19 could be that it acts as a scaffold protein involved in regulating mitochondrial function or protein degradation pathways.
