# ZNF540: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Retinal Expression and Clinical CNV Associations

**Date:** 2026-03-23T03:26:22.710864

**Quality Score:** 2.25/10  (E=4.2, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF540 encodes a 660 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 5-77) that recruits the corepressor complex via TRIM28/KAP1, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 187-392 that mediate sequence-specific DNA recognition. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in retina (23.9 nTPM), with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, vesicles, and cytosol. STRING interactions reveal functional associations with FAM78A (0.572), TRIM28 (0.522), RIMS4 (0.505), ALKAL1 (0.479), and MVP (0.465), suggesting roles in chromatin remodeling and vesicular trafficking. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19 copy number variations (gains and losses) affecting the 19q13.12 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity. The KRAB-TRIM28 interaction mechanism suggests ZNF540 recruits heterochromatin-forming complexes to silence target genes through H3K9me3 deposition. Retinal expression combined with clinical CNVs suggests potential roles in retinal development or maintenance, with haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity contributing to 19q chromosomal disorder phenotypes.
