## Description

InterPro domain analysis identifies TMEM86B as a member of the lysoplasmalogenase-like family (IPR012506, positions 18-220; GO:0016020 membrane), belonging to the YhhN family (PF07947, positions 49-216) and GH04784P superfamily (PTHR31885, positions 18-220). These domains define TMEM86B as a membrane-associated hydrolase specializing in ether lipid metabolism, specifically catalyzing the hydrolysis of 2-acyl-lysoplasmalogens (ether-linked lysophospholipids) to produce glycerophosphocholine and free fatty acids. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q8N661-F1) shows exceptionally high global confidence (pLDDT 89.6), indicating a well-folded, stable tertiary structure typical of enzymes with defined catalytic pockets - this places TMEM86B among the most structurally characterized dark genes.
