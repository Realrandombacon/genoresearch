## Description
SNX13 (UniProt Q9Y5W8), also known as Sorting Nexin-13 or ER-endolysosomal interorganelle tethering protein, encodes a massive 968 aa high-confidence membrane-associated protein functioning as a critical regulator of lysosomal cholesterol export, ER-endolysosomal contact site formation, and degradative protein sorting through a multi-domain architecture combining N-terminal Phox-associated domain, central RGS domain, C-terminal PX phosphoinositide-binding domain, and sorting nexin C-terminal module. InterPro analysis reveals sophisticated multi-domain organization: PX domain/Phox homology (IPR001683/IPR036871/IPR037437/cd06873/G3DSA:3.30.1520.10, residues 557-687/570-691/573-693/557-687) mediating phosphatidylinositol binding and endosomal membrane targeting; RGS domain (IPR016137/IPR036305/IPR037896/cd08719/G3DSA:1.10.167.10, residues 373-513/371-517/377-511/377-511/368-516) providing G-protein signaling regulation capability; Phox-associated domain (IPR003114, residues 97-284) contributing to membrane association; Sorting nexin C-terminal domain (IPR013937, residues 803-912) defining family membership; and GO annotations for phosphatidylinositol binding (GO:0035091) and positive regulation confirming molecular function. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 75.9) indicating a well-folded stable protein with ordered domains suitable for precise membrane targeting and protein-protein interactions at organelle contact sites. Functional hypothesis: SNX13 operates through integrated membrane tethering and lipid transport mechanisms: (1) ER-endolysosomal tethering - N-terminal Phox-associated domain (97-284) anchors to ER membrane while PX domain (557-687) binds phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate on endolysosomal surface forming interorganelle bridge; (2) Lysosomal cholesterol export - tethering complex facilitates cholesterol transfer from lysosome to ER via non-vesicular transport pathway identified by CRISPR screen; (3) RGS-mediated signaling - central RGS domain (373-513) potentially regulates G-protein signaling at membrane contact sites coordinating trafficking decisions; (4) Degradative sorting - SNX13-SNX2/SNX27 network (0.580/0.593) directs cargo to lysosome for degradation including ARC repressor in cardiac tissue; (5) Disease mechanism - loss-of-function variants cause cholesterol accumulation in lysosomes disrupting cellular lipid homeostasis leading to neurodevelopmental defects; (6) Conservation - mouse Q6PHS6 (957 aa) with yeast Mdm1 (0.620) indicating deep eukaryotic conservation of tethering function.
