STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with uncharacterized protein status. However, ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains/duplications involving the 20p13-20q13.33 region: variant 2579201 (20p13-11.21 ~23.5 Mb trisomy), variant 155359 (20p13-11.23 ~18.6 Mb trisomy), variant 153731 (20p13-11.1 ~26.2 Mb trisomy), variant 146596 (20p13-q13.33 ~64.2 Mb trisomy), variant 144514 (20p13-q11.1 ~30.1 Mb trisomy), variant 59195 (20p12.2-q12 ~29.5 Mb trisomy), variant 59191 (20p13-11.23 ~19.0 Mb trisomy), variant 57514 (20p13-11.21 ~25.6 Mb trisomy), and variant 57354 (20p13-11.23 ~19.1 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 20p12.1 chromosomal region contains critical developmental, chromatin remodeling, and metabolic-associated genes including MACROD2 (mono-ADP-ribosylhydrolase 2, histone ADP-ribosylation, chromatin regulation, cancer susceptibility), PLCB4 (phospholipase C beta 4, G-protein signaling, calcium mobilization, craniofacial development), JAG2 (jagged canonical Notch ligand 2, Notch signaling, somitogenesis, limb development), and DNMT3B (DNA methyltransferase 3 beta, de novo DNA methylation, immunodeficiency-centromeric instability-facial anomalies syndrome). Functional hypothesis: LOC613266 likely represents a critical 20p12.1 developmental regulatory protein, potentially functioning as a nuclear chromatin modifier or signaling scaffold coordinating MACROD2-PLCB4-JAG2-DNMT3B expression in epigenetic regulation, G-protein coupled calcium signaling, and Notch pathway activation (developing limb bud, craniofacial mesenchyme, somites, neural crest derivatives) or as a non-coding RNA precursor regulating MACROD2 splicing or DNMT3B methylation targeting. The 9 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications 18.6-64.2 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number dysregulates MACROD2-mediated chromatin modification and JAG2-mediated Notch signaling. Disease mechanism involves MACROD2 overexpression causing chromatin dysregulation (histone ADP-ribosylation excess, transcriptional alteration, cancer predisposition), MACROD2 loss causing epigenetic instability (reduced ADP-ribosylation, chromatin compaction defect, genome instability), PLCB4 mutation causing craniofacial defects (phosphoinositide signaling defect, calcium mobilization abnormality, cleft palate, micrognathia), JAG2 overexpression causing Notch pathway hyperactivation (somitogenesis defect, limb anomalies, Alagille-like syndrome), JAG2 loss causing Notch signaling deficiency (impaired cell fate specification, developmental delay), DNMT3B mutation causing ICF syndrome (DNA methylation defect, centromeric instability, immunodeficiency, facial anomalies), and 20p12 duplication syndrome presenting with developmental delay, craniofacial dysmorphism, limb anomalies, and intellectual disability. The 20p12.1 region is associated with developmental-epigenetic-signaling genetic variation. The uncharacterized protein-coding classification combined with ClinVar evidence (9 duplications 18.6-64.2 Mb) and MACROD2-PLCB4-JAG2-DNMT3B neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a nuclear chromatin/signaling protein critical for epigenetic regulation-Notch signaling-craniofacial development coordination.
