FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SSX2B functions as a KRAB domain-containing transcriptional repressor normally restricted to immune-privileged testis where it regulates germ cell-specific gene expression programs, but becomes oncogenic when chromosomal translocation t(X;18)(p11.2;q11.2) fuses SSX2B to SS18 (SYT), creating the SS18-SSX2B fusion oncoprotein driving synovial sarcoma pathogenesis.
