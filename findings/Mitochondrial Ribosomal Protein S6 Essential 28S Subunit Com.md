## Description
MRPS6 encodes a 125 aa mitochondrial ribosomal protein (UniProt P82932) that serves as a structural component of the 28S small subunit of the mitoribosome. InterPro analysis reveals conserved ribosomal protein S6 domains (IPR000529, PF01250, PTHR21011) spanning residues 3-96, with GO terms for rRNA binding and translation. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 92.7), consistent with its role as a stable ribosomal scaffold protein. STRING interactions show high-confidence associations (scores 0.972-0.999) with other mitoribosomal proteins including MRPS5, MRPS7, MRPS9, MRPS10 (small subunit) and MRPL17, MRPL27, MRPL32 (large subunit), indicating integration into the complete mitoribosome complex. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with enrichment in choroid plexus (320.1 nTPM) and kidney (308.2 nTPM), reflecting high metabolic demand tissues. ClinVar reports 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 21 copy number variations affecting the 21q22.11 region. Literature (29 papers) reveals emerging roles beyond canonical translation: (1) host restriction factor against porcine deltacoronavirus (2024), (2) modulation of glucose-stimulated insulin secretion via mitochondrial unfolded protein response (2023), (3) dysregulation in breast cancer affecting tumorigenesis (2021), and (4) rs9982601 polymorphism associated with myocardial infarction susceptibility and mortality. The gene's location on chromosome 21 suggests potential contribution to Down syndrome phenotypes through mitoribosomal dysfunction. Mechanistically, MRPS6 likely facilitates mitochondrial protein synthesis essential for oxidative phosphorylation complex assembly, with defects leading to cardiometabolic disease through impaired ATP production and mitochondrial stress responses.
