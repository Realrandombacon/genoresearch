# DHX30 (DExH-Box Helicase 30): Mitochondrial RNA Helicase Essential for Neurodevelopment, Regulating Mitochondrial Transcription via POLRMT-SSBP1 Complex, mRNA Stability in Neuropathic Pain, and Corpus Callosum Formation through RNA Metabolism and Ribosome Biogenesis

## Description
DHX30 encodes a 1194 aa ATP-dependent RNA helicase (UniProt Q7L2E3), also known as DDX30, that functions as a critical mitochondrial RNA metabolism enzyme regulating transcription, processing, and ribosome biogenesis through DExH-box helicase activity. The protein features a multi-domain DEAH-box helicase architecture: dual P-loop NTP hydrolase domains (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 408-611/634-805) containing the conserved DEXH motif (IPR002464, residues 554-563) for ATP hydrolysis; an N-terminal helicase-associated domain (IPR007502, residues 850-940) for RNA binding; a C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650/cd18791, residues 616-795/654-827) forming the RHA family core; an OB-fold nucleic acid binding domain (IPR011709, residues 1016-1102) for single-stranded RNA recognition; and additional G3DSA domains (3.30.160.20 at residues 52-145/244-335; 1.20.120.1080 at residues 830-932) for protein-protein interactions. STRING interactions confirm mitochondrial RNA metabolism specialization: POLRMT (0.842), mitochondrial RNA polymerase for transcription initiation; SSBP1 (0.728), mitochondrial single-stranded DNA-binding protein stabilizing replication/transcription intermediates; YBX1 (0.694), Y-box binding protein regulating mRNA stability/translation; ILF3 (0.688), interleukin enhancer-binding factor 3 for mRNA processing; NCBP2 (0.660), nuclear cap-binding protein subunit 2; DDX28 (0.581), another mitochondrial DEAD-box helicase; PTCD3 (0.558), pentatricopeptide repeat protein for mitochondrial RNA stabilization; GRSF1 (0.511), G-rich sequence factor 1 regulating mitochondrial RNA granules; FASTKD2 (0.480) and FASTKD3 (0.400), Fas-activated serine/threonine kinase domain proteins controlling mitochondrial RNA processing. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with mitochondrial and cytosolic localization, annotated for ribosome biogenesis, helicase/RNA-binding/hydrolase molecular functions, and intellectual disability disease involvement—consistent with housekeeping mitochondrial RNA metabolism roles with neurodevelopmental specificity. ClinVar lists 117 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Ile609Leu, p.Ala584Thr, p.Gln1038His, p.Val476Met, p.Pro404Arg), nonsense (p.Gln1032Ter), frameshift (p.His621fs), splice site (c.29-1G>T), and copy number gains spanning 3p26.3-14.3 (2.2-52Mb)—these cause Neurodevelopmental Disorder with Severe Motor Impairment and Absent Language (NEDMIAL), corpus callosum dysgenesis, and intellectual disability. AlphaFold structure prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT 81.1), revealing a well-folded multi-domain helicase with proper DEXH catalytic core geometry for ATP-dependent RNA unwinding. Mechanistically, DHX30 functions through multiple integrated modes: (1) As a mitochondrial RNA helicase, DHX30 uses ATP hydrolysis via the DEXH motif to unwind RNA secondary structures, facilitating POLRMT-mediated transcription elongation and mitochondrial ribosome assembly; (2) Mitochondrial transcription regulation—interaction with POLRMT-SSBP1 complex positions DHX30 at transcription sites to resolve RNA-DNA hybrids (R-loops) and prevent transcription-replication conflicts; (3) Neurodevelopmental specificity—despite ubiquitous expression, DHX30 deficiency causes severe neurological phenotypes (NEDMIAL, absent language, motor impairment, corpus callosum agenesis), suggesting neurons have heightened sensitivity to mitochondrial RNA metabolism defects due to high energy demands; (4) Neuropathic pain modulation—recent studies (2024) show LncRNA Anxa10-203 recruits DHX30 to stabilize Mc1r mRNA in trigeminal ganglion, linking DHX30 to pain signaling pathways; (5) Mitochondrial RNA granule organization—interactions with GRSF1, FASTKD2/3 suggest roles in organizing mitochondrial RNA processing factories; (6) Nuclear functions—YBX1, ILF3, NCBP2 interactions indicate potential nuclear roles in mRNA stability and cap-dependent translation. Disruption of DHX30 would cause defective mitochondrial transcription, impaired oxidative phosphorylation, R-loop accumulation, neuronal energy failure, and neurodevelopmental defects—phenotypes directly observed in NEDMIAL patients with DHX30 mutations.
