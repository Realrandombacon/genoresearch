## Description
CWC15 (CWC15 Spliceosome Associated Protein Homolog) is a 229 aa nuclear protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 27 literature references, recently implicated in spliceosome function, transcription elongation, and male infertility. Multi-omics data reveals CWC15 as a well-folded Prp19C/NTC (NineTeen Complex) subunit with conserved roles across eukaryotes. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the Cwf15/Cwc15 pre-mRNA splicing factor family (IPR006973/PF04889, aa 1-229) with GO terms for mRNA splicing via spliceosome (GO:0000398) and spliceosomal complex localization (GO:0005681). The PTHR12718 family assignment places CWC15 within cell cycle control proteins, consistent with Prp19C roles in genome stability. AlphaFold predicts confident structure (pLDDT 74.9), indicating a stable globular fold typical of spliceosome scaffolding proteins. HPA shows low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, dual subcellular localization to nuclear speckles (canonical spliceosome compartments) and mitochondria (unexpected, suggesting potential mitochondrial RNA processing roles), and classification as an essential protein involved in mRNA processing and splicing. STRING interactions reveal exclusive high-confidence associations with Prp19C/NTC core subunits and spliceosome components: PRPF19 (0.999, Prp19 ubiquitin ligase complex scaffold), CDC40 (0.999, Prp19C subunit), CDC5L (0.999, cell division cycle 5-like, Prp19C core), CRNKL1 (0.999, crooked neck-like 1, NTC-associated), BUD31 (0.999, Prp19C subunit), EFTUD2 (0.999, elongation factor Tu GTP-binding domain 2, U5 snRNP component), BCAS2 (0.998, Prp19C-associated), RBM22 (0.953, RNA-binding motif 22, spliceosomal), SNRPA1 (0.901, small nuclear ribonucleoprotein A1), CTNNBL1 (0.860, beta-catenin-like 1, spliceosome-associated). ClinVar lists 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.560+59G>A, c.560+61G>A), intronic deletions (c.442-19_442-18del), and large 11q14.1-22.1/11q14.1-22.3 copy number losses (chr11:81041448-98151664x1, chr11:81478509-104667040x1, chr11:80318996-96116221x1, chr11:77855209-98002445x1) encompassing the CWC15 locus. Recent literature (2020-2025) identifies CWC15 as: (1) A fertility-associated gene where mutations negatively impact embryonic development (2025, infertility investigation); (2) A miRNA biogenesis promoter in Arabidopsis via microprocessor modulation (2024, 9 citations); (3) A TREX complex occupancy and transcription elongation factor functioning with Syf2/Prp19C (2024, 5 citations; 2023); (4) An integral spliceosomal component (2020). The scarcity of human mechanistic literature (27 papers, mostly yeast/Arabidopsis studies) despite clear spliceosome function and disease associations makes CWC15 a high-priority dark gene for RNA biology.
