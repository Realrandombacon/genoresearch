## Description
NAP1L4 encodes a 375 aa nucleosome assembly protein 1-like 4 (UniProt Q99733) located at 11p15.5, functioning as a chromatin chaperone with emerging roles in cancer and cardiovascular disease. InterPro analysis confirms nucleosome assembly protein family membership (IPR002164/PF00956/PTHR11875, aa 46-348) with conserved NAP-like superfamily domain (IPR037231/SSF143113, aa 42-344) and CATH structural domains (1.20.5.1500, 3.30.1120.90). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.2), indicating a well-folded protein with stable 3D conformation. STRING interactions reveal a chromatin network: NAP1L1 (0.927), H2BC21 (0.759), H2AC8 (0.581), H2AZ1 (0.407) - all histone chaperones and core histones; MAGED2 (0.801), DGKZ (0.727), PHLDA2 (0.546) - imprinted gene regulators; and PES1 (0.460), NPM1 (0.459) - ribosome biogenesis factors. ClinVar reports 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 11p15.5-15.4 copy number gains (x3), suggesting clinical significance in Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome region disorders. HPA shows no data, but literature (44 papers) indicates testis-enriched expression and roles in: (1) Pulmonary hypertension - circNAP1L4 regulating PASMC proliferation via NAP1L4-mediated super-enhancer-driven glycolysis gene HKII (8 citations, 2024); (2) Hepatocellular carcinoma - NAP1L4 progression and treatment impact (0 citations, 2025); (3) Alphavirus infection - NAP1L1/NAP1L4 binding to Chikungunya virus nsP3 protein requiring phosphorylation (17 citations, 2021); (4) Myeloid neoplasms - NAP1L4/NUTM1 fusion from t(11;15) with PDGFRA rearrangement (24 citations, 2020). Functional hypothesis: NAP1L4 likely functions as a chromatin chaperone regulating nucleosome dynamics, with tissue-specific roles in testis development and disease contexts. Mechanistically, it may: (1) Facilitate histone H2A-H2B dimer deposition/eviction; (2) Regulate super-enhancer activity driving glycolytic genes in PASMCs; (3) Serve as fusion partner in myeloid neoplasms activating PDGFRA signaling; (4) Participate in viral replication complex assembly. The 11p15.5 location within an imprinted region and interactions with MAGED2/PHLDA2 suggest roles in epigenetic regulation of growth and development. Despite 44 papers, NAP1L4 remains partially characterized with unexplored mechanisms in chromatin remodeling, cancer metabolism, and viral pathogenesis.
