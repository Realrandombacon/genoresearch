# RBM28: Nucleolar RNA-Binding Protein Linking Ribosome Biogenesis to p53 Tumor Suppression

## Description
RBM28 (RNA-binding protein 28, UniProt Q9NW13) is a 759 aa nucleolar protein containing four canonical RNA recognition motif (RRM) domains (IPR000504) at positions 5-81, 115-190, 335-417, and 487-581. AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 70.2). HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleolar localization. STRING reveals 10 high-confidence interactions (scores 0.973-0.990) with nucleolar ribosome biogenesis factors including NOP2 (0.990), NIFK (0.988), BMS1 (0.986), DDX27 (0.986), and NOC2L (0.973). ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants associated with ANE syndrome (alopecia-neurological defects-endocrinopathy), hypotrichosis, and intellectual disability. Literature (23 papers) reveals dual functionality: (1) essential 60S ribosomal subunit assembly factor, and (2) negative regulator of p53 tumor suppressor that translocates from nucleolus to nucleoplasm under stress. USP36-mediated deubiquitination stabilizes RBM28, promoting p53 suppression in colorectal cancer.
