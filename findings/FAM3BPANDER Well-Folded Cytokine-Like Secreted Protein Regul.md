## Description
FAM3B (FAM3 metabolism regulating signaling molecule B, alias PANDER, C21orf11) encodes a 235-amino acid secreted cytokine-like protein with a well-defined three-dimensional structure (AlphaFold pLDDT=93.1, very high confidence). The protein belongs to the FAM3 family (IPR039220) and contains an ILEI/PANDER domain (IPR039477/PF15711, residues 107-195) characteristic of interleukin-like epithelial-mesenchymal transition inducers. HPA RNA-seq shows tissue-enhanced expression with exceptionally high levels in pancreas (191.7 nTPM), intestine (134.3 nTPM), salivary gland (112.4 nTPM), and stomach (108.3 nTPM), consistent with its established role in pancreatic beta-cell function and insulin secretion regulation. The protein is classified as secreted and localized to vesicles, supporting its function as an extracellular signaling molecule. STRING interactions include CWC15 (0.862, spliceosome complex), FUT3 (0.693, fucosyltransferase), RPGR (0.693, retinal degeneration protein), and DNAJC28 (0.662, co-chaperone), suggesting potential roles in protein processing and secretion pathways. ClinVar documents 75 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses on chromosome 21q, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is strong: mouse ortholog (Q9D309, 235 aa, ~85% identity) confirms functional constraint. The high structural confidence (pLDDT 93.1) distinguishes FAM3B from intrinsically disordered dark genes, indicating a stable globular fold suitable for receptor binding. Mechanistically, FAM3B/PANDER likely functions as a beta-cell-derived cytokine that binds to yet-unidentified cell surface receptors, triggering apoptotic signaling in insulin-producing cells and contributing to glucose homeostasis regulation.
