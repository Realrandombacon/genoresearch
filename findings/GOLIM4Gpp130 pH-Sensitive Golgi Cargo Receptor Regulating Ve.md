## Description
GOLIM4 (Golgi Integral Membrane Protein 4, also known as Gpp130, O00461) encodes a 696 aa type II transmembrane protein functioning as a pH-sensitive cargo receptor that cycles between the Golgi apparatus and endosomes, regulating vesicular transport and retrograde trafficking. Structural architecture reveals a Golgi integral membrane protein family domain (IPR042336/PTHR22909, residues 1-696) with extensive C-terminal coiled-coil region (PF30464, residues 205-696) mediating oligomerization and protein-protein interactions characteristic of Golgi tethering factors. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 67.6) reveals partially structured architecture with ordered transmembrane/luminal domains and flexible coiled-coil regions enabling conformational changes during cargo binding/release. STRING interactions confirm Golgi trafficking machinery network with syntaxin STX5 (0.847, SNARE component for Golgi fusion), conserved oligomeric Golgi complex subunits COG4/COG8/COG1/COG2/COG7/COG3 (0.744-0.466, retrograde trafficking regulators), golgin GOLGA5/GM130 (0.630, Golgi matrix protein), GOSR1 (0.450, Golgi SNARE), and GOLPH3 (0.408, Golgi phosphoprotein 3). HPA localizes GOLIM4 to the Golgi apparatus with ubiquitous expression across all tissues, classified as membrane protein involved in transport; immune cell-enhanced expression pattern. ClinVar documents 22 pathogenic variants including 3q22-29 CNVs and splice site mutations (c.313-9G>A). Literature (24 papers) demonstrates emerging oncogenic roles: monensin induces Golgi pH-dependent GOLIM4 exocytosis to suppress EMT-driven cancer motility (2025), miR-202-5p targets GOLIM4/PI3K/AKT axis to alleviate renal ischemia-reperfusion injury (2025), IRE1-dependent expression controls protein secretion modulating glioblastoma adhesion/migration (2024), RBFOX2-mediated splicing activates vesicular transport promoting nasopharyngeal carcinoma metastasis (25x citations, 2021). Mechanistic hypothesis: GOLIM4 binds manganese and cargo proteins in neutral-pH Golgi via luminal domain; upon endosomal acidification, conformational change triggers cargo release; COG complex recognizes GOLIM4 cytoplasmic tail for retrograde return to Golgi; in cancer, GOLIM4 overexpression enhances secretion of metalloproteinases/cytokines promoting EMT and metastasis; monensin (ionophore) disrupts Golgi pH gradient, causing premature GOLIM4 exocytosis and depletion from Golgi, blocking metastatic cargo secretion.
