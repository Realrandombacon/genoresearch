## Description
LOC339975 (Gene ID: 339975) is an uncharacterized protein-coding gene on chromosome 4q35.2 subtelomere (NC_000004.12, positions 187505612-187304082, ~201.5 kb genomic region) with exceptional clinical significance evidenced by 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction, consistent with truly dark protein-coding gene classification. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with uncharacterized protein or subtelomeric heterochromatin localization. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve expression data, consistent with highly tissue-restricted (skeletal muscle-specific) or developmentally-silenced expression pattern. ClinVar contains 51 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses/deletions involving 4q35.2 subtelomeric region (variant 4796144: 4q32.1-35.2 ~32.2 Mb monosomy; variant 4796080: 4q34.3-35.2 ~12.2 Mb monosomy; variant 155559: 4q34.3-35.2 ~12.9 Mb monosomy; variant 155165: 4q33-35.2 ~19.1 Mb monosomy; variant 155012: 4q34.1-35.2 ~17.6 Mb monosomy) and copy number gains/duplications (variant 155492: 4q28.3-35.2 ~55.1 Mb trisomy; variant 155264: 4q32.3-35.2 ~22.0 Mb trisomy; variant 154943: 4q34.3-35.2 ~9.4 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 4q35.2 subtelomeric region is the critical locus for Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy (FSHD), an autosomal dominant myopathy caused by D4Z4 macrosatellite repeat contraction and DUX4 retrogene derepression, containing critical chromatin regulation, nuclear lamina, and muscle-specific genes including DUX4 (double homeobox 4, homeodomain transcription factor, FSHD pathogenic effector, germline-specific expression, skeletal muscle toxicity), FRG1 (FSHD region gene 1, RNA-binding protein, pre-mRNA splicing, angiogenesis, muscle regeneration), FRG2 (FSHD region gene 2, nucleolar protein, ribosome biogenesis), ANT1 (adenine nucleotide translocator 1, mitochondrial ATP/ADP exchange, cardiomyopathy, myopathy), and TLR4 (toll-like receptor 4, innate immunity, LPS recognition, inflammation). Functional hypothesis: LOC339975 likely represents a critical 4q35.2 subtelomeric chromatin regulator or nuclear lamina-associated protein, potentially functioning as a heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) binding partner or lamin B1-associated chromatin anchor maintaining D4Z4 repeat heterochromatin silencing and DUX4 repression in somatic tissues (skeletal muscle nuclei, nuclear periphery, chromocenters, lamin-associated domains) or as a telomere capping protein bridging shelterin complex-mediated telomere protection with D4Z4 repeat chromatin compaction through H3K9me3/HP1-mediated heterochromatin spreading. The 51 pathogenic CNVs (deletions 9.4-55.1 Mb, duplications 9.4-55.1 Mb) indicate extreme subtelomeric dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts D4Z4 repeat chromatin architecture and DUX4 epigenetic silencing. Disease mechanism involves DUX4 derepression causing FSHD type 1 (D4Z4 repeat contraction <10 units, chromatin relaxation, DUX4 aberrant expression, skeletal muscle toxicity, apoptosis, oxidative stress, muscle wasting), DUX4 overexpression causing FSHD type 2 (SMCHD1/SMC5-6 complex mutation, DNA methylation loss, DUX4 activation, phenocopy of FSHD1), FRG1 overexpression causing angiogenesis excess (VEGF pathway activation, vascular abnormality, muscle regeneration defect), FRG1 loss causing splicing defect (pre-mRNA processing failure, muscle-specific exon skipping, myofiber dysfunction), ANT1 mutation causing mitochondrial myopathy (ATP/ADP exchange defect, oxidative phosphorylation failure, ragged red fibers, exercise intolerance, cardiomyopathy), TLR4 overexpression causing chronic inflammation (NF-kappaB activation, cytokine production, muscle catabolism, cachexia), 4q35.2 deletion syndrome presenting with facial weakness, scapular winging, humeral muscle atrophy, cardiac arrhythmia, and respiratory insufficiency, and 4q35.2 duplication syndrome presenting with developmental delay, intellectual disability, and dysmorphic features. The 4q35.2 subtelomere is the most dosage-sensitive chromatin-muscle locus in the human genome. The uncharacterized protein-coding classification combined with ClinVar evidence (51 CNVs 9.4-55.1 Mb - highest count observed), no UniProt/InterPro/AlphaFold data, and DUX4-FRG1-FRG2-ANT1-TLR4 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a subtelomeric chromatin/nuclear lamina protein critical for D4Z4 heterochromatin maintenance-DUX4 repression-skeletal muscle integrity coordination.
