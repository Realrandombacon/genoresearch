## Description
TMEM134 encodes a 195 amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt: Q9H6X4) localized to the cytosol and perinuclear region with ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity). InterPro analysis reveals membership in the TMEM134/230 family (IPR008590 at positions 57-193; IPR039714 spanning full length 1-195; PF05915 at 57-193; PTHR13558), indicating a conserved transmembrane domain architecture. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 58.7, medium confidence) suggests a partially structured protein with both ordered transmembrane regions and potentially disordered cytosolic domains, consistent with proteins involved in dynamic membrane interactions. STRING network identifies 10 interaction partners including IP6K1 (score 0.584, inositol hexakisphosphate kinase regulating inositol phosphate signaling), UBXN4 (0.558, ubiquitin regulatory protein), FAM185A (0.499), PPP1R26 (0.489, protein phosphatase regulator), PTRH1 (0.484, peptidyl-tRNA hydrolase), TOR2A (0.451, torsin-2A involved in ER/nuclear envelope dynamics), WLS (0.450, Wnt ligand secretion mediator essential for Wnt trafficking), GPR152 (0.442, G-protein coupled receptor), VPS13C (0.427, vacuolar protein sorting homolog implicated in Parkinson's disease and lipid transfer at membrane contact sites), and RNF170 (0.425, E3 ubiquitin ligase involved in ER-associated degradation). The convergence of interactions with WLS (Wnt secretion), VPS13C (vesicular trafficking/lipid transfer), TOR2A (ER dynamics), and RNF170 (ERAD) strongly suggests TMEM134 functions in endoplasmic reticulum-to-Golgi vesicular trafficking pathways, potentially facilitating cargo sorting or membrane protein quality control. HPA classification as transporter involved in host-virus interaction aligns with this trafficking role. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains and losses spanning 11q12-13 regions (e.g., chr11:65741431-67705669 deletion, chr11:65138976-67574402 duplication), indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is complete across mammals: mouse Q8R0J4 (195 aa, ~100% identity), cattle Q05B54 (195 aa). The mechanistic hypothesis proposes TMEM134 acts as a cytosolic adaptor or scaffold at ER/Golgi membranes, coordinating vesicle budding or cargo selection through interactions with ubiquitin machinery (UBXN4, RNF170) and lipid transfer proteins (VPS13C), with disruption potentially affecting Wnt signaling and contributing to chromosomal syndrome phenotypes.
