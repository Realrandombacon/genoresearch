## Description
ZNF625 encodes a 306-amino acid Krüppel C2H2-type zinc finger protein (UniProt Q96I27) with three tandem C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096 positions 97-119, 153-175, 209-231; IPR013087) that confer sequence-specific DNA binding to GC-rich promoter elements. The AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 76.0) shows a well-folded zinc finger array typical of transcriptional repressors. HPA data reveals retina-enriched expression (8.2 nTPM) with cancer-enhanced patterns, suggesting tissue-specific regulatory roles. STRING interactions identify TRIM28 (0.522 score) as a key partner—TRIM28 recruits SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 for heterochromatin formation, implying ZNF625 functions as a sequence-specific repressor recruiting chromatin silencing machinery to target loci. Additional partners SYNJ2BP-COX16 (0.511) and ST20-MTHFS (0.447) suggest potential mitochondrial or metabolic regulatory connections. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic copy number variants (deletions/amplifications at 19p13.2), indicating dosage sensitivity—haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupts transcriptional programs, potentially affecting retinal development or tumor suppression. The nuclear-mitochondrial dual localization suggests ZNF625 may coordinate nuclear transcription with mitochondrial function, a mechanism observed in metabolic stress responses. Conservation data needed for cross-species validation.
