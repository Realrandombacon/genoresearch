## Description
TBC1D10A (TBC1 Domain Family Member 10A, also known as TBC1D10A or EPI64A) is a 508 aa Rab GTPase-activating protein representing a partially characterized gene with 15 literature references, functioning as a key regulator of exosome secretion and endocytic vesicle trafficking through Rab35 inactivation. Multi-omics data reveals TBC1D10A as a conserved Rab-GAP with well-defined domain architecture and interaction network, yet with limited human disease characterization despite clear clinical significance.
