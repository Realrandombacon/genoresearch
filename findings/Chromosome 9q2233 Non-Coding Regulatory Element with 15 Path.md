# Chromosome 9q22.33 Non-Coding Regulatory Element with 15 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants in FOXE1-TGFBR1-XPA Thyroid Development-DNA Repair Locus

## Description
LOC286359 (Gene ID: 286359) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 9q22.33 (NC_000009.12, positions 97396690-97390835, ~6 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 8 copy number gains/duplications (variant 160862: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 155409: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 153518: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 151006: 9q21.12-31.3 ~42 Mb; variant 150340: 9p11.2-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 150050: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 149828: 9p24.3-q34.3 ~138 Mb; variant 147624: 9q21.11-31.2 ~38 Mb) and 2 copy number losses/deletions (variant 145977: 9q22.32-22.33 ~3.4 Mb; variant 144971: 9q22.32-31.3 ~13.6 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 9q22.33 chromosomal region contains critical thyroid development, TGF-beta signaling, DNA repair, and mRNA processing-associated genes including FOXE1 (forkhead box E1, thyroid transcription factor, thyroid development, thyroid hormone synthesis, Bamforth-Lazarus syndrome, thyroid cancer), TGFBR1 (transforming growth factor beta receptor 1, serine/threonine kinase, TGF-beta signaling, cell growth inhibition, Loeys-Dietz syndrome, cancer metastasis), XPA (XPA nucleotide excision repair protein, DNA damage recognition, nucleotide excision repair, xeroderma pigmentosum group A, UV sensitivity, skin cancer), NCBP1 (nuclear cap binding protein subunit 1, mRNA 5 cap binding, pre-mRNA splicing, nuclear export, translation initiation), and PTCH1 (patched 1, sonic hedgehog receptor, basal cell carcinoma, Gorlin syndrome). Functional hypothesis: LOC286359 likely represents a critical 9q22.33 enhancer element within the FOXE1-TGFBR1 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a thyroid-specific enhancer coordinating FOXE1-TGFBR1-XPA expression in thyroid follicular cells or as a chromatin boundary separating thyroid development genes (FOXE1) from TGF-beta signaling genes (TGFBR1) and DNA repair genes (XPA). The 15 pathogenic CNVs (mix of duplications and deletions, 3.4-138 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts FOXE1-mediated thyroid transcription and TGFBR1-mediated growth suppression. Disease mechanism involves FOXE1 mutation causing Bamforth-Lazarus syndrome (thyroid agenesis, cleft palate, spiky hair, hypothyroidism), FOXE1 polymorphism causing thyroid cancer (papillary thyroid carcinoma susceptibility), TGFBR1 mutation causing Loeys-Dietz syndrome (aortic aneurysm, arterial tortuosity, skeletal abnormalities), TGFBR1 overexpression causing cancer metastasis (EMT promotion, invasion, immunosuppression), XPA mutation causing xeroderma pigmentosum (nucleotide excision repair defect, UV sensitivity, skin cancer, neurodegeneration), NCBP1 dysfunction causing mRNA processing defects (splicing impairment, nuclear export defect, translation inhibition), and 9q22.33 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with thyroid abnormalities and cancer predisposition. The 9q22.33 region is a well-established basal cell nevus syndrome locus with PTCH1 germline mutations. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (15 CNVs 3.4-138 Mb) and FOXE1-TGFBR1-XPA-NCBP1-PTCH1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a thyroid/epithelial-specific enhancer critical for thyroid development-TGF-beta signaling-DNA repair coordination.
