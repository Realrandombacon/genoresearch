# AIMP2: Multifunctional Scaffold Linking tRNA Synthetase Assembly to Parthanatos and Leukodystrophy

## Description
AIMP2 (UniProt Q13155) encodes a 320-amino acid non-enzymatic scaffold protein essential for the assembly and stability of the multi-tRNA synthetase complex (MSC). InterPro analysis reveals a modular architecture: an N-terminal lysyl-tRNA synthetase binding domain (PF16780/IRP031889, aa 1-44), a central thioredoxin-like domain (PF18569/IPR041503, aa 118-208), and a C-terminal glutathione S-transferase (GST) domain (PF00043/IPR004046, aa 245-308). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT: 81.1) consistent with these distinct functional modules.\n\nSTRING interactions confirm its role as the MSC hub, showing exclusive high-confidence links (scores 0.994-0.999) to aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (KARS1, MARS1, DARS1, QARS1, RARS1, LARS1, IARS1) and complex partners AIMP1 and EEF1E1. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with extreme enrichment in skeletal muscle (151.1 nTPM) and cytosolic localization.\n\nCritically, AIMP2 has a well-defined 'dark' mechanism outside translation: upon genotoxic stress, it translocates to the nucleus to bind and hyperactivate PARP-1, triggering 'parthanatos' (PARP-1 dependent cell death). This pathway is implicated in Parkinson's disease (2024 literature). Furthermore, biallelic loss-of-function mutations cause Hypomyelinating Leukodystrophy Type 17 (HLD17), supported by 68 pathogenic ClinVar variants including deletions and missense mutations (e.g., c.157C>T). Recent studies also link AIMP2 to ovarian cancer progression via MTCH2-mediated metabolic regulation. Thus, AIMP2 is a paradigmatic moonlighting protein: a cytosolic translation scaffold that doubles as a nuclear death executor and a critical factor in myelin maintenance.
