## Description
RNASEK (Ribonuclease Kappa, UniProt Q6P5S7) is a 137 amino acid intracellular and membrane-associated protein with limited characterization (23 papers) that functions as an essential subunit of the V-type proton ATPase (V-ATPase) complex required for lysosomal acidification, lysosomal hydrolase delivery, autophagosome degradation, and early embryonic development, with dual RNA endonuclease activity that facilitates viral entry through clathrin-mediated endocytosis. The protein contains a conserved Ribonuclease kappa domain (IPR026770/PTHR31733 residues 42-133) with RNA endonuclease activity (GO:0004521) involved in nucleic acid metabolic processes (GO:0090304), and a V-type proton ATPase subunit f-like domain (PF23489 residues 48-131) confirming its role as a V-ATPase complex component.
