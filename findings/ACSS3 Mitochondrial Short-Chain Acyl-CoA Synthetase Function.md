## Description
ACSS3 (Acyl-CoA synthetase short-chain family member 3, Q9H6R3), also known as mitochondrial short-chain fatty acid ligase, encodes a 686 aa enzyme functioning as a critical activator of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) such as propionate and butyrate into their corresponding acyl-CoA thioesters within the mitochondrial matrix, driving lipid metabolism, gluconeogenesis, and the TCA cycle anaplerosis, with emerging roles in suppressing p53-mediated ferroptosis in non-small cell lung cancer, regulating epithelial metabolic homeostasis to alleviate pulmonary fibrosis, modulating glycolysis via Wnt/beta-catenin signaling in keloids, and altering glucose control/fatty acid metabolism in lean mice upon hepatic overexpression.
