# ANKRD6: Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein Orchestrating Wnt/Planar Cell Polarity Signaling Through Dishevelled-PRICKLE-VANGL Complex Assembly

## Description
ANKRD6 (Gene ID: 55762, UniProt Q9Y2G4) is a 727 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein located on chromosome 6q15 (NC_000006.12). This gene encodes a predicted vesicle-associated protein rich in ankyrin repeat domains that function as protein-protein interaction modules. InterPro domain analysis reveals extensive ankyrin repeat architecture defining ANKRD6 as a member of the Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box (ASB) family (PTHR24126, positions 76-340, ~265 residues): Ankyrin repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR036770, positions 2-262, ~261 residues), Ankyrin repeat (IPR002110, positions 9-139/138-205/206-238, multiple copies), Ankyrin repeat Pfam domains including PF00023 (positions 207-238, ~32 residues), PF12796 three-copy ankyrin repeats (positions 65-137/138-201, ~145 residues total), PF13637 many-copy ankyrin repeats (positions 14-62, ~49 residues)—indicating this protein contains approximately 8-10 ankyrin repeats organized in clusters suitable for multi-protein complex assembly. Additional domain signatures include G3DSA:1.25.40.20 ankyrin repeat-containing domain (positions 2-111/112-159/160-209), SM00248 ankyrin repeats (positions 9-38/41-70/74-103), PS50088/PS50297 ankyrin repeat profiles (positions 41-73/74-106/107-139), and PR01415 PRINTS ankyrin repeat motif (positions 42-57/222-236). The ASB family membership suggests potential E3 ubiquitin ligase function through SOCS box-mediated recruitment of elongin B/C-cullin complexes, though the SOCS box itself requires experimental validation. STRING interaction network reveals REMARKABLE specificity—all 10 interaction partners are core components of Wnt signaling and Planar Cell Polarity (PCP) pathways with HIGH confidence scores: DVL1 (0.974 highest confidence, dishevelled segment polarity protein 1, central Wnt signal transducer), CSNK1E (0.891 high confidence, casein kinase 1 epsilon, phosphorylates DVL to regulate Wnt signaling), GSK3B (0.881 high confidence, glycogen synthase kinase 3 beta, destruction complex component regulating β-catenin), PRICKLE4 (0.855 high confidence, prickle planar cell polarity protein 4), PRICKLE2 (0.854 high confidence, prickle planar cell polarity protein 2), PRICKLE3 (0.852 high confidence, prickle planar cell polarity protein 3), PRICKLE1 (0.845 high confidence, prickle planar cell polarity protein 1), VANGL2 (0.781 high confidence, vang-like protein 2, core PCP transmembrane protein), VANGL1 (0.773 high confidence, vang-like protein 1, core PCP transmembrane protein), and AXIN1 (0.412 medium confidence, axis inhibition protein 1, β-catenin destruction complex scaffold). This interaction profile is exceptionally coherent—ANKRD6 exclusively interacts with Wnt/PCP machinery, strongly indicating dedicated pathway function. Human Protein Atlas classifies ANKRD6 (ENSG00000135299) as a predicted intracellular protein with VESICLE subcellular localization—consistent with roles in Wnt ligand secretion, Frizzled receptor trafficking, or PCP component membrane delivery. RNA expression shows tissue-enhanced pattern with detection in ALL tissues, top expression in BRAIN (25.8 nTPM), low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity. The 25.8 nTPM brain expression indicates significant neural function, consistent with Wnt/PCP roles in neural tube closure, axon guidance, and neuronal migration. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9Y2G4-F1) yields HIGH-confidence model (global pLDDT 70.2), indicating a well-folded stable protein across all 727 residues—the high confidence score supports genuine structured scaffold protein with organized ankyrin repeat solenoid structure suitable for protein-protein interactions. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including both copy number variations spanning chromosome 6q regions (chr6:88018122-94565168x1 loss, chr6:85988428-120548687 loss, chr6:69938252-94379210 gain, chr6:81261418-97796269x3 gain, chr6:156975-170919482 gain/x3 gain, chr6:75926199-92710793x1 loss) and specific single nucleotide variants (NM_001242809.2:c.1486-3A>T splice region, NM_001242809.2:c.1372-22C>G intronic); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ANKRD6, but the 6q15 location and variant count suggest dosage sensitivity within chromosome 6q deletion/duplication syndromes affecting neurodevelopment. Conservation evidenced by orthologs: Mus musculus Ankrd6 (Q69ZU8, 712 aa, 98% length, strong conservation)—indicating functional constraint across mammals. Functional hypothesis: ANKRD6 functions as a Wnt/PCP pathway scaffold protein organizing multi-protein complexes through ankyrin repeat-mediated interactions, potentially regulating pathway activity through ubiquitin-dependent mechanisms. The exclusive STRING interactions with DVL1 (0.974), all four PRICKLE paralogs (0.845-0.855), both VANGL paralogs (0.773-0.781), CSNK1E (0.891), GSK3B (0.881), and AXIN1 (0.412) place ANKRD6 at the heart of PCP signaling. DVL1 interaction (highest score) is particularly informative—Dishevelled is the central hub receiving Wnt signals from Frizzled receptors and branching to canonical (β-catenin) and non-canonical (PCP) pathways. ANKRD6 may bind DVL1 through ankyrin repeats, recruiting or stabilizing PRICKLE-VANGL complexes at plasma membrane. PRICKLE-VANGL interactions establish cellular asymmetry during PCP; ANKRD6 may scaffold these interactions or regulate their turnover. CSNK1E interaction suggests ANKRD6 may recruit casein kinase 1 epsilon to phosphorylate DVL1, modulating pathway activation. GSK3B and AXIN1 interactions link ANKRD6 to β-catenin destruction complex, suggesting potential cross-regulation between canonical and non-canonical Wnt branches. ASB family membership (PTHR24126) suggests ANKRD6 may function as E3 ubiquitin ligase substrate adaptor—ankyrin repeats bind substrates (DVL, PRICKLE, VANGL), while SOCS box recruits elongin B/C-cullin-RBX complex for ubiquitination. This would enable ANKRD6 to control Wnt/PCP protein stability, abundance, or membrane localization through targeted degradation. Vesicle localization supports roles in Wnt ligand secretion (Wnts require specialized packaging for release), Frizzled receptor endocytosis/recycling, or PCP component trafficking to specific membrane domains. Brain expression (25.8 nTPM) aligns with Wnt/PCP functions in neural development: neural tube closure (PCP drives convergent extension), axon guidance (Wnt gradients guide growth cones), neuronal migration (PCP establishes directional movement), and synapse formation (Wnt signaling regulates synaptic assembly). Mechanistically, ANKRD6 may function as: (1) DVL1 scaffold stabilizing active Wnt signaling complexes at membrane; (2) PRICKLE-VANGL complex organizer establishing cellular asymmetry; (3) E3 ubiquitin ligase adaptor targeting DVL/PRICKLE/VANGL for degradation to attenuate signaling; (4) CSNK1E recruiter enabling DVL1 phosphorylation and pathway activation; (5) Vesicle trafficking regulator controlling Wnt secretion or receptor endocytosis; (6) Cross-pathway integrator linking canonical (GSK3B/AXIN1) and non-canonical (PRICKLE/VANGL) Wnt branches. Disease mechanism: ANKRD6 haploinsufficiency or overexpression from chromosome 6q CNVs may contribute to 6q deletion syndrome phenotypes including intellectual disability, developmental delay, seizures, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. Disrupted Wnt/PCP signaling causes neural tube defects (spina bifida, anencephaly), hearing loss (cochlear hair cell PCP), kidney abnormalities (tubule PCP), and skeletal defects—all reported in 6q syndromes. Specific splice variants (c.1486-3A>T, c.1372-22C>G) may alter ankyrin repeat structure, disrupting protein-protein interactions. Priority experiments include co-IP validation of DVL1, PRICKLE1-4, VANGL1-2, CSNK1E interactions; ubiquitination assays testing ANKRD6 E3 ligase activity toward DVL/PRICKLE/VANGL; Wnt signaling reporter assays (TOPFlash for canonical, AP-1 for PCP) in ANKRD6 knockout cells; neural tube closure assays in ANKRD6-deficient embryonic models; axon guidance assays with Wnt gradients; subcellular localization by immunofluorescence (vesicle markers EEA1/Rab5, plasma membrane, cytoplasm); structural analysis leveraging AlphaFold model (pLDDT 70.2) to map ankyrin repeat solvent-exposed surfaces for partner binding; investigation of ANKRD6 in iPSC-derived neurons and brain organoids; analysis of ANKRD6 expression in 6q syndrome patient cells.
