## Description
FARP2 (UniProt O94887), also known as FIR, FRG, or PLEKHC3, encodes a 1054 aa multi-domain Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor functioning as a critical regulator of cytoskeletal dynamics and cell polarity in neuronal development and epithelial organization. InterPro analysis reveals a sophisticated modular architecture characteristic of FERM-containing RhoGEFs: an N-terminal FERM domain complex (Four.1/Ezrin/Radixin/Moesin) comprising F1 subdomain (cd17190, residues 44-128), B-lobe (cd14473, residues 136-226), and C-lobe (cd13193, residues 221-341) providing membrane-cytoskeleton linkage and protein-protein interaction platforms; a central Dbl homology (DH) domain (cd00160/G3DSA:1.20.900.10, residues 537-720/532-736) containing the catalytic core for guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity (GO:0005085) that activates Rho GTPases by promoting GDP-to-GTP exchange; and two C-terminal Pleckstrin homology (PH) domains (cd01220/cd13235/G3DSA:2.30.29.30, residues 749-855/928-1025/737-855/928-1031) providing membrane localization through phosphoinositide binding and potential autoinhibitory regulation of DH domain activity. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 74.6) indicating a well-folded multi-domain protein where FERM domains create an extended membrane-binding platform, the DH domain forms a catalytic bundle for GTPase engagement, and tandem PH domains enable complex membrane recruitment dynamics. STRING interactions define a semaphorin-plexin-Rho GTPase signaling network: CDC42 (0.925, cell division control protein 42 homolog, master regulator of filopodia and cell polarity), SRC (0.910, Src kinase, tyrosine phosphorylation regulator), RAP1A (0.907, Ras-related protein 1A, cell adhesion control), PIP5K1C (0.907, phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate 5-kinase type 1 gamma, PIP2 synthesis), PLXNA1 (0.875, plexin-A1, semaphorin receptor), PLXNA4 (0.829, plexin-A4, semaphorin receptor), SEMA3A (0.827, semaphorin-3A, repulsive guidance cue), RAC1 (0.773, Ras-related C3 botulinum toxin substrate 1, lamellipodia formation), FES (0.767, Fes/Fps tyrosine kinase), and RAP1B (0.765). The ultra-high confidence CDC42/SRC/PLXNA connections confirm FARP2 operates downstream of semaphorin-plexin repulsive signaling controlling neuronal morphogenesis. HPA classifies FARP2 as low tissue specificity with detection in all tissues, low cancer/immune specificity, localized to cytosol, reflecting broad roles in cytoskeletal regulation across cell types though recent literature identifies specific functions in corneal fibroblasts (TGF-β1-mediated myofibroblast differentiation) and neurons (dendrite morphogenesis). ClinVar lists 109 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting primarily of large copy number losses (2q37.3x1, 2q37.2-37.3x1, 2q35-37.3x1) and gains (2q33.3-37.3x3, 2q37.2-37.3x3) plus one splice site variant (c.183+2T>A) though no single-gene disorder is definitively assigned suggesting contribution to 2q37 deletion syndrome (brachydactyly-mental retardation syndrome) or neurodevelopmental phenotypes. Functional hypothesis: FARP2 operates through integrated semaphorin-Rho GTPase mechanisms: (1) Plexin signal transduction - Binds activated plexin-A1/A4 receptors following SEMA3A engagement, relieving autoinhibition and activating DH domain; (2) CDC42/RAC1 activation - Catalyzes GDP-to-GTP exchange on CDC42 (primary) and RAC1 driving filopodia/lamellipodia formation for growth cone collapse or extension; (3) SRC-mediated regulation - Tyrosine phosphorylation by SRC kinase modulates GEF activity and subcellular localization creating feedback loops; (4) Membrane recruitment - Tandem PH domains bind PIP2/PIP3 generated by PIP5K1C bringing FARP2 to specific membrane compartments; (5) Polarity establishment - aPKCι-dependent phosphorylation controls tight junction assembly in epithelia linking FARP2 to apical-basal polarity; (6) Disease mechanism - CNV losses cause haploinsufficiency disrupting semaphorin-guided axon pathfinding and neuronal migration leading to neurodevelopmental defects; 2q37.3 location places it in brachydactyly-mental retardation syndrome critical region; (7) Conservation - Mouse Farp2 (Q91VS8, 1065 aa) indicates essential conserved function. Dark aspects requiring investigation include: structural basis for plexin-FARP2 interaction interface, identification of specific phosphorylation sites by SRC/aPKCι, mechanism of autoinhibition relief upon semaphorin stimulation, precise GTPase substrate preference (CDC42 vs RAC1 vs RAP1), contribution to specific 2q37 deletion syndrome phenotypes, genotype-phenotype correlations for CNV variants, role in corneal fibrosis progression and therapeutic potential, functional divergence from paralog FARP1 (CHN), cell-type specific functions in neurons vs epithelia vs fibroblasts, post-translational modifications regulating membrane recruitment, and crosstalk with other RhoGEF pathways.
