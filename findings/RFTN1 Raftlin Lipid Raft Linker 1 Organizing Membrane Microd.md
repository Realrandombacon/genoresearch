## Description
RFTN1 (Raftlin, lipid raft linker 1, UniProt Q14699) is a 578 amino acid intracellular protein with moderate characterization (38 papers) that functions as a critical organizer of lipid raft microdomains, facilitating the clustering of signaling receptors (such as TLR4 and B-cell receptors) to modulate immune responses, inflammation, and cancer progression. The protein belongs to the Raftlin family (IPR028169/PF15250 residues 1-471/1-565, PTHR17601 residues 1-565), a unique domain architecture conserved from plants to humans that mediates membrane curvature and protein scaffolding within cholesterol-rich membrane regions.
