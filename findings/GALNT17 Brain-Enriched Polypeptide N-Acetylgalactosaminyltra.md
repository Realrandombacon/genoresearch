GALNT17 (Polypeptide N-Acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 17, UniProt Q6IS24) is a 598 aa glycosyltransferase catalyzing the initial reaction in mucin-type O-linked oligosaccharide biosynthesis by transferring N-acetyl-D-galactosamine (GalNAc) to serine/threonine residues on protein receptors.
