## Description
PRPS1L1 (Phosphoribosyl pyrophosphate synthetase 1 like 1, also known as PRS-III, PRPS3, PRPSL, Ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase 3) is a 318 amino acid metabolic enzyme (UniProt P21108) with very-high-confidence folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 94.6), characteristic of well-characterized phosphoribosyltransferase enzymes that catalyze nucleotide biosynthesis through structured active sites. The protein contains a Phosphoribosyltransferase type I domain (cd06223, residues 151-274), Phosphoribosyltransferase domain (IPR000836, residues 151-274; PF00156), Phosphoribosyl pyrophosphate synthetase conserved site (IPR000842, residues 128-143) enabling magnesium ion binding (GO:0000287) and ribose phosphate diphosphokinase activity (GO:0004749), Ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase family (IPR005946, residues 1-316) for nucleotide biosynthetic process (GO:0009165), Phosphoribosyltransferase-like superfamily (IPR029057, residues 6-306), Ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase N-terminal domain (IPR029099, residues 4-120), and bacterial-type ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase family (IPR037515, residues 4-314). PRPS1L1 is located on chromosome 7p21.1 as an intronless gene specifically expressed in testis, encoding a protein highly homologous to PRPS1 and PRPS2 X-linked subunits that convert pyrimidine, purine or pyridine bases to corresponding ribonucleoside monophosphates.
