## Description
EMC7 (ER membrane protein complex subunit 7, aliases C11orf3, C15orf24, HT022, ORF1-FL1) is a 242 amino acid predicted ER membrane protein (UniProt Q9NPA0) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 72.9) containing canonical EMC subunit architecture including ER membrane protein complex subunit 7 beta-sandwich domain (IPR019008/PF09430, residues 57-168) forming a 112 aa module (~46% of protein) characteristic of the EMC7 subunit family that mediates protein-protein interactions within the EMC insertase complex via beta-sandwich fold structure with GO:0030246 carbohydrate binding, Carbohydrate-binding-like fold superfamily (IPR013784/SSF49452, residues 42-116) forming a 75 aa module (~31%) for substrate recognition, ER membrane protein complex subunit 7 family (IPR039163/PTHR13605, residues 12-225) forming a 214 aa module (~88%) defining the EMC7 signature, and molecular functions including membrane insertase activity, protein insertion into ER membrane by stop-transfer membrane-anchor sequence, tail-anchored membrane protein insertion into ER membrane, and endoplasmic reticulum membrane localization, functioning as a ubiquitously-expressed EMC subunit that regulates ER membrane protein insertion and tail-anchored protein biogenesis through beta-sandwich domain-mediated complex assembly and substrate recognition via partnerships with EMC1, EMC2, EMC3, EMC4, EMC6, EMC8, EMC9, EMC10, MMGT1, and C1orf43.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals EMC7 contains extensive EMC subunit architecture: The beta-sandwich domain (residues 57-168) forms the core structural element that mediates interactions with other EMC subunits within the multi-subunit insertase complex. The carbohydrate-binding-like fold (residues 42-116) may recognize glycosylated substrates or mediate additional protein interactions.
