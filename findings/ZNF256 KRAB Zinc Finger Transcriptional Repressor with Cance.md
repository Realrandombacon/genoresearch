# ZNF256: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Cancer-Enhanced Expression

**Date:** 2026-03-23T10:09:12.206574

**Quality Score:** 2.5/10  (E=4.7, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF256 encodes a 627-amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q9Y2P7) containing an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (InterPro IPR001909, PF01352, residues 14-96) and multiple C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096 at positions 90-117, 239-266, 267-294, 323-345). The protein localizes to the nucleoplasm and functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding repressor. HPA classifies it as a transcription factor with cancer-enhanced expression pattern, detected in all tissues but upregulated in malignancies. STRING interactions reveal association with TRIM28/KAP1 (score 0.532), the canonical KRAB corepressor that recruits heterochromatin machinery, plus other zinc finger proteins ZNF416 (0.416), ZNF569 (0.408), and ZIK1 (0.446), suggesting heteromeric repression complexes. Interaction with ALDOA (aldolase A, 0.654) may link chromatin regulation to glycolytic metabolism. Additional partners include RTBDN (retbindin, 0.541) and LOC112694756 (0.658). ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 19q13.43 copy number gains, indicating dosage sensitivity and clinical relevance. The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28 which scaffolds SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 heterochromatin proteins, establishing H3K9me3 repressive marks. ZNF256 likely silences endogenous retroviral elements or developmentally regulated genes, with cancer overexpression potentially contributing to epigenetic dysregulation and tumor suppressor silencing.
