## Description
KDELR1 (P24390) is a 212-aa KDEL endoplasmic reticulum protein retention receptor with very high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 92.5), indicating a stable well-folded transmembrane protein. InterPro analysis reveals ER lumen protein retaining receptor family domain (IPR000133/PF00810: positions 28-169) with conserved signature sequences (PS00951: positions 31-48, PS00952: positions 126-135), characteristic of pH-dependent cargo binding receptors. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to Golgi apparatus, vesicles, and cytosol, functioning in ER-Golgi transport and protein retention. STRING interactions demonstrate exceptionally strong binding to paralog KDELR3 (0.906), cargo receptor SURF4 (0.904), ER chaperone-associated proteins SDF2L1 (0.877) and RCN1 (0.848), plus high-confidence interactions with ARF1 (0.825), RCN2 (0.822), SDF2 (0.778), and COPI coat component COPB1 (0.613). Literature (32 papers) establishes KDELR1 cycles between ER and cis-Golgi, recognizing KDEL/HDEL tetrapeptide signals on soluble ER chaperones (BiP, GRP94, PDI) and packaging them into COPI-coated retrograde vesicles via ARF1 GTPase activation. Recent 2025-2026 studies reveal KDELR1 promotes malignant progression in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma and chondrosarcoma through Integrin-Hippo-YAP1 axis, with regulatory circuits between KDELR1/KDELR3 fine-tuning early secretory pathway composition. ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.92-41C>G, c.92-27C>G, c.92-15G>C, c.352-45C>T) and copy number gains at 19q13.33, though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. The very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 92.5) suggests KDELR1 undergoes pH-dependent conformational changes between Golgi (acidic, cargo-binding) and ER (neutral, cargo-release) compartments, with ARF1/COPB1 interactions enabling COPI vesicle recruitment for retrograde transport.
