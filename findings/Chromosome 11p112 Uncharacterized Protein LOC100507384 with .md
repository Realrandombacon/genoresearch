The 11p11.2 chromosomal region is the critical locus for 11p11.2 deletion syndrome (Potocki-Shaffer syndrome region) and 11p11.2 duplication syndrome, containing critical craniofacial development, coagulation, GTPase signaling, and neurodevelopmental-associated genes including ALX2 (aristaless-like homeobox 2, homeodomain transcription factor, craniofacial development, parietal foramina), ALX4 (aristaless-like homeobox 4, homeodomain transcription factor, skull ossification, parietal foramina), F2 (coagulation factor II/prothrombin, thrombin generation, hemostasis, thrombophilia), RAP1GDS1 (RAP1 guanine nucleotide exchange factor SGEF, Rho GTPase activation, cytoskeleton organization), DHCR7 (7-dehydrocholesterol reductase, cholesterol biosynthesis, Smith-Lemli-Opitz syndrome), and MLLT2 (myeloid translocation location on chromosome 2, transcriptional elongation, leukemia).
