## Description
ENOX2 encodes a 610-amino acid cell surface oxidoreductase that functions as an ecto-NOX disulfide-thiol exchanger, catalyzing protein disulfide-thiol interchange coupled with NADH oxidation. This LIGHT GRAY gene has 85 literature references - more than truly dark genes but less mechanistic depth than paradigmatic cancer genes. InterPro analysis reveals multiple domain architectures: RNA recognition motif (IPR000504, PF00076, residues 128-207), Ecto-NOX exchanger family (IPR038876, residues 15-609), ENOX1-like domain (PF23267, residues 410-504), and C-terminal helix-turn-helix domain (IPR060245, residues 531-598). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 77.1), consistent with stable enzymatic function. Human Protein Atlas shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, localized to plasma membrane and cytosol, with oxidoreductase molecular function involved in electron transport, growth regulation, and biological rhythms. STRING interactions reveal connections to ACE2 (0.698), POP5 (0.611), RPP30 (0.587), KLHL20 (0.534), and RNase P components, suggesting potential roles in RNA processing or ribonucleoprotein complexes. ClinVar documents 174 pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains spanning Xq23-28, indicating dosage sensitivity. Recent literature (2023-2025) highlights ENOX2/tNOX as: (1) prognostic marker in malignant melanoma, (2) lung cancer biomarker detected by electrochemical aptamer biosensors, (3) immune checkpoint target where ENOX2 inhibition enhances effector memory T cell infiltration, (4) novel ENOX2::RUNX1 fusion in AML-MDS detected by optical genome mapping. Functional hypothesis: ENOX2 maintains cell surface redox homeostasis through constitutive disulfide-thiol exchange, regulating receptor conformation, growth factor signaling, and immune synapse formation. Mechanistic proposal: As a cancer-specific constitutive ENOX protein (tNOX), ENOX2 exhibits drug-sensitive oscillatory activity distinct from constitutive ENOX1, making it a therapeutic target; copy number alterations disrupt redox balance affecting tumor growth and immune evasion.
