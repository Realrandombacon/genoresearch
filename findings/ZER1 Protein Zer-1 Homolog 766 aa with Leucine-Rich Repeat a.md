## Description
ZER1 encodes Protein Zer-1 Homolog (UniProt Q7Z7L7), a 766-amino acid cytosolic protein that functions as a critical substrate recognition adapter for CUL2-based E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes mediating N-terminal glycine (Gly/N-degron) recognition and ubiquitin-dependent protein degradation. The protein contains conserved multi-domain architecture including N-terminal leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains (IPR032675/IPR056845/PF25013/G3DSA:3.80.10.10, positions 47-145/157-303/193-333) forming curved solenoid structure for substrate binding, central and C-terminal armadillo repeats (IPR000225/IPR011989/IPR016024, positions 427-467/511-556/558-600/390-756) providing protein-protein interaction scaffold, ZER1-specific C-terminal domain (IPR051341/IPR055142/PF22964/PTHR12904, positions 5-740/401-755/401-755) defining family membership as Zyg-11-related ubiquitin ligase adapter, with GO annotations for protein binding (GO:0005515) and Ubl conjugation pathway involvement, indicating essential role as molecular adapter coordinating substrate recognition, CUL2-RBX1-ELOB-ELOC complex assembly, and ubiquitin-mediated proteasomal degradation.
