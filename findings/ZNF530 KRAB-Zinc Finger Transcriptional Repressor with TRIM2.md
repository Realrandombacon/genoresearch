# ZNF530: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with TRIM28 Co-repressor Recruitment

**Date:** 2026-03-20T19:31:22.045081

**Quality Score:** 2.7/10  (E=5.1, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF530 encodes a 599 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein (UniProt Q6P9A1) that functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 14-94) which mediates transcriptional repression through recruitment of the co-repressor TRIM28/KAP1, and multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 239-444 that confer DNA sequence specificity. AlphaFold structure prediction (pLDDT 67.7) indicates medium confidence with structured zinc finger regions and potentially disordered linker regions typical of KRAB-ZFPs. HPA RNA-seq shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (5.9 nTPM), vesicular subcellular localization, and low cancer specificity. STRING interactions identify TRIM28 (score 0.580) as the primary binding partner—the canonical KRAB co-repressor that recruits heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) and histone methyltransferases to establish repressive chromatin marks (H3K9me3). Additional interactions with ZNF16 (0.479) suggest potential heterodimerization with related zinc finger proteins. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 19q13.41-13.43, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation analysis shows orthologs in primates (Macaca fascicularis 597 aa, 99.7% identity) and mammals (Chlorocebus sabaeus 556 aa), confirming functional constraint. The mechanistic model proposes ZNF530 binds specific DNA sequences via its C2H2 zinc finger array, recruits TRIM28 through the KRAB domain, and establishes facultative heterochromatin at target loci to regulate gene expression programs in muscle tissue and potentially neurodevelopment.
