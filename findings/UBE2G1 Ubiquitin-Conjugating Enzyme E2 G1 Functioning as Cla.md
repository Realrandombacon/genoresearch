## Description
UBE2G1 (Ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 G1, P62253) encodes a compact 170 aa enzyme functioning as a class III E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme that catalyzes the transfer of ubiquitin from E1 activating enzymes to substrate proteins via E3 ligases, playing critical roles in endoplasmic reticulum-associated degradation (ERAD), proteasomal protein quality control, and emerging functions in hematopoietic stem cell aging and gestational diabetes pathogenesis, representing an essential component of the ubiquitin-proteasome system with tissue-specific expression patterns. Structural architecture reveals an exceptionally well-folded organization with very high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 94.1): UBCc catalytic domain (cd23795, residues 5-160) forming the canonical E2 fold with a central four-stranded antiparallel beta-sheet surrounded by alpha-helices containing the active site cysteine for ubiquitin thioester formation, UBC catalytic core domain (IPR000608/PF00179/SM00212, residues 5-166/10-160/8-166) spanning nearly the entire protein length defining the ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme superfamily, UBC active site signature (PS00183, residues 79-94) containing the catalytic cysteine residue essential for ubiquitin charging and transfer, UBC/RWD-like superfamily (IPR016135/G3DSA:3.10.110.10/SSF54495, residues 4-167/4-167/8-164) establishing evolutionary relationship to RWD domain-containing proteins, E2-like family (IPR050113/PTHR24067, residues 7-165/7-165) confirming functional classification, GO term ubiquitin-protein transferase activity indicating enzymatic function; the very high pLDDT score reflects a stable, rigid catalytic core optimized for precise ubiquitin handling without regulatory disordered regions.
