# FADS3: Sphingadiene Synthase Linking Ceramide Desaturation to Cancer Metabolism and Immune Regulation

## Description
FADS3 is a 445 aa bifunctional enzyme containing N-terminal cytochrome b5-like heme-binding domain (IPR001199/PF00173, aa20-97) fused to C-terminal fatty acid desaturase domain (IPR005804/PF00487, aa158-416). AlphaFold predicts exceptionally confident structure (pLDDT 93.9) indicating stable enzymatic fold. Unlike FADS1/FADS2 which produce omega-3/6 polyunsaturated fatty acids, FADS3 specifically catalyzes introduction of cis double bonds at C4 and C14 positions of sphingoid bases, generating 4,14-sphingadiene—the unique unsaturated ceramide species in mammals. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with nucleolar fibrillar center localization and immune cell enhancement in blood. STRING reveals high-confidence interactions with lipid metabolism enzymes: SCD/stearoyl-CoA desaturase (0.905), CYB5B/cytochrome b5 (0.900), DEGS1/sphingolipid delta-4 desaturase (0.520), CH25H/cholesterol 25-hydroxylase (0.544), FAXDC2 (0.544), plus ferredoxins FDX1/FDX2 (0.413-0.571) providing reducing equivalents. This positions FADS3 in coordinated sphingolipid/sterol biosynthesis requiring electron transfer. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants including splice site (c.748-2A>G) and copy number gains/deletions at 11q12.2, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. The mechanistic model: FADS3 uses cytochrome b5 domain to accept electrons from NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase, transferring them to di-iron catalytic center in desaturase domain for oxygen activation and C-H bond abstraction during sphingoid base desaturation. Recent 2025 studies reveal emerging oncology functions: (1) fuels clear cell renal cell carcinoma progression via lipid droplet accumulation and TGF-β receptor axis bridging metabolic reprogramming with epithelial plasticity, (2) prognostic biomarker in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma correlating with immune infiltration, (3) WTAP-mediated m6A modification stabilizing FADS3 in lung adenocarcinoma. With only 113 publications versus thousands for FADS1/FADS2, FADS3 represents an incompletely characterized sphingolipid desaturase connecting ceramide diversity to tumor metabolism.
