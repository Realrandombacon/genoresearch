# CORO6: WD40-Repeat Coronin Family Member Regulating Cancer Progression via WNT Signaling Activation, Glycolysis Enhancement, and Actin Cytoskeleton Remodeling through Cofilin Interactions

## Description
CORO6 (Coronin-6, also known as ClipinE, UniProt Q6QEF8) is a 472-amino acid intracellular actin-binding protein belonging to the coronin family that functions as a regulator of cell migration, cancer progression, and cytoskeletal dynamics through WD40-repeat mediated protein-protein interactions. The protein architecture features an N-terminal domain of unknown function DUF1899 (IPR015048, PF08953, aa 4-69) of unclear functional significance, followed by a large WD40 repeat β-propeller domain (IPR001680, IPR015943, IPR036322, PF00400, aa 67-351) containing multiple WD40 repeats (aa 78-109, 122-160, 174-203, 344-386) with conserved Trp-Asp signature motifs (PS00678, PS50082, aa 147-161) forming the characteristic seven-bladed β-propeller scaffold for partner recruitment, and a C-terminal coronin family domain (IPR015505, aa 1-466). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.4), indicating a well-folded stable β-propeller structure optimal for scaffolding multiple protein interactions. STRING interaction network places CORO6 within the actin cytoskeleton and coronin family machinery with medium-confidence associations to: CORO1C (0.636), coronin-1C paralog regulating actin dynamics; DUT (0.540), dUTP diphosphatase linking to nucleotide metabolism; KLHL35 (0.537), kelch-like protein 35 substrate adaptor for CUL3 E3 ligase; FPGS (0.523), folylpolyglutamate synthase in folate metabolism; CFL2 (0.507) and CFL1 (0.499), cofilin-2 and cofilin-1 actin depolymerizing factors key downstream effectors; ARHGEF5 (0.483), Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor 5 linking to Rho GTPase signaling; IRGQ (0.482), immunity-related GTPase family Q; and CORO1A (0.455) and CORO1B (0.415), additional coronin paralogs. HPA expression profiles demonstrate group-enriched expression with highest levels in skeletal muscle (342.1 nTPM) and tongue (180.0 nTPM), detected in many tissues, Golgi apparatus subcellular localization suggesting roles in vesicular trafficking or secretion, cancer-enhanced RNA specificity indicating upregulation in tumors, and absence from immune cells distinguishing it from immune-focused coronins like CORO1A. ClinVar records document 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.634-2A>C, c.634-1G>T affecting intron 5 splice acceptor), large deletions on 17q11.2 (27.6-29.6Mb), and copy number gains spanning 17p11.2-q21.2 (21.7-38.8Mb regions), though no specific monogenic syndrome has been definitively assigned suggesting potential dosage sensitivity in development or cancer predisposition. Literature analysis (20 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic roles: METTL3/YTHDF1 m6A methylation axis stabilizes CORO6 mRNA promoting osteosarcoma progression through enhanced glycolysis; USP52 deubiquitinase stabilizes CORO6 protein by reducing ubiquitination driving clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) growth and malignancy; CORO6 activates WNT signaling pathway promoting ccRCC cell growth and invasion; RBM25 regulates CORO6 alternative splicing affecting immune and inflammatory processes in cardiomyocytes; crystal structure studies reveal actin-binding interfaces. We hypothesize that CORO6 functions through a multi-mechanism scaffolding model: (1) In cancer cells, the WD40 β-propeller recruits cofilins (CFL1/CFL2) enhancing actin filament severing and turnover promoting cell migration and invasion; (2) CORO6 activates WNT signaling potentially by stabilizing β-catenin or recruiting Dishevelled components through WD40-mediated interactions; (3) In osteosarcoma, m6A-modified CORO6 transcripts are stabilized by YTHDF1 reader increasing protein levels that enhance glycolytic flux supporting rapid proliferation; (4) USP52-mediated deubiquitination prevents proteasomal degradation creating a stabilizing axis in ccRCC; (5) Golgi localization suggests roles in vesicle trafficking or secretory pathway regulation distinct from plasma membrane-focused coronins; (6) High skeletal muscle expression implies specialized function in muscle cell migration or regeneration; (7) Coronin paralogs (CORO1A/B/C) may form hetero-oligomers with CORO6 providing functional redundancy or specificity; (8) KLHL35 interaction suggests regulation by CUL3-mediated ubiquitination controlling stability; (9) ClinVar splice variants may cause exon skipping disrupting the WD40 propeller structure. Therapeutic targeting of CORO6-cofilin interface or USP52-CORO6 axis could block metastasis in renal cancer and osteosarcoma.
