## Description
ZNF404 encodes a 552 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q494X3) with canonical transcriptional repression architecture. InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 5-78) followed by three tandem C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 131-214) and additional C-terminal zinc finger arrays (PF13912, residues 327-351). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 73.6) consistent with stable DNA-binding domains.

STRING interactions identify ZNF404 within chromatin regulatory networks: partners include E3 ubiquitin ligase scaffold CUL7 (0.465), polycomb-like L3MBTL3 (0.428), DNA repair nuclease ANKLE1 (0.425), and leucine-rich repeat proteins LRRC37A2/A3 (0.474/0.432). This interaction profile suggests roles in heterochromatin formation and genome stability.

HPA shows nucleolar/nucleolar rim localization with intermediate filament association, low tissue specificity, and detection in many tissues. ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic copy number variants on 19q13.31, indicating dosage sensitivity for proper development.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: ZNF404 functions as a KRAB-dependent transcriptional corepressor that recruits KAP1/TRIM28 complexes to silence retrotransposons and repetitive elements via H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. The nucleolar localization suggests additional roles in ribosomal DNA silencing or nucleolar organization.

MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: ZNF404 binds specific genomic loci via its C2H2 zinc finger array, recruits the KAP1-SETDB1 histone methyltransferase complex through KRAB-KAP1 interaction, and collaborates with CUL7 ubiquitination machinery for substrate turnover. Disease-associated CNVs disrupt target gene repression networks.
