# SDHAF3: Mitochondrial LYR Motif Chaperone Essential for Succinate Dehydrogenase Complex II Assembly and Iron-Sulfur Cluster Maturation

## Description
SDHAF3 (Succinate Dehydrogenase Assembly Factor 3, mitochondrial, UniProt Q9NRP4) is a 125 aa mitochondrial chaperone belonging to the LYR motif protein family that functions in the assembly and maturation of succinate dehydrogenase (SDH/Complex II) of the mitochondrial respiratory chain. The molecular mechanism involves an N-terminal LYR motif (Leucine-Tyrosine-Arginine, cd20270, positions 9-64) characteristic of mitochondrial complex assembly factors that mediates specific protein-protein interactions during iron-sulfur cluster insertion into the SDHB subunit. Literature (9 papers) establishes SDHAF3 roles in: (1) SDHAF3 analysis in familial and sporadic pheochromocytoma and paraganglioma showing no germline mutations but establishing SDHAF3 as a candidate tumor suppressor (2017, 21 citations), (2) LYR factors SDHAF1 and SDHAF3 mediating maturation of the iron-sulfur subunit of succinate dehydrogenase (2014, 111 citations), (3) SDHAF3 working cooperatively with SDHAF1 to insert the [2Fe-2S] cluster into SDHB during Complex II biogenesis. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity and mitochondrial localization, consistent with housekeeping role in cellular respiration. STRING interactions reveal dedicated SDH assembly network: SDHAF2 (0.849), SDHAF1 (0.502), SDHAF4 (0.561) - other assembly factors; SDHA (0.573), SDHB (0.651), SDHD (0.588) - SDH complex subunits; LYRM4 (0.722), LYRM1 (0.577) - related LYR motif proteins; NDUFAB1 (0.835) - Complex I assembly factor. ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 7q21.2-22.1 and deletions, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 88.9) with well-folded LYR domain. Conservation: yeast Sdh7 (Q04401) is 133 aa, Drosophila Sdhaf3 (Q8SZ16) is 120 aa, rat Sdhaf3 (Q6TUF2) is 125 aa - highly conserved across eukaryotes. Functional hypothesis: SDHAF3 acts as a specialized chaperone that binds nascent SDHB via its LYR motif, facilitating iron-sulfur cluster insertion required for SDH enzymatic activity and electron transport chain function. Loss of SDHAF3 function would impair Complex II assembly, reduce succinate oxidation, increase ROS production, and stabilize HIF-1α through succinate accumulation - a mechanism shared with SDH subunit mutations in pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma. The pseudohypoxic drive from SDH dysfunction links SDHAF3 to tumorigenesis despite limited direct disease associations.
