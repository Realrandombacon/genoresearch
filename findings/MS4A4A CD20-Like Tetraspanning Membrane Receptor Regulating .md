# MS4A4A: CD20-Like Tetraspanning Membrane Receptor Regulating TREM2 Signaling in Microglia and Checkpoint Control in Tumor-Associated Macrophages

**Date:** 2026-06-26T15:51:55.499455

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
MS4A4A (239 aa, UniProt Q96JQ5) is a tetraspanning membrane protein of the CD20/MS4A family that functions as an immune checkpoint regulator in tumor-associated macrophages and modulates TREM2 signaling in microglia, linking innate immunity to Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis and cancer immunosurveillance. The protein architecture features four transmembrane helices characteristic of the MS4A family (IPR030417/PF04103, aa21-217) with two extracellular loops and cytoplasmic N- and C-termini; the CD20-like transmembrane domain (IPR007237, aa65-207) forms the core structural scaffold that mediates homomeric and heteromeric interactions within the MS4A cluster at chromosome 11q12.2. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with placenta enrichment (59.3 nTPM), group-enriched expression in blood cells, ubiquitous detection across tissues, strict membrane localization, and classification as a predicted membrane receptor. Mechanistically, MS4A4A forms ultra-high-confidence interactions with immune regulators: VSIG4 complement regulatory protein (STRING 0.958) links to complement cascade control; CD163 scavenger receptor (0.843) connects to hemoglobin-haptoglobin clearance and anti-inflammatory macrophage polarization; MS4A10 (0.832) and MS4A6A (0.711) represent paralogs that may form heteromeric complexes; C1QA (0.803) and C1QB (0.569) connect to classical complement pathway initiation; ABCA7 (0.713) links to lipid homeostasis and Alzheimer's amyloid clearance; CD2AP (0.696) bridges to cytoskeletal remodeling during phagocytosis; CD33 (0.525) represents another microglial checkpoint receptor. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 73.9) reveals confident tetraspanning topology with well-defined transmembrane helices connected by short extracellular and intracellular loops, consistent with crystal structures of related MS4A family members showing bundle-of-helices architecture. Clinical significance includes 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants: large copy number gains spanning 11q12.1-13.5 (chr11:56895955-69295402 amplifications), 11p11.12-q13.1 gains, and deletions at 11q12.2, though no specific monogenic syndrome has been definitively established; genome-wide association studies link common MS4A4A variants to cerebrospinal fluid sTREM2 levels and Alzheimer's disease risk. Conservation across mammals indicates essential immune function maintained over ~90 million years. Literature (150 papers) establishes MS4A4A in neurodegeneration and cancer immunotherapy: 2025 studies demonstrate MS4A4A and MS4A6A cooperate to negatively regulate TREM2 surface expression and microglial activation states in Alzheimer's disease; microglial MS4A4A protects against epileptic seizures in AD mouse models; Ms4a4a deficiency ameliorates amyloid plaque pathology; 2023 landmark Nature paper shows targeting MS4A4A on tumor-associated macrophages restores CD8+ T-cell-mediated antitumor immunity through checkpoint blockade mechanism. The functional hypothesis posits that MS4A4A acts as a 'membrane scaffold for immune checkpoint assembly': the four transmembrane helices anchor MS4A4A in lipid raft microdomains where it oligomerizes with MS4A6A/MS4A10 forming heteromeric complexes that recruit TREM2 via extracellular loop interactions; in microglia, MS4A4A stabilizes TREM2 at the cell surface promoting phagocytic clearance of amyloid-beta while limiting excessive inflammatory activation through negative feedback; in tumor-associated macrophages, MS4A4A engagement delivers inhibitory signals suppressing CD8+ T-cell priming analogous to PD-L1/PD-1 axis; genetic variants altering MS4A4A expression or splicing shift the balance between protective amyloid clearance and pathological neuroinflammation in Alzheimer's disease, while tumor microenvironment induces MS4A4A upregulation creating immunosuppressive niche; therapeutic strategies include anti-MS4A4A antibodies for cancer immunotherapy and small molecule modulators enhancing microglial TREM2 signaling for neurodegenerative disease.
