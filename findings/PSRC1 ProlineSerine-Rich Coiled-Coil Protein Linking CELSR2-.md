## Description
PSRC1/Q6PGN9 (363 aa) is a partially structured (AlphaFold pLDDT: 57.4) intracellular coiled-coil protein functioning as a component of the CELSR2-PSRC1-SORT1 genomic locus that regulates lipoprotein metabolism, mitotic spindle assembly, and endoplasmic reticulum stress responses. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the proline/serine-rich coiled-coil protein 1/G2 and S phase-expressed protein 1 family (IPR026657, residues 3-133) with an N-terminal GSPE1 domain (IPR032768/PF15259, residues 9-94) characteristic of cell cycle-regulated proteins, classifying it within the DDA3/GSPE1 superfamily (PTHR21584). HPA data confirms group-enriched expression with highest levels in brain (91.4 nTPM) and retina (38.7 nTPM), nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization, and involvement in cell cycle, cell division, and mitosis processes, with cancer-enriched expression patterns suggesting proliferative roles. STRING interactions define a high-confidence network linking PSRC1 to PCSK9 (0.812), myosin-binding protein MYBPHL (0.799), sortilin-related receptors SORCS2 (0.670), SORCS1 (0.669), and SORCS3 (0.669), cadherin EGF LAG seven-pass G-type receptor CELSR2 (0.658), kinesin KIF2A (0.657), microtubule-associated protein MAPRE1 (0.650), PIMREG (0.642), and sortilin SORT1 (0.483), positioning it at the intersection of lipid trafficking, microtubule dynamics, and mitotic spindle function. Literature (153 papers) establishes diverse mechanistic roles: 2025 studies identify PSRC1 as part of medium-HDL-targeting therapeutic potential for coronary artery disease through multi-omics lipoprotein profiling; phosphocreatine alleviates monocrotaline-induced liver injury via PSRC1-regulated ER stress pathways; PSRC1 ensures proper spindle assembly and chromosome segregation during mouse oocyte maturation; and pleiotropic eQTL effects in the CELSR2-PSRC1-SORT1 cluster associate with LDL-C levels and resting metabolic rate. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 1p regions (1p13.3-13.2, 1p21.3-13.2, 1p21.2-12, 1p22.1-11.2, 1p36.33-q44), implicating PSRC1 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. The convergence of coiled-coil structural architecture, CELSR2-PSRC1-SORT1 locus positioning, PCSK9/SORT1 lipid metabolism partnerships, KIF2A/MAPRE1 microtubule interactions, and dual roles in cardiovascular disease and mitotic fidelity suggests PSRC1 functions as a molecular adaptor coordinating lipid homeostasis with cell division machinery, with therapeutic implications for coronary artery disease, metabolic disorders, and cancer proliferation.
