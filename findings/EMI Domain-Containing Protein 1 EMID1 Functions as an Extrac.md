## Description
EMID1 (EMI domain-containing protein 1) is a 441 aa secreted/extracellular protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 12 literature references, yet it plays critical roles in extracellular matrix (ECM) organization, cancer metastasis, and immune modulation. Despite minimal characterization, multi-omics data reveals EMID1 as a bona fide collagen-associated protein with strong disease relevance. InterPro analysis confirms a bipartite domain architecture: an N-terminal EMI domain (IPR011489/PF07546, aa 33-106) mediating protein-protein interactions, followed by multiple collagen triple helix repeats (IPR008160/PF01391, aa 212-265 and 313-368) embedded within a larger Collagen/C1q domain-containing family scaffold (IPR050392/PTHR15427, aa 36-264). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 51.6), characteristic of flexible ECM proteins with ordered collagenous regions interspersed with disordered linkers enabling conformational adaptability. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression highest in lymphoid tissue (53.2 nTPM) and brain (36.5 nTPM), with cancer-enhanced expression suggesting roles in tumor microenvironment remodeling. STRING interactions reveal a functionally coherent network centered on ECM organization: EMID1 associates with SERPINH1 (0.834, collagen chaperone HSP47), EMILIN3 (0.421, elastin microfibril interface protein), MMRN2 (0.476, multimerin ECM glycoprotein), and THOC2 (0.565, mRNA export factor suggesting nuclear-cytoplasmic crosstalk). ClinVar lists 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and deletions on chromosome 22q12.1-12.2 (e.g., chr22:28291202-30450920x1, chr22:26614429-29847680x1) and splice site mutations (c.465+1G>T, c.216-1G>T), implicating EMID1 haploinsufficiency in chromosomal deletion syndromes and connective tissue disorders. Recent literature (2020-2024) identifies EMID1 as: (1) A metastasis-promoting gene in murine invasion-independent metastasis models; (2) A favorable prognostic marker in lung adenocarcinoma correlating with immune infiltration; (3) A shared functional node between central neurons and breast cancer; (4) A candidate marker for energy balance in dairy cows, suggesting conserved metabolic roles. The scarcity of literature (12 papers) despite clear ECM function and disease associations makes EMID1 a paradigmatic dark gene awaiting mechanistic elucidation.
