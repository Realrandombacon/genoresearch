## Description
OR11H7 encodes a 314 amino acid olfactory receptor belonging to the class A rhodopsin-like GPCR superfamily, located at chromosome 14q11.2. InterPro analysis reveals canonical olfactory receptor architecture with IPR000276 (GPCR rhodopsin-like), IPR000725 (Olfactory receptor), IPR017452 (7TM), PF13853 domains, and cd15913 (OR11G subfamily) spanning residues 25-306. The AlphaFold predicted structure (Q8NGC8) shows exceptional confidence folding (pLDDT=90.6) with characteristic 7-transmembrane helix bundle. STRING found no interaction partners, typical for orphan olfactory receptors. HPA has no expression data, but literature reveals critical ectopic expression: OR11H7 is expressed in human kidney tubule cells alongside olfactory signaling components AC3 and Golf G-protein (Pluznick et al., 2016, cited 43x), suggesting non-canonical chemosensory function in renal physiology. Critically, ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic copy number variants (6 gains, 3 losses) at 14q11.2 region (accessions 155681, 155306, 155119, 146230, 59933, 59932, 57745, 57743, 57246), indicating dosage sensitivity. Orthologs exist in cattle, dog, marmot, and lemur demonstrating mammalian conservation. Despite only 2 publications, this gene has clear clinical relevance and emerging extra-nasal function.
