# ZNF599: KRAB-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Dosage-Sensitive Clinical Variants

## Description
ZNF599 encodes a 588 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to chromosome 19q13.11. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical domain architecture: an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 8-80) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 199-282). The KRAB domain is predicted to recruit the TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex to silence target genes via histone modification. STRING interactions confirm functional partnerships: TRIM28 (score 0.536) as the canonical KRAB cofactor, along with SCGB2B2 (0.511), BCL2L2-PABPN1 (0.450), and TASOR2 (0.402), suggesting roles in transcriptional repression complexes. HPA data shows broad expression across all tissues (detected in all, low tissue specificity) with subcellular localization primarily in the nucleoplasm, but also detected in the plasma membrane and cytosol, indicating potential shuttling or multiple isoforms. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic copy number variants including large deletions (19q11-13.13) and duplications (19p13.3-q13.43), indicating strong dosage sensitivity where gene copy number alterations cause disease phenotypes. The combination of the KRAB repression domain, triple zinc finger array for specific DNA recognition, TRIM28 interaction, and clinical CNVs supports a model where ZNF599 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor regulating developmentally important genes, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression disrupting target gene silencing programs.
