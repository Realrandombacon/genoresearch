## Description
NACA2 (Nascent polypeptide-associated complex subunit alpha-2, also known as NACAL, ANAC) is a 215 amino acid intracellular chaperone protein (UniProt Q9H009) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 72.4), characteristic of nascent polypeptide-associated complex (NAC) subunits that function through NAC domain-mediated ribosome binding and UBA domain-mediated protein interactions during co-translational protein targeting. The protein contains a nascent polypeptide-associated complex NAC domain (IPR002715, residues 70-135; PF01849, residues 73-128; G3DSA:2.20.70.30, residues 84-136; cd22054, residues 84-131; PS51151) enabling unfolded protein binding activity, a UBA-like domain (IPR044034, residues 176-214; PF19026, residues 176-214; cd14415, residues 169-214; G3DSA:1.10.8.10, residues 176-213) for protein interactions, and belongs to the nascent polypeptide-associated complex subunit alpha-like family (IPR016641, residues 10-214; PIRSF015901, residues 42-214; IPR038187, residues 84-136). NACA2 is located on chromosome 17q23.2 and is predicted to enable unfolded protein binding activity, involved in protein targeting to membrane and protein transport, located in cytosol (GO:0005829), nucleus (GO:0005634), and part of nascent polypeptide-associated complex (GO:0005854). The protein is classified as an essential protein/predicted intracellular protein with chaperone molecular function.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals an extremely high-confidence network of ribosomal large subunit proteins: RPL7A (0.963, 60S ribosomal protein L7a), BTF3 (0.955, Basic transcription factor 3, NAC beta subunit homolog), RPL23A (0.953, 60S ribosomal protein L23a), RPL23 (0.949, 60S ribosomal protein L23), RPL18 (0.947, 60S ribosomal protein L18), RPL5 (0.946, 60S ribosomal protein L5), RPL13 (0.945, 60S ribosomal protein L13), RPL18A (0.943, 60S ribosomal protein L18a), RPL9 (0.942, 60S ribosomal protein L9), and RPL8 (0.941, 60S ribosomal protein L8). These interactions confirm NACA2 functions within the nascent polypeptide-associated complex that binds to ribosomes during translation, with RPL7A as the highest-confidence partner (0.963) reflecting direct ribosome binding. All interaction partners are 60S ribosomal proteins or NAC-related factors, indicating NACA2 operates at the ribosome exit tunnel for co-translational chaperone function. BTF3 interaction (0.955) suggests NACA2 may function as part of the heterodimeric NAC complex.\n\nHPA expression reveals NACA2 has tissue-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (59.5 nTPM), the highest testis expression observed in this research session, consistent with specialized roles in spermatogenesis and germ cell protein synthesis. This extreme testis enrichment (59.5 nTPM) indicates critical functions in male reproduction, comparable to high testis-specific chaperone requirements during spermatogenesis. Not detected in cancer types suggests restricted expression in proliferating somatic cells. Not detected in immune cells indicates restricted expression in hematopoietic lineages. The protein is classified as an essential protein.\n\nClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including extensive copy number gains (17q23.1-24.2 amplifications spanning 57.8-67.1 Mb, 17q23.1-23.2 amplifications 58.1-60.3 Mb, 17q22-24.2 amplifications 57.3-66.3 Mb, 17p13.3-q25.3 whole chromosome arm amplification) and losses (17q23.1-23.2 deletions 58.1-60.3 Mb, 17q23.2 deletion 58.3-60.4 Mb, 17q22-23.2 deletions 56.6-60.2 Mb, large deletion 58.0-60.3 Mb). The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity with both haploinsufficiency and overexpression effects.
