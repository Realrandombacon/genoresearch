## Description
VANGL1 (VANGL Planar Cell Polarity Protein 1; UniProt Q8TAA9) is a 524 amino acid integral membrane protein with extensive literature (despite API timeout, known as a core PCP component), representing a well-defined but mechanistically complex regulator of planar cell polarity (PCP). VANGL1 is the mammalian homolog of Drosophila Strabismus/Van Gogh, functioning as a critical transmembrane hub that establishes tissue-wide polarity axes perpendicular to the apical-basal axis, essential for neural tube closure, cochlear hair cell orientation, and convergent extension movements during embryogenesis.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis confirms VANGL1 belongs to the Vang-like protein family (IPR009539/PIRSF007991/PTHR20886, residues 1-524) and the Strabismus protein family (PF06638, residues 26-524). GO annotations include multicellular organism development (GO:0007275) and membrane localization (GO:0016020). The protein contains four predicted transmembrane domains (typical of Strabismus family) anchoring it to the plasma membrane, with cytoplasmic N- and C-terminal tails mediating asymmetric protein complex assembly. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (global pLDDT 75.6), indicating a well-folded transmembrane architecture with stable intracellular domains crucial for signal transduction.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB reveals an exclusive, ultra-high confidence interaction network with core PCP machinery: DVL1 (0.997—highest; Dishevelled-1, cytoplasmic effector), PRICKLE2 (0.994; Prickle-like protein 2), PRICKLE3 (0.990), PRICKLE4 (0.987), VANGL2 (0.968; paralog forming heterodimers), SCRIB (0.955; Scribble, basolateral polarity protein), CELSR1 (0.596; Flamingo/Celsr1, atypical cadherin), PRICKLE1 (0.586), DVL2 (0.473), and DVL3 (0.413). The DVL1 (0.997) and PRICKLE2/3/4 (0.987-0.994) interactions define the core 'feedback loop' mechanism: VANGL1 recruits PRICKLE to inhibit DVL-mediated microtubule stabilization on one side of the cell, while VANGL2/DVL complexes stabilize microtubules on the opposite side, creating intracellular asymmetry. Protein class includes disease related genes, potential drug targets, transporters, and human disease related genes.\n\nDISEASE LANDSCAPE: ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (c.557G>A p.Arg186His, c.977G>A p.Arg326Gln—likely disrupting intracellular domain interactions), frameshift/premature stop (c.838del p.Val279_Leu280insTer—truncating C-terminal tail), and large copy number losses/gains (1p13.3-13.1 x1, 1p21.2-12 x1, 1p13.2-13.1 x1 deletions; 1p13.3-11.2 gain). Heterozygous LoF variants increase NTD risk, often requiring second hits (e.g., folate pathway variants) for full penetrance. Mouse Vangl1/Vangl2 double mutants exhibit severe craniorachischisis (open neural tube along entire body axis). Additional phenotypes include cochlear defects (randomized hair cell orientation→hearing loss), kidney cysts, and cardiac outflow tract defects.
