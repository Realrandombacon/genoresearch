TMEM141 (Gene ID: 84277, UniProt Q96I45) is a 108 amino acid small transmembrane protein located on chromosome 9q34.3 (NC_000009.12). This gene encodes a predicted membrane protein with dual subcellular localization at cell junctions and mitochondria—suggesting roles in intercellular adhesion signaling coupled to mitochondrial metabolism or apoptosis regulation.
