## Description
MAP3K9 (Mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase kinase 9, also known as MLK4, UniProt P80192) is a 1104 aa serine/threonine protein kinase belonging to the mixed lineage kinase (MLK) family that activates JNK and p38 MAPK stress response pathways. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture characteristic of MLK family kinases: an N-terminal SH3 domain (IPR001452, PF00018, positions 52-116; G3DSA:2.30.30.40, positions 54-122; cd12059, positions 56-113) for protein-protein interactions, followed by a serine/threonine kinase catalytic domain (IPR000719, PF00069, positions 144-412; IPR001245, positions 144-403; G3DSA:1.10.510.10, positions 225-456; cd14145, positions 137-406) with a defined active site motif (IPR008271, positions 264-276). AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 56.9), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered catalytic domains and extensive intrinsically disordered regions typical of signaling scaffolds. HPA data confirms brain-enriched expression (16.6 nTPM, tissue enhanced) with nucleoplasm and nucleoli localization, classified within apoptosis, stress response, and transcription regulation biological processes. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with JNK pathway components and calcium signaling: NCS1 (0.621, neuronal calcium sensor), CDC42 (0.612, Rho GTPase upstream activator), MAPK8/JNK1 (0.584, direct downstream substrate), ARMC10 (0.535), HPCAL1 (0.519, calcium-binding protein), CLINT1 (0.456), JUN (0.454, AP-1 transcription factor downstream of JNK), BCL2 (0.453, apoptosis regulator), MYC (0.449, oncogene), SRF (0.426, serum response factor). ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 14q11.2-32.33 affecting MAP3K9 locus. Literature (81 papers) establishes MAP3K9 roles in: (1) gastric cancer cisplatin resistance via circUGGT2/miR-186-3p/MAP3K9 axis (2024, 28 citations), (2) esophageal squamous cell carcinoma proliferation/metastasis via circ_DLG1 upregulation (2025), (3) glioblastoma invasion/migration inhibition via CBLB-mediated ubiquitination regulating MAPK-p38 pathway (2025), (4) hepatocellular carcinoma risk gene (multi-ancestry GWAS 2025), and (5) bovine intramuscular adipogenesis via MAP3K9/JNK/CCND1 axis. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: MAP3K9 serves as a stress-responsive kinase scaffold integrating calcium signaling (NCS1, HPCAL1) and Rho GTPase signals (CDC42) to activate JNK/p38 cascades, regulating apoptosis, cell migration, and chemoresistance in cancer. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The SH3 domain mediates protein-protein interactions with upstream regulators (CDC42) and scaffolding proteins, while the kinase domain phosphorylates MKK4/7 leading to JNK activation and JUN/AP-1 transcription. The low pLDDT score suggests extensive disordered regions functioning as flexible scaffolds for signalosome assembly. In gastric cancer, MAP3K9 upregulation confers cisplatin resistance. In glioblastoma, CBLB-mediated ubiquitination targets MAP3K9 for degradation, inhibiting invasion. The 10 ClinVar CNVs suggest MAP3K9 dosage sensitivity may contribute to disease phenotypes.
