## Description
VMO1 (Vitelline Membrane Outer Layer Protein 1 Homolog) is a 202-amino acid predicted secreted protein with evolutionary origins in egg vitelline membrane formation, now functioning in human immune regulation and tumor suppression. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the VMO1 family with domain IPR005515 (Vitelline membrane outer layer protein I, positions 11-201), PF03762 (VOM1 repeat, 35-201), cd00220 (VMO-I domain, 35-201), and superfamily IPR036706, indicating a stable β-sheet rich extracellular structure. STRING interactions reveal associations with cilia and microtubule-related proteins: EFHC1 (0.508, microtubule-binding protein linked to juvenile myoclonic epilepsy), TMEM200A (0.465), SPEF1 (0.460, sperm flagellar protein), TMEM252 (0.420), CHID1 (0.417, chitinase-like immune regulator), and STYXL1 (0.409, pseudophosphatase), suggesting potential roles in cellular organization and immune modulation. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression in pituitary gland (29.2 nTPM) and lymphoid tissue (22.8 nTPM), with immune cell enrichment in blood and classification as a predicted secreted protein. ClinVar contains 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on chromosome 17p13.3-13.2, though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked as '?' for most variants). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q7Z5L0-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 82.1), indicating a well-folded secreted protein. With only 35 papers, VMO1 represents a 'gray' gene transitioning from dark: while its avian ortholog is well-characterized in egg biology, human VMO1's specific physiological functions, receptor interactions, and contribution to pituitary/immune disorders remain largely unexplored despite emerging evidence for tumor suppressor activity in lung adenocarcinoma via methylation-regulated expression and C-mannosylation-dependent stability.
