## Description
COX4I2 (Cytochrome c Oxidase Subunit 4 Isoform 2, mitochondrial, UniProt Q96KJ9) is a 171 aa nuclear-encoded subunit of cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV) belonging to the cytochrome c oxidase subunit IV family that functions in mitochondrial electron transport and oxygen sensing. The molecular architecture includes a mitochondrial targeting sequence at the N-terminus and conserved cytochrome c oxidase subunit IV domain (IPR004203/PF02936, positions 39-170; cd00922 Cyt_c_Oxidase_IV, positions 39-164) with three signature motifs (positions 41-54, 85-103, 126-139) critical for heme-copper oxidase assembly and function. Literature (92 papers) establishes COX4I2 roles in: (1) EBF1-COX4I2 signaling axis promoting myofibroblast-like phenotype in cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) and immunosuppressive microenvironment in colorectal cancer (2024, 11 citations), (2) SIRT3-COX4I2 axis reprogramming mitochondrial respiratory chain to attenuate osteoarthritis progression (2023, 79 citations), (3) Rpl13a snoRNAs downregulating smooth muscle cell COX4I2 to promote neointimal hyperplasia via ROS modulation (2025, 1 citation), (4) COX4I2 exosomal transfer from CAFs as therapeutic target in osteosarcoma ferroptosis (2025), (5) hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF-1α) directly activating COX4I2 transcription under low oxygen to replace COX4I1 and optimize respiratory efficiency. HPA confirms tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in placenta (108.4 nTPM) and lung (48.5 nTPM), consistent with hypoxic environments requiring optimized oxygen utilization, plus cancer-enhanced pattern indicating oncogenic role. STRING interactions reveal Complex IV and respiratory chain network: COX5A (0.999), COX5B (0.994) - cytochrome c oxidase subunits 5A/5B; COX6A1 (0.936), COX6A2 (0.988), COX7C (0.991) - additional Complex IV subunits; UQCRFS1 (0.993), CYC1 (0.993), UQCRC2 (0.992), UQCRQ (0.992), UQCRH (0.982) - Complex III (ubiquinol-cytochrome c reductase) subunits, reflecting respiratory supercomplex organization. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Gly31Arg in mitochondrial targeting sequence, p.Arg161Leu in catalytic domain), in-frame deletion (p.Phe112del), and multiple CNVs at 20q11.21-11.23, though specific disease phenotypes remain under investigation. AlphaFold shows high-confidence structure (pLDDT 79.6) indicating well-folded four-helix bundle typical of cytochrome c oxidase subunits with stable transmembrane topology. Conservation: highly conserved across vertebrates with orthologs in mouse, rat, and other mammals. Functional hypothesis: COX4I2 functions as the hypoxia-inducible isoform of cytochrome c oxidase subunit IV that replaces constitutively expressed COX4I1 under low oxygen conditions to optimize Complex IV assembly, electron transfer efficiency, and ROS production. The molecular mechanism involves HIF-1α binding to hypoxia response elements in the COX4I2 promoter, inducing transcription while simultaneously activating LONP1 protease to degrade COX4I1. COX4I2-containing Complex IV exhibits altered kinetic properties with lower Km for oxygen and reduced ROS generation, enabling cellular adaptation to hypoxia. In placenta (physiologically hypoxic) and lung (variable oxygen), COX4I2 ensures efficient respiration. In cancer, COX4I2 upregulation in CAFs promotes myofibroblast differentiation, immunosuppression, and tumor progression through metabolic reprogramming. The p.Gly31Arg variant in the mitochondrial targeting sequence may impair mitochondrial import, while p.Arg161Leu in the catalytic domain could disrupt heme binding or subunit interactions. COX4I2 represents a metabolic switch protein linking oxygen sensing to mitochondrial respiration with emerging roles in cancer, cardiovascular disease, and osteoarthritis.
