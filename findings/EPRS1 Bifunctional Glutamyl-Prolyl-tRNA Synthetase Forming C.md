## Description
EPRS1 (Glutamyl-Prolyl-tRNA Synthetase 1, also known as EPRS, GLUPRORS, PARS, QPRS, UniProt P07814) is a massive 1512-amino acid bifunctional aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase functioning as the only known enzyme capable of charging both glutamate and proline tRNA species through its dual catalytic domains, serving as a core structural and functional component of the multi-tRNA synthetase complex (MSC) while also executing non-canonical signaling functions through AKT-dependent nuclear translocation that activates PARP1 in breast cancer cells and drives T cell proliferation in tubulointerstitial nephritis-induced fibrosis. The protein architecture features modular domain organization with N-terminal glutaminyl-tRNA synthetase catalytic core domain (cd00807 aa 197-506) enabling glutamate tRNA aminoacylation, central WEPRS_RNA binding domains (cd00936 aa 753-802, 826-875, 904-952) mediating tRNA anticodon recognition and MSC assembly, C-terminal prolyl-tRNA synthetase core architecture (cd00778 aa 1023-1286) catalyzing proline tRNA charging, prolyl-tRNA synthetase anticodon-binding zinc finger domain (cd00862 aa 1292-1512) ensuring tRNA specificity, glutathione S-transferase C-terminal-like alpha helical domain (cd10309 aa 74-157) potentially mediating protein-protein interactions, multiple RNA-binding S15/NS1 domains (G3DSA:1.10.287.10 aa 749-805, 825-878, 895-952) for tRNA substrate recognition, HUPs domain superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.620 aa 172-507) characteristic of class I aaRS enzymes, anticodon-binding domain fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.800), Bira bifunctional protein domain 2 (G3DSA:3.30.930.10 aa 1016-1296) linking GluRS and ProRS activities, and C-terminal ProRS domain (G3DSA:3.30.110.30 aa 1424-1511), with GO term annotations for aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase activity (GO:0004817), ligase molecular function (GO:0016874), RNA-binding capability (GO:0003723), multifunctional enzyme classification, protein biosynthesis (GO:0006412), and translation regulation (GO:0006417).
