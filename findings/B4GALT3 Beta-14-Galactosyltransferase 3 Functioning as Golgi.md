## Description
B4GALT3 (Beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase 3, O60512) encodes a 393 aa type II transmembrane glycosyltransferase functioning in the Golgi apparatus to catalyze the transfer of galactose from UDP-galactose to N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) residues during poly-N-acetyllactosamine core structure formation in glycoproteins and glycosphingolipids. Structural architecture reveals a highly conserved glycosyltransferase organization with exceptional AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 88.2, high confidence) indicating a well-folded stable 3D structure: Beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase family domain (IPR003859/PF02709/PF13733/PTHR19300, residues 30-347) with GO terms for glycosyltransferase activity (GO:0016757) and carbohydrate metabolic process (GO:0005975) forming the catalytic core, Galactosyltransferase N-terminal domain (IPR027995, residues 77-210) and C-terminal domain (IPR027791, residues 215-292) establishing the characteristic bi-lobed architecture of GT-A fold glycosyltransferases, Nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferases superfamily (IPR029044/G3DSA:3.90.550.10/SSF53448, residues 70-346) confirming evolutionary relationship to sugar nucleotide-dependent enzymes, and Beta-4-Galactosyltransferase CDD domain (cd00899, residues 122-341) defining substrate specificity for beta-1,4 linkage formation.
