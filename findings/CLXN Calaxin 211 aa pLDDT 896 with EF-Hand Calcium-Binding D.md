## Description
CLXN (Calaxin, UniProt Q9HAE3) is a 211 amino acid calcium-binding protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 12 literature references despite critical function as an outer dynein arm (ODA) docking factor essential for motile cilia assembly and function.
