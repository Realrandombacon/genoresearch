# Chromosome 15q11.2 Immunoglobulin Domain Pseudogene with 13 Pathogenic Copy Number Variants in Prader-Willi/Angelman Imprinting Critical Region

## Description
LOC107983983 is an uncharacterized putative V-set and immunoglobulin domain-containing-like protein (IGHV4OR15-8) on chromosome 15q11.2 (NC_000015.10, positions 22185492-22183502, ~2 kb region) with significant clinical importance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (variants include 2579285, 151938, 151934, 147837, 59369, 58577, 58574, 58569, 58568, 58539), all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 15q11.1-13.3 region. These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, monosomy) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy/tetrasomy) ranging from focal alterations (~5-10 Mb) to whole-arm changes (12+ Mb). The 15q11-q13 chromosomal location is clinically exceptional as this region constitutes the most extensively studied imprinted domain in the human genome, containing the Prader-Willi syndrome (PWS) and Angelman syndrome (AS) critical regions. This locus lies within the 15q11.2 breakpoint cluster region (BP1-BP2) associated with microdeletion syndromes. The region contains multiple parent-of-origin expressed genes including SNRPN (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide N, paternal expression, PWS), UBE3A (ubiquitin protein ligase E3A, maternal expression, Angelman syndrome), GABRB3 (GABA-A receptor beta3 subunit, neuronal inhibition, epilepsy), GABRA5 (GABA-A receptor alpha5), GABRG3 (GABA-A receptor gamma3), NDN (necdin, growth suppression, paternal), MAGEL2 (MAGE-like protein 2, circadian regulation, paternal), and MKRN3 (makorin ring finger 3, puberty timing, paternal). Functional hypothesis: LOC107983983 likely represents a processed immunoglobulin pseudogene or regulatory element embedded within the 15q11-q13 imprinting control domain. The IGHV4OR15-8 designation suggests homology to immunoglobulin variable heavy chain regions, potentially indicating an ancient V(D)J recombination event captured in germline DNA. The 13 pathogenic CNVs indicate dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations disrupt normal imprinting regulation through chromatin boundary disruption or enhancer hijacking. Disease mechanism likely involves disruption of the PWS imprinting center (PWS-IC) or AS imprinting center (AS-IC) leading to loss of parent-of-origin-specific DNA methylation patterns. Deletions cause Prader-Willi syndrome (paternal 15q11-q13 loss: hypotonia, hyperphagia, obesity, intellectual disability, hypogonadism) or Angelman syndrome (maternal 15q11-q13 loss: severe intellectual disability, ataxia, epilepsy, inappropriate laughter). Duplications cause 15q duplication syndrome (autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability, seizures). The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (13 variants) and neurodevelopmental imprinting disorder association suggests this locus participates in chromatin domain organization controlling monoallelic expression of imprinted genes through CTCF-mediated insulation or long non-coding RNA regulation.
