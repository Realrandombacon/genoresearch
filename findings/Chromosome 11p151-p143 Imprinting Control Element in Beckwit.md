## Description
LOC102723370 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 11p15.1-p14.3 (NC_000011.10, positions 21753205-22119898, ~367 kb region) within the most extensively studied imprinted domain in the human genome. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. ClinVar contains no pathogenic variants for this specific locus. However, the 11p15 chromosomal location is clinically exceptional as this region constitutes the Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome (BWS) and Silver-Russell syndrome (SRS) imprinted critical region, containing multiple parent-of-origin expressed genes including IGF2 (insulin-like growth factor 2, fetal growth, paternal expression), H19 (long non-coding RNA, tumor suppressor, maternal expression), H19-ICR (imprinting control region, differentially methylated domain), PHLDA2 (pleckstrin homology-like domain A2, growth restriction, maternal expression), KCNQ1 (potassium voltage-gated channel, cardiac repolarization, paternal expression of KCNQ1OT1 lncRNA), CDKN1C (cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor 1C, cell cycle inhibition, maternal expression), and TSSC4 (tumor suppressing subtransferable candidate 4). Functional hypothesis: LOC102723370 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary within the 11p15 imprinting control domain, potentially functioning as an enhancer, silencer, or insulator element controlling parent-of-origin-specific expression of IGF2 and H19 through CTCF-mediated chromatin looping and differential DNA methylation at the H19-ICR. The absence of protein-coding features combined with location in the most dosage-sensitive imprinted region suggests this locus participates in epigenetic programming of fetal growth regulation. Disease mechanism likely involves dysregulation of imprinting control leading to biallelic IGF2 expression (causing Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome with overgrowth, macroglossia, omphalocele, and embryonal tumor predisposition) or reduced IGF2 expression (causing Silver-Russell syndrome with intrauterine growth restriction and postnatal growth failure). The 11p15 region shows frequent copy number variations, uniparental disomy, and methylation defects in these imprinting disorders.
