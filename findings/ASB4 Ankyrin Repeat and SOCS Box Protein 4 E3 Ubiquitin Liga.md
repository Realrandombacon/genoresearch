## Description
ASB4 (Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box-containing Protein 4) is a 426 amino acid substrate recognition component of ECS (Elongin-Cullin-SOCS box) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes that targets specific proteins for proteasomal degradation, serving as a critical regulator of trophoblast differentiation, metabolic homeostasis, and cancer stem cell antigenicity. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic bipartite architecture including N-terminal ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110, PF12796, PS50088, PS50297, residues 74-321) with multiple repeat units (74-138, 139-249, 251-283) for protein-protein interactions and substrate recognition, ankyrin repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR036770, G3DSA:1.25.40.20, residues 13-321) providing structural scaffold for target binding, C-terminal SOCS box domain (IPR001496, PF07525, PS50225, cd03723, residues 379-420) for E3 ligase complex assembly, and SOCS box-like domain superfamily (IPR036036, G3DSA:1.10.750.20, residues 374-421) mediating ubiquitination machinery recruitment. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9Y574-F1) predicts very high confidence (pLDDT 92.7) across the 426 residue sequence, indicating an exceptionally well-folded bipartite protein with stable ankyrin repeat stack and SOCS box suitable for simultaneous substrate binding and E3 ligase recruitment. HPA data classifies ASB4 as a predicted intracellular protein involved in Ubl conjugation pathway, with tissue-enhanced expression (adrenal gland: 44.5 nTPM, skeletal muscle: 32.0 nTPM), cancer-enhanced distribution, detected in some tissues, cell type-enhanced specificity, and implications in metabolic and pregnancy-related disorders. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling to the ECS E3 ubiquitin ligase machinery: CUL5 (Cullin-5, 0.961) and CUL2 (0.940) as scaffold proteins; ELOB (Elongin-B, 0.929) and ELOC (Elongin-C, 0.923) as adaptor proteins linking SOCS box to cullins; RBX1 (RING-box protein 1, 0.914) as the RING finger component recruiting E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes; APPBP2 (Amyloid beta precursor protein-binding protein 2, 0.813) for substrate presentation; COMMD1 (Copper metabolism MURR1 domain-containing protein 1, 0.698) as a potential substrate; RNF7 (RING finger protein 7, 0.603) as related E3 ligase; KLHDC2 (Kelch domain-containing protein 2, 0.559) as another substrate receptor; and ZER1 (Meiosis-specific protein ZER1, 0.427). ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.979-1G>A, c.188-3C>T), large copy number losses on 7q21.3, 7q21.2-22.1, 7q11.23-21.3 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 74-100 Mb), deletions, and complex rearrangements, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in metabolic disorder or pregnancy complication susceptibility. Literature (58 papers) establishes ASB4 as a context-dependent metabolic and developmental regulator: ASB4 variants are linked to human obesity with ASB4 modulating central melanocortinergic neurons and calcitonin signaling to control satiety and glucose homeostasis; insulin elevates ID2 expression in trophoblasts and aggravates preeclampsia in obese ASB4-null mice establishing ASB4 as a protective factor promoting trophoblast differentiation; ASB4 antigen on cancer stem cells serves as a target for CTL immunotherapy of colorectal cancer with adoptive CTL therapy demonstrating CSCs are the minimal necessary target to control tumor growth; diet-induced obesity aggravates preeclampsia pathogenesis through ASB4-dependent mechanisms; and ASB4 functions as an oxygen-sensitive regulator in placental development. As a well-studied E3 ligase substrate receptor with defined roles in metabolism and reproduction, ASB4 represents a critical node connecting ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation to trophoblast lineage commitment, energy balance, and cancer stem cell immunogenicity through targeted destruction of differentiation inhibitors like ID2.
