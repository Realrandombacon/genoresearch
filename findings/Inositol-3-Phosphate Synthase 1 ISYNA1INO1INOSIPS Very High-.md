## Description
ISYNA1 (UniProt Q9NPH2), also known as INO1, INOS, IPS, or IPS-1, encodes a 558 aa very high-confidence metabolic enzyme functioning as the rate-limiting catalyst in myo-inositol biosynthesis that converts glucose-6-phosphate to myo-inositol-3-phosphate through integrated NAD-binding Rossmann fold-mediated isomerization mechanisms critical for phospholipid biosynthesis, osmotic stress response, and lithium-sensitive signaling pathways.
