## Description
MTMR7 (Myotubularin-related protein 7, UniProt Q9Y216) is a 660 aa phosphoinositide phosphatase with a PH-GRAM domain (cd13344, positions 1-103) for membrane targeting and a catalytic protein tyrosine phosphatase domain (IPR003595, positions 264-432) containing the active site motif (IPR016130, positions 336-346). AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 83.6), indicating a stable, well-folded enzyme. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in retina (26.3 nTPM) and brain (11.6 nTPM), localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol. STRING interactions identify partners in phosphoinositide metabolism: MTMR9 (0.990, forms heterodimer), FIG4 (0.933, PI(3,5)P2 phosphatase), PIKFYVE (0.926, PI3P 5-kinase), INPP4B/A (0.912/0.910, phosphoinositide phosphatases), INPP5F (0.909), PIP4P1/2 (0.900), and PIK3C3 (0.820, PI3-kinase). ClinVar contains 83 pathogenic variants at 8p23.3-21.2. Literature (36 papers) shows MTMR7 suppresses pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cell proliferation in pulmonary hypertension via ERK/STAT3 inhibition, regulates vascular smooth muscle phenotypic switching via p62/mTORC1-mediated glucose metabolism, controls spermatogonial stem cell proliferation via FLNB targeting, and inhibits WNT signaling while promoting RAS degradation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: MTMR7 acts as a lipid signaling checkpoint that dephosphorylates PI3P and related phosphoinositides, controlling endosomal trafficking and mTORC1 activation in vascular and germ cells. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: MTMR7 forms a heterodimer with MTMR9 (STRING 0.990) to enhance catalytic activity. By converting PI3P to PI, MTMR7 reduces PI(3,5)P2 production (via PIKFYVE), dampening mTORC1 signaling and suppressing vascular smooth muscle proliferation. In spermatogonial stem cells, MTMR7 targets FLNB (filamin B) to regulate cytoskeletal dynamics and cell migration. The high retina/brain expression suggests specialized roles in neuronal and photoreceptor endosomal trafficking.
