# ZNF165: Testis-Enriched SCAN-Zinc Finger Transcriptional Regulator

**Date:** 2026-03-23T03:59:58.917496

**Quality Score:** 2.15/10  (E=3.0, D=1.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF165 encodes a 485-amino acid Krüppel-type zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt P49910) with a distinctive domain architecture: an N-terminal SCAN oligomerization domain (PF02023, IPR003309, residues 45-157) followed by three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 290-422). The SCAN domain mediates homo- and hetero-oligomerization with other SCAN-containing zinc finger proteins, enabling combinatorial DNA recognition. HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (18.5 nTPM) with nuclear speckle localization, consistent with its annotated role in spermatogenesis. ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 6p25.3-q27, though no specific monogenic disease is yet attributed to ZNF165 alone. The C2H2 zinc fingers (consensus CX2CX12HX3H) recognize GC-rich DNA sequences, positioning ZNF165 as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor or activator depending on cofactor recruitment. Conservation across vertebrates suggests functional constraint on both oligomerization and DNA-binding interfaces.
