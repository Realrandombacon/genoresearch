## Description
ZNF785 (zinc finger protein 785) encodes a 405 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt A8K8V0) with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (HPA: low tissue specificity, detected in all). The protein contains a canonical N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (InterPro IPR001909, IPR036051, Pfam PF01352, residues 29-100) followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (IPR013087, PF00096 at positions 172-194, 228-250, 256-278) that confer sequence-specific DNA recognition. AlphaFold prediction yields medium confidence structure (pLDDT 63.2), consistent with folded zinc finger domains connected by flexible linkers. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses at 16p11.2 and a splice site variant (c.334+14A>G), indicating dosage sensitivity. The KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors that assemble heterochromatin via histone methyltransferases (SETDB1) and DNA methyltransferases, establishing transcriptional silencing. As a member of the Ikaros C2H2-type zinc finger family (IPR050589), ZNF785 likely recognizes GC-rich promoter/enhancer motifs and recruits chromatin remodeling complexes to repress target genes involved in cell cycle regulation or differentiation. The ubiquitous expression pattern suggests housekeeping transcriptional repression functions, while pathogenic CNVs at 16p11.2 are associated with neurodevelopmental phenotypes, implicating ZNF785 dosage in brain development. Mechanistically, ZNF785 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding scaffold that tethers epigenetic silencing machinery to genomic loci, maintaining transcriptional homeostasis.
