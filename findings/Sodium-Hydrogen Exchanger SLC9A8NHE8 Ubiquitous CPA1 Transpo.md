## Description
SLC9A8 encodes a 581 amino acid sodium/hydrogen exchanger 8 (NHE8, UniProt Q9Y2E8) belonging to the solute carrier family 9 (SLC9) and CPA1 cation/proton antiporter family. InterPro analysis reveals canonical Na+/H+ exchanger architecture including IPR004709 (Na+/H+ exchanger, aa 63-490), IPR006153 (Cation/H+ exchanger transmembrane domain, aa 71-471), IPR018422 (CPA1 family, aa 46-564), PF00999 (Na+/H+ exchanger transmembrane, aa 71-471), PR01084 (Na+/H+ exchanger signature motifs), PTHR10110 (SLC9 family, aa 46-564), and TIGR00840 (NHE3 family, aa 63-490). GO annotations include sodium:proton antiporter activity (GO:0015385), sodium ion transport (GO:0006814), and pH regulation (GO:0006885). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9Y2E8-F1) shows high confidence (pLDDT 76.5) across full 581 aa, indicating stable folded transporter with 12 transmembrane helices typical of CPA1 family. HPA expression reveals ubiquitous detection in all tissues with low tissue specificity and group-enriched single cell type specificity, consistent with housekeeping ion homeostasis function. Critically, ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 20q13 copy number variations (gains and losses), though specific diseases remain unannotated. Literature search returned 33 papers with studies linking SLC9A8 to acute pancreatitis attenuation via lysosome inhibition and autophagy flux regulation, colorectal cancer metastasis suppression through IL6-JAK1/STAT3 pathway, retinal pigmented epithelium function, and intestinal microbiota β-diversity regulation via mGWAS. The combination of CPA1 transporter domains, ubiquitous expression, NHERF regulatory protein interactions, clinical CNV burden, and emerging autophagy/cancer literature suggests SLC9A8 functions as a plasma membrane Na+/H+ exchanger regulating intracellular pH, autophagy-lysosome pathway, and epithelial ion homeostasis, with copy number alterations potentially contributing to gastrointestinal and metabolic disorders.
