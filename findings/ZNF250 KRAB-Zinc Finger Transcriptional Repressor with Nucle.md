## Description
ZNF250 (zinc finger protein 250, aliases ZFP647/ZNF647) encodes a 560 amino acid protein (UniProt P15622) localized to chromosome 8q24.3. InterPro domain analysis reveals a canonical KRAB repressor architecture: N-terminal KRAB box domain (IPR001909/PF01352, aa 22-93) for transcriptional repression, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096, aa 199-282) enabling sequence-specific DNA recognition. This domain organization classifies ZNF250 as a Krüppel-associated box zinc finger protein (KZFP), a large family of epigenetic silencers that recruit heterochromatin machinery. STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners with medium confidence: ZNF549 (0.653), SPINT1 (0.591), TRIM28 (0.557), ZNF329 (0.554), ZNF16 (0.551), KAT14 (0.527), ZNF773 (0.493), ZC4H2 (0.472), ZNF548 (0.471), and RPS7 (0.456). The TRIM28 (KAP1) interaction is particularly significant—TRIM28 is the canonical co-receptor for KRAB domains, recruiting SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 to establish H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. Human Protein Atlas data shows nuclear speckle and cytosolic localization with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), consistent with housekeeping transcriptional regulation functions. ClinVar documents 56 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (8q24.3 amplifications) and splice variants (c.346+9A>G, c.284-5del), though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. The KRAB-TRIM28 interaction mechanism suggests ZNF250 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor targeting endogenous retroviral elements or developmental genes, with loss-of-function potentially disrupting epigenetic silencing and genome stability.
