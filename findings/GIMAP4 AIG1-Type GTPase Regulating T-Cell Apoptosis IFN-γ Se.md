## Description
GIMAP4 encodes GTPase IMAP Family Member 4 (UniProt Q9NUV9), a 329-amino acid intracellular GTPase of the AIG1 (AvrRpt2-Induced Gene 1) family functioning in T-cell survival, apoptosis regulation, and cytokine secretion during Th cell differentiation. The protein contains a canonical AIG1-type guanine nucleotide-binding domain (IPR006703, positions 28-238; PF04548, positions 31-238; cd01852, positions 31-231) characteristic of immunity-associated GTPases, classified within the P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (IPR027417, positions 25-253; G3DSA:3.40.50.300, positions 29-253; SSF52540, positions 25-235) with conserved G domain profile (PS51720, positions 28-230) and GTPase GIMA/IAN/Toc family membership (IPR045058, positions 30-287; PTHR10903, positions 30-287) indicating GTP-dependent conformational switching for immune signaling regulation. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.5, residues 1-329) indicating stable folding with well-defined GTPase core suitable for nucleotide binding and hydrolysis. With 62 publications, GIMAP4 has emerging roles in immunity and cancer: GIMAP4 accelerates T-cell death and serves as apoptosis regulator (2006, 85 cit; 2007, 33 cit), tubulin- and actin-associating GIMAP4 is required for IFN-γ secretion during Th cell differentiation (2014, 53 cit), prognostic value in lung adenocarcinoma tumor microenvironment promoting immune cell infiltration (2022, 13 cit), immune-related prognostic biomarker associated with remodeling in cervical cancer tumor microenvironment (2021, 27 cit), natural hypomorphic variant affects apoptosis regulation.
