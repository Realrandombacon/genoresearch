## Description
SLC25A42 encodes a 318 aa mitochondrial inner membrane coenzyme A transporter (UniProt Q86VD7), functioning as a critical importer of free CoA into the mitochondrial matrix for acyl-CoA metabolism and energy production. InterPro analysis reveals canonical mitochondrial carrier architecture with three homologous domains (IPR002067, IPR023395, PF00153; aa 26-316), characteristic triple-repeat transmembrane structure (IPR018108; aa 31-120, 129-216, 224-314), and SLC25A16-like family membership (IPR002167), consistent with nucleotide/cofactor translocation mechanisms. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.8), indicating an exceptionally stable, well-folded carrier with canonical 6-transmembrane helix topology per repeat. HPA shows liver-enriched expression (76.0 nTPM, tissue enhanced) with ubiquitous detection, mitochondrial localization, and blood cell group enrichment, consistent with its role in hepatic fatty acid oxidation and hematopoietic metabolism. STRING interactions reveal connections to DNA replication/repair machinery (RPL18 0.903, PCNA 0.740, RFC3 0.738, MCM7 0.613), nucleotide metabolism (TYMS 0.755, RRM1 0.687, GART 0.611), and pantothenate/CoA biosynthesis (PPCS 0.564), suggesting coordination between mitochondrial CoA availability and nuclear DNA synthesis. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic variants (splice sites c.187+1G>A, c.82-2A>T, c.380+2T>A; frameshift c.163del p.Asp55fs; nonsense c.309C>G p.Tyr103Ter; in-frame deletions c.828_845del, and 19p CNVs), causing a recently described mitochondrial disorder with expanding phenotypic spectrum. Recent literature (36 papers) demonstrates SLC25A42 promotes gastric cancer growth by conferring ferroptosis resistance through enhancing CPT2-mediated fatty acid oxidation (2025, 7 citations), reveals dual mitochondrial CoA import roles with SLC25A16 (2025, 3 citations), and documents new clinical cases with pantothenic acid supplementation showing in vitro CoA level improvement (2025). As the primary mitochondrial CoA importer, SLC25A42 supplies substrate for β-oxidation, TCA cycle, and ketogenesis. CoA deficiency impairs fatty acid catabolism, causing lipid accumulation and metabolic crisis. The ferroptosis resistance mechanism links SLC25A42 to cancer metabolism—enhanced FAO consumes polyunsaturated fatty acids, reducing lipid peroxidation substrates. The 16 ClinVar variants indicate strong disease causality, with emerging genotype-phenotype correlations and potential therapeutic intervention via pantothenate supplementation to boost intracellular CoA pools. SLC25A42 represents a critical metabolic node connecting mitochondrial energetics, cancer metabolism, and inherited metabolic disease.
