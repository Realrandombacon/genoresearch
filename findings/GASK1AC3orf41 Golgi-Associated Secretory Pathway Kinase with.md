## Description
GASK1A (alias C3orf41, FAM198A, golgi associated kinase 1A) encodes a 575 amino acid partially structured vesicular protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 68.4) containing a FAM198 family domain (IPR029207, aa 2-573), FAM198 protein Pfam domain (PF15051, aa 253-572), and belonging to the PTHR15905 family (golgi-associated kinase 1B-related, aa 2-573). HPA shows low tissue specificity with detected in many tissues distribution, vesicles subcellular localization, cancer-enhanced specificity, predicted intracellular/secreted protein class, not detected in immune cells, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 3p26.3-14.3, 3p24.3-21.31, 3p26.3-q29, and 3p22.1-21.31 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with secretory pathway kinases and Golgi trafficking proteins: FAM20A (family with sequence similarity 20 member A, ER-Golgi intermediate compartment, biomineralization, 0.665), FAM20B (family with sequence similarity 20 member B, Golgi kinase, glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis, 0.644), FJX1 (four-jointed box 1, Golgi kinase, 0.620), RNF225 (ring finger protein 225, ubiquitination, 0.606), ZNF662 (zinc finger protein 662, transcriptional regulation, 0.506), HEXD (helicase with CXXC domain, chromatin remodeling, 0.448), FAM20C (family with sequence similarity 20 member C, Golgi casein kinase, secretory pathway phosphorylation, 0.445), ARMC7 (armadillo repeat containing 7, ciliogenesis, 0.419), OR4D6 (olfactory receptor 4D6, GPCR signaling, 0.418), and ALG10B (asparagine-linked glycosylation 10 homolog B, N-glycosylation, 0.418). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The FAM198 domain architecture combined with gene name "golgi associated kinase" and high-confidence interactions with FAM20A/B/C secretory pathway kinases strongly suggests GASK1A functions as a Golgi-localized kinase involved in secretory pathway protein phosphorylation and glycosylation regulation. FAM20A interaction (0.665) is mechanistically central—FAM20A forms a complex with FAM20C in the Golgi apparatus and regulates secretory pathway protein phosphorylation, particularly of biomineralization proteins and extracellular matrix components. FAM20B association (0.644) extends this role to glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis—FAM20B phosphorylates xylose kinase in the Golgi, initiating glycosaminoglycan chain synthesis. FJX1 interaction (0.620) reinforces Golgi kinase function—FJX1 is a Golgi-localized kinase phosphorylating protocadherins and regulating cell adhesion. FAM20C association (0.445) is particularly informative—FAM20C is the canonical Golgi casein kinase phosphorylating hundreds of secretory pathway proteins at S-x-E motifs, suggesting GASK1A may function as a FAM20C regulatory subunit or substrate kinase. ALG10B interaction (0.418) links to N-glycosylation—ALG10B catalyzes alpha-1,2-mannosyltransferase activity in the Golgi, suggesting GASK1A coordinates phosphorylation with glycosylation events. Vesicles subcellular localization indicates trafficking through secretory compartments, consistent with Golgi apparatus residence. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests upregulation in tumor cells, potentially through enhanced secretory pathway activity and protein secretion in rapidly proliferating cells. Low tissue specificity with detected in many pattern indicates ubiquitous expression across cell types, consistent with housekeeping secretory pathway function. Cell type-enhanced pattern indicates specific upregulation in secretory cell lineages. Moderate pLDDT score (68.4) indicates partially structured protein with flexible regions, consistent with kinase regulatory proteins requiring conformational adaptability for substrate recognition and complex assembly. Pathogenic 3p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting Golgi kinase activity, secretory pathway phosphorylation, and protein glycosylation, potentially contributing to 3p deletion syndrome (3p- syndrome) phenotypes including growth retardation, intellectual disability, and developmental defects through impaired protein secretion and extracellular matrix modification.
