# TMEM252: Ciliary-Associated Transmembrane Scaffold with Renal-Intestinal Expression and Disease-Linked CNVs

## Description
TMEM252 (Transmembrane Protein 252, UniProt Q8N6L7) encodes a 170-amino acid single-pass membrane protein belonging to the TMEM252 family (InterPro IPR031363, Pfam PF15664, Panther PTHR35682, residues 29-166). The protein localizes to nuclear speckles, plasma membrane, primary cilium, and basal body according to Human Protein Atlas subcellular mapping. RNA expression shows group-enriched tissue distribution with highest levels in kidney (33.3 nTPM) and intestine (20.7 nTPM), plus cancer-enriched expression patterns and immune cell enrichment in blood. STRING interaction network reveals 9 medium-confidence partners (scores 0.40-0.59): SMCO1 (0.591), SMR3B (0.507), CNDP1 (0.452), VMO1 (0.420), TMEM176A (0.412), EVA1B (0.410), PGM5 (0.410), LNX1 (0.404), and MPDZ (0.404). Notably, TMEM176A is a lysosomal membrane protein and MPDZ is a PDZ-domain scaffold at tight junctions, suggesting TMEM252 may function in membrane microdomain organization. ClinVar documents 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 9 copy number gains/losses spanning 9q21.11 where TMEM252 resides, indicating dosage sensitivity. The ciliary/basal body localization combined with renal expression suggests a potential role in primary cilium function in kidney epithelia, possibly affecting ciliary signaling or membrane protein trafficking. Interaction with PDZ scaffold MPDZ hints at involvement in cell junction organization. Chromosomal CNVs affecting TMEM252 dosage may disrupt ciliary function in kidney and intestinal epithelia, contributing to disease phenotypes.
