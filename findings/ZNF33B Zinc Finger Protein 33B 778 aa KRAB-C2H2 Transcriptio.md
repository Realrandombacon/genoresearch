## Description
ZNF33B (Zinc Finger Protein 33B, UniProt Q06732) is a 778 amino acid nuclear transcription factor with limited characterization (14 papers) that functions as a KRAB domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger protein (KRAB-ZFP) acting as a transcriptional repressor through TRIM28/KAP1 recruitment, with recently discovered roles in viral immunity where it facilitates Japanese encephalitis virus (JEV) replication by controlling HSPB1/8-mediated SUMOylation of viral nonstructural protein 5 and regulating m6A modification on Ifih1 and Irf3 transcripts to antagonize antiviral responses. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB box repression domain (IPR001909/PF01352 residues 11-83/11-52, GO:0006355 regulation of DNA-templated transcription) belonging to the KRAB domain superfamily (IPR036051 residues 8-68), followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096/PS00028 residues 329-351/357-379/385-407/320-353/354-381/382-410) arranged in tandem arrays typical of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors, belonging to the Krueppel C2H2-type Zinc Finger family (IPR050826 residues 12-495) and C2H2 superfamily (IPR036236 residues 297-422/421-478/477-534).
