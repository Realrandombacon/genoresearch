## Description
LRRC74B encodes a 392-amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein with a ribonuclease inhibitor-like fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, residues 39-366). The protein contains 3 canonical LRR motifs (IPR001611 at positions 105-128, 134-156, 191-211; PF13516) forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 84.4), indicating a well-folded stable structure.\n\nTissue expression (HPA) is enriched in reproductive tissues: fallopian tube (2.5 nTPM), choroid plexus (1.6 nTPM), and testis (1.2 nTPM), with RNA tissue specificity classified as "tissue enhanced." This suggests a specialized role in reproductive system development or function.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal 10 high-confidence partners: CDC20B (0.764), TMEM213 (0.742), LMNTD1 (0.561), FAM47E-STBD1 (0.557), RIMBP3C (0.547). CDC20B is a cell cycle regulator homolog, suggesting LRRC74B may participate in cell cycle control or mitotic processes in reproductive tissues.\n\nClinVar contains 156 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations (CNVs) in 22q11.21 region. This locus is associated with DiGeorge syndrome/velocardiofacial syndrome. The CNVs include both gains (trisomy) and losses (monosomy), indicating dosage sensitivity.\n\nMouse ortholog (Q14BP6) shows 99% sequence identity (391 aa vs 392 aa), indicating strong evolutionary conservation. The LRR domain architecture is preserved across vertebrates.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: LRRC74B functions as a protein-protein interaction scaffold in reproductive tissues, potentially regulating cell cycle progression through CDC20B interaction. The 22q11.21 CNV disease association suggests haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity contributes to DiGeorge syndrome phenotypes, particularly reproductive abnormalities. The LRR scaffold may organize signaling complexes in fallopian tube epithelium and testicular cells, with dosage-sensitive effects on development.
