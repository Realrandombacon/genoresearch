## Description
CCDC167 (alias C6orf129, HSPC265, coiled-coil domain containing 167) encodes a 97 amino acid well-folded cytosolic protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 87.8) containing a coiled-coil domain (IPR028194/PF15188, aa 11-93) and belonging to the PTHR31759 family (aa 1-97). HPA shows ubiquitous tissue distribution (detected in all) with low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, cytosol subcellular localization, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell pattern. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 6p25.3-21.1 and 6p22.1-q14.1 copy number gains/losses associated with chromosomal duplication/deletion syndromes. STRING interactions reveal network with ESCRT-III membrane scission and glycosylation regulators: CHMP7 (charged multivesicular body protein 7, ESCRT-III polymerization, 0.493), LRRTM4 (leucine rich repeat transmembrane neuronal 4, synaptic organization, 0.488), CCDC178 (coiled-coil domain containing 178, 0.465), PRKCSH (protein kinase C substrate 80K-H, N-glycan processing, 0.455), FN3KRP (fructosamine-3-kinase related protein, protein deglycation, 0.449), CCDC106 (coiled-coil domain containing 106, 0.448), SAC3D1 (SAC3 domain containing 1, histone pre-mRNA processing, 0.443), METTL26 (methyltransferase like 26, RNA methylation, 0.425), PKIA (protein kinase A inhibitor alpha, cAMP signaling, 0.425), and ARFGEF3 (ADP ribosylation factor guanine nucleotide exchange factor 3, vesicle trafficking, 0.407). Conservation across vertebrates indicates functional constraint. The coiled-coil domain architecture combined with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 87.8) and cytosolic localization strongly suggests CCDC167 functions as a cytosolic coiled-coil scaffold protein involved in ESCRT-III-mediated membrane scission and vesicle trafficking. CHMP7 interaction (0.493) places CCDC167 at the ESCRT-III machinery interface—CHMP7 initiates ESCRT-III polymerization at the nuclear envelope and endosomal membranes, driving membrane abscission during cytokinesis, nuclear envelope repair, and multivesicular body formation. The high pLDDT score (87.8) indicates a stable folded coiled-coil structure, consistent with scaffold proteins requiring defined helical interfaces for oligomerization and protein-protein interactions. PRKCSH association (0.455) links CCDC167 to N-glycan processing in the ER/Golgi, potentially coordinating vesicle trafficking with glycoprotein quality control. FN3KRP interaction (0.449) suggests coupling to protein deglycation pathways, potentially protecting cytosolic proteins from non-enzymatic glycation damage. ARFGEF3 association (0.407) indicates role in ARF GTPase activation for vesicle coat assembly, connecting ESCRT-III function to early secretory pathway trafficking. Ubiquitous expression indicates housekeeping function in membrane scission and vesicle trafficking across all cell types. Cell type-enhanced pattern suggests specialized upregulation in specific lineages, potentially reflecting differential membrane trafficking demands. Pathogenic 6p CNVs likely cause dosage effects disrupting ESCRT-III assembly, membrane scission, and vesicle trafficking, potentially contributing to 6p duplication/deletion syndrome phenotypes including developmental defects through impaired cytokinesis, nuclear envelope integrity, and endolysosomal sorting.
