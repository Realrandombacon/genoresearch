## Description
OR2D3 (Olfactory receptor 2D3, UniProt Q8NGH3) is a 330-amino acid G protein-coupled receptor located on chromosome 11p15.5 within the large olfactory receptor gene cluster. InterPro analysis confirms this as a canonical class A rhodopsin-like GPCR containing the olfactory receptor family domain (IPR000725/PF13853, aa 47-322), seven transmembrane helices characteristic of rhodopsin-like receptors (IPR017452, aa 57-306; G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, aa 31-330), and membership in the olfactory receptor subfamily 2D (cd15428, aa 54-317). The protein contains conserved GPCR signature motifs (PS00237, aa 126-142; PR00237/PR00245) critical for G protein coupling and odorant binding. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 86.9), indicating a well-folded stable 7-transmembrane bundle architecture typical of functional olfactory receptors ready for ligand binding and signal transduction. Human Protein Atlas shows no detection in standard tissue panels with cell type enhanced specificity, which is expected for olfactory receptors expressed exclusively in olfactory sensory neurons of the nasal epithelium not profiled in HPA. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners comprising both paralogous olfactory receptors and the canonical olfactory signal transduction cascade: OR10A4 (0.987, paralogous olfactory receptor), OR6A2 (0.790), OR10A2 (0.717), GNAL (0.714, olfactory-specific Gα subunit), ARRB2/ARRB1 (0.684, β-arrestins mediating desensitization), GNB1 (0.682, Gβ subunit), OR10A5 (0.679), GNG13 (0.678, Gγ subunit), and OR2D2 (0.478, closest paralog). This interactome confirms OR2D3 functions through the canonical cAMP-mediated olfactory transduction pathway. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number gains across 11p15.5-15.4 and 11p15.5-q25, demonstrating dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting the olfactory receptor cluster cause disease phenotypes or genomic instability. Conservation across mammals indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to chemosensory function. OR2D3 likely functions as a volatile odorant receptor detecting specific molecular features, activating GNAL to stimulate adenylate cyclase III, increasing cAMP, opening cyclic nucleotide-gated channels, and depolarizing olfactory sensory neurons.
