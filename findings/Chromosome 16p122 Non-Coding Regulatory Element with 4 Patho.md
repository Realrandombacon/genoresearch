## Description
LOC100190986 (Gene ID: 100190986) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 16p12.2 (NC_000016.10, positions 21434454-21432002, ~2.5 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all are copy number gains/duplications (variant 4686601: 16p13.11-12.1 ~13.0 Mb tetrasomy; variant 151527: 16p12.2-11.2 ~7.9 Mb trisomy; variant 144327: 16p13.3-11.2 ~24.5 Mb trisomy; variant 58085: 16p13.11-12.1 ~13.4 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 16p12.2 chromosomal region contains critical cell cycle regulation, DNA repair, and epithelial ion transport-associated genes including PLK1 (polo like kinase 1, serine/threonine kinase, G2/M transition, centrosome maturation, mitotic spindle assembly, cancer overexpression, chemoresistance), PALB2 (partner and localizer of BRCA2, BRCA2 interaction, homologous recombination repair, Fanconi anemia complementation group N, breast/pancreatic/ovarian cancer susceptibility), SCNN1B (sodium channel epithelial 1 subunit beta, amiloride-sensitive sodium channel, sodium reabsorption, Liddle syndrome, pseudohypoaldosteronism type 1, cystic fibrosis-like lung disease), SCNN1G (sodium channel epithelial 1 subunit gamma, amiloride-sensitive sodium channel, sodium reabsorption, Liddle syndrome, pseudohypoaldosteronism type 1), and EEF2K (eukaryotic elongation factor 2 kinase, elongation factor 2 inhibition, protein synthesis regulation, autophagy induction, cancer cell survival under stress). Functional hypothesis: LOC100190986 likely represents a critical 16p12.2 proliferative/epithelial enhancer element within the PLK1-PALB2 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a basal epithelial cell-specific enhancer coordinating PLK1-PALB2-SCNN1B expression in rapidly dividing tissues (basal layer of epidermis, intestinal crypts, hematopoietic progenitors) or as a chromatin boundary separating cell cycle kinase genes (PLK1) from DNA repair genes (PALB2) and ion channel genes (SCNN1B/SCNN1G). The 4 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications, 7.9-24.5 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number disrupts PLK1-mediated mitotic progression and PALB2-mediated homologous recombination. Disease mechanism involves PLK1 overexpression causing cancer (mitotic checkpoint override, aneuploidy, genomic instability, poor prognosis, chemoresistance), PLK1 inhibition causing mitotic arrest (G2/M block, apoptosis, cancer therapy), PALB2 mutation causing Fanconi anemia (DNA interstrand crosslink repair defect, bone marrow failure, congenital anomalies, cancer predisposition), PALB2 mutation causing hereditary breast/pancreatic/ovarian cancer (homologous recombination deficiency, BRCA2 localization defect, genomic instability), SCNN1B/SCNN1G mutation causing Liddle syndrome (constitutive sodium channel activation, hypertension, hypokalemia, metabolic alkalosis, low renin/aldosterone), SCNN1B/SCNN1G mutation causing pseudohypoaldosteronism type 1 (sodium channel loss-of-function, salt wasting, hyperkalemia, metabolic acidosis, neonatal crisis), SCNN1B/SCNN1G dysfunction causing cystic fibrosis-like lung disease (impaired airway surface liquid clearance, chronic infection, bronchiectasis), EEF2K overexpression causing cancer survival (translation inhibition, autophagy induction, stress resistance), EEF2K inhibition causing protein synthesis restoration (sensitization to chemotherapy), and 16p12.2 duplication syndrome presenting with developmental delay, dysmorphic features, and behavioral abnormalities. The 16p12.2 region is associated with recurrent microduplication syndromes. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (4 duplications 7.9-24.5 Mb) and PLK1-PALB2-SCNN1B-SCNN1G-EEF2K neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a proliferative/epithelial-specific enhancer critical for cell cycle-DNA repair-sodium transport coordination.
