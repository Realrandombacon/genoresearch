## Description
PLK5 (Polo-Like Kinase 5; UniProt Q496M5) is a 336 amino acid intracellular protein with only 47 publications, representing a GENUINE DARK gene encoding an atypical 'inactive' polo-like kinase that functions as a cell cycle regulator and tumor suppressor despite lacking catalytic kinase activity. Unlike canonical PLK family members (PLK1-4) that drive mitotic progression through serine/threonine phosphorylation, PLK5 retains the characteristic polo-box domain architecture but possesses a truncated, catalytically dead kinase domain, functioning through protein-protein interactions to control mitotic exit, neuron differentiation, and glioblastoma suppression via its unique structural configuration and brain-enriched expression pattern.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals PLK5 contains a distinctive domain organization characteristic of polo-like kinases but with critical inactivating features: (1) N-terminal truncated kinase domain (IPR000719/PF00069/PS50011/G3DSA:1.10.510.10/IPR011009/SSF56112, residues 1-65/1-81/1-98, ~65-98 aa) — severely truncated compared to canonical ~250-300 aa kinase domains, lacking critical catalytic residues (DFG motif, activation loop, ATP-binding lysine), rendering enzyme catalytically inactive; (2) Central linker region (residues 66-199, ~134 aa) — unstructured connection between kinase remnant and polo-box domains; (3) First Polo-Box Domain (IPR033701/cd13118/G3DSA:3.30.1120.30/IPR036947, residues 200-275, ~75 aa) — phosphopeptide-binding module recognizing phosphorylated substrates; (4) Second Polo-Box Domain (IPR000959/PF00659/PS50078/G3DSA:3.30.1120.30/IPR036947, residues 255-336, ~81 aa) — second phosphopeptide-binding module completing the tandem PBD architecture; (5) Full-length family (PTHR24345, residues 1-223, ~223 aa) — defines PLK family despite catalytic inactivation.
