## Description
LOC105372990 (Gene ID: 105372990) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 22q12.2 (NC_000022.11, positions 30432685-30436500, ~3.8 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID (ENSG00000273428), consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 6 duplications/gains (variant 149114: 22q11.23-12.3 ~13.0 Mb; variant 148861: 22q11.21-12.3 ~16.3 Mb; variant 145336: 22q11.1-13.33 ~33.8 Mb; variant 144164: 22q11.1-13.33 ~33.8 Mb; variant 57132: 22q11.21-12.3 ~13.6 Mb; variant 57002: 22q12.1-12.3 ~7.0 Mb) and 1 deletion/loss (variant 155348: 22q12.1-12.2 ~5.0 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 22q12.2 chromosomal region contains critical tumor suppressor and oncogene-associated genes including NF2 (neurofibromin 2/merlin, FERM domain tumor suppressor, Hippo pathway regulation, neurofibromatosis type 2, vestibular schwannoma, meningioma, ependymoma), EWSR1 (EWS RNA binding protein 1, RNA-binding protein, Ewing sarcoma t(11;22) translocation, desmoplastic small round cell tumor), and TUG1 (taurine up-regulated 1, long non-coding RNA, chromatin remodeling, cancer progression). Functional hypothesis: LOC105372990 likely represents a critical NF2 distal enhancer element within the 22q12.2 tumor suppressor region, potentially functioning as a Schwann cell-specific enhancer coordinating NF2-EWSR1 expression in neural crest-derived tissues (merlin production, cell-cell contact signaling, contact inhibition) or as a chromatin boundary separating tumor suppressor genes (NF2) from RNA-binding protein genes (EWSR1) and lncRNA genes (TUG1). The 7 pathogenic CNVs (predominantly duplications, 5.0-33.8 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts NF2-mediated tumor suppression and EWSR1-mediated RNA processing. Disease mechanism involves NF2 haploinsufficiency causing neurofibromatosis type 2 (bilateral vestibular schwannomas, meningiomas, ependymomas, merlin dysfunction, Hippo pathway inactivation), NF2 mutation causing sporadic schwannoma (unilateral vestibular schwannoma, hearing loss, balance dysfunction), EWSR1 translocation causing Ewing sarcoma (EWSR1-FLI1 fusion oncoprotein, primitive neuroectodermal tumor, bone/soft tissue sarcoma), EWSR1 rearrangement causing desmoplastic small round cell tumor (EWSR1-WT1 fusion, abdominal malignancy, poor prognosis), and 22q12.2 deletion syndrome presenting with tumor predisposition and developmental abnormalities. The 22q12.2 region is a well-established tumor suppressor locus with NF2 germline mutations causing neurofibromatosis type 2. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (7 CNVs 5.0-33.8 Mb) and NF2-EWSR1-TUG1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a neural crest-specific enhancer critical for NF2-mediated contact inhibition and EWSR1-mediated RNA processing coordination.
