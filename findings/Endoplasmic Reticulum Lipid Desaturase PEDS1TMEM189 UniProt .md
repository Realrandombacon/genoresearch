## Description
PEDS1 (UniProt A5PLL7, Plasmanylethanolamine desaturase 1, also known as TMEM189) is a 270 amino acid (~31 kDa) small endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein representing the catalytic enzyme responsible for introducing the characteristic vinyl ether double bond in plasmalogen phospholipids with distinctive lipid desaturase domain architecture characteristic of membrane-bound oxidoreductases involved in ether lipid biosynthesis. This domain architecture positions PEDS1 as a specialized membrane oxidoreductase optimized for catalyzing the final and defining step in plasmalogen biosynthesis through iron-dependent oxygen chemistry.
