## Description
AHCTF1 (AT-hook containing transcription factor 1) is likely involved in nuclear assembly and transcription regulation. 
  The protein contains domains similar to Quinoprotein alcohol dehydrogenase-like superfamily (IPR011047) and ELYS-like domain (IPR025151), 
  suggesting a role in nuclear structure and function. 
  High-confidence interactions with nucleoporins (NUP37, NUP160, SEC13, NUP107, NUP98, NUP43, SEH1L, NUP133, NUP85) indicate a role in nuclear 
  transport and assembly. 
  The protein is expressed in various tissues, including the nucleus and nuclear membrane (HPA). 
  Pathogenic variants in AHCTF1 have been associated with various diseases (ClinVar).
