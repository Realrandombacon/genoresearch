## Description
NPIPB8 (Nuclear pore complex-interacting protein family member B8, UniProt E9PQR5) is a 432-amino acid nuclear protein that functions as a component of the nuclear pore complex (NPC) interacting machinery involved in nucleocytoplasmic transport. InterPro analysis reveals defining NPIP architecture: Nuclear pore complex interacting protein family domain (IPR009443/PTHR15438, aa 2-432) spanning nearly the entire protein, with a specific N-terminal NPIP domain (IPR054697/PF06409, aa 79-259) characteristic of proteins that associate with nuclear pore complexes and facilitate macromolecular trafficking between nucleus and cytoplasm. GO annotations predict intracellular and membrane protein classes with nucleoplasmic subcellular localization. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves medium confidence (pLDDT 52.1), indicating a partially structured protein with mixed ordered and disordered regions — this suggests NPIPB8 may function through intrinsically disordered region-mediated protein-protein interactions common among nucleoporins and NPC-associated factors, or contain flexible linker regions connecting structured domains. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enriched expression for NPIPB8 (ENSG00000255524) with testis-specific enrichment at 16.9 nTPM, detected in many tissues but highest in testis, with group-enriched single cell type specificity and nucleoplasmic subcellular location — this testis-enriched pattern suggests potential roles in germ cell development, meiosis, or spermatogenesis-specific nuclear transport processes. The presence of testis-specific partner MALRD1 and zinc finger proteins suggests dual roles in germ cell biology and chromatin regulation. NPIPB8 likely functions as an NPC-associated factor that uses its NPIP domain to dock at nuclear pores, facilitating selective transport of cargoes (potentially germ cell-specific transcripts or transcription factors) through interaction with nucleoporins and transport receptors, with disordered regions (pLDDT 52.1) providing flexibility for accommodating diverse cargoes — the testis-enriched expression pattern suggesting specialized roles in spermatogenesis where extensive nuclear remodeling and mRNA transport occur.
