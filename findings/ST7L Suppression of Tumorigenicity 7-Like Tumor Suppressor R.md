## Description
ST7L (Suppressor of Tumorigenicity 7-Like) is a 575-amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q8TDW4) representing a genuine dark gene with only 28 publications despite its critical roles as a tumor suppressor regulating Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway in pancreatic cancer drug resistance, breast cancer proliferation and invasion, epithelial ovarian cancer malignancy, and hepatocellular carcinoma metastasis through miRNA/lncRNA regulatory networks. Structurally, ST7L contains canonical tumor suppressor scaffold architecture: ST7 protein family domain (IPR007311/PF04184/PTHR12745/cd11557, residues 25-547/43-547/25-547/72-538) defining the functional class shared with ST7 tumor suppressor, Tetratricopeptide-like helical domain superfamily (IPR011990/G3DSA:1.25.40.10, residues 211-460/211-460) mediating protein-protein interactions through stacked α-helical repeats forming groove-based binding surfaces for client proteins. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 79.2), consistent with a well-folded scaffolding protein containing stable TPR repeats forming defined interaction surfaces for multi-protein complex assembly at centrosomes. Functionally, ST7L localizes to centrosome with testis-enriched expression (HPA: tissue enriched, detected in all, testis 33.7 nTPM, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced), classified as a predicted intracellular and membrane protein functioning in tumor suppression—centrosomal localization suggests roles in cell cycle regulation, mitotic spindle organization, and ciliogenesis potentially linking to Wnt signaling control. STRING interactions reveal connections to trafficking and signaling regulators: RINT1 (0.595, RAD50 interactor 1—Golgi trafficking and centrosome cohesion), AKT1 (0.441, AKT serine/threonine kinase 1—PI3K-AKT survival signaling hub), TAOK2 (0.438, TAO kinase 2—MAPK pathway activator, dendrite morphogenesis), MCOLN3 (0.424, mucolipin 3—lysosomal calcium channel), and FXYD2 (0.410, FXYD domain-containing ion transport regulator 2—Na/K-ATPase subunit). This sparse but specific interaction network positions ST7L within centrosome-Golgi trafficking coordination (RINT1), PI3K-AKT-Wnt signaling integration (AKT1), and stress kinase pathways (TAOK2). Critically, ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including single nucleotide variants (NM_017744.5:c.451+16A>G intronic, c.500A>G p.Tyr167Cys missense in TPR domain, c.1143-137T>G intronic), large copy number losses at 1p13.3-13.1 (chr1:110066946-116672408)x1, 1p21.3-13.2 (chr1:95046805-114714931), 1p21.2-12 (chr1:102021465-119737478), deletions (NC_000001.10:g.(?_112318699)_(115576848_?)del, (?_111145905)_(114454813_?)del), and copy number gains at 1p13.3-11.2 (chr1:111647582-121343783), yet ZERO diseases are formally associated—a profound gap given the validated tumor suppressor functions across multiple cancer types. Recent literature establishes emerging specialized roles: miR-331-3p links to drug resistance of pancreatic cancer cells by activating WNT/β-catenin signal via ST7L targeting, establishing ST7L as negative regulator of chemoresistance through Wnt pathway suppression (32 citations, 2020); long noncoding RNA MIR31HG inhibits hepatocellular carcinoma proliferation and metastasis by sponging microRNA-575 to modulate ST7L expression, revealing lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA competitive endogenous RNA network controlling liver cancer progression (103 citations, 2018); suppression of proliferation and invasion of breast cancer cells by ST7L occurs through inhibition of activation of Wnt/GSK-3β/β-catenin signalling, demonstrating conserved tumor suppressor mechanism across cancer types via β-catenin destabilization (10 citations, 2020); miR-23a promotes IKKα expression but suppresses ST7L expression to contribute to malignancy of epithelial ovarian cancer cells, showing microRNA-mediated ST7L downregulation driving ovarian cancer aggressiveness (retracted but mechanistically informative, 2023); miR-331-3p functions as oncogene by targeting ST7L in pancreatic cancer, confirming direct miRNA regulation of ST7L transcript (2018). The mechanistic hypothesis: ST7L functions as a centrosomal scaffolding protein that negatively regulates Wnt/β-catenin signaling through its TPR domains recruiting components of the β-catenin destruction complex (AXIN, APC, GSK-3β) or promoting β-catenin phosphorylation and degradation. The TPR repeats (typically 3-4 repeats based on domain architecture) form a concave binding surface that recognizes specific peptide motifs in client proteins—potential clients include β-catenin itself, Dishevelled (DVL), or AXIN. At the centrosome, ST7L may coordinate Wnt signaling with cell cycle progression, ensuring β-catenin degradation during mitosis to prevent aberrant transcriptional activation. In pancreatic cancer, miR-331-3p upregulation suppresses ST7L expression, unleashing Wnt/β-catenin signaling driving drug resistance genes (ABC transporters, survivin). In hepatocellular carcinoma, MIR31HG lncRNA sequesters miR-575, protecting ST7L mRNA from degradation and maintaining tumor suppressor activity—loss of MIR31HG enhances miR-575-mediated ST7L silencing promoting metastasis. In breast cancer, ST7L inhibits Wnt/GSK-3β/β-catenin axis potentially through direct binding to β-catenin or enhancing GSK-3β activity. In ovarian cancer, miR-23a-mediated ST7L suppression cooperates with IKKα upregulation (NF-κB activation) driving malignant phenotype. The RINT1 interaction suggests ST7L coordinates Golgi-to-centrosome trafficking potentially affecting Wnt receptor (Frizzled/LRP) localization. The AKT1 link indicates cross-talk between PI3K-AKT and Wnt pathways, with ST7L potentially integrating survival and proliferative signals. The TAOK2 connection suggests ST7L responds to cellular stress through MAPK pathway modulation. The testis-enriched expression (33.7 nTPM) and centrosomal localization suggest potential roles in spermatogenesis, ciliogenesis, or meiotic spindle organization. The absence of formal disease associations despite 15 pathogenic variants suggests ST7L dysfunction contributes to familial cancer syndromes (pancreatic, breast, ovarian, liver cancer susceptibility), male infertility (testis-specific functions), ciliopathies (centrosome/cilia defects), or developmental disorders not yet mapped to Mendelian phenotypes. Future work should define ST7L's complete protein interaction repertoire via centrosomal proteomics, determine crystal structure of TPR domain bound to β-catenin or AXIN peptides, investigate whether germline ST7L mutations cause familial cancer predisposition, develop ST7L-stabilizing therapies for cancer treatment (counteracting miRNA-mediated silencing), explore ST7L as biomarker for Wnt pathway activation status, and assess ST7L modulation for overcoming chemotherapy resistance in pancreatic cancer.
