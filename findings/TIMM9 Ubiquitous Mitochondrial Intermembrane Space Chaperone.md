## Description
TIMM9 (Translocase of Inner Mitochondrial Membrane 9, UniProt Q9Y5J7) is a 89 amino acid mitochondrial intermembrane space chaperone with partial characterization (15 papers) that functions as an essential subunit of the TIM9-TIM10 hexameric complex responsible for importing hydrophobic carrier proteins into the mitochondrial inner membrane. The protein contains a Tim10-like domain (IPR004217/PF02953/SSF144122, residues 9-69/9-69/13-80) with zinc finger structure characteristic of the small Tim chaperone family, belongs to the Tim10-like domain superfamily (IPR035427/G3DSA:1.10.287.810, residues 1-89/1-89), and is part of the mitochondrial import inner membrane translocase subunit family (IPR050673/PTHR13172, residues 1-88/1-88) with chaperone function (GO:0003757) and protein translocation activity (GO:0006605), placing it in the essential mitochondrial protein import machinery. STRING interactions reveal very high-confidence partnerships with TIMM complex components: AGK (0.989, acylglycerol kinase, mitochondrial membrane protein), TIMM10 (0.972, Tim10 partner in hexameric complex), TIMM8B (0.968, Tim8 paralog B), TIMM23 (0.961, inner membrane translocase subunit 23), TIMM22 (0.813, inner membrane carrier translocase), TIMM10B (0.794, Tim10 paralog B), TIMM8A (0.761, Tim8 paralog A, Mohr-Tranebjaerg syndrome), CHCHD4 (0.516, mitochondrial intermembrane space import protein), TIMM13 (0.416, Tim13 partner), and TIMM29 (0.401, Tim29 translocase subunit), confirming membership in the TIM22 carrier import pathway.
