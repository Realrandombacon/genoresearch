## Description
UQCC1 (Ubiquinol-Cytochrome C Reductase Complex Assembly Factor 1, also known as CBP3 homolog, UniProt Q9NVA1) is a 299 amino acid mitochondrial chaperone representing a genuine dark gene with only 30 literature references despite its critical role in Complex III (cytochrome bc1 complex) biogenesis. UQCC1 contains a defining Ubiquinol-cytochrome c chaperone CBP3 domain (IPR007129, positions 1-298; IPR021150, positions 136-271; PF03981, positions 136-271) conserved from yeast to humans, characteristic of proteins that facilitate cytochrome b insertion and cofactor assembly during respiratory chain maturation. The AlphaFold structure shows confident folding (pLDDT 74.4), indicating a stable alpha-helical bundle architecture typical of mitochondrial membrane-associated chaperones, with the core CBP3 domain forming a structured platform for protein-protein interactions.
