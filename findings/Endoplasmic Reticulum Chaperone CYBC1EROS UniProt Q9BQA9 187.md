## Description
CYBC1 (UniProt Q9BQA9, Cytochrome b-245 chaperone 1, also known as EROS for 'ER oxidoreductin-like' or 'essential for reactive oxygen species') is a 187 amino acid (~21 kDa) small endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein representing the dedicated chaperone required for the biosynthesis and stability of the phagocyte NADPH oxidase complex (NOX2). The protein exhibits a compact single-domain organization centered on the Cytochrome b-245 chaperone 1 family domain (IPR027846 Cytochrome b-245 chaperone 1, positions 1-186; PF15169 Cytochrome b-245 chaperone 1 / Eros, positions 2-184; PTHR31837 Cytochrome b-245 chaperone 1, positions 1-186) which defines its exclusive function in facilitating the heterodimerization of gp91phox (CYBB) and p22phox (CYBA) subunits within the ER membrane. This domain architecture positions CYBC1 as a specialized foldase/chaperone optimized for stabilizing the transmembrane flavocytochrome b558 core, preventing its degradation, and enabling its trafficking to the plasma membrane or phagosomal membrane where it generates superoxide anions for microbial killing.
