## Description
PITPNM2 (Phosphatidylinositol Transfer Protein Membrane-Associated 2, also known as Nir3 or RDGB homolog) is a 1349 aa membrane-associated lipid transfer protein representing a genuine partially characterized dark gene with only 16 literature references, functioning as a critical phosphatidylinositol transfer protein that regulates T cell receptor (TCR) signaling, phagocytosis, and immune cell activation through PI(4,5)P2 replenishment at membrane contact sites. Multi-omics data reveals PITPNM2 as a vertebrate-conserved Class IIA PITP with defined SRPBCC lipid-binding domain, DDHD domain, and LNS2/PITP domain yet emerging disease characterization through recent 2020-2022 discoveries in immunometabolism and sleep regulation. UniProt accession Q9BZ72 confirms the 1349 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q6ZPQ6, 1335 aa, ~98% identity), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the phosphoinositide transfer machinery across mammals. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the phosphatidylinositol transfer protein family (IPR001666, aa 1-1308; GO:0005548 phospholipid transporter activity, GO:0015914 phospholipid transport) containing an N-terminal SRPBCC lipid-binding domain (cd08889, aa 1-258; PF02121, aa 1-250; IPR055261, aa 1-250), a central DDHD domain (IPR004177, aa 715-963; PF02862, aa 715-962; GO:0046872 metal ion binding), and a C-terminal LNS2/PITP domain (IPR031315, aa 1108-1239) with HAD-like superfamily fold (IPR023214, aa 1111-1204). AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 66.6) across the full 1349 aa length, indicating a partially structured protein with ordered lipid-binding domains and potentially disordered linker regions—consistent with membrane-associated lipid transfer proteins requiring conformational flexibility for membrane extraction/insertion cycles. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with top expression in lymphoid tissue (79.3 nTPM), immune cell-enhanced blood cell specificity, vesicle subcellular localization, and classification as a plasma protein/predicted intracellular protein—consistent with roles in immune cell signaling and membrane trafficking. STRING interactions reveal a focused membrane contact site and lipid signaling network: PTK2B (0.876, proline-rich tyrosine kinase 2 beta, highest confidence partner), VAPA (0.813, vesicle-associated membrane protein-associated protein A), VAPB (0.756, VAMP-associated protein B), ESYT1 (0.523, extended synaptotagmin 1), OGFOD2 (0.510, oxygenase family member), C2CD2L (0.477, C2 calcium-dependent domain containing 2-like), OSBPL5 (0.475, oxysterol-binding protein-like 5), OSBPL8 (0.432), PRKACA (0.427, protein kinase A catalytic subunit), OSBP (0.424, oxysterol-binding protein). The PTK2B/VAPA/VAPB interactions confirm PITPNM2 operates at ER-plasma membrane contact sites where lipid transfer occurs. ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 12q copy number variations (gains: chr12:99532287-133777902x3, chr12:116422123-133777902x3, chr12:117533207-133777902x3, chr12:121551496-133777902x3, chr12:120880079-133777902x3, chr12:117461902-133841395x3, chr12:173787-133777902x3; loss: chr12:122169403-129084163x1), implicating PITPNM2 dosage sensitivity in chromosome 12q deletion/duplication syndromes though specific disease phenotypes are not yet delineated. The 16 papers (including 2022 Nir3/PITPNM2 promotes PI(4,5)P2 replenishment/TCR signaling/T cell development and survival study with 13 citations, 2022 Nir2/Nir3 sustain phosphoinositide signaling/actin dynamics during phagocytosis study with 10 citations, 2022 rdgB/PITPNM2 knockdown reduced nocturnal sleep in Drosophila study with 5 citations, 2020 schizophrenia/cardiometabolic disease pleiotropic gene GWAS study with 31 citations) confirm PITPNM2 as a genuine dark gene—core lipid transfer function defined but disease mechanisms (immunodeficiency, autoimmunity, sleep disorders, neuropsychiatric disease) and full clinical spectrum awaiting characterization.
