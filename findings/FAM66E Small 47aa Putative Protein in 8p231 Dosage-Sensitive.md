## Description
FAM66E (Gene ID: 100132103, UniProt P0C841) encodes a 47 amino acid putative protein mapping to chromosome 8p23.1 (7,955,012-8,008,754 bp, ~54 kb span). Multiple evidence lines suggest this is a small peptide in a dosage-sensitive genomic region: (1) UniProt: P0C841 (47 aa) - classified as "putative protein" indicating uncertain function; (2) InterPro: API error (no domain data retrieved); (3) STRING interactions: no protein found (too small for stable interactions or low expression); (4) HPA expression: no data (not in HPA database - likely low/absent expression or non-coding); (5) ClinVar: 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number variants spanning 8p23.3-21.2 to 8p23.3-q24.3 (deletions 449kb-23.8Mb, duplications 226kb-145Mb) - 8p23.1 deletions cause congenital heart defects, developmental delay, intellectual disability, diaphragmatic hernia; (6) AlphaFold structure: pLDDT 69.1 (medium confidence, residues 1-47) - partially structured small peptide. The 8p23.1 region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes: GATA4 (8p23.1, cardiac transcription factor, septal defects, tetralogy of Fallot), CSMD1 (8p23.1, complement regulatory tumor suppressor), and multiple olfactory receptor clusters. Mechanistic hypothesis: FAM66E may encode a micropeptide or small regulatory peptide rather than a canonical protein. At 47 amino acids, it falls below typical protein-coding threshold (~100 aa). The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure suggests some folding potential but could represent translation of a small open reading frame (sORF) within a long non-coding RNA. 8p23.1 rearrangements cause GATA4 haploinsufficiency (cardiac defects) and CSMD1 loss (cancer predisposition); FAM66E may be co-deleted but not causative. Alternative hypothesis: FAM66E is a pseudogene or non-coding transcript misannotated as protein-coding. Absence from HPA and STRING databases supports minimal/lack of protein expression. If functional, the small peptide could act as: (a) competitive inhibitor of protein-protein interactions; (b) signaling micropeptide; or (c) regulator of larger protein stability. This represents a "dark gene" requiring ribosome profiling and mass spectrometry to confirm translation.
