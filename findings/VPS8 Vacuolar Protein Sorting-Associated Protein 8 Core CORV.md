## Description
VPS8 encodes a 1428 aa intracellular trafficking protein (UniProt Q8N3P4) that functions as an essential core subunit of the CORVET (Class C Core Vacuole/Endosome Tethering) complex, integrating WD40 repeat-mediated protein-protein interactions with C-terminal RING-type zinc finger complex assembly to coordinate Rab5-dependent early endosomal vesicle tethering, fusion, and cargo sorting. This genuine dark gene has substantial clinical significance with 48 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants though specific monogenic diseases await characterization beyond chromosomal instability syndromes involving 3q copy number alterations. The protein features specialized multi-domain architecture enabling coordinated vesicle tethering and HOPS/CORVET complex integration: N-terminal YVTN repeat-like/quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase beta chain-like domain (G3DSA:2.130.10.10/G3DSA:3.30.40.10/IPR011044/IPR015943, residues 87-339/165-536) providing structural scaffold; multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680/IPR036322, residues 195-236/160-297) mediating protein-protein interactions within tethering complexes; VPS8-specific central domain (IPR025941, residues 614-796) unique to this subfamily; C-terminal RING-type zinc finger (IPR001841/IPR013083/cd16687/G3DSA:3.30.40.10, residues 1256-1310/1258-1310/1251-1371) enabling stable complex assembly through zinc coordination. STRING interactions reveal VPS8 operates within extremely high-confidence CORVET/HOPS tethering complex networks: VPS11 (0.999), vacuolar protein sorting-associated protein 11 core subunit shared between CORVET and HOPS complexes; VPS33B (0.970), VPS33 homolog B Sec1/Munc18-family protein essential for vesicle fusion; VPS41 (0.898), vacuolar protein sorting-associated protein 41 HOPS-specific subunit; TGFBRAP1 (0.859), TGF-beta receptor-associated protein 1 linking to signaling pathways; VPS18 (0.804), vacuolar protein sorting-associated protein 18 core tethering component; VPS33A (0.720), VPS33 homolog A alternative Sec1/Munc18 partner; RAB5A (0.586), Ras-related protein Rab-5A small GTPase marking early endosomes and recruiting CORVET; VPS16 (0.484)/VPS39 (0.482), additional HOPS/CORVET subunits; CHMP2A (0.447), charged multivesicular body protein 2A linking to ESCRT machinery. HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type enhanced pattern consistent with housekeeping endosomal trafficking function, classified as predicted intracellular protein involved in protein transport/transport functioning in vesicle fusion/localized to early endosome. ClinVar lists 48 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large-scale copy number gains (3q22.1-29 chr3:132561657-197851986 x3, 3q26.31-29 chr3:174764228-197851986 x3, 3q26.33-29 chr3:179313373-197851444 x3) and losses (3q27.1-27.2 chr3:183020090-185760128 x1, 3q26.33-27.2 chr3:179391972-185539073 x1) plus single nucleotide variants including nonsense mutation p.Glu1030Ter causing truncation, splice site deletions (c.4003-5del, c.2263-13del), and missense variant p.Asp181Val, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity with both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity potentially causing neurodevelopmental disorders, trafficking defects, or lysosomal storage-like phenotypes. AlphaFold structure prediction yields confident global fold (pLDDT 74.6), revealing a well-folded multi-domain protein with structured WD40 repeat regions connected to ordered RING zinc finger suitable for stable CORVET complex assembly. Mechanistically, VPS8 operates through integrated modes: (1) CORVET complex nucleation—ultra-high confidence VPS11/VPS33B/VPS18 interactions position VPS8 as essential scaffold recruiting CORVET to early endosomes; (2) Rab5 effector function—RAB5A interaction (0.586) enables GTP-dependent recruitment to early endosomal membranes; (3) Vesicle tethering—multi-domain architecture bridges opposing membranes facilitating SNARE-mediated fusion; (4) HOPS-CORVET conversion—VPS41/VPS39 interactions suggest VPS8 participates in CORVET-to-HOPS maturation during endosome-to-lysosome progression; (5) Cargo sorting—WD40 repeats may recognize specific cargo proteins for endosomal sorting decisions; (6) Signaling integration—TGFBRAP1 interaction links endosomal trafficking to TGF-beta signaling pathways; (7) ESCRT coordination—CHMP2A interaction connects tethering to multivesicular body formation. Loss-of-function causes defective CORVET assembly, impaired early endosomal fusion, cargo accumulation in endosomes, disrupted lysosomal degradation, aberrant receptor recycling, and potential neurodevelopmental disorders or lysosomal dysfunction phenotypes.
