## Description
TMEM11 (transmembrane protein 11, UniProt P17152) is a 192-amino acid mitochondrial inner membrane protein that functions as a regulator of mitochondrial morphogenesis and cristae organization. The protein contains a conserved mitochondrial morphogenesis regulator domain (PF14972, residues 28-187) and belongs to the PM1 protein family (PTHR15099). STRING interaction analysis reveals TMEM11 forms high-confidence complexes with core MICOS complex subunits including IMMT/MIC60 (0.732), MICOS10 (0.758), MICOS13/CHCHD7 (0.726), and CHCHD3 (0.713), as well as mitochondrial chaperone HSPA9 (0.760) and membrane remodeling factors DNAJC11 (0.892), APOOL (0.789), and BNIP3 (0.778). These interactions position TMEM11 as a peripheral scaffold that stabilizes MICOS complex assembly at cristae junctions. Human Protein Atlas data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, consistent with essential housekeeping mitochondrial function. ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants affecting the 17p11.2 locus, including deletions and duplications, suggesting dosage sensitivity and potential disease associations with mitochondrial dysfunction.
