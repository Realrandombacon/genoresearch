# UFSP2: UFM1-Specific Protease Regulating ER Export and GPCR Trafficking via Ufmylation Cycle with Roles in Skeletal Dysplasia and Infantile Epileptic Spasms

## Description
UFSP2 (Ufm1-specific protease 2, UniProt Q9NUQ7) is a 469 aa cysteine protease that serves as the dedicated deconjugating enzyme for UFM1 (ubiquitin-fold modifier 1), a ubiquitin-like protein critical for ER stress response and protein trafficking. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture: an N-terminal UFSP2-specific domain (IPR049387, PF20908, positions 37-256) unique to UFSP2, and a C-terminal catalytic domain (IPR012462, PF07910, positions 278-461) belonging to peptidase family C78 within the Ufm1-specific isopeptidase family (PTHR48153, positions 14-469). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 91.2), indicating a well-folded protease with stable catalytic geometry. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues) with nucleoplasm and cytosol localization, classified within the Ubl conjugation pathway with hydrolase/protease/thiol protease molecular functions. STRING interactions reveal tight functional clustering with the complete ufmylation machinery: UFM1 (0.998, the ubiquitin-like modifier substrate), DDRGK1 (0.914, ER membrane adaptor), UBA5 (0.910, E1 activating enzyme), UFL1 (0.879, E3 ligase), UFC1 (0.857, E2 conjugating enzyme), CDK5RAP3 (0.815, UFL1 cofactor), ODR4 (0.737, ER export factor), ZMYM4 (0.505), TRIP4 (0.495), RPL26 (0.486). ClinVar contains 130 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 4q32.1-35.2 and missense variants affecting the catalytic cysteine. Literature (54 papers) establishes UFSP2 roles in: (1) spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia type Di Rocco via catalytic cysteine variants causing disproportionate short stature, (2) infantile epileptic spasms syndrome from biallelic variants, (3) high glucose-stimulated pyroptosis in retinal Müller cells via NLRP3 inflammasome activation, and (4) a 2026 study showing the UFSP2-C1orf27 complex positions nascent GPCRs to the ufmylation system for ER export control. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: UFSP2 acts as a reversible switch for the ufmylation pathway, cleaving UFM1 from target proteins to regulate ER-to-Golgi trafficking, ribosome quality control, and ER stress responses. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The catalytic C78 domain cleaves isopeptide bonds between UFM1 C-terminal glycine and lysine residues on substrates (DDRGK1, ribosomal proteins). The N-terminal UFSP2-specific domain may mediate substrate recognition or subcellular targeting. UFSP2 depletion causes UFM1 accumulation on substrates, disrupting ER export of GPCRs and triggering ER stress. In skeletal development, UFSP2 dysfunction impairs ufmylation of trafficking machinery in chondrocytes, causing spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia. In neurons, UFSP2 loss disrupts ER homeostasis leading to epileptic spasms. The 130 ClinVar variants indicate UFSP2 is a disease gene with pleiotropic effects across skeletal, neurological, and metabolic systems.
