## Description
SIGIRR (Single Ig and TIR domain containing) is likely involved in immune response regulation. 
  It contains an immunoglobulin domain (G3DSA:2.60.40.10) and a Toll/interleukin-1 receptor homology (TIR) domain (IPR000157), 
  suggesting a role in signaling pathways. High-confidence interactions with MYD88, IRAK1, and TRAF6 support this hypothesis. 
  Its expression in various tissues with low specificity and presence in immune cells imply a broad immune function. 
  The presence of 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar suggests its dysfunction may contribute to disease.
