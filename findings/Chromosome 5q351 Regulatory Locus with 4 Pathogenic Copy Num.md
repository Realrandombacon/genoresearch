## Description
LOC101928093 is an uncharacterized genetic locus on chromosome 5q35.1 (NC_000005.10, positions 172755502-172762561, ~7 kb region) with clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases, and Human Protein Atlas found no expression data (ENSG00000253295). STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants (variants 152731, 146233, 144365, 58118), all representing duplications/gains spanning 5q32-35.3 to 5q35.1-35.3 regions (149-181 Mb ranges). NCBI gene search revealed a nearby heart enhancer 25 (LOC108004526) in the same chromosomal region, suggesting regulatory function. The 5q35.1 locus is clinically notable as this region contains NSD1, mutations in which cause Sotos syndrome (cerebral gigantism). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928093 likely represents a cis-regulatory element (enhancer or chromatin boundary) controlling expression of neighboring genes in the 5q35 region, particularly NSD1 or cardiac development genes. The pathogenic copy number gains suggest dosage sensitivity where duplication disrupts normal chromatin architecture or enhancer-promoter interactions. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence and proximity to characterized enhancers indicates this locus functions in transcriptional regulation rather than encoding a protein product. Disease mechanism likely involves position effects or enhancer hijacking in chromosomal rearrangements.
