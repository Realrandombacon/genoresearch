## Description
ANKRD17 encodes a 2603 amino acid nuclear scaffold protein (UniProt O75179) that functions as a cell cycle regulator and RNA-binding platform. The protein contains extensive ankyrin repeat arrays (15+10 repeats, IPR002110, positions 210-736 and 1039-1526) that mediate protein-protein interactions, a type I KH RNA-binding domain (IPR004087, positions 1724-1794) for nucleic acid recognition, and a UDM1 ubiquitin-dependent DSB recruitment module (cd22249, positions 822-887) suggesting DNA damage response involvement. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasm and nuclear membrane localization. STRING interactions reveal functional partnerships with ANKHD1 (0.812), PUM1 (0.801), RAB3GAP1 (0.663), USP34 (0.656), and GIGYF2 (0.639), indicating roles in post-transcriptional regulation and vesicular trafficking. ClinVar documents 217 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (c.6878del), nonsense mutations (c.7214C>A p.Ser2405Ter), and missense changes linked to intellectual disability and developmental disorders. The nuclear localization signals, cyclin-binding RXL motif, and ankyrin scaffold architecture position ANKRD17 as a molecular hub coordinating cell cycle progression, RNA metabolism, and DNA damage responses through multivalent protein assembly.
