## Description
TRAPPC3 encodes a 180 amino acid core subunit (UniProt O43617) of the Transport Protein Particle (TRAPP) complex, a conserved multi-subunit guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that activates Rab GTPases to drive vesicle trafficking between the ER and Golgi apparatus. The protein adopts a compact alpha-beta fold (AlphaFold pLDDT 90.6) containing the characteristic Bet3-TRAPPC3 domain (aa 15-169, cd14942) with a ligand-binding superfamily architecture (aa 12-171, IPR024096) that forms the structural core of TRAPP complexes. STRING interaction network reveals TRAPPC3 functions exclusively within the TRAPP complex machinery with ultra-high confidence connections to all core subunits: TRAPPC2 (0.999), TRAPPC4 (0.999), TRAPPC2L (0.999), TRAPPC5 (0.999), TRAPPC1 (0.997), and larger complex members TRAPPC8 (0.996), TRAPPC9 (0.998), TRAPPC10 (0.987). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with Golgi apparatus and cytosolic localization, consistent with constitutive secretory pathway function. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Arg62Trp, p.Ser92Gly, p.Asn174Ser) and copy number variations on chromosome 1p34-35, suggesting dosage sensitivity and functional constraint. The Bet3 subunit forms a dimeric platform that anchors the TRAPP complex to membranes and positions catalytic subunits for Rab1/Rab11 nucleotide exchange, making it essential for COPII vesicle tethering, Golgi biogenesis, and ciliary trafficking. Disruption likely causes multi-system trafficking defects affecting secretion, autophagy, and organelle homeostasis.
