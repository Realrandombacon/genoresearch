## Description

The gene encodes a 272 amino acid centromere protein V-like enzyme (UniProt P0DPI3) with preserved catalytic domain architecture. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (global pLDDT 71.4), indicating a well-folded protein with stable 3D architecture characteristic of enzymatic proteins. STRING interactions reveal 1 medium-confidence partner: SORD (sorbitol dehydrogenase, score 0.595), suggesting potential metabolic pathway connections. HPA shows brain-enriched expression at 4.0 nTPM with tissue-specific distribution. ClinVar contains 80 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly X chromosome CNVs including deletions, duplications, and copy number gains/losses spanning Xp22.33-q28), with literature linking CENPVL2 deletions to syndromic X-linked intellectual disability. Two publications (2017, 2019) describe Xp11.22 deletions encompassing CENPVL1, CENPVL2, MAGED1, and GSPT2 as causes of intellectual disability with developmental delays. CENPVL2's preserved CENP-V/GFA domain structure (homologous to glutathione-dependent formaldehyde-activating enzymes) combined with brain expression and 80 ClinVar variants suggests it may function as: (1) a metabolic enzyme processing formaldehyde or related aldehydes in neuronal tissue, (2) a centromere-associated protein regulating chromosome segregation during neurodevelopment, (3) a dosage-sensitive gene where hemizygous deletions in males contribute to X-linked intellectual disability syndrome, or (4) a metal-dependent carbon-sulfur lyase involved in neuronal detoxification pathways. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 71.4) supports enzymatic functionality. The 80 ClinVar CNVs and 2 literature reports warrant functional investigation for X-linked intellectual disability mechanisms, particularly given the brain-enriched expression pattern.
