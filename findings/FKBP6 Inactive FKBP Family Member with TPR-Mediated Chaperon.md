# FKBP6: Inactive FKBP Family Member with TPR-Mediated Chaperone Function in Spermatogenesis and Williams Syndrome

## Description
FKBP6 (FKBP Prolyl Isomerase Family Member 6, inactive) is a 327 amino acid nucleolar/cytosolic protein (UniProt O75344) with moderate characterization (89 papers) that functions in spermatogenesis, piRNA pathway regulation, and Williams syndrome pathogenesis. Despite containing an FKBP-type peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase domain (IPR001179/PF00254, residues 52-143), FKBP6 is catalytically inactive due to critical active site substitutions, suggesting neofunctionalization. The protein contains three tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) motifs (IPR019734, residues 171-204, 219-252, 253-286) and a TPR-like helical domain superfamily (IPR011990, residues 168-317) mediating protein-protein interactions. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with HSP90AA1 (0.965, heat shock protein 90), PPIL4 (0.838, peptidyl-prolyl isomerase), GTF2IRD1 (0.836, Williams syndrome transcription factor), CDK5RAP3 (0.832, centrosomal protein), HSPA2 (0.822, heat shock protein), UBE2I (0.819, SUMO-conjugating enzyme), DIDO1 (0.812, apoptosis regulator), TBL2 (0.807, transducin beta-like protein), FZD9 (0.798, Wnt receptor deleted in Williams syndrome), and TRIM50 (0.782, E3 ubiquitin ligase), placing FKBP6 in chaperone complexes, SUMOylation machinery, and Williams syndrome critical region. HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (53.0 nTPM, tissue enriched, detected in single tissue), cancer-enhanced pattern, and subcellular localization to nucleoli fibrillar center, microtubules, cytokinetic bridge, and cytosol, consistent with meiotic and spermatogenic functions. ClinVar contains 166 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and deletions at 7q11.23 (Williams syndrome critical region), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—haploinsufficiency contributes to Williams-Beuren syndrome cardiovascular and neurodevelopmental phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 90.3) indicates a very high confidence, well-folded protein with stable TPR superhelix. Functional hypothesis: FKBP6 acts as a scaffolding protein in spermatogenesis through TPR-mediated assembly of chaperone complexes (HSP90AA1, HSPA2) and SUMOylation machinery (UBE2I) at the cytokinetic bridge during meiotic division, regulating piRNA pathway factors for transposon silencing in germ cells, with Williams syndrome haploinsufficiency causing cardiovascular defects through disrupted GTF2IRD1/FZD9 interactions and Wnt signaling dysregulation, making it a dual-function protein in male fertility and neurodevelopmental disorders.
