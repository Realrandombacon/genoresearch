## Description
SPIRE2 (Spire Type Actin Nucleation Factor 2, Protein Spire Homolog 2, Q8WWL2) encodes a 714 aa actin nucleation factor functioning as a vesicle transport coordinator that bridges actin polymerization with myosin motor activity for long-range intracellular trafficking. Structural architecture reveals a KIND domain (IPR011019/G3DSA:1.10.510.10, residues 22-207/19-208) mediating phospholipid binding and membrane recruitment, four tandem WH2/WASP-homology-2 motifs (cd22065/cd22079/cd22186/cd22081, residues 233-264/275-304/307-329/339-360) that bind G-actin monomers for filament nucleation, and a C-terminal FYVE-related domain (IPR011011/cd15768, residues 536-612/566-680) targeting phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate on vesicle membranes; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 65.8) indicates medium confidence with partially structured domains connected by flexible linkers enabling conformational adaptability during vesicle capture. STRING interactions define actin nucleation and vesicle transport networks: myosin-5a MYO5A (0.963) motor protein for actin-based vesicle movement, formins FMN1 (0.851)/FMN2 (0.525) cooperating in actin filament assembly, actin nucleation promoter JMY (0.599), Rab11 effector DEF8 (0.564), and PCSK9 (0.670) linking to cholesterol metabolism. HPA localizes SPIRE2 to nucleoplasm, vesicles, intermediate filaments, and primary cilium with tissue-enhanced expression peaking in brain (24.9 nTPM) and pituitary gland (21.0 nTPM), classified as intracellular protein involved in protein transport with actin-binding molecular function. ClinVar documents 45 pathogenic variants including 16q23.1-24.3 CNVs, deletions (chr16:89865477-89895213), duplications, and splice site mutations (c.1273-11T>G, c.245-9_245-5del). Literature (32 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: Spire2-Rab11a synergistic activation of myosin-5b motor function for metaphase chromosome positioning in oocytes (4x citations, 2024), decreased SPIRE2 expression involved in epilepsy through disrupted neuronal vesicle trafficking (6x citations, 2022), inhibitory fragments selectively targeting Spire2-FMN2 interaction for therapeutic intervention (1x citation, 2023). Mechanistic hypothesis: SPIRE2 KIND domain anchors the protein to PI3P-enriched vesicle membranes via FYVE domain while WH2 motifs nucleate actin filaments from vesicle surface; the four WH2 domains sequentially bind G-actin monomers, positioning them for filament elongation; SPIRE2 recruits myosin-5a/5b motors via direct interaction, coupling newly polymerized actin tracks to vesicle movement; cooperation with FMN2 formin enables processive actin assembly for long-range transport; in neurons, SPIRE2-mediated vesicle trafficking delivers synaptic cargo, with loss causing epilepsy through impaired neurotransmitter release; primary cilium localization suggests role in ciliogenesis and ciliary vesicle trafficking; epilepsy association indicates critical function in maintaining neuronal excitability through regulated vesicle dynamics.
