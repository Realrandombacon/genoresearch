## Description
ANO8 (Anoctamin-8) is a 1232-amino acid predicted membrane protein with 22 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein contains conserved anoctamin family domains including anoctamin family domain (IPR007632, positions 46-907) that defines the TMEM16/anoctamin protein family, anoctamin transmembrane domains (IPR049452, positions 237-581 and 708-877) forming the characteristic dual-domain architecture of calcium-activated chloride channels, and calcium-activated chloride channel family domains (PF04547, positions 237-581 and 708-877) that mediate ion conductance. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 62.1), consistent with large membrane proteins containing both ordered transmembrane domains and flexible cytoplasmic regions. HPA reveals brain-enhanced expression (26.8 nTPM top tissue), tissue-enhanced distribution pattern, detected-in-all tissues distribution, predicted membrane protein classification, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutation (c.2418+2T>G) and chromosome 19p13.12-12/19p13.3-q13.43 copy number gains and losses, though specific disease phenotypes beyond intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy remain uncharacterized. STRING interactions reveal ANO8 functions within the anoctamin family network with medium confidence connections to C10orf71 (0.527), BTBD17 (0.519), FAM20C (0.511) - a Golgi casein kinase, STIM1 (0.502) - stromal interaction molecule 1 that senses ER calcium stores, plus GFOD2 (0.499), TBCCD1 (0.499), ARL14EPL (0.496), FAM20A (0.484), ANO1 (0.461) - the canonical calcium-activated chloride channel TMEM16A, and ANO5 (0.459). Recent studies (2020) established ANO8 as a genetic risk factor for intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP) through whole-exome sequencing, while 2024 reviews highlight that intracellular anoctamins (including ANO8) remain much less characterized than plasma membrane-localized ANO1/2/6. We hypothesize ANO8 functions as an intracellular calcium-activated chloride channel or phospholipid scramblase localized to endoplasmic reticulum or Golgi membranes, distinct from plasma membrane anoctamins. The STIM1 interaction (0.502) is particularly notable - STIM1 senses ER calcium depletion and activates store-operated calcium entry, suggesting ANO8 may participate in ER calcium homeostasis or calcium-dependent chloride flux at intracellular membranes. The FAM20C/FAM20A connections suggest potential coordination with Golgi phosphorylation machinery. Brain-enhanced expression (26.8 nTPM) indicates neuronal functions potentially related to intracellular ion homeostasis or membrane trafficking. The ANO1/ANO5 interactions confirm functional relationship with characterized anoctamin family members, while the dual transmembrane domain architecture (positions 237-581 and 708-877) matches the canonical TMEM16 fold. Intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy association suggests ANO8 variants disrupt bile acid transport or hepatocyte chloride homeostasis during pregnancy, revealing tissue-specific vulnerability to anoctamin dysfunction.
