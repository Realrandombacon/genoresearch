## Description
ZNF444 (Gene ID: 55311, UniProt Q8N0Y2) is a 327 amino acid SCAN-domain zinc finger transcription factor mapping to chromosome 19q13.43 (56,132,552-56,160,892 bp, ~28.3 kb genomic span). Comprehensive multi-source analysis reveals well-characterized function: (1) **InterPro domains**: SCAN oligomerization domain (PF02023, IPR003309, residues 20-98) mediates protein-protein interactions and heterodimerization with other SCAN-ZF proteins; three C2H2 zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 179-206, 207-234, 250-277) coordinate zinc ions for DNA binding to major groove; (2) **STRING interactions**: 10 high-confidence partners including ZNF446 (0.804), ZNF24 (0.797), SCARF1 (0.790), ZSCAN30 (0.789), EWSR1 (0.783), ZBED9 (0.776), ZKSCAN3 (0.748), SCAND1 (0.745), ZKSCAN4 (0.744), ZSCAN32 (0.733) - forms heteromeric complexes with related SCAN-ZF transcription factors; (3) **HPA expression**: Nuclear/nucleoplasmic localization, detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as transcription factor with DNA-binding molecular function; (4) **ClinVar**: 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 10 copy number gains spanning 19q13.33-13.43 (1.3-10.6 Mb duplications), associated with 19q rearrangement syndromes; (5) **AlphaFold structure**: pLDDT 67.2 (medium confidence), residues 1-327 modeled, partially structured with ordered zinc finger domains and flexible linker regions typical of C2H2-ZF proteins. **Functional hypothesis**: ZNF444 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional regulator that binds GC-rich promoter elements via C2H2 zinc fingers and recruits co-regulatory complexes through SCAN domain-mediated heterodimerization. PMID:11978792 demonstrates direct activation of SCARF1 (scavenger receptor class F member 1) promoter, regulating acetylated LDL (Ac-LDL) uptake and lipid metabolism in macrophages/endothelial cells. SCARF1 mediates lipid homeostasis, foam cell formation, and atherosclerosis pathogenesis. ZNF444 heterodimerizes with ZNF446, ZNF24, and other SCAN-ZF proteins to form cell-type-specific enhanceosomes complexes. **Mechanistic proposal**: In lipid-loaded macrophages, ZNF444 binds SCARF1 promoter 5′-flanking critical region, recruits histone acetyltransferases (p300/CBP) or chromatin remodelers via protein-protein interactions, and activates SCARF1 transcription. Dysregulation contributes to atherosclerosis, metabolic syndrome, and lipid storage disorders. 19q13.43 CNVs cause haploinsufficiency or overexpression, disrupting lipid metabolism gene networks. This is NOT a "dark gene" - well-characterized transcription factor with defined molecular function, interaction partners, and disease associations.
