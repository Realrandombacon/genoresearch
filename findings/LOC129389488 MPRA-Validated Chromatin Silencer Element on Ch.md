# LOC129389488: MPRA-Validated Chromatin Silencer Element on Chromosome 6p25 with Single CNV Association

## Description
LOC129389488 is a non-coding cis-regulatory DNA element classified as a transcriptional silencer, validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This 200bp genomic region (chr6:32291637-32291837, GRCh38) at 6p25.3 demonstrates capacity to repress super core promoter (SCP1) activity, confirming silencer function. As a regulatory DNA element, it lacks protein-coding features: no UniProt accession, no InterPro domains, no STRING interactions, and no HPA expression data. Clinical significance is modest: ClinVar documents 1 pathogenic copy number variant (CNV)—a duplication (x3) spanning 6p25.3-12.3 (chr6:156974-46789291), indicating some dosage sensitivity though lower burden than other silencer elements (LOC129389713: 14 CNVs; LOC129390646: 6 CNVs). Hypothesis: LOC129389488 functions as a chromatin silencer recruiting repressive complexes (Polycomb PRC2, HDACs, or KRAB-ZNF co-repressors) to silence target genes on 6p25, a telomeric region containing developmentally important loci. The single CNV association suggests moderate dosage sensitivity—disruption may contribute to 6p25 deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes including ocular anterior segment dysgenesis and hearing loss. K562 erythroid validation indicates potential relevance to blood cell development, though the lower CNV burden compared to 6q25 silencers suggests this element regulates less critical target genes or has functional redundancy with neighboring regulatory elements.
