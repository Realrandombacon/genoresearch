## Description
CHAMP1 encodes an 812 aa chromosome alignment-maintaining phosphoprotein (UniProt Q96JM3) functioning as a zinc finger scaffold for mitotic chromosome segregation and heterochromatin organization. The protein contains multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, aa14-37/62-85/709-732) for DNA binding, classic zinc finger superfamily folds (G3DSA:3.30.160.60, aa12-106/683-763), and CHAMP1-specific family signatures (IPR039330/PTHR37354, full-length). AlphaFold prediction shows low confidence (pLDDT 47.6), indicating a predominantly intrinsically disordered protein with structured zinc finger clusters—characteristic of scaffolds functioning through flexible protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with nucleoplasm and nuclear bodies localization, and disease association with intellectual disability. STRING interactions reveal the CHAMP1 complex membership: POGZ (0.716, heterochromatin assembly partner), chromobox proteins CBX1/3/5 (0.727-0.789, HP1 family heterochromatin readers), RAD50 (0.793, DNA damage repair), ADNP (0.534, chromatin regulator), and MAD2L2 (0.456, spindle checkpoint). ClinVar contains 178 pathogenic variants including nonsense (p.Gln98Ter), frameshift (p.Glu688fs), and missense mutations causing autosomal dominant intellectual disability, developmental delay, and dysmorphic features. Literature (51 papers) demonstrates CHAMP1 forms a complex with POGZ and HP1α directing heterochromatin assembly, promotes homology-directed DNA repair, reduces replication stress, and maintains kinetochore-microtubule attachments during metaphase. CHAMP1 likely functions as a disordered chromatin scaffold using zinc fingers for DNA recognition while flexible regions recruit heterochromatin modifiers (HP1, chromobox proteins) and DNA repair machinery (RAD50, MAD2L2), coupling chromosome segregation fidelity with epigenetic maintenance. Haploinsufficiency disrupts heterochromatin integrity and DNA repair, causing neurodevelopmental phenotypes through accumulated replication stress and chromosomal instability in developing neurons.
