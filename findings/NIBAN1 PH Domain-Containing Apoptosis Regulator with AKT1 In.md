## Description
NIBAN1 (C1orf24/FAM129A) encodes a 928 amino acid apoptosis regulator protein (UniProt Q9BZQ8) with medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 68.6). InterPro analysis reveals Niban-like family architecture (IPR026088, aa1-880), Niban 1/2/3 domain (IPR059060, aa337-506), PF26086 Niban 2-like domain (aa337-506), and critically a PH domain (PF26089, aa62-200) indicating phosphoinositide lipid-binding capability for membrane recruitment. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with heart muscle showing highest expression (153.6 nTPM), immune cell enhancement, plasma membrane and cytosol localization, and cancer-related gene classification with stress response and translation regulation functions. STRING interactions identify 6 partners (0.413-0.505 scores): STT3A (STT3 alpha catalytic subunit of oligosaccharyltransferase complex, 0.505), OSBPL10 (oxysterol binding protein-like 10, 0.479), CCDC179 (coiled-coil domain containing 179, 0.477), ARID4A (AT-rich interaction domain 4A, 0.455), EPHA2 (EPH receptor A2, 0.418), and AKT1 (AKT serine/threonine kinase 1, 0.413). This network positions NIBAN1 at the intersection of N-glycosylation (STT3A), lipid signaling (OSBPL10, PH domain), chromatin regulation (ARID4A), receptor tyrosine kinase signaling (EPHA2), and apoptosis control (AKT1). ClinVar contains 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses spanning 1q24.2-32.3 region, indicating strong dosage sensitivity. The 1q25.3 chromosomal location and cancer overexpression suggests oncogenic potential through AKT1 pathway modulation. Mechanistic hypothesis: NIBAN1 functions as a PH domain-containing scaffold protein that recruits to PIP2/PIP3-enriched plasma membrane microdomains to assemble AKT1 signaling complexes regulating p53-mediated apoptosis; its PH domain (aa62-200) mediates membrane localization while the Niban core domain (aa337-506) provides protein-protein interaction surfaces for STT3A-dependent glycosylation coupling and ARID4A chromatin regulation; overexpression in cancer cells enhances AKT1 survival signaling and disrupts apoptotic thresholds, while haploinsufficiency from 1q25.3 deletions impairs stress response capacity, explaining the dosage-sensitive phenotypes associated with copy number variants.
