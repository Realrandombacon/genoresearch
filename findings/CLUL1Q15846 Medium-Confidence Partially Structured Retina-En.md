## Description
CLUL1 (clusterin like 1, aliases RA337M) is a 466 amino acid predicted intracellular/secreted protein (UniProt Q15846) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 64.6) containing canonical clusterin-like architecture including Clusterin-like family (IPR000753/PTHR10970, residues 1-466) forming a 466 aa module (~100% of protein) characteristic of the clusterin family that mediates misfolded protein binding and chaperone activity via conserved two-chain structure, Clusterin N-terminal domain (IPR016014/SM00030, residues 26-237) forming a 212 aa module (~45%) for protein-protein interactions, Clusterin C-terminal domain (IPR016015/SM00035, residues 238-460) forming a 223 aa module (~48%) for substrate binding, Clusterin family (PF01093, residues 33-463) forming a 431 aa module (~92%) defining the CLUL1 signature, and molecular functions including misfolded protein binding, chaperone activity, protein stabilization, and endoplasmic reticulum/plasma membrane localization, functioning as a retina-enriched secreted/intracellular protein that regulates retinal protein homeostasis and misfolded protein binding through clusterin domain-mediated chaperone activity and retinol-binding protein association via partnerships with RBP3, ENOSF1, AKAIN1, LPIN2, GUCA1A, THOC1, CETN1, MYOM1, and COLEC12.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals CLUL1 contains extensive clusterin-like architecture: The N-terminal clusterin domain (residues 26-237) forms the beta-chain that mediates protein-protein interactions and chaperone activity. The two-domain structure is characteristic of clusterin family proteins that function as extracellular chaperones.
