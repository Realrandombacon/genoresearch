# ACSM5: Mitochondrial Medium-Chain Acyl-CoA Synthetase Activating Fatty Acids for β-Oxidation and Modulating Ferroptosis in Hepatocellular Carcinoma

## Description
ACSM5 (Q6NUN0) is a 579-aa mitochondrial acyl-CoA synthetase with very high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 90.4), indicating a stable well-folded enzyme with canonical ANL superfamily architecture. InterPro analysis reveals AMP-dependent synthetase/ligase domain (IPR000873/PF00501: positions 69-422), AMP-binding conserved site (IPR020845/PS00455: positions 227-238), C-terminal domain (IPR025110/PF13193: positions 486-566), and mitochondrial acyl-CoA synthetase medium-chain family signatures (IPR051087/PTHR43605). HPA expression shows liver-enriched pattern (258.6 nTPM), localized to vesicles, functioning in fatty acid and lipid metabolism. STRING interactions identify high-confidence binding to HSDL2 (0.706), citrate synthase CS (0.688), and medium-confidence interactions with related acyl-CoA synthetases ACSF2 (0.616), ACSF3 (0.558), plus β-oxidation enzymes HAO2 (0.541), HADH (0.514), ACAA2 (0.506), ACADS (0.481), HADHB (0.469), ACADL (0.422). Literature (46 papers) establishes ACSM5 activates medium-chain fatty acids to acyl-CoA thioesters for mitochondrial β-oxidation. Recent 2025-2026 studies reveal ACSM5 regulates ferroptosis in hepatocellular carcinoma by upregulating POR and modulating lipid metabolism, inhibits ligamentum flavum hypertrophy via FABP4/PPAR signaling, and serves as prognostic biomarker for immunotherapy efficacy in non-small cell lung cancer. ACSM5 c.1273C>A polymorphism promotes lung adenocarcinoma progression in Xuanwei region. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 16p13.11-12.1 and splice variants (c.1436+35A>G), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. The very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.4) suggests ACSM5 employs two-step adenylation-thioesterification mechanism typical of ANL superfamily enzymes, positioning medium-chain fatty acids for β-oxidation while simultaneously regulating lipid peroxidation susceptibility in ferroptosis pathways.
