## Description
RAB3IL1 (RAB3A interacting protein-like 1, Q8TBN0), also known as Rabin3, encodes a 382 aa cytosolic protein functioning as a specialized guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that activates specific Rab GTPases (primarily RAB8A and RAB11A) through catalytic Sec2-homology domain-mediated nucleotide exchange, with critical roles in post-Golgi vesicular trafficking, ciliogenesis, centriolar satellite organization, and polarized membrane delivery based on interaction networks and subcellular localization, representing an understudied regulator of the Rab GTPase cycle essential for cellular logistics and structural integrity. Structural architecture reveals a well-folded organization with high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 78.4): Sec2 N-terminal domain (IPR009449/PF06428/SSF144284/G3DSA:1.20.5.4880, residues 48-163/75-159/48-163/71-154) forming an extended coiled-coil structure characteristic of the Sec2p family that likely mediates dimerization or scaffolding interactions with upstream regulators like Rab11, Rabin8-like C-terminal domain (PF25555/cd21069, residues 218-377/216-378) adopting the catalytic fold responsible for binding inactive GDP-bound Rab substrates and promoting GDP release to allow GTP binding, RAB3IL/RAB3IP/Sec2 family domain (IPR040351/PTHR14430, residues 4-381/4-381) spanning the full length defining the specific functional lineage within the broader GEF superfamily, GO terms guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity (GO:0005085) confirming canonical mechanism of action.
