## Description
SAP30L (SAP30 Like; UniProt Q9HAJ7) is a 183 amino acid nuclear protein with 31 publications, representing a partially characterized corepressor subunit of the Sin3-histone deacetylase (HDAC) complex containing a redox-sensitive zinc finger domain and Sin3-binding region that regulates chromatin silencing through recruitment of HDAC1/2 enzymatic activity.\n\nSTRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE: InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture optimized for HDAC complex assembly and DNA interaction. The N-terminal region contains a SAP30 zinc-finger domain (IPR025717/PF13866/G3DSA:3.40.1800.30, residues 26-95/24-90) belonging to the Cys2His2-type zinc finger superfamily, which mediates DNA binding and is sensitive to redox-dependent disulfide bond formation affecting structural integrity. The C-terminal region contains a Sin3 binding domain (IPR025718/PF13867/G3DSA:6.10.160.20, residues 114-166/91-181) that directly interacts with SIN3A scaffold protein to anchor SAP30L within the corepressor complex. The full-length protein belongs to the SAP30/SAP30-like family (IPR024145/PTHR13286, residues 28-181/28-181) and SAP30 C-terminal domain superfamily (IPR038291, residues 91-181).
