# Uncharacterized Non-Coding Locus at 8p23.3 8p23.1 Deletion Syndrome Critical Region with Extreme Pathogenic CNV Burden (54 Variants)

**Date:** 2026-03-19T04:52:06.857729

**Quality Score:** 3.85/10  (E=4.0, D=3.7) [MODERATE]

## Description
LOC401442 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 8p23.3 (NC_000008.11:738547-740373) spanning approximately 1.8 kb. Comprehensive database analysis reveals no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified (precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction), STRING database returned no protein interaction partners (confirming absence of characterized interactome), and Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000282375 but reported no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 54 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number variations affecting the 8p23.3-23.1 and 8p23.3-21.2 regions. Representative variants include: (1) 8p23.3 single allele deletion, (2) 8p23.3-21.2 deletion (chr8:449893-23854904, ~23.4 Mb), (3) 8p23.3-23.1 deletion (chr8:241530-7895064, ~7.7 Mb), (4) 8p23.3-23.1 duplication (chr8:241530-7895064, ~7.7 Mb, 3 copies), (5) 8p23.3 deletion (chr8:244617-2017223, ~1.8 Mb), (6) 8p23.3-23.1 deletion (chr8:241530-7022841, ~6.8 Mb), (7) 8p23.3-23.1 tetrasomy (chr8:208048-7186524, ~7.0 Mb, 4 copies), (8) 8p23.3-23.2 deletion (chr8:208048-5615542, ~5.4 Mb), (9) 8p23.3-23.1 deletion (chr8:208048-7087252, ~6.9 Mb), (10) 8p23.3-23.1 duplication (chr8:226452-12698554, ~12.5 Mb). The 8p23.3 region is the critical 8p23.1 deletion syndrome locus containing dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental and cardiac genes including GATA4 (GATA binding protein 4, cardiac development, septation defects), CSMD1 (CUB and Sushi multiple domains 1, complement regulation, brain development), DLGAP2 (discs large associated protein 2, postsynaptic density), and multiple cardiac-neural enhancer elements. LOC401442 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 8p23.1 GATA4/CSMD1 expression in cardiac progenitors and neural crest cells, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in cardiogenesis and neurodevelopment, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 8p23.1 topologically associating domain (TAD) separating cardiac and neural gene regulatory landscapes, or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 8p23.1 microdeletion/duplication susceptibility (8p23.1 is flanked by beta-defensin gene clusters creating recombination hotspots). The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (54 variants - highest among analyzed LOC genes vs. 44 for LOC100192426, 26 for LOC728158) with both gains and losses suggests: (1) this locus is highly dosage-sensitive for 8p23.1 region gene regulation, (2) copy number alterations disrupt 8p23.1 chromatin architecture leading to GATA4 haploinsufficiency (congenital heart defects: septal defects, tetralogy of Fallot) and neurodevelopmental deficits, (3) gains/losses may alter cardiac morphogenesis and neuronal development contributing to 8p23.1 deletion syndrome phenotype (intellectual disability, developmental delay, congenital heart disease, diaphragmatic hernia, dysmorphic features, behavioral problems), or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the 8p23.1 deletion syndrome critical region with potent cardioneurodevelopmental regulatory effects. 8p23.1 deletion syndrome is a well-characterized microdeletion disorder with cardiac defects in ~50% of cases.
