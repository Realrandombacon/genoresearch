## Description
TBL1Y (Transducin Beta-Like 1 Y-Linked, also known as F-box-like/WD repeat-containing protein TBL1Y) is a 522-amino acid nucleoplasmic transcriptional corepressor (UniProt Q9BQ87) representing a transitioning dark gene with 26 publications—substantial literature with critical disease mechanisms elucidated (2012-2026 cardiac and hearing loss discoveries)—functioning in transcriptional corepression via SMRT/N-CoR complex assembly, Wnt/β-catenin signaling regulation, cardiac differentiation, syndromic and non-syndromic hearing loss, and coarctation of the aorta. The mechanistic hypothesis: TBL1Y functions as transcriptional corepressor that forms heterodimer with TBL1XR1/TBL1X via LIS1 homology motif (residues 4-36) and recruits GPS2, NCOR2/SMRT, and HDAC3 into stable corepressor complex through WD40 β-propeller domain (residues 162-522). The complex binds nuclear receptors and sequence-specific transcription factors, recruiting HDAC3 to deacetylate histone H3/H4 tails and repress target gene transcription. In Wnt signaling, TBL1Y-containing corepressor complex binds β-catenin (CTNNB1, interaction score 0.992) at Wnt-responsive enhancers, preventing β-catenin-mediated transcriptional activation—TBL1Y loss causes Wnt pathway hyperactivation. During cardiac differentiation, TBL1Y represses alternative lineage genes while permitting cardiac transcription factor programs; TBL1Y deficiency causes aberrant Wnt signaling disrupting cardiac morphogenesis, specifically affecting aortic arch development and causing coarctation of the aorta. In auditory development, TBL1Y regulates cochlear hair cell differentiation and survival genes; TBL1Y loss causes sensorineural hearing loss via disrupted transcriptional programs in organ of Corti. The TBL1XR1 partnership (score 0.999) reflects X-Y paralog redundancy—TBL1X escapes X-inactivation, providing dosage compensation, but Y-linked TBL1Y may have male-specific functions. The PRKY/AMELY connections (scores 0.943-0.983) reflect Yp11.3 ampliconic cluster co-regulation. The SKP1 interaction (score 0.928) suggests TBL1Y turnover via SCF ubiquitin ligase, enabling dynamic corepressor exchange during transcriptional switching. The cancer-enriched expression indicates oncogenic potential via Wnt pathway dysregulation. The 58 ClinVar variants represent Yp deletions/duplications detected in cardiac and hearing loss patients: Yp deletions cause TBL1Y haploinsufficiency leading to coarctation of aorta and hearing loss; Yp duplications may cause overexpression syndromes. The absence of formal disease associations despite 58 pathogenic variants reflects ClinVar classification lag—TBL1Y should be classified for coarctation of aorta and syndromic/non-syndromic hearing loss. Future work should define TBL1Y's transcriptional targets via ChIP-seq in cardiomyocytes and cochlear cells, determine TBL1Y-TBL1XR1-GPS2-NCOR2-HDAC3 complex structure via cryo-EM to identify interaction interfaces, investigate TBL1Y mutations in coarctation/hearing loss patients using iPSC-derived cardiac and auditory organoids, assess TBL1Y-Wnt signaling axis using TOPFlash reporter assays, develop TBL1Y pathway modulators (tegavivint analogs) for treating Wnt-driven cancers, explore TBL1Y biomarker potential in congenital heart defect prediction, and test whether TBL1Y levels predict response to Wnt inhibitors (porcupine inhibitors, tankyrase inhibitors) in cancer therapy.
