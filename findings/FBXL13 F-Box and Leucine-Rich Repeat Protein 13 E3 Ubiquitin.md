## Description
FBXL13 (F-Box and Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein 13) is a 735 amino acid substrate recognition component of SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box protein) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes that targets specific proteins for proteasomal degradation, serving as a critical regulator of centrosome homeostasis, renal sodium handling, and cell migration through ubiquitin-mediated proteolysis. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic bipartite architecture including N-terminal F-box domain (IPR001810, PF12937, PS50181, cd22124, SSF81383, residues 152-200) with protein binding capability (GO:0005515) for SKP1 interaction and SCF complex assembly, multiple leucine-rich repeat cysteine-containing subtypes (IPR006553, SM00367, residues 244-269/295-320/323-348) for substrate recognition, LRR domain superfamily (IPR032675, PF25372, residues 231-372/477-657) providing curved solenoid structure for protein-protein interactions, F-box-like domain superfamily (IPR036047, residues 156-195), and ribonuclease inhibitor-like structural homology (G3DSA:3.80.10.10, SSF52047, residues 148-398/399-529/530-692) forming the LRR scaffold. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8NEE6-F1) predicts very high confidence (pLDDT 88.6) across the 735 residue sequence, indicating an exceptionally well-folded bipartite protein with stable F-box and extensive LRR solenoid suitable for simultaneous SCF complex recruitment and substrate binding. HPA data classifies FBXL13 as a predicted intracellular protein involved in Ubl conjugation pathway, with tissue-enhanced expression (stomach: 4.8 nTPM, testis: 3.7 nTPM), low cancer specificity, detected in some tissues, group-enriched blood cell distribution, and cell type-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal tight functional coupling to the SCF E3 ligase machinery and centrosomal/ciliary proteins: CETN2 (Centrin-2, 0.915) and CETN3 (0.764) as centrosomal calcium-binding proteins; CUL1 (Cullin-1, 0.914) as the SCF scaffold protein; CEP152 (Centrosomal protein 152 kDa, 0.779) for centriole duplication; DRC3 (Dynein Regulatory Complex subunit 3, 0.716) and DRC7 (0.646) as nexin-dynein regulatory complex components for ciliary/flagellar function; RBX1 (RING-box protein 1, 0.651) as the RING component recruiting E2 enzymes; FBXL3 (0.663) as a related F-box protein; SKP1 (S-phase kinase-associated protein 1, 0.413) as the adaptor linking F-box to CUL1; and TCTE1 (T-complex-associated-testis-expressed protein 1, 0.670) for ciliary function. ClinVar lists 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses on 7q22.1-31.31, 7q22.1-31.1, 7q22.1 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 2-19 Mb), copy number gains on 7p22.3-q36.3, 7q22.1-36.3, and single nucleotide variants (c.597G>T p.Leu199=, c.765+55C>A, c.1278G>A p.Gln426=), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in renal disorder or ciliopathy susceptibility. Literature (19 papers) establishes FBXL13 as a context-dependent ubiquitination regulator: FBXL13 directs proteolysis of CEP192 to regulate centrosome homeostasis and cell migration establishing it as a centrosomal E3 ligase; FBXL13 mediates cGMP-dependent NKCC2 (Na-K-2Cl cotransporter) ubiquitination in thick ascending limbs linking nitric oxide and atrial natriuretic peptide signaling to renal NaCl reabsorption control; Nexin-Dynein regulatory complex component DRC7 but not FBXL13 is required for sperm flagellum formation and male fertility in mice revealing partial functional redundancy in ciliogenesis; and FBXL13 regulates centrosome homeostasis through ubiquitin-mediated proteolysis affecting cell migration and potentially cancer metastasis. As a moderately characterized F-box protein with defined roles in centrosome biology and renal physiology, FBXL13 represents a critical node connecting SCF-mediated ubiquitination to centriole duplication, ciliary function, salt homeostasis, and cell motility through targeted degradation of centrosomal proteins like CEP192 and renal transporters like NKCC2.
