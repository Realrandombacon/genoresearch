## Description
LOC126806534 is an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) at chromosome 2q36.1 (genomic coordinates 224219806-224221005, ~1.2kb span). This locus is NOT a protein-coding gene but a functional regulatory DNA element validated through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs). The gene_info summary identifies this as a MED14-INDEPENDENT GROUP 3 ENHANCER that was experimentally validated in HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells. Critically, this enhancer depends on BRD2, BRD4, P300/CBP, and CDK7 cofactors but shows LIMITED OR NO DEPENDENCE on the MED14 core Mediator complex subunit. This cofactor profile distinguishes it from canonical Mediator-dependent enhancers and suggests it operates through BET-dependent chromatin remodeling mechanisms that bypass core Mediator recruitment. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants affecting this locus through large-scale chromosomal rearrangements. This 7:2 gain-to-loss ratio (78% amplification) suggests oncogene or dosage-sensitive developmental gene regulation. The 2q region contains critical developmental genes including SATB2 (special AT-rich binding protein 2, 2q33.1, craniofacial and neuronal development), PAX3 (paired box 3, 2q35, neural crest development, Waardenburg syndrome), and multiple other developmental transcription factors. No UniProt accession exists (non-coding DNA), STRING found no interactions (not a protein), HPA could not resolve (not protein-coding), and PubMed has zero publications for this specific locus. This represents a genuine dark regulatory element with clinical relevance — the MED14-independent enhancer activity combined with amplification-dominant pathogenicity suggests LOC126806534 functions as a developmental gene enhancer where amplification drives overexpression.
