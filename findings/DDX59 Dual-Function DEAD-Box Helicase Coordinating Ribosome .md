## Description
DDX59 encodes a 619 amino acid ATP-dependent RNA helicase (UniProt Q5T1V6) belonging to the DEAD-box superfamily (IPR011545, PF00270). Despite its classification as a helicase, DDX59 exhibits a unique domain architecture combining canonical RNA unwinding machinery with a specialized HIT-type zinc finger (IPR007529, PF04438, residues 105-132) that may mediate protein-protein interactions or nucleic acid recognition.\n\nSTRUCTURAL FEATURES: AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 77.2) across all 619 residues. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite helicase core: N-terminal DEAD/DEAH-box domain (residues 227-393, cd17962) containing the conserved Q motif (IPR014014, residues 203-231) for ATP binding, followed by a C-terminal helicase domain (residues 417-549, cd18787, PF00271). The P-loop NTPase fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.300) spans both regions (residues 169-600), forming a continuous nucleotide-binding cleft. The N-terminal HIT zinc finger distinguishes DDX59 from other DEAD-box proteins and may recruit specific cofactors.\n\nINTERACTION NETWORK: STRING-DB identifies 10 interaction partners revealing dual functional roles. Ciliary trafficking cluster: TCTN3 (0.570, tectonic family ciliopathy protein), CPLANE1 (0.548, ciliopathy-associated complex), TBC1D32 (0.574, Rab GTPase regulator). DHX30 (0.580) is a mitochondrial RNA helicase suggesting potential metabolic links.\n\nEXPRESSION & LOCALIZATION: HPA confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity) with low cancer specificity. Subcellular localization is remarkably diverse: nucleoplasm (consistent with ribosome biogenesis), vesicles, actin filaments, and critically—primary cilium and cilium tip. This dual localization supports coordination between nuclear RNA processing and ciliary assembly.\n\nCLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: ClinVar reports 35 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses (1q21.1-44, 1q25.3-32.1, 1q31.3-32.1), frameshift mutations (p.His400fs), splice site variants (c.804+1G>A), and missense changes (p.Pro4Ala). While specific monogenic diseases are not fully annotated, HPA classifies DDX59 as involved in ciliopathy, consistent with its ciliary localization and interaction with TCTN3/CPLANE1.\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: Recent literature (24 papers) reveals emerging roles: (1) Glioma proliferation via miR-628-5p regulation, (2) Bovine milk synthesis through PI3K-DDX59 signaling, (3) Lung adenocarcinoma prognosis via antisense transcript DDX59-AS1. Mechanistically, DDX59 likely functions as a molecular bridge: in the nucleolus, it processes rRNA precursors via the PeBoW complex (PES1-BOP1-WDR12) for ribosome assembly; during ciliogenesis, it shuttles to the cilium tip where it may unwind structured RNAs required for intraflagellar transport or regulate ciliary mRNA localization. The HIT zinc finger may recruit DDX59 to specific RNA substrates or protein complexes at each location. Loss-of-function would simultaneously impair protein synthesis capacity and ciliary signaling (Hedgehog, Wnt), explaining the ciliopathy phenotype.
