## Description
PAGR1 (UniProt Q9BTK6) encodes a 254-amino acid PAXIP1-associated glutamate-rich protein containing a C-terminal PTIP-binding domain (PF15364, aa 86-218) within the PTIP-associated protein 1 family (IPR028213). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT: 62.9), consistent with scaffold proteins containing both ordered interaction domains and flexible regions for complex assembly.\n\nSTRING interactions reveal PAGR1 as an integral component of the MLL3/4 histone methyltransferase complex: exclusive high-confidence partnerships with PAXIP1/PTIP (0.999), H3K4 methyltransferases KMT2C/MLL3 (0.988) and KMT2D/MLL4 (0.966), core complex members WDR5 (0.996), ASH2L (0.994), RBBP5 (0.994), DPY30 (0.996), demethylase KDM6A (0.997), and coactivator NCOA6 (0.987). HPA shows brain-enriched expression (54.3 nTPM) with ubiquitous detection, localizing to the nucleus as expected for chromatin regulators.\n\nWith only 17 papers, PAGR1 is a transitional dark gene with rapidly emerging functional understanding. Recent breakthroughs include: (1) bi-allelic variants causing microcephaly and severe neurodevelopmental disorder (2021, 7 citations); (2) STAG2-PAXIP1/PAGR1 axis suppressing lung adenocarcinoma tumorigenesis (2024); (3) regulation of cohesin chromatin association in sister chromatid cohesion-defective cells (2022, 13 citations); and (4) glucose-responsive PAGR1-regulated skeletal muscle gene program controlling systemic glucose homeostasis (2025, 3 citations). ClinVar contains 289 pathogenic variants, predominantly 16p11.2 copy number gains/losses. This positions PAGR1 as a critical epigenetic scaffold bridging MLL3/4-mediated transcription to cohesin function, neurodevelopment, cancer suppression, and metabolic regulation.
