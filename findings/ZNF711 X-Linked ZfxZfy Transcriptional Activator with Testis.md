# ZNF711: X-Linked Zfx/Zfy Transcriptional Activator with Testis-Specific Expression and Intellectual Disability Variants

## Description
ZNF711 (zinc finger protein 711) encodes a 761 amino acid X-linked transcriptional activator (UniProt Q9Y462) belonging to the Zfx/Zfy family. The protein features a distinctive N-terminal transcriptional activation domain (InterPro IPR006794, Pfam PF04704, residues 66-368) followed by three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (IPR013087, PF00096 at positions 383-405, 505-527, 562-581). HPA data reveals testis-specific high expression (34.9 nTPM) with nuclear localization, consistent with a role in spermatogenesis or X-chromosome regulation in male germ cells. ClinVar documents 228 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including frameshifts (p.His740fs), missense mutations (p.Thr760Ala, p.Asp191Tyr), and large Xq CNVs, directly linking ZNF711 loss-of-function to X-linked intellectual disability (MRX65/MRX97). AlphaFold prediction yields low confidence (pLDDT 54.6), suggesting an intrinsically disordered N-terminal activation domain connected to structured zinc fingers, a hallmark of transcriptional co-activators that recruit chromatin remodelers. Mechanistically, ZNF711 likely binds GC-rich promoter elements via its zinc fingers and recruits histone acetyltransferases or SWI/SNF complexes through its acidic activation domain to drive expression of neurodevelopmental and spermatogenesis genes. Haploinsufficiency disrupts this activation, leading to cognitive deficits and potentially male infertility.
