## Description
CCDC144A (UniProt A2RUR9) is a 1427 amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein representing an understudied component of the human dark genome with emerging clinical significance. The protein architecture features three major structural modules: an N-terminal CCDC144A/B/C family domain (IPR040118/PTHR22245, residues 21-690) defining membership in this conserved protein family; a central extensive coiled-coil region (IPR039497/PF14915, residues 703-1183) spanning 480 amino acids that likely mediates homo- or hetero-oligomerization through alpha-helical bundle formation—a hallmark structural mechanism for scaffold proteins; and a C-terminal domain of unknown function DUF3496 (IPR021885/PF12001, residues 1245-1353) of 109 amino acids with no characterized biochemical activity, representing a key target for functional elucidation. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (global pLDDT 61.0) spanning the full 1-1427 residue length, consistent with a protein containing ordered coiled-coil domains interspersed with flexible linker regions—structural characteristics typical of scaffolding proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly at cellular junctions. HPA classifies CCDC144A as both a predicted intracellular and membrane protein with tissue-enhanced RNA expression detected in many tissues, showing highest expression in retina (11.9 nTPM), suggesting specialized function in photoreceptor cells or retinal pigment epithelium. Subcellular localization to cell junctions and cytosol aligns with the coiled-coil domain's predicted role in cytoskeletal organization and intercellular adhesion complexes. STRING interaction network reveals medium-confidence associations with functionally diverse partners: CDRT15L2 (0.599, CMT1A duplicated region transcript 15-like 2), ANKRD7 (0.574, ankyrin repeat domain 7), ZNF654 (0.529, zinc finger protein 654, transcriptional regulator), SLC35G6 (0.507, solute carrier family 35 member G6), OR3A2 (0.487, olfactory receptor 3A2), LRRC3C (0.481, leucine-rich repeat containing 3C), TBC1D28 (0.480, TBC domain containing 28, Rab GTPase activator), C17orf64 (0.443, chromosome 17 open reading frame 64), SLC35G3 (0.421, solute carrier 35G3), EFCAB13 (0.413, EF-hand calcium binding domain 13). This interaction profile suggests roles in vesicular trafficking (TBC1D28), calcium signaling (EFCAB13), and potentially transcriptional regulation (ZNF654). ClinVar contains 59 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains affecting chromosome 17p11.2-12 (chr17:15759103-20564268, chr17:15694772-20582794, chr17:16651293-20450566), a region associated with Smith-Magenis syndrome (17p11.2 deletion) and Potocki-Lupski syndrome (17p11.2 duplication). While no specific diseases are currently attributed solely to CCDC144A mutations, its location within this critical dosage-sensitive region suggests haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity may contribute to neurodevelopmental phenotypes observed in 17p11.2 CNV syndromes. Evolutionary conservation across vertebrates (pan troglodytes isoform 3, 570 aa) indicates functional constraint despite limited characterization. The mechanistic model positions CCDC144A as a cytoskeletal scaffold protein using its extensive coiled-coil domain (480 aa) for oligomerization and protein-protein interactions at cell junctions, potentially linking membrane receptors to cytoskeletal elements in retina and other tissues. The uncharacterized DUF3496 domain may represent a novel protein-binding module or enzymatic activity awaiting discovery. Retina-enriched expression suggests potential involvement in photoreceptor outer segment maintenance, synaptic transmission at photoreceptor ribbon synapses, or retinal pigment epithelium barrier function. Loss-of-function through 17p11.2 deletions may disrupt cell junction integrity and intercellular communication, contributing to developmental abnormalities.
