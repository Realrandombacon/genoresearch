# Uncharacterized Locus at 16q24.3 Telomere within 16q24 Microdeletion Syndrome Region with Extreme Pathogenic CNV Burden

**Date:** 2026-03-19T03:51:54.152405

**Quality Score:** 2.7/10  (E=3.6, D=1.8) [WEAK]

## Description
LOC101927817 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 16q24.3 telomere (NC_000016.10:89430917-89454493) spanning approximately 23.6 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 73 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all copy number alterations including large deletions (chr16:87640702-89570635, ~1.9 Mb; chr16:89018178-89560695, ~543 kb; chr16:89462287-89526985, ~65 kb; chr16:89472880-89612756, ~140 kb; chr16:89151360-89518831, ~368 kb; chr16:89505521-89592077, ~87 kb) and duplications (chr16:46432879-90294753, ~43.9 Mb whole arm; chr16:82865402-90163542, ~7.3 Mb). These structural variants encompass the 16q24.3 telomeric region and are associated with 16q24 microdeletion syndrome and KBG syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, craniofacial dysmorphism (macrodontia of upper central incisors), skeletal anomalies, and seizures. The 16q24.3 region contains ANKRD11 (ankyrin repeat domain 11), a transcriptional coregulator whose haploinsufficiency causes KBG syndrome, and FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated gene) at 16q12.2. LOC101927817 likely functions as part of the 16q24.3 telomeric cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a telomeric non-coding RNA (TERRA-like element) whose copy number alteration contributes to 16q24 syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of telomere capping, chromatin architecture, or long-range regulation of ANKRD11. The absence of protein-coding features combined with extreme pathogenic CNV burden (73 variants - highest observed in this study) indicates this locus resides within the most dosage-sensitive telomeric region of chromosome 16 where structural variation profoundly disrupts neurodevelopmental processes via epigenetic dysregulation and telomere position effects.
