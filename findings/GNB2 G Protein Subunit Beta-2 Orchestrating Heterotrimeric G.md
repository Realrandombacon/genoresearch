## Description
GNB2 (G Protein Subunit Beta-2) is a 340 amino acid WD40 repeat-containing protein that functions as a critical component of heterotrimeric G protein complexes, mediating GPCR signal transduction, GIRK channel activation, and ferroptosis regulation through seven-bladed beta-propeller WD40 domain-mediated G gamma subunit binding and HSPA5/GPX4 pathway modulation in cardiac, immune, and cancer tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic G protein beta subunit architecture including WD40 domain (cd00200, residues 48-340) forming the signature seven-bladed beta-propeller structure for protein-protein interactions, G-protein beta subunit-like WD40 repeats (IPR001632, PR00319, residues 51-67/70-84/89-104) for G gamma binding interface, WD40 repeat family (IPR001680, PF25391, PS00678, PS50082, residues 44-125/132-340/51-92/139-179/180-221) with conserved Trp-Asp signatures for structural integrity, G-protein subunit beta 1-5 family (IPR016346, PIRSF002394, residues 1-340/2-340) defining evolutionary relationship to transducin beta chains, WD40/YVTN repeat-like-containing domain superfamily (IPR015943, IPR036322, G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 1-340/3-340) providing protein binding scaffold (GO:0005515), PAC1/LIS1-like WD-40 repeats (IPR020472, residues 70-84/157-171/199-213) for signal transduction function (GO:0007165), and GPROTEINBRPT domain signatures (PR00320) defining functional motifs. AlphaFold structure (AF-P62879-F1) predicts very high confidence (pLDDT 96.9) across the 340 residue sequence, indicating an exceptionally well-folded seven-bladed beta-propeller with stable WD40 platform suitable for G gamma dimerization and effector recruitment. HPA data classifies GNB2 as a disease-related gene and potential drug target involved in RAS pathway with plasma membrane subcellular localization reflecting GPCR signaling roles, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated signal transduction function; disease involvement includes intellectual disability and disease variants. ClinVar lists 60 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (c.227A>G p.Asp76Gly, c.658C>T p.Gln220Ter, c.746C>T p.Thr249Met, c.89T>C p.Leu30Pro, c.629T>G p.Leu210Arg, c.286C>G p.Arg96Gly, c.1009A>C p.Lys337Gln), duplication (c.389_397dup p.Asn132_Val133insGluGlyAsn), intronic variants (c.57+62T>A), and large deletions (NC_000007.14:g.99487704_102394107del), with recent identification of heterozygous c.155G>T mutation causative for sick sinus syndrome and sinus bradycardia. Literature (98 papers) establishes GNB2 as an emerging disease gene: GNB2 promotes breast cancer progression by up-regulating HSPA5/GPX4 and inhibiting ferroptosis revealing cell death regulation mechanism; novel GNB2 variant identified as cause of sick sinus syndrome establishing cardiac arrhythmia etiology; patient-specific hiPS cell line with heterozygous GNB2 mutation UKMi003-A causative for human sinus node dysfunction generated for disease modeling; LncRNA CCAT2 promotes colorectal cancer proliferation and metastasis through ERK and Wnt signaling activation by regulating GNB2 expression demonstrating oncogenic lncRNA axis; SNHG5 enhances colorectal cancer metastasis through RNA-protein interaction network. As an emerging G protein beta subunit with cardiac and cancer associations, GNB2 represents a critical node connecting GPCR signal transduction to cardiac arrhythmia, cancer progression, and ferroptosis resistance through WD40 beta-propeller-mediated GNG5/GNG4/GNG12 binding for G beta-gamma dimer formation, GIRK channel activation for cardiac pacemaker function, HSPA5/GPX4 pathway upregulation for ferroptosis inhibition in breast cancer, CCAT2/SNHG5 lncRNA-mediated expression control in colorectal cancer metastasis, and ERK/Wnt signaling integration disrupted in sick sinus syndrome, breast cancer progression, colorectal cancer metastasis, and intellectual disability.
