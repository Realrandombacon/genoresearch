## Description
LRRC49 (UniProt Q8IUZ0) is a 686 amino acid intracellular protein representing an understudied but structurally well-characterized member of the leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein superfamily with emerging roles in ciliary biology, microtubule regulation, and tissue-specific functions in heart muscle and male reproductive system. The protein exhibits sophisticated domain architecture combining multiple structural elements: (1) extensive leucine-rich repeat regions comprising multiple LRR domains (IPR001611 at 113-134, 135-156, 157-178; IPR003591 typical subtype at 133-155, 177-198, 199-220; IPR025875 LRR4 at 113-153; PF12799 at 113-153; PF14580 at 183-323; SM00365 SDS22-like subfamily at 111-132, 133-154, 155-176), forming curved solenoid structures characteristic of protein-protein interaction scaffolds, (2) Ribonuclease Inhibitor-like superfamily fold (G3DSA:3.80.10.10) spanning residues 52-177 and 178-332, indicating structural similarity to RNase inhibitor horseshoe architecture repurposed for scaffold function, (3) defining ciliary and flagellar integrity-associated protein family signature (IPR050576/PTHR45973) spanning residues 91-648 (81% of sequence), strongly implicating LRRC49 in ciliary assembly, maintenance, or function.
