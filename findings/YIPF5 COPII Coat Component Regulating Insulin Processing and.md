## Description
Yip1 domain family member 5 (YIPF5) is a critical Golgi membrane protein and COPII coat component essential for ER-to-Golgi vesicle-mediated transport. Structurally, it contains a conserved Yip1 domain (PF04893, positions 110-254) with medium confidence AlphaFold modeling (pLDDT 67.6), indicating a partially structured core with intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) likely responsible for dynamic interactions with COPII subunits (SEC24B, YIF1A) and RAB GTPases (RAB1A, RAB6A). Functionally, YIPF5 is involved in insulin processing and regulation of secretory pathway dynamics. Despite 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar and associations with Diabetes Mellitus and Epilepsy, no specific disease syndrome is cataloged, classifying it as a 'dark gene' with a pleiotropic phenotype. Loss of function disrupts insulin maturation and neuronal protein homeostasis, leading to metabolic and neurological defects.
