Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000231131 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains (trisomy) affecting 10q: large duplications spanning 10q11.21-21.3 (chr10:42112187-67400675, ~25.3 Mb), 10q11.23-23.2 (chr10:50729367-87147204, ~36.4 Mb), and 10q11.21-22.2 (chr10:42685306-73715908, ~31.0 Mb). The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (3 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this locus resides within a critical 10q dosage-sensitive genomic region where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) PTEN haploinsufficiency leading to PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway hyperactivation, (2) 10q chromatin architecture affecting tumor suppressor expression, (3) cell cycle control and DNA damage response, or (4) developmental processes, leading to macrocephaly, intellectual disability, hamartoma formation, and cancer predisposition phenotypes associated with 10q duplication syndromes.
