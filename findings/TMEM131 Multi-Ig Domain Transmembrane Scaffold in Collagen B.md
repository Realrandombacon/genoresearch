## Description
TMEM131 (Transmembrane Protein 131, UniProt Q92545) is a large 1883 amino acid type I transmembrane protein localized to chromosome 2q11.2 that functions as a collagen-binding scaffold protein involved in collagen biosynthetic processes and vesicular trafficking. The protein architecture is dominated by five immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR013783, G3DSA:2.60.40.10) positioned at residues 106-207, 317-428, 827-975, plus specialized TMEM131-specific Ig domains (IPR056311, IPR045695, IPR055435-437) and an N-terminal TMEM131-specific domain (IPR022113/IPR039877, PF12371, residues 109-191). This multi-Ig domain architecture suggests TMEM131 functions as an extracellular matrix scaffold that binds collagen triple helices through Ig-like β-sandwich folds. STRING interaction network reveals 10 high-confidence partners: RALGAPB (0.702, Ral GTPase activating catalytic subunit beta), TRAPPC8 (0.614, trafficking protein particle complex subunit 8 linking to ER-Golgi vesicle transport), UBP1 (0.540, transcriptional repressor), AMIGO3 (0.535, adhesion molecule), CNOT6L (0.513, deadenylase complex), SH3BP2 (0.498, signaling adaptor), ERICH1 (0.488), ZDHHC14 (0.486, palmitoyltransferase), MIA3 (0.476, collagen cargo receptor), and ST20 (0.446). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, subcellular localization to vesicles and intermediate filaments, consistent with collagen trafficking function. ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 2q11.1-11.2 and missense variant p.Arg1079Gln, implicating TMEM131 dosage sensitivity in disease. Evolutionary conservation is strong: mouse ortholog O70472 (1877 aa, 99% identity) and Drosophila homolog Q9V7H4 (1567 aa, ~60% identity) indicate ancient origin. Functional hypothesis: TMEM131 acts as an ER/Golgi membrane scaffold that chaperones procollagen folding and vesicular export through Ig-domain-mediated collagen binding, with TRAPPC8 interaction coupling to COPII vesicle formation. Disease mechanism: haploinsufficiency or copy number variation disrupts collagen biosynthetic flux, potentially contributing to connective tissue disorders.
