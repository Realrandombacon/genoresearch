## Description
SNX12 (Sorting Nexin 12) is a small 162-amino acid intracellular protein with 21 publications confirming its partially characterized dark gene status. The protein belongs to the sorting nexin family (IPR051074, positions 2-162; PTHR45963, positions 2-162) containing a canonical Phox Homology (PX) domain (IPR001683, positions 27-152; PF00787, positions 59-148; PS50195, positions 28-152; SM00312, positions 27-149) within the PX domain superfamily (IPR036871, positions 23-161; G3DSA:3.30.1520.10, positions 23-161; SSF64268, positions 25-149), with specific phosphoinositide binding function (GO:0035091). The PX domain of SNX12 (cd07294, positions 27-158) mediates membrane targeting to phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate enriched endosomal compartments. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded compact structure (pLDDT 90.6, very high confidence), consistent with stable PX domain architecture. HPA reveals ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, with annotated functions in protein transport and general transport processes. ClinVar documents 121 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly X-chromosome Xp22.33-q28 and Xq11.1-22.3 copy number gains/losses and duplications with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong dosage sensitivity potentially affecting X-linked neurodevelopmental programs. STRING interactions reveal SNX12 functions within the retromer complex and endosomal sorting machinery with very high confidence connections to VPS35 (0.933) - vacuolar protein sorting 35 homolog core retromer component, SNX6 (0.910) - sorting nexin 6 paralog, SNX5 (0.901) - sorting nexin 5, VPS26A (0.891) and VPS26B (0.882) - vacuolar protein sorting 26 homologs A and B, high confidence interactions with SNX3 (0.854) - sorting nexin 3 closest homolog, SNX1 (0.824) - sorting nexin 1, VPS29 (0.823) - vacuolar protein sorting 29 homolog, SNX2 (0.821) - sorting nexin 2, and SNX27 (0.791) - sorting nexin 27. Recent studies (2011-2020) established SNX12 as essential and selective regulator of endocytic and retrograde cargo transport, SNX12 affecting Aβ generation through regulating subcellular localization of BACE1 (beta-secretase 1), and miR-140-5p targeting SNX12 aggravating isoflurane-induced neurotoxicity in diabetic rats. We hypothesize SNX12 functions as a PX domain-dependent endosomal trafficking regulator that specifically mediates retrograde transport from endosomes to trans-Golgi network through retromer complex interactions (VPS35 0.933, VPS26A/B 0.882-0.891, VPS29 0.823), with particular importance for BACE1 trafficking and amyloid-beta production. The small size (162 aa) and well-folded structure (pLDDT 90.6) suggest a compact membrane-targeting module without extensive regulatory domains. The PX domain (positions 27-152) binds phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate for endosomal localization. Close homology to SNX3 (0.854) suggests functional overlap in cargo recognition. The 121 ClinVar X-chromosome CNVs indicate strong purifying selection and potential neurodevelopmental dosage sensitivity. SNX12 represents an uncharacterized sorting nexin linking endosomal trafficking with neurotoxicity, Alzheimer's pathology (BACE1 regulation), and anesthetic-induced cognitive impairment through miR-140-5p mediated repression.
