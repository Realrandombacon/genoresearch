# RALBP1-Associated Eps Domain-Containing Protein 1 (REPS1): EH Domain Scaffold Regulating Clathrin-Mediated Endocytosis and Synaptic Vesicle Recycling

## Description
REPS1 encodes a 796 aa cytoplasmic adaptor protein (UniProt Q96D71) that functions at the intersection of endocytosis, signaling, and cytoskeletal remodeling. The protein architecture features two tandem EH (Eps15 Homology) domains (IPR000261, PF12763) at positions 3-113 and 278-373, which recognize NPF (Asn-Pro-Phe) motifs on partner proteins to mediate membrane trafficking complexes. Additionally, REPS1 contains EF-hand calcium-binding domains (IPR002048, IPR011992) at residues 4-97 and 276-371, suggesting potential calcium-dependent regulation of its membrane association or partner binding. STRING interactions place REPS1 within the clathrin-mediated endocytosis machinery with ultra-high confidence partnerships: AAK1 (0.958), an adaptor-associated kinase that phosphorylates AP2; AP2 complex subunits AP2B1 (0.864), AP2M1 (0.837), and AP2A1 (0.780); SGIP1 (0.864), a synaptic endocytosis factor; ITSN1 (0.704), a scaffolding protein for clathrin-coated pits; and RALBP1 (0.677), its namesake binding partner involved in vesicle trafficking. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with localization to vesicles, plasma membrane, and cytosol, and notably flags involvement in neurodegeneration diseases. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Ser615Gly, p.Ile268Ser, p.Thr539Met) and copy number variations, with the gene alias NBIA7 linking it to Neurodegeneration with Brain Iron Accumulation type 7. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 57.1) indicates medium confidence with structured EH/EF-hand domains embedded in flexible linker regions—consistent with adaptor proteins requiring conformational flexibility for multi-protein complex assembly. Mechanistically, REPS1 likely functions as a molecular bridge: its EH domains bind NPF-motif proteins like Eps15 and intersectins at clathrin-coated pits, while EF-hands may sense local calcium transients during synaptic activity, coordinating vesicle scission and recycling in neurons. Disruption of this scaffolding function could impair receptor internalization, neurotransmitter release, or iron homeostasis, explaining the NBIA7 neurodegenerative phenotype.
