## Description
CCDC175 (Coiled-coil domain-containing protein 175, UniProt P0C221) is a 793 amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 14q23.1 (genomic coordinates NC_000014.9: 59,505,066-59,576,811). InterPro analysis reveals a dominant coiled-coil domain spanning residues 29-786 (IPR038834, PTHR35347), comprising ~95% of the protein length, strongly suggesting a structural scaffolding function mediated by extensive protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold prediction confirms a well-folded structure with global pLDDT score of 75.6 (high confidence), indicating stable tertiary architecture. Human Protein Atlas data shows tissue-enhanced RNA expression with highest levels in brain (4.1 nTPM), choroid plexus (2.6 nTPM), and testis (2.0 nTPM); subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and cytosol, suggesting roles in both nuclear and cytoplasmic processes. STRING-DB identifies 10 interaction partners with medium confidence scores: JKAMP (0.643), TTLL9 (0.596), ANKFN1 (0.594), TCTEX1D2 (0.594), CNKSR3 (0.563), SORCS3 (0.512), PDE1A (0.505), RTN1 (0.440), CLVS2 (0.406), and VWC2 (0.406). Notably, JKAMP (JKA1 membrane protein) and TTLL9 (tubulin tyrosine ligase-like family member 9) suggest potential involvement in membrane trafficking and cytoskeletal regulation. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1355+1G>A, c.1355+2T>A) and multiple copy number variations spanning 14q22.3-24.3 regions, though specific disease associations remain undefined. Conservation across mammals is evident with orthologs in mouse (822 aa, Q5PQJ9), rat (817 aa, E9PVB3), and bovine (724 aa, Q2T9Z6).
