## Description
KLRF1 (Killer Cell Lectin-Like Receptor Subfamily F Member 1, also known as AICL - Activation-Induced C-type Lectin) is a 231-amino acid type II transmembrane protein with 59 publications confirming its partially characterized status as an NK cell and T cell activating receptor. The protein belongs to the C-type lectin receptor immune modulator family (IPR051379, positions 1-231; PTHR46746, positions 1-231) containing a canonical C-type lectin-like domain (IPR001304, positions 114-230; IPR016186, positions 107-231; IPR016187, positions 109-231; IPR033992, positions 114-230; PF00059, positions 133-230; PS50041, positions 121-230; SM00034, positions 114-230; cd03593, positions 114-230; G3DSA:3.10.100.10, positions 107-231; SSF56436, positions 109-231) characteristic of natural killer cell receptors. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 78.1, high confidence), consistent with stable C-type lectin-like fold architecture. HPA reveals tissue-enhanced expression with lymphoid tissue (17.0 nTPM) and bone marrow (12.0 nTPM) as top tissues, detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, immune cell enriched blood cell specificity, cell type enriched single cell specificity, with annotated functions in immunity and innate immunity, plasma membrane subcellular location, and receptor molecular function. ClinVar documents 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly chromosome 12p13.33-11.1 and 12p13.33-q13.12 copy number gains with uncertain clinical significance), indicating strong dosage sensitivity potentially affecting NK cell development and function. STRING interactions reveal KLRF1 functions within the NK cell receptor and immune activation network with high confidence connections to CLEC1B (0.848) - C-type lectin domain family 1 member B (CLEC-2), CD226 (0.805) - DNAM-1 adhesion and costimulatory receptor, CLEC2A (0.795) - C-type lectin domain family 2 member A, NCR2 (0.793) - NKp44 natural cytotoxicity triggering receptor 2, medium confidence interactions with KLRD1 (0.675) - killer cell lectin-like receptor subfamily D member 1 (CD94), NCR3 (0.569) - NKp30 natural cytotoxicity triggering receptor 3, CD160 (0.471) - CD160 antigen, CLEC2B (0.453) - C-type lectin domain family 2 member B, SLAMF6 (0.443) - signaling lymphocytic activation molecule family member 6, and NCR1 (0.423) - NKp46 natural cytotoxicity triggering receptor 1. Recent studies (2022-2026) established KLRF1 as novel marker of CD56bright NK cells predicting improved survival for patients with locally advanced bladder cancer, AICL-KLRF1 axis supporting CD4-CD8 T cell communication and cytokine competence in pre-exhausted CD8+ T cells, and KLRF1 mRNA expression alterations in acute myeloid leukemia. We hypothesize KLRF1 functions as a C-type lectin-like activating receptor that mediates NK cell and T cell cytotoxicity through homodimeric receptor architecture (CTLD positions 114-230) with CD226/DNAM-1 costimulatory signaling (0.805), analogous to other NK cell receptors (NCR1/NKp46 0.423, NCR2/NKp44 0.793, NCR3/NKp30 0.569). The C-type lectin-like domain (positions 114-230) likely mediates ligand recognition without calcium-dependent carbohydrate binding, characteristic of NK cell C-type lectin-like receptors. Strong interaction with CLEC1B/CLEC-2 (0.848) suggests potential heterodimerization or shared signaling pathways. The KLRD1/CD94 interaction (0.675) indicates functional association with classical NK cell receptor complexes. The immune cell enriched expression pattern (lymphoid tissue 17.0 nTPM, bone marrow 12.0 nTPM) confirms NK cell and lymphoid lineage specificity. The 37 ClinVar chromosome 12 CNVs reflect dosage sensitivity affecting the NK gene complex at 12p13. KLRF1 represents an uncharacterized NK cell activating receptor linking C-type lectin-like domain-mediated ligand recognition with CD226-dependent cytotoxicity, pre-exhausted CD8+ T cell cytokine competence, and bladder cancer prognosis through homodimeric receptor signaling mechanisms.
