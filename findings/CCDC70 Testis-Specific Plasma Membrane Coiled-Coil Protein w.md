# CCDC70: Testis-Specific Plasma Membrane Coiled-Coil Protein with Extreme Dosage Sensitivity

## Description
CCDC70 (Coiled-Coil Domain Containing 70) is a 222-amino acid intracellular protein encoded on chromosome 13q14.3 (UniProt Q6NSX1). The gene name and protein classification indicate extensive coiled-coil heptad repeat architecture enabling alpha-helical oligomerization and protein-protein interaction scaffold formation. Human Protein Atlas classifies CCDC70 as a predicted intracellular protein with highly restricted tissue expression pattern: testis-enriched (126.8 nTPM, tissue enriched), detected in single tissue only, cell type enriched specificity, and not detected in cancer or immune cells. Subcellular localization is reported as plasma membrane, suggesting potential roles in membrane organization, cell-cell adhesion, or signal transduction complexes. ClinVar documents an extraordinary 60 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants encompassing CCDC70, including extensive large-scale copy number losses and gains on chromosome 13 (13q13.1-21.32, 13q12.3-14.3, 13q11-31.3, 13q13.2-21.1, 13q14.11-21.2, 13q14.2-32.2, 13q13.2-21.2, 13q11-34, 13p13-q34, 13q14.11-14.3 regions), indicating extreme dosage sensitivity and strong purifying selection. The coiled-coil domain architecture, testis-specific high expression (126.8 nTPM), plasma membrane localization, and extensive clinical CNV burden suggest CCDC70 functions as a structural or scaffolding protein critical for spermatogenesis, potentially organizing membrane microdomains in germ cells, mediating sperm-egg recognition, or stabilizing cell-cell junctions during spermatid maturation. The extreme variant burden (60 pathogenic CNVs) indicates haploinsufficiency is strongly deleterious, likely causing male infertility or developmental defects through disrupted testis-specific protein complexes. Given the restricted expression pattern and membrane localization, CCDC70 may participate in acrosome biogenesis, sperm motility apparatus assembly, or blood-testis barrier integrity.
