## Description
NUDT6 (Nudix hydrolase 6, 316 aa, UniProt P53370) is a dual-function protein encoded from the antisense strand of the FGF2 gene, combining NUDIX hydrolase enzymatic activity with regulatory roles in mood and vascular biology. Structural analysis reveals: (1) N-terminal pre-NUDIX domain (IPR040618/PF18290, aa 46-129) for substrate recognition; (2) catalytic NUDIX hydrolase domain (IPR000086/PF00293, aa 141-273) with conserved Nudix box signature (aa 176-197, PS00893) for hydrolyzing phosphorylated substrates; (3) C-terminal region (aa 283-316) of unknown function. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 86.7), indicating stable enzymatic fold. STRING interactions identify HDGF (0.762) as top partner, with connections to FGF2 (0.601), FGF13 (0.630), BOLA3 (0.674), and NUDIX family members (NUDT17/18/22). HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, cell type enhanced). ClinVar contains 19 pathogenic variants including large copy number gains/losses at 4q24-35. Literature (50 papers) reveals emerging roles: (1) depression - NUDT6 promotes inflammation and suppresses neurogenesis in hippocampus independent of FGF2 signaling, acting as depressogenic factor; (2) vascular disease - lncRNA NUDT6 targeting enhances smooth muscle cell survival limiting atherosclerosis progression; (3) mitochondrial function - distinct hydrolysis activity compared to NUDT9, suggesting specialized substrate preference; (4) cardiac development - interaction with GATA4 suggests role in heart transcription networks. Functional hypothesis: NUDT6 functions as (a) mitochondrial NUDIX hydrolase sanitizing oxidized nucleotides to prevent mutagenesis, and (b) antisense-encoded regulatory protein modulating neuroinflammation and vascular smooth muscle phenotype through FGF2-independent pathways. Mechanistic proposal: The NUDIX domain hydrolyzes specific diphosphate-linked substrates (potentially ADP-ribose derivatives given mitochondrial localization) while the antisense-encoded region interacts with inflammatory signaling complexes (possibly via HDGF partnership), creating a bifunctional protein linking nucleotide metabolism to mood regulation and vascular homeostasis—positioning NUDT6 as therapeutic target for depression and atherosclerosis.
