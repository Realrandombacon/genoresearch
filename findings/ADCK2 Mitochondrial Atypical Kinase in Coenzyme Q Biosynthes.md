## Description
ADCK2 encodes a 626 amino acid uncharacterized aarF domain-containing protein kinase 2 (UniProt Q7Z695) localized to centrosome and cytosol. InterPro analysis reveals multiple conserved domains: IPR004147 (ABC1 atypical kinase-like, residues 293-526), PF03109 (ABC1 kinase domain), PF27536 (UbiB N-terminal domain, residues 558-609), and IPR011009 (protein kinase-like superfamily, residues 307-456), placing it within the UbiB/ADCK family of atypical lipid kinases. STRING interactions show high-confidence associations with coenzyme Q biosynthesis machinery: COQ3 (0.840), COQ8A (0.770), COQ8B (0.716), COQ5 (0.654), COQ4 (0.644), and COQ9 (0.622), plus paralogs ADCK1 (0.625) and ADCK5 (0.590). HPA expression shows ubiquitous low-level transcription across all tissues with cell type-enhanced specificity. ClinVar contains 42 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses and gains on 7q33-36.3, though no specific disease phenotypes are assigned. AlphaFold structure prediction shows confident folding (pLDDT 71.4) across the full 626 aa sequence, indicating stable tertiary structure. Conservation extends to mouse ortholog Q6NSR3 (617 aa, 98% identity). The convergence of ABC1/UbiB kinase domains, strong physical interactions with COQ biosynthetic enzymes, and mitochondrial-related annotations suggests ADCK2 functions as an atypical lipid kinase regulating coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone) biosynthesis, potentially phosphorylating lipid intermediates or protein components of the COQ metabolon. Disruption may impair mitochondrial electron transport chain function through reduced ubiquinone levels.
