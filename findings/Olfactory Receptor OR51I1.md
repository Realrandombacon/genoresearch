## Description
The protein OR51I1 is an olfactory receptor, a type of G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) that plays a crucial role in detecting odorant molecules and transmitting signals to the brain. It is part of the olfactory receptor family 51 subfamily I. The protein has a 7-helix transmembrane structure, characteristic of GPCRs. It interacts with other olfactory receptors and G-proteins, such as GNAL, ARRB2, and ARRB1. While not extensively studied, variations in this gene have been associated with certain conditions, including copy number gains.
