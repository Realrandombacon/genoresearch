# USH1 Protein Network Component Harmonin-Binding Protein 1 (USHBP1): PDZ Domain Scaffold Linking Usher Syndrome Network to Nucleolar Function

**Date:** 2026-06-17T10:29:42.547543

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
USHBP1 encodes a 703 aa intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q8N6Y0) characterized by a conserved PDZ-binding domain (IPR019536, PF10506, residues 299-362) that mediates interactions with PDZ domain-containing partners. The protein belongs to the USHBP1-like family (IPR040171) and is related to the colorectal mutant cancer protein MCC (PTHR23347). STRING interactions reveal USHBP1 embedded in two distinct functional networks: the Usher syndrome protein complex via ultra-high confidence binding to USH1C/harmonin (0.926), a PDZ-containing scaffold mutated in Usher syndrome type 1C causing deafness-blindness; and the biotin metabolism pathway through MCCC2 (3-methylcrotonyl-CoA carboxylase component beta, 0.960), MCCC1 (0.603), and BTD (biotinidase, 0.812). Additional partners include MMRN2 (multimerin-2, 0.754), an extracellular matrix protein. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression peaking in heart muscle (12.8 nTPM) with specific nucleolar localization—an unexpected finding for a protein named for its harmonin interaction. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic variants, all large copy number gains/losses spanning chromosome 19p13, suggesting dosage sensitivity though no specific disease phenotype has been definitively assigned. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 68.0), consistent with a scaffold protein containing structured domains embedded in flexible linker regions typical of proteins requiring conformational adaptability for multi-protein complex assembly. Mechanistically, USHBP1 likely functions as a molecular bridge: its PDZ-binding motif at residues 299-362 binds the PDZ domains of harmonin (USH1C), potentially competing with or modulating other harmonin partners like cadherin-23 or myosin-7a in stereocilia of hair cells. The nucleolar localization suggests an additional, uncharacterized role in ribosome biogenesis or cellular stress response—possibly linking metabolic state (via biotin enzyme interactions) to protein synthesis capacity. The strong MCCC2 interaction is intriguing given MCCC2 deficiency causes 3-methylcrotonyl-CoA carboxylase deficiency, a metabolic disorder. USHBP1 may coordinate metabolic enzyme activity with scaffolding functions, or the interaction may reflect shared subcellular localization rather than direct complex formation. Disruption of USHBP1 could impair both hair cell mechanotransduction (via harmonin network disruption) and cardiac muscle function (via heart-enriched expression), potentially explaining the pathogenic CNVs observed.
