## Description
D2HGDH (D-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenase, mitochondrial, Q8N465) encodes a 521 aa FAD-dependent oxidoreductase functioning as the exclusive catabolic enzyme for the oncometabolite D-2-hydroxyglutarate (D-2HG), converting it to alpha-ketoglutarate (2-oxoglutarate) to maintain cellular metabolic and epigenetic homeostasis. Structural architecture reveals an exceptionally well-folded multi-domain organization with very high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 92.4): FAD-linked oxidase N-terminal domain (IPR006094, residues 101-238) forming the primary flavin-binding pocket with GO term for FAD binding (GO:0050660), FAD-binding domain PCMH-type (IPR016164, residues 96-275) establishing the conserved fold for electron transfer, FAD-binding type PCMH subdomain 1 (IPR016167, residues 96-200) and subdomain 2 creating the catalytic cleft, FAD-binding oxidoreductase/transferase type 4 C-terminal domain (IPR004113, residues 275-515) completing the barrel structure with GO terms for catalytic activity (GO:0003824) and FAD binding (GO:0050660), FAD-linked oxidase-like C-terminal superfamily (IPR016164, residues 249-517) stabilizing the dimer interface, Vanillyl-alcohol oxidase chain A domain 4 (G3DSA:1.10.45.10, residues 477-516) capping the active site, and multiple CATH superfamily domains (G3DSA:3.30.43.10, 3.30.465.10, 3.30.70.2190, 3.30.70.2740) defining the complex topology required for substrate specificity against the chiral D-2HG enantiomer.
