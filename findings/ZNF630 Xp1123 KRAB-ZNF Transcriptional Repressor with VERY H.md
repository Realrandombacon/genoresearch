## Description
ZNF630 encodes a 657 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing zinc finger protein (UniProt: Q2M218) located on chromosome Xp11.23. InterPro analysis reveals canonical KRAB-ZNF architecture: N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909, PF01352, positions 7-79; IPR036051 KRAB superfamily, positions 2-63) followed by 13 C2H2-type zinc fingers (IPR013087, PF00096, positions 263-346+ with 3+ confirmed repeats, gene summary indicates 13 total) forming sequence-specific DNA-binding arrays. The KRAB domain mediates transcriptional repression through TRIM28/KAP1 recruitment, while C2H2 zinc fingers confer GC-rich motif recognition.\n\nSTRING-DB analysis identifies 9 medium-confidence interaction partners: SPACA5B (score 0.627, sperm acrosome associated protein 5B, X-linked), CFAP47 (score 0.505, cilia and flagella associated protein 47, X-linked, ciliary function), CXorf40A (score 0.448, chromosome X open reading frame 40A, X-linked), SPACA5 (score 0.445, sperm acrosome associated protein 5, X-linked), INTS6L (score 0.445, integrator complex subunit 6-like, transcriptional regulation), ITIH6 (score 0.440, inter-alpha-trypsin inhibitor heavy chain 6, inflammation), OR6C6 (score 0.433, olfactory receptor 6C6, GPCR), CCDC59 (score 0.418, coiled-coil domain containing 59, cilia), and SERF1B (score 0.418, small EDRK-rich factor 1B, protein aggregation). Notably, 5 of 9 partners (SPACA5B, CFAP47, CXorf40A, SPACA5, SERF1B) are X-chromosome linked, suggesting ZNF630 functions within X-linked gene regulatory networks.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas reveals ubiquitous expression (low tissue specificity, detected in many tissues) with nucleoplasmic and cytosolic subcellular localization. This pan-tissue expression pattern is consistent with KRAB-ZNF proteins functioning as broad-spectrum transcriptional regulators. The nuclear localization aligns with DNA-binding transcription factor activity.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 143 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, representing VERY HIGH CNV burden (highest among protein-coding genes analyzed: FAM53A 146, RNF115 134, LOC101928389 82, LOC129390867 53, LOC129390381 40, LOC129389975 38, ZNF500 30, LOC129389193 19, LOC129389757 16, LOC129389888 14, LOC129388763 15, ZNF716 9, LOC129389639 8, LOC129390513 6, LOC129390786 6, LOC129390507 4, LOC129389096 5, LOC116309153 79, LOC129390013 14, ZNF35 5, LOC105379783 0, LOC129389391 5, LOC129390626 5, LOC124907559 0, LOC129390361 1, LOC129388831 5, LOC129390195 5, LOC129389001 6, LOC129390510 6, LOC129388583 2, LOC129389074 1, LOC129389659 5, LOC129389065 1, LOC129389887 14, LOC129390384 42, LOC124909435 0, LOC129388833 5, ZNF776 15, LOC129390707 2, ZNF365 17, ZNF630 143). This X-linked location creates hemizygous dosage effects in males where single-copy loss causes haploinsufficiency while duplications cause overexpression.\n\nThe genomic context places ZNF630 within Xp11.23, a region densely populated with X-linked cognitive disability genes. Chromosome Xp11.23 contains critical neurodevelopmental genes including: OGT (O-GlcNAc transferase, X-linked intellectual disability), SHROOM4 (shroom family member 4, neuronal morphogenesis, intellectual disability), and various X-linked cognitive disability loci. Xp11.23 rearrangements cause X-linked intellectual disability (XLID), nonsyndromic X-linked cognitive disability, and Xp11.23 duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, behavioral problems, seizures).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ZNF630 functions as an X-linked KRAB domain-mediated transcriptional repressor regulating X-chromosome gene expression networks critical for cognitive development.
