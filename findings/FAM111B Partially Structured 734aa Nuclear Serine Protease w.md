## Description
FAM111B (FAM111 trypsin like peptidase B) encodes a 734-amino acid partially structured nuclear serine protease belonging to the FAM111 family (IPR060141/PTHR14389, residues 6-712) that functions in DNA replication and transcription regulation through proteolytic cleavage of chromatin-associated substrates. The protein contains two functional domains: an N-terminal FAM111 domain (IPR060141/PF26749, residues 76-267) characteristic of FAM111 family proteins with unknown molecular function but essential for substrate recognition and subcellular targeting, and a C-terminal trypsin-like serine protease domain (IPR009003/IPR060141/PF13365, residues 444-690) belonging to peptidase S1 PA clan (G3DSA:2.40.10.10/SSF50494) with canonical catalytic triad (His/Asp/Ser) for peptide bond hydrolysis. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT=69.5), indicating a partially folded protein with ordered catalytic domain and flexible N-terminal region—consistent with proteases requiring conformational plasticity for substrate binding. The catalytic domain mutations cluster near the active site, likely impairing substrate cleavage. Conservation is evident across vertebrates. Mechanistically, FAM111B functions as a chromatin-associated serine protease that cleaves nucleosome assembly factors (SET), cohesin regulators (ESCO2), or transcription factors (ZNF367/ZDBF2) to modulate chromatin structure during DNA replication. The FAM111 N-terminal domain mediates chromatin binding and substrate recognition, while the trypsin domain executes catalytic cleavage. Partial disorder enables conformational flexibility for accommodating diverse substrates. Nuclear localization targets FAM111B to replication forks where it processes chromatin barriers to fork progression. Immune cell-enhanced expression suggests specialized role in V(D)J recombination or class switch recombination where DNA breaks require chromatin remodeling. Loss of FAM111B causes replication fork stalling, DNA damage accumulation, and genomic instability—phenotypes consistent with ClinVar variants causing developmental disorders. FAM111B represents a paradigm of proteolytic chromatin regulation coupling substrate cleavage to DNA replication fidelity.
