## Description
NFATC2IP (Nuclear Factor of Activated T-cells 2 Interacting Protein, UniProt Q8NCF5) is a 419-amino acid nuclear protein functioning as a critical mediator of genome stability under conditions of suppressed protein SUMOylation, featuring two C-terminal SUMO-like domains (SLDs) that mimic the ubiquitin-fold to interact with the SUMO machinery. InterPro analysis confirms a unique domain architecture: N-terminal region of unknown function followed by two tandem SUMO-like domains (SLD1: cd17078/IPR000626, aa 263-336; SLD2: cd17079/IPR022617/PF11976, aa 346-418) with beta-grasp ubiquitin-like folds (G3DSA:3.10.20.90, aa 244-338/340-419) characteristic of Rad60/SUMO-like proteins; membership in the NFATC2-Interacting & DNA Repair family (IPR052324/PTHR47187, aa 59-419); GO annotation for protein binding (GO:0005515).
