## Description
CHSY3 encodes an 882 aa chondroitin sulfate synthase 3 (UniProt Q70JA7), functioning as a glycosyltransferase enzyme that catalyzes chondroitin sulfate chain elongation through alternating addition of N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) and glucuronic acid (GlcA) residues to the proteoglycan linkage region. The protein contains a chondroitin N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase domain (IPR008428, PF05679, aa328-864) with nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily architecture (IPR029044, SSF53448, aa249-381/609-871) and belongs to the chondroitin sulfate glycosyltransferase family (IPR051227, PTHR12369, aa2-866). Two G3DSA structural domains are present: SpsA-like polysaccharide biosynthesis fold (G3DSA:3.90.550.10, aa609-871) and related superfamily (G3DSA:3.90.550.50, aa163-431), consistent with glycosyltransferase catalytic mechanism. AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 76.9), indicating a well-folded enzyme with stable catalytic domains. Human Protein Atlas classifies CHSY3 as tissue-enhanced with highest expression in blood vessel (10.0 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced—this expression pattern suggests roles in vascular extracellular matrix production and ubiquitous proteoglycan biosynthesis. STRING interactions reveal CHSY3 integrates into chondroitin sulfate biosynthesis machinery with 10 partners: CSGALNACT2 (0.924, chondroitin sulfate N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 2—highest confidence, paralogous enzyme), CSGALNACT1 (0.924, chondroitin sulfate N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 1—paralogous enzyme), GALNT3 (0.762, polypeptide N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 3—O-glycosylation initiator), FGF23 (0.570, fibroblast growth factor 23—phosphate regulation hormone requiring glycosylation), CHPF (0.553, chondroitin polymerizing factor—chondroitin synthesis enzyme), CHSY1 (0.545, chondroitin sulfate synthase 1—paralogous enzyme), CHPF2 (0.508, chondroitin polymerizing factor 2—chondroitin synthesis enzyme), CSPG4 (0.468, chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan 4—major substrate proteoglycan), KL (0.447, klotho—anti-aging protein requiring glycosylation), B4GALT7 (0.418, beta-1,4-galactosyltransferase 7—linker region synthesis enzyme). The 0.924 CSGALNACT1/2 interactions define the chondroitin sulfate synthase complex—CHSY3 likely functions in complex with these paralogs for coordinated chain elongation. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses (5q23.2-31.1, 5q23.3-31.1, 5q23.2-31.2, 5q14.3-23.3, 5p15.1-q35.2) and gains (5q14.3-23.3, 5p15.33-q35.3), plus splice region variants (c.1087-48G>A, c.1086+69C>T), though no specific CHSY3-associated disease is yet assigned—this suggests dosage sensitivity where CHSY3 copy number alterations disrupt chondroitin sulfate biosynthesis, potentially causing skeletal, vascular, or connective tissue defects. Literature (32 papers) documents CHSY3 roles in: (1) gastric cancer proliferation and migration promotion with immune infiltration association; (2) prognostic biomarker in gastric cancer and clear cell renal cell carcinoma; (3) intervertebral disc degeneration reversal via celecoxib-mediated CHSY3 upregulation; (4) tumor-promoting functions and immune evasion in stomach adenocarcinoma. CHSY3 functions as a bifunctional glycosyltransferase in the chondroitin sulfate biosynthesis pathway—chondroitin sulfate is a major component of extracellular matrix proteoglycans, providing structural support, hydration, and signaling functions. The enzyme catalyzes alternating GalNAc and GlcA transfer from UDP-sugar donors to the growing glycosaminoglycan chain attached to core proteins like CSPG4. The high-confidence STRING interactions with CSGALNACT1/2 (0.924) suggest CHSY3 may form hetero-oligomeric complexes for coordinated synthesis, similar to the CHSY1-CHPF complex. The blood vessel expression (10.0 nTPM) indicates vascular extracellular matrix production—chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans regulate vascular permeability, angiogenesis, and smooth muscle cell function. The cancer literature suggests CHSY3 overexpression promotes tumor progression through: (1) enhanced extracellular matrix remodeling facilitating invasion; (2) altered growth factor signaling via proteoglycan co-receptor modification; (3) immune evasion through glycosylation-dependent checkpoint modulation. The intervertebral disc degeneration link reflects chondroitin sulfate's critical role in disc matrix—aggrecan proteoglycans require chondroitin sulfate chains for water retention and load-bearing capacity. The 11 ClinVar CNVs without assigned disease suggest CHSY3 dosage alterations cause phenotypes not yet mapped—potential mechanisms include: (1) skeletal dysplasia from defective cartilage matrix; (2) vascular abnormalities from impaired vessel wall proteoglycans; (3) connective tissue disorders affecting multiple organ systems. CHSY3 represents a partially characterized dark gene with clear enzymatic function but incomplete understanding of disease mechanisms, substrate specificity, and regulatory control.
