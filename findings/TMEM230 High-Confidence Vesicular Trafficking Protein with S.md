## Description
TMEM230 (Gene ID: 29058, UniProt Q96A57, aliases C20orf30, HSPC274) is a 120 amino acid multi-pass transmembrane protein located on chromosome 20p13-p12.3 (NC_000020.11, positions 5113075-5059115, ~54kb genomic region). This gene encodes a well-folded membrane protein with clear functional annotation in vesicular trafficking but incompletely characterized molecular mechanism. InterPro domain analysis reveals membership in the TMEM230/134 protein family (IPR008590/PF05915, positions 13-118; IPR044234, positions 14-118; PTHR15664 C20ORF30 protein, positions 14-118), indicating evolutionary conservation of this transmembrane protein architecture. GO annotations confirm transporter activity. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners forming a striking Parkinson's disease-associated cluster: CHCHD2 (0.707, coiled-coil-helix-coiled-coil-helix domain-containing protein 2, mitochondrial import protein mutated in familial PD), VPS13C (0.693, vacuolar protein sorting homolog, PD risk locus), RAB39B (0.622, small GTPase regulating vesicle trafficking, X-linked parkinsonism), ATP11A (0.608, P-type ATPase flippase), DNAJC6 (0.599, auxilin, co-chaperone for clathrin uncoating, juvenile PD), LRP10 (0.599, LDL receptor-related protein, PD risk gene), PTRHD1 (0.583, peptidyl-tRNA hydrolase), FBXO7 (0.578, F-box protein, early-onset parkinsonian-pyramidal syndrome), DNAJC13 (0.432, auxilin-like, PD-linked), and VPS35 (0.423, retromer complex component, autosomal dominant PD). This interaction network places TMEM230 squarely within vesicular trafficking and autophagy-lysosomal pathways implicated in PD pathogenesis. Human Protein Atlas classifies TMEM230 (ENSG00000089063) as a predicted membrane protein and transporter with endoplasmic reticulum subcellular location, detected in all tissues with low tissue/cancer/immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. HPA explicitly annotates disease involvement including neurodegeneration, Parkinson disease, and parkinsonism. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q96A57-F1) yields high-confidence model (global pLDDT 81.1), indicating a well-folded stable transmembrane architecture across all 120 residues—consistent with multi-pass membrane proteins that form defined helical bundles. ClinVar lists 33 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations (p.Arg78Gln, p.Ile41Leu), 5' UTR variants (c.-6C>T), and large copy number gains/losses spanning chromosome 20p regions; however, no specific monogenic disease syndrome is definitively attributed solely to TMEM230 in ClinVar summary despite strong PD association. Conservation is exceptional: mouse (Q8CIB6), rat (Q5BJP5), cattle (Q5E975), and orangutan (Q5R8X3) orthologs all show identical 120 aa lengths, indicating strong evolutionary constraint. Functional hypothesis: TMEM230 functions as a multi-pass transmembrane protein involved in synaptic vesicle trafficking and recycling, potentially operating at the interface between endoplasmic reticulum and secretory vesicles. The ER localization suggests roles in vesicle biogenesis or cargo sorting. STRING interactions suggest TMEM230 may function within (1) mitochondrial-ER contact sites (via CHCHD2), (2) retromer-mediated endosomal sorting (via VPS35, VPS13C), (3) clathrin-coated vesicle uncoating (via DNAJC6, DNAJC13), or (4) synaptic vesicle exocytosis/recycling (via RAB39B). Disease mechanism: TMEM230 dysfunction likely contributes to Parkinson's disease pathogenesis through disruption of vesicular trafficking pathways essential for dopaminergic neuron survival. The 33 ClinVar variants suggest dosage sensitivity and loss-of-function mechanisms; missense mutations may disrupt transmembrane helix packing or protein-protein interactions. ER localization combined with PD-associated interactors suggests TMEM230 may regulate ER stress responses, alpha-synuclein clearance via autophagy-lysosomal pathway, or dopamine transporter trafficking. Priority experiments include co-IP/MS validation of STRING-predicted interactions (particularly CHCHD2, VPS35, DNAJC6), determination of precise subcellular localization via super-resolution microscopy, assessment of TMEM230 role in alpha-synuclein aggregation using cellular models, investigation of TMEM230 expression in PD patient brains versus controls, generation of Tmem230 knockout mice to assess dopaminergic neuron vulnerability, and structural studies to identify potential drug-binding pockets within the high-confidence AlphaFold model (pLDDT 81.1).
