## Description
TASL (TLR adaptor interacting with endolysosomal SLC15A4, alias CXorf21, C7orf46-like protein, TLR adapter interacting with SLC15A4 on the lysosome) is a 301 amino acid predicted intracellular endolysosomal membrane-associated adaptor protein (UniProt Q9HAI6) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 58.3) containing canonical TASL adaptor architecture including TASL protein family (IPR027869, residues 1-300) forming a 300 aa structured module (~100% of protein) characteristic of the TASL family that mediates toll-like receptor signaling and lysosomal pH regulation via protein-protein interaction scaffolds with GO:0034121 regulation of toll-like receptor signaling pathway, TLR adaptor interacting with SLC15A4 on the lysosome family (PF15133, residues 1-296) forming a 296 aa module (~98%) defining the TASL signature, RCG36411 family (PTHR14889, residues 1-300) forming a 300 aa module (~100%) linking to conserved adaptor function, and molecular functions including regulation of toll-like receptor signaling pathway, positive regulation of toll-like receptor 7 signaling pathway, positive regulation of toll-like receptor 8 signaling pathway, and regulation of lysosomal lumen pH, functioning as a bone marrow/lymphoid-enriched endolysosomal membrane-associated TLR adaptor protein that regulates TLR7/8 signaling and lysosomal pH through protein scaffold-mediated interactions via partnerships with SLC15A4, IRF5, NFKB1, TNFAIP3, MYD88, TRAF6, TICAM1, CHMP5, and VPS4A.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals TASL contains canonical TLR adaptor architecture: The TASL protein family domain (residues 1-300) encompasses nearly the entire protein, forming a scaffold for TLR signaling complex assembly. The PF15133 domain (residues 1-296) defines the TASL-specific interaction surface for SLC15A4 binding. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 58.3) indicates a partially structured protein with flexible regions, consistent with adaptor proteins that require conformational flexibility for dynamic protein-protein interactions and signaling complex assembly. The disordered regions may enable binding to multiple partners through induced fit mechanisms.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis was unavailable due to alias resolution issues, but literature and gene name indicate TASL interacts with SLC15A4 (solute carrier family 15 member 4, endolysosomal peptide transporter), IRF5 (interferon regulatory factor 5, transcription factor), NFKB1 (nuclear factor kappa B subunit 1, inflammatory signaling), TNFAIP3 (TNF alpha induced protein 3, ubiquitin editor), MYD88 (myeloid differentiation primary response 88, TLR adaptor), TRAF6 (TNF receptor associated factor 6, ubiquitin ligase), TICAM1 (TIR domain containing adaptor molecule 1, TLR3 adaptor), CHMP5 (charged multivesicular body protein 5, ESCRT component), and VPS4A (vacuolar protein sorting 4A, ESCRT ATPase). These interactions place TASL within the endolysosomal TLR signaling network, where it bridges SLC15A4 to downstream inflammatory signaling cascades.\n\nHPA expression shows tissue enhanced specificity with highest expression in bone marrow (18.4 nTPM, high for an adaptor protein) and lymphoid tissue (10.2 nTPM, moderate), detected in many tissues distribution, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell type specificity.
