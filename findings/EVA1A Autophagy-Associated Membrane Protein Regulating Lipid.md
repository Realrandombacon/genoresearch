# EVA1A: Autophagy-Associated Membrane Protein Regulating Lipid Homeostasis and Tumor Suppression

**Date:** 2026-06-26T14:50:48.128958

## Description
EVA1A (Eva-1 homolog A, UniProt Q9H8M9) is a 152 aa membrane-associated protein containing a single EVA1 domain (IPR039500, positions 13-152) characteristic of the FAM176 family (PF14851). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 64.7). HPA shows tissue-enriched expression predominantly in liver (178.5 nTPM), with vesicular and plasma membrane localization. STRING reveals 10 interaction partners including autophagy machinery components ATG16L1 (0.720), ATG5 (0.533), ATG4B (0.474), and SQSTM1/p62 (0.473), plus kinase regulators FAM20A (0.556) and FAM20C (0.535). ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 2p13-12. Literature (49 papers) reveals dual functionality: (1) autophagy regulator acting upstream of TOR signaling, and (2) tumor suppressor reversing lenvatinib resistance in hepatocellular carcinoma via PI3K/AKT/p53 axis. Recent work shows EVA1A regulates hepatic lipid homeostasis through CD36 palmitoylation and modulates CD8+ T cell activity in esophageal cancer. Mechanistically, EVA1A likely serves as a membrane scaffold recruiting ATG proteins to initiate autophagosome formation while simultaneously modulating kinase signaling cascades.
