## Description
STEAP2 (Six-Transmembrane Epithelial Antigen of Prostate 2, also known as STAMP1) is a 490 amino acid NADPH-dependent metalloreductase that functions as a critical regulator of cellular iron and copper homeostasis through ferric reductase activity (Fe3+ to Fe2+ reduction) and cupric reductase activity (Cu2+ to Cu+ reduction) at endosomal and plasma membrane compartments, facilitating metal ion uptake via DMT1 and CTR1 transporters for erythropoiesis, mitochondrial respiration, and androgen receptor signaling in prostate epithelium and metabolically active tissues.
