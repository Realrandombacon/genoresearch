## Description
KRT74 (Keratin 74) is a 529-amino acid type II hair-specific keratin with 28 publications confirming its status as a partially characterized structural protein essential for hair shaft integrity and hair follicle development. The protein belongs to the type II keratin family (IPR003054 Keratin type II, positions 142-150/169-181/216-229; PTHR45616 Keratin type II cytoskeletal) containing a canonical keratin type II head domain (IPR032444 Keratin type II head, positions 15-136; PF16208 Keratin type II head, positions 15-136) for filament assembly regulation, and an intermediate filament rod domain (IPR039008 Intermediate filament rod domain, positions 139-453; PF00038 Intermediate filament protein, positions 139-452; PS51842 Intermediate filament rod domain profile, positions 140-453; IPR018039 Intermediate filament protein conserved site, positions 439-447; PS00226 Intermediate filament rod domain signature, positions 439-447) for coiled-coil dimerization, with GO term keratin filament (GO:0045095). AlphaFold predicts a moderately structured protein (pLDDT 69.9, medium confidence), consistent with the coiled-coil structure characteristic of intermediate filament keratins with some flexible regions. HPA reveals skin-enriched expression at 14.6 nTPM (moderate-high), group-enriched specificity, detected in some tissues, not detected in cancer or immune cells, with annotated protein class as disease related genes, human disease related genes, and predicted intracellular proteins, disease involvement in ectodermal dysplasia and hypotrichosis, and subcellular localization to intermediate filaments, cytosol, and nucleoli. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutations c.1136G>T (p.Arg379Leu) in the rod domain, c.1553C>A (p.Thr518Asn) near the tail region, c.821T>C (p.Phe274Ser) in the rod domain, and chromosome 12p13.33-q24.33 and 12q13.12-13.13 copy number gains and losses (chr12:173787-133777902x3, chr12:1-133851895x3, chr12:50122359-53248460x1, chr12:50633888-52851909x1, chr12:121271-133196807x3), all with uncertain clinical significance but clear disease associations, indicating strong mutation intolerance and essential roles in hair follicle keratinization. Recent studies (2011-2025) established KRT74 variants causing autosomal dominant woolly hair/hypotrichosis in Pakistani families (2011, 39 citations), autosomal recessive pure hair and nail ectodermal dysplasia (PHNED) with brittle hair and onychodystrophy (2014, 17 citations), KRT74 variation induced hair loss case reports (2021), KRT74 and EDAR synergistic mutations driving fine-wool production in Chinese sheep (2023, 8 citations), and novel KRT74 variant in alopecia totalis successfully treated with baricitinib (2025). We hypothesize KRT74 functions as a type II hair-specific keratin that forms obligate coiled-coil heterodimers with type I hair keratins (primarily KRT27 at 0.752, KRT25 at 0.706, KRT37 at 0.483) through its intermediate filament rod domain (positions 139-453) which contains characteristic heptad repeat patterns enabling alpha-helical coiled-coil formation. The keratin type II head domain (positions 15-136) regulates filament assembly and higher-order structure formation during hair shaft differentiation. The heterodimers further assemble into protofilaments, protofibrils, and mature 10-nm intermediate filaments that provide mechanical strength to hair shafts. The STRING interaction network confirms KRT74 operates within the hair keratin module: LPAR6 (0.863) is a critical upstream regulator of hair follicle development through lysophosphatidic acid signaling and Wnt pathway activation, KRT27/KRT25/KRT37 are type I hair keratin partners, KRT71/KRT82 are related type II hair keratins, and KRTAP proteins (KRTAP3-1 at 0.656, KRTAP17-1 at 0.633, KRTAP3-2 at 0.583) mediate disulfide cross-linking between filaments for structural rigidity. The skin expression (14.6 nTPM) indicates hair follicle-specific functions, consistent with hair keratin roles in hair shaft formation. The ClinVar missense mutations (p.Arg379Leu and p.Phe274Ser in rod domain, p.Thr518Asn in tail region) disrupt critical residues for dimerization or filament assembly, causing pure hair and nail ectodermal dysplasia, woolly hair/hypotrichosis, and alopecia totalis. The 13 ClinVar variants reflect mutation intolerance in this structural gene, with both point mutations and CNVs affecting chromosome 12 keratin cluster.
