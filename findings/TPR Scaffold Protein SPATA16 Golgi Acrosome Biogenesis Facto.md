# TPR Scaffold Protein SPATA16: Golgi Acrosome Biogenesis Factor in Spermatogenesis

## Description
SPATA16 encodes a 569 amino acid testis-specific tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) scaffold protein (UniProt Q9BXB7) essential for acrosome biogenesis during spermatogenesis. InterPro analysis reveals an N-terminal spermatogenesis-associated domain (PF15015, IPR029161; residues 5-567) and a central TPR superfamily domain (IPR011990, G3DSA:1.25.40.10, SSF48452; residues 176-320) mediating protein-protein interactions via alpha-helical stacking. HPA data shows testis-specific expression (55.6 nTPM) with Golgi apparatus localization, consistent with acrosomal vesicle formation. STRING interactions reveal 10 high-confidence partners including MEA1 (0.830, manchette-associated protein), GOPC (0.813, Golgi-associated PDZ protein), PICK1 (0.741, protein kinase C-binding protein), AURKC (0.673, meiotic kinase), and DPY19L2 (0.634, acrosome formation factor), forming a Golgi-acrosome assembly complex. ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic variants including 3q chromosomal copy number alterations, splice site mutations (c.933+2T>G), and missense variants (p.Arg263Trp), causing male infertility through acrosome defects. The TPR domain architecture positions SPATA16 as a molecular scaffold bridging Golgi trafficking machinery (GOPC, PICK1) to acrosomal biogenesis factors (DPY19L2, MEA1). Disruption of SPATA16 function through 3q deletions or point mutations impairs acrosomal vesicle formation, explaining clinical associations with globozoospermia and male factor infertility.
