## Description
SPAG11B encodes a 103 aa sperm-associated antigen 11B (UniProt Q08648) located at 8p23.1, functioning as an epididymis-specific secretory protein critical for sperm maturation, motility acquisition, capacitation, and egg-sperm interaction. InterPro analysis reveals a compact HE2/defensin-like domain architecture: Sperm-associated antigen 11 A/B family domain (IPR007988, aa 1-87) characteristic of epididymal secretory proteins, Sperm antigen HE2 Pfam domain (PF05324, aa 5-71) conferring beta-defensin-like structural features, PTHR14081 superfamily membership, and extracellular region localization (GO:0005576). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 64.3), indicating a partially structured protein with potential intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with small secretory proteins that undergo conformational flexibility for partner binding and glycan recognition. STRING interactions reveal a defensin-centered network highly overlapping with SPAG11A: DEFB126 (0.741, beta-defensin 126 critical for sperm function), DEFB104A (0.731, beta-defensin 104A), DEFB103A (0.696, beta-defensin 103A), DEFB4A (0.670, beta-defensin 4A), DEFB107A (0.661, beta-defensin 107A), PRR23D1/PRR23D2 (0.599, proline-rich proteins), DEFB106B (0.594), DEFB105A (0.583), DEFB106A (0.543) - all beta-defensin family antimicrobial peptides. ClinVar reports 52 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 8p23.1 copy number losses (x1) and gains (x3) with uncertain significance, suggesting clinical relevance in chromosomal disorder syndromes affecting male reproduction and potentially immune function. Literature (21 papers) details emerging reproductive and immune functions: (1) Rheumatoid arthritis biomarker - Mendelian randomization analysis identifying SPAG11B as potential RA susceptibility factor (1 citation, 2025), suggesting unexpected immune system role beyond reproduction; (2) Sperm maturation - involvement in spermatozoa maturation, motility acquisition, capacitation, and egg-sperm interaction (multiple studies); (3) Bull fertility - SPAG11B exon 3 SNPs associated with conception rate in Murrah bulls, demonstrating conserved reproductive function across mammals; (4) Molecular modeling - structural characterization of human SPAG11B protein revealing HE2/defensin-like fold (4 citations, 2015). Functional hypothesis: SPAG11B likely functions through: (1) Sperm Maturation Factor - epididymal secretion binding sperm surface during transit through caput/corpus epididymis, modifying sperm membrane proteins and glycans for motility acquisition and fertilization competence; (2) Antimicrobial Defense - HE2/defensin-like structure providing broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity in epididymal lumen, protecting sperm from bacterial/fungal pathogens via membrane disruption mechanisms parallel to beta-defensins (DEFB126, DEFB104A); (3) Sperm-Egg Recognition - glycan-binding capability facilitating sperm-oviduct interaction and sperm-zona pellucida binding during fertilization, potentially through lectin-like activity; (4) Immune Modulation - unexpected role in rheumatoid arthritis pathogenesis suggesting SPAG11B may regulate immune cell function or autoantibody production, possibly through molecular mimicry between sperm antigens and joint epitopes; (5) Epididymal Immune Privilege - maintaining immunosuppressive environment in epididymis to prevent autoimmune responses against sperm antigens while preserving antimicrobial surveillance. The HE2-defensin architecture places SPAG11B within the epididymal antimicrobial peptide family, parallel to DEFB126 and other beta-defensins, with SPAG11A as its closest paralog. Disease implications include: male subfertility/infertility, sperm motility defects, fertilization failure, rheumatoid arthritis susceptibility, 8p23 chromosomal disorder syndromes, potential autoimmune orchitis. Despite 21 papers, SPAG11B's specific glycan ligands, antimicrobial mechanism, immune system role in RA, and therapeutic potential for male infertility remain critically underexplored.
