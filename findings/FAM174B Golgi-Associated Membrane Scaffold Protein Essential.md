# FAM174B: Golgi-Associated Membrane Scaffold Protein Essential for Secretory Pathway Integrity

**Date:** 2026-03-23T10:08:26.260285

**Quality Score:** 3.15/10  (E=6.0, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
FAM174B encodes a 159-amino acid membrane protein (UniProt Q3ZCQ3) belonging to the FAM174-like family (InterPro IPR009565, Pfam PF06679). The protein localizes to intermediate filaments and is essential for Golgi structural integrity. Expression is tissue-enhanced with highest levels in pancreas (75.0 nTPM), consistent with a role in secretory pathway function. STRING interactions reveal associations with membrane trafficking proteins including SCAMP4 (secretory carrier membrane protein, score 0.477), GAK (auxilin/clathrin uncoating factor, score 0.430), and LYSMD3/4 (lysozyme domain-containing membrane proteins, scores 0.574/0.496). Additional partners include FAM124B (paralog, 0.521), NUDT8 (nucleoside diphosphatase, 0.467), and XXYLT1 (glycosyltransferase, 0.444), suggesting involvement in Golgi glycosylation and vesicle trafficking. ClinVar documents 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses at 15q26, indicating clinical relevance. Orthologs exist in mouse (Fam174b, 153 aa, ~96% identity), zebrafish (133 aa, ~84% identity), and cow (159 aa), demonstrating strong vertebrate conservation. The interaction network and pancreatic enrichment suggest FAM174B functions as a Golgi membrane scaffold that maintains cisternal stacking and facilitates secretory granule formation, particularly in exocrine tissues. Disruption may impair protein glycosylation and secretion, contributing to disease phenotypes.
