## Description
UBE2Z/Q9H832 (354 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 79.4) ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme (E2) functioning as a mediator of protein ubiquitination through transfer of ubiquitin from E1 activating enzymes to substrates or E3 ligases. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical UBC catalytic core domain (IPR000608/PF00179, residues 99-253/103-226) containing the essential active site architecture for thioester bond formation with ubiquitin, classifying it within the ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme/RWD-like superfamily (IPR016135/G3DSA:3.10.110.10/SSF54495). HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue and cell type specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol, consistent with fundamental roles in global protein homeostasis, apoptosis regulation, and the ubiquitin-like conjugation pathway. STRING interactions define a high-confidence ubiquitination cascade network linking UBE2Z to E1 activating enzymes UBA1 (0.851), UBA6 (0.712), and UBA3 (0.704), ubiquitin precursors UBD (0.830), UBB (0.810), and UBA52 (0.729), E1 enzyme UBA7 (0.583), ribosomal biogenesis factor WDR12 (0.533), and paralogous E2 enzymes UBE2G2 (0.519) and UBE2Q1 (0.499), positioning it within the core ubiquitination machinery. Literature (41 papers) highlights emerging clinical relevance in ferroptosis regulation and cancer: 2024-2026 studies demonstrate BMSC-derived exosomal miR-219-5p alleviates ferroptosis in neuronal cells after spinal cord injury via the UBE2Z/NRF2 pathway; low UBE2Z expression as miR-500a target associates with poor prognosis in triple-negative breast cancer; 3-gene ferroptosis-related signature including UBE2Z predicts lung cancer outcomes; UBE2Z-UBR2-Ecadherin axis identified as beta-catenin-specific vulnerability in colorectal cancer; and methionine depletion inhibits hepatocellular carcinoma through ARNT2/UBE2Z pathway inducing ferroptosis and suppressing EMT. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly large copy number losses and gains on chromosome 17q regions (17q12-22, 17q21.32-22, 17q21.31-25.3, 17q23.1-25.1), implicating UBE2Z dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. The convergence of conserved UBC catalytic machinery, E1 enzyme partnerships, ferroptosis regulation through NRF2 signaling, and cancer prognostic associations suggests UBE2Z functions as a versatile ubiquitin carrier coordinating protein turnover with oxidative stress responses, with therapeutic potential in cancer biomarker development and understanding ferroptosis in neurodegeneration and spinal cord injury.
