## Description
TWF2 (Twinfilin Actin Binding Protein 2, also known as A6RP, MSTP011, PTK9L, UniProt Q6IBS0) is a 349-amino acid actin monomer-sequestering protein functioning as a critical regulator of actin cytoskeleton dynamics through its dual ADF-H (Actin-Depolymerizing Factor Homology) domains that bind G-actin and prevent filament polymerization, localized to the plasma membrane where it coordinates dendritic spine morphogenesis in neurons, tumor progression in renal cell carcinoma, and cilium biogenesis/degradation processes. The protein architecture features two tandem ADF-H domains (IPR002108/PF00241/PS51263/SM00102: N-terminal aa 4-142/7-138/4-139/11-139, C-terminal aa 176-311/180-313/177-313/184-313) characteristic of the cofilin/tropomyosin-type actin-binding protein superfamily enabling high-affinity G-actin sequestration, complete Twinfilin family membership (IPR028458/PTHR13759 aa 1-340/1-340) indicating evolutionary conservation from yeast to humans with specialized actin dynamics regulation, ADF-H/Gelsolin-like domain superfamily classification (IPR029006 aa 1-141, 176-322) confirming structural homology to actin-severing proteins, Severin homologous superfamily assignment (G3DSA:3.40.20.10 aa 1-141, 176-322) linking to actin filament disassembly machinery, and GO term annotations for actin binding (GO:0003779), negative regulation of actin filament polymerization (GO:0030837), and cilium biogenesis/degradation biological processes.
