## Description
CIR1 (corepressor of RBPJ and splicing regulator, aliases CIR, CIRSR, THE1B/CIR1) is a 450 amino acid predicted intracellular transcriptional corepressor (UniProt Q86X95) with medium-confidence partially-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 59.7) containing canonical CIR family architecture including CBF1-interacting co-repressor CIR N-terminal domain (IPR019339/PF10197/SM01083, residues 13-49) forming a 37 aa structured module (~8% of protein) characteristic of the CIR family that mediates RBPJ binding, Corepressor interacting with RBPJ 1 family (IPR040014/PTHR13151, residues 1-437) forming a 437 aa module (~97% of protein) defining the CIR corepressor signature, and molecular functions including transcription corepressor activity (GO:0003714), histone deacetylase binding, protein kinase binding, and negative regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II, functioning as a ubiquitous nuclear speckle transcriptional corepressor/splicing regulator that regulates Notch signaling repression and mRNA splicing through CIR domain-mediated RBPJ binding and corepressor complex recruitment via partnerships with RBPJ, SNW1, RBPJL, NCOR2, HDAC2, HDAC1, SAP30, CWC25, CWC15, and FRG1.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals CIR1 contains canonical CIR corepressor architecture: The CIR N-terminal domain (residues 13-49) forms a 37 aa RBPJ-binding module that directly interacts with the Notch transcription factor RBPJ (CBF1) to repress Notch target genes. The Corepressor interacting with RBPJ 1 family (IPR040014) encompasses most of the protein (residues 1-437, 97%) with conserved residues for corepressor complex assembly. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 59.7) indicates a partially structured protein with the N-terminal CIR domain likely ordered for RBPJ binding, embedded in flexible regions that enable dynamic corepressor complex assembly and splicing factor interactions.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places CIR1 within the Notch signaling repression/splicing network with very-high-to-high confidence interactions: strongest interaction with RBPJ (0.989, recombination signal binding protein for immunoglobulin kappa J region, Notch transcription factor), followed by SNW1 (0.954, SNW domain containing 1, splicing/coactivator), RBPJL (0.937, RBPJ-like, Notch pathway), NCOR2 (0.936, nuclear receptor corepressor 2, SMRT), HDAC2 (0.927, histone deacetylase 2), HDAC1 (0.921, histone deacetylase 1), SAP30 (0.909, SAP30 histone deacetylase complex subunit), CWC25 (0.871, CWC25 spliceosome associated protein homolog), CWC15 (0.861, CWC15 spliceosome associated protein homolog), and FRG1 (0.809, FSHD region gene 1). The RBPJ-RBPJL interactions (0.989/0.937) confirm CIR1's primary role as a Notch pathway corepressor that binds RBPJ to silence Notch target genes in the absence of Notch activation. The NCOR2-HDAC1/2-SAP30 interactions (0.936/0.921/0.927/0.909) identify the canonical corepressor complex that CIR1 recruits for histone deacetylation and transcriptional silencing.
