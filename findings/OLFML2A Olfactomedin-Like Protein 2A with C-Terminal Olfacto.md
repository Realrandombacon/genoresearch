## Description
OLFML2A (Olfactomedin-Like Protein 2A, UniProt Q68BL7) is a 652 aa secreted extracellular matrix protein belonging to the olfactomedin domain-containing family (IPR003112/IPR050605/PF02191/PS51132/PTHR23192/SM00284, positions 392-652/37-650/392-650/394-652/37-650/397-652) that functions as an extracellular regulator of cell cycle progression and cancer progression through EZH2-mediated epigenetic regulation. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal signal peptide for secretion followed by a large unstructured region and a C-terminal olfactomedin domain (positions 392-652) forming a beta-propeller structure characteristic of olfactomedin family proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions in the extracellular matrix. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 67.1) indicating flexible N-terminal region connected to ordered C-terminal olfactomedin domain—typical architecture for secreted ECM proteins requiring conformational flexibility for matrix integration and partner binding.
