## Description

InterPro analysis reveals classification as a high-sulphur keratin-associated protein (IPR007659, aa 3-88) belonging to the PMG-type keratin-associated protein family (IPR007951/PF05287, aa 7-87) and the KRTAP3-3-related family (PTHR23260, aa 3-88). GO annotations confirm structural molecule activity and keratin filament association. KRTAP25-1 likely functions as an intrinsically disordered scaffold protein in the hair shaft cortex, using its high-sulphur domains to form extensive disulfide cross-links with keratin intermediate filaments — critical for hair mechanical strength and texture, with dysfunction potentially contributing to hair shaft disorders though genetic redundancy masks individual gene effects.
