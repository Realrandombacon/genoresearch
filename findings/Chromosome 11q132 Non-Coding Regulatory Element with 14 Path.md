## Description
LOC100130987 (Gene ID: 100130987) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 11q13.2 (NC_000011.10, positions 67317838-67391686, ~74 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 6 single nucleotide variants in POLD4 (DNA polymerase delta subunit 4, variant 4375415: c.72C>T p.Ser24=) and CLCF1 (cardiotrophin-like cytokine factor 1, variants 3769216: c.1A>T p.Met1Leu; 3630002: c.365G>A p.Arg122His; 1694057: c.392G>A p.Arg131His; 713415: c.660_666del p.Gly221fs; 712600: c.88C>T p.Arg30Cys) and 4 copy number variants (variant 154814: 11q13.1-13.2 ~2.0 Mb deletion; variant 146304: 11q13.2-13.3 ~2.2 Mb deletion; variant 59757: 11q13.2 ~1.7 Mb duplication; variant 58892: 11q13.2 ~0.8 Mb deletion). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 11q13.2 chromosomal region contains critical DNA repair, cytokine signaling, and detoxification-associated genes including POLD4 (DNA polymerase delta subunit 4, lagging strand DNA synthesis, DNA repair, replication fidelity, cancer susceptibility), CLCF1 (cardiotrophin-like cytokine factor 1, IL-6 family cytokine, CNTF receptor binding, motor neuron survival, neuromuscular junction), GSTP1 (glutathione S-transferase pi 1, xenobiotic detoxification, glutathione conjugation, cancer chemoresistance, oxidative stress), LRP5 (LDL receptor related protein 5, Wnt co-receptor, bone density, osteoporosis-pseudoglioma syndrome), ACTN3 (actinin alpha 3, Z-disc structural protein, muscle contraction, athletic performance), GRK2 (G protein-coupled receptor kinase 2, GPCR desensitization, beta-adrenergic signaling, heart failure), and PPP1CA (protein phosphatase 1 catalytic subunit alpha, serine/threonine dephosphorylation, cell cycle, glycogen metabolism). Functional hypothesis: LOC100130987 likely represents a critical 11q13.2 enhancer element within the POLD4-CLCF1 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a DNA damage response enhancer coordinating POLD4-CLCF1-GSTP1 expression in proliferating tissues (hematopoietic cells, epithelial cells, neurons) or as a chromatin boundary separating DNA repair genes (POLD4) from cytokine genes (CLCF1) and detoxification genes (GSTP1). The 14 pathogenic variants (mix of SNVs and CNVs, 0.8-2.2 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered sequence/copy number disrupts POLD4-mediated DNA replication fidelity and CLCF1-mediated neurotrophic signaling. Disease mechanism involves POLD4 mutation causing DNA repair deficiency (replication fork stalling, mutation accumulation, cancer predisposition, microsatellite instability), CLCF1 mutation causing neuromuscular disorders (motor neuron degeneration, muscle weakness, neuromuscular junction defects, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis-like phenotype), GSTP1 overexpression causing chemoresistance (drug detoxification, cisplatin resistance, doxorubicin resistance, oxidative stress tolerance), LRP5 mutation causing bone disorders (osteoporosis-pseudoglioma syndrome, low bone mineral density, fracture susceptibility), ACTN3 deficiency causing muscle performance defects (alpha-actinin-3 absence, sprinting impairment, endurance adaptation), GRK2 dysregulation causing cardiovascular disease (beta-adrenergic desensitization, heart failure progression, hypertension), and 11q13.2 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with cancer predisposition and neuromuscular abnormalities. The 11q13.2 region is a well-established cancer amplification locus with GSTP1 polymorphisms associated with cancer risk. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (14 variants 0.8-2.2 Mb) and POLD4-CLCF1-GSTP1-LRP5-ACTN3 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a hematopoietic/neuronal-specific enhancer critical for DNA repair-neurotrophic signaling-detoxification coordination.
