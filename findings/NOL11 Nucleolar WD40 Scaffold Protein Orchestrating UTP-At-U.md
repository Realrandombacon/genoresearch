## Description
NOL11 (Nucleolar Protein 11, Q9H8H0) encodes a 719 aa nucleolar scaffold protein functioning as an essential component of the UTP-A/t-UTP subcomplex within the small subunit (SSU) processome that coordinates early pre-rRNA transcription and processing during ribosome biogenesis. Structural architecture reveals bipartite domain organization: N-terminal WD40-repeat domain (IPR012584/PF08168/IPR036322/SSF50978, residues 1-332/22-303) forming beta-propeller scaffold for protein-protein interactions, and C-terminal domain (IPR048897/PF20998, residues 399-719) mediating complex assembly; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 78.8) confirms high-confidence well-folded structure consistent with stable multi-protein complex integration. STRING interactions define SSU processome network with ultra-high confidence: UTP4 (0.999) core t-UTP component, WDR43 (0.995)/WDR75 (0.992) WD40 proteins forming UTP-A triad, UTP15 (0.986)/UTP20 (0.979)/UTP3 (0.968) additional t-UTP subunits, HEATR1 (0.983) ribosome assembly factor, EMG1 (0.978) 18S rRNA methyltransferase, NOC4L (0.968) ribosome biogenesis factor, and DCAF13 (0.968) substrate adapter linking to ubiquitination. HPA localizes NOL11 to nucleoli as essential intracellular protein with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, classified as transcription activator involved in ribosome biogenesis/rRNA processing/transcription regulation. ClinVar documents 14 pathogenic variants including 17q24.2 copy number losses (chr17:65709215-65952716)x1, 17q24.1-25.3 gains (chr17:62778720-81041938)x3, 17q23.1-24.2 duplications, and splice variant c.142-49A>G. Literature (13 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: NOL11 as novel prognostic biomarker in hepatocellular carcinoma (2025), LINC01940 lncRNA promotes gastric cancer chemoresistance via TAF15-mediated NOL11 SUMOylation enhancing ribosome biogenesis (2025), NOL11 required for optimal rDNA transcription and craniofacial development in Xenopus (43x citations, 2015), NOL11 implicated in North American Indian Childhood Cirrhosis pathogenesis required for pre-rRNA transcription/processing (97x citations, 2012), NOL11 required for timely mitotic Cdk1 activation (2016). Mechanistic hypothesis: NOL11 WD40 N-terminal domain forms seven-bladed beta-propeller serving as structural scaffold within UTP-A complex (NOL11-WDR43-WDR75 triad); C-terminal domain mediates interactions with t-UTP subunits (UTP4/UTP15/UTP20/UTP3) bridging UTP-A to larger SSU processome; complex assembles at rDNA promoter recruiting RNA Pol I machinery for 47S pre-rRNA transcription initiation; NOL11 stabilizes early cleavage events at sites A0/A1 generating 18S-E precursor; SUMOylation by TAF15 enhances NOL11 stability/activity promoting ribosome biogenesis in cancer cells; in hepatocellular carcinoma, elevated NOL11 drives proliferation through increased protein synthesis capacity; craniofacial defects from NOL11 deficiency reflect ribosopathy mechanism where impaired ribosome production affects neural crest cell development; 17q CNVs cause syndromic phenotypes through NOL11 dosage effects on global translation.
