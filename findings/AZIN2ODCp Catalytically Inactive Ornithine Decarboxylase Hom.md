## Description
AZIN2 (Antizyme Inhibitor 2, also known as ODCp, ODC1L, ADC, AZIB1, UniProt Q96A70) is a 460-amino acid catalytically inactive ornithine decarboxylase homolog belonging to the antizyme inhibitor family that functions as a critical regulator of cellular polyamine homeostasis through high-affinity antizyme binding without decarboxylase activity. The protein architecture features a Type III Pyridoxal 5-phosphate (PLP)-Dependent Enzyme Ornithine Decarboxylase fold (cd00622 aa 40-408) retaining structural similarity to active ODC despite catalytic residue degeneration, Ornithine/DAP/Arg decarboxylase family membership (IPR000183 aa 67-85/87-99/189-202) with conserved PLP-binding barrel architecture (IPR029066), complete Ornithine decarboxylase domain (IPR002433 aa 24-411) forming the characteristic (βα)8 TIM barrel fold, Alanine racemase/group IV decarboxylase C-terminal domain (IPR009006 aa 36-420), N-terminal domain (IPR022644 aa 47-281), conserved pyridoxal-phosphate binding site (IPR022653 aa 67-85) though catalytically inactive, and conserved site motif (IPR022657 aa 223-240). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 83.6), indicating a well-folded protein with stable three-dimensional architecture maintaining the canonical ODC TIM barrel fold despite loss of catalytic function—consistent with proteins retaining structural scaffold for protein-protein interactions.
