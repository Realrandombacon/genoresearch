The 9q13 localization places it in a gene-dense region, raising the possibility of position effects.\n\nThis gene exemplifies the "dark genome" problem: clinically implicated but molecularly uncharacterized. Priority research needs include: antibody generation for detection, CRISPR knockout phenotyping, and interactome mapping via AP-MS.
