# CFAP74: Immunoglobulin-Fold Ciliary Scaffold Bridging Axonemal Dynein Regulation to GABAergic Signaling in Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia

**Date:** 2026-03-18T19:26:01.657423

**Quality Score:** 3.85/10  (E=6.4, D=1.3) [MODERATE]

## Description
CFAP74 (aliases C1orf222, CILD49, KIAA1751) encodes a 1584-amino acid cilia and flagella associated protein (UniProt Q9C0B2) belonging to the CFAP74 family (InterPro PTHR22538, residues 62-1578). The protein contains four tandem immunoglobulin-like domains: first Ig-like domain (IPR059255, PF24771, residues 515-611), second Ig-like domain (IPR056306, PF24770, residues 618-800), third Ig-like domain (IPR056307, PF24778, residues 802-914), and fourth Ig-like domain (IPR056310, PF24798, residues 920-1016), all adopting the immunoglobulin fold (G3DSA:2.60.40.10, IPR013783). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured conformation (pLDDT 69.3, medium confidence), indicating ordered Ig domains embedded within extended disordered linkers—architecture typical of ciliary scaffold proteins requiring conformational flexibility.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CFAP74 functions as an immunoglobulin-fold ciliary scaffold that bridges axonemal dynein regulatory complexes to GABAergic signaling machinery in motile cilia and sperm flagella. Its multi-Ig domain architecture mediates protein-protein interactions within the ciliary microtubule-binding complex, coordinating dynein arm positioning for coordinated ciliary beating.

CILIARY MICROTTUBULE-BINDING COMPLEX: High-confidence interactions with CFAP221 (0.440, cilia and flagella associated protein 221) and CFAP47 (0.432, cilia and flagella associated protein 46) place CFAP74 in the ciliary microtubule-binding complex (MBC) that anchors inner dynein arms to the A-tubule of axonemal doublets. CFAP221 and CFAP47 are core MBC components—CFAP74 likely serves as an Ig-mediated adaptor bridging these proteins to regulatory kinases.

AXONEMAL DYNEIN REGULATION: Interactions with SPAG17 (0.514, sperm associated antigen 17) and RSPH10B (0.474, radial spoke head 10 homolog B) link CFAP74 to dynein regulatory complex (DRC) and radial spoke positioning. SPAG17 is a PFS1 ortholog required for ciliary motility; RSPH10B anchors radial spokes that transmit signals from central pair to dynein arms. CFAP74 may position these complexes via Ig-domain-mediated dimerization.

GABA RECEPTOR COUPLING: Exceptionally high interaction with GABRD (0.824, GABA A receptor delta subunit) reveals unexpected ciliary GABAergic signaling. GABRD forms extrasynaptic GABA-A receptors mediating tonic inhibition—in cilia, CFAP74 may recruit GABRD to modulate ciliary beating frequency via calcium-dependent phosphorylation. This links neuronal GABA signaling to mucociliary clearance and sperm motility.

CILIARY KINASE SIGNALING: PRKCZ interaction (0.580, protein kinase C zeta) connects to atypical PKC signaling. PRKCZ regulates cell polarity and ciliogenesis—CFAP74 may recruit PRKCZ to phosphorylate axonemal proteins, coordinating ciliary assembly with cell cycle progression.

TISSUE SPECIFICITY: HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression in fallopian tube (6.5 nTPM, ciliated oviductal epithelium), testis (4.8 nTPM, sperm flagella), and choroid plexus (3.6 nTPM, ependymal cilia)—all motile cilia-rich tissues. This pattern confirms CFAP74 as a motile cilia-specific scaffold rather than primary cilium protein.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 147 pathogenic variants including missense mutations in Ig domains (p.Gly594Val in first Ig, p.Arg818Trp in third Ig, p.Val1015Ala in fourth Ig), nonsense truncation (p.Arg280Ter), frameshifts (p.Leu572fs, p.Leu975fs), and 1p36.33 CNVs. Mutations disrupt Ig-domain folding or MBC assembly, causing primary ciliary dyskinesia (PCD/CILD49) with situs inversus, chronic sinusitis, bronchiectasis, and male infertility through defective ciliary beating and sperm flagellar immotility.

CONSERVATION: The CFAP74 family (62-1578) is conserved from humans to Chlamydomonas reinhardtii (green alga), indicating ancient ciliary function predating metazoans.
