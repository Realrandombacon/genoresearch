## Description
EFHC1 encodes Myoclonin1, a calcium-binding EF-hand protein involved in cell cycle regulation and apoptosis. It is expressed in cells with motile cilia and modulates dopamine signaling at the synapse. Clinically, pathogenic variants in EFHC1 are associated with Juvenile Myoclonic Epilepsy (JME). With 132 literature papers and known functional roles, EFHC1 is definitively a 'well-studied' gene and does not meet the criteria for a 'dark gene'.
