## Description
DHRS3 (Dehydrogenase/reductase 3, also known as short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase 3 or retinaldehyde reductase, O75911) encodes a 302 aa NAD(P)-dependent oxidoreductase functioning as a critical microsomal enzyme in retinoid homeostasis by catalyzing the reduction of all-trans-retinaldehyde to all-trans-retinol (vitamin A), thereby controlling retinoic acid levels essential for embryonic development, vision, and cellular differentiation. Structural architecture reveals an exceptionally well-folded enzymatic organization with very high AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT 94.2): human 17-beta-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase XI-like classical SDR domain (cd05339, residues 53-281) defining the substrate-binding pocket and catalytic machinery, NAD(P)-binding Rossmann-like domain superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.720/SSF51735/IPR036291, residues 29-292/34-288) forming the characteristic βαβ fold for cofactor binding with conserved GXGXXG nucleotide-binding motif, Short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase SDR family (IPR002347/PF00106/PTHR24322, residues 40-229/8-301) establishing the conserved catalytic tetrad (Tyr-X-X-X-Lys) for hydride transfer, Ketoreductase domain (IPR057326/SM00822, residues 39-221) providing the active site architecture optimized for carbonyl reduction, GDHRDH PRINTS signature motifs (PR00081, residues 40-57/115-126/162-178) marking essential catalytic residues, and SDRFAMILY PRINTS signatures (PR00080, residues 115-126/168-176/188-207) confirming family membership.
