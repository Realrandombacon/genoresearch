# ADAP1: Brain-Enriched ArfGAP with Dual PH Domains Regulating Neuronal Membrane Trafficking in Alzheimer's and Autoimmune Demyelination

**Date:** 2026-05-07T12:01:32.110154

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
ADAP1 encodes a 374-amino acid Arf-GAP with dual PH domain-containing protein 1 (UniProt O75689), also known as Centaurin alpha-1 (CentA1), a very well-folded GTPase-activating protein functioning in neuronal membrane trafficking and receptor signaling. The protein contains an N-terminal ArfGAP catalytic domain (IPR001164, positions 7-126; cd08843, positions 2-113; G3DSA:1.10.220.150, positions 1-128) with GTPase activator activity (GO:0005096) that stimulates GTP hydrolysis on Arf GTPases, plus two C-terminal Pleckstrin homology domains (IPR001849, positions 129-232/252-358; cd13252 PH1 positions 130-238; cd01251 PH2 positions 252-357; G3DSA:2.30.29.30, positions 129-239/240-370) for phosphatidylinositol bisphosphate binding (GO:1902936) and membrane targeting, classified within the ADAP1 family (IPR037849, positions 130-238; IPR037278 ARFGAP/RecO-like zinc finger, positions 6-121). AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 95.9, residues 1-374) indicating stable folding with well-defined ArfGAP catalytic core and dual PH domain architecture. STRING interaction analysis reveals ADAP1 forms part of the Arf signaling and membrane trafficking network with high-confidence partners: NCL (0.870, nucleolin), NRDC (0.799, nardilysin convertase), ERBB4 (0.767, receptor tyrosine-protein kinase erbB-4), PLEK2 (0.727, pleckstrin-2), PLEK (0.707, pleckstrin), ARF1 (0.678, ADP-ribosylation factor 1), RANBP9 (0.669, Ran-binding protein 9), KIF13A (0.565, kinesin-like protein KIF13A), KIF13B (0.523, kinesin-like protein KIF13B), and ARF6 (0.450, ADP-ribosylation factor 6), confirming direct Arf1/Arf6 substrate interactions and links to receptor trafficking. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with striking brain enrichment (131.1 nTPM, highest expression), detected in many tissues, plasma membrane and cytosol subcellular localization consistent with membrane trafficking function, annotated for GTPase activation molecular function. ClinVar contains 40 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (7p22.3-14.3, 7p22.3-22.1, 7p22.3-21.3, 7p22.3-21.1) and losses (7p22.3-22.2, 7p22.3, 7p22.3-21.3), plus deletions and complex variants, though specific Mendelian disorders remain undefined. With 53 publications, ADAP1 has emerging neuroscience roles: ADAP1/CentA1 loss protects against autoimmune demyelination (2025, 1 cit), ADAP1 deficiency ameliorates cognitive impairment and neuropathological hallmarks in Alzheimer's disease mouse model (2025), ArfGAPs ADAP1 and ARAP1 regulate CD63 incorporation in multivesicular bodies (2024), lncRNA AC098613.1 promotes AML differentiation through CDC5L/ADAP1/NRD1 axis (2026). The functional hypothesis: ADAP1 serves as a brain-enriched ArfGAP that terminates Arf1/Arf6 signaling by catalyzing GTP hydrolysis, regulating membrane trafficking, receptor endocytosis, and cytoskeletal dynamics in neurons; the dual PH domains target ADAP1 to specific membrane compartments via phosphatidylinositol bisphosphate binding, positioning the ArfGAP domain to inactivate membrane-bound Arf-GTP; ARF1 interaction suggests roles in Golgi-to-ER retrograde trafficking and vesicle coat assembly; ARF6 interaction indicates regulation of plasma membrane endocytosis, actin remodeling, and receptor recycling critical for synaptic function; ERBB4 interaction links ADAP1 to neuregulin-ErbB4 signaling important for oligodendrocyte development and myelination, explaining autoimmune demyelination protection upon ADAP1 loss; NCL (nucleolin) and NRDC (nardilysin) interactions suggest roles in growth factor receptor processing and neuronal development; PLEK/PLEK2 interactions indicate coordination with pleckstrin-mediated signaling in platelets and immune cells; KIF13A/KIF13B kinesin interactions suggest ADAP1 regulates vesicle transport along microtubules in axons and dendrites; Alzheimer's disease amelioration upon ADAP1 deficiency suggests Arf-mediated endocytic trafficking contributes to amyloid-beta production or tau pathology, potentially through APP processing or receptor-mediated clearance; 7p CNVs may disrupt ADAP1 dosage causing neuronal trafficking defects contributing to neurodevelopmental disorders; very high structural confidence (pLDDT 95.9) indicates stable catalytic and membrane-binding domains suitable for drug targeting.
