## Description
ZBED1 (UniProt: O96006, aliases ALTE/DREF/TRAMP/hDREF) encodes a 694 amino acid transcription factor and E3 SUMO-protein ligase representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 27 publications in Semantic Scholar. This gene is uniquely located in the pseudoautosomal region 1 (PAR1) of both X and Y chromosomes (Xp22.33-Yp11.32), escaping X-inactivation and expressed from both sex chromosomes. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture characteristic of hAT transposase-derived transcription factors: IPR003656/PF02892/SM00614/PS50808 (BED-type zinc finger, residues 20-78, GO:0003677 DNA binding) for sequence-specific DNA recognition, IPR008906/PF05699/SSF140996 (hAT C-terminal dimerization domain, residues 571-650, GO:0046983 protein dimerization activity) for homo/heterodimerization, plus two RNase-like domains (PF27041 residues 198-282, PF27039 residues 289-398) and IPR012337 (Ribonuclease H-like superfamily, residues 184-652) suggesting evolutionary derivation from transposase ancestors. Additional classifications include IPR036236 (Zinc finger C2H2 superfamily), IPR052717/PTHR46169 (Vacuolar processing and transposase activity regulators family). AlphaFold structural prediction yields high confidence (global pLDDT: 82.8), indicating a well-folded stable protein with ordered DNA-binding and dimerization domains. STRING interaction analysis identifies 10 protein partners: DHRSX (dehydrogenase/reductase SDR family member X, score 0.773), ASMTL (acetylserotonin O-methyltransferase-like, 0.737), CHD3 (chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 3/Mi2-alpha, 0.698), UBE2I (SUMO-conjugating enzyme UBC9, 0.690), GTPBP6 (GTP binding protein 6, 0.659), SMARCA1 (SWI/SNF related matrix associated actin dependent regulator of chromatin subfamily A member 1, 0.647), AKAP17A (A-kinase anchoring protein 17A, 0.621), H1-0 (H1.0 linker histone, 0.613), SMARCA5 (0.609), and KPNB1 (importin subunit beta-1, 0.590). The CHD3 and UBE2I interactions directly support ZBED1's documented E3 SUMO ligase function that sumoylates CHD3/Mi2-alpha causing its release from DNA (PubMed:27068747). SMARCA1/SMARCA5 interactions suggest involvement in ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling complexes. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enhanced expression detected in all tissues with highest expression in skeletal muscle (67.6 nTPM), nuclear/nucleoplasm/centrosome localization, and cell type-enriched single cell specificity. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (Xp22.33-11.4, Xp22.33), copy number loss (Xp22.33-22.2), and duplications, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Recent literature (2025) reveals ZBED1 promotes glioblastoma stem cell stemness and tumorigenesis through reciprocal EGFR/ZBED1 regulation, while 2022 studies demonstrate ZBED1 regulates genes important for multiple placental biological processes in trophoblast cells. Based on domain architecture (BED zinc finger DNA binding + hAT dimerization + RNase-like domains), interaction partners (CHD3, UBE2I, SMARCA1/5 chromatin remodelers), high-confidence AlphaFold structure, and emerging literature, ZBED1 likely functions as a dual-function transcription factor and E3 SUMO ligase that coordinates chromatin remodeling through CHD3 sumoylation, regulates placental development and trophoblast differentiation, and promotes cancer stem cell maintenance in glioblastoma. The PAR1 location suggests dosage-sensitive functions affecting both sexes equally, potentially explaining copy number variant pathogenicity. The hAT transposase-derived architecture indicates evolutionary co-option of transposon DNA-binding machinery for transcriptional regulation.
