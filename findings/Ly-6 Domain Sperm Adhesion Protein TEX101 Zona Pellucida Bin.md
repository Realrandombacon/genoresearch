# Ly-6 Domain Sperm Adhesion Protein TEX101: Zona Pellucida Binding Factor with Fertility Implications

**Date:** 2026-03-23T03:19:54.682630

**Quality Score:** 2.75/10  (E=4.5, D=1.0) [WEAK]

## Description
TEX101 encodes a 249 amino acid testis-specific membrane protein (UniProt Q9BY14) functioning as a sperm adhesion factor essential for fertilization. InterPro analysis reveals two tandem Ly-6/u-PAR domains (IPR016054, PF00021; residues 47-116 and 137-215) characteristic of glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored surface proteins, plus a snake toxin-like superfold (IPR045860, SSF57302; residues 138-194) enabling protein-protein interactions. HPA data shows extreme testis-specific expression (111.4 nTPM) with plasma membrane and acrosomal vesicle localization, consistent with sperm surface function. STRING interactions reveal 10 partners including DPEP3 (0.816, dipeptidase), ACRV1 (0.598, acrosomal vesicle protein), LDHC (0.582, lactate dehydrogenase C), PLAUR (0.473, urokinase receptor), and LY6K (0.418, Ly-6 family member), forming a sperm surface adhesion complex. ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic variants, predominantly 19q chromosomal copy number alterations, suggesting haploinsufficiency causes male infertility. The Ly-6 domain architecture positions TEX101 as a GPI-anchored scaffold mediating sperm-zona pellucida binding via interaction with ACRV1 and PLAUR. Disruption of TEX101 function through 19q deletions likely impairs sperm motility and oocyte recognition, explaining clinical associations with male factor infertility.
