## Description
SAA4 (UniProt P35542), or serum amyloid A-4 protein (also known as constitutive serum amyloid A), encodes a compact 130-amino acid secreted apolipoprotein belonging to the serum amyloid A protein family (IPR000096/IPR052464/PF00277/PIRSF002472/PTHR23424/SM00197/G3DSA:1.10.132.110, aa 1-130). Unlike the acute-phase inducible SAA1/SAA2 isoforms, SAA4 is constitutively expressed and maintains stable plasma levels independent of inflammation. The protein architecture features a conserved serum amyloid A fold with four amphipathic alpha-helices (signature motif PS00992, aa 56-72) that mediate lipid binding and HDL association. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (global pLDDT: 85.2), indicating a well-folded helical bundle consistent with apolipoproteins requiring stable lipid-binding interfaces.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals SAA4's integration into lipoprotein metabolism and acute-phase response networks: SAA2 (0.935), APOA1 (0.894, major HDL apolipoprotein), APOA2 (0.816), APOC3 (0.771), SAA1 (0.762), APOF (0.758), APOA4 (0.757), APOC2 (0.756), APOB (0.736, LDL/VLDL component), and TTR (0.526, transthyretin). This partner profile positions SAA4 as a structural HDL component that competes with or complements acute-phase SAAs for lipoprotein binding sites.\n\nWith 175 papers but incomplete mechanistic understanding, SAA4 qualifies as a dark gene with breakthrough immunometabolic implications emerging in 2024-2026. Recent studies reveal: (1) low SAA4 expression associated with advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) stage and poor prognosis—paradoxical tumor suppressor role unlike pro-inflammatory SAA1; (2) SAA4-MLKL axis reprograms macrophages to assassinate lung tumor cells via necroptosis induction; (3) SAA4 characterized as 'underdog' within SAA superfamily with distinct functions from acute-phase isoforms; (4) SAA4 expressed in human primary monocytes and macrophages independent of inflammatory stimuli. HPA data confirms extreme liver enrichment (2067.9 nTPM)—highest among SAA family members—reflecting constitutive hepatic secretion; classified as plasma protein involved in acute-phase response. ClinVar lists 13 pathogenic variants including 11p15.1-15.5 copy number losses/gains, confirming dosage sensitivity potentially linked to Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome region disorders. We hypothesize SAA4 functions as a homeostatic counterbalance to acute-phase SAA1/SAA2: by occupying HDL binding sites constitutively, SAA4 prevents excessive pro-inflammatory signaling during basal conditions while maintaining cholesterol efflux capacity; the SAA4-MLKL macrophage reprogramming represents an innate immune surveillance mechanism where SAA4-loaded HDL delivers necroptotic signals to transformed cells; low SAA4 in HCC removes this protective brake allowing tumor progression; the 11p15 CNVs disrupt imprinted gene clusters affecting SAA4 dosage, linking metabolic dysregulation to developmental disorders.
