## Description
ATXN7L3 (Ataxin-7-Like Protein 3) is a 347 amino acid nuclear protein that functions as a critical component of the SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5-Acetyltransferase) chromatin-modifying complex, mediating histone H2B deubiquitination through USP22 interaction and transcriptional activation via zinc finger and SCA7 domain-mediated DNA/chromatin binding for cell cycle progression, DNA repair, and developmental gene regulation in proliferating and differentiated tissues. Literature (43 papers) establishes ATXN7L3 as an emerging chromatin regulator: de novo variants lead to developmental delay/hypotonia/facial features defining new neurodevelopmental syndrome; novel variant underlies HATONS with pre-axial polydactyly expanding phenotypic spectrum; enhancer retargeting of CDX2 and UBTF::ATXN7L3 fusion define high-risk B-progenitor acute lymphoblastic leukemia subtype revealing oncogenic mechanism; metformin ameliorates trophoblastic immunometabolic disorders via attenuating TLR4/NF-κB signaling through ATXN7L3-mediated histone H2B monoubiquitination establishing placental disease role. As an emerging SAGA complex component with neurodevelopmental and oncogenic associations, ATXN7L3 represents a critical node connecting chromatin modification to developmental gene regulation through ENY2-USP22-SUPT20H network integration for H2B deubiquitination-mediated transcriptional activation, SCA7 domain/zinc finger-mediated chromatin engagement, 17q12 copy number variation disorder pathogenesis via dosage sensitivity, and HATONS/B-ALL/trophoblastic disorder disruption in neurodevelopment, hematological malignancy, placental inflammation, and glycometabolic reprogramming.
