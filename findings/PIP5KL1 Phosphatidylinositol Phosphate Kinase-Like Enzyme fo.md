## Description
PIP5KL1 encodes a 394 aa phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate 5-kinase-like protein 1 (UniProt Q5T9C9), functioning as a lipid kinase that regulates phosphoinositide metabolism, PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling, and tumor suppression through phosphatidylinositol phosphate kinase activity. The protein contains a canonical PIPK catalytic domain (IPR002498/PF01504, aa36-394/aa130-391) with phosphatidylinositol kinase activity (GO:0052742) for phosphatidylinositol metabolic process (GO:0046488), belonging to the phosphatidylinositol-4/5-phosphate 5/4-kinase family (IPR023610, aa45-391) with N-terminal domain (IPR027484/G3DSA:3.30.800.10, aa44-206) and C-terminal domain superfamily (IPR027483/G3DSA:3.30.810.10, aa207-394), classified within the PIP5K enzyme family (PTHR23086, aa36-393) with conserved catalytic site (cd17304, aa50-392). PIP5KL1 functions as a phosphoinositide kinase—its PIPK catalytic domain phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate to generate phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate (PIP2), a critical second messenger for membrane trafficking, cytoskeletal dynamics, and signal transduction. The ultra-high confidence PIK3CD/PIK3CB/PIK3CA interactions (0.940/0.925/0.922) indicate PIP5KL1 operates within the PI3K signaling network where PIP2 serves as substrate for PI3K to generate PIP3, activating AKT/mTOR pathways. The PIP5K1A/B/C interactions (0.935/0.921/0.930) suggest PIP5KL1 functions alongside canonical type I PIP5Ks, potentially as a regulatory subunit or alternative catalytic isoform. PIP4K2A interaction (0.924) links PIP5KL1 to phosphatidylinositol-5-phosphate metabolism. Cancer suppression reflects PIP5KL1 overexpression inhibiting proliferation through modulation of phosphoinositide signaling—altered PIP2/PIP3 ratios may dampen PI3K/AKT hyperactivation common in cancers. Copy number variants on 9q may cause PIP5KL1 dosage effects contributing to cancer susceptibility or developmental disorders. The cytosolic localization and well-folded structure (pLDDT 84.5) enable PIP5KL1 to function as a stable kinase coordinating phosphoinositide metabolism at membrane-cytosol interfaces.
