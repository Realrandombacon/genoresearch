## Description
GCC2 (GRIP and Coiled-Coil Domain-Containing Protein 2, also known as GCC185, Q8IWJ2) encodes a massive 1684 aa peripheral membrane protein functioning as an essential golgin tethering factor localized to the trans-Golgi network (TGN) that mediates retrograde transport of mannose-6-phosphate receptors (MPR) and integrins from endosomes to the Golgi, playing pivotal roles in maintaining Golgi apparatus integrity, regulating EGFR signaling pathways, and modulating immune responses.
