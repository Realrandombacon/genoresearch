## Description
FAM131C (Gene ID: 348487, UniProt Q96AQ9, alias C1orf117) is a 280 amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 1p36.13 (NC_000001.11, positions 16057768-16073650, ~16kb genomic region). This gene encodes a member of the FAM131 protein family with conserved domains suggestive of cell signaling function. InterPro domain analysis reveals three overlapping annotations: Protein FAM131 family (IPR026782, positions 7-242, spanning most of the protein), Putative cell signaling domain (PF15010, positions 72-184, central ~112 residue region), and PROTEIN FAM131B-RELATED panther family (PTHR15736, positions 7-242). The PF15010 domain occupies the protein core, suggesting it constitutes the primary functional module despite being labeled 'putative'. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners: HPCAL4 (0.713, hippocalcin-like protein 4, calcium sensor), TMEM82 (0.654, transmembrane protein 82), PRAMEF18 (0.582, preferentially expressed antigen of melanoma family 18), PRAMEF7 (0.574), PPIAL4D (0.570, peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase A-like 4D), VSNL1 (0.565, visinin-like protein 1, neuronal calcium sensor), NIPAL3 (0.526, NIPA-like domain containing 3), HR (0.523, hairless transcriptional repressor), DRICH1 (0.481, aspartate-rich protein 1), and MAN1C1 (0.429, mannosidase alpha class 1C member 1, Golgi enzyme). The interaction profile strongly suggests roles in calcium-mediated signaling (HPCAL4, VSNL1 both EF-hand calcium-binding proteins), protein folding/isomerization (PPIAL4D), and glycosylation trafficking (MAN1C1). Human Protein Atlas classifies FAM131C (ENSG00000185519) as a predicted intracellular protein with nucleoli, intermediate filaments, and cytosol subcellular localization. RNA tissue specificity is 'group enriched' with highest expression in brain (72.1 nTPM—moderate-high expression) and kidney (19.0 nTPM), detected in many tissues. Cancer specificity is group enriched, not detected in immune cells, with cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. This brain-enriched expression pattern combined with calcium sensor interactions suggests neuronal function, potentially in synaptic plasticity, calcium homeostasis, or neuronal development. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q96AQ9-F1) yields medium-confidence model (global pLDDT 57.9), indicating a partially structured protein with mixed ordered and disordered regions across all 280 residues—consistent with intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) characteristics or flexible linker regions between structured domains. ClinVar lists 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large chromosomal copy number variations spanning chromosome 1p36 regions (e.g., chr1:4436802-22782007 deletion, chr1:16194137-20561434 deletion, chr1:99125-34026935 triplication, chr1:6758933-19287770 deletion, chr1:849466-17525065 deletion); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to FAM131C point mutations, suggesting dosage sensitivity within larger chromosomal syndromes rather than haploinsufficiency. The 1p36 deletion syndrome is a well-characterized contiguous gene deletion disorder. Conservation evidenced by PTHR15736 panther family membership across vertebrates and FAM131B paralog. Functional hypothesis: FAM131C functions as an intracellular signaling scaffold or adapter protein involved in calcium-mediated neuronal processes. The PF15010 'putative cell signaling' domain likely mediates protein-protein interactions with calcium sensors (HPCAL4, VSNL1). Nucleolar localization suggests potential roles in ribosome biogenesis regulation or stress response. Medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 57.9) suggests conformational flexibility consistent with signaling adapter function. Interaction with neuronal calcium sensors (VSNL1, HPCAL4) and Golgi mannosidase (MAN1C1) suggests dual roles in neuronal calcium signaling and protein trafficking/glycosylation. Brain-enriched expression (72.1 nTPM) indicates specialized function in neuronal biology—potentially synaptic transmission, neuronal development, or calcium-dependent signaling cascades. Disease mechanism: FAM131C haploinsufficiency or overexpression may contribute to chromosome 1p36 deletion/duplication syndromes. The 22 ClinVar CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity; 1p36 deletion syndrome features include intellectual disability, seizures, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. FAM131C may contribute to neurological phenotypes given brain expression and calcium sensor interactions. Triplication may cause aberrant signaling complex assembly while deletion may impair calcium-mediated neuronal processes. Priority experiments include co-IP validation of STRING interactions (especially HPCAL4, VSNL1, MAN1C1), subcellular localization confirmation via immunofluorescence (nucleoli vs cytosol), investigation of FAM131C role in calcium signaling using FRET-based calcium imaging, assessment of FAM131C expression during neuronal differentiation, structural analysis leveraging AlphaFold model (pLDDT 57.9) to identify flexible regions and potential interaction surfaces, and generation of knockout mice to assess neurological phenotype.
