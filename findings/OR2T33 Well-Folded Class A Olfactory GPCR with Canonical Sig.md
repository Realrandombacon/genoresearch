## Description
OR2T33 (Olfactory receptor 2T33, UniProt Q8NG76) is a 320-amino acid G protein-coupled receptor located on chromosome 1q44 within the large olfactory receptor gene cluster. InterPro analysis confirms this as a canonical class A rhodopsin-like GPCR containing the olfactory receptor family domain (IPR000725/PF13853, aa 32-303), seven transmembrane helices characteristic of rhodopsin-like receptors (IPR017452, aa 39-288; G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, aa 17-318), and membership in the olfactory receptor subfamily 2T (cd15421, aa 23-299). The protein contains conserved GPCR signature motifs (PS00237, aa 108-124) critical for G protein coupling. AlphaFold structure prediction achieves high confidence (pLDDT 88.7), indicating a well-folded stable 7-transmembrane bundle architecture typical of functional olfactory receptors ready for ligand binding and signal transduction. Human Protein Atlas shows no detection in standard tissue panels, which is expected for olfactory receptors expressed exclusively in olfactory sensory neurons of the nasal epithelium not profiled in HPA. STRING interaction network reveals 10 partners comprising the complete canonical olfactory signal transduction cascade: PRKACA/PRKACB/PRKACG (0.650, cAMP-dependent protein kinase catalytic subunits), GNAL (0.650, olfactory-specific Gα subunit), GNG7 (0.650, Gβγ subunit), ARRB2 (0.650, β-arrestin mediating desensitization), GRK2/GRK3 (0.547-0.569, G protein-coupled receptor kinases), GNG13 (0.566), and GNB1 (0.503). This interactome confirms OR2T33 functions through the canonical cAMP-mediated olfactory transduction pathway. ClinVar contains an extraordinary 71 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number losses and gains across 1q41-q44, deletions, and duplications, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting the olfactory receptor cluster cause disease phenotypes or genomic instability. Conservation across mammals (hyena A0A6G1BIU5, 300 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to chemosensory function. OR2T33 likely functions as a volatile odorant receptor detecting specific molecular features, activating GNAL to stimulate adenylate cyclase III, increasing cAMP, opening cyclic nucleotide-gated channels, and depolarizing olfactory sensory neurons.
