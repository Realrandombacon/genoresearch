## Description
PDCD2L encodes a 358 aa uS5 assembly chaperone (UniProt Q9BRP1), functioning as a ribosome biogenesis factor that regulates cell cycle progression and cancer development through mitochondrial ribosome assembly and protein arginine methylation networks. The protein contains a YwqG-like homologous superfamily domain (G3DSA:2.30.320.10, aa14-227) characteristic of ribosome assembly factors, a C-terminal programmed cell death protein 2 domain (IPR007320/PF04194, aa247-350) localized to cytoplasm (GO:0005737), and belongs to the programmed cell death 2-like regulator family (IPR052815/PTHR46421, aa1-358). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence structure (pLDDT 75.4), indicating a well-folded stable protein with defined domain architecture. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression detected in all tissues with highest levels in skeletal muscle (24.2 nTPM), classified as cancer-related intracellular protein involved in cell cycle, with mitochondrial subcellular localization. STRING interactions reveal PDCD2L forms ribosome biogenesis and methylation networks: PRMT3 (0.915, protein arginine methyltransferase 3 - highest confidence), RPS2 (0.769, small ribosomal subunit protein uS5), ITGB1BP2 (0.685, integrin beta-1-binding protein 2), GGN (0.666, gametogenetin), NAP1L5 (0.659, nucleosome assembly protein 1-like 5), SLX4IP (0.558, SLX4 interacting protein), ZNF277 (0.516, zinc finger protein 277), PNO1 (0.495, partner of NOB1 40S pre-ribosome assembly factor), MRPS5 (0.492, mitochondrial ribosomal protein S5), BYSL (0.409, bystin-like ribosome biogenesis factor). ClinVar contains 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 19q11-13.13 copy number loss, 19q11-13.2/19p13.11-q13.2/19q11-13.33/19p13.3-q13.43/19q12-13.12 copy number gains. Literature (27 papers) documents PDCD2L overexpression as unfavorable prognostic marker in gastric cancer with therapeutic potential upon inhibition, BTF3 transcriptionally upregulating PDCD2L to inactivate p53 signaling in hepatocellular carcinoma progression, and Drosophila orthologue Trus required for proper cell proliferation, larval developmental timing, and oogenesis with implications for ribosome biogenesis and cell cycle regulation. PDCD2L functions as a uS5 ribosomal assembly chaperone—the YwqG-like domain (aa14-227) structurally resembles bacterial ribosome assembly factors, while the C-terminal PDCD2 domain (aa247-350) mediates protein-protein interactions. The ultra-high confidence PRMT3 interaction (0.915) is mechanistically significant: PRMT3 methylates arginine residues on ribosomal proteins including RPS2, and PDCD2L likely coordinates this methylation during 40S subunit assembly. The RPS2 interaction (0.769) directly links PDCD2L to its substrate—RPS2/uS5 is a core component of the small ribosomal subunit. PNO1 (0.495) and BYSL (0.409) interactions place PDCD2L in the 40S pre-ribosome assembly pathway, while MRPS5 (0.492) connection suggests dual roles in both cytoplasmic and mitochondrial ribosome biogenesis. The mitochondrial localization from HPA supports this dual function. Cancer pathogenesis reflects PDCD2L dysregulation disrupting ribosome biogenesis checkpoint control—overexpression accelerates cell cycle progression through enhanced translation capacity, while BTF3-mediated upregulation inactivates p53 tumor suppressor signaling. The skeletal muscle-enriched expression (24.2 nTPM) may reflect high protein synthesis demands in muscle tissue. Copy number variants on 19q may cause PDCD2L dosage effects contributing to developmental abnormalities or cancer predisposition. The high confidence structure (pLDDT 75.4) reflects stable domain organization suitable for chaperone function in ribosome assembly.
