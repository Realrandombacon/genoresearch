## Description
LCMT1 (Q9UIC8) encodes a 334 amino acid S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)-dependent methyltransferase that functions as a critical regulator of protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) holoenzyme assembly by catalyzing methylation of the C-terminal leucine residue (Leu309) of the PP2A catalytic subunit alpha (PPP2CA), thereby controlling the recruitment of specific B regulatory subunits particularly B55α (PPP2R2A) to form active heterotrimeric phosphatase complexes that dephosphorylate key substrates including tau, Akt, and cell cycle regulators. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical methyltransferase fold built on the VP39 superfamily: a Vaccinia Virus protein VP39 homologous superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.150, positions 3-329) forming the core catalytic scaffold; a Methyltransferase Ppm1/Ppm2/Tcmp family domain (IPR007213/PF04072, positions 32-216) defining the substrate recognition module with methyltransferase activity (GO:0008168) and methylation function (GO:0032259); a Leucine carboxyl methyltransferase 1 family domain (IPR016651/PIRSF016305/PTHR13600, positions 1-333/1-333/1-325) confirming specificity for PP2A substrates; an S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase superfamily domain (IPR029063/SSF53335, positions 3-329/24-322) providing the SAM-binding pocket for methyl group transfer; and a prokaryotic membrane lipoprotein lipid attachment site profile (PS51257, positions 1-19) at the N-terminus potentially mediating membrane association. AlphaFold predicts a very high confidence structure (pLDDT 93.4) consistent with a well-folded enzyme where the SAM-binding pocket forms between the N-terminal and C-terminal lobes creating a deep cleft for substrate binding and catalysis typical of class I methyltransferases, suitable for small molecule inhibitor development. Literature (59 papers) defines LCMT1 as an emerging oncology and neurodegeneration target: low-dose benzo[a]pyrene exposure induces hepatic lipid deposition through LCMT1/PP2Ac-mediated autophagy inhibition linking environmental toxins to NAFLD pathogenesis via PP2A inactivation; LCMT1 loss and biased PP2A heterotrimerization drive prostate cancer progression and therapy resistance where reduced LCMT1 impairs B55α recruitment enhancing Akt signaling and survival; hepatic LCMT1 contributes to high fat diet-induced glucose intolerance through glycogen metabolism regulation connecting to type 2 diabetes; HIF-1α causes LCMT1/PP2A deficiency mediating tau hyperphosphorylation and cognitive dysfunction during chronic hypoxia establishing mechanism for hypoxia-induced Alzheimer's pathology; LCMT1 knockout mice show embryonic lethality confirming essential function. PPME1 partnership (score 0.920) creates reversible methylation cycle where PPME1 demethylates PPP2CA allowing dynamic regulation of PP2A holoenzyme composition in response to cellular signals; B55α (PPP2R2A) recruitment (score 0.425) is methylation-dependent where unmethylated PPP2CA preferentially binds B56 family subunits while methylated PPP2CA favors B55α creating functional specificity;
