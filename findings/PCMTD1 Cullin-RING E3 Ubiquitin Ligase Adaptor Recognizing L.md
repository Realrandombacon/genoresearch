# PCMTD1: Cullin-RING E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Adaptor Recognizing L-Isoaspartyl-Damaged Proteins for Ubiquitin-Mediated Degradation

## Description
PCMTD1 (Q96MG8) is a 357-aa protein-L-isoaspartate O-methyltransferase domain-containing protein with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 84.2), indicating a stable well-folded enzyme. InterPro analysis reveals protein-L-isoaspartate O-methyltransferase domain (IPR000682/PF01135: positions 1-223/10-221), S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase superfamily (IPR029063/SSF53335: positions 6-313/74-313), and Vaccinia virus VP39-like fold (G3DSA:3.40.50.150: positions 6-226), characteristic of SAM-dependent methyltransferases that recognize damaged protein substrates. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to nucleoplasm and plasma membrane. STRING interactions demonstrate high-confidence binding to PLEKHA7 (0.728), plus medium-confidence interactions with CUL5 (0.578), FAM102A (0.601), COL11A1 (0.596), C10orf53 (0.519), WDYHV1 (0.475), DPM2 (0.468), PUS7 (0.455), EPDR1 (0.451), ST18 (0.446). Literature (54 papers) establishes PCMTD1 as substrate adaptor for Cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligase complex (CRL5-PCMTD1) that recognizes proteins harboring spontaneous L-isoaspartyl damage - a major form of age-related protein damage where normal L-aspartyl residues isomerize to kinked L-isoaspartyl or D-aspartyl forms. Recent 2025-2026 cryoEM studies reveal structural basis for L-isoaspartyl-containing protein recognition by PCMTD1, while tumor suppressor studies show PCMTD1-127aa isoform suppresses osteosarcoma progression by competitively binding USP10 to promote c-MYC degradation. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 8p11.23-q24.3 and 3'UTR variant (c.*1943G>T), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 84.2) and CUL5 interaction suggest PCMTD1 functions as damage-sensing adaptor that recruits L-isoaspartyl-damaged proteins to CRL5 E3 ligase for ubiquitin-mediated proteasomal degradation, maintaining protein homeostasis and preventing accumulation of age-related protein damage implicated in cancer and neurodegeneration.
