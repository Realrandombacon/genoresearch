## Description
MMP17 (Q9ULZ9) encodes a 603 amino acid glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored membrane-type matrix metalloproteinase (MT4-MMP) that functions as a cell surface protease regulating extracellular matrix remodeling, growth factor shedding, and G-protein signaling pathways. InterPro analysis reveals a modular metalloproteinase architecture: an N-terminal peptidoglycan binding-like domain (IPR002477, positions 48-105) for substrate recognition, a catalytic metallopeptidase M10 domain (IPR001818/IPR006026, positions 132-295) containing the zinc-binding HEXXH motif essential for proteolytic activity, and a C-terminal hemopexin-like domain (IPR000585/IPR018487, positions 329-523) with four repeats mediating substrate specificity and protein-protein interactions, classified within the peptidase M10A family (IPR021190, positions 21-543). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 76.3, high confidence) consistent with stable multi-domain architecture required for membrane-anchored protease function. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with signaling and proteolysis machinery: GNAI1/2/3 (0.903 each), the inhibitory G-protein alpha subunits suggesting direct coupling to GPCR signaling; FURIN (0.773), the proprotein convertase activating pro-MMP17; HBEGF (0.586), the EGF receptor ligand shed by MMP17; TNF (0.532), the inflammatory cytokine; MMP14/MT1-MMP (0.505), the related membrane metalloproteinase; and TIMP1/2/3 (0.403-0.486), the tissue inhibitors regulating MMP activity. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with brain enrichment (49.9 nTPM), cancer-enriched expression profile, classified under hydrolase/metalloprotease/protease molecular functions with predicted intracellular and secreted localization. ClinVar contains 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning chromosome 12q24.21-24.33 regions (e.g., chr12:116422123-133777902 duplications, chr12:117533207-133777902 gains) and splice site variants (c.1052-15C>A), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (94 papers) demonstrates MMP17 serves as a lung adenocarcinoma biomarker identified via machine learning; CCZ1 accelerates cervical squamous cell carcinoma progression by promoting MMP2/MMP17 expression; MMP11/MMP17 are prognostic biomarkers for uterine corpus endometrial carcinoma; doxycycline inhibits malignant rhabdoid tumor of kidney progression by targeting MMP17/MMP1 through PI3K-Akt signaling; MMP17 variants associate with ischemic stroke risk in Chinese populations. The mechanistic hypothesis: MMP17 functions as a GPI-anchored cell surface protease that cleaves extracellular matrix components (collagen, gelatin) and sheds membrane-bound growth factors (HBEGF) to activate EGFR signaling promoting cell migration and invasion; direct interaction with GNAI1/2/3 suggests MMP17 modulates G-protein coupled receptor signaling independent of protease activity, potentially regulating chemotaxis and inflammatory responses; FURIN-mediated pro-domain cleavage activates MMP17 at the cell surface enabling localized ECM degradation during cancer metastasis and blood-brain barrier remodeling; TIMP inhibition provides spatial-temporal control preventing excessive tissue destruction; brain enrichment (49.9 nTPM) suggests roles in neuronal plasticity and blood-brain barrier integrity, with variants predisposing to ischemic stroke through altered cerebrovascular remodeling; copy number gains on 12q may drive oncogenic programs through elevated protease activity enhancing tumor cell invasion and growth factor release; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 76.3) indicates stable domain organization enabling precise substrate positioning within the catalytic cleft.
