## Description
ZNF652 encodes a 606 amino acid nuclear protein containing three canonical C2H2-type zinc finger domains (PF00096, IPR013087) positioned at residues 246-379, forming a Krueppel-type DNA-binding module (PTHR24393). The protein shows medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 56.0) with structured zinc finger regions and likely disordered N/C-terminal regions typical of transcriptional regulators. HPA localizes ZNF652 to nucleoplasm with low tissue specificity, expressed across many tissues. STRING interactions reveal functional partnership with transcriptional corepressor CBFA2T3 (score 0.827), suggesting ZNF652 recruits corepressor complexes to target genes. Additional partners include GUCD1 (0.606), PLCD3 (0.581), and chromatin-associated proteins. ClinVar documents 13 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 17q copy number losses/gains encompassing the ZNF652 locus, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease. Conservation: mouse (Q5DU09, 608 aa, ~95% identity) and rat (A1L1J6, 608 aa) orthologs confirm strong vertebrate conservation of zinc finger architecture. Functional hypothesis: ZNF652 acts as sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor, recruiting CBFA2T3/NCOR corepressor complexes to silence target genes involved in cell differentiation or proliferation. Dosage disruption via CNVs likely causes haploinsufficiency phenotypes. This represents a partially characterized dark gene—domain architecture is clear but specific DNA targets and pathway roles remain undefined.
