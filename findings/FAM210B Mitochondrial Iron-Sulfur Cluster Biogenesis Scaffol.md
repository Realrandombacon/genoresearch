## Description
FAM210B (UniProt Q96KR6) encodes a 192 amino acid mitochondrial protein (chromosome 20q13.2) with conserved FAM210A/B-like structural signatures. InterPro analysis reveals membership in the FAM210A/B-like family (IPR045866, residues 51-190; PTHR21377, residues 51-190) with a conserved FAM210A/B-like domain (IPR009688, residues 87-174; PF06916, residues 87-174) characteristic of mitochondrial inner membrane-associated scaffold proteins. STRING interactions show medium-confidence associations with mitochondrial heme biosynthesis and iron-sulfur cluster pathways: FECH (0.628, ferrochelatase, terminal enzyme in heme biosynthesis), DMTN (0.610, dematin actin-binding protein, erythrocyte membrane), SLC25A39 (0.547, mitochondrial glutathione transporter), EPB42 (0.515, erythrocyte membrane protein band 4.2), STRADB (0.491, STE20-related kinase adaptor beta), CHST4 (0.467, carbohydrate sulfotransferase 4), UROD (0.420, uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase, heme biosynthesis), TRIM58 (0.416, erythroid differentiation E3 ubiquitin ligase), BLVRB (0.413, biliverdin reductase B, heme catabolism), and TSPAN5 (0.406, tetraspanin). ClinVar contains 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses on 20q13.2-13.33 spanning the FAM210B locus, indicating dosage sensitivity. AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 67.4) across the 192 aa sequence, consistent with partially structured mitochondrial protein architecture containing both ordered domains and flexible regions. Conservation data shows strong vertebrate conservation: mouse ortholog (190 aa, ~99% identity). The convergence of mitochondrial localization, FAM210A/B-like domain, interaction with heme biosynthesis enzymes (FECH, UROD), mitochondrial transporter SLC25A39, erythrocyte membrane proteins (DMTN, EPB42, TRIM58), and 20q13.2 CNV pathogenicity suggests FAM210B functions as a mitochondrial inner membrane scaffold protein that facilitates iron-sulfur cluster biogenesis and heme biosynthesis by recruiting ferrochelatase and associated enzymes to specific mitochondrial subdomains. Its interaction network places FAM210B within the mitochondrial heme/iron metabolic pathway; 20q13.2 CNV pathogenicity implicates FAM210B haploinsufficiency in porphyria-like disorders or erythroid differentiation defects.
