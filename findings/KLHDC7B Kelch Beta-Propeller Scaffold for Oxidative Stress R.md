## Description
KLHDC7B encodes a 594 aa Kelch domain-containing protein (UniProt Q96G42) functioning as a substrate recognition scaffold in oxidative stress response and auditory maintenance pathways. The protein contains a BACK domain (cd18484, aa218-294) characteristic of BTB-Kelch family proteins, plus a C-terminal Kelch-type beta-propeller (IPR015915/G3DSA:2.120.10.80, aa316-563) with four Kelch repeat motifs (IPR006652/PF01344/SM00612, aa344-493) forming a circularized beta-sheet platform for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 69.0), indicating a structured Kelch beta-propeller with flexible N-terminal regions typical of substrate adaptors. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with parathyroid gland enrichment (12.8 nTPM), lymphoid tissue expression (4.8 nTPM), and plasma membrane localization. STRING interactions are sparse (CLRN2 0.490, OR4F21 0.416, ZNF487 0.400), suggesting KLHDC7B functions in specialized contexts rather than stable complexes. ClinVar contains 97 pathogenic variants, predominantly 22q13.33 copy number losses overlapping the Phelan-McDermid syndrome region, though KLHDC7B-specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (37 papers) reveals emerging roles: GWAS-identified candidate for age-related hearing loss essential for mammalian auditory function, UVB phototherapy target in psoriasis regulating CHOP and KEAP1/NRF2 oxidative stress pathway, SLE susceptibility gene in epidermal keratinocytes, and lncRNA KLHDC7B-DT upregulation in head/neck cancers. KLHDC7B likely functions as a Kelch-family substrate adaptor analogous to KEAP1, using its beta-propeller to recruit specific cargo proteins (potentially transcription factors or ion channels) for ubiquitination or subcellular trafficking. The hearing loss phenotype suggests roles in cochlear hair cell maintenance through oxidative stress management or mechanotransduction complex regulation. 22q13.33 deletions may contribute to Phelan-McDermid syndrome features through KLHDC7B haploinsufficiency affecting auditory and immune homeostasis.
