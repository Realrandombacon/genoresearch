However, ClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains/duplications involving the 5p13.2-5p11 region: variant 4796452 (5p13.3-11 ~15.4 Mb trisomy), variant 2683745 (5p13.3-12 ~16.6 Mb trisomy), variant 149824 (5p15.33-q13.3 ~74.4 Mb trisomy), variant 148227 (5p13.2-q12.1 ~26.7 Mb trisomy), variant 146130 (5p15.33-11 ~46.1 Mb trisomy), variant 58093 (5p13.2-q11.1 ~14.7 Mb trisomy), variant 58092 (5p13.2-12 ~9.6 Mb trisomy), and variant 58067 (5p15.33-12 ~45.6 Mb trisomy). The 5p12-5p13 chromosomal region contains critical developmental, growth factor signaling, and hematologic-associated genes including FGFR1 (fibroblast growth factor receptor 1, tyrosine kinase, Kallmann syndrome, Pfeiffer syndrome, FGF signaling), TGFBI (transforming growth factor beta induced, extracellular matrix protein, corneal dystrophy, cell adhesion), EGR1 (early growth response 1, zinc finger transcription factor, immediate early gene, cell proliferation, differentiation), and CTNNA1 (catenin alpha 1, adherens junction protein, cell-cell adhesion, lissencephaly). Functional hypothesis: LOC100132356 likely represents a critical 5p12 growth/developmental regulatory protein, potentially functioning as a secreted extracellular matrix protein or transmembrane adaptor coordinating FGFR1-TGFBI-EGR1-CTNNA1 expression in FGF signaling, cell adhesion, and immediate early transcriptional response (mesenchymal cells, osteoblasts, neural crest derivatives, hematopoietic progenitors) or as a non-coding RNA precursor regulating FGFR1 splicing or stability. The 8 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications 9.6-74.4 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number dysregulates FGFR1-mediated FGF signaling and CTNNA1-mediated cell adhesion. Disease mechanism involves FGFR1 overexpression causing craniosynostosis syndromes (Pfeiffer, Apert, Crousy; suture fusion, midface hypoplasia, limb anomalies), FGFR1 loss causing Kallmann syndrome (anosmia, hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, GnRH neuron migration defect), TGFBI mutation causing corneal dystrophy (amyloid deposition, visual impairment, recurrent erosion), EGR1 overexpression causing cell proliferation excess (growth factor signaling hyperactivation, cancer progression, leukemia), EGR1 loss causing differentiation defect (impaired myeloid differentiation, hematologic malignancy), CTNNA1 mutation causing lissencephaly or cardiomyopathy (adherens junction defect, neuronal migration abnormality, heart failure), and 5p13 duplication syndrome (tetrasomy 5p) presenting with craniofacial dysmorphism, intellectual disability, growth retardation, skeletal anomalies, and congenital heart defects. The 5p12-5p13 region is associated with growth factor-craniofacial-hematologic genetic variation. The uncharacterized protein-coding classification combined with ClinVar evidence (8 duplications 9.6-74.4 Mb) and FGFR1-TGFBI-EGR1-CTNNA1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a secreted/transmembrane protein critical for FGF signaling-cell adhesion-immediate early transcription coordination.
