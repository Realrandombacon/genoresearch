## Description
LOC90246 (Gene ID: 90246) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 3q21.3 (NC_000003.12, positions 128507834-128510594, ~2.8 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 3 copy number losses/deletions (variant 154720: 3q21.3-22.1 ~6.0 Mb; variant 150138: 3q13.2-21.3 ~16.1 Mb; variant 148370: 3q21.3 ~2.1 Mb) and 2 copy number gains/duplications (variant 154258: 3q11.1-24 ~51.9 Mb; variant 145614: 3q13.11-29 ~94.7 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 3q21.3 chromosomal region contains critical hematopoiesis, DNA replication, and endosomal trafficking-associated genes including GATA2 (GATA binding protein 2, C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor, hematopoietic stem cell maintenance, megakaryocyte/erythroid differentiation, GATA2 deficiency syndrome, myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukemia), MCM2 (minichromosome maintenance complex component 2, DNA replication licensing factor, pre-replication complex, S-phase entry, cancer proliferation marker), RAB7A (RAB7A member RAS oncogene family, small GTPase, late endosome-lysosome trafficking, autophagy, Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 2B neuropathy), RUVBL1 (RuvB like AAA ATPase 1, chromatin remodeling ATPase, transcriptional co-activation, telomerase assembly, cancer epigenetics), and RPN1 (ribophorin I, endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein, N-glycosylation complex, protein folding, quality control). Functional hypothesis: LOC90246 likely represents a critical 3q21.3 enhancer element within the GATA2-MCM2 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a hematopoietic stem cell-specific enhancer coordinating GATA2-MCM2-RAB7A expression in myeloid/erythroid progenitors or as a chromatin boundary separating hematopoietic transcription factor genes (GATA2) from DNA replication genes (MCM2) and endosomal trafficking genes (RAB7A). The 5 pathogenic CNVs (mix of deletions and duplications, 2.1-94.7 Mb range) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts GATA2-mediated hematopoietic transcription and MCM2-mediated DNA replication licensing. Disease mechanism involves GATA2 haploinsufficiency causing GATA2 deficiency syndrome (monocytopenia, mycobacterial/fungal susceptibility, lymphedema, deafness, MDS/AML predisposition), GATA2 overexpression causing acute megakaryoblastic leukemia (transient myeloproliferative disorder, Down syndrome-associated AMKL), MCM2 overexpression causing cancer (replication hyperactivation, uncontrolled proliferation, poor prognosis), MCM2 deficiency causing replication stress (fork stalling, DNA damage, genomic instability), RAB7A mutation causing Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 2B (peripheral neuropathy, axonal degeneration, endosomal trafficking defect, ulceration, amputation), RUVBL1 dysregulation causing cancer epigenetics (chromatin remodeling defect, transcriptional dysregulation, telomerase activation), RPN1 dysfunction causing protein folding defects (N-glycosylation impairment, ER stress, unfolded protein response), and 3q21.3 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with hematologic abnormalities and cancer predisposition. The 3q21.3 region is a well-established GATA2 locus with germline mutations causing familial MDS/AML. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (5 CNVs 2.1-94.7 Mb) and GATA2-MCM2-RAB7A-RUVBL1-RPN1 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a hematopoietic-specific enhancer critical for hematopoiesis-DNA replication-endosomal trafficking coordination.
