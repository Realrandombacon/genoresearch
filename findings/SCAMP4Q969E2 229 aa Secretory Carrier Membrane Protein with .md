## Description
SCAMP4 (Secretory Carrier-Associated Membrane Protein 4, UniProt Q969E2) is a 229 aa membrane trafficking protein representing a genuine dark gene with only 9 literature references despite clinical significance. Structural analysis reveals canonical SCAMP family architecture: N-terminal cytoplasmic domain with potential phosphorylation sites, central SCAMP domain (aa 3-202, IPR007273/PF04144/PTHR10687) forming the conserved core for membrane association, and C-terminal region for protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT=86.2) consistent with well-folded membrane-associated protein containing multiple transmembrane helices characteristic of secretory carrier proteins. STRING interactions place SCAMP4 within membrane trafficking and glycosylation networks: SH3GL1/Endophilin-A1 (0.541, endocytic vesicle formation), FAM174B (0.477, uncharacterized membrane protein), POMT2 (0.459, protein O-mannosyltransferase), REEP5 (0.459, reticulon-like ER shaping protein), GDPD5 (0.453, glycerophosphodiesterase), ADAT3 (0.446, tRNA deaminase), XXYLT1 (0.444, xylosyltransferase), REXO1 (0.421, RNA exonuclease), and DUS3L (0.412, tRNA dihydrouridine synthase) — suggesting SCAMP4 functions in coordinated membrane trafficking with glycosylation enzymes and vesicle formation machinery. HPA expression confirms ubiquitous distribution: detected in all tissues with low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, and subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus, vesicles, lipid droplets, and plasma membrane consistent with secretory carrier function shuttling between compartments. ClinVar identifies 49 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains (19p13.3, chr19:260912-2934171)x3 and multiple single nucleotide variants, though specific SCAMP4-associated disease phenotypes remain undefined — variants occur in 19p13.3 region affecting multiple genes including ADAT3. Literature establishes SCAMP4 function in: (1) senescent cell secretome enhancement — SCAMP4 highly abundant on senescent cell surface regulating SASP factor secretion (47 citations), (2) glioma prognostic marker — SCAMP4 expression correlated with tumor progression and immune infiltration (18 citations). Functional hypothesis: SCAMP4 acts as secretory carrier membrane protein facilitating vesicular transport of cargo proteins between Golgi, plasma membrane, and lipid droplets, potentially serving as scaffold for coordinating glycosylation enzymes (POMT2, XXYLT1) with vesicle formation machinery (SH3GL1, REEP5). The lipid droplet localization suggests additional role in lipid metabolism or lipoprotein secretion. Disease mechanism: SCAMP4 dysregulation may contribute to neurodevelopmental disorders via 19p13.3 copy number variants, cancer progression through altered secretory pathway affecting tumor microenvironment (glioma immune infiltration), and cellular senescence through SASP modulation. Priority experiments: (a) identify SCAMP4 cargo proteins via immunoprecipitation-MS, (b) characterize 19p13.3 CNV patient phenotypes for SCAMP4-specific contributions, (c) test SCAMP4 role in lipid droplet dynamics via live-cell imaging, (d) validate glioma biomarker potential in independent cohorts. SCAMP4 exemplifies 'dark trafficking protein' where domain architecture and expression pattern are defined but molecular mechanism, cargo specificity, and disease associations remain unexplored despite 49 ClinVar variants.
