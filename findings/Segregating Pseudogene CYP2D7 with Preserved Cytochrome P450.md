# Segregating Pseudogene CYP2D7 with Preserved Cytochrome P450 Structure and Population-Dependent Function

**Date:** 2026-06-29T00:54:53.841849

**Quality Score:** 7/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
CYP2D7 encodes a 515 amino acid cytochrome P450 protein (UniProt A0A087X1C5) classified as a 'segregating pseudogene' — meaning some individuals carry functional alleles while others carry non-functional variants. This creates population-level polymorphism in drug metabolism capacity beyond the well-characterized CYP2D6.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals canonical cytochrome P450 domains: PF00067 (cytochrome P450 core), IPR001128 (P450 family), IPR002401 (E-class group I), IPR008069 (CYP2D-like subgroup), and IPR017972 (conserved P450 site at positions 454-463). The heme-binding cysteine motif is preserved, suggesting potential monooxygenase activity in functional alleles.\n\nAlphaFold predicts a highly confident 3D structure (pLDDT 91.0) with the characteristic P450 fold (G3DSA:1.10.630.10), indicating the protein is well-folded when expressed. However, HPA expression data could not resolve CYP2D7, suggesting low or tissue-restricted expression compared to CYP2D6.\n\nClinVar reports 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number variations (gains/losses) spanning 22q13.2, though no specific disease associations are established. These CNVs likely affect the CYP2D6-CYP2D7-CYP2D8 locus complexity.\n\nWith only 72 publications (vs 12,823 for CYP2D6), CYP2D7 remains understudied despite its clinical relevance. Literature focuses on genotyping interference and structural variation rather than intrinsic function. The gene likely contributes to interindividual variability in xenobiotic metabolism, particularly when CYP2D6 is deleted or duplicated.
