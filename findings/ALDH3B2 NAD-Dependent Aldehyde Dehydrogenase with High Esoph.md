## Description

Functional hypothesis: ALDH3B2 functions as a detoxification enzyme metabolizing toxic aldehydes (potentially lipid peroxidation products, alcohol-derived acetaldehyde) with tissue-specific roles in esophageal epithelium protection; cancer-enhanced expression suggests tumor cells exploit ALDH3B2 for aldehyde detoxification and redox homeostasis promoting survival; pancreatic duct expression indicates role in exocrine cell metabolism with loss triggering beta-cell neogenesis. Dosage sensitivity (14 CNVs) suggests haploinsufficiency could impair detoxification capacity, potentially contributing to cancer susceptibility or metabolic disorders. This gene represents a moderately characterized metabolic enzyme with emerging roles in cancer biology and regenerative medicine.
