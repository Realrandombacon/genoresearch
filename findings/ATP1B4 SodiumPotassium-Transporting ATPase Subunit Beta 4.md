## Description
ATP1B4 encodes a subunit of the sodium/potassium-transporting ATPase complex, 
                    which plays a crucial role in maintaining the resting potential and 
                    regulating the balance of sodium and potassium ions across cell membranes. 
                    The protein is highly expressed in skeletal muscle and has been 
                    associated with various diseases through ClinVar variants.
