# IL17RC: SEFIR Domain-Containing Receptor Mediating IL-17A/F Signaling in Mucocutaneous Immunity, Ankylosing Spondylitis, and Allergic Inflammation

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
IL17RC (Q8NAC3, 791 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 73.6) type I transmembrane receptor functioning as an essential component of the IL-17 signaling pathway through heterodimerization with IL17RA to mediate responses to both IL-17A and IL-17F cytokines. InterPro analysis reveals a characteristic architecture with an N-terminal IL-17 receptor extracellular ligand-binding region (IPR027841/PF15037, residues 142-530) enabling cytokine recognition, followed by a C-terminal SEFIR domain (IPR013568/PF08357, residues 583-737) mediating homotypic interactions with adaptor protein TRAF3IP2 for downstream NF-κB and MAPK pathway activation, classifying it within the IL-17 receptor-like family (IPR039465/PTHR15583). HPA data confirms expression across many tissues with low tissue specificity but cell type-enhanced patterns, consistent with broad roles in inflammatory responses and mucosal immunity. STRING interactions define an exceptionally high-confidence IL-17 signaling network linking IL17RC to cognate ligands IL17F (0.999) and IL17A (0.999), obligate heterodimerization partner IL17RA (0.998), related receptors IL17RD (0.988), IL17RE (0.957), and IL17RB (0.755), cytokine IL25 (0.908), and critical adaptor TRAF3IP2 (0.865), positioning it as a central node in Th17-mediated immunity. Literature (112 papers) establishes clinically relevant mechanistic insights: novel duplication variants cause isolated chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis (CMC) through impaired IL-17A/F-responsive pathway function without other significant manifestations; missense variants identified in Chinese families link IL17RC dysfunction to multiple osteochondromas and ankylosing spondylitis susceptibility; IL17RC-IL33-NF-κB signaling axis drives pediatric allergic rhinitis pathogenesis; and evolutionary studies reveal poly(I:C)-induced expression changes and m6A modifications regulating il17ra/il17rc in teleost fish immune responses. ClinVar documents 75 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg401Ter), frameshifts (p.Gln443fs), splice site variants, and large copy number losses/gains on chromosome 3p regions, strongly implicating IL17RC haploinsufficiency in primary immunodeficiency phenotypes particularly CMC. The convergence of SEFIR-mediated TRAF3IP2 recruitment, IL-17A/F dual specificity, mucosal epithelial expression patterns, and monogenic CMC associations suggests IL17RC functions as a non-redundant gatekeeper of antifungal immunity at barrier surfaces, with therapeutic implications for biologics targeting the IL-17 pathway in psoriasis, ankylosing spondylitis, and chronic fungal infections.
