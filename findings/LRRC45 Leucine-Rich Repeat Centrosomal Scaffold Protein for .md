# LRRC45: Leucine-Rich Repeat Centrosomal Scaffold Protein for Ciliogenesis

**Date:** 2026-03-20T14:07:28.409150

**Quality Score:** 3.32/10  (E=6.35, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
LRRC45 (Leucine-Rich Repeat-Containing Protein 45) is a 670 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein (UniProt Q96CN5) that contains multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains (IPR001611, PF13516) at positions 85-106, 146-165, and 171-193, characteristic of protein-protein interaction modules. The protein belongs to the centrosome and cilium assembly protein family (IPR052116, PTHR23170) and localizes to the centrosome, basal body, plasma membrane, cytosol, and sperm mid piece according to HPA data.

STRING interaction analysis reveals LRRC45 forms a high-confidence centrosomal complex with CROCC (rootletin, score 0.981), CNTLN (centolin, score 0.923), CEP250 (C-NAP1, score 0.834), and CDK5RAP2 (score 0.767), all established centrosome linker proteins critical for centrosome cohesion and ciliogenesis. Additional partners include KXD1 (0.743), FBF1 (0.709, a ciliary transition zone protein), and CEP135/CEP131/CEP89 (0.42-0.48, centriolar satellite proteins). This interaction network positions LRRC45 as a structural scaffold bridging centrosome linker complexes to ciliary assembly machinery.

ClinVar contains 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1402-2A>G), intronic deletions (c.834-20_834-7del), and large chromosomal rearrangements on chromosome 17q, suggesting LRRC45 dysfunction contributes to human disease, potentially ciliopathy or centrosome-related disorders. Orthologs exist in mouse (Q8CIM1, 670 aa, 100% identity), indicating strong evolutionary conservation.

Mechanistic hypothesis: LRRC45 uses its LRR domains to mediate protein-protein interactions at the centrosome, recruiting ciliary vesicle components and transition zone proteins (FBF1) during ciliogenesis initiation. The LRR superfamily (IPR032675) typically forms curved solenoid structures that serve as protein binding platforms. LRRC45 may stabilize the CROCC-CNTLN-CEP250 centrosome linker complex while simultaneously recruiting distal appendage proteins required for ciliary membrane docking, with dysfunction leading to defective ciliogenesis and human ciliopathy phenotypes.
