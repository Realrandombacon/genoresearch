## Description
LOC100128993 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 8p22-p21.3 (NC_000008.11, positions 19245521-19183673, ~62 kb region) with exceptional clinical significance despite lacking protein-coding features. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000253557 but no expression data was available. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 42 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (highest count among analyzed loci), all representing large-scale copy number variations spanning the 8p23.3-21.2 region (variants include 2579290, 2579268, 161037, 161033, 161019, 155441, 154791, 154680, 154449, 153413). These CNVs include both deletions (copy number loss, hemizygous) and duplications (copy number gain, trisomy) ranging from focal alterations (~2-5 Mb) to whole-arm changes (145+ Mb). The 8p21-22 chromosomal location is clinically notable as this region contains multiple tumor suppressor genes and is frequently deleted in various cancers (prostate, breast, lung, hepatocellular carcinoma). The region contains multiple dosage-sensitive genes including WRN (Werner syndrome RecQ helicase-like, DNA repair, premature aging, genomic stability), CSF1R (colony stimulating factor 1 receptor, macrophage differentiation, oncogenic fusion in myeloid neoplasms), MSR1 (macrophage scavenger receptor 1, innate immunity, atherosclerosis), DUSP4 (dual specificity phosphatase 4, MAPK pathway regulation, tumor suppressor), ADCY8 (adenylate cyclase 8, cAMP signaling), and CHRNA2 (cholinergic receptor nicotinic alpha 2, neuronal signaling). Functional hypothesis: LOC100128993 likely represents a cis-regulatory element or chromatin boundary controlling expression of neighboring tumor suppressor and DNA repair genes in the 8p21-22 critical region, particularly WRN which is essential for genomic stability through RecQ helicase activity and homologous recombination repair. The 42 pathogenic CNVs indicate extreme dosage sensitivity where copy number alterations disrupt normal transcriptional regulation of DNA repair and tumor suppressor genes. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency of WRN (leading to Werner syndrome-like premature aging and cancer predisposition through defective DNA repair), dysregulation of CSF1R (affecting macrophage differentiation and myeloid oncogenesis), or altered DUSP4 expression affecting MAPK signaling and tumor suppression. The absence of protein-coding features combined with exceptional ClinVar evidence (42 variants, highest among analyzed loci) and oncological/DNA repair disease association suggests this locus functions as a master chromatin regulatory hub controlling genomic stability and tumor suppression through long-range gene regulation.
