## Description
ARID4A (AT-Rich Interactive Domain-Containing Protein 4A, UniProt P29374) is a 1257 amino acid nucleoplasmic chromatin regulator with moderate characterization (57 papers) that functions as a core component of the SIN3/HDAC transcriptional repressor complex involved in chromatin remodeling, transcriptional silencing, and mRNA stability regulation. The protein contains an ARID DNA-binding domain (IPR001606/G3DSA:1.10.150.60, residues 309-402/290-410), two Tudor domains (IPR002999/cd20390/cd20459, residues 58-114/4-61/62-114) for protein-protein interactions, a chromo/chromo shadow domain (IPR000953/IPR016197, residues 588-640/564-669) for chromatin binding, a PWWP domain (IPR012603, residues 170-262) for histone recognition, and belongs to the ARID4A/B family with RNA binding capability (IPR025995). STRING interactions reveal maximum confidence partnerships with SIN3/HDAC complex components: BRMS1 (0.999, breast cancer metastasis suppressor), SIN3A (0.995, scaffold protein), SIN3B (0.990), SUDS3 (0.987), SAP30 (0.986), HDAC1 (0.986, histone deacetylase), ING1 (0.981, inhibitor of growth), ING2 (0.725), BRMS1L (0.667), and RB1 (0.480, retinoblastoma protein), placing ARID4A in the SIN3-HDAC transcriptional repression machinery. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue and cancer specificity, nucleoplasmic and plasma membrane localization, with biological processes including differentiation, spermatogenesis, transcription, and transcription regulation. ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses at 14q22.3-23.2, copy number gains at 14q22.3-24.1/14q11.2-23.1, and splice site variants (c.3212-27A>T, c.906+53A>T), though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential neuropsychiatric, reproductive, or cancer phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 56.3) indicates medium confidence with partially structured domains and extensive disordered regions typical of chromatin regulators. Functional hypothesis: ARID4A acts as a dual-function chromatin regulator and mRNA stability factor through its multi-domain architecture, where the ARID domain binds AT-rich DNA sequences recruiting the SIN3/HDAC complex (SIN3A/B-SUDS3-SAP30-HDAC1) for histone deacetylation and transcriptional silencing, Tudor domains recognize methylated histone marks (H3K4me3, H4K20me) for chromatin targeting, and the PWWP domain binds H3K36me3 for elongation-coupled repression, with recent 2025 research demonstrating ARID4A suppresses breast tumor metastasis by enhancing MTSS1 mRNA stability (post-transcriptional regulation beyond chromatin), 2022 studies identifying heterozygous missense variants in Han Chinese families with schizophrenia that interfere with DNA-binding activity (neuropsychiatric risk), 2018-2022 research linking ARID4A polymorphisms to sperm quality and male fertility through Sertoli cell function regulation, and 2018 work showing miR-30d-mediated ARID4A downregulation promotes prostate cancer progression, suggesting ARID4A functions as a context-dependent tumor suppressor (breast, prostate) and neurodevelopmental regulator with 14q copy number variants potentially contributing to schizophrenia, infertility, or developmental disorders through chromatin remodeling defects and mRNA stability dysregulation, making ARID4A a potential biomarker for metastatic breast cancer risk and therapeutic target for SIN3/HDAC-dependent malignancies.
