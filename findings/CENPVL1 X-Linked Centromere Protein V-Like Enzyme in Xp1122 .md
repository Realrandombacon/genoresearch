## Description
CENPVL1 (Centromere Protein V Like 1, Gene ID: 389857) is a very dark gene with only 2 publications on Semantic Scholar, representing an understudied X-linked protein located at Xp11.22. The gene encodes a 272 amino acid centromere protein V-like enzyme (UniProt A0A0U1RR11) with preserved catalytic domain architecture. InterPro analysis reveals extensive CENP-V/GFA family domains: IPR006913 (CENP-V/GFA domain, positions 133-246), PF04828 (Glutathione-dependent formaldehyde-activating enzyme, 133-228), PS51891 (CENP-V/GFA domain profile, 133-246), PTHR28620 (Centromere protein V-like family, 94-257), and IPR011057 (Mss4-like superfamily, 131-228). GO annotations include carbon-sulfur lyase activity (GO:0016846) and metal ion binding. AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (global pLDDT 69.9), indicating a partially folded protein with both ordered catalytic domains and flexible regions. STRING interactions reveal 7 medium-confidence partners including GSPT2 (0.662), SORD (0.595), MAGED1 (0.516), FAM120C (0.480), SHROOM4 (0.472), TSPYL2 (0.418), and IQSEC2 (0.416) - notably, GSPT2 and MAGED1 are co-deleted in Xp11.22 deletion syndrome. HPA shows brain-enriched expression at 3.7 nTPM with tissue-specific distribution. ClinVar contains 81 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly X chromosome CNVs including deletions, duplications, and copy number gains/losses spanning Xp11.22 and Xp22.33-q28), with literature linking CENPVL1 deletions to syndromic X-linked intellectual disability. Two publications (2017, 2019) describe Xp11.22 deletions encompassing CENPVL1, CENPVL2, MAGED1, and GSPT2 as causes of intellectual disability with developmental delays. CENPVL1's preserved CENP-V/GFA domain structure (homologous to glutathione-dependent formaldehyde-activating enzymes) combined with brain expression and 81 ClinVar variants suggests it may function as: (1) a metabolic enzyme processing formaldehyde or related aldehydes in neuronal tissue, (2) a centromere-associated protein regulating chromosome segregation during neurodevelopment, (3) a dosage-sensitive gene where hemizygous deletions in males contribute to X-linked intellectual disability syndrome alongside co-deleted MAGED1 and GSPT2, or (4) a metal-dependent carbon-sulfur lyase involved in neuronal detoxification pathways. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 69.9) supports partial enzymatic functionality. The STRING interaction network with GSPT2 and MAGED1 (both in the Xp11.22 deletion syndrome critical region) suggests these proteins may function in a coordinated pathway affecting neuronal development. The 81 ClinVar CNVs and 2 literature reports warrant functional investigation for X-linked intellectual disability mechanisms, particularly given the brain-enriched expression pattern.
