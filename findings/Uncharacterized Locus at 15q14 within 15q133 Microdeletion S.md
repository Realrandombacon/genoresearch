# Uncharacterized Locus at 15q14 within 15q13.3 Microdeletion Syndrome Critical Region

## Description
LOC105370941 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 15q14 (NC_000015.10:39782905-39785616) spanning approximately 2.7 kb. No UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000261136 but found no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 1 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variant: a large copy number loss (deletion) at chr15:32635803-40233825 spanning ~7.6 Mb. This deletion encompasses the 15q13.3-15.1 region, which is a well-established microdeletion syndrome critical region. The 15q13.3 microdeletion syndrome is associated with epilepsy, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, schizophrenia, and dysmorphic features. The CHRNA7 gene (cholinergic receptor nicotinic alpha 7 subunit) within this region is considered the primary dosage-sensitive gene driving the neuropsychiatric phenotype. LOC105370941 likely functions as part of the CHRNA7 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose deletion contributes to 15q13.3 microdeletion syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin architecture or long-range gene regulation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with location within a well-characterized dosage-sensitive region suggests this locus may participate in neuronal development and synaptic function through non-coding mechanisms.
