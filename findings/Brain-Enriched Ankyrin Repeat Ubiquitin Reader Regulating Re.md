## Description
ANKRD13B encodes a 626 amino acid ankyrin repeat domain protein localized to vesicles, plasma membrane, and perinuclear cytoplasm. The protein contains three N-terminal ankyrin repeats (PF12796, IPR002110, residues 18-103) mediating protein-protein interactions, followed by a conserved ANKRD13 C-terminal domain (PF11904, residues 163-490) of unknown function. Two ubiquitin-interacting motifs (UIMs, IPR003903, PS50330, residues 503-522 and 585-604) enable binding to ubiquitin-modified substrates, consistent with its annotated role as a ubiquitin-modified protein reader. HPA data shows brain-enriched expression (44.3 nTPM) with vesicular and plasma membrane localization, suggesting neuronal endosomal function. STRING interactions include GNAI3 (0.545), a G-protein alpha subunit involved in receptor signaling, and TMEM25 (0.533), a transmembrane protein. Interaction with UBA52 (0.487), a ubiquitin-ribosomal protein fusion, supports ubiquitin pathway involvement. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and deletions at 17q11.2, implicating ANKRD13B dosage in disease. The combination of ankyrin repeats (scaffolding), UIMs (ubiquitin binding), and endosomal localization suggests ANKRD13B functions as an adaptor protein that recognizes ubiquitinated membrane receptors, recruiting them to endosomal compartments for sorting or degradation. This positions ANKRD13B as a negative regulator of receptor internalization, potentially controlling GPCR and growth factor receptor signaling duration in neuronal tissues.
