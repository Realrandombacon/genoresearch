## Description
CKMT1A (Creatine kinase U-type, mitochondrial 1A, also known as sarcomeric mitochondrial creatine kinase) is a 417 aa mitochondrial enzyme representing a genuine dark gene with only 19 literature references despite essential roles in cellular energy homeostasis and cancer metabolism. Multi-omics data reveals CKMT1A as a highly conserved phosphotransferase catalyzing reversible transfer of phosphate between ATP and creatine to generate phosphocreatine (PCr), the primary energy buffer in high-demand tissues. InterPro analysis confirms a bipartite domain architecture characteristic of guanidino kinases: an N-terminal ATP:guanido phosphotransferase domain (G3DSA:1.10.135.10/IPR022413, aa 23-132) binding ATP, and a C-terminal catalytic domain (G3DSA:3.30.590.10/IPR014746/IPR022414, aa 139-417) containing the active site (IPR022415, aa 316-322). The full phosphagen kinase fold (cd00716/IPR000749, aa 49-406) enables substrate binding and catalysis. AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 89.3), indicating a well-folded dimeric enzyme typical of mitochondrial creatine kinases. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with esophagus (82.6 nTPM), brain (73.9 nTPM), and intestine (70.3 nTPM) as top tissues, consistent with high energy demand. STRING interactions reveal a functionally coherent metabolic network: CKMT1A associates with lactate dehydrogenases LDHAL6A (0.741) and LDHAL6B (0.740), LDHC (0.725), paralog CKMT2 (0.696), cytosolic paralog CKMT1B (0.634), aminotransferase GOT1L1 (0.633), mitochondrial AAA+ ATPase CLPB (0.623), and creatine biosynthesis enzymes GAMT (0.601) and GATM (0.584). ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 15q15.2-21.2 copy number alterations (losses: chr15:42850434-49592633x1, chr15:43759773-53252240x1, chr15:43868571-44014518x0; gains: chr15:22770421-50347130x3, chr15:22770422-102429112x3), suggesting CKMT1A dosage sensitivity. Recent literature (2018-2024) identifies CKMT1A as: (1) A target of the lncRNA n335586/miR-924 axis promoting hepatocellular carcinoma migration and invasion; (2) A prognostic biomarker in endometrial cancer; (3) A pan-cancer oncogenic driver with metabolic reprogramming roles. The scarcity of mechanistic literature (19 papers) despite clear enzymatic function makes CKMT1A a paradigmatic dark gene awaiting comprehensive characterization.
