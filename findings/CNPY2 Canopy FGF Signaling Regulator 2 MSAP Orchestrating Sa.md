## Description
CNPY2 (Canopy FGF Signaling Regulator 2, also known as MSAP/MIR-interacting Saposin-like Protein) is a 182 amino acid secreted/endoplasmic reticulum protein that functions as a critical regulator of FGF-MAPK signaling modulation, lipid binding and trafficking, and endoplasmic reticulum stress response through its saposin-like structural fold enabling lipid interaction and chaperone activity for proper folding of growth factor receptors and membrane proteins in metabolically active and proliferating tissues. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic canopy/saposin-like architecture including Saposin B type domain (IPR008139, residues 24-175) forming the lipid-binding core with four alpha-helices arranged in saposin fold for membrane interaction and glycolipid binding capability, Domain of Unknown Function DUF3456 (IPR021852, residues 27-171) defining conserved region of unknown function now linked to TLR4 regulation, Protein canopy homolog family (IPR042415, PTHR13341, residues 1-182) establishing evolutionary relationship to FGF signaling modulators across vertebrates, TLR4 regulator and MIR-interacting MSAP Pfam family (PF11938, residues 27-171) linking to Toll-like receptor 4 pathway regulation and MIR protein interaction for ER quality control, and Saposin-like superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.225.10, residues 23-95/137-180) providing structural homology to lipid-binding proteins including saposins A-D and granulysin. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9Y2B0-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 84.9) across the 182 residue modeled sequence, indicating a well-folded stable saposin-like fold with ordered alpha-helical bundle suitable for lipid binding, protein-protein interactions, and ER chaperone function. HPA data classifies CNPY2 as a plasma protein and predicted intracellular protein with cytosol subcellular localization reflecting ER-to-secretion pathway trafficking, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with regulated signaling modulator and chaperone function. STRING interactions reveal coupling to lipid metabolism, myosin regulation, and ER stress networks: MYLIP (Myosin Regulatory Light Chain Interacting Protein/IDOL, 0.922) as primary partner for ubiquitin-mediated LDLR degradation and cholesterol homeostasis; MYLPF (Myosin Light Chain Phosphatase Regulatory Subunit, 0.883) for myosin light chain dephosphorylation; PSAPL1 (Prosaposin-Like Protein 1, 0.859) and PSAP (Prosaposin, 0.752) as saposin family members for glycosphingolipid metabolism; LRRTM4 (Leucine Rich Repeat Transmembrane Neuronal 4, 0.854) for synapse organization; MYL12B (Myosin Light Chain 12B, 0.829) for actomyosin contractility; CNPY4 (0.646) and CNPY3 (0.583) as canopy family paralogs for coordinated FGF signaling regulation; HSPA5 (GRP78/BiP, 0.605) as ER chaperone for unfolded protein response; and PTPRS (Protein Tyrosine Phosphatase Receptor Type S, 0.535) for receptor tyrosine phosphatase signaling. ClinVar lists 7 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletion (c.46_50del, p.Gly16fs), large inversion on 12q13.2-14.1 (chr12:56173943_56407078inv), and copy number gains on 12q13.2-14.1 (chr12:55552371-62126304x3), 12p13.33-q24.33 (chr12:173787-133777902x3, chr12:1-133851895x3, chr12:121271-133196807x3), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in developmental disorders at the 12q locus. Literature (78 papers) establishes CNPY2 as an emerging metabolic and oncologic regulator: CNPY2 aggravates renal tubular cell ferroptosis in diabetic nephropathy by regulating PERK/ATF4/CHAC1 pathway and mitochondria-associated ER membrane (MAM) integrity revealing ER stress-ferroptosis axis; CNPY2 drives DSS-induced colitis via macrophage-ROS axis establishing inflammatory bowel disease mechanism; CNPY2 expression serves as diagnostic and prognostic biomarker in non-small cell lung cancer; CNPY2 promotes proliferation, migration, invasion in gastric cancer, hepatocellular carcinoma, and prostate cancer via multiple pathways; lncRNA ZFAS1/SRSF1 regulate CNPY2 expression in cancer progression. As an emerging canopy/saposin-like protein with metabolic and cancer associations, CNPY2 represents a critical node connecting FGF-MAPK signaling to lipid homeostasis through saposin B domain-mediated lipid binding and membrane interaction, TLR4/MSAP pathway regulation for innate immune modulation, HSPA5 chaperone coupling for ER quality control and unfolded protein response, 12q copy number variation disorder pathogenesis via dosage sensitivity affecting development and cancer predisposition, and diabetic nephropathy ferroptosis/cancer progression/inflammatory bowel disease disruption in PERK/ATF4/CHAC1-mediated ferroptotic cell death, macrophage ROS production, lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA regulatory axes, and growth factor receptor folding/trafficking.
