## Description
SH2D2A (SH2 domain containing 2A, also known as TSAd or VRAP) is a 389 amino acid intracellular adaptor protein essential for T cell receptor (TCR) signal transduction and lymphocyte activation. The protein contains a canonical Src homology 2 domain (SH2, IPR000980, PF00017, positions 87-196) that mediates phosphotyrosine-dependent protein-protein interactions with signaling kinases. AlphaFold prediction shows medium structural confidence (pLDDT 61.2), consistent with a structured SH2 domain flanked by intrinsically disordered regions that enable conformational plasticity for multi-protein complex assembly. HPA data reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (13.8 nTPM), detected in many tissues, with intracellular localization consistent with cytoplasmic signaling functions. STRING interaction network identifies 10 partners in TCR signaling pathways: lymphocyte-specific kinase LCK (0.966), phospholipase C gamma 2 PLCG2 (0.912), proto-oncogene tyrosine kinase SRC (0.876), interleukin-2-inducible T cell kinase ITK (0.834), PI3K regulatory subunit PIK3R1 (0.732), DNA repair protein RAD54B (0.720), fibrinogen silencer-binding protein FSBP (0.708), SH2 domain protein SHB (0.691), PLCG1 (0.500), and VEGF receptor KDR (0.435). ClinVar reports 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and splice site mutations though specific disease phenotypes remain under investigation. Literature (55 papers) establishes roles as favorable prognosis indicator in bladder cancer enriched in activated Treg cells; modulates T cell-mediated protection against B cell tumors in transgenic mice; polymorphisms associated with inflammatory neuropathies; recent work demonstrates the intrinsically disordered insert containing Tyr290 can rewire CD19 CAR signaling for enhanced immunotherapy. SH2D2A likely functions as a molecular bridge connecting activated TCR complexes to downstream kinase cascades through SH2 domain-mediated recruitment of LCK and ITK, enabling phosphorylation events that drive T cell activation, proliferation, and effector differentiation—with emerging applications in engineered CAR-T cell therapies through exploitation of disordered region flexibility.
