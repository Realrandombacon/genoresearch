## Description
MED20 (Q9H944) is a highly conserved, well-folded (pLDDT 91.6) subunit of the Mediator complex. It contains a unique TRF (TATA-binding related factor) domain (PF08612) and interacts with core Mediator subunits (MED1, MED10, etc.) with high confidence. While ubiquitously expressed and acting as a transcription activator, MED20 has 14 pathogenic variants but no specific disease associations in ClinVar, suggesting uncharacterized developmental defects likely due to loss of complex structural stability.
