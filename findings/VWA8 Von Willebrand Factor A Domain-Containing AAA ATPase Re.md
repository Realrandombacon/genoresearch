## Description
VWA8 (Von Willebrand factor A domain-containing protein 8, 1905 aa, UniProt A3KMH1) is a large multi-domain AAA+ ATPase with emerging roles in mitochondrial function, lipid metabolism, retinal homeostasis, and cancer biology, representing a partially characterized enzyme with significant clinical implications in retinal dystrophy and immunotherapy responses.
