## Description
BOD1 (Biorientation of Chromosomes in Cell Division Protein 1, UniProt Q96IK1) is a 185 amino acid nucleoplasmic protein with moderate characterization (56 papers) that functions as a dual-purpose regulator bridging mitotic chromosome segregation with epigenetic control through COMPASS complex interactions, with emerging roles in neurodevelopment including autism spectrum disorder, cerebellar motor coordination, and syndromic intellectual disability. The protein contains a conserved BOD1/SHG1 domain (IPR055264 residues 55-150) homologous to yeast COMPASS component Shg1 (PF05205), belonging to the Biorientation of chromosomes in cell division protein 1-like 1 family (IPR043244/PTHR47391 residues 42-160/42-160), suggesting evolutionary conservation from yeast to human in linking chromatin regulation to cell division. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships spanning two functional modules: (1) Mitotic spindle apparatus: SKA3 (0.933), SKA1 (0.932), SKA2 (0.927) forming the Spindle and Kinetochore Associated complex for microtubule attachment, BUB1B (0.924, BubR1—spindle assembly checkpoint kinase), SPDL1 (0.917, Spindlin—microtubule binding), CENPF (0.915, Centromere protein F—kinetochore scaffold); (2) COMPASS histone methylation complex: CXXC1 (0.908, Cfp1—CpG island binding), WDR5 (0.887), RBBP5 (0.875), ASH2L (0.864) forming the core H3K4 methyltransferase machinery, indicating BOD1 physically bridges mitotic progression with epigenetic inheritance. HPA data shows low tissue specificity but ubiquitous detection, low cancer and immune cell specificity, cell type-enhanced single cell specificity, classification as predicted intracellular proteins, with biological processes including cell cycle, cell division, and mitosis. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (NM_138369.3:c.451C>T p.Arg151Ter, c.334C>T p.Arg112Ter), splice variants (c.362+76T>G, c.363-278G>A, c.238-8C>G), and copy number variations at 5q35.1-35.2/5q35.1-35.3/5q32-35.3/5q34-35.2, though no specific diseases are directly assigned despite associations with intellectual disability and autism. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 73.6) indicates high confidence with a well-folded compact structure typical of chromatin-associated regulatory factors.
