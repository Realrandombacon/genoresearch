# LRRC71: Leucine-Rich Repeat Protein with Tektin Interactions Suggesting Ciliary/Spermatogenic Function

## Description
LRRC71 (Leucine-Rich Repeat-Containing Protein 71, UniProt Q8N4P6) is a 559-amino acid intracellular protein characterized by multiple leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domains functioning in protein-protein interactions within specialized cellular contexts. The protein contains canonical LRR motifs (IPR001611/PF13516, residues 222-240, 251-273, 279-301) arranged in a solenoid architecture typical of the ribonuclease inhibitor-like superfamily (G3DSA:3.80.10.10/SSF52047, residues 127-324, 472-497), forming a curved horseshoe-shaped scaffold that mediates specific protein binding interactions (GO:0005515). LRRC71 belongs to a conserved vertebrate family (IPR53040/PTHR46984, residues 27-551) with mouse ortholog Q9D3W5 (558 aa, 99% identity), indicating strong evolutionary constraint. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT=71.4, high confidence) consistent with stable LRR solenoid folds required for sustained protein complex assembly. HPA reveals highly restricted expression pattern: group-enriched tissue distribution with highest levels in testis (23.6 nTPM), fallopian tube (11.6 nTPM), and choroid plexus (10.8 nTPM), cancer-enriched specificity, and cell type-enhanced expression — suggesting specialized roles in ciliated epithelia and germ cell development. STRING interaction network reveals striking associations with ciliary/flagellar structural proteins: TEKT3 (0.636, tektin-3, component of ciliary microtubule doublets essential for sperm flagella motility), TEKT1 (0.535, tektin-1, axonemal filament protein required for ciliary assembly), TSNAXIP1 (0.612, translin-associated factor X-interacting protein 1), ETV3L (0.595, ETS variant transcriptional repressor), ANKUB1 (0.580, ankyrin-repeat ubiquitin ligase), and ANHX (0.567, anhydroxylase). The tektin interactions are particularly informative: tektins form filamentous structures within ciliary microtubules, and mutations cause primary ciliary dyskinesia and male infertility. ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses across 1q21.1-q23.2 encompassing the LRRC71 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity where gene amplification or deletion may contribute to disease phenotypes potentially involving ciliary dysfunction or reproductive defects. The combination of LRR protein-protein interaction capacity, testis-enriched expression, tektin interaction partners, ciliary tissue expression (fallopian tube, choroid plexus), and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: LRRC71 functions as a scaffolding protein within ciliary/flagellar axonemal complexes, potentially stabilizing tektin filaments or recruiting regulatory factors to motile cilia during spermatogenesis and mucociliary clearance, with dosage disruption causing ciliopathy-like phenotypes through impaired ciliary assembly or motility.
