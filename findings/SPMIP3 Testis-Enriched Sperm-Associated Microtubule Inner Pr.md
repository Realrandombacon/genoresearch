## Description
SPMIP3 (Sperm-Associated Microtubule Inner Protein 3 / Chromosome 1 Open Reading Frame 100) encodes a 147 aa small intracellular protein (UniProt Q5SVJ3) with testis-specific expression and emerging roles in spermatogenesis. InterPro analysis reveals SPMIP3 belongs to the sperm-associated microtubule inner protein family: IPR037668 (Sperm-associated microtubule inner protein 3, aa 1-145), PF17670 (DUF5530 - Domain of Unknown Function, aa 1-140), PTHR31763 (Hypothetical protein LOC689766, aa 1-145). The DUF5530 designation indicates this is a genuine dark gene with uncharacterized molecular function. STRING interactions returned no partners, consistent with poorly characterized proteins lacking curated interaction data. HPA shows tissue-enriched expression exclusively in testis (21.1 nTPM), detected in single tissue distribution, with subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, cytosol, and calyx (specialized actin structure in sperm head). ClinVar contains 94 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number variations at 1q43-44 (deletions and duplications spanning chr1:229373250-249224684), though no specific disease names are listed in ClinVar. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.4) indicates medium confidence with partially structured regions, suggesting the protein may contain both ordered domains and intrinsically disordered segments typical of regulatory proteins. Literature search confirms VERY DARK status with only 1 publication, which studied the related gene SPMIP8/Tepp in mouse spermatogenesis rather than SPMIP3 directly. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SPMIP3 functions as a microtubule-associated protein specifically expressed during spermatogenesis, likely localizing to the sperm flagellum axoneme or manchette (transient microtubule structure in developing spermatids). The 'sperm-associated microtubule inner protein' nomenclature suggests SPMIP3 may be a component of microtubule inner proteins (MIPs) that stabilize axonemal microtubules in the sperm tail, similar to MIP1/MIP2/MIP3 proteins in Chlamydomonas flagella. Testis-enriched expression (21.1 nTPM) with calyx localization indicates roles in sperm head-tail coupling or acrosomal formation. The 94 ClinVar variants at 1q43-44 suggest SPMIP3 haploinsufficiency or dosage effects contribute to 1q43-44 deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes, which include intellectual disability, microcephaly, seizures, and agenesis of corpus callosum. However, these large CNVs encompass many genes, making SPMIP3's specific contribution unclear. The medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.4) with partial disorder suggests SPMIP3 may function through flexible interactions with microtubule components or actin cytoskeleton. The DUF5530 domain represents an uncharacterized protein fold that may define a novel class of spermatogenesis-specific microtubule regulators. SPMIP3 is a genuine dark gene requiring experimental characterization of its microtubule-binding properties, interaction partners in sperm, and contribution to male fertility and 1q43-44 chromosomal disorder phenotypes.
