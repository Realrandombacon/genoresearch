## Description
ZNF275 (Zinc Finger Protein 275, UniProt Q9NSD4) is a 429-amino acid Krüppel-associated C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor functioning primarily as a transcriptional repressor within nucleolar compartments. The protein contains multiple tandem C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087/PF00096/SM00355, residues 101-128, 129-156, 181-208) arranged in clusters for sequence-specific genomic recognition, classified within the C2H2-type zinc finger superfamily (IPR036236/G3DSA:3.30.160.60/SSF57667, residues 100-151, 179-274, 217-330). These domains form beta-beta-alpha structural motifs that coordinate zinc ions to stabilize DNA-binding interfaces, enabling ZNF275 to recognize specific promoter or enhancer sequences. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded architecture (pLDDT=72.1, high confidence) consistent with ordered zinc finger folds embedded within flexible linker regions required for chromatin scanning and multi-protein complex assembly. HPA classifies ZNF275 as a transcription factor with DNA-binding and repressor molecular functions, tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in adrenal gland (67.9 nTPM), ubiquitous detection across tissues, and specific subcellular localization to nucleoli — suggesting roles in ribosomal biogenesis regulation or nucleolar stress responses particularly in steroidogenic tissues. STRING interaction network reveals associations with nuclear and metabolic regulators: PIP4K2C (0.496, phosphatidylinositol-5-phosphate 4-kinase type 2 gamma involved in nuclear signaling and mTOR regulation), SPTY2D1 (0.429, spt-like 2 derived 1, chromatin-associated protein), MAP7D3 (0.422, microtubule-associated protein 7 domain containing 3), ATP1B4 (0.412, Na+/K+ transporting subunit beta 4, regulatory roles in cell adhesion), H2AB3 (0.407, histone H2A type 3, core nucleosome component), CNBP (0.405, cellular nucleic acid binding protein, RNA/DNA chaperone), and TEX28 (0.405, testis expressed 28). The PIP4K2C interaction suggests potential links between ZNF275-mediated repression and nuclear phosphoinositide signaling pathways controlling cell growth. ClinVar documents an exceptionally high burden of 196 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal deletions and duplications spanning Xq23-q28 encompassing the ZNF275 locus, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity causes disease phenotypes potentially involving adrenal dysfunction, developmental delays, or X-linked disorders given its chromosomal location. The combination of C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding capacity, repressor function, nucleolar localization, adrenal-enriched expression, PIP4K2C interaction partners, and extensive CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: ZNF275 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor recruited to target gene promoters via its zinc finger array, potentially silencing genes involved in cell cycle progression or steroidogenesis through recruitment of co-repressor complexes or direct interference with basal transcription machinery, with additional roles in nucleolar homeostasis linking ribosomal biogenesis to adrenal function, where dosage disruption causes disease through dysregulated gene networks critical for development and endocrine homeostasis.
