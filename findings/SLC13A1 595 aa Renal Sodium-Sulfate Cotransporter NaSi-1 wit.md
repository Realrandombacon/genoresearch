## Description
SLC13A1 (Q9BZW2, 595 aa), also known as Sodium-Sulfate Cotransporter 1 or NaSi-1, is a sodium-dependent sulfate transporter belonging to solute carrier family 13 (SLC13/NaS family) within the sodium/solute symporter superfamily (PTHR10283).
