## Description
ABCB10 (ATP-binding cassette sub-family B member 10, mitochondrial, also known as MTABC3 or ABCB10, Q9NRK6) encodes a 738 aa homodimeric half-transporter functioning as an essential mitochondrial inner membrane exporter that transports biliverdin and potentially heme intermediates from mitochondria to cytosol, thereby protecting against oxidative stress, enabling hemoglobin synthesis during erythropoiesis, and regulating ferroptosis through mitochondrial quality control.
