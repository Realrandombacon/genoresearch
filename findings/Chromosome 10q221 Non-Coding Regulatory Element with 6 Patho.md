## Description
LOC101928994 (Gene ID: 101928994) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 10q22.1 (NC_000010.11, positions 69232489-69215332, ~17 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000229261 but reported no detectable protein expression across any tissue, consistent with non-coding function. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 6 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 single nucleotide variant (variant 1321242: NM_025130.4(HKDC1):c.173C>T p.Thr58Met, hexokinase domain containing 1 missense mutation) and 5 copy number variants (3 duplications: variant 148945: 10q11.23-23.2 ~36.4 Mb; variant 146115: 10q21.3-22.3 ~12.2 Mb; variant 145481: 10q11.21-22.2 ~31 Mb; 2 deletions: variant 147477: 10q21.3-22.2 ~11.9 Mb; variant 58726: 10q21.1-22.2 ~16 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 10q22.1 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including PRF1 (perforin 1, cytotoxic T-cell/NK cell granule exocytosis, familial hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis type 2, immune cytotoxicity), PSAP (prosaposin, lysosomal sphingolipid activator protein, Gaucher disease, metachromatic leukodystrophy, Krabbe disease), HK1 (hexokinase 1, glycolysis rate-limiting enzyme, red blood cell metabolism, hemolytic anemia), DDX21 (DExD-box helicase 21, RNA unwinding, ribosome biogenesis, nucleolar function, cancer progression), and NODAL (nodal growth differentiation factor, TGF-beta superfamily, embryonic patterning, left-right axis determination, cancer stemness). Functional hypothesis: LOC101928994 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 10q22.1 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a hematopoietic/immune cell-specific enhancer coordinating PRF1 cytotoxic granule expression in NK cells and CD8+ T cells (perforin transcription, immune surveillance, viral clearance) or as a chromatin boundary separating immune genes (PRF1) from metabolic genes (HK1/HKDC1) and lysosomal genes (PSAP). The HKDC1 c.173C>T p.Thr58Met variant suggests functional coupling between LOC101928994 and hexokinase gene regulation. The 6 pathogenic variants indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs simultaneously. Disease mechanism involves PRF1 haploinsufficiency causing familial hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis type 2 (cytotoxic granule exocytosis defects, hyperinflammation, macrophage activation syndrome), PRF1 overexpression altering immune cytotoxicity threshold (autoimmunity, graft-versus-host disease), PSAP deficiency causing lysosomal sphingolipid accumulation (Gaucher disease, metachromatic leukodystrophy, neurodegeneration), HK1/HKDC1 dysfunction causing glycolytic defects (hemolytic anemia, metabolic reprogramming, Warburg effect), DDX21 alteration impairing ribosome biogenesis (nucleolar stress, cancer progression), and NODAL dysregulation affecting embryonic development (heterotaxy, cancer stemness). The 10q22.1 duplication syndrome may present with immune dysregulation, lysosomal storage defects, and metabolic abnormalities. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (6 variants including HKDC1 missense + CNVs 11.9-36.4 Mb) and PRF1-PSAP-HK1-DDX21-NODAL gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for immune cytotoxicity-lysosomal function-glycolytic metabolism coordination.
