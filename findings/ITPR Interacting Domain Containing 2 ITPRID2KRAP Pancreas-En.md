## Description
ITPRID2 (ITPR Interacting Domain Containing 2), also known as KRAP (Ki-ras-induced actin-interacting protein), is a 1259-amino acid scaffold protein with only 3 publications confirming its very dark gene status. The protein contains conserved domains including ITPR-interacting domain (IPR029325, positions 146-303) mediating signaling receptor binding (GO:0005102) to inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptors, sperm-specific antigen 2 C-terminal domain (IPR029326, positions 864-1033), and protein TESPA1-like family architecture (IPR043444, positions 10-1257) with PFAM domains PF14722 (Ki-ras-induced actin-interacting protein-IP3R-interacting domain, positions 149-300) and PF14723 (sperm-specific antigen 2 C-terminus, positions 864-1033). AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 50.2), consistent with large scaffold proteins containing ordered interaction domains embedded in flexible regions for multi-protein complex assembly. HPA reveals pancreas-enriched expression (165.7 nTPM top tissue), tissue-enhanced distribution pattern, detected-in-all tissues distribution, actin-binding molecular function, and nucleoplasm/plasma membrane/cytosol subcellular localization. ClinVar documents 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (primarily chromosome 2q31.1-32.2/2q31.3-33.1/2q31.1-35 copy number losses and gains), though specific disease phenotypes remain uncharacterized. STRING interactions reveal ITPRID2 functions within actin cytoskeleton and calcium signaling networks with high confidence connection to COBL (0.863) - cordon-bleu actin nucleation factor that nucleates unbranched actin filaments, plus medium confidence interactions with TESPA1 (0.670) - thymocyte expressed protein involved in selection 1 (paralog), CBL (0.629) - E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase, WAS (0.607) - Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome protein (actin regulator), APPBP2 (0.518) - amyloid beta precursor protein binding protein 2, IZUMO4 (0.497) - sperm-egg fusion protein, VPS33B (0.477) - vacuolar protein sorting-associated protein, TEX2 (0.464) - testis expressed 2, CAMK2D (0.447) - calcium/calmodulin dependent protein kinase II delta, and FKBP15 (0.439) - FK506 binding protein 15. Recent studies (2024) established KRAP/ITPRID2 regulates mitochondrial Ca2+ uptake by licensing IP3 receptor activity and stabilizing ER-mitochondrial junctions, while 2025 studies identified ITPRID2 in macrophage biomarker signatures for bladder cancer and hypoxia/mitochondrial-related gene signatures in lung adenocarcinoma. We hypothesize ITPRID2 functions as a multi-domain scaffold protein that bridges IP3 receptors at ER membranes with actin cytoskeleton and mitochondrial calcium uptake machinery, coordinating calcium signaling at ER-mitochondrial contact sites (MAMs - mitochondria-associated membranes). The ITPR-interacting domain (positions 146-303) directly binds IP3R calcium channels, while actin-binding function and COBL interaction (0.863) suggest cytoskeletal anchoring of IP3R clusters. The TESPA1 paralog connection (0.670) indicates functional redundancy within this scaffold protein subfamily. Pancreas enrichment (165.7 nTPM) suggests specialized roles in exocrine/endocrine pancreatic calcium signaling critical for enzyme secretion or insulin release. The IZUMO4 interaction hints at potential reproductive functions consistent with sperm-specific antigen domain architecture. The 30 ClinVar variants indicate ITPRID2 dosage sensitivity, with chromosome 2q copy number alterations potentially disrupting ER-mitochondrial calcium homeostasis contributing to developmental disorders. ITPRID2 represents an uncharacterized scaffold linking calcium signaling, actin dynamics, and organelle contact site organization.
