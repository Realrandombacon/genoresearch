## Description
ZNF304 (Gene ID: 84667, UniProt Q9HCX3) is a 659 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) domain-containing C2H2 zinc finger protein located on chromosome 19q13.42 (NC_000019.10). This gene encodes a classical two-module transcriptional repressor architecture: N-terminal KRAB repression domain coupled to C-terminal DNA-binding zinc finger arrays. InterPro domain analysis reveals canonical KRAB-ZFP organization: KRAB-A box domain (cd07765, positions 14-53), KRAB box domain (IPR001909/PF01352, positions 13-96, ~83 residues forming transcriptional repression module), KRAB superfamily (IPR036051/G3DSA:3.30.160.60, positions 9-68), and multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087/PF00096/PS00028/PS50157 at positions 89-111, 115-144, 251-278, 279-301, 307-329, 335-357 and beyond—spanning C-terminal ~570 residues with 10+ tandem zinc fingers). The KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressor complexes that recruit heterochromatin machinery (HP1, SETDB1 histone methyltransferase, DNMT1 DNA methyltransferase) for transcriptional silencing. C2H2 zinc fingers recognize specific DNA sequences through modular base contacts, with each finger typically recognizing 3-4 bp. STRING interaction network identifies 10 medium-to-high confidence partners forming a functionally coherent epigenetic silencing interactome: TRIM28/KAP1 (0.861, high confidence, tripartite motif-containing protein 28, essential KRAB-ZFP coreceptor), DNMT1 (0.799, high confidence, DNA methyltransferase 1, maintains CpG methylation patterns), SETDB1 (0.645, medium confidence, SET domain bifurcated histone lysine methyltransferase 1, catalyzes H3K9me3), USP28 (0.510, ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase 28, deubiquitinating enzyme stabilizing proteins), NICN1 (0.462), TMEM178A (0.448), MAFG (0.447, MAF bZIP transcription factor G, small Maf protein partnering with CNC factors), ZC3H4 (0.428), ZNF416 (0.412, another KRAB-ZFP suggesting cooperative regulation), and OR8B4 (0.410, olfactory receptor family member). The TRIM28-DNMT1-SETDB1 triad confirms ZNF304 functions within canonical KRAB-ZFP silencing machinery recruiting both histone modification (H3K9me3) and DNA methylation for stable epigenetic repression. Human Protein Atlas classifies ZNF304 (ENSG00000131845) as a predicted intracellular protein and transcription factor with nuclear speckles and cytosol subcellular localization. RNA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in ALL tissues (ubiquitous housekeeping expression), low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, and low cell type specificity—indicating fundamental transcriptional regulatory function. Classified under angiogenesis, transcription, and transcription regulation biological processes with activator, chromatin regulator, and DNA-binding molecular functions. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9HCX3-F1) yields MEDIUM-confidence model (global pLDDT 64.9), indicating a partially structured protein with mixed ordered and disordered regions across all 659 residues—the KRAB domain likely forms an α-helical bundle while zinc fingers form compact ββα folds stabilized by Zn²⁺ coordination. Medium confidence reflects the challenging nature of predicting large proteins with extensive linker regions between zinc fingers, typical of KRAB-ZFPs. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple large chromosomal copy number gains spanning chromosome 19q13 regions (chr19:54196216-58759679x3, chr19:260912-58956888x3, chr19:50489390-59095359x3, chr19:52143873-58445521x3, chr19:50152520-58581203x3, chr19:52612432-58581203x3, chr19:52955056-58581203x3, chr19:56363208-58581203x3) and one intronic variant (NM_020657.4:c.33+194A>T); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ZNF304, suggesting dosage sensitivity within larger chromosomal syndromes. Conservation is evidenced by orthologs across vertebrates indicating functional importance despite sequence divergence in zinc finger count. Functional hypothesis: ZNF304 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor regulating developmentally important genes and cancer-related pathways through epigenetic silencing. The N-terminal KRAB domain (residues 13-96) recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor complex, which recruits SETDB1 (H3K9me3 methyltransferase), DNMT1 (DNA methyltransferase), HP1 heterochromatin proteins, and NuRD complex for chromatin compaction and transcriptional silencing. The C-terminal zinc finger array (residues ~89-659) recognizes specific DNA sequences through combinatorial zinc finger code—each finger contributes 3-4 bp specificity, enabling recognition of 30-40+ bp target sites. High-confidence TRIM28 interaction (0.861) confirms canonical KRAB-ZFP mechanism. DNMT1 interaction (0.799) links ZNF304 to DNA methylation maintenance, suggesting roles in long-term epigenetic memory. SETDB1 interaction (0.645) confirms H3K9me3-mediated heterochromatin formation. MAFG interaction suggests potential partnership with CNC-MAF transcription factors regulating antioxidant response or differentiation programs. ZNF416 interaction indicates cooperative regulation with other KRAB-ZFPs. Nuclear speckles localization suggests involvement in splicing regulation or transcription factory organization. Ubiquitous expression reflects fundamental role in transcriptional infrastructure. Angiogenesis classification suggests roles in vascular development through repression of angiogenic inhibitors or activators. Disease mechanism: ZNF304 haploinsufficiency or overexpression from chromosome 19q13 CNVs may contribute to developmental disorders or cancer. The 15 ClinVar variants suggest strong dosage sensitivity; 19q13 deletions/duplications cause diverse phenotypes including intellectual disability, growth abnormalities, and cancer predisposition. ZNF304 may contribute through ectopic silencing of target genes (developmental regulators, cell cycle controllers, angiogenesis factors) or disruption of epigenetic programming. Priority experiments include ChIP-seq to identify genomic binding sites and H3K9me3/DNMT1 colocalization, co-IP validation of TRIM28, DNMT1, SETDB1 interactions, reporter assays testing repression activity and MAFG partnership, CRISPR knockout to assess phenotypic consequences particularly in cancer and endothelial cells, structural analysis leveraging AlphaFold model (pLDDT 64.9) to map DNA-binding interface, investigation of ZNF304 role in angiogenesis via tube formation assays, and analysis of ZNF304 expression in 19q syndrome patient cells and tumor tissues.
