# LOC129389747: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 7p22.3 with High Burden of Recurrent Pathogenic CNVs

**Date:** 2026-03-20T20:21:23.616324

**Quality Score:** 1.85/10  (E=3.4, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
LOC129389747 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 7p22.3 (GRCh38: 12223009-12223209), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its classification as a non-coding regulatory element rather than a protein-coding gene.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—the highest burden among characterized silencer elements. Variants include 6 duplications (7p22.3-15.2, 7p22.3-15.3, 7p22.1-15.2 spanning ~27-37Mb) and 5 deletions (7p21.3-21.2, 7p21.3-15.2, 7p22.3-q36.3 spanning ~1.6-159Mb). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 7p duplication/deletion syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism (including Robinow syndrome phenotypes), skeletal abnormalities, and hematologic defects.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389747 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 7p22.3 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes. Candidate targets include TWIST1 (craniofacial development, haploinsufficiency causes Saethre-Chotzen syndrome), EGFR (epidermal growth factor receptor), or HOXA cluster genes. The silencer recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The exceptionally high CNV burden (11 variants) suggests extreme dosage sensitivity—both silencer loss (derepression of proto-oncogenes/developmental regulators) and gain (co-repressor titration, TAD disruption) cause disease. The 7p22.3 location near TWIST1 and craniofacial development genes explains the Robinow syndrome phenotypes in deletion carriers. K562 erythroleukemia validation supports hematopoietic regulatory roles, potentially contributing to thrombocytopenia and neutropenia in 7p syndromes.
