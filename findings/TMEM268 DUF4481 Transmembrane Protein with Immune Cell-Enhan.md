The DUF4481 domain may mediate protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity, positioning TMEM268 as an adaptor that coordinates membrane lipid composition with ubiquitin-dependent cargo sorting. Immune cell-enhanced expression suggests potential roles in immunological synapse formation or cytokine receptor trafficking.
