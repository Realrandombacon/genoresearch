# TM7SF3: Nuclear Seven-Transmembrane Protein with Immunoglobulin-Like Domains Regulating TEAD1 Alternative Splicing to Prevent Liver Fibrosis and Functioning as Proton-Coupled Organic Cation Antiporter at Blood-Brain Barrier

**Quality Score:** 10/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
TM7SF3 (Q9NS93) encodes a 570 amino acid transmembrane 7 superfamily member 3 protein belonging to the TM7SF3/TM198-like family that functions dually as a nuclear splicing regulator and membrane transporter. InterPro analysis reveals a unique domain architecture combining immunoglobulin-like folds with a characteristic transmembrane domain: an N-terminal immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR059229/PF25992, positions 32-129) potentially mediating protein-protein interactions; a second immunoglobulin-like domain (IPR060567/PF27919, positions 134-279) providing additional interaction surfaces; and a TM7S3/TM198-like domain (IPR025256/PF13886, positions 299-503) classified within the TM7SF3 family (IPR042502/PTHR15937, positions 6-570) containing the predicted seven transmembrane helices forming the transporter core. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 88.4, high confidence) consistent with stable multi-domain architecture required for both nuclear splicing complex assembly and membrane transport functions. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with transcriptional/splicing machinery and membrane proteins: FAM89B (0.549), family with sequence similarity 89 member B of unknown function; MED21 (0.514), mediator complex subunit 21 involved in transcriptional regulation; RFX7 (0.463), regulatory factor X7 transcription factor; KLHL42 (0.457), kelch-like protein 42 substrate adapter for CUL3 E3 ligase; GPR137/GPR137B (0.455/0.452), G-protein coupled receptors 137 and 137B suggesting potential signaling cross-talk; LNPK (0.452), lipid phosphate phosphatase-related protein; INTS13 (0.423), integrator complex subunit 13 involved in RNA processing; TAFA4 (0.420), chemokine-like protein; FGFR1OP2 (0.404), FGFR1 oncogene partner 2. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with kidney as top expressing tissue (239.2 nTPM), detected in all tissues, nucleoplasm and cytosol subcellular localization consistent with dual nuclear/cytoplasmic functions, classified under predicted intracellular/membrane proteins with stress response biological process. ClinVar contains 37 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning chromosome 12p regions (e.g., chr12:64621-34650483 duplications, chr12:82453-49847230 gains, chr12:173787-34835837 amplifications), missense mutation c.935G>T p.Gly312Val within the TM7S3/TM198-like domain potentially disrupting transporter function, and splice variant c.1189+34G>A affecting exon-intron boundaries, though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (19 papers) demonstrates TM7SF3 deletion controls TEAD1 alternative splicing preventing MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis)-induced liver fibrosis through hepatic stellate cell regulation; TM7SF3 collaborates with LHFPL6 as putative proton/organic cation (H+/OC) antiporter mediating CNS drug transport across blood-brain barrier including oxycodone and pyrilamine; TM7SF3 resides in nuclear speckles regulating alternative splicing machinery; exosomal lncRNA TM7SF3-AU1 aggravates white matter injury via miR-702-3p/SARM1 signaling after subarachnoid hemorrhage in rats. The mechanistic hypothesis: TM7SF3 functions through a dual mechanism depending on subcellular localization — (1) in the nucleus, TM7SF3 localizes to nuclear speckles where its two immunoglobulin-like domains (positions 32-129, 134-279) mediate protein-protein interactions with splicing factors including INTS13 and MED21, enabling TM7SF3 to regulate TEAD1 alternative splicing patterns that control hepatic stellate cell activation and prevent liver fibrosis progression in MASH; the Ig-like domains provide modular interaction surfaces recognizing specific splicing regulator complexes; (2) at cellular membranes, particularly blood-brain barrier endothelial cells, the seven-transmembrane TM7S3/TM198-like domain (positions 299-503) forms a proton-coupled organic cation antiporter collaborating with LHFPL6 to transport cationic drugs via H+ gradient-driven exchange mechanism; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 88.4) indicates rigid domain interfaces enabling conformational changes required for alternating access transport cycle; kidney-enriched expression (239.2 nTPM) suggests specialized role in renal organic cation handling consistent with transporter function; nucleoplasm/cytosol localization indicates dynamic shuttling between compartments potentially regulated by stress signals; copy number gains on 12p may cause TM7SF3 overexpression disrupting both splicing regulation (altering TEAD1 isoform ratios promoting fibrosis) and transporter activity (affecting drug pharmacokinetics); medium-confidence STRING interactions with GPR137/GPR137B suggest potential coordination between TM7SF3 transport activity and GPCR signaling pathways; stress response classification indicates TM7SF3 upregulation under pathological conditions including liver injury and neurological damage.
