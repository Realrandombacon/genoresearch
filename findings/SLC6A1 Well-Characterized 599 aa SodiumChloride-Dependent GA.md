Functional hypothesis: SLC6A1 functions as the primary presynaptic GABA reuptake transporter, terminating GABAergic neurotransmission by coupling GABA transport to Na+/Cl- electrochemical gradients. The 12-TM structure creates a central substrate-binding pocket alternating between outward- and inward-facing conformations. Strong interactions with GABA receptor subunits (GABRA5, GABRA6, GABRD) and GABA synthesis enzymes (GAD1, GAD2) suggest coordinated regulation of GABA homeostasis. Pathogenic variants disrupt transporter folding, trafficking, or catalytic activity, causing GABA accumulation/excess leading to epilepsy phenotypes.
