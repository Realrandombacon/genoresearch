## Description
FBXO41 (F-Box Only Protein 41, UniProt Q8TF61) is a 875 aa F-box protein belonging to the F-box protein family (IPR001810/IPR032675/IPR036047/IPR52283/PF12937/PTHR15739, positions 557-594/585-841/557-636/1-872/557-594/1-872) that functions as a substrate recognition subunit of SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box protein) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes regulating protein degradation, autophagy, neuronal development, and cancer suppression. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal C2H2-type zinc finger domain (IPR057038/PF23165, positions 6-35) for DNA/protein binding, a central F-box domain (IPR001810/cd22109/PF12937/SSF81383, positions 547-594/557-594) for SKP1 binding and SCF complex assembly, and C-terminal leucine-rich repeat (LRR) domain superfamily (IPR032675/G3DSA:3.80.10.10/SSF52047, positions 585-841/651-825) for substrate recognition and protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 67.9) indicating ordered F-box and LRR domains connected by flexible linkers—typical architecture for F-box proteins requiring conformational flexibility for substrate recognition. Literature (19 papers) establishes FBXO41 roles in: (1) breast cancer growth suppression by inducing autophagic cell death through facilitating proteasomal degradation of oncogene SKP2 (2022, 8 citations), (2) neuronal synaptic transmission and hippocampal network maturation with Fbxo41 null mice showing behavioral deficits and early lethality (2022, 5 citations), (3) epileptic encephalopathies as a neuron-specific E3 ligase subunit, (4) neuronal primary cilia disassembly promoting brain development (2019, 13 citations), (5) arsenic trioxide-mediated autophagic death of cancer cells (2022, 3 citations), (6) evolutionary history analysis (2024).
