## Description
RIBC2 (RIB43A domain with coiled-coils 2, also known as C22orf11, TRIB) is a 382 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q9H4K1) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 86.6), characteristic of axonemal structural proteins that function through RIB43A domain-mediated protein-protein interactions in cilia and flagella. The protein belongs to the RIB43A family (IPR008805, residues 6-382; PF05914, residues 13-382; PTHR14517, residues 6-382). RIBC2 is located on chromosome 22q13.31 and is predicted to be involved in flagellated sperm motility, located in axonemal microtubules. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals a high-confidence network of cilia/flagella and microtubule-associated proteins: TRIB1 (0.944, Tribbles homolog 1, pseudokinase scaffold), TRIB3 (0.937, Tribbles homolog 3, pseudokinase), EFHC1 (0.877, EF-hand domain-containing protein 1, microtubule/cilia protein), EFHC2 (0.871, EF-hand domain-containing protein 2, microtubule-associated), CFAP45 (0.860, Cilia and flagella associated protein 45, axonemal protein), TEKT3 (0.860, Tektin-3, axonemal structural protein), TEKT2 (0.845, Tektin-2, axonemal structural protein), FAM183A (0.808, Family with sequence similarity 183 member A, uncharacterized), TRIB2 (0.800, Tribbles homolog 2, pseudokinase), and MNS1 (0.800, Meiosis-specific protein 1, sperm flagella protein). These interactions confirm RIBC2 functions within cilia/flagella assembly and sperm motility pathways, with TRIB1 and TRIB3 as the highest-confidence partners (0.944/0.937) reflecting potential pseudokinase scaffold regulation. EFHC1, EFHC2, CFAP45, TEKT2, TEKT3, and MNS1 interactions (0.877-0.800) link RIBC2 directly to axonemal microtubule structure and sperm flagella function.\n\nHPA expression reveals RIBC2 has group-enriched expression with highest levels in testis (28.8 nTPM), choroid plexus (12.6 nTPM), and fallopian tube (9.2 nTPM), consistent with roles in ciliated cell function and sperm motility. This testis enrichment (28.8 nTPM) indicates specialized functions in male reproduction and spermatogenesis. Cancer enhanced specificity indicates elevated expression in cancer types. Not detected in immune cells suggests restricted expression in hematopoietic lineages. Subcellular localization to perinuclear theca, calyx, connecting piece, mid piece, and principal piece confirms its role in sperm tail/flagella structure. Cell type enhanced single cell specificity indicates specialized functions in ciliated cells and sperm. The protein is classified as a predicted intracellular protein.\n\nClinVar documents 69 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, a high burden indicating strong functional constraint. Variants include extensive copy number losses (22q13.31-13.33 deletions spanning 45.1-51.2 Mb, 22q13.2-13.33 deletions 43.1-51.2 Mb, 22q13.2-13.31 deletions 43.9-46.5 Mb) and gains (22q13.31-13.33 amplifications), plus splice variants (c.903+175A>G). The variant burden indicates dosage sensitivity with both haploinsufficiency and overexpression effects. While no specific Mendelian disease is annotated, 22q13 CNVs (Phelan-McDermid syndrome region) are associated with developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorders, and hypotonia. Given RIBC2's axonemal function and testis-enriched expression, loss-of-function may cause defective sperm motility, impaired cilia function, or disrupted microtubule dynamics leading to male infertility and ciliopathy phenotypes.\n\nConservation is present across vertebrates: human (Q9H4K1, 382 aa), mouse (Q9D4Q1, 377 aa), rat (Q6AXN9, 377 aa), bovine (Q32LJ7, 377 aa), indicating the RIB43A architecture is highly conserved in mammals with nearly identical protein length, suggesting strong purifying selection on axonemal microtubule functions.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: RIBC2 functions as an axonemal microtubule-associated protein that regulates sperm flagella motility and cilia function through RIB43A domain-mediated protein interactions. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 86.6) indicates a well-folded protein with stable 3D architecture suitable for structural roles in the axoneme. Through TRIB1 and TRIB3 interactions (0.944/0.937), RIBC2 may be regulated by tribbles pseudokinase scaffolds for signaling integration. EFHC1 and EFHC2 interactions (0.877/0.871) link RIBC2 to EF-hand calcium-binding microtubule proteins for calcium-dependent motility regulation. CFAP45 interaction (0.860) connects RIBC2 to cilia/flagella assembly machinery. TEKT2 and TEKT3 interactions (0.845/0.860) suggest RIBC2 cooperates with tektin filament proteins for axonemal structural integrity. MNS1 interaction (0.800) links RIBC2 to meiosis-specific sperm flagella assembly. Testis enrichment (28.8 nTPM) and sperm tail localization (perinuclear theca, calyx, connecting piece, mid piece, principal piece) position RIBC2 in sperm motility. Choroid plexus and fallopian tube expression indicates roles in ependymal and oviduct cilia function. 22q13 CNVs cause RIBC2 haploinsufficiency, potentially contributing to Phelan-McDermid syndrome phenotypes including hypotonia and developmental delay through cilia dysfunction. RIBC2 represents a candidate gene for male infertility, primary ciliary dyskinesia, and 22q13 deletion syndrome, and may serve as a therapeutic target for ciliopathies.
