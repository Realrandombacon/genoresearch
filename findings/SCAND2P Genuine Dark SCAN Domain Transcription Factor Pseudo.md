## Description

The SCAN domain is a well-characterized ~100 amino acid protein-protein interaction module found in zinc finger transcription factors (particularly ZNF/KRAB-ZFP family), mediating homo- and hetero-oligomerization essential for transcriptional regulation. STRING interactions are unavailable (no protein found), HPA expression data is absent (gene not in database), and ClinVar contains no variants - all consistent with pseudogene annotation and limited characterization. SCAN domain proteins function as transcriptional regulators through oligomerization-dependent mechanisms: the SCAN domain mediates specific protein-protein interactions that determine partner selection, while associated zinc finger domains (C2H2-type) provide DNA sequence specificity. Many SCAN-ZFP proteins function as repressors through KRAB domain recruitment of KAP1/TRIM28 and heterochromatin formation, though SCAND2P's specific regulatory role is unknown.
