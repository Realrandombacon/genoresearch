## Description
CLEC4C (C-type lectin domain family 4 member C, also known as BDCA-2/CD303, UniProt Q8WTT0) is a 213 aa type II transmembrane C-type lectin receptor exclusively expressed on plasmacytoid dendritic cells (pDCs). InterPro analysis reveals a canonical C-type lectin-like domain (CTLD, IPR001304, PF00059, positions 83-208) within a CD209-like lectin superfamily (IPR033989, positions 83-208), featuring the conserved C-type lectin signature (PS00615, positions 180-206) and mannose-binding protein fold (G3DSA:3.10.100.10, positions 67-213). AlphaFold structure shows very high confidence (pLDDT 92.1), indicating a well-folded lectin domain with stable carbohydrate-binding geometry. HPA data confirms immune cell-enriched expression with lymphoid tissue specificity (5.1 nTPM), annotated to adaptive/innate immunity biological processes. STRING interactions reveal functional clustering with dendritic cell and immune regulators: ITGAX/CD11c (0.858, integrin alpha-X), CD1C (0.838, MHC class I-like antigen-presenting molecule), THBD (0.795, thrombomodulin), CD2 (0.557, T-cell co-receptor), IL3RA (0.529, IL-3 receptor alpha), LILRA4 (0.465, leukocyte immunoglobulin-like receptor), FCER1G/FCER1A (0.413/0.402, Fc epsilon receptor subunits). ClinVar contains 46 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains at 12p13.33-11.1, though no specific disease associations are yet defined. Literature (49 papers) establishes CLEC4C as the canonical pDC surface marker (BDCA-2) used to quantify circulating pDCs, with roles in type I interferon production, antiviral immunity, and antigen capture. The p.K210del variant causes impaired cell surface transport in ALS pDCs. CLEC4C expression serves as a biomarker for primary graft dysfunction after heart transplantation (23 citations). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CLEC4C acts as a pattern recognition receptor on pDCs that captures glycosylated antigens via its CTLD, internalizes them for processing, and modulates TLR7/9-mediated type I interferon production. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The CTLD binds carbohydrate moieties on viral glycoproteins or damaged self-antigens. CLEC4C internalization via clathrin-mediated endocytosis delivers cargo to endosomal compartments where TLR7/9 reside. The ITGAX interaction (STRING 0.858) suggests CLEC4C may cooperate with integrin-mediated adhesion during pDC migration to lymph nodes. CD1C interaction (0.838) indicates potential coordination with lipid antigen presentation. FCER1G association (0.413) implies CLEC4C may signal through ITAM-bearing adaptor chains similar to other C-type lectin receptors. The 46 ClinVar variants suggest CLEC4C dysfunction contributes to immunodeficiency or autoimmunity, potentially through altered pDC homeostasis or interferon dysregulation.
