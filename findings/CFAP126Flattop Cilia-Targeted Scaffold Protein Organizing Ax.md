The AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 72.6), indicating a well-folded stable structure. HPA expression shows tissue enhancement with highest levels in choroid plexus (202.0 nTPM) and fallopian tube (142.6 nTPM), with subcellular localization to primary cilium, plasma membrane, midpiece, and vesicles. ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses at 1q23.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The Flattop domain architecture and interaction network position CFAP126 as a structural scaffold that anchors ciliary membrane proteins to the axonemal microtubule core, potentially bridging the transition zone complex to distal axonemal components. High expression in choroid plexus implicates a role in cerebrospinal fluid flow via ependymal cilia, while fallopian tube enrichment suggests function in oocyte transport. Disease variants may cause ciliopathies affecting neural development or reproductive function.
