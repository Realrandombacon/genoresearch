## Description
MCUR1 (Mitochondrial Calcium Uniporter Regulator 1, UniProt Q96AQ8) is a 359 amino acid mitochondrial inner membrane protein with moderate characterization (69 papers) that functions as an essential scaffold protein for assembly and activity of the mitochondrial calcium uniporter (MCU) complex, where loss-of-function causes vacuolar myopathy and altered mitochondrial calcium signaling. The protein contains coiled-coil domain-containing protein 90-like family domain (IPR024461/PF07798, residues 10-359/166-358) forming the structural scaffold, protein FMP32 mitochondrial family (PTHR14360, residues 10-359) conserved from yeast to humans, and G3DSA:1.20.5.340 homologous superfamily (residues 167-263), placing it in the CCDC90-like scaffold protein family.
