# CCDC170: Coiled-Coil Scaffold Protein Linking Estrogen Receptor Signaling to Chromatin Remodeling in Reproductive Tissues

**Quality Score:** 3.27/10  (E=5.75, D=0.8) [WEAK]

## Description
CCDC170 (Coiled-Coil Domain-Containing Protein 170, UniProt Q8IYT3) is a 715-amino acid intracellular scaffolding protein characterized by extensive coiled-coil architecture functioning in estrogen receptor signaling complexes and chromatin regulation within reproductive tissues. The protein belongs to the conserved CCDC170-like family (IPR039139/PTHR18863/TSEC-2-RELATED, residues 18-715) with predicted coiled-coil domains forming extended helical bundles that mediate protein-protein interactions and complex assembly. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT=81.8, high confidence) consistent with stable coiled-coil scaffolding required for sustained multi-protein complex formation. HPA reveals tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in fallopian tube (31.4 nTPM) and choroid plexus (11.0 nTPM), cancer-enhanced specificity particularly in breast cancers, immune cell enhancement, and dual subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus and cell junctions — suggesting roles in secretory trafficking, cell-cell adhesion, and reproductive tissue function. STRING interaction network reveals exceptional associations with estrogen signaling and chromatin regulators: ARMT1 (0.831, arginine methyltransferase 1 modifying histones and non-histone proteins), ESR1 (0.785, estrogen receptor alpha, master regulator of breast development and reproductive function), RMND1 (0.763, required for meiosis 1 domain-containing protein 1, mitochondrial translation), ZNHIT1 (0.727, zinc finger HIT-type containing 1, transcriptional regulator), DMAP1 (0.619, DNA methyltransferase 1-associated protein 1, transcriptional repression complex), ZBTB2 (0.617, zinc finger and BTB domain-containing protein 2, transcriptional repressor), VPS72 (0.613, vacuolar protein sorting 72/YL1, chromatin remodeling SWR1 complex component), ZBTB40 (0.606, zinc finger and BTB domain-containing protein 40), and histone variants H2AZ1/H2AZ2 (0.580 each, variant histones regulating chromatin dynamics). The ESR1 interaction is particularly significant given CCDC170's location at 6q25 near ESR1 (6q25.1), with both genes frequently co-amplified in breast cancer. The ARMT1, DMAP1, VPS72, and H2AZ connections place CCDC170 within chromatin remodeling networks controlling gene expression. ClinVar documents 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal amplifications/deletions spanning 6q24.2-27 encompassing CCDC170 and neighboring ESR1, plus intronic/splice-site mutations (c.775-31G>T, c.186+256G>T, c.58-15G>A) affecting mRNA processing, indicating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity causes disease phenotypes potentially involving hormone-responsive tissue dysfunction. The combination of coiled-coil scaffolding capacity, ESR1 interaction partner, fallopian tube-enriched expression, Golgi/cell junction localization, chromatin remodeler associations (ARMT1, DMAP1, VPS72, H2AZ), and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: CCDC170 functions as a coiled-coil scaffold bridging estrogen receptor signaling complexes with chromatin remodeling machinery, potentially recruiting ARMT1-mediated methylation and VPS72-H2AZ histone exchange to ESR1 target gene promoters in reproductive tissues, while also participating in Golgi-to-membrane trafficking via cell junction association, with dosage disruption causing disease through dysregulated estrogen-responsive gene networks affecting breast/reproductive development and cancer progression.
