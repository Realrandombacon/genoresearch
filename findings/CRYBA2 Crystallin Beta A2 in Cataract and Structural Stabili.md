## Description
CRYBA2 (Crystallin beta A2) is a well-studied human protein (UniProt P53672) located on chromosome 2q35, functioning as a key structural component of the vertebrate eye lens to maintain transparency and refractive index. It belongs to the Beta/gamma crystallin superfamily (InterPro IPR001064, PF00030), characterized by a conserved Greek key motif. Structurally, it is a highly stable, well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 92.6). Functionally, it interacts with other crystallins (CRYAA, CRYAB) and cytoskeletal proteins (BFSP1, CFAP65) via high-confidence STRING interactions. Clinically, it is associated with Autosomal Dominant Congenital and Presenile Cataracts, supported by 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar (including CNVs and SNVs like p.Ala80Thr) and confirmed by literature (37 papers). Expression data (HPA) shows tissue enhancement in pancreas and pituitary, with a molecular function tag for 'Eye lens protein' and disease involvement in 'Cataract'.
