## Description
CHRFAM7A (Gene ID: 89832) is a human-specific chimeric gene located at 15q13.2, formed by partial duplication of CHRNA7 (exons 5-10) fused with FAM7A (exons A-E). The canonical isoform (UniProt Q494W8) encodes 412 amino acids containing neurotransmitter-gated ion-channel domains (IPR006029, IPR006201, IPR006202) with ligand-binding (residues 28-140) and transmembrane regions (residues 147-396). AlphaFold structure prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 68.9), indicating partially ordered structure with potential disordered regions. STRING interactions reveal high-confidence binding to CHRNA7 (score 0.969) and OTUD7A (0.962), plus moderate interactions with other nAChR subunits (CHRNB2 0.686, CHRNA1 0.618). Mechanistically, CHRFAM7A functions as a dominant-negative regulator: it heteromerizes with full-length α7-nAChR pentamers but lacks complete pore-forming domains, reducing surface expression and channel conductance. ClinVar analysis identifies 97 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses spanning 15q11.2-13.3, associated with 15q13.3 microdeletion syndrome (CHD7, epilepsy, intellectual disability, schizophrenia susceptibility). HPA shows no expression data, consistent with pseudogene/partial duplication status. This gene is NOT truly "dark" but represents a human-specific regulatory element modulating cholinergic neurotransmission through stoichiometric interference with canonical α7-nAChR assembly.
