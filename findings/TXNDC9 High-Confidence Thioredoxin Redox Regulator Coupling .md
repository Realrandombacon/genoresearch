## Description
TXNDC9 (O14530, 226 aa) is a well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 84.3) cytosolic thioredoxin domain-containing protein functioning as a redox-sensitive signaling hub. InterPro analysis confirms a canonical thioredoxin fold (IPR013766, PF00085, residues 79-162) within the Phd_like_TxnDC9 subfamily (cd02989, residues 68-180), embedded in the thioredoxin-like superfamily (IPR036249, residues 25-187), indicating conserved disulfide reductase activity. HPA data reveals ubiquitous expression across all tissues with cytosolic localization and low specificity, consistent with fundamental redox homeostasis roles. STRING interactions define a striking functional network centered on 14-3-3 scaffolding proteins (YWHAG: 0.874, YWHAE: 0.852, YWHAB: 0.829, SFN: 0.851), the insulin receptor INSR (0.956), huntingtin HTT (0.864), and cytokeratin KRT17 (0.898), positioning TXNDC9 at the intersection of metabolic signaling, neurodegeneration, and epithelial integrity. Literature (only 20 papers) establishes oncogenic functions: TXNDC9 promotes colorectal cancer via HSP90 interaction and autophagy repression, gastric cancer through ZNF263-mediated transcription, lung adenocarcinoma via YWHAG targeting, and prostate cancer through oxidative stress-induced androgen receptor signaling. ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains/losses spanning chromosome 2q regions, implicating TXNDC9 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. The convergence of thioredoxin redox chemistry, 14-3-3 phospho-protein scaffolding, and insulin receptor coupling suggests TXNDC9 functions as a redox-sensitive switch regulating metabolic signaling fidelity, with therapeutic potential in cancer metabolism rewiring and neurodegenerative diseases involving huntingtin aggregation.
