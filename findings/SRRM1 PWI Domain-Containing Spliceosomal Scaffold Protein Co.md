## Description
SRRM1/Q8IYB3 (904 aa) is a partially structured (AlphaFold pLDDT: 51.6) serine/arginine-rich spliceosomal scaffold protein functioning as a critical regulator of pre-mRNA splicing and exon junction complex (EJC) assembly through its N-terminal PWI domain and C-terminal RS-rich disordered regions. InterPro analysis reveals canonical architecture containing an N-terminal PWI domain (IPR002483/PF01480/G3DSA:1.20.1390.10/SM00311/SSF101233, residues 27-126/46-115/32-126/40-115/25-133) for RNA-protein interactions within the PWI domain superfamily (IPR036483, residues 25-133), classified within the serine/arginine repetitive matrix protein family (IPR052225/PTHR23148, residues 1-454) characteristic of splicing factors, with extensive C-terminal intrinsically disordered RS-rich regions mediating phase separation and protein-protein interactions. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity but cell type-enhanced pattern, classification as essential proteins/plasma proteins/predicted intracellular proteins/transporters with mRNA processing and splicing biological processes (GO), DNA-binding and RNA-binding molecular functions, localized to nuclear speckles consistent with spliceosomal function.
