## Description
MIF4GD (A9UHW6) encodes a 222 amino acid MIF4G domain-containing protein that functions as a specialized translation regulation factor coupling ribosome biogenesis to replication-dependent histone mRNA translation during S-phase, while also modulating cell cycle progression through p27^Kip1 stability control and contributing to hepatic carcinogenesis. InterPro analysis reveals a compact single-domain architecture characteristic of translation initiation factor middle domains: a MIF4G-like type 3 domain (IPR003890/SM00543, positions 3-205) homologous to the middle domain of eukaryotic initiation factor 4G (eIF4G) mediating protein-protein interactions in translation complexes; an armadillo-type fold (IPR016024/SSF48371, positions 11-207) forming stacked alpha-helical repeats creating an extended interaction surface for RNA-binding partners; G3DSA helical superfamily (G3DSA:1.25.40.180, positions 2-222) indicating structural conservation; MIF4G domain repeat (PF02854, positions 7-203) confirming membership in the eIF4G-related protein family; and mRNA translation regulation/replication-dependent histone mRNA translation family classification (IPR051367/PTHR23254, positions 2-220/2-220) with RNA binding (GO:0003723) and protein binding (GO:0005515) molecular functions. AlphaFold predicts a very high confidence structure (pLDDT 91.8) consistent with extremely stable armadillo repeat fold required for precise partner recognition in translation complexes.
