## Description
LOC102723517 (Gene ID: 102723517) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 17q24.3 (NC_000017.11, positions 72057733-72034106, ~23.6 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number gains despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve to Ensembl ID, consistent with non-coding or highly tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: all are copy number gains/duplications (variant 155275: 17q24.3-25.3 ~13.2 Mb trisomy; variant 148363: 17q23.1-25.1 ~38.6 Mb trisomy; variant 58703: 17q24.3-25.3 ~13.9 Mb trisomy). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 17q24.3 chromosomal region contains critical cardiac electrophysiology, skeletal development, auditory function, and stress kinase signaling-associated genes including KCNJ2 (potassium inwardly rectifying channel subfamily J member 2/Kir2.1, inward rectifier potassium channel, cardiac action potential stabilization, Andersen-Tawil syndrome, short QT syndrome, atrial fibrillation), SOX9 (SRY-box transcription factor 9, HMG-box transcription factor, chondrocyte differentiation, testis determination, campomelic dysplasia, Pierre Robin sequence), KCNJ16 (potassium inwardly rectifying channel subfamily J member 16/Kir5.1, inward rectifier potassium channel heteromer partner, renal potassium handling, auditory function, EAST/SeSAME syndrome), MAP2K6 (mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase 6/MKK6, p38 MAPK kinase, stress response signaling, cell cycle arrest, inflammation, cancer), and LINC00511 (long intergenic non-protein coding RNA 511, oncogenic lncRNA, breast cancer progression, EMT regulation). Functional hypothesis: LOC102723517 likely represents a critical 17q24.3 cardiac/skeletal enhancer element within the KCNJ2-SOX9 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a cardiomyocyte/chondrocyte-specific enhancer coordinating KCNJ2-SOX9-KCNJ16-MAP2K6 expression in excitable tissues (cardiac myocytes, skeletal chondrocytes, inner ear hair cells) or as a chromatin boundary separating potassium channel genes (KCNJ2/KCNJ16) from transcription factor genes (SOX9) and kinase signaling genes (MAP2K6). The 3 pathogenic CNVs (all duplications 13.2-38.6 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where increased copy number disrupts KCNJ2-mediated cardiac repolarization and SOX9-mediated chondrogenesis. Disease mechanism involves KCNJ2 mutation causing Andersen-Tawil syndrome (periodic paralysis, ventricular arrhythmias, dysmorphic features, QT prolongation, U waves), KCNJ2 mutation causing short QT syndrome (atrial fibrillation, sudden cardiac death, abbreviated QT interval), KCNJ2 overexpression causing bradycardia (excessive repolarization, conduction delay), SOX9 haploinsufficiency causing campomelic dysplasia (bowing of long bones, sex reversal, cleft palate, respiratory insufficiency, perinatal lethality), SOX9 mutation causing Pierre Robin sequence (mandibular hypoplasia, glossoptosis, cleft palate, airway obstruction), SOX9 overexpression causing chondrosarcoma (cartilage tumor, uncontrolled chondrocyte proliferation), KCNJ16 mutation causing EAST/SeSAME syndrome (epilepsy, ataxia, sensorineural deafness, tubulopathy, hypokalemia), MAP2K6 overexpression causing cancer (p38 MAPK hyperactivation, cell survival, chemoresistance, inflammation), MAP2K6 dysfunction causing stress response defect (apoptosis susceptibility, impaired cytokine signaling), and 17q24.3 duplication syndrome presenting with cardiac arrhythmia, skeletal anomalies, and developmental delay. The 17q24.3 region is associated with cardiac-skeletal-auditory genetic variation. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (3 duplications 13.2-38.6 Mb) and KCNJ2-SOX9-KCNJ16-MAP2K6-LINC00511 neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a cardiac/skeletal-specific enhancer critical for electrophysiology-chondrogenesis-stress signaling coordination.
