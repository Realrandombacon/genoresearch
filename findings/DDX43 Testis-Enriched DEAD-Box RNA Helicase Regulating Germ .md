## Description
DDX43 (DEAD-Box Helicase 43, UniProt Q9NXZ2) is a 648-amino acid testis-enriched RNA helicase functioning as an ATP-dependent enzyme that unwinds RNA secondary structures to regulate germ cell development, spermatogenesis, and cancer-testis antigen expression networks. InterPro analysis confirms characteristic DEAD-box helicase architecture: N-terminal K Homology (KH) domain (IPR004087/IPR004088/G3DSA:3.30.1370.10, aa 66-133/76-129/70-160) providing RNA binding capability (GO:0003723); central DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545/cd17958, aa 266-436/253-449) containing the conserved DEAD motif (IPR000629, aa 394-402) essential for ATP hydrolysis; C-terminal helicase domain (IPR001650/cd18787, aa 460-621/461-589) completing the catalytic core; P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase superfamily (G3DSA:3.40.50.300, aa 187-451/454-626) providing Walker A/B motifs for ATP binding (GO:0005524) and hydrolysis; Helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001) defining the SF2 helicase family.
