# SLC25A18 (GC2): A Brain-Enriched Mitochondrial Glutamate Carrier Regulating Alzheimer's Pathology and Colorectal Cancer via Wnt Signaling and Metabolic Reprogramming

## Description
SLC25A18 (UniProt Q9H1K4) encodes a 315 amino acid mitochondrial glutamate carrier 2 (GC2) that transports glutamate from cytosol into the mitochondrial matrix via proton symport. InterPro analysis confirms canonical mitochondrial carrier architecture with three Solcar repeats (IPR002067, PF00153) spanning aa 6-308, forming the characteristic 6-transmembrane helix bundle (IPR018108, IPR023395). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q9H1K4-F1) predicts a well-folded transporter with confident stability (global pLDDT 73.1), consistent with active metabolite exchange. HPA expression reveals exceptional brain enrichment (139.0 nTPM) and liver expression (106.7 nTPM), with mitochondrial localization. Literature (16 papers) confirms roles in Alzheimer's disease (Aβ42-induced mitochondrial dysfunction and apoptosis) and colorectal cancer prognosis via Warburg effect suppression through Wnt signaling inhibition. ClinVar lists 67 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (predominantly copy number gains/losses on chr22q11.1-11.21), indicating strong clinical significance despite no specific monogenic disease annotation, suggesting contributions to neurodevelopmental disorders or metabolic syndromes. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners including GC (0.907), GUCA1C (0.760), SLC25A22 (0.601), and MTCH1 (0.579), placing GC2 within mitochondrial metabolite transport networks and apoptotic regulation. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A18 functions as a critical mitochondrial glutamate importer that maintains TCA cycle anaplerosis and neurotransmitter balance; its dysregulation contributes to Alzheimer's pathology via Aβ42-induced mitochondrial dysfunction and colorectal cancer progression via Wnt-mediated metabolic reprogramming, where copy number variations disrupt glutamate homeostasis leading to neurodegeneration or tumor growth.
