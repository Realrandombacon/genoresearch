## Description
ALDH8A1 (Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 8 Family Member A1, also known as 2-aminomuconic semialdehyde dehydrogenase or ALDH12) is a 487 amino acid nucleoplasmic/centrosomal enzyme (UniProt Q9H2A2) with moderate characterization (36 papers) that functions as a critical oxidoreductase in the kynurenine pathway of tryptophan catabolism. The protein contains a canonical aldehyde dehydrogenase domain (IPR015590/PF00171, residues 23-483) with oxidoreductase activity (GO:0016491), featuring a cysteine active site (IPR016160, residues 280-291) and glutamic acid active site (IPR029510, residues 252-259) essential for catalytic function, placing it in the aldehyde/histidinol dehydrogenase superfamily (IPR016161, residues 7-486). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with ACMSD (0.961, aminocarboxymuconate semialdehyde decarboxylase—direct kynurenine pathway enzyme), EPHX2 (0.819, epoxide hydrolase), ALDH18A1 (0.723, aldehyde dehydrogenase 18), HSDL2 (0.721, hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase-like protein), ALDH6A1 (0.718, methylmalonate semialdehyde dehydrogenase), AGXT (0.642, alanine-glyoxylate aminotransferase), ADHFE1 (0.627, alcohol dehydrogenase), HBS1L (0.618), APOC2 (0.600, apolipoprotein C2), and ADH4 (0.596, alcohol dehydrogenase 4), placing ALDH8A1 in metabolic enzyme networks spanning tryptophan catabolism, lipid metabolism, and alcohol metabolism. The 2018 landmark study (27 citations) reassigned ALDH8A1 from ethanol metabolism to the kynurenine pathway, where it converts 2-aminomuconic semialdehyde to 2-aminomuconate, regulating quinolinic acid production and NAD+ biosynthesis. HPA data shows liver-enriched expression (231.1 nTPM, group enriched), kidney expression (67.2 nTPM), cancer group enriched pattern, immune cell enhanced specificity, and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and centrosome. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses and deletions at 6q23-24, though no specific diseases are directly assigned—suggesting potential metabolic, neurodevelopmental, or hepatic phenotypes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 97.4) indicates exceptional confidence with a perfectly folded aldehyde dehydrogenase TIM barrel structure. Functional hypothesis: ALDH8A1 acts as 2-aminomuconic semialdehyde dehydrogenase in the kynurenine pathway that converts 2-aminomuconic semialdehyde to 2-aminomuconate, regulating the branch point between quinolinic acid (NAD+ precursor, NMDA receptor agonist, neurotoxic) and picolinic acid production, with hepatic expression supporting systemic tryptophan catabolism, centrosomal localization suggesting cell cycle regulation, and ACMSD partnership controlling metabolic flux toward NAD+ biosynthesis versus neurotoxic quinolinic acid accumulation, making it a therapeutic target for neurodegenerative diseases (Alzheimer's, Huntington's), psychiatric disorders (depression, schizophrenia), immune dysregulation, and hepatic metabolic disorders through modulation of kynurenine pathway metabolites.
