## Description
ANKRD36C (Gene ID: 124907565, UniProt Q5JPF3) encodes a large 1778 amino acid intracellular scaffold protein located on chromosome 2q11.1. InterPro domain analysis reveals extensive ankyrin repeat architecture (IPR002110, IPR036770, PF00023, PF13637, PF14915) spanning residues 24-279 with multiple repeat units (positions 36-129, 130-162, 163-228, 64-96, 97-129, 130-162), plus a C-terminal CCDC144C-like coiled-coil domain (IPR039497, PF14915, residues 1361-1716). Ankyrin repeats are well-established protein-protein interaction motifs that mediate scaffold assembly in signaling complexes. Human Protein Atlas shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in salivary gland (14.6 nTPM) and bone marrow (13.9 nTPM), with predicted nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization. STRING database identified 10 interaction partners including near-paralogs ANKRD36 (0.999 confidence) and ANKRD36B (0.896 confidence), plus cell cycle regulators CDKN2D (p19INK4d, 0.499) and CDKN2C (p18INK4c, 0.480), transcription factor ZNF717 (0.507), and FAHD2B (0.519). The interaction with CDKN2D/CDKN2C cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitors suggests ANKRD36C may function as a scaffold protein that modulates cell cycle progression through INK4 family regulation. ClinVar identified 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number losses or gains with uncertain significance (?) spanning chromosome 2q11.1-11.2 regions, indicating the locus is associated with genomic instability but no specific monogenic disease. The combination of large size, extensive ankyrin repeat scaffold domain, cell cycle regulator interactions, and hematopoietic/bone marrow expression suggests ANKRD36C functions as a nuclear/cytoplasmic scaffold protein that organizes cell cycle regulatory complexes, potentially coordinating INK4-mediated G1/S checkpoint control in proliferating cells.
