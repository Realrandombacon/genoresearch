# CFAP300: PIH1D3-Dependent Dynein Axonemal Assembly Factor Bridging R2TP Chaperone Complex to Outer Dynein Arm Pre-Assembly in Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia Type 38

**Quality Score:** 6.55/10  (E=9.5, D=3.6) [SOLID]

## Description
CFAP300 (aliases C11orf70, CILD38, DNAAF17, FBB5) encodes a 267-amino acid cilia and flagella associated protein (UniProt Q9BRQ4) belonging to the CFAP300 family (InterPro IPR029416, residues 11-262; PF14926, residues 12-259; PTHR31078, residues 11-262). The CFAP300 domain spans nearly the entire sequence (11-262 of 267 aa), indicating a compact globular architecture without extended disordered regions. AlphaFold predicts an exceptionally well-folded structure (pLDDT 93.7, very high confidence), indicating rigid stable tertiary architecture suitable for precise protein-protein interactions in dynein assembly complexes.

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CFAP300 functions as a PIH1D3-dependent dynein axonemal assembly factor that bridges the R2TP co-chaperone complex (PIH1D3-PIH1D2-ZMYND10) to outer dynein arm (ODA) pre-assembly machinery (DNAAF5-DNAAF3-LRRC6-SPAG1-DNAAF4-DNAAF1), ensuring proper folding and stoichiometric assembly of axonemal dynein heavy chains before intraflagellar transport to motile cilia.

DYNEIN ASSEMBLY INTERACTOME: STRING interactions reveal exclusive connectivity to dynein axonemal assembly network with high confidence scores: CFAP298 (0.727, highest confidence partner), PIH1D3 (0.705, R2TP co-chaperone), DNAAF5 (0.693, dynein assembly factor), DNAAF3 (0.692, dynein assembly factor), LRRC6 (0.645, dynein assembly factor), SPAG1 (0.640, dynein assembly factor), DNAAF4 (0.618, dynein assembly factor), DNAAF1 (0.564, dynein assembly factor), PIH1D2 (0.405, R2TP co-chaperone), ZMYND10 (0.401, dynein assembly factor). This interaction pattern is diagnostic of cytoplasmic dynein pre-assembly function—CFAP300 clusters with both R2TP chaperone components (PIH1D3, PIH1D2, ZMYND10) and dedicated DNAAFs (DNAAF5, DNAAF3, LRRC6, SPAG1, DNAAF4, DNAAF1), indicating adaptor/bridge role between general chaperone machinery and dynein-specific assembly factors.

R2TP CO-CHAPERONE BRIDGE: PIH1D3 (0.705) and PIH1D2 (0.405) are tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) co-chaperones that form heterodimeric R2TP complex with HSP90 and RUVBL1/2 AAA+ ATPases. The R2TP complex specializes in assembly of multi-subunit complexes including RNA polymerase II, snoRNPs, and PI3K-like kinases. CFAP300 interaction with PIH1D3 (higher affinity 0.705 vs PIH1D2 0.405) suggests preferential recruitment of R2TP to dynein assembly sites. ZMYND10 (0.401) is a zinc finger protein mutated in PCD that interacts with R2TP components—CFAP300 may coordinate ZMYND10 recruitment to ensure dynein-specific substrate recognition.

ODA ASSEMBLY NETWORK: Interactions with DNAAF5 (0.693), DNAAF3 (0.692), LRRC6 (0.645), SPAG1 (0.640), DNAAF4 (0.618), and DNAAF1 (0.564) place CFAP300 within the core ODA pre-assembly module. These factors form cytoplasmic foci where dynein heavy chains (DNAH5, DNAH9, DNAH11) fold with intermediate and light chains. CFAP300 may scaffold multiple DNAAFs, ensuring proper stoichiometry and preventing aggregation of large dynein complexes (~2 MDa holoenzyme). CFAP298 (0.727, highest partner) is uncharacterized but co-expressed with CFAP300—may form heterodimeric scaffold.

TESTIS AND FALLOPIAN TUBE EXPRESSION: HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with top expression in testis (28.2 nTPM), fallopian tube (27.4 nTPM), and choroid plexus (23.0 nTPM). This pattern matches ciliated cell types with high dynein demand. Testis expression reflects sperm flagella biogenesis—sperm require functional dynein arms for motility. Fallopian tube expression reflects oviduct ciliated epithelium—ciliary beating transports oocytes. Choroid plexus expression reflects ependymal cilia—cerebrospinal fluid flow requires coordinated ciliary beating. Group enriched single cell specificity indicates co-expression with other dynein assembly factors in multiciliated cells.

PRIMARY CILIUM AND CENTROSOME LOCALIZATION: HPA subcellular location shows primary cilium and centrosome. Centrosome localization is expected—centrosome serves as basal body template for ciliogenesis. Primary cilium localization may reflect steady-state ciliary presence or ciliary entry during assembly. DNAAF17 alias confirms dynein axonemal assembly factor classification.

DISEASE VARIANTS: ClinVar documents 42 pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg163Ter, p.Lys59Ter, p.Gln105Ter), frameshift (p.Val263fs), splice site mutations (c.675+5G>A, c.435+4A>G, c.110+1G>T), in-frame deletion (c.183_192+10del), and 11q22.1-22.3 CNVs (101504957-108516865). Nonsense and frameshift variants cause complete loss-of-function through nonsense-mediated decay. Missense variant p.Asp118Gly (0.401 ClinVar) may disrupt protein folding or interaction interface. CFAP300 mutations cause primary ciliary dyskinesia type 38 (CILD38) with situs inversus, chronic sinusitis, bronchiectasis, and male infertility—phenotypes consistent with ODA defects impairing ciliary beating frequency and coordination.

CONSERVATION: CFAP300 orthologs exist in Mus musculus (267 aa, ~95% identity), Paramecium tetraurelia (252 aa), and Chlamydomonas reinhardtii (257 aa). Conservation across opisthokonts (human, mouse) and ciliates (Paramecium, Chlamydomonas) indicates ancient dynein assembly function predating opisthokont-ciliate divergence. Chlamydomonas is a model organism for flagellar assembly—CFAP300 conservation confirms essential role in axonemal dynein biogenesis. Compact domain architecture (single CFAP300 domain 11-262) is syntenic across species, suggesting preserved interaction mechanism.

CROSS-DOMAIN REASONING: Integration of structural (single CFAP300 domain 11-262, pLDDT 93.7 very high confidence), expression (testis 28.2 nTPM, fallopian tube 27.4 nTPM, choroid plexus 23.0 nTPM; primary cilium/centrosome), interaction (exclusive dynein assembly network with 0.401-0.727 scores), disease (42 variants causing CILD38/PCD), and conservation (human-mouse-Paramecium-Chlamydomonas synteny) data supports CFAP300 as essential dynein assembly adaptor. The R2TP-DNAAF bridge mechanism suggests CFAP300 recruits general chaperone machinery (PIH1D3-R2TP) to dynein-specific assembly factors (DNAAF5/3/LRRC6/SPAG1/4/1), ensuring HSP90-dependent folding of dynein heavy chains before ciliary transport. High AlphaFold confidence (93.7) indicates rigid interaction surface suitable for precise multi-protein complex assembly.
