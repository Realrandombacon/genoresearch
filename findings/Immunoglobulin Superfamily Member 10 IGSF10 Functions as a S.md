## Description
IGSF10 (Immunoglobulin superfamily member 10) is a massive 2623 aa secreted glycoprotein functioning as an extracellular matrix (ECM) scaffold and developmental regulator. With 57 literature references, IGSF10 represents a 'partially characterized' dark gene where its role in GnRH neuron migration and bone development is established, but its emerging functions as a RET receptor antagonist in Ewing sarcoma and a tumor suppressor in lung adenocarcinoma are recent discoveries (2024-2025). InterPro analysis reveals a complex multi-domain architecture typical of large ECM proteins: an N-terminal region containing Leucine-Rich Repeats (LRRs) (IPR001611/IPR003591, aa 57-141) flanked by cysteine-rich regions (IPR000483, aa 219-280), followed by multiple Immunoglobulin-like domains (IPR003598/IPR003599/IPR007110/IPR013098, scattered across aa 461-1741 and 2546-2612) that mediate protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 58.4), reflecting the inherent flexibility of large secreted scaffolds with long linker regions between rigid Ig/LRR domains. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression, highest in ovary (21.1 nTPM), with localization to vesicles and predicted secretion, consistent with an ECM component. STRING interactions reveal a functional network centered on neuronal guidance and adhesion: IGSF10 associates with Adhesion GPCRs (ADGRL1/2/3: 0.514-0.526), collagen components (COL12A1: 0.490), and critical developmental regulators including PROKR2 (0.447), GNRH1 (0.416), and transcription factors ZNF605/ZSCAN29 (0.412-0.428) linked to Kallmann syndrome. ClinVar lists 41 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 3q22.1-29, missense mutations (p.Ser1396Phe, p.Asn349Ser, p.Asp2277Gly, p.Ile2420Thr, p.Ser962Asn, p.Arg156Leu), and frameshift deletions (p.Gln433fs), definitively linking IGSF10 haploinsufficiency to self-limited delayed puberty and hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. Recent literature (2024-2025) establishes IGSF10 as: (1) Direct antagonist of the RET receptor tyrosine kinase, inhibiting Ewing sarcoma growth and regulating GnRH neuron migration; (2) Inhibitor of lung adenocarcinoma metastasis via the Spi-B/Integrin-β1 signaling pathway; (3) Promoter of osteogenic bone formation alleviating metabolic bone disorders; (4) Attenuator of periodontitis-induced bone resorption via anti-inflammatory mechanisms.
