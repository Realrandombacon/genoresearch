## Description
SH3BGR encodes a 239 aa SH3 domain-binding glutamic acid-rich protein (UniProt P55822), also known as 21-GARP, functioning as a chromosome 21-localized adaptor protein with glutaredoxin-like fold implicated in Down syndrome-associated cardiac defects, angiogenesis, and STAT3-mediated cell migration. The protein contains a GRX_SH3BGR domain (cd03030, aa65-160) with glutaredoxin-like structural fold (G3DSA:3.40.30.10, aa60-176) belonging to the thioredoxin-like superfamily (IPR036249/SSF52833, aa63-159), suggesting potential redox-sensing or protein-protein interaction capabilities despite lacking canonical catalytic cysteines. AlphaFold prediction shows medium confidence (pLDDT 66.4), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered glutaredoxin-like core and disordered N/C-terminal regions typical of adaptor proteins. HPA shows group-enriched expression with exceptional skeletal muscle expression (1188.4 nTPM) and tongue expression (573.1 nTPM), plasma membrane and cytosolic localization, cancer-enhanced and immune cell-enhanced specificity. STRING interactions reveal SH3BGR associates with chromatin regulator HMGN1 (0.878, high confidence), cyclic nucleotide-gated channel CNGB1 (0.773), lebercilin homolog LCA5L (0.658), defensin DEFB127 (0.571), GET1 (0.440, ER membrane insertion), XKR6 (0.431, phospholipid scramblase), MTMR9 (0.430, myotubularin phosphatase), IMPA1 (0.430, inositol monophosphatase), EIF2B3 (0.428, translation initiation), and PSMG1 (0.425, proteasome assembly). ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of 21q11.2-22.3 copy number gains (trisomy 21) and losses, directly linking SH3BGR dosage to Down syndrome phenotypes. Literature (39 papers) demonstrates SH3BGR is triplicated in Down syndrome contributing to congenital heart defects (CHD), METTL3-mediated m6A methylation regulates SH3BGR expression affecting cardiomyocyte apoptosis, SH3BGR/STAT3 pathway regulates cell migration and angiogenesis induced by KSHV microRNA in Kaposi sarcoma, SH3BGR affects energy metabolism in neonatal cardiomyocytes, and SH3BGR family members show conserved expression patterns in zebrafish development. SH3BGR likely functions as a redox-sensitive adaptor coupling chromatin state (via HMGN1 interaction) to transcriptional programs controlling cardiac morphogenesis and angiogenesis. The glutaredoxin-like fold may sense cellular redox status during heart development, while SH3-binding capacity enables recruitment of signaling complexes to specific genomic loci. Trisomy 21-mediated SH3BGR overexpression disrupts stoichiometric balance of cardiac transcriptional complexes, contributing to the 40-50% CHD incidence in Down syndrome. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests oncogenic potential through STAT3 pathway activation and angiogenesis promotion.
