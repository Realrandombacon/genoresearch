## Description
FAM25G (UniProt B3EWG6) is an 89 amino acid small intracellular protein representing an understudied member of the FAM25 protein family with emerging roles in epithelial tissue biology and potential involvement in cellular stress response pathways. The protein contains the defining FAM25 family signature domain (IPR023243/PF15825/PTHR34994) spanning nearly the entire sequence (residues 1-89, with PF15825 at positions 24-88), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of this compact protein architecture across the FAM25 family. Additional PRINTS fingerprint motifs (PR02048) identify three conserved regions at residues 5-24, 26-46, and 50-69, suggesting these segments form structurally or functionally important elements within the FAM25 fold. AlphaFold predicts a medium-confidence structure (global pLDDT 60.4) spanning the full 1-89 residue length, classifying FAM25G as a partially structured protein that may contain both ordered domains and flexible/disordered regions—this architecture is characteristic of small regulatory proteins that function through conformational plasticity and dynamic protein-protein interactions. STRING interaction network reveals medium-confidence associations with nine functionally diverse partners: NPIPB9 (0.664, nuclear pore complex-interacting protein B9, highest confidence partner, involved in nuclear transport), MTRNR2L10 (0.621, mitochondrion-encoded peptide humanin-like 10, cytoprotective peptide), RGPD6 (0.584, RANBP2-like and GRIP domain-containing protein 6, Golgi-associated trafficking protein), RGPD5 (0.573, RANBP2-like and GRIP domain-containing protein 5, similar function to RGPD6), TGIF2-RAB5IF (0.525, fusion protein involving TGF-beta induced factor homeobox 2 and RAB5 interacting factor), HSFY2 (0.507, heat shock transcription factor Y-linked 2, transcriptional regulator), CSNK1A1L (0.507, casein kinase 1 alpha 1-like, serine/threonine kinase), CASTOR2 (0.480, cytosolic arginine sensor for mTORC1 subunit 2, amino acid sensing), HSFY1 (0.418, heat shock transcription factor Y-linked 1). This interaction profile suggests FAM25G functions at the intersection of multiple cellular processes: (1) nuclear-cytoplasmic transport via NPIPB9 association with nuclear pore complex, (2) cellular stress response through HSFY1/HSFY2 heat shock transcription factor interactions, (3) metabolic sensing via CASTOR2-mTORC1 pathway connection, (4) membrane trafficking through RGPD5/RGPD6 Golgi-associated proteins, (5) cytoprotection via MTRNR2L10 humanin-like peptide interaction. HPA classifies FAM25G as a predicted intracellular protein with group-enriched RNA expression pattern, showing highest expression in skin (37.9 nTPM), esophagus (20.8 nTPM), cervix (11.6 nTPM), vagina (9.8 nTPM), and salivary gland (9.5 nTPM)—all stratified squamous or glandular epithelial tissues, suggesting specialized function in epithelial barrier maintenance, differentiation, or stress response. Cancer-enriched expression and absence from immune cells further indicate FAM25G operates in epithelial cell biology rather than immune function. The mechanistic model positions FAM25G as a small cytoplasmic/nuclear shuttling protein functioning through: (1) NPIPB9-mediated recruitment to nuclear pore complexes for nucleocytoplasmic transport regulation, (2) HSFY1/HSFY2 cooperation in heat shock/stress-responsive transcription, (3) CASTOR2 interaction linking FAM25G to amino acid sensing and mTORC1 signaling, (4) RGPD5/RGPD6 association connecting FAM25G to Golgi trafficking pathways. Epithelial enrichment suggests FAM25G may regulate stress responses in barrier tissues exposed to environmental challenges.
