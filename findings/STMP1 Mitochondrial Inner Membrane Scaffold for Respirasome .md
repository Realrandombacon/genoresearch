# STMP1: Mitochondrial Inner Membrane Scaffold for Respirasome Assembly

## Description
STMP1 encodes a 47-amino acid short transmembrane mitochondrial protein (UniProt E0CX11) that localizes to the mitochondrial inner membrane and functions in cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV) and respirasome assembly. The protein contains a DUF4535 domain (PF15054, residues 5-44) and is highly conserved across vertebrates (mouse: 47 aa, zebrafish: 46 aa, >95% identity). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded transmembrane helix structure with high confidence (pLDDT 84.8). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with mitochondrial localization, consistent with its housekeeping role in oxidative phosphorylation. STRING interactions reveal associations with C2orf81 (0.620), GOLGA6L7 (0.507), and other coiled-coil proteins, suggesting STMP1 acts as a scaffold recruiting assembly factors to the inner membrane. ClinVar contains 34 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and splice variants, indicating disease relevance when disrupted. The small size (47 aa) and single transmembrane domain architecture suggests STMP1 functions as a membrane anchor that stabilizes nascent Complex IV subunits during respirasome biogenesis, potentially bridging the inner membrane with soluble assembly chaperones. Loss of STMP1 would impair mitochondrial respiration, explaining the pathogenic variant burden.
