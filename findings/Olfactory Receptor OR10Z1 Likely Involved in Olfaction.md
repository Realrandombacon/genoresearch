## Description
OR10Z1 is a member of the olfactory receptor family, with a predicted 7-transmembrane structure. It interacts with G-protein coupled receptor signaling molecules (GNAL, ARRB2, ARRB1) and is specifically expressed in sensory tissues, particularly in the olfactory epithelium. The presence of 15 pathogenic variants suggests its potential involvement in olfactory disorders.
