## Description
UGGT1 encodes a 1555 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum luminal enzyme (UniProt Q9NYU2), also known as UDP-Glucose:Glycoprotein Glucosyltransferase 1, that functions as the central folding sensor of the ER quality control (ERQC) system, recognizing misfolded N-glycoproteins and reglucosylating their N-glycans to regenerate monoglucosylated forms that rebind calnexin/calreticulin chaperones for additional refolding attempts.
