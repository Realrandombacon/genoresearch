## Description
FOXL3 (Forkhead Box Protein L3, UniProt A0A1W2PRP0) is a 233 amino acid intracellular transcription factor with limited human characterization (40 papers total, predominantly in teleost fish models) representing a genuine dark gene in human biology that functions as a germ cell-autonomous sexual switch regulating oogenesis commitment through antagonistic interactions with DMRT1 and transcriptional control of steroidogenic enzymes. The protein contains a highly conserved Forkhead domain (IPR001766/PF00250 residues 32-114) with winged helix DNA-binding domain superfamily architecture (G3DSA:1.10.10.10 residues 20-131) conferring DNA-binding transcription factor activity (GO:0003700), sequence-specific DNA binding (GO:0043565), and regulation of DNA-templated transcription (GO:0006355). STRING interactions reveal medium-confidence partnerships defining sex determination pathways: DMRT1 (0.590, Doublesex and mab-3 related transcription factor 1—antagonistic regulator of germline sex), FBXO47 (0.507, F-box protein 47—downstream of foxl3 in medaka), KLHL23 (0.437, Kelch-like protein 23), DMRT2 (0.433, Doublesex and mab-3 related transcription factor 2), CYP19A1 (0.419, Aromatase—estrogen synthesis enzyme), AMH (0.415, Anti-Müllerian hormone—male sex differentiation), and DMRTA2 (0.411, DMRT-like protein A2), confirming integration into germline sex determination and steroidogenic pathways. HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (2.6 nTPM) with tissue-enriched RNA specificity, cell type-enriched single cell specificity, and classification as predicted intracellular proteins with transcription/DNA-binding functions, indicating restricted expression to germ cells or testicular somatic cells.
