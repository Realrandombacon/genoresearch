# RARS1: Cytoplasmic Arginyl-tRNA Synthetase with Emerging Roles in Leukodystrophy and Ferroptosis Regulation

**Date:** 2026-05-07T11:07:44.806929

**Quality Score:** 8/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
RARS1 encodes a 660-amino acid cytoplasmic arginyl-tRNA synthetase (ArgRS, UniProt P54136) that catalyzes ATP-dependent ligation of arginine to its cognate tRNA, a fundamental step in protein biosynthesis. The protein contains a well-defined domain architecture: N-terminal arginyl-tRNA synthetase domain (IPR005148, positions 78-166), catalytic core domain (cd00671, positions 193-457) with class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase signature (IPR001412, positions 201-212), and DALR anticodon-binding domain (IPR008909, positions 534-660) for tRNA recognition. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 92.1, residues 1-660) indicating stable tertiary folding across all functional domains. STRING interaction analysis reveals RARS1 forms part of the multi-tRNA synthetase complex (MSC) with ultra-high confidence interactions: MARS1 (0.999), LARS1 (0.999), QARS1 (0.999), KARS1 (0.999), EPRS1 (0.999), IARS1 (0.999), DARS1 (0.996), and scaffolding proteins AIMP1 (0.999), AIMP2 (0.994), EEF1E1 (0.996), confirming its role as an integral MSC component. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasm, nucleoli, and cytosol localization, consistent with housekeeping roles in translation. ClinVar contains 93 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (p.Gly528fs), missense mutations in catalytic domains (p.His237Arg, p.Cys369Phe, p.Val346Ile, p.His595Arg), and splice variants (c.369+1G>A), causing hypomyelinating leukodystrophy-9 (HLD-9) and developmental/epileptic encephalopathy. With only 16 publications, RARS1 is partially characterized: recent studies reveal RARS1 integration into MSC is crucial for mammalian brain development (2025), RARS1 inhibits ENO1 ubiquitination/degradation to protect against ferroptosis in hepatocellular carcinoma (2025), and biallelic variants cause CNS manifestations including hypomyelination and epilepsy (2023-2024). The functional hypothesis: RARS1 has dual functions—canonical tRNA charging for protein synthesis and non-canonical MSC-mediated signaling regulating neuronal myelination and metabolic stress response; disease variants may disrupt MSC assembly or catalytic efficiency, causing tissue-specific vulnerability in white matter and developing brain through impaired translation fidelity and altered ENO1-mediated glycolytic/ferroptosis pathways.
