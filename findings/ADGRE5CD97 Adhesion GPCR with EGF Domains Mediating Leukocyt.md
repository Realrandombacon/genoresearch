## Description
ADGRE5 (Adhesion G Protein-Coupled Receptor E5, alias CD97, TM7LN1) is an 835-amino acid type II transmembrane protein located on chromosome 19p13.12. InterPro analysis reveals a characteristic EGF-TM7 architecture: five N-terminal EGF-like calcium-binding domains (IPR001881, positions 25-208) for ligand binding, a GPCR proteolysis site (GPS motif, IPR000203, positions 491-542) mediating autoproteolytic cleavage into extracellular and transmembrane subunits, and a 7-transmembrane rhodopsin-like domain (G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, positions 542-817) belonging to secretin-like GPCR family 2 (IPR000832). Human Protein Atlas shows highest expression in bone marrow (200.1 nTPM) and blood vessels (165.3 nTPM), with plasma membrane and Golgi localization consistent with receptor trafficking. STRING interactions identify CD55 (complement decay-accelerating factor, score 0.999) as the primary binding partner, plus LPAR1 (0.973), GNA12 (0.811), CD59 (0.778), and THY1 (0.751), indicating roles in complement regulation and G-protein signaling. AlphaFold structure (P48960) shows high confidence (pLDDT 78.1), reflecting stable folded domains. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic variants including missense (p.Ile759Ser) and copy number alterations. ADGRE5 functions as a leukocyte activation marker that binds CD55 to regulate complement-mediated cell lysis, with the EGF domains determining ligand specificity and the GPS-mediated cleavage enabling tethered agonist signaling through Gα12/13 pathways.
