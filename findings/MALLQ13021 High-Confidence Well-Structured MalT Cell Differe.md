## Description
MALL (Mal, T cell differentiation protein like, also known as BENE, MAL-like protein) is a 153 amino acid tetraspanning membrane proteolipid (UniProt Q13021) with high-confidence well-structured architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 89.9), characteristic of MARVEL domain-containing proteins that function through membrane raft organization and vesicular trafficking in epithelial and endothelial cells. The protein contains a MARVEL domain (IPR008253, residues 22-153; PS51225, residues 22-153), belongs to the myelin and lymphocyte (MAL) protein family (IPR013295, residues 36-48/57-70/88-103; PR01884, residues 36-48/57-70/88-103), MARVEL domain-containing and chemokine-like factor proteins family (IPR050578, residues 18-148; PTHR22776, residues 18-148), and contains a membrane-associating domain (PF01284, residues 24-148). MALL is located on chromosome 2q13 and is predicted to be a membrane protein involved in raft-mediated trafficking, located in glycolipid- and cholesterol-enriched membrane (GEM) rafts.
