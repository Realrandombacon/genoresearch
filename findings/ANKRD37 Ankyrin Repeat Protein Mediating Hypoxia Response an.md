## Description
ANKRD37 (Gene ID: 84272, UniProt Q7Z713) is a 158 amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 4q35.1 (NC_000004.12). InterPro domain analysis reveals a canonical ankyrin repeat architecture characteristic of protein-protein interaction scaffolds: multiple ankyrin repeat domains (IPR002110/PF12796/SM00248, positions 30-59/63-92/96-125) forming three tandem repeats within an ankyrin repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR036770/G3DSA:1.25.40.20, positions 1-123). ANKRD37 belongs to the ankyrin repeat and cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor family (IPR050776/PTHR24201, positions 8-154), suggesting potential roles in cell cycle regulation or protein complex assembly. AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q7Z713-F1) yields a high-confidence model (global pLDDT 72.3), indicating ANKRD37 is a well-folded protein with stable tertiary structure typical of ankyrin repeat proteins that mediate specific protein-protein interactions through their curved solenoid architecture. Human Protein Atlas classifies ANKRD37 as a predicted intracellular protein localized to nucleoplasm and cytosol; expressed in all tissues with low tissue specificity and low cancer specificity, functioning as a group-enriched protein at single-cell resolution. The ubiquitous expression pattern suggests housekeeping or fundamental cellular functions. The BNIP3/HIF1A/NDRG1/P4HA1 interaction network strongly implicates ANKRD37 in hypoxia response pathways. ClinVar lists 115 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number losses or gains spanning 4q regions (multi-megabase CNVs from ~100-190Mb affecting many genes); no specific monogenic disease is attributed solely to ANKRD37, but 4q deletions cause various developmental syndromes. Conservation is evident across mammals with mouse ortholog Q569N2 (Ankrd37, 159 aa, ~90% identity), indicating strong evolutionary constraint on ankyrin repeat structure. Functional hypothesis: ANKRD37 functions as a protein-protein interaction scaffold that modulates hypoxia response signaling by bridging HIF1A transcriptional machinery with downstream effectors like BNIP3 and NDRG1. The ankyrin repeat domains provide a stable structural platform for assembling multiprotein complexes during cellular stress responses. Potential roles include regulating apoptosis under hypoxic conditions (via BNIP3 interaction), coordinating collagen biosynthesis during tissue remodeling (via P4HA1), or modulating transcriptional elongation (via AFF4/CDK8). Disease mechanism: Given absence of specific ANKRD37 point mutations in ClinVar (only large CNVs), this gene is unlikely to cause monogenic disease when disrupted alone; however, ANKRD37 haploinsufficiency within 4q deletion syndromes may contribute to developmental phenotypes alongside other deleted genes. Priority experiments include co-IP validation of BNIP3/HIF1A/NDRG1 interactions, hypoxia chamber experiments to assess ANKRD37 expression changes under low oxygen, CRISPR knockout to evaluate effects on hypoxia-induced apoptosis and HIF1A target gene expression, and investigation of ANKRD37 variants in cancer cohorts where hypoxia response is dysregulated.
