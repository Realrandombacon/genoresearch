## Description
AKR7A3 encodes a 331-amino acid aldo-keto reductase family 7 member A3 (UniProt O95154), also known as aflatoxin B1 aldehyde reductase member 3, an exceptionally well-folded cytosolic enzyme functioning in aldehyde/ketone detoxification through NADP-dependent oxidoreductase domain architecture.
