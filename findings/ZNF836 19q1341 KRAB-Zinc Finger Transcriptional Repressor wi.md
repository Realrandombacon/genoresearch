## Description
ZNF836 (Zinc finger protein 836, UniProt Q6ZNA1) is a 936 amino acid transcription factor encoded on chromosome 19q13.41. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical KRAB-ZFP architecture: an N-terminal KRAB domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 7-83) serving as a potent transcriptional repression module, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger domains (IPR013087, PF00096, at least 3 clusters spanning residues 218-301) that mediate sequence-specific DNA binding. Additional zinc finger regions (residues 408-714) suggest extended DNA recognition capacity. The AlphaFold structure prediction failed (server error), but the domain architecture strongly suggests a modular repressor structure typical of the KRAB-ZFP family.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis shows surprisingly limited partners: IPP (inosine pyrophosphatase, score 0.542) and CBWD6 (cobalamin biosynthesis protein homolog, score 0.430). This sparse interactome is atypical for KRAB-ZFPs, which usually show strong co-expression networks with other ZNFs. This may indicate ZNF836 functions more independently or targets a specialized gene set. Human Protein Atlas data classifies ZNF836 as "Tissue enhanced" with expression detected in all tissues but peak expression in the adrenal gland (25.5 nTPM), suggesting a potential role in adrenal steroidogenesis or stress response regulation. It is annotated as a transcription factor involved in transcription regulation.\n\nClinVar records 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, with a striking and unusual pattern: ALL 15 variants are copy number gains (duplications), ranging from focal 19q13.41 amplifications (~6 Mb) to whole-arm 19q duplications (~32 Mb) and even whole chromosome 19 trisomy events. No deletions are recorded. This extreme duplication bias (15:0 ratio) is highly unusual and indicates that ZNF836 is exquisitely dosage-sensitive to overexpression but tolerant of haploinsufficiency. These CNVs are associated with 19q duplication syndromes, characterized by developmental delay, intellectual disability, and dysmorphic features.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ZNF836 functions as a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor that silences adrenal-specific genes or stress-response pathways under basal conditions. Its KRAB domain recruits KAP1/TRIM28 and the heterochromatin machinery (HP1, SETDB1) to deposit H3K9me3 marks at target promoters. The adrenal gland enrichment suggests it may repress steroidogenic genes (e.g., CYP11B1, CYP21A2) or catecholamine synthesis enzymes in non-adrenal tissues, or fine-tune their expression within adrenal cortex cells. The extreme duplication bias indicates that ZNF836 overexpression causes pathogenic hyper-repression: triplication leads to ectopic silencing of essential developmental genes or saturation of the KAP1 co-repressor pool, disrupting global heterochromatin balance. The absence of deletion phenotypes suggests functional redundancy with other 19q ZNFs or that 50% expression is sufficient for normal function. The weak STRING network may reflect cell-type specific interactions not captured in general databases, or a more specialized regulatory role focused on adrenal/stress pathways.
