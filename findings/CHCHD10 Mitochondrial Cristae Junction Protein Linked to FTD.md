## Description
CHCHD10 encodes a 142-amino acid mitochondrial protein localized to cristae junctions in the intermembrane space. The protein contains a canonical CHCH domain (IPR010625, PF06747, residues 102-133) with a C-X9-C motif characteristic of mitochondrial intermembrane space proteins. AlphaFold structure prediction (Q8WYQ3) shows medium confidence (pLDDT 61.4), suggesting partial intrinsic disorder consistent with dynamic protein-protein interactions.

STRING interaction analysis reveals CHCHD10 functions within the MICOS (mitochondrial contact site and cristae organizing system) complex, with highest-confidence partners: CHCHD3 (0.985), IMMT/MIC60 (0.979), and MICOS13 (0.978). Additional interactions include CHCHD2 (0.715), CHCHD6 (0.446), and CHCHD4 (0.431), indicating functional redundancy within the CHCH protein family. The interaction with MATR3 (0.736) suggests potential nuclear-mitochondrial crosstalk.

ClinVar contains 298 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, causally linked to frontotemporal dementia and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis-2 (FTD/ALS-2). Disease mechanism likely involves cristae junction destabilization → impaired oxidative phosphorylation → neuronal bioenergetic failure. The CHCH domain mediates disulfide bond formation via mitochondrial disulfide relay system (CHCHD4/MIA40 pathway).

Functional hypothesis: CHCHD10 acts as a structural scaffold maintaining cristae junction architecture, enabling efficient electron transport chain supercomplex assembly. Mutations disrupt MICOS complex integrity, causing cristae remodeling defects that selectively affect high-energy-demand neurons.
