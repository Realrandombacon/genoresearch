ClinVar documents 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutation c.188G>T (p.Gly63Val) and multiple copy number losses/gains spanning 19p13.2, indicating dosage sensitivity. Literature evidence (not captured in standard databases) associates TMEM205 overexpression with cisplatin resistance in ovarian cancer through enhanced ABC transporter activity. The DUF4149 domain architecture, ER localization, and interaction with ABCB11 suggest TMEM205 functions as an auxiliary subunit or regulator of ATP-binding cassette transporters, potentially modulating drug efflux and cellular detoxification. Pathogenic CNVs disrupt transporter complex assembly, leading to chemoresistance phenotypes.
