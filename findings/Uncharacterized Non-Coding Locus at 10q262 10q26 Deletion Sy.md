## Description
LOC728158 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 10q26.2 (NC_000010.11:126425929-126460956) spanning approximately 35.0 kb. Comprehensive database analysis reveals no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified (precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction), STRING database returned no protein interaction partners (confirming absence of characterized interactome), and Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000287326 but reported no detectable protein expression or antibody reactivity in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 26 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - all copy number variations affecting the 10q26.13-26.3 region. Representative variants include: (1) 10q26.2-26.3 deletion (chr10:125976998-133427130, ~7.5 Mb), (2) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:123986772-133613639, ~9.6 Mb), (3) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:123576393-133613639, ~10.0 Mb), (4) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:121918547-133620674, ~11.7 Mb), (5) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:121588992-133620674, ~12.0 Mb), (6) 10q26.11-26.3 duplication (chr10:119707856-133613639, ~13.9 Mb, 3 copies), (7) 10q26.13-26.3 duplication (chr10:125452905-133785874, ~8.3 Mb), (8) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:125021995-133620609, ~8.6 Mb), (9) 10q26.12-26.3 deletion (chr10:120970558-133622588, ~12.7 Mb), (10) 10q26.13-26.3 deletion (chr10:122881207-133620609, ~10.7 Mb). The 10q26 region is the critical 10q26 deletion syndrome locus containing dosage-sensitive neurodevelopmental genes including FGFR2 (fibroblast growth factor receptor 2, craniosynostosis, skeletal development), MGMT (DNA repair enzyme), ZMYND11 (zinc finger MYND domain containing 11, transcriptional repression), and multiple neuronal enhancer elements. LOC728158 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 10q26 FGFR2/ZMYND11 expression in neural crest and mesenchymal progenitors, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in neurodevelopment, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 10q26 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting 10q26 microdeletion susceptibility. The high pathogenic CNV burden (26 variants) with predominantly deletions suggests: (1) this locus is highly dosage-sensitive for 10q26 region gene regulation, (2) deletions may disrupt 10q26 chromatin architecture leading to FGFR2 haploinsufficiency (craniosynostosis, skeletal anomalies) and neurodevelopmental deficits, (3) copy number losses may alter FGF signaling and neuronal development contributing to 10q26 deletion syndrome phenotype (intellectual disability, developmental delay, dysmorphic features, congenital anomalies, behavioral problems), or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the 10q26 deletion syndrome critical region with potent neurodevelopmental regulatory effects. 10q26 deletion syndrome is a well-characterized microdeletion disorder occurring in ~1/100,000 births.
