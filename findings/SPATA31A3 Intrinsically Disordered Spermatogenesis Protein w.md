## Description
SPATA31A3 (Spermatogenesis-associated protein 31A3, UniProt Q5VYP0) is a 1347-amino acid intracellular protein located on chromosome 9p24.3 within the SPATA31 gene cluster. InterPro analysis reveals spermatogenesis-specific domain architecture: SPATA31-like domain (IPR027970/PF15371/DUF4599, aa 71-156), SPATA31/FAM75 family domain (IPR039509/PF14650, aa 396-754), and membership in the spermatogenesis-associated SPATA31 family (PTHR21859, aa 1-1340). AlphaFold structure prediction achieves low confidence (pLDDT 38.5), indicating an intrinsically disordered protein — this is functionally significant for spermatogenic proteins that require conformational flexibility to participate in dynamic protein-protein interactions during acrosome formation and sperm maturation. Human Protein Atlas shows testis-enriched expression (4.4 nTPM), detected in single tissue, with subcellular localization to the acrosome and equatorial segment of sperm, classified as a predicted membrane protein — confirming its specialized role in sperm-egg recognition and fertilization. STRING interaction network reveals only 1 partner: CNTNAP3B (0.621, contactin-associated protein-like 3B, a neuronal adhesion protein also expressed in testis), suggesting SPATA31A3 may function in cell-cell adhesion events during spermatogenesis or fertilization. ClinVar contains 45 pathogenic variants including massive copy-number gains and losses across 9p24.3-q21.13, demonstrating extreme dosage sensitivity where genomic rearrangements disrupting the 9p SPATA31 cluster cause disease phenotypes potentially affecting male fertility and spermatogenic failure. Conservation across mammals (hyena A0A6G1A6C6, 1392 aa; orangutan A0A2J8XQW6, 1340 aa; chimpanzee A0A2J8IJU9, 1347 aa) indicates ancient evolutionary origin tied to fundamental reproductive function. SPATA31A3 likely functions as a structural or regulatory component of the acrosomal matrix, potentially mediating sperm-egg binding through its disordered regions that enable multivalent interactions with zona pellucida glycoproteins or oolemma receptors during the acrosome reaction.
