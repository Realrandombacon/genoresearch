## Description
OR51B5 encodes Olfactory receptor 51B5 (312 aa, Q9H339), a class A rhodopsin-like G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) functioning in chemosensory signal transduction. InterPro analysis reveals canonical olfactory receptor architecture: 7-transmembrane helix bundle (IPR017452, positions 39-290), rhodopsin-like GPCR domain (IPR000276/G3DSA:1.20.1070.10, positions 18-310), and olfactory receptor family signature (IPR000725/PF13853, positions 29-306) with conserved DRY motif and N-terminal odorant-binding pocket. GO annotations confirm G protein-coupled receptor activity (GO:0004930), olfactory receptor activity (GO:0004984), and G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway (GO:0007186). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence GPCR signaling network: GNAL (0.735, olfactory-specific Gα subunit), ARRB1/ARRB2 (0.683, β-arrestins for receptor desensitization), GNB1 (0.682, Gβ subunit), GNG7/GNG13 (0.678/0.566, Gγ subunits), PRKACA/PRKACG (0.671, cAMP-dependent protein kinases), GRK2/GRK3 (0.670, GPCR kinases) - indicating canonical olfactory GPCR signaling cascade. HPA shows group-enriched expression pattern with low/undetectable levels in most tissues (typical for olfactory receptors); literature reveals ectopic expression in multiple cancers. ClinVar documents 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 11p15.5 CNVs (gains) indicating dosage sensitivity, though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely characterized. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 89.6) with well-folded 7TM bundle architecture typical of class A GPCRs. Literature (25 papers) reveals emerging oncogenic roles: 2025 study demonstrates OR51B5 suppresses esophageal cancer via Calcium/N-Ras signaling activation; 2024 research identifies TBX6 as transcriptional regulator of OR51B5; 2024 functional characterization shows OR51B5/OR1G1 as drug targets for non-type 2 lung diseases regulating bronchial epithelial cell death; 2022 study identifies OR51B5 as candidate oncogene in scirrhous-type gastric cancer development. Functional hypothesis: OR51B5 functions as an ectopically expressed olfactory GPCR in non-olfactory tissues (cancer cells, lung epithelium) where it regulates cell proliferation, apoptosis, and migration through cAMP/Ca2+ signaling; dual oncogenic/tumor suppressor roles depend on tissue context and downstream signaling pathway activation (N-Ras vs other effectors); TBX6 regulation suggests developmental control of ectopic expression. Mechanistic proposal: Odorant ligand binding (identity unknown) induces conformational change activating GNAL/Gαs → adenylate cyclase → cAMP → PKA (PRKACA/PRKACG) signaling cascade; β-arrestin recruitment (ARRB1/2) mediates receptor internalization and potential MAPK signaling; calcium influx through TRP channels activates N-Ras in esophageal cancer; GRK2/3 phosphorylation regulates receptor desensitization; ectopic expression in cancers suggests hijacking of chemosensory machinery for growth regulation; 11p15.5 CNVs (imprinting control region) suggest epigenetic dysregulation contributing to aberrant expression; lung epithelial expression indicates potential role in airway chemosensation and inflammatory responses. This gene represents a moderately characterized ectopic olfactory receptor with emerging roles in cancer biology and respiratory disease.
