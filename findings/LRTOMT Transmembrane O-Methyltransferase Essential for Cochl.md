## Description
LRTOMT (Leucine Rich Transmembrane and O-Methyltransferase Domain Containing / TOMT) encodes a 291 aa transmembrane O-methyltransferase (UniProt Q8WZ04) critical for nonsyndromic hearing loss prevention. InterPro analysis reveals canonical SAM-dependent methyltransferase architecture: IPR002935 (Class I-like SAM-dependent O-methyltransferase, aa 75-288, GO:0008171), IPR029063 (S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase superfamily, aa 77-290), PF01596 (O-methyltransferase domain, aa 116-246), PS51682 (SAM-dependent O-methyltransferase class I profile, aa 75-288), PTHR43836 (Cation-dependent O-methyltransferase, aa 36-288), SSF53335 (SAM-dependent methyltransferases, aa 78-266), G3DSA:3.40.50.150 (Vaccinia VP39-like, aa 77-290), and cd02440 (AdoMet_MTases, aa 135-247). STRING interactions show exceptional confidence with catecholamine metabolism enzymes: MAOB (0.990), MAOA (0.989), DDC/dopa decarboxylase (0.976), CYP1B1 (0.966), DBH/dopamine beta-hydroxylase (0.965), CYP1A1 (0.957), ALDH3A1 (0.931), ALDH3B2 (0.927), ALDH3B1 (0.925), PNMT (0.732), positioning LRTOMT within neurotransmitter degradation pathways. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (8.5 nTPM), fallopian tube expression (4.8 nTPM), group-enriched RNA specificity, microtubule subcellular localization, and disease involvement in deafness/non-syndromic deafness. ClinVar contains 63 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Arg8Ter, p.Trp105Ter, p.Cys142Ter), frameshifts (p.Val46_Val47insTer, p.Gln284fs), missense variants (p.Leu214Pro, p.Leu159Arg, p.Gly116Glu), splice site mutations (c.555+2T>G), and duplications, causing autosomal recessive nonsyndromic hearing loss. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 90.4) indicates very high confidence with well-folded catalytic domain. Literature (32 papers) documents LRTOMT mutations in Mauritanian, Iranian, Egyptian, and Chinese families with congenital profound sensorineural hearing loss, and rare co-occurrence with Bietti crystalline dystrophy (CYP4V2). FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: LRTOMT functions as a transmembrane SAM-dependent O-methyltransferase that methylates catecholamine substrates or related metabolites in cochlear hair cells and supporting cells, with microtubule association suggesting transport or cytoskeletal regulation roles. The strong interaction network with MAOA/MAOB (monoamine oxidases), DDC (dopa decarboxylase), DBH (dopamine beta-hydroxylase), and PNMT (phenylethanolamine N-methyltransferase) indicates LRTOMT completes the catecholamine metabolic pathway, potentially methylating catechol substrates to regulate neurotransmitter levels critical for auditory signal transduction. Loss-of-function variants disrupt catecholamine homeostasis in the cochlea, causing hair cell dysfunction and progressive sensorineural hearing loss. The testis-enriched expression suggests additional roles in germ cell development, consistent with transmembrane methyltransferase functions in multiple tissues.
