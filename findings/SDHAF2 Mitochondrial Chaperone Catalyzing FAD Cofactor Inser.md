## Description
SDHAF2 (Succinate Dehydrogenase Assembly Factor 2, Q9NX18), also known as SDH5, encodes a 166 aa mitochondrial matrix protein functioning as an essential chaperone that catalyzes the covalent insertion of FAD cofactor into the SDHA flavoprotein subunit of mitochondrial Complex II (succinate dehydrogenase), a critical step enabling electron transfer from succinate to ubiquinone in the TCA cycle and electron transport chain.
