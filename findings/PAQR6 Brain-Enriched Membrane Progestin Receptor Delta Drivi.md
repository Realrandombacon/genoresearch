## Description
PAQR6/Q6TCH4 (344 aa) is an exceptionally well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT: 90.8) seven-transmembrane membrane progestin receptor functioning as a critical mediator of rapid non-genomic steroid signaling in cancer progression and potentially neuroendocrine functions. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical AdipoR/Haemolysin-III-related architecture (IPR004254/PF03006/PTHR20855, residues 7-286/43-271/7-286) containing the characteristic 7-transmembrane domain fold of the PAQR family that enables membrane progesterone binding and G protein-coupled signal transduction, classifying it within the adipoQ/progestin receptor superfamily with mPRδ (membrane progesterone receptor delta) designation. HPA data confirms brain-enriched expression (911.8 nTPM) with tissue-enriched specificity, cancer-enriched expression pattern, immune cell enrichment, and classification as a receptor/predicted membrane protein consistent with its role in steroid hormone sensing and signal transduction. STRING interactions define a high-confidence PAQR family oligomerization network linking PAQR6 to family members PAQR9 (0.915), PAQR7 (0.816), PAQR4 (0.446), and PAQR3 (0.437), plus progesterone receptor membrane components PGRMC2 (0.677) and PGRMC1 (0.636), neurotrophic factor NENF (0.543), cytochrome b5 domain proteins CYB5D2 (0.459) and CYB5B (0.421), and classical nuclear progesterone receptor PGR (0.415), positioning it at the intersection of membrane steroid signaling, cytochrome-mediated redox regulation, and neurotrophic pathways. Literature (32 papers) establishes emerging roles in oncology: 2024 studies identify PAQR6 as a prognostic biomarker and potential therapeutic target in kidney renal clear cell carcinoma; 2021 research demonstrates PAQR6 copy number variations serve as novel prognostic signatures for bladder cancer, and PAQR6 upregulation correlates with androgen receptor (AR) signaling and unfavorable prognosis in prostate cancers; 2018 characterization shows PAQR6 expression enhancement suggests worse prognosis in prostate cancer patients. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants predominantly consisting of large chromosome 1q CNVs (1q21.1-25.3 gains, 1q21.1-44 gains, 1q21.3-22 gains, 1q22-23.2 gains, 1q21.3-23.1 losses) implicating PAQR6 dosage sensitivity in developmental disorders though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined due to contiguous gene effects. The convergence of very high confidence 7-transmembrane structure, PAQR family oligomerization partnerships, PGRMC/PGR progesterone receptor crosstalk, brain-enriched expression (911.8 nTPM), and multi-cancer prognostic value (prostate/kidney/bladder) suggests PAQR6 functions as a membrane-proximal steroid signaling checkpoint coordinating rapid progesterone responses with AR signaling amplification, tumor progression, and potential neuroendocrine regulation, with therapeutic potential as a prognostic biomarker in urological cancers and target for disrupting membrane steroid signaling in hormone-driven malignancies.
