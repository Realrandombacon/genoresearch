## Description
GBP3/Q9H0R5 (595 aa) is a highly confident (AlphaFold pLDDT: 88.4) large GTPase functioning as an interferon-inducible guanylate-binding protein mediating cell-autonomous antimicrobial defense and inflammasome regulation through its bipartite GTPase architecture. InterPro analysis reveals canonical GBP family architecture containing an N-terminal GB1/RHD3-type guanine nucleotide-binding G domain (IPR030386/cd01851/IPR015894/IPR027417, residues 35-276/32-274/19-280/1-281) with GTPase activity (GO:0003924) and GTP binding function (GO:0005525), coupled to a C-terminal guanylate-binding protein/Atlastin domain (IPR003191/IPR036543/IPR037684/cd16269/G3DSA:1.20.1000.10/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 282-578/282-579/288-574/282-577) mediating membrane association and oligomerization, classified within the P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase superfamily characteristic of dynamin-related large GTPases. HPA data confirms tissue-enhanced expression (detected in many tissues) with highest expression in intestine (74.1 nTPM), classification as predicted intracellular proteins with antiviral defense/immunity/innate immunity biological processes, hydrolase molecular function, consistent with interferon-stimulated gene (ISG) profile. STRING interactions define a moderate-confidence interferon signaling network linking GBP3 to IFI44 (0.651), ISG15 (0.643), MAP4K4 (0.640), IRF9 (0.619), IRGM (0.608), IFNG (0.554), GBP2 (0.533), GBP1 (0.464), RTP4 (0.459), and STAT1 (0.445) - collectively representing the interferon-gamma response machinery (IFNG-STAT1-IRF9), ISG effector network (IFI44-ISG15-IRGM), and GBP family paralogs (GBP1-GBP2). Literature (81 papers) establishes emerging inflammatory and vascular disease mechanisms: 2025 landmark studies demonstrate miR-210-3P/GBP3 axis modulates NLRP3 inflammasome in royal jelly acid-mediated ulcerative colitis therapy (2 citations); reveal STAT1-GBP3-STING positive feedback loop governs inflammation, oxidative stress, and DNA damage triggering acute aortic dissection; 2024 work shows adipose-derived stem cell extracellular vesicles inhibit ischemic stroke-induced pyroptosis through GBP3 regulation of NLRP3/GSDMD signaling pathway (6 citations); 2023 study establishes GBP3 association with lupus nephritis progression by regulating cell proliferation, inflammation and pyroptosis (15 citations); pan-cancer bioinformatics characterizes GBP3 function across human cancers. ClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 1p copy number alterations (1p31.3-21.3 losses, 1p31.1-22.2 losses, 1p34.1-22.2 gains, 1p36.33-q44 gains, 1p31.3-22.1 losses) and splice site variants (c.626-18T>G, c.1363-1G>C) implicating GBP3 dosage sensitivity in immune dysregulation though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined due to contiguous 1p gene effects. The convergence of highly confident bipartite GTPase architecture (pLDDT 88.4), interferon signaling partnerships (STAT1-IRF9-ISG15 axis), intestine-enriched expression (74.1 nTPM), 2025-established STAT1-GBP3-STING positive feedback in acute aortic dissection, NLRP3 inflammasome regulation in ulcerative colitis and lupus nephritis, pyroptosis control via NLRP3/GSDMD pathway, and chromosome 1p dosage sensitivity suggests GBP3 functions as an interferon-gamma-inducible large GTPase coordinating cell-autonomous antimicrobial defense through GTP-dependent oligomerization and membrane recruitment, regulating NLRP3 inflammasome assembly and pyroptotic cell death via STING pathway coupling, modulating oxidative stress and DNA damage responses in vascular smooth muscle cells during aortic dissection pathogenesis, and maintaining intestinal immune homeostasis through highest tissue expression (74.1 nTPM), with therapeutic potential in targeting STAT1-GBP3-STING axis in acute aortic dissection, modulating GBP3-mediated NLRP3 inflammasome in ulcerative colitis and lupus nephritis, exploiting extracellular vesicle-GBP3 delivery in ischemic stroke neuroprotection, and investigating chromosome 1p deletion syndrome immune phenotypes.
