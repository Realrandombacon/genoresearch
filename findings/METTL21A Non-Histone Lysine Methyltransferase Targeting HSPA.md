## Description
METTL21A (Methyltransferase 21A, also known as HSPA-KMT or FAM119A, Q8WXB1) encodes a 218 aa intracellular enzyme functioning as a non-histone lysine methyltransferase that specifically modifies heat shock protein 70 (HSP70) family members and co-chaperones to regulate protein folding, stress response, and cancer progression.
