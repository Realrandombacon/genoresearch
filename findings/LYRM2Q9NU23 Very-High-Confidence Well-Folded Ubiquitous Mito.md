LYR proteins function as chaperones that deliver iron-sulfur clusters to respiratory chain complexes, particularly complex I (NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase) and complex II (succinate dehydrogenase). The LYR motif is essential for iron-sulfur cluster binding and transfer to target subunits. LYRM2 LYR domain (IPR045293, residues 17-79) forms a 63 aa module (~72%) placing LYRM2 within the LYRM2-specific subfamily, distinguishing it from other LYR proteins like LYRM1, LYRM4, LYRM7, and LYRM9.
