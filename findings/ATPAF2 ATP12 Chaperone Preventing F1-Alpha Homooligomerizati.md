# ATPAF2: ATP12 Chaperone Preventing F1-Alpha Homooligomerization During Mitochondrial ATP Synthase Assembly

## Description
ATPAF2 (Q8N5M1) is a 289-aa mitochondrial ATP synthase F1 complex assembly factor with high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 85.6), indicating a well-folded stable protein. InterPro analysis reveals ATP12 chaperone protein domain (IPR011419/PF07542: positions 47-166) and ATP12 orthogonal bundle domain (IPR023335: positions 106-277), characteristic of F1-ATPase assembly chaperones. HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with low specificity, localized to cytosol, classified as chaperone with primary mitochondrial disease involvement. STRING interactions demonstrate exceptionally strong binding to paralog ATPAF1 (0.986), plus high-confidence interactions with F1 subunits ATP5F1C (0.837) and ATP5F1A (0.767), and assembly factors FMC1 (0.825), TMEM70 (0.426). Literature (14 papers) establishes ATPAF2/ATP12 binds specifically to F1-alpha subunit (ATP5F1A) to prevent nonproductive homooligomerization during enzyme assembly, working cooperatively with ATPAF1. ClinVar contains 152 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense (p.Glu146Ter), missense (p.Ser54Ile, p.Asn250Tyr, p.Ile162Ser, p.Arg263Gln), and copy number losses at 17p11.2 (Smith-Magenis syndrome region). The high-confidence structure (pLDDT 85.6) suggests ATPAF2 forms a stable chaperone scaffold that shields hydrophobic surfaces of nascent F1-alpha subunits, enabling proper hetero-oligomeric assembly with beta and gamma subunits. Sequence variation near ATPAF2 associates with dementia risk, implicating mitochondrial dysfunction in neurodegeneration.
