## Description
LOC100129148 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 7q34 (NC_000007.14, positions 139427525-139417461, ~10 kb region) with substantial clinical significance. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. No mRNA transcript was identified in NCBI nucleotide databases. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression across any tissue. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (variants include 2579171, 2579170, 155687, 155640, 154735, 152912, 150858, 149975, 149905, 149061), predominantly representing copy number gains (trisomy/amplification) with some copy number losses (monosomy/deletion). All variants have uncertain significance (?). The 7q34-36 chromosomal region contains several well-characterized oncogenes and tumor suppressors including BRAF (B-Raf proto-oncogene, serine/threonine kinase, RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK signaling pathway, melanoma, colorectal cancer, hairy cell leukemia, ~60% of melanomas harbor BRAF V600E mutations), PRSS1 (serine protease 1, trypsin, hereditary pancreatitis), and HIPK2 (homeodomain interacting protein kinase 2, p53 phosphorylation, apoptosis regulation, Wnt signaling, tumor suppressor activity). Functional hypothesis: LOC100129148 likely represents a cis-regulatory enhancer or chromatin organizer within the 7q34-36 amplicon region, potentially regulating expression of neighboring oncogenes through long-range chromatin interactions. The 18 pathogenic CNVs indicate strong dosage sensitivity where amplification drives oncogene overexpression (particularly BRAF) and deletion causes haploinsufficiency of tumor suppressors (HIPK2). Disease mechanism likely involves co-amplification with BRAF contributing to constitutive MAPK pathway activation driving uncontrolled cell proliferation, survival, and therapeutic resistance in melanoma, glioblastoma, and colorectal cancer. Deletions may disrupt HIPK2-mediated p53 activation impairing apoptosis and DNA damage response. The 7q34 region is recurrently amplified in multiple cancer types including glioblastoma multiforme, breast cancer, and lung adenocarcinoma. LncRNAs and enhancer elements in amplicon regions can function as oncogenic drivers through transcriptional activation of neighboring proto-oncogenes. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (18 CNV variants) and kinase oncogene neighborhood suggests this locus participates in 7q34-driven oncogenesis through enhancer RNA activity, chromatin looping to BRAF promoter, or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary maintenance affecting MAPK pathway gene expression.
