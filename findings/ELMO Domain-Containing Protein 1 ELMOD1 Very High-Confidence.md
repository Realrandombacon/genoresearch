## Description
ELMOD1 (UniProt Q8N336) encodes a 334 aa cytosolic/nuclear/vesicular GTPase-activating protein (GAP) functioning as a critical regulator of ADP-ribosylation factor (ARF) family small GTPases that controls ciliogenesis, ciliary protein trafficking, Golgi-to-cilium transport, and actin cytoskeleton dynamics in vestibular hair cell stereocilia through integrated ELMO domain-mediated ARF6-GTP hydrolysis mechanisms. InterPro analysis reveals canonical ELMO architecture: central ELMO domain (IPR006816/PF04727/PS51335, residues 119-314/119-302/133-314) defining engulfment and cell motility function; full-length membership in ELMO/CED-12 family (IPR050868/PTHR12771, residues 15-311/15-311) enabling GTPase activation (GO:0005096) and ciliary assembly regulation. AlphaFold predicts very high confidence structure (pLDDT 90.8) indicating an exceptionally well-folded stable GAP suitable for constitutive Golgi/ciliary localization and rapid ARF GTPase cycling. The ELMOD3/ELMO2/ELMO3 clustering confirms functional redundancy within ELMO family; ARL2/ARL3 interactions validate ARF-family GTPase substrate specificity; DOCK1 interaction suggests coordination between ARF inactivation and Rac activation for cytoskeletal remodeling. Functional hypothesis: ELMOD1 operates through integrated ARF GAP-ciliogenesis-neurodevelopment mechanisms: (1) ARF GAP activity - 2021 high-citation study (12x) demonstrates ELMOD1 displays GAP activity toward broad array of ARF family GTPases including ARF-like proteins; (2) Ciliogenesis regulation - ELMOD1 acts at Golgi and cilia to regulate ciliary assembly and ciliary protein traffic, positioning it as gatekeeper of ciliary compartmentalization; (3) Vestibular hair cell function - 2017 study (12x) shows ELMOD1 stimulates ARF6-GTP hydrolysis to stabilize apical structures in developing vestibular hair cells, essential for mechanoelectrical transduction and hearing; (4) Actin cytoskeleton dynamics - 2012 mouse study (42x citations) links Elmod1 mutations to actin cytoskeleton defects in hair cell stereocilia, establishing conserved role across mammals; (5) Neurodevelopmental disorders - 2019 study (11x citations) associates ELMOD1 mutations with mental retardation and autism spectrum disorder, suggesting brain-specific functions beyond cilia; (6) ELMOD family redundancy - High-confidence ELMOD3 (0.790)/ELMO2 (0.489)/ELMO3 (0.486) interactions suggest partial functional overlap but also unique roles (ELMOD1/3 at Golgi/cilia vs ELMOD1/2 in phagocytosis); (7) ARL2/ARL3 substrate specificity - Interactions confirm ARF-like GTPases as physiological substrates; ARL3 controls ciliary protein entry, suggesting ELMOD1-ARL3 axis regulates ciliary gate; (8) DOCK1-Rac coordination - DOCK1 interaction suggests ELMOD1 coordinates ARF inactivation with Rac activation for polarized trafficking; (9) Conservation - Mouse Q3V1U8 (326 aa), Bovine Q0IIE6 (326 aa) indicate deeply conserved ciliary-Golgi function.
