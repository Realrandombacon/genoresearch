## Description
LMBRD1 (LMBR1 Domain Containing 1) is a 540 aa multi-pass transmembrane protein functioning as an essential escort factor for the lysosomal export of vitamin B12 (cobalamin) to the cytosol, a critical step in one-carbon metabolism and mitochondrial methylmalonyl-CoA mutase activity. InterPro analysis identifies a unique LMBR1-like membrane protein domain (IPR006876/PF04791, aa 17-287) spanning the N-terminal half, likely forming the transmembrane channel or scaffold, and a full-length family signature (IPR050854) defining the lysosomal cobalamin transport machinery. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 83.8) consistent with a stable, folded membrane transporter containing multiple helical bundles. STRING interactions place LMBRD1 at the core of the intracellular B12 trafficking network with ultra-high confidence partners: MMADHC (0.976), MMAA (0.975), MMAB (0.955), MMACHC (0.881), MTRR (0.863), and MTR (0.843), alongside the parallel exporter ABCD4 (0.503) and the receptor CD320 (0.600). HPA expression shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low specificity, localized to vesicles (lysosomes), reflecting its fundamental housekeeping role in nutrient processing; it is also immune-cell enhanced. ClinVar contains 80 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including frameshifts (e.g., p.Leu356fs, p.Ala231fs), nonsense mutations (p.Trp15Ter), and large copy number losses, causing Cobalamin F (cblF) deficiency. This rare autosomal recessive disorder manifests as combined methylmalonic aciduria and homocystinuria, megaloblastic anemia, and severe developmental defects, including a Dyskeratosis Congenita-like phenotype and failure of gastrulation. Literature (37 papers) confirms LMBRD1's non-redundant role with ABCD4, its necessity for embryonic development (knockout is lethal at gastrulation), and its involvement in cardiac hypertrophy when haploinsufficient.
