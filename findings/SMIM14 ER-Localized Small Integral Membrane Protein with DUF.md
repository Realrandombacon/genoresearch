# SMIM14: ER-Localized Small Integral Membrane Protein with DUF2615 Domain Linked to Chromosome 4p Copy Number Disorders

## Description
SMIM14 (small integral membrane protein 14, also known as C4orf34) encodes a 99-amino acid single-pass membrane protein localized to the endoplasmic reticulum. The protein contains a DUF2615 domain (PF11027, residues 3-97) characteristic of the small integral membrane protein family (IPR020309). HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with liver enrichment (110.6 nTPM), classified as tissue-enhanced with low cancer specificity. STRING analysis reveals 10 medium-confidence interaction partners (scores 0.495-0.622) including UGDH (UDP-glucose 6-dehydrogenase, 0.622), LIAS (lipoic acid synthetase, 0.574), TIGD4 (0.558), CNPPD1 (0.545), and TMEM131L (0.540), suggesting functional associations with metabolic enzymes and transmembrane proteins. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 4p copy number gains spanning the SMIM14 locus, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease pathogenesis. The ER localization combined with interaction partners involved in metabolic processes (UGDH, LIAS) suggests SMIM14 may function as an ER membrane scaffold facilitating metabolic enzyme complex assembly or substrate channeling. The DUF2615 domain architecture and small size (99 aa) indicate a structural/regulatory role rather than catalytic activity.
