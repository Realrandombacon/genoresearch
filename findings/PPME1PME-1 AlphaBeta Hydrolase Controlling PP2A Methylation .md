# PPME1/PME-1: Alpha/Beta Hydrolase Controlling PP2A Methylation State in ERK Pathway Activation, Cancer Progression, and Skeletal Muscle Homeostasis

**Date:** 2026-06-26T16:02:30.447697

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
PPME1 (386 aa, UniProt Q9Y570), also known as PME-1 (protein phosphatase methylesterase 1) or ABDH19, is a nuclear alpha/beta hydrolase enzyme that functions as the critical regulator of protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) activity through demethylation of the PP2A catalytic subunit C-terminal leucine residue, thereby controlling ERK pathway signaling, cell proliferation, and tumor progression. The protein architecture features a canonical alpha/beta hydrolase fold-1 domain (IPR000073/PF12697, aa79-362) containing the catalytic triad characteristic of serine esterases; the full eukaryotic protein phosphatase methylesterase family signature spans aa2-383 (IPR016812/PIRSF022950/PTHR14189); structural superfamilies include G3DSA:3.40.50.1820 and IPR029058 alpha/beta hydrolase fold (aa39-386) with SSF53474 alpha/beta-hydrolase cores (aa67-191, 275-367). HPA confirms low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, low immune cell specificity, strict nucleoplasmic and nuclear bodies localization, and classification as an enzyme with hydrolase and serine esterase molecular functions. Mechanistically, PPME1 forms ultra-high-confidence interactions within the PP2A holoenzyme regulatory network: PPP2CA (STRING 0.999) is the primary catalytic subunit substrate whose C-terminal leucine methylation state PPME1 controls; PPP2R5C (0.981), PPP2R5D (0.896), PPP2R2A (0.873), and PPP2R2D (0.860) are B-type regulatory subunits determining PP2A substrate specificity; LCMT1 (0.920) is leucine carboxyl methyltransferase 1 that oppositely methylates PP2CA creating a methylation-demethylation cycle; PPP2R1A (0.786) and PPP2R1B (0.738) are scaffold A subunits assembling the PP2A trimeric holoenzyme; PTPA/PPP2R4 (0.756) is phosphotyrosyl phosphatase activator facilitating PP2A activation; PPP2CB (0.709) is the alternate catalytic subunit isoform. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 82.6) reveals high-confidence well-folded alpha/beta hydrolase structure with central beta-sheet surrounded by alpha-helices forming the catalytic pocket consistent with crystallographic studies of methylesterase enzymes. Clinical significance includes 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants: large copy number gains spanning 11q12.2-13.5/11q13.4-23.3/11p15.5-q25 (chr11:59923608-76272324, 71588805-116680918, 230616-134938470 amplifications), copy number losses at 11q13.4-14.1/11q13.4-13.5 (chr11:71969881-78232895, 71928796-77064521 deletions), and internal deletions (c.965-11_965-10del); PPME1 overexpression correlates with malignant glioma progression and poor prognosis. Conservation across mammals (mouse Q8BVQ5 386 aa, rat Q4FZT2 386 aa, orangutan Q5R4F9 386 aa) indicates essential PP2A regulatory function maintained over ~90 million years. Literature (22 papers) establishes PPME1 in cancer and muscle biology: 2022 studies demonstrate hsa_circ_0050102 regulates pancreatic cancer development via miR-218-5p/PPME1 axis; 2020 research shows DNAH17-AS1 lncRNA promotes pancreatic carcinoma by increasing PPME1 expression via miR-432-5p inhibition; 2021 work identifies HOTAIR lncRNA/microRNA-761 sponge regulating PPME1 influencing thyroid carcinoma cell biological functions; 2018 characterization identifies PPME1 expression in skeletal muscle linking to atrophy pathways. The functional hypothesis posits that PPME1 acts as a 'PP2A methylation rheostat controlling ERK pathway output': PPME1 resides in nucleoplasm and nuclear bodies where it encounters newly assembled or activated PP2A holoenzymes; the alpha/beta hydrolase catalytic pocket recognizes C-terminally methylated leucine on PPP2CA hydrolyzing the methyl ester bond; demethylated PP2A exhibits altered substrate preference favoring dephosphorylation of specific targets including RAF-MEK-ERK cascade components; in cancer, oncogenic lncRNAs (DNAH17-AS1, HOTAIR) and circRNAs (hsa_circ_0050102) sponge tumor-suppressive miRNAs (miR-432-5p, miR-218-5p, miR-761) elevating PPME1 expression which hyperactivates ERK signaling through PP2A inhibition promoting proliferation, migration, and invasion; in skeletal muscle, PPME1 regulates atrophy responses through PP2A-mediated control of FOXO transcription factors and protein degradation machinery; LCMT1-PPME1 opposition creates dynamic methylation cycle allowing rapid PP2A activity modulation in response to growth factors and stress signals; therapeutic strategies include PPME1 inhibitors for pancreatic/thyroid cancer, PPME1 modulators for glioma treatment, and PPME1-targeted interventions for muscle wasting conditions.
