## Description
RABGAP1L (Rab GTPase-Activating Protein 1-Like, UniProt Q5R372) is an 815 aa intracellular/membrane protein belonging to the TBC (Tre-2/Bub2/Cdc16) domain Rab-GAP family that functions as a negative regulator of Rab GTPases in endosomal membrane trafficking, cell-autonomous immunity, and antiviral defense.
