## Description
SWT1 (SWT1 RNA Endoribonuclease Homolog, also known as C1orf26, HsSwt1, UniProt Q5T5J6) is a 900-amino acid PIN domain-containing RNA endoribonuclease that functions as a critical regulator of oxidative stress response in cardiomyocytes, chemotherapy resistance mediator in acute myeloid leukemia, and testis-enriched nuclear protein through its catalytic PIN domain-mediated RNA cleavage activity.
