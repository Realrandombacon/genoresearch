# HCFC2: Host Cell Factor 2 Scaffold Mediating IRF-Dependent TLR3 Transcription, COMPASS-like Histone Methylation Complex Assembly, and Viral Infection Survival

## Description
HCFC2 (Host Cell Factor C2, UniProt Q9Y5Z7) is a 792 aa nuclear scaffold protein that functions as a paralog of HCFC1, mediating interferon regulatory factor (IRF)-dependent Toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) transcription and innate immune response to viral infection through assembly of COMPASS-like histone methylation complexes. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a confidently folded architecture (pLDDT=72.9) featuring N-terminal Kelch-type beta-propeller domains (IPR015915/G3DSA:2.120.10.80, aa 3-355) enabling protein-protein interactions, central fibronectin type III domains (IPR003961/cd00063, aa 357-778) providing structural scaffolding, and a conserved host cell factor family region (IPR043536/IPR059124, aa 5-412) characteristic of VP16-interacting proteins. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal tight coupling to epigenetic regulatory machinery: paralog HCFC1 (0.982) sharing functional overlap, transcriptional regulator CREBZF (0.971), COMPASS complex core components WDR5 (0.959), RBBP5 (0.955), ASH2L (0.941) mediating H3K4 methylation, CREB3 (0.924), and histone methyltransferases KMT2B/MLL4 (0.907), SETD1A (0.887), KMT2A/MLL1 (0.883). HPA shows ubiquitous expression with nucleoplasmic, plasma membrane, and cytosolic localization, classified with low tissue and cancer specificity. ClinVar identifies 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains at 12q23.1-24.33 and 12p13.33-q24.33, copy number losses at 12q23.2-24.11, and splice variants (c.682+5G>A), though no specific Mendelian disease is annotated - these CNVs overlap with neurodevelopmental disorder and hematologic malignancy regions. Literature (7 papers) reveals emerging mechanistic insights: HCFC2 is essential for IRF1- and IRF2-dependent Tlr3 transcription enabling survival during viral infections (2017, 22 citations), interacts with VP16 herpes simplex virus protein through beta-propeller domain but fails to initiate viral infection unlike HCFC1, and appears in epigenetic signatures predicting lung adenocarcinoma survival (2020-2022). The gene represents a 'semi-dark' transcriptional co-regulator where canonical HCFC family function is suggested, but specific mechanisms distinguishing HCFC2 from HCFC1 in innate immune gene activation, COMPASS complex recruitment specificity, and tissue-specific vulnerability in viral susceptibility remain underexplored therapeutic targets.
