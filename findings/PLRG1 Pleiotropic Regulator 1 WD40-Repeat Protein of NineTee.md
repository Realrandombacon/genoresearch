## Description
PLRG1 encodes Pleiotropic Regulator 1 (UniProt O43660), a 514-amino acid WD40-repeat protein essential for pre-mRNA splicing and spliceosome integrity. The protein contains multiple conserved WD40 repeat domains (IPR001680, positions 193-399/402-449/452-490; PF00400, positions 195-232/236-274/279-316; cd00200, positions 196-490) classified within the WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943, positions 197-492; G3DSA:2.130.10.10, positions 197-492; IPR036322, positions 189-489) and Prp46/PLRG1-like family (IPR045241, positions 1-509) with annotated mRNA splicing via spliceosome (GO:0000398) and protein binding (GO:0005515) functions, indicating essential role in spliceosome assembly and catalytic activation. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 77.4, residues 1-514) indicating stable folding with well-defined WD40 beta-propeller architecture suitable for protein-protein interactions and spliceosome complex assembly. STRING interaction analysis reveals PLRG1 forms an extremely high-confidence spliceosome and NTC network with key partners: XAB2 (0.999; XPA binding protein 2, spliceosome), CDC40 (0.999; cell division cycle 40, spliceosome), SNRPF (0.998; small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide F, U1 snRNP), SNRPD2 (0.998; small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide D2, core snRNP), PRPF19 (0.996; pre-mRNA processing factor 19, NTC core), SNRPA1 (0.989; small nuclear ribonucleoprotein polypeptide A1, U2 snRNP), SF3B1 (0.965; splicing factor 3b subunit 1, U2 snRNP), RBM22 (0.949; RNA binding motif protein 22, spliceosome), SYF2 (0.779; pre-mRNA-splicing factor SYF2, NTC), and YJU2 (0.713; pre-mRNA-splicing factor YJU2, NTC), confirming PLRG1's central integration into the NineTeen Complex (NTC) for spliceosome activation and catalytic step transitions. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity detected in all tissues, nucleoplasm/nuclear membrane/nucleoli fibrillar center/nuclear speckles subcellular localization consistent with spliceosome assembly and pre-mRNA processing functions, annotated for mRNA processing and mRNA splicing biological processes, classified as essential protein, plasma protein, and predicted intracellular protein. ClinVar contains 31 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including deletions (c.10-17_10-13del, c.10-3del), copy number gains (4q27-35.2, 4q24-35.2, 4p12-q35.2, 4q26-32.3, 4q28.3-32.3, 4q28.1-35.1, 4q31.3-35.2), and copy number losses (4q31.3-32.1), though no specific Mendelian disorder name is assigned, suggesting PLRG1 dosage sensitivity affects splicing fidelity and genome stability across multiple organ systems. With 28 publications, PLRG1 is a genuinely understudied spliceosome component with emerging roles in cancer and genome stability: YBX1 promotes epithelial-mesenchymal transition in hepatocellular carcinoma via transcriptional regulation of PLRG1 (2024, 3 cit), elevated PLRG1 critical for proliferation and genome stability maintenance of tumor cells (2023, 5 cit), RNA helicase DHX37 facilitates liver cancer progression by cooperating with PLRG1 to drive superenhancer-mediated transcription of Cyclin D1 (2022, 21 cit), crystal structure of WD40 domain of human PLRG1 reveals spliceosome integrity mechanism (2020, 3 cit).
