## Description
CRIP3 (Cysteine rich protein 3, also known as CRP-3, TLP, TLP-A, h6LIMo, thymus LIM protein) is a 217 amino acid intracellular protein (UniProt Q6Q6R5) with high-confidence folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 74.4), characteristic of LIM domain proteins that function through protein-protein interactions and transcriptional regulation. The protein contains two LIM zinc-binding domains (IPR001781, residues 3-64 and 122-183; PF00412, residues 5-60 and 124-177) enabling metal ion binding activity (GO:0046872), LIM zinc-binding domain signatures (PS00478, residues 5-39 and 124-158), LIM domain profiles (PS50023, residues 3-64 and 122-183), and SMART LIM domains (SM00132, residues 4-56 and 123-175). CRIP3 also contains CDD domains for the first LIM domain of thymus LIM protein (cd09476, residues 5-58) and second LIM domain (cd09477, residues 124-177), belongs to the cysteine-rich intracellular family (PTHR46074, residues 110-187), and is classified within the Cysteine Rich Protein superfamily (G3DSA:2.10.110.10, residues 4-78 and 123-191).
