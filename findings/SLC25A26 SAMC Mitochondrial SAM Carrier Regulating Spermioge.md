## Description

InterPro analysis confirms the conserved MCF domain architecture (IPR018108, PF00153, PTHR45667) spanning aa 4-272, featuring three transmembrane repeats essential for proton-linked antiport. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q70HW3-F1) predicts a highly stable, well-folded dimeric transporter with very high confidence (global pLDDT 87.9), consistent with its role in active transmembrane transport. HPA expression reveals ubiquitous tissue distribution with cell-type enhancement in specific lineages, and localization to the inner mitochondrial membrane. Literature search identified 30 papers, confirming partial characterization in spermiogenesis and cardiac hypertrophy. ClinVar lists 25 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (including p.Ala57del and p.Arg261Ter), predominantly single nucleotide variants and microsatellite deletions, though no specific monogenic disease is yet annotated, suggesting involvement in complex mitochondrial disorders. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners NFS1 (score 0.771), MTCH1 (0.725), and MTCH2 (0.705), placing SLC25A26 within the mitochondrial matrix enzyme complex. Functional hypothesis: SLC25A26 functions as a mitochondrial SAM carrier that coordinates translation and bioenergetics; its interaction with FBXO24 (which targets it for K6-linked polyubiquitination) regulates its stability during spermiogenesis and cardiac hypertrophy, where SAM compartmentalization is critical for metabolic flux.
