## Description
SLC30A6 encodes a 461 amino acid zinc transporter 6 (ZnT6, UniProt Q6NXT4), a member of the SLC30/ZnT family of cation diffusion facilitators. InterPro analysis confirms canonical CDF architecture including IPR002524 (Cation efflux family, aa 34-330), IPR052005 (Cation Diffusion Facilitator SLC30A, aa 1-458), PF01545 (Cation efflux transmembrane domain, aa 35-255), and TIGR01297 (CDF transporter, aa 34-330), indicating conserved zinc efflux via the characteristic 6-transmembrane helix fold with cytoplasmic zinc-binding domains. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q6NXT4-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 71.1), consistent with a functional multi-pass membrane transporter. HPA expression reveals ubiquitous tissue distribution with Golgi apparatus localization, suggesting specialized roles in secretory pathway zinc homeostasis and protein maturation. STRING interactions identify 10 partners exclusively from the zinc transporter network including SLC30A5 (0.997, ZnT5 heterodimer partner), SLC39A7 (0.859, ZIP7 Golgi importer), ALPL (0.856, alkaline phosphatase), SLC30A9 (0.726), SLC39A13 (0.698), SLC39A9 (0.667), SLC30A1 (0.523), SLC30A4 (0.511), SLC30A10 (0.505), and SLC39A6 (0.446), forming a coherent Golgi zinc handling complex balancing import (ZIP) and export (ZnT) for metalloenzyme activation. ClinVar contains 43 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 2p copy number gains and losses (2p24.1-22.2, 2p25.3-22.3, 2p23.1-21), though no specific disease phenotype is currently annotated. Literature (27 papers) identifies SLC30A6 as critical for pancreatic cancer prognosis and immunomodulation, neonatal cardiac conduction disease, cardiomyocyte dysfunction via mitochondrial impairment and K-acetylation activation, rheumatoid arthritis severity protection, and mammary gland zinc secretion into milk. The combination of CDF domains, confident AlphaFold structure, Golgi localization, exclusive SLC30A/SLC39A interaction network, 2p CNV burden, and emerging cardiac/cancer/immune functions suggests SLC30A6 functions as a Golgi zinc efflux regulator coordinating secretory pathway metalloenzyme maturation, cardiac conduction, and tumor immunomodulation, with copy number variations potentially contributing to arrhythmias, cancer progression, and autoimmune phenotypes.
