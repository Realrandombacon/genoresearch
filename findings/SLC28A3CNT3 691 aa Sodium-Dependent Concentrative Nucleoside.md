## Description
SLC28A3 (Q9HAS3, 691 aa), also known as CNT3 (Concentrative Nucleoside Transporter 3), is a high-affinity, sodium-dependent transporter responsible for the uphill uptake of pyrimidine and purine nucleosides into cells. InterPro identifies a canonical architecture featuring an N-terminal regulatory domain (IPR002668, PF01773, aa 206-277), a central nucleoside recognition gate (IPR011642, PF07670, aa 285-383), and a large C-terminal transport core (IPR011657, PF07662, aa 388-610) belonging to the concentrative nucleoside transporter family (IPR008276, IPR018270, PTHR10590, TIGR00804, aa 85-630). This structure forms a complex membrane topology with multiple transmembrane helices optimized for coupling Na+ influx to nucleoside accumulation. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 80.3, residues 1-691), indicating a stable, well-folded transporter with distinct domains for substrate selection and ion coupling. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in the choroid plexus (31.6 nTPM), detected in many tissues; classified as a metabolic protein and transporter involved in symport. STRING reveals 10 high-confidence interactions defining the nucleoside metabolic network: SLC29A1 (0.894, ENT1 equilibrative transporter), SLC29A2 (0.894, ENT2), SLC29A3 (0.760), SLC29A4 (0.702), TYMP (0.561, thymidine phosphorylase), CBR3 (0.557, carbonyl reductase), DCK (0.553, deoxycytidine kinase), CDA (0.465, cytidine deaminase), TYMS (0.431, thymidylate synthase), and ABCC5 (0.402, MRP5 efflux pump). The strong links to both equilibrative (SLC29) and metabolic enzymes (DCK, TYMP) confirm its role as the primary importer feeding the salvage pathway. ClinVar contains 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains (chr9:203861-141020389, chr9:71416475-141020389) and inversions (chr9:12246100_101559378inv) affecting the 9q21 region; although no specific monogenic disease is explicitly named, the variant burden and choroid plexus expression implicate it in cerebrospinal fluid nucleoside homeostasis and potential neurodevelopmental or chemotherapeutic response defects. Functional hypothesis: SLC28A3/CNT3 functions as a broad-specificity (Km ~10-50 μM) Na+:nucleoside symporter (2:1 or 3:1 stoichiometry) that concentrates adenosine, uridine, cytidine, and guanosine against their gradients using the electrochemical sodium potential. Unlike CNT1/2 (pyrimidine/purine selective), CNT3 transports both classes, making it critical for maintaining intracellular nucleotide pools for DNA/RNA synthesis, especially in non-dividing cells like neurons or during rapid proliferation. In the choroid plexus, it likely mediates nucleoside clearance from CSF or supply to the brain parenchyma. Interaction with DCK suggests channeling of imported deoxynucleosides directly into phosphorylation for dNTP synthesis. Disruption via CNVs leads to altered nucleoside availability, potentially causing impaired salvage, increased reliance on de novo synthesis, or altered sensitivity to nucleoside analog drugs (e.g., gemcitabine, zidovudine). Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 703 aa, rat 705 aa) confirms essential metabolic role.
