## Description
N4BP1 encodes an 896 aa nucleocytoplasmic shuttling protein (UniProt O75113) that functions as a critical checkpoint regulator of innate immune responses through coordinated ubiquitin-dependent endoribonuclease activity, suppressing Toll-like receptor (TLR) signaling and cytokine production while integrating stress response pathways. This moderately studied gene (71 publications) has emerging clinical significance with 18 pathogenic copy number variants though specific monogenic diseases remain undefined. The protein features a multi-domain architecture enabling coordinated RNA metabolism and ubiquitin signaling: N-terminal first type I KH domain (cd09032/IPR056629/PF23050, residues 8-73/8-74) mediating RNA binding; second type I KH domain (cd22476/IPR056630/PF23052, residues 75-142/75-191) providing additional RNA recognition capacity; central UBA-like domain (IPR056631, residues 346-389) enabling ubiquitin chain recognition; C-terminal NYN ribonuclease domain (IPR021869/PF11977/cd18728, residues 617-769/620-746) containing the PIN-domain catalytic center for RNA cleavage; classified within ZC3H12/N4BP1 ribonuclease regulator family (IPR051101, residues 57-851) with GO terms for RNA nuclease activity, mRNA binding, ubiquitin binding, cellular response to UV, and negative regulation of viral genome replication. STRING interactions reveal N4BP1 operates within high-confidence innate immunity and ubiquitin signaling networks: IKBKG (0.901), inhibitor of nuclear factor kappa-B kinase subunit gamma essential for NF-κB activation; CASP8 (0.790), caspase-8 that inactivates N4BP1 during inflammation; NEDD4 (0.775), E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase providing the namesake binding partner; UBC (0.765), ubiquitin C polyubiquitin precursor; ITCH (0.665), E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase Itchy homolog; TRIM25 (0.555), tripartite motif-containing protein 25 E3 ligase; ZCCHC2 (0.531), zinc finger CCHC domain-containing protein 2; RNF19B (0.498), E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase; ZFAND3 (0.497), zinc finger AN1-type domain-containing protein 3; UBFD1 (0.479), ubiquitin fold modifier-binding protein 1. HPA confirms immune cell enriched expression with low tissue specificity, detected in all tissues, low cancer specificity, cell type enriched pattern, nucleoplasm/nucleoli fibrillar center/cytosol localization consistent with nucleocytoplasmic shuttling function, classified as predicted intracellular protein involved in immunity/innate immunity functioning as hydrolase/nuclease/RNA-binding protein. ClinVar lists 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants dominated by large-scale copy number losses (16q12.1 chr16:47430978-50956172 x1) and gains across 16q region (16q11.2-24.3 chr16:46432879-90294753 x3, 16q11.2-21 chr16:46503573-62203182 x3, 16q11.2-24.3 chr16:46503968-90155062 x3, 16p11.2-q21 chr16:34197492-64509054 x3, 16p13.3-q24.3 chr16:85881-90155062 x3) plus missense variant (c.2512A>T p.Ser838Cys) within the UBA domain region, demonstrating strong dosage sensitivity though specific disease phenotypes await characterization beyond immune dysregulation and potential developmental defects. AlphaFold structure prediction yields medium confidence (global pLDDT 66.8), revealing a partially structured protein with ordered catalytic domains connected by flexible linker regions consistent with intrinsically disordered regions enabling conformational plasticity during substrate recognition. Mechanistically, N4BP1 operates through integrated modes: (1) TLR signaling suppression—2024 study (25 citations) demonstrates N4BP1 coordinates ubiquitin-dependent crosstalk within IκB kinase family to limit MyD88-dependent TLR signaling and cytokine production; (2) Caspase-8 inactivation—during active inflammation caspase-8 cleaves and inactivates N4BP1 creating a feedback loop permitting inflammatory responses; (3) AML chemoresistance—2025 study (4 citations) shows exosomal miR-92b-5p regulates N4BP1 enhancing PTEN mono-ubiquitination in doxorubicin-resistant acute myeloid leukemia; (4) NF-κB modulation—2025 review (1 citation) establishes N4BP1 as emerging critical regulator of NF-κB pathway controlling inflammation and immune responses; (5) Heat shock protection—2025 study demonstrates N4BP1 recognizes NEDD8 aggregates protecting cells under heat stress via nucleocytoplasmic shuttling; (6) RNA metabolism—dual KH domains enable mRNA binding while NYN domain provides endoribonuclease activity for targeted RNA degradation; (7) Ubiquitin signaling—UBA domain binds polyubiquitin chains coordinating with NEDD4/ITCH E3 ligases; (8) Antiviral defense—negative regulation of viral genome replication suggests role in intrinsic immunity. Loss-of-function causes hyperactive TLR signaling, excessive cytokine production, impaired stress response, altered chemosensitivity, and potential immune dysregulation.
