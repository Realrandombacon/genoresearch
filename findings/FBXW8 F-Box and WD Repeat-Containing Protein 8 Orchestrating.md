## Description
FBXW8 (F-Box and WD Repeat-Containing Protein 8) is a 598 amino acid cytosolic substrate recognition component of SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box protein) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes that mediates ubiquitin-dependent proteasomal degradation of specific substrates including PPT1 (Palmitoyl-Protein Thioesterase 1), viral nucleocapsid proteins, and stemness factors through F-box-dependent CUL7/CUL1 scaffold assembly and WD40 repeat-mediated substrate binding. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic F-box/WD40 architecture including N-terminal F-box domain (IPR001810, PF12937, cd22134, IPR036047, residues 113-162) for SKP1 binding and SCF complex assembly with protein binding function (GO:0005515), seven WD40 repeats (IPR001680, PS50082, residues 198-236/239-285/289-326 and beyond) forming β-propeller structure for substrate recognition and protein-protein interactions (GO:0005515), YVTN repeat-like/quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase superfamily domains (G3DSA:2.130.10.10, residues 195-331/361-583) providing structural scaffold, quinoprotein alcohol dehydrogenase-like superfamily (IPR011047, residues 207-526) for fold conservation, and WD40/YVTN repeat-containing domain superfamily (IPR015943) defining the substrate-binding platform. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8N3Y1-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 76.8) across the 598 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded F-box/WD40 protein with stable β-propeller structure suitable for substrate recognition and E3 ligase complex assembly. HPA data classifies FBXW8 as an intracellular protein involved in Ubl (ubiquitin-like) conjugation pathway with cytosolic localization reflecting SCF complex function, ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), low tissue/cancer specificity, and cell type-enhanced distribution consistent with housekeeping ubiquitination machinery. STRING interactions reveal exceptionally tight coupling to SCF E3 ligase machinery: CUL7 (Cullin-7, 0.999) as primary scaffold partner for FBXW8-specific complex assembly; GLMN (Glomulin, 0.979) for CRL complex regulation; OBSL1 (Obscurin-Like 1, 0.909) as substrate/adaptor; IRS1 (Insulin Receptor Substrate 1, 0.825) as potential degradation target; CUL1 (0.813) as alternative cullin scaffold; UBA3 (Ubiquitin-Like Modifier Activating Enzyme 3, 0.745) for NEDD8 activation; RBX1 (RING Box Protein 1, 0.698) as E2 recruiting subunit; FBXO4 (0.545) and SKP2 (0.469) as related F-box proteins; and SKP1 (0.428) as universal adaptor. ClinVar lists 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 12q23.1-24.33, 12q24.21-24.33, 12q24.22-24.33, 12p13.33-q24.33 (spanning 17-133 Mb regions), copy number losses on 12q24.21-24.23, splice site mutations (c.588+1G>A, c.1240-186G>T), and in-frame deletions (c.319-10_319-9del), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in cancer susceptibility and developmental disorders. Literature (71 papers) establishes FBXW8 as an emerging E3 ligase regulator: FBXW8-mediated degradation of PPT1 suppresses epithelial-mesenchymal transition and metastasis in hepatocellular carcinoma revealing tumor suppressor function; tumor cell-derived exosomal METTL3 induces choriocarcinoma malignant progression by targeting FBXW8 establishing oncogenic regulation; KAP1 regulates Nanog protein stability by interfering with FBXW8-dependent ubiquitination linking to embryonic stem cell pluripotency control; FBXW8 suppresses porcine deltacoronavirus (PDCoV) proliferation via NPD52-dependent autophagic degradation of viral nucleocapsid protein demonstrating antiviral restriction function; and FBXW8 involvement in choriocarcinoma JEG-3 cell proliferation confirming reproductive cancer role. As a well-characterized F-box protein with emerging disease associations, FBXW8 represents a critical node connecting SCF E3 ubiquitin ligase assembly to substrate-specific protein degradation, cancer metastasis suppression, stemness factor turnover, and antiviral immunity through F-box-mediated CUL7/CUL1 recruitment and WD40-dependent substrate recognition disrupted in hepatocellular carcinoma, choriocarcinoma, and viral infection.
