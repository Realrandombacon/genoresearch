## Description
SRPRB (Signal Recognition Particle Receptor Subunit Beta, Q9Y5M8), also known as SR-beta or docking protein beta subunit, encodes a 271 aa type II transmembrane GTPase functioning as the essential membrane anchor of the SRP receptor complex that mediates co-translational targeting of secretory and membrane proteins to the endoplasmic reticulum (ER).
