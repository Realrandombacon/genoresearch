## Description
GNPDA2 encodes glucosamine-6-phosphate deaminase 2, an enzyme involved in carbohydrate metabolism. It interacts with AMDHD2, GNPDA1, and GFPT1, suggesting a role in glucosamine metabolism. High expression in various tissues, including the brain, heart, and liver, indicates its importance in cellular processes. Pathogenic variants associated with copy number gains may contribute to developmental disorders.
