# Coiled-Coil Scaffold Protein CCDC28A: Nucleoporin Fusion Partner in Myeloproliferative Neoplasms

## Description
CCDC28A encodes a 274 amino acid coiled-coil scaffold protein (UniProt Q8IWP9) functioning as a nuclear-cytoplasmic adaptor with oncogenic potential. InterPro analysis identifies a C-terminal coiled-coil domain (IPR025271, residues 91-273; PF13270, 173-258) mediating protein dimerization and complex assembly. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured conformation (pLDDT 61.5), consistent with intrinsically disordered regions enabling conformational flexibility for multi-partner binding. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic and plasma membrane localization, supporting a shuttling adaptor role. STRING interactions reveal 9 high-confidence partners including ECT2L (0.777, RhoGEF cytokinesis regulator), NUP98 (0.635, nucleoporin), KIAA1109 (0.616, microtubule-associated protein), and MYOG (0.534, myogenic transcription factor). ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic variants, predominantly 6q chromosomal copy number alterations, with recurrent NUP98-CCDC28A fusion in acute leukemias driving myeloproliferative neoplasms. The coiled-coil architecture positions CCDC28A as a molecular scaffold bridging nucleoporin complexes to cytoskeletal regulators (ECT2L, KIAA1109) and transcriptional machinery (MYOG). Oncogenic NUP98-CCDC28A fusions likely dysregulate nucleocytoplasmic transport and cell cycle progression via constitutive ECT2L activation, promoting myeloid proliferation.
