# CEP19: Centrosomal 19kDa Protein Orchestrating Ciliary IFT-B Complex Assembly via RABL2 GTPase for BBSome-Mediated GPCR Export in Obesity and Ciliopathy Pathogenesis

**Confidence:** HIGH

## Description
CEP19 (Centrosomal Protein of 19 kDa, Q96LK0) encodes a 163 aa centrosomal protein functioning as a critical ciliary gate regulator that initiates intraflagellar transport (IFT) by bridging RABL2 GTPase with the IFT-B complex for BBSome-mediated ciliary GPCR trafficking. Structural architecture reveals a CEP19-specific domain (IPR029412/PF14933/PTHR31539, residues 1-156/6-156/1-98) forming a compact alpha-helical bundle; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 82.8) confirms well-folded stable structure consistent with protein-protein interaction hub function at the ciliary base. STRING interactions define ciliary assembly and centrosomal networks: Rab-like GTPase RABL2A (0.948) forming mutually exclusive GTPase complex, centrosomal markers CEP350 (0.702)/CEP89 (0.645) anchoring to basal body, single-pass membrane protein SMCO1 (0.633), phosphatase regulatory subunit PPP2R3C (0.610), ubiquitin-binding protein UBXN7 (0.598), WD-repeat protein WDR53 (0.593), centriolar satellite protein SCLT1 (0.557), FGFR1 oncogene partner FGFR1OP (0.469), and RABL2B paralog (0.454). HPA localizes CEP19 to nucleoplasm/centrosome/basal body/cytosol with involvement in cilium biogenesis/degradation and obesity as disease phenotype, classified as disease-related intracellular protein with low tissue specificity detected in many tissues. ClinVar documents 94 pathogenic variants including 3q29 copy number losses/gains (chr3:195951024-197620100 deletions, chr3:132561657-197851986 duplications), splice site mutations (c.130+1G>A), and large deletions (chr3:196051941_197546443del). Literature (26 papers) demonstrates established ciliary roles: CEP19-RABL2-IFT-B axis controls BBSome-mediated ciliary GPCR export essential for Hedgehog signaling (6x citations, 2022), novel homozygous c.169C>T p.Arg57* nonsense variant causing severe early-onset obesity with diabetic ketoacidosis (1x citation, 2023), CEP19-RABL2 GTPase complex binding IFT-B to initiate intraflagellar transport at ciliary base (102x citations, 2017), RABL2-CEP19 interaction required for ciliary assembly and sperm motility (75x citations, 2017). Mechanistic hypothesis: CEP19 localizes to the transition zone at ciliary base where it acts as molecular switch for IFT initiation; in GDP-bound state, RABL2A binds CEP19 forming heterodimeric complex that recruits IFT-B particles (IFT88/IFT81/IFT74/IFT70) to basal body; GTP loading on RABL2A triggers conformational change releasing IFT-B for anterograde transport into cilium; CEP19-RABL2 interaction is mutually exclusive with IFT-B binding enabling cyclic recruitment; BBSome complex (BBS1/BBS2/BBS4/BBS5/BBS7/BBS8/BBS9/BBS10/BBS12/BBS18) loaded with ciliary GPCRs (SSTR3, MCHR1, GPR161) docks at CEP19-RABL2 platform for export from cilium; loss of CEP19 disrupts GPCR trafficking causing defective Hedgehog signaling leading to obesity through impaired leptin/melanocortin receptor ciliary localization; 3q29 CNVs cause syndromic ciliopathy with obesity, intellectual disability, and cardiac defects; early-onset obesity phenotype mirrors Bardet-Biedl syndrome supporting ciliary dysfunction mechanism.
