# ZNF620: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Centrosomal Localization and 3p22.1 Dosage Sensitivity

**Date:** 2026-03-23T00:22:45.564363

**Quality Score:** 1.8/10  (E=3.3, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
ZNF620 (zinc finger protein 620) encodes a 422 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcriptional repressor (UniProt Q6ZNG0) with unusual centrosomal subcellular localization. The protein features a canonical N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (InterPro IPR001909, IPR036051, Pfam PF01352, residues 10-82) that recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors, followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (IPR013087, PF00096 at positions 196-279) and a zinc-finger double domain (PF13465, residues 350-375). HPA data indicates low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues and immune cell enhancement, suggesting a housekeeping transcriptional repression function with potential roles in hematopoietic lineages. The centrosomal localization is atypical for KRAB zinc fingers and may indicate a dual role in cell cycle regulation or mitotic spindle organization. ClinVar documents 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains spanning 3p26.3-22.1, indicating dosage sensitivity at the 3p22.1 locus. The KRAB domain mechanistically recruits heterochromatin-forming complexes including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase to silence target genes. As a C2H2 zinc finger protein, ZNF620 likely recognizes GC-rich promoter/enhancer motifs and establishes repressive chromatin states. Haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity disrupts transcriptional homeostasis, potentially contributing to 3p deletion syndrome phenotypes including intellectual disability and growth retardation.
