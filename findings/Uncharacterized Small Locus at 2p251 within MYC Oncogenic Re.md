## Description
LOC101929551 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 2p25.1 (NC_000002.12:7899654-7886766) spanning approximately 12.9 kb within the critical 2p25 MYC amplification region. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000226506 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - predominantly copy number gains (duplications): (1) chr2:236816-45983232 dup (~45.7 Mb, 2p25.3-21), (2) chr2:12770-25039694 dup (~25 Mb, 2p25.3-23.3), (3) chr2:12770-33711509 dup (~33.7 Mb, 2p25.3-22.3), (4) chr2:17019-20001056 dup (~20 Mb, 2p25.3-24.1), (5) chr2:1664615-23664142 dup (~22 Mb, 2p25.3-24.1), (6) chr2:17019-26318846 dup (~26.3 Mb, 2p25.3-23.3), (7) chr2:30341-28419664 dup (~28.4 Mb, 2p25.3-23.2), (8) chr2:66097-55570637 dup (~55.6 Mb, 2p25.3-16.1), (9) chr2:50661-9652907 dup (~9.6 Mb, 2p25.3-25.1), plus 1 deletion (chr2:6531172-16103799 del ~9.6 Mb). The 2p25 region is the critical locus for MYC proto-oncogene (N-MYC in neuroblastoma, c-MYC in lymphomas, transcription factor, cell proliferation, apoptosis regulation) amplifications and rearrangements in multiple cancers, containing dosage-sensitive oncogenes including MYC (transcriptional activator, DNA binding, chromatin remodeling), PAM (peptidylglycine alpha-amidating monooxygenase, neuropeptide biosynthesis), THRAP2 (thyroid hormone receptor-associated protein 2, transcriptional coactivator), and multiple enhancer elements. LOC101929551 likely functions as: (1) a cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 2p25 MYC expression in proliferating cells, (2) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) with epigenetic regulatory function in oncogenic transformation, (3) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 2p25 topologically associating domain (TAD) containing MYC, or (4) a recombination hotspot boundary affecting MYC amplification susceptibility. The moderate pathogenic CNV burden (11 variants) with predominantly duplications suggests: (1) this locus is dosage-sensitive for MYC regulation, (2) duplications may disrupt 2p25 chromatin architecture leading to MYC overexpression, (3) copy number gains may alter cell cycle control contributing to neuroblastoma, Burkitt lymphoma, small cell lung cancer, or breast cancer pathogenesis, or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus with potent oncogenic regulatory effects. The 2p25 region is frequently amplified in neuroblastoma (N-MYC) and lymphoid malignancies (c-MYC), with MYC amplification being a key oncogenic driver.
