## Description
TMBIM1 (Transmembrane BAX Inhibitor Motif Containing 1), also known as Lifeguard 3 (LFG3), is a 311 amino acid multi-pass membrane protein that functions as an apoptosis suppressor and stress-responsive regulator of cell survival, mitophagy, and epithelial integrity through modulation of BAX-mediated cell death pathways and autophagic cargo degradation. InterPro analysis identifies characteristic Bax inhibitor-1 related architecture including Bax inhibitor 1-related family (IPR006214, residues 49-310) defining the TMBIM superfamily, Inhibitor of apoptosis-promoting Bax1 domain (PF01027, residues 95-310) for anti-apoptotic function, BAX INHIBITOR-RELATED family signature (PTHR23291, residues 49-308), and proteins similar to lifeguard (LFG) putative apoptosis regulator domain (cd10428, residues 89-308). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q969X1-F1) predicts confident folding (pLDDT 76.8) across the 311 residue sequence, indicating a stable multi-pass transmembrane protein suitable for ER/Golgi membrane integration and apoptotic signaling modulation. HPA data classifies TMBIM1 as a predicted intracellular and membrane protein involved in transport, with vesicular subcellular localization reflecting trafficking through secretory pathway compartments, low tissue specificity (detected in all tissues), cell type-enhanced distribution, and implications in cancer and inflammatory disease. STRING interactions reveal functional coupling to apoptosis regulation and extracellular matrix remodeling: TMBIM6 (Bax inhibitor-1, 0.670) as the founding family member with shared anti-apoptotic function; GHITM (Growth hormone inducible transmembrane protein, 0.657) for mitochondrial integrity; MMP2 (Matrix metalloproteinase 2, 0.548) and MMP9 (0.530) for extracellular matrix degradation during metastasis; RUFY4 (RUN and FYVE domain containing 4, 0.469) for endosomal trafficking; APOOL (Apoptosis associated mitochondrial protein, 0.432) for mitochondrial apoptosis; and ZNF142 (Zinc finger protein 142, 0.424) for transcriptional regulation. ClinVar lists 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains on 2q35-37.3, 2q33.3-37.3, 2q34-36.1, and 2p25.3-q37.3 (multiple independent CNV events spanning 178-243 Mb), copy number losses on 2q34-35, and missense mutations (p.Arg276Gln), though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting potential roles in cancer susceptibility or inflammatory disorder. Literature (38 papers) establishes TMBIM1 as a multifunctional survival factor: the TMBIM1-YBX1 axis orchestrates myeloid-derived suppressor cell (MDSC) recruitment and immunosuppressive microenvironment in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC); TMBIM1 ameliorates sepsis-induced cardiac dysfunction by promoting Parkin-mediated mitophagy to clear damaged mitochondria; TMBIM1 promotes epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) by accelerating autophagic degradation of E-cadherin in glioblastoma; loss of epithelial homeostasis driven by TMBIM1 depletion via E-cadherin junction disassembly contributes to colorectal cancer pathogenesis; and TMBIM1 functions as a stress-responsive protein balancing cell survival and death decisions. As a well-studied apoptosis regulator with emerging roles in cancer immune evasion and inflammatory disease, TMBIM1 represents a critical node connecting ER stress response to autophagic cargo selection, mitochondrial quality control, and tumor microenvironment modulation through YBX1-dependent immunosuppressive signaling.
