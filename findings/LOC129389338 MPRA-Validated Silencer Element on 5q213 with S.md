# LOC129389338: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 5q21.3 with Strong Deletion-Biased CNV Burden (11 Del:1 Dup)

## Description
LOC129389338 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 5q21.3 (GRCh38: 108303174-108303374), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 12 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a strong deletion-biased burden (11 deletions:1 duplication ratio). Deletions span 5q14.3-33.2 regions (~20-50Mb) while the single duplication spans 5q21.3-22.1 (~4Mb). This striking deletion predominance (92% deletions) suggests strong haploinsufficiency where silencer loss causes derepression of target genes. These recurrent CNVs are associated with 5q deletion syndromes (5q- syndrome, cri-du-chat spectrum) featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and hematologic abnormalities including myelodysplastic syndrome. The 5q21-22 region is gene-dense, containing critical dosage-sensitive genes such as APC (Adenomatous Polyposis Coli, Wnt signaling tumor suppressor), MEF2C (Myocyte Enhancer Factor 2C, neurodevelopment), NRG1 (Neuregulin-1, schizophrenia susceptibility), and IL5/IL13 cytokine cluster (Th2 immune response).\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389338 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 5q21-22 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental and immune genes. Candidate targets include MEF2C (cortical development, intellectual disability) or APC (Wnt pathway regulation, intestinal homeostasis). The silencer likely recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The strong deletion-biased CNV burden (11:1 ratio) indicates haploinsufficiency predominance where silencer loss causes target gene derepression. The K562 validation suggests potential roles in hematopoietic lineage specification, consistent with 5q- syndrome myelodysplastic features. Deletions may cause MEF2C derepression leading to neurodevelopmental phenotypes or APC overexpression causing Wnt pathway dysregulation, contributing to 5q deletion syndrome with intellectual disability and hematologic malignancy predisuction.
