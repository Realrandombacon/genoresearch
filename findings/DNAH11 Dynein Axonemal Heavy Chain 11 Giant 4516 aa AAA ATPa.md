## Description
DNAH11 (Dynein Axonemal Heavy Chain 11, UniProt Q96DT5) is a giant 4516 amino acid cytoplasmic and ciliary protein with moderate characterization (222 papers) that functions as an outer dynein arm (ODA) motor protein generating the force for ciliary and flagellar beating through ATP-dependent microtubule sliding, essential for mucociliary clearance, embryonic left-right axis determination, and sperm motility. The protein contains multiple dynein motor domains including P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase domains (G3DSA:3.40.50.300), AAA+ ATPase domains characteristic of dynein heavy chains, dynein heavy chain N-terminal domains (G3DSA:1.20.140.100/3.20.180.20 residues 1475-1723), Region D6 dynein motor domain (G3DSA:1.10.8.720 residues 4048-4210), and multiple additional catalytic and microtubule-binding domains distributed across its massive length, belonging to the axonemal dynein heavy chain family. STRING interactions reveal high-to-medium confidence partnerships with ciliary assembly and dynein arm components: DNAI1 (0.986, dynein axonemal intermediate chain 1—ODA component), RSPH4A (0.857, radial spoke head protein 4A—ciliary structure), DNAI2 (0.836, dynein axonemal intermediate chain 2), CCDC39 (0.811, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 39—inner dynein arm assembly), CCDC40 (0.809, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 40), RSPH9 (0.806, radial spoke head protein 9), DNAAF1 (0.805, dynein axonemal assembly factor 1—pre-assembly chaperone), CCDC114 (0.738, coiled-coil domain-containing protein 114—ODA docking complex), NME8 (0.724, nucleoside diphosphate kinase 8/thioredoxin-like—ciliary function), and DNAAF2 (0.673, dynein axonemal assembly factor 2), confirming integration into the complete ciliary motility apparatus.
