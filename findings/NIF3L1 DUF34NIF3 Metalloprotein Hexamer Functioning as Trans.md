## Description
NIF3L1 (NGG1 Interacting Factor 3 Like 1, NIF3-like Protein 1, Q9GZT8) encodes a 377 aa conserved metalloprotein functioning as a transcriptional corepressor that coordinates neural differentiation through COP9 signalosome interaction and couples transcription to mRNA export via THO complex association. Structural architecture reveals DUF34/NIF3 domain (IPR002678/IPR017222/IPR036069/PF01784/TIGR00486, residues 27-377/21-377/27-376/28-373/28-372) forming a dinuclear metal center hexameric assembly characteristic of NIF3 family proteins; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 91.8) indicates very high confidence well-folded structure with two NIF3-like subdomains (G3DSA:3.40.1390.30/SSF102705, residues 22-147/218-342) suggesting dual metal-binding sites enabling oligomerization. STRING interactions define transcriptional regulation and mRNA processing networks: THO complex subunit THOC7 (0.957) linking to TREX mRNA export machinery, COP9 signalosome subunit COPS2 (0.780) mediating cullin-RING ubiquitin ligase regulation, peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase PPIL3 (0.758), transcriptional adapter TADA3 (0.751), pyruvate kinase PKLR (0.722), ESCRT-I component VPS28 (0.703), iron-sulfur cluster assembly protein CIAO1 (0.693), Max dimerization protein MXD4 (0.680), carbohydrate-responsive element-binding protein MLX (0.651), and RNA polymerase II phosphatase CTDSP1 (0.484). HPA localizes NIF3L1 to vesicles as intracellular/membrane protein with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues and cell type-enhanced specificity. ClinVar documents 32 pathogenic variants including extensive 2q31-37 copy number losses (chr2:181362315-202911548, chr2:194127471-206791898, chr2:194305623-215261531) and gains (chr2:186698504-223918111, chr2:178397959-243007457). Literature (13 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: NIF3L1 functions as transcriptional corepressor in early neural differentiation via cooperation with Trip15/CSN2 (34x citations, 2003), novel cytoplasmic interaction partner NIF3L1 BP1 identified (19x citations, 2003), highly conserved from bacteria to mammals (18x citations, 2000). Mechanistic hypothesis: NIF3L1 forms hexameric ring structure with dinuclear metal centers (likely zinc/iron) at subunit interfaces stabilizing oligomeric assembly; N-terminal subdomain binds metal cofactors while C-terminal subdomain mediates protein-protein interactions; as transcriptional corepressor, NIF3L1 recruits COP9 signalosome via COPS2 interaction to deconjugate NEDD8 from cullin-RING ligases, modulating ubiquitin-dependent degradation of neural differentiation factors; THOC7 interaction links NIF3L1 to TREX complex coupling transcription elongation to mRNA nuclear export; vesicular localization suggests additional role in secretory pathway or exosome-mediated signaling; 2q CNVs cause syndromic phenotypes through NIF3L1 dosage effects on neural development; conservation from bacteria indicates ancient function in gene regulation possibly predating eukaryotic transcription machinery.
