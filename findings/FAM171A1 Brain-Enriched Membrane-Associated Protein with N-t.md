## Description
FAM171A1 (Family with Sequence Similarity 171 Member A1, UniProt Q5VUB5) is an 890-amino acid intracellular/membrane-associated protein characterized by conserved N-terminal and C-terminal domains functioning primarily in neuronal tissues with potential roles in cell adhesion and signal transduction. The protein belongs to the FAM171 family (IPR018890/PTHR31626/SUSHI DOMAIN-CONTAINING PROTEIN, residues 3-890) with distinct structural modules: an N-terminal FAM171 domain (IPR048530/PF10577, residues 34-287) and a C-terminal FAM171 domain (IPR049175/PF20771, residues 397-888) that together form the functional unit. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured architecture (pLDDT=57.1, medium confidence) consistent with a protein containing ordered domain regions embedded within flexible linker segments, potentially enabling dynamic conformational changes required for signal transduction or scaffolding functions. HPA reveals striking brain-enriched expression (97.5 nTPM, highest among all tissues) with tissue-enhanced specificity, detection in many tissues, group-enriched blood cell specificity, cell type enhancement, and dual subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and plasma membrane — suggesting roles in neuronal signaling, nuclear-cytosolic shuttling, or membrane-associated scaffold functions particularly in the central nervous system. STRING interaction network reveals associations with signaling regulators and adhesion molecules: RASSF10 (0.634, Ras association domain-containing protein 10, tumor suppressor regulating apoptosis and microtubule stability), PCDHGB1 (0.534, protocadherin gamma-B1, calcium-dependent cell-cell adhesion protein critical for neuronal circuit formation), NMT2 (0.491, N-myristoyltransferase 2, lipid modification enzyme targeting proteins to membranes), ARHGEF4 (0.438, Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor 4, activating RhoA GTPase for cytoskeletal dynamics), UNKL (0.437, uncharacterized kinase-like protein), CERK (0.429, ceramide kinase, sphingolipid metabolism and inflammatory signaling), CAMKK1 (0.426, calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase kinase 1, calcium signaling cascade), DUSP7 (0.414, dual specificity phosphatase 7, MAPK pathway regulation), TNFRSF17 (0.412, TNF receptor superfamily member 17/BCMA, B-cell survival), and ADGRA3 (0.411, adhesion GPCR A3, orphan receptor). The RASSF10 and PCDHGB1 interactions are particularly informative, placing FAM171A1 within networks controlling cell adhesion (protocadherins essential for synaptic specificity), apoptosis regulation, and neuronal connectivity. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large chromosomal deletions and duplications spanning 10p15.3-12.31 encompassing the FAM171A1 locus, indicating dosage sensitivity where haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity causes disease phenotypes potentially involving neurodevelopmental disorders given the brain-enriched expression pattern. The combination of brain-specific expression, membrane/nucleoplasmic localization, RASSF10/PCDHGB1 interaction partners, N-terminal/C-terminal domain architecture, partial structural ordering, and CNV-associated pathology supports a mechanistic model: FAM171A1 functions as a membrane-associated scaffolding protein in neurons, potentially bridging protocadherin-mediated cell adhesion (PCDHGB1) with RASSF10-regulated apoptotic/cytoskeletal signaling pathways, with N-myristoylation (NMT2 interaction) targeting the protein to plasma membrane compartments where it modulates neuronal connectivity, calcium signaling (CAMKK1), and MAPK pathway activity (DUSP7), while nucleoplasmic pools may regulate transcriptional programs, with dosage disruption causing neurodevelopmental disease through impaired neuronal circuit assembly or dysregulated apoptotic signaling.
