## Description
TMEM121 (Gene ID: 80757, UniProt Q9BTD3) is a 319 amino acid transmembrane protein located on chromosome 14q32.33 (NC_000014.9, positions 105526582-105530197). This protein belongs to the CECR6/TMEM121 family (IPR032776, residues 73-267; PF14997, residues 73-267; IPR042314, residues 1-319; PTHR31046, residues 1-319), a conserved protein family shared with CECR6 (cat eye syndrome chromosome region 6). AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q9BTD3-F1) shows confident folding with global pLDDT score of 72.8, indicating stable tertiary structure consistent with a well-folded protein despite lacking homology to characterized structural folds. Human Protein Atlas classifies TMEM121 as a predicted membrane protein with tissue-enhanced RNA expression, detected in many tissues with top expression in brain (11.8 nTPM). Subcellular localization is reported as cytosol, suggesting it may be a peripheral membrane protein or shuttle between compartments. Expression is also immune cell-enhanced, indicating potential roles in both neural and immune systems. STRING interaction network reveals 7 protein partners with medium confidence scores: TEDC1 (0.526, tubulin epsilon and delta complex), ADAM18 (0.472, disintegrin/metalloproteinase), RD3L (0.450, retina degeneration 3-like), MAMDC4 (0.443, MAM domain-containing), TEX22 (0.417, testis-expressed), SIVA1 (0.416, apoptosis regulator), and ZNF772 (0.403, zinc finger protein). Notable interactors include SIVA1 (pro-apoptotic protein) and TEDC1 (tubulin chaperone), suggesting potential roles in cytoskeletal dynamics and cell death regulation. ClinVar contains 57 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number variations spanning chromosome 14q32 regions including deletions (chr14:101180490-106329074)x1 and duplications (chr14:97521552-107285437)x3, indicating strong dosage sensitivity of this genomic region. The 14q32.33 locus is associated with Temple syndrome, Kagami-Ogata syndrome, and various developmental disorders when imprinted gene expression is disrupted. Conservation across vertebrates is evident with orthologs in mouse and other mammals. Functional hypothesis: TMEM121 functions as a cytosolic regulatory protein that modulates cytoskeletal organization and apoptotic signaling, potentially through interaction with tubulin chaperones (TEDC1) and apoptosis regulators (SIVA1). The CECR6/TMEM121 family domain architecture suggests it may act as a scaffold protein facilitating protein-protein interactions. Brain-enriched expression (11.8 nTPM) combined with 14q32.33 location near imprinted gene clusters suggests TMEM121 may contribute to neurodevelopmental processes sensitive to gene dosage. Disease mechanism likely involves haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity where altered TMEM121 dosage disrupts cytoskeletal dynamics and apoptotic thresholds during brain development, potentially contributing to 14q32 deletion/duplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, growth abnormalities, and neurological deficits. Priority experiments include validation of STRING-predicted interactions via co-immunoprecipitation, subcellular fractionation to confirm cytosolic vs membrane localization, CRISPR knockout phenotyping in neuronal models, and investigation of imprinting status given 14q32.33 location.
