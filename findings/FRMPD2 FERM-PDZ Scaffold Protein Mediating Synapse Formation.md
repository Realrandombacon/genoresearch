## Description
FRMPD2 (FERM and PDZ domain-containing protein 2, 1309 aa, UniProt Q68DX3) is a partially characterized scaffolding protein with emerging roles in synapse formation, NMDA receptor clustering, and cell polarization. Only 15 publications exist, confirming genuine partial characterization. InterPro analysis reveals canonical scaffold architecture: three FERM subdomains (F1: cd17196/341-434, B-lobe: cd14473/443-542, C-lobe: cd13187/546-647) for membrane-cytoskeleton linkage, plus two PDZ domains (PDZ2: cd06792/947-1033, PDZ3: cd06695/1078-1166) for protein-protein interactions. STRING interactions confirm high-confidence partnerships with PTPN20 (0.951), PKP4/plakophilin-4 (0.803), SARS1 (0.609), CTNND1/p120-catenin (0.597), PKN2 (0.539), ARVCF (0.503), PTPN7 (0.500), PTPN18 (0.488), REM1 (0.470), and CTNND2/δ-catenin (0.426) - all junctional/scaffolding proteins. HPA shows retina-enriched expression (22.9 nTPM, tissue enriched), Golgi apparatus/vesicle localization, cell type enhanced pattern, not detected in immune cells. ClinVar contains 47 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including chromosome 10q CNVs (copy number losses/gains, deletions) though specific disease associations remain undefined. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.6, medium confidence) indicates partially structured protein with ordered FERM/PDZ domains and flexible linker regions typical for scaffolding proteins. Literature reveals: human-specific multicopy FRMPD2 promotes synapse formation via neuroligin 1 recruitment (2026); second PDZ domain binds GluN2A subunit of NMDA receptors (2019, 5 citations); crystal structure of PDZ2 domain solved (2019); FRMPD2 as novel GluN2A-interacting scaffold in synaptic excitatory transmission (2018); role in cell polarization and zebrafish development (2017); basolateral membrane localization in polarized epithelial cells associated with tight junction formation. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: FRMPD2 functions as FERM-PDZ scaffold protein that bridges membrane-cytoskeleton linkage (via FERM domain) with synaptic/junctional protein clustering (via PDZ domains), specifically recruiting NMDA receptors (GluN2A binding) and neuroligin 1 to postsynaptic densities in retina and excitatory synapses. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: FERM domain (341-647) anchors FRMPD2 to plasma membrane and cytoskeleton at basolateral membranes/synapses, while PDZ2 domain (947-1033) directly binds GluN2A C-terminus and PDZ3 domain (1078-1166) recruits neuroligin 1 and other synaptic proteins. Interaction with p120-catenin (CTNND1, 0.597) and δ-catenin (CTNND2, 0.426) links FRMPD2 to adherens junctions. High-confidence PTPN20 interaction (0.951) suggests regulation by tyrosine phosphatase signaling. Retina enrichment (22.9 nTPM) indicates specialized role in visual synapse formation. With only 15 papers, defined FERM-PDZ architecture, known GluN2A/neuroligin interactions, 47 ClinVar variants, and emerging synaptic roles, FRMPD2 represents a genuine partially-characterized dark gene worthy of further investigation.
