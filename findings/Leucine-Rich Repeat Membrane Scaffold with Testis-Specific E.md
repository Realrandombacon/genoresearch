# Leucine-Rich Repeat Membrane Scaffold with Testis-Specific Expression and Cancer Association

## Description
LRRC69 encodes a 347 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) protein anchored to the plasma membrane. The protein contains multiple LRR domains (residues 36-233, IPR001611/PF13855) forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 91.6) consistent with a stable LRR solenoid fold. HPA data shows testis-enriched expression (2.7 nTPM) with plasma membrane localization and cancer-enhanced expression pattern, suggesting roles in germ cell development and oncogenesis. STRING interactions reveal associations with GDPGP1 (0.665), PRRG3 (0.650), and SHOX (0.624), implicating LRRC69 in GTPase signaling, cytoskeletal organization, and developmental pathways. The 35 ClinVar pathogenic variants including copy number alterations at 8q21.3 indicate dosage sensitivity and disease relevance. LRR proteins typically serve as molecular scaffolds that nucleate signaling complexes at membranes. I propose LRRC69 functions as a testis-specific membrane scaffold that organizes GTPase-dependent signaling complexes during spermatogenesis, with dysregulation contributing to germ cell tumors through aberrant signal transduction. The LRR domain architecture enables simultaneous binding to multiple partners, positioning LRRC69 as a hub protein coordinating membrane-proximal signaling events.
