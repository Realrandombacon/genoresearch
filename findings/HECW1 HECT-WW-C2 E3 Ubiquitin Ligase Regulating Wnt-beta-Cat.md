## Description
HECW1 (HECT, C2 and WW Domain Containing E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase 1, UniProt Q76N89) is a 1,606-amino acid HECT-family E3 ubiquitin ligase functioning as a key regulator of Wnt/β-catenin signaling through DVL1 ubiquitination and showing cancer prognostic value across multiple tumor types. InterPro analysis confirms canonical HECT E3 ligase architecture: HECT domain (IPR000569/cd00078, aa 1250-1606) with GO annotation for ubiquitin-protein transferase activity (GO:0004842) catalyzing ubiquitin transfer from E2 conjugating enzymes to substrate proteins; two WW domains (IPR001202/cd00201, aa 829-862 & 1018-1051) with GO annotation for protein binding (GO:0005515) mediating substrate recognition through proline-rich motif interactions; C2 domain (IPR000008/cd08691, aa 182-318) characteristic of NEDD4-like ubiquitin ligases enabling calcium-dependent membrane association; CATH structural superfamilies including HECT catalytic domains (G3DSA:3.30.2160.10, G3DSA:3.30.2410.10, G3DSA:3.90.1750.10, aa 1210-1605) and WW domain folds (G3DSA:2.20.70.10, G3DSA:2.60.40.150). Functional hypothesis: HECW1 serves as a NEDD4-family HECT E3 ubiquitin ligase that negatively regulates Wnt/β-catenin signaling through ubiquitination and degradation of DVL1 (dishevelled), acting as a context-dependent tumor suppressor in cervical cancer while showing oncogenic roles in gastric cancer and renal cell carcinoma, with NDFIP1 serving as critical adaptor protein recruiting HECW1 to specific substrates.
