## Description
NAA11 (ARD1B) encodes a 229 aa catalytic subunit of the NatA N-terminal acetyltransferase complex (UniProt Q9BSU3), functioning as a tissue-specific paralog of NAA10/ARD1. The protein contains the GNAT acetyltransferase domain (IPR000182/PF00583, aa1-152) with N-acyltransferase superfamily fold (cd04301, aa42-110) and Ard1-like acetyltransferase architecture (IPR045047, aa1-201). AlphaFold prediction shows confident structure (pLDDT 79.4), indicating a well-folded GNAT fold typical of acetyltransferases. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (5.8 nTPM) with additional placenta expression (3.9 nTPM), and subcellular localization to nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, primary cilium, centrosome, and basal body—suggesting specialized roles in germ cell development and ciliary function. STRING interactions reveal tight NatA complex membership: NAA16 (auxiliary, 0.998), NAA15 (scaffold, 0.997), NAA10 (catalytic paralog, 0.935), NAA50 (0.876), HYPK (0.759), plus connections to NatC/NatE complexes (NAA35 0.962, NAA30 0.944). ClinVar contains 14 pathogenic variants (primarily 4q copy number losses/gains), though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. Literature (9 papers) demonstrates tissue-restricted expression regulated by DNA methylation, with NAA11 enabling NatA activity when NAA10 is absent or in specific developmental contexts. NAA11 likely functions as a specialized catalytic subunit providing NatA activity in testis and placenta, where NAA10 expression may be limited, enabling co-translational acetylation of nascent polypeptides with Ser-, Ala-, Thr-, Gly-, Val-, or Cys- N-termini. The distinct subcellular localization (cilium, centrosome, basal body) suggests NAA11 may acetylate ciliary/centrosomal proteins critical for spermatogenesis and embryonic development.
