## Description
CDK5RAP2 (aliases: Cep215, MCPH3) is a 1893 aa protein acting as a critical scaffold for centrosome duplication and microtubule nucleation. Structural analysis reveals a modular architecture with an N-terminal Centrosomin motif (IPR012943) and C-terminal Myomegalin domain (IPR060527), connected by a central coiled-coil region (IPR056273). AlphaFold modeling (pLDDT 59.1) suggests the protein possesses intrinsically disordered regions, potentially facilitating dynamic interactions or phase separation. High-confidence STRING interactions (0.93–0.99) with key centrosomal proteins (PCNT, CEP152, ASPM, MCPH1) confirm its role in the MTOC. ClinVar data identifies 152 pathogenic variants, predominantly frameshifts (e.g., p.His1264fs, p.Ile223fs), which likely disrupt the oligomerization or structural integrity of the scaffold. This dysregulation is hypothesized to cause defective centriole engagement and microcephaly, representing a 'dark gene' mechanism where loss of scaffold stability leads to neurodevelopmental defects.
