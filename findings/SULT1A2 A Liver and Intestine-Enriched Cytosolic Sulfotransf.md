## Description
SULT1A2 (Sulfotransferase Family 1A Member 2) is a 295-amino acid cytosolic enzyme characterized by a canonical sulfotransferase domain architecture (IPR000863/PF00685/PTHR11783/G3DSA:3.40.50.300/IPR027417/SSF52540, positions 38-287 for sulfotransferase core domain, 1-295 for P-loop containing nucleotide triphosphate hydrolase fold, 26-285 for cytosolic sulfotransferase 1 family) containing the conserved 5'-phosphosulfate-binding loop (P-loop) and catalytic Tyr149 residue mediating sulfonate transfer from PAPS (3'-phosphoadenosine-5'-phosphosulfate) donor to phenolic acceptor substrates, classified under sulfotransferase activity (GO:0008146), catecholamine metabolism (GO:0006584), lipid metabolism (GO:0006629), and steroid metabolism (GO:0008202). STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network exclusively with sulfotransferase family members: SULT1A1 (0.999, sulfotransferase 1A1/phenol-preferring phenol sulfotransferase - highest confidence paralogue indicating functional redundancy), SULT1A4 (0.830, thermolabile phenol sulfotransferase), GOT1 (0.769, glutamate oxaloacetate transaminase 1 linking to amino acid metabolism), SULT1C2 (0.708), SULT1A3 (0.644, monoamine-preferring phenol sulfotransferase), SULT4A1 (0.569), STS (0.543, steroid sulfatase), SULT1C4 (0.526), APOBR (0.519, apolipoprotein B receptor linking to lipid metabolism), and SULT2B1 (0.445), indicating SULT1A2 functions as an integral component of the phase II detoxification network. HPA data shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in intestine (49.1 nTPM) and liver (47.6 nTPM), detected in many tissues, cancer-enhanced specificity, group-enriched blood cell specificity, cell type-enriched single cell specificity, classified as an enzyme and metabolic protein with transferase molecular function. ClinVar contains 73 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number gains and losses spanning 16p12.1-11.2, 16p12.2-11.2, 16p11.2, and 16p12.2-13.2 regions (duplications and deletions affecting chromosome 16 short arm including the SULT1A cluster), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in metabolic disorders and hematologic malignancies. AlphaFold structure (AF-P50226-F1) predicts very high confidence folding (pLDDT 96.7) consistent with an extremely well-structured enzyme containing ordered catalytic domains typical of cytosolic sulfotransferases with defined PAPS-binding pocket and substrate recognition cleft. With 123 papers, SULT1A2 represents a 'gray' gene with established biochemical function but emerging clinical associations (2018-2026): SNP rs1059491 associates with obesity and dyslipidemia in southern Chinese adults identifying it as a metabolic risk locus; SULT1A2 identified as principal human enzyme catalyzing vitamin B6 sulfonation via Tyr149-dependent mechanism linking to micronutrient metabolism; coding variants associated with obesity in Chinese children through whole-exome sequencing; SULT1A2 expression serves as prognostic biomarker in bladder cancer with chemical carcinogenic implications; and SULT1A2 polymorphisms investigated for drug metabolism variability. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that SULT1A2 functions as a phenol-preferring sulfotransferase that: (1) utilizes PAPS as sulfate donor transferring sulfonate groups to phenolic xenobiotics, catecholamines, steroids, and vitamin B6 through Tyr149 nucleophilic attack mechanism; (2) exhibits tissue-specific expression in intestine (49.1 nTPM) and liver (47.6 nTPM) aligning with first-pass metabolism and systemic detoxification roles; (3) shows functional redundancy with SULT1A1 (0.999 score) providing backup capacity for phenolic compound conjugation explaining why monogenic deficiency may be tolerated; (4) contributes to obesity and dyslipidemia pathogenesis through altered sulfonation of lipid signaling molecules or catecholamine hormones affecting energy homeostasis; (5) undergoes cancer-enhanced expression in bladder tumors potentially activating procarcinogens through sulfonation bioactivation; chromosome 16p11.2-12.2 CNVs affecting the SULT1A gene cluster may produce dosage effects on detoxification capacity influencing drug response, chemical susceptibility, and metabolic disease risk.
