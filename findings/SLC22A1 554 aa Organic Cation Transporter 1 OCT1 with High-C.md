## Description
SLC22A1 (O15245, 554 aa), also known as OCT1 (Organic Cation Transporter 1), HOCT1, or oct1_cds, is a polyspecific organic cation transporter belonging to solute carrier family 22 (SLC22) within the Major Facilitator Superfamily (MFS). Functional hypothesis: SLC22A1/OCT1 functions as a facilitative polyspecific organic cation transporter mediating bidirectional cellular uptake of endogenous cations (dopamine, serotonin, histamine, thiamine, creatinine) and xenobiotic drugs (metformin, morphine, tramadol, cimetidine) across hepatocyte basolateral membranes via alternating access mechanism.
