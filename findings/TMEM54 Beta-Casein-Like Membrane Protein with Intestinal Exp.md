## Description
TMEM54 (also known as BCLP, CAC-1, or CAC1) encodes a 222 amino acid single-pass transmembrane protein (UniProt Q969K7) belonging to the beta-casein-like protein family. The protein contains a conserved beta-casein-like domain (IPR020977/PF12304, positions 7-218) and is classified within the keratinocyte-associated protein 3 family (PTHR31258). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 77.8) consistent with stable membrane protein architecture. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with exceptionally high levels in intestine (242.6 nTPM), detection in many tissues, and vesicular subcellular localization suggesting roles in endosomal/lysosomal trafficking or secretory pathways. STRING interactions reveal associations with transmembrane protein TMEM92 (0.448), zinc finger proteins ZNF518B (0.416) and ZNF506 (0.405), galactose-3-sulfotransferase GAL3ST1 (0.412), keratin-related protein KRBA2 (0.411), TMEM134 (0.404), and small E-Rich factor SERF1B (0.404), indicating potential roles in transcriptional regulation, glycolipid modification, and protein aggregation control. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains at 1p35.1. Orthologs exist in mouse and rat with conserved domain architecture. The beta-casein-like fold combined with intestinal enrichment and vesicular localization positions TMEM54 as a potential regulator of intestinal epithelial secretion, mucin processing, or nutrient absorption through modulation of vesicular trafficking and membrane protein sorting.
