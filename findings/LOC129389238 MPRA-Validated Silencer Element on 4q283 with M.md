# LOC129389238: MPRA-Validated Silencer Element on 4q28.3 with Moderate Duplication-Biased CNV Burden (3 Del:5 Dup)

## Description
LOC129389238 is a 200bp cis-regulatory element (CRE) located on chromosome 4q28.3 (GRCh38: 133529760-133529960), functionally validated as a transcriptional silencer through STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in K562 erythroleukemia cells. This element lacks canonical promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signatures but demonstrates robust repressive activity against the super core promoter SCP1. As a non-coding regulatory locus, it has no UniProt accession, protein domains, or STRING interaction partners. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, consistent with its non-coding regulatory classification.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants disrupting this region—a moderate duplication-biased burden (3 deletions:5 duplications ratio). Deletions span 4q26-31.21 (~28-29Mb regions) while duplications span 4q27-35.2 (~68-74Mb regions). These recurrent CNVs are associated with 4q deletion/duplication syndromes featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, skeletal anomalies, and genitourinary malformations. The 4q28-35 region is gene-dense, containing critical dosage-sensitive genes such as HAND2 (Heart And Neural Crest Derivatives 2, limb and cardiac development), ADH gene cluster (Alcohol Dehydrogenases), and multiple homeobox transcription factors including HBBOX family genes.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: LOC129389238 functions as a critical distal silencer within the 4q28-35 topologically associating domain (TAD), regulating expression of dosage-sensitive developmental genes. Candidate targets include HAND2 (limb morphogenesis, cardiac neural crest development) or neighboring homeobox genes. The silencer likely recruits co-repressor complexes (PRC2, NCoR/SMRT, HDACs) to deposit H3K27me3 marks at target promoters. The moderate duplication-biased CNV burden (3:5 ratio) suggests triplosensitivity predominance where silencer gain causes hyper-repression of developmental genes. The K562 validation suggests potential roles in hematopoietic lineage specification. Duplications may cause HAND2 or homeobox gene hyper-repression leading to limb/cardiac phenotypes (tetralogy of Fallot, Holt-Oram spectrum) while deletions may cause derepression, contributing to 4q deletion/duplication syndrome with genitourinary and skeletal anomalies.
