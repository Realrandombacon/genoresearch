## Description
WDR41 encodes a 459 aa WD40 repeat-containing protein (UniProt Q9HAD4) that functions as an essential regulatory subunit of the C9orf72-SMCR8-WDR41 heterotrimeric complex, acting as a GTPase-activating protein (GAP) for Rab8a and Rab11a to control autophagy and endolysosomal trafficking. InterPro analysis reveals a canonical WD40 repeat architecture with multiple blade-forming repeats (IPR001680, IPR019775, IPR020472; aa 32-159, 211-249, 311-359) and a specific WDR41 family domain (IPR040102, PF25178; aa 1-458) annotated for autophagy regulation (GO:0010506). The structure features YVTN repeat-like folds (G3DSA:2.130.10.10) typical of beta-propeller scaffolds that mediate protein-protein interactions. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 82.3), indicating a stable beta-propeller fold capable of scaffolding the C9orf72 complex. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, localizing to the nucleoplasm, Golgi apparatus, and cytosol, consistent with its role in global membrane trafficking and autophagic flux. STRING interactions demonstrate an exceptionally tight functional cluster with the core complex members SMCR8 (0.999) and C9orf72 (0.999), and strong associations with Rab GTPases RAB39B (0.971), RAB8A (0.962), and ARF1 (0.859), as well as autophagy machinery ATG101 (0.785) and lysosomal regulators LAMTOR1/2/3 (0.540-0.736). This interaction network confirms WDR41's central role in bridging the C9orf72 complex to Rab-mediated vesicle transport. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses/gains at 5q13-14 and missense mutations (c.335A>G p.Asp112Gly), implicating WDR41 in genomic instability disorders and potentially modifying ALS/FTD severity, though specific monogenic phenotypes are still emerging. Recent literature (50 papers) highlights the 2020 cryo-EM structure (60 citations) defining the complex as a Rab8a/Rab11a GAP, critical for restraining innate immunity and maintaining lysosomal homeostasis. Mutations in C9orf72 cause ALS/FTD, and WDR41 is indispensable for complex stability and GAP activity. Mechanistically, WDR41 deficiency likely impairs Rab8a/Rab11a inactivation, disrupting autophagosome-lysosome fusion, causing accumulation of toxic protein aggregates and hyperactivation of STING-dependent inflammatory pathways. Emerging data (2025-2026) suggests WDR41 lactylation links exercise to improved metabolic steatosis via autophagy induction, and small molecules targeting ARF1-C9orf72-WDR41 interfaces are being explored for ALS therapy. WDR41 represents a critical node connecting genetic risk (C9orf72 expansions), membrane trafficking, and neurodegeneration.
