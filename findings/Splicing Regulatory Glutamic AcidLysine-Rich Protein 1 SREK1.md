# Splicing Regulatory Glutamic Acid/Lysine-Rich Protein 1 (SREK1/SRrp86): RRM-Containing Spliceosome Component Modulating Alternative Splicing

## Description
SREK1 (also known as SRrp86 or SFRS12) encodes a 508 aa nuclear protein (UniProt Q8WXA9) that functions as a splicing regulatory factor within the serine/arginine-rich (SR) protein family. The protein contains a canonical RNA recognition motif (RRM) domain (IPR000504, PF00076, residues 66-142) responsible for binding pre-mRNA substrates and recognizing specific splice site sequences. InterPro analysis further identifies the RRM2 subdomain (cd12260, residues 62-147) and classification within the RNA-processing regulator family (PTHR32343). STRING interactions reveal SREK1 embedded in the core splicing machinery with high-confidence partnerships to multiple SR proteins: SRSF3 (0.934), SRSF11 (0.933), SRSF10 (0.850), SRSF2 (0.789), SRSF1 (0.718), and SRSF6 (0.705), plus its dedicated interacting partner SREK1IP1 (0.791). HPA confirms ubiquitous expression across all tissues with specific localization to nucleoplasm and nuclear speckles—the characteristic subnuclear compartments where splicing factors concentrate. The biological process annotation explicitly states mRNA processing and splicing. ClinVar contains 13 pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses/gains on chromosome 5q12.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity though no specific disease phenotype has been definitively assigned. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 63.4) shows medium confidence consistent with a folded RRM domain surrounded by intrinsically disordered glutamine/lysine-rich regions typical of SR proteins that require flexibility for dynamic spliceosome assembly. Mechanistically, SREK1 likely functions as a splicing enhancer or repressor by binding exonic/intronic splicing enhancer sequences via its RRM domain, recruiting or stabilizing U1/U2 snRNP components through protein-protein interactions with other SR proteins, thereby modulating alternative splice site selection during co-transcriptional spliceosome assembly.
