Ubiquitous expression reflects housekeeping role in protein quality control and signal transduction. Pathogenic CNVs disrupting RNF25 dosage may cause NF-κB dysregulation, chronic inflammation, or immune dysfunction. Conservation across vertebrates (mouse 456 aa, bovine 458 aa, zebrafish 513 aa) confirms essential regulatory function.
