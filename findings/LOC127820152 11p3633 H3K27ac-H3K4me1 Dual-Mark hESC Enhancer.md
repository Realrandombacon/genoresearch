# LOC127820152: 11p36.33 H3K27ac-H3K4me1 Dual-Mark hESC Enhancer Active in Naive and Primed States with No Clinical Variants — Constitutive Pluripotency Regulatory Element

## Description
LOC127820152 is a functionally characterized cis-regulatory element on chromosome 11p36.33 (genomic coordinates: 2097259-2097901 on NC_000011.10, spanning approximately 642 bp). This genomic region was validated as an active enhancer by ChIP-STARR-seq massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) in BOTH naive AND primed human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), where it is marked by BOTH H3K27ac (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation — active enhancer mark) AND H3K4me1 (histone H3 lysine 4 monomethylation — enhancer lineage mark) histone modifications. The dual H3K27ac-H3K4me1 signature represents a high-confidence active enhancer state, and activity across both naive (pre-implantation epiblast-like) and primed (post-implantation epiblast-like) pluripotent states indicates this element functions as a constitutive enhancer maintaining regulatory activity throughout pluripotency transitions. A subregion was also identified as an uncharacterized cis-regulatory element (CRE) lacking promoter, enhancer, or CTCF-bound insulator chromatin signature, and validated as a silencer that could repress activity of super core promoter SCP1 by STARR-seq MPRAs in K562 erythroleukemia cells. The element lacks protein-coding evidence (no UniProt entry, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression), confirming its identity as a non-coding regulatory element with dual enhancer/silencer activity depending on cellular context. ClinVar analysis identified 0 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, indicating no known clinical significance to date. The 11p36 chromosomal region contains critical dosage-sensitive genes including ETS1 (ETS proto-oncogene 1 - transcription factor, immune cell development), THBS1 (thrombospondin 1 - angiogenesis regulation), and multiple developmental transcription factors. As a constitutive hESC enhancer marked by H3K27ac-H3K4me1 and active across pluripotency states, LOC127820152 likely functions to maintain stable transcription of target genes in cis during embryonic development through naive-to-primed transitions.
