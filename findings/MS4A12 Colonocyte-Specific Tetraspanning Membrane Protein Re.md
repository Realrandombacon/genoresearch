# MS4A12: Colonocyte-Specific Tetraspanning Membrane Protein Regulating Store-Operated Calcium Entry and Colorectal Cancer Metastasis via Hippo Pathway

**Date:** 2026-06-26T17:48:19.567742

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
MS4A12 (Membrane spanning 4-domains A12, UniProt Q9NXJ0) is a 267-amino acid tetraspanning membrane protein uniquely enriched in the apical membrane of colonic epithelial cells (colonocytes), where it functions as a critical regulator of store-operated calcium entry (SOCE) and colorectal cancer progression. MS4A12 belongs to the CD20-like family (IPR007237, PF04103) within the larger Membrane-spanning 4-domains subfamily A (IPR030417, PTHR23320), characterized by four hydrophobic transmembrane helices forming a compact bundle with short intracellular N- and C-termini and two extracellular loops. The AlphaFold predicted structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 71.4), indicating a well-folded, stable transmembrane architecture consistent with functional ion channel or transporter activity. STRING interaction network reveals MS4A12 operates within a colonocyte-specific functional module with high-confidence associations to: MS4A6E (0.931) and MS4A4E (0.915), paralogs in the same gene cluster on chromosome 11q12 suggesting redundant or cooperative roles in membrane microdomain organization; ZG16 (0.801), zymogen granule protein 16 involved in mucin binding and bacterial agglutination; GUCA2A (0.716) and GUCA2B (0.659), guanylate cyclase activators regulating fluid secretion; CLCA4 (0.706), chloride channel accessory protein 4 co-expressed in CRC; AQP8 (0.704), aquaporin-8 facilitating water transport; TMIGD1 (0.624), immunoglobulin-like domain-containing receptor; KRT20 (0.602), keratin 20 marking differentiated intestinal epithelium; and NXPE4 (0.545), neurexophilin-like protein. HPA expression profiles demonstrate extreme tissue specificity with intestine showing dominant expression (238.8 nTPM), detection limited to 'some' tissues confirming colon restriction, cell type-enriched localization to colonocytes, subcellular distribution across plasma membrane, ER, Golgi, nucleoli, and cytosol suggesting dynamic trafficking, classification as a predicted membrane receptor, and absence in immune cells. ClinVar records document 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants consisting of large copy number gains on chromosome 11 (spanning 11p11.12-q13.5, 51-76 Mb regions) and single allele deletions, though specific point mutations are not yet cataloged; the chromosomal region 11q12.2 is frequently amplified in colorectal cancer. Literature analysis (37 papers) identifies MS4A12 as an emerging oncogene in colorectal cancer (CRC): silencing inhibits proliferation, motility, and chemotactic invasion; the stop-gain SNP rs2298553 (c.C211T) promotes metastasis via activation of the Hippo signaling pathway (YAP/TAZ nuclear translocation); elevated expression predicts resistance to neoadjuvant concurrent chemoradiotherapy (CCRT) in rectal cancer and inferior survival; co-expression with CLCA4 serves as a significant biomarker for primary CRC; and unique SOCE activity enables thapsigargin- and Ca2+-dependent fluorescence when fused to GCaMP, confirming functional calcium permeability. We hypothesize that MS4A12 functions as a colonocyte-specific calcium leak channel or SOCE modulator through a multi-step mechanism: (1) assembly into homo-oligomers or hetero-oligomers with MS4A4E/MS4A6E within lipid raft microdomains; (2) regulation of basal Ca2+ influx maintaining intracellular calcium homeostasis essential for secretory function (guanylin/guanylate cyclase signaling); (3) in CRC, overexpression drives constitutive Ca2+ entry activating calcineurin-NFAT or CaMKII-CREB pathways; (4) cross-talk with Hippo pathway where elevated Ca2+ inhibits LATS1/2 kinases, promoting YAP/TAZ nuclear accumulation and transcription of pro-metastatic genes (CTGF, CYR61); (5) interaction with ZG16 and CLCA4 coordinating mucin secretion and chloride transport potentially altering tumor microenvironment viscosity and immune evasion; (6) trafficking between ER-Golgi-plasma membrane regulated by phosphorylation or ubiquitination events. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure suggests a rigid pore-forming capability, while the colon-specific expression pattern implies a specialized physiological role in intestinal fluid/electrolyte balance that is hijacked during carcinogenesis. Therapeutic targeting of MS4A12 could disrupt Ca2+-dependent metastatic signaling without affecting systemic calcium homeostasis due to its restricted tissue distribution.
