## Description

Functional mechanism: RAB35 operates through GTPase cycle: (1) Inactive state - GDP-bound RAB35 cytosolic form associated with GDI (guanine nucleotide dissociation inhibitor) maintaining solubility; (2) Membrane recruitment - Specific membrane targeting sequences and prenylation at C-terminal cysteines anchor RAB35 to endosomal/plasma membranes; (3) Activation - DENND family GEFs (DENND1A/B/C, MADD) catalyze GDP-GTP exchange converting RAB35 to active conformation exposing switch regions; (4) Effector recruitment - GTP-bound RAB35 binds effectors including FSCN1 (actin bundling), OCRL (lipid phosphatase), RUSC2 (scaffold) coordinating downstream processes; (5) Inactivation - TBC1D10 family GAPs (TBC1D10A/B, ACAP2) stimulate intrinsic GTPase activity hydrolyzing GTP to GDP; (6) Recycling - Inactivated RAB35 extracted by GDI for subsequent rounds. Biological roles include: (a) Endosomal recycling - RAB35 controls fast recycling pathway returning internalized receptors (transferrin receptor, MHC-I, integrins) from early endosomes to plasma membrane distinct from RAB4/RAB11 pathways; (b) Cytokinesis - RAB35 localizes to intercellular bridge recruiting ESCRT machinery and actin regulators for abscission completion preventing binucleation; (c) Phagocytosis - RAB35 regulates phagosome maturation in macrophages; (d) Neurite outgrowth - RAB35 controls membrane supply for axon extension; (e) Viral entry - Some viruses hijack RAB35 pathway for cellular entry.
