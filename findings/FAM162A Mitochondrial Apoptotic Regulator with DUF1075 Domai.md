# FAM162A: Mitochondrial Apoptotic Regulator with DUF1075 Domain Interacting with VDAC2 and BNIP3

## Description
FAM162A (family with sequence similarity 162 member A, also known as HGTD-P or C3orf28) encodes a 154-amino acid mitochondrial protein containing a DUF1075 domain of unknown function (IPR009432, PF06388, residues 2-154). The protein is classified as growth and transformation-dependent (PTHR13674). HPA data confirms mitochondrial localization with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity, low cancer specificity). STRING analysis reveals 10 medium-confidence interaction partners including VDAC2 (0.617), NSL1 (0.583), CMC2 (0.514), AWAT2 (0.514), and BNIP3 (0.495). The VDAC2 interaction is mechanistically significant as VDAC channels regulate mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization and cytochrome c release during apoptosis. BNIP3 interaction links FAM162A to hypoxia-induced mitophagy pathways. ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on chromosome 3q, suggesting dosage sensitivity. Conservation is high: mouse ortholog (Q9D6U8) is 155 aa with near-complete sequence identity. Functional hypothesis: FAM162A functions as a mitochondrial outer membrane scaffold protein that modulates VDAC2 channel activity during cellular stress, facilitating cytochrome c release and apoptotic signaling. The DUF1075 domain likely mediates protein-protein interactions within the mitochondrial permeability transition pore complex. Hypoxia-induced upregulation combined with BNIP3 interaction suggests dual roles in apoptosis and mitophagy during oxygen deprivation.
