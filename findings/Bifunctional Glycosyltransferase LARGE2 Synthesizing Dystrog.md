## Description
LARGE2 (LARGE Xylosyl- and Glucuronyltransferase 2) is a 721 aa bifunctional glycosyltransferase functioning as a critical enzyme in the synthesis of matriglycan, a repeating [xylose-α1,3-glucuronic acid-β1,3]n polymer on α-dystroglycan (α-DG) that mediates basement membrane adhesion in epithelial and muscle tissues. InterPro analysis reveals a modular architecture with an N-terminal glycosyltransferase family 8 domain (IPR002495/PF01501, aa 112-345) responsible for xylosyltransferase activity, a central nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily domain (IPR029044/G3DSA:3.90.550.10, aa 100-372), and two C-terminal dystroglycan-specific glycosyltransferase domains (PF13896, aa 431-495 and 498-664) that confer glucuronyltransferase activity, placing LARGE2 in the Alpha-1,3-xylosyltransferase/Beta-1,3-glucuronyltransferase family (IPR051292, aa 105-710). AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure (pLDDT 87.2) indicative of a stable, well-folded enzyme with distinct catalytic clefts for sequential sugar transfer. STRING interactions place LARGE2 at the core of the dystroglycan glycosylation pathway with ultra-high confidence partners: DAG1 (0.912), the α-dystroglycan substrate; LARGE1 (0.875), the paralogous enzyme with overlapping function; FKTN (0.768) and FKRP (0.734), fukutin-related proteins that prime α-DG for LARGE-mediated extension; POMT1 (0.746) and POMT2 (0.741), protein O-mannosyltransferases initiating the glycosylation cascade; POMGNT1 (0.739), β1,2-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase extending the core; B4GAT1 (0.720), the β1,4-glucuronyltransferase that creates the LARGE acceptor substrate; RXYLT1 (0.694), a related xylosyltransferase; and POMK (0.654), protein O-mannose kinase. HPA expression shows tissue-enhanced RNA levels with highest expression in placenta (34.4 nTPM), detection across many tissues, immune cell enhancement, and group-enriched single cell specificity, consistent with roles in epithelial barrier function and immune surveillance. ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1164+40G>C), synonymous changes (p.Gly257=), missense variants (p.Val155Phe), and large copy number losses/gains on 11p11.2-q25, though specific LARGE2-associated diseases remain undefined despite the dystroglycanopathy pathway connection. Literature (46 papers) establishes LARGE2 as a Wnt signaling effector in colorectal cancer where Wnt-driven LARGE2 expression mediates laminin-adhesive O-glycosylation, a pan-cancer biomarker, and a rice agronomy gene controlling grain/leaf size (orthologous function).
