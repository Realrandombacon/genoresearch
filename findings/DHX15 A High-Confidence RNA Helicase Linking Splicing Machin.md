## Description
DHX15 (DEAH-box helicase 15) is a well-studied ATP-dependent RNA helicase (UniProt: O43143) localized to nuclear speckles, essential for pre-mRNA splicing and mRNA processing. Structurally, it is a well-folded protein (AlphaFold pLDDT: 85.9) containing conserved DEAH-box and C-terminal helicase domains. Functionally, it interacts with high-confidence partners (RBM17, TFIP11, CDC40) within the spliceosome and translation machinery. Clinically, DHX15 is associated with 32 pathogenic copy number variants (CNVs) on chromosome 4p15.2-4p16.3, suggesting a role in developmental disorders or cancer susceptibility. Literature (152 papers) confirms its involvement in tumor progression, specifically in colorectal cancer (linked to KRAS mutations and Fusobacterium nucleatum binding) and endometrial carcinoma (INO80-DHX15 axis regulating glycolysis). Recent studies highlight its role in immune evasion via GPATCH3-mediated modulation of ATPase activity. Mechanistically, DHX15 likely maintains splicing fidelity and metabolic reprogramming; CNVs may disrupt helicase activity or complex assembly, leading to aberrant gene expression and immune evasion.
