## Description
HDGFL3 (HDGF Like 3, also known as HRP3/HDGF-2/HRP-3, UniProt Q9Y3E1) is a 203 aa nucleoplasmic protein functioning as a chromatin-associated transcriptional regulator through its conserved PWWP domain that recognizes the minor groove of double-stranded DNA and recruits HRP3 to chromatin. Structurally, AlphaFold predicts a well-folded architecture (pLDDT=70.8, confident) featuring a canonical PWWP domain (IPR000313/PF00855/cd20150/SM00293, aa 4-96) within the Tudor/PWWP/MBT superfamily (SSF63748, aa 5-107) and hepatoma-derived growth factor family (PTHR12550, aa 4-180), enabling DNA binding, chromatin recruitment, and protein-protein interactions characteristic of epigenetic readers. Functionally, STRING interactions reveal coupling to transcription and chromatin machinery: cyclin-dependent kinases CDK13 (0.492) and CDK12 (0.489) mediating transcription elongation and RNA polymerase II CTD phosphorylation, chromatin regulators BAZ2B (0.440) and BRPF1 (0.422) as histone acetyltransferase complex components, mitochondrial transcription termination factor MTERF2 (0.421), large extracellular matrix protein HMCN2 (0.507), and paraoxonase PON3 (0.410). HPA shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, immune cell enhanced expression in blood cells, cell type enhanced single cell specificity, nucleoplasmic localization, classified as predicted intracellular growth factor. ClinVar identifies 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains at 15q25.2-25.3 (chr15:82558402-85243616), though no specific Mendelian disease is directly annotated - these CNVs overlap with neurodevelopmental disorder regions. Literature (only 3 papers) confirms VERY DARK status: 2019 study demonstrates HRP3 PWWP domain recognizes DNA minor groove and recruits to chromatin (28 citations), 2023 bulk RNA-seq reveals HDGFL3 involvement in prostate cancer growth and metastasis (3 citations), and 2026 spatial transcriptomics in gallbladder carcinogenesis. The gene represents a 'dark chromatin regulator' where canonical PWWP domain-mediated DNA/chromatin binding is established, CDK12/13 interactions suggest roles in transcription elongation and RNA processing similar to CDK12-associated transcriptional programs, BAZ2B/BRPF1 partnerships implicate HDGFL3 in histone acetylation and chromatin remodeling complexes, and 15q25.2 CNV pathogenesis may contribute to neurodevelopmental phenotypes through haploinsufficiency or dosage effects. Therapeutic targeting of HDGFL3-mediated chromatin recruitment could represent novel approach for prostate cancer and other malignancies exploiting PWWP domain inhibitors, while CNV screening at 15q25.2 should consider HDGFL3 dosage in neurodevelopmental syndrome patients.
