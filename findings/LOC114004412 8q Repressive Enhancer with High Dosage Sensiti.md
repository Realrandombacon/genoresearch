## Description
LOC114004412 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 1869 located on chromosome 8q24 (genomic coordinates: 141516648-141516942 on NC_000008.11). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element (silencer) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number gains (triplifications), indicating strong dosage sensitivity where over-dosage disrupts cellular homeostasis. The high variant count (21 vs 2 for LOC121725039, 3 for LOC121725085) suggests this element has high clinical penetrance and is under strong purifying selection. The 8q24 chromosomal region is a gene-dense area containing important oncogenes including MYC (8q24.21, frequently amplified in cancers), RECQL4 (8q24.3, Rothmund-Thomson syndrome), and multiple tumor suppressors. As a repressive enhancer/silencer, this element likely suppresses expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 8q domain. The exclusive copy number gain pathogenicity pattern (21 gains, 0 losses) suggests this silencer normally restrains oncogene expression - loss of silencing through amplification leads to overexpression of growth-promoting genes. This element may contribute to 8q amplification syndromes, cancer predisposition (particularly hepatocellular carcinoma given HepG2 validation), or developmental disorders through dysregulation of critical 8q genes requiring transcriptional repression.
