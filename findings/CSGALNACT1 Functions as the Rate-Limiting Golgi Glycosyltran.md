## Description
CSGALNACT1 (Chondroitin Sulfate N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase 1) is a 532 aa Type II transmembrane glycosyltransferase representing a partially characterized dark gene with 70 literature references, functioning as the critical initiating enzyme for chondroitin sulfate (CS) biosynthesis. Multi-omics data reveals CSGALNACT1 as the master regulator of CS chain formation on proteoglycan core proteins. InterPro analysis confirms defining catalytic architecture: Chondroitin N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase family (IPR008428/PF05679, aa 73-507) with GO terms for acetylgalactosaminyltransferase activity (GO:0008376) and Golgi cisterna membrane localization (GO:0032580); Chondroitin sulfate glycosyltransferase superfamily (IPR051227, aa 170-508); and membership in the nucleotide-diphospho-sugar transferase superfamily (IPR029044/G3DSA:3.90.550.10/SSF53448, aa 250-531), indicating a GT-A fold mechanism for sugar donor binding and transfer. The enzyme catalyzes the transfer of N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) from UDP-GalNAc to the glucuronic acid (GlcA) residue of the tetrasaccharide linker region attached to serine residues on core proteins, initiating the polymerization of the repeating [-GlcA-GalNAc-]n disaccharide unit characteristic of chondroitin sulfate. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 87.2), indicating a well-folded catalytic domain typical of glycosyltransferases with stable active site geometry. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in the thyroid gland (70.7 nTPM), cancer-enhanced pattern, immune cell enhancement, and strict Golgi apparatus/cytosol subcellular localization, consistent with its role in secretory pathway glycosylation. STRING interactions reveal a dedicated functional cluster of chondroitin/dermatan sulfate biosynthesis enzymes: CSGALNACT2 (0.693, paralogous initiator), CHST7 (0.665, sulfotransferase), CHST12 (0.665, sulfotransferase), CHST11 (0.644, sulfotransferase), CHSY1 (0.564, copolymerase), CHPF (0.532, copolymerase), B4GALT7 (0.451, linker synthesis enzyme), CHPF2 (0.427, copolymerase), CHSY3 (0.416, copolymerase), and B3GAT3 (0.400, linker synthesis). This network represents the complete 'chondroitin factory' within the Golgi. ClinVar lists 108 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshifts (c.1043dup/p.Ser350fs, c.168dup/p.Tyr57fs, c.338dup/p.Glu114fs), nonsense mutations (c.868C>T/p.Gln290Ter), start codon deletions (c.1ATG[2]/p.Met3del), and large copy number losses (chr8:158049-26626500x1, chr8:6194677-25015979x1), causing autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia and dwarfism. The 70 literature references include seminal work on perineuronal nets in the brain (2017, 36 citations) showing CSGalNAcT1 knockout mice exhibit abnormal extracellular matrix structures affecting neuronal plasticity and behavior, recent studies identifying CSGALNACT1 as a proliferation driver in papillary thyroid carcinoma (2025), and reports of novel mutations causing skeletal dysplasia in pediatric patients (2022). Unlike elongation enzymes (CHSY1/CHPF), CSGALNACT1 is the gatekeeper determining whether a core protein becomes a chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan (CSPG).
