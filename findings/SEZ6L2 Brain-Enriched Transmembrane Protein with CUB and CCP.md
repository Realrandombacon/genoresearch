# SEZ6L2: Brain-Enriched Transmembrane Protein with CUB and CCP Domains Regulating Synaptic Function, Cerebellar Integrity, and Autoimmune Ataxia via Autoantibody Targeting

**Date:** 2026-06-14T18:50:33.981627

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
SEZ6L2 (Q6UXD5) encodes a 910 amino acid type I transmembrane protein belonging to the seizure-related 6 (SEZ6) family, characterized by a modular extracellular architecture containing three CUB domains (IPR000859/G3DSA:2.60.120.290, positions 173-286/349-459/527-638) and three Complement Control Protein (CCP/Sushi) domains (IPR000436/G3DSA:2.10.70.10, positions 288-347/462-525/642-701), classified within the SEZ6/CSMD/C4BPB neuronal and immune regulators family (IPR051277, positions 335-765). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 71.9, high confidence) consistent with stable domain organization required for protein-protein interactions at the synapse. STRING interactions reveal partnerships with synaptic and chromatin remodeling machinery: DOC2A (0.695), double C2 domain calcium sensor for vesicle fusion; ASPHD1 (0.619), aspartate beta-hydroxylase domain-containing protein; KCTD13 (0.597), potassium channel tetramerization domain protein linked to neurodevelopmental disorders; INO80E (0.583) and HIRIP3 (0.580), chromatin remodeling factors; YPEL3 (0.559), cell cycle regulator; KIF22 (0.527), kinesin motor protein; PRRT2 (0.513), proline-rich transmembrane protein associated with paroxysmal kinesigenic dyskinesia; and TAOK2 (0.486), thousand-and-one amino acid kinase involved in dendritic arborization. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with strong brain enrichment (99.0 nTPM) and pituitary gland expression (88.0 nTPM), consistent with a specialized neuronal function, classified under predicted membrane/intracellular proteins. ClinVar contains a striking 289 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number gains and losses spanning the 16p11.2 region (e.g., chr16:29584162-30181038 duplications, chr16:29555975-30178708 gains, chr16:28824858-30321320 deletions), highlighting the critical dosage sensitivity of this locus in neurodevelopmental disorders such as autism spectrum disorder and schizophrenia, although specific monogenic disease associations for SEZ6L2 alone remain undefined. Literature (92 papers) demonstrates SEZ6L2 as a target of autoantibodies inducing cerebellar ataxia in mice and humans, presenting with gait disturbances, falls, and slurred speech; IGF2BP2 facilitates cisplatin resistance in non-small cell lung cancer by stabilizing SEZ6L2 expression; SEZ6L2 autoimmunity is linked to subacute cerebellar ataxia cases. The mechanistic hypothesis: SEZ6L2 functions as a synaptic adhesion or signaling molecule where its CUB domains mediate specific ligand binding (potentially growth factors or extracellular matrix components) and CCP domains regulate complement activation or protein-protein interactions to maintain synaptic integrity; the strong brain enrichment and interaction with DOC2A and PRRT2 suggest a role in regulating synaptic vesicle exocytosis and neuronal excitability; autoantibody targeting of extracellular domains disrupts these interactions leading to cerebellar Purkinje cell dysfunction and ataxia; copy number variations at 16p11.2 alter SEZ6L2 dosage affecting neurodevelopmental trajectories, potentially through disrupted synaptic pruning or dendritic arborization mediated by TAOK2 and KCTD13 pathways; the high number of ClinVar variants underscores the genomic instability of the 16p11.2 region and the severe phenotypic consequences of SEZ6L2 dysregulation.
