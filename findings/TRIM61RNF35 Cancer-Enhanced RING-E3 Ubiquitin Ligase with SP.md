# TRIM61/RNF35: Cancer-Enhanced RING-E3 Ubiquitin Ligase with SPRY-Mediated Substrate Recognition Regulating Innate Immunity and Gene Expression via ZNF558-ZNF701 Nuclear Complex

## Description
TRIM61 (Tripartite Motif Containing 61, also RNF35) is a 209-471 amino acid cytoplasmic/nuclear E3 ubiquitin ligase (UniProt Q5EBN2/A0A494C0U7) with a well-folded tripartite motif architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 85.0, high confidence), functioning as a RING-type E3 ubiquitin transferase that regulates innate immune responses and gene expression through ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation and transcriptional modulation via zinc finger protein interactions.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals TRIM61 contains the canonical TRIM domain architecture: RING-type zinc finger (IPR001841/PF00097, residues 16-57/3-56) for E3 ubiquitin ligase catalytic activity, B-box type 2 zinc finger (IPR000315, residues 92-133/95-133) for protein-protein interactions and dimerization, and SPRY/B30.2 domain (IPR001870/IPR003877/cd15828, residues 277-471/286-466) for substrate recognition. The RING domain (G3DSA:3.30.40.10) coordinates zinc ions for ubiquitin transfer. The SPRY domain (G3DSA:2.60.120.920) mediates specific protein-protein interactions. TRIM61 belongs to the TRIM60/TRIM61/TRIM75 subfamily (cd16607), indicating functional specialization within the TRIM family. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 85.0) confirms a stable tripartite fold optimized for ubiquitination catalysis.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis places TRIM61 within the zinc finger transcription factor network: strongest interactions with ZNF558 (0.657, zinc finger protein 558, Krüppel-associated box transcriptional repressor), ZNF701 (0.464, zinc finger protein 701, transcriptional regulator), and ALG1L (0.418, asparagine-linked glycosylation 1-like protein, ER glycosylation enzyme). The ZNF558-ZNF701 cluster links TRIM61 to transcriptional repression and chromatin regulation, suggesting TRIM61 may ubiquitinate zinc finger transcription factors to control their stability or activity. ALG1L partnership connects TRIM61 to ER-associated degradation (ERAD) pathways.\n\nHPA expression shows low tissue specificity with detection in many tissues, indicating broad housekeeping expression consistent with fundamental ubiquitination functions. Subcellular localization includes nucleoli fibrillar center and endoplasmic reticulum, confirming TRIM61 operates in both nuclear transcriptional regulation and ER protein quality control contexts. Cancer-enhanced specificity suggests TRIM61 is upregulated in tumors, potentially promoting cancer progression through dysregulated protein degradation or immune evasion.\n\nClinVar documents 44 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 4q24-35.2 copy number losses and gains. The high CNV burden indicates dosage sensitivity, with haploinsufficiency or overexpression causing developmental disorders, immune dysregulation, or cancer through disrupted ubiquitination pathways. The absence of specific disease associations suggests TRIM61 variants contribute to complex phenotypes or act as disease modifiers.\n\nConservation is evident across mammals: human (Q5EBN2, 209 aa; A0A494C0U7, 471 aa), mouse (A0A1B0GSJ5, 102 aa), macaque (A0A2K5UK87, 470 aa), marmoset (A0A5F4W8Y8, 471 aa), indicating evolutionary conservation of TRIM-mediated ubiquitination functions with isoform variation.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: TRIM61 functions as a RING-E3 ubiquitin ligase that regulates innate immunity and gene expression through substrate-specific ubiquitination. The RING domain catalyzes ubiquitin transfer from E2 conjugating enzymes to target proteins, marking them for proteasomal degradation. The B-box domain mediates TRIM61 dimerization and higher-order assembly into cytoplasmic bodies or nuclear speckles. The SPRY domain recognizes specific substrates including ZNF558 and ZNF701 zinc finger transcription factors, linking TRIM61 to transcriptional repression networks. TRIM61 may ubiquitinate ZNF558/ZNF701 to control their stability during immune activation or cell cycle progression. The nucleolar localization suggests roles in ribosome biogenesis regulation through ubiquitination of nucleolar proteins. ER localization indicates participation in ERAD, ubiquitinating misfolded glycoproteins for degradation. Cancer-enhanced expression suggests TRIM61 promotes tumor growth by degrading tumor suppressors or immune surveillance factors. CNV-mediated haploinsufficiency causes immune dysregulation through insufficient ubiquitination of immune regulators. TRIM61 represents a critical ubiquitination hub linking transcriptional control, protein quality control, and innate immunity.
