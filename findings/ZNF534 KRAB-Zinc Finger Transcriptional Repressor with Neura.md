## Description
ZNF534 encodes a 674 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein functioning as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor. The protein contains a canonical KRAB domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 8-92) that recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors to establish heterochromatin via histone methylation, plus multiple C2H2 zinc finger arrays (PF00096, IPR013087) at positions 199-333 that recognize specific DNA motifs. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 67.3) indicates moderate structural confidence with ordered zinc finger domains and potentially disordered linker regions typical of transcriptional regulators. Tissue expression profiling reveals enrichment in brain (2.4 nTPM), testis (1.8 nTPM), and retina (1.0 nTPM) with cancer-enriched expression pattern, suggesting roles in neurodevelopment and germ cell regulation. Protein interaction networks identify functional associations with ZNF202 (0.611), NANOG (0.453), ZNF282 (0.433), ZNF777 (0.416), and FOXP1 (0.416), placing ZNF534 within pluripotency and chromatin remodeling complexes. Clinical significance is demonstrated by 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants in ClinVar, predominantly chromosome 19q duplications spanning the ZNF534 locus, implicating dosage sensitivity in disease etiology. The KRAB-ZFP architecture suggests ZNF534 functions as an epigenetic silencer targeting retrotransposons or developmentally regulated gene networks.
