## Description
DCTPP1 (dCTP Pyrophosphatase 1, UniProt Q9H773) is a 170 amino acid intracellular protein with moderate human characterization (50 papers, predominantly 2024-2025 cancer metabolism and nucleotide homeostasis studies with emerging mechanistic depth) representing a genuine dark gene in human biology that functions as a MazG-like nucleoside triphosphate pyrophosphohydrolase maintaining deoxyribonucleotide pool fidelity by hydrolyzing noncanonical dCTP and preventing mutagenic nucleotide incorporation during DNA replication.
