## Description
ZNF260 encodes a 412-amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor (UniProt Q3ZCT1) located on chromosome 19q13.12. The protein functions as a cardiac regulator and effector of alpha1-adrenergic signaling, binding to PE response elements (PERE) in target gene promoters including ANF/NPPA (atrial natriuretic peptide).

InterPro domain analysis reveals extensive C2H2 zinc finger architecture: 3 canonical C2H2-type domains (IPR013087, PF00096) at positions 27-110, plus a Krueppel-type zinc finger region (IPR050826, PTHR24377) spanning residues 182-411. The C-terminal region contains a zinc-finger double domain (PF13465, 375-399), suggesting dimerization or cooperative DNA binding capability.

STRING interaction network shows ZNF260 associates with cardiac transcriptional machinery: GATA4 (score 0.607), a key cardiac developmental transcription factor; NPPA (0.554), the atrial natriuretic peptide precursor; EDN1 (0.454), endothelin-1 vasoconstrictor; and NELL1 (0.453), osteoblast differentiation factor. Additional partners include ZNF616/615/738 (0.43-0.44), suggesting heteromeric zinc finger complex formation.

HPA expression shows ubiquitous low-level expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic and cytosolic localization. ClinVar contains 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all copy number losses or gains affecting 19q13.12, indicating haploinsufficiency or dosage sensitivity causes disease. BLAST analysis demonstrates strong evolutionary conservation with 86.3% sequence identity to mouse ortholog (E-value: 0.0, score: 706).

Functional hypothesis: ZNF260 functions as a cardiac stress-responsive transcription factor that cooperates with GATA4 to regulate PERE-containing genes (NPPA, EDN1) during alpha1-adrenergic signaling. The C2H2 zinc finger array enables sequence-specific DNA recognition, while the Krueppel-type C-terminal region mediates protein-protein interactions with cardiac transcriptional complexes. Copy number variants cause dosage-sensitive cardiac phenotypes through disrupted transcriptional regulation.
