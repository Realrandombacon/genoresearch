## Description
NOP53 (Q9NZM5) encodes a 478 amino acid nucleolar protein that functions as a critical ribosome biogenesis factor required for late-stage 60S ribosomal subunit maturation and nuclear export, with emerging roles in liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS), tumor radio-resistance, and autophagy suppression. InterPro analysis reveals NOP53 belongs to the conserved Nop53/GLTSCR2 family (IPR011687, positions 1-478) containing the Nop53 Pfam domain (PF07767, positions 42-445) essential for 60S ribosomal biogenesis, classified within the tumor suppressor protein Gltscr2/p60 type superfamily (PIRSF017302, positions 1-478) and glioma suppressor candidate region gene 2 family (PTHR14211, positions 35-477). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 78.7, high confidence) consistent with stable nucleolar protein architecture required for ribonucleoprotein complex assembly. The mechanistic hypothesis: NOP53 functions as a scaffold protein in late-stage 60S ribosomal subunit assembly, recruiting methyltransferases (FTSJ3), GTPases (GTPBP4, GNL2), and export factors (SDAD1) to coordinate rRNA processing, ribosomal protein incorporation, and nuclear export; LLPS capability enables NOP53 to concentrate ribosome biogenesis factors within nucleolar condensates, enhancing reaction efficiency; during DNA damage from radiation therapy, NOP53-mediated LLPS may sequester DNA repair factors or alter chromatin accessibility, conferring radio-resistance in tumor cells; autophagy suppression via ZKSCAN3 pathways suggests NOP53 links nutrient sensing to ribosome production, potentially preventing degradation of ribosomal components during stress; copy number losses on 19q may cause haploinsufficiency leading to ribosomopathy phenotypes with impaired protein synthesis, while duplications may drive oncogenic programs through elevated ribosome biogenesis; high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 78.7) indicates NOP53 adopts a stable fold enabling multivalent interactions required for condensate formation and ribonucleoprotein complex assembly.
