## Description

InterPro analysis reveals canonical olfactory receptor architecture with IPR000276 (GPCR rhodopsin-like), IPR000725 (Olfactory receptor), and PF13853 domains spanning residues 32-307. HPA reports no detection in standard tissues (nTPM=0), consistent with olfactory receptor restriction to nasal olfactory epithelium. STRING found no interaction partners, expected for membrane-localized sensory receptors. Critically, ClinVar documents 3 pathogenic copy number gain variants (accessions 4852057, 155448, 58109) at 1q23 region, suggesting dosage sensitivity despite zero Semantic Scholar publications. Orthologs exist in dog, pig, opossum, and cat indicating evolutionary conservation across mammals. This represents a genuine dark gene with clinical relevance but no mechanistic studies.
