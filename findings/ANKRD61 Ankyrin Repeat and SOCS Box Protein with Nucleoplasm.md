# ANKRD61: Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box Protein with Nucleoplasmic Scaffolding and Cell Cycle Regulation

**Date:** 2026-03-20T13:21:29.119713

**Quality Score:** 2.95/10  (E=4.1, D=1.8) [WEAK]

## Description
ANKRD61 encodes a 418 amino acid ankyrin repeat-containing protein (UniProt A6NGH8) that functions as a nucleoplasmic scaffold protein with potential roles in cell cycle regulation and protein complex assembly. The protein contains multiple ankyrin repeat arrays (IPR002110, positions 75-317) forming a curved solenoid structure for protein-protein interactions, and belongs to the Ankyrin Repeat and SOCS Box family (IPR050663/PTHR24193, positions 145-320) suggesting E3 ubiquitin ligase adaptor activity through Cullin-RING ligase recruitment. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 84.9) consistent with stable ankyrin repeat architecture. HPA shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in bone marrow (5.7 nTPM) and testis (5.6 nTPM), nucleoplasmic localization, and detection in some tissues. STRING interactions reveal partnerships with radial spoke head proteins RSPH10B (0.713) and RSPH10B2 (0.604), aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase complex-interacting multifunctional protein AIMP2 (0.607), DNA mismatch repair protein PMS2 (0.457), cytohesin-3/CYTH3 (0.428), and keratin-associated protein KAZN (0.426), indicating roles in ciliary function, tRNA scaffolding, DNA repair, and vesicular trafficking. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains at 7p22.1. Orthologs in mouse (Q9CQM6, 421 aa), rat (Q641X1, 418 aa), and bovine (Q2T9W8, 424 aa) show strong conservation. The ankyrin-SOCS box architecture combined with PMS2 interaction and nucleoplasmic localization positions ANKRD61 as a potential regulator coordinating protein degradation, DNA damage response, and cell cycle progression through cullin-RING E3 ligase recruitment.
