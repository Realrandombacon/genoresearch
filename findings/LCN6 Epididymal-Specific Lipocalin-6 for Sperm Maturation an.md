## Description
LCN6 encodes a 163 aa epididymal-specific lipocalin-6 (UniProt P62502), also known as LCN5 or UNQ643, functioning as a secreted lipocalin family member that binds and transports small hydrophobic molecules in the epididymal lumen during sperm maturation and capacitation. The protein contains a canonical lipocalin/calycin beta-barrel fold (IPR000566/PF00061, aa33-160) with eight-stranded antiparallel beta-barrel structure forming a hydrophobic ligand-binding pocket, belonging to the lipocalin family (IPR002345/PTHR11430, aa4-160) with conserved lipocalin signature motif (IPR022272/PS00213, aa27-38) and calycin superfamily architecture (IPR012674/G3DSA:2.40.128.20, aa19-163). AlphaFold prediction shows high confidence (pLDDT 79.8), indicating a well-folded lipocalin barrel structure typical of small molecule transporters. HPA shows extreme epididymis-enriched expression (1493.0 nTPM) with tissue-enriched specificity, cancer-enriched pattern, secreted protein localization, and role in fertilization. STRING interactions reveal LCN6 associates with lipocalin family members: LCN10 (0.872, lipocalin-10), LCN8 (0.830, lipocalin-8), LCN12 (0.589, lipocalin-12), TEDDM1 (0.579, testis-expressed domain-containing), OBP2A/OBP2B (0.445-0.576, odorant-binding proteins), CST11 (0.542, cystatin-11), LCNL1 (0.517, lipocalin-like 1), LCN9 (0.427), and LCN1 (0.412, tear lipocalin). ClinVar contains 47 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple 9q34.3 copy number losses and gains. Literature (17 papers) demonstrates LCN6 knockout mice show impaired sperm maturation with defective acrosome reaction and reduced fertilization capacity; LCN6 is a novel human epididymal lipocalin secreted into seminal plasma; LCN6 expression is androgen-regulated in epididymal epithelium; LCN6 binds retinoic acid and other hydrophobic ligands modulating sperm signaling; LCN6 serves as biomarker in breast cancer and heart failure through machine learning identification; and LCN6 phylogenetic analysis reveals primate-specific epididymal adaptation. LCN6 functions as a specialized epididymal lipocalin—its beta-barrel structure sequesters hydrophobic ligands (retinoids, steroids, fatty acids) protecting them from oxidation and delivering them to maturing spermatozoa. The epididymal enrichment reflects LCN6's role in post-testicular sperm maturation: LCN6-bound ligands modulate sperm membrane fluidity, acrosome reaction competence, and capacitation timing. Male infertility pathogenesis may reflect LCN6 loss-of-function: absent ligand transport impairs sperm maturation, causing acrosomal defects and fertilization failure. Cancer-enriched expression suggests LCN6 may serve as tumor biomarker through lipocalin-mediated lipid metabolism dysregulation or immune modulation. Copy number variants may cause dosage effects on epididymal function, potentially contributing to idiopathic male infertility. The lipocalin family interaction network indicates functional redundancy among epididymal lipocalins (LCN8/9/10/12) with specialized ligand specificities.
