## Description
TMEM232 (C9JQI7) encodes a 657 amino acid transmembrane protein belonging to the TMEM232 family (IPR031747/PF15877/PTHR28651, positions 3-653 and 42-485). The AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 73.2), indicating a well-folded membrane protein. HPA expression reveals group-enriched RNA with highest levels in testis (15.8 nTPM) and fallopian tube (13.5 nTPM)—both ciliated tissues—consistent with its predicted role in cilium biogenesis/degradation and flagellated sperm motility. Subcellular predictions indicate membrane localization with activity at the outer dense fiber of sperm flagella. STRING interactions identify TMEM232 within a network connecting to diverse cellular processes: RAG2 (0.881, V(D)J recombination), FAU (0.796, ubiquitin-like protein), ribosomal proteins RPL21 (0.694) and RPL8 (0.588), mitochondrial dynamics protein SLC25A46 (0.470), E3 ubiquitin ligase CUL7 (0.548), and olfactory receptor OR10A3 (0.593). The connection to FAU and CUL7 suggests potential involvement in ubiquitin-mediated protein turnover during spermatid differentiation. ClinVar documents 28 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number variations spanning 5q15-q23.2, though no specific monogenic disease has been definitively assigned. The convergence of testis-enriched expression, ciliary/flagellar localization, outer dense fiber positioning, and ubiquitin system interactions suggests TMEM232 may function as a structural or regulatory component of the sperm flagellum, potentially coordinating protein complex assembly or stability during spermiogenesis through ubiquitin-dependent mechanisms.
