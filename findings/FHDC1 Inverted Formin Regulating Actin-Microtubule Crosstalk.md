## Description
FHDC1 (FH2 Domain-Containing Protein 1, also known as Inverted Formin 2/IF2) encodes a large 1143-amino acid partially structured protein (AlphaFold pLDDT 57.5) that functions as a cytoskeletal regulator mediating actin-microtubule crosstalk and cilia length control. This LIGHT GRAY gene has 24 literature references, with seminal 2018 work (38 citations) demonstrating FHDC1's actin-dependent regulation of cilia length, and recent 2024 research (7 citations) revealing its role in collective dynamics of actin-microtubule networks and cortical traveling waves. InterPro analysis confirms FHDC1 as a member of the Formin Homology Diaphanous subfamily (IPR051412, PTHR45691, residues 30-1027) with canonical FH2 domain architecture (IPR015425, PF02181, PS51444, SM00498, residues 87-527) essential for actin nucleation and polymerization. Human Protein Atlas reveals tissue-enhanced expression with parathyroid gland (16.5 nTPM) and thyroid gland (14.7 nTPM) enrichment, localized to primary cilium, centrosome, and basal body—key microtubule-organizing centers—supporting its role in cilium biogenesis and actin-binding function. STRING interactions identify a cytoskeletal network: PFN4 (0.816 high confidence, profilin actin-binding protein), SSH2 (0.700, slingshot phosphatase regulating cofilin), RNF135 (0.684, E3 ubiquitin ligase), NUFIP1 (0.674), PFN3 (0.668), PFN1 (0.650), EVI2B (0.650), NF1 (0.605, neurofibromin Ras-GAP), EVI2A (0.537), SUZ12 (0.454, PRC2 chromatin regulator), placing FHDC1 at the intersection of actin dynamics (profilins), microtubule organization (centrosome/cilia), and Ras signaling (NF1). ClinVar documents 27 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number variations spanning 4q22.1-35.2 region including duplications (123399154-190957473, 101203509-190957473, 93071152-190957473) and deletions (153061243-157994448, 153203431-162912359), indicating dosage sensitivity though specific monogenic phenotypes remain undefined. Functional hypothesis: FHDC1 acts as an inverted formin that nucleates actin filaments while simultaneously binding microtubules, coordinating cytoskeletal dynamics required for cilia assembly, maintenance, and length control. Mechanistic proposal: Through its FH2 domain, FHDC1 processively elongates actin filaments at the basal body/centrosome interface; interaction with profilins (PFN1/3/4) enhances actin monomer delivery, while SSH2-mediated cofilin regulation controls filament turnover; this actin scaffold organizes microtubule nucleation for cilia extension, with NF1 interaction potentially linking cytoskeletal dynamics to Ras signaling; copy number alterations disrupt cilia length homeostasis, contributing to 4q genomic disorder phenotypes potentially including ciliopathy features (neurodevelopmental, skeletal, endocrine dysfunction given parathyroid/thyroid expression).
