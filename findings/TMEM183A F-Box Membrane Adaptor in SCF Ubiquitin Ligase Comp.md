## Description

Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm, plasma membrane, and cell junctions, consistent with dual membrane/nuclear functions. AlphaFold structure prediction (Q8IXX5) yields high confidence (pLDDT 72.6) across residues 1-376, indicating a well-folded stable structure. Evolutionary conservation is strong: mouse ortholog Tmem183 shares 99.7% sequence identity (375/376 aa).\n\nFUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TMEM183A functions as a membrane-anchored substrate adaptor for SCF ubiquitin ligase complexes, recruiting specific transmembrane or membrane-associated proteins for ubiquitination and degradation. The F-box domain mediates binding to SKP1 scaffold, while the C-terminal domain may confer substrate specificity. Dual nucleoplasmic/membrane localization suggests it may shuttle between compartments to regulate protein turnover at membrane contact sites or during cellular stress responses.
