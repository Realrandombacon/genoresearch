# DUSP1/MKP-1: Dual-Specificity MAP Kinase Phosphatase Governing Stress Response Termination via MAPK Dephosphorylation and Emerging Therapeutic Target in Cardiotoxicity, Sepsis, and Hepatocellular Carcinoma

**Date:** 2026-06-28T00:24:45.324973

**Quality Score:** 7/10 (GOOD)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH

## Description
DUSP1 (UniProt P28562), also known as MKP-1 or CL100, encodes a 367-amino acid dual-specificity phosphatase containing an N-terminal rhodanese-like domain (IPR001763/G3DSA:3.40.250.10, aa 10-137) for MAPK binding and a C-terminal catalytic phosphatase domain (IPR000340/PF00782, aa 181-311) with dual specificity for tyrosine and threonine dephosphorylation (GO:0017017). The protein belongs to the MAP kinase phosphatase family (IPR008343, aa 3-366) within the protein tyrosine phosphatase superfamily (IPR000387/IPR003595/G3DSA:3.90.190.10, aa 175-328) with conserved active site motif (IPR016130, aa 256-266). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded two-domain architecture (pLDDT: 79.2), consistent with stable enzyme-substrate complex formation.\n\nSTRING interactions define DUSP1's position as a central MAPK pathway negative regulator: strongest links to ERK1/MAPK3 (0.996), JNK1/MAPK8 (0.992), p38β/MAPK11 (0.973), JNK2/MAPK9 (0.968), immediate-early transcription factor FOS (0.961), zinc finger protein ZFP36/TTP (0.934), ERK2/MAPK1 (0.671), p38α/MAPK14 (0.622), p38δ/MAPK13 (0.587), and p38γ/MAPK12 (0.552). HPA confirms ubiquitous expression with cancer-enhanced specificity, immune cell enrichment, and nucleoli/cytosol localization.\n\nWith 1344 papers, DUSP1 is well-established as an immediate-early gene induced by stress stimuli (UV, cytokines, growth factors) that terminates MAPK signaling through direct dephosphorylation of activated ERK, JNK, and p38. However, 2024-2025 literature reveals emerging therapeutic applications: (1) NDUFS4-SIRT5-DUSP1 axis mediates Ginsenoside Rb1 cardioprotection via mitochondrial quality control in ischemia-reperfusion; (2) ligustrazine-based nanodelivery targets SIRT5-DUSP1 for doxorubicin-induced cardiotoxicity; (3) Formosanin C activates DUSP1/AMPK/ULK1/Beclin1 autophagy pathway in hepatocellular carcinoma; (4) Astragaloside IV alleviates septic myocardial injury through DUSP1-Prohibitin 2 mitochondrial-ER crosstalk. ClinVar contains 20 pathogenic variants (5q35.1 CNVs including splice variant c.514-11C>A). This positions DUSP1 as a stress-responsive MAPK brake with untapped therapeutic potential in cardioprotection and cancer.
