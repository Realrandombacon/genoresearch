Literature (1 paper, 2024) reveals critical role: GARIN5B contributes to murine male fertility by assuring correct morphogenesis of sperm heads—sperm heads contain nucleus and acrosome (cap-like structure derived from Golgi apparatus); GARIN family proteins localize to Golgi and interact with RAB2B to regulate acrosome biogenesis and sperm head shaping during spermiogenesis. Functional hypothesis: GARIN5B functions as (a) Golgi-associated adaptor protein bridging RAB2B GTPase with vesicle trafficking machinery to regulate acrosomal vesicle formation and transport during spermatid differentiation, and (b) scaffold protein organizing Golgi matrix components for proper sperm head morphogenesis through intrinsically disordered region-mediated protein-protein interactions. Mechanistic proposal: GARIN5B localizes to Golgi apparatus via N-terminal GARIN family domain, where Rab2B-binding domain (aa 98-165) interacts with GTP-bound RAB2B to recruit vesicle tethering factors and SNARE proteins for acrosomal vesicle budding from Golgi cisternae—during spermiogenesis (haploid spermatid differentiation), GARIN5B coordinates Golgi-derived vesicle transport to the developing acrosome, ensuring proper acrosome cap formation over the anterior nucleus (essential for sperm-egg recognition and fertilization); intrinsically disordered regions (low pLDDT 43.8) provide conformational flexibility enabling GARIN5B to bind multiple partners simultaneously (RAB2B, GOLGA proteins, cytoskeletal elements) and undergo phosphorylation/acetylation regulation during spermatid maturation—copy number variants at 19q13.33-13.43 cause gene dosage effects: haploinsufficiency impairs acrosome biogenesis leading to globozoospermia (round-headed sperm without acrosome) and male infertility; overexpression disrupts Golgi trafficking balance causing aberrant sperm head shaping—positioning GARIN5B as diagnostic marker for male infertility (semen analysis, genetic testing) and potential therapeutic target (gene therapy, RAB2B pathway modulation) for obstructive and non-obstructive azoospermia.
