## Description
FIRRM (C1orf112) encodes an 853 aa nucleoplasmic protein with a DUF4487 domain (IPR027902/PF14868, residues 176-728) of unknown function. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 86.8). HPA shows nuclear/nucleolar localization with highest expression in parathyroid gland (12.7 nTPM). Functionally linked to chromosome segregation, interstrand cross-link repair, and protein kinase binding via interaction with FIGNL1 (fidgetin-like helicase). ClinVar contains 28 pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 1q24.2, suggesting disease relevance. Mouse ortholog (Q3TQQ9, 903 aa) shows ~85% sequence conservation. The DUF4487 domain architecture suggests FIRRM acts as a scaffold recruiting DNA repair machinery to replication forks or mitotic spindle apparatus. High structural confidence plus disease variants indicate functional importance despite domain annotation as "unknown.
