## Description
SCNM1 encodes Sodium Channel Modifier 1 (UniProt Q9BWG6), a 230-amino acid zinc-finger protein that functions as a minor intron splicing factor within the major spliceosome complex, serving as an essential RNA processing factor required for efficient splicing of minor (U12-type) introns and primary cilia formation, with biallelic pathogenic variants causing orofaciodigital (OFD) syndrome ciliopathy characterized by craniofacial, oral cavity, and extremities abnormalities.
