## Description
SKIC2 (Superkiller Complex Protein 2), also known as THES2, is a 1246-amino acid RNA helicase with only 9 publications confirming its genuine dark gene status despite causing severe Mendelian disease. The protein contains multiple conserved helicase domains including DEXH-box helicase domain of SKIV2L (cd18027, positions 303-481) characteristic of DEAH-box RNA helicases, C-terminal helicase domain of Ski2 family helicases (cd18795, positions 483-720), DEAD/DEAH-box helicase domain (IPR011545, positions 314-460) mediating ATP binding (GO:0005524) and nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676), ATP-dependent RNA helicase Ski2/MTR4 C-terminal domain (IPR012961, positions 1069-1245), exosome RNA helicase MTR4-like beta-barrel domain (IPR025696, positions 832-1046), helicase superfamily 1/2 ATP-binding domain (IPR014001, positions 307-490), P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase domains (IPR027417, G3DSA:3.40.50.300, positions 276-483/484-741), and circadian oscillator component FRH-like family architecture (IPR016438, positions 50-1245) with RNA binding (GO:0003723), RNA helicase activity (GO:0003724), and RNA catabolic process (GO:0006401) functions. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 80.7), consistent with stable multi-domain RNA helicase architecture. HPA reveals nucleoplasm, nuclear bodies, mitotic spindle, and cytosol subcellular localization with helicase, hydrolase, and RNA-binding molecular functions, disease variant involvement, low tissue specificity with detected-in-many distribution, and cell type-enhanced single cell specificity. ClinVar documents 177 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.354+1G>T, c.1064+2T>C, c.2732-1G>A, c.3070-1G>A), frameshift deletions/duplications (c.1258del p.Val420fs, c.2370dup p.Lys791fs, c.2795_2796dup p.Leu933fs), and nonsense mutations (c.1048C>T p.Gln350Ter, c.2476C>T p.Gln826Ter), though specific disease phenotypes beyond tricho-hepato-enteric syndrome remain uncharacterized. STRING returned no interactions, typical for superkiller complex components where interactions are defined by stable complex assembly rather than dynamic networks. Recent studies (2024-2025) established SKIC2 as causative for tricho-hepato-enteric syndrome (THES type 2), a rare autosomal recessive disorder characterized by intractable diarrhea, woolly hair, growth restriction, and hemochromatosis, with mid-trimester ultrasound findings now documented. We hypothesize SKIC2 functions as the catalytic RNA helicase component of the SKI (superkiller) complex that activates the cytoplasmic exosome for 3'-to-5' RNA degradation, unwinding structured RNA substrates to enable exosomal processing. The DEXH/DEAH-box helicase domains (positions 303-481/314-460) provide ATP-dependent RNA unwinding activity, while the MTR4-like beta-barrel domain (positions 832-1046) and C-terminal Ski2 domain (positions 1069-1245) mediate exosome recruitment and substrate channeling. The FRH-like architecture (positions 50-1245) suggests evolutionary relationship to fungal RNA helicases involved in RNA surveillance. THES pathology likely results from defective mRNA turnover in rapidly renewing tissues (intestinal epithelium, hair follicles, liver), where impaired RNA degradation causes accumulation of aberrant transcripts disrupting cellular homeostasis. The 177 ClinVar variants indicate extreme dosage sensitivity, with loss-of-function mutations (splice, frameshift, nonsense) causing complete SKI complex inactivation. SKIC2 represents an uncharacterized cytoplasmic RNA helicase essential for post-transcriptional gene regulation through exosome-mediated RNA decay.
