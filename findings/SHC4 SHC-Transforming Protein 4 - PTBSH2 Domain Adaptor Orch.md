## Description
SHC4 (SHC-transforming protein 4, UniProt Q6S5L8, 630 aa) is a 'PARTIALLY CHARACTERIZED' gene with 52 papers in literature, representing an intracellular adaptor protein that orchestrates receptor tyrosine kinase (RTK) signaling, β-catenin-mediated metastasis, and STAT3 activation in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). InterPro domains identify characteristic SHC4 architecture: Phosphotyrosine-binding PTB domain (IPR006019/IPR006020/cd01209, aa176-347) for phosphotyrosine motif recognition and membrane recruitment, Src homology 2 SH2 domain (IPR000980/IPR035676/PF00017/cd09925, aa518-618) for phosphotyrosine binding and signalosome assembly, PH-like domain superfamily (IPR011993/G3DSA:2.30.29.30, aa177-341) for membrane localization, SH2 domain superfamily (IPR036860/G3DSA:3.30.505.10, aa511-620), and SHC4/CEP152 family membership (IPR051235, aa20-622). STRING interactions reveal exclusive partnerships with RTK signaling network: GRB2 (0.968), IGF1R (0.945), ERBB4 (0.942), NTRK1 (0.936), SRC (0.929), FGFR3 (0.923), ERBB2 (0.704), ERBB3 (0.587), NTRK2 (0.479), EGFR (0.449). ClinVar contains 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including 15q CNVs (copy number gains/losses at 15q11.2-q22.2) though no specific disease named. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 58.6) shows medium/low confidence with partially disordered architecture typical of flexible adaptor proteins enabling dynamic protein-protein interactions. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: SHC4 functions as a cytosolic adaptor protein that bridges activated receptor tyrosine kinases (IGF1R, ERBB family, NTRK, FGFR) to downstream signaling cascades (RAS-MAPK, PI3K-AKT, JAK-STAT, Wnt-β-catenin) through PTB domain-mediated membrane recruitment and SH2 domain-dependent signalosome assembly, promoting tumor proliferation, metastasis, and extracellular vesicle secretion in multiple cancer types.
