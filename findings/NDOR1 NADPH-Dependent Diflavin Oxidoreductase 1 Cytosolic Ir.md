## Description
NDOR1 (NADPH-Dependent Diflavin Oxidoreductase 1, UniProt Q9UHB4) is a 597 amino acid cytosolic/nucleoplasmic/intermediate filament-associated diflavin oxidoreductase with partial characterization (26 papers) that functions as an essential electron transfer enzyme in cytosolic iron-sulfur (Fe-S) cluster biogenesis, transferring electrons from NADPH to anamorsin/CIAPIN1 for Fe-S cluster assembly. The protein contains flavodoxin-like domain (IPR001094, residues 7-20/55-66/89-99) with FMN binding (GO:0010181), oxidoreductase FAD/NAD(P)-binding domain (IPR001433, residues 455-559) with oxidoreductase activity (GO:0016491), flavoprotein pyridine nucleotide cytochrome reductase domain (IPR001709, residues 382-389/416-425/454-473), sulfite reductase NADPH flavoprotein alpha-component-like FAD-binding domain (IPR003097, residues 203-421), flavodoxin/nitric oxide synthase domain (IPR008254, residues 6-150), NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase domain 3 (G3DSA:1.20.990.10, residues 252-380), translation factors homologous superfamily (G3DSA:2.40.30.10, residues 208-251/381-445), and nucleotide-binding domain of ferredoxin-NADP reductase module (G3DSA:3.40.50.80, residues 446-597), placing it in the diflavin oxidoreductase family with dual FMN/FAD cofactor binding.
