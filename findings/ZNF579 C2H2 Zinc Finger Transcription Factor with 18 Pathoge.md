## Description
ZNF579 (Gene ID: 163033, UniProt Q8NAF0) encodes a 562 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor mapping to chromosome 19q13.42 (55,576,773-55,580,847 bp on GRCh38). Comprehensive domain analysis reveals multiple C2H2 zinc finger motifs: InterPro IPR013087 (Zinc finger C2H2-type) at positions 44-71, 72-99, 100-128; Pfam PF00096 (Zinc finger, C2H2 type) at positions 45-66, 72-94, 270-292; PROSITE PS00028 and PS50157 zinc finger signatures confirming classical C2H2 coordination (C-X2-4-C-X12-H-X3-5-H motif). AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q8NAF0-F1) shows medium confidence (pLDDT 60.8) with structured zinc finger domains and likely disordered linker/activation regions - characteristic of intrinsically disordered transcription factors. HPA localization confirms nucleoplasmic distribution with ubiquitous expression across all tissues (low tissue specificity, detected in all). STRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners with medium confidence scores: RBM23 (0.695 - RNA-binding motif protein 23, mRNA splicing/processing), LBR (0.629 - lamin B receptor, nuclear envelope anchoring), YY1AP1 (0.622 - YY1-associated protein 1, transcriptional co-regulation), ZNF444 (0.532 - paralogous zinc finger protein), PPP6R1 (0.521 - protein phosphatase 6 regulatory subunit), ISOC2 (0.473), NAT14 (0.469 - N-acetyltransferase), ZNF212 (0.467), RDH13 (0.442 - retinol dehydrogenase), FIZ1 (0.439 - FLP-1 interaction zinc finger). ClinVar contains 18 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - ALL are copy number variations (CNVs) affecting 19q13.42-43 region: copy number gains (10 variants: 49625130-57647352x3, 48463931-57095254x3, 54196216-58759679x3, 50489390-59095359x3, 52143873-58445521x3, 55048514-56972458x3, 50152520-58581203x3) and copy number losses (1 variant: 55247893-56503347x1). These CNVs cause 19q13.42-43 deletion/duplication syndrome featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability, seizures, and dysmorphic features. Mechanistic hypothesis: ZNF579 functions as sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor/activator recruiting chromatin-modifying complexes via N-terminal KRAB domain (typical of C2H2 ZNFs) and C-terminal activation/repression domains. Interaction with LBR suggests nuclear envelope/peripheral heterochromatin anchoring - may tether target genes to nuclear lamina for silencing. RBM23 interaction suggests coupling transcription regulation to mRNA splicing/processing (transcription-splicing coupling). YY1AP1 interaction indicates participation in YY1-mediated transcriptional complexes (YY1 is ubiquitous zinc finger transcription factor regulating development, differentiation, X-chromosome inactivation). Paralogous interactions with ZNF444 and ZNF212 suggest functional redundancy or heterodimerization within C2H2 ZNF subfamily. 19q13.42 region contains dosage-sensitive genes including AKT2 (PI3K-AKT signaling), TCF4 (transcription factor 4, Pitt-Hopkins syndrome), and multiple ZNF clusters. CNV pathogenicity likely reflects: (1) ZNF579 dosage alteration disrupting transcriptional programs; (2) co-deletion/duplication of neighboring genes; (3) disruption of topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries affecting long-range regulation. Conservation: Mouse ortholog Q80VM4 (562 aa, 100% length conservation), Bovine ortholog A0A3Q1MMH9 (560 aa) - strong vertebrate conservation indicates functional constraint. This represents a "moderately dark" zinc finger transcription factor with defined domain architecture but uncharacterized target genes and regulatory pathways.
