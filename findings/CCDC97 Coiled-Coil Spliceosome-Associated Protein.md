## Description
CCDC97 encodes a 343-amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein (UniProt Q96F63) with strong evolutionary conservation (mouse ortholog Q9DBT3 is 340 aa, 99% length identity). The protein contains a CCDC97-like family domain (IPR018613, residues 50-339) and a C-terminal coiled-coil domain (IPR040233/PF09747, residues 156-330). STRING analysis reveals 10 high-confidence protein interactions, predominantly with spliceosome complex components: SF3B5 (0.896), SF3B1 (0.843), SF3B3 (0.806), SF3A1 (0.799), SF3B4 (0.788), SF3A3 (0.765), ZRSR2 (0.713), and SF3B6 (0.624). This interaction profile strongly suggests CCDC97 functions as a peripheral spliceosome factor, potentially stabilizing SF3B complex assembly or facilitating pre-mRNA splicing fidelity. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic, plasma membrane, and cytosolic localization, consistent with housekeeping splicing functions. ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic copy number gain variants on chromosome 19, indicating dosage sensitivity—overexpression may disrupt spliceosome stoichiometry and cause aberrant splicing. The coiled-coil domain likely mediates oligomerization or scaffold formation within the spliceosome holoenzyme.
