# CCDC124: Ribosomal Scaffold Protein Regulating Translation During Cell Division

## Description
CCDC124 encodes a 223 amino acid coiled-coil domain protein (UniProt Q96CT7) with strong structural confidence (AlphaFold pLDDT 79.9). InterPro analysis reveals conserved coiled-coil domains (IPR010422, IPR054414, PF06244) spanning residues 1-222, characteristic of scaffold proteins that mediate protein-protein interactions. STRING analysis identifies 10 high-confidence interaction partners (all scores >0.90), exclusively ribosomal proteins: FAU (0.952), RPS5 (0.924), RPS19 (0.922), RPL5 (0.918), RPS15 (0.918), RPL23A (0.917), RPL11 (0.917), RACK1 (0.916), RPL8 (0.910), and RPS7 (0.908). This interaction profile strongly suggests CCDC124 functions as a ribosomal scaffold or assembly factor. HPA data shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, localized to cytosol and plasma membrane, consistent with ribosome-associated function. The gene is annotated for cell cycle and cell division processes, aligning with the known requirement for elevated translation capacity during proliferation. ClinVar documents 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number gains/losses on chromosome 19p13.11, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation extends to mouse (Q9D8X2, 217 aa, 97% identity) and bovine orthologs. Mechanistic hypothesis: CCDC124 acts as a coiled-coil scaffold that stabilizes ribosomal subunit assembly or facilitates translation initiation complex formation, particularly during cell division when protein synthesis demand peaks. The exclusive ribosomal protein interactions suggest it may bridge small (40S) and large (60S) subunit components or recruit translation factors like RACK1 to the ribosome.
