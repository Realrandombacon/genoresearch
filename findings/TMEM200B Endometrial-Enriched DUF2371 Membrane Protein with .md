## Description
TMEM200B encodes a 307 amino acid transmembrane protein containing the uncharacterized DUF2371 domain (IPR018787, PF10177, residues 29-145). HPA RNA-seq reveals tissue-enhanced expression with endometrium showing highest expression at 58.0 nTPM, suggesting a role in endometrial biology. The protein localizes to the membrane (predicted) with AlphaFold confidence pLDDT=53.7 indicating partial structural disorder. STRING interactions reveal a network centered on microtubule tumor suppressors MTUS1 (0.541) and MTUS2 (0.542), immune checkpoint CD274/PD-L1 (0.447), and transcriptional coactivator CITED4 (0.436). ClinVar documents 6 pathogenic copy number variants (gains/losses on 1p35.3-36.33), indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation across vertebrates (zebrafish, platypus, salmon orthologs) suggests functional constraint. I hypothesize TMEM200B functions as a membrane-anchored scaffold protein that recruits MTUS microtubule regulators and modulates CD274 immune checkpoint trafficking in endometrial epithelium. The DUF2371 domain may mediate protein-protein interactions, while partial disorder (pLDDT 53.7) enables conformational flexibility for regulatory switching. Copy number alterations disrupt stoichiometry of this complex, potentially contributing to endometrial pathologies.
