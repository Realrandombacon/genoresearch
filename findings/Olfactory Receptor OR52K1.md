## Description
OR52K1 is an olfactory receptor that plays a crucial role in detecting odorant molecules and transmitting signals to the brain. It is a G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) with a seven-transmembrane domain structure. The protein interacts with GNAL, ARRB2, and ARRB1, which are involved in signal transduction pathways. OR52K1 is specifically expressed in the olfactory epithelium and is responsible for recognizing and binding to specific odorant molecules. Mutations in this gene have been associated with various diseases, including olfactory disorders. The protein has a high degree of conservation with other olfactory receptors and is a potential target for therapeutic interventions.
