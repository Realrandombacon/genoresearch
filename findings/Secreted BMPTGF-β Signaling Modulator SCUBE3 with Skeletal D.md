## Description
SCUBE3 (Signal peptide, CUB and EGF-like domain-containing protein 3) is a 993-amino acid secreted glycoprotein functioning as a co-receptor/modulator in BMP and TGF-β signaling pathways, with critical roles in osteogenic differentiation, skeletal development, and cancer progression. InterPro analysis reveals a multi-domain architecture: three EGF-like calcium-binding domains (IPR001881, positions 29-69, 70-111, 112-152), one CUB domain (IPR000859, positions 804-916), growth factor receptor cysteine-rich domain superfamily (IPR009030), and multiple EGF_CA domains (cd00054), consistent with extracellular ligand-binding functions. STRING interactions identify high-confidence partners in TGF-β signaling and extracellular matrix remodeling: TGFBR2 (0.875), MMP9 (0.845), SCUBE1 (0.758), MMP2 (0.502), plus TCP11 (0.678) and ANKS1A (0.580), suggesting coordination between growth factor signaling and proteolytic processing. HPA data shows group-enriched expression in blood vessel (42.6 nTPM) and thyroid gland (25.9 nTPM), with cancer-enhanced patterns and plasma membrane localization, classified as a secreted disease-related protein. ClinVar contains 30 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including nonsense mutations (p.Gln661Ter, p.Cys74Ter, p.Glu807Ter), missense variants (p.Tyr347His, p.Asn833Asp, p.Glu807Val), and splice site mutations, causing Short stature, facial dysmorphism, and skeletal anomalies with or without cardiac anomalies 2 (OMIM disorder). AlphaFold structure (AF-Q8IX30-F1) shows high confidence (pLDDT 77.1), indicating a stable multi-domain extracellular protein. Literature (92 papers) describes SCUBE3's role in promoting osteogenic differentiation via BMP2/TGF-β pathway, tumor-promoting effects in tongue squamous cell carcinoma through CCL2/CEBPA axis, antibody-mediated targeting for cancer therapy, and novel homozygous variants in skeletal dysplasia patients—suggesting dual roles in development and oncogenesis through growth factor modulation.
