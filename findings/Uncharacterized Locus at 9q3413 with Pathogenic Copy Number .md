## Description
LOC105376306 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 9q34.13 (NC_000009.12:133030421-133028151) lacking protein-coding annotation. No UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, and Human Protein Atlas found no expression data (ENSG00000288989 not in HPA database), suggesting this locus does not encode a canonical protein-coding gene. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 24 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, predominantly copy number variations including deletions (chr9:137552409-137879159, chr9:137552082-137728529, chr9:136926151-138059181) and duplications (chr9:132386553-138059695, chr9:137552409-138059181). These structural variants span the 9q34.13-34.3 region and are associated with 9q34 duplication syndrome (Kleefstra syndrome spectrum) phenotypes including intellectual disability, developmental delay, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. The recurrent pathogenic CNVs indicate this locus resides within a dosage-sensitive genomic region critical for neurodevelopment and chromatin regulation. This locus likely functions as part of the EHMT1 gene regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose disruption through copy number alteration contributes to 9q34 syndrome pathogenesis. The absence of protein-coding features combined with significant clinical variant burden suggests a regulatory or non-coding functional role.
