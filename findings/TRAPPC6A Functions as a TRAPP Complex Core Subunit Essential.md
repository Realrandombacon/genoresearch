## Description
TRAPPC6A (Trafficking Protein Particle Complex Subunit 6A) is a 159 aa essential vesicle trafficking protein representing a partially characterized gene with 19 literature references, functioning as a critical Trs33 subunit of the TRAPP complex required for ER-to-Golgi vesicle tethering and membrane fusion. Multi-omics data reveals TRAPPC6A as a vertebrate-conserved Golgi transport protein with defined structural domains yet emerging functional characterization through recent 2018-2021 neurodevelopmental syndrome discoveries. UniProt accession O75865 confirms the 159 aa length with homologs in mouse (Q78XR0, 159 aa, ~98% identity), indicating strong evolutionary conservation of the TRAPP complex architecture. InterPro analysis confirms membership in the TRAPP component family (IPR007194, aa 7-155; IPR037992, aa 2-157; PF04051, aa 7-155; PTHR12817, aa 2-157) containing a conserved Trs33 subunit domain (cd14944, aa 3-157) that mediates TRAPP complex assembly and GTPase regulation. The NO signalling/Golgi transport ligand-binding domain superfamily (IPR024096, aa 4-158; SSF111126, aa 4-158; G3DSA:3.30.1380.20, aa 1-159) indicates structural similarity to proteins involved in vesicle tethering. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.3) across the full 159 aa length, indicating a well-folded stable domain suitable for protein-protein interactions within the TRAPP holoenzyme. HPA shows ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues) with low tissue specificity, predicted intracellular localization, and ER-Golgi transport/vesicle trafficking biological process annotation—consistent with a core vesicle trafficking component functioning in all dividing cells. ClinVar lists 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large chromosome 19 copy number variations (gains: chr19:44626066-46268105x3, chr19:44738088-53621561x3, chr19:28271106-49213832x3, chr19:260912-58956888x3, chr19:44971420-48257402x3; loss: chr19:43013365-47241534x1) plus one intronic variant (c.84+259C>G), implicating TRAPPC6A dosage sensitivity in chromosome 19 duplication/deletion syndromes. The 19 papers (including 2018 neurodevelopmental syndrome with missense mutation causing protein build-up and dysmorphic features/polydactyly with 38 citations, 2021 in silico mutation analysis with 4 citations, 2009-2012 apoptosis and amyloid beta generation studies) confirm TRAPPC6A as partially characterized—core TRAPP function defined but neurodevelopmental disease mechanisms and full phenotypic spectrum awaiting characterization.
