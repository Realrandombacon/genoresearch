## Description
ZNF701 encodes a 531 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein localized to the nucleoplasm and centriolar satellites. The protein contains an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 74-146) followed by three C2H2-type zinc finger motifs (PF00096, IPR013087) at residues 336-414, characteristic of sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factors. STRING interaction network reveals 10 medium-confidence partners (scores 0.464-0.651) including ZER1 (0.651), ZNF808 (0.643), TRIM28 (0.580), ZNF816 (0.554), and ZNF468 (0.542), suggesting participation in heterochromatin formation complexes via TRIM28/KAP1 recruitment. HPA data shows ubiquitous low-level expression across all tissues with nucleoplasmic localization. ClinVar documents 15 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 19q13.41 copy number gains affecting the ZNF701 locus, though specific disease phenotypes remain undefined. The KRAB domain mediates transcriptional repression through heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) recruitment, while the C2H2 zinc fingers confer DNA sequence specificity. This architecture positions ZNF701 as an epigenetic silencer targeting endogenous retroviral elements or lineage-specific genes.
