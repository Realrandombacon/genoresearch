## Description

ClinVar documents 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 3q CNVs (gains and losses) indicating dosage sensitivity, though specific disease phenotypes remain incompletely characterized. AlphaFold structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 59.8) with structured homeodomain region and disordered N/C-terminal regions typical of transcription factors requiring conformational flexibility for cofactor interactions. Literature (466 papers) reveals established and emerging roles: 2024-2025 studies establish SHOX2/RASSF1A methylation as diagnostic biomarker for lung cancer and malignant pleural effusion; cardiac development research identifies SHOX2 as essential for sinoatrial node formation and pacemaker cell differentiation; limb development studies show SHOX2 redundancy with SHOX in skeletal growth; cancer biology reveals SHOX2 overexpression in breast, ovarian, and gastric cancers promoting proliferation and metastasis. Functional hypothesis: SHOX2 functions as a developmental transcription factor with dual roles in embryonic patterning (cardiac pacemaker specification, limb development) and cancer progression (cell cycle regulation, EMT promotion); methylation status serves as epigenetic biomarker for early cancer detection; cardiac expression critical for HCN4 regulation and pacemaker current establishment. Mechanistic proposal: Homeodomain binds specific DNA sequences (5'-TAAT-3' core) recruiting transcriptional co-activators or repressors via OAR domain; TBX3/TBX5/TBX18 interactions suggest cooperative regulation of cardiac gene regulatory network; HCN4 interaction indicates direct transcriptional control of pacemaker channel expression; nuclear import via IPO13 enables transcriptional activity; cancer-enhanced expression suggests oncogenic transformation through cell cycle gene activation (CCND1, CDK6) and EMT transcription factor induction; 3q CNVs suggest haploinsufficiency could disrupt cardiac development contributing to arrhythmias or skeletal dysplasia; promoter methylation silencing in normal tissues but hypomethylation in cancers enables diagnostic utility. This gene represents a moderately characterized homeobox transcription factor with established developmental roles and emerging clinical applications as cancer biomarker.
