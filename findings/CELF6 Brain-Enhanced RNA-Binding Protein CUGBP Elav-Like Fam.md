## Description
CELF6 encodes a 481 aa CUGBP Elav-like family member 6 (UniProt Q96J87), also known as BRUNOL6, functioning as an RNA-binding protein that regulates pre-mRNA alternative splicing, mRNA stability, and translation through three RNA recognition motif (RRM) domains. The protein belongs to the CELF/BRUNOL family characterized by two N-terminal RRM domains (RRM1: aa41-127/46-127, RRM2: aa133-213/134-214), a divergent linker region (~160-230 aa), and one C-terminal RRM domain (RRM3: aa392-470/396-474). Domain architecture includes RNA recognition motif domains (IPR000504, PF00076, cd12632/cd12635/cd12639, aa36-479), CELF-3/4/5/6-specific RRM1 (IPR034648, aa41-127), nucleotide-binding alpha-beta plait domain superfamily (IPR012677, aa36-221/375-479), and RNA-binding domain superfamily (IPR035979, aa36-478) with RNA binding function (GO:0003723).
