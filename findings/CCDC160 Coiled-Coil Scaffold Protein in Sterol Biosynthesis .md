## Description

AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with global pLDDT 72.3, indicating confident structural modeling. The coiled-coil domain architecture combined with specific interactions with rate-limiting sterol biosynthesis enzymes suggests CCDC160 functions as a regulatory scaffold protein that organizes the cholesterol biosynthetic metabolon, potentially facilitating substrate channeling between sequential enzymes in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane.
