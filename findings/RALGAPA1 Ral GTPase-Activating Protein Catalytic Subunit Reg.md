## Description
RALGAPA1 (Ral GTPase-activating protein subunit alpha-1, 2036 aa, UniProt Q6GYQ0) is a partially characterized large catalytic subunit of the RalGAP complex with emerging roles in neurodevelopment, neuronal migration, and cancer biology, representing an understudied GTPase regulator with significant clinical implications.
