## Description
LOC122128458 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 3396 located on chromosome 1q (genomic coordinates: 160978498-160978792 on NC_000001.11). This 294 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA expression). ClinVar analysis identified 3 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 2 are copy number gains (triplifications) spanning 1q21.2-25.2 and 1q23.1-25.1 regions, while 1 is a copy number loss spanning 1q23.2-24.1. The gain-to-loss ratio (2:1) suggests that over-dosage of this repressive element is more frequently pathogenic than haploinsufficiency, indicating the element normally functions to fine-tune transcriptional repression of target genes in cis on 1q. The 1q21-25 region contains numerous dosage-sensitive genes including developmental regulators and tumor suppressors (such as CHD1L at 1q21.1, a chromatin helicase involved in DNA repair). Amplification of this repressor may lead to aberrant silencing of critical genes, while deletion may cause derepression. The presence of both gain and loss pathogenic variants demonstrates bidirectional dosage sensitivity, though gains predominate—suggesting cellular homeostasis is more sensitive to excess repressive activity. This regulatory element likely participates in epigenetic fine-tuning of 1q gene expression, with disruption contributing to developmental disorders or cancer predisposition.
