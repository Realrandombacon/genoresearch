## Description
SLC9B1 (UniProt Q4ZJI4) encodes a 515 amino acid sodium/hydrogen antiporter protein localized to the acrosome and principal piece of the sperm cell (HPA). InterPro analysis confirms canonical cation/proton antiporter domains (IPR006153, PF00999, PTHR31102) spanning aa 118-482, consistent with its function in ion homeostasis and fertilization. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q4ZJI4-F1) predicts a well-folded, stable 3D structure with high confidence (global pLDDT 79.5), supporting its role as a functional membrane transporter despite limited expression. HPA expression reveals highly restricted tissue distribution, with strong enrichment in testis (nTPM 37.8) and low expression elsewhere, aligning with its role in spermatogenesis. ClinVar contains 22 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses on 4q24, with no specific monogenic disease annotated, suggesting a role in complex traits or susceptibility to conditions like fetal intolerance of labor. Literature search returned 18 papers, confirming partial characterization but lack of comprehensive functional studies. The convergence of testis-specific expression, antiporter domain architecture, high structural confidence, and CNV burden supports a functional hypothesis: SLC9B1 functions as a testis-specific sodium/hydrogen exchanger that regulates intracellular pH and ion balance during spermatogenesis; disruption via copy number variations may impair sperm maturation or contribute to reproductive disorders.
