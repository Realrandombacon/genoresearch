## Description
PLEKHG1 (Pleckstrin Homology and RhoGEF Domain Containing G1, UniProt Q9ULL1) is a 1385 aa intracellular nucleoplasmic protein belonging to the PLEKHG1/2/3 pleckstrin homology domain family (IPR043324) with Dbl homology RhoGEF domain (IPR000219/PF00621/G3DSA:1.20.900.10, positions 113-293/117-291/91-312) and PH domain (IPR001849/PF22697/G3DSA:2.30.29.30, positions 317-418/318-413/313-438) that functions as a Rho family guanine nucleotide exchange factor activating CDC42 and RAC1 GTPases for actin cytoskeleton regulation, is activated by FYN kinase phosphorylation, serves as a candidate gene for periventricular white matter abnormalities, represents a GWAS locus for white matter hyperintensities, and is associated with 6q24-27 copy number variant disorders. The molecular architecture consists of N-terminal region (positions 1-112) for protein stability and localization, central Dbl homology (DH) domain (positions 113-293, IPR000219) catalyzing GDP-GTP exchange on Rho GTPases, PH domain (positions 317-418, IPR001849) mediating membrane targeting via phosphoinositide binding, and extensive C-terminal region (positions 419-1385) for protein-protein interactions and regulatory functions. AlphaFold predicts a mostly disordered protein (pLDDT 49.8, low confidence) indicating intrinsically disordered regions with some structured DH/PH domains—consistent with RhoGEF scaffold protein requiring flexible regions for dynamic protein complex assembly and membrane recruitment.
