## Description
UTP15 (U3 Small Nucleolar RNA-Associated Protein 15 Homolog, also known as Small Subunit Processome Component UTP15, UniProt Q8TED0) is a 518 amino acid nucleolar protein with minimal characterization (9 papers) that functions as an essential scaffold component of the SSU (Small Subunit) processome, a massive 5 megadalton ribonucleoprotein complex required for 18S ribosomal RNA processing and small ribosomal subunit biogenesis. The protein contains multiple WD40 repeats (IPR001680/PF00400/PS50082 residues 31-66/69-108/111-193/71-108/114-150/155-193, GO:0005515 protein binding) forming a beta-propeller scaffold for protein-protein interactions, a UTP15 C-terminal domain (IPR018983/PF09384 residues 343-490, GO:0006364 rRNA processing, GO:0005730 nucleolus) specific to the SSU processome, and belongs to the WD40/YVTN repeat-like superfamily (IPR015943/G3DSA:2.130.10.10 residues 27-157/158-333) with conserved WD signature (IPR019775/PS00678 residues 137-151). STRING interactions reveal extremely high-confidence partnerships with SSU processome components: WDR3 (0.999, WD repeat-containing protein 3—t-UTP subcomplex), UTP4 (0.999, U3 small nucleolar RNA-associated protein 4—t-UTP subcomplex), WDR75 (0.999, WD repeat-containing protein 75—NOP subcomplex), NOC4L (0.999, nucleolar complex protein 4 homolog—UTP-B subcomplex), UTP18 (0.998, UTP18 small subunit processome component), BYSL (0.998, Bystin-like—UTP-C subcomplex), RRP9 (0.998, U3 small nucleolar RNA-associated protein 9—UTP-A subcomplex), MPHOSPH10 (0.998, M-phase phosphoprotein 10—UTP-C subcomplex), UTP6 (0.998, UTP6 small subunit processome component), and NOP58 (0.998, Nop58 ribonucleoprotein—C/D box snoRNP), confirming integration into the complete SSU processome assembly line.
