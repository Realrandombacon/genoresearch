# Uncharacterized Locus at 7q22.1 within 7q21 Microdeletion/Microduplication Syndrome Region with Pathogenic Copy Number Variants

## Description
LOC101927550 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 7q22.1 (NC_000007.14:99013164-99036491) spanning approximately 23.3 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve this locus to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, all copy number alterations including large deletions (chr7:98454022-100723798, ~2.3 Mb; chr7:54185-159282390, ~159.2 Mb whole arm) and duplications (chr7:97419852-158923762, ~61.5 Mb; chr7:98288474-101259804, ~3.0 Mb). These structural variants encompass the 7q21.3-22.1 region and are associated with 7q21 microdeletion syndrome and 7q21 microduplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and genitourinary anomalies. The 7q22.1 region contains HLCS (holocarboxylase synthetase), essential for biotin metabolism, and SGCE (epsilon-sarcoglycan), mutations in which cause myoclonus-dystonia syndrome. LOC101927550 likely functions as part of the 7q22.1 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to 7q21 syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology or long-range gene regulation of dosage-sensitive genes. The absence of protein-coding features combined with pathogenic CNV burden (4 variants) indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where structural variation disrupts neurodevelopmental and metabolic processes.
