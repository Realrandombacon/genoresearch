## Description
LOC105369595 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 12p13.33 telomere (NC_000012.12:586485-585697) spanning approximately 0.8 kb. This represents the most telomeric annotated feature on chromosome 12p. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000256020 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 23 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants - the highest burden observed for any 12p locus in this survey. Variants include massive 12p copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications spanning 12p13.33-11.1 (chr12:64621-34650483, ~34.6 Mb; chr12:64620-34682902, ~34.7 Mb; chr12:54427-34608071, ~34.6 Mb), 12p13.33-q13.12 (chr12:82453-49847230, ~49.8 Mb), 12p13.33-13.32 (chr12:54427-3639603, ~3.6 Mb; chr12:54427-4004912, ~4.0 Mb), 12p13.33-13.31 (chr12:418421-6235914, ~5.8 Mb), and deletions spanning 12p13.33 (chr12:121255-3003320, ~2.9 Mb; chr12:1-3750000 del, ~3.8 Mb). The 12p13.33 telomeric region is the critical locus for 12p duplication syndrome (Pallister-Killian syndrome is typically 12p tetrasomy via isochromosome) and 12p deletion syndrome, containing dosage-sensitive proto-oncogenes and tumor suppressors including KRAS (Kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene homolog, cancer), CDKN1B (cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor 1B, multiple endocrine neoplasia), ETV6 (ets variant 6, leukemia), SLC6A12 (solute carrier), and GPR133 (G-protein coupled receptor). LOC105369595 likely functions as: (1) a subtelomeric cis-regulatory enhancer controlling 12p13.33 oncogene expression, (2) a telomere position effect (TPE) boundary element modulating telomeric chromatin silencing, (3) a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) regulating 12p13.33 gene transcription in cis, (4) a chromatin boundary/insulator element within the 12p13.33 topologically associating domain (TAD), or (5) a telomere-associated repeat element affecting chromosome end stability. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (23 variants) despite absence of protein evidence indicates this telomeric locus represents a critical dosage-sensitive regulatory element where copy number alterations disrupt: (1) telomere position effect regulating subtelomeric gene expression, (2) 3D genome architecture of the 12p13.33 oncogene cluster, (3) KRAS/CDKN1B/ETV6 allele-specific expression balance, or (4) telomere capping function affecting chromosome stability, leading to 12p duplication syndrome (developmental delay, intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, cancer predisposition) or 12p deletion syndrome (growth retardation, dysmorphic features, developmental delay, hematologic abnormalities).
