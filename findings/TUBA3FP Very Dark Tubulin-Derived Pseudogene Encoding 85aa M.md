# TUBA3FP: Very Dark Tubulin-Derived Pseudogene Encoding 85aa Micropeptide with Immune Expression and Extreme CNV Burden in Cervical Cancer

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
TUBA3FP (tubulin alpha 3f pseudogene) is located at chromosome 22q11.21 and encodes an 85 amino acid micropeptide derived from the tubulin alpha family. Despite pseudogene annotation, emerging literature (2025) demonstrates this locus produces a functional peptide encoded by a long non-coding RNA that undergoes HPV E6/E7-induced acetylation to inhibit ferroptosis and promote cervical cancer malignancy—representing a newly discovered class of lncRNA-encoded regulatory microproteins. The AlphaFold predicted structure (A0A994J5G1) shows medium confidence folding (pLDDT=60.8), consistent with a partially structured micropeptide that may retain tubulin-like structural elements despite truncation. HPA reports RNA expression detected in some tissues with immune cell enrichment and cell type specificity, confirming active transcription. Critically, ClinVar documents 170 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—overwhelmingly copy number losses affecting 22q11.21 (e.g., accession 4852072: chr22:18857119-21109442 deletion; accession 4852069: chr22:18857118-21109441 deletion)—indicating extreme dosage sensitivity in this DiGeorge syndrome critical region. STRING found no interaction partners, and InterPro scan failed, reflecting limited characterization. Only 3 publications exist (status: VERY DARK), with the most recent revealing mechanistic involvement in ferroptosis regulation through acetylation. The tubulin alpha derivation suggests potential microtubule-interacting capacity, while the 22q11.21 location warrants investigation into contributions to DiGeorge syndrome phenotypes and immune dysregulation. This represents a paradigm example of 'functional pseudogenes' producing disease-relevant microproteins from annotated lncRNAs.
