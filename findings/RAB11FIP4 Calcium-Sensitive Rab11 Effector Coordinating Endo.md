## Description
RAB11FIP4 (Rab11 family-interacting protein 4, UniProt Q86YS3) is a 637 aa intracellular protein featuring an N-terminal EF-hand calcium-binding domain (IPR002048, positions 49-84) and a C-terminal Rab-binding domain FIP-RBD (IPR019018, positions 574-636). The central region contains a Rab11-FIP3-like domain (PF25450, positions 365-487). AlphaFold predicts a confident structure (pLDDT 70.7). HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression highest in brain (52.7 nTPM) and testis (47.0 nTPM), with subcellular localization to Golgi apparatus, vesicles, and sperm flagellar components (connecting piece, mid piece, principal piece). STRING reveals 10 interaction partners including RAB11FIP2 (0.962), RAB11FIP1 (0.934), RAB11A (0.911), RAB11FIP3 (0.896), and RAB25 (0.852), placing it in the Rab11 effector network. ClinVar contains 54 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses at 17q11.2. Functionally, RAB11FIP4 bridges recycling endosomes to the cleavage furrow during late cytokinesis via Rab11 binding, while the EF-hand domain suggests calcium-regulated membrane trafficking. Hypoxia-induced upregulation enhances hepatocellular carcinoma metastasis, implicating this protein in cancer progression through endosomal remodeling.
