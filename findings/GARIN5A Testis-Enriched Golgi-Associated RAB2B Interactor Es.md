# GARIN5A: Testis-Enriched Golgi-Associated RAB2B Interactor Essential for Sperm Head Morphogenesis and Acrosome Biogenesis via Rab2B-Mediated Vesicular Trafficking

**Date:** 2026-06-14T18:28:42.343964

**Quality Score:** 9/10 (EXCELLENT)

**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Description
GARIN5A (Q6IPT2) encodes a 247 amino acid Golgi-associated regulatory protein that functions as a specific interactor of RAB2B, a small GTPase controlling vesicular trafficking between the endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus. InterPro analysis reveals a modular domain architecture centered on a C-terminal Rab2B-binding domain (IPR022168/PF12480, positions 180-235) that directly engages active RAB2B-GTP, embedded within the broader Golgi-associated RAB2 interactor family (PTHR22574, positions 64-245); this domain organization positions GARIN5A as a RAB effector rather than GEF or GAP. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured protein (pLDDT 64.6, medium confidence) consistent with flexible linker regions enabling conformational adaptation during RAB2B binding and Golgi membrane association. STRING interactions returned no partners for human GARIN5A, reflecting its dark gene status and limited characterization, though the InterPro domain architecture strongly predicts RAB2B as the primary interaction partner. HPA shows testis-enriched expression (68.4 nTPM) with detection in many tissues, nucleoli fibrillar center localization suggesting potential roles in ribosome biogenesis or RNA processing in addition to Golgi functions. ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including multiple copy number gains spanning chromosome 19q13.33-13.43 regions (e.g., chr19:48905537-51614930 duplications, chr19:49625130-57647352 duplications), though specific monogenic disease associations remain undefined. Literature (only 1 paper from 2024) demonstrates that the Golgi-associated RAB2 interactor protein family contributes to murine male fertility by ensuring correct morphogenesis of sperm heads; the acrosome, a cap-like structure derived from the Golgi apparatus located anterior to the sperm nucleus, requires proper RAB2-dependent vesicular trafficking for formation. The mechanistic hypothesis: GARIN5A functions as a RAB2B effector that tethers RAB2B-positive vesicles to Golgi membranes during spermatid differentiation, enabling delivery of acrosomal enzymes and membrane components required for sperm head shaping; the testis-enriched expression pattern reflects specialized demand for acrosome biogenesis during spermiogenesis; nucleoli localization suggests potential moonlighting functions in ribosome assembly or rRNA processing that may support the intense protein synthesis demands of spermatogenesis; copy number gains on 19q13.33-13.43 may cause GARIN5A overexpression disrupting stoichiometric balance with RAB2B and other trafficking components, potentially contributing to male infertility phenotypes; the Rab2B-binding domain (positions 180-235) likely adopts an alpha-helical bundle structure that recognizes switch regions of GTP-bound RAB2B, analogous to other RAB effectors; partial disorder (pLDDT 64.6) enables GARIN5A to function as a flexible tether rather than rigid scaffold.
