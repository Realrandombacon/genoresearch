## Description
LOC105374194 is an uncharacterized genetic locus on chromosome 3q26.1 (NC_000003.12, positions 166898682-166931823) that remains poorly defined at the protein level. No UniProt accession exists, and both HPA expression and STRING interaction databases failed to resolve this gene, indicating absence of well-characterized protein product data. However, ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number gain variants spanning the 3q25-29 region (accessions: 155627, 154213, 152257, 150609, 150519, 145614, 57993, 57984, 57983, 57982), all with uncertain significance, suggesting this locus may be dosage-sensitive. PubMed literature (PMID:34417961) associates this locus with serum mineral element homeostasis (copper, zinc, calcium, magnesium, iron) in Chinese Han populations through genome-wide association studies. The chromosomal location at 3q26.1 places it near known oncogenic regions (EVI1/MECOM locus at 3q26), raising possibility of regulatory or structural RNA function. Without protein-coding evidence, this may represent a non-coding regulatory element, pseudogene, or genuinely dark protein-coding gene awaiting characterization. Functional hypothesis: LOC105374194 may encode a small regulatory peptide or long non-coding RNA involved in mineral ion transport or homeostasis, with copy number variations disrupting dosage-sensitive regulatory networks affecting trace element metabolism.
