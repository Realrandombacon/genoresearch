## Description
AGR2 (Anterior Gradient Protein 2) is a 175 amino acid endoplasmic reticulum-resident protein disulfide isomerase (PDI) family member that catalyzes disulfide bond formation and isomerization in secretory pathway proteins, serving as a critical regulator of mucin production, epithelial integrity, and cancer progression. InterPro analysis identifies canonical thioredoxin-fold architecture including AGR domain (cd02960, residues 47-175) characteristic of anterior gradient proteins, thioredoxin-like fold (IPR012336, PF13899, residues 54-133) with redox-active cysteine motifs for disulfide exchange, glutaredoxin superfamily homology (G3DSA:3.40.30.10, residues 37-175), and Anterior Gradient and Thioredoxin Domain family signature (IPR051099, PTHR15337, residues 2-171). AlphaFold structure (AF-O95994-F1) predicts high confidence (pLDDT 86.2) across the 175 residue sequence, indicating a well-folded oxidoreductase with stable thioredoxin core suitable for substrate protein binding and disulfide catalysis. HPA data classifies AGR2 as a disease-related proto-oncogene and intracellular protein involved in protein folding, with endoplasmic reticulum localization, tissue-enhanced expression in gastrointestinal and reproductive mucosa (stomach: 1323.9 nTPM, intestine: 1148.3 nTPM, cervix: 600.4 nTPM), cell type-enhanced distribution, and implications in inflammatory bowel disease and cancer. STRING interactions reveal functional coupling to mucosal secretion and hormone signaling: DAG1 (Dystroglycan 1, 0.885) for basement membrane anchoring; TFF3 (Trefoil factor 3, 0.799) and TFF1 (0.482) for mucosal protection; mucins MUC5AC (0.797) and MUC5B (0.726) as substrate clients; ESR1 (Estrogen receptor alpha, 0.772) and FOXA1 (Forkhead box A1, 0.709) for transcriptional regulation in breast cancer; PDIA3 (Protein disulfide isomerase A3, 0.698) as fellow PDI family member; SPDEF (SAM pointed domain containing ETS factor, 0.605) for goblet cell differentiation; and LYPD3 (LY6/PLAUR domain containing 3, 0.510) for epithelial barrier function. ClinVar lists 47 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including frameshift deletions (p.His83fs, p.Asp35fs, p.Asp32fs), nonsense mutations (p.Trp54Ter), splice site variants (c.257-1G>C, c.256+2T>C), and large copy number gains/losses on chromosome 7p, though no specific monogenic syndrome is defined, suggesting roles in cancer susceptibility or inflammatory disease. Literature (858 papers) establishes AGR2 as a multifunctional oncogenic driver: mediates pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) progression through AGR2/IGF1 paracrine signaling with cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs); human cytomegalovirus UL82 upregulates AGR2 promoting colorectal cancer cell cycle progression; AGR2 controls antiviral immune response by promoting thiol oxidation of TRAF3, inhibiting interferon production; functions as an ER stress sensor regulating protein synthesis fidelity; serves as a predictive biomarker for endocrine therapy resistance in breast cancer; and extracellular eAGR2 promotes metastasis through integrin-mediated signaling. As a well-studied proto-oncogene with established roles in mucosal biology and cancer, AGR2 represents a critical node connecting protein folding quality control to epithelial homeostasis, immune modulation, and tumor progression through redox-dependent regulation of secretory pathway clients and cytokine signaling.
