## Description
LOC105447645 is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 19q13.33 (NC_000019.10, positions 48703847-48702449, ~1.4 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas could not resolve the gene to an Ensembl ID, indicating no detectable protein expression across any tissue. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions. However, ClinVar contains 4 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 single nucleotide variant (variant 225366: NM_000511.6(FUT2):c.604C>T, p.Arg202Ter, nonsense mutation in FUT2 gene causing secretor deficiency) and 3 copy number gains (variant 59117: 19q13.33-13.43, chr19:47929575-58572206, ~10.6 Mb; variant 59116: 19q13.33-13.43, chr19:47908540-58539965, ~10.6 Mb; variant 59115: 19q13.32-13.33, chr19:46658791-49050450, ~2.4 Mb). All variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 19q13.33 chromosomal region contains critical disease-associated genes including BAX (BCL2 associated X, apoptosis regulator, mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization, cytochrome c release, tumor suppressor, chemosensitivity), KLK3 (kallikrein related peptidase 3/PSA, prostate-specific antigen, serine protease, semen liquefaction, prostate cancer biomarker), FGF21 (fibroblast growth factor 21, metabolic hormone, glucose homeostasis, ketogenesis, lipolysis, insulin sensitivity, obesity), IRF3 (interferon regulatory factor 3, type I interferon induction, antiviral immunity, TBK1/IKKepsilon phosphorylation, innate immune response), and PRMT1 (protein arginine methyltransferase 1, histone H4R3 methylation, transcriptional coactivation, RNA splicing, cancer epigenetics). Functional hypothesis: LOC105447645 likely represents a critical regulatory element within the 19q13.33 dosage-sensitive region, potentially functioning as a metabolic-immune enhancer coordinating FGF21 expression in hepatocytes and adipocytes (glucose homeostasis, ketogenesis) or as a chromatin boundary separating apoptosis genes (BAX) from prostate-specific genes (KLK3). The FUT2 c.604C>T (p.Arg202Ter) nonsense variant is particularly significant as FUT2 encodes alpha(1,2)fucosyltransferase responsible for ABO blood group antigen secretion in bodily fluids, determining secretor status (Se/se polymorphism), gut microbiome composition (Bifidobacterium abundance), and norovirus susceptibility (non-secretors resistant to common norovirus strains). The 3 pathogenic CNVs indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts multiple gene expression programs. Disease mechanism involves BAX dysregulation altering apoptotic threshold (cancer chemoresistance), KLK3/PSA elevation indicating prostate pathology, FGF21 deficiency causing metabolic syndrome (obesity, insulin resistance), IRF3 impairment reducing antiviral immunity, PRMT1 overexpression driving oncogenic epigenetic programs, and FUT2 deficiency causing secretor-negative phenotype (altered microbiome, norovirus resistance, Crohn disease susceptibility, type 1 diabetes risk). The 19q13.33 duplication syndromes may present with metabolic abnormalities, prostate disorders, immune dysfunction, and cancer predisposition. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (4 variants including functional FUT2 SNP) and BAX-KLK3-FGF21-IRF3 gene cluster suggests this locus functions as a tissue-specific enhancer or topologically associating domain (TAD) boundary critical for apoptosis-prostate-metabolism-immunity coordination.
