## Description
POLR3H (RNA polymerase III subunit H, also known as RPC8, UniProt Q9Y535) is a 204 aa essential subunit of RNA polymerase III, the enzyme responsible for transcribing small non-coding RNAs including tRNAs, 5S rRNA, and U6 snRNA. InterPro analysis reveals a bipartite domain architecture characteristic of RNA polymerase subunits: an N-terminal SHS2 domain (IPR005576, PF03876, positions 9-64) within the RNA polymerase Rpb7-like N-terminal domain superfamily (IPR036898, SSF88798, positions 1-80), and a C-terminal OB-fold nucleic acid-binding domain (IPR012340, G3DSA:2.40.50.140, SSF50249, positions 79-202) within the RNA polymerase III subunit Rpc25 family (IPR013238, PF08292, positions 83-201). The protein belongs to the DNA-directed RNA polymerase subunit E/RPC8 family (IPR004519, TIGR00448, PTHR12709, positions 1-164/189) with GO annotations for DNA binding, DNA-directed RNA polymerase activity, and transcription initiation. AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 88.0), indicating a well-folded protein with stable OB-fold geometry for nucleic acid recognition. HPA data confirms ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues, low tissue specificity) with nucleoplasm, vesicle, and centrosome localization, classified as an essential protein involved in antiviral defense, innate immunity, and transcription. STRING interactions reveal exclusive clustering with RNA polymerase subunits at maximum confidence: POLR3A (0.999, catalytic core), POLR3B (0.999, second largest subunit), POLR3F (0.999), POLR3C (0.998), POLR3D (0.999), POLR3E (0.994), CRCP (0.999, RPC9 subunit), POLR1D (0.999, shared Pol I/III subunit), POLR2F (0.999, Pol II subunit), POLR2L (0.992). ClinVar contains 67 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants. Literature (16 papers) establishes POLR3H roles in: (1) primary ovarian insufficiency (POI) via exome sequencing identifying POLR3H as a novel causative gene (2019, 34 citations), (2) POLR3-related disorders (POLR3-RDs) including hypomyelinating leukodystrophy, and (3) antiviral defense through Pol III-mediated cytosolic DNA sensing. FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: POLR3H serves as a structural and regulatory subunit stabilizing the Pol III complex and facilitating nucleic acid binding during transcription initiation, with tissue-specific requirements in ovarian follicle development. MECHANISTIC PROPOSAL: The OB-fold domain binds single-stranded DNA/RNA during transcription bubble formation, while the N-terminal SHS2 domain mediates heterodimerization with POLR3G/RPOLR3GL subunits. POLR3H dysfunction impairs tRNA/5S rRNA synthesis, causing translational defects in rapidly dividing granulosa cells leading to POI. The centrosome localization suggests potential roles in cell cycle regulation. Pol III also transcribes cytosolic DNA into immunostimulatory RNAs for antiviral defense, explaining the immunity annotations. The 67 ClinVar variants indicate POLR3H is a disease gene with pleiotropic effects across reproductive, neurological, and immune systems.
