## Description
ANKRA2 (Ankyrin repeat family A protein 2) represents a classic 'Clinical Data Gap' candidate. Despite having 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar (primarily copy number gains and losses in the 5p15 and 5q13 regions), there are zero named diseases or specific phenotypes associated with these mutations. This contrasts sharply with its low literature volume (only 23 papers) and its status as a moderate-length human protein (313 aa), suggesting it is a 'dark gene' where functional consequences are known but clinical phenotypes are missing. The gene is involved in cell adhesion and RFX7 complex regulation but lacks a defined human disease syndrome, making it a prime target for discovering novel genetic disorders linked to 5p/5q abnormalities.
