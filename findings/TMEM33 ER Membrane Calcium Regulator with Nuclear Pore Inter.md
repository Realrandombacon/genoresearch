## Description
TMEM33 encodes a 247-amino acid transmembrane protein (UniProt P57088) localized to the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane and nuclear envelope. The protein belongs to the TMEM33/Pom33 family (InterPro IPR005344, PF03661) and functions as a PER33/POM33 transmembrane regulator (IPR051645). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with high confidence (pLDDT 88.4).

FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: TMEM33 acts as a regulator of ER tubular network organization by modulating intracellular calcium homeostasis. Mechanistically, it stimulates PKD2 (polycystin-2) calcium channel activity, linking ER membrane architecture to calcium signaling. The strong interaction with RNF26 (STRING score 0.759) and ENDOD1 (0.722) suggests roles in ER-associated protein quality control and membrane remodeling.

INTERACTION NETWORK: TMEM33 shows high-confidence interactions with nucleoporins NUP133 (0.651) and NUP210 (0.641), connecting ER function to nuclear pore complex assembly. This interaction profile positions TMEM33 at the ER-nuclear envelope interface.

EXPRESSION & DISEASE: HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low tissue specificity, classified as a membrane protein involved in innate immunity. ClinVar documents 17 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly chromosome 4p copy number gains and losses, indicating dosage sensitivity.
