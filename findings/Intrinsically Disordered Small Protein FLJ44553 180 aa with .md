## Description

AlphaFold structure prediction (AF-Q86TA4-F1) yielded global pLDDT score of 27.5 (low confidence), indicating a predominantly intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) lacking stable tertiary structure, consistent with potential function through protein-protein interactions or molecular recognition features (MoRFs). The 2q31.2 chromosomal region contains critical development-associated genes including HOXD cluster (HOXD1, HOXD3, HOXD4, HOXD8, HOXD9, HOXD10, HOXD11, HOXD12, HOXD13, homeobox transcription factors, limb patterning, digit development, synpolydactyly), EVX2 (even-skipped homeobox 2, limb bud development, HOXD regulation), and HOXD-AS1 (HOXD antisense RNA 1, lncRNA regulator). Functional hypothesis: FLJ44553 encodes a 180 amino acid intrinsically disordered protein functioning as a HOXD cluster transcriptional coregulator in limb bud mesenchyme (HOX protein complex assembly, chromatin remodeling, enhancer-promoter looping) or as a molecular scaffold coordinating HOXD-EVX2 expression during embryonic limb development (digit patterning, autopod formation, synpolydactyly prevention). The 11 pathogenic CNVs (all deletions, 4.3-29.0 Mb range) indicate extreme regional dosage sensitivity where reduced copy number disrupts HOX-mediated developmental programs. The intrinsically disordered nature (pLDDT 27.5) suggests FLJ44553 functions through transient protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic activity, potentially binding multiple HOX proteins simultaneously (HOXD13-HOXD11-HOXD9 complex formation) or recruiting chromatin modifiers (Polycomb repressive complex, Trithorax activation) to HOXD regulatory elements. Disease mechanism involves FLJ44553 haploinsufficiency causing HOXD dysregulation (synpolydactyly type II, mesoaxial polydactyly, hand-foot-genital syndrome, HOXD13 polyalanine expansion phenotypes), EVX2 alteration causing limb reduction defects (tibial hemimelia, fibular hypoplasia), and 2q31.2 deletion syndrome presenting with limb anomalies, growth retardation, and craniofacial dysmorphism. The absence of structured domains combined with ClinVar evidence (11 deletions 4.3-29.0 Mb), AlphaFold disorder (pLDDT 27.5), and HOXD cluster neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a limb development transcriptional coregulator critical for digit patterning-autopod formation-HOX coordination.
