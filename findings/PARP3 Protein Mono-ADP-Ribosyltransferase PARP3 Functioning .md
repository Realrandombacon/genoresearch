## Description
PARP3 (Q9Y6F1) encodes a 533 amino acid protein mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase that functions as a critical DNA damage response factor detecting double-strand breaks (DSBs) and initiating repair through non-homologous end joining (NHEJ), while also regulating epigenetic states during differentiation and driving inflammatory signaling in macrophages. InterPro analysis reveals a tripartite domain architecture characteristic of the PARP family: an N-terminal WGR domain (IPR008893/cd08002/G3DSA:2.20.140.10/IPR036930, positions 58-157/43-154) mediating DNA binding and protein-protein interactions essential for damage sensing; a central regulatory domain (IPR004102/G3DSA:1.20.142.10/IPR036616, positions 176-321/182-319) modulating catalytic activity and substrate specificity; and a C-terminal catalytic domain (IPR012317/cd01437/G3DSA:3.90.228.10, positions 313-533/182-532) belonging to the ADP-ribosyltransferase diphtheria toxin-like superfamily (IPR050800) performing mono-ADP-ribosylation using NAD+ as substrate (GO:0003950). AlphaFold predicts a highly confident structure (pLDDT 85.4) consistent with stable multi-domain fold required for precise DNA lesion recognition and enzymatic activation. STRING interactions demonstrate partnerships with DNA repair machinery and cell death regulators: CASP9 (0.914) and CASP7 (0.913), caspases linking DNA damage to apoptosis execution; GZMB (0.903), granzyme B indicating immune-mediated cell death connections; PARG (0.819), poly(ADP-ribose) glycohydrolase reversing ADP-ribosylation marks; PARP10 (0.770) and PARP15 (0.750), other mono-ADP-ribosyltransferases suggesting functional redundancy or complex formation; APLF (0.633), aprataxin and PNK-like factor in NHEJ; XRCC6 (0.610), Ku70 subunit of the Ku heterodimer essential for DSB recognition; PARP16 (0.595), ER-associated ADP-ribosyltransferase; CASP3 (0.478), executioner caspase. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with low tissue specificity detected in all tissues reflecting fundamental role in genome maintenance, nucleoplasmic and nuclear bodies localization consistent with DNA repair foci formation, classified under FDA approved drug targets/metabolic proteins/predicted intracellular proteins with glycosyltransferase/nucleotidyltransferase/transferase molecular functions, involvement in DNA damage/DNA repair biological processes, confirming therapeutic relevance. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including missense mutation in catalytic domain (c.1222C>T p.Arg408Cys) potentially disrupting NAD+ binding or catalysis; splice site variant (c.501+5G>T) affecting proper mRNA processing; and multiple chromosome 3p copy number variations (gains: chr3:2263690-55016039x3, chr3:45153770-53878616, chr3:61892-197851986x3; losses: chr3:51962412-53969609x1, chr3:51149374-59265315x1, chr3:51394434-55064449x1, chr3:49461000-55314500x1) though specific monogenic disease associations remain incompletely annotated beyond emerging links to cancer susceptibility and developmental disorders. Literature (179 papers) defines PARP3 as multifunctional regulator: Parp3 assists muscle function and skeletal muscle differentiation by selectively adjusting H3K27me3 enrichment linking DNA repair machinery to epigenetic reprogramming during myogenesis; PARP3 promotes macrophage inflammation via mono-ADP-ribosylation of Ppia (cyclophilin A) at Glu140 driving acute lung injury pathology; PARP3 promotes acute myeloid leukemia (AML) progression via activation of PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling suggesting oncogenic driver role; PARP3 inhibition enhances cisplatin drug sensitivity by influencing PDGF and G-coupled signal pathways revealing therapeutic vulnerability in resistant tumors. The mechanistic hypothesis: PARP3 functions as a DNA damage sensor where the WGR domain (positions 58-157, pLDDT 85.4) binds directly to DNA double-strand ends recruiting the Ku70/Ku80 heterodimer (XRCC6 score 0.610) to initiate NHEJ while the catalytic domain (positions 313-533) mono-ADP-ribosylates histone H2B and other substrates creating recruitment platforms for repair factors; high-confidence AlphaFold structure indicates rigid domain arrangement enabling allosteric activation upon DNA binding; nucleoplasmic localization ensures rapid response to genomic insults; interaction with caspases (CASP9/7/3 scores >0.9) links unrepaired damage to apoptotic commitment preventing propagation of mutated cells; PARG partnership (score 0.819) enables dynamic turnover of ADP-ribose marks allowing repair completion and signal termination; muscle differentiation role operates through PARP3-mediated regulation of H3K27me3 levels at myogenic gene promoters where mono-ADP-ribosylation of chromatin modifiers alters Polycomb repressive complex activity; macrophage inflammation drives acute lung injury through Ppia Glu140 modification altering cyclophilin A conformation and enhancing pro-inflammatory cytokine production; AML progression involves PARP3-dependent activation of PI3K/AKT/mTOR axis potentially through ADP-ribosylation of pathway inhibitors or receptors; cisplatin resistance reversal occurs because PARP3 inhibition disrupts DNA repair capacity and sensitizes cells to platinum-induced crosslinks while modulating growth factor signaling; pathogenic variants disrupt distinct mechanisms: catalytic mutations (p.Arg408Cys) abolish enzymatic activity preventing repair initiation; splice variants reduce functional protein causing haploinsufficiency; CNVs cause dosage imbalance affecting genome stability thresholds.
