## Description
LOC114004373 is a Sharpr-MPRA regulatory region 7735 located on chromosome 2p16.1-11.2 (genomic coordinates: 71348358-71348652 on NC_000002.12). This 295 bp genomic sequence was predicted as a transcriptional regulatory region based on ENCODE chromatin state analysis and experimentally validated as a functional repressive element (silencer) using Sharpr-MPRA in HepG2 liver carcinoma cells. As a non-coding regulatory element, it lacks protein-coding potential (no UniProt entry, no InterPro domains, no AlphaFold structure, no STRING interactions, no HPA protein expression). ClinVar analysis identified 1 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variant: a copy number gain (triplification) on 2p16.1-11.2, indicating dosage sensitivity where over-dosage disrupts cellular homeostasis. The low variant count (1 vs 5 for LOC114004388, 21 for LOC114004412) suggests lower clinical penetrance or under-ascertainment. The 2p chromosomal region is gene-dense and contains ALK (anaplastic lymphoma kinase at 2p23.2, oncogene in neuroblastoma/lymphoma), MYCN (N-myc proto-oncogene at 2p24.3, amplified in neuroblastoma), and BCL11A (B-cell lymphoma/leukemia 11A at 2p16.2, transcriptional repressor in hemoglobin switching). As a repressive enhancer/silencer, this element likely suppresses expression of one or more target genes in cis within the 2p domain. The pathogenic copy number gain suggests this silencer regulates genes requiring precise repression - over-dosage may cause ectopic over-repression of tumor suppressors or developmental genes. This element may contribute to 2p deletion/duplication syndromes, cancer predisposition (particularly neuroblastoma given MYCN/ALK proximity), or hematologic disorders through dysregulation of critical 2p genes requiring tight transcriptional control.
