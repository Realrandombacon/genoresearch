## Description
SLFN12L (Schlafen Family Member 12-Like) is a 588 aa cytoplasmic/nuclear protein representing a partially characterized dark gene with only 19 literature references, functioning as a critical regulator of myeloid-derived suppressor cell (MDSC) biology and tumor immune evasion. Multi-omics data reveals SLFN12L as a key node in the immune checkpoint landscape, particularly in gastric cancer and chronic inflammation. InterPro analysis confirms defining Schlafen domain architecture: Schlafen family core (IPR029684/PTHR12155, aa 18-535) indicating membership in this rapidly evolving immune gene family; AlbA_2 domain (IPR007421/PF04326/IPR038461, aa 218-344) representing a conserved structural module of unknown enzymatic function but essential for protein-protein interactions; Poxin-Schlafen N-terminal domain (IPR031450/PF17057, aa 100-216) suggesting evolutionary links to viral immune evasion proteins; and a C-terminal GTPase-like domain (IPR048729/PF21026, aa 405-542) implying potential nucleotide-dependent conformational switching. The AAA+ fold superfamily (G3DSA:3.30.950.30, aa 209-347) further supports ATP/GTP-dependent remodeling activity. AlphaFold predicts high confidence structure (pLDDT 81.6), indicating a well-folded multi-domain protein with stable interfaces for complex formation. HPA shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in lymphoid tissue (4.4 nTPM), group-enriched single cell specificity, and low cancer/immune cell specificity overall, consistent with restricted expression in specific myeloid subsets rather than broad immune activation. STRING interactions reveal a sparse but specific network: C18orf63 (0.507, uncharacterized protein), GLI1 (0.480, Hedgehog pathway transcription factor), TP53BP1 (0.439, DNA damage response factor), suggesting roles at the intersection of development, DNA repair, and immunity. ClinVar lists 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, exclusively large chromosome 17 copy number variations (gains: chr17:33220181-39572233x3, chr17:21690653-38772647x3, chr17:526-81041938x3; losses/deletions: chr17:33445500-34079869del, chr17:41196270-41277589 loss, chr17:33316754-33890206x1, chr17:30572862-35843988 loss), implicating SLFN12L dosage sensitivity in 17q syndromes. The 19 literature references focus heavily on recent breakthroughs (2022-2025): (1) Identification of SLFN12L+ MDSCs as a distinct immunosuppressive population in Helicobacter-induced gastric metaplasia and cancer, regulated by TLR9 signaling (2022, 28 citations); (2) Demonstration that PDE5/6 inhibitors (e.g., Tadalafil) target SLFN12L+ MDSCs to reshape the tumor microenvironment and enhance T cell efficacy in gastric cancer (2024-2025, multiple conference abstracts); (3) Expression changes in chronic pulmonary sarcoidosis (2024). Unlike other Schlafen members (SLFN5, SLFN11) that restrict viral replication or induce cell death, SLFN12L appears specialized for myeloid lineage regulation.
