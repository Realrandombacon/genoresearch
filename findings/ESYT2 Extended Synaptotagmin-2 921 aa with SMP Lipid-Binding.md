## Description
ESYT2 encodes Extended Synaptotagmin-2 (UniProt A0FGR8), a 921-amino acid multi-C2 domain membrane protein that functions as an endoplasmic reticulum-plasma membrane (ER-PM) tether mediating non-vesicular lipid transport at membrane contact sites, serving as a critical component of lipid homeostasis machinery with roles in cell migration and stress survival, and 88 pathogenic variants suggesting disease association though no specific Mendelian disorder is named.
