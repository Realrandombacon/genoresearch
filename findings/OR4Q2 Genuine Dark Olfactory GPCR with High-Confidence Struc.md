## Description
OR4Q2 encodes a 314 amino acid olfactory receptor belonging to the class A rhodopsin-like GPCR superfamily, located at chromosome 14q11.2. Despite being annotated as a potential pseudogene (OR4Q2P), InterPro analysis confirms intact canonical olfactory receptor architecture with IPR000276 (GPCR rhodopsin-like), IPR000725 (Olfactory receptor), IPR017452 (7TM), IPR50427 (Human Olfactory Receptors), PF13853 domains, and cd15938 (OR family 4Q) spanning residues 25-291. The AlphaFold predicted structure (P0C623) shows high confidence folding (pLDDT=87.8), indicating a stable 7-transmembrane helix bundle that strongly argues against true pseudogenization. STRING found no interaction partners, consistent with orphan olfactory receptors functioning as solitary sensory detectors coupled to Golf G-proteins. HPA has no expression data, reflecting the extreme tissue restriction to nasal olfactory epithelium not captured in standard panels. Critically, ClinVar documents 2 pathogenic copy number variants: one gain (accession 59932, chr14:20000611-38984415 duplication) and one loss (accession 57743, chr14:19755249-22741281 deletion), implying significant dosage sensitivity and potential clinical relevance in genomic disorders. Orthologs exist in cattle (Bos taurus, 313 aa), white-faced capuchin (Cebus imitator, 307 aa), and golden snub-nosed monkey (Rhinopithecus roxellana, 307 aa), demonstrating strong primate and mammalian conservation. With only 1 indirect publication (sheep CNV study), OR4Q2 represents a genuine dark gene with structural integrity and emerging clinical significance.
