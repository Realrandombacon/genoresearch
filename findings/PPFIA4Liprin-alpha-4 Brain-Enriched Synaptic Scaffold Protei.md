# PPFIA4/Liprin-alpha-4: Brain-Enriched Synaptic Scaffold Protein Organizing Active Zone Assembly Through SAM Domain-Mediated Protein Networks, Cancer Metabolism Regulator via PFKFB3 Glycolytic Control, and Atrial Fibrillation Risk Locus with Clinical Variant Burden

## Description
PPFIA4 (PTPRF Interacting Protein Alpha 4, also known as Liprin-alpha-4, UniProt O75335) is a 1185-amino acid scaffold protein belonging to the liprin-alpha family that organizes protein complexes at presynaptic active zones and regulates synaptic vesicle exocytosis through multi-domain protein-protein interaction networks. The protein architecture features three C-terminal Sterile Alpha Motif (SAM) domains (IPR001660/PF00536 repeat 1 aa 826-895, repeat 2 aa 941-1008, repeat 3 aa 1029-1101) mediating homo- and hetero-oligomerization critical for scaffold assembly, complete Liprin family membership (IPR029515 aa 30-1130), Liprin-alpha SAM domain subtypes (IPR037620/21/22 for each repeat), coiled-coil CC2 domain (IPR057892 aa 226-477) enabling dimerization and partner recruitment, SAM/Pointed domain superfamily architecture (IPR013761 aa 796-928/942-1021/1022-1117), and GO term annotations for protein binding (GO:0005515). The AlphaFold predicted structure shows medium confidence (pLDDT 65.8), indicating a partially structured protein with ordered SAM domain cores embedded in flexible disordered regions—a feature consistent with scaffold proteins requiring conformational plasticity for dynamic partner recruitment and phase separation at synaptic membranes. STRING interaction network reveals high-confidence associations to synaptic organizing complexes: PPFIBP1 (0.813, liprin-beta-1 synaptic scaffold), PTPRS (0.694, receptor-type tyrosine-protein phosphatase S presynaptic receptor), RIMS1 (0.684, regulating synaptic membrane exocytosis protein 1 active zone organizer), PPFIBP2 (0.670, liprin-beta-2 paralog), LIN7A (0.666, lin-7 homolog A polarity complex component), RAB3A (0.569, Ras-related protein 3A synaptic vesicle GTPase), TSPOAP1 (0.557, translocator peripheral benzodiazepine receptor associated protein 1), LIN7B (0.555, lin-7 homolog B), GIT1 (0.533, G protein-coupled receptor kinase interactor 1 ArfGAP), and PTPRF (0.450, receptor-type tyrosine-protein phosphatase F). HPA expression profiles demonstrate tissue-enriched expression with brain as top tissue (96.8 nTPM), detected in many tissues consistent with ubiquitous scaffold functions, cancer-enhanced expression indicating upregulation in tumors suggesting metabolic reprogramming role, plasma proteins and predicted intracellular proteins classification, not detected in immune cells, and cell type-enhanced single cell type specificity. ClinVar records document 21 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number gains spanning 1q25.3-32.1 (chr1:180800361-203181850)x3, 1q21.1-44 (chr1:143932350-249224684)x3, 1q32.1 (chr1:199373229-204335027)x3, copy number loss at 1q31.3-32.1 (chr1:197216705-203683110)x1, deletion NC_000001.10:g.(?_190829412)_(216061974_?)del, duplication NC_000001.10:g.(?_200522516)_(208391267_?)dup, missense variant (c.1680C>A p.Asp560Glu in coiled-coil region potentially disrupting dimerization), intronic variants (c.3393+34G>T, c.1620+282C>T), and microsatellite variation (c.3575-18TC[5]), though no specific monogenic syndrome has been definitively assigned; notably, 2025 literature identified SNP rs3737883 in PPFIA4 associated with atrial fibrillation risk in Chinese population establishing cardiac arrhythmia susceptibility locus. Literature analysis (46 papers, confirming partially characterized status with emerging disease roles) reveals: 2025 studies identified PPFIA4 knockdown inhibiting hepatocellular carcinoma growth and glycolysis via PFKFB3 (phosphofructokinase-3 regulatory subunit), PPFIA4 mediating glucose metabolism reprogramming altering breast cancer biological characteristics through Warburg effect modulation, SNP rs3737883 association with atrial fibrillation risk, and 2024 study demonstrating enterotoxin-related genes PPFIA4 and SCN3B promoting colorectal cancer development and progression. We hypothesize that PPFIA4 functions through a multi-domain scaffold mechanism integrating synaptic organization with metabolic regulation: (1) The three tandem SAM domains (aa 826-1101) form polymeric networks through head-to-tail oligomerization creating higher-order scaffold assemblies at presynaptic membranes similar to other liprins; (2) Medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 65.8) suggests flexible linker regions between structured SAM domains enabling conformational adaptability for diverse partner binding; (3) High-confidence PPFIBP1 interaction (0.813) indicates heteromeric liprin-alpha/beta complexes forming core synaptic scaffold analogous to neuromuscular junction organization; (4) PTPRS/PTPRF associations (0.450-0.694) place PPFIA4 downstream of receptor tyrosine phosphatases linking extracellular adhesion cues to intracellular scaffold assembly; (5) RIMS1 interaction (0.684) positions PPFIA4 within active zone cytomatrix coordinating calcium channel clustering and vesicle docking; (6) RAB3A association (0.569) connects scaffold to synaptic vesicle cycle through GTPase-dependent vesicle tethering; (7) LIN7A/LIN7B interactions (0.555-0.666) implicate PPFIA4 in cell polarity complexes potentially regulating asymmetric protein distribution in neurons; (8) GIT1 association (0.533) links scaffold to ArfGAP activity controlling membrane trafficking and focal adhesion turnover; (9) Brain-enriched expression (96.8 nTPM) reflects heightened requirement for synaptic scaffolding in neuronal communication where precise active zone assembly determines neurotransmission fidelity; (10) Cancer-enhanced expression with glycolysis regulation via PFKFB3 suggests non-canonical metabolic function where PPFIA4 scaffolds glycolytic enzymes at membrane compartments enhancing Warburg effect in tumors; (11) Hepatocellular carcinoma and breast cancer studies indicate PPFIA4 promotes tumor growth through metabolic reprogramming rather than solely synaptic functions; (12) Colorectal cancer progression via enterotoxin-related pathway suggests PPFIA4 responds to microbial signals modulating epithelial proliferation; (13) Atrial fibrillation SNP rs3737883 association indicates cardiac conduction system requiring PPFIA4-mediated scaffold organization at cardiomyocyte intercalated discs analogous to neuronal synapses; (14) Clinical CNV burden without defined syndrome suggests dosage sensitivity where both haploinsufficiency and triplosensitivity disrupt scaffold stoichiometry affecting synaptic/metabolic/cardiac functions; (15) Coiled-coil CC2 domain (aa 226-477) likely mediates homodimerization essential for scaffold nucleation; (16) Missense variant p.Asp560Glu in coiled-coil region may impair dimerization reducing scaffold assembly efficiency; (17) Large CNVs spanning megabase regions suggest PPFIA4 dosage alterations contribute to genomic disorder phenotypes through combined gene effects. Therapeutic strategies could target PPFIA4-mediated glycolytic scaffolding in cancer through small molecules disrupting PFKFB3-PPFIA4 interaction, while antisense oligonucleotides correcting rs3737883 risk allele effects might reduce atrial fibrillation susceptibility, and synaptic scaffold stabilizers could treat neurological disorders involving active zone dysfunction.
