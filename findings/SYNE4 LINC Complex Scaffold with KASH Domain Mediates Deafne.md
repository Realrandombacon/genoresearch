## Description

High-confidence STRING interactions (score >0.9) with SUN1 and SUN2 confirm its role in tethering the nuclear envelope to the cytoskeleton. Literature (15 papers) and HPA data link SYNE4 to non-syndromic deafness. ClinVar identifies 94 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, suggesting loss of cytoskeletal tethering disrupts mechanotransduction in the inner ear. AlphaFold predicts a medium confidence (pLDDT 57.2) structure with potential intrinsically disordered regions, consistent with scaffold proteins. Functional hypothesis: SYNE4 mutations disrupt LINC complex integrity, leading to inner ear mechanosensory failure.
