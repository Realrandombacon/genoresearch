# ACSM2B: Medium-Chain Acyl-CoA Synthetase

## Description
ACSM2B encodes a medium-chain acyl-CoA synthetase involved in fatty acid metabolism. It interacts with ACSM2A, HSDL2, and GLYAT, and is expressed in various tissues, including the liver, kidney, and skeletal muscle. The protein contains an AMP-dependent synthetase/ligase domain and an AMP-binding site. Pathogenic variants include copy number gains and missense mutations associated with various diseases.
