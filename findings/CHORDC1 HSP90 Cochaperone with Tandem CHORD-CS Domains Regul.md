## Description
CHORDC1 encodes a 332 aa cysteine and histidine-rich domain-containing protein (UniProt Q9UHD1) functioning as a specialized HSP90 cochaperone with critical roles in extracellular vesicle secretion, tau protein regulation, and vascular homeostasis. InterPro analysis confirms its canonical cochaperone architecture: tandem CHORD domains (IPR007051/PF04968, aa 4-64 and 155-216) for client protein binding, CS domain (IPR007052/PF04969, aa 227-316) for HSP90 interaction, and HSP20-like chaperone superfamily fold (IPR008978/SSF49764, aa 211-332). AlphaFold predicts high-confidence structure (pLDDT 81.8), indicating a stable, well-folded cochaperone capable of precise client recognition. STRING interactions reveal a dedicated HSP90 chaperone network: HSP90AA1 (0.949, HSP90-alpha, primary partner), HSP90AB1 (0.942, HSP90-beta), SUGT1 (0.729, suppressor of G2 allele), TTC4 (0.672, tetratricopeptide repeat protein), PFDN4 (0.634, prefoldin-4, miR-26a target), HSPH1 (0.666), and AHSA1 (0.610, AHA1 activator). HPA expression data unavailable. ClinVar reports 16 pathogenic variants, predominantly large 11q14.1-22.1 copy number losses (x6) and a nonsense mutation (p.Lys190Ter, aa 190) truncating the CS domain, suggesting haploinsufficiency and loss-of-function mechanisms. Literature (39 papers) confirms emerging roles: (1) Extracellular Vesicle Secretion - miR-26a targets CHORDC1 to regulate EV secretion in prostate cancer (47 citations); (2) Tau Protein Interaction - novel interacting partner of tau in Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis; (3) Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm - circRNA Chordc1 protects mice by contributing to vascular smooth muscle cell phenotype and growth (32 citations); (4) Cancer Progression - PFDN4/CHORDC1 axis in prostate cancer. Functional hypothesis: CHORDC1 functions through: (1) HSP90 Cochaperone Activity - CS domain binding HSP90 to facilitate client protein folding and stabilization; (2) CHORD Domain Client Recognition - tandem CHORD domains binding specific client proteins (tau, signaling kinases); (3) EV Secretion Regulation - modulating vesicle trafficking and exosome release in cancer cells; (4) Vascular Protection - maintaining VSMC contractile phenotype to prevent aneurysm formation. Disease implications include Prostate Cancer, Alzheimer's Disease, Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm, and HSP90-related proteostasis disorders. Despite high structural confidence and clear cochaperone mechanism, the full client protein repertoire and tissue-specific functions remain partially unexplored.
