## Description
CNTN3 (Contactin-3, Q9P232), also known as F3 contactin homolog 3, encodes a 1028 aa glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored cell surface receptor functioning as a neural cell adhesion molecule that mediates axon guidance, neuronal migration, and cell-cell recognition through homophilic and heterophilic interactions with partner receptors.
