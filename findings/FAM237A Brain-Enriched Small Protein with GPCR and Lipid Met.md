## Description

The protein is classified as intracellular but gene ontology predicts extracellular localization. STRING interactions reveal associations with ACSBG2 (acyl-CoA synthetase bubblegum family member 2, score 0.473), ADGRB1 (adhesion GPCR B1, score 0.428), and GPR83 (G protein-coupled receptor 83, score 0.418), suggesting potential roles in lipid metabolism and GPCR signaling pathways. ClinVar contains 10 pathogenic variants all representing large 2q33-34 chromosomal CNVs (megabase-scale deletions/duplications), confirming dosage sensitivity of this genomic region. The FAM237 domain is vertebrate-specific with no characterized molecular activity. FAM237A likely functions as a brain-specific regulatory scaffold protein that modulates GPCR signaling (ADGRB1, GPR83) and fatty acid activation (ACSBG2) in neurons, potentially linking lipid metabolism to neuropeptide signaling through protein-protein interactions.
