# VEGFB: Heparin-Binding Growth Factor Coordinating Metabolic Homeostasis Through VEGFR-1 Signaling in Cardiac Lipid Uptake, Insulin Sensitivity, and Diabetic Complications

## Description
VEGFB (207 aa, UniProt P49765), also known as vascular endothelial growth factor B or VEGF-related factor (VRF), is a heparin-binding growth factor of the PDGF/VEGF family that functions as a selective ligand for VEGFR-1 (FLT1) and neuropilin-1 (NRP1), regulating endothelial cell survival, cardiac lipid metabolism, and insulin sensitivity distinct from the angiogenic VEGFA pathway. The protein architecture features a canonical PDGF/VEGF domain (IPR000072/PF00341, aa34-129) containing the characteristic cystine-knot cytokine fold (G3DSA:2.10.90.10/IPR029034, aa31-139) stabilized by six conserved cysteine residues forming three intrachain disulfide bonds; the PDGF family signature motif spans aa70-82 (IPR023581/PS00249) with critical residues for receptor binding specificity. The full PDGF/VEGF growth factor family signature spans aa2-139 (IPR050507/PTHR12025). HPA confirms low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues, immune cell enrichment in blood cells, classification as a cancer-related gene and FDA-approved drug target with growth factor, heparin-binding, and mitogen molecular functions. Mechanistically, VEGFB forms ultra-high-confidence interactions within the VEGF signaling network: FLT1/VEGFR-1 (STRING 0.999) serves as the primary high-affinity receptor mediating VEGFB signaling; KDR/VEGFR-2 (0.994) represents the major angiogenic receptor with weaker VEGFB binding; VEGFC (0.971) and VEGFD (0.790) are paralogous lymphangiogenic factors; PGF/placental growth factor (0.964) is another VEGFR-1 selective ligand functioning cooperatively with VEGFB; ADGRL1/ELTD1 (0.969), ADGRL2 (0.969), and ADGRL3 (0.969) are adhesion GPCRs linked to angiogenesis; NRP1 (0.849) serves as co-receptor enhancing VEGFB-FLT1 binding affinity; FLT4/VEGFR-3 (0.789) is the lymphatic endothelial receptor. AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 73.5) reveals high-confidence cystine-knot structure with well-defined beta-sheet core and characteristic loop regions containing receptor-binding determinants consistent with crystal structures of VEGF family members showing dimeric arrangement. Clinical significance includes 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic ClinVar variants: large copy number gains spanning 11q12.1-13.5 (chr11:56895955-69295402 amplifications), 11q12.2-13.5 gains, deletions at 11q12.3-13.1 (chr11:62893226-64335043 losses), and intronic variants (c.410+83C>T, c.374+74C>G); VEGFB polymorphisms associate with diabetic nephropathy susceptibility and coronary artery disease risk. Conservation across mammals (mouse P49766 207 aa, rat O35485 207 aa, bovine Q9XS49 207 aa) indicates essential metabolic function maintained over ~90 million years. Literature (499 papers) establishes VEGFB in metabolic disease and cancer: 2024 studies demonstrate VEGFB ameliorates insulin resistance in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) through PI3K/AKT signaling pathway activation; 2025 research shows growth hormone-VEGFB/VEGFA axis determines liver graft suitability from brain-dead donors with alcohol-associated liver disease; gemcitabine inhibits ovarian cancer progression by suppressing HIF-1α/VEGFB/FGF2/FGFR1 signaling cascade; VEGFB emerges as therapeutic target for diabetes and diabetic complications including nephropathy. The functional hypothesis posits that VEGFB acts as a 'metabolic survival factor distinct from angiogenic VEGFA': secreted as disulfide-linked homodimers that bind VEGFR-1 with high affinity but unlike VEGFA does not induce vascular permeability or robust angiogenesis; in cardiac muscle, VEGFR-1 signaling upregulates fatty acid transport proteins (CD36, FABP3) enhancing myocardial lipid uptake preventing lipotoxicity; in liver, VEGFB-PI3K/AKT activation improves insulin receptor substrate phosphorylation restoring glucose homeostasis in NAFLD; in diabetic nephropathy, VEGFB protects podocytes from hyperglycemia-induced apoptosis through VEGFR-1-mediated survival signaling; in cancer, tumor-derived VEGFB promotes metastasis through lymphatic vessel remodeling via VEGFR-1/NRP1 complexes on tumor-associated macrophages; therapeutic strategies include recombinant VEGFB for cardiac protection in heart failure, VEGFB mimetics for diabetic complications, and VEGFB-neutralizing antibodies for cancer metastasis prevention.
