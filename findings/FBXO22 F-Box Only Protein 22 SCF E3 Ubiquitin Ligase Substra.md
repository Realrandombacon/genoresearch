## Description
FBXO22 (F-Box Only Protein 22, UniProt Q8NEZ5) is a 403 amino acid intracellular protein with moderate characterization (114 papers) that functions as a substrate recognition subunit of SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box protein) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes, targeting specific proteins including KDM4A/B histone demethylases, BACH1 transcriptional repressor, and NFKBIA (IκBα) for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation to regulate epigenetic remodeling, oxidative stress response, and NF-κB signaling. The protein contains an F-box domain (IPR001810/PF00646/cd22097 residues 23-70/28-61, GO:0005515 protein binding) for binding SKP1 and assembling the SCF complex, and a FIST C-domain (IPR019494/PF10442/SM01204 residues 232-366/299-364) of unknown function potentially involved in substrate recognition, belonging to the SCF-type ubiquitin ligase substrate recognizer family (PTHR14939 residues 14-402).
