## Description
ORMDL3 (ORM1-like protein 3) is a small (153 aa), well-folded (AlphaFold pLDDT 94.0) protein localized to the Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER) membrane. It belongs to the ORMDL family and functions as a critical regulator of sphingolipid biosynthesis via the Serine Palmitoyltransferase (SPT) complex. Recent research identifies ORMDL3 as a key negative regulator of Type I Interferon signaling by promoting the degradation of the sensor protein RIG-I. The high structural confidence suggests ORMDL3 acts as a stable scaffold or chaperone that facilitates RIG-I ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation, thereby dampening innate immune responses. Dysregulation of this pathway is implicated in asthma, COPD, and metabolic disorders like Hashimoto's thyroiditis, positioning ORMDL3 as a nexus between lipid metabolism, ER stress, and immune homeostasis.
