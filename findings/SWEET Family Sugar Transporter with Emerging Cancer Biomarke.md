# SWEET Family Sugar Transporter with Emerging Cancer Biomarker Roles

## Description
SLC50A1 encodes a 221 aa Sugar transporter SWEET1 (UniProt Q9BRV3), a member of the SWEET (Sugars Will Eventually be Exported Transporter) family. InterPro analysis reveals canonical SWEET architecture with two SWEET repeat domains (IPR004316, PF03083, aa 10-95 and 127-212) forming a 7-transmembrane helix structure characteristic of sugar efflux transporters. AlphaFold predicts a very high-confidence structure (pLDDT 90.3), indicating a well-folded, stable transporter. HPA shows ubiquitous expression with epididymis enrichment (95.6 nTPM) and plasma membrane localization. STRING interactions reveal connections to other sugar transporters (SLC2A6 0.452, SLC2A13 0.449, SLC2A12 0.417), signaling adaptors (SHC2 0.698, EFNA1 0.504), and ribosomal proteins (RPS5 0.491, RPS25 0.451), suggesting potential roles in nutrient sensing and growth signaling. ClinVar contains 15 pathogenic variants (mostly CNVs on 1q21.1-q23.1), though no specific disease associations are established. Literature (13 papers) indicates emerging roles in cancer metabolism: SLC50A1 inhibits doxorubicin sensitivity in hepatocellular carcinoma by regulating tumor glycolysis (6 citations), serves as a diagnostic/prognostic biomarker in early breast cancer (11 citations), and may function in serum-based cancer detection. The SWEET family mediates bidirectional sugar transport across membranes, and SLC50A1 likely facilitates glucose/fructose efflux to modulate intracellular sugar pools, influencing glycolytic flux and mTOR signaling in cancer cells. Its epididymis enrichment suggests additional roles in male reproductive metabolism. As a partially characterized transporter with cancer biomarker potential but undefined substrate specificity and regulatory mechanisms, SLC50A1 represents an emerging therapeutic target in oncology.
