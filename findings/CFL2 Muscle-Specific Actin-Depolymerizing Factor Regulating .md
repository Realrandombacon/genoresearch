FUNCTIONAL HYPOTHESIS: CFL2 functions as muscle-specific actin-severing protein that regulates sarcomere actin filament turnover through pH- and phosphorylation-dependent binding to F-actin, with critical roles in muscle contraction, nemaline myopathy pathogenesis, and emerging involvement in diabetic retinopathy and cancer progression.
