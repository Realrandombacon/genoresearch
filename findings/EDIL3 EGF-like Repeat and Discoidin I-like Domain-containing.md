## Description
EDIL3 (EGF-like Repeat and Discoidin I-like Domain-containing Protein 3, also known as Del-1 or Developmental Endothelial Locus-1, UniProt O43854) is a 480 aa secreted matricellular protein belonging to the EGF-like repeat and discoidin domain family that functions as an integrin-binding extracellular matrix regulator controlling cell adhesion, vascular remodeling, immune cell trafficking, and tissue repair through integrin αVβ3 recognition and ERK1/2 signaling modulation.
