## Description
LOC100289230 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 5q21.1 (NC_000005.10:98929133-98931008) spanning approximately 1.9 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas resolved this locus to Ensembl ID ENSG00000248489 but found no detectable protein expression in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, comprising a mix of large deletions (copy number losses: chr5:90079852-103658165, ~13.6 Mb; chr5:91411708-131319563, ~39.9 Mb; chr5:93193104-113287795, ~20.1 Mb; chr5:92899734-119614119, ~26.7 Mb; chr5:90374606-128076423, ~37.7 Mb; chr5:84603580-111435081, ~26.8 Mb; chr5:89081352-104687248, ~15.6 Mb; chr5:87376883-101524443, ~14.1 Mb) and duplications (copy number gains: chr5:96454445-114050905, ~17.6 Mb; chr5:74163186-110809453, ~36.6 Mb). These structural variants encompass the 5q14.3-q23.3 region and are associated with 5q deletion syndrome (5q- syndrome) and 5q duplication syndrome phenotypes including developmental delay, intellectual disability, hypotonia, craniofacial dysmorphism, cardiac defects, and hematologic abnormalities (particularly myelodysplastic syndrome in 5q- syndrome). The 5q21.1 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including APC (adenomatous polyposis coli), a tumor suppressor critical for Wnt signaling and colorectal cancer prevention, and MEF2C (myocyte enhancer factor 2C), a transcription factor essential for neurodevelopment whose haploinsufficiency causes MEF2C-related intellectual disability syndrome. LOC100289230 likely functions as part of the 5q21.1 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number alteration contributes to 5q syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology, topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries, or long-range regulation of dosage-sensitive genes like MEF2C and APC. The absence of protein-coding features combined with a high burden of pathogenic structural variants (10 variants) indicates this locus resides within a critical dosage-sensitive genomic region where both deletions and duplications disrupt neurodevelopmental, hematologic, and cell cycle processes via gene dosage effects and 3D genome reorganization.
