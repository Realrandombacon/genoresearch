# Ankyrin Repeat Scaffold Protein with Mitochondrial Localization and Neural Expression

**Date:** 2026-03-27T11:51:39.655906

**Quality Score:** 4.55/10  (E=8.1, D=1.0) [MODERATE]

## Description
ANKRD34C encodes a 535 amino acid ankyrin repeat domain-containing protein (UniProt: P0C6C1) localized to mitochondria with tissue-enhanced RNA expression in brain (2.6 nTPM). InterPro analysis reveals extensive ankyrin repeat architecture including IPR002110 (ankyrin repeats at positions 10-39, 43-147), IPR036770 (ankyrin repeat superfamily), IPR042637 (AN34A/B/C family), and PF12796 (3 ankyrin repeat copies at 65-141). These repeats mediate protein-protein interactions (GO:0005515). AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 54.7) indicates medium confidence structure with partially ordered ankyrin domains and potentially disordered C-terminal regions. STRING network identifies 10 interaction partners including RASGRF1 (score 0.598, Ras guanine nucleotide release factor involved in neuronal signaling), RALYL (0.494, RNA-binding protein), BEGAIN (0.473, brain-enriched guanylate kinase-associated protein), KCNK9 (0.471, potassium channel implicated in Birk-Barel syndrome), and CTSH (0.454, cathepsin H protease). ClinVar documents 20 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains/losses spanning 15q24-26 and a missense variant c.1462G>T (p.Ala488Ser). Conservation is evident across vertebrates: mouse ortholog Q8BLB8 (534 aa, ~99% identity), cattle Q0II61 (395 aa), and tilapia I3KYS1 (530 aa). The combination of mitochondrial localization, brain expression, ankyrin-mediated protein binding, and interaction with neuronal signaling proteins (RASGRF1, BEGAIN, KCNK9) suggests ANKRD34C functions as a mitochondrial scaffold organizing neuronal signaling complexes. Pathogenic CNVs disrupting this locus may impair mitochondrial organization in neurons, potentially contributing to neurodevelopmental phenotypes observed in 15q25 deletion/duplication syndromes.
