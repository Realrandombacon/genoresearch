## Description
INPPL1 encodes Phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate 5-phosphatase 2, a lipid-modifying enzyme involved in cell migration and signaling. It is clinically significant as the causative gene for Opsismodysplasia, a rare skeletal dysplasia characterized by delayed bone maturation and growth impairment. With 125 literature papers and established roles in lipid metabolism and skeletal development, INPPL1 is definitively a 'well-studied' gene and does not meet the criteria for a 'dark gene'.
