## Description
XKR6 encodes a 641 aa XK-related protein 6 (UniProt Q5GH73) located at 8p23.1, functioning as a phospholipid scramblase involved in lipid metabolism, apoptotic cell clearance, and membrane dynamics. InterPro analysis confirms XK-related phospholipid scramblase family membership (IPR050895/PTHR16024, aa 115-601) with XK-related protein domain (IPR018629/PF09815, aa 130-500) and membrane localization (GO:0016020). AlphaFold predicts medium-confidence structure (pLDDT 62.8), suggesting a partially ordered transmembrane protein with both structured domains and flexible regions typical of scramblase enzymes. STRING interactions reveal connections to: immune signaling (FAM167A 0.661, BLK 0.467), lipid/carbohydrate metabolism (SLC35G5 0.580, PPP1R3B 0.499, MTMR9 0.408), vascular integrity (CCM2 0.528), Kell blood group system (KEL 0.505), DNA repair (LCA5L 0.529), redox regulation (MSRA 0.495), and PARP signaling (TNKS 0.451). ClinVar reports 62 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 8p23.1-23.3 copy number losses (x1) and gains (x3), suggesting clinical significance in chromosomal disorder syndromes. Literature (46 papers) details: (1) XKR6 rs7014968 SNP increasing serum total cholesterol and coronary heart disease/ischemic stroke risk (7 citations, 2020); (2) XKR6 rs7819412 SNP associated with lipid profiles and coronary artery disease risk (16 citations, 2019); (3) XKR6 variants associated with childhood-onset systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) susceptibility (30 citations, 2018). Functional hypothesis: XKR6 likely functions as a phospholipid scramblase that: (1) Translocates phospholipids between membrane leaflets during apoptosis, exposing phosphatidylserine for macrophage recognition; (2) Regulates lipid metabolism affecting serum cholesterol levels and atherosclerosis risk; (3) Modulates immune cell membrane dynamics influencing autoimmune susceptibility (SLE); (4) Interacts with Kell blood group glycoprotein (KEL) in erythrocyte membrane organization. The FAM167A interaction is notable as both genes are SLE susceptibility loci, suggesting coordinated immune regulation. Disease implications include: coronary artery disease, ischemic stroke, systemic lupus erythematosus, potential hemolytic anemia from Kell system dysfunction, and developmental disorders from 8p23 CNVs. Despite 46 papers, XKR6 remains partially characterized with unexplored mechanisms in phospholipid scrambling kinetics, tissue-specific expression patterns, and therapeutic targeting potential.
