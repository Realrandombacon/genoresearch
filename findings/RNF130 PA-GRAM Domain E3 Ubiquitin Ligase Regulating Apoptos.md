## Description
RNF130 encodes a 419 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase (UniProt Q86XS8) with a distinctive domain architecture combining a PA-GRAM domain (PF02225, residues 83-164) and a C3HC4 RING-type zinc finger (PF13639, residues 263-305; IPR001841). The AlphaFold structure shows high confidence (pLDDT 74.0), indicating a well-folded catalytic core. STRING interactions reveal functional connections to ubiquitination machinery (UBE2D1 score 0.498, UBE2D3 score 0.476), related RING ligases (RNF223 score 0.495, MKRN1 score 0.436), and signaling proteins (BRAF score 0.556, GNAI1 score 0.535). HPA expression shows ubiquitous tissue distribution with enrichment in blood cells, consistent with its annotated role in apoptosis and Ubl conjugation pathways. ClinVar documents 26 pathogenic variants including copy number losses and gains on 5q35.3, suggesting dosage sensitivity. The PA-GRAM domain (homologous to Drosophila g1 mesoderm protein) likely mediates membrane association while the RING finger recruits E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes for substrate ubiquitination. This architecture positions RNF130 as a membrane-associated regulator of growth factor withdrawal-induced apoptosis in myeloid precursors, potentially ubiquitinating signaling intermediates in the BRAF/MAPK pathway.
