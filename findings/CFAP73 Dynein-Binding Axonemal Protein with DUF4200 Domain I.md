# CFAP73: Dynein-Binding Axonemal Protein with DUF4200 Domain Implicated in Ciliary Motility and Human Disease

**Date:** 2026-03-28T00:35:59.552107

**Quality Score:** 3.3/10  (E=4.8, D=1.8) [WEAK]

## Description
CFAP73 (Cilia and Flagella Associated Protein 73, UniProt A6NFT4) is a 308-amino acid coiled-coil domain-containing protein localized to motile cilia and axonemal doublet microtubules. The protein contains a conserved DUF4200 domain (IPR025252/PF13863, residues 35-152) embedded within a larger cilia-associated structural region (IPR051147, residues 3-294). AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure with high confidence (pLDDT=88.0), suggesting stable tertiary architecture suitable for protein-protein interactions. HPA RNA-seq shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in fallopian tube (33.5 nTPM) and choroid plexus (29.6 nTPM) — both tissues rich in motile ciliated epithelia. STRING interaction network reveals functional associations with established axonemal components including CCDC38 (0.632), CFAP100 (0.605), CCDC39 (0.478), and CCDC40 (0.475) — proteins known to form the nexin-dynein regulatory complex critical for ciliary beat regulation. ClinVar documents 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including copy number gains and losses across 12q24.13, implicating CFAP73 dosage sensitivity in human disease, potentially primary ciliary dyskinesia or related ciliopathies. The high-confidence AlphaFold structure combined with dynein-complex binding predictions suggests CFAP73 functions as a structural scaffold stabilizing inner dynein arm assemblies within the axoneme.
