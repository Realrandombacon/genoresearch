## Description
LONP2 (Lon Peptidase 2 Peroxisomal, Lon Protease Homolog 2, Q86WA8) encodes an 852 aa ATP-dependent serine protease functioning as a peroxisomal protein quality control enzyme that selectively degrades oxidatively damaged proteins and regulates metabolic signaling pathways. Structural architecture reveals an N-terminal Lon protease domain (IPR003111/G3DSA:2.30.130.40, residues 12-222/6-128) for substrate recognition, central AAA+ ATPase domain (IPR003593/IPR003959/G3DSA:3.40.50.300, residues 367-512/371-509/333-510) providing ATP-dependent unfolding activity, and C-terminal peptidase S16 catalytic domain (IPR008268, residues 740-748) with serine-type endopeptidase active site; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 77.4) confirms well-folded multi-domain structure consistent with hexameric protease complex assembly. STRING interactions define peroxisomal protein homeostasis networks: peroxisomal thioesterase TYSND1 (0.898) processing peroxisomal targeting signals, peroxin PEX5 (0.883) mediating peroxisomal matrix protein import, E3 ubiquitin ligase CRBN (0.856) linking to ubiquitin-proteasome system, AAA+ chaperones CLPB (0.843)/CLPX (0.827) cooperating in protein disaggregation, chaperonin HSPD1 (0.762), chaperonin subunit CCT3 (0.713), sterol carrier protein SCP2 (0.713) for cholesterol metabolism, and mitochondrial Clp protease CLPP (0.479). HPA classifies LONP2 as intracellular hydrolase/protease/serine protease with ubiquitous expression detected in all tissues. ClinVar documents 47 pathogenic variants including 16q12.1 CNVs and single nucleotide variants. Literature (30 papers) demonstrates emerging roles: circRNA-LONP2 promotes endothelial inflammation and atherosclerosis via NRF2/HO1 signaling (11x citations, 2024), LONP2 depletion reveals differential peroxisomal function requirements in cholesterol metabolism (2x citations, 2023), miR-3664-5p editing modulates LONP2-mediated glycolysis in breast cancer (2026), LONP2 upregulation in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (5x citations, 2022), curcumin alleviates oxidative neuronal damage via miR-1287-5p/LONP2 axis (17x citations, 2021). Mechanistic hypothesis: LONP2 forms hexameric ring structure typical of AAA+ proteases; N-terminal domain recognizes oxidatively damaged peroxisomal proteins bearing specific degradation signals; AAA+ ATPase domain hydrolyzes ATP to unfold substrate proteins and translocate them into central proteolytic chamber; S16 serine protease active site cleaves peptide bonds within the chamber; maintains peroxisomal proteostasis by clearing ROS-damaged enzymes from beta-oxidation and plasmalogen synthesis pathways; regulates NRF2/HO1 antioxidant signaling through controlled degradation of pathway components; in atherosclerosis, shear-stress-induced circRNA-LONP2 enhances endothelial inflammation; in cancer, elevated LONP2 supports glycolytic metabolism and tumor growth; neuroprotective effects of curcumin involve LONP2 upregulation reducing oxidative stress.
