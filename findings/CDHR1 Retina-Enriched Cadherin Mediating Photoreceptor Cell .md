## Description
CDHR1 (Cadherin-Related Family Member 1, UniProt Q96JP9) is an 859 amino acid Golgi apparatus-localized cell adhesion receptor with moderate characterization (68 papers) that functions as a retina-enriched cadherin essential for photoreceptor outer segment disc morphogenesis and maintenance. The protein contains multiple cadherin tandem repeat domains (cd11304, residues 42-130/139-241/250-349), cadherin-like domains (IPR002126, residues 42-135/135-353/359-472), cadherin conserved sites (IPR020894, residues 460-470/564-574), and belongs to the cadherin superfamily (IPR015919/IPR039808, residues 39-135/129-238/234-354/224-839) with PF00028 cadherin domains (residues 42-113/140-237/253-343), placing it in the calcium-dependent cell-cell adhesion machinery (GO:0007156, GO:0005509 calcium ion binding). STRING interactions reveal high-confidence partnerships with retinal disease proteins: ABCA4 (0.761, ATP-binding cassette transporter mutated in Stargardt disease), MINPP1 (0.743, multiple inositol polyphosphate phosphatase), RAX2 (0.737, retina and anterior neural fold homeobox protein 2), AIPL1 (0.622, aryl hydrocarbon receptor-interacting protein-like 1, Leber congenital amaurosis), GUCY2D (0.591, guanylate cyclase 2D, cone-rod dystrophy), RPGRIP1 (0.520, RPGR-interacting protein 1, Leber congenital amaurosis), PROM1 (0.520, prominin-1, retinal degeneration), and CERKL (0.511, ceramide kinase-like, retinitis pigmentosa), placing CDHR1 in the photoreceptor structural integrity network. HPA data shows retina enrichment (291.2 nTPM), skin expression (202.3 nTPM), group-enriched RNA tissue specificity, Golgi apparatus localization, cancer-enhanced expression, and cone-rod dystrophy disease involvement. ClinVar contains 164 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including splice site mutations (c.1321-1G>A, c.526-1G>C, c.1554-2A>C, c.297+1G>T, c.1168-2A>G, c.152-2A>G), nonsense mutations (c.1066G>T p.Glu356Ter), and missense variants (c.963G>C p.Gln321His, c.1486G>C p.Ala496Pro), though ClinVar lists 0 diseases—however literature clearly establishes CDHR1 mutations cause autosomal recessive cone-rod dystrophy (CORD) and macular dystrophy. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 78.8) indicates high confidence with well-folded cadherin domains typical of cell adhesion proteins. Functional hypothesis: CDHR1 acts as a calcium-dependent homophilic cell adhesion receptor localized to photoreceptor outer segment discs where it mediates disc membrane stacking and structural integrity through its extracellular cadherin repeats, where recent 2024-2026 research demonstrates: (1) CDHR1 biallelic pathogenic variants cause posterior segment findings with retinal degeneration (2024, 2 citations), (2) CDHR1 rescue restores cone and rod photoreceptor function in age-related retinal degeneration models (2024, 1 citation), (3) Hungarian and Japanese cohorts expand CDHR1-associated retinal dystrophy clinical spectrum with marked intrafamilial phenotypic variability (2026), (4) hypomorphic CDHR1 variants cause milder phenotypes suggesting genotype-phenotype correlations, (5) STRING partners (ABCA4, AIPL1, GUCY2D, RPGRIP1) are all established retinal dystrophy genes indicating CDHR1 functions in the same photoreceptor maintenance pathway, with retina enrichment (291.2 nTPM) confirming photoreceptor-specific function and 164 ClinVar variants representing a significant mutational burden causing cone-rod dystrophy through disrupted outer segment disc morphogenesis, making CDHR1 a diagnostic target for inherited retinal dystrophies and potential candidate for gene therapy approaches.
