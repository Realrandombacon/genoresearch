## Description
TRIR (Q9BQ61) is a Telomerase RNA component interacting RNase with predicted roles in RNA metabolism and telomere maintenance. It shows high confidence interactions with several mitochondrial proteins (COX5A, COX7C, UQCRB, COX5B, UQCR10, UQCRQ, UQCRFS1, UQCRC2, CYC1, MT-CO3), suggesting a potential link between telomere maintenance and mitochondrial function. The presence of 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in ClinVar indicates its importance in human health. Expressed across all tissues with low specificity, TRIR's function appears fundamental to cellular processes.
