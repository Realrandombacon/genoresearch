# CYB561A3: Adrenal Gland-Enriched Lysosomal Ascorbate-Dependent Ferrireductase Regulating Intracellular Iron Homeostasis via Transmembrane Electron Transport

**Date:** 2026-04-06T13:27:00.907810

**Quality Score:** 4.73/10  (E=7.65, D=1.8) [MODERATE]

## Description
CYB561A3 (Cytochrome b561 Family Member A3, also CYBASC3, LCYTB) is a 242 amino acid lysosomal and nucleolar membrane protein (UniProt Q8NBI2) with a very well-folded transmembrane architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 92.3, very high confidence), characteristic of the cytochrome b561 family of ascorbate-dependent ferric reductases that catalyze transmembrane electron transport for iron homeostasis.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals CYB561A3 contains a canonical cytochrome b561/ferric reductase transmembrane domain (IPR006593/PS50939/SM00665, residues 12-219/12-219/46-175) with six predicted transmembrane helices that coordinate two heme b groups for electron transfer. The protein belongs to the cytochrome b561/cytochrome b reductase 1-like family (IPR043205/PTHR10106, residues 10-227/10-227) with oxidoreductase activity (GO:0016491). A vertebrate-specific CYBASC3 domain (cd00930, residues 19-188) and eukaryotic cytochrome b561 PF03188 domain (residues 46-181) define the catalytic core. The enzyme enables transmembrane ascorbate ferrireductase activity, reducing Fe3+ to Fe2+ using ascorbate as electron donor for iron export from lysosomes.\n\nSTRING interaction analysis reveals CYB561A3 functions within lysosomal iron metabolism and membrane trafficking networks: strongest interactions with TMEM138 (0.585, transmembrane protein 138, ciliogenesis and vesicle trafficking), CYB561D2 (0.581, cytochrome b561 domain-containing protein 2, paralog ferrireductase), IPP (0.559, isopentenyl-diphosphate delta-isomerase, cholesterol biosynthesis), FRRS1 (0.478, ferric reductase domain-containing protein 1, iron metabolism), AOC1 (0.457, amine oxidase copper-containing 1, histamine degradation), CLN3 (0.446, ceroid-lipofuscinosis neuronal protein 3, lysosomal membrane transporter), POLR3H (0.411, RNA polymerase III subunit H), and PIGL (0.408, N-acetylglucosaminyl-phosphatidylinositol deacetylase, GPI anchor biosynthesis). The CYB561D2 and FRRS1 interactions confirm functional redundancy within the ferrireductase family, while CLN3 links CYB561A3 to lysosomal storage disease pathways.\n\nHPA expression shows adrenal gland-enriched specificity (292.2 nTPM) with detection in all tissues and nucleoli/vesicles subcellular localization, consistent with lysosomal iron recycling in steroidogenic cells. The protein is classified as enzyme, predicted membrane protein, and intracellular protein with electron transport and transport biological processes, and oxidoreductase/translocase molecular functions.\n\nClinVar documents 11 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, including a 3'-UTR variant (c.*383G>A) and large chromosome 11p11.12-q13.5 copy number variations (gains spanning 11p11.12-q12.2 to 11p13-q25 regions). These CNVs suggest dosage sensitivity potentially causing iron metabolism disorders, adrenal insufficiency, or lysosomal storage defects through impaired ferric iron reduction.\n\nConservation is evident from mouse (Q6P1H1, 242 aa), rat (Q5U2W7, 256 aa), cow (A5D9A7, 265 aa), and zebrafish (A3KPR5, 247 aa) orthologs, indicating ancient evolutionary conservation of lysosomal iron homeostasis mechanisms.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: CYB561A3 acts as a lysosomal membrane ferrireductase that reduces Fe3+ to Fe2+ using cytosolic ascorbate as electron donor, enabling iron export from lysosomes via the divalent metal transporter DMT1/SLC11A2. During autophagy and ferritinophagy, iron-containing cargo (ferritin, hemoglobin, iron-sulfur clusters) is delivered to lysosomes where acidic pH releases Fe3+; CYB561A3 reduces this to Fe2+ for cytosolic export and reuse in heme synthesis, iron-sulfur cluster assembly, or storage. The adrenal gland enrichment reflects high iron demand for cytochrome P450 enzymes in steroid hormone biosynthesis (CYP11A1, CYP17A1, CYP21A2 are heme-dependent). Nucleolar localization suggests additional roles in ribosome biogenesis through iron-sulfur cluster delivery to nucleolar assembly factors. CLN3 interaction links CYB561A3 to neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis pathways, where lysosomal iron accumulation may contribute to neurodegeneration. CYB561D2 paralog provides functional redundancy. CNV-mediated haploinsufficiency disrupts lysosomal iron export, causing iron sequestration, oxidative stress from Fenton chemistry, and impaired steroidogenesis. The 3'-UTR variant may affect mRNA stability or localization.
