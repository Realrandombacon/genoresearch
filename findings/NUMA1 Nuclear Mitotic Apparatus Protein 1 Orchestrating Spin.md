## Description
NUMA1 (Nuclear Mitotic Apparatus Protein 1) is a 2115 amino acid large coiled-coil scaffolding protein that serves as a master regulator of mitotic spindle pole organization, chromosome segregation fidelity, and DNA damage repair through microtubule anchoring at spindle poles, dynein-dynactin complex recruitment, and PARP1-mediated DNA damage response coordination. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q14980-F1) predicts medium confidence (pLDDT 64.6) across the 2115 residue sequence, reflecting the characteristic architecture of large scaffolding proteins with structured terminal domains (hook and LGNBD) connected by extended coiled-coil regions prone to intrinsic disorder, suitable for flexible protein-protein interaction platforms. As a well-studied essential mitotic scaffold with emerging cancer associations, NUMA1 represents a critical node connecting spindle pole organization to chromosome segregation fidelity, DNA damage response, and chemoresistance through hook domain-mediated microtubule anchoring, LGNBD-dependent GPSM2 recruitment for spindle orientation, and PARP1-coordinated DNA repair disrupted in cancer progression and treatment resistance.
