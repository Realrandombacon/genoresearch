## Description
BLTP3B (A0JNW5) encodes a massive 1464 amino acid protein belonging to the bridge-like lipid transfer protein (BLTP) family that functions in intracellular lipid trafficking and membrane contact site organization. InterPro analysis reveals an extended domain architecture spanning nearly the entire protein length: a Bridge-like lipid transfer protein family member 3A/B domain (IPR026728/PF24917, positions 1-1462) classified as a repeat unit suggesting modular lipid-binding or membrane association capability; and a CHOREIN N-terminal domain-containing protein classification (PTHR22774, positions 1-1461) linking BLTP3B to the chorein family involved in lipid sensing and vesicular trafficking. AlphaFold predicts a partially structured architecture (pLDDT 66.8, medium confidence) consistent with large lipid transfer proteins that often contain flexible linker regions connecting ordered lipid-binding domains, potentially enabling conformational changes required for lipid extraction and delivery between membranes. HPA shows ubiquitous expression across all tissues with low specificity, classified under predicted intracellular proteins with multi-compartment localization including nucleoplasm, vesicles, and cytosol suggesting dynamic shuttling between cellular compartments during lipid transfer cycles. Literature search returned zero publications on Semantic Scholar confirming BLTP3B as a genuinely unstudied dark gene with no functional characterization to date.
