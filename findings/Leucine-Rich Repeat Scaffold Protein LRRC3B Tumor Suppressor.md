# Leucine-Rich Repeat Scaffold Protein LRRC3B: Tumor Suppressor with Adhesion GPCR Interaction Network

**Date:** 2026-03-23T03:17:17.580905

**Quality Score:** 3.8/10  (E=5.5, D=2.1) [MODERATE]

## Description
LRRC3B encodes a 259 amino acid leucine-rich repeat (LRR) scaffold protein (UniProt Q96PB8) functioning as a tumor suppressor. InterPro analysis reveals multiple LRR domains (IPR000372, IPR001611, PF00560, PF01462) spanning residues 33-202, forming a curved solenoid structure typical of protein-protein interaction scaffolds. AlphaFold predicts a well-folded structure (pLDDT 85.8), consistent with stable LRR architecture. HPA data shows tissue-enhanced expression with highest levels in brain (23.8 nTPM), skeletal muscle (8.8 nTPM), and testis (6.7 nTPM), plus cancer-enhanced expression pattern. STRING interactions reveal a network of 10 partners including adhesion GPCRs ADGRL3 (0.566), ADGRL1 (0.551), ADGRL2 (0.551), transcription factor FOXP1 (0.474), and chromatin regulator CGGBP1 (0.504). ClinVar contains 16 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number alterations on chromosome 3p, consistent with tumor suppressor haploinsufficiency. The LRR domain architecture suggests LRRC3B functions as a molecular scaffold bridging adhesion GPCR signaling to transcriptional regulation via FOXP1/CGGBP1 complexes. Reduced expression in gastric, renal, colorectal, lung, and breast cancers (per gene_info) combined with 3p chromosomal losses supports a tumor suppressor mechanism where LRRC3B loss disrupts cell adhesion signaling and transcriptional control.
