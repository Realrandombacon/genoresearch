## Description
TIMM10B (Translocase of Inner Mitochondrial Membrane 10B), also known as FXC1 or Tim10 B, is a 103-amino acid mitochondrial intermembrane space protein that functions as a chaperone subunit of the TIM9-TIM10 complex mediating import and insertion of hydrophobic membrane proteins into the mitochondrial inner membrane. InterPro analysis reveals a specialized Tim10-like architecture: a Tim10/DDP family zinc finger domain (PF02953, residues 6-68) containing a Tim10-like domain (IPR004217, residues 6-68; IPR035427, residues 1-88) belonging to the mitochondrial import inner membrane translocase subunit family (IPR050673, residues 3-93; PTHR13172, residues 3-93). The Tim10-like domain superfamily (G3DSA:1.10.287.810, residues 1-88; SSF144122, residues 14-82) forms a conserved 'twin CX3C' zinc finger motif characteristic of mitochondrial intermembrane space chaperones that bind hydrophobic precursor proteins.
