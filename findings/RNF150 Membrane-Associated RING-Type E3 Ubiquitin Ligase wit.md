## Description
RNF150 (Ring Finger Protein 150) is a 438 amino acid E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase located at chromosome 4q31.21 (positions 141212899-140859806) with well-defined domain architecture and disease associations. UniProt accession Q9ULK6 confirms protein-coding status. InterPro analysis reveals two key functional domains: (1) N-terminal PA domain (PF02225, IPR003137, residues 87-179) - originally identified in Pseudomonas aeruginosa exoenzyme S, mediates protein-protein interactions and membrane association; (2) C-terminal RING finger domain (PF13639, IPR001841, residues 276-319) - C3HC4-type zinc finger conferring E3 ubiquitin ligase activity by recruiting E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes. AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 75.1) predicts confident folded conformation. STRING interactions identify 10 protein partners with medium confidence (0.406-0.530): TIGD4 (0.530), TMEM131L (0.517), RMND5B (0.513), RNF175 (0.489), SNX15 (0.487), COMMD8 (0.485), CNN1 (0.478), SMIM14 (0.438), SYNPO2 (0.427), RNF183 (0.406). Interaction with RNF175 and RNF183 suggests functional clustering within RING finger E3 ligase family. HPA shows low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues, consistent with housekeeping ubiquitination machinery. ClinVar contains 32 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly copy number losses/gains spanning 4q31.21, indicating dosage sensitivity. Conservation is strong: mouse ortholog Q5DTZ6 (437 aa, ~95% identity), zebrafish Q566M8 (419 aa, ~85% identity). Functional hypothesis: RNF150 functions as membrane-associated E3 ligase targeting specific substrates for proteasomal degradation, potentially regulating membrane protein turnover or signal transduction pathways. PA domain may mediate substrate recognition or subcellular localization.
