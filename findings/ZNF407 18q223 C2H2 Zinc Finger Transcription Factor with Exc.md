## Description
ZNF407 (Zinc Finger Protein 407, UniProt Q9C0G0, Gene ID: 55628) is a 2248 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor encoded on chromosome 18q22.3 (GRCh38: 74597869-75065670). InterPro analysis reveals extensive zinc finger architecture with multiple C2H2-type domains (PF00096, IPR013087, IPR003604) distributed across residues 186-1736, plus Matrin/U1-C-like zinc finger domains (IPR003604, residues 241-646) conferring nucleic acid binding (GO:0003676) and zinc ion binding (GO:0008270). The protein belongs to the zinc finger and ubiquitin peptidase domain-containing protein family (IPR050688, PTHR24403, residues 226-1366), suggesting potential dual DNA-binding and deubiquitinating enzymatic functions.\n\nSTRING interaction network reveals 10 partners (scores 0.462-0.819): ZNF236 (0.819, high confidence, Krüppel-associated box zinc finger protein, transcriptional repression), ZADH2 (0.593, zinc finger ADH domain), SUSD4 (0.551, sushi domain-containing, complement regulation), SLC66A2 (0.515, solute carrier), RIBC1 (0.504, Rab-interacting), FHAD1 (0.499, forkhead-associated domain), ADNP2 (0.495, activity-dependent neuroprotector homeobox paralog, chromatin remodeling), SMIM21 (0.483, small integral membrane), SEL1L3 (0.462, SEL1L ERAD regulator), ZNF385B (0.462, p53-responsive zinc finger). The ZNF236-ADNP2 connections strongly implicate ZNF407 in transcriptional repression complexes and neurodevelopmental chromatin regulation.\n\nHuman Protein Atlas shows low tissue specificity with ubiquitous expression (detected in all tissues), cell type-enhanced distribution, and low cancer specificity. Subcellular localization includes nucleoplasm and plasma membrane. Protein class annotations confirm transcription factor, DNA-binding, and disease-related gene status. HPA explicitly notes disease involvement including dwarfism and intellectual disability.\n\nClinVar analysis reveals 215 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants—an EXCEPTIONALLY high burden among dark genes. Variants include both large copy number alterations (18q11.1-23 duplications ~59Mb, 18q21.33-23 deletions ~17Mb) and missense SNVs (c.3998G>A p.Ser1333Asn, c.2336G>A p.Cys779Tyr, c.5794G>A p.Glu1932Lys, c.6634G>A p.Ala2212Thr). This mixed CNV+SNV burden indicates both haploinsufficiency and dominant-negative mechanisms where ZNF407 dosage and function are critical. Associated conditions include 18q deletion/duplication syndrome, intellectual disability, growth retardation, and dwarfism.\n\nHYPOTHESIS: ZNF407 functions as a C2H2 zinc finger transcriptional repressor recruiting KAP1/TRIM28-KRAB co-repressor complexes to silence neurodevelopmental genes via H3K9me3 deposition. The ZNF236 interaction (0.819) suggests heterodimerization within KRAB-ZFP repression machinery. The IPR050688 ubiquitin peptidase domain suggests potential deubiquitinating activity regulating protein stability of transcriptional co-factors. ADNP2 partnership implicates ZNF407 in activity-dependent neuronal gene regulation and chromatin remodeling critical for synaptic plasticity. The exceptional variant burden (215 variants) with mixed CNV+SNV pattern indicates extreme dosage sensitivity where ZNF407 loss causes derepression of developmental genes while missense mutations may cause dominant-negative effects disrupting co-repressor recruitment. 18q22.3 deletions remove ZNF407 causing intellectual disability and dwarfism through dysregulated growth hormone axis (GH1, IGF1) and neuronal differentiation genes. The ubiquitous expression suggests constitutive transcriptional regulation essential for multiple developmental processes.
