## Description
HS2ST1 (Heparan Sulfate 2-O-Sulfotransferase 1, UniProt Q7LGA3) is a 356 amino acid Golgi-resident enzyme with moderate characterization (43 papers) that functions as the sole enzyme responsible for 2-O-sulfation of glucuronic acid residues in heparan sulfate proteoglycans, a critical post-synthetic modification that determines heparan sulfate binding specificity for growth factors (FGF, VEGF, Wnt), morphogens, and extracellular matrix proteins essential for development, angiogenesis, and neural function. The protein contains a sulfotransferase family domain (IPR005331/PF03567 residues 72-320, GO:0008146 sulfotransferase activity, GO:0016020 membrane), heparan sulfate 2-O-sulfotransferase family (IPR007734/PTHR12129 residues 18-332), and P-loop containing nucleoside triphosphate hydrolase domain (IPR027417/G3DSA:3.40.50.300/SSF52540 residues 69-356/73-221) for binding the sulfate donor PAPS (3'-phosphoadenosine-5'-phosphosulfate). STRING interactions reveal high-to-medium confidence partnerships with heparan sulfate biosynthesis enzymes: EXT1 (0.813, exostosin-1—glycosyltransferase for heparan sulfate polymerization), EXT2 (0.783, exostosin-2—glycosyltransferase complex partner), HS3ST5 (0.737, heparan sulfate 3-O-sulfotransferase 5), HS6ST2 (0.735, heparan sulfate 6-O-sulfotransferase 2), NDST2 (0.670, N-deacetylase/N-sulfotransferase 2), HS6ST1 (0.618), HS3ST1 (0.614), GLCE (0.605, glucuronyl C5-epimerase), NDST3 (0.572), and NDST1 (0.505), confirming integration into the complete heparan sulfate biosynthetic pathway.
