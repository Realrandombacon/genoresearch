## Description

AlphaFold structure prediction reveals extremely low confidence (pLDDT 27.4/100), indicating a predominantly intrinsically disordered protein (IDP) lacking stable tertiary structure - characteristic of proteins functioning through dynamic protein-protein interactions rather than enzymatic catalysis. The high pathogenic CNV burden (43 variants) with both gains and losses suggests: (1) dosage sensitivity for 7q36.3 gene regulation, (2) copy number alterations disrupt 7q36.3 chromatin architecture leading to SHH/HOXA dysregulation, (3) gains/losses alter limb-brain development contributing to holoprosencephaly, tibial hemimelia, preaxial polydactyly, and hand-foot-genital syndrome phenotypes, or (4) this represents a non-coding "dark" locus within the 7q36.3 SHH/HOXA critical region with potent developmental regulatory effects. The disordered structure (pLDDT 27.4) supports IDP hypothesis - consistent with transcriptional coactivators, chromatin regulators, and signaling scaffolds.
