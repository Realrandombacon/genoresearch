## Description
IP6K3 (Inositol Hexakisphosphate Kinase 3) is a 410-amino acid metabolic enzyme characterized by a canonical inositol polyphosphate kinase domain architecture (IPR005522/IPR038286/PF03770/PTHR12400, positions 12-408 for full domain, 198-404 for PF03770 core) containing the inositol polyphosphate kinase superfamily fold (G3DSA:3.30.470.160, positions 28-70 and 189-409; SSF56104 SAICAR synthase-like, positions 20-73, 196-334, 362-408) catalyzing ATP-dependent phosphorylation of inositol hexakisphosphate (InsP6) to generate diphosphoinositol polyphosphates (PP-InsPs including InsP7 and InsP8), classified under kinase activity (GO:0016301) and inositol phosphate biosynthetic process (GO:0032958). STRING interactions reveal an exceptionally tight functional network exclusively with inositol phosphate metabolism enzymes: PPIP5K1 (0.996, diphosphoinositol pentakisphosphate kinase 1 - highest confidence), PPIP5K2 (0.992), IP6K1 (0.913, the canonical paralogue), IP6K2 (0.913), ITPKB (0.761, inositol-trisphosphate 3-kinase B), plus medium-confidence connections to adducin cytoskeletal proteins ADD1 (0.576), ADD2 (0.588), ADD3 (0.581), IPPK (0.464), and ITPKA (0.426), indicating IP6K3 functions as an integral component of the inositol phosphate signaling network. HPA data shows group-enriched expression with exceptionally high levels in skeletal muscle (290.7 nTPM) and tongue (77.8 nTPM), detected in many tissues, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, group-enriched single cell specificity, subcellular localization to nucleoplasm and cytosol, classified as an enzyme and metabolic protein with kinase/transferase molecular function. ClinVar contains 5 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, all large copy number gains spanning 6p25.3-21.31, 6p22.1-q14.1, and 6p25.3-q27 regions (duplications affecting chromosome 6), though specific monogenic disease phenotypes remain under characterization (significance marked '?' for most); the genomic location overlaps with regions implicated in immune regulation and metabolic disorders. AlphaFold structure (AF-Q96PC2-F1) predicts high confidence folding (pLDDT 75.4) consistent with a well-structured kinase containing ordered catalytic domains typical of inositol phosphate kinases. With 36 papers, IP6K3 represents a 'gray' gene with emerging clinical relevance (2021-2026): IP6K3 and IPMK variations show association with late-onset Alzheimer's disease (LOAD) and longevity suggesting a multifaceted signaling network at the crossroad between neurodegeneration and survival; SNP rs791903 associates with Hashimoto's thyroiditis in Egyptian cohorts; nicotine dependence partially mediates IP6K3 genetic variation effects on lung squamous cell carcinoma risk among smokers; recent positive selection implicates IP6K3 as metabolically relevant locus in South Asians; and IP6K family members regulate vascular smooth muscle cell calcification in chronic kidney disease. The mechanistic hypothesis posits that IP6K3 functions as a tissue-specific inositol phosphate kinase that: (1) generates PP-InsPs (InsP7/InsP8) as high-energy signaling molecules regulating insulin sensitivity and glucose homeostasis in skeletal muscle (290.7 nTPM enrichment); (2) modulates neurodegeneration risk through PP-InsP-mediated regulation of protein phosphorylation cascades and autophagy pathways relevant to Alzheimer's pathology; (3) interacts with adducin cytoskeletal proteins (ADD1/2/3) linking inositol phosphate signaling to actin-spectrin membrane skeleton organization; (4) contributes to vascular calcification through PP-InsP regulation of phosphate metabolism in smooth muscle cells; its skeletal muscle enrichment aligns with roles in metabolic regulation explaining associations with longevity and neurodegeneration, while chromosome 6 CNVs may contribute to autoimmune thyroid disease and cancer susceptibility through dysregulated inositol phosphate signaling.
