# LCE2C: Late Cornified Envelope Protein 2C

**Date:** 2026-03-29T20:19:56.253434

**Quality Score:** 1.9/10  (E=3.5, D=0.3) [POOR]

## Description
LCE2C (ENSG00000187180) encodes a late cornified envelope protein, which is involved in keratinization. The protein is highly expressed in skin (313.4 nTPM) and has been associated with cancer. Protein interactions suggest it is part of a larger complex with other LCE proteins. Structural domains indicate a role in epidermis development. Pathogenic variants have been identified, including copy number losses and gains.
