Functional hypothesis: ZNF671 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor that recruits the KAP1/TRIM28 corepressor complex via its KRAB domain to silence target genes through heterochromatin formation. Dosage sensitivity (ClinVar duplications) suggests precise ZNF671 levels are critical for maintaining balanced repression of target gene networks, with overexpression causing aberrant silencing of developmentally regulated or tumor suppressor genes. The medium AlphaFold confidence reflects functional disorder in the KRAB effector region, enabling dynamic corepressor complex assembly.
