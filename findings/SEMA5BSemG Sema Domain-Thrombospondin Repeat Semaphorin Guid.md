## Description
SEMA5B (Semaphorin 5B, also known as SEMAG, SemG, UniProt Q9P283) is a 1151-amino acid transmembrane semaphorin belonging to the class 5 semaphorin family that functions as a critical axon guidance cue and developmental signaling molecule through plexin receptor binding and thrombospondin-mediated extracellular matrix interactions. The protein architecture features an N-terminal Sema domain (IPR001627/cd11264 aa 103-554/118-554) forming the characteristic semaphorin immunoglobulin-like fold for receptor binding, five Thrombospondin type-1 (TSP1) repeats (IPR000884 aa 664-720/722-771/853-908 plus two additional at aa 608-663/721-771) mediating extracellular matrix and cell surface interactions, Plexin repeat/PSI domain (IPR002165/IPR016201 aa 555-602) contributing to receptor specificity, complete Semaphorin family membership (IPR027231 aa 73-1071), Sema domain superfamily architecture (IPR036352), WD40/YVTN repeat-like-containing domain (IPR015943 aa 98-554), and GO term annotations for protein binding (GO:0005515), semaphorin receptor binding (GO:0030215), developmental protein molecular function, neurogenesis and differentiation biological processes.
