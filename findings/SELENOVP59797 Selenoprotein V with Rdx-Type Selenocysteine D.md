## Description
SELENOV (Selenoprotein V, also known as SELV) is a 346 amino acid intracellular selenoprotein (UniProt P59797) containing a selenocysteine (Sec) residue encoded by a UGA codon with a SECIS (Sec insertion sequence) element in the 3' UTR. The protein belongs to the Rdx-type selenoprotein family (IPR011893/PF10262, residues 265-336) within the broader Selenoprotein W and related family (IPR051441/PTHR15124, residues 63-346).
