# NEUROD4: Basic Helix-Loop-Helix Transcription Factor Driving Retinal and Pituitary Neurogenesis

## Description
NEUROD4 (neuronal differentiation 4, aliases ATH-3, MATH-3, bHLHa4) encodes a 331-amino acid basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factor belonging to the NeuroD family (IPR016637). The protein contains a canonical bHLH DNA-binding domain (IPR011598/PF00010, residues 87-145) that mediates E-box binding and protein dimerization, plus a neuronal-specific domain (PF12533, residues 146-264) of unknown function. AlphaFold predicts medium confidence structure (pLDDT=62.7) with the bHLH region well-ordered and flanking regions partially disordered, consistent with transcription factor architecture. HPA RNA-seq shows group-enriched expression with highest levels in retina (28.0 nTPM) and pituitary gland (7.7 nTPM), indicating specific roles in photoreceptor/amacrine cell development and pituitary hormone cell differentiation. STRING interactions reveal a neurogenic transcription factor network: FOXN4 (0.944, retinal ganglion cell specification), ASCL1 (0.801, proneural bHLH factor), LHX3 (0.721, pituitary development), PAX6 (0.644, eye development master regulator), VSX2 (0.653, retinal progenitor), and ISL1 (0.480, motor neuron differentiation). This interaction profile places NEUROD4 within the proneural bHLH cascade that drives neuronal fate commitment. ClinVar documents 5 pathogenic copy number gain variants on chromosome 12, suggesting gene dosage sensitivity. Conservation is strong across vertebrates: mouse ortholog (O09105, 330 aa, ~90% identity in bHLH domain), Xenopus (P79920), and chicken (P79766), indicating functional constraint. Mechanistically, NEUROD4 likely heterodimerizes with E-proteins (TCF3/12) to bind E-box motifs (CANNTG) in neurogenic gene enhancers, activating downstream effectors that promote cell cycle exit and neuronal differentiation.
