## Description
LOC100129434 (Gene ID: 100129434) is an uncharacterized non-coding genetic locus on chromosome 2p16.1 (NC_000002.12, positions 56185769-56173533, ~12.2 kb region) with substantial clinical significance evidenced by 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic copy number variants despite complete absence of molecular characterization. No UniProt accession exists, preventing InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. Human Protein Atlas reported no data for Ensembl ID ENSG00000233251, consistent with non-coding or highly tissue-restricted expression. STRING-DB detected no protein-protein interactions, consistent with non-coding function. However, ClinVar contains 2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants: 1 copy number loss/deletion (variant 147996: 2p16.1 ~5.0 Mb monosomy) and 1 copy number gain/duplication (variant 59143: 2p22.1-16.1 ~17.1 Mb trisomy). Both variants have uncertain significance (?) but are classified as pathogenic/likely pathogenic. The 2p16.1 chromosomal region contains critical neuronal excitability, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, and transcriptional regulation-associated genes including SCN1A (sodium voltage-gated channel alpha subunit 1, Nav1.1, action potential initiation, neuronal excitability, Dravet syndrome, generalized epilepsy with febrile seizures plus, familial hemiplegic migraine), ZEB2 (zinc finger E-box binding homeobox 2, EMT transcriptional repressor, SMAD binding, TGF-beta signaling, neural crest development, Mowat-Wilson syndrome, Hirschsprung disease), REL (RELA proto-oncogene, NF-kappaB subunit, inflammatory signaling, cell survival, apoptosis inhibition, lymphoid malignancy), BCL11A (B cell lymphoma/leukemia 11A, zinc finger transcription factor, gamma-globin repression, beta-thalassemia therapeutic target, B cell development), and FANCB (Fanconi anemia complementation group B, DNA interstrand crosslink repair, Fanconi anemia, bone marrow failure, cancer predisposition). Functional hypothesis: LOC100129434 likely represents a critical 2p16.1 neuronal/epithelial enhancer element within the SCN1A-ZEB2 regulatory region, potentially functioning as a cortical GABAergic interneuron-specific enhancer coordinating SCN1A-ZEB2-REL expression in neuronal excitability niches (cortical pyramidal neurons, hippocampal interneurons, cerebellar Purkinje cells) or as a chromatin boundary separating voltage-gated sodium channel genes (SCN1A) from EMT transcription factor genes (ZEB2) and NF-kappaB signaling genes (REL). The 2 pathogenic CNVs (1 deletion 5.0 Mb, 1 duplication 17.1 Mb) indicate regional dosage sensitivity where altered copy number disrupts SCN1A-mediated action potential initiation and ZEB2-mediated EMT repression. Disease mechanism involves SCN1A loss-of-function causing Dravet syndrome (severe infantile myoclonic epilepsy, febrile seizures, developmental regression, SUDEP risk, SCN1A haploinsufficiency), SCN1A gain-of-function causing familial hemiplegic migraine (migraine with aura, hemiparesis, cerebellar ataxia), SCN1A mutation causing generalized epilepsy with febrile seizures plus (GEFS+, temperature-sensitive seizures, variable expressivity), ZEB2 haploinsufficiency causing Mowat-Wilson syndrome (Hirschsprung disease, intellectual disability, distinctive facial features, corpus callosum agenesis, genitourinary anomalies), ZEB2 overexpression causing cancer metastasis (EMT activation, invasion, chemoresistance, poor prognosis), REL amplification causing lymphoid malignancy (NF-kappaB hyperactivation, cell survival, chemoresistance, diffuse large B cell lymphoma), BCL11A overexpression causing B cell malignancy (B cell development arrest, lymphoma/leukemia), BCL11A inhibition causing fetal hemoglobin induction (beta-thalassemia/sickle cell disease therapeutic strategy), FANCB mutation causing Fanconi anemia (DNA repair defect, bone marrow failure, congenital anomalies, cancer predisposition, aplastic anemia), and 2p16.1 deletion/duplication syndrome presenting with neurodevelopmental abnormalities and epilepsy. The 2p16.1 region is associated with SCN1A-related epilepsy syndromes. The absence of protein-coding features combined with ClinVar evidence (2 CNVs 5.0-17.1 Mb) and SCN1A-ZEB2-REL-BCL11A-FANCB neighborhood suggests this locus functions as a neuronal/epithelial-specific enhancer critical for neuronal excitability-EMT-NF-kappaB coordination.
