## Description
BICDL1 (BICD family like cargo adaptor 1, aliases BICDR-1, BICDR1, CCDC64, CCDC64A) is a 573 amino acid predicted intracellular dynein-dynactin adapter protein (UniProt Q6ZP65) with high-confidence well-folded architecture (AlphaFold pLDDT 75.1) containing canonical Spindly/BICDR family architecture including Spindly/BICDR Dynein Motor Adapter family (IPR051149/PTHR32123, residues 24-554) forming a 531 aa module (~93%) characteristic of dynein adapters, Spindly domain (PF28219, residues 99-352) forming a 254 aa module (~44%) for dynein light chain binding, BICD family-like cargo adapter C-terminal domain (IPR060493/PF27654, residues 489-542) forming a 54 aa module (~9%) for cargo recognition, and molecular functions including dynactin binding activity, small GTPase binding activity, Golgi to secretory granule transport, neuron projection development, vesicle transport along microtubule, nucleoplasm/vesicles/cytosol localization, and predicted intracellular protein classification, functioning as a kidney-enhanced large dynein-dynactin adapter that regulates vesicle trafficking and organelle positioning through Spindly/BICDR domain-mediated motor complex assembly and dynein-dynactin network association via partnerships with ACTR1A, CAPZB, DYNC1H1, DYNC1I2, DCTN4, ACTR10, DYNC1LI2, DCTN6, RAB6A, and DCTN5.\n\nInterPro analysis reveals BICDL1 contains canonical Spindly/BICDR adapter architecture: The Spindly/BICDR family domain (residues 24-554) spans nearly the entire protein (~93%), defining BICDL1 as a dedicated dynein-dynactin adapter. The Spindly domain (residues 99-352) occupies the central region (~44%), providing the characteristic coiled-coil structure for dynein light chain (DYNLL) binding and regulation of dynein motility. The C-terminal BICD-like domain (residues 489-542) provides a specific cargo-binding interface (~9%). The high-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 75.1) indicates a well-folded protein with stable coiled-coil architecture, consistent with adapter proteins that require rigid scaffolds to bridge motor complexes and cargo.
