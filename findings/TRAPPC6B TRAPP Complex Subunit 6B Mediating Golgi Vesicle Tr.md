## Description
TRAPPC6B (Trafficking Protein Particle Complex Subunit 6B, UniProt Q86SZ2) is a 158 aa core component of the TRAPP (TRAnsport Protein Particle) complex belonging to the TRAPP component family (IPR007194/IPR037992/PTHR12817, positions 7-155/2-157/2-157) that functions in Golgi vesicle transport, neurodevelopment, and autophagy regulation. The molecular architecture consists of a Trs33 subunit domain (cd14944/G3DSA:3.30.1380.20/SSF111126, positions 3-156/1-158/3-157) forming a NO signalling/Golgi transport ligand-binding domain superfamily (IPR024096, positions 3-157) with alpha-helical bundle structure for protein-protein interactions within the TRAPP complex. Literature (19 papers) establishes TRAPPC6B roles in: (1) TRAPPC6B biallelic variants cause neurodevelopmental disorder with TRAPP II and trafficking disruptions (2023, 5 citations), (2) circular RNA TRAPPC6B inhibits intracellular Mycobacterium tuberculosis growth while inducing autophagy in macrophages by targeting microRNA-874-3p (2021, 36 citations), (3) RAB5A and TRAPPC6B are novel targets for Shiga toxin 2a inactivation in kidney epithelial cells (2020, 10 citations), (4) exosomal co-delivery of CircRNA-TRAPPC6B and rifampicin for enhanced immunotherapy against tuberculosis (2025), (5) clinical phenotype and gene variation analysis of child with neurodevelopmental disorder caused by homozygous TRAPPC6B variation (2025). Functional hypothesis: TRAPPC6B functions as a core scaffold subunit of the TRAPP II complex that activates Rab GTPases (RAB1, RAB11, RAB5A) for Golgi-to-endosome vesicle tethering and fusion, regulating protein trafficking, autophagy, and neurodevelopment. The mechanistic model proposes: (1) Trs33 domain forms alpha-helical bundle binding TRAPPC2-TRAPPC3-TRAPPC4-TRAPPC5 core complex, (2) TRAPPC6B stabilizes TRAPP II assembly for RAB11 activation in Golgi vesicle transport, (3) TRAPPC6B-RAB5A interaction mediates endosomal trafficking and Shiga toxin inactivation in kidney epithelial cells, (4) Circular RNA TRAPPC6B sponges miR-874-3p inducing autophagy for Mtb clearance in macrophages, (5) Biallelic loss-of-function variants disrupt TRAPP complex assembly, impairing Golgi trafficking and causing neurodevelopmental defects. In neurodevelopmental disorder, homozygous frameshift/nonsense variants cause complete TRAPPC6B loss, disrupting neuronal protein trafficking and synaptic development leading to intellectual disability and epilepsy. In tuberculosis, circRNA-TRAPPC6B enhances autophagy-mediated bacterial clearance. TRAPPC6B represents an understudied TRAPP complex subunit linking vesicle trafficking to neurodevelopment and infectious disease immunity.
