## Description
ZNF318 encodes a massive 2279 amino acid C2H2-type zinc finger protein (UniProt: Q5VUA4) at chromosome 6p21.1 that functions as a transcriptional regulator with RNA processing connections. The domain architecture features two Matrin/U1-C-like C2H2 zinc finger domains (IPR003604/SM00451, residues 1063-1097 and 1133-1167) mediating nucleic acid binding, a ZNF318 helical domain (IPR060578/PF27978, residues 881-980), and belongs to the ZNF318-like family (IPR055309/PTHR15577, residues 1-2175). The enormous protein size places ZNF318 among the largest human zinc finger proteins.\n\nHPA expression profiling shows ubiquitous detection across all tissues with low tissue specificity and immune cell enhancement. Subcellular localization spans nucleoplasm and cytosol, consistent with transcriptional regulatory functions. The protein is classified as both transcriptional activator and repressor, indicating context-dependent regulatory activity. Biological process annotations include meiosis and transcription regulation.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 10 partners enriched for RNA processing and transcription elongation factors: ZFP36L2 (0.670, zinc finger protein 36-like 2, mRNA decay factor), CDK13 (0.610, cyclin-dependent kinase 13, transcription elongation), PPP1CC (0.595, protein phosphatase 1 catalytic subunit), CDC5L (0.595, cell division cycle 5-like, spliceosome component), PRPF39 (0.588, pre-mRNA processing factor 39, spliceosome), RBM25 (0.522, RNA binding motif protein 25, splicing regulator), and TNPO3 (0.460, transportin-3, nuclear import receptor). This interactome positions ZNF318 at the interface of transcription and pre-mRNA splicing.\n\nClinVar contains 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large 6p copy number gains (5 variants) and deletions (4 variants), plus one intronic SNV c.2770+197A>G. The 6p21.1 region is dosage-sensitive; ZNF318 haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity may contribute to developmental phenotypes through transcriptional dysregulation.\n\nAlphaFold structure prediction yields very low confidence (pLDDT 40.8), indicating extensive intrinsic disorder characteristic of transcriptional regulators with flexible activation domains. The structured zinc finger regions likely mediate DNA binding while disordered regions recruit co-regulators.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: ZNF318 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulator coupling transcription elongation (via CDK13, PPP1CC interactions) with co-transcriptional splicing (via CDC5L, PRPF39, RBM25). The C2H2 zinc fingers recognize target gene promoters/enhancers while disordered regions recruit splicing machinery. This coupling ensures coordinated gene expression and mRNA processing, particularly in immune cells and during meiosis.
