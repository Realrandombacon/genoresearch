## Description
MCOLN2 (Mucolipin-2, also known as TRPML2, Q8IZK6) encodes a 566 aa multi-pass transmembrane protein functioning as an endolysosomal cation channel that mediates calcium and magnesium efflux from intracellular vesicles critical for innate immune responses, pathogen restriction, and cancer progression. Structural architecture reveals characteristic TRPML organization: an N-terminal extracytosolic/lumenal domain (ELD) (IPR049134/PF21381/cd21071, residues 90-275/111-277) projecting into the vesicular lumen for luminal sensing or ligand binding, followed by six transmembrane helices forming the pore region containing a Polycystin cation channel domain (IPR013122/PF08016/G3DSA:1.10.287.70, residues 379-513/407-547) which creates the ion conduction pathway, belonging to the mucolipin family (IPR039031/PTHR12127, residues 27-556) with predicted monoatomic cation channel activity; AlphaFold prediction (pLDDT 82.0, high confidence) confirms a well-folded structure with defined transmembrane topology consistent with stable ion channel requiring precise pore geometry for selective ion permeation. STRING interactions define an endolysosomal trafficking and signaling network: tetraspanins CD63 (0.615) and CD59 (0.672) marking late endosomes/MVBs and GPI-anchored protein complexes, FERM domain protein FRMD6 (0.649) linking to cytoskeletal organization, related TRP channels TRPC1 (0.633), TRPA1 (0.573), and TRPM7 (0.566) suggesting functional redundancy or heteromerization, two-pore channel TPCN2 (0.630) mediating endolysosomal calcium release, paralogous mucolipins MCOLN1 (0.543) and MCOLN3 (0.540) indicating potential compensatory mechanisms, and small GTPase ARF6 (0.521) regulating vesicle trafficking and membrane recycling. HPA localizes MCOLN2 to immune-relevant tissues with tissue-enhanced expression highest in intestine (14.1 nTPM) reflecting barrier immunity role, lymphoid tissue (12.3 nTPM) for adaptive/innate immune coordination, and adrenal gland (8.5 nTPM), detected in many tissues, immune cell enhanced blood cell specificity, and cell type enhanced single cell specificity. ClinVar documents 19 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants including large copy number losses on chromosome 1p: 1p31.3-22.3 (chr1:66341994-88260975x1, chr1:61397219-85940743x1, chr1:65412037-95735764x1), 1p31.1-22.2 (chr1:76492334-91166951x1, chr1:73616197-87012961x1, chr1:68180293-92731957), and gains at 1p34.1-22.2 (chr1:44475302-89585894x3) and 1p31.1-22.2 (chr1:80804502-89490384x3), plus splice variants c.848-109C>G and c.1110+5G>C potentially disrupting channel function; these CNVs are associated with immunodeficiency and cancer susceptibility. Literature (45 papers) reveals MCOLN2 as a host restriction factor for Salmonella Typhi where human genetic variants enhance magnesium deprivation in phagosomes limiting bacterial replication, promotes prostate cancer progression via IL-1β/NF-κB pathway activation through endolysosomal calcium signaling, and functions in colorectal cancer and osteosarcoma; high conservation from mouse (Q8K595, 566 aa) to rat (Q499S1, 566 aa) underscores essential physiological role; ARF6 interaction suggests MCOLN2 trafficking is regulated during immune activation; CD63 colocalization confirms endolysosomal localization where MCOLN2 modulates vesicular pH and ion homeostasis affecting pathogen survival and cytokine processing.
