## Description
ARID3B (AT-Rich Interactive Domain-Containing Protein 3B, UniProt Q8IVW6) is a 561 aa nuclear transcription factor belonging to the ARID/BRIGHT family (IPR001606/IPR023334/IPR036431/IPR045147/PF01388/PTHR15348, positions 215-308/419-517/196-323/6-515/217-303/6-515) that functions as a DNA-binding regulator of stem cell programs, tumor suppression, and craniofacial development. The molecular architecture consists of an N-terminal ARID DNA-binding domain (G3DSA:1.10.150.60/SSF50110, positions 196-323/204-329) forming a helix-turn-helix fold for AT-rich DNA sequence recognition, a C-terminal REKLES domain (IPR023334, positions 419-517) of unknown function potentially mediating protein-protein interactions, and extensive intrinsically disordered regions (AlphaFold pLDDT 58.0) enabling liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS) for transcriptional condensate formation. Literature (91 papers) establishes ARID3B roles in: (1) nonsyndromic cleft lip/palate susceptibility through LLPS-mediated transcriptional programs (2025), (2) ischemia-reperfusion-induced acute kidney injury via circ_0067098/miR-671-5p/ARID3B axis (2025), (3) Ser89 phosphorylation controlling nuclear localization and transcriptional activity (2026), (4) gastric cancer tumor suppression with better prognosis prediction (2023), (5) stem cell program regulation across multiple tumor types.
