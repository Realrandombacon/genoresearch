# Uncharacterized Locus at 22q13.33 within Phelan-McDermid Syndrome Region with Extreme Pathogenic CNV Burden

## Description
LOC105373100 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome 22q13.33 (NC_000022.11:50738168-50735828) spanning approximately 2.3 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of canonical protein-coding function. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000225929 but reported no detectable protein expression data, indicating this locus is not represented in the HPA protein atlas. However, ClinVar analysis reveals 69 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region, predominantly copy number losses (deletions): chr22:50682948-50759338 (~76 kb focal 22q13.33 del), chr22:50083300-50808467 (~725 kb), chr22:44549957-50789329 (~6.2 Mb terminal 22q del), chr22:49757859-50740457 (~983 kb), chr22:44702479-50806138 (~6.1 Mb), chr22:44245760-50806121 (~6.6 Mb), chr22:49181210-50759297 (~1.6 Mb), chr22:46467175-50759338 (~4.3 Mb), chr22:45819932-50737806 (~4.9 Mb). These structural variants are associated with Phelan-McDermid syndrome (22q13 deletion syndrome) featuring global developmental delay, severe speech impairment or absent speech, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, hypotonia, and dysmorphic features. The 22q13.33 region contains SHANK3 (SH3 and multiple ankyrin repeat domains 3), a critical postsynaptic scaffolding protein essential for excitatory synapse formation and function, whose haploinsufficiency is the primary driver of Phelan-McDermid syndrome pathogenesis. LOC105373100 likely functions as part of the 22q13.33 cis-regulatory landscape or encodes a non-coding RNA whose copy number loss contributes to 22q13 deletion syndrome pathogenesis through disruption of chromatin topology, topologically associating domain (TAD) boundaries, or long-range dysregulation of SHANK3 and other dosage-sensitive synaptic genes. The absence of protein-coding features combined with extreme pathogenic CNV burden (69 variants, highest among analyzed LOC genes) indicates this locus resides within the most dosage-sensitive telomeric region of chromosome 22 where hemizygosity severely disrupts neurodevelopmental processes via SHANK3 haploinsufficiency and 3D genome reorganization.
