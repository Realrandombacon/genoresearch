# ZNF425: KRAB Zinc Finger Transcriptional Repressor with Ubiquitous Expression and 7q36.1 Dosage Sensitivity

**Date:** 2026-03-23T00:21:48.450249

## Description
ZNF425 (zinc finger protein 425) encodes a 752 amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger transcriptional repressor (UniProt Q6IV72) localized to the nucleoplasm. The protein features a canonical N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (InterPro IPR001909, IPR036051, Pfam PF01352, residues 9-80) that recruits KAP1/TRIM28 corepressors, followed by multiple C2H2-type zinc finger DNA-binding motifs (IPR013087, PF00096 at positions 190-380) and an Ikaros C2H2-type zinc-finger family domain (IPR050589, residues 496-640). HPA data indicates low tissue specificity with expression detected in many tissues, consistent with a housekeeping transcriptional repression function. ClinVar documents 64 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly large copy number losses and gains spanning 7q33-36.3, indicating extreme dosage sensitivity at the 7q36.1 locus. The KRAB domain mechanistically recruits heterochromatin-forming complexes including SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and DNA methyltransferases to silence target genes. As a member of the Ikaros zinc finger family, ZNF425 likely recognizes GC-rich promoter/enhancer motifs and establishes repressive chromatin states. Haploinsufficiency or triplosensitivity disrupts transcriptional homeostasis, potentially contributing to 7q terminal deletion syndrome phenotypes including growth retardation, craniofacial anomalies, and intellectual disability. The ubiquitous expression pattern suggests ZNF425 maintains basal repression of developmental genes across somatic tissues.
