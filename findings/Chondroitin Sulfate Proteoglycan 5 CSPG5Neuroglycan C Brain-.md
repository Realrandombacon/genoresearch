The protein belongs to the chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan family with specific neural CSPG cytoplasmic domain (IPR009505, positions 447-565; PF06567, positions 447-565) and sulfate attachment domain (IPR010555, positions 33-277; PF06566, positions 33-277), classified within the CSPG5-related family (PTHR15381, positions 95-559). We hypothesize CSPG5 functions as a brain-enriched chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan that regulates synaptic plasticity and elimination through interactions with receptor tyrosine phosphatases (PTPRZ1) and growth factors (PTN, FBLN2), modulating extracellular matrix stiffness and signaling gradients critical for cerebellar development. The low-confidence structure (pLDDT 51.8) reflects the flexible nature of the chondroitin sulfate attachment domain (positions 33-277) required for glycosaminoglycan chain display. The cytoplasmic domain (positions 447-565) may mediate intracellular trafficking or signaling crosstalk. Interactions with other CSPGs (NCAN, BCAN) and glypicans (GPC2) suggest cooperative roles in ECM organization. The 8 ClinVar CNVs indicate 3p dosage sensitivity affecting neurodevelopmental programs. CSPG5 represents an uncharacterized neural proteoglycan linking ECM remodeling with synaptic pruning, glioma progression, and lens pathology through chondroitin sulfate chain-dependent mechanisms.
