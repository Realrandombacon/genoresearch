## Description
ZNF621 encodes a 439 amino acid C2H2 zinc finger transcription factor containing an N-terminal KRAB repressor domain (IPR001909, PF01352, residues 10-83) and three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (IPR013087, PF00096, residues 151-234). AlphaFold prediction yields medium confidence (pLDDT=63.0), indicating a partially structured architecture with ordered zinc finger DNA-binding regions and flexible linker regions typical of KRAB zinc finger proteins.\n\nHPA expression profiling demonstrates ubiquitous transcription across all tissues (low tissue specificity) with nuclear speckle subcellular localization. This pan-tissue expression pattern suggests a fundamental transcriptional regulatory role rather than tissue-specific function. Nuclear speckle localization is consistent with roles in mRNA processing coordination or transcriptional hub organization.\n\nSTRING interaction network identifies 10 protein partners with LRIG2 (leucine-rich repeat immunoglobulin protein, negative growth factor signaling, score 0.786, high confidence) as the strongest interactor. Additional partners include CBX5 (chromobox homolog 5, heterochromatin protein HP1-alpha, 0.636), TRIM28 (transcription intermediary factor 1-beta, KRAB corepressor, 0.585), C7orf31 (chromosome 7 ORF, 0.505), ZNF37A (zinc finger protein 37A, 0.486), TRIM39 (E3 ubiquitin ligase, 0.477), LRP4 (LDL receptor-related protein 4, Wnt signaling, 0.456), DCAF16 (DDB1-CUL4 substrate receptor, 0.453), CLHC1 (chloride channel protein, 0.450), and NR2C2 (nuclear receptor subfamily 2 group C, 0.450). The TRIM28 and CBX5 interactions are particularly notable—TRIM28 is the canonical KRAB domain corepressor that recruits heterochromatin machinery via HP1 (CBX5) binding.\n\nClinVar contains 8 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, predominantly 3p copy number gains and losses, indicating dosage sensitivity at the 3p22.1 locus.\n\nConservation across mammals is evident: bovine (419 aa), puma (439 aa), baboon (504 aa) orthologs show length conservation, indicating functional constraint on the KRAB-zinc finger architecture.\n\nMechanistic hypothesis: ZNF621 functions as a sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional repressor that recruits heterochromatin silencing machinery via the KRAB-TRIM28-CBX5 pathway. The three C2H2 zinc fingers likely recognize specific DNA sequence motifs in target gene promoters or enhancers. KRAB domain recruits TRIM28 (KAP1), which scaffolds SETDB1 histone methyltransferase and HP1 (CBX5) to establish H3K9me3 heterochromatin marks. Ubiquitous expression and nuclear speckle localization suggest ZNF621 may coordinate transcriptional silencing with mRNA splicing or processing. LRIG2 interaction (highest score) suggests potential crosstalk with growth factor signaling pathways. ZNF621 dysfunction may contribute to developmental disorders through dysregulated target gene expression.
