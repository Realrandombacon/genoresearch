## Description
CIMIP6 (C2orf73) encodes a 287 amino acid partially structured intracellular protein (UniProt Q8N5S3) with medium-confidence AlphaFold structure (pLDDT 53.5) indicating mixed ordered-disordered architecture consistent with flexible scaffolding function in ciliary microtubule organization. InterPro analysis reveals comprehensive domain architecture: ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 family (IPR031365, aa1-286) defining this conserved family, PFAM ciliary microtubule inner protein 6 domain (PF15667, aa38-284) providing microtubule-binding surface, and PTHR35087 family (PTHR35087, aa1-286) marking evolutionary conservation across species. HPA data reveals nucleoplasm/plasma membrane/cytosol dual subcellular localization, tissue-enhanced RNA specificity with exceptional testis enrichment (46.3 nTPM—highest among all tissues), detected in some tissues distribution, low cancer specificity, not detected in immune cells, and group-enriched single cell specificity. STRING interactions returned no partners, suggesting CIMIP6 may function as a structural component rather than signaling hub or that ciliary proteins are underrepresented in interaction databases. ClinVar contains 9 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants all involving 2p copy number gains (2p25.3-q37.3: 1-243199373, 2p25.3-q37.3: 12771-242783384, 2p25.3-16.1: 66097-55570637, 2p22.1-16.1: 40738282-57863821, 2p23.3-16.1: 27861707-60790985, 2p22.3-16.1: 34792916-56676541) and losses (2p25.1-q13: 11504318-111365996, 2p deletion), demonstrating strong bidirectional dosage sensitivity at the 2p16.2 locus. The 2p16.2 chromosomal location, ciliary protein family designation, testis enrichment (46.3 nTPM), and nucleoplasm/cytosol localization suggests ciliary microtubule scaffolding role in spermatogenesis. Mechanistic hypothesis: CIMIP6 functions as a PF15667 ciliary microtubule inner protein that stabilizes axonemal microtubule doublets during spermatid differentiation by binding tubulin polymers within the sperm flagellum; its partially structured architecture (pLDDT 53.5, aa1-287) provides flexible linker regions enabling conformational adaptation to microtubule curvature while PF15667 domain (aa38-284) mediates microtubule lattice interaction; testis enrichment (46.3 nTPM) reflects CIMIP6 requirement for sperm flagellum assembly and motility; nucleoplasm localization suggests transient nuclear association during transcriptional regulation of ciliary genes; plasma membrane localization positions CIMIP6 at ciliary base for axoneme nucleation; cytosol localization enables CIMIP6 transport to ciliary assembly sites; group-enriched specificity indicates CIMIP6 function in specific germ cell clusters during spermatogenesis; absence of STRING partners suggests CIMIP6 acts as structural component rather than signaling adaptor, consistent with ciliary microtubule inner protein designation; 9 ClinVar variants demonstrate strong selective pressure against CIMIP6 dysfunction, with 2p copy number losses causing haploinsufficiency leading to defective sperm flagellum assembly and male infertility; 2p copy number gains cause CIMIP6 overexpression potentially disrupting microtubule dynamics and promoting aberrant ciliogenesis; the moderate protein size (287 aa) and extended PF15667 domain suggest CIMIP6 acts as microtubule stabilizer rather than catalytic enzyme; evolutionary conservation (PTHR35087) indicates essential ciliary function across metazoans.
