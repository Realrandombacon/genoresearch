## Description
LOC401589 is an uncharacterized genomic locus on chromosome Xp11.22 (NC_000023.11:52054254-52051170) spanning approximately 3.1 kb. Comprehensive database searches reveal no evidence of canonical protein-coding function: no UniProt accession was identified, precluding InterPro domain analysis and AlphaFold structure prediction. STRING database returned no protein interaction partners, confirming absence of characterized interactome. Human Protein Atlas identified Ensembl ID ENSG00000182776 but reported no detectable protein expression data in surveyed tissues. However, ClinVar analysis reveals an extraordinary 80 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in this region - the highest CNV burden observed in this dark gene survey to date. Variants include massive X chromosome copy number alterations: whole-arm duplications (trisomy) spanning Xp22.33-q28 (chrX:2757837-156030895, ~153.3 Mb; chrX:26102-155996431, ~155.9 Mb; chrX:10679-156022206, ~156.0 Mb) and deletions (monosomy) spanning Xp22.33-q28 (chrX:14245-155999293, ~156.0 Mb; chrX:26102-155996431, ~155.9 Mb; chrX:10679-156013167, ~156.0 Mb; chrX:10679-156022206, ~156.0 Mb), plus focal Xp11.22 deletion (chrX:51707794-53229764, ~1.5 Mb). These structural variants are associated with X-linked intellectual disability, developmental delay, epilepsy, and dysmorphic features. The Xp11.22 region contains several dosage-sensitive genes including KDM5C (lysine demethylase 5C, X-linked intellectual disability), PHF6 (PHD finger protein 6, Börjeson-Forssman-Lehmann syndrome), and TCEAL4 (transcription elongation factor A-like 4, NF-kappaB signaling regulator). LOC401589 likely functions as a critical X chromosome cis-regulatory element (enhancer/silencer/TAD boundary) or encodes an X-linked long non-coding RNA (lncRNA) whose copy number alteration causes severe neurodevelopmental phenotypes through: (1) X chromosome inactivation (XCI) escape disruption, (2) dosage-sensitive transcriptional regulation of X-linked neuronal genes, (3) chromatin topology disruption affecting X chromosome 3D organization, or (4) position effect variegation on neighboring critical genes. The extreme pathogenic CNV burden (80 variants) on the X chromosome - where hemizygosity in males amplifies dosage effects - indicates this locus represents one of the most dosage-sensitive genomic elements in the human genome, with both hemizygosity and trisomy causing severe neurodevelopmental disruption.
