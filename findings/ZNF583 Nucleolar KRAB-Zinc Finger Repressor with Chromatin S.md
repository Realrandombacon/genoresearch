# ZNF583: Nucleolar KRAB-Zinc Finger Repressor with Chromatin Silencing Function

**Date:** 2026-03-23T04:02:04.669785

**Quality Score:** 2.25/10  (E=4.2, D=0.3) [WEAK]

## Description
ZNF583 encodes a 569-amino acid Krüppel-associated box (KRAB) zinc finger protein (UniProt Q96ND8) with a canonical domain architecture: an N-terminal KRAB repression domain (PF01352, IPR001909, residues 5-77) followed by three C-terminal C2H2 zinc finger DNA-binding domains (PF00096, IPR013087, residues 212-295). The KRAB domain recruits TRIM28/KAP1 corepressor (STRING score 0.522), which scaffolds heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) and SETDB1 histone methyltransferase to deposit H3K9me3 repressive marks. HPA data shows broad expression across tissues with nucleolar and cytosolic localization, suggesting potential roles in ribosomal RNA regulation or nucleolar stress response. STRING partners include WDR17 (0.511), TMEM247 (0.511), and BEND4 (0.483), plus paralogous ZNF570 (0.471), indicating potential heteromeric KRAB-ZFP complexes. ClinVar documents 18 pathogenic copy number variants spanning 19q13.33-q13.43, demonstrating dosage sensitivity; deletions and duplications in this region are associated with developmental phenotypes though ZNF583-specific disease remains undefined. Mouse ortholog (Q3V080, 568 aa) shows strong conservation, indicating functional constraint. The three zinc fingers likely recognize GC-rich DNA sequences, positioning ZNF583 as a sequence-specific transcriptional silencer potentially regulating genes involved in chromatin organization, nucleolar function, or developmental patterning.
